# Integrated Regulatory Reporting System

Concept node for the CBUAE's Integrated Regulatory Reporting System, the mandated channel through which Licensed Financial Institutions submit regulatory returns at the prescribed frequency and format, and through which the Central Bank publishes changes to reporting obligations via system alerts. Institutions must monitor those alerts during the working day and maintain a formal internal process to meet filing duties. Relevant to all Licensed Financial Institutions; failure to file or filing incorrect data is a sanctionable violation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
