# Article 12: Cancellation of Previous Resolution and Circulars

Article 12 of the CBUAE Restricted Licence Banks Regulation lists the earlier Board resolutions and Board circulars that the Regulation repeals and replaces, and sits alongside Article 11 (the Regulatory Development Division as interpretive authority) and Article 13 (publication in the Official Gazette and entry into force one month after publication). It is a transitional/repeal provision rather than a conduct obligation. Useful for confirming which legacy instruments ceased to apply to Restricted Licence Banks and from what date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
