# Cabinet Resolution No. 13 of 2013 - Fees on Third Party Health Insurance Administrators

Cabinet Resolution No. 13 of 2013 prescribes the schedule of fees payable to the Insurance Authority by third party health insurance administrators (licence examination, registration and renewal of companies and branches, data amendments, grievances, official extracts) and sets administrative fines for late renewal or late annotation of register data. It binds TPAs licensed by the IA to administer health insurance claims. Use it for fee amounts, penalty calculation (per month of delay with annual caps) and effective-date questions.
