# Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation (Circular 25/2022)

Node for the Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation (Circular 25/2022) as the instrument under which the insurance-sector risk management and internal control standards are issued. It establishes the obligation to establish, implement and maintain proportionate risk management and internal control systems and assigns ultimate responsibility for risk oversight to the board. Binds insurance and Takaful companies licensed by the Central Bank and their group entities.
