# Principle 3 - Incorporation into Business Strategy

Principle 3 requires climate transition planning to form part of, and be aligned with, the financial firm's activities on climate-related risks and opportunities and its business strategy: embedding it in short-, medium- and long-term business strategies and board-approved strategic plans, considering transition impacts on the business model, customer base, stakeholders, operations, policies and financial planning, and embedding transition considerations in the risk management framework including risk appetite and scenario analysis. Expressed as 'should' guidance for UAE financial firms, with overlap into prudential risk governance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
