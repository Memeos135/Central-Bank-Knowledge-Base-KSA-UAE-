# External Ratings-Based Approach (SEC-ERBA)

Node covering the external ratings-based approach within the CBUAE securitisation capital framework, one of the hierarchy of methods for assigning risk weights to securitisation exposures. The surrounding provisions address how the exposure amount is determined (100 percent credit conversion factor for non-mitigant off-balance-sheet facilities, counterparty credit risk treatment for securitisation-related derivatives), the multiplication of exposure amount by the applicable risk weight, risk-weight caps, overlapping exposures, and deduction from CET1 of equity increases arising from securitisation. Binds UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

The ECAI eligibility criteria that govern the standardised approach carry through to SEC-ERBA, which is one of only two permitted securitisation approaches alongside SEC-SA.
