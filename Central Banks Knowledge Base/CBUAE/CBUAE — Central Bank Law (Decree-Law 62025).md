# Central Bank Law (Decree-Law 6/2025)

This node captures the Central Bank Law as defined for these instruments: Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 on the Central Bank, Regulation of Financial Institutions and Activities, and Insurance Business, which is the enabling statute for the Insurance Company Licensing Regulation (Circular No. 4/2026). It also carries the regulation's definitional context, including Authorized Manager, Beneficiary, Board, Branch, Company, Control Function and Corporate Governance, and the companion instruments the regulation must be read with (Commercial Companies Law, Financial Free Zones Law, minimum capital and financial regulations decisions). It binds all insurance Companies, including Takaful insurers and branches of Foreign Insurance Companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
