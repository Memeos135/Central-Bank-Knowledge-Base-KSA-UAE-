# Net Interest Income (NII)

Defines Net Interest Income (NII) for the purposes of the Model Management Standards/Guidance, expressly covering both conventional net interest income and Islamic net profit income, as total interest (profit) income less expense over a horizon net of hedging; ∆NII (earnings at risk, EAR) is the change under stressed rate scenarios versus reporting-date rates. It sits alongside EVE as the earnings-based IRRBB metric and requires the underlying models to follow the model life-cycle, with assumptions escalated beyond ALM or market risk to a governance forum reporting to the Model Oversight Committee. Binds CBUAE-supervised institutions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
