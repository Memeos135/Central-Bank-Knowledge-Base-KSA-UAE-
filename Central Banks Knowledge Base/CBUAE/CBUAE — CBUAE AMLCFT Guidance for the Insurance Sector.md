# CBUAE AML/CFT Guidance for the Insurance Sector

CBUAE guidance applying the UAE AML/CFT framework to the insurance sector, mapping sector participants (conventional and Takaful insurers, reinsurers, tied agents, brokers, health insurance third-party administrators) and the product split between life/investment-related insurance and general insurance. It states that only life and other investment-related insurance products fall within the AML/CFT obligations under the AML-CFT Decision, making product classification the gateway to scope. It binds CBUAE-licensed insurers, reinsurers, agents and brokers, which must each assess their own ML/FT risks.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
