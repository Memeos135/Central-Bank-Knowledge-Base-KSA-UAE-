# Risk Management Regulation and Standards

Cross-reference node: the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation and Standards must be read together with the Risk Management Regulation and Standards (which set banks' overarching risk approach and define the Risk Governance Framework) and the Operational Risk Management Regulation and Standards (business continuity and disaster recovery). It frames the scope rule that the outsourcing requirements apply to all Banks in the UAE on a solo and Group-wide basis, and the core principle that outsourcing must not weaken obligations to customers or impede Central Bank supervision, with Banks remaining fully responsible for outsourced risks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Market Risk Regulation|Market Risk Regulation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are assessing whether a bank's market risk limit framework is adequate, you must satisfy both the general risk management regime and the specialised market risk regime, which state the same core requirement in materially identical terms: limits calibrated to risk appetite, risk profile and capital strength, matched to management capability, and actually communicated to and understood by relevant staff. The overlap means the market risk regulation operates as the detailed expression of a duty already owed under the enterprise-wide risk management framework, so board and senior management accountability runs through both. Practically, a deficiency in market risk limits is simultaneously a governance failure under the risk management regulation, and remediation should be documented against both instruments rather than one.
