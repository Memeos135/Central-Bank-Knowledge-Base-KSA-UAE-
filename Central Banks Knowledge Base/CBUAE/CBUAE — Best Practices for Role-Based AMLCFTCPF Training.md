# Best Practices for Role-Based AML/CFT/CPF Training

CBUAE best-practices guidance on role-based AML/CFT/CPF training for licensed financial institutions, setting out how training should be tailored to the specific ML/TF/PF risks and responsibilities of particular roles rather than delivered as generic awareness training. It situates role-based training within a wider programme (new-hire, annual enterprise-wide, group-mandatory, localized, and board/senior-management training) and frames content by reference to UAE regulatory requirements and supervisory guidance, global financial-crime compliance standards, internal policies, and the institution's products, customers and geographies. Addressed to LFIs, including registered hawala providers, and expressed as expectations under a risk-based approach rather than hard rules.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
