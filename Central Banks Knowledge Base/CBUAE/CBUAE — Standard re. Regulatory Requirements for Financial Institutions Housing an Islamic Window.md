# Standard re. Regulatory Requirements for Financial Institutions Housing an Islamic Window

CBUAE Standard setting minimum regulatory and supervisory requirements for conventional financial institutions that house an Islamic Window, aimed at sound Shari'ah governance, financial stability and consumer protection. It covers scope (solo and group-wide, including subsidiaries, affiliates and international branches), Islamic Window organisation and the Head of Islamic Window (CBUAE approval and reporting lines), asset-liability segregation, separate liquidity reporting, treatment of non-Shari'ah-compliant income, separate branding, and separate regulatory reporting. It binds all Institutions housing an Islamic Window and must be read with HSA standards and resolutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection, other
