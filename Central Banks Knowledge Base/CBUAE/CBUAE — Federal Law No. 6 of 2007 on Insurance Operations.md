# Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 on Insurance Operations

Reference node for UAE Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 on the Organization of Insurance Operations, its amendments and Executive Regulations — the primary insurance statute that the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation and Standards supplement rather than replace. It is cited as part of the legal basis and hierarchy for insurance-sector rules, alongside the Central Bank Law and legacy Insurance Authority decisions. Relevant when tracing the statutory authority behind an insurance governance obligation or the scope of defined insurance activities and intermediaries.
