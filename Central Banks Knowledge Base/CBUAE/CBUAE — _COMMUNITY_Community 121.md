# AML/CFT Financial Institution Obligations

## Why this community

Core AML/CFT obligations imposed on financial institutions: the statutory offence and duty framework, its implementing executive regulation, and the supervisory procedures that operationalise CDD, reporting and controls.

## How members connect

- Clean instrument hierarchy: AML decree-law → cabinet-level executive regulation → board-level supervisory AML/CFT procedures, each narrowing the preceding layer.
- The supervisory procedures cite both the law and the executive regulation as their legal basis, so obligations must be read cumulatively rather than substituted.
- The earlier AML circular is carried forward by reference, raising a supersession question about which text governs where the older guidance conflicts.
- 'Financial Institution' is the defined scope term that determines who bears the obligations; its perimeter differs from customer/consumer definitions used in conduct regimes and should be checked before applying duties cross-regime.
