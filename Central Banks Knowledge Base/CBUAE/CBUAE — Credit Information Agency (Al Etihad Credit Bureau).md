# Credit Information Agency (Al Etihad Credit Bureau)

Defines "Credit Information Agency" as Al Etihad Credit Bureau and any other credit information agency established for credit reporting purposes, within a regulation whose "Customer" is limited to SMEs obtaining or prospectively obtaining financial products/services. The operative consequence is that Licensed Financial Institutions should obtain the relevant credit bureau details before extending credit, alongside monitoring of credit-granting and sales representatives and complaint-handling duties. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions (banks and finance companies) in their SME dealings.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection, BNPL/finance
