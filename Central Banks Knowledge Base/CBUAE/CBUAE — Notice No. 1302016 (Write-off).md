# Notice No. 130/2016 (Write-off)

A repealed CBUAE notice on write-off of fully provisioned non-performing loans, cancelled by Article 17 of the Credit Risk Management Regulation. Write-off duties now sit in Article 12, which requires a Board-approved non-performing asset strategy, timely full or partial write-off where there is no reasonable recovery expectation, and realistic recovery assumptions. Useful for dating legacy write-off practice and locating the successor obligation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
