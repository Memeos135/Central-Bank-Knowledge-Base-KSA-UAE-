# CDD and EDD Measures (TBML)

The operative obligations section of CBUAE's TBML guidance, anchoring customer due diligence timing and minimum content (customer and beneficial owner identification and verification, understanding the nature of the business and purpose of the relationship, ongoing monitoring) in the UAE AML-CFT Decision, and applying it to trade. It stresses that the institution must identify which party to a trade transaction is its own customer — due diligence is not owed on every counterparty — and that open account trade warrants heightened attention and business-model enquiry, with EDD for higher-risk customers. Binds licensed financial institutions under UAE law; for KSA work it is persuasive comparison only, as SAMA/AML Law obligations govern.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
