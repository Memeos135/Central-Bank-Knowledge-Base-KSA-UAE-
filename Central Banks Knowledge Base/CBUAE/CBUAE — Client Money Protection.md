# Client Money Protection

The client money and disclosure provisions of the CBUAE loan-based crowdfunding framework: a crowdfunding company may not take full ownership of client funds, must hold funds received or disbursed on a client's behalf in segregated/escrow accounts in the client's name, must have those accounts externally audited monthly (Category 1) or quarterly (Category 2), and may only place client funds with Central Bank-licensed local retail banks. Adjacent requirements cover disclosure of terms and conditions and material risk warnings, information sharing with Al Etihad Credit Bureau, conflict-of-interest situations, the prohibition on shareholders/directors/employees borrowing on the platform, the bar on giving advice or directly marketing offers, and dispute handling, grievance redress and collections policies. Binds crowdfunding companies conducting loan-based crowdfunding activities in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, payments, other
