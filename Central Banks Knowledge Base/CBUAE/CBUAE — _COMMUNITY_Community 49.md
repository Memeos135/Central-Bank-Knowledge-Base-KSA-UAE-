# Insurance Dispute Resolution

## Why this community

Institutional architecture for resolving insurance complaints outside the courts — the Ombudsman Unit, its funding and its appeal/escalation path — with an adjacent banking prudential item on own-share acquisition limits.

## How members connect

- The Establishment Regulation is the constituting instrument; the Ombudsman Unit is the obligated body, and Complaint/Determination are its defined inputs and outputs.
- Obligation chain: a Complaint is decided by a Determination, which is reviewable by the Appeals Committee, with the Insurance Dispute Resolution Committee as the parallel/alternative statutory forum — scope boundaries between these fora matter for a decision.
- Funding is tied to the Unit by the Annual Levy and Case Fee, making participating insurers cost-bearing as well as subject to outcomes.
- The Banks' Acquisition of Own Shares Regulation and its 10% ceiling sit here only via the drafting/issuing Regulatory Development Division, not by subject matter — treat as a separate prudential regime.
