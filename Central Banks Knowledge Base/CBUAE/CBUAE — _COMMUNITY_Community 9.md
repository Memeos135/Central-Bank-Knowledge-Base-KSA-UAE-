# Sanctions & Proliferation Red Flags

## Why this community

Targeted financial sanctions, terrorist financing and proliferation financing: the legal duty to screen, freeze and report without delay, plus the typology/red-flag material used to detect TF and sanctions-evasion behaviour, including risks arising from new payment products and services.

## How members connect

- Law-to-guidance chain: the AML/CTF law and its implementing Cabinet Decision set the freezing and reporting duties; central-bank guidance and the Executive Office guidance translate them into supervisory expectations for FIs and DNFBPs.
- Red-flag sets for TF and for sanctions circumvention are derivative indicators — they inform suspicion and escalation, but the enforceable obligation remains in the law/decision, not in the indicator lists.
- International sources (FATF standards and typologies, FFIEC manual, FINTRAC indicators) are cited as persuasive benchmarks; they shape risk assessment but are not themselves binding instruments.
- New payment products and services connect the sanctions regime to the payments/PSP regime, where anonymity, speed and cross-border reach change the screening and CDD risk profile.
