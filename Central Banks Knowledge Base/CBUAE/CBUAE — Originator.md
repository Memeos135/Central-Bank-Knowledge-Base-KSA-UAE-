# Originator

Covers the Originator role and the originator-side information set required to accompany a qualifying virtual asset transfer, including full name, wallet or account number (or a unique traceability reference where none exists), and address, national identity or travel document number, customer identification number, and date and place of birth. The originator's VASP must not execute a transfer unless this data is collected, verified and transmitted immediately and securely, subject to the rule's threshold and exemption paragraphs; beneficiary-side VASPs and intermediaries must retain it and report systemic failures to supply it. Binds VASPs and Intermediary Providers within the UAE territorial scope.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
