# Multiple Invoicing

A TBML typology entry within CBUAE guidance on trade-based money laundering, sitting alongside over-/under-invoicing and over-/under-shipment in the typologies section. It describes how the same shipment of goods or services can be invoiced more than once to justify repeated payments and thereby move illicit value under the cover of a single underlying trade transaction. It is directed at licensed financial institutions (including trade finance providers) as risk-understanding material supporting their CDD, transaction monitoring and suspicious-activity detection duties; note this is UAE (CBUAE), not SAMA, guidance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
