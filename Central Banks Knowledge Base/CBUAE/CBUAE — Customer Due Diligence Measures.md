# Customer Due Diligence Measures

Node covering the customer due diligence and beneficial owner identification provisions (Division Three, Articles 6–15) of the UAE Cabinet Resolution issuing the Executive Regulations to the AML/CTF/PF Decree-Law. It sets when identity of the Customer and Beneficial Owner must be verified (before or during onboarding, with a limited low-risk deferral), the triggers for applying CDD (new business relationship, suspicion of a Crime, doubts about previously obtained data, occasional transactions), application of CDD to pre-existing relationships on a risk/materiality basis, the prohibition on establishing or continuing a relationship where CDD cannot be completed, the tipping-off carve-out with an STR to the Unit, and the bar on dealing with Shell Banks. It binds Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and Virtual Asset Service Providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
