# Federal Law No. 1 of 2006 (Electronic Commerce)

A cross-reference node for UAE Federal Law No. 1 of 2006 on Electronic Commerce and Transactions as it is invoked in the Insurance Authority's Electronic Insurance Regulations (Board Resolution No. 18 of 2020). It is cited both as a legal basis for that resolution and as the residual legal framework governing electronic conclusion of insurance contracts and other e-insurance matters not specifically addressed by the regulations. Practically, it binds licensed insurance companies, insurance-related professions and banks marketing policies electronically, whose e-contracting validity rests on the electronic transactions law.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
