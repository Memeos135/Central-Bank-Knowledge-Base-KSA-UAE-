# Key Facts Statement

Defined-term node for the Key Facts Statement: a concise, plain-language summary of the key features and risks of a financial product or service provided to a Consumer. It is the anchor for pre-contractual disclosure obligations under the Consumer Protection Regulation and related standards, and sits alongside defined terms such as Lender/Financier, Credit Products, Credit Information Agency and Personal Data. Relevant to all Licensed Financial Institutions marketing or selling products to Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info

- **What this link tells you:** For a decision on whether a consumer-facing product meets the disclosure and transparency obligation, the Key Facts Statement is the operative instrument through which that obligation is discharged, alongside terms and conditions, product disclosure documents and application forms. The cross-reference links the general transparency duty to the specific content requirements for KFS documents, including warnings to Consumers about the consequences of failing to meet their obligations to the Licensed Financial Institution. So a transparency assessment should be evidenced at KFS level: if the required warnings and disclosures are absent or buried, the institution is exposed under the disclosure article even if other marketing material is accurate.
