# Politically Exposed Person (PEP)

Covers the Politically Exposed Person concept and the mandatory PEP check under the CBUAE exchange business framework: all PEPs, whether natural-person customers or beneficial owners of legal persons/arrangements, must undergo EDD regardless of transaction type or value, and CID/CDD alone is insufficient. Adjacent provisions address related higher-risk categories such as non-profit organisations, shell banks/companies, and PEP screening of all parties in trade-related and third-party remittances. Binds Licensed Persons and their compliance function.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS

- **What this link tells you:** If a customer, or the beneficial owner of a legal person or arrangement, is identified as a PEP, the due diligence decision is already made: enhanced due diligence applies, and neither transaction size nor apparent low risk permits standard or simplified measures. The PEP provisions operate as a mandatory trigger into the EDD requirements within the wider CDD framework, which also governs review, updating and reliance on third parties.
