# Regulation of Insurance Dispute Committees (VER1)

Version 1 of the UAE Insurance Authority's regulation establishing standing committees to settle and resolve insurance disputes (IA Board resolution). It sets out definitions (Company, Insured, Beneficiary, Insurance Dispute, Complainant, Defendant), the committees' formation, reporting to the Authority, procedural powers outside ordinary civil procedure rules, jurisdictional scope over disputes of any value against licensed insurers, and express carve-outs (interim/summary matters and attachment, disputes already pending in court before entry into force, and arbitration-clause disputes). It binds insurance companies licensed in the State, including branches, agents and Takaful operators, and governs the rights of insureds and beneficiaries bringing complaints.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
