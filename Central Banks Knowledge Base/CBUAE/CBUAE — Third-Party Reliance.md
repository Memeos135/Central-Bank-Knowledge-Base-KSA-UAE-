# Third-Party Reliance

Sets out when a CBUAE-licensed financial institution may rely on another financial institution or DNFBP to perform specified CDD elements — customer identification/verification, beneficial owner identification, and understanding the purpose and nature of the relationship — and the conditions attached, including immediate access to the information, a non-objection letter filed with the Central Bank, and retention of ultimate responsibility by the institution's Compliance Officer. It expressly excludes outsourcing, service provider, vendor and agency arrangements from the reliance concept, and adds expectations on written agreements, senior-official approval, ongoing review and termination for deficient third parties. Relevant when assessing analogous third-party CDD reliance questions in other jurisdictions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
