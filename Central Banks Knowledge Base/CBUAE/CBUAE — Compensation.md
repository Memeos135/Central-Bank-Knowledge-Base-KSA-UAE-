# Compensation

Section 12 of the CBUAE Corporate Governance Standards for Banks (83/2019), governing the bank-wide compensation system: the mandate and independence of the compensation committee, its annual review of compensation plans and outcomes, and an external independent assessment at least every five years. It requires Board oversight of remuneration for all staff (not only Senior Management), alignment with strategy and Risk Appetite, contraction of Board compensation and staff bonus pools on negative performance or net loss, and compensation of Control Function staff on terms independent of the business lines they monitor. It binds bank boards, compensation committees and Senior Management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Control Functions|Control Functions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When setting or reviewing a remuneration package, the first question is whether the individual sits within a Control Function, because that classification changes the permitted pay mix rather than merely the amount. The compensation rule expressly keys off the Control Functions definition, and the control-functions provisions set out how those functions must be structured in a branch — locally reporting but accountable to group function heads. Practically, staff identified under that definition must receive a pay structure weighted toward fixed rather than variable compensation, so misclassifying a risk, compliance or audit role creates a remuneration breach as well as an independence weakness.
