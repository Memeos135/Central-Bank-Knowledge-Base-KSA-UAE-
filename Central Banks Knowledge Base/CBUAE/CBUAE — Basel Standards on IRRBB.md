# Basel Standards on IRRBB

Identifies the Basel Committee's April 2016 "Interest rate risk in the banking book" standards as the external reference the Model Management Guidance incorporates alongside the CBUAE's own IRRBB standards. Specific Basel articles are pulled in operationally — e.g. article 132 for gap-based EVE by non-LSIs, articles 120–124 for fixed rate loan prepayment risk (with Table 3 scalers), and articles 125–129 for term deposit early redemption risk — with institutions permitted to substitute documented internal scenario-specific behavioural rates. Relevant to CBUAE-supervised institutions building IRRBB and behavioural models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
