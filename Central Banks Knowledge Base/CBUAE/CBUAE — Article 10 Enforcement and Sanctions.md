# Article 10: Enforcement and Sanctions

Article 10 of the CBUAE Restricted Licence Banks (minimum capital) Regulation sets the consequence side of that instrument: breaching any provision of the Regulation or its accompanying Standards exposes the institution to supervisory, administrative and financial measures at the Central Bank's discretion. It indicates the range of available measures, including curtailing or replacing Senior Management or Board powers, interim management of the bank, fines, and barring individuals from the sector. It binds Restricted Licence Banks and, in effect, their directors and senior officers; read together with Article 9, which applies the general Bank Regulations to these entities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
