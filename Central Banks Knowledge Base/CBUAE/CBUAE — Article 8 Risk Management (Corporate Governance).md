# Article 8: Risk Management (Corporate Governance)

This node covers the risk management article of the Corporate Governance Standards, including senior management's accountability to the board for day-to-day prudent management, implementation of risk management systems, risk culture and internal controls, and non-interference with the independence of risk, compliance, internal audit and Shari'ah control functions. It also addresses group-wide risk management by a bank acting as Controlling Shareholder, local entity boards' assessment of compatibility with local law, and mitigation of risks from complex or non-transparent structures. It binds boards and senior management of licensed banks and their group entities, including foreign branches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
