# Annex 1 Operational Risk Tools

Annex to the CBUAE operational risk instrument listing illustrative tools for identifying and assessing Operational Risk, including event management processes, internal loss/event data collection and analysis (including near misses), and external loss data analysis. It is explanatory rather than prescriptive, supporting the substantive reporting duties (risk profile, limits, residual risks, control deficiencies, root cause analysis, emerging risks). Relevant to licensed financial institutions designing and evidencing their operational risk identification and assessment practices.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
