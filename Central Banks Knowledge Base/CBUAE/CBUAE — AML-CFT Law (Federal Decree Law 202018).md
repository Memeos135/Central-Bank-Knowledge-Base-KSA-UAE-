# AML-CFT Law (Federal Decree Law 20/2018)

Concept node for the UAE's primary AML/CTF statute, Federal Decree-Law 20/2018 (as amended, including by Federal Decree-Law 26/2021), on combating money laundering, terrorist financing and financing of illegal organisations. It is the enabling law underpinning customer due diligence, suspicious transaction reporting and the prompt application of UN Security Council-based targeted financial sanctions directives (Article 16.1). It binds all licensed financial institutions and DNFBPs in the UAE, including licensed exchange houses supervised by CBUAE.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
