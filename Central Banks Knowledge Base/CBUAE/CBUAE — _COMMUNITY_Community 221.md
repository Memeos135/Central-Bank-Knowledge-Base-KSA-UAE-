# Record Keeping

## Why this community

Record-keeping obligations: what documentation a supervised entity must retain, in what form, and for how long, so that transactions, customer files and compliance decisions remain reconstructable for supervisory review.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster; its legal force comes from being an obligation owed to the supervisor rather than to the customer, and it underpins evidentiary proof of compliance with all other articles.
- Interacts across regimes: AML/CTF retention duties (CDD records, transaction records, STR files) typically set the floor, while sector rules (payments, finance/BNPL, consumer protection) add product- and complaint-specific retention.
- Feeds directly into the supervisor's inspection and information-request powers - inability to produce records is itself a compliance failure.
