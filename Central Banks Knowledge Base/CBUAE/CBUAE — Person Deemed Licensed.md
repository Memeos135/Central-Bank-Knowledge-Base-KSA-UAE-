# Person Deemed Licensed

Defined status under the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation for entities treated as licensed for Open Finance purposes without a separate Open Finance Licence. The node clarifies that Technical Service Providers supporting Open Finance Providers or Persons Deemed Licensed do not themselves need a licence provided they do not carry on regulated activities, that regulatory responsibility and liability cannot be outsourced to a Technical Service Provider or other third party, and that the Central Bank may levy licensing and other fees on Open Finance Providers including Persons Deemed Licensed. Relevant to perimeter and outsourcing/accountability analysis for UAE participants.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, data/credit info
