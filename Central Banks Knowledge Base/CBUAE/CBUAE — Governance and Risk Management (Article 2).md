# Governance and Risk Management (Article 2)

Article 2 of the Outsourcing Regulation places full responsibility for outsourcing risk on the bank and requires a documented process for assessing materiality of outsourced activities. The Risk Governance Framework must cover assessment, identification, measurement, monitoring and reporting of outsourcing risk on a bank-wide and, where applicable, group-wide basis, with a Board-approved policy, Board (or Board committee) approval of material arrangements, conflict-of-interest management, clear allocation of roles, and inclusion of outsourced critical functions in disaster recovery and business continuity plans. Banks remain accountable for the service provider's information security and for compliance with all laws applicable to outsourced activities.
