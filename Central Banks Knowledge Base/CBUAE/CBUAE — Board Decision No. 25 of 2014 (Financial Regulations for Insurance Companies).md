# Board Decision No. 25 of 2014 (Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies)

Referenced instrument: Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 25 of 2014 containing the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies, the source of the asset distribution and allocation (investment) limits, capital adequacy requirements and the periodic investment reporting obligations applied by Decision No. 22 of 2017. It sets the prudential baseline for conventional insurers operating in the UAE. Relevant where a question turns on the substantive limit, solvency measure or reporting article rather than its application mechanics.
