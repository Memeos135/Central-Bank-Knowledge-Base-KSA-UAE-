# UNSCR 1373 (2001)

Concept node for UNSCR 1373 (2001) as the basis for domestic and foreign-requested designations, distinct from committee-based UN listing. It underpins the Council's power to list, re-list, de-list and periodically review Local Lists on reasonable grounds and irrespective of criminal proceedings, and to request designations by other states supported by identifying information meeting the 1373 criteria. It binds the Council, the Ministry of Justice in the review process, law enforcement and other state bodies supplying information, and feeds the freezing duties of obliged entities.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
