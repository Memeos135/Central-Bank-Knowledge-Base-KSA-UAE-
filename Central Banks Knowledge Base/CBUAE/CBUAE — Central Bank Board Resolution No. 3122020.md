# Central Bank Board Resolution No. 31/2/2020

A CBUAE Board of Directors resolution amending the Regulations Regarding Mortgage Loans (C 31/2013) and reflected in the consolidated version as of 8 April 2020, following the earlier amendment by Resolution 96/2019. It operates on the mortgage lending framework rather than creating one, and binds all mortgage loan providers, including banks and finance companies. Consult it to date the current wording and identify which mortgage requirements were revised.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
