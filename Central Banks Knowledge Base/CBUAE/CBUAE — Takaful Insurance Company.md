# Takaful Insurance Company

Defined-term node for the Takaful Insurance Company under the CBUAE External Shari'ah Audit Standard: an insurer incorporated and operating under the Insurance Law, its Executive Regulation and the Takaful Insurance Regulation, with all business and activities conducted in accordance with Islamic Shari'ah Provisions. The node also carries the Standard's scope provisions, applying to all incorporated Takaful companies, including those with group relationships, on voluntary or mandated appointment of an External Shari'ah Auditor. Useful for confirming which entities fall inside the Takaful Shari'ah audit perimeter.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
