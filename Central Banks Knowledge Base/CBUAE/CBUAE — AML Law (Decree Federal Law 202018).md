# AML Law (Decree Federal Law 20/2018)

Defined term covering Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 on AML/CTF and illegal organisations together with Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 (its Implementing Regulation), as amended, and all related instructions, guidelines and notices. It is cited as a legal basis of the Finance Companies Regulation, so finance companies and restricted licence finance companies are bound by the whole AML/CTF package through this compound definition.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, BNPL/finance, sanctions/TFS

### [[CBUAE — Finance Companies Regulation|Finance Companies Regulation]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If a finance company is assessing whether sector-specific licensing and prudential rules satisfy its obligations, the answer is no — AML/CFT duties operate cumulatively, not as alternatives. The AML obligation is framed as applying in addition to and without prejudice to other applicable AML/CFT laws and regulations, while the Finance Companies Regulation governs licensing, organisation and conduct of finance and restricted-licence finance companies. The decision consequence is that compliance must be tested against both regimes, and where they diverge the stricter requirement governs; satisfying the prudential regulation is never a defence to an AML failing.
