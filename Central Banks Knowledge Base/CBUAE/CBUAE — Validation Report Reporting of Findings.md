# Validation Report Reporting of Findings

Node covering the independent model validation requirement under the CBUAE Model Management Standards, including its place in the three-lines-of-defence structure (model owner, independent validator, internal audit) and the Effective Challenge principle. It sets out that validation must be periodic, independent of development, cover both qualitative and quantitative dimensions, and be supported by defined responsibilities, metrics, limits and reporting; incomplete quantitative validation must be escalated to the Model Oversight Committee. Binds UAE-licensed institutions using models, including where validation is outsourced to third parties (the institution retains ownership of reports and responsibility for acting on them).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
