# Sanctions Screening Program

Concept node on the sanctions screening programme, treating screening of all customer relationships and transactions as a minimum legal requirement that the risk-based approach may enhance but never displace. It sets expectations on sanctions risk assessment, automated updating of sanctions lists, training where manual processes are used, quality assurance, and active board and senior-management oversight including timely escalation of calibration or system failures. Binds LFIs and their governing bodies.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk

The screening programme is therefore not a standalone technical control but an element the governing body must be able to evidence it supervises, including staff competence and role-specific training. The practical consequence is that deficiencies in screening are attributable upward — weak oversight or untrained screening staff can itself be the finding, independent of whether a specific match was missed.
