# Annexure: Maximum Limits for Fees and Commissions

This is the Annexure to the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation/Standards listing maximum caps (in AED) on fees and commissions that Licensed Financial Institutions may charge on retail customer services, covering personal accounts, debit cards, consumer loans/financing, car finance and remittances. It operationalises the Standards' fee provisions, under which institutions must not exceed Central Bank-prescribed caps, must obtain prior Central Bank approval for fees and fee increases, and must give consumers the applicable fee schedule. It binds all Licensed Financial Institutions dealing with retail consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential
