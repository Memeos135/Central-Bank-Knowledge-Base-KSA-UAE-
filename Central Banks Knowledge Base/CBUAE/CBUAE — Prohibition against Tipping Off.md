# Prohibition against Tipping Off

Concept node on the confidentiality duty and tipping-off prohibition attaching to suspicious activity reporting. LFIs must keep both the reported information and the fact of reporting confidential, protect it from unauthorised access, respond precisely to FIU information requests, and follow any FIU instructions on the transaction and the business relationship after filing. Post-filing decisions (retain, exit or restrict the relationship) rest on the LFI's own policies and risk appetite but must weigh the tipping-off risk; also covers expedited 24-hour filing where circumstances require.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
