# Import and Export Factoring

Describes import and export factoring (international factoring) — purchase of an exporter's receivables by an Export Factor, with collection from the importer via an Import Factor — as a growing open-account financing channel. It sets out the specific ML vulnerabilities: factoring companies may apply weaker CDD/KYC and trade controls than banks, market themselves to de-banked clients, and receive far fewer trade documents, limiting understanding of the goods and parties. Relevant to LFIs and non-bank factoring companies assessing trade finance risk.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential
