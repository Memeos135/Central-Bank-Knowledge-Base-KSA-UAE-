# Disclosure Requirements (Article 25)

Node for Article 25 (Disclosure Requirements) of the CBUAE Takaful Insurance Regulation, which sits immediately after the solvency article and governs what Takaful Insurance Companies must disclose. The retrieved context contains only the table of contents entry and the article heading, not the operative text, so the substantive obligations must be read in the source document. It binds companies licensed to conduct Takaful Insurance business, including those operating through a Takaful Insurance Window.
