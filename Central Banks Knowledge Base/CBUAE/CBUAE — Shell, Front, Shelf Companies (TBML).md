# Shell, Front, Shelf Companies (TBML)

A TBML typology entry distinguishing shell, front and shelf companies and explaining how each is used to conceal beneficial owners, obscure payment flows and defeat risk rating in trade transactions. It includes examples of front companies used to evade export controls and dormant shelf companies that acquire unwarranted longevity-based comfort from institutions. Aimed at licensed financial institutions' beneficial ownership identification, customer risk rating and ongoing monitoring; issued by CBUAE.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
