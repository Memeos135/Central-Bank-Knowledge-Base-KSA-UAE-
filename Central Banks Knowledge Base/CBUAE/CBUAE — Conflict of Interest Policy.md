# Conflict of Interest Policy

Requires every Licensed Financial Institution to maintain and operate a written Conflict of Interest policy proportionate to the nature, scale and complexity of its Licensed Financial Activities, identifying circumstances that could harm Consumers and specifying mitigation procedures and non-compliance handling. Where conflicts are unavoidable, the institution must disclose their nature/source and obtain the Consumer's written acknowledgement before proceeding, and implement controls. Also covers commission-based distribution through Authorized Agents (disclosure, agent duty to act in Consumers' best interests, monitoring for abusive sales practices), information barriers between business lines and connected parties regarding Personal Data, gifts and inducements rules, and Board-level control over outsourcing/insourcing conflicts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, data/credit info
