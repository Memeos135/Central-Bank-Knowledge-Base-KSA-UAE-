# Points of Sale Affiliated to Insurance Companies

Definitional and scope node for "Points of Sale" as used in Insurance Authority Board Decision 37/2017: a body established by an insurance company under those instructions, together with the related defined terms (Company, Register, Insurance Policy, Authority). It fixes which functions a Point of Sale may perform, where it may be located, and the prohibition on operating without Authority authorisation and registration. Relevant to insurers determining whether a distribution outlet falls inside the Point of Sale regime.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection
