# ERM for Solvency Purposes Standards

Instrument node for the CBUAE Enterprise Risk Management for Solvency Purposes Standards for insurance companies, which set requirements for an integrated ERM framework supporting risk identification, capital adequacy assessment, risk governance and the ORSA. They apply to all Companies on a solo and Group-wide basis where there are Subsidiaries, Affiliates or international branches, while branches of foreign companies must adapt the framework to local operations. Enforcement is proportionate, with CBUAE deciding the extent to which a smaller Company must meet the specifics.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
