# Independent Committee for Reserves

A Board-formed independent committee mandated to scrutinise the use of profit-equalization reserves (PER and IRR) and recommend to the Board, with the stated aim of protecting non-shareholder stakeholders in line with HSA and ISSC resolutions. Its minimum composition includes an independent non-executive director (preferably chairing) and a Board member; it sits within the Islamic Bank's governance framework for profit equalization and reserve utilisation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
