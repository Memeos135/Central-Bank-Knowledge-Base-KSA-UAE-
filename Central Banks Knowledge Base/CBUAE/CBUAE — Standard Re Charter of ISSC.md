# Standard Re Charter of ISSC

CBUAE standard setting the requirements for the charter (functional rules) of the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee in Takaful insurance companies, complementing the Shari'ah Governance Standard for such companies. It requires companies to have Shari'ah governance policies and mechanisms ensuring the adopted charter meets this standard plus Central Bank and Higher Shari'ah Authority regulations, standards and resolutions, and annexes a minimum-content charter template that may be expanded but not contradicted. It binds all Takaful insurance companies licensed by the Central Bank, with a one-year compliance window from issuance, and preserves the Central Bank's power to impose additional requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
