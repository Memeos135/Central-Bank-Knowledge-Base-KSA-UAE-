# Cryptographic Key Management

Guidance on cryptographic key management for DLT and related environments: controls over key generation, secure storage and transmission of private keys, treatment of offline root keys (split among multiple owners) and online root keys (in hardware security modules), and issuance/revocation of keys to staff on leaving or changing roles. It links to physical and environmental security, prohibition on keeping personal data on-ledger, and data retention approaches including purging ledger records and destroying encryption keys to render data inaccessible, plus business continuity planning and testing for DLT services. Directed at financial institutions operating DLT applications.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, other
