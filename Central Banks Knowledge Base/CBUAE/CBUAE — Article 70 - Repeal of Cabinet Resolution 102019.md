# Article 70 - Repeal of Cabinet Resolution 10/2019

Final-provisions article of UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025 (Executive Regulations of Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 on AML/CFT and proliferation financing) repealing Cabinet Resolution No. 10 of 2019 and any conflicting provisions. Read with Article 71, which sets entry into force thirty days after publication in the Official Gazette. Relevant to anyone mapping legacy AML obligations and cross-references to the 2019 regulations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
