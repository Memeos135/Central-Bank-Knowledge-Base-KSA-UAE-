# Large Exposures & Credit Risk

## Why this community

Prudential credit risk containment for banks: concentration limits on single and connected exposures, plus the classification, provisioning and write-off framework for credit portfolios.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: the Central Bank Law is the enabling statute; the Large Exposures Regulation and Credit Risk Management Regulation are made under it; standards, circulars and notices sit beneath as implementing detail.
- The Credit Risk Management Regulation cross-refers to the Large Exposures Regulation, so concentration limits are read as one component of the wider credit risk governance duty.
- Transitional layer: the Grandfathering Scheme and the large exposure monitoring circular determine how legacy exposures are treated against current limits — decisive for breach assessment.
- Accounting-facing instruments (loan classification, write-off, IFRS 9 guidance) feed the provisioning inputs that the credit risk regulation assumes.
- Peripheral statutory links (Higher Shari'ah Authority, Insurance Law) reflect the enabling law's broader scope rather than credit-specific obligations.
