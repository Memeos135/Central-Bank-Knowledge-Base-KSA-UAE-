# Equity Position Risk

Node setting out the equity position risk capital charge in the CBUAE trading book/market risk standard: a specific risk charge of 8% of gross equity positions (long plus short, absolute values) and a general market risk charge of 8% of the overall net position, computed market by market. It also covers equity derivatives, stock indices and index arbitrage, position calculation, and related specific risk rules for correlation trading and securitisation exposures, including the cap at maximum possible loss. Applies to banks in the UAE holding equity and equity-derivative trading positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
