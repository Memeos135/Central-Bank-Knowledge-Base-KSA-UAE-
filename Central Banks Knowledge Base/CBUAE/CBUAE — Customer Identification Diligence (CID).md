# Customer Identification Diligence (CID)

Concept node on customer identification and due diligence in the hawala context, spanning both LFIs identifying hawala-provider customers and RHPs identifying their own natural- and legal-person customers. It covers identity verification (including Emirates ID online validation), beneficial ownership at the 25% threshold and fallback to the senior managing official, understanding ownership/control structure and nature of business, sanctions screening of all related parties, document retention for five years, and the duty to refuse or exit a relationship that cannot be understood. Binds LFIs and RHPs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
