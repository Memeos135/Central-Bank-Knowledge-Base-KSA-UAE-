# CBUAE

Defined-entity node for the Central Bank of the UAE as the issuing and supervisory authority under the Model Management Standards. It is the recipient of institutions' gap self-assessments, remediation plans and periodic progress and model-management reporting, and it approves compliance timeframes on a proportionate basis reflecting institution size and complexity. Relevant when tracing who exercises approval, review and reporting-recipient powers in the model risk regime.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
