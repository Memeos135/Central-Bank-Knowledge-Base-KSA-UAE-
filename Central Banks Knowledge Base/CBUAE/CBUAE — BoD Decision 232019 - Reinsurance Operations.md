# BoD Decision 23/2019 - Reinsurance Operations

Insurance Authority (now CBUAE) Board of Directors Decision No. 23 of 2019 setting the regulatory framework for reinsurance operations, including defined terms (cession, retrocession, retention, facultative, treaty and facultative-obligatory reinsurance) and the conditions under which a reinsurance company may write both persons/fund-accumulation and property/liability reinsurance, or add Takaful reinsurance. It binds reinsurance companies established in the State, branches of foreign reinsurers, and licensed insurers in respect of ceded and accepted reinsurance business, as well as pools and underwriting syndicates; P&I Clubs are addressed separately. Conditions include authorisation in the Articles of Association, Shariah compliance for Takaful reinsurance, and full technical and financial separation between conventional and Takaful reinsurance books.
