# Business Strategy Incorporation

## Why this community

Requirement to embed the climate transition plan into the institution's business strategy, rather than maintaining it as a parallel disclosure exercise.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; it is the bridge principle linking governance ownership to operational execution of the transition objectives.
- Cross-regime relevance: strategy integration touches risk management, capital and business planning obligations, so consequences extend beyond the climate instrument itself.
