# Over- and Under-Invoicing

Explains over- and under-invoicing of goods and services as a core TBML technique for transferring value across borders through collusive misstatement of price between supplier and buyer. It sets the expectation that LFIs identify such manipulation through independent market-value enquiry and external pricing sources (price validation), particularly for commodities. Sits within the guidance's framing of TBML as defined by FATF and binds LFIs supervised by CBUAE.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
