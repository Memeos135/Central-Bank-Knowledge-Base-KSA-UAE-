# Standardised Approach (Operational Risk)

Node for the Standardised Approach to operational risk capital within the CBUAE capital standards, including the qualifying criteria a bank must meet — board and senior management oversight, integration of operational risk data into risk and management reporting, documented policies and controls, independent validation, and external audit or Central Bank review. Related context covers the comprehensive assessment of all material risks (credit, concentration, securitisation, large exposures) in the internal capital adequacy process. Binds UAE-licensed banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
