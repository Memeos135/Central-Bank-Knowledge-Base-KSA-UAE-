# Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book

Concept node on interest rate risk in the banking book as a material risk that must be captured in the bank's internal capital adequacy assessment, requiring a measurement process covering all material interest rate positions with full repricing and maturity data, behavioural and maturity assumptions, and documented techniques. It sits alongside related Pillar 2 expectations on market risk stress testing and concentration management. Binds banks assessing and holding internal capital for IRRBB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
