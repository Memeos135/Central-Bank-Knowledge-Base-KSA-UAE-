# External Auditor

Concept node for the defined role of the External Auditor across several CBUAE instruments, anchored in the Financial Reporting and External Audit Standards (162/2018). It sets the auditor's eligibility and independence obligations (including professional indemnity insurance in the UAE, annual independence reporting to the board audit committee, securities dealing restrictions during blackout, and a two-year bar on joining the bank's board or senior management), and mandates engagement terms allowing supervisory meetings with the Central Bank, disapplying confidentiality toward the Central Bank, and granting access to working papers. It binds the external auditor and the bank contracting it.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
