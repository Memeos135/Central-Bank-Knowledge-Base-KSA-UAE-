# Federal Law No. 29 of 2006 (People of Determination)

Reference node for UAE Federal Law No. 29 of 2006 on the rights of People of Determination (persons with special needs), as incorporated by the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation. Within this Regulation it operates as an external compliance benchmark: Licensed Financial Institutions must be able to demonstrate their compliance with that Law in relation to People of Determination, alongside the accessibility, product-design and staff-training duties in Article 10. Relevant to institutions serving Consumers with disabilities.
