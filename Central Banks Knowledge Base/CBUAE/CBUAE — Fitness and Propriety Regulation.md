# Fitness and Propriety Regulation

CBUAE Fitness and Propriety Regulation (Circular No. 4 of 2024), which establishes the framework for assessing the fitness and propriety of Applicants, Authorized Individuals, Material Risk Takers and other persons designated by the Central Bank. It sets licensee responsibilities — including for branches of foreign licensees, staff suitability, collective board suitability, and notification of assessment outcomes to the Central Bank — and must be read with the corporate governance, finance companies, retail payment services and stored value facilities regulations. It binds all Licensed Financial Institutions, Insurance Companies, Takaful Insurance Companies and Insurance-Related Professions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments
