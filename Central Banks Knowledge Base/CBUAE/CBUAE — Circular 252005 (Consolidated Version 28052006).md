# Circular 25/2005 (Consolidated Version 28/05/2006)

Consolidated version of CBUAE Circular 25/2005 as amended by Notice No. 2418/2006, effective 28 May 2006, binding on banks, finance companies and investment companies. It reproduces the share-purchase lending caps of the original circular with the key amendment raising the limit on loans secured by shares of companies operating for more than five years from 70% to 80% of market value; other caps, the GCC-company limit, collateral prioritisation rule and book-lending sanction remain unchanged.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
