# Corporate Governance Standards for Insurance Companies

CBUAE Corporate Governance Standards for Insurance Companies, issued as an extension of the Corporate Governance Regulation (Circular No. 24/2022) and mirroring its article structure. It sets binding governance requirements — definitions (Affiliate, Authorised Manager, Board, Company, Related Parties, Independent Member, Material Risk Takers), board and senior management duties, group-level governance and internal control frameworks. It binds all insurance companies incorporated in the UAE and UAE branches of foreign insurers, including Takaful companies, with local governance structures responsible for local implementation even within a group framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
