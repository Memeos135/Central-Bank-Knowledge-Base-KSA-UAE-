# Ministerial Resolution No. 22 of 1985

A historical UAE Ministry of Economy resolution setting the conditions and procedures for registration in the register of actuaries, cited as a predecessor instrument in the Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 9 of 2017 on licensing and registration of actuaries. The 2017 Decision expressly repeals it together with its amendments, so it is relevant mainly for legacy status, transitional regularisation of previously registered actuaries and interpreting rights accrued before the new regulations. It bound persons seeking registration as actuaries and the ministry/authority administering that register.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
