# Financial Intelligence Unit

This node covers the Financial Intelligence Unit established as an independent unit within the Central Bank, the exclusive recipient of Suspicious Transaction Reports from Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and Virtual Asset Service Providers, with powers to demand additional information, analyse and disseminate to Concerned Authorities, and exchange information with foreign counterpart units subject to use limitations. It also covers the Chief of the Unit's provisional powers to suspend a suspicious transaction for up to ten working days and to freeze suspected funds for up to thirty days without prior notice, extendable by the Attorney General. Reporting entities must lift freezes on cancellation or expiry unless extended.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
