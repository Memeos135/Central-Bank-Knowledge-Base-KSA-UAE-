# Ongoing Monitoring

Node covering the ongoing monitoring element of CDD/KYC in the CBUAE's CDD/KYC guidance for Licensed Financial Institutions, anchored on Article 7 of the AML-CFT Decision. It treats monitoring as a continuous obligation across the whole business relationship — confirming activity matches the profile built at onboarding and feeding suspicious activity reporting — and sets risk-based expectations for periodic and event-driven customer reviews, review frequency by risk rating, alert-threshold calibration, and enhanced monitoring of high-risk or previously reported customers. It also addresses using digital identification systems to authenticate customers on an ongoing basis. Binds all LFIs supervised by CBUAE; automated transaction monitoring is expected of larger LFIs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** If the question is whether monitoring can be switched off or reduced for a customer after onboarding, the answer is governed by the ongoing monitoring duty in the AML/CFT decision, which the guidance simply applies. That duty attaches to all customers for the life of the business relationship and links continuous CDD/KYC with the trigger for suspicious activity reporting, so monitoring gaps are both a CDD failure and a reporting-risk failure. Decisions to apply simplified measures must therefore be calibrated within that continuing obligation rather than treated as an exemption from it.
