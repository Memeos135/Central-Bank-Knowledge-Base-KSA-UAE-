# Fitness and Propriety Regulation Circular 4/2024

This node captures the cross-reference in the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation requiring Open Finance Providers and the API Hub to remain at all times in full compliance with the CBUAE Fitness and Propriety Regulation and accompanying Standards issued by Circular No. 4/2024 (25/07/2024), as amended. It also reflects the requirement that persons holding or proposed for Authorized Individual positions obtain Central Bank authorisation, sits within the corporate governance provisions (confidentiality, data privacy compliance, governance monitoring and audit), and connects to Board composition rules including Central Bank representation and veto on the API Hub Board.
