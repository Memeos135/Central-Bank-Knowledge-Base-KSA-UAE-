# SVF Licensing Requirements

Part I of the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation setting the scope of application and the requirement for prior Central Bank licensing before issuing or operating an SVF in the State. It covers the carve-out for Single-purpose Stored Value Facilities, a discretionary risk-based exemption route (subject to value and customer-number thresholds and possible FinTech sandbox testing, with the Central Bank able to demand information or revoke the exemption), and a prohibition on unlicensed overseas SVF schemes advertising or soliciting the UAE public. Binds any issuer or operator of an SVF, including foreign schemes targeting UAE customers.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
