# Targeted Financial Sanctions (Payment Sector)

The sanctions/risk-understanding component of the CBUAE payment-sector AML/CFT guidance, framing how LFIs are exposed to payment participants that may be regulated financial institutions in some jurisdictions and unregulated in others, directly or indirectly. It walks through the roles in a card-based e-commerce transaction (payment gateway, scheme operator, payment processor, issuing and acquiring banks, payment account information services) to show where screening visibility and regulatory gaps arise. Relevant to LFIs assessing sanctions and ML/FT exposure across cross-border and intermediated payment flows.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, payments
