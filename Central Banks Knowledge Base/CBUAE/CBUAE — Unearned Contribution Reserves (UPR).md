# Unearned Contribution Reserves (UPR)

Technical-provision concept in the Takaful financial regulations: the Unearned Contribution Reserve is calculated pro rata from risk inception, with a larger reserve required where the risk pattern over the policy period is non-uniform, and a 25% minimum of annual contributions for marine cargo (individual shipment) unless the Actuary justifies a lower figure with Authority approval. UPR is mandatory for each line of business and requires at least annual actuarial certification; where inadequate, an Unexpired Risk Reserve must be added. Binds Takaful/Re-Takaful companies and their appointed Actuary.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
