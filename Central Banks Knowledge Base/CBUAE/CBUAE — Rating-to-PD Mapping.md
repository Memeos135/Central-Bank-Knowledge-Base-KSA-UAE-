# Rating-to-PD Mapping

Covers the master rating scale and the use of rating models — how model scores map onto a single internal rating scale used across credit risk management, provisioning and capital assessment, and the principles for grade granularity, distribution of obligors across grades, and robust PD estimation per grade. It also sets re-rating expectations after a new or recalibrated model (70% of in-scope obligors within six months, 95% within nine months), batch rating by experts independent of business lines, and monitoring of rating archiving, frequency and correct model-to-obligor-type application.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
