# Article 33: Corporate Governance

Article 33 (Part 9) requires effective, robust and well-documented corporate governance arrangements, including a clear organisational structure with defined and transparent lines of responsibility, proportionate to the nature, scale and complexity of the Payment Token Services provided. Minimum content includes an organisation chart with named responsible individuals and function descriptions, conflict-of-interest controls, integrity and transparency controls, and controls to ensure legal and regulatory compliance. For this Article, "Payment Token Service Provider" means a Licensee or Registree, excluding Registered Foreign Payment Token Issuers.
