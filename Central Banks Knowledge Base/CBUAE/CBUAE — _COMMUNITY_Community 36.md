# Islamic Profit Equalization

## Why this community

Prudential and conduct treatment of profit smoothing in Islamic banking: how reserves are built and used to manage displaced commercial risk borne in respect of unrestricted investment account holders.

## How members connect

- The Profit Equalization Standard is the operative instrument and cross-refers to the broader Islamic bank risk management standards, creating a specific-within-general hierarchy.
- Shared defined terms drive the obligations: PER (smoothing returns to IAHs and shareholders) and IRR (absorbing future investment losses of IAHs) are the two regulated reserve mechanisms.
- DCR is the risk concept that justifies both reserves; its incidence falls on IAHs, linking prudential capital treatment to fair-treatment/disclosure duties toward account holders.
- Consequence for decisions: reserve usage, disclosure and governance approvals must be traceable to the standard's conditions, not to management discretion.
