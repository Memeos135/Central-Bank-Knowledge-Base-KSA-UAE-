# Notice No. 458/2018 (IFRS 9 Guidance)

A repealed CBUAE notice containing final guidance on IFRS 9, cancelled along with related guidance and amendments by Article 17 of the Credit Risk Management Regulation. Expected credit loss and provisioning expectations are now carried by the Regulation and its mandatory accompanying Credit Risk Standards, which apply to all LFIs providing credit facilities, including on a consolidated basis for UAE-incorporated groups. Consult for legacy IFRS 9 interpretation history rather than current obligations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
