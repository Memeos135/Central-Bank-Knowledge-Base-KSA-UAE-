# Article 2: Circulation of Counterfeit/Forged UAE Currency

Article 2 of the strict liability sanctions regulation restates the central bank's exclusive currency issuance right and the statutory prohibition on issuing or circulating counterfeit or forged notes, coins or bearer instruments resembling legal tender. It makes LFIs strictly liable for circulating counterfeit or forged notes at collection, at transaction, or on delivery to their central bank account, whether deliberate, reckless or negligent, with a fixed fine per item plus the face value; lawfully withdrawn or replaced currency is not treated as counterfeit.
