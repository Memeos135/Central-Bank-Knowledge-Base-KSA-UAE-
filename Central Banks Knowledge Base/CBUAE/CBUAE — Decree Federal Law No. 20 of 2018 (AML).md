# Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 (AML)

Node for Decree Federal Law No. (20) of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organizations — the UAE primary AML/CTF statute, referenced here as the legal basis of CBUAE Board Decision 59/4/2019. It supplies the substantive AML/CTF obligations and the catalogue of administrative sanctions the Central Bank may impose. It binds Financial Institutions licensed or registered by the CBUAE and their concerned persons.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
