# Takaful Insurance Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing and Shari'ah-compliant structuring of takaful insurance business: how an insurance company is authorised, and how a separate takaful insurance fund is constituted, governed and contractually linked to the operator.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the Central Bank Law and the Islamic commercial transactions regulation down to the takaful regulation and the insurance company licensing regulation, with the Higher Shari'ah Authority as the common Shari'ah oversight anchor cited across them.
- An obligation chain governs the takaful fund lifecycle: establishment, fund charter, board of trustees and its duties, legal representation, terms and conditions, and assignment of accounts — each article cross-referencing the charter or the wakala contract.
- Licensing articles form a sequential procedural chain (classification of business, application and annexed attachments, licensing decision, registration, commencement, branch openings inside and outside the State).
- Shared defined terms (Licensee, Insurance Company, Takaful Insurance Fund, Wakala Contract) tie the prudential/licensing regime to nominate Islamic contract forms (murabaha, mudaraba, ijarah) and to consumer-facing bodies such as the customer protection unit.
