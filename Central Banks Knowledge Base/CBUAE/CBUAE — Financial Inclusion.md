# Financial Inclusion

Article 10 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation on financial inclusion (10.1) and gender and racial equality (10.2). It requires Licensed Financial Institutions to design products and services with vulnerable groups and People of Determination in mind, offer basic low-cost products, train staff to identify and assist such Consumers, ensure accessible branches and ATMs, demonstrate compliance with Federal Law No. 29 of 2006, adopt an anti-discrimination code of conduct, and (as good practice) track disaggregated Consumer data on access and usage. Binds all Licensed Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
