# Islamic Shari'ah Supervision

## Why this community

Supervisory-level Shari'ah oversight: the central-authority/ISSC relationship, the operating Shari'ah compliance function, and the special regime for conventional institutions running an Islamic window.

## How members connect

- Statutory base (Central Bank Law) → Shari'ah Governance Standard → guidance notes on the ISSC charter and on the Shari'ah compliance function, i.e. a clean law→regulation→guidance chain.
- The Higher Shari'ah Authority sits above institution-level ISSCs; its rulings bind the ISSC and, through it, the compliance function.
- The Shari'ah compliance function's duties are elaborated operationally through review-process stages, annual plan, prioritisation parameters, sampling methodology, product checklist and field review — all directed at controlling Shari'ah non-compliance risk.
- The Islamic window standard extends the same governance and ALM requirements to institutions that are not wholly Islamic, marking a scope boundary.
