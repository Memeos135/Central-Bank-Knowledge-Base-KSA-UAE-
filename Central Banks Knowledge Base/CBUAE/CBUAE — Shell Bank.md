# Shell Bank

Node anchoring the Shell Bank definition (a bank licensed in a jurisdiction where it has no physical presence and is not part of a regulated Financial Group) and its operative use in the correspondent banking rules (Article 26) of the UAE AML/CTF Executive Regulations, alongside record-keeping duties and the High-Risk Customers definition. Financial Institutions must not enter into or continue a correspondent banking or similar relationship with a Shell Bank or with any institution permitting its accounts to be used by Shell Banks, and must gather sufficient information on respondent institutions beforehand. Binds Financial Institutions; related record-retention and information-availability duties extend to DNFBPs and VASPs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential
