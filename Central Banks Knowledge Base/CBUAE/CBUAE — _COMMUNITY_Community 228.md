# Credit Risk Models

## Why this community

Prudential treatment of credit risk models — the governance, validation and use-test expectations attaching to models that drive credit risk measurement and capital or provisioning outcomes.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster: the obligation sits on the licensee's board/senior management to own model design, approval and periodic validation rather than on any external body.
- Functions as a downstream detail of a broader risk-management/governance regulation, so it should be read together with internal audit and independent review duties found in adjacent clusters.
- Isolated node — no internal cross-references captured, so scope limits must be taken from the parent instrument, not inferred.
