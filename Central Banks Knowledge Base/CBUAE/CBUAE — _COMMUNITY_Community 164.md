# Shari'ah Compliance & Settlement

## Why this community

Shari'ah compliance obligations for financial services offered to consumers, and their concrete effect on early settlement (early repayment) treatment in financing contracts.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: a general Shari'ah-compliance requirement in the regulatory article is given specific content by a Shari'ah Higher Authority resolution on early settlement.
- Direct citation chain — the article invokes the HSA resolution, making the resolution binding on how early settlement amounts are calculated and charged.
- Practical consequence: pricing and early-termination terms in Islamic financing products must satisfy both the SAMA consumer/finance regime and the cited Shari'ah determination.
