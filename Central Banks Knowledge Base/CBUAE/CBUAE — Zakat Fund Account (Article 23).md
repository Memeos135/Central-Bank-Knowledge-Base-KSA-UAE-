# Zakat Fund Account (Article 23)

Article 23 of the CBUAE Takaful Insurance Regulation is a permissive-but-conditional provision on the Zakat fund account: a Takaful Insurance Company may establish one only if its articles of association allow it, and once established it must be kept segregated from the Company's other accounts and from the Takaful Insurance Fund. The management method requires Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC) approval, disbursements require a Board decision consistent with ISSC-approved Shari'ah rulings, the Board must adopt a governing policy, and those appointed to manage or supervise the fund may not be remunerated. In all cases the Company must compute Zakat due on shareholders and disclose it, after ISSC approval, in the annual financial statements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
