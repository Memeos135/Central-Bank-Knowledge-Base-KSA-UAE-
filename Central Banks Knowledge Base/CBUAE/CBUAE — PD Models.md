# PD Models

Node covering probability of default modelling in the CBUAE Model Management Guidance, positioned as the calibration layer downstream of rating models and linked to macroeconomic modelling for point-in-time estimates. The associated material frames PD alongside TTC/PIT distinctions, ECL and stress testing uses, and expects statistical rigour to be combined with causality analysis, economic meaning and documented judgement. Guidance-level expectations for UAE institutions developing or acquiring PD and related credit risk models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
