# Basel III Framework (BCBS)

Orientation node for the Basel III liquidity framework as incorporated by reference into the CBUAE liquidity regulation and guidance, covering the BCBS LCR (January 2013) and NSFR (October 2014) standards. It explains the two-track approach: banks either remain under ELAR/ASRR or apply for approval to be assessed under Basel III, after which reversion is not permitted and both LCR and NSFR must be met. Also relevant to proportionality: qualitative requirements are not relaxed for Basel III-aspiring banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
