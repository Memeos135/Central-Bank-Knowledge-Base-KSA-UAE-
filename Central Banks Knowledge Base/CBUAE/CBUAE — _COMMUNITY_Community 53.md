# Market Risk Controls

## Why this community

Prudential treatment of market risk: trading book scope, valuation discipline, capital requirements and the systems, validation and reporting controls supervisors expect around internal market-risk models, including Islamic banking variants.

## How members connect

- Rule-to-detail chain: the Market Risk Regulation sets capital, reporting and systems-and-controls obligations; the Market Risk Standards elaborate valuation, controls and Islamic banking treatment.
- Definitional dependency: valuation obligations only bite on positions falling inside the defined Trading Book, so boundary classification drives the capital outcome.
- Model-governance loop: internal validation and back-testing are the evidentiary conditions for relying on internal approaches, sitting under the systems and controls requirement.
- Legal basis: the regulation traces its authority to the central bank establishing law, with Islamic banking provisions as a parallel scope carve-out rather than an exemption.
