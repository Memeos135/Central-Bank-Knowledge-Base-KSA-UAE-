# Shari'ah Compliance for Financial Services

Article 11 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation, addressing Shari'ah compliance in the delivery of financial services, together with related definitions (Higher Shari'ah Authority, Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee). It obliges Islamic Financial Institutions to uphold Shari'ah principles, comply with their ISSC, and have the Board and Senior Management embed Shari'ah compliance in culture, processes, operations and code of conduct. Also notes that the Regulation supplements other regulators' requirements and does not alter financial crime compliance obligations.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
