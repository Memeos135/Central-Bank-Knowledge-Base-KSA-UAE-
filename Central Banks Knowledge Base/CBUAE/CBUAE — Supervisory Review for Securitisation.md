# Supervisory Review for Securitisation

Node on the CBUAE securitisation framework and supervisory review, including defined terms (early amortisation provision, excess spread, implicit support, originating bank, pool, resecuritisation exposure) and the capital treatment of resecuritisation exposures with a 100% risk-weight floor. It prohibits originators from providing implicit support and imposes capital, gain-on-sale and public disclosure consequences where support is given. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks acting as originators, sponsors or investors.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
