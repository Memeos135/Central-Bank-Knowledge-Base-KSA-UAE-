# Designated Functions

Covers the concept of Designated Functions — the roles requiring Central Bank Authorisation, listed in Attachment 1 to the Standards and subject to amendment — and how Licensees must apply the regime by substance of the job description rather than job title. It sets selection requirements (assessment and verification of qualifications, experience, references and professional memberships, propriety checks, and contribution to Board Collective Suitability), and the rules on role changes, unexpected or temporary vacancies, interim appointments (normally up to six months, extendable with justification) and ongoing monitoring of fitness, propriety and suitability. Binds all Licensees and the individuals nominated for or holding such functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
