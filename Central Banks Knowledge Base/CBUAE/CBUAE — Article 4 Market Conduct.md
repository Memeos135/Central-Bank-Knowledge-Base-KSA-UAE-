# Article 4: Market Conduct

Article 4 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards governs responsible market conduct: institutions must act with integrity, fairness, honesty, competence and transparency, meet consumers only in premises ensuring confidentiality and transaction security, screen and verify retail staff (no fraud/financial-crime record or misconduct dismissal, fit and proper at all times) and require staff to identify themselves and their employer. The extract also shows compliance-culture duties including mystery shopping, thematic reviews, complaint data analysis, non-financial performance metrics and escalation of material code-of-conduct breaches. Binds licensed financial institutions and their staff and agents in dealings with consumers.
