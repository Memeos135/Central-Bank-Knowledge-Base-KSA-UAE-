# Surveyor and Loss Adjuster Registration

Provisions from the UAE regulation on Surveyors and Loss Adjusters covering the scope of licensable surveying/loss-adjustment work, the special Register maintained at the Insurance Authority, conditions and cancellation of registration (including that registration is personal and non-transferable), and ongoing obligations on books, records, annual audited submissions and supervisory audits. It also sets out the Director General's power to impose disciplinary sanctions for breaches of the Law or the Regulations. It binds natural and corporate surveyors and loss adjusters operating in the UAE, and is administered by the Insurance Authority/Director General (functions now under CBUAE).

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
