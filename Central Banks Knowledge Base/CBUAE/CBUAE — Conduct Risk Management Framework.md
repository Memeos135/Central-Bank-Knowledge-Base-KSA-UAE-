# Conduct Risk Management Framework

Concept node drawn from Article 3 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards, requiring Licensed Financial Institutions to maintain a Board-approved conduct risk framework to identify, assess, monitor, mitigate and control conduct risk, with documented risk appetite and limits, mitigation strategies, and annual reporting to the Board through the Consumer Protection & Conduct Risk Management report. It also mandates a governance structure covering all retail products, services and Retail Operations including Third Parties, a Board-approved control framework, appropriate mandates for Compliance, Risk and Audit, staff competence in the Consumer Protection Regulation and Standards, and remuneration structures that deter mis-selling and irresponsible risk-taking. A linked requirement obliges a comprehensive Board-approved product approval framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
