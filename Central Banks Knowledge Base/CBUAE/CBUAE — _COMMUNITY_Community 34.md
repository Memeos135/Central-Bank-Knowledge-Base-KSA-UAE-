# Retail Payment Services Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct of payment service providers and card schemes, and the AML/CFT overlay that applies to payment and stored-value products.

## How members connect

- The Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation is the hub: it defines Payment Service Provider, Retail Payment Service and Retail Payment Service User, and carries its Annexes as binding scope and control detail.
- Annex I fixes the taxonomy of services (and therefore licence category); Annex II imposes the technology risk and information security expectations on the same licensees.
- Cross-regime link: the payments regulation cites the AML/CTF law, so a PSP carries both prudential/conduct duties and customer due diligence, screening and reporting duties.
- The AML/CFT payments guidance sits below the law as interpretive material and pulls in the terrorism-list decision and the Stored Value Facilities Regulation, extending sanctions/TFS screening expectations across payment and e-money products.
