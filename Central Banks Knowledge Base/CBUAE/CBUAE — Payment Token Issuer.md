# Payment Token Issuer

Defined actor under the CBUAE Payment Token Services framework: the licensed (Dirham) or registered Foreign entity that performs Payment Token Issuing, i.e. sells or transfers payment tokens to Customers/Tokenholders. The node covers issuer-facing obligations including the prohibition on interest-like benefits tied to holding period, Central Bank powers to cap volume, value or Customer numbers (including on-boarding restrictions, and separately for Payment Token Conversion Providers and Custodian/Transferors), redemption duties and Customer Agreement/White Paper disclosure on transfer finality, forks and failed transfers. Binds licensed Dirham payment token issuers and foreign payment token issuers dealing with Persons in the UAE (CBUAE jurisdiction, not SAMA).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
