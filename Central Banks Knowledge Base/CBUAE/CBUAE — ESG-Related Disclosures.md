# ESG-Related Disclosures

This node covers the substantive entity-level sustainability disclosure content set out in Principle 3: governance, strategy (including transition plans and short/medium/long-term effects), risk management integration with the overall risk framework including capital and liquidity, and metrics and targets. It states minimum expected disclosures for Reporting Entities, with additional requirements possible by entity type. Consult it for the TCFD-style four-pillar structure adopted in the UAE principles.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
