# Insurance Authority Decision No. 50 of 2019

A node for Insurance Authority Decision No. 50 of 2019, surfaced in the context of the CBUAE Standard on Shari'ah Governance for Takaful Insurance Companies, which is issued under the insurance operations law and the CBUAE law and complements the Takaful Insurance Regulation and the Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies. The surrounding material sets out defined terms (Takaful Company, Shari'ah non-compliance risk, Confidential Information, Higher Shari'ah Authority) and the general requirement that a takaful company's objectives, activities, operations and codes of conduct comply with Islamic Shari'ah at all times. It binds licensed takaful insurance companies and their governance bodies; note this is a UAE (CBUAE/Insurance Authority) instrument, not a SAMA one.
