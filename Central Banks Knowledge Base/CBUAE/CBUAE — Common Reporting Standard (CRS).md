# Common Reporting Standard (CRS)

The Common Reporting Standard itself as incorporated by the UAE regulations, including the OECD CRS commentary which is treated as an integral interpretive part of the Standard for purposes of automatic exchange under a scheduled Agreement. It also fixes that the English version aligned to the OECD model prevails over the Arabic translation in case of conflict. Relevant to any Reporting Financial Institution determining classification, due diligence and reporting obligations for Reportable Accounts.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, other
