# Country & Transfer Risk Governance

## Why this community

Enterprise risk governance applied to specific risk types — country and transfer risk, IRRBB and solvency self-assessment — anchored in board responsibility and a documented risk governance framework.

## How members connect

- Board responsibilities article is the source obligation; the risk governance framework and an independent risk management function are the required delivery mechanisms.
- Two parallel regulations (country/transfer risk; IRRBB) reuse the same governance architecture, so the framework and function definitions carry across risk types.
- Stress testing is the common analytical obligation linking country risk ratings, IRRBB and the insurance-side ORSA.
- A regulation-plus-standards pairing (circular and accompanying standards) shows the detailed-requirements layer beneath the headline rule; country risk rating systems are the operative classification tool.
