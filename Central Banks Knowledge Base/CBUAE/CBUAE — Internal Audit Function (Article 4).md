# Internal Audit Function (Article 4)

Article 4 of the CBUAE Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Standards (161/2018) sets the mandate, independence, staffing and scope requirements for a bank's internal audit function. It requires a written internal audit policy (covering standing, authority, reporting line to the Board, use for advisory work, adherence to IIA professional standards and Code of Ethics, and coordination with the external auditor), conflict-of-interest safeguards including cooling-off after rotation, collective competence and ongoing training, and confidentiality. Scope must extend to the whole bank including subsidiaries, branches, outsourced activities and matters of regulatory interest. It binds licensed banks, their boards/audit committees and the head of internal audit.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
