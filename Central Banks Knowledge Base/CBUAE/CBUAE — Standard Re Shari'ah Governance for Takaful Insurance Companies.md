# Standard Re Shari'ah Governance for Takaful Insurance Companies

CBUAE standard setting minimum Shari'ah governance requirements for incorporated Takaful insurance companies, covering the internal Shari'ah supervision committee, internal Shari'ah control and audit divisions, external Shari'ah audit and related fit-and-proper and reporting requirements. It is issued under the UAE insurance law and the central bank decretal law, and complements the Takaful Insurance Regulation and the Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies. It binds Takaful companies on a solo and group-wide basis (including subsidiaries, affiliates and international branches), and the regulator may impose requirements beyond the stated minima.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
