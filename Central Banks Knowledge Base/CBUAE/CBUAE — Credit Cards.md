# Credit Cards

Article 5 of the original regulation defining Credit Cards as cards linked to an electronic network with a set credit limit, where the issuer settles purchases and cash withdrawals on the holder's behalf for repayment in the following month or by instalment. It sets issuer obligations: minimum annual income eligibility (or issuance against a pledged deposit of a specified minimum), separate statistical recording for non-customer/resident status, monthly expense statements, immediate investigation of disputed items, and use of the prescribed agreement form. Binds card-issuing banks and finance companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, BNPL/finance
