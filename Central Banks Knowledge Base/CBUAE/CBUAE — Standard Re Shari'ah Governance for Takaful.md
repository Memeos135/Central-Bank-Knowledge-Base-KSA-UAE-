# Standard Re Shari'ah Governance for Takaful

Concept node for the CBUAE Standard Re Shari'ah Governance for Takaful Insurance Companies, referenced as the framework that the Annual Shari'ah Report standard complements alongside the Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance. It underpins the requirement that Takaful companies maintain Shari'ah governance policies and mechanisms ensuring the ISSC's annual report meets applicable standards, with the Central Bank able to impose additional requirements beyond the stated minimums. Binds Takaful Insurance Companies licensed by the Central Bank and their ISSCs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
