# Chapter 4: Continuing Obligations

Chapter 4 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets the ongoing conditions a Licensed Person must satisfy after authorisation, as the counterpart to the initial licensing standards in Chapter 3. It forms part of Part A of the Standards and underpins the supervisory regime applied through Central Bank examinations and the reporting forms listed in the appendices. It binds all holders of a CBUAE Exchange Business licence across Category A, B and C activities.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
