# Article 18: Authentication

Article 18 of the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation requires Licensees acting as Data Holders and Service Owners to apply authentication whenever a User accesses Account or Product information via a Data Sharing Provider or initiates a Transaction via a Service Initiation Provider. The procedure must reliably verify identity and authority, use at least two factors drawn from knowledge, possession or inherence, be strengthened in higher-risk circumstances, and meet any further Central Bank requirements. Data Sharing and Service Initiation Providers may rely on the Data Holder's or Service Owner's authentication.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, data/credit info
