# Distributed Ledger Technology Governance

## Why this community

Regulatory treatment of distributed ledger technology in the KSA financial sector, focusing on governance accountability, permissible design choices, and the control risks of permissionless/anonymous ledgers.

## How members connect

- The DLT concept node is the definitional hub; governance framework, design principles and the permissionless/anonymity item are its scoping and conditioning limbs.
- Design principles flow directly into cryptographic key management, making key custody and lifecycle a technical precondition for an acceptable DLT design.
- The permissionless/anonymity item functions as a scope limit or heightened-scrutiny trigger, since anonymity cuts against CDD, traceability and wire-transfer originator/beneficiary obligations.
- Hierarchy runs from the governance framework (who is accountable) down to design and key-management expectations (how the arrangement must be built and controlled).
