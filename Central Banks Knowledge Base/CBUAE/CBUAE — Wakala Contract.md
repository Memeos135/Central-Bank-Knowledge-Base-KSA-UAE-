# Wakala Contract

Defines the Wakala Contract as the agency arrangement under which the Takaful Insurance Fund (or the Participant) appoints the Company as agent to manage the Fund or the Participants' Investment Account for a Wakala Fee, and mandates Wakala as the operating model for licensed property/liability and persons/fund-accumulation takaful business. It requires segregation of the Takaful Fund account, the Participants' Investment Account and the Shareholders' Account, with revenues and expenses attributed to each. Binds takaful and re-takaful companies and insurers operating a Takaful Insurance Window under CBUAE supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
