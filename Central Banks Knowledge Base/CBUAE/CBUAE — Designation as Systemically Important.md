# Designation as Systemically Important

Concept node covering the ongoing prudential and governance requirements applying to an LVPS that is systemically important or designated, expressed as the PFMI principles restated in the Regulation. These include a sound legal basis, transparent governance, comprehensive risk management framework, credit and liquidity risk controls, settlement finality by end of value date, and settlement in central bank money where practicable. The obligations bind the System Operator and/or Settlement Institution of the designated system.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential
