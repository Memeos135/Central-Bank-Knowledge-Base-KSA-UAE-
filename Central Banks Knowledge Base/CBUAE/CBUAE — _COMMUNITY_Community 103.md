# Exchange Business Licensing

## Why this community

Entry and continuing prudential conditions for exchange business, including who qualifies as a Licensed Person, the activities in scope, and the capital and guarantee floors that condition the licence.

## How members connect

- 'Licensed Person' is the anchor defined term; every other member is either an activity it may conduct (Exchange Business, IMTS Provider) or a condition it must continuously satisfy.
- Minimum Paid-up Capital and Minimum Bank Guarantee operate as licence conditions — breach is a prudential failure, not merely a reporting matter.
- IMTS provision pulls the licensee into the AML/CTF regime, so money transfer activity triggers customer due diligence and reporting duties in addition to prudential floors.
- Scope note: obligations attach to the licensed entity; activity-specific thresholds may differ by category of licence.
