# Financial Reporting

## Why this community

Periodic financial reporting to SAMA — the form, content and timing of financial statements a licensee must produce.

## How members connect

- Depends on the accounting and audit obligation (Art. 11) for the underlying records and audit sign-off.
- Overlaps in scope with the broader reporting duties (Art. 14); read the two together to distinguish financial statements from other regulatory returns and notifications.
