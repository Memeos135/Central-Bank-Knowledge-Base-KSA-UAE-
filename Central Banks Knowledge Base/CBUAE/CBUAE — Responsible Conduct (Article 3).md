# Responsible Conduct (Article 3)

This node represents Article 3 (Responsible Conduct) of the SME Market Conduct Regulation, which sits between the disclosure requirements (Article 2) and responsible financing practice (Article 4) in the instrument's structure. It addresses how Licensed Financial Institutions and their Staff and Authorised Agents must conduct themselves towards SME Customers in promoting, selling and servicing Financial Products and Services. Note that the surrounding extract largely reproduces the table of contents and neighbouring articles, so the operative Article 3 text should be checked in the source.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
