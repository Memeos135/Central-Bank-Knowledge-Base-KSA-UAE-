# Article 4: Risk Measurement and Use of Models (Standards)

Article 4 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards requires banks to use risk measurement methodologies proportionate to their risk profile, nature, size, complexity and structure, such as VaR, scenario analysis and stress testing, with outcomes analysis and back-testing. It requires explicit treatment of known model weaknesses and model error, and mandates that model-based approaches be supplemented by qualitative assessment, particularly for hard-to-quantify risks such as operational, compliance and reputational risk. It binds CBUAE-licensed banks.
