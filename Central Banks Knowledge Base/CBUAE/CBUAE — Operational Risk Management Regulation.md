# Operational Risk Management Regulation

The CBUAE Operational Risk Management Regulation sets governance and control requirements for operational risk and operational resilience across Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs). It allocates responsibility to the Board (ultimate authority, risk reporting requirements, mandatory Board operational risk committee for systemically important LFIs), to Senior Management (implementing the framework, monitoring the risk profile, training, risk culture), and to an independent Operational Risk management function under the Chief Risk Officer. It binds all LFIs supervised by the CBUAE, with escalating expectations by size, complexity and systemic importance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
