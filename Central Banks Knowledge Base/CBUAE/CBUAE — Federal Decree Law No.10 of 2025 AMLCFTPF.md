# Federal Decree Law No.10 of 2025 AML/CFT/PF

Federal Decree by Law No. (10) of 2025 is the UAE's primary AML/CFT/CPF statute, covering the money laundering, terrorism financing and proliferation financing offences, provisional measures, the Financial Intelligence Unit, obligations on Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and Virtual Asset Service Providers, and penalties. It repeals Federal Decree Law No. (20) of 2018, while regulations, resolutions and circulars issued under the 2018 law remain in force so far as they do not conflict, pending replacement. It enters into force two weeks after publication in the Official Gazette; Executive Regulations and an administrative-penalties resolution are to be issued by the Cabinet.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Financial Intelligence Unit|Financial Intelligence Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where suspicious transaction reports and related disclosures must be directed, the operative body is the Financial Intelligence Unit as defined and constituted by the AML/CFT/PF decree law itself. The decree law both defines the Unit and establishes it as an independent body housed within the Central Bank, so its powers, independence and reporting channel derive directly from statute rather than from subordinate guidance. Practically, obligations owed to "the Unit" in any downstream rule or guidance must be read as obligations owed to this statutory body, and its independence limits any argument that reporting to another supervisory function discharges the duty.
