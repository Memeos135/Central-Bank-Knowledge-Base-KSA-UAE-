# External Shari'ah Audit

Node addressing External Shari'ah Audit: an annual assessment by an external body of the IFI's compliance with Islamic Shari'ah and of the adequacy and effectiveness of its Shari'ah supervision governance systems. It also sets the audit committee's related duties — reviewing the scope, results and adequacy of the external Shari'ah audit, facilitating the external auditor's work, reviewing its reports to confirm senior management has acted on them, and meeting the external Shari'ah auditor at least once per financial year.

- **What this link tells you:** Where an Islamic financial institution must evidence Shari'ah compliance, the controlling question is whether the annual external assessment has been performed by a body meeting the defined term. The Shari'ah Governance Standard defines External Shari'ah Audit as an annual external assessment of both compliance with Islamic Shari'ah and the adequacy and effectiveness of Shari'ah supervision governance, and the related provision contemplates appointing an external Shari'ah auditing firm to carry out head-of-internal-audit responsibilities where capacity is being built. The consequence is that the external audit is a recurring, independently sourced obligation; internal Shari'ah audit work alone does not discharge it, and any transitional appointment arrangement should be checked against the primary text.
