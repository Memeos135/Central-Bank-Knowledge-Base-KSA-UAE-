# Enterprise Risk Assessment (Insurance)

Node on the enterprise-wide ML/FT risk assessment expected of insurance operators under CBUAE guidance. Its analytical anchor is that only life and other investment-related insurance (long-term savings/investment-type cover) falls within the UAE AML/CFT perimeter, while general property and liability insurance does not — so the risk assessment must be product-scoped, and cover customers, distribution channels (tied agents versus independent brokers), and geography. Binds each licensed insurer, reinsurer, agent and broker individually.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
