# UN Consolidated List

Defines the UN Consolidated List as the UNSC-issued designation list that LFIs must source from the official UN website and keep current, including mandatory registration with the Executive Office for automated listing/de-listing alerts under Cabinet Decision 74 Art. 21. It underpins customer and transaction screening across the customer lifecycle, and where third-party vendor lists are used the LFI retains responsibility for vendor due diligence and completeness of the list. Also frames false-positive dispositioning and the controlled use of cleared-name (white) lists.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
