# Consumer Protection Standards

## Why this community

Saudi consumer protection and market-conduct regime for financial institutions: pre-contractual disclosure, product-level conduct rules, advertising, and institutional accountability for how products are designed, sold and serviced to Consumers.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular 8-2020) sits above the Consumer Protection Standards, which are then broken into article-level obligations (Disclosure and Transparency, Institutional Oversight, Market Conduct, Business Conduct) and product-specific sections.
- Shared defined terms carry the obligation chain: the Key Facts Statement, APR/profit-rate disclosure and Base Lending/Financing Rate recur across the general, deposit, credit/financing, investment and remittance provisions, so a disclosure breach is usually a breach at several levels at once.
- Institutional Oversight links conduct outcomes back to governance artefacts - Conduct Risk Management Framework, Product Approval Framework, the annual consumer protection/conduct risk report and the No Objection Letter gate - making the board and senior management the accountable parties.
- Business Conduct aggregates consumer-remedy rules (cooling-off, consumer mobility, unauthorized transaction handling, responsibility for authorized agents) and interacts with the Shari'ah governance standard for Islamic financial institutions offering the same products.
