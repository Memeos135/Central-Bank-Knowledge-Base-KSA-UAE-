# Climate-related Financial Risk Management Regulation

The CBUAE Climate-related Financial Risk Management Regulation is a prudential/governance instrument requiring Financial Institutions to identify, measure, manage and report Climate-related Financial Risks (Physical, Transition and Liability risk). It covers board and senior management responsibilities, risk appetite, strategy, integration into the risk management framework (credit, market, operational, liquidity), scenario analysis and stress testing, and capital/solvency assessment. It binds licensed FIs including banks and insurance companies, with enforcement and administrative/financial sanctions for breach.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
