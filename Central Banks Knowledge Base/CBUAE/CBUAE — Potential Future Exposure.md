# Potential Future Exposure

Potential future exposure is the add-on component of counterparty credit risk exposure, calculated per netting set from asset-class add-ons and combined with replacement cost to derive exposure at default. It is also referenced where derivative exposures arise inside securitisations and funds, with a prescribed proportion of notional used as a substitute where PFE cannot be determined. Binds banks measuring regulatory capital for derivative and long-settlement exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
