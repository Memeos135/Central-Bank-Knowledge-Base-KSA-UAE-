# AML-CFT Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018)

The UAE federal AML/CFT statute (Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018, as amended by Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2021) establishing the offences and core obligations for anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing and combating the financing of illegal organisations. Relevant provisions include the duty to report suspicions promptly to the FIU with no minimum threshold, criminal sanctions (imprisonment and/or fines) for failure to report intentionally or by gross negligence, and safe-harbour protection from administrative, civil and criminal liability for good-faith reporting by institutions, board members, employees and authorised representatives. It binds all persons and entities within scope, including Licensed Financial Institutions and their managers and staff.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
