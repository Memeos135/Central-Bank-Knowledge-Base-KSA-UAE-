# Third-Party CDD Reliance

## Why this community

Customer due diligence architecture under the AML/CTF regime: the baseline CDD obligation, when it escalates to enhanced measures (notably for politically exposed persons), which non-bank sectors are pulled into scope, and the conditions for relying on a third party to perform elements of CDD.

## How members connect

- All members hang off the core CDD obligation as the parent duty; EDD is a risk-triggered intensification of it rather than a separate regime.
- PEP status is the classic trigger condition that converts baseline CDD into enhanced measures, including source-of-funds/wealth and senior-management approval style controls.
- DNFBPs and VASPs are scope-extension nodes: they import the same CDD/EDD duties into non-bank sectors, which matters for who can be a 'third party' at all.
- Reliance on a third party is conditional and does not transfer accountability - the relying institution retains ultimate responsibility, so the reliance rule must be read against the CDD article it cross-references.
