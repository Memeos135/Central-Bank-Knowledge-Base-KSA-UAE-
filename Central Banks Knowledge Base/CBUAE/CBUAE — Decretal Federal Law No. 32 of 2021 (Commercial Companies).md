# Decretal Federal Law No. 32 of 2021 (Commercial Companies)

Reference node for Decretal Federal Law No. 32 of 2021 on Commercial Companies, cited in the Insurance Brokers' Regulation both as a recital and in the licensing article: applicants must be incorporated in the State under this law with insurance brokerage as their objective (or fall within the alternative licensing routes). Relevant to corporate form, incorporation and eligibility questions for broker licence applicants.
