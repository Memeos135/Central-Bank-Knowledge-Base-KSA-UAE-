# Staff Screening and Training

Section 8.2 of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions, sitting under the governance/internal-controls chapter and keyed to the AML-CFT Decision provisions on employee screening and ongoing training. It sets the supervisory expectation that FIs screen employment candidates to high standards and deliver risk-calibrated, regularly refreshed AML/CFT training so staff understand the institution's ML/FT exposure and can identify suspicious transactions; training design should reflect the NRA and topical risk assessments, business/customer risk profile, and delivery, frequency and effectiveness-testing considerations. It binds financial institutions supervised by CBUAE, with practical responsibility on the Compliance Officer and senior management.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
