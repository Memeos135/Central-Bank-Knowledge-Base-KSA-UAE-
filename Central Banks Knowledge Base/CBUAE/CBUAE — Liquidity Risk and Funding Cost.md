# Liquidity Risk and Funding Cost

Part of the ICAAP guidance's material-risk taxonomy addressing liquidity risk and funding cost alongside market and operational risk under Pillar 2. It sits within the expectation that banks identify, measure and hold capital or take mitigating action for risks not fully captured by Pillar 1, including amortised cost portfolio analysis and operational risk shortfalls. It binds banks conducting the ICAAP and their Boards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
