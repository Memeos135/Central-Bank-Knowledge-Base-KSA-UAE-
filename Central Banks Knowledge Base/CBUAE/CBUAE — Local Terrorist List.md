# Local Terrorist List

Covers the UAE Cabinet-issued Local Terrorist List, published by the Executive Office, which LFIs must obtain from the official source and register to receive listing/de-listing notifications. It operates in parallel with the UN Consolidated List for screening, freezing and reporting, and feeds the definition of Listed Person together with related concepts such as Funds, control, and direct/indirect relationship. Binds all CBUAE-supervised LFIs, including those relying on external vendor list feeds.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
