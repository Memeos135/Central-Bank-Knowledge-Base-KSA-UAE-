# Geographic Risk

Concept node treating jurisdictional exposure as a driver of correspondent banking risk: where the respondent institution, its parent, branches and subsidiaries are located and operate, and whether those jurisdictions are high-risk. Presence in high-risk jurisdictions is listed as a trigger for enhanced due diligence on top of the baseline correspondent-specific measures. Applies to CBUAE-licensed financial institutions acting as correspondents.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, sanctions/TFS
