# Article 20: Unclaimed Funds

Article 20 requires a Licensed Person to comply with the Central Bank's Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Funds Regulation and to have board-approved (or owner-approved) policies and procedures to assess, document, disclose and monitor unclaimed funds until paid to the customer or transferred to the Central Bank, with defined roles and responsibilities. Unclaimed Funds is a defined term covering money handed over for onward delivery to a beneficiary that stays undelivered where the rightful owner cannot be contacted for one year. It binds CBUAE-licensed exchange businesses and interacts with customer protection and internal audit scope.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, payments
