# Enabling Technology Guidelines

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for financial institutions adopting enabling technologies (APIs, biometrics, digital identity), including where such adoption is treated as outsourcing and therefore pulls in third-party risk obligations.

## How members connect

- The enabling-technology guidelines are the parent instrument; the defined term Enabling Technology scopes which technologies and use cases the expectations bite on.
- The guidelines expressly route technology arrangements into the outsourcing/OSP regime, meaning accountability remains with the institution and outsourcing controls and notification duties apply.
- Biometrics sits inside a digital-identity chain — credential service provider and identity lifecycle management — which is the point of contact with CDD/identification obligations in the AML regime.
- APIs are treated as a delivery mode of enabling technology, linking this cluster to data-sharing and third-party access controls.
