# IRRBB Risk Governance Framework

Node covering Article 2 (risk governance framework) and Article 3 (risk management function) of the IRRBB Regulation 165/2018. It requires a bank-wide and, where relevant, group-wide IRRBB strategy and governance framework aligned with the risk appetite statement, risk profile and systemic importance, with Board approval and at least annual review, Board- and Senior Management-approved limits, model validation, exception tracking and reporting MIS. Obligations fall on the Bank, with allocated duties for the Board, Senior Management and the risk management function.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
