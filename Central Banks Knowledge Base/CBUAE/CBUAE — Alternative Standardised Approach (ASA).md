# Alternative Standardised Approach (ASA)

Concept node covering the Alternative Standardised Approach, one of the Pillar 1 methods for computing operational risk capital, in which an average volume of outstanding loans and advances scaled by a fixed factor replaces gross income as the exposure indicator for the retail and commercial banking lines. It sets out which credit portfolios feed the commercial loans and advances measure and the rationale of avoiding distortions from differing income margins. It binds banks subject to the Central Bank's capital adequacy framework, and use of the approach — as well as any reversion to the Standardised Approach — is conditional on supervisory satisfaction and approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
