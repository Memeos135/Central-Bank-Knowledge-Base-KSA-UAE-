# Outsourcing Regulation for Banks

The CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation for Banks, setting the framework for outsourcing arrangements: defined terms (Outsourcing, Confidential Data, Master System of Record, Material Business Activity, Group/Parent/Subsidiary), governance and risk requirements, an outsourcing register, data protection duties, mandatory contractual clauses and conditions for outsourcing outside the UAE. Key constraints include retained bank and customer ownership of data, CBUAE access and on-site inspection rights over service providers, in-country maintenance of the Master System of Record (with a limited exception for foreign bank branches), and prior CBUAE approval plus customer consent before sharing Confidential Data abroad. Binds banks licensed in the UAE, including on a group basis.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, data/credit info
