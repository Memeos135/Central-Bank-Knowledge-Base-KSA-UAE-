# Common Infrastructural Services

This node covers the Common Infrastructural Services component of the UAE Open Finance Framework, detailed in Schedule 1 of the Open Finance Regulation. Minimum contents include a Consent and Authorization Manager (a standalone User app or participant APIs supporting creation, management, enforcement and revocation of privacy/consent directives) and a sandbox for ongoing participant testing and official conformance certification. It matters to Licensees, Open Finance Providers and the API Hub for consent lifecycle and conformance obligations.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, consumer protection
