# GoAML System

This node describes goAML, the UAE FIU's reporting platform through which financial institutions file suspicious transaction reports and receive FIU feedback and trend analysis. The Guidelines treat registration as an enforceable step: FIs must register on goAML, keep registration active, and newly licensed FIs must register immediately after obtaining their financial services licence so they are ready to report from the outset; the compliance officer is the designated system user. It binds all supervised FIs and is the operational channel for the STR and related reporting duties.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
