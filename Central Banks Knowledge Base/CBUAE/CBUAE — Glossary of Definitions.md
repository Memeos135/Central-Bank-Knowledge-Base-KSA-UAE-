# Glossary of Definitions

The defined-terms section of the Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies, fixing the meaning of terms such as Mathematical Reserve, Insurance or Underwriting Surplus, Beneficiary, Insurance Agent, External Auditor, Authority Examiners and Unit Linked Insurance Policies. The glossary is expressly stated to apply across both Parts of the regulations, and a separate article fixes the scope of application to domestic and licensed foreign Takaful insurers. Use it to confirm how a term is defined before relying on any substantive article.
