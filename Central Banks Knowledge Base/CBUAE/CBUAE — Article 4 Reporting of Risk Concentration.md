# Article 4: Reporting of Risk Concentration

Article 4 requires the Parent to report significant risk concentrations at Group level to the Central Bank at the frequency and deadlines it sets. It specifies the minimum exposures to be assessed — direct and indirect exposures to individual and interconnected counterparties, geographic areas or industry sectors, and natural disasters/catastrophes — and provides that the Central Bank sets reporting thresholds by reference to solvency capital requirements and/or technical provisions and may mandate risk types always reportable. Binds the Parent of a supervised insurance Group.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
