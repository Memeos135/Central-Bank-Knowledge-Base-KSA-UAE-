# Eligible CVA Hedges

Guidance node explaining which instruments may be recognised as eligible hedges when computing the credit valuation adjustment (CVA) capital charge, and the exclusions that apply. It confines eligibility to index CDS, single-name CDS or contingent CDS and equivalent instruments directly referencing the hedged counterparty (including swaptions without knock-out on a credit event), excludes tranched and nth-to-default products, requires the hedge to have been entered into expressly to hedge the counterparty credit spread component of CVA risk, and removes recognised hedges from market risk capital. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE calculating counterparty credit risk and CVA capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
