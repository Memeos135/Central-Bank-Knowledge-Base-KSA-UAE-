# Standardised Approach (SA) for Operational Risk

The business-line-based method for the operational risk capital charge, using gross income and prescribed beta factors per business line, with Alternative Standardised Approach options substituting loans and advances for commercial and retail banking and applying different beta combinations. The guidance notes gross income is a risk-insensitive proxy, so banks must consider a Pillar 2 add-on. Binds UAE-licensed banks, with a Shari'ah-compliant equivalent calculation expected where applicable.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
