# Special Considerations for External Audit (Article 4)

Article 4 sets special considerations for the external audit, obliging the Board audit committee to assess the external auditor's overall quality at least annually, obtain the audit firm's internal quality control report, review significant audit matters and the management representation letter, and evaluate adherence to the audit plan with documented reporting to the Board. It grants the committee the right to meet the external auditor without Senior Management present and requires discussion of matters affecting regulatory capital or disclosures. It also requires an approved policy on permitted and prohibited non-audit services, with restrictions on the audit firm and its affiliates providing such services.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
