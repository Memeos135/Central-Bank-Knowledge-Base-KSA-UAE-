# Scenario Testing (Article 8)

Article 8 requires recovery plans to be tested against at least an idiosyncratic stress scenario, a system-wide stress scenario and a combined scenario, so as to demonstrate the adequacy of indicators, governance and recovery options. Scenarios must reflect how other institutions' actions affect option feasibility (with scenario-adjusted impacts), align with capital and liquidity planning stress tests, and be severe enough to bring the institution to an identified point of near failure, with reverse stress testing encouraged as a design starting point. Each scenario must show indicator evolution and trigger breaches with and without recovery actions, use conservative point estimates, and explain option choice, sequencing and timing.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
