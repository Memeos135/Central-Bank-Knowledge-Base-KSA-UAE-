# Customer Risk Profile

A concept node on assessing and classifying customer or business-relationship risk, which drives the intensity of CDD applied. It sets out relevant risk factors — customer type and base, maturity and nature of the relationship, ownership and control complexity, transparency of beneficial ownership, regulated versus unregulated activity, PEP and higher-risk sector links, and non-resident status — and requires the classification to reflect the NRA, Topical Risk Assessments and the institution's own business risk assessment, with input from the AML/CFT compliance officer. It binds financial institutions; methodology may scale with size and complexity.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
