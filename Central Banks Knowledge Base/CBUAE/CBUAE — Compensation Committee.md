# Compensation Committee

Node on compensation governance for insurance companies: the compensation committee oversees management's implementation of the company-wide compensation system, monitors whether it creates the right incentives for managing risk, capital and liquidity, and works with the risk committee on incentive evaluation. It must have clear terms of reference, review compensation plans, processes and outcomes at least annually, and obtain an independent external assessment at least every five years; the Board retains oversight of the whole compensation structure, which must align with strategy and Risk Appetite and not incentivise excessive risk-taking.
