# Islamic Finance Company

Defined-term node for "Islamic Finance Company" under the CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation: a Finance Company whose permitted financing activities are conducted in compliance with Islamic Sharia'a provisions. It must have a Sharia'a supervision committee (with members experienced in Islamic finance) whose formation and functions are set in the articles of association, and which is accountable for its Sharia'a decisions and opinions; the board retains ultimate governance responsibility but is expected to be guided by the committee. Disputes between the committee and management must be referred to the Higher Sharia'a Authority, and the corporate governance framework must address Sharia'a compliance, the committee's role and protection of investment account holders' rights.
