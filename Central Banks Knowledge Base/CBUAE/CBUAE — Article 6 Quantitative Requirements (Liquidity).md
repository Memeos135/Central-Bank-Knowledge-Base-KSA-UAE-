# Article 6: Quantitative Requirements (Liquidity)

Article 6 covers the quantitative liquidity requirements applicable to Islamic Banks, i.e. the Shari'ah-adapted calibration of the Eligible Liquid Asset Ratio, Advances to Stable Resources Ratio, Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Net Stable Funding Ratio referred to in the Standard's structure. It determines how Shari'ah-compliant liquid assets and funding sources are recognised for ratio purposes. It binds Islamic Banks; Islamic Windows report these measures on a stand-alone basis for monitoring only.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
