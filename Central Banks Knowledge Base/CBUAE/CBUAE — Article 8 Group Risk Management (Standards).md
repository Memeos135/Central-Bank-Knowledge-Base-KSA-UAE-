# Article 8: Group Risk Management (Standards)

Node for Article 8 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards, addressing risk management on a group basis; the supplied context covers adjacent material on Article 7 approval procedures for new products and strategic initiatives and on the roles of the risk management, compliance, internal audit and business line functions within the risk governance framework. Relevant where a bank has subsidiaries or affiliates and must apply consistent bank-wide and group-wide risk identification, measurement and control. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks and their boards.
