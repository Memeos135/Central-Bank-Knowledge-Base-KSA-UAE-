# Risk Mitigation

Concept node covering the preventive and mitigating controls expected across the correspondent banking lifecycle: enterprise-wide and relationship-level risk assessment, correspondent agreement terms (including termination/limitation causes and confirmation that the respondent does not serve shell banks or prohibited customer types), risk-based scaling of due diligence, and EDD for higher-risk respondents. Controls must sit inside the LFI's wider AML/CFT/CPF programme with governance, independent audit and training support, and be adjusted as the respondent's risk changes. Guidance is not exhaustive; LFIs remain bound by the UAE legal and regulatory framework in force.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** Targeted financial sanctions obligations — notably freezing without delay in respect of UNSC and UAE-imposed designations — are not discharged by screening alone; the linked risk mitigation provisions specify the governance structure and three-lines-of-defence model through which those obligations must be managed, particularly in correspondent banking relationships.
