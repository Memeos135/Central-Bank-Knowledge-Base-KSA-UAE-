# Economic Value of Equity (EVE)

Defines and sets modelling expectations for Economic Value of Equity (EVE) — present value of banking-book assets less liabilities — as an IRRBB metric under the CBUAE Model Management Guidance. It fixes the estimation principles (all interest-rate-sensitive banking book assets, liabilities and off-balance-sheet items; run-off assumption; exclusion of own equity), differentiates granularity expectations between LSIs (facility-level full revaluation) and non-LSIs (gap-based aggregation per Basel IRRBB), and requires all institutions to compute ∆EVE against the reporting-date baseline. Binds CBUAE-supervised institutions managing interest rate and profit rate risk in the banking book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
