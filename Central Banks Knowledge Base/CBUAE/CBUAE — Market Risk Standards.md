# Market Risk Standards

CBUAE Market Risk Standards addressing internal models for market risk measurement: required risk factor coverage for equity, commodity and options exposures (including market-wide equity factors, directional, forward gap/interest rate and basis risk for commodities, and volatility/Vega risk for options), with proportionality to the size and complexity of positions. It also mandates Board-approved policies providing for at least annual independent internal audit review of the market risk measurement system, covering model scope, data accuracy and independence, valuation and pricing model approval, and integration of risk measures into daily risk management. Binds banks using internal models for market risk capital and their boards, risk functions and internal audit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's market risk measurement system is adequate, the risk-factor and measurement requirements in the market risk standards are not the end point: the stress testing instrument sets a floor requiring that internal capital adequacy for market risk be assessed at minimum through stress testing, including concentration risk and illiquidity under stressed scenarios. The two sit in the same prudential chain, with the standards specifying measurement inputs and the stress testing text specifying how those inputs must be exercised for capital purposes. A measurement system that is technically compliant on risk-factor coverage can still fail if the stressed and illiquidity dimensions are absent.
