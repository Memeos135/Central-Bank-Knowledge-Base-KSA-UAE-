# Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies

Concept node for the Financial Regulations applicable to insurance companies, referenced as the benchmark for many violations in the fines schedule (investment basics, solvency margin and minimum guarantee fund, technical provisions calculation, assets matching insurance liabilities). The surrounding schedule text also enumerates penalties for insurance brokers, insurance agents and health insurance TPAs for breaches of their respective licensing, conduct, segregation-of-funds and registration obligations. Relevant to insurers and licensed intermediaries supervised by the Insurance Authority.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
