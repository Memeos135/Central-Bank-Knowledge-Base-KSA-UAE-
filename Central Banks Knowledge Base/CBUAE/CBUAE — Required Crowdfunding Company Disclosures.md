# Required Crowdfunding Company Disclosures

Concept node covering the disclosure and transparency duties imposed on crowdfunding companies, including disclosing business terms and conditions and updates, written warnings of material risks, conflict-of-interest situations that must be identified and managed, publication of default rates, and the further disclosure items listed in the regulation's appendix. It sits alongside related conduct prohibitions (no advice to Clients, no direct marketing of offers/Borrowers, no borrowing by shareholders, directors or employees) and information-sharing with Al Etihad Credit Bureau. It binds licensed crowdfunding companies toward their Clients and the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, data/credit info
