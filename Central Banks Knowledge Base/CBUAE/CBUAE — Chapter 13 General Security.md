# Chapter 13: General Security

Chapter 13 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business is the general (physical/premises) security chapter of the Risk Management and Security Standards part, addressing the baseline protective measures a Licensed Person must maintain. The supplied extract contains only the table of contents and unrelated Chapter 2 material on license categories, legal forms, capital and bank guarantees, so the chapter's operative requirements are not reproduced here.
