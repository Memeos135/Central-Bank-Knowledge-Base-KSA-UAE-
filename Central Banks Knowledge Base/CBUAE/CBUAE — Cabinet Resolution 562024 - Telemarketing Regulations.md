# Cabinet Resolution 56/2024 - Telemarketing Regulations

A UAE federal instrument (Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024) governing telemarketing activity generally, cited as a companion text that the CBUAE Telemarketing Regulation must be read alongside, together with its penalties resolution (No. 57 of 2024), the Consumer Protection Regulation and the Ombudsman Unit Regulation. It sets the baseline national telemarketing rules on which the Central Bank's minimum standards for Licensed Financial Institutions are layered. It binds persons conducting telemarketing in the State; for financial-sector firms it operates cumulatively with CBUAE requirements rather than in substitution.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
