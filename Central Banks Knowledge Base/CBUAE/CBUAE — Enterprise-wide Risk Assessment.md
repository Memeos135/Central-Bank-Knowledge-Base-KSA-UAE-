# Enterprise-wide Risk Assessment

Node covering the enterprise-wide ML/TF risk assessment and risk-based approach expectations in the CBUAE trade-based money laundering (TBML) guidance, anchored to Article 4 of the UAE AML-CFT Decision. It requires licensed financial institutions to identify, document and periodically update their risk exposure across customer, intermediary, geographic, product/service, delivery-channel and operational categories, with explicit treatment of trade and trade finance risk (including an optional standalone trade finance risk assessment). Binds LFIs supervised by the CBUAE; note this is UAE, not SAMA, source material and does not apply to KSA-licensed entities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
