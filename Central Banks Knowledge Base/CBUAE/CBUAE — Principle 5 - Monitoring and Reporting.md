# Principle 5 - Monitoring and Reporting

Principle 5 requires financial firms to ensure internal reporting systems can monitor material climate-related financial risks and produce relevant, accurate and timely information for board and senior management decision-making, with reporting to relevant stakeholders where required. It also requires firms to collect, analyse and aggregate climate risk data, embed it in monitoring and escalation processes, and address identified information and data gaps. Extent, form and frequency of internal reporting should be proportionate to the nature and scale of exposures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
