# Financial Reporting & Audit

## Why this community

Financial reporting, external audit and market disclosure obligations for banks — the assurance and transparency layer sitting on top of corporate governance.

## How members connect

- The Financial Reporting and External Audit Regulation is the anchoring instrument; it expressly links to the Corporate Governance Regulation, so audit duties and board duties are read together.
- External audit obligations are benchmarked to international auditing standards, and audit scope reaches into Pillar 3 disclosure content.
- Board responsibilities and the compensation system are governance inputs that must be disclosed/reported, closing the loop between governance and transparency.
