# LGD Models

Section of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance on Loss Given Default models, treating TTC LGD and PIT LGD as separate models each subject to the full MMS life-cycle (development, ownership, validation, documentation). It sets minimum development steps (data collection, realised LGD computation, driver and segmentation analysis, TTC then PIT modelling) and modelling constraints such as handling bimodal LGD distributions and testing logical features (collateral and recovery-period effects) during validation, plus analysis of the LGD–macro factor relationship. Guidance-level expectations for UAE institutions using LGD for provisioning, portfolio management and capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
