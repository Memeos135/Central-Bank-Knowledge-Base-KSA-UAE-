# Article 10: Credit Risk Mitigation

Article 10 addresses Credit Risk Mitigation, defined in the regulation as cash flows from liquidation of collateral and other sources usable to reduce financial loss, as further specified in the accompanying standards. It operates alongside the underwriting requirements, which already require collateral and risk mitigation to be covered in credit policies and procedures. It binds LFIs and should be read with the Article 1 definitions of Credit Facility, Credit Risk and Country/transfer risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
