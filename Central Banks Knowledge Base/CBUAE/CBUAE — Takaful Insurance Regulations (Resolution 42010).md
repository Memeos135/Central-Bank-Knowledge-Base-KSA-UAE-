# Takaful Insurance Regulations (Resolution 4/2010)

Cross-reference node for the Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 4 of 2010 (Takaful Insurance Regulations) as applied through the reinsurance decision. It supplies the defined terms for Takaful and Takaful reinsurance business and is made applicable to reinsurers to the extent consistent with Takaful reinsurance, alongside Shariah compliance and full technical and financial separation between conventional and Takaful reinsurance operations. Binds Takaful insurance and Takaful reinsurance companies and conventional reinsurers that add a Takaful reinsurance window.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
