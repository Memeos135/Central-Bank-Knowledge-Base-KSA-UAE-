# AAOIFI Shari'ah Standards

Concept node for the AAOIFI Shari'ah Standards as the reference standards used within the CBUAE Shari'ah governance framework for assessing Shari'ah compliance of products, contracts and operations. It is relevant where the ISSC and Higher Shari'ah Authority determine Shari'ah parameters and issue resolutions on institutional activities. Applies indirectly to Islamic financial institutions and Islamic windows through the Shari'ah governance regime rather than as a standalone binding instrument.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
