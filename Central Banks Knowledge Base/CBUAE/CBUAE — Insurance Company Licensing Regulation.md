# Insurance Company Licensing Regulation

CBUAE Insurance Company Licensing Regulation (Circular 4/2026, addressed to all insurance companies), setting the framework for licensing insurance companies incorporated in the UAE and branches of Foreign Insurance Companies, and for opening branches and subsidiaries inside and outside the State. It covers classification of insurance business, licence application and process, registration, commencement of activities, cancellation of prior regulatory instruments, enforcement and sanctions, with an annex of required application documents. It binds founders/applicants and licensed insurance companies, and must be read with the Central Bank Law and related company, free-zone and minimum-capital instruments.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
