# Proliferation Finance & Trade Risk

## Why this community

Proliferation financing and trade-based money laundering risk in trade finance, including export-control evasion, dual-use goods and transshipment abuse.

## How members connect

- Two supervisory guidance documents anchor the cluster and are mutually reinforcing: PF risk guidance and TBML/transshipment guidance share typologies and red-flag logic.
- Substantive linkage runs from trade finance vulnerability to dual-use/controlled goods and export-control circumvention, with transshipment as the concealment mechanism.
- The competent authority (EOCN) is the referenced control and reporting counterpart for export-control and non-proliferation matters.
- Industry standards (Wolfsberg/ICC/BAFT) are cited as persuasive benchmarks, not binding rules - treat as expectation-setting, not enforceable text.
- Cross-jurisdiction flag: CBUAE material; PF obligations in KSA rest on the separate SAMA/TFS framework.
