# Information Systems & Reporting

## Why this community

Obligations on risk-related information systems, data quality and internal reporting lines that supply the board and senior management with timely, accurate risk information.

## How members connect

- Same Regulation-to-Standards pairing: the Standards article elaborates the corresponding Regulation article at matching numbering.
- Reporting duties are the delivery mechanism for other risk obligations — measurement outputs and stress-test results must reach the governing body through these systems.
