# CDD Measures

Node setting out general customer due diligence expectations for trade-related business under Article 5 of the UAE AML-CFT Decision: identification and verification of the customer and beneficial owner, understanding the nature and purpose of the relationship, and ongoing monitoring, performed before or during onboarding or before an occasional transaction. It addresses the trade-specific question of which party to a trade transaction is the LFI's "customer" (due diligence is not owed on all trade parties), heightened attention to open account trade, record-keeping of onboarding and refreshed CDD, and pre-onboarding plus ongoing screening of customers, beneficial owners, authorised persons, directors/managers, executives of legal arrangements and known principal counterparties. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
