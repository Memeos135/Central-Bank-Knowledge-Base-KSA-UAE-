# Reclaim Procedures

Node on the claim and reclaim pathway under the CBUAE dormant accounts regime: how customers, legal heirs or beneficiaries claim dormant balances and other unclaimed funds from the institution (Article 4) and how institutions reclaim amounts already remitted to the Central Bank (Article 8). Covers reactivation and identity re-verification of dormant accounts, record-keeping of payments made, and the one-month settlement deadline for claims absent valid reasons for delay. Binds licensed financial institutions handling claims, including insurance companies for unclaimed insurance funds.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
