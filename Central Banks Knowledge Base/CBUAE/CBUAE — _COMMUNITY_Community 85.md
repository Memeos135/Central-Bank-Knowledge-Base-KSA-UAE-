# Fit & Proper Designated Functions

## Why this community

Individual authorisation regime: which roles are controlled functions, the suitability test applied to the persons holding them, and the ongoing competence obligation once authorised.

## How members connect

- The fit and proper process is the procedural hub, linking the substantive fit and proper criteria to the scope of roles caught by the regime.
- Scope is fixed by the defined term designated functions, which is populated by the attached list rather than by open-ended interpretation.
- The authorized individual is the obligated person: subject to the fit and proper process at entry and to continuing professional development thereafter, making suitability an ongoing rather than one-off condition.
