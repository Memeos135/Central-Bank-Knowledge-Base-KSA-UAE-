# Transfer of Funds to Central Bank

Node on Article 8 (and the linked Article 9 retention rules) of the CBUAE dormant accounts regime: the duty to transfer net dormant-account balances and unclaimed instrument proceeds to the designated Unclaimed Balances Accounts at the Central Bank after the prescribed dormancy period, subject to conditions such as the customer having no other active account and an unknown current address. It also covers reporting formats for movements in unclaimed insurance funds, the requirement to settle customer or heir claims only after receiving funds back from the Central Bank, and the cessation of interest accrual once funds are held centrally. Binds licensed financial institutions, with separate tracks for insurance companies and exchange businesses.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
