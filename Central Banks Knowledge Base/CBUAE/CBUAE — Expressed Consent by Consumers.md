# Expressed Consent by Consumers

Sets the consent standard for use and sharing of Consumer Personal Data: prior written disclosure of the intent to use/share and of the recipients, consent given freely and explicitly in clear and plain language, with notice of the right to refuse. Informed expressed consent is mandatory before use or transfer of Personal Data for direct marketing (including to Authorized Agents), the consent record must be kept five years after the relationship ends, and Consumers may withdraw consent at any time except where processing is needed for business operations on their own products and services. Related marketing conduct rules treat Consumers as opted out absent opt-in consent and bar telephone marketing of loans/financing to individuals.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info
