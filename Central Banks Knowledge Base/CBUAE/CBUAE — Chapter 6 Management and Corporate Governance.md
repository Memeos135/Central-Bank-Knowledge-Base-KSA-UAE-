# Chapter 6: Management and Corporate Governance

Chapter 6 sets management and corporate governance standards for Licensed Persons conducting Exchange Business, covering how powers and responsibilities are distributed within the management structure. It includes concrete requirements on the Head Office (must be in the UAE, staffed by the Manager in Charge and functional heads, not in a free zone or access-restricted location, records available or accessible for examination, relocation only with a Letter of No Objection from the Banking Supervision Department) and on opening a separate Management Office subject to a Letter of No Objection. It binds Licensed Persons, their Boards of Directors (or Owners/Partners where no Board exists) and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
