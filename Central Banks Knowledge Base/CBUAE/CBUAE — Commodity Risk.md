# Commodity Risk

A market-risk concept node within CBUAE capital adequacy guidance covering how commodity exposures are identified and capitalised, including restrictions on offsetting between positions and the two standardised computation routes (simplified and maturity ladder). It also sits within the wider Pillar 1/Pillar 2 market risk framework, where banks must calibrate analysis to portfolios, asset classes, risk factors, geographies, products and tenors. It binds banks licensed in the UAE for capital calculation and ICAAP purposes; supervisory approval is required for correlation-based offsetting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
