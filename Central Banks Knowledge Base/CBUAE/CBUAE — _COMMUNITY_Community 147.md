# IFRS 9 Provisions & TESS

## Why this community

Prudential and accounting treatment of expected credit loss provisioning under IFRS 9, and the transitional capital relief and pandemic-era support measures that modify how provisions feed into regulatory capital.

## How members connect

- The transitional arrangements regulation is the operative instrument; it expressly builds on IFRS 9 ECL provisioning outcomes to determine add-backs to regulatory capital over a phase-in period.
- The same regulation references the support scheme, linking deferred/restructured exposures under that scheme to staging, significant-increase-in-credit-risk judgements and provisioning consequences.
- Hierarchy runs accounting standard (IFRS 9) → SAMA prudential regulation → scheme-specific supervisory expectations, so the standard defines measurement while SAMA controls capital effect.
- Time-bound scope: transitional relief and the support scheme are period-limited, so any answer must fix the reporting date before stating the applicable treatment.
