# Federal Law No. 6 of 1985 (Banks, Financial Institutions)

UAE Federal Law No. 6 of 1985 on banks, financial institutions and Islamic investment companies, listed among the legal bases for IA Board Decision No. 13 of 2018 on marketing insurance policies through banks. It supports the licensing framework for the bank counterparties in bancassurance arrangements, including Islamic institutions. Cited authority only; the enforceable marketing, reporting and penalty provisions sit in the IA instructions themselves.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
