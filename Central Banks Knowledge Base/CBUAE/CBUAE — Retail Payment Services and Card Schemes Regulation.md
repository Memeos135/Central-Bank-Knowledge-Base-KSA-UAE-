# Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation

The CBUAE Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation (Circular 15/2021), which sets the licensing rules and ongoing requirements for providing the nine categories of Retail Payment Services (payment account and payment instrument issuance, merchant acquiring, payment aggregation, domestic and cross-border fund transfers, payment token, payment initiation and payment account information services) and for operating Card Schemes. It also requires contractual arrangements between account-issuing PSPs and initiation/account-information providers, addresses Card Scheme fees reporting and regulation, and sets conditions for PSPs participating in wages distribution and accessing the Wages Protection System. It binds licensed Payment Service Providers, applicants and Card Schemes in the UAE.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk
