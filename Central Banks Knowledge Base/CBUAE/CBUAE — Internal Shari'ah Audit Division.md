# Internal Shari'ah Audit Division

Node on the Internal Shari'ah Audit Division or Section, which performs Shari'ah audit and monitors IFI compliance with Islamic Shari'ah under an annual plan approved by the ISSC in coordination with the audit committee. It sets independence safeguards (promotion, bonus, appraisal and removal decisions escalate to the Board via the audit committee, not to audited management), a prohibition on executive powers over audited activities, qualification and minimum five-year experience requirements for internal Shari'ah auditors, and duties such as maintaining an internal Shari'ah audit manual. The audit committee must assess the function's independence, scope and effectiveness and meet its head at least twice yearly.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
