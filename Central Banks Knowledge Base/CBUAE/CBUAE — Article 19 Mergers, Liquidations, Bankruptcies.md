# Article 19: Mergers, Liquidations, Bankruptcies

Article 19 covers corporate-event controls for Insurance Brokers: no merger, amalgamation or joint venture with any Person without prior written Central Bank approval, and a procedure for voluntary cancellation of licence supported by a closure rationale and plan, alongside treatment of liquidations, bankruptcies and financial restructuring. Binds licensed Insurance Brokers and their owners contemplating structural change or exit; UAE/CBUAE regime, comparator only for SAMA analysis.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
