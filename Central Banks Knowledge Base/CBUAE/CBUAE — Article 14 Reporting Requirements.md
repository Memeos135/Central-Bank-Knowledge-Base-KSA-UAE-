# Article 14: Reporting Requirements

Article 14 of the CBUAE Insurance Brokerage Regulation sets the supervisory reporting obligations that licensed Insurance Brokers owe the Central Bank, sitting immediately after the financial reporting duties in Article 13 (IFRS-compliant statements, an annual signed activity report by the Broker's Representatives and audited annual accounts filed within ninety days of financial year-end). It binds Insurance Brokers licensed in the UAE and, indirectly, their Representatives who sign off on submissions. Note this is a UAE/CBUAE instrument, not a SAMA rule, so it is only relevant comparatively for KSA work.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
