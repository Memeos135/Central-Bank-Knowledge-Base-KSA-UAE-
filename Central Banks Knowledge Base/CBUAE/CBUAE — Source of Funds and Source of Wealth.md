# Source of Funds and Source of Wealth

Covers source of funds (SoF) and source of wealth (SoW) within the CBUAE CDD/KYC guidance, positioning them as additional information to be gathered as the customer's risk profile rises, alongside business description, expected turnover, counterparties and transacting countries. The purpose is to build a baseline of expected activity that supports risk-based ongoing monitoring, customer segmentation and risk rating, and decisions on simplified versus enhanced due diligence. Applies to CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; relevant as comparative material rather than binding KSA obligation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
