# AML/CFT Guidance on Payment Risks

This node is the operative preventive-measures part of the CBUAE AML/CFT guidance on payment-sector risks, explaining how licensed financial institutions (LFIs), including non-bank payment service providers, should apply risk-based controls across payment products, services and systems. It covers segmented expectations for LFIs serving payment customers directly (consumers and merchants, payers and payees) versus those serving other payment-sector participants, and addresses transaction monitoring and suspicious transaction/activity reporting to the UAE FIU. It is guidance rather than new law, but sets supervisory expectations for demonstrating compliance with the AML-CFT Law and AML-CFT Decision; relevant where payment chains are intermediated and each LFI retains its own monitoring responsibility.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
