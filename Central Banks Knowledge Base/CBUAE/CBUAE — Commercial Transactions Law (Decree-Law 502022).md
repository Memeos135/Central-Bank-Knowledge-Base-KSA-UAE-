# Commercial Transactions Law (Decree-Law 50/2022)

Federal Decree-Law No. 50/2022 issuing the Commercial Transactions Law is the parent statute; Article 471 (Book Three, Chapter Six) is the express enabling provision under which CBUAE issued Regulation 1/2026. The Regulation defines 'Commercial Transactions Law' as this Decree-Law and derives its authority from it together with Higher Shari'ah Authority approval and a CBUAE Board decision. Relevant for tracing the legal basis and hierarchy of the Islamic commercial transactions rules, and their interaction with Federal Decree-Law No. 6/2025 on the Central Bank and the regulation of financial institutions, activities and insurance business.
