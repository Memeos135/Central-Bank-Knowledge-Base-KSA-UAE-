# MMS Numerical Thresholds

Appendix table consolidating all numerical thresholds in the CBUAE Model Management Standards with their source articles and their binding strength (mandatory versus strongly recommended). It covers the self-assessment deadline after the MMS effective date, quarterly Model Oversight Committee meetings, quarterly/annual reporting of model life-cycle and Model Risk to the Committee and Board, annual rating frequency, quarterly monitoring and validation reporting, maximum validation/monitoring intervals, and the twelve-month cap on remediating high severity findings. Useful as the quick reference for deadline and frequency obligations binding institutions under the MMS.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
