# AML/CFT Training

Sets the AML/CFT training obligations in the exchange-business Standards, covering who must be trained (all employees plus the Manager in Charge, functional heads, board directors and owners/partners/shareholders), timing (within 30 calendar days for new joiners, who cannot serve customers unaided before completion), risk-based refresher frequency with an annual minimum for customer/product-facing staff, and event-driven retraining when laws, regulations, notices, the Standards or internal policy change. It also requires periodic awareness assessment, training registers and retention of training evidence for supervisory inspection, and periodic review of training materials. Binds Licensed Persons (exchange houses) and, through them, all staff and governing persons; the surrounding context also covers AML/CFT policy approval and Compliance Officer appointment/seniority and reporting lines.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
