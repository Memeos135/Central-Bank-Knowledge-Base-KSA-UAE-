# Beneficial Owner Identification

Covers identification and verification of beneficial owners under the UAE AML-CFT Decision as applied to insurance operators: for legal-person customers, all natural persons with a controlling ownership interest of 25 percent or more, falling back to senior managing officials only as a last resort where no such person is identified; for legal arrangements, the settlor, trustee(s) and equivalent positions. It also links BO uncertainty to enhanced due diligence — corroborating source of wealth, external intelligence reports, screening benefactors, and scrutinising customers holding multiple policies with a common beneficial owner. Binds licensed insurers, reinsurers, agents and brokers conducting CDD.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
