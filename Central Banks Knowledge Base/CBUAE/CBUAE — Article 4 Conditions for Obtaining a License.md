# Article 4: Conditions for Obtaining a License

Licensing gateway provision of the CBUAE Regulation on Representation Offices of Foreign Insurance and Reinsurance Companies, setting the cumulative eligibility conditions an applicant must meet before a licence is granted. It requires the applicant to be a foreign insurance or reinsurance company not otherwise writing business in the UAE, licensed and directly supervised in its home jurisdiction, with at least ten continuous years of supervised operation (reducible by Central Bank decision), and requires the Director and other designated individuals to pass the Fit and Proper Process; group financial position may also be weighed. The surrounding context also covers the approval/rejection notification window, one-year licence term with annual renewal, and prior notice for suspension of operations.
