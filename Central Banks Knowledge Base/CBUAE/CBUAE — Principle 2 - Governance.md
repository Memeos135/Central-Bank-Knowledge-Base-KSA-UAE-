# Principle 2 - Governance

Principle 2 covers governance of climate transition planning, including board- and management-level oversight, allocation of responsibility, and embedding transition objectives into firm culture and practices; it contemplates that firms may align remuneration and incentives with climate transition objectives and run change management and internal communication programmes. These are discretionary or 'should' expectations rather than hard obligations. Applies to UAE financial firms.
