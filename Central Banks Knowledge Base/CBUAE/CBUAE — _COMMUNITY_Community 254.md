# Disclosure Requirements

## Why this community

Transparency obligations of takaful operators — what must be disclosed, to whom (participants, SAMA, the market) and on what cycle.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster imposing reporting/disclosure duties that make the prudential and fund-segregation rules verifiable.
- Functionally paired with the solvency and participants'-fund provisions: disclosure is the mechanism by which compliance with those substantive rules is evidenced.
- Overlaps the consumer-protection regime where the disclosure runs to participants (clarity, timeliness, no misleading content).
