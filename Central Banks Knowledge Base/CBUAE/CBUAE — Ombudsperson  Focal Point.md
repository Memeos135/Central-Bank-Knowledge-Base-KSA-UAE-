# Ombudsperson / Focal Point

Concept node for the de-listing channels available to designated persons — the UN Ombudsperson (for the ISIL/Al-Qaida regime) and the Focal Point for other regimes — and the national procedures supporting them. It covers the Office's role in responding to requests for additional information, coordinating with competent authorities, submitting de-listing requests for deceased persons or defunct organisations with supporting evidence, and ensuring frozen funds are not transferred to other listed persons or listed heirs. It binds the Office and cooperating authorities, and is the due-process route for Listed Persons and their representatives.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS
