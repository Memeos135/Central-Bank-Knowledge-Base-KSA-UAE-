# Board of Directors and Senior Management

A concept node describing the AML/CFT/CPF training expectations specific to an LFI's Board of Directors, owners/partners/shareholders and senior management, covering oversight of the compliance programme, enterprise-wide risk assessment findings, emerging ML/TF/PF typologies, audit follow-up, regulatory developments and tone-at-the-top culture. Training should be delivered as needed and at least annually, with attendance and materials recorded, supplemented by ongoing updates on regulatory issues and risk exposure. It binds the governing body and senior management of CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
