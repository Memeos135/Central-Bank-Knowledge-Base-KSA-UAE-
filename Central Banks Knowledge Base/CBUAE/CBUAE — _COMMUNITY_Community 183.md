# Climate Transition Planning

## Why this community

Sets the purpose and the measurement discipline of a climate transition plan: why a supervised institution prepares one and how progress against it is evidenced over time.

## How members connect

- Both sit in the same principles-based instrument on climate transition planning, so they share its scope, defined terms and addressee institutions.
- Obligation chain: the stated transition objectives determine which metrics and targets are relevant, and the metrics/targets provision is the accountability mechanism that makes the objectives supervisable.
- Conceptually paired - objectives without calibrated targets are unenforceable in practice, and targets are only assessable against declared objectives.
