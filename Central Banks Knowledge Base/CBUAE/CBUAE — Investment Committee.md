# Investment Committee

Describes the required investment governance architecture: Board-approved investment and risk management policies reviewed annually, an overall investment strategy policy covering objectives, risk/liability profile, strategic asset allocation, restricted asset types and derivatives/structured products use, and a Board-level Investment Committee with its own charter and mandate. The Committee's minimum remit includes proposing strategy and policy, setting guidelines, monitoring investments, and working with the Audit Committee on audit scope; related duties cover limit monitoring, counterparty creditworthiness assessment, and at least annual stress testing (demonstrated at head-office level for foreign branches).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
