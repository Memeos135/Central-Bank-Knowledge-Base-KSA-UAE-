# Article 23: Accounts and External Audit

Article 23 governs the books of accounts and external audit of a Licensed Person: records must be accurate, reflect financial position at all times and be available within the UAE. The external auditor may only be appointed after a Central Bank letter of no objection for each financial year, and draft audit reports and financial statements must be submitted for no objection before publication, with annual (and consolidated, where there are subsidiaries) reporting to the Central Bank. Related provisions require agreed-upon procedures by an external auditor over remittance intermediate accounts, reported monthly to the board or owner.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, banking prudential
