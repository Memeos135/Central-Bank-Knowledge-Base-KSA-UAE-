# Licensed Financial Activities

The concept of "Licensed Financial Activities" under Article 61 of Federal Decree-Law 6/2025: the enumerated activities that may not be carried on or promoted without a Central Bank licence, including deposit-taking (incl. Shari'ah-compliant), credit and funding facilities, open finance services, exchange and money transfer, payment services using Virtual Assets, stored value/retail payment/digital money services, arranging and marketing of such activities, and principal dealing in financial products. Licensees must operate only within the scope of their licence, no person may hold themselves out as licensed, and the Board of Directors may grant exemptions; related provisions list grounds for suspension, withdrawal or revocation of a licence. Binds any person carrying on or promoting these activities in the State.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, governance/risk, BNPL/finance
