# Charter of Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee

The annexed template charter that a Takaful insurance company must adopt for its Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, covering the committee's definition, member qualifications, meeting mechanics, decision-making and other procedural matters. It represents the minimum content; additional articles are permitted only if consistent with applicable regulations, standards and resolutions. It operates on the company's board/management in adopting the charter and on ISSC members through fit-and-proper eligibility requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
