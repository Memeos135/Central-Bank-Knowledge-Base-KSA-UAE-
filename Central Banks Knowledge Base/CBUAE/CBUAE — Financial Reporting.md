# Financial Reporting

The financial reporting component of CBUAE Regulation 162/2018: banks must keep appropriate records and prepare financial statements in line with IFRS and CBUAE instructions, with board responsibility for governance and control over fair-value measurement and valuation/estimation processes. Audited statements with the independent External Auditor's opinion must be submitted to the CBUAE at least three weeks before the general assembly and within three months of year-end, published on the bank's website within four months, and made available on request. Distribution or repatriation of profits, retained earnings, reserves or other regulatory capital components, and any public release or presentation of audited statements, require the CBUAE's prior written no-objection.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** For any question about the correct accounting or disclosure basis, the answer is that IFRS is incorporated by reference into the supervisory financial reporting obligation, not applied as a purely voluntary professional standard. The financial reporting requirement obliges regulated banks to keep records and prepare statements in accordance with IFRS as issued by the IASB together with the regulator's own instructions, with external audit opinion on published annual statements. The consequence is a layered test: IFRS compliance alone is insufficient where supervisory instructions impose additional or stricter treatment, and a departure from IFRS is simultaneously a regulatory breach.
