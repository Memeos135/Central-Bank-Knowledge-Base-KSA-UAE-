# Payment Token Customer Agreements

## Why this community

Contractual and disclosure duties owed by a Payment Token Service Provider to its customers - what must be agreed in writing, what must be disclosed before contracting, and what transaction-level information must be provided afterwards.

## How members connect

- Sequential obligation chain within one instrument: the requirement to have a customer agreement, then the mandated content and pre-contractual disclosure, then ongoing transactional information duties.
- All three provisions attach to the same obliged entity, the Payment Token Service Provider, so scope turns on that definition.
- Consumer-protection style transparency obligations sitting inside a payments/token licensing regime - read alongside general SAMA consumer protection principles for overlap.
