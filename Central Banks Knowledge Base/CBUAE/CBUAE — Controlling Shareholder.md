# Controlling Shareholder

Definition node for Control, Controlling Shareholder and Controlling Shareholding in the CBUAE Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation: ownership or control of more than 50% of issued ordinary shares and convertible instruments or of related votes, control over election of a majority of directors or board decisions, or controlling influence over policy, strategy, governance, management or financial affairs as determined by the CBUAE. It also defines Group and treats direct, indirect, sole and acting-in-concert holdings alike. Determines when prior approval, notification and ongoing reporting duties are triggered for banks and shareholders.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
