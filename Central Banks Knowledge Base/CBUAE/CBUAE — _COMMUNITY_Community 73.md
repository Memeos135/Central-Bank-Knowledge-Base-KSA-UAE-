# Digital Identity Verification

## Why this community

Remote customer identification and verification (e-KYC) using national digital identity infrastructure, and the data-protection constraints that attach to relying on government identity attributes for CDD purposes.

## How members connect

- Shared defined activity: identity proofing/enrollment as the operative step that satisfies the CDD duty to identify and verify a customer before or during onboarding.
- Obligation chain: e-KYC guidance points to authoritative identity sources (national ID credential, digital ID app, government validation gateway) as acceptable verification evidence, making source reliability a compliance condition rather than a technical choice.
- Cross-regime interaction: use of identity attributes triggers data protection and privacy obligations (consent, purpose limitation, retention) alongside AML/CTF record-keeping.
- Comparative note for KSA users: members are UAE-jurisdiction instruments; they are persuasive analogues, not SAMA obligations, and cannot be cited as authority for Saudi e-KYC acceptance.
