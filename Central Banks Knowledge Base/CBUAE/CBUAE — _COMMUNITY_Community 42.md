# Insurance Licensing & Climate Risk

## Why this community

Insurance-sector authorisation and prudential regulation (broker/representation-office licensing, fit-and-proper, group supervision) together with the climate-related financial risk regime applied to licensed financial firms. The problem-space is who may be licensed, how a group is supervised, and how climate risk must be governed, capitalised and disclosed.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the central bank law and the insurance activities decree-law are the enabling statutes cited by the subordinate regulations (brokers, representation offices, group supervision, climate risk), with the 2025 central bank decree-law superseding/referencing the 2018 instrument.
- Obligation chain on entry and exit: licensing conditions feed the fit-and-proper test and the powers of revocation, suspension, restriction and variation; representation-office rules import the same licensing and fitness gates plus director responsibilities.
- Group supervision links parent responsibilities, holding-company identification, supervisory colleges and mandatory reporting of risk concentration and intragroup transactions into a single consolidated-oversight obligation set.
- Climate strand shares defined terms (physical, transition and liability risk; scenario analysis) and runs from international soft law (Paris Agreement, NGFS, BCBS-aligned principles, national sustainable finance working group outputs) into binding governance, strategy, risk-management and capital articles.
