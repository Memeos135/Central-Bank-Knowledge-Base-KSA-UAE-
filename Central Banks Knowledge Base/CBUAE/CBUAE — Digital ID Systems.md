# Digital ID Systems

CBUAE guidance on the use of digital identity systems for CDD by licensed financial institutions. It requires an LFI to assess both the system's assurance levels (performed itself with due diligence on the IDSP, or via audit/certification from a competent expert body applying publicly disclosed assurance frameworks) and its appropriateness for the institution's business and risk profile, with both assessments documented and updated periodically and on trigger events. It also flags the interaction with official identity infrastructure (Emirates ID online validation gateway) and with data protection and privacy law.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
