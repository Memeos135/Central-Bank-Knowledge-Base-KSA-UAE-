# Standards for Exchange Business in the UAE

The CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business (Version 1.20, November 2021), issued under the Regulations regarding Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business, setting baseline compliance requirements for all exchange houses operating in the UAE, including new entrants. Organised into licensing and continuing obligations, management and governance, risk management and security, AML compliance, customer protection and appendices, with permitted activities limited to currency exchange, remittance, WPS wage payments and Central Bank-approved special products. Binds every Licensed Person continuously; the Board (or Owner/Partners) is accountable, and breaches attract non-compliance charges under Appendix 4.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Transaction Monitoring|Transaction Monitoring]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are assessing an exchange house, the sector-specific Standards do not displace the general transaction-monitoring expectations — they sit on top of them. The Standards for exchange business operate within the same AML/CFT parent regime that requires ongoing, retrospective monitoring of customer activity to detect behaviour anomalous to expected profile, so the monitoring guidance supplies the substantive benchmark against which an exchange house's controls are tested. For a decision, read the two together: compliance with licensing-specific conditions alone will not answer a supervisory challenge to the adequacy of monitoring coverage or scenario design.
