# Exposures to Related Parties

Article 18 of the CBUAE Large Exposures regulation, which layers additional aggregate and individual exposure caps on related parties over and above the general large exposure limits, including for shareholders holding 5% or more of a bank's capital together with their group of connected counterparties and related parties. The surrounding context also covers board notification and ex-post approval where an existing exposure becomes large without new lending, and enhanced monitoring/reporting of large exposures across credit, legal, compliance, market and interest rate risk. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE (note: UAE instrument, not a SAMA rule).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
