# Remittances Intermediate Account (Article 24.11)

Node covering the remittances intermediate account requirement in Article 24 of the CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation (Circular 7/2025), read together with the Article 1 definitions of Remittances, Payer, Payee and Payment Account. The surrounding Article 24 provisions constrain how a Licensed Person may hold and move customer money — notably the prohibition on lending to any party (including customers, owner, directors, partners, shareholders) or maintaining current accounts on their behalf — and require a Letter of No Objection for acquisitions, mergers, joint ventures, subsidiaries and divestments. Binds Licensed Persons carrying on Exchange Business (currency exchange and/or remittances) in the UAE; the extracted context does not itself reproduce the text of Article 24.11, so open the source to confirm its exact wording.
