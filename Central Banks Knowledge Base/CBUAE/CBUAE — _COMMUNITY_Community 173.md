# Insurance Brokerage Agreements

## Why this community

Conduct and contractual duties in insurance intermediation: the required content and status of brokerage agreements, and the handling of client money flowing through the broker (premiums, claim settlements, refunds).

## How members connect

- The premium/claims/refund duties operate on amounts arising under the brokerage agreement, so the money-handling article is read against the contractual scope set by the agreement article.
- Explicit cross-reference between the two members creates an obligation chain: agreement terms define the mandate; the money article constrains collection, remittance and repayment within that mandate.
- Both are conduct-of-business obligations owed toward insureds and insurers, with fiduciary-style implications for segregation and timeliness of transfers.
