# Central Bank Law (Union Law 10 of 1980)

Definitional node capturing the enabling statute referenced by Risk Management Regulation 153/2018 — Union Law No. 10 of 1980 on the Central Bank, the Monetary System and Organization of Banking, as amended or replaced — alongside related defined terms (Bank, Board, Central Bank, Central Bank regulations, Group, Parent, Affiliate, Islamic Financial Services). It matters mainly for scope and hierarchy: it identifies the legal basis on which CBUAE resolutions, regulations, circulars, rules, standards and notices bind licensed banks. Note that the 1980 law has been superseded in UAE practice, so the "as amended or replaced" wording is the operative link.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
