# Basel Committee on Banking Supervision

Concept node for the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision as the source of the international minimum standards that the CBUAE capital adequacy Standards implement (Basel II standardised approach, adopted in the UAE in 2009, and Basel III). The introduction explains that the Standards are issued under the Central Bank Law, follow Basel calibration (1250% maximum risk weight on an 8% total capital base) but impose a higher UAE minimum of 10.5% excluding buffers, and that the Central Bank may add requirements beyond those listed. Binds all banks licensed in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
