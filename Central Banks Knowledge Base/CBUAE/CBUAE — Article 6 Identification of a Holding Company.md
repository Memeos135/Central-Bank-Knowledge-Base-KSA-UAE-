# Article 6: Identification of a Holding Company

Article 6 sets the criteria and process by which the Central Bank identifies a Holding Company under the Insurance Law: control or acquisition of 15% of insurance activity volume in the State, or insurance activity and related services exceeding 50% of revenues, with calculation rules based on audited financial statements and proportional capital shares. The entity must supply requested data; the Central Bank issues and notifies a determination, may disregard temporarily met criteria, and may maintain Holding Company status where criteria lapse only temporarily.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
