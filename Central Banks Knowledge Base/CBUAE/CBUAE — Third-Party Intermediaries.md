# Third-Party Intermediaries

A concept node covering the role of third-party intermediaries in trade transactions as a TBML and sanctions-evasion risk factor, set against the guidance's defined trade finance terms (buyer, bank guarantee, letter of credit, forfaiting, factoring, open account trade, dual-use items). The material illustrates how intermediaries and layered networks of import/export companies obscure true counterparties, origin of goods and beneficial ownership. Relevant to LFIs applying CDD, beneficial ownership identification and red-flag analysis to trade chains involving brokers, agents and re-exporters.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
