# Internal Audit Report Forms

Specifies the internal audit, risk management and AML/CTF reporting forms that UAE insurance companies and insurance-related professionals must submit to the Authority alongside annual financial statements. It prescribes mandatory internal audit report content (executive summary, scope, methodology, findings, recommendations, challenges), the minimum internal controls to be reviewed (AML policies, risk-based approach, CDD/EDD/ongoing DD, STRs, record keeping, training), and the external auditor's report on AML control effectiveness. It binds internal audit staff, compliance functions and external auditors of licensed insurers and related professions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
