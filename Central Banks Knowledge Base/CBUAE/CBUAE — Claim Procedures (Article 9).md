# Claim Procedures (Article 9)

Article 9 of the IA Code of Conduct and Ethics (original 2010 text) sets the conduct obligations an insurance Company owes when handling a claim from a Client: a documented claims mechanism, free clear claim forms, defined required documents, a stated settlement timeframe, progress updates on request, a decision notification within 15 days of receiving complete documents (extendable with reasons notified), prompt payment against a discharge/subrogation document, and written reasons for any full or partial rejection. It binds insurers registered and operating in the UAE (and, as appropriate, insurance-related professions) in favour of the Insured and Beneficiary. Useful as the baseline fair-treatment standard for claims turnaround and rejection transparency.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
