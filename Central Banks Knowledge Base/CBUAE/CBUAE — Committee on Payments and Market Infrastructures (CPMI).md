# Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI)

Reference concept: the Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures, which with IOSCO issued the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures. The Regulation defines PFMI by reference to these bodies and requires designated LVPS to comply with PFMI requirements, making the international standards operative through the CBUAE instrument rather than as standalone soft law.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
