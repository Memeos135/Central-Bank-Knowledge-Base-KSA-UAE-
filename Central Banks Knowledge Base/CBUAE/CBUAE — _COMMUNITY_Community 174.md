# Loan Restructuring & Provisioning

## Why this community

Treatment of restructured and forborne exposures and the provisioning/classification consequences that follow, including whether restructuring permits stage or classification improvement.

## How members connect

- The restructuring article expressly feeds the classification and provisioning article: restructuring is a trigger event, not a reset.
- Both articles share the default/SICR definitions used elsewhere in the credit risk framework, so they cannot be applied in isolation.
- Practical consequence: cure periods and upgrade conditions sit in the restructuring rule, while the resulting provision level is set by the classification rule.
