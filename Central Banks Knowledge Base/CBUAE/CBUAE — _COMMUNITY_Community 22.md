# Compliance Officer & Reporting

## Why this community

Internal control and governance architecture for an AML/CFT-regulated institution: appointment and role of the Compliance Officer (and alternate), periodic compliance reporting to the supervisor, and the suspicious-transaction reporting chain into the FIU, sitting alongside risk, security and information-security chapters of the same rulebook.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the AML/CFT Law is the primary source; the rulebook chapter on AML/CFT compliance operationalises it into named functions (Compliance Officer, Alternate) and deliverables (bi-annual report).
- Obligation chain: Compliance Officer is the designated recipient of internal escalations, the filer of STRs to the FIU, and the signatory of periodic compliance reporting; the Alternate inherits the same duties in absence.
- FIU reporting links to provisional measures and freezing — i.e. the report is not the end of the obligation; downstream restraint/no-tipping-off consequences follow.
- Risk management, general security and information security chapters cross-reference the compliance chapter, making AML controls part of the institution-wide control framework rather than a standalone silo.
