# Circular No. 163/2018 Operational Risk Regulation

This node concerns Circular No. 163/2018 (the earlier CBUAE Operational Risk Regulation and Operational Risk Standards, issued 29 August 2018), which has been cancelled and replaced by the current Operational Risk and Operational Resilience Regulation. The surrounding material shows the successor regime's content: Article 7 requirements on operational risk reporting (risk profile, limits and tolerances, residual risks and control deficiencies, loss events with root-cause analysis, emerging risks, audit recommendations) and Annex 1 examples of risk identification and assessment tools such as event management and internal/external loss data collection. Useful mainly for historical/transition analysis of obligations applying to LFIs before the current regulation took effect.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
