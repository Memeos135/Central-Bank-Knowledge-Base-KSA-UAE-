# Payment Message Specifications

## Why this community

Technical field-level specification for payment messages in a central bank payment system — the mandatory content and formatting of instruction data that supports downstream traceability and settlement.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in the graph: no internal cross-references, so it should be read as a standalone operational annex rather than part of an obligation chain.
- Its regulatory significance is indirect — message field content underpins originator/beneficiary information duties in the payments and AML/CTF wire-transfer regimes, so interpret it alongside those rules rather than on its own.
- Note jurisdictional scope: this is a UAE central bank artefact and is not itself a SAMA obligation; treat it as comparative material unless a SAMA instrument adopts equivalent requirements.
