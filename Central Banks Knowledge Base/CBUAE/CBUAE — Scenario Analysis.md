# Scenario Analysis

Concept node setting out the CBUAE requirement for climate scenario analysis as a core measurement and management tool for Climate-related Financial Risks. It requires at least annual analysis, integration into ICAAP baseline and stress scenarios where ICAAP applies, coverage of Physical, Transition and Liability risk drivers over short, medium and long horizons, qualitative and quantitative outputs showing impacts on provisions, capital and liquidity against a baseline, and use of results to test adequacy; institutions must build internal capability and may use existing external climate scenarios in the interim. Binds Financial Institutions supervised by the CBUAE, with ILAAP and stress-testing linkages.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
