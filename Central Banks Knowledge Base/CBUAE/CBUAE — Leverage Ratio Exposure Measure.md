# Leverage Ratio Exposure Measure

Node setting out the CBUAE leverage ratio: Tier 1 capital as the capital measure, quarter-end calculation, the regulatory scope of consolidation, and the composition of the total exposure measure (on-balance-sheet, derivative, SFT and off-balance-sheet exposures). It restricts recognition of collateral, guarantees and other credit risk mitigation in the exposure measure and addresses possible temporary exemption of central bank reserves with offsetting recalibration and disclosure. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks on an ongoing basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
