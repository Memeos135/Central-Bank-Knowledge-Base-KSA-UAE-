# International Cooperation and Asset Recovery

Chapter of the AML/CTF/CPF Executive Regulations governing international cooperation and asset recovery: Concerned Authorities must provide the widest possible cooperation promptly and constructively, exchange information spontaneously and on request, prioritise incoming requests, conclude bilateral arrangements, join multilateral asset-recovery networks, and enable informal pre-request assistance. Application is subject to domestic legislation, treaties to which the State is a party, or reciprocity. Binds state Concerned Authorities rather than supervised entities directly, though it drives information demands on them.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, other
