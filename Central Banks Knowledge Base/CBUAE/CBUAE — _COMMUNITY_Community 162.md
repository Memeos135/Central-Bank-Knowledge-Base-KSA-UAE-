# Loans for Share Purchase

## Why this community

Prudential/credit restriction on bank lending extended to finance the purchase of company shares, addressing leverage-driven equity speculation and concentration risk.

## How members connect

- Two versions of the same instrument: the original circular and its later consolidated text, so the consolidated version governs the currently applicable wording.
- Version linkage matters for compliance dating — any conclusion on the permitted scope of share-purchase lending must cite the consolidated text rather than the superseded original.
