# Specialized Bank

This node is the defined-term node for "Specialized Bank" (formally 'Specialized Bank with Low Risk'), the licensed entity category created by the CBUAE Circular 21/2022 regime. It identifies an institution licensed to conduct only a narrow set of Licensed Financial Activities in UAE Dirham for UAE Residents, subject to deposit caps (e.g. natural-person aggregate limit and juridical-person concentration limit), Dirham-only deposit accounts, and constrained wholesale lending and funding ratios. The term determines who falls within the whole regulation; all UAE branches of one Specialized Bank count as a single entity, and the commercial name may use "specialized bank" but not "bank" alone.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
