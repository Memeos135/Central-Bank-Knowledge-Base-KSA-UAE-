# Model Performance Monitoring

Concept node for ongoing model performance monitoring under the MMS, the recurring post-implementation check on whether a model still performs in line with its design objective. Monitoring is narrower in scope than independent validation but shares metrics with it and its results must feed the validation process; it operates within the MMS scope of all decision-support models and the associated CBUAE reporting expectations. Binds institutions subject to the MMS, with escalation of performance deterioration through the model governance chain.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
