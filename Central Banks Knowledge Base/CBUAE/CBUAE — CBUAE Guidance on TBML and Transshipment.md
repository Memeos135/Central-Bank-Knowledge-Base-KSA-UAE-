# CBUAE Guidance on TBML and Transshipment

CBUAE guidance addressed to licensed financial institutions (LFIs) on identifying, understanding and mitigating trade-based money laundering and illicit transshipment risk, set against the UAE's role as a trade and free-zone hub. It covers TBML typologies and red flags, preventive measures across CDD and trade-document review, suspicious activity reporting, and expects these controls to sit within a risk-appropriate AML/CFT programme organised on the three-lines-of-defence model with MLRO independence, resourcing and board/senior-management oversight, plus independent audit. Guidance-level expectations, but binding in practice on all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
