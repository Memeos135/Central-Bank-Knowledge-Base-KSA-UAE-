# Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law 14/2018)

This node is the Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities, as amended), the enabling statute relied on across multiple CBUAE regulations, here surfaced through the definitions and scope provisions of the Retail Payment Systems Regulation. It supplies the Central Bank's powers to license, designate and oversee Financial Infrastructure Systems, and underpins defined terms such as Clearing, Clearing and Settlement System, Designated System, Currency, Default Arrangements, Financial Free Zones and the Grievances & Appeals Committee. It binds licensed financial institutions and infrastructure operators in the State and is the source of appeal rights against Central Bank decisions.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk, other
