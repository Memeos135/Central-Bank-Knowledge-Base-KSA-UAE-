# Consumer Assessment for Affordability

Affordability limb of the CBUAE consumer assessment duties, linking the Article 5 conduct requirement to assess affordability with Article 7 provisions (7.1.4.4–7.1.4.7) on interest/profit-rate stress testing, examination of the consumer's credit record and past credit behaviour, and determination of affordability from information supplied by the consumer and the Credit Information Agency. It requires a written stress-test summary acknowledged in writing by the consumer and a dated, signed affordability assessment summary given to the consumer, and ties the outcome to prudent observance of the Central Bank's Debt Burden Ratio. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions granting credit to Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
