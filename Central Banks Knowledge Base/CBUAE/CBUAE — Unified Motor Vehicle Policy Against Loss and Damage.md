# Unified Motor Vehicle Policy Against Loss and Damage

The unified standard-form motor loss and damage (own damage) policy, covering the insurer's obligations to the insured and the circumstances in which the insurer has recourse against the driver or insured — including driving under the influence, intentional causation of the accident, uncovered trailers, off-road use without additional cover, and theft (recourse against the thief). It also governs policy termination: neither party may cancel while the vehicle licence is valid, with limited exceptions (licence cancellation, replacement policy, transfer of title) and pro-rata premium refund under the short rate schedule where no claims are paid or outstanding. Binds insurers issuing own-damage motor cover and defines insured and driver exposure.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
