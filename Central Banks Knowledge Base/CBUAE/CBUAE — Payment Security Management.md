# Payment Security Management

A technology and payment-security risk component of the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation, requiring Licensees to maintain a risk management framework covering computer and payment systems and the safety and efficiency of SVF scheme operations, proportionate to the nature, size, complexity and technology of the business. It also imposes an incident management framework with senior oversight, including timely notification to the Central Bank of confirmed technology-related fraud, major security breaches and cyber-attacks, prolonged service disruption and systemic incidents causing customer monetary loss or data leakage, plus a stakeholder communication strategy. Binds SVF Licensees (and, for the general risk/internal control provisions, licensed banks conducting SVF business in the UAE).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
