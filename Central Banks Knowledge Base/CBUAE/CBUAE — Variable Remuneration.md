# Variable Remuneration

Concept node on variable (performance-based) remuneration under the CBUAE Remuneration Regulation, including deferral, performance adjustment, malus and clawback-style contractual provisions. It sets minimum deferral thresholds for Material Risk Takers (at least 40% deferred over at least three years) and Senior Material Risk Takers (at least 60% over at least five years), requires the variable proportion to rise with seniority and responsibility, and requires contract terms allowing reduction or reversal before vesting for poor performance, realised risk, misconduct or breaches of law, regulation or policy. It binds Banks and Insurance/Reinsurance Companies in respect of designated risk-taking staff.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
