# Risk Assessment

Concept node drawing on two CBUAE guidance documents, covering the institution-level risk assessment as the basis for a risk-based approach to sanctions compliance and transaction monitoring, together with supporting expectations on board and senior management oversight, staff training, quality assurance and integration into the wider AML/CFT programme. It makes clear that the risk-based approach cannot justify omitting sanctions screening of any customer relationship or transaction, which is a minimum legal requirement; risk sensitivity only justifies additional or more rigorous controls. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions and their boards.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
