# Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk (Article 16)

Node for the Shari'ah non-compliance risk article of the CBUAE Islamic banks risk management Standard. The Standard treats this as a material risk to be managed within the risk governance framework and the three-lines-of-defence model, requires zero tolerance in the risk appetite statement, and assigns Risk Committee supervision and control-setting in consultation with the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee via the internal Shari'ah control function, with Internal Shari'ah Audit reflected in the ICAAP internal control review. Binds Islamic banks and Islamic Windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
