# Transition Risk

Concept node for Transition Risk — the financial effects on Financial Institutions of the shift to a low-carbon economy — within the CBUAE climate-related financial risk framework. It drives credit/underwriting assessment of customers' economic sectors, in-house or external rating and scoring of current and prospective customers, market valuation effects, and adverse liquidity impacts under normal and stressed conditions. Applies to Financial Institutions supervised by the CBUAE as part of their risk identification, measurement and mitigation obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
