# Article 6: Insurance Brokerage Agreements

Governs the written Insurance Brokerage agreements between brokers and Companies, and confirms the Central Bank may require amendment of specific terms case by case to protect the public interest. Read with the Article 1 definitions of Insurance Broker and Insurance Brokerage (solicit, negotiate, sell) that frame the independent intermediary role and remuneration by the Company.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
