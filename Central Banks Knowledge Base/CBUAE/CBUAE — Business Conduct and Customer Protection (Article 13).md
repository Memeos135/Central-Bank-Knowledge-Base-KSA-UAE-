# Business Conduct and Customer Protection (Article 13)

Article 13 of the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation governs business conduct and Customer protection: SVF schemes must be operated prudently and competently without adversely affecting the interests of Customers or potential Customers, and Licensees must also comply with the Central Bank's existing consumer protection regulatory requirements. It requires responsible, honest and professional conduct and equitable, honest and fair treatment of both Customers and merchants at all stages of the relationship, and conduct that does not undermine the stability of any payment system in the State. Binds SVF Licensees and licensed banks carrying out SVF business.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments
