# Tawteen System

Defined-term node for the Tawteen System, the CBUAE platform used to facilitate and oversee recruitment and employment of UAE Nationals in the banking, financial and insurance sectors. It is the prescribed reporting channel under the Emiratization Violations Regulation, where Licensed Institutions must notify CBUAE of Beneficiaries who do not work full-time or cease employment after onboarding, and of changes to the terms of benefit. Failure to update the system attracts an administrative fine of AED 20,000 per National.
