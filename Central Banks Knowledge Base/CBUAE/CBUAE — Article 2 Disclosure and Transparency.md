# Article 2: Disclosure and Transparency

Article 2 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards sets the disclosure and transparency baseline for all financial products and services across every channel (branch, phone, mobile, internet). It mandates bilingual Arabic/English disclosure, accessibility for People of Determination, and clear, plain, readable presentation; the extract also shows cross-references from Article 5 for credit products, structured products (prior CBUAE approval before sale/marketing) and remittances/transfers/FX (fixed-amount, pre-disclosed fees, including correspondent bank fees). Binds licensed financial institutions towards Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, banking prudential
