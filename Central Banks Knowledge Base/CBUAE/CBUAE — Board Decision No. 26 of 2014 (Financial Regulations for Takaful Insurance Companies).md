# Board Decision No. 26 of 2014 (Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies)

Referenced instrument: Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 26 of 2014 containing the Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies, the Shari'a-compliant counterpart to Decision No. 25 of 2014. It supplies the investment limits, capital and solvency requirements applied to Takaful operators, including through Decision No. 22 of 2017 on applying investment limits. It binds Takaful insurance companies licensed in the UAE.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
