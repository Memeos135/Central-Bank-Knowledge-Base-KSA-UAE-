# Bank Liquidity Regulations

## Why this community

Bank liquidity regulation: the qualitative governance requirements and the quantitative ratios (ELAR/ASRR, LCR/NSFR) banks must meet, with Basel III as the underlying standard.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: central bank law → liquidity regulation (qualitative vs quantitative articles) → guidance manual/circulars, with an earlier circular expressly superseded.
- The quantitative article is the hub referencing each ratio; the ratios in turn depend on the shared defined term High Quality Liquid Assets and cite Basel III/BCBS as source.
- Qualitative requirements chain into governance artefacts — liquidity risk framework, contingency funding plan and stress testing — which condition how the ratios are managed, not just calculated.
- An Islamic-banking standard sits alongside as a scope variant, signalling that the same ratios apply with Shariah-specific treatment of liquid assets and funding.
