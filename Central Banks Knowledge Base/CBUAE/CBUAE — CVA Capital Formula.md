# CVA Capital Formula

Sets out the conceptual basis and formula for the standardised CVA capital charge under CBUAE standards: a value-at-risk construct at a one-year, 99% confidence level, giving the 2.33 multiplier applied to the standard deviation of changes in CVA, with the Central Bank using simpler notation than the BCBS formula while producing the same result. Also confirms the exclusion of own-credit/DVA effects and the limited set of eligible hedges. Relevant to banks computing CVA capital and to reviewers validating the calculation's inputs and confidence-level assumptions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
