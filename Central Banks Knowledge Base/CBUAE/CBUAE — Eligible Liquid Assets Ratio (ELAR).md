# Eligible Liquid Assets Ratio (ELAR)

Concept node on eligible liquid assets and the ELAR, the simpler liquidity ratio available to banks not applying the Basel III LCR. The surrounding guidance defines Level 1 assets (0% risk-weighted under the Basel II standardised approach, no haircut, with a 25% sub-cap on unrated 0% risk-weighted assets), Level 2A and 2B assets with haircuts and caps (40% overall, 15% for Level 2B), the unencumbrance requirement, currency matching and diversification expectations, and national discretion for unrated UAE GRE/PSE issuers. Applies to banks in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
