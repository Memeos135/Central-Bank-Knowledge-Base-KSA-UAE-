# Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies (Decision 25/2014)

Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 25 of 2014 issuing the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies: a consolidated prudential rulebook with a binding glossary plus seven sections on policyholder-rights investment, solvency margin and minimum guarantee fund, technical provisions, admissible assets, mandatory records and data to be made available to the supervisor, accounting books for companies/agents/brokers, and reporting formats. It binds insurance companies incorporated in the State and licensed foreign branches, with defined terms applying throughout unless context requires otherwise. **Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
