# External Audit

The external audit component of CBUAE Regulation 162/2018: banks must annually appoint one or more CBUAE-approved External Auditors, with the board audit committee recommending appointment, reappointment, dismissal and remuneration. It mandates rotation of the audit firm at least every six years and of the engagement partner every three, prohibits non-audit services that could impair objectivity, and requires audits under the International Standards on Auditing. The CBUAE may meet the auditor, access working papers, require rescission of an appointment, commission special-purpose reports at the bank's expense, and act against auditors who breach independence or duties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
