# Remittance Intermediate Account

Prudential/safeguarding rule requiring Licensed Persons holding a Category B or C licence to maintain a Designated Remittance Intermediate Account with a CBUAE-licensed bank, deposit customer remittance funds into it by end of banking hours on the next business day, and use those funds solely to settle remittances with foreign correspondents. Also prescribes the General Ledger accounting treatment (separate control account, optional sub-accounts, transfer to correspondent ledgers on payment order transmission) and is subject to external auditor agreed-upon procedures verifying ring-fencing and funding. Binds Licensed Persons conducting remittance business and their external auditors.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk
