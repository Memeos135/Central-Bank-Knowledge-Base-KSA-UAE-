# Debt Burden Ratio (DBR)

Provisions on the Debt Burden Ratio and assessment of a consumer's total debt repayment obligations (7.1.4.7–7.1.4.9), requiring compliance with the Central Bank-prescribed DBR, comprehensive due diligence on overall secured and unsecured indebtedness, and verification with the Credit Information Agency. It prescribes how the approvable credit amount is calculated, including treatment of interest/profit-only construction-phase mortgages and discounted introductory rates (use the highest applicable rate; refuse the financing if the DBR is breached), and prohibits balloon structures used to circumvent DBR, personal financing limits or loan-to-value ratios. It also bars granting excessive credit beyond what the consumer needs or requested. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential, data/credit info
