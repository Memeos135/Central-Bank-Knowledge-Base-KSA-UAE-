# Internal Audit (Article 13.7)

Corporate-governance provision requiring an Exchange Business licensee to establish an internal audit function reporting directly to the Board of Directors (or the owner of a sole establishment), with a scope covering all business activities, core and non-core functions, branches and outlets, head and management offices, reconciliation and unreconciled items, Unclaimed Funds and financial ratios, in addition to AML/CFT and regulatory compliance. Sits alongside the mandatory appointment of natural persons to Designated Functions under the Fitness and Propriety Regulation. Binds Licensed Persons and their boards/owners.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
