# Annex II: Technology Risk & Information Security Guidance

Annex II is the Regulation's guidance on best practices for technology risk and information security, referenced from the binding technology-risk article. That article requires each Payment Service Provider to maintain a proportionate technology and cyber-security risk management framework covering IT controls, cyber resilience and the reliability, security and availability of its systems, with the Annex itself framed as guidance to consult rather than a standalone mandate. Additional security and cyber obligations attach to PSPs providing Payment Token Services, including per-token security risk assessment and periodic consideration of penetration and cyber-attack simulation testing above a monthly transaction-value threshold.
