# FATF Recommendation 7 (TFS related to PF)

A concept node on FATF Recommendation 7, the standard requiring countries to implement targeted financial sanctions related to proliferation financing fully and without delay, with monitoring, inter-agency coordination and measures against sanctions evasion. The guidance situates it alongside Recommendations 1 and 2 (PF risk assessment; national cooperation) and related FATF guidance covering sanctions on North Korea and Iran, including activity-based and sectoral measures falling outside R.7. It is a supervisory benchmark informing CBUAE expectations of LFIs rather than a directly binding rule.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
