# Bank Loans & Consumer Credit

## Why this community

Retail and consumer credit conduct rules for banks — product perimeter (personal loans, car loans, credit cards), pricing/interest calculation method, and affordability limits on instalment deductions.

## How members connect

- A single regulation acts as the parent instrument and defines each credit product type, the interest computation method, and the deduction cap; the products are obligations-bearing sub-nodes rather than independent rules.
- Version lineage matters: the consolidated version supersedes/amends the earlier version, so the operative text for any obligation must be taken from the consolidated instrument.
- The instalment cap is an affordability constraint attached primarily to personal lending and interacts with the interest computation formula in determining a compliant offer.
- Older labour/salary legislation is cited as the source of the salary concept underpinning the deduction cap, an external cross-reference outside the banking regime.
