# AML/CFT Training & Risk Assessment

## Why this community

Institutional-level AML/CFT/CPF programme foundations: the risk-based approach, enterprise-wide risk assessment, proliferation financing risk analysis, and role-differentiated staff training.

## How members connect

- Both best-practice notes derive from the same statutory base (AML-CFT Law plus Cabinet Decision) and elaborate programme-level duties rather than transaction-level ones.
- Logical chain: institutional risk assessment determines risk appetite and controls, which in turn determines what each role must be trained on - training adequacy is judged against the assessed risk profile.
- PF risk (threat/vulnerability/consequence) extends the same methodology from ML/TF to proliferation financing, aligning with the FATF standard cited by all members.
- FATF materials function as the interpretive benchmark; they are persuasive, not directly enforceable, and should be distinguished from the binding local instruments.
