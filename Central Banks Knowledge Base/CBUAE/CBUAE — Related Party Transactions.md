# Related Party Transactions

Node covering Related Parties and Related Party Transactions in the CBUAE insurance governance framework, including the defined scope (Group, Controlling Shareholders, Board and Senior Management and their Relatives, and persons with control or significant influence) and the wide transaction perimeter (on- and off-balance sheet exposures, service contracts, asset sales, leases, derivatives, borrowings, write-offs). It requires arm's length terms, no more favourable terms than for non-related counterparties, policies to identify and aggregate exposures, independent review or audit, escalation of exceptions, and ongoing Senior Management monitoring with Board oversight; it connects to the conflict-of-interest policy including abstention from voting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
