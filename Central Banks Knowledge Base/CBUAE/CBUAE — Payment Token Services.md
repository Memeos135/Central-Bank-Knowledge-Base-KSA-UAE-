# Payment Token Services

Defines "Payment Token Services" as the Retail Payment Services comprising Payment Token Issuing, Buying, Selling, Facilitating the Exchange of Payment Tokens and related activities, placing them inside the CBUAE licensing perimeter for Retail Payment Services. The node also carries the perimeter exclusions: Security Tokens and Commodity Tokens fall to the Securities and Commodities Authority, and Virtual Asset Token services sit outside the Regulation; it further treats Payment Token Services as inherently high ML/TF risk requiring risk assessment, mitigation and adherence to FATF virtual-asset guidance. Binds Payment Service Providers licensed or seeking licence to carry on these activities.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk
