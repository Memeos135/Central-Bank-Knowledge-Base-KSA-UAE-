# APA Form (Authorised Person's Appointment)

Node labelled for the Authorised Person's Appointment (APA) form used to obtain Central Bank approval for designated positions at a Licensed Person. The extracted context, however, is substantive AML/CFT material: KYC on representatives and beneficial owners of funds (including insisting the beneficial owner attend in person and terminating/reporting if refused), enhanced due diligence and conditions for trade-related remittances by legal persons (commercial rationale, invoices in the entity's name, sanctions screening of all parties), and wire transfer duties for ordering and intermediary institutions. Useful mainly if you are tracing appointment approvals or the adjacent EDD/wire-transfer rules; verify which of the two you need before opening.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
