# Islamic Banking Market Risk

This node covers the market risk requirements applicable to banks offering Islamic financial services, sitting alongside the quarterly Report on Market Risk Exposures and back-testing exception reporting obligation, and the specification of equity and commodity risk factors for internal models. It requires such banks to maintain a market risk management and reporting framework covering all assets (especially illiquid or highly volatile ones), to set and annually review a market risk strategy and risk appetite reflecting contractual arrangements with fund providers and restricted investment account portfolios, to disclose that strategy to fund providers, and to support tradable-asset risk appetite with capital. It binds banks as supervised entities, with the Islamic-specific provisions applying to those offering Islamic financial services.
