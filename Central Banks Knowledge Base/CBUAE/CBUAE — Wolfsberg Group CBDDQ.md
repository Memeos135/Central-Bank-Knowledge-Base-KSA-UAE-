# Wolfsberg Group CBDDQ

A descriptive section of CBUAE correspondent banking guidance explaining the Wolfsberg Group and its Correspondent Banking Due Diligence Questionnaire (CBDDQ, revised February 2023) as the industry tool for collecting information on a respondent institution's ownership, regulatory status, products, AML/CFT/CPF, sanctions, fraud and ABC programmes, and its CDD/KYC, EDD, monitoring and testing controls. It sits alongside guidance on assessing the respondent's licensing and home-jurisdiction AML/CFT/CPF regime, and stresses that the same risk factors apply to branches, affiliates and subsidiaries that are direct customers. It informs how licensed financial institutions structure correspondent due diligence; the Wolfsberg materials themselves are industry standards, not binding law.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
