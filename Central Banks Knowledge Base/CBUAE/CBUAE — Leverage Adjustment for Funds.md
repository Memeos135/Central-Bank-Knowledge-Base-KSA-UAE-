# Leverage Adjustment for Funds

Sets out the leverage adjustment applied to the average risk weight of a fund when determining the risk weight for a bank's equity investment in that fund. Leverage is the ratio of total unweighted fund assets to fund equity, derived from actual holdings under the look-through approach or from the maximum leverage permitted by the mandate or governing regulations under the mandate-based approach; the resulting risk weight (average risk weight multiplied by leverage) is capped at 1250 percent. Binds all UAE banks holding in-scope equity positions in funds on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
