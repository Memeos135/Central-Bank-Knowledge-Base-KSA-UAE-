# Article 7: Strategic and Operational Decisions (Standards)

Article 7 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards requires banks to have approval procedures for new products, material product modifications and strategic or major operational initiatives (systems, business model changes, acquisitions), with board or board-committee approval and possible delegation of product approvals to management while the board remains ultimately responsible. It prescribes minimum content: scenario-based risk assessment including pessimistic cases, an assessment of whether control and support functions have the expertise and tools to manage the risks (with power to withhold approval), and ongoing performance-versus-projection review. It also flags special risk challenges from mergers, acquisitions, disposals and group restructuring. Binds all CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
