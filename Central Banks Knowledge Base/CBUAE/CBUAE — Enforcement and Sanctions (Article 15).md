# Enforcement and Sanctions (Article 15)

Article 15 of the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation makes any breach of the Regulation subject to supervisory action and administrative and financial sanctions as the Central Bank sees fit, including replacing or restricting the powers of senior management or the board, interim management of the Licensee, fines, or barring individuals from the UAE financial sector. The surrounding articles cover interpretation (Regulatory Development Division), repeal of the 2016 Regulatory Framework for Stored Value and Electronic Payment Systems, publication/application, and the one-year transition period requiring existing Licensees to complete implementation and submit an independent assessment report. Binds SVF Licensees and their senior individuals.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
