# Designated Nonfinancial Businesses and Professions (DNFBP)

A concept node explaining when DPMS and real estate agents/brokers qualify as Designated Nonfinancial Businesses and Professions under Article 3 of the AML-CFT Decision — for DPMS, single or linked transactions exceeding AED 55,000; for agents/brokers, concluding property purchase and sale operations for customers — and the consequence that they become subject to preventive measures substantially equivalent to those on LFIs. Classification is made by the Ministry of Economy, which also supervises obliged DNFBPs; LFIs need not classify but must establish DNFBP status through CDD and must not treat non-DNFBP status as evidence of low risk.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
