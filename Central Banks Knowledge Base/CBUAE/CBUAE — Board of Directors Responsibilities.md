# Board of Directors Responsibilities

Concept node on Board of Directors composition, structure and responsibilities under the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks. It covers chair accountability, minimum six meetings a year, a non-member board secretary, minute-keeping, delegation of authority but not responsibility to committees, UAE-national chair and majority requirement, twelve-year cap on Independent Member tenure, multiple-directorship limits, government shareholder representation, a rigorous candidate selection and Fit and Proper Process with a 20% female candidate requirement, and Central Bank no-objection before appointment, renewal or removal. It binds Banks and individual Board Members.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
