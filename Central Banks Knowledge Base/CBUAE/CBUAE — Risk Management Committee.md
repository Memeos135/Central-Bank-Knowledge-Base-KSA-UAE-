# Risk Management Committee

Node on the Board-elected risk management committee for insurance companies under the CBUAE governance standards, set in the context of board committee mandates (alongside the audit committee reporting obligations). The committee proposes risk management policies, risk tolerance and Risk Appetite for Board approval, follows up and updates them annually, ensures regular risk assessments, monitors the risk management process and obtains assurance from internal and external providers, including on the resources and expertise of the risk management function.
