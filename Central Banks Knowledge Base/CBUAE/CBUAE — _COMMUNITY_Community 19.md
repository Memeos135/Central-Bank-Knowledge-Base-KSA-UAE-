# Securitisation Framework

## Why this community

Prudential capital treatment of securitisation and resecuritisation exposures under SAMA's adoption of the Basel securitisation framework, including hierarchy of calculation approaches and the qualifying conditions for preferential treatment.

## How members connect

- The Securitisation Standard sits at the top of the hierarchy and points down to each calculation method (SEC-ERBA, SEC-SA) and to the qualifying regime for STC transactions.
- Both approaches share the same defined risk-parameter vocabulary — attachment/detachment points, tranche thickness and tranche maturity — so a definitional change propagates across all risk-weight outcomes.
- Scope and integrity conditions (resecuritisation exclusions, ABCP conduit treatment, clean-up call limits, prohibition on implicit support) act as gating conditions: failing them removes recognition of risk transfer or preferential STC weights.
- Clean-up calls and implicit support are conceptually linked as forms of non-contractual or residual sponsor exposure that can force re-consolidation of transferred risk.
