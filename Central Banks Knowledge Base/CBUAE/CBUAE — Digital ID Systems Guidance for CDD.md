# Digital ID Systems Guidance for CDD

CBUAE guidance on when and how licensed financial institutions may rely on digital identity systems to perform customer identification and verification under the CDD obligation, which is treated as technology-neutral and open to non-documentary evidence. It requires LFIs to assess a digital ID system's assurance levels (self-assessed or via a credible expert body) and its appropriateness for the institution's business and risk profile, and to document and periodically refresh both assessments. Scope is limited to identity proofing/enrollment and authentication for natural-person customers; it does not cover legal-person representatives or beneficial owners, nor portability/interoperability.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
