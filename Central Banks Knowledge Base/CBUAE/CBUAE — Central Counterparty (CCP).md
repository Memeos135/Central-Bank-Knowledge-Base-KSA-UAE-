# Central Counterparty (CCP)

Explains the CBUAE rationale and framework for capitalising bank exposures to central counterparties, distinguishing qualifying from non-qualifying CCPs and assigning lower risk weights where qualification standards are met. Surrounding context also covers combined replacement cost and PFE treatment where one margin agreement spans multiple netting sets. Applies to banks clearing or providing client clearing for OTC derivatives.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
