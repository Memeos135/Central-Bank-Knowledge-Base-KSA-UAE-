# Major Acquisition

Defined term in the Major Acquisitions Regulation: an acquisition or investment by a bank in assets, including shares but excluding debt other than convertible debt, whose aggregate amount in a single juridical person exceeds 5% of Total Regulatory Capital, or which the Central Bank deems in advance to be a Major Acquisition. It is the threshold that triggers the regulation's prudential approval and governance obligations, and is distinguished from a Notification Acquisition (above 1% but below the Major Acquisition criteria). Purchase Price is measured gross, and the definition binds all banks subject to the regulation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
