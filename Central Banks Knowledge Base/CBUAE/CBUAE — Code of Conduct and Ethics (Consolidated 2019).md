# Code of Conduct and Ethics (Consolidated 2019)

The consolidated (as at 16/09/2019) version of IA Board Resolution No. 3 of 2010, the Instructions on the Code of Conduct and Ethics for insurance companies and insurance-related professions in the UAE. It sets market-conduct duties covering utmost good faith, disclosure and transparency, accurate information to Clients, policy issuance and renewal, advertising standards, claims handling, dealings with the regulator, and settlement of inter-company balances and disputes. It binds insurers registered by the Insurance Authority and operating in the State (including via agents or brokers) and, proportionately, licensed insurance-related professions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
