# Legal Representation (Article 8)

Article 8 of the CBUAE Standard on controls and procedures for establishing a Takaful Insurance Fund, dealing with who represents the Fund externally. It makes the Takaful Company the Fund's exclusive legal representative before third parties and judicial authorities, and channels all lawsuits or claims arising from Takaful Insurance Policies against the Company, subject to a judicial carve-out where a conflict of interest exists. It binds licensed Takaful Insurance Companies (including insurers operating Takaful Windows) managing a Fund that has separate legal personality.
