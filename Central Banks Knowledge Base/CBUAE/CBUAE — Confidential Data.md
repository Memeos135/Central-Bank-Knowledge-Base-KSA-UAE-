# Confidential Data

Defined term in the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation for Banks: account or other data relating to an identified or identifiable bank customer, including where identification is possible by combining that data with other information available to a person granted access. The definition sets the perimeter for the Regulation's strongest controls — ownership retention, access restrictions, authorisation before onward disclosure, breach notification, and the prohibition on sharing outside the UAE without approval and customer consent.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, banking prudential
