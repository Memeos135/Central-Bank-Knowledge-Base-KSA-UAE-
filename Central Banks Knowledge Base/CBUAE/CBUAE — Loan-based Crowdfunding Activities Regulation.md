# Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation

CBUAE Circular No. 7/2020, the Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities (LCA) Regulation, establishing the licensing, regulatory and monitoring framework and minimum standards for loan-based crowdfunding in the UAE. It defines the Loan-based Crowdfunding Company and platform model (lenders select opportunities, the platform prices the loan), on-boarding, personal loan and regulated activity, and tiers firms into Category 1 (large) and Category 2 (small) by annual cumulative lending volume against an AED 5,000,000 threshold. It binds crowdfunding companies engaging in LCAs in the UAE wherever their platform is hosted, excluding the Financial Free Zones, with stated aims of financial-system protection and consumer protection.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
