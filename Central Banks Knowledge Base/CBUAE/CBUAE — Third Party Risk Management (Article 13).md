# Third Party Risk Management (Article 13)

Article 13 of the CBUAE Operational Risk and Operational Resilience Regulation governs LFIs' management of risk arising from Third-Party Service Providers, including ongoing monitoring of provider financial condition and of dependencies supporting Critical Operations. It requires reliance on third parties to be reflected in the operational risk and resilience framework, the Business Continuity Plan and the mapping of critical assets, and requires viable contingency and exit plans plus an assessment of substitutability where timely replacement would be costly, high-risk or impossible. The Central Bank retains discretion to require discontinuation of a third-party arrangement or of an over-dependent Critical Operation, so the article binds LFIs and constrains outsourcing decisions.
