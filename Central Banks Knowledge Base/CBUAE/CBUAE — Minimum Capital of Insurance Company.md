# Minimum Capital of Insurance Company

Concept node isolating the substantive capital requirement in Cabinet Resolution No. 42 of 2009: the subscribed and paid-up capital floors for insurers and reinsurers, the ownership condition requiring a majority stake held by UAE/GCC nationals or wholly national-owned juridical persons, and the Authority-approval gateway for increasing or reducing capital. Scope covers all licensed companies except those in free zones. Relevant to licensing, shareholder changes and capital restructuring decisions.

**Regimes:** other
