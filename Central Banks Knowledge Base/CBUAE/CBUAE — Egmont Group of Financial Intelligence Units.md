# Egmont Group of Financial Intelligence Units

A glossary/context node defining the Egmont Group as the intergovernmental network of Financial Intelligence Units that enables secure exchange of financial intelligence and expertise to counter ML/FT. It appears in the definitions section of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines together with FATF, FSRBs, Financial Group and Financial Institution, and supports the Guidelines' discussion of FIU information sharing with foreign counterparts. It imposes no direct duty on FIs but frames how STR-derived intelligence may travel internationally.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
