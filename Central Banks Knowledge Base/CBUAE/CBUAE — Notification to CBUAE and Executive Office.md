# Notification to CBUAE and Executive Office

Operative notification duty within the CBUAE sanctions compliance guidance: it sets out when a Licensed Financial Institution must immediately inform the Central Bank and, separately, the Executive Office, anchored in Cabinet Decision 74 (Art. 21(5) and Art. 15(2)). Triggering events include freezing action taken under relevant UNSCRs or the Local Terrorist List, confirmed matches with Listed Persons, discovery that a current, previous or occasional customer is listed, suspicion of a direct or indirect relationship with a Listed Person, unresolvable potential matches where no action could be taken for lack of identifying data, and unfreezing of funds with related particulars. Attempted transactions are reportable alongside executed ones; the duty binds all LFIs supervised by CBUAE.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
