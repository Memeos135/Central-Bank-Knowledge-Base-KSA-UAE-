# Regulations Regarding Mortgage Loans (Consolidated VER2)

Consolidated bilingual (Arabic/English) version of CBUAE Circular No. 31/2013 on mortgage loans, containing the same framework as Version 1 in cleaner, readable form: purpose, application, Article 1 definitions (mortgage loan, LTV, property appraisal, mortgage loan providers, tenor) and Article 2 risk-management requirements including a board-approved, standalone mortgage lending policy with limits on property-lending exposure and the overall loan book. It applies to banks, finance companies and other financial institutions offering mortgage loans, sets only a floor, and permits more conservative internal limits. This is the preferred reading copy where the Version 1 scan is degraded.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
