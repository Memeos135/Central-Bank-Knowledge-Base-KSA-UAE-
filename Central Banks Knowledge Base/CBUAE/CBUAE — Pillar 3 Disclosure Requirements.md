# Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements

This node concerns Pillar 3 disclosure requirements as defined and applied in the corporate governance framework — the Basel Committee's consolidated and enhanced disclosure framework (March 2017 and subsequent revisions) — together with the annual corporate governance statement disclosures. It requires published qualitative and quantitative remuneration disclosures for Senior Management and Material Risk Takers (system design, fixed and variable pay, guaranteed bonuses, sign-on and severance payments, deferred remuneration), plus disclosure of ownership, related party transactions, board composition and risk exposures. It binds licensed banks, including additional Shari'ah reporting for banks offering Islamic financial services.
