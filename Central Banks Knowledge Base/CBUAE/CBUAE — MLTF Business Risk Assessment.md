# ML/TF Business Risk Assessment

A concept node on the enterprise-wide ML/FT business risk assessment required as the first step of a risk-based approach under the AML-CFT Law and implementing Decision. It covers identification of inherent risks across customers, products, services and transactions, delivery channels and geographies, the mapping of mitigating controls, and the determination of residual risk and control gaps to inform risk appetite and resource allocation. It binds supervised financial institutions, which must document, maintain and update the assessment and produce it to the Supervisory Authority on request.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
