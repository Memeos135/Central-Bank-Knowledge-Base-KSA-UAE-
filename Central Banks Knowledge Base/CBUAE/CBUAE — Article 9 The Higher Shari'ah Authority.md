# Article 9: The Higher Shari'ah Authority

Article requiring Licensed Financial Institutions that conduct all or part of their business under Islamic Shari'ah to file an annual Shari'ah report prepared by their Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee with the Higher Shari'ah Authority, by end-February and within two months of financial year end, so the Authority can comment before the general assembly. Grounded in Article 80 of the Central Bank Law. Non-filing triggers a fixed fine with a daily escalation up to a cap, and a separate penalty for erroneous regulatory returns.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
