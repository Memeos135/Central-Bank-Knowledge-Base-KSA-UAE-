# Organisation for Economic Co-Operation and Development

Definitional node identifying the OECD as the body that develops, amends and publishes the Common Reporting Standard and its interpretive commentary, which the UAE Insurance Authority CRS Regulations incorporate as an integral part of the Standard. It also fixes that the English version following the OECD international model prevails over the Arabic translation in case of conflict. Relevant to insurance companies supervised as Reporting Financial Institutions and to account/policy holders within scope.
