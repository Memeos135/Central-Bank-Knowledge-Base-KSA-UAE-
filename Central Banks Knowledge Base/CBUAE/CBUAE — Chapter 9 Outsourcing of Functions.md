# Chapter 9: Outsourcing of Functions

Chapter 9 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets the conditions under which a Licensed Person may have a third party perform a function, in whole or in part, on its behalf. It permits outsourcing generally but prohibits it outright for the AML compliance function (except document retention, with certain specific AML tasks allowed only after a Letter of No Objection from the Banking Supervision Department) and for the Licensed Person's Permitted Activities such as currency exchange and money transfer execution. Accountability for outsourced functions stays with the Licensed Person and its Board (or Owner/Partners), which must continue to meet all regulatory obligations and maintain internal ownership of the arrangement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments
