# Board of Trustees (Article 6)

This node governs the Board of Trustees of a Takaful Insurance Fund: a separate Board per Fund, a five-member composition (two independent non-executive Board members, one ISSC member, two Participant representatives), the single-Fund membership rule with limited two-Fund exceptions, appointment of an independent chairman, CBUAE prior approval for dismissal, notification and replacement timelines on resignation (7 business days / 30 days), minimum three-year terms, at least two meetings per financial year, and Fund-borne remuneration and meeting costs. It binds the Company and its Board in constituting and maintaining the Board of Trustees.
