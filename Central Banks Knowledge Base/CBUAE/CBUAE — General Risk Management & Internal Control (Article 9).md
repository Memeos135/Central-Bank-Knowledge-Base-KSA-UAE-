# General Risk Management & Internal Control (Article 9)

Node for Article 9 of the CBUAE SVF Regulation on general risk management and internal control systems, requiring a Licensee to maintain risk management policies and procedures proportionate to the scale and complexity of its SVF scheme, a board-approved risk management framework, and dedicated staff with sufficient expertise to oversee risk and internal control. It also addresses liquidity risk management processes. Unlike the information/accounting article, these general requirements expressly extend to licensed banks conducting SVF business in the State.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
