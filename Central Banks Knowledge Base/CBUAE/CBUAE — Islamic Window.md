# Islamic Window

Concept node for the Islamic Window itself: the dedicated channel through which all Shari'ah-compliant activities and businesses of a conventional institution must be offered. Requirements include appointing a dedicated Head of Islamic Window approved by CBUAE, reporting lines to the CEO/Executive Management Committee, prior notification of the organisational structure, and separate liquidity metrics (LCR/NSFR/ELAR) with a Shari'ah-compliant HQLA stock against 30-day net outflows plus documentation retention. Binds institutions housing an Islamic Window and their senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
