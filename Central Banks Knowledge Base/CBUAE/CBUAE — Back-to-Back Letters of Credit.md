# Back-to-Back Letters of Credit

A typology node explaining back-to-back letters of credit: two linked LCs, one issued by the buyer's institution in favour of an intermediary and one by the intermediary's institution in favour of the supplier. The guidance recognises legitimate commercial uses (broker confidentiality on mark-up) but flags the structure's use to complicate financing arrangements, obscure the flow of funds and conceal sanctioned parties from detection. Directs LFIs handling such structures to heightened scrutiny of counterparties and underlying documentation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
