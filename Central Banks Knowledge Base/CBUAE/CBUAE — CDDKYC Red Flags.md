# CDD/KYC Red Flags

Covers general CDD/KYC application expectations in the CBUAE guidance, including group-wide standardisation of CDD/KYC across branches and majority-owned subsidiaries, applying UAE standards where a host country's AML/CFT rules are less stringent, use of tailored onboarding forms by customer type, and heightened diligence in correspondent banking. It frames CDD/KYC as an ongoing, risk-based process performed at onboarding and at intervals thereafter, feeding risk rating and the identification of unusual or suspicious activity. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions and their foreign branches/subsidiaries.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
