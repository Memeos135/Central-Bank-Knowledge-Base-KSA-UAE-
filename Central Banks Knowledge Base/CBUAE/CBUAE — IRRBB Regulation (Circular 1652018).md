# IRRBB Regulation (Circular 165/2018)

Cross-reference node to the CBUAE Interest Rate and Rate of Return Risk in the Banking Book Regulation and accompanying Standards (Circular 165/2018), cited by the Islamic banks risk management Standard as the instrument to be read alongside its rate of return risk requirements. It sets the baseline banking-book rate risk regime that Islamic banks must satisfy in addition to the Islamic-specific requirements. Relevant to licensed banks generally, including Islamic banks and banks housing an Islamic Window.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
