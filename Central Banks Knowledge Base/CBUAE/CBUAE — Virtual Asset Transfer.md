# Virtual Asset Transfer

Covers the defined term Virtual Asset Transfer, the transaction trigger for the UAE Travel Rule, whether executed on behalf of an Originator, received on behalf of a Beneficiary, routed through an Intermediary Provider, or sent to/from an Unhosted Wallet. It governs what information must accompany the transfer, thresholds for verification, linked-transfer aggregation in risk assessment, and permissible responses where required data is absent; transaction (gas) fees are excluded from scope. Relevant to any VASP or Intermediary Provider determining whether a given movement of virtual assets falls within the rule.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
