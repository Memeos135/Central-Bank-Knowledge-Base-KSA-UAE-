# Professional Liability Insurance Policy

Node on the professional liability (professional indemnity) insurance requirement for licensed actuaries under the 2017 actuaries regulations. It specifies that the policy be issued by an Authority-licensed insurer in the actuary's name, be non-revocable and valid for the licence term, carry a minimum sum insured of AED 1,000,000 with deductible not exceeding AED 30,000 per accident, require a replacement policy at least seven days before expiry if not renewed, and that amendments need the Authority's written approval. Binds actuaries seeking or holding a licence.
