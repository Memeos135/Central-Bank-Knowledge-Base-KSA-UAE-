# Standard Re Shari'ah Governance for IFIs

CBUAE standard on Shari'ah governance for Islamic financial institutions and Islamic windows, setting the mandate, supervision duties and reporting obligations of the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC) within the framework set by the Higher Shari'ah Authority. It covers ISSC responsibility for Shari'ah supervision of all activities, products, contracts, profit allocation between investment account holders and shareholders, internal/external Shari'ah audit oversight, and includes the template for the annual Shari'ah report to shareholders. Binds licensed Islamic financial institutions, their boards and senior management (who remain responsible for actual Shari'ah compliance) and their ISSCs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
