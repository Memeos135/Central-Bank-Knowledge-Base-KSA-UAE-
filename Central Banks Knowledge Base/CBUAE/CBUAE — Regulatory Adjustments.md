# Regulatory Adjustments

The set of deductions and adjustments applied to regulatory capital under the CBUAE capital adequacy standards, including full deduction of goodwill and other intangibles (net of associated deferred tax liabilities) and of DTAs from carried-forward losses, with deducted items excluded from credit risk RWA. Related Pillar 3 disclosure templates require banks to publish the composition of regulatory capital and its reconciliation to the balance sheet. Binds banks reporting regulatory capital to the CBUAE, with disclosure obligations extending to local banks and, on a scaled basis, branches of foreign banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
