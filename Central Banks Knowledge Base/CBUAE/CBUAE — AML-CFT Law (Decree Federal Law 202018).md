# AML-CFT Law (Decree Federal Law 20/2018)

This node is the UAE federal AML/CFT primary statute (Decree Federal Law 20/2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organisations), cited as the legal basis for CBUAE guidance on legal persons and legal arrangements. It sits above the implementing Cabinet Decision 10/2019 (AML-CFT Decision) and Cabinet Decision 58/2020, and supplies the defined terms (beneficial owner, legal person, legal arrangement, settlor, trust) used in CDD obligations. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs) supervised by the CBUAE, whose CDD, beneficial ownership identification and risk-based controls must be embedded in their wider AML/CFT compliance programme with governance and training support.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
