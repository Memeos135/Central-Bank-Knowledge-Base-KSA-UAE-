# LCA Governance (Article 5)

Article 5 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation covers corporate governance of licensed crowdfunding companies, including fitness and propriety bars (e.g. confiscation of property, court receivership absent rehabilitation or pardon) and compliance with Emiratisation requirements and subsequent Central Bank requirements. The surrounding definitions article fixes key defined terms such as Borrower, Client, Client money, Pricing Platform, Commitment period and Controlling interest. It binds companies licensed or seeking licence for LCAs in the UAE outside the Financial Free Zones.
