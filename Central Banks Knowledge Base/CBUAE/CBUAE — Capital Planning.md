# Capital Planning

Covers capital planning within the ICAAP: banks must project capital under baseline and stress scenarios and identify credible, quantifiable management actions with trigger points tied to the internal risk appetite, reporting stressed results both with and without Board-approved actions. Planned capital increases may only be counted where they have Senior Management approval, form part of the official capital plan and have been discussed with the Central Bank; reputational consequences of management actions must also be weighed. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
