# BoD Decision 25/2016 - Consolidated Version (2021)

Consolidated version (as of 18/01/2021) of Insurance Authority Board of Directors' Decision No. 25 of 2016 regulating the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies, incorporating amendments made by Decisions 42/2017 and 26/2020. It sets the mandatory framework for issuing third party liability and loss-and-damage motor cover: duty to issue TPL cover on request, limits on refusal or discrimination in loss-and-damage cover, conduct and disclosure duties, claims handling timelines (written acknowledgement, missing-document notice, settlement within fifteen days, reasoned rejection), total loss and market value compensation, and tariff compliance. It binds insurance companies licensed to write motor business in the State and repeals Ministerial Resolution 54/1987.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
