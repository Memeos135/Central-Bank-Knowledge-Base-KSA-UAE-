# Third Line of Defense

Concept node for the third line of defence — independent internal audit — within the CBUAE AML/CFT governance model for licensed financial institutions. It sits alongside the first and second lines in the suspicious activity detection, investigation and reporting framework set out in CBUAE STR/SAR guidance, which rests on Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 and its implementing decisions. Binds all natural and legal persons licensed or supervised by CBUAE across banking, exchange, finance, payments, hawala and insurance categories.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
