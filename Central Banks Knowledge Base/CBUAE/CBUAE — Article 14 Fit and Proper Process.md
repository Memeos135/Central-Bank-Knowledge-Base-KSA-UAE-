# Article 14: Fit and Proper Process

Article 14 of the CBUAE Representation Offices Regulation requires a Fit and Proper Process for the proposed Director and other persons designated by the Central Bank, assessed on qualifications, knowledge and experience, integrity, competence and financial soundness, with minimum experience thresholds (reduced for UAE nationals with higher qualifications) and accreditation of academic degrees. It binds the representation office of a foreign insurer or reinsurer as a condition of appointment and continued licensing, and links to the staffing requirements in Article 13 (notification of Director appointment, vacancy filling within thirty days, adequate qualified staff).
