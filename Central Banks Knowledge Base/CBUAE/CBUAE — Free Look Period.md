# Free Look Period

Defined term and substantive rule: the Free Look Period is the window during which a life/family takaful policy may be cancelled or surrendered against refund of premium paid. The instructions set a minimum of 30 calendar days running from the earliest of policy issuance, commencement of cover, or client signature, bar the selling distribution channel from demanding an explanation for cancellation, permit only non-selling staff to seek reasons (pressure being a conduct breach), and require a policy for full premium refund on surrender within the period. Binds insurers and their distribution channels.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
