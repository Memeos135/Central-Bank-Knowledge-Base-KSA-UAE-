# Beneficial Owner (BO)

Addresses the beneficial owner concept in the exchange business context, including the 'beneficial owner of funds' in third-party transactions where a representative transacts for another natural or legal person. Sets conditions for accepting such transactions (power of attorney or verified letter of authority with validity limits, identification of both parties, EDD on remitter and beneficial owner, common shareholding or a valid agreement for cross-border legal entities, capture of all party names at point of sale, and PEP and sanctions screening of every party). Binds Licensed Persons; unresolved doubt requires the beneficial owner to attend in person, failing which the relationship is terminated and the case reported to the FIU.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
