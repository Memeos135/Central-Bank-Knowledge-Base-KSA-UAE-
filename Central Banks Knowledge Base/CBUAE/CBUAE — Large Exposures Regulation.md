# Large Exposures Regulation

The CBUAE Large Exposures Regulation is the prudential instrument setting how banks identify, measure, limit, monitor and report concentrated credit exposures to single counterparties and connected groups, expressed as percentages of Tier 1 capital. It covers exposure measurement (on- and off-balance sheet, banking and trading book, counterparty credit risk), eligible credit risk mitigation, exemptions and special treatments (sovereigns, public sector entities, interbank), related-party limits, and board-level approval, monitoring and breach-notification duties. It binds banks licensed/operating in the UAE and is supervised by the Central Bank of the UAE; it is not a SAMA instrument, so it is relevant mainly as comparative GCC material.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
