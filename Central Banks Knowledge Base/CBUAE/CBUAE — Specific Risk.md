# Specific Risk

Node on the specific risk capital charge for trading book positions, including the correlation trading portfolio (larger of aggregate net long or net short specific risk charges), securitisation exposures (banking book risk weight multiplied by 8%, with 1250%-weighted positions excludable from general market risk), the cap at maximum possible loss, hedged positions with currency, maturity or asset mismatches, and first-to-default credit derivatives. Binds banks holding credit derivatives, securitisation or correlation trading exposures in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
