# Authorized Individual

Defined-term node for "Authorized Individual" under the Fitness and Propriety Regulation: any natural person authorised under the Central Bank Laws to carry on a Designated Function, extended to Senior Employees in the case of Insurance Companies and Insurance-Related Professionals. The surrounding provisions set the consequential obligations — prior Central Bank Authorisation for appointment, reappointment or contract amendments affecting tenure, no public announcement before Authorisation, removal from the Designated Function if assessed not fit and proper (replacement generally within 90 days), and Central Bank non-objection (plus HSA non-objection for ISSC members and heads of internal Shari'ah control/audit) before termination or dismissal. It binds Licensees and the individuals concerned.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When deciding whether a person may continue in a controlled role, the fitness assessment is not purely an internal licensee call: the regulator is a parallel assessor whose adverse finding compels removal from the designated function. Practically, a compliance team must treat any negative fitness outcome — its own or the regulator's — as a mandatory removal trigger, and expect regulator engagement rather than a self-certification outcome.
