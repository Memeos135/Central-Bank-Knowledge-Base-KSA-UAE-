# Actuarial Function

Concept node for the actuarial function as a second-line control function, whose authority and responsibilities must be documented in the company's governance framework and staffed by suitably qualified persons with unrestricted access to information. Its duties include the sufficiency, accuracy and quality of data, methods and assumptions for technical provisions, back-testing best estimates against experience, opining on reinsurance arrangements and product development, pricing and underwriting capital, and reporting a reasoned reliability and sensitivity analysis to the board and senior management. Binds insurance and Takaful companies and the head of the actuarial function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
