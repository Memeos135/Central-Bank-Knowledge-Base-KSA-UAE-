# Risk Limits

A concept node within the CBUAE Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) standards for insurance companies, covering the self-imposed Risk Limits structure that a Company must establish as guardrails on its Risk Profile without jeopardising commitments to policyholders. Limits must be calibrated against strategic objectives, business plans, solvency position, Risk Appetite and projected outcomes of plausible but severe stress scenarios, and must interact with reinsurance arrangements and capital planning. It binds insurance and Takaful companies (Board, risk committee, Senior Management and the Risk Management function), with Takaful entities assessed both consolidated and per fund/account.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
