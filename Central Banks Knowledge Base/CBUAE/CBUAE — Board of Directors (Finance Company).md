# Board of Directors (Finance Company)

Sets the composition, qualification and responsibility requirements for the board of directors of a Finance Company: minimum five members, at least 60% with finance/banking (or Islamic finance) experience, a UAE-national majority and chair, all members non-executive with no overlap with general management, and at least one third independent. Board members owe duties of care and loyalty, must ensure effective control and a robust corporate governance policy proportionate to risk, approve and oversee strategy, risk management, compliance and internal/external audit, and set the organisational structure and the authorities of the board and Senior Management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
