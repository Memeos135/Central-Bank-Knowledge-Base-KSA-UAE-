# Shari'ah Governance Standard (SGS)

Concept node for the CBUAE Shari'ah Governance Standard, the primary standard for licensed financial institutions conducting all or part of their business in accordance with Islamic Shari'ah. It sets the governance framework the Guidance Note on the ISSC Charter supplements, including eligibility, competency and independence requirements for ISSC members and rules on their appointment, term, dismissal and resignation. It binds Islamic Financial Institutions and must be read together with standards and resolutions of the Higher Shari'ah Authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
