# UAE Securities & Commodities Authority (SCA)

A cross-reference concept node for the UAE Securities & Commodities Authority (and Local Licensing Authorities) as it appears in the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation. It marks the points where the Regulation's prohibitions (e.g. on payment token transfers outside permitted dirham/foreign payment tokens, on algorithmic stablecoins and privacy tokens, and on related promotions) bite on all Persons regardless of being licensed or regulated by SCA or a local authority for virtual asset activities, and where CBUAE may demand evidence of compliance. It also flags the discretionary exemption where equivalent white-paper style documentation or obligations already exist under SCA or local licensing authority rules.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
