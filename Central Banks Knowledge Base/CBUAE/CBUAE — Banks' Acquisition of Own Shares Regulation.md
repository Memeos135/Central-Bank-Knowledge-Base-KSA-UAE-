# Banks' Acquisition of Own Shares Regulation

CBUAE Regulation (Circular 20/2021) implementing Article 93(3) of the Central Bank Law on banks acquiring, buying back or dealing in their own shares. It prohibits such acquisition without prior written Central Bank approval (which may carry conditions), caps direct or indirect holdings of own shares at 10% of Paid Up Share Capital, and requires disposal of excess shares devolved in debt settlement within two years, extendable by the Central Bank. It applies to all Banks licensed under the Central Bank Law.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
