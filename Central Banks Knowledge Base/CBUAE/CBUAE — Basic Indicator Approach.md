# Basic Indicator Approach

Node for the Basic Indicator Approach, the simplest gross-income-based method for calculating the operational risk capital charge under the CBUAE capital standards. The surrounding standard text also addresses credit risk mitigation (simple vs comprehensive collateral approaches, minimum legal-certainty and liquidation conditions) and the look-through, mandate-based and fall-back approaches for equity investments in funds. Applies to banks in the UAE under CBUAE supervision; use of certain approaches requires prior Central Bank approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
