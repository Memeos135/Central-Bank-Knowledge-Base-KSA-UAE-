# Chapter 1: Scope of License and Exchange Business

Chapter of the Exchange Business Standards defining the permitted scope of an exchange licence and the activities a Licensed Person may carry out — foreign currency exchange, remittance operations and wage payment via WPS — and confirming that Exchange Business requires written licensing by the Governor or a delegate. It makes operating beyond licence scope a serious supervisory matter subject to non-compliance charges. It binds every Licensed Person conducting Exchange Business in the UAE.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
