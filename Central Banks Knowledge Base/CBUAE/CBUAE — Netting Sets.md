# Netting Sets

Sets out when netting sets may be recognised for counterparty credit risk purposes: banks must run an internal process assessing legal enforceability with review and challenge, and the Central Bank reviews netting-set identification supervisorily rather than pre-approving jurisdictions or counterparties. A standard ISDA agreement alone does not establish valid netting, and where recognition conditions fail each transaction forms its own single-trade netting set. It also covers replacement cost aggregation across netting sets under a margin agreement, including variation margin and NICA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
