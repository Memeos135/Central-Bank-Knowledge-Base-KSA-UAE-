# Licence Application (Article 3)

Article 3 of the CBUAE Insurance Company Licensing Regulation, governing who submits a licence application and in what form: the founders' committee for a company to be established in the State, or the Foreign Insurance Company itself for a branch. It requires the documents and statements in Annex 1 plus anything further the Central Bank requests, and sets assessment considerations including whether the company will introduce new or strongly demanded products and increase premium retention in the State; the Central Bank may liaise with the foreign home supervisor.
