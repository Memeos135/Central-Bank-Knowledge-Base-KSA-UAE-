# Corporate Governance Standards for Banks 83/2019

This node is the Corporate Governance Standards for Banks (Circular 83/2019), which expand on the Corporate Governance Regulation and are mandatory and enforceable in the same manner as the Regulation. It sets out definitions (Affiliate, Board, Control Functions, Controlling Shareholder, Related Parties, Material Risk Takers, Risk Appetite) and detailed requirements on board composition and assessment, senior management, risk governance, related party transactions, group structure, code of conduct, whistleblowing and disclosure. It binds all licensed banks, including branches of foreign banks and banks offering Islamic financial services.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
