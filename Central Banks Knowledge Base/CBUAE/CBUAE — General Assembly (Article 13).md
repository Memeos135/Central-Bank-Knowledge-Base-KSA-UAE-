# General Assembly (Article 13)

Article 13 of the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies, governing the general assembly and shareholding. It sets the minimum national shareholding requirement (by reference to Cabinet Resolution No. 42 of 2009), obliges the board to ensure shareholder voting complies with the Central Bank Law and the Insurance Law, requires notification to the Central Bank when a proposed shareholding change is on the general assembly agenda, and preserves the Central Bank's powers to attend meetings (without voting) and to require a general assembly be convened. It binds insurance companies, their boards and shareholders.
