# Disclosure Principles (Jun 2024)

Refers to the SFWG's Principles for Sustainability-related Disclosures for Reporting Entities, issued June 2024, intended to prepare relevant UAE financial firms to produce high-quality, relevant sustainability disclosures. It sits alongside the Risk Management Principles and the Climate Transition Planning Principles as the reporting limb of the UAE sustainable finance framework, and is cross-referenced for the reporting and transparency expectations. Directed at UAE reporting entities in the financial sector.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
