# Valuation

This node sets prudent valuation expectations for market risk positions: sourcing and periodic review of market inputs, use of generally accepted valuation methodologies, independent development, approval, testing and periodic review of valuation models, formal change control and secure model copies, and awareness of model weaknesses by an independent risk management function. It also requires procedures for valuation adjustments — including where third-party valuations are used or positions are marked to model — with credit valuation adjustments, unearned credit spreads, close-out costs and operational risk among the minimum items to be considered. Related internal audit provisions require annual independent review of pricing model and valuation system approval processes used by front- and back-office staff. It binds Banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
