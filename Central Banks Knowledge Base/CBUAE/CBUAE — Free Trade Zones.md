# Free Trade Zones

Section on PF vulnerabilities arising from trade, covering trade-based money laundering techniques (false, over- or under-valued invoices, misdescription of goods) and the contrast between documentary trade finance and open-account transactions, where wire messages carry little information about the underlying trade. It expects LFIs to assess which of their products and services could be misused to finance WMD programmes or the acquisition of dual-use or proliferation-sensitive goods. Applies to Licensed Financial Institutions offering trade finance and cross-border payment services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments, governance/risk
