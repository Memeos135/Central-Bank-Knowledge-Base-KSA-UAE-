# Financial Data Extension

## Why this community

Adjustment of the reporting periods for submission of financial data by insurance-sector entities — a timing relief/extension measure against standing periodic reporting duties.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a board resolution that varies deadlines rather than creating a new substantive obligation.
- Legal effect is parasitic on the underlying periodic financial-reporting requirements in the insurance regulation; it must be read together with them to determine the operative due date.
- Time-bound instrument: compliance teams must confirm whether the extension remains in force or has reverted to the baseline reporting cycle.
