# Equity Risk

Node grouping equity risk within the CBUAE ICAAP material-risk taxonomy, where banks must identify, measure, report, monitor and mitigate all risks material to their business model and risk profile across the group, including branches and subsidiaries, and feed that assessment into Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 capital demand. The surrounding context addresses market and operational risk treatment under Pillar 2 and the Board/Senior Management approval of the risk-estimation framework; the document contains limited equity-specific text, so open it mainly for the general material-risk and Pillar 2 expectations. Binds UAE banks preparing ICAAP.
