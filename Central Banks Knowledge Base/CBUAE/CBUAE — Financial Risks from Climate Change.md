# Financial Risks from Climate Change

Node covering the treatment of financial risks from climate change within the ICAAP, largely through supervisory Q&A. It indicates that no specific capital charge methodology is prescribed, but banks are expected to understand climate-related impacts on sustainability and business strategy, develop quantification methodologies proportionate to size and business model, and explore stress testing and scenario analysis (including green asset ratio assessment). It applies to banks preparing ICAAP submissions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
