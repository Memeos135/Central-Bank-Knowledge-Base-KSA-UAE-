# Privacy Token

Concept node for "Privacy Token" under the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation, which prohibits any Person in or targeting the UAE from issuing Privacy Tokens, performing services relating to them, or promoting them — including Persons licensed or regulated by SCA or a Local Licensing Authority. It connects to the traceability logic of the regime (identifiable transfer of disposal power between Wallets, white paper and customer agreement disclosure) and to the Central Bank's powers to limit volumes, values and customer numbers for token issuers and service providers.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, other
