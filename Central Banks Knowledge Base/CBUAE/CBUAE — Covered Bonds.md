# Covered Bonds

Article 14 of the CBUAE Large Exposures regulation, governing how holdings of covered bonds are attributed to the issuing bank as counterparty: 100% of nominal value by default, or at least 20% where the instrument meets the covered bond definition and eligibility conditions on legal supervision and the cover pool. Related context covers exposure values for options and fund/securitisation structures and confirms that trading book covered bonds follow the same treatment. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks measuring counterparty exposure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
