# BoD Decision 25/2016 - Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies

Insurance Authority Board of Directors Decision No. 25 of 2016 issuing the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies — the mandatory standard forms for third party liability cover and for loss and damage cover, which insurers may not amend except in favour of the insured or beneficiary. It defines key terms (including Motor Vehicle Driver), obliges insurers to issue third party liability cover for licensed vehicles on request, restricts refusal to insure or renew loss-and-damage cover, and requires compliance with the Orange Card treaty for cross-border Arab cover. Binds all insurance companies, including Takaful insurers subject to Takaful terminology.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
