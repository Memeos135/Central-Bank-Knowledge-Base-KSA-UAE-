# CBUAE Model Management Standards

CBUAE standards that sit alongside, and are enforceable to the same degree as, the Credit Risk Regulation, covering credit risk governance (board and senior management duties), write-off and memorandum account record-keeping, and the use, validation and override of credit risk models. The model-related articles require licensed financial institutions to hold analytical capability proportionate to portfolio complexity, to document why models are not used where that is the case, and to justify, document and report model output overrides. Binds all Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs) supervised by the CBUAE; note this is UAE rather than SAMA material.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
