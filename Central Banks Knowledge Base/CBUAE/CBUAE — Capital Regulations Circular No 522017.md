# Capital Regulations Circular No 52/2017

Node for the CBUAE Capital Adequacy Regulation (Circular 52/2017) as referenced in the capital standards: it fixes the minimum ratios (including total capital of at least 10.5% of RWA) and the buffer architecture — capital conservation buffer, countercyclical buffer, D-SIB buffer and any individual Supervisory Capital Guidance add-on from the SREP. It also underpins recognition limits for Tier 1 and total capital issued by consolidated subsidiaries to third-party investors, including surplus-capital deductions. Binds all banks licensed by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
