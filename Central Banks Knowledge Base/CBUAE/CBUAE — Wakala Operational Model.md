# Wakala Operational Model

This node covers Articles 6–8 of the CBUAE Takaful standard on establishing the Takaful Insurance Fund and setting the operational model, which must be a Wakala model for both property/liability takaful and insurance of persons and fund accumulation. It requires ISSC approval before submission to the Central Bank and HSA, public disclosure on the company website, and clear articulation of contracting parties, financial flows and the contractual relationships between Participant/Beneficiary, Fund and Company in the Fund Charter, membership and takaful policies. It binds companies applying for or holding a Takaful Insurance licence.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
