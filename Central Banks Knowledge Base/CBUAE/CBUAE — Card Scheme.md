# Card Scheme

Defines Card Scheme as the rule-set, practices and standards enabling card-based payment transactions in the UAE, distinct from the underlying payment system infrastructure, and includes the Card Scheme Governing Body accountable for its operation; it may be publicly or privately operated. Related definitions distinguish Card Issuer, Cardholder, Card-based Payment Transactions, and Three- versus Four-Party Card Schemes (a three-party scheme converts to four-party once it licenses other PSPs, co-brands or uses agents). Card Schemes must hold a Card Scheme Licence and are subject to ongoing obligations and Central Bank oversight of fees and charges.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
