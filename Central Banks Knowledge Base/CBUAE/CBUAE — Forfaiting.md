# Forfaiting

Defines forfaiting as the discounted, without-recourse purchase of an exporter's receivables by a bank or specialised finance firm, distinguishing it from factoring by the non-recourse feature, typical guarantee from the importer's bank, and conversion of receivables into tradable debt instruments. It notes typical use in capital goods and commodities trading. Relevant to LFIs assessing TBML exposure in non-documentary trade finance products.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
