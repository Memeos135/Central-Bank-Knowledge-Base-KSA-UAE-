# Pillar 3 - Market Disclosure

Node for the CBUAE Pillar 3 market disclosure standard, which supersedes the 2009 Pillar 3 requirements and prescribes the disclosure tables, their quarterly, semi-annual or annual frequency, and publication as a stand-alone report on the bank's UAE-specific website (or as a discrete section of financial reporting with the tables still disclosed separately). It applies to all UAE banks at consolidated level and to all branches of foreign banks, effective from Q2 2020, and extends to banks offering Islamic financial services for Shari'ah-compliant activities parallel to those described.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
