# Principles for effective management of climate-related financial risks

A set of supervisory principles on the effective management of climate-related financial risks, addressed to financial firms and framed around board and senior management accountability. It covers board oversight and risk appetite, integration of material climate risk exposures into overall business strategy, documented assignment of roles and responsibilities, embedding climate risk into the internal risk management framework (identification, assessment, measurement, mitigation, monitoring, reporting), group-wide coordination, and internal reporting systems and data-gap remediation. Drafted as expectations ("should") issued in accordance with the relevant laws of each participating jurisdiction rather than as a single binding rulebook.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
