# Fraud Detection and Investigation

Sub-sections 6.2.2–6.2.3 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards covering fraud detection systems proportionate to product risk, consumer notification of theft/loss/fraud reporting procedures, a dedicated fraud investigation and reporting function for financial crime compliance, and consumer alerts where fraud patterns emerge. It sets a reporting cascade to the Central Bank: immediate notification of significant fraud events, quarterly reporting of fraud-related consumer complaints and security vulnerabilities, and an annual summary report by 31 January. Adjacent provisions impose due diligence on third parties, authorised agents and staff, ATM maintenance records retained one year, and potential liability of the institution for direct losses from its own control breaches.
