# Equity Investments in Funds (EIF)

Node explaining the capital treatment of banks' equity investments in funds held in the banking book, based on the Basel hierarchy of the look-through approach, the mandate-based approach and the fall-back approach. It sets out scope (banking book only; trading book equity sits in market risk), confirms the CBUAE declined national discretions to exempt zero-risk-weight-debt entities and official programme investments, and describes how the fund's average asset risk weight is scaled by leverage subject to a 1250% cap. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks holding fund equity exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
