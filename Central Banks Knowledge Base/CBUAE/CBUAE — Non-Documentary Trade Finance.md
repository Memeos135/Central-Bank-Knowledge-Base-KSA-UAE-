# Non-Documentary Trade Finance

A concept node within CBUAE's trade-based money laundering guidance distinguishing non-documentary trade finance and open account trade from traditional documentary products (LCs, guarantees, documentary collections). It explains that here the LFI finances or settles trade without payment being tied to presentation of the full set of trade documents, so visibility into the underlying goods and counterparties is materially reduced. It informs the risk-based controls LFIs must calibrate to their role in the transaction and the information actually available to them.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, banking prudential
