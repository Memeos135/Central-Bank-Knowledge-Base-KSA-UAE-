# Correspondent Banking Guidance

Section of CBUAE guidance explaining what correspondent banking is — a bilateral relationship in which a correspondent institution supplies services (cash management, cross-border wire transfers, cheque clearing, letters of credit, FX) to a respondent institution — and the ML/TF/PF exposure it creates. It sets supervisory expectations for risk-based due diligence on respondent and parent institutions, use of the Wolfsberg CBDDQ as the industry-standard due diligence questionnaire by both correspondents and respondents, and monitoring for correspondent banking red flags. It binds UAE Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs), not SAMA-supervised entities; useful as comparative/benchmark material for KSA correspondent banking analysis.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
