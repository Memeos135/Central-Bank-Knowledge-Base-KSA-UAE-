# Principle 4 - Metrics and Targets

A principle within the CBUAE Sustainable Finance Working Group's Principles for Climate Transition Planning (UAE, not KSA), dealing with metrics and targets. It expects financial firms to select quantitative and qualitative metrics and targets built on robust, science-aligned methodologies across defined time horizons, to disclose limitations and any data proxies used, and to monitor and review them regularly. Addressed to UAE financial sector entities collectively termed 'financial firms'; drafted as non-binding guiding principles rather than enforceable rules, and to be read with the SFWG Risk Management and Disclosure Principles.
