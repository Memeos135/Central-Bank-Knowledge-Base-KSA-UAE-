# Displaced Commercial Risk (Article 15)

Node for the displaced commercial risk article of the CBUAE Islamic banks risk management Standard — the risk arising where an Islamic bank foregoes part of its own returns to sustain payouts to Investment Account Holders. It is a mandatory component of the risk governance framework, must be expressly covered in the risk appetite statement, and must be measurable and reportable through the bank's risk information systems under Risk Committee oversight. Binds Islamic banks and banks housing an Islamic Window.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
