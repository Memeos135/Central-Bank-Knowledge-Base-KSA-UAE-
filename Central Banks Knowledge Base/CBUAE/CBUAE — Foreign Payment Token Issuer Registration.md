# Foreign Payment Token Issuer Registration

Concept node covering the CBUAE registration route for Foreign Payment Token Issuers (and related Registrees such as Registered Foreign Payment Token Custodians and Transferors and Registered Payment Token Conversion Providers) under the Payment Token Services Regulation. It frames the Central Bank's licensing, registration, supervisory and ongoing reporting powers, and its power to cap the volume, value or number of UAE customers to which a Foreign Payment Token Issuer may sell or transfer tokens. It binds foreign issuers seeking to have their tokens used or promoted in the UAE, and constrains licensees dealing in such tokens.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
