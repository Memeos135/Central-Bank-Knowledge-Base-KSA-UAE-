# Internal Control Framework

Concept node for the internal control framework banks must establish, with the board and senior management responsible for ensuring it is adequate for a properly controlled operating environment reflecting the bank's risk profile. Senior management must design controls that identify, measure, monitor and control all risks, covering organisational structure, accounting and financial reporting, checks and balances, safeguarding of assets and investments, and measures against unauthorised trading and system intrusion; organisational structures should reflect a three-lines-of-defence approach with balanced skills and authority in back-office and control functions. Binds banks and, where applicable, groups.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
