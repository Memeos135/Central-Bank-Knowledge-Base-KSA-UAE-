# Shell and Front Companies

Guidance section defining shell companies (incorporated but without real operations, assets or staff) and front companies (operating businesses used to disguise illicit activity), and explaining how proliferators use opaque ownership structures and nominee shareholders to conceal beneficial ownership. It sets the expectation that LFIs apply risk-based CDD/KYC and EDD, including beneficial ownership identification and lowered ownership thresholds for higher-risk legal persons. Addressed to Licensed Financial Institutions onboarding or servicing legal-entity customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
