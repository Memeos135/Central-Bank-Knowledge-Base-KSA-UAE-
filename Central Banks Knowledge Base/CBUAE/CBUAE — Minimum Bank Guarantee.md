# Minimum Bank Guarantee

Sets the minimum bank guarantee requirement for Exchange Business applicants and licensees: an unconditional, irrevocable, auto-renewing guarantee in favour of the CBUAE payable on first written demand, with a permitted alternative of an interest/profit-free cash deposit held at the Central Bank (supported by a notarised authorisation to use it for customer obligations). Source-of-funds evidence may be demanded, compliance is ongoing, and the guarantee sits on top of — and cannot be funded from — minimum Paid-up Capital. Binds applicants and Licensed Persons for exchange/remittance activity.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk
