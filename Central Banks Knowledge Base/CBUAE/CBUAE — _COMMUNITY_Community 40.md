# National AML/CFT Risk Assessment

## Why this community

The national and institutional risk-assessment architecture for AML/CTF: how the country-level risk picture and national strategy drive each institution's business risk assessment, risk factors, and the enhanced measures applied to higher-risk customers, products, channels and jurisdictions.

## How members connect

- Top-down obligation chain: the national committee sets the strategy, the strategy rests on the national risk assessment, and institutions must take that assessment into account when producing their own ML/TF business risk assessment.
- Risk factors and typologies feed the risk assessment, which in turn determines where a risk-based approach requires enhanced due diligence rather than standard CDD.
- EDD acts as the hub obligation, attaching to specified higher-risk categories — correspondent relationships, high-risk jurisdictions designated through the national process, money or value transfer services and non-profit exposures.
- Payment-channel and sectoral rules sit alongside: wire transfer originator/beneficiary information requirements extend to virtual asset transfers, while international industry material (Wolfsberg) is persuasive guidance only, not an enforceable SAMA source.
