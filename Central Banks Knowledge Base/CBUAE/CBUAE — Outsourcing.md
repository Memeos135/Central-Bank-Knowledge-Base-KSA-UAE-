# Outsourcing

A thematic node gathering the CBUAE outsourcing requirements applicable to licensed insurance Companies: board and senior management responsibility for outsourcing risk, mandatory written contracts, due diligence on the service provider's capacity, authorisation, conflicts of interest and financial viability, and inclusion of outsourced critical functions in business continuity and disaster recovery planning. It also sets contractual minimums on Confidential Data — unfettered access to the Company's own data, non-disclosure, destruction or return on termination, limits and conditions on subcontracting, and an express right of access and on-site inspection for the Central Bank or its appointed agent. Material outsourcing requires prior non-objection, and core insurance activities plus key management and Control Functions (including senior management oversight and internal audit) are in principle not outsourceable.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, other
