# Regulations on Licensing of Actuaries (IA Decision 9/2017)

Node for Insurance Authority Board of Directors Decision No. 9 of 2017 on licensing and registration of Actuaries and regulation of their operations (functions now under CBUAE). It sets entry conditions, the Register, duties, supervisory and disciplinary measures (warning, suspension, cancellation, two-year bar on re-application, appeal within 30 days), transitional regularisation within one year, and repeals Ministry of Economy Resolution No. 22 of 1985. Binds actuaries and insurance/Takaful companies engaging them.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Insurance Authority|Insurance Authority]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Before applying the actuary licensing and registration requirements, identify who currently exercises the licensing, registration and enforcement powers: the regulation was issued under the Insurance Authority's decision-making framework and vests those powers in the Authority and its Board. Every approval, condition, notification and sanction in the instrument therefore runs to that defined regulator, not to the insurer or the actuary's professional body. Because insurance supervision in the UAE has since been consolidated under the Central Bank, the reader should verify in the primary text and any transfer instrument which body now receives the filing or grants the licence before relying on the named authority.
