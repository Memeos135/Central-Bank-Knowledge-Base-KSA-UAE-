# Medical Service Providers

Node on the TPA's relationship with Medical Service Providers: the tripartite/parallel agreements setting out rights, obligations, tariffs and settlement periods, evidenced by an insurer document confirming the TPA contracted on the insurer's behalf. It also carries the TPA's conduct duties toward providers and insureds — notifying the insurer of any new, amended or terminated provider contract, impartiality in claim settlement, confidentiality of data obtained, restrictions on advertising without insurer consent, and investigation rights such as reviewing a patient's file. Binds TPAs, with cooperation duties on providers, beneficiaries and insurers toward the TPA's physician.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, data/credit info
