# Principle 1: Policies, Procedures and Systems

Principle 1 of the UAE SFWG Principles for Sustainability-Related Disclosures: Reporting Entities should have adequate policies, procedures and systems to report on sustainability matters, covering internal reporting systems for material sustainability risks and data-gap remediation within a data governance framework, timely internal reporting to the board and senior management, and timely public reporting. Expressed as expectations ('should'), binding only once translated into a member Authority's regulatory framework.
