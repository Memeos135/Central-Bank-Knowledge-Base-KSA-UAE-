# Executive Office

Defined-term node for "the Office", the Executive Office of the Committee for Goods Subjected to Import and Export Control, under Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020. It publishes and circulates the Sanctions List and Local Lists to supervisory authorities, financial institutions and DNFBPs without delay and without prior notice to the listed person, receives and routes de-listing and unfreezing requests to the Council, collects implementation outcomes and statistics, and conducts outreach, training and guidance on compliance. It is the operational interface between the designation authorities and regulated entities implementing freezing measures.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
