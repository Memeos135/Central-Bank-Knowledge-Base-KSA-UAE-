# Look-Through Approach (LTA)

Defines the look-through approach, the first and most granular of the three permitted methods for risk weighting banks' banking-book equity investments in funds under the CBUAE capital adequacy standard. It requires the bank to risk weight the fund's underlying exposures (including derivative-related counterparty credit risk) as if held directly, and is mandatory where the fund reports with sufficient frequency and granularity and the underlying exposures are verified by an independent third party such as a custodian, depository or fund management company. Binds all UAE banks on a consolidated basis holding in-scope equity positions in funds.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
