# Regulations re Capital Adequacy (Circular 52/2017)

CBUAE Capital Adequacy Regulations issued under Circular 52/2017, aligning the regulatory capital framework for UAE banks with the Basel III standards on capital quality, minimum ratios, buffers, disclosure and transitional arrangements. It sets out the composition of regulatory capital (CET1, Additional Tier 1, Tier 2), regulatory adjustments and deductions, minimum ratio requirements, the capital conservation and countercyclical buffers, the D-SIB surcharge, supervisory capital guidance add-ons, reporting and interpretation. It binds all banks licensed in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
