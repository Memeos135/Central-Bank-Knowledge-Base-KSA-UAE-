# Group Recovery Plans (Article 4)

Article 4 of the CBUAE recovery planning regulation sets how recovery plans must be prepared at group level, requiring coverage of every Financial Institution in the group, intra-group financial support arrangements, material intra-group links, and any legal or practical impediments to cross-entity recovery options. It allocates approval to the parent's board and differentiates scope by structure: UAE-headquartered institutions must cover the whole group, UAE subsidiaries of foreign parents must have a plan covering the subsidiary that is consistent with the group plan, and UAE branches must tailor the plan to local operations. It also sets the minimum content a foreign group plan must contain for the Central Bank to accept it as sufficient for local operations, and preserves the Central Bank's power to require standalone plans for group entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
