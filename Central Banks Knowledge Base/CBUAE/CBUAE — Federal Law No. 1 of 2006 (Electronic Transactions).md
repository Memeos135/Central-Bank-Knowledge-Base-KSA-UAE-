# Federal Law No. 1 of 2006 (Electronic Transactions)

UAE Federal Law No. 1 of 2006 on electronic transactions and commerce, cited as one of the legal bases for the bancassurance instructions. Its relevance in this context is enabling the use of the Authority's adopted Electronic Means for applications such as temporary or full suspension of marketing, resumption, and notification of agreement termination. It is a referenced enabling statute rather than the substantive source of insurance-distribution obligations.
