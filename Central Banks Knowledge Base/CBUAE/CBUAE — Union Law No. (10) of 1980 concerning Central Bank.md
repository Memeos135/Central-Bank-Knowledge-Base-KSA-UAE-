# Union Law No. (10) of 1980 concerning Central Bank

Enabling statute cited as the legal basis for the mortgage loans regulations: Union Law No. (10) of 1980 concerning the Central Bank, the Monetary System and Organization of Banking, specifically Articles 5, 18, 94 and 96. It confers the CBUAE's rule-making and supervisory powers over banks, finance companies and other financial institutions.
