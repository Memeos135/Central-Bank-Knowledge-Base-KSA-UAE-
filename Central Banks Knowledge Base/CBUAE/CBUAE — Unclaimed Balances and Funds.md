# Unclaimed Balances and Funds

Node covering the CBUAE Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Balances/Funds Regulation's treatment of unclaimed balances and funds — including unclaimed bankers' cheques, bank drafts and cashier orders, safe deposit box contents, and unclaimed insurance and exchange-house funds. It sets out how licensed financial institutions must ring-fence such amounts in a dedicated internal ledger/unclaimed balances account after the customer-contact period expires, control access to dormant customer records, and disclose dormancy terms in account-opening, underwriting, exchange and safe-deposit terms and conditions; it also bars exchange businesses from booking unclaimed funds as income. Binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions, with sector-specific carve-outs for insurance companies and exchange businesses.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
