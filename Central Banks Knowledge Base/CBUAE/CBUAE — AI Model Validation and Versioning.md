# AI Model Validation and Versioning

Supervisory guidance provisions on the lifecycle control of material Big Data Analytics and AI models, covering explainability, data governance and representativeness of training data, independent pre-deployment validation and testing, and the ability to suspend an application at will. It also sets expectations for a documented post-deployment review framework (monitoring, re-training, calibration, bias and fairness review, contingency/human intervention), error detection, remediation and escalation, and disciplined model versioning with records. Addressed to financial institutions adopting these enabling technologies, framed as 'should' expectations rather than prescriptive rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, other
