# Net Present Value Models

A model-type node covering net present value models, one of the six model families listed in the CBUAE Model Management Guidance alongside rating, PD, LGD, macro and IRRBB models. The MMG does not mandate use of these models; it sets minimum expected practices where an institution chooses to use them, so that model risk is managed appropriately. Scope of institutions follows the Model Management Standards, with branches/subsidiaries of foreign institutions applying the more conservative of MMG or home-regulator expectations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
