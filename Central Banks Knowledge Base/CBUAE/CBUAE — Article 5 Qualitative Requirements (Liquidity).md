# Article 5: Qualitative Requirements (Liquidity)

Article 5 sets the qualitative liquidity requirements for Islamic Banks: a board-approved strategy and policies covering normal and stressed scenarios, a framework to identify, mitigate and manage liquidity risk across on- and off-balance sheet items, impact analysis for new products, internal controls, limits and internal audit. It also layers Shari'ah governance onto liquidity risk, requiring ISSC involvement in approving Shari'ah-compliant liquidity and hedging instruments, verifying non-commingling of funds with conventional parents, and vetting placements. It addresses contract-specific liquidity risks (Murabaha, Ijarah, Salam, Mudarabah, Musharakah, Sukuk) and the interaction of funding and market liquidity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
