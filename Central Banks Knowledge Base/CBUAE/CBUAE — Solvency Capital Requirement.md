# Solvency Capital Requirement

Defines the Solvency Capital Requirement for Takaful companies as risk-based capital covering underwriting, market and liquidity (investment), credit and operational risk, calculated across all Participants' Risk Funds and the Shareholders' Funds consistently with their risk profiles. It requires the SCR and Minimum Guarantee Fund to be met by Basic Own Funds plus only 50% of Ancillary Own Funds, with the entire Shareholders' Funds available for Qard Hasan to cover any PRF deficit and Qard Hasan assets accounted for and valued separately. Binds Takaful insurance companies, with immediate notification and recovery-plan duties to the Authority on breach.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
