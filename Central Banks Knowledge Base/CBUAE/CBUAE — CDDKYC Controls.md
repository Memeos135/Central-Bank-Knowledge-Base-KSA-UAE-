# CDD/KYC Controls

Treats CDD/KYC procedures as a key mitigating control to be identified, measured and scored when assessing control effectiveness in the institutional risk assessment. Effectiveness indicators referenced include rates of compliance with CDD/KYC requirements alongside monitoring, training and audit measures. Relevant where an LFI must evidence that its customer due diligence framework actually reduces inherent risk, rather than merely exists.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
