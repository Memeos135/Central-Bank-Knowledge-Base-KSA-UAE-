# International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB)

A reference node explaining that the Disclosure Principles acknowledge existing voluntary frameworks (GRI, SASB, IIRC, TCFD, CDSB, ISSB) and the international move toward convergence, including ISSB standards and Basel Committee Pillar 3 climate disclosures. It provides context and objectives rather than obligations. Open it to understand which external standards the UAE principles are aligned to or expected to converge with.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
