# EDD on Unhosted Wallet Transfers

Covers the enhanced due diligence and handling regime for Virtual Asset Transfers sent to or received from an Unhosted Wallet, excluding transaction (gas) fees. It requires obtaining missing originator/beneficiary information, considering delay or return of the asset, declining execution where requested originator information is not provided, and grounding decisions in the institution's ML/TF/PF risk assessment (purpose and nature of the relationship, transfer value, linked transfers). Binds Virtual Asset Service Providers acting for originators or beneficiaries, with parallel duties for Intermediary Providers including reporting systemic counterparty failures.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
