# Terrorism Lists & Sanctions

## Why this community

Targeted financial sanctions and terrorism listing: how persons get on UN and domestic lists, who decides, and the freezing and reporting duties that bite on financial institutions and DNFBPs without delay.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: UNSCR 1373 and the UN Sanctions Committee feed the international list; the Cabinet Decision operationalises listing domestically, with the national security council as designating authority and the Executive Office as the implementing/communicating body.
- Obligation chain: listing (UN or local) → notification via the Executive Office → immediate freezing and prohibition on making funds available → reporting to the competent authority.
- Shared defined terms: 'Local Lists' versus 'UN Sanctions List' determine the legal basis and the delisting route (Ombudsperson/Focal Point versus domestic petition).
- Cross-regime link: sanctions screening duties sit on top of, and are distinct from, risk-based CDD under the AML/CTF law; a sanctions hit is a strict prohibition, not a risk factor.
