# Chapter 3: Licensing

Chapter 3 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets out the licensing requirements and application process for parties seeking authorisation to conduct Exchange Business in the UAE, including the content of a new licence application (e.g. details of the proposed legal form, cross-referenced from the capital/legal form chapter). It sits within Part A (Licensing and Continuing Obligations Standards) and operates together with the three licence categories (A, B, C) that delimit permitted activities: foreign currency exchange, remittance operations and wage payment via WPS. It binds applicants for, and holders of, a CBUAE Exchange Business licence (Licensed Persons).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
