# Duration Method

Concept node for the alternative duration-based method of calculating the general market risk charge for interest rate positions, under which banks with adequate capability may, with Central Bank consent, measure price sensitivity of each position to specified rate shocks and slot the results into time bands subject to a vertical disallowance and zone-based horizontal disallowances. Use of the method requires continuous application unless a change is approved, and separate maturity ladders and capital charges per currency. Binds banks electing this approach for trading book interest rate risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
