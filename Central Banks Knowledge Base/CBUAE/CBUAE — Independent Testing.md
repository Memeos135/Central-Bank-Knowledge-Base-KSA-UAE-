# Independent Testing

Guidance component of the CBUAE AML/CFT/CPF risk-based approach guidance describing Independent Testing as an objective, risk-based evaluation of the overall AML/CFT/CPF programme, covering policies, procedures, systems and controls and identifying remediation gaps. It indicates an expected testing cycle of roughly every 12–18 months or upon material change to risk profile, controls or compliance staffing, and allows internal audit, external auditors, consultants or other qualified independent parties to perform it. It addresses licensed financial institutions (LFIs) supervised by the CBUAE, with emphasis on preserving the autonomy/independence of the testing function; it sits alongside the training-controls expectations in the same section.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
