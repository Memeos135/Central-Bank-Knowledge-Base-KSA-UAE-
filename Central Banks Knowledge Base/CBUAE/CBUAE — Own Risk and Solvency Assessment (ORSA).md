# Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA)

Concept node for the Own Risk and Solvency Assessment, the forward-looking internal assessment linking a company's risk profile, risk appetite and its own view of capital needs over and above regulatory capital requirements. ORSA results and related action plans must be reported to the board at least annually and interact with stress testing, model validation and solvency requirements. Applies to insurance and Takaful companies and, where relevant, at group level.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)|Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a company's ORSA is compliant, it cannot be judged as a standalone exercise: the ORSA is expressly positioned as a component of the wider risk management system and is assigned to the risk management function. The ERM instrument supplies the surrounding architecture the ORSA must sit in, including the feedback loop by which changes in assessed risk must feed back into risk policy, risk limits and mitigation. Consequently, a deficiency in the ERM framework (governance, limits, feedback into policy) undermines the ORSA's regulatory sufficiency even if the ORSA document itself looks complete, and both texts should be read together when scoping a review or remediation.

### [[CBUAE — Solvency Capital Requirement|Solvency Capital Requirement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** In assessing solvency adequacy, the ORSA cannot be treated as a free-standing internal exercise: it is the forward-looking, stressed assessment measured against the regulatory capital benchmarks, so the SCR (and MCR) are the reference points the ORSA must test the company against. The definitional cross-reference means the ORSA's prospective solvency conclusions feed directly into whether a breach is foreseeable and whether recovery planning is engaged, with the recovery plan required to restore compliance within the prescribed period from observation of non-compliance. For the reader, an ORSA that flags prospective shortfall is a trigger point for capital and recovery action, not merely an internal governance document.
