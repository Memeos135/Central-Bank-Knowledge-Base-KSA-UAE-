# Settlement Finality

Defines settlement finality for Retail Payment Systems as the discharge of an obligation by a transfer of funds that is irrevocable and unconditional, extinguishing legal rights that would otherwise allow reversal of a Transfer Order processed in an RPS. It ties finality and netting certainty to the Central Bank Law provisions on Financial Infrastructure Systems, including treatment of netting where a participant is insolvent or bankrupt, preservation of rights in underlying transactions, and notification of insolvency. It binds System Operators and Settlement Institutions of licensed/designated RPS, and affects participants' insolvency-related rights.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
