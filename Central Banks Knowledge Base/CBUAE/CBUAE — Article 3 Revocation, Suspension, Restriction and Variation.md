# Article 3: Revocation, Suspension, Restriction and Variation

Enforcement article empowering the Central Bank to revoke, suspend, restrict or vary an Insurance Broker's licence at any time on stated grounds, including breach of licensing conditions, false or misleading information supplied by the broker or its Representatives, threats to the interests of Clients, and liquidation (ordered or voluntary). Binds licensed Insurance Brokers and their Representatives; relevant when assessing supervisory sanction exposure.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
