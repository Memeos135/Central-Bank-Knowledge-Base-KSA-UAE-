# Chapter 7: Accounts and Audit

Chapter 7 sets standards on accounts and audit for Licensed Persons, including the internal audit function: adherence to internal audit professional standards and ethics, breadth of audit coverage (all business activities, core and non-core functions, branches, Head Office and Management Office, reconciliation and unreconciled items, unclaimed funds, liquidity, capital adequacy, plus AML and regulatory compliance), access to staff, data and records, and the Annual Internal Audit Plan approved by the Board (or Owner/Partners). It also requires timely remediation of audit findings and annual review of the Internal Audit Charter and Plan by the Audit Committee with a summary to the Board. It binds Licensed Persons, their Boards/Owners, Audit Committees and internal auditors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
