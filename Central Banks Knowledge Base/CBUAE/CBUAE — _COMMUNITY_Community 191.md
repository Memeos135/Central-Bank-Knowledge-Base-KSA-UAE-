# Outsourcing Requirements

## Why this community

Conditions under which a regulated entity may outsource functions to a third party while retaining full regulatory responsibility for the outsourced activity.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster with no captured internal linkage; it is a horizontal obligation that attaches to multiple regimes.
- Legally relevant as a non-delegation principle: outsourcing does not transfer accountability for AML/CFT, risk management, data protection or customer-protection duties.
- Should be cross-read with governance, technology-risk and third-party reliance provisions in the operative regime (e.g. reliance on third parties for CDD).
