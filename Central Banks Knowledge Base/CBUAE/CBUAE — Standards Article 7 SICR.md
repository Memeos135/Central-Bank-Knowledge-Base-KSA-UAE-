# Standards Article 7: SICR

Article 7 of the CBUAE Credit Risk Standards sets the mandatory approach for identifying a Significant Increase in Credit Risk (SICR) since initial recognition, requiring documented, regular assessment aligned with accounting standards and internal policy. It fixes specific triggers — a deferral indicator (three or more deferrals in a rolling 24-month period on material Wholesale facilities above a 5% threshold), a rebuttable 30 days-past-due presumption, and forward-looking qualitative indicators — and requires assessment at facility, obligor and group level, with a watch list as a precursor. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs) and interacts with the cross-default rules preceding it and with Stage 2 classification under Article 9.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
