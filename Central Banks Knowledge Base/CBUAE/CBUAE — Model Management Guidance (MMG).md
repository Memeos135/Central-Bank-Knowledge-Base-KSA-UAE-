# Model Management Guidance (MMG)

Node for the CBUAE Model Management Guidance, the companion document to the Model Management Standards that elaborates supervisory expectations on model governance, development, calibration, validation and documentation. It shares the MMS definitional framework (Model, Model Risk, Materiality, Model Calibration) and the same effective date and compliance timeline. It applies to the same population of CBUAE-licensed institutions, including UAE branches and subsidiaries of foreign institutions and UAE parent groups.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
