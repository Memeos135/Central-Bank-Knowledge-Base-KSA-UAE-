# Circular No. 28/2010 (Loan Classification)

A repealed CBUAE instrument on classification of loans and their provisions (together with its clarifications and guidelines manual), expressly cancelled by Article 17 of the Credit Risk Management Regulation. Its subject matter is now governed by the Regulation's Article 9 classification and provisioning duties and the accompanying Credit Risk Standards' stages. Relevant mainly for legacy/transition analysis and for tracing where an old classification requirement now sits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
