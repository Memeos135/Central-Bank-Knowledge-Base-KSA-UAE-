# Profit Equalization Reserve (PER)

PER is a reserve created by setting aside amounts from profits before allocation between the Investment Account Holder and the Islamic Bank, usable in whole or part to lift IAH returns when the investment pool underperforms market expectations. The CBUAE Profit Equalization Standard binds Islamic Banks and Islamic Windows to Shari'ah approval of PER policies, contractual and website/branch disclosure of PER practices and ownership, Board-level oversight, and prescribed treatment on voluntary liquidation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
