# Interest Rate Risk

Node on interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), and its Islamic-banking analogue profit rate risk in the banking book (PRRBB), which the CBUAE treats as a material Pillar 2 risk (trading-book interest rate risk being covered under Pillar 1 market risk). It requires a Board-approved IRRBB risk appetite, supporting policies and a full identification-measurement-reporting-monitoring-mitigation process, with measurement drawing on CBUAE ICAAP standards, the 2018 interest rate/rate of return risk rules, model management standards and the Basel SRP 31 framework, and covering basis risk, repricing gaps, commercial margins, currency optionality and non-maturing deposits. Binds UAE banks and their boards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Market Risk|Market Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** The link is a scoping one: both interest rate risk in the banking book and market risk appear in the same mandatory minimum list of risks a bank must cover in its internal capital adequacy assessment, so neither can be omitted and neither substitutes for the other. There is no hierarchy between them — IRRBB is a banking-book risk with its own measurement and supervisory outlier expectations, while market risk covers trading-book positions, and conflating the two would understate coverage. Practical consequence: your risk inventory must evidence both separately; because the two nodes were captured from an identical enumerating sentence, check the primary text for the distinct substantive requirements attaching to each.
