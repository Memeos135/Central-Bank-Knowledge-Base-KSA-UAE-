# Terms and Conditions for Fund (Article 9)

Article 9 (read with the Fund Charter requirements in Article 5) of the CBUAE Standard on establishing a Takaful Insurance Fund, governing the terms and conditions under which the Fund operates and contracts with the Company. It requires the Fund to act per its Charter, fixes the Charter as the basis of the Fund–Company contractual relationship, and underpins Charter content on participation terms, Beneficiaries, asset/liability separation, liquidation distribution, the 100-year renewable term, and the Company's entitlement limited to the Wakala Fee. Binds Takaful Insurance Companies establishing or managing a Fund.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
