# Money or Value Transfer Services (MVTS)

Guidance section on financial institutions' relationships with Money or Value Transfer Services, referencing Articles 26 and 30 of the AML-CFT Decision, plus the associated originator/beneficiary information requirements for wire transfers. It requires risk-based CDD on MVTS counterparties: confirming licensing or registration (including physical inspection of the original Hawala Provider registration certificate issued by the Central Bank), assessing the MVTS's AML/CFT controls, agent network, ownership and customer base. It binds FIs that open accounts for or maintain business relationships with MVTS providers and Hawala Providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
