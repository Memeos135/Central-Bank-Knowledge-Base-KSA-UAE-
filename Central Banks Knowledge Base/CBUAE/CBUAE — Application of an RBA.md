# Application of an RBA

Core definitional section explaining the Risk-Based Approach: LFIs must identify, assess and manage ML/TF/PF risks across customers, products and services, delivery channels, geographic locations/markets and operating structure, then apply AML/CFT/CPF measures proportionate to those risks — enhanced where risk is higher, simplified where lower, subject always to minimum UAE legal and regulatory requirements. Also contains the guidance's abbreviations (PEP, TFS, VASP, STR/SAR, TBML). Binds licensed financial institutions supervised by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
