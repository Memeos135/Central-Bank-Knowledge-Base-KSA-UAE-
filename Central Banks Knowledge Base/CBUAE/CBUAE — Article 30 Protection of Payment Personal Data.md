# Article 30: Protection of Payment Personal Data

Article 30 of the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation sets the data-protection obligations attaching to Personal Data handled in connection with payment token activity, including localisation of storage in the UAE absent Central Bank approval, a secure separate backup, and a five-year retention period. It requires licensed Payment Token Service Providers to meet applicable data protection law and the Consumer Protection Regulation, and to control, process and retain only Personal Data necessary for the service and only with the Customer's explicit consent. It binds licensed providers of payment token issuing, custody and transfer, and conversion services.
