# Insurance Company (Ombudsman Reg)

Explains how Insurance Companies fall within the scope of the CBUAE Ombudsman Unit Regulation, following the transfer of the Insurance Authority's competencies to the Central Bank, so that the Regulation covers both Insurance Companies under the Insurance Law and Licensed Financial Institutions under the Central Bank Law. It sets the scope of application, the prevalence of this Regulation over conflicting requirements of other regulators, and the Central Bank's power to impose additional requirements and issue guidance. It binds all Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies in their dealings with the Ombudsman Unit.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
