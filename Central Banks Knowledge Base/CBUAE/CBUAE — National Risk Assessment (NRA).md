# National Risk Assessment (NRA)

This node covers the enterprise-level ML/FT risk assessment expectations in the CBUAE Guidelines: identifying inherent risks across customers, products and services, transactions, delivery channels and geographies, calibrating mitigating controls, and determining residual risk and control gaps. It requires the assessment methodology and rationale to be documented, kept current and made available to the supervisory authority on request, with proportionality to the size and complexity of the business. It binds supervised financial institutions and underpins risk appetite, resource allocation and CDD intensity decisions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
