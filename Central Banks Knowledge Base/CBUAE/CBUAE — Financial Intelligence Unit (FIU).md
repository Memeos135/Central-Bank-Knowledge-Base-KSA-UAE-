# Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)

Defined-entity node for the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit, the national body that receives suspicious transaction/activity reports and issues typologies and risk publications used by institutions. In the sourced guidance it appears both as the mandatory reporting destination for STRs/SARs and as a credible source input to country and customer risk assessments. Relevant to every licensed financial institution subject to AML/CTF reporting duties.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

- **What this link tells you:** This matters when you are allocating responsibility for suspicious transaction reporting and deciding who may communicate with the Financial Intelligence Unit.
