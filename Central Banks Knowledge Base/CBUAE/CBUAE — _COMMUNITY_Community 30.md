# Insurance Due Diligence

## Why this community

The AML/CFT customer due diligence regime as applied in the insurance sector: identifying and verifying customers and beneficial owners, calibrating measures by risk, monitoring the relationship, and escalating to suspicious transaction reporting.

## How members connect

- All members hang off the statutory CDD duty in the AML/CFT law; the generic CDD node is the hub, with SDD, EDD, beneficial owner identification and ongoing monitoring as branches of the same obligation.
- The risk-based approach is the switch that determines which branch applies: risk profiling at onboarding sets the tier (simplified vs enhanced), and source of funds/wealth enquiry is the principal EDD add-on.
- Insurance-specific nodes (general CDD measures, simplified DD for insurance) are sectoral applications of the general standards - read them as overlays, not replacements; note the policyholder/beneficiary distinction, since the insurance 'customer' is not always the payer or the beneficial owner.
- Ongoing monitoring feeds the risk profile and red-flag indicators, which in turn trigger the STR duty - a closed obligation chain from onboarding to reporting.
- Cross-regime flag: the cash-intensive business guidance imports merchant/commercial-customer risk typologies into the insurance CDD analysis.
