# Licensed Financial Institution

Node on the status of a Licensed Financial Institution under Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025, covering the grounds on which the Central Bank may suspend, revoke or withdraw a licence in whole or in part — including failure to take required measures, ceasing licensed activity or operations for over a year, inability to meet obligations, non-compliance with final judgments, liquidity/solvency or capital shortfalls, merger, bankruptcy, non-cooperation with Central Bank examiners, and revocation abroad for foreign institutions. It also covers external audit obligations: reading and publication of the auditors' and board reports, transmission to head office for foreign or Financial Free Zone incorporated entities, independence restrictions on auditors, a ban on extending credit facilities to auditors, and auditor liability with possible Central Bank action against them. Binds licensed financial institutions, their boards and their approved auditors.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
