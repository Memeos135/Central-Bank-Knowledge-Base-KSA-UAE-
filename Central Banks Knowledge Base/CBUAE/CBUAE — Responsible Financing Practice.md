# Responsible Financing Practice

Article 7 of the Consumer Protection Regulation on responsible financing practice, requiring institutions to obtain credible and independent information on a Consumer's financial situation, assess obligations, dependants, capacity and needs before granting credit, and ensure the credit does not cause over-indebtedness or undue financial burden. It also prohibits unreasonable fees and the provision of excessive credit beyond what the Consumer requires. Applies across the whole credit granting process and ongoing management by Licensed Financial Institutions; the adjacent Article 4 sets responsible market conduct duties.
