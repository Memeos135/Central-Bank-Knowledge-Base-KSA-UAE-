# Insurance Brokers' Regulation

The CBUAE Insurance Brokers' Regulation governs licensing, ongoing requirements, fit and proper assessment, brokerage agreements, premiums and claim settlement flows, financial soundness, corporate governance, risk governance and internal controls, conduct of business, financial and supervisory reporting, and record keeping. It binds all insurance brokers and insurance/reinsurance companies operating in the UAE, across primary insurance and (proportionately) reinsurance operations. Notably it prohibits brokers from collecting premiums, claim settlements or premium refunds in primary insurance, requiring direct client-company payment.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
