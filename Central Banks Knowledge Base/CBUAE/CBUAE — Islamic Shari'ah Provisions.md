# Islamic Shari'ah Provisions

Concept node for compliance with Islamic Shari'ah Provisions as the substantive benchmark against which a Takaful company's objectives, activities, operations and conduct are assessed. Context sets out the internal Shari'ah audit division's mandate to test that compliance through an annual plan and to assess the adequacy of internal procedures and the Shari'ah governance framework, plus fit-and-proper criteria (qualifications, professional certification, minimum experience, full-time and no-dual-role requirements, integrity) for the division head, whose appointment requires central bank approval.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
