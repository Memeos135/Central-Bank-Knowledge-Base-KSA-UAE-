# Article 7: Strategic & Operational Decisions (Regulation)

Article 7 of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (153/2018) is the provision on strategic and operational decisions, sitting between the internal reporting requirements (Article 6) and group risk management (Article 8) in the regulation's structure. It addresses how risk considerations must inform a Bank's strategic and operational decision-making within the board-approved risk governance framework.
