# Application Process

Sets out the three-stage sandbox application process — preliminary (indicative suitability within 15 working days of a complete submission), evaluation (engagement on adjustments and required safeguards, up to 21 working days) and decision (final determination within 15 working days of resubmission, with reasons if rejected). Also lists the supporting information to accompany an application, including test scenarios and expected outcomes, operational readiness and testing plan, exit and transition strategy, reporting plan and metrics, client acquisition and communication strategy, wind-down plan, risk assessment with mitigations, and Consumer-facing disclosures. Binds Applicants; admission remains solely at the Central Bank's discretion and case by case.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
