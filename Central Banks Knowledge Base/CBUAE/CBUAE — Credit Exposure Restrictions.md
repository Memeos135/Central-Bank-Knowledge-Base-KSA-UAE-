# Credit Exposure Restrictions

This node covers Article 6 (Credit Exposure Restrictions) of the Specialized Banks with Low Risk Regulation, defining a Large Credit Exposure as exposure to a single borrower or group of Related Entities at or above 5% of Aggregate Capital Funds, with specified deductible items. The surrounding provisions set capital ratio floors (12.5% of total assets, 17% in the first three years), prior Central Bank approval for profit distributions and capital changes, a 20% single-shareholding cap, application of the Banks' liquidity Regulation, and Article 7-8 duties on corporate governance, AML/CFT (including STR filing to the Central Bank's FIU), consumer protection and mandatory Al Etihad Credit Bureau reporting and enquiry. It binds Specialized Banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, data/credit info
