# Risk Management Regulation

## Why this community

Prudential risk-management obligations for SAMA-supervised institutions: board-level risk governance, an independent risk function, risk appetite, stress testing of material risks and their feed into capital adequacy assessment.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Central Bank Law supplies supervisory authority; the Risk Management Regulation sets the binding obligations; the accompanying Standards elaborate them article-by-article in mirrored numbering (e.g. Article 5 stress testing in both instruments).
- Obligation chain runs board → risk governance framework → risk appetite statement → risk management function → stress testing outputs used in the ICAAP.
- Corporate governance rules cross-reference the Regulation, so risk-management duties are enforceable both as a prudential requirement and as a board governance duty.
- Defined constructs (material risk, risk appetite, ICAAP) are shared across Regulation and Standards and should be read consistently.
