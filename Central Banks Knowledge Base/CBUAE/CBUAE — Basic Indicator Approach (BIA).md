# Basic Indicator Approach (BIA)

One of the Basel-based methods referenced in CBUAE guidance for calculating the Pillar 1 operational risk capital charge, alongside the Standardised and Alternative Standardised Approaches. The guidance flags that gross-income-based measures such as this are risk-insensitive and may understate exposure, so banks are expected to test adequacy under Pillar 2. Applies to UAE-licensed banks determining operational risk capital under the Capital Adequacy Standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
