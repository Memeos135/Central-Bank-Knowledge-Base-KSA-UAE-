# Shari'ah Governance & Audit

## Why this community

Shari'ah governance architecture for Islamic financial institutions and takaful insurers: who certifies Shari'ah compliance, how it is controlled internally, and how it is independently audited and reported.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the governing Shari'ah governance standard (and the takaful-specific Shari'ah governance standard / Insurance Authority Decision No. 50 of 2019) down to institution-level framework, divisions and audit obligations.
- The ISSC is the hub definition: the internal Shari'ah control and internal Shari'ah audit divisions report to or support it, and the external Shari'ah auditor's report is addressed to/interacts with it.
- Three Lines of Defense allocates the obligation chain — business first line, Shari'ah control second, Shari'ah audit third, with external audit as an outside assurance layer.
- Takaful-specific substantive rules (insurance surplus, qard hasan from the shareholders' account) are the transactions the ISSC and Shari'ah auditors must opine on; AAOIFI standards supply the substantive compliance benchmark.
