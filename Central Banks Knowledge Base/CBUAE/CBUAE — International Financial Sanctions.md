# International Financial Sanctions

Node covering the international dimension of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines: the intergovernmental and standard-setting bodies (UN and its anti-money-laundering programme, FATF and its Recommendations and Immediate Outcomes, FATF-style regional bodies such as MENAFATF, the Egmont Group of FIUs) whose standards the UAE transposes, and the guidelines' treatment of international financial sanctions. It explains the source and status of these standards rather than creating stand-alone obligations, but frames the sanctions-screening and targeted financial sanctions duties applicable to supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
