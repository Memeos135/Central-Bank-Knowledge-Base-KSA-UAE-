# Fictitious Emiratization

Concept node covering "fictitious Emiratization" under the CBUAE Administrative and Financial Penalties for Emiratization Violations Regulation (Circular 6/2025): the sham or artificial appointment of UAE Nationals to inflate Emiratization ratios or obtain NAFIS benefits. It sets out the consequences — penalties multiplied by the number of Nationals fictitiously appointed, CBUAE discretion to compel the institution to train and qualify the affected Nationals at its own cost, and a possible mandated salary increase of 10–30% after training. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, insurance companies and insurance-related professionals, and bars them from recovering incentive amounts from the National; grievance against CBUAE penalty decisions lies only to the Central Bank within ten working days.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
