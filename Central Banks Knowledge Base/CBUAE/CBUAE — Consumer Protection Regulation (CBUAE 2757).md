# Consumer Protection Regulation (CBUAE 2757)

The CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular No. 8/2020, dated 25/11/2020), issued under Article 121 of Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018, is the overarching principles-based conduct framework for protecting consumers of financial products and services in the UAE. It sets institutional obligations on business and market conduct, governance over product design and sale, responsible financing and over-indebtedness, disclosure and transparency, complaint redress and financial literacy, supplemented by detailed Standards. It binds all Licensed Financial Institutions in the State and must be read with other CBUAE regulations, including Shari'ah governance rules.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
