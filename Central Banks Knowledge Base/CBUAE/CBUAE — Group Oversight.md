# Group Oversight

Section 8.3 of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines addresses group-wide AML/CFT programmes, keyed to AML-CFT Decision Articles 20, 31 and 32. It requires an FI that forms part of a financial group to implement and apply group-wide AML/CFT policies across all branches and majority-owned subsidiaries, including those abroad, at standards mirroring those applicable in the UAE. It binds FIs that are members of a Financial Group (as defined in the Guidelines) and is the entry point for host/home country conflict and foreign-branch standards questions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
