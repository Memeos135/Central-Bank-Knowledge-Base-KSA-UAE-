# Admissible Assets

Defines admissible assets as total assets after applying the constraints and limits used in calculating the solvency margin, and sits within the CBUAE takaful rules on assets meeting accrued insurance liabilities. It ties to the prudent person principle, Shari'ah compliance, asset security/quality/liquidity/localisation requirements, and special treatment where investment risk is borne by participants (e.g. unit-linked). Binds takaful companies and branches of foreign takaful operators in asset valuation and solvency reporting.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
