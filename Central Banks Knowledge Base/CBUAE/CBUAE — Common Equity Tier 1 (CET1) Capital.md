# Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Capital

The highest-quality regulatory capital tier under the CBUAE capital adequacy framework, used both to meet minimum capital ratios and, in the portion not already consumed by Tier 1 and total capital minimums, to satisfy buffer requirements. The material illustrates how freely available CET1 is calculated against the effective combined buffer (capital conservation buffer, countercyclical buffer, D-SIB surcharge) and the consequent earnings-retention and distribution restrictions. Relevant to all banks subject to CBUAE capital adequacy and buffer rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
