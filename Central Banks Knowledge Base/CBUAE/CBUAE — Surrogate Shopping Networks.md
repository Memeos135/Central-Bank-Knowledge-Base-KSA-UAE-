# Surrogate Shopping Networks

A typology node in the CBUAE TBML guidance describing networks of surrogates or personal shoppers who buy goods on behalf of others to circumvent customs, export control or tariff restrictions, and who can be paid with criminal proceeds. The guidance highlights the decentralised and often anonymous character of these purchases, which obscures fund flows and true beneficiaries, and pairs it with an illustrative drug-proceeds/point-of-sale aggregation example. Relevant to LFIs calibrating customer risk ratings, transaction monitoring and suspicion assessment for card and merchant activity linked to trade.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
