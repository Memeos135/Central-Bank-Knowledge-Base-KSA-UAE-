# Obligations of FIs and DNFBPs

Concept node collecting the compliance duties placed on financial institutions and DNFBPs under the TFS decision: immediate notification of matches, frozen funds, attempted transactions, unfrozen funds and unresolved false positives; internal controls and procedures to ensure compliance; a tipping-off prohibition covering customers and third parties; and cooperation with the Office and supervisory authority. It sits alongside the parallel duties of supervisory authorities to collect and forward this information within set timeframes, supervise onsite and offsite, and impose administrative sanctions. It binds FIs, DNFBPs and their supervisors, with the Office as national coordination point.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
