# Islamic Banking Regulation

## Why this community

Islamic banking treatment within the Regulation — how the general requirements apply to, or are modified for, Shariah-compliant activity and its governance.

## How members connect

- Stand-alone Regulation article acting as a scope/qualification provision: it conditions how the preceding general articles apply rather than creating a separate regime.
- No paired Standards article in this cluster, so supervisory detail must be sought outside the community; do not assume elaboration exists.
- Interacts with the governance, group risk and disclosure articles because Shariah governance arrangements affect decision authority and what is reported.
