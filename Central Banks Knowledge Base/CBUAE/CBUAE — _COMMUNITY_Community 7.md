# Bank Corporate Governance

## Why this community

Prudential corporate governance and remuneration obligations for banks (and insurers/takaful): board and senior management accountability, control-function independence, and pay structures that must not incentivise excessive risk.

## How members connect

- Central Bank Law is the enabling statute; the Corporate Governance Regulation (Circular 83/2019) and the Remuneration Regulation for banks and insurance companies are the implementing instruments, with accompanying standards elaborating them.
- Remuneration obligations chain from the board → remuneration committee → remuneration policy/framework → variable remuneration, material risk takers, and malus/clawback.
- Control-function independence is a shared thread: independent audit function, risk committee responsibilities, and separate pay rules for control-function staff protect against conflicts.
- Cross-regime touchpoint: senior management governance duties pull in sanctions/TFS obligations (international financial sanctions, terrorism lists decision); proportionality is addressed for small organisations.
