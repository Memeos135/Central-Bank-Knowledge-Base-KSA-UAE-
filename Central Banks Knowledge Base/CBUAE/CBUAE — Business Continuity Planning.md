# Business Continuity Planning

Concept node collecting the business continuity expectations in the CBUAE enabling-technologies guidelines, applied both to material cloud computing arrangements (BCP ownership, key risk indicators and triggers, dependency on a single provider, roles, recovery objectives, at-least-annual testing) and to DLT applications (loss of data/processing capability, cyber-crime, periodic testing). Where the arrangement is outsourced, the institution must be satisfied with the Outsourcing Service Provider's own continuity plan and test jointly. Binds CBUAE-licensed Institutions' business continuity and crisis management functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
