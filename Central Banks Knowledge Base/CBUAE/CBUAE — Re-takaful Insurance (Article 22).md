# Re-takaful Insurance (Article 22)

Requires the Company, acting as agent of the Takaful Insurance Fund, to set a Shari'ah-compliant re-takaful policy for the Fund, approved by the Board, the Board of Trustees (where established) and the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee. Related provisions cover the optional (and potentially CBUAE-mandated) Board of Trustees protecting Fund interests and the licensing and Shari'ah governance conditions for operating a Takaful Insurance Window. Binds takaful and re-takaful companies and insurers with takaful windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
