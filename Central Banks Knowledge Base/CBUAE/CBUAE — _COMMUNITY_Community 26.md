# Specialized Banks Regulation

## Why this community

A mixed licensing-and-conduct cluster anchored on the central bank law: the prudential perimeter for low-risk specialised banks, recovery planning duties, the regulatory sandbox route for innovators, and telemarketing/consumer-consent restrictions.

## How members connect

- Common statutory parent: the specialised banks regulation, sandbox conditions and recovery-planning articles all derive authority from the central bank law, giving a law → regulation → circular hierarchy.
- Specialised bank licensing is defined by a triangle of permitted activities, minimum capital, and credit exposure restrictions, with credit-bureau reporting as an underwriting input.
- Recovery planning is an internally sequenced obligation chain: plan requirement → indicators → options → scenario testing → recovery capacity, with central bank liquidity facilities excluded/limited as a recovery option, plus continuity and communication annexes.
- The sandbox and telemarketing/DNCR consent rules sit here through the consumer protection regulation, which is the conduct bridge between innovation-stage firms and customer-contact restrictions — a different regime from prudential licensing and should not be read as one.
