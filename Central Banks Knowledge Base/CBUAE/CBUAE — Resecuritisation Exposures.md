# Resecuritisation Exposures

Concept node for resecuritisation exposures, defined as securitisation exposures where the risk of an underlying pool is tranched and at least one underlying exposure is itself a securitisation exposure. The node sits alongside the standard's other definitions (early amortisation, excess spread, implicit support, originating bank, pool) and is significant because resecuritisations are excluded from STC-compliant treatment and attract more conservative capital treatment. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks holding, originating or sponsoring such positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
