# Large Exposure Definition

This node captures the defining threshold and limits: an exposure is a "large exposure" when total exposure values to one counterparty or Group of Connected Counterparties reach or exceed 10% of Tier 1 capital, while the hard cap is 25% of Tier 1 capital at all times (15% for G-SIB-to-G-SIB exposures, with a 12-month transition after FSB listing). It also introduces the "unknown client" concept, aggregating undisclosed underlying counterparties into a single notional counterparty subject to the limit. Breaches must be reported immediately to the Central Bank and rectified, with Tier 1 capital reduced by the excess unless the Central Bank waives this.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
