# Article 19: Secure Communication

Article 19 of the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation requires all Open Finance participants to use common, secure open communication standards for identification, authentication, notification, information exchange and security measures. It covers interactions among Licensees that are Data Holders and/or Service Owners, Data Sharing Providers, Service Initiation Providers, Users, Payers, Payees and other relevant parties, and sits alongside obligations on protecting personalised security credentials and on Service Initiation Providers identifying themselves to the Service Owner for each Transaction.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, data/credit info
