# Targeted Financial Sanctions Obligations

Concept node covering targeted financial sanctions duties applicable to registered hawala providers and licensed financial institutions under UNSC and UAE listings. It explains freezing without delay (24 hours of the listing decision), acting without prior notice to the customer, suspending transactions on a potential match, record-keeping on match confirmation, and reporting listed persons and actions taken to the competent authorities. Binding on RHPs and LFIs as an absolute, risk-independent obligation distinct from CDD.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
