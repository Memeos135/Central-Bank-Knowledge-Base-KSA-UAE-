# Unclaimed Funds

Chapter 18 of the exchange-business Standards defines when money-transfer funds become unclaimed (e.g. outward transfers to accounts not repaid to the remitter within seven calendar days of cancellation or return; cash payouts not collected within ninety calendar days; expired demand drafts) and requires the Licensed Person to recover funds already settled with the bank or transfer provider. It then imposes management duties: board-approved policies and procedures, defined roles, segregation of the funds in local currency cash or a local bank account with no use in day-to-day business, ongoing customer contact to refund, monthly assessment and disclosure as a separate current liability in the monthly return, and internal audit review reporting to the board. Binds Licensed Persons as a customer-protection obligation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, governance/risk
