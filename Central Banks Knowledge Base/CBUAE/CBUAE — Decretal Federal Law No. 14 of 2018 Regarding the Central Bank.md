# Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 Regarding the Central Bank

The UAE Central Bank Law, as amended, establishing the CBUAE and regulating financial institutions and activities; it is the enabling statute cited across CBUAE regulations, including the definition of a licensed Bank and the scope of licensed financial activities. Here it appears within a CBUAE regulation containing proportionality, definitions (Affiliate, Board, Group, Insurance Company, Climate-related Financial Risks, Critical Functions, Liability Risk) applying to banks and insurance companies. Binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
