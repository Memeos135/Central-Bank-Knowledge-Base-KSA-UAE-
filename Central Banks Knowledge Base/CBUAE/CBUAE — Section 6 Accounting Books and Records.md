# Section 6: Accounting Books and Records

Section 6 of the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies sets the principles for organising accounting books and records of insurance companies, agents and brokers, and the data those books must contain. It prescribes minimum book types (ledgers, journals, technical accounting books), separation of books between Insurance of Persons/Fund Accumulation and Property and Liability operations, backup storage in a separate location, auditing of books, and the regulator's inspection and information-request powers. It binds licensed insurers (including foreign branches) and their agents and brokers.
