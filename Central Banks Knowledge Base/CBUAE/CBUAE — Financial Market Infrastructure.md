# Financial Market Infrastructure

Defines Financial Market Infrastructure as a multilateral system among Participant Persons (including its operator) used to clear, settle or record payments, securities, derivatives or other financial transactions, established or overseen by a Regulatory Authority in the State, together with related defined terms (Settlement Institution, Default Arrangements, Transfer Order). It also opens the Central Bank's chapter of powers over such infrastructures, including designation as systemically important. Binds FMI operators, settlement institutions and participants subject to Central Bank oversight.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
