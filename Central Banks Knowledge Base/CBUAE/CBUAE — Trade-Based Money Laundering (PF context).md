# Trade-Based Money Laundering (PF context)

A concept node within CBUAE proliferation-financing (PF) guidance explaining how trade-based money laundering techniques — mis-invoicing, over/under-valuation and misdescription of goods — can be exploited to fund or procure WMD-related and dual-use items. It contrasts documentary trade finance (richer data on goods, counterparties, routes, vessels) with open-account/wire-based trade, where message data is thin and PF risk is harder to assess. It is directed at Licensed Financial Institutions assessing PF threats and vulnerabilities across their own products and services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
