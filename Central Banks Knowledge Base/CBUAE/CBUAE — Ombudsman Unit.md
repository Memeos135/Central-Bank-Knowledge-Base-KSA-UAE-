# Ombudsman Unit

Defined-entity node for the Ombudsman Unit, the CBUAE-established body responsible for receiving, handling and resolving consumer complaints against Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies. Its governance comprises a Board of the Ombudsman Unit with ultimate responsibility, a Chief Ombudsman for day-to-day operations, and staff subject to independence and conduct requirements. Relevant to consumer-protection teams assessing complaint escalation routes and levy obligations.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
