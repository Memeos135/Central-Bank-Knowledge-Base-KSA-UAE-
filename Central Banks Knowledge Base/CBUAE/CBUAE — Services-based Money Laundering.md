# Services-based Money Laundering

Concept node on services-based money laundering within the CBUAE TBML guidance: the misuse of intangible services (for example consulting or advisory billing) rather than physical goods to move value, often between entities sharing a beneficial owner. Because no shipment exists to verify, pricing and volume misrepresentation is harder to detect, raising the importance of understanding customer profile and economic rationale. Directed at LFIs assessing trade and service-related payment flows.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
