# Record-keeping

Node on record-keeping duties attached to correspondent banking relationships, specifying a minimum five-year retention of due diligence records including the respondent's licence, the correspondent agreement, due diligence and AML/CFT/CPF assessments, a completed Wolfsberg CBDDQ, the anti-shell-bank statement, site visit and call reports, and the relationship risk assessment with CDD/KYC files, correspondence and STR/SAR material. It also requires secure storage and access controls consistent with data protection obligations. Binds LFIs; the surrounding context also covers tailored AML/CFT/CPF training expectations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, data/credit info
