# Risk Management Regulation (Circular 153/2018)

The CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (Circular 153/2018) and its accompanying Standards are the baseline framework for risk governance in licensed banks: board-approved risk governance framework and risk appetite statement, three lines of defence, coverage of credit, market, liquidity and operational risk, ICAAP and internal control review, risk reporting and disclosures, and the role of the risk management function/CRO. Sector-specific standards (including the Islamic banks standard) are issued under it and are enforceable in the same manner. It binds licensed banks' boards, risk committees and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
