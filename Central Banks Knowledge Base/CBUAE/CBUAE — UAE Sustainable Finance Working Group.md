# UAE Sustainable Finance Working Group

Institutional node describing the UAE Sustainable Finance Working Group, established 2019, comprising UAE ministries, financial regulators (CBUAE, SCA, ADGM FSRA, DFSA) and exchanges, which issued the Guiding Principles on Sustainable Finance and the Principles for Sustainability-Related Disclosures. It sets a common minimum expectation on ESG/sustainability disclosure for Reporting Entities, with implementation left to each member Authority's own framework. Not directly binding on firms; relevant as soft-law/comparative material for KSA analysts (UAE, not SAMA).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
