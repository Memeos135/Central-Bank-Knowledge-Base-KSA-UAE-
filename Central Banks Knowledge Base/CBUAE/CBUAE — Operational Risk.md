# Operational Risk

Defines operational risk as loss from inadequate or failed internal processes, people or systems, or from external events, expressly including legal and compliance risk but excluding strategic and reputational risk. Covers the operational risk capital charge (converted to risk-weighted assets by a 12.5 multiplier) and the expectation that banks assess whether Pillar 1 gross-income-based approaches understate their exposure, addressing any shortfall in Pillar 2 / ICAAP. Binds UAE-licensed banks, including those operating on a Shari'ah-compliant basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
