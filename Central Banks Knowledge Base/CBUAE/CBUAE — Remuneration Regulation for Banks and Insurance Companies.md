# Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies

Circular No. 5/2026, the CBUAE Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies, establishing minimum requirements for responsible, risk-sensitive remuneration frameworks so that pay is commensurate with performance and risk outcomes. It applies to all Banks and Insurance Companies (including Reinsurance Companies), collectively termed Financial Institutions, and is read with the Central Bank Law and the Risk Management, Corporate Governance (banks and insurers) and Consumer Protection Regulations. It does not prescribe remuneration amounts for any Person, including Relevant Persons, but requires continuous demonstrable compliance to the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
