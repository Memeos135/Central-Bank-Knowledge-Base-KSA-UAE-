# Exchange House AML/CFT

## Why this community

AML/CFT obligations applied to licensed exchange houses / money-changing businesses, including the statutory basis, supervisory standards and sector-specific guidance.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy is visible: primary AML/CFT statute → sectoral standards chapter for exchange business → supervisory guidance for licensed exchange houses, with an article-level AML/CFT procedures provision operationalising them.
- All members anchor to the FATF Standards as the interpretive benchmark, so guidance is read as elaborating, not extending, the statutory duties.
- Duplicate labelling of the same decree (cited two ways) means the statute is the single common authority behind both the guidance and the general AML/CFT requirements.
- Scope caution: the cited Federal Decree Law is a UAE instrument, not KSA legislation — before relying on this cluster for a SAMA question, confirm the equivalent Saudi AML Law and its Implementing Regulation apply instead.
