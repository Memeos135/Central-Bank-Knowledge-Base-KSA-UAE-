# Digital ID Due Diligence

## Why this community

Use of digital identity systems to perform AML/CTF customer due diligence: when a digital ID is fit for identification and verification, how its assurance is assessed, and the rules for relying on third parties or outsourced providers.

## How members connect

- The digital ID CDD guidance is the hub; appropriateness assessment and assurance level assessment are its two decision tests for accepting a digital ID for identification/verification.
- Standards chain: the guidance cites external sources (FATF digital identity guidance and the NIST 800-63 identity assurance levels) which supply the benchmarks used in the assurance assessment.
- Third-party reliance appears twice — in the digital ID context and in the insurance outsourcing context — reflecting the same legal principle that reliance does not transfer ultimate CDD responsibility from the regulated entity.
- Scope caution: the external standards are referenced as guidance for calibrating assurance, not as independent SAMA obligations.
