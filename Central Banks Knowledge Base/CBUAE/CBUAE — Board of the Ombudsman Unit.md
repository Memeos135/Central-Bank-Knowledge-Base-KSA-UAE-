# Board of the Ombudsman Unit

Node on the Board of the Ombudsman Unit: its composition, appointment by resolution of the CBUAE Board of Directors on the Governor's recommendation, three-year renewable terms capped at two terms, vacancy-filling within three months, and independence from Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies for at least two years before and throughout appointment. It also covers quorum, voting, five-year record retention of meetings and decisions, chairperson responsibilities and the power to make internal procedural rules subject to CBUAE direction. It binds Board members, the Chairperson and the Ombudsman Unit's internal governance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
