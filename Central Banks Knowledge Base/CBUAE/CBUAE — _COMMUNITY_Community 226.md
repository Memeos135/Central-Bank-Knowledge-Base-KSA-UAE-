# Financial Soundness

## Why this community

Prudential standing of the licensed entity — the requirement to maintain financial soundness on a continuing basis as a condition of operating.

## How members connect

- Stands alone as the prudential limb of the licence, but is functionally dependent on the on-going requirements article and on the governance/internal control cluster that must monitor and evidence it.
- Supervisory consequence: deterioration in financial soundness is a trigger point for SAMA intervention and for reassessment of continued authorisation.
