# Correspondent Banking Risk Factors

Concept node within CBUAE guidance on correspondent banking, covering the categories of ML/TF/PF risk factors that a correspondent institution must weigh when assessing a respondent institution (jurisdiction, ownership and management, products/services, customer base, nesting and payable-through arrangements). It frames correspondent banking as a bilateral relationship in which one licensed financial institution provides services such as cash management, cross-border wire transfers, cheque clearing, loans, letters of credit and FX to another. It binds LFIs supervised by the CBUAE and feeds the enterprise-wide risk assessment obligation under Article 4 of the AML-CFT Decision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
