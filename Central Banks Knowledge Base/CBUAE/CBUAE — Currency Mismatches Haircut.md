# Currency Mismatches Haircut

Node associated with currency mismatch adjustments and haircut mechanics in the CBUAE capital standard, drawing on the comprehensive-approach haircut rules (including scaling for re-margining/revaluation frequency and holding-period conversion) and on the foreign exchange risk capital requirements for net open positions. It explains how currency differences between exposure and collateral or protection are penalised through haircuts. Applies to CBUAE-licensed banks calculating credit risk mitigation benefit and market risk capital.
