# Article 9: Disclosure (Regulation)

Article 9 of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (153/2018) requires a Bank to make publicly available — including in its annual report and on its website — information on its Risk Governance Framework and on the nature and extent of its risk exposures. It is a public disclosure/transparency obligation binding CBUAE-licensed banks, complementing the internal reporting duties in Article 6.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
