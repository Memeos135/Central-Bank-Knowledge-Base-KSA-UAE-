# Compensation Policy (Article 8)

Node on Article 8 remuneration duties for CBUAE-licensed insurance/takaful companies: a board-approved compensation system aligned with sound governance, prudent risk-taking and long-term financial soundness. It mandates risk-symmetric and deferred payouts with malus and clawback, fixed-only pay for board members (no performance bonuses), predominantly fixed pay for risk, compliance and internal audit staff, performance-based elements for Senior Management and Material Risk Takers, and caps on individual bonuses (with escalating board/general assembly approval) and on the aggregate staff bonus pool relative to net profit. It binds the Board, which approves Senior Management pay and oversees the policy.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
