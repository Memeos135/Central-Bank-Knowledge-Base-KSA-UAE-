# Article 8: Reporting Requirements

Core reporting article of the CBUAE financial sanctions regulation, setting the obligation of all Licensed Financial Institutions to file regulatory returns through the Integrated Regulatory Reporting System at the required frequency and format, with differentiated monthly/quarterly deadlines for banks, exchange houses and other licensed institutions. It also fixes per-violation fines for late filing, daily escalation rates with caps, and separate penalties for submitting erroneous data. Binds every category of Licensed Financial Institution; the penalty grid is summarised in the regulation's appendix.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, other
