# Cross-Border Payments & SWIFT

## Why this community

Cross-border payment execution and messaging, covering correspondent banking channels, SWIFT message content and relationship-management arrangements, and migration to the ISO 20022 data standard.

## How members connect

- Correspondent banking guidance frames cross-border payments as the operating context in which messaging obligations bite.
- Payment message integrity (originator/beneficiary data) is the shared obligation linking cross-border payments to SWIFT message rules.
- RMA relationships with non-customers are treated as a distinct exposure within the same messaging infrastructure.
- ISO 20022 is conceptually linked as the evolving data standard affecting the content and quality of payment messages.
