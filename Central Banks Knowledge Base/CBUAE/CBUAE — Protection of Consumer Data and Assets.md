# Protection of Consumer Data and Assets

Article 6 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation, covering Consumer Data protection (6.1) and protection of Consumer assets, information and Data against financial crime, misappropriation and misuse (6.2). It builds on the confidentiality duty in Article 120 of Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018, requiring data minimisation, informed and express Consumer consent to collection, use and sharing (internally and with third parties), prevention of misuse, compensation for actual harm from breaches, and sound first-line security management. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions handling Consumer data and assets.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection, governance/risk
