# Sanctions List Management

A concept node within CBUAE guidance on transaction monitoring and sanctions screening that covers how licensed financial institutions should govern the sanctions lists they screen against — list selection and scope, internal versus vendor sourcing, maintenance and update controls, enrichment of list entries, and the controlled use of whitelists to manage false positives. It also addresses when screening must occur in the transaction lifecycle and the treatment of paper-based versus electronic transaction flows. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions (LFIs); screening against UNSC and UAE Cabinet lists is mandatory, while other lists may be applied on a risk basis.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
