# Article 4: Risk Management

Article 4 is the operative risk management article: FIs must embed Climate-related Financial Risks in their risk management framework (as a stand-alone category or within existing ones) through a structured process of identification, data gathering, measurement, reporting, control and mitigation. It addresses data management frameworks (without prejudice to UAE data protection law), metrics and indicators, and the treatment of climate drivers within credit/underwriting, market, operational (including business continuity and outsourcing), and liquidity risk. It also sets scenario analysis and stress testing requirements, including at least annual analysis, integration into ICAAP baseline and stress scenarios, documentation of scenarios and assumptions, and use of results in internal capital and liquidity planning.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
