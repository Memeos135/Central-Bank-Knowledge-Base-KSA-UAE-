# SVF Corporate Governance

Article (8) of the CBUAE SVF Regulation setting corporate governance and risk management expectations for SVF Licensees, including risk policies proportionate to the scale and complexity of the scheme and board-level governance responsibilities. It expressly disapplies these requirements to licensed banks conducting SVF business, which instead follow the Central Bank's bank corporate governance regulation and standards. Adjacent text also covers Central Bank discretion to impose higher financial resource requirements and an unconditional irrevocable bank guarantee for paid-up capital.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, banking prudential
