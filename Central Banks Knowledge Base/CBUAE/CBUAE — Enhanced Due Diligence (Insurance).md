# Enhanced Due Diligence (Insurance)

Node covering enhanced due diligence expectations for the insurance sector under CBUAE AML/CFT guidance, applied to higher-risk customers, products and distribution arrangements within in-scope insurance business. It sits on the same scope basis as the sector's general CDD: life and investment-related insurance products are within the AML/CFT framework, general insurance is not, and each licensed insurer, reinsurer, agent and broker must assess its own ML/FT risk. Binds CBUAE-licensed insurance operators and intermediaries.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
