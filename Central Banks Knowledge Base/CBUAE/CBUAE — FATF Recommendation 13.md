# FATF Recommendation 13

An annex-level orientation to the global standards underpinning correspondent banking supervision, centred on FATF Recommendation 13, which sets enhanced due diligence expectations for institutions providing correspondent services to foreign respondent institutions and to similar cross-border relationships. It explains the rationale (limited visibility over the respondent's customers, non-equivalent supervision abroad) and lists the UAE legal instruments and acronyms/defined terms used throughout the guidance. Useful for tracing how international standards are mapped onto national obligations for licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
