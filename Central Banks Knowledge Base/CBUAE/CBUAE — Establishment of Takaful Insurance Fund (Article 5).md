# Establishment of Takaful Insurance Fund (Article 5)

This node covers the establishment requirements for the Takaful Insurance Fund together with the Standard's definitions article (Wakala Fee, Contribution, Re-takaful Insurance, Participants' Investment Account). It fixes the Shari'ah-based economics of the arrangement: Participants donate Contributions (tabarru') to the Fund, while the Company acts as agent for a Wakala Fee in managing and investing Fund assets. It binds the Company establishing and operating the Fund, including insurers with Takaful windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
