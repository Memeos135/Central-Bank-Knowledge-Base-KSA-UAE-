# Risk Appetite Statement

Concept node for the risk appetite statement under the CBUAE Risk Management Standards: the board must approve, maintain and oversee the risk governance framework including the risk appetite statement and risk limits allocated by legal entity, business line or management unit consistent with that statement. The risk management function must develop metrics tied to the risk appetite statement, monitor and report on them and escalate breaches, while business lines operationalise the limits. Capital and liquidity implications must be reflected in the risks the bank is prepared to assume and in the associated limits.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
