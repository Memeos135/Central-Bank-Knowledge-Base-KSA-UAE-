# Article 3: Strategy

Article 3 requires Financial Institutions to assess and monitor how Climate-related Financial Risks affect their business environment, business model and activities across short, medium and long-term horizons. After accounting for existing resources and mitigants, the FI must develop measures or changes to its business strategy and cascade them internally for timely implementation. Strategic responses may touch products and services, processes and policies, market presence, customer engagement, organisational structure, IT infrastructure and resources.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
