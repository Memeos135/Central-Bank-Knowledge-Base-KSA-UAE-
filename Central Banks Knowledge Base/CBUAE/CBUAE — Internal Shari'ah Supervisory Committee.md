# Internal Shari'ah Supervisory Committee

Concept node for the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC) as used in the CBUAE recovery planning regulation and related definitions of Islamic Shari'ah Provisions (HSA resolutions and ISSC resolutions that must not contradict them). In the recovery planning context, an Islamic Financial Institution must additionally obtain ISSC approval confirming the recovery plan complies with Islamic Shari'ah Provisions, on top of Senior Management involvement, internal audit self-assessment and board approval. Binds Islamic Financial Institutions and Takaful insurers within the recovery planning governance chain.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
