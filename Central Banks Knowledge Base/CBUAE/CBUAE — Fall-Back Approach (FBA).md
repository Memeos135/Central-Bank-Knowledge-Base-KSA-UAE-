# Fall-Back Approach (FBA)

Node for the fall-back approach, the third and most conservative method in the CBUAE hierarchy for risk weighting banking-book equity investments in funds, applied where neither the look-through nor the mandate-based approach conditions can be satisfied. The supplied context frames it only by its position after the MBA calculation of the fund's average risk weight; the detailed treatment sits in the underlying standard. Applies to all UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
