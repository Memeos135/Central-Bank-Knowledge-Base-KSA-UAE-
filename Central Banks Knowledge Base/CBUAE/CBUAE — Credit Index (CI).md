# Credit Index (CI)

Definitional node from the CBUAE Model Management Standards/Guidance glossary setting out the Credit Index (CI) as a synthetic quantity derived from observable default rates (e.g. via probit transformation) representing a systemic driver of creditworthiness. It is the pivot concept linking macroeconomic forecasts to through-the-cycle transition matrices and forward point-in-time PD estimates. Applies to institutions interpreting and applying the MMS/MMG credit modelling requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
