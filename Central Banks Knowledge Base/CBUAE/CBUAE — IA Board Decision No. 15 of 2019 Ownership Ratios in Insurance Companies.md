# IA Board Decision No. 15 of 2019 Ownership Ratios in Insurance Companies

UAE Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 15 of 2019 issuing instructions on ownership ratios in the capital of insurance companies, including the regime for applications to acquire control. The excerpted articles cover the Authority's 30-day decision period on a control application, grounds for rejection (unmet conditions, harm to policyholders/company/sector, conflict of interest, competition and anti-monopoly concerns), and a 20-working-day grievance route to the Board. Binds prospective and existing shareholders seeking control in insurance companies, and the companies themselves.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
