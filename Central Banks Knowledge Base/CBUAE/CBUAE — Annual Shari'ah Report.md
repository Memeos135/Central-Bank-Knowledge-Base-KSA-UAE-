# Annual Shari'ah Report

Node covering periodic Shari'ah reporting and the three-lines-of-defence reporting architecture within a Takaful company's Shari'ah governance framework. It addresses how the internal Shari'ah audit division reports to the board, routes Shari'ah matters through the ISSC for resolution and then to the audit committee, and submits reports at least semi-annually; it also insulates Shari'ah audit staff from the senior management they audit on promotion, bonus, appraisal and termination decisions. Binds the Takaful company, its board, audit committee and ISSC.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Higher Shari'ah Authority (HSA)|Higher Shari'ah Authority (HSA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** In judging whether an Annual Shari'ah Report is adequate, the benchmark is external rather than internal policy: the report is issued by the ISSC but must meet the requirements of the applicable standard, which itself operates within the rules and standards set by the HSA. The company's obligation is to have governance policies and mechanisms that ascertain this compliance, so the board cannot treat issuance of the report as discharging the duty. Consequently, a report that departs from HSA-derived content or form expectations is a governance deficiency at company level, not merely an ISSC drafting matter.

### [[CBUAE — Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC)|Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat the Annual Shari'ah Report as the ISSC's own mandated output, not a management report the committee merely endorses. The governance framework requires the institution to have policies and mechanisms ensuring the report issued by the ISSC meets the applicable standard on content and form, so authorship, opinion and scope of assurance rest with the committee. For a decision on disclosure adequacy, check that the report reflects the ISSC's independent conclusion on the institution's Shari'ah compliance and satisfies the prescribed reporting requirements before it is published or filed.
