# Prioritization Parameters

Concept node covering how an IFI selects subjects for the SCF annual review plan. The Guidance Note expects the Board and senior management to approve the annual plan and secure cooperation from relevant department heads, and expects the institution to build a prioritization matrix based on review frequency, historical incidents, and product size and complexity. The appendix gives illustrative parameters, including review cycles for products, branches and distribution channels, early review of new products, and priority for areas with past systemic or major incidents.
