# Insurance Surplus (Article 20)

Concerns Insurance Surplus in takaful: amounts remaining in the Takaful Insurance Fund at financial year-end from contributions, investment returns and other revenues after settling all Fund obligations. Distribution must follow the approved surplus policy, be certified by the appointed actuary and receive prior CBUAE approval; a contingency provision may be retained, and up to 10% of surplus may be granted to the Company for sound management if provided in the Participation Membership Policy and decided when the surplus arises. Binds takaful/re-takaful companies and window operators, and shapes participants' entitlements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
