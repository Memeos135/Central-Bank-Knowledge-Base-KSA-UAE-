# Delta-Plus Method

An options treatment under the intermediate approach to market risk in CBUAE capital adequacy guidance: options enter the standardised calculation as delta-weighted positions (market value of the underlying times delta), with separate additional charges for gamma and vega risk. It is distinguished from the simplified approach for hedged and outright option positions, and specific risk is charged on the delta-equivalent. Binds UAE-licensed banks holding option exposures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
