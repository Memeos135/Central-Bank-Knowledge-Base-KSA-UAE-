# Takaful Insurance

A defined-term node for "Takaful Insurance" as used in the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies (Circular 24/2022) and related insurance instruments: a collective cooperative contractual arrangement in which participants contribute to a participants' account from which compensation is paid, managed and invested by the takaful company for a defined fee. The definition determines which companies fall into the Shari'ah-governance overlay (Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, Higher Shari'ah Authority oversight) on top of ordinary insurance corporate governance duties. It binds licensed insurance companies offering takaful products in the UAE, and their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
