# Recovery Options (Article 7)

Article 7 defines recovery options as capital, liquidity, restructuring and other measures a Financial Institution can deploy in response to actual or expected stress to restore or maintain viability. It requires the plan to set out a broad range of implementable options, an approach for selecting and sequencing them by stress circumstance, and processes for valuing and assessing marketability of Core Business Lines, operations and assets. Islamic Financial Institutions have an added ongoing obligation to monitor debt-based asset proportions in portfolios earmarked for sale so the sale remains permissible.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
