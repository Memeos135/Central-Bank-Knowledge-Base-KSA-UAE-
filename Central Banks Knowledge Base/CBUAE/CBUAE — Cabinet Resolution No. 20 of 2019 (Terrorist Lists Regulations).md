# Cabinet Resolution No. 20 of 2019 (Terrorist Lists Regulations)

Referenced instrument: the UAE regulation on terrorism lists and domestic implementation of UN Security Council resolutions concerning terrorism, terrorist financing and proliferation financing. It is cited as a legal basis for sector instructions (here, life insurance and Family Takaful), meaning listed-person screening and freezing duties sit alongside the AML/CTF due diligence regime. It binds entities subject to targeted financial sanctions obligations under UAE law and their supervisors.
