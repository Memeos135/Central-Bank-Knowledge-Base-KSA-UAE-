# Standardised Approach for Securitisation (SEC-SA)

Concept node for the Standardised Approach to securitisation exposures (SEC-SA) within the CBUAE capital hierarchy. It sits below the External Ratings-Based Approach (SEC-ERBA) for ordinary securitisations and is the starting point for resecuritisations, with a 1250% risk weight applying where neither approach can be used; internal ratings-based approaches are not permitted for UAE banks. Relevant to banks calculating banking-book securitisation capital; CBUAE instrument, not SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
