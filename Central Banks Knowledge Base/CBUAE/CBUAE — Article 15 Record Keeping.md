# Article 15: Record Keeping

Article 15 requires Insurance Brokers to retain all necessary records of Personal Data for ten years from receipt, unless other applicable law or the Central Bank requires otherwise, and to notify the Central Bank immediately of any material change affecting the accuracy or completeness of information given in a licensing application, with supporting information and documents. The notification duty falls on the Applicant or the Insurance Broker depending on the stage. UAE/CBUAE instrument; useful as a comparator to SAMA retention and change-notification duties.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, other
