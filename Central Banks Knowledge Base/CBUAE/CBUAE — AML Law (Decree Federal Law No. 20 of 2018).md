# AML Law (Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018)

A defined-term node in the CBUAE Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation (Circular 15/2021) that fixes what "AML Law" means for licensed payment actors — the UAE federal AML/CTF decree-law and its implementing Cabinet decision, together with related Central Bank instructions and guidance, as amended. It anchors the Regulation's substantive AML/CFT article, which obliges Payment Service Providers to apply risk-based preventive measures, maintain internal AML/CFT policies and controls, conduct enterprise-wide and relationship-level ML/FT risk assessments, periodically risk-profile Retail Payment Service Users, and report suspicious transactions to the Central Bank's Financial Intelligence unit without hiding behind banking or contractual secrecy. It binds all licensed Payment Service Providers (and, through the incorporation clause, tracks amendments to the underlying AML framework).

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
