# Board Decision 37/2017 - Points of Sale Licensing

UAE Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 37 of 2017 setting the licensing, registration and operating rules for Points of Sale established by insurance companies in the UAE. It limits permitted Point of Sale functions (issuing motor, health and travel policies, assessing vehicle damage, receiving claim documents), bars payment of compensation, and prescribes the initial-approval documentation and registration in the Authority's register. It binds insurance companies incorporated in the UAE and licensed foreign insurers operating through a branch or insurance agent, including Takaful companies.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk
