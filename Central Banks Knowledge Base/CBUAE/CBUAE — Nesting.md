# Nesting

Concept node within the CBUAE payment-sector AML/CFT guidance describing nesting: where a payment-sector participant obtains indirect access to banking or correspondent services through another institution's relationship, so the account bank has no visibility of the ultimate underlying customers or merchants. It explains why long, multi-party payment chains (merchant acquiring, payment aggregation, gateways, card scheme operators) raise the likelihood of nested relationships and of layered reliance on someone else's merchant due diligence. Relevant to licensed financial institutions that bank or serve other payment-sector participants, and instructive for KSA analysts assessing analogous downstream-access and KYB/reliance risk.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
