# Recovery Capacity (Article 9)

Article 9 defines recovery capacity as the maximum financial benefit achievable from the most effective and credible combination of recovery options across stress scenarios, distinguishing actual capacity (currently available options) from potential capacity (planned improvements or new options where concrete development steps exist). The plan must assess capacity in monetary terms and regulatory metrics, the contribution of each option, and implementation and benefit timelines, and must describe planned improvements. Minimum metrics are specified for Banks (CET1, leverage, LCR, NSFR, or eligible liquid asset and loans-to-stable-resources ratios) and for Insurance Companies (solvency, liquidity and earnings ratios).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
