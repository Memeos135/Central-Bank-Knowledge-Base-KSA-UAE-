# Insurance Group Supervision

Concept node for "insurance Group supervision" as applied under the CBUAE regulation: supervision exercised at the level of a Company incorporated in the State that is a Parent of at least one Company or of a foreign (re)insurer, or at the level of a Holding Company. The Central Bank may apply it only at the ultimate domestic parent level, includes all group companies and branches in scope, and may exclude entities whose risks are negligible or whose inclusion would be misleading. Group entities incorporated in the State must supply the Parent with the data needed to meet group obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
