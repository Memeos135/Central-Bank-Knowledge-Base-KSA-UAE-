# Unhosted Wallet

Defines the Unhosted Wallet (a wallet, address, smart contract or similar decentralised technology self-operated by an Originator or Beneficiary without third-party service provision beyond the technology itself) and sets the specific treatment of transfers to and from such wallets. A VASP must perform enhanced due diligence, request missing originator/beneficiary information and decline execution if it is not provided, and on inbound transfers consider obtaining missing data from the Beneficiary, delaying execution or returning the asset, guided by its risk assessment. Binds VASPs handling customer transfers involving self-hosted wallets; gas fees are out of scope.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
