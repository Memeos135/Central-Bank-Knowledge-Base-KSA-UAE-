# Circular No. 24/2019 (Registered Hawala Providers Regulation)

Concept node for CBUAE Circular No. 24/2019, the registration framework for hawala providers in the UAE, as referenced throughout the hawala guidance. It underpins the Hawala Provider Certificate, the obligation to manage the business personally rather than through UAE-based intermediaries (anti-nesting), and the requirement to hold a settlement account with a UAE bank and notify the CBUAE of its details. It binds hawala providers operating in the UAE and indirectly shapes how licensed financial institutions treat them as customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
