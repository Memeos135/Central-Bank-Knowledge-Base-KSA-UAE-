# Model Development

Concept node for the model development stage under the MMS, covering model design, specification, calibration and the documentation and data adequacy expectations attaching to it. It stresses suitability of calibration for statistical models using internal or external data, refuses lack of data as justification for shortfalls in material models (requiring interim mitigants instead), and warns against over-generalisation; it sits within the MMS scope covering risk, pricing, valuation and stress-testing models. Binds institutions subject to the MMS and their model development functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
