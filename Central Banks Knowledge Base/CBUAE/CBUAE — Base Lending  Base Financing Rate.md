# Base Lending / Base Financing Rate

Concept node on the Base Lending / base financing Rate under the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards: where such a rate forms part of the final interest/profit rate offered, the Licensed Financial Institution must disclose the rate's separate components (base rate plus a stated number of basis points), explain the concept with a clear example, state how often the rate may be revised, and say where rates are publicly posted. It sits alongside the obligations to disclose whether a Credit Product rate is fixed, variable or combined and to disclose all applicable Fees. Related material in Article 7 covers staff training, remuneration and suitability/affordability assessment for lending/financing.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
