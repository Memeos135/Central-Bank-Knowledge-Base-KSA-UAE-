# Governance Principle

## Why this community

Allocation of board and senior management responsibility for climate transition planning - who owns approval, oversight and internal accountability.

## How members connect

- Standalone governance principle within the climate transition planning framework; it names the accountable bodies that the other principles' obligations attach to.
- Interacts with the broader SAMA governance/risk regime (board oversight, risk appetite, internal control), so it should be read alongside general corporate governance requirements rather than in isolation.
