# Dirham Payment Token

The Dirham Payment Token is the token category referenced against AED and issued by a Dirham Payment Token Issuer under the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation; issuance requires a Central Bank licence. The Central Bank may cap the total volume or value of tokens such an issuer may sell or transfer, cap total customer numbers, or restrict new customer on-boarding. Means of Payment that are neither Dirham Payment Tokens nor Foreign Payment Tokens (including Algorithmic Stablecoins and Privacy Tokens) are subject to prohibitions on issuance, promotion and related services.
