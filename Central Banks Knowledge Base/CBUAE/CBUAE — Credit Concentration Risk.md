# Credit Concentration Risk

Concept node defining credit concentration risk as incremental credit loss caused by high correlation between the credit risk drivers of exposures, arising from name concentration, sectoral concentration or correlated obligor drivers even in apparently granular portfolios. Treated by the Central Bank as a key material Pillar 2 risk for all UAE banks, measured for ICAAP purposes on a broader basis than the regulatory large-exposure threshold, with a specific requirement to analyse real estate exposures. Binds banks in their ICAAP risk identification and Pillar 2 capital calibration.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
