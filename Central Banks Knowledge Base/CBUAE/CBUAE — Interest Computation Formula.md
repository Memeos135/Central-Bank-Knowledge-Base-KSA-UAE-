# Interest Computation Formula

Article 6 (with Appendix 1) of the original regulation prescribing how interest or profit is calculated on personal loans, car loans, overdrafts and unpaid credit card balances. It mandates a stated computation method, prohibits deducting interest up front from the loan amount by requiring reducing-balance calculation on the outstanding monthly balance, limits credit card interest to balances outstanding after the full-payment due date, and requires prepayment/top-up penalty rates and applicable interest rates to be declared in the published rate table. Binds banks and finance companies pricing retail credit.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
