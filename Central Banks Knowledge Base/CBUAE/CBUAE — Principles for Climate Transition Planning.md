# Principles for Climate Transition Planning

This node is the CBUAE/UAE Sustainable Finance Working Group's Principles for Climate Transition Planning, a bilingual guidance instrument setting out eight principles (objectives, governance, integration into strategy and risk management, metrics and targets, data and customer engagement, reporting and transparency, implementation, and review) for developing climate transition plans. It is expressed as expectations for 'financial firms' — UAE financial sector entities generally, whether new to transition planning or already producing plans — without distinguishing business models or licence categories. It is guidance rather than a binding rulebook, and is to be read alongside the SFWG Risk Management Principles and Disclosure Principles.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
