# Continuous Professional Development (CPD)

Sets the Continuous Professional Development obligations attaching to Authorized Individuals and Material Risk Takers, who must maintain current and sufficient knowledge, skills and experience for their positions. Licensees must provide or facilitate CPD access, ensure a default minimum of ten hours per calendar year unless other Regulations specify otherwise, and keep detailed participation records with supporting evidence available to the Central Bank on request. The Central Bank may set function-specific competency, qualification and programme requirements.
