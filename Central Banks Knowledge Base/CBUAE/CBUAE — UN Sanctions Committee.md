# UN Sanctions Committee

Defined-term node for "Sanctions Committee" under Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020: any UN Security Council committee established under its resolutions, including the 1267/1989 (ISIL and Al-Qaida), 1988 (Afghanistan) and 1718 (DPRK proliferation) committees. The node sits alongside related defined terms such as Listed Person, Ombudsperson and Focal Point, and is referenced in the procedure by which the Council proposes designations to the UN. It matters for distinguishing UN-level designations from Cabinet-issued Local Lists and for identifying the correct de-listing channel.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
