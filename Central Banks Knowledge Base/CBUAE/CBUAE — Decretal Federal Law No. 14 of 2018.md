# Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018

Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities is the primary enabling statute for CBUAE rulemaking, licensing and supervision. It is cited across CBUAE regulations (including the loan-based crowdfunding, payments and related instruments in this set) as the source of the Central Bank's power to issue and enforce those regulations. Open it to verify the legal basis, licensing perimeter and enforcement powers underpinning a specific CBUAE regulation.
