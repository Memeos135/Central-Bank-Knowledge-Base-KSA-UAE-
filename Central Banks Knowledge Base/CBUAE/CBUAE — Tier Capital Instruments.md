# Tier Capital Instruments

A concept node covering the composition and calculation of the tiers of regulatory capital (CET1, AT1, Tier 1, Tier 2, Total Capital), including how amounts issued by a parent and by subsidiaries to third parties are aggregated into consolidated capital. It also touches capital planning and stress-testing expectations, including management actions and Central Bank engagement before counting planned capital increases. Applies to banks in the UAE for capital adequacy and ICAAP purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
