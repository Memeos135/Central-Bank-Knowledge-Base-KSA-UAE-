# Correspondent Banking Red Flags

Explanatory and annex material describing how correspondent banking works (correspondent versus respondent institution, services provided under bilateral agreement, cross-border exposure) and setting out red flag indicators correspondent institutions should detect through continuous monitoring. It also sets best-practice expectations around the Wolfsberg Group 2023 Correspondent Banking Due Diligence Questionnaire as the industry standard for assessing a respondent's AML/CFT/CPF, sanctions, fraud and ABC controls. Directed at LFIs acting as correspondents, with expectations also articulated for respondent institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
