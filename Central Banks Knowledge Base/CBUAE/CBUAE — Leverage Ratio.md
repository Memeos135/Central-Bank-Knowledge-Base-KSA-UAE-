# Leverage Ratio

Node on the CBUAE leverage ratio, a simple, transparent, non-risk-based backstop that caps total exposure relative to capital and complements risk-based capital adequacy requirements. It sets out the exposure measure starting from total on-balance-sheet assets with prescribed adjustments, separate treatment of derivatives and securities financing transaction exposures, credit conversion factors for off-balance-sheet items, and gross treatment of collateral. Applies to banks licensed by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
