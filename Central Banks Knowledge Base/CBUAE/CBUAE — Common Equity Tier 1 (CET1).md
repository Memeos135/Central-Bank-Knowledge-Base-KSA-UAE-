# Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)

Concept node for Common Equity Tier 1, the highest-quality component of regulatory capital and the base for the leverage ratio's Tier 1 capital measure, buffer requirements and MDA restrictions. The context covers recognition of minority interest from common shares issued by fully consolidated subsidiaries (only where the instrument would qualify as common shares if issued by the bank and the issuing subsidiary is itself a bank), and the exclusion of profit-sharing investment accounts, investment risk reserves and profit equalisation reserves from Islamic banks' regulatory capital. Binds banks, including Islamic banks, calculating regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
