# Credit Risk Management Regulation

Parent node for the CBUAE Credit Risk Management Regulation, which sets the enforceable framework for how licensed financial institutions govern, underwrite, classify, provision for and mitigate credit risk across the full credit lifecycle. It binds LFIs (including branches of foreign banks, with limited head-office accommodations) and allocates ultimate accountability to the Board, with a CCO-led credit review function, an independent CRO-led credit risk management function holding veto power over proposals, and active roles for internal audit, compliance and external audit. Articles 1-18 cover definitions, governance, framework, oversight roles, underwriting, default, SICR, restructuring, classification/provisioning, mitigation, portfolio management, non-performing assets and write-off, models, counterparty liability, enforcement and interpretation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
