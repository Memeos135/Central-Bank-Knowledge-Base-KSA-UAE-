# General Provisions for Credit Products

General disclosure and conduct requirements for Credit/Financing Products under the CBUAE consumer protection framework, applied on top of the baseline provisions of the general section. Covers pre-contract disclosure of the expected Annual Percentage Rate, security/guarantor/collateral requirements and disposal rights, disclosure of how interest/profit is calculated at contracting, and rules on credit cards: no issuance, bundling or automatic limit increases without express Consumer consent, a fresh Al Etihad Credit Bureau check plus express consent before any limit increase, and notice with reasons for limit decreases or closures. Also regulates bundling (right to refuse bundled products, written explanation of benefits, Shari'ah compliance, and the credit insurance/takaful exception). Binds Licensed Financial Institutions offering credit or financing to Consumers in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
