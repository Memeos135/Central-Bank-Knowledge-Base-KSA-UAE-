# Acceptance Letter to Join Sandbox

Concept node for the acceptance letter that authorises entry into the insurance FinTech sandbox. It specifies the letter's mandatory content (approved solution summary, test period, approved tools, customer-protection measures), the Authority's right to suspend testing and withdraw the letter for breach of its conditions, and its evidentiary use with other financial services firms. Critically, the letter is not a licence to conduct the underlying business outside the sandbox and cannot be used to evade obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
