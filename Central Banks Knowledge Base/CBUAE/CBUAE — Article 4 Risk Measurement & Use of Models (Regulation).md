# Article 4: Risk Measurement & Use of Models (Regulation)

Article 4 of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (153/2018) requires each Bank to maintain risk measurement and monitoring systems proportionate to its risk profile, nature, size, structure and complexity, and requires the Board to have enough expertise to understand and oversee those systems, including any models used. Where models are used to measure risk components, the Bank must have internal development and approval processes and carry out regular independent validation and testing, with the Board remaining ultimately accountable even if model approval is delegated to management. The surrounding text also imposes immediate escalation of breaches of risk policies/limits and immediate notification to the Central Bank of significant deviations from the board-approved risk appetite statement or unaddressed material risks. Binds banks licensed by the CBUAE (and their boards/senior management).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
