# Correspondent Banking Red Flags

## Why this community

Indicators of suspicion arising in correspondent banking relationships, used to trigger enhanced scrutiny and suspicious transaction reporting.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in the graph: no internal edges, so its legal effect is understood only by reading it against the correspondent banking due diligence and monitoring cluster.
- Functions as a supervisory expectation feeding ongoing monitoring and escalation, rather than a standalone obligation.
