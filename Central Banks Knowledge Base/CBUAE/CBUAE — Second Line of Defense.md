# Second Line of Defense

Concept node covering the second line of defence (compliance function) in an LFI's AML/CFT control structure. It provides policy advice, guidance, assurance, oversight and challenge to the first line, oversees the investigations programme across automated and manual monitoring, and holds the exclusive authority for the actual filing of STRs/SARs through the Compliance Officer or MLRO — even where Financial Crime Operations units in the first line conduct the investigation. Applies to all CBUAE-licensed and supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
