# Mandate-Based Approach (MBA)

Defines the mandate-based approach, the second tier in the hierarchy for risk weighting banks' banking-book equity investments in funds where the conditions for the look-through approach are not met. It risk weights the fund's assets on the conservative assumption that the mandate is used to the maximum extent in the highest risk-weighted assets, with prescribed fallbacks for derivative replacement cost and potential future exposure, a 1.5 multiplier for CVA risk, and calculation of the fund's average risk weight. Binds all UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
