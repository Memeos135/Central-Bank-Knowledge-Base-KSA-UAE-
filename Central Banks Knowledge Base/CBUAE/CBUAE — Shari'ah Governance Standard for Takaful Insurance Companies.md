# Shari'ah Governance Standard for Takaful Insurance Companies

Node covering the CBUAE External Shari'ah Audit Standard for Takaful Insurance Companies and its interface with the separate Shari'ah Governance Standard for Takaful Insurance Companies. It sets minimum requirements for appointing and using an external Shari'ah audit firm — applying both where appointment is voluntary and where a regulatory requirement exists — and addresses the fallback where a company has no head of Internal Shari'ah Audit, allowing an approved external firm to perform that role for up to five years (subject to Central Bank and HSA approval) while prohibiting the same firm from also performing the external Shari'ah audit. It binds all incorporated Takaful Insurance Companies, including those with group/subsidiary relationships established in the UAE.
