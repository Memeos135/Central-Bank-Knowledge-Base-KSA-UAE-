# Capital Adequacy

Concept node on Capital Adequacy and solvency within the CBUAE ERM/ORSA standards for insurance companies, linking risk quantification (stress testing, scenario analysis, models) to capital and solvency assessment. It sets expectations that the ORSA report present the year-end and at least twelve quarters of projected solvency positions, analyse material risks (underwriting, investment, credit, operational, liquidity), incorporate climate-related financial risks, and compare actual versus planned capital outcomes. Binds insurance and Takaful companies subject to the Relevant Regulations and their financial regulations on solvency.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
