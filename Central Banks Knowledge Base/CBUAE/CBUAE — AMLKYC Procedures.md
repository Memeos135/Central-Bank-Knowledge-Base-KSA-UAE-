# AML/KYC Procedures

Cross-regime concept: CRS permits a Reporting Financial Institution to rely on information collected and maintained under its AML/KYC procedures for certain determinations, notably identifying Controlling Persons and (below stated thresholds) their residence. It illustrates the interaction between AML customer due diligence records and tax information-reporting classification, while leaving the CRS obligation itself with the institution.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other, data/credit info
