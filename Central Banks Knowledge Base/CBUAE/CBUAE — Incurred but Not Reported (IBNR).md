# Incurred but Not Reported (IBNR)

Technical provision for claims incurred but not reported, covering both claims not yet reported (IBNYR) and incomplete provisions for reported claims. The regulations require sufficient data (with management certifying completeness, appropriateness and accuracy), actuarial methods proportionate to size, scale and complexity, consistency of method year to year, and explanation to the Authority of any material methodology change; specialised portfolios (treaty/excess-of-loss accepted, credit guarantee) may need other methods. Binds Takaful and Re-Takaful companies, their management and appointed Actuary.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
