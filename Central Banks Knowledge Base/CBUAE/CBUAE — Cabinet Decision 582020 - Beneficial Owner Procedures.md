# Cabinet Decision 58/2020 - Beneficial Owner Procedures

This node covers Cabinet Decision 58/2020 on Beneficial Owner Procedures, which requires UAE-registered legal persons to identify, record in a Register, and report their beneficial owners (and nominee directors) to the relevant registrar, with limited exemptions for listed companies and their subsidiaries, and a fallback to a controlling individual where no 25% owner exists. In the CBUAE guidance context it frames what LFIs should expect from corporate customers: beneficial owners must be natural persons, ownership chains must be traced to individuals holding at least 25%, and customer statements alone cannot satisfy verification. It binds UAE legal persons directly and, indirectly, LFIs assessing customer credibility — a customer unaware of these obligations should be treated as at least high risk.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
