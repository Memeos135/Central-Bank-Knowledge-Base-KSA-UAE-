# Systems and Controls

The systems and controls component of the CBUAE market risk regulation, requiring banks' market risk measurement, monitoring and control systems to support continuous capital adequacy and adherence to intra-day and other limits. It mandates at least annual independent internal review of the measurement system and overall market risk management process by functionally independent and competent staff, timely capture of all transactions, internal validation of models by parties independent of model development, periodic and as-needed external validation, and a back-testing programme comparing model outputs with realised trading results. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE, with duties falling on the Board, Senior Management and internal audit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
