# International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)

Concept node for the use of International Financial Reporting Standards as the mandatory accounting basis under the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies. It fixes IFRS (with IASB/IFRIC interpretations) plus the Authority's own accounting policies and prescribed forms as the basis for preparing and disclosing financial statements, and sets out the required content of notes to the accounts. It binds licensed insurance companies, including those writing both persons/funds accumulation and property/liability business (which must produce separate and consolidated statements).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Financial Reporting|Financial Reporting]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For any question about the correct accounting or disclosure basis, the answer is that IFRS is incorporated by reference into the supervisory financial reporting obligation, not applied as a purely voluntary professional standard. The financial reporting requirement obliges regulated banks to keep records and prepare statements in accordance with IFRS as issued by the IASB together with the regulator's own instructions, with external audit opinion on published annual statements. The consequence is a layered test: IFRS compliance alone is insufficient where supervisory instructions impose additional or stricter treatment, and a departure from IFRS is simultaneously a regulatory breach.
