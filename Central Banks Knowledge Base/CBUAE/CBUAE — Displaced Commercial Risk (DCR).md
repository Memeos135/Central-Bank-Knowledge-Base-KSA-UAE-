# Displaced Commercial Risk (DCR)

Displaced Commercial Risk is the exposure arising where an Islamic Bank's return on assets financed by Investment Account Holders' funds underperforms competitors' returns, pressuring the bank to support payouts. The Standard requires Islamic Banks to develop, apply systematically and regularly review a sound methodology to identify, monitor, measure and report DCR impact and the mitigation amount, with changes justified and approved at the appropriate management level and by the Board where applicable; permitted mitigants include IRR, PER, forgoing Mudarib profit, and Hibah transfers, alone or combined.
