# Bank Loans & Other Services Regulation (VER1)

CBUAE Regulations No. 29/2011 (23/02/2011) on bank loans and other services offered to individual (retail) customers, issued under the 1980 Union Law on the Central Bank and addressed to all banks and finance companies. It defines retail lending terms and imposes conduct and affordability limits — e.g. a 50% gross salary deduction cap on instalments, scheduling so that only 30% of income or pension is deducted where repayment extends past retirement age, a cap on security cheques at 120% of loan value, and special rules for armed forces staff including a prohibition on taking or copying military ID cards. Binds conventional and Islamic banks and finance companies in their dealings with individual customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
