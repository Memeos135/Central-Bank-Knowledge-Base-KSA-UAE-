# Sovereign Exposures and Public Sector Entities

Sets the exemptions and special limits for sovereign and public sector counterparties: exposures to the UAE Federal Government, the CBUAE, and foreign sovereigns rated at least AA- (and their central banks) are exempt from the large exposure limit, and exposures guaranteed or secured by instruments issued by these counterparties are similarly treated. Emirates (local) government exposures carry a 150% aggregate Tier 1 cap including their non-commercial PSEs (25% individual for those PSEs), while commercial government entities face 25% individual / 100% aggregate caps. Self-sustainable commercial PSEs may, with Central Bank approval, be treated as separate counterparties subject only to the general limit; the annexed table summarises all limits, including interbank and intragroup branch exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
