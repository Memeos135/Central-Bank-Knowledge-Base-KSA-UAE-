# Technical Provisions Calculation

Section 3 of the Financial Regulations, prescribing the types and calculation basis of technical provisions: unearned premium and unexpired risk reserves, outstanding loss and IBNR reserves, allocated and unallocated loss adjustment expense reserves, and mathematical reserves, together with rules on reinsurance credit and on holding matching investments inside the UAE gross or net of reinsurance. It binds insurance companies and imposes duties on the Board to appoint a registered Actuary who reviews and approves provisions, assesses data quality, is professionally liable for advice, and reports annually to the supervisor. Relevant to reserving adequacy, actuarial sign-off and localisation of assets backing liabilities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
