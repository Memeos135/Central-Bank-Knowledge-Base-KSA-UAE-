# Article 13: Financial Reporting

Requires Insurance Brokers to keep appropriate records and prepare financial statements under IFRS and Central Bank Regulations, and to file within 90 days of financial year-end a detailed activity report signed by the Insurance Broker's Representatives plus audited annual financial reports with the external auditor's report. Adjacent disclosure duties to Clients (remuneration basis, cancellation rights, brokerage agreements, conflicts of interest) and the quarterly reporting regime (reviewed interim statements, debt/credit accounts, company-by-company premiums and remuneration, complaints statistics, quarterly activity report within 30 days, and immediate notification of Matters of Significance) appear in the same context.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
