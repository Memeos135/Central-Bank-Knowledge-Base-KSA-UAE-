# International Standards on Auditing (ISA)

A concept node for the International Standards on Auditing as incorporated by reference into the CBUAE Financial Reporting and External Audit Regulation (Circular 162/2018) applicable to banks. It fixes the methodology the external auditor must follow — a risk- and materiality-based approach to planning and performing the audit — alongside related duties on auditor appointment, rotation (firm and engagement partner), audit committee oversight, and prohibition of objectivity-impairing non-audit services. It binds banks in their engagement of external auditors and, indirectly, the approved auditors themselves.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
