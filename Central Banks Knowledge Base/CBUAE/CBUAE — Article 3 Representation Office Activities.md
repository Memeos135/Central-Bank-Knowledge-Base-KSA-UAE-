# Article 3: Representation Office Activities

Article 3 of the Representation Offices Regulation defines the closed list of activities a representation office may carry out — market and economic research on the UAE insurance sector, gathering and supplying information and clarifications to the foreign (re)insurer, its clients and third parties, supporting local parties active in the foreign company's home markets, and maintaining relationships and following the parent's interests with clients outside the State. The permitted-activity boundary is enforced through the surrounding provisions on conduct of business, continuous commitments (approved premises, no other business at the premises, notification of status changes, data provision on request), naming requirements, and grounds for licence cancellation. Binds the representation office and its staff.
