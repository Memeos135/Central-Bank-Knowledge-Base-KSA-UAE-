# IA BOD Resolution No. 6 of 2010 - Surveyors and Loss Adjusters Regulations

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 6 of 2010 setting the regulatory framework for Surveyors and Loss Adjusters in the UAE: definition of the activity (investigating causes of damage, assessing coverage, determining payable compensation), the mandatory Register maintained by the Authority, personal and non-transferable registration, registration requirements and grounds for cancellation. It binds natural and juridical persons practising surveying and loss adjustment, and also constrains insurance companies, which may not engage unregistered surveyors except in defined special-expertise cases notified to the Director General.
