# HSA Resolution No. 76/3/2019 on Early Settlement

A cross-reference node to Higher Shari'ah Authority Resolution No. 76/3/2019 on early settlement, as applied through the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards. The effect is that Licensed Financial Institutions may impose Early Settlement Fees only to the extent of actual costs incurred from the early settlement process, consistent with the HSA resolution, and must reflect this in product disclosure statements; related provisions address waiver of part of outstanding debt and permissible limits of debt/liability that IFIs may retain. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, with specific relevance to Islamic financing.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
