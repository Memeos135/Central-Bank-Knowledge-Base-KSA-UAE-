# Registration (Article 5)

Article 5 of the CBUAE Insurance Company Licensing Regulation sets the Register entry requirements for licensed insurance Companies: application on the Central Bank's prescribed form with proof of fee payment, a one-year registration term, and annual renewal at least one month before expiry. It also obliges the Company to notify changes to registration data or attached documents via an endorsement application within fifteen business days, and to display its name, registration number and registration date on all publications, contracts, correspondence, notices, certificates and Insurance Policies. It binds all licensed Companies, including branches of Foreign Insurance Companies.
