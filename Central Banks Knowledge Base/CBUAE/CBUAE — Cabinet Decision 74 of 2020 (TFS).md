# Cabinet Decision 74 of 2020 (TFS)

A referenced UAE instrument (Cabinet Decision/Resolution No. 74 of 2020) that establishes the national framework for Targeted Financial Sanctions, covering the UN Consolidated List and the UAE Local Terrorist List and the prompt implementation of UNSC Chapter VII designations. In the surrounding CBUAE guidance it is cited as the source of licensed financial institutions' freezing and screening duties, including the Article 21 expectation that firms maintain suitable risk management arrangements. It binds CBUAE-supervised entities (banks and foreign bank branches, exchange houses, finance companies, payment service providers, registered hawala providers, and insurance/reinsurance firms, agents and brokers) alongside their AML/CFT statutory obligations.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
