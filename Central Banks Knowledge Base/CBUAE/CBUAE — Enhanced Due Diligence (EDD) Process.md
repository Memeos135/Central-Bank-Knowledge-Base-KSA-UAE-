# Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Process

Describes Enhanced Due Diligence as the highest KYC tier under the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business: additional information on source of funds, nature of business and purpose of transaction, plus more intensive monitoring and acceptance controls, applied in addition to CDD. Triggered by value thresholds for natural persons (e.g. foreign exchange or outward transfers at or above AED 55,000, aggregated over defined day windows) and automatically for legal persons/arrangements, counterparties, PEPs, DNFBPs/DPMS, high-risk customers or circumstances, and third-party transactions. Binds Licensed Persons.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

### [[CBUAE — KYC Process|KYC Process]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** EDD is not a separate regime but the highest tier of the same KYC obligation chain, which runs from Customer Identification to Customer Due Diligence to Enhanced Due Diligence. That structure means EDD is triggered by risk factors identified during the earlier stages and presupposes that CID and CDD have been properly completed; it cannot substitute for them. For a decision, a file that applies EDD measures without a sound CID/CDD foundation, or that stops at CDD where higher-risk indicators are present, would not satisfy the KYC requirement.
