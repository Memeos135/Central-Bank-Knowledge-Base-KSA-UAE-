# Law Enforcement Info Exchange

## Why this community

Cross-border and inter-agency exchange of law enforcement information in the AML/CTF cooperation chapter, i.e. the legal gateway allowing competent authorities to share intelligence and case information with counterparts.

## How members connect

- Single-article community sitting in the international cooperation block that runs alongside judicial cooperation and foreign-judgment provisions.
- Operates at the authority-to-authority level (law enforcement/competent authority), distinct from formal judicial channels and from FI reporting duties.
- Relevant to supervised entities only indirectly: information they report can lawfully travel abroad through this gateway.
