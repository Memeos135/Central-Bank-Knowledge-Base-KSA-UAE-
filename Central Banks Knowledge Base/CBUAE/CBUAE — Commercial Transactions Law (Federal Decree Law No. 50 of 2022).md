# Commercial Transactions Law (Federal Decree Law No. 50 of 2022)

A defined-term node for UAE Federal Decree Law No. 50 of 2022 (Commercial Transactions Law), referenced in the CBUAE External Shari'ah Audit Standard as part of the definition of compliance with Islamic Shari'ah Provisions. Section Six of Book Three of that Law, and its implementing bylaws, form the first tiers of the Shari'ah compliance reference hierarchy, ahead of Higher Shari'ah Authority and ISSC resolutions. Relevant to Islamic financial institutions and their Shari'ah auditors in the UAE.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
