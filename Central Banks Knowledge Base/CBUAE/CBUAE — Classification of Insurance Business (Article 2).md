# Classification of Insurance Business (Article 2)

Article 2 of the CBUAE Insurance Company Licensing Regulation, which classifies insurance business into categories and enumerates the classes of property and liability insurance (fire, transport, marine and aviation hull, satellites, railways, land vehicles, engineering, oil, health, and miscellaneous accident lines such as personal accident, fidelity guarantee, cash/securities, theft, glass, professional indemnity, workers' accidents and employer liability, agriculture and livestock). It determines the scope of activity a licence may authorise and therefore what an applicant or licensee may write.
