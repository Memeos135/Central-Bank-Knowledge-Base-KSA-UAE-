# Settlement Report Contents

Sets the minimum mandatory content of the settlement (loss adjustment) report prepared by a Surveyor and Loss Adjuster, including identification of insured/beneficiary, the requesting party, policy terms, causes and quantification of damage, use of outsourced specialists, and the technical opinion on coverage and payable compensation. Adjacent provisions impose record-keeping duties and general conduct obligations (acting within registration classification, integrity and transparency, informing affected parties, assessing insurer liability under the policy). Binds registered surveyors and loss adjusters when handling claims for insurers and claimants.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection
