# Compliance & Internal Audit Controls

## Why this community

Internal governance architecture requiring banks to maintain an independent compliance function and an independent internal audit function within a defined internal control system.

## How members connect

- Regulation sets the binding requirement; the accompanying standards specify mandate, independence, reporting lines and board/audit-committee access for each function.
- The three lines of defence model is the organising concept that allocates the compliance article (second line) and internal audit article (third line) and prevents overlap of assurance duties.
- 'Internal Control' is the defined umbrella term that fixes the scope both functions must cover, so a gap in the control system is simultaneously a compliance and an audit finding.
- This cluster is the accountability layer other regimes (outsourcing, risk, conduct) rely on for evidence of ongoing supervision and independent challenge.
