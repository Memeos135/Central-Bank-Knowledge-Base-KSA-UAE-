# Claims on Banks

Node defining the bank exposure class, covering interbank claims, nostro accounts, certificates of deposit issued by banks and repurchase agreements. Unrated bank claims attract 50% (long term) and 20% (short term) risk weights, subject to a floor that no unrated bank claim may be weighted below the claim on its sovereign of incorporation; intragroup exposures follow the external rating of the counterparty entity. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks calculating risk-weighted assets on bank counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
