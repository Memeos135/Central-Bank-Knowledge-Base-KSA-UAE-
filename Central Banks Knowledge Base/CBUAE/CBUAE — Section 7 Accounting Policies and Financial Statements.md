# Section 7: Accounting Policies and Financial Statements

Section 7 of the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies governs the accounting policies to be adopted and the forms used to prepare and present reports and financial statements. It requires preparation under IFRS plus the Authority's policies and prescribed forms, separate plus consolidated statements where a company writes both Insurance of Persons/Fund Accumulation and Property and Liability business, bilingual annual submissions (Arabic and English) and Arabic quarterly submissions, and preserves the regulator's right to amend the prescribed forms. It binds licensed insurance companies, including UAE operations of foreign branches and related companies.
