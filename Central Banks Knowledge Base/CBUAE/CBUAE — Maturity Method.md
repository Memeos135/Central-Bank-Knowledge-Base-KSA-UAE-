# Maturity Method

Concept node for the maturity-based approach to measuring interest rate risk in the trading book, in which positions are slotted into time bands and zones with vertical and horizontal disallowance factors, and for related maturity definitions used elsewhere in the capital framework (maturity mismatch of hedges, and weighted-average versus legal maturity for securitisation tranches). It sets the conservative conventions banks must use when defining effective maturity of exposures and credit protection. Applies to banks computing risk-weighted assets and market risk capital under CBUAE standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
