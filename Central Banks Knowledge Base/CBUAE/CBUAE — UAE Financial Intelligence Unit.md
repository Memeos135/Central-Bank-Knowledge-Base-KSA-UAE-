# UAE Financial Intelligence Unit

A concept node referencing the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit as the recipient of suspicious activity reporting arising from trade-based financial crime detection. The surrounding guidance frames LFIs as uniquely placed to spot trade-based money laundering and illicit transshipment across fragmented trade transactions and to report them so law enforcement can connect the dots. Relevant to LFIs determining when and how trade-related suspicions must be escalated to the FIU.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
