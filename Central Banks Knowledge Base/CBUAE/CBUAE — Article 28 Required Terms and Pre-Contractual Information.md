# Article 28: Required Terms and Pre-Contractual Information

Article 28 lists the minimum terms and pre-contractual information that a Payment Token Service Provider (Licensee or Registree, excluding Registered Foreign Payment Token Issuers) must include in and provide with its Customer Agreement before any service is supplied. Content includes the fee, charge and commission schedule (redemption fees, conversion rates, withdrawal charges), provider and agent identification, consent and consent-withdrawal procedures for transfers, communication channels, safeguarding arrangements, notification timelines and liability allocation for unauthorised or incorrectly executed transfers, change-acceptance and termination terms, service levels and complaints procedures.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments
