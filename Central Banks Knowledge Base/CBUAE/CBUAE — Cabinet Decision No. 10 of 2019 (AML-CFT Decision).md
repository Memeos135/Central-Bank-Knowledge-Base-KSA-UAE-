# Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 (AML-CFT Decision)

Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 is the Implementing Regulation of the UAE AML-CFT Law, referenced as the detailed rulebook with which exchange houses' AML/CFT policies, procedures, CDD, monitoring, reporting and record-keeping must comply. In this node it appears as the governing implementing instrument underpinning the CBUAE Standards' AML/CFT chapter rather than as reproduced text. It binds supervised financial institutions and DNFBPs, including Licensed Persons carrying on exchange business.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
