# Consumer Education and Awareness

Article 9 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation on Consumer education and awareness, grounded in Article 121/2 of the Decretal Law requiring the Central Bank and Licensed Financial Institutions to jointly raise public awareness of banking services, financial products and their inherent risks. It places responsibility on institutions to improve Consumers' financial literacy and ability to assess products, as a risk-mitigation element of the consumer protection framework. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions; also frames a shared Central Bank role.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
