# Higher Shari'ah Authority

Concept node for the Higher Shari'ah Authority, the central Shari'ah body whose resolutions and requirements sit alongside the Central Bank's own rules for Islamic financial institutions and Takaful Companies. It appears in the corporate governance and disclosure context as the source of binding Shari'ah rulings, the reference point for annual Shari'ah reports, and a co-setter of requirements for a Takaful Company's internal Shari'ah governance framework. It binds boards of Takaful/Islamic institutions, which must demonstrate full Shari'ah compliance and disclose compliance with HSA resolutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee|Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Where an internal Shari'ah supervision committee has ruled on a product or practice, that ruling does not sit at the top of the chain. Each Islamic financial institution must constitute such a committee, but its determinations operate within, and are subordinate to, the resolutions of the Higher Shari'ah Authority. The decision consequence is hierarchical: an internal committee opinion inconsistent with an Authority resolution cannot be relied on as a compliance defence, and the higher-level resolution should be verified before acting.
