# Additional Tier 1 (AT1)

Concept node for Additional Tier 1 capital: the eligibility criteria for AT1 instruments (including no features hindering recapitalisation, no bank-funded purchase, SPV proceeds availability for Islamic banks, and loss absorbency at the point of non-viability), treatment of related share premium, and recognition limits for Tier 1 issued by consolidated subsidiaries to third parties. It interacts with CET1 recognition rules and feeds the Tier 1 capital measure used in the leverage ratio. Binds banks issuing or classifying capital instruments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
