# Institutional Oversight

Article 3 of the Consumer Protection Regulation, setting the institutional oversight requirement: Licensed Financial Institutions must have robust management oversight and responsibility structures across product design, development, promotion, sales and distribution, plus compliance, risk and audit controls, current policies, procedures and training, and qualified staff. It places an explicit duty on the board and senior management to instil a consumer-focused culture and to actively monitor, identify and address misconduct and market conduct risk. Binds all Licensed Financial Institutions and their governing bodies.
