# Article 6: Information Systems and Internal Reporting (Standards)

Article 6 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards (issued under Notice 153/2018) sets requirements for risk data aggregation, management information systems and internal risk reporting supporting a bank's comprehensive approach to risk management. It sits alongside the surrounding provisions on stress testing, bank-wide/group-wide consistency of risk identification and the feed of stress results into ICAAP and contingency funding. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE and is mandatory and enforceable in the same manner as the Risk Management Regulation, with the board ultimately accountable.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
