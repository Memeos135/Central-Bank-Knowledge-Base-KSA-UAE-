# Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP)

Defines the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process requirement in the CBUAE Risk Management Standards: a bank must maintain a formal documented process for assessing overall capital adequacy against its risk profile, plus a strategy to hold capital above regulatory minima, documented and submitted annually to the Central Bank as the ICAAP Report. It lists the elements the ICAAP must demonstrate — board and senior management oversight, a sound capital assessment process, comprehensive assessment of credit, market, operational, interest rate, concentration, liquidity and other risks, monitoring and reporting of exposures and capital needs, and internal control review. The ICAAP must explicitly incorporate all material risks identified through the bank's risk management approach, with stress test results feeding capital and liquidity planning.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
