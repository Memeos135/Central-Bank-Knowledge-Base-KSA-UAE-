# Article 26: Payment Token White Papers

Article 26 governs the payment token White Paper: the mandatory disclosure document a Payment Token Issuer must publish, including due diligence on the underlying distributed ledger, the point at which lawful power of disposal passes between Tokenholders on a transfer, and risk warnings about failed, reversed or delayed transfers and redemption/compensation routes. The Central Bank may exempt an issuer from one or more of these requirements where equivalent documentation has been published or equivalent obligations complied with under SCA or Local Licensing Authority rules.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, other
