# Beneficial Ownership Identification

Node on beneficial ownership identification and verification under Article 9 of the AML-CFT Decision and the AML-CFT Law definition of beneficial owner, as explained in CBUAE CDD/KYC guidance. It sets the cascade for legal-person customers: identify natural persons with a controlling ownership interest of 25 percent or more, and where none exists identify those exercising control by other means; it also links to Cabinet Decision 58 on Beneficial Owner Procedures and permits documentary, non-documentary or digital identification methods. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions onboarding and reviewing legal persons and legal arrangements.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info
