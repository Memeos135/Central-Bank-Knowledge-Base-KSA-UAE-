# Large Value Payment Systems Regulation

CBUAE Circular 9/2020 (effective January 2021) issuing the Large Value Payment Systems Regulation, addressed to all System Operators and Settlement Institutions of licensed and/or designated large-value payment systems in the UAE. It sets the licensing requirements for operating an LVPS in the State and the ongoing obligations applying to a designated LVPS, including compliance with the PFMI and provisions of the Central Bank Law on netting, insolvency and preservation of rights. Note this is a UAE (CBUAE) instrument, not a SAMA rule, and is relevant for cross-border or comparative analysis only.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
