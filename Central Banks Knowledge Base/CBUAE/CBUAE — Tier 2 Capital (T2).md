# Tier 2 Capital (T2)

Concept node for Tier 2 capital as defined within the CBUAE capital adequacy standards, setting out which elements count as the supplementary (going/gone-concern) layer of the regulatory capital base — notably general provisions or general loan loss reserves capped by reference to credit risk-weighted assets, qualifying Tier 2 instruments not already counted in Tier 1, and related share premium. It also ties into the treatment of Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruments issued to third-party investors by fully consolidated subsidiaries, including the surplus-capital deduction mechanics. Binds banks licensed in the UAE when computing eligible capital and capital adequacy ratios on a solo and consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
