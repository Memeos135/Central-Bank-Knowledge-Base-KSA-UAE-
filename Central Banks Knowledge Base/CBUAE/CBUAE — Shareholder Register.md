# Shareholder Register

Article 2 of the CBUAE Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation makes the board responsible for establishing and maintaining an up-to-date shareholder register and written records of all shareholders and their shareholdings, supported by policies and procedures to identify and record Significant and Controlling Shareholders. It is paired here with Article 3's prior written approval requirements for obtaining, increasing or divesting shareholdings at or above 5%, and Article 8's reporting and divestment consequences. Binds bank boards and, indirectly, shareholders seeking to transfer interests.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
