# BCBS Principles for Climate-related Financial Risks (June 2022)

This node identifies the Basel Committee's Principles for the effective management and supervision of climate-related financial risks (June 2022) as a reference listed in the Annex to the CBUAE climate-related financial risk instrument. It is a non-binding international standard used as the conceptual source for the CBUAE principles rather than a directly enforceable UAE obligation; the binding expectations sit in the CBUAE principles themselves. Useful for tracing the provenance and intended interpretation of the local governance, risk management and scenario analysis principles.
