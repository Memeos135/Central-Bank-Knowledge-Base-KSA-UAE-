# Fund and Investment Transfer

## Why this community

Movement of monies between the participants' (takaful) fund and the shareholders'/investment account — when transfers are permitted and on what conditions.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster governing fund segregation, the structural principle underlying the takaful model.
- Directly constrains the solvency and surplus-distribution analysis: transfers change the assets available to each fund.
- Any permitted transfer is expected to be visible through the disclosure obligations, linking this article to the reporting cluster.
