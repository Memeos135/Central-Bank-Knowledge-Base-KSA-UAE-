# Claims on Public Sector Entities

Node on the public sector entity exposure class, distinguishing non-commercial PSEs (administrative bodies and non-commercial undertakings of the UAE federal, emirate or local governments) from commercial government-related entities. Non-commercial PSEs do not automatically get sovereign treatment; preferential treatment is limited to UAE entities, requires satisfaction of all seven classification criteria and CBUAE approval, with exceptional sovereign-equivalent treatment only where formal arrangements remove the distinction from sovereign risk. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks classifying and risk-weighting PSE counterparties, with internal audit/compliance expected to review classification periodically.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
