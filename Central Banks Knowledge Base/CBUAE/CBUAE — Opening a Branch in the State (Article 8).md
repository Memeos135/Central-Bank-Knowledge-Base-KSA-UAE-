# Opening a Branch in the State (Article 8)

Article 8 sets the approval process for an insurance Company opening a Branch within the UAE: an application on the Central Bank's prescribed form supported by a Board resolution (or a decision of the Authorized Manager for a branch of a Foreign Insurance Company), an economic feasibility study and work plan, the Branch organizational structure, and the names of the Branch manager and senior officers. It binds both Companies established in the State and UAE branches of Foreign Insurance Companies, and sits alongside the Article 7 regime for branches outside the State.
