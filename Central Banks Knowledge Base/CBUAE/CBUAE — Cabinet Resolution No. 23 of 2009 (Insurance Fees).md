# Cabinet Resolution No. 23 of 2009 (Insurance Fees)

A referenced UAE Cabinet instrument on fees for insurance supervision, control and insurance transactions; it appears here only as a cited authority in the surrounding text, which is Cabinet Resolution No. 13 of 2013 setting licence/registration fees and administrative fines for third party health insurance administrators. The operative fee schedule and delay penalties in the provided context bind TPAs licensed by the Insurance Authority and are collected in the manner set by the Ministry of Finance. Useful only for fee/penalty quantum and the legal basis chain, not for conduct or prudential obligations.
