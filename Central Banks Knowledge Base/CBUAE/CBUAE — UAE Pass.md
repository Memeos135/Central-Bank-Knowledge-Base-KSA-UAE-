# UAE Pass

Node covering UAE Pass, the UAE national digital identity and digital signature solution, as referenced in CBUAE digital ID and AML/CFT guidance. It is presented as a national-level identification system LFIs should understand and may use for remote identification, e-KYC verification of identity evidence, and digital signing, alongside the Federal Authority validation gateway. Relevant to LFIs designing non-face-to-face onboarding and identity verification consistent with CDD requirements.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
