# Wolfsberg Guidance on Sanctions Screening

A referenced industry standard (Wolfsberg guidance on sanctions screening) cited in the CBUAE guidance's treatment of name and customer screening, screening operations, list maintenance and evasion detection. It informs expectations on the ownership/control (50%) rule, fuzzy matching, weak aliases and false-positive resolution, but the binding obligations on LFIs come from Cabinet Decision 74 and the AML-CFT framework, not the industry paper. Useful for benchmarking screening programme design expectations at policy level.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, governance/risk, AML/CTF
