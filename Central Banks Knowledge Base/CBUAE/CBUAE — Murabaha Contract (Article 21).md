# Murabaha Contract (Article 21)

Article 21 of CBUAE Regulation 1/2026 governs the murabaha contract, placed in Chapter Four on Islamic banking financing alongside instalment sale (Art. 20), istisna'a (Art. 22), salam (Art. 23) and ijarah (Art. 24). It should be read with the general contract rules of Chapter One and the promise/wa'd provisions of Articles 7-9, which matter for purchase-order murabaha structures. It binds Islamic financial institutions offering murabaha-based financing in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
