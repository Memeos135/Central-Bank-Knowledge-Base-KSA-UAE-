# Financial Regulations for Insurance/Takaful Companies

Defined-term node for the "Financial Regulations", meaning Insurance Authority Board Decisions No. 25 of 2014 (Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies) and No. 26 of 2014 (Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies), as applicable. It is the baseline prudential framework whose Chapter Two solvency requirements Decision 14 of 2018 extends to branches of foreign insurers, and it sits alongside the related definitions of Branch of the foreign company, Parent company and Home country. Relevant to insurers, Takaful operators, reinsurers and their UAE branches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
