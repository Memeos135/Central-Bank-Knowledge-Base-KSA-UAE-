# Ownership and Management Structure Risk

Concept node on how a respondent institution's legal form, ownership transparency and executive management composition affect ML/TF/PF risk — state-owned, publicly listed versus privately held, opaque structures, UBOs or senior managers in high-risk jurisdictions, and PEPs in ownership or management. It directs correspondent institutions to document beneficial ownership and management structures before onboarding and throughout the relationship, and identifies PEP presence as an EDD trigger. Binds CBUAE-supervised LFIs providing correspondent services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
