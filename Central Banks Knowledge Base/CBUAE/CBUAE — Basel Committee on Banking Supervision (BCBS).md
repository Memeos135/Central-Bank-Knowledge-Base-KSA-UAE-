# Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS)

A concept node for the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision as the international standard-setter whose frameworks underpin the local capital rules discussed here, notably the leverage ratio as a non-risk-based backstop to risk-based capital and the CVA capital charge introduced to close the Basel II gap on derivative fair-value credit losses. It provides background rationale rather than direct obligations, but frames how Central Bank capital requirements are to be read as a mutually reinforcing package binding banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
