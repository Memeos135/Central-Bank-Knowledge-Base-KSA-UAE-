# Reliance on a Third Party

Consolidated guidance (across two CBUAE AML/CFT guidance instruments) on reliance on a third party for all or part of the CDD process, including introduced business, under Article 19 of the AML-CFT Decision. Reliance is permitted only where the third party is regulated and supervised and applies compliant CDD and record-keeping, and where identification data and documentation can be obtained from it without delay; the relying FI retains ultimate responsibility for CDD outcomes, customer risk classification and ongoing monitoring. It binds FIs that outsource or rely on introducers, group entities or other regulated parties for customer identification and beneficial ownership verification.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
