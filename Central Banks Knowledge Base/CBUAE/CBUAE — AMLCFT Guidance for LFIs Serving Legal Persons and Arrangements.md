# AML/CFT Guidance for LFIs Serving Legal Persons and Arrangements

CBUAE AML/CFT guidance for Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs) on customers that are legal persons and legal arrangements (UAE regime, not SAMA). It explains the risk that corporate and trust structures conceal controlling or benefiting natural persons, and sets out CDD information to be collected and verified under Article 8(b) of the AML-CFT Decision — name, legal form, memorandum and articles of association, head office/principal place of business, local legal representative for foreign entities, and senior management — plus verification and CDD on the natural person authorised to act for the customer under Article 8(a). Binds LFIs and must be integrated into the wider AML/CFT compliance programme with supporting governance and training; risk-based differentiation between entity types is expected.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
