# Takaful Insurance External Shari'ah Audit (Article 6)

A CBUAE instrument section addressing external audit and Shari'ah audit arrangements for Takaful insurance companies, with the excerpted text setting out the Board audit committee's duties to appoint, monitor and annually assess the External Auditor. It binds the Board and its audit committee, Senior Management and the engaged audit firm (including affiliates and subsidiaries), covering audit quality review, private meetings without Senior Management, escalation of matters affecting regulatory capital or disclosures, and an approved policy restricting non-audit services. Relevant when assessing auditor independence, engagement scoping or audit-committee governance obligations of a Takaful/insurance company.
