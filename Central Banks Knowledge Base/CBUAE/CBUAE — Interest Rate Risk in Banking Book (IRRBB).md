# Interest Rate Risk in Banking Book (IRRBB)

Concept node for interest rate risk in the banking book (and its Islamic-banking analogue, profit rate risk in the banking book), defined as the risk of loss in the banking book from interest rate changes and addressed under Pillar 2 rather than the Pillar 1 market risk framework. Requires a Board-approved IRRBB risk appetite, supporting policies and a full identification-measurement-reporting-monitoring-mitigation process, with measurement referencing the ICAAP Standards, the dedicated interest rate and rate of return risk regulation, model management standards and the Basel SRP 31 framework, and covering basis risk, repricing gaps, commercial margins, optionality and non-maturing deposits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
