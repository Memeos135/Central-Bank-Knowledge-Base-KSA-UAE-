# Minimum Bank Capital Requirements

## Why this community

Prudential capital adequacy for banks: the minimum capital floor for licensing and ongoing operation, and how it sits within the Basel three-pillar architecture.

## How members connect

- The Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation is the binding instrument; it fixes the Minimum Capital Requirement as a licensing and continuing condition, distinct from risk-weighted ratio requirements.
- The Basel Framework is the adopted reference standard from which the Pillar 1 / Pillar 2 / Pillar 3 structure and the leverage ratio derive.
- Pillar 1 methodologies (Foundation IRB, standardised counterparty credit risk) determine risk-weighted exposures; Pillar 2 captures risks not fully covered in Pillar 1; Pillar 3 imposes the corresponding public disclosure duty.
- The leverage ratio operates as a non-risk-based backstop cross-referencing Pillar 1 exposure measurement, including counterparty credit exposures.
