# Name Screening

Addresses name screening obligations under CBUAE AML guidance: screening customers, beneficial owners, natural persons acting for the customer and other related parties against ML/TF/PF information sources, negative media, PEP databases and internal watchlists before onboarding, with records of true matches kept in the KYC file. It also links screening to periodic and event-driven CDD refreshes, where re-screening is required and a true hit on a sanctions, PEP or adverse-media list is itself a trigger for review. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; interacts with the separate TFS/sanctions screening regime.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
