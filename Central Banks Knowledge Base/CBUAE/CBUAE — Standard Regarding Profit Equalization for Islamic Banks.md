# Standard Regarding Profit Equalization for Islamic Banks

A mandatory and enforceable CBUAE Standard, issued under the Central Bank Law, setting supervisory requirements for profit equalization in Islamic Banks; it supplements the Standards Re Risk Management Requirements for Islamic Banks and must be read with HSA Shari'ah requirements. It covers permitted equalization techniques (PER, IRR, Mudarib profit waiver, Hibah), governance and Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee approval, and disclosure duties toward Investment Account Holders. Applies to all Islamic Banks and banks with an Islamic Window in the UAE, on a solo and group-wide basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection
