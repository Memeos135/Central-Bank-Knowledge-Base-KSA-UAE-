# Remuneration Committee

Article 5 of the CBUAE remuneration regulation, specifying the mandate, composition, expertise and documented terms of reference of the board's remuneration committee, including adequate independent non-executive representation. It sets the committee's duties: reviewing senior management remuneration levels and structures, aligning pay with long-term interests and risk appetite, setting performance and remuneration standards for Relevant Persons, periodic policy review, engagement with risk, compliance and audit committees, and access to Control Function information. It binds the boards and remuneration committees of CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
