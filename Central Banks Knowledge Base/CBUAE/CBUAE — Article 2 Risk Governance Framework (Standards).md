# Article 2: Risk Governance Framework (Standards)

Article 2 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards requires each bank to establish, document and maintain a board-approved risk governance framework of policies, processes, procedures, systems and controls, proportionate to its risk profile, nature, size, complexity and structure. It allocates minimum roles to the board, senior management, the risk management function, compliance, internal audit and business line management, requires probability-and-impact assessment of risks, and lists the material risk categories the framework must at least cover. It binds CBUAE-licensed banks, with deviations from the listed elements only if the bank can demonstrate a comprehensive approach to the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
