# Structured Trade Finance

Covers structured trade finance — lending across the production-to-sale cycle, including pre-export finance and warehouse (inventory) financing where goods serve as collateral in an approved or third-party managed warehouse. It sits within the guidance's taxonomy of non-documentary trade products and feeds the accompanying glossary of trade finance defined terms (bank guarantee, letter of credit, open account trade, dual-use items). Relevant to LFIs financing multi-stage trade flows.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential
