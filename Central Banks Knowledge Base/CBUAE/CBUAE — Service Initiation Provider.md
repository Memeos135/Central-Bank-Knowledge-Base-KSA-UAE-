# Service Initiation Provider

Defined term in the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation for a juridical person licensed by the UAE Central Bank to carry on Service Initiation activities, i.e. electronically initiating transactions on relevant Accounts or Products at the User's instruction. The node sets out its conduct duties (act only on explicit User consent and instructions, secure communication with the Service Owner) and its liability to the User for non-execution, late or defective execution, including failures of authorisation, authentication, accurate recording or secure communication, with the burden of proving correct processing resting on the provider in a dispute. It binds licensed Service Initiation Providers and interacts with Service Owner duties; note this is a UAE instrument, not a SAMA rule.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, consumer protection, governance/risk
