# Basis of Investing Rights of Policyholders

Section 1 of the Financial Regulations, setting the basis for investing the rights of policyholders: general investment requirements and investment policy, asset distribution and concentration limits with transitional compliance periods, investment risk management, domiciling of investments, derivatives, outsourced investment activity, borrowed funds and supervisory reporting, plus addendums forming an integral part. It binds licensed insurance companies and feeds directly into solvency work, since admissible asset values are capped by these limits. Consult it for any question on asset eligibility, concentration breaches or investment outsourcing.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
