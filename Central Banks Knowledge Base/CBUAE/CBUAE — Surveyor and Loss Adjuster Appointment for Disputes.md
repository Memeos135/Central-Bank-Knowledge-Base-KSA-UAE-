# Surveyor and Loss Adjuster Appointment for Disputes

Node on the insurer's post-accident repair and settlement duties under the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy, including restoring the vehicle to pre-accident condition, paying market value where damage exceeds 50% of market value, replacing a total-loss vehicle or paying cash at the claimant's request, and using agency repair shops. It also covers the rule that parts of vehicles under one year old are replaced new without depreciation, and the framework for valuation/adjustment where damage assessment is disputed. Binds insurance companies settling motor claims in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
