# Real Estate Sector Risk

A concept node describing CBUAE's assessment of ML/TF risk arising from the real estate and DPMS sectors, including the sectors' economic significance, their attractiveness to illicit actors, their facilitation of cross-border value movement, and the FATF Mutual Evaluation findings that both sectors are highly important for risk and materiality in the UAE. It also covers licensing and supervision arrangements for real estate professionals across mainland, commercial and financial free zones. It informs risk assessment and customer risk rating obligations of CBUAE-licensed LFIs serving these sectors.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
