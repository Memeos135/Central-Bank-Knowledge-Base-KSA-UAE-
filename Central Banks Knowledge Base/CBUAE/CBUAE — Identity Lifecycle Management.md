# Identity Lifecycle Management

Concept node on biometric identity management in the CBUAE enabling-technologies guidelines: identity proofing and enrolment (validation of documentary evidence, verification that the identity belongs to the person), ongoing authentication, credential protection, multi-factor and risk-based/adaptive authentication, and monitoring across the whole Identity Lifecycle. It also carries data-protection compliance and controlled/auditable access where biometric functions are outsourced. Binds CBUAE-licensed Institutions deploying Biometric Applications; relevant where identity proofing intersects with customer due diligence and onboarding.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, AML/CTF
