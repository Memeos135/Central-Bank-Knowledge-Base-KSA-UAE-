# Notice No. 3871/2012

Earlier CBUAE notice on mortgage/real-estate lending that is expressly withdrawn by Article 10 of the mortgage loans regulations as from the date those regulations take effect (one month after publication in the Official Gazette). It has no continuing force; relevant only for historical or transitional analysis of obligations before Circular 31/2013. Interpretation of the superseding regulations rests with the Central Bank's Legal Development Unit.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, other
