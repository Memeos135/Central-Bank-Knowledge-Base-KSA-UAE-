# Exchange Business

Defined activity term anchoring the scope of the Exchange Business Regulation: the licensed financial activities of currency exchange and money transfer/remittance services (and, under a Category I licence, salary processing through WPS subject to a separate LNO) referred to in Article 65 of the Central Bank Law. Carrying on or engaging in Exchange Business in the UAE triggers the obligation to apply for one of the four licence categories, unless the person already holds another Central Bank licence acceptable for that purpose. Relevant to any perimeter question about whether an activity or entity falls inside the exchange-house regime.

**Regimes:** payments, other
