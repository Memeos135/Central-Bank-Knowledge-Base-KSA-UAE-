# Enabling Technology

The umbrella node for the joint Guidelines issued by CBUAE, SCA, DFSA and FSRA on adopting Enabling Technologies (APIs, cloud, biometrics, big data/AI, DLT) in financial services, including their legal basis, objectives, scope and cross-cutting key principles. Those principles cover data protection compliance, effective audit/compliance/risk control functions, periodic independent review or audit, and adequate skills, knowledge and expertise, with responsibility retained even where activities are outsourced. Applies across the four Supervisory Authorities' regulated populations and is principles-based rather than prescriptive.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, other
