# Third-Party Reliance (Digital ID)

Explains reliance on a third party for customer identification and verification at onboarding in a digital ID context, anchored in the AML-CFT Decision's reliance provision, including the condition that the relying institution immediately obtain the identification/verification information and applicable assurance levels. It distinguishes reliance (using another regulated entity's own CDD on its existing customer) from outsourcing (a provider performing control functions under the institution's own policies), and confirms that ultimate responsibility for CDD stays with the relying institution. Also links authentication data to ongoing due diligence and transaction monitoring. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
