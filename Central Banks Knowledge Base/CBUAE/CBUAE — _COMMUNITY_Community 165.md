# Islamic Bank Board & Reserves

## Why this community

Board-level governance over Islamic bank profit-smoothing reserves — allocation of decision rights between the board and a dedicated independent committee.

## How members connect

- The Board of an Islamic bank is the accountable governing body and delegates or oversees reserve decisions through the Independent Committee for Reserves.
- This pairing is the governance layer that operationalises the PER/IRR and DCR requirements in the profit equalization standard cluster.
- Practical consequence: reserve transfers and drawdowns require the committee's independent determination, limiting unilateral management action.
