# Third-Party Reliance and Outsourcing (Insurance)

A node within CBUAE's insurance-sector AML/CFT guidance addressing reliance on third parties and outsourcing of CDD functions. It is relevant because the insurance distribution chain involves tied agents, independent brokers and health insurance third-party administrators, each of which may touch customer onboarding on the insurer's behalf. The key regulatory consequence is allocation of responsibility: reliance on an intermediary does not displace the licensed insurer's own CDD and record-keeping obligations — confirm the specific conditions in the source text, as the excerpt supplied is the sector participant definitions.
