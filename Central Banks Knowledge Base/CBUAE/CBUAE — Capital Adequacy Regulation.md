# Capital Adequacy Regulation

The anchor instrument node: CBUAE's capital adequacy framework and the supporting standards that elaborate supervisory expectations on minimum capital, capital components and buffers, issued under the Central Bank Law and aligned to Basel II standardised and Basel III calibrations. It fixes the UAE-specific minimum total capital requirement above the Basel 8% baseline (excluding buffers) and permits the Central Bank to impose requirements beyond the minimum lists in individual articles. Binds all CBUAE-licensed banks and shapes consolidated-group capital treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
