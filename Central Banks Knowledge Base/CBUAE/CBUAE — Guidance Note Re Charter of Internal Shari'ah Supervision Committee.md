# Guidance Note Re Charter of Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee

CBUAE Guidance Note on the Charter of the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, issued to complement the Shari'ah Governance Standard and to clarify supervisory expectations for the Charter. It sets a minimum-content template covering the ISSC's definition, member qualifications, independence, appointment, membership term, dismissal/resignation, and responsibilities, which institutions may supplement but not contradict. It applies to all licensed Islamic Financial Institutions, which were to align their Charters with the template from 21 April 2021.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
