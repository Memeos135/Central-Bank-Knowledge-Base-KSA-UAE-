# Annual Percentage Rate / Interest/Profit Rate Disclosure

Concept node on rate disclosure under the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards, spanning both advertising (Section 2.3.3) and pre-contract disclosure (Article 2.1). Advertising of an Annual Percentage Rate for term lending/financing must be accompanied by a worked example showing total interest/profit and Fees over the term and any qualifying conditions, with an express carve-out for residential mortgages; deposit advertising must state whether the Annual Interest/Profit Rate is fixed or variable, per-term rates in equal prominence, minimum tenor/amount, payment frequency, calculation basis and conditions. Separately, institutions must prominently disclose to Consumers whether a Credit Product rate is fixed, variable or a combination, and the method of calculation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
