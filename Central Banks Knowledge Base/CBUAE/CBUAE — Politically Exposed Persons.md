# Politically Exposed Persons

Node covering the politically exposed persons regime (Division Four, Article 16) of the UAE AML/CTF Executive Regulations together with the PEP definition. It requires risk management systems to detect PEP status, senior management approval to establish or continue the relationship, reasonable measures to establish source of funds and wealth, and enhanced ongoing monitoring for foreign PEPs, with a risk-triggered application of the same measures to domestic PEPs and persons holding prominent functions in international organisations; separate duties apply to life insurance beneficiaries before payout. Binds Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and Virtual Asset Service Providers; the definition extends to immediate family members and known close associates.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
