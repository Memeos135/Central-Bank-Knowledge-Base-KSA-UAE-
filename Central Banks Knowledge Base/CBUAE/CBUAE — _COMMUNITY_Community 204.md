# Advances to Deposits Ratio

## Why this community

Prudential liquidity/funding constraint expressed as a ceiling on lending relative to deposit funding, issued by circular rather than as a standalone regulation.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a circular-level instrument, so it sits below primary regulation in the hierarchy and should be read as a supervisory limit or reporting requirement layered onto existing banking rules.
- Isolation in the graph means the operative definitions of 'advances' and 'deposits' must be sourced from the parent banking framework rather than from within the cluster.
- Confirm issuing authority and jurisdiction before citing, as the label does not itself establish which central bank issued it.
