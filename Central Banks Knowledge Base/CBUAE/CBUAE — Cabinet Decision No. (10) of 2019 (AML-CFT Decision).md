# Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019 (AML-CFT Decision)

Concept node for Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019 (the AML-CFT Decision), the implementing regulation cited as the legal source of the obligations in the hawala guidance. Articles referenced include the requirement to establish a risk-based AML/CFT programme (Arts. 4, 20, 26), the appointment and duties of a Compliance Officer (Art. 21), and the enterprise risk assessment (Art. 4.1). It binds hawala providers and licensed financial institutions as subject entities under the UAE AML/CFT framework.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
