# Federal Law 6/2007 - Establishment of Insurance Authority

Node for UAE Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 on the Establishment of the Insurance Authority and the Organization of its Operations, as amended, the enabling statute for insurance supervision in the State (supervisory functions later transferred to the CBUAE). It is the legal basis relied on for subordinate instruments such as the Executive Regulation (Decision 2/2009), the AML/CTF procedures for insurance activities (Decision 13/2015 and Administrative Decision 79/2015) and the Points of Sale licensing instructions (Decision 37/2017). It binds insurance companies, branches of foreign insurers, Takaful operators and related insurance intermediaries operating in the State.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
