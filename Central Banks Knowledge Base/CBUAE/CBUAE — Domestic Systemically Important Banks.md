# Domestic Systemically Important Banks

Node for the Domestic Systemically Important Banks concept as it sits within the CBUAE Capital Adequacy Regulations (Circular 52/2017), the UAE's Basel III implementation. The surrounding text governs the capital buffer framework — capital conservation buffer with graduated earnings-retention/dividend-restriction tiers keyed to CET1 ratios, remediation planning tied to the internal capital adequacy assessment process, a Central Bank-agreed timeline to restore the buffer, and the countercyclical buffer. It binds banks licensed and supervised by the CBUAE (with heightened buffer expectations for banks designated as systemically important domestically).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
