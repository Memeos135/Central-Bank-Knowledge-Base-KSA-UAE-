# Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 (Central Bank)

Reference node for Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities, as amended, cited as one of the enabling powers for the CBUAE Insurance Brokers' Regulation. It is the statutory basis for CBUAE licensing, prudential and supervisory authority over insurance brokers alongside the 2023 insurance law. Useful when tracing the legal authority behind a CBUAE regulation or sanction.
