# Ministerial Resolution 23/1985 - Insurance Consultancy

Reference node for Ministerial Resolution No. 23 of 1985 on the Regulation of Insurance Consultancy and its amendments, cited in the recitals of BoD Decision 12 of 2018 as part of the legal basis for the current insurance consultant regime. Useful only for tracing the historical/predecessor framework for consultancy licensing; the operative requirements are those in the 2018 regulation.
