# Simplified Approach (Options)

Node on the simplified option treatment, available to banks whose option books consist only of purchased options, presented alongside the simplified approach for commodities (a 15% charge on the net position plus 3% of the gross position per commodity, valued at current spot prices). It marks the boundary between banks eligible for simplified carve-outs and those required to use delta-plus. Binds banks calculating market risk capital for option and commodity positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
