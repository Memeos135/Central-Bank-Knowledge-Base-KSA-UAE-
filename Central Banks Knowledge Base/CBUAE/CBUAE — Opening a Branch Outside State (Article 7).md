# Opening a Branch Outside State (Article 7)

Article 7 governs a State-established insurance Company opening a Branch outside the State or in a Financial Free Zone, or a subsidiary inside or outside the State: an application on the Central Bank's prescribed form must be filed with supporting documents including a Board resolution and a business plan. The Central Bank may approve, approve with conditions or limitations, or reject with written reasons, and prior Central Bank approval plus evidence of the host supervisor's consent is required before relocating or closing such a Branch. It binds Companies established in the UAE pursuing cross-border or free-zone expansion.
