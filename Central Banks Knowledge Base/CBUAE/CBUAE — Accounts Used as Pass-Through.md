# Accounts Used as Pass-Through

A TBML typology in the CBUAE guidance covering accounts opened principally to obscure the audit trail, where funds are received and rapidly moved on so that value linked to trade transactions becomes difficult to trace. It frames the risk that account relationships with little apparent commercial substance are used as layering conduits in trade schemes. Relevant to LFIs conducting CDD, ongoing monitoring and suspicious transaction reporting on trade-linked accounts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
