# Technical Provisions

Sets out the types and calculation basis of Technical Provisions for Takaful companies — unearned contribution, unexpired risk, outstanding loss, IBNR, loss adjustment expense reserves and mathematical reserve — computed both gross and net of Re-Takaful. It also imposes localisation rules requiring investments matching specified provision categories to be held in the State, and actuarial requirements including appointment of an Authority-registered Actuary, actuarial review and approval of provisions, data quality assessment and annual reporting to the Authority. Binds Takaful insurance companies and, professionally, their appointed Actuaries.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
