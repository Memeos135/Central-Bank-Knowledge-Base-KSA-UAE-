# Chapter 10: Risk Management

Chapter 10 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business requires each Licensed Person to operate a risk management framework covering identification, measurement, recording, mitigation and monitoring of business risks. Ultimate responsibility rests with the Board (or Owner/Partners), which must approve a Risk Management Policy reviewed at least annually, and a designated Risk Officer must hold overall responsibility, with role combination allowed only subject to conditions cross-referenced to the audit, AML and compliance chapters. It also mandates maintaining a risk register logging risk analysis results, mitigating measures and risk ownership.
