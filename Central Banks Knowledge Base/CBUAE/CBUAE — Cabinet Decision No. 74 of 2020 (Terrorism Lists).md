# Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020 (Terrorism Lists)

This node covers UAE Cabinet Decision No. (74) of 2020 on the Terrorism Lists Regulation and implementation of UN Security Council resolutions on terrorism, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, as cited in the CBUAE payments guidance. It supplies the legal framework for targeted financial sanctions in the UAE, including the Local Terrorist List and the UN Consolidated List, alongside the duty to apply competent-authority directives promptly. It binds licensed financial institutions, which are expected to maintain a sanctions compliance programme covering customer and transaction screening.
