# Retail Payment Regulation Amendment

## Why this community

Interface with the existing retail payments/stored-value framework: the instrument amends the Retail Payment and SVF Regulation and sets the timeline for incumbents to come into compliance.

## How members connect

- Direct hierarchical linkage — the amendment article modifies a prior SAMA regulation, so the two texts must be read as a consolidated regime rather than in isolation.
- The transition article conditions the amendment: it fixes when amended obligations bite and what status existing licensees hold in the interim.
- Together they determine grandfathering scope — which entities may continue operating under prior terms and for how long, a threshold question before any enforcement analysis.
- Cross-regime consequence: amended SVF/retail payment scope can change whether an activity is licensable, which in turn changes AML/CTF and consumer-protection obligations attaching to that activity.
