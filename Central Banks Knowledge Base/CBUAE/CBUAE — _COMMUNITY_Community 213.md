# Business Continuity

## Why this community

Operational resilience obligation requiring a supervised entity to maintain continuity of critical services and recover from disruption without harming customers or the payment ecosystem.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster: the business continuity duty sits within the entity's ongoing governance and risk-management licensing conditions rather than as a standalone regime.
- Practically chained to supervisory oversight — failure to maintain continuity is the kind of breach that feeds the enforcement and information-gathering articles elsewhere in the instrument.
- Read alongside broader SAMA governance/BCM expectations, which sit hierarchically above this article as general framework requirements.
