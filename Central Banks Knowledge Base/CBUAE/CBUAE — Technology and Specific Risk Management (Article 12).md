# Technology and Specific Risk Management (Article 12)

Node for Article 12 of the CBUAE SVF Regulation on technology and specific risk management, requiring a Licensee to design and operate technology and risk management systems in line with international best practice and to establish an effective technology and cyber security risk management framework covering IT control adequacy, cyber resilience, and the quality, reliability, robustness, stability, availability and security of its systems. These requirements apply to SVF Licensees and expressly also to licensed banks carrying out SVF business in the State.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
