# Executive Regulation (Cabinet Decision 10/2019)

Node for Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019, the Executive Regulation implementing Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 on AML/CTF. It is the instrument whose Article 2 defines the financial activities and operations that determine whether a licensed or registered entity falls within the definition of "Financial Institution" for AML purposes, and it carries the detailed implementing obligations. It binds Financial Institutions and their concerned persons, alongside CBUAE instructions and guidance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
