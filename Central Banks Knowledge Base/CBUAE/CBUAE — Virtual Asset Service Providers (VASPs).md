# Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs)

Guidance addressing virtual assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers within the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines, pointing FIs to the FATF Recommendations and risk-based guidance on VAs/VASPs, and extending originator/beneficiary information (travel rule) expectations to virtual asset transfers between originating and beneficiary VASPs. The same extract covers intermediary FI obligations to detect transfers lacking required originator or beneficiary data and to decide whether to execute, reject or suspend them, plus insurance-sector laundering typologies. It binds FIs dealing with VA/VASP exposure and intermediary institutions in payment chains.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
