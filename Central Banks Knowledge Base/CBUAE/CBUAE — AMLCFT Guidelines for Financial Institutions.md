# AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions

This is the CBUAE's AML/CFT and counter-financing-of-illegal-organisations Guidelines for Financial Institutions, a joint supervisory-authority document explaining how licensed FIs should meet their statutory duties under the UAE AML-CFT Law and its implementing Cabinet Decision. It sets out minimum expectations on the risk-based approach, business-wide ML/FT risk identification, assessment, documentation and updating, plus core obligations such as customer due diligence, appointment of a qualified compliance officer, internal controls and policies, suspicious-transaction indicators, STR filing and cooperation with competent authorities. It binds supervised financial institutions (and informs their senior management and staff), while expressly leaving the FI solely responsible for compliance with the underlying law, decision, notices and circulars; UN targeted financial sanctions guidance sits in a separate instrument.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
