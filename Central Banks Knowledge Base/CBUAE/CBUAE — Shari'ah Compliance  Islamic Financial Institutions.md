# Shari'ah Compliance / Islamic Financial Institutions

Concept node covering Article 11 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards, which binds Islamic Financial Institutions (IFIs) offering Islamic Financial Products and/or Services to ensure Board and Senior Management oversight of Shari'ah compliance, with the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee responsible for Shari'ah compliance and fairness under the separate Shari'ah Governance Standard. It also requires fair profit-distribution mechanisms between shareholders and investment accountholders, embedding Shari'ah compliance in culture and operations, independent oversight, and Consumer education on the differences between conventional and Islamic products. Detailed Shari'ah governance rules sit in a separate CBUAE regulation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, BNPL/finance
