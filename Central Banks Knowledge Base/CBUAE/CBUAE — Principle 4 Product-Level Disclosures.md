# Principle 4: Product-Level Disclosures

Principle 4 of the CBUAE/Sustainable Finance Working Group ESG disclosure principles addresses product-level disclosure for sustainability-related products, covering naming, labelling and classification, and disclosure of sustainability objectives and strategies in offering documents. It is framed as an expectation on market participants offering such products, aimed at preventing misleading or inconsistent sustainability claims (greenwashing). As a principles document it is not directly binding; each Authority decides how and to whom it is translated into its own regulatory framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
