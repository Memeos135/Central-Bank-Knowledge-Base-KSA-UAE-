# Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation 05/2020

A cross-referenced instrument — Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation 05/2020 of 15 April 2020 — defined within the National Shareholding in Banks Regulation as the rules governing transfers of significant shareholdings in banks. The national shareholding regulation obliges bank boards and shareholders to comply with its requirements alongside the sixty percent national ownership floor. Relevant to banks incorporated in the UAE and to parties acquiring or transferring significant stakes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
