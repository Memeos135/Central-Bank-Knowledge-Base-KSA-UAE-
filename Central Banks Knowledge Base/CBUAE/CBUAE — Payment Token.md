# Payment Token

Definitional node for "Payment Token" under the CBUAE Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation: a fiat-backed Crypto-Asset that is digitally tradable and functions as a medium of exchange, unit of account and/or store of value, without legal tender status and without representing any equity or debt claim. The definition is the gateway to the Payment Token regime — issuance requires a Category I Licence, a White Paper and prior CBUAE approval before any offer to the public — and it sits alongside neighbouring definitions (Payment Service Provider, Payment Instrument, Payment Account, Payment Initiation Service). Relevant to entities seeking to classify a stablecoin-type product, and to distinguish it from Virtual Asset Tokens, Security Tokens and Commodity Tokens.

**Regimes:** payments, other
