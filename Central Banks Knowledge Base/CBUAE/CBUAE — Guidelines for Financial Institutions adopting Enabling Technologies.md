# Guidelines for Financial Institutions adopting Enabling Technologies

Joint Guidelines for Financial Institutions adopting Enabling Technologies, issued by CBUAE with SCA, DFSA and FSRA under their respective statutes. They set cross-authority key principles for firms using Enabling Technologies in Innovative Activities, covering data protection, control functions (audit, compliance, risk), periodic independent review, and adequacy of skills, knowledge and expertise, including expectations where technology audit is outsourced to an outsourced service provider. They apply to institutions licensed or authorised by any of the four Supervisory Authorities, including new entrants.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
