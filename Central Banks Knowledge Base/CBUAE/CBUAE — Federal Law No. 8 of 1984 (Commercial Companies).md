# Federal Law No. 8 of 1984 (Commercial Companies)

Reference node for the UAE Commercial Companies Law (Federal Law No. 8 of 1984, as amended or replaced), cited as a legal basis and defined term in the insurance minimum capital resolution. It matters here because capital increases and reductions of insurance companies remain subject to general company-law requirements in addition to Insurance Authority approval. Not itself a sectoral instrument; consult for corporate-law overlay on capital actions.
