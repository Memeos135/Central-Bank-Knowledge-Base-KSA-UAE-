# Circular No. 30/2012 (superseded)

Node recording the repeal provision (Article 9): Circular No. 30/2012 of 12 July 2012 on liquidity regulations at banks is withdrawn from the effective date of the current liquidity regulations. It sits alongside the commencement and transition timeline — ELAR and the qualitative requirements from 1 July 2015, LCR and NSFR transition from 1 January 2016 for approved banks, with NSFR compliance required from 1 January 2018. Useful for dating obligations and checking which instrument governed a given period.
