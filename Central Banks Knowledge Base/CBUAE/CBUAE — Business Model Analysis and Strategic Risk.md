# Business Model Analysis and Strategic Risk

Section of the CBUAE ICAAP guidance dealing with business model analysis (BMA) and strategic risk as a Pillar 2 material risk. It frames BMA as the risk that the bank's organisation, expertise, systems and processes fail to deliver its business and strategic objectives, and strategic risk as the business model or strategy ceasing to be adequate for Board-set objectives. It binds banks subject to the ICAAP regime, with responsibility sitting with the Board and Senior Management for risk identification, measurement and capital calibration.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
