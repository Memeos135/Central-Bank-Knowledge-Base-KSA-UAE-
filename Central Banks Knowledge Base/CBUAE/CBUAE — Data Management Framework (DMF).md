# Data Management Framework (DMF)

Defines the Data Management Framework required under the MMS for data used in model development and validation (expressly not routine risk analysis and reporting data). It requires a formal, resourced framework approved by Senior Management and the Board, documented with limitations and assumptions, covering the whole institution and all material risk types, independently validated, and comprising source identification, regular collection, quality control, secure storage/access and robust infrastructure, with named data owners and documented, largely automated collection across credit, market, concentration, liquidity, operational and fraud risk. Binds institutions subject to the MMS and the designated data owners within them.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, data/credit info
