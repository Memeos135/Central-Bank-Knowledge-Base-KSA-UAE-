# Consumer Protection & Conduct Risk Management Annual Report

Concept node for the annual Consumer Protection & Conduct Risk Management report that Licensed Financial Institutions must produce for the Board under the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards. It is the reporting vehicle through which compliance-culture assessment, code-of-conduct adherence monitoring (including mystery shopping, thematic reviews and complaint data analysis), and the type and level of conduct risk versus risk appetite are evaluated and escalated. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, their Control functions, Senior Management and the Board.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
