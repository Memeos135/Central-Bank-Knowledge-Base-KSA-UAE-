# Correspondent Banking Relationship

Concept node on Correspondent Banking Relationships as defined and applied in CBUAE guidance, anchored on Article 25 of the AML-CFT Decision: prohibition on dealing with shell banks, information gathering on the respondent, assessment of its AML/CFT controls, senior management approval, and clarity on each party's responsibilities. The guidance extends these duties to correspondent-type relationships with payment-sector participants (SVF providers, retail payment services, card schemes) and stresses that each institution remains responsible for monitoring transactions passing through it, including detecting nesting. Binds licensed financial institutions providing account or clearing access to other institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
