# Independent Audit and Testing

Sub-element of the SCP on independent audit and risk-based testing: regular assessment of sanctions policies, procedures, systems and controls by internal audit and/or a competent external auditor, resourced with skilled staff and scaled to the sophistication of the program. The audit function must be independent of audited activities with sufficient authority and resources; negative findings require immediate action and compensating controls pending root-cause remediation. Separately requires independent risk-based testing that screening produces expected alerts and that thresholds and rules align with the stated risk appetite.
