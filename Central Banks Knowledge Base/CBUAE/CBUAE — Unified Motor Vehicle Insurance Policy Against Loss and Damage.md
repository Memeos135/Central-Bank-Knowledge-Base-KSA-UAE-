# Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Loss and Damage

The Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Loss and Damage (own-damage motor cover), setting mandatory standard policy terms: the insurer's compensation obligations, total-loss and depreciation treatment, salvage/recovery and repair-quality duties, and the exclusions for which no compensation is payable (e.g. indirect loss, overloading, unlicensed or improperly licensed drivers, racing, prohibited use). It standardises the contractual bargain between insurer and insured, binding insurers writing motor loss-and-damage cover in the UAE and defining policyholder entitlements.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
