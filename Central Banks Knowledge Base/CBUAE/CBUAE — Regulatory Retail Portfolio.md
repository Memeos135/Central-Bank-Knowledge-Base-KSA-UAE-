# Regulatory Retail Portfolio

A capital-adequacy asset-class concept defining which exposures qualify for the regulatory retail portfolio under the standardised approach, by reference to the Credit Risk Standard's four tests (orientation, product, granularity and value criteria), with non-qualifying retail claims risk weighted at 100%. It explains how aggregated exposure to one counterparty is measured on a gross basis (after credit conversion factors for off-balance-sheet items) and how connected small businesses are treated as a single beneficiary. Claims secured by residential property and past-due retail loans are carved out and handled in separate asset classes; it binds banks calculating regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
