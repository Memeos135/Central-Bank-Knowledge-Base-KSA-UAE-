# Requirement for Approval

Provision within a CBUAE bank ownership/shareholding regulation setting the prior-written-approval gateway for acquiring or increasing a Significant or Controlling Shareholding (or equivalent voting rights) in a bank, directly or indirectly. It also deals with breaches: a holding above five percent acquired without approval triggers an immediate approval application, suspension of voting rights and dividends on the excess portion, and a bar on disposal without Central Bank direction, with the Board responsible for enforcing these restrictions. Related articles let the Central Bank deem persons acting in concert to be significant/controlling shareholders and specify the content of the approval application (fit-and-proper questionnaire, direct/indirect and nominee holdings, three years of financial information). Binds shareholders and prospective acquirers of bank shares and the bank's Board.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
