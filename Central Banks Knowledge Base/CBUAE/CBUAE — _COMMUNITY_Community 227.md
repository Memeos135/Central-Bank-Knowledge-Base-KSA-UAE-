# Credit Risk Mitigation

## Why this community

Recognition and valuation of collateral, guarantees and other credit risk mitigants, and the limits on using them to reduce measured credit risk or provisions.

## How members connect

- Stands as the eligibility gate feeding the classification and provisioning framework — mitigants only reduce provisioning if they satisfy this article's recognition conditions.
- Isolated in the graph but substantively dependent on the default and staging definitions; read alongside the provisioning article rather than alone.
- Watch the interaction with capital adequacy treatment: the same mitigant may be recognised differently for provisioning and for capital.
