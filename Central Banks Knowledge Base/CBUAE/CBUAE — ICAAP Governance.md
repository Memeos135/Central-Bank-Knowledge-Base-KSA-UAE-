# ICAAP Governance

Sets the governance requirements around the ICAAP: the Board of Directors owns the ICAAP, must approve it annually, and must approve the ICAAP governance framework with clear assignment of responsibilities, with assumptions and conclusions challenged at expert-committee, Senior Management and Board levels. Also frames the required ICAAP report elements (capital composition against minimum requirements, three-year forward-looking capital plan including dividends, stress scenario analysis with mitigation, assessment of risk management processes and a concluding capital adequacy opinion). Binds CBUAE-licensed banks; for foreign bank operations, Board references map to the local senior governance body.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
