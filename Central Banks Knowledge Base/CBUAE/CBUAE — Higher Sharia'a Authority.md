# Higher Sharia'a Authority

Identifies the Higher Sharia'a Authority as the external supra-entity body whose directions and guidance on Sharia'a governance bind Islamic Finance Companies, and which decides disputes escalated by a Sharia'a supervision committee against company management. Islamic Finance Companies must also notify the Higher Sharia'a Authority, alongside the Central Bank, of material information affecting the fitness and probity of a Sharia'a supervision committee member.
