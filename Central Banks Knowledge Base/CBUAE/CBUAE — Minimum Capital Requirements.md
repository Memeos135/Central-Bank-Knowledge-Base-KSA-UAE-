# Minimum Capital Requirements

This node covers Article 4 (Minimum Capital Requirements) of the Specialized Banks with Low Risk Regulation, together with the surrounding Article 3 balance-sheet limits. It defines Aggregate Capital Funds (paid-up capital, reserves excluding revaluation reserve, retained earnings, less accumulated losses and goodwill), cross-refers minimum paid-up capital to the Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation, and imposes a 60% UAE national ownership floor, alongside caps on participations in other companies, interbank deposit placements, immovable property holdings, total consolidated assets and off-balance-sheet exposures. It binds Specialized Banks and their shareholders.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
