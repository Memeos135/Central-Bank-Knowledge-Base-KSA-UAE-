# Cloud Computing Materiality Assessment

Node defining when a cloud computing arrangement is material under the CBUAE enabling-technologies guidelines: where service disruption or a breach of security or confidentiality could materially affect business operations, risk management capability, regulatory compliance, or the confidentiality/integrity of the Institution's or Customer's Personal Data. It sets out the factors for the materiality and risk assessment (criticality, likelihood and impact of failure or data breach, cost) and expects early engagement with the relevant Supervisory Authority before implementing a material arrangement, consistent with existing outsourcing requirements. The materiality conclusion drives the depth of due diligence and the strength of mitigating controls.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info
