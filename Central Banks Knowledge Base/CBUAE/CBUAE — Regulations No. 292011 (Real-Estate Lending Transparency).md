# Regulations No. 29/2011 (Real-Estate Lending Transparency)

Node for a related/predecessor real-estate lending instrument referenced alongside the mortgage loans regulations; the available text is drawn from the Circular 31/2013 consolidated version, principally Article 4 (disclosure and transparency) and the definitions/risk-management provisions. The substantive obligations shown require lenders to give borrowers sufficient, transparent information on costs and risks, to publish fees, charges and interest/profit rates with the calculation method, and to document property, borrower contribution, loan amount, repayment period, instalments, rate, insurance, disbursement and prepayment terms, with fees in a separate schedule annexed to the loan contract. It binds mortgage loan providers dealing with individual borrowers; verify the exact instrument number against the source before citing.
