# Treatment of Options

Node setting out how options are captured in market risk capital: two alternative methods, a simplified approach available to banks holding only purchased options and a delta-plus approach for more complex option books. It also explains how to identify the underlying where this is ambiguous (e.g. FX) and the use of nominal value where the underlying's market value may be zero. Binds banks with option or option-like positions (including caps, floors and swaptions) in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
