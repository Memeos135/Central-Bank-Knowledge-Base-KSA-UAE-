# Big Data & AI Governance

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for financial institutions deploying big data analytics and artificial intelligence, covering the governance, ethical and model-lifecycle controls that must sit around such use before and during deployment.

## How members connect

- A single overarching big data/AI subject node anchors the cluster, with the governance framework, ethics/fairness and model validation items operating as its mandatory control components.
- The governance framework supplies the institutional layer (ownership, approval, oversight) that the fairness and validation obligations are executed under.
- Model validation and versioning is the operational evidence trail that supports ethics/fairness claims, so the two are read together rather than independently.
- Cross-regime relevance: AI-driven decisioning touches consumer protection (fair treatment, explainability) and data/credit information rules where personal data feeds the models.
