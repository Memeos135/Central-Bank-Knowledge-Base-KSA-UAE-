# Article 11: Accounting and Audit

Obliges Insurance Brokers to appoint a CBUAE-approved external auditor to audit annually both the financial statements (per accepted accounting standards) and the systems and controls of the brokerage operations. Empowers the Central Bank to require submission of the audit report in a prescribed form and timeframe, to request additional information, and to enlarge or extend the audit scope or require other examinations; the auditor must also verify positions with all Companies the broker deals with. Binds licensed Insurance Brokers and, indirectly, their appointed external auditors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
