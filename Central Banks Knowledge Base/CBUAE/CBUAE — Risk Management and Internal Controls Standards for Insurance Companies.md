# Risk Management and Internal Controls Standards for Insurance Companies

Standards setting out the required systems of risk management and internal controls for insurance and Takaful companies, including the board-approved three lines of defence model, mandatory risk policy coverage (credit, market/ALM, reserving, insurance, reinsurance, operational, concentration and group risk), and the minimum content of the internal controls system. It also addresses control functions (risk management, compliance, actuarial, internal audit), model validation, stress testing and annual board or audit committee review of control effectiveness. Binds the board and senior management of licensed insurance companies, extending to subsidiaries, affiliates and international branches.
