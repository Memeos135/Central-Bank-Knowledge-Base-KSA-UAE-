# First Line of Defense

Concept node describing the first line of defence — customer-facing and business-area employees — within an LFI's transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting framework. It explains that institutions must not rely on automated monitoring alone, and that trained staff are expected to recognise red flags relevant to their customers, products, channels and geographies and escalate promptly to management and the second line without displacing their own reporting duty. Relevant to all CBUAE-licensed and supervised financial institutions, including exchange houses, finance companies, PSPs, hawala providers and insurers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
