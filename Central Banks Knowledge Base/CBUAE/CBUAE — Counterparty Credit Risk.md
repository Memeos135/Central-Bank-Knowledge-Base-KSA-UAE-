# Counterparty Credit Risk

Node covering counterparty credit risk within the CBUAE capital standards (Doc 1691 v2), spanning specific-risk capital treatment of credit derivatives (including currency, maturity and asset mismatches, and first-to-default basket products) and the supervisory expectation that credit risk be comprehensively assessed in the ICAAP. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE, requiring methodologies for borrower- and portfolio-level credit risk, internal rating systems, and treatment of concentrations and complex credit derivatives. Relevant only as comparative material for a SAMA analyst; it is UAE, not KSA, law.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
