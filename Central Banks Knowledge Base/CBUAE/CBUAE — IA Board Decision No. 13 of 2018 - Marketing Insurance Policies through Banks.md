# IA Board Decision No. 13 of 2018 - Marketing Insurance Policies through Banks

Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 13 of 2018 setting the instructions for bancassurance: insurance and Takaful companies may market policies through banks (including finance companies licensed by the Central Bank) only with prior IA approval, and only for an exhaustive list of product classes. It caps the bank relationship to a marketing channel (no agency, brokerage or consultancy), limits marketing to the bank's own customers, and imposes quarterly/annual reporting of premiums, expenses and claims per bank and per line. Binds insurance companies operating in the State (free-zone companies excluded unless the Law says otherwise), with warnings, suspension or cancellation of approval as sanctions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
