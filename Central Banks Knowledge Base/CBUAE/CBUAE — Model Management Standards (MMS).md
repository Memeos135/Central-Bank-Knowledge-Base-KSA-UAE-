# Model Management Standards (MMS)

Node for the CBUAE Model Management Standards, the binding standards governing how licensed institutions develop, validate, use and govern quantitative models across the model life-cycle. Scope covers all models supporting decision-making (credit risk rating/scorecards, pricing, valuation, stress testing and others), with defined terms such as Model, Model Risk, Materiality and Model Calibration, plus self-assessment, remediation-plan submission and ongoing reporting duties to the CBUAE. It binds UAE-incorporated institutions and their branches/subsidiaries, and UAE branches or subsidiaries of foreign institutions (applying the more stringent parent-regulator elements where relevant).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** The threshold question is scoping: whether a given model (rating, PD, LGD, macro, IRRBB, NPV and similar) falls within the enumerated model types, because inclusion pulls it into the model risk governance, validation and ownership obligations of the standard rather than leaving it as an uncontrolled analytical tool. The list of covered model types and the model risk provisions are parts of the same instrument, so the taxonomy is definitional for the obligation, not merely illustrative. Consequence: classify each model against the list first; anything captured must be carried into the institution's risk taxonomy and governed accordingly, and exclusions should be documented and defensible.
