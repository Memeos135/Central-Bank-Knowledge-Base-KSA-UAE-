# Pillar 2 - ICAAP

Node for the CBUAE Pillar 2 / Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process standard, setting the principles of supervisory review across interest rate risk in the banking book, credit risk (stress testing, residual and concentration risk), operational risk, securitisation and cross-border supervisory cooperation. Key scope constraints: only a Pillar 1 Plus approach is permitted, internal models may not be used to estimate ICAAP capital requirements (reported figures must follow the Standardised Approach), buffers are additive with no offsetting against Pillar 1, and the Central Bank alone determines the capital quality (CET1, AT1, Tier 2) required for Pillar 2 risks. Binds all banks required to submit an ICAAP to the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
