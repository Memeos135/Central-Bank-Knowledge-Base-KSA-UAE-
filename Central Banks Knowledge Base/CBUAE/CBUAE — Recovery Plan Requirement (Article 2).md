# Recovery Plan Requirement (Article 2)

This node covers the CBUAE recovery planning requirements applying to Financial Institutions, including how a local recovery plan must align with and complement a group recovery plan, and the tailored treatment of UAE branches of foreign institutions where the Central Bank may accept, or require changes to, the group plan. It also sets minimum content for a group plan to be treated as covering local operations, governance and communication arrangements, information systems supporting the plan, consistency with a bank's liquidity contingency funding plan, insurers' use of ERM/ORSA inputs, and recovery indicators with calibrated triggers. Obligations here are mandatory in character and bind Financial Institutions, Banks and Insurance Companies within scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
