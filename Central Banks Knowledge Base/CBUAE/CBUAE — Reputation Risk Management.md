# Reputation Risk Management

Sets out reputation risk management duties within the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation, sitting alongside the general risk management and internal control requirements (board-approved risk framework, liquidity risk process). Licensees must operate a reputation risk process proportionate to size and complexity, embed due diligence to assess reputational implications of plans and activities, act proactively to avoid or contain risks, conduct surveillance to detect emerging reputational threats, and provide early warning and assurance to management. Binds SVF Licensees; the general risk and internal control provisions also apply to licensed banks conducting SVF business.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
