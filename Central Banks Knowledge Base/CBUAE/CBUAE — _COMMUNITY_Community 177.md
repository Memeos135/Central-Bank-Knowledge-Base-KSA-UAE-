# Remittance Accounts & Audit

## Why this community

Segregation, accounting and assurance over customer/remittance funds: how the entity must hold and account for funds in dedicated accounts and subject its financial statements to external audit.

## How members connect

- The remittances intermediate account provision expressly references the accounts and external audit article, creating a direct obligation chain from fund segregation to independent verification.
- Shared subject matter: customer funds held on an intermediate basis are within the scope of the accounting records and audit duty.
- Hierarchical reading — general accounts/audit duty first, then the specific treatment for remittance intermediate balances as a carve-out or specification.
- Cross-regime relevance: segregated remittance flows also matter for AML/CTF traceability of transfers and for consumer claims on held funds.
