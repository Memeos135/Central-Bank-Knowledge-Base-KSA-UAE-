# Foundation Internal Ratings Based Approach

A concept node for the Foundation Internal Ratings Based approach as it appears in the CBUAE capital adequacy standards and guidance. Its practical significance here is negative: internal ratings-based approaches are not accepted for UAE bank capital calculations, so the securitisation hierarchy starts with the external ratings-based approach (SEC-ERBA), then the standardised approach (SEC-SA), with a 1250% risk weight where neither applies. Relevant to all CBUAE-licensed banks computing risk-weighted assets, including ECAI selection, multiple-rating and unsolicited-rating rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When deciding whether a bank's internal capital allocation for credit risk is defensible, the credit risk chapter does not let the standardised outcome stand alone: it directs the bank to run a parallel estimation on the foundation internal ratings based basis and compare the two across all exposures and asset classes. The link is an obligation chain within the same supervisory instrument — the credit risk requirement is operationalised by the FIRB benchmarking step. Practically, a credit risk capital conclusion supported only by the standardised figure is incomplete for ICAAP purposes, and the comparison and any resulting add-on should be documented.
