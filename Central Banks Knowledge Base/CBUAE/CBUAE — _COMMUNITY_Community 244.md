# Repeal of Prior Resolution

## Why this community

Transitional/repeal provision terminating the prior Cabinet Resolution instrument and establishing which text governs going forward.

## How members connect

- Final-provisions article: no substantive obligations of its own, but it determines the temporal validity of the whole instrument's obligations.
- Critical for citation hygiene - references to the repealed resolution in older policies, circulars or contracts must be re-mapped to the current text.
- Interacts with every other community by fixing the effective legal basis for their obligations.
