# Complaint Resolution Process

Covers the Complaint resolution mechanism created by the CBUAE Ombudsman Unit Regulation, including its objectives, the end-to-end path from Consumer Complaint to Determination, and the 30-business-day window for either party to appeal before a Determination becomes final and enforceable. It also frames the standards the Unit follows and the referral routes to the Appeals Committee (banking) or the Insurance Dispute Resolution Committee (insurance). It binds the Ombudsman Unit, Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies, and sets the access rights of Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
