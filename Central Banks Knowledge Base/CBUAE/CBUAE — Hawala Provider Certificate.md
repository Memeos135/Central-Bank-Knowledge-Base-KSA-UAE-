# Hawala Provider Certificate

Concept node for the Hawala Provider Certificate — the CBUAE authorisation instrument evidencing registration as a hawala provider. Article 3 material covers the Central Bank's discretion to grant or refuse, written notification with reasons on refusal, a one-year validity renewable for like periods, and the Central Bank's power to attach terms and conditions; grant is conditional on fit-and-proper style criteria (good conduct, no honour/honesty convictions, no unresolved insolvency, adequate education and managerial/professional experience) and on undertakings including electronic reporting of remitter and beneficiary data. Binds applicants for and holders of the certificate.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a hawala counterparty may lawfully operate, the decisive fact is a valid certificate issued by the central bank, not a commercial or trade licence. The certificate regime also reaches agents: a person acting for a registered provider by contract or under its direction operates inside that provider's regulatory and AML/CTF responsibility rather than as an unregulated third party. Consequently, onboarding or correspondent decisions should turn on certificate validity, scope and the provider's accountability for its agents' activity.
