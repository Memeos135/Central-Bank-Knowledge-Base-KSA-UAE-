# AML Laws

Defined-term node for "AML Laws" as used in the CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation, pointing to the UAE federal AML/CTF decree-law and its implementing Cabinet decision, together with related instructions, guidelines and notices, as amended. It is the anchor for the Regulation's AML/CFT compliance obligation, which requires a Licensed Person conducting Exchange Business to comply with those laws and to maintain comprehensive internal AML/CFT policies, procedures and controls. Binds licensed exchange houses/money transfer providers supervised by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
