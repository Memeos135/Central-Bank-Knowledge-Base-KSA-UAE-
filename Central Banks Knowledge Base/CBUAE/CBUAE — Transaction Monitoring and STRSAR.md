# Transaction Monitoring and STR/SAR

Sets out ongoing monitoring and reporting expectations for PF risk, referencing the obligations under Articles 7 and 16 of the AML-CFT Decision to monitor all transactions for consistency with customer information and to identify activity warranting an STR or SAR. It describes risk-based measures such as PF-calibrated monitoring rules with lower alert thresholds, more frequent periodic reviews for high-risk files, pre-approval of atypical transactions, and lists transactional red-flag indicators. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions and their compliance functions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
