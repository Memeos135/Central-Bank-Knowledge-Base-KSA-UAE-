# Three-Stage Classification System

This node sets out the mandatory three-stage classification system under Article 9, including the Stage 3 sub-categories (3.a–3.c) split by days-past-due with separate wholesale and retail bands for CBUAE reporting. It also fixes the cure and probation conditions for upward migration: Stage 3 to Stage 2 requires settlement of all arrears plus a minimum number of instalments funded from the obligor's own cash flows, and Stage 2 to Stage 1 requires SICR criteria to cease plus prescribed repayment frequencies over the probation period. LFIs may use more granular internal grading but must map it to these categories and justify their assessment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
