# Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 (AML-CFT Law)

Reference node for UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering, Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organizations, cited as the primary statutory basis of the CBUAE TFS guidance. Together with its implementing regulation it requires financial institutions to promptly apply directives of UAE competent authorities implementing UNSC Chapter VII decisions. It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs subject to the UAE AML/CFT framework.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
