# Stored Value Facility (SVF)

Defines a Stored Value Facility as a non-cash facility into which a customer (or someone on their behalf) pays money or Money's Worth in exchange for storage of that value and the issuer's Relevant Undertaking to supply goods/services or make payment to a merchant or third party. The definition covers device-based and non-device-based (network account, e-wallet) facilities and distinguishes single-purpose/closed-loop facilities; Money's Worth extends to reward points, crypto-assets and virtual assets. It determines who falls within the SVF licensing perimeter and, by cross-reference, the retail payment systems framework.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF
