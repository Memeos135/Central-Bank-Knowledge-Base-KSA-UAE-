# Mathematical Reserve

Defines the mathematical reserve for family takaful / insurance of persons and fund accumulation business under the CBUAE takaful financial regulations, including prudent assumption setting, margins for adverse deviation, treatment of negative reserves, guaranteed surrender values, unit-linked business, bonuses and expense allowances. It constrains the actuary's discretion, e.g. barring lapse or surrender allowances that would reduce liabilities. Applies to takaful companies writing life/persons business and to their appointed actuaries.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
