# National AML/CFT Strategy

A concept node within the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions covering the UAE's national AML/CFT strategy framework and the resulting minimum statutory obligations placed on supervised institutions. It orients the reader to the federal legislative architecture (the AML-CFT Law and its implementing Cabinet Decision) and to core duties such as risk identification and assessment, CDD scoping, appointment of a compliance officer, internal controls, suspicious transaction indicators and STR reporting. It binds licensed financial institutions supervised by CBUAE, and their senior management and staff; note this is a UAE instrument, not a SAMA rule.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
