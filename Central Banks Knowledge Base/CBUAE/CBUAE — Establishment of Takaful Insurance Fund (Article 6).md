# Establishment of Takaful Insurance Fund (Article 6)

Article 6 of the CBUAE Takaful Operational Model Standard, a bridging obligation requiring the Company to establish the Takaful Insurance Fund in line with the separate Central Bank Standard on controls and procedures for establishing a Takaful Insurance Fund. The surrounding definitions confirm the Fund's independent legal personality and financial liability, its ownership of donated Contributions, and the Wakala Contract structures between Fund/Participant (principal) and Company (agent). Binds licensed Takaful Insurance Companies, Re-takaful companies and insurers with Takaful Windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
