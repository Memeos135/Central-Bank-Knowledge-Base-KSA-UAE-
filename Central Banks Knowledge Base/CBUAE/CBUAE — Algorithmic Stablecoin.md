# Algorithmic Stablecoin

Concept node for the CBUAE prohibition on Algorithmic Stablecoins: no Person, within the UAE or directing activity at Persons in the UAE, may issue them, perform services relating to them, or promote their issuance or related services. The ban is regulator-agnostic, applying to all Persons including those licensed or regulated by SCA or a Local Licensing Authority for virtual asset activities, and the Central Bank may demand evidence or periodic reporting of compliance.

**Regimes:** payments, other
