# Red Flag Indicators for Sanctions Circumvention

An annex section of the CBUAE targeted financial sanctions guidance listing red-flag situations, drawn from the Executive Office's typologies on circumvention of targeted sanctions against terrorism and WMD proliferation, that may signal attempts by customers or counterparties to evade listings. It is an indicator set rather than a standalone obligation, feeding the suspicion-assessment, monitoring and screening duties that licensed financial institutions (and DNFBPs, by analogy) owe under the UAE AML-CFT framework and Cabinet Decision 74. Relevant to LFIs designing sanctions evasion detection and to escalation/reporting decisions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
