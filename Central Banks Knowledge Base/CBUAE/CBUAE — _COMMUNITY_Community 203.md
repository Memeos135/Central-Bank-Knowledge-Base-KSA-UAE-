# United Nations

## Why this community

The United Nations as the source of international sanctions and targeted financial sanctions listings and of AML/CFT standards that domestic regimes implement.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: functions as the external authority referenced by sanctions/TFS and AML obligations elsewhere in the graph.
- Relevant as the origin of consolidated list obligations (freeze without delay, no funds made available) that local rules transpose.
