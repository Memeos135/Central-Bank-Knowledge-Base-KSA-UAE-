# Suspicious Transaction/Activity Reporting

Concept node on suspicious transaction and suspicious activity reporting in the correspondent banking context, covering when individual transactions or patterns become reportable and the escalation from monitoring alert to STR/SAR filing. It addresses the correspondent institution's use of targeted requests for information (RFIs) to the respondent, including information about the respondent's own customers, to assess reasonableness before deciding to report. Binds LFIs as reporting entities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
