# Internal Auditor Roles

Concept node on internal audit and internal control expectations for insurance companies under the Financial Regulations, particularly around investment activities. It requires documented investment procedures under effective oversight, segregation of duties between execution, recording, authorisation, reconciliation and assurance, and audit procedures giving full coverage of investment activity; where performed internally, the audit function must be independent and report to the Audit Committee or its equivalent in a foreign insurance company's governance structure. It binds the insurance company's board and management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
