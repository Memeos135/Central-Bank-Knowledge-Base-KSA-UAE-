# Fit and Proper Criteria

Sets out the Licensee's obligations to establish, implement and monitor a comprehensive fit-and-proper and suitability assessment framework, with the Board accountable for ensuring Board and Senior Management remain fit and proper on an ongoing basis. Prescribes the minimum content of policies and procedures: minimum criteria, process timing and accountabilities, triggers for reassessment (such as adverse press, risk-profile change, enforcement or supervisory action, performance deficiencies), remedial steps and Central Bank communication where a person is found not fit and proper, and required documentation. Also covers the Central Bank's Authorisation powers, including interviews, meetings with the Licensee, exit interviews, and discretionary bans on unsuccessful applicants.
