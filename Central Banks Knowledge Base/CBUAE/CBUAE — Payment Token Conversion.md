# Payment Token Conversion

A defined activity within the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation: Payment Token Conversion is one of the three licensable Payment Token Services (alongside Payment Token Issuing and Payment Token Custody and Transfer), performed by way of business by a Payment Token Conversion Provider. The node also carries the Central Bank's power to cap the volume or value of Payment Tokens a Conversion Provider may buy, sell or admit to trading on its platform, and to cap or freeze customer on-boarding. It binds licensed Payment Token Conversion Providers and Registered Payment Token Conversion Providers (including Virtual Assets Exchange Platform Operators, Banks and Exchange Houses holding a Non-Objection Registration).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
