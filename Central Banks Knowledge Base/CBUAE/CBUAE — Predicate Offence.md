# Predicate Offence

Concept node explaining predicate offences in the UAE ML framework: ML prosecution is independent of the predicate offence, and suspicion does not require proof that a predicate offence occurred or that proceeds are illicit. It also records that any felony or misdemeanour can serve as a predicate, and lists absolute prohibitions on FIs (anonymous/fictitious accounts, relationships where CDD cannot be completed, dealings with shell banks, invoking secrecy to avoid reporting, bearer shares). Directed at UAE supervised financial institutions and their STR/CDD decision-making.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
