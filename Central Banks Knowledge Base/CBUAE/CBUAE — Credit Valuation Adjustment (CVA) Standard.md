# Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) Standard

The CBUAE Standard governing calculation of risk-weighted assets for credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk, based on the Basel III standardised CVA approach. It scopes in all derivative transactions other than those faced directly with a central counterparty, plus fair-valued securities financing transactions unless the Central Bank deems the CVA loss exposure immaterial, and defines the CVA portfolio as all covered transactions together with eligible CVA hedges. Applies to all UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
