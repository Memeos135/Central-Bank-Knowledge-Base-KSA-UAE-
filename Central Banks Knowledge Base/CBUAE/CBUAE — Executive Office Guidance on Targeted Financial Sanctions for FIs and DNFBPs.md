# Executive Office Guidance on Targeted Financial Sanctions for FIs and DNFBPs

Orientation node for the CBUAE-issued guidance on targeted financial sanctions, which sits on top of the Executive Office's TFS guidance for FIs and DNFBPs and rests on UAE Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018, Cabinet Decision 10 of 2019 and Cabinet Decision 74 of 2020. It explains the institutional architecture (Executive Office as national TFS coordinator, UN Consolidated List and Local Terrorist List sourcing and registration for notifications) and expectations on customer/name screening, false-positive handling and detection of sanctions evasion. It binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions (LFIs), including banks, exchange houses, finance companies, payment service providers, registered hawala providers and insurers; note this is a UAE instrument, not SAMA, and is relevant only comparatively for KSA work.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
