# Minimum Paid-up Capital

Establishes minimum Paid-up Capital and related financial-ratio obligations for Exchange Business licensees, including the requirement of a CBUAE no-objection (LNO) before any increase or decrease, CBUAE discretion to impose additional capital based on risk, size and complexity, ongoing compliance after licensing, and evidence of source of funds for capital injections. It also fixes the Paid-up Capital to equity ratio and remediation where the ratio falls below the stipulated level. Binds applicants and Licensed Persons across licence categories.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, governance/risk
