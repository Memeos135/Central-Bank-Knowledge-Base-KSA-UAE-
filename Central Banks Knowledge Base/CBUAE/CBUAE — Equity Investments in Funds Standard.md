# Equity Investments in Funds Standard

The CBUAE Standard on capital requirements for banks' banking-book equity investments in funds, aligned to BCBS 266, requiring RWA to be computed as if the bank held the fund's underlying exposures directly, via a hierarchy of look-through, mandate-based and fall-back approaches. It covers off-balance-sheet exposures such as unfunded commitments to future capital calls, excludes exposures deducted from capital, and requires a leverage adjustment (assets to equity) applied to the fund's average risk weight, with an overall 1250% risk weight cap. Applies to all UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
