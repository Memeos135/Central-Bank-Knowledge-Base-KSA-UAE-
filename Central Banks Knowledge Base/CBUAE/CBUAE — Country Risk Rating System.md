# Country Risk Rating System

A concept node on the internal country risk assessment and rating approach expected under the CBUAE Country and Transfer Risk Standards (154/2018). It covers quantitative and qualitative ongoing assessment of each country of exposure, formal analysis at least annually with interim monitoring, the risk categories to be captured in the board-approved risk appetite statement, and the setting and monitoring of individual and aggregate country limits. It binds banks with material country and transfer risk exposure, whose boards and senior management must receive bank-wide (and where relevant group-wide) exposure reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
