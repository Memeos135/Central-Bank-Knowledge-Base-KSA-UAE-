# Standard Customer Due Diligence

Concept node setting out baseline CDD applied to a respondent institution at onboarding and on an ongoing basis: identification and verification via reliable independent sources (corporate and beneficial ownership registries, licensing authorities), identification and reasonable verification of UBOs, understanding the nature and purpose of the relationship for risk profiling, confirming the respondent is not a shell bank, and event-driven and periodic refresh. It also touches minimum due diligence for transactional non-customer RMA (SWIFT relationship management application) counterparties, including name screening against PEP and sanctions lists. Binds CBUAE-supervised LFIs; cross-refers to the CBUAE CDD/KYC and record-keeping guidance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, banking prudential
