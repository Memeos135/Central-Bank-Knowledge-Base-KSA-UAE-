# AML-CFT Law (Decree-Law 20/2018)

Node capturing the UAE AML-CFT legal anchor (Federal Decree-Law 20/2018 and its implementing Cabinet Decision) as referenced in the cash-intensive business guidance, principally the obligation to report suspicious transactions or activity to the UAE FIU whenever there are reasonable grounds to suspect a link to criminal proceeds or a crime, regardless of amount or whether the transaction was completed. It also frames the duty to maintain policies, procedures and controls to identify, assess and mitigate risk in higher-risk relationships. Binds LFIs and other regulated entities under UAE law.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
