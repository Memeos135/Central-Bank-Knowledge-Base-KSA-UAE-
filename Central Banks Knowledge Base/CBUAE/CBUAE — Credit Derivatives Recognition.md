# Credit Derivatives Recognition

Node on the regulatory treatment of credit derivatives in the CBUAE capital framework, spanning their recognition as unfunded credit protection, the meaning of a written credit derivative and offsetting of purchased protection for leverage ratio purposes, and the counterparty credit risk add-on calculation for credit derivative hedging sets. It addresses conditions such as reference-name identity, pool versus single-name protection, and supervisory factors by rating. Applies to CBUAE-licensed banks measuring capital, leverage and counterparty exposure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
