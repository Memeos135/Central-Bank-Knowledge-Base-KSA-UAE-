# Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)

Concept node for the Net Stable Funding Ratio: a structural ratio of available stable funding to required stable funding, to be maintained at 100% or more on an ongoing basis over a one-year horizon. It complements the LCR by limiting maturity cliff effects, and the underlying BCBS October 2014 standard prevails over the guidance manual where there is ambiguity. It applies to banks approved under the Basel III liquidity framework, with reporting preceding the compliance date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
