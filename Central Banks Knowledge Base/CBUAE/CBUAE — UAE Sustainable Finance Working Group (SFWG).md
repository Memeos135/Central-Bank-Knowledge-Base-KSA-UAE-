# UAE Sustainable Finance Working Group (SFWG)

Institutional node describing the UAE Sustainable Finance Working Group, established in 2019 as a cross-government and cross-regulator body (ministries, CBUAE, SCA, ADGM FSRA, DFSA and the UAE exchanges) to promote sustainable finance nationally. Its outputs include the 2020 Guiding Principles on Sustainable Finance and public statements setting three workstreams on ESG reporting, corporate governance and risk management, and taxonomy. It is a standard-setting/coordination forum, not a licensing authority; its principles take effect through each member regulator's own legal framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
