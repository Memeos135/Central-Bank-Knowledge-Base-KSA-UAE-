# Standards Re Risk Management Requirements for Islamic Banks

The baseline CBUAE Standards on risk management for Islamic Banks, referenced here as the framework that the Profit Equalization Standard supplements with additional requirements. Relevant mainly as the parent instrument governing identification, measurement and governance of Islamic banking risks, including the risk types addressed by profit equalization techniques. Binds Islamic Banks and banks operating an Islamic Window in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
