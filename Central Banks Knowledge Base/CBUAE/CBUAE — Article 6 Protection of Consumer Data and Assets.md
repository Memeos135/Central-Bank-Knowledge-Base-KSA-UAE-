# Article 6: Protection of Consumer Data and Assets

Article 6 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation/Standards sets the core duties of Licensed Financial Institutions on collection, protection, confidentiality and authorised use of Consumer Data and Personal Data, anchored in Article 120 of Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018. It requires documented policies, procedures and control frameworks, written disclosure to Consumers on how their data is processed, a secure and confidential environment across all delivery channels, and confidentiality that survives outsourcing to Authorized Agents, subject only to legally compelled disclosure or the Consumer's expressed consent. It binds all CBUAE Licensed Financial Institutions and extends to profiling, data mining, marketing and sale of services via new technologies and social media.
