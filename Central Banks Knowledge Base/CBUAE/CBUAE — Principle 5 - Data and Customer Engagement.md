# Principle 5 - Data and Customer Engagement

Principle 5 of the CBUAE/SFWG Principles for Climate Transition Planning (UAE), covering data strategies and customer engagement. It expects financial firms to collect customer data regularly, set criteria for identifying and prioritising customers for engagement, tailor engagement goals and methods, and feed engagement outcomes back into transition objectives, product offerings and escalation arrangements. Applies to UAE 'financial firms' as guidance; note the 'customer' concept here is commercial/climate-related and distinct from AML/CTF customer due diligence obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
