# Sanctions Screening of Trade Transactions

Node describing expected sanctions screening practice for trade transactions: screening the data points carried in trade-specific financial messaging (originator, beneficiary, ultimate parties, country codes, intermediary banks, free-text high-risk terms) and screening names of individuals, entities and vessels irrespective of their role, plus the trade documents made available to the institution. It links sanctions/TFS obligations to the TBML and illicit transshipment typologies elsewhere in the guidance. Applies to CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; treat as supervisory expectation rather than KSA obligation.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
