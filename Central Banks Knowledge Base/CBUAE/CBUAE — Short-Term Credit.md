# Short-Term Credit

Defined product category within the Finance Companies Regulation governing small-ticket short-term lending by Restricted Licence Finance Companies and their Agents. Core obligations: no interest may be charged; total fees including late-payment fees are capped at 30% of the original credit (maximum recoverable 130%, including after restructuring, rescheduling or roll-over); repayment term capped at twelve months from the original agreement; the original term must be honoured with no early demand for full payment; and borrower movable or immovable assets may not be taken as security. The adjacent credit-reporting article requires reporting borrower data to the Credit Information Agency, mandatory credit enquiry before extending credit of AED 5,000 or more, and express borrower consent for sharing or accessing credit information, plus data-protection compliance.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, data/credit info
