# Annex III: Card Scheme Reporting

A node drawn from the CBUAE Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation covering the Card Scheme strand: the defined terms that scope the regime (Card-based Payment Transactions, Card Issuer, Cardholder, Card Scheme, Card Scheme Governing Body, Card Scheme Licence, Category I and II Licences) together with ongoing conduct obligations on licensed Card Schemes. The obligations shown are governance-facing — a Board-approved compliance policy, conflicts-of-interest and code-of-conduct requirements, internal escalation of suspicious transactions to the AML/CTF compliance officer and reporting to the competent authority, and an independent, permanent internal audit function operating under a Board-audit-committee-approved charter. It binds Card Schemes licensed by CBUAE and their Boards and Management; note this is a UAE instrument, relevant to SAMA work only as a comparator, not as KSA law.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, AML/CTF

The annex relies on the regulation's defined terms — including the distinction between three-party and four-party scheme structures and the underlying acquiring and instrument-issuing service categories — so scope and licensing triggers are set by the regulation, not the annex. Practically, a scheme classified under the regulation must map its activities to those definitions first; the annex then fixes the content and cadence of what it reports to the regulator.
