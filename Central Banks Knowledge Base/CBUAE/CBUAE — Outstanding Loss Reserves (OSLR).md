# Outstanding Loss Reserves (OSLR)

One of the mandatory technical provisions listed in the Takaful financial regulations: the Outstanding Loss Reserve (case reserves) is calculated for each claim reported but still outstanding at the reporting date. It sits alongside UPR, URR, IBNR, loss adjustment expense reserves and mathematical reserves, and investments equivalent to OSLR (net of Re-Takaful) must be held inside the UAE. Binds Takaful and Re-Takaful companies in provisioning, asset localisation and solvency reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
