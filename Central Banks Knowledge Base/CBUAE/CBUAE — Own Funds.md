# Own Funds

Defines the capital resources eligible to cover Takaful solvency requirements: Own Funds equal Basic Own Funds (excess of admissible assets over liabilities, less treasury shares, plus approved subordinated liabilities) plus Ancillary Own Funds (loss-absorbing items callable with prior Authority approval, e.g. rights to call supplementary contribution). Sets eligibility limits: the Minimum Capital Requirement must be met 100% by Basic Own Funds, while the Solvency Capital Requirement and Minimum Guarantee Fund are met by Basic Own Funds plus only 50% of Ancillary Own Funds. Binds Takaful and Re-Takaful companies; breaches trigger immediate reporting and an Authority-approved recovery plan.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
