# Non-Objection Registration

Describes the Non-Objection Registration route under the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation, available to a Virtual Assets Exchange Platform Operator, Bank or Exchange House applying to the Central Bank, and the associated application process and conditions. Applicants must disclose their SCA or Local Licensing Authority licensing status and the nature and extent of that licensed business, and demonstrate they will meet the conditions and requirements the Central Bank imposes. It sits alongside the full Payment Token Service Licence and the Foreign Payment Token Issuer Registration as distinct authorisation types, and covers modifications to the scope of a granted registration.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
