# White Paper

Defines the White Paper as the mandatory pre-offer disclosure document for Payment Tokens under the CBUAE regime, specifying required content: descriptions of the holding, storage and transfer arrangements, risks of the issuer, the token, the public offer and the project, governance arrangements including third parties responsible for operation, investment and custody of the Reserve of Assets, the reserve and its custody and segregation, investment policy, and the nature and enforceability of holders' rights including direct redemption and treatment in insolvency. It also sets form requirements — plain, non-misleading language, dated, endorsed by Management, published on the issuer's website only after CBUAE approval. Binds Payment Service Providers issuing Payment Tokens; no public offering may proceed without it.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk
