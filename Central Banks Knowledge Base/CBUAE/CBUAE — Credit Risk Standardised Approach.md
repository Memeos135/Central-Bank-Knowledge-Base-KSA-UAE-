# Credit Risk Standardised Approach

Concept node covering credit risk under the standardised approach in the CBUAE capital adequacy standards, including risk weighting of exposures, specific risk charges on credit derivatives (including first-to-default baskets and hedging offsets), and the supervisory review expectation that banks assess credit risk at borrower and portfolio level. Sets out the minimum coverage of the internal credit review for capital adequacy purposes: rating systems, portfolio aggregation, securitisation and complex credit derivatives, and large exposures/concentrations. Binds banks calculating credit risk-weighted assets and their ICAAP-type internal capital assessments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** Before finalising a risk weight under the standardised approach, the reader must check whether recognised credit risk mitigation applies, because CRM adjusts the exposure or weight produced by the credit risk rules.
