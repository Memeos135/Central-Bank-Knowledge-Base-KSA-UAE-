# Anti-Fraud Framework

## Why this community

Fraud prevention and detection duty: the entity must establish and maintain an anti-fraud framework covering identification, monitoring, reporting and mitigation of fraud affecting its services and customers.

## How members connect

- Standalone article; legally it is a specific-control obligation nested under the broader risk management duty.
- Interacts across regimes — fraud typologies overlap with AML/CTF suspicion reporting and with consumer protection remedies for unauthorised transactions.
- No internal edges: linkage is through shared defined terms (customer, payment transaction) rather than express cross-reference in this cluster.
