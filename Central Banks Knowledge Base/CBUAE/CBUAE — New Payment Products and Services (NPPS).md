# New Payment Products and Services (NPPS)

This node is the concept of New Payment Products and Services within the CBUAE payments AML/CFT guidance: internet- and smartphone-based payment applications, prepaid cards and devices, and similar innovative channels offered by both banks and non-traditional participants. It matters because such participants may be regulated as financial institutions in some jurisdictions and not others, creating direct and indirect exposure for UAE LFIs. It shapes risk assessment and customer due diligence expectations for LFIs dealing with these providers or their underlying users, including crowdfunding platforms and charities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
