# IA Board Resolution No. 11 of 2016 Pricing Policy Revision

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 11 of 2016 requires insurers writing property and liability business to have their pricing policy reviewed and assessed twice per financial year by an Actuary licensed and registered with the Authority. It prescribes the minimum content of the actuarial report (risk factors, expense and reinsurance ratios, profit margin, claims history and IBNR provisioning, pricing controls, product-level adequacy, impact on financial position and on policyholder protection), sets submission and management-response deadlines, and empowers the Director General to order corrective measures.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
