# Complaint

Sets out admissibility and handling rules for a Complaint before the CBUAE Ombudsman Unit: prerequisites (prior escalation to the Licensed Financial Institution or Insurance Company and a 30 complete business day response window), time-bar limits, standing of Complainants, filing format, withdrawal, and grounds for refusal or discontinuance (frivolous or bad-faith complaints, no vested interest, matters better suited to Courts, previously decided matters absent new material evidence, or a reasonable settlement offer). Notably excludes complaints materially relating to an institution's risk management, internal pricing policy, or Anti-Money Laundering policies and practices. Binds Complainants, Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies; the Ombudsman Unit's decision on jurisdiction and acceptance is final.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk
