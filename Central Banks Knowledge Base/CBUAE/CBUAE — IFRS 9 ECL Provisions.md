# IFRS 9 ECL Provisions

Concept node for IFRS 9 expected credit loss provisions as treated under the CBUAE transitional-arrangements regulation: only Stage 1 and Stage 2 IFRS 9 provisions qualify for the phase-in, measured as the excess over the 31 December 2019 baseline. It expressly does not cover the separate CBUAE provisioning requirements under the loan classification rules (Circular 28/2010), and it supersedes the transitional guidance in the March 2018 IFRS 9 implementation guidance note. Useful for confirming which provisions are in or out of scope of capital add-back for banks and finance companies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
