# Country and Transfer Risk Regulation (Circular 154/2018)

CBUAE Country and Transfer Risk Regulation issued by Circular 154/2018, addressed to all banks and made under the Central Bank Law, with accompanying Standards. It sets minimum acceptable requirements for banks' management of country risk and transfer risk (the risk a borrower cannot convert local currency to foreign exchange to service foreign-currency debt), requiring a risk governance framework with policies and procedures to identify, measure, monitor and report such risks across international funding, lending and investment, on a bank-wide or group-wide basis including intra-group exposures. Banks with material exposure must additionally have board-approved risk appetite limits, documented roles and responsibilities, and a definition of materiality reflecting cross-border exposures relative to total business; the Central Bank may impose requirements beyond the listed minimums.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
