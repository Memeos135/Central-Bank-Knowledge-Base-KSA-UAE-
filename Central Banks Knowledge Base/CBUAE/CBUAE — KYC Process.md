# KYC Process

Sets the KYC framework for Licensed Persons in exchange business: a risk-based obligation to identify customers and satisfy themselves that funds have legitimate sources and uses, understand the purpose of the relationship and the customer's ownership and control structure. It distinguishes three tiers — Customer Identification, Customer Due Diligence and Enhanced Due Diligence — applied according to ML/FT risk and specified transaction value thresholds, and requires refusal or termination plus consideration of suspicious reporting to the FIU where KYC cannot be completed. Additional provisions address repeat low-value exchanges, acceptable identification such as seafarer documents within capped weekly limits, and demonstrating adequacy to CBUAE examiners.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
