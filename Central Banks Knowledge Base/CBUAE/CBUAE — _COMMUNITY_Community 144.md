# Sustainable Finance Disclosure

## Why this community

Sustainable finance disclosure expectations for reporting entities, drawing on international sustainability reporting standards and a governance/strategy/risk-metrics disclosure principle. Note the scope limit: this material originates from a UAE working group, so it is comparative or guidance-level context rather than a SAMA enforceable obligation.

## How members connect

- The working group is the issuing body; the reporting-entity concept defines the addressee of the disclosure expectations, creating the who-must-disclose link.
- Principle 3 attaches substantive disclosure content (governance arrangements, strategy, risk management and metrics) to those same reporting entities.
- The ISSB reference operates as an external standard-setting anchor, i.e. the principles are framed as convergence with an international baseline rather than as free-standing rules.
- Hierarchy runs standard-setter (ISSB) -> national/regional working-group guidance -> principle-level disclosure expectations on entities.
