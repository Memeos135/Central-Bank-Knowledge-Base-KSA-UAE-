# Article 32: Certainty of Transfers of Payment Tokens

Article 32 is the final business-conduct provision of the payment token conduct block (Articles 25–32) and addresses certainty and finality of payment token transfers, including the extent to which a provider is responsible for completion timing given execution on Distributed Ledger Technology, alongside a requirement that a comprehensive audit trail be available to the Customer. It bears on disclosure expectations to Customers about settlement on the ledger. It binds licensed payment token service providers dealing with Customers.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
