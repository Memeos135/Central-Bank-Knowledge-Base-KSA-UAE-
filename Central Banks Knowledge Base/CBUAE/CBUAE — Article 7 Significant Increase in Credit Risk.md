# Article 7: Significant Increase in Credit Risk

Article 7 requires LFIs to regularly assess and document whether credit risk on a financial instrument has increased significantly since initial recognition, aligning the assessment with accounting standards, their internal SICR policy and the regulation's accompanying standards. SICR findings must drive facility classification and associated provisioning, and the SICR methodology must apply to all Obligor types and be consistent with the unlikeliness-to-pay test in Article 6. It binds all LFIs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
