# BoD Decision 3/2010 - Code of Conduct and Ethics

Node for the Insurance Authority Board of Directors' Decision No. 3 of 2010 on the Code of Conduct and Ethics for insurance companies operating in the State, as amended. It is incorporated by reference into the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies Regulation, which requires insurers to observe it alongside the insurance principles of utmost good faith, disclosure and transparency when issuing policies and dealing with customers. It binds licensed insurance companies (including branches of foreign insurers and Takaful companies) in their market conduct toward applicants, insured persons and beneficiaries.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
