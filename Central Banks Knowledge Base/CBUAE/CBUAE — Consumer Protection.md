# Consumer Protection

Node on Article 7 of the CBUAE dormant accounts regime, which preserves the customer's (or heirs') ownership of dormant balances and unclaimed funds and constrains institution conduct: interest continues to accrue on interest-bearing accounts until transfer to the Central Bank, no fees beyond those already agreed may be charged, no debits or system-based charges may be applied, credits may still be received, and statements are suppressed without excluding the customer from other communications. It also requires dormancy terms to be disclosed in customer documentation, alongside customer-side expectations on updating contact details and retaining correspondence. Binds licensed financial institutions in their dealings with retail and other customers.
