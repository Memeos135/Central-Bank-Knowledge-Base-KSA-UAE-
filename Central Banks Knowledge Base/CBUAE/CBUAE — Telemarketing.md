# Telemarketing

Covers the substantive conduct rules for Telemarketing by Licensed Financial Institutions: prohibition on contacting opted-out or DNCR-listed Customers, mandatory telemarketing logs and records across all communication channels in the form and timeframe set by the Central Bank, a ban on untrue or fraudulent statements with full disclosure of Product/Service features, risks, charges and terms, immediate termination on Customer request, and restrictions on calling time (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM State time) and frequency. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions including where telemarketing is performed by Telemarketers on their behalf.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
