# Innovative Solution Owner

Defined-party node for the Innovative Solution Owner, the participant admitted to the insurance FinTech experimental environment. It sets out that party's obligations: identifying and controlling risks from the solution, readiness and testing methodology, an exit plan protecting insureds and beneficiaries, periodic risk reporting, and compliance with Authority-imposed restrictions on customer numbers, transaction type and size, holding client funds and customer consents/disclosures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF, other
