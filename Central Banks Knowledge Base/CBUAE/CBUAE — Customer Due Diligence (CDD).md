# Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

A cross-cutting concept node on Customer Due Diligence as applied across CBUAE-licensed financial institutions, with detailed treatment for Exchange Houses. It covers customer identification (CID), CDD and EDD triggers by customer type, activity and value (including high-risk natural persons, high-risk circumstances and third-party transactions), beneficial ownership, PEPs, and customer-base risk segmentation. It also anchors ongoing monitoring under Article 7 of the AML-CFT Decision: profiles must be reviewed and refreshed at least annually or on expiry of ID/trade licence/authorised-signatory ID, whichever is earlier.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
