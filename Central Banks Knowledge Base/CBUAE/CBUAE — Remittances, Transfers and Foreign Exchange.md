# Remittances, Transfers and Foreign Exchange

Consumer protection provisions specific to remittance, transfer and foreign exchange products and services, layered on the general requirements of the relevant section. Requires clear disclosure of all fees and applicable buy/sell exchange rates (including best efforts to disclose or estimate correspondent bank and disbursing provider fees), prominent in-branch display of buy/sell rates for major currencies and remittance buy rates, and prescribed warning statements in advertising about correspondent-side fees, penalties for customer error or incomplete information, and FX-rate exposure. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions, including exchange houses and money transfer providers, in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments
