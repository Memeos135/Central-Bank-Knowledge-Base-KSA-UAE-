# Training

Node combining AML/CFT governance, independent audit, training and record-keeping expectations across two CBUAE instruments. It sets the three-lines-of-defence model with senior management and board oversight, MLRO/compliance authority and independence, periodic and risk-calibrated TBML training with attendance records and knowledge testing, and record retention under Article 16 of the AML-CFT Law and Article 24 of the AML-CFT Decision covering risk assessments, transactions, CDD material, account files and SAR/STR analysis. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
