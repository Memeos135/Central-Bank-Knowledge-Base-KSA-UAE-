# Central Bank Governance & Authority

## Why this community

Constitutional and institutional layer of central banking: the central bank's legal mandate, the Governor's powers, licensing of financial activities/institutions, resolution powers over licensees, oversight of financial market infrastructures, currency issuance, and the administrative/financial sanctioning regime with its appeal route.

## How members connect

- All nodes hang off the central bank as the source of authority; licensing, resolution, FMI designation and currency are separate heads of the same statutory mandate.
- Definitional chain: 'Licensed Financial Activities' determines who qualifies as a 'Licensed Financial Institution', which in turn fixes the perimeter of resolution and sanctioning powers.
- 'Designated Infrastructure' is a sub-set of 'Financial Market Infrastructure', triggering heightened oversight obligations.
- Enforcement chain: sanctions imposed by the authority are reviewable by the Grievances & Appeals Committee — the due-process counterweight to the sanctioning power.
