# Article 27: Customer Agreement

Article 27 requires every Payment Token Service Provider (defined here as a Licensee or Registree, excluding Registered Foreign Payment Token Issuers) to set out its contractual terms with each Customer in a Customer Agreement — a Framework Agreement or Single Payment Token Service Agreement — supplied far enough in advance for an informed decision, and to give Customers and Tokenholders a copy on request. It also covers advance notice of at least 30 calendar days for term changes, a free termination right where the Customer rejects revised terms, and extension of the agreement's rights and obligations to Tokenholders even where the issuer does not know their identity.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments
