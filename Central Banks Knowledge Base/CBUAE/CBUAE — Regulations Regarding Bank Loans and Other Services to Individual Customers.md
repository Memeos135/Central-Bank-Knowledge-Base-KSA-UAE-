# Regulations Regarding Bank Loans and Other Services to Individual Customers

Cross-referenced CBUAE instrument governing bank loans and other services offered to individual customers, invoked by the mortgage loans regulations as the source of the maximum debt burden ratio (stated there as 50 per cent of gross salary and any regular income from a defined and specific source at any time). The mortgage rules direct that this ceiling is a cap, not a default: lenders must still assess the individual borrower's repayment capacity and exposure. Relevant to banks and finance companies lending to retail/individual customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
