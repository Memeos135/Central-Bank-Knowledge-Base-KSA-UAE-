# Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies (7031)

CBUAE Circular No. 5/2026 (13/03/2026), the Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies, setting minimum requirements for responsible, risk-sensitive remuneration frameworks so that pay is commensurate with performance and risk outcomes. It applies to all Banks and Insurance Companies (including Reinsurance Companies), does not prescribe pay amounts, and is read with the Central Bank Law and related regulations on risk management, corporate governance, consumer protection, fitness and propriety, and Shari'ah governance; it takes precedence where it conflicts with other regulations. Compliance must be demonstrable to the Central Bank on a continuous basis.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
