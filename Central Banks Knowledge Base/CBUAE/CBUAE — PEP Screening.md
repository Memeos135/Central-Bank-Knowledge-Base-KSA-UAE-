# PEP Screening

Node on identifying and classifying customers and beneficial owners as PEPs or Related Customers, which the guidance expects to occur at the CDD stage before the relationship begins, with ongoing screening thereafter. It notes the differing legal standards (risk management systems for foreign PEPs; sufficient measures for domestic PEPs and HIOs) but recommends screening all customers in practice, including nationals who may qualify as foreign PEPs, and touches on looking beyond the 25% beneficial ownership threshold where PEP families hold fragmented stakes. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
