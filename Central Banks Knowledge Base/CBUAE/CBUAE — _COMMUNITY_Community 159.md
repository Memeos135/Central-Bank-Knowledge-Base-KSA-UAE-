# Group Risk Management

## Why this community

Group-level risk management duties — how risks arising from, or shared with, affiliated entities are identified, controlled and reported, and the extent to which group frameworks may be relied on locally.

## How members connect

- Same Regulation/Standards hierarchy as community 158: the Regulation article imposes the duty, the Standards article specifies how compliance is demonstrated.
- The Standards article references the Regulation article, inheriting its scope and defined terms (group, affiliate, risk framework) rather than creating new ones.
- Obligation chain runs into the decision-authority article (Article 7): reliance on group risk functions is constrained by which decisions must remain with the local governing body.
- Group risk outputs feed the disclosure obligation (community 189), linking internal risk reporting to external reporting duties.
