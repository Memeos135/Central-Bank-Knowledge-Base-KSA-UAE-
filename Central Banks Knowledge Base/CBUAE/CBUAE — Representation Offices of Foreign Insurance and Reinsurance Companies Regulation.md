# Representation Offices of Foreign Insurance and Reinsurance Companies Regulation

CBUAE Circular No. 5 of 13 February 2025 issuing the Regulation on Representation Offices of Foreign Insurance Companies and Foreign Reinsurance Companies, setting licensing, regulatory and supervisory rules for such offices operating in the UAE. It must be read with the Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law 14/2018 as amended), the Insurance Activities Law (48/2023), the Commercial Companies Law (32/2021), the Financial Free Zones Law (8/2004) and the supervision fees Cabinet Resolution. It binds representation offices of foreign (re)insurers in the State and their staff — not a licence to carry on insurance business.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
