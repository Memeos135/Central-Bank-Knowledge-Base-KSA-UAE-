# Shareholders' Account

Article 14 (with related definitions) defines the Shareholders' Account as the account representing the Company's own assets and liabilities and lists the revenue streams it may receive from takaful business: Wakala Fee from the Fund, Wakala Bi Al-Istithmar fee from the Participants' Investment Account, investment returns on shareholder funds, the performance incentive for generating Surplus, and repayment of Qard Hasan. It also confirms the Fund may grant up to 10% of Surplus to the Company and that the Shareholders' Account must meet its own obligations and expenses. It binds Takaful Insurance Companies in segregating shareholder and Fund monies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
