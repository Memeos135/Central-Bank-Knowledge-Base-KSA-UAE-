# Central Bank Law (Federal Decree-Law 6/2025)

This node is the Central Bank Law — Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 on the Central Bank, Regulation of Financial Institutions and Activities, and Insurance Business — the enabling statute under which CBUAE regulations (including the Telemarketing Regulation) are issued. It underpins licensing of Banks and other Licensed Financial Institutions and supplies foundational defined terms used across CBUAE instruments (Affiliate, Bank, Board, Chief Executive Officer, Central Bank). It binds all entities carrying on Licensed Financial Activities and insurance business in the State, and establishes the Central Bank's rule-making and supervisory authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
