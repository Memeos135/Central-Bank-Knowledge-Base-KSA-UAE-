# Article 2: Responsibilities of the Board

This node covers the board responsibilities article of the Corporate Governance Standards: board composition, size and use of committees, annual self-assessment of the board, its committees and individual members (with an external independent assessment at least every five years), fit and proper suitability review, and chairman duties on agenda, participation and shareholder communication. It also addresses quorum and voting, coordination between audit and risk committees, and independent Shari'ah compliance oversight for banks offering Islamic financial services. It binds the boards of all licensed banks, with local governance structures required for branches of foreign banks.
