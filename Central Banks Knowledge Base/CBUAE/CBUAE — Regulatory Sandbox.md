# Regulatory Sandbox

Defines the Regulatory Sandbox as a supervisory tool allowing accepted Participants to test innovative financial products, services and business models within a defined space and time under Central Bank oversight, in lieu of holding a Licence. Sets scope limits: it is unavailable for deposit-taking of all types, insurance activities in the State, and acting as principal in financial products affecting the person's financial position (e.g. FX, derivatives, bonds and sukuk, equities, commodities). Licensed Financial Institutions may not participate; their out-of-scope innovation must instead be discussed with the Central Bank before market deployment.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
