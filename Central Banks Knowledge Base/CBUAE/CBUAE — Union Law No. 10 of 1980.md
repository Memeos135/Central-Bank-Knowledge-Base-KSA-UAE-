# Union Law No. 10 of 1980

This node is the pre-2018 UAE federal banking statute (Union Law No. 10 of 1980 on the Central Bank, the Monetary System and Organization of Banking) as invoked in the CBUAE Regulations No. 29/2011 on bank loans and services offered to individual customers. It functions as the enabling authority for those retail lending/consumer regulations (cited articles 5, 18, 94 and 96) and as the sanctioning basis, with Article 107 supplying the fine imposed per violation and accrued daily once a breach is established. It binds banks and finance companies dealing with individual customers, with violations referred to the Central Bank's Legal Development Unit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk
