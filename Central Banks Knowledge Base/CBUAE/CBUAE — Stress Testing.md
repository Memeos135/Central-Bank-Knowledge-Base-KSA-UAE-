# Stress Testing

This node aggregates stress testing obligations across several instruments, centred on market risk: a Bank must run supervisory scenarios (both those requiring no simulation, such as reporting largest losses against internal capital measures, and those requiring simulated stress scenarios), plus internally developed scenarios reflecting its own portfolio characteristics, and submit results to the regulator. Scenario design must cover past periods of significant market disturbance and sensitivity to shifts in volatility and correlation assumptions, including extreme historical values. It binds Banks and interacts with back-testing, internal model validation and capital adequacy assessment.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
