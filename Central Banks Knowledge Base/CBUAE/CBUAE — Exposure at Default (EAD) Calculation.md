# Exposure at Default (EAD) Calculation

Covers how exposure amounts are built up for capital and leverage purposes under CBUAE prudential standards: total balance-sheet assets as the starting point for the leverage ratio, exclusion of derivative and SFT-related balance-sheet items from the on-balance-sheet component, gross treatment of collateral, and credit conversion factors for commitments (including the 10% CCF for unconditionally cancellable commitments to issue direct credit substitutes versus 100% for securitisation-related items). Applies to banks calculating risk-weighted assets and the leverage ratio. Consult it to resolve which bucket an exposure falls into and which CCF applies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
