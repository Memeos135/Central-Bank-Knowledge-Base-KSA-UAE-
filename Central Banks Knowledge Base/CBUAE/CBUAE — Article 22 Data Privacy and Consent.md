# Article 22: Data Privacy and Consent

Article 22 of the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation sets the consent standard for processing Personal Data in Open Finance: explicit, purpose-specific, informed, unambiguous and freely given consent expressed through a clear affirmative act, with consent invalid where it extends beyond data necessary for the stated purpose. It also requires anonymisation or separate consent where User Data contains Personal Data of third-party natural persons, prescribes the transaction details consent must cover for Service Initiation, and preserves the prohibitions on Sensitive Data processing elsewhere in the Regulation. It binds Open Finance Providers (and Licensees generally) and is expressly without prejudice to other applicable data protection laws.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, payments, consumer protection
