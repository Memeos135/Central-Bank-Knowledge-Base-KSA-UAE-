# Tranche Thickness

Concept node explaining tranche thickness, the size of a securitisation tranche relative to the whole transaction, measured as the gap between attachment and detachment points. It is an input to risk-weight calculations under both SEC-ERBA and SEC-SA, with thinner tranches generally riskier at a given attachment point and non-senior tranches subject to a thickness adjustment subject to a floor. Applies to banks computing capital on securitisation positions under the CBUAE framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
