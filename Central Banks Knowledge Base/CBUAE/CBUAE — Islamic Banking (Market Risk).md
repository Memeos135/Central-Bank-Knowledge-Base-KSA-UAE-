# Islamic Banking (Market Risk)

Article 8 of the CBUAE Market Risk Regulation, addressing application of the market risk framework to Islamic banking activity, within an instrument whose core is the market risk governance framework (Article 2) and systems and controls (Article 3). Those articles require a Board-approved market risk strategy, policies and processes giving a bank-wide and group-wide view of market risk, clear roles, separation of duties and Senior Management implementation under Board oversight. Binds banks, including those conducting Islamic banking business, subject to Shari'ah-compliant adaptation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
