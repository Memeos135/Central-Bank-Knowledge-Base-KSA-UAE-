# High-Risk Countries Requirements

Concept node on the risk-based approach and enhanced due diligence obligations, including the specific duty to apply CDD measures and countermeasures toward High Risk Countries designated by the UAE National Committee, reflecting UN Security Council Chapter VII decisions, the Terrorist Financing Suppression Convention, the NPT, and FATF/FSRB calls. It links business-wide ML/FT risk assessment (inherent versus residual risk, control gaps, risk appetite) to EDD triggers such as foreign PEPs, source of funds enquiry and intensified ongoing monitoring. Binds UAE supervised financial institutions, which must maintain internal policies and controls proportionate to size and risk.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — FATF|FATF]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding whether enhanced due diligence or countermeasures are triggered for a jurisdiction, the FATF public listings are an operative input, not merely background: the high-risk country requirements direct institutions to the FATF call-for-action and increased-monitoring lists alongside national committee guidance and the NRA. Because those lists are periodically revised, country risk classifications and the associated EDD triggers must be refreshed against the current listing rather than a static internal table. Consequence: a jurisdiction newly listed can change the required due diligence level without any change to the domestic rule text.
