# Islamic Financial Institution

Defined-term node for Islamic Financial Institution (IFI) as used in CBUAE Shari'ah governance instruments, covering full-fledged Islamic institutions and, where indicated, institutions operating Islamic windows. Being an IFI triggers obligations to maintain Shari'ah governance policies, an ISSC with supervisory authority over all activities, products, contracts, policies, financial statements and profit allocation, and internal (and where applicable external) Shari'ah audit, within the parameters set by the Higher Shari'ah Authority. Senior management remains responsible for the institution's actual compliance with Islamic Shari'ah.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
