# National Shareholding in Banks Regulation

CBUAE Circular No. 18/2021 (15/11/2021) setting the regulation on national shareholding in banks, requiring that national shareholding never fall below sixty percent of the capital of banks incorporated in the UAE, with natural persons holding that share being UAE citizens and juridical-person ownership computed by look-through to citizen shareholding. It places duties on the board and shareholders to take all reasonable measures to maintain compliance, including scheduling general assembly items, amending articles of association, or issuing reasoned voting advice, and to ensure shareholder voting complies with the Central Bank Law. It binds all banks incorporated in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
