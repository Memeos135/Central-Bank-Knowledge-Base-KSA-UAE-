# Participation Membership Policy (Article 9)

Article 9 requires the Takaful Company, acting as agent for the Fund, to prepare and sign with the Participant a Participation Membership Policy regulating the Participant–Fund relationship, drafted in Arabic (or with accurate Arabic translation) and matched to the takaful type and class. It prescribes minimum disclosure content in clear, non-misleading language, including definitions of the Fund and its Charter, membership term, contribution allocation, surplus distribution and deficit-handling policies. Templates must be approved by the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee and Board of Trustees, cleared by the Central Bank and HSA via non-objection, and published on the Company's website and branches.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
