# Related Customer

Node within CBUAE guidance on politically exposed persons dealing with the concept of a Related Customer, i.e. a PEP's direct family members (spouse, children, children's spouses, parents) and close associates (joint ownership or other close business ties, or persons holding interests on the PEP's behalf), who must be treated as if they were PEPs themselves. It explains that the listed relationships are a mandatory minimum rather than an exhaustive list, and that risk rating should reflect the underlying PEP's risk plus the nature, strength and persistence of the connection, including where multiple PEP links exist (higher rating applies). Binds licensed financial institutions supervised by the CBUAE under the AML-CFT Decision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
