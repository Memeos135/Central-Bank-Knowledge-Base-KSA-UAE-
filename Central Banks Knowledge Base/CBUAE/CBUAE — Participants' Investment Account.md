# Participants' Investment Account

This node covers the Participants' Investment Account under the CBUAE Takaful standard: the account holding the investment portion of Contributions for insurance of persons and fund accumulation, managed under a Wakala Bi Al-Istithmar contract between Company and Participant. It sets how Contributions are split (or allocated then partly donated) between the Takaful Insurance Fund and the investment account, requires transparency and material disclosure to Participants, confirms invested amounts are owned by the Participant (including inheritance rules), and identifies income sources and the Company's duty to ensure the account meets its obligations. It binds licensed Takaful insurers writing fund-accumulation products.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
