# Article 6: Information Systems and Internal Reporting (Regulation)

Article 6 of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (153/2018) requires a Bank to maintain information systems capable of measuring, assessing and reporting the size, composition and quality of risk exposures bank-wide and, where applicable, group-wide, across all risk types, products and counterparties. Reports must reach the Board and Senior Management on a timely basis and in formats they can use and understand. This is a reporting/MI obligation on banks, linked to the wider risk governance framework and its compliance and internal audit components.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
