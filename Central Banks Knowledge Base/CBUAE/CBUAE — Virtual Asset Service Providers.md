# Virtual Asset Service Providers

A defined category under the Executive Regulations for Virtual Asset Service Providers, requiring any person conducting VASP activities, offering related products or services, or executing transactions from within the State to be licensed, registered or listed by the competent Supervisory Authority. VASPs must comply with specified AML/CFT obligations proportionate to their activities, and the Supervisory Authority is tasked with detecting and sanctioning unlicensed operators using public and non-public sources. Relevant for perimeter questions on virtual asset activity and for the overlap between VASP duties and those of Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
