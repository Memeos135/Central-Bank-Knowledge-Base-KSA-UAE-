# Financial Reporting and External Audit Standards

The CBUAE Financial Reporting and External Audit Standards (162/2018) govern the appointment, rotation, independence and oversight of a Bank's External Auditor and the Board audit committee's supervisory duties. They require re-tendering and firm rotation at least every six years with a three-year cooling-off period, audit partner rotation every three years, annual assessment of auditor quality, committee review of the audit plan, representation letter and significant audit matters, and an approved policy on permitted and prohibited non-audit services (read together with the UAE auditing profession law and the IESBA Code of Ethics). They bind Banks, their Boards and audit committees, and shape the terms on which External Auditors may be engaged.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
