# CBUAE Regulations re Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business

Parent instrument: the CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation issued by Circular 7/2025 (16 June 2025) to exchange houses under the Central Bank Law, covering licensing, licence categories and permitted activities (currency exchange, remittances, WPS salary processing), ongoing supervision and enforcement. It also confirms that the accompanying Standards are legally binding and elaborate the detailed obligations. Binds any Person carrying on or engaging in Exchange Business in the UAE and applicants for such licences.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
