# e-KYC / User Identification and Verification

Section of the CBUAE payment-sector AML/CFT guidance dealing with CDD, EDD and ongoing monitoring where onboarding is digital rather than face-to-face (e-KYC). It confirms that payment-sector participants (stored value facilities, retail payment services, card schemes) generally form business relationships rather than occasional-customer dealings, so the full CDD set applies to every customer type, with added emphasis on confirming the person using the service is the identified customer and on ID-document verification. It binds LFIs offering or exposed to payment products and services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
