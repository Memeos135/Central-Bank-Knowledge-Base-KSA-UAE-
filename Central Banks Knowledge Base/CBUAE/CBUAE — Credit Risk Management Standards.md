# Credit Risk Management Standards

The accompanying CBUAE Credit Risk Standards, mandatory and enforceable on the same footing as the Credit Risk Management Regulation. They set the scope of the credit risk management framework (group-wide view, all products, activities, geographies and instruments including off-balance-sheet and derivatives), its alignment with risk appetite and risk profile, climate-related credit risk, and the detailed powers and duties of the Credit Risk Management Function and the CRO. Binds LFIs and, operationally, their boards, senior management and risk functions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
