# Role of Board of Directors (Article 4)

Article 4 allocates ultimate, non-delegable responsibility to Board members for ensuring the LFI has an adequate operational risk management framework integrated into its broader risk management framework and covering Operational Resilience. It reserves to the full Board (not a committee) annual approval and review of key operational risk and resilience strategies, the Risk Appetite and Tolerance statement with Risk Limits, and the tolerance for disruption including where capabilities fall short. It also requires a Board operational risk (or equivalent) committee for LFIs designated systemically important, and allows the Central Bank to impose that on other LFIs based on size and complexity.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
