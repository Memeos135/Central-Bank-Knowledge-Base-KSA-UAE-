# Personal Loan

Article 2 of the original (version 1) regulation defining the Personal Loan as a loan to individual customers repaid from salary, end-of-service benefits or other verifiable regular income from a defined source. It fixes the maximum exposure at twenty times the borrower's salary or total income, limits the repayment tenor, ties monthly deductions to the salary-deduction ceiling in Article 7, extends the same treatment to loans to sole proprietorships and companies secured by owner/partner salaries, and requires use of the prescribed application/agreement form. Binds banks and finance companies granting personal loans.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
