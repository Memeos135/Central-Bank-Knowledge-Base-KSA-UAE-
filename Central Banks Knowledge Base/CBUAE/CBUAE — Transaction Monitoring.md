# Transaction Monitoring

A cross-cutting concept node on the continuous transaction monitoring duty of licensed financial institutions, grounded for Exchange Houses in Article 7 of the AML-CFT Decision and Paragraph 16.24 of the Standards. It addresses monitoring transactions against the customer/counterparty profile, re-rating risk on profile change, triggering EDD or relationship termination (notably after an STR/SAR filing), and maintaining an auditable record of alert generation, investigation and disposition. It also lists indicative transaction red flags (structuring below CDD thresholds, unexplained complexity, activity inconsistent with financial standing, attempts to dissuade reporting).

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
