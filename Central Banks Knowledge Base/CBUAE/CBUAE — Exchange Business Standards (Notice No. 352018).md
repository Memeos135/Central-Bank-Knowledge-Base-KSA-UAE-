# Exchange Business Standards (Notice No. 35/2018)

Defined term for the Standards for the Regulations Regarding Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business, issued by CBUAE on 1 March 2018 via Notice No. 35/2018 and any later amendments. The Standards are expressly legally binding and supplement the Exchange Business Regulation with the detailed regulatory obligations and supervisory requirements. Any compliance assessment of a Licensed Person must read the Regulation together with these Standards, not the Regulation alone.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
