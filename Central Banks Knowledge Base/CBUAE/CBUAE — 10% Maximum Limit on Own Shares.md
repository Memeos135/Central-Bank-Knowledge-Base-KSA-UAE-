# 10% Maximum Limit on Own Shares

This node captures the 10% ceiling on a bank's holdings of its own shares under the CBUAE Regulation on limits for banks' acquisition of their own shares (Circular 20/2021), issued under Article 93(3) of the UAE Central Bank Law. It sets the maximum direct or indirect purchase, buy-back, acquisition or holding of own shares at ten percent of Paid Up Share Capital, and sits alongside the prior written approval requirement and the two-year disposal window for shares devolved to the bank in settlement of a debt (extendable by the Central Bank). It binds all banks licensed by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
