# Terrorism Financing

## Why this community

Financing of terrorism as a distinct predicate/standalone offence driving suspicious transaction reporting, targeted financial sanctions screening and freezing duties.

## How members connect

- Isolated definitional node that supplies the trigger term for TF-specific reporting and freeze obligations referenced across the AML/CTF and sanctions clusters.
- Should be read against the money laundering cluster, since TF may involve lawfully sourced funds and therefore does not follow the proceeds-of-crime logic.
