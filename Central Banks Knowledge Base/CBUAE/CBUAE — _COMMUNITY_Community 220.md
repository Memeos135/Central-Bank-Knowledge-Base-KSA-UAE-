# Reporting Requirements

## Why this community

General regulatory reporting and notification duties owed to SAMA, beyond periodic financial statements — the ongoing information flow that sustains supervision.

## How members connect

- Sits at the end of the obligation chain: records (Art. 11) → financial statements (Art. 13) → returns and notifications to SAMA (Art. 14).
- Shares addressee and enforcement route with the conduct-of-business duties; failure to report is independently actionable.
