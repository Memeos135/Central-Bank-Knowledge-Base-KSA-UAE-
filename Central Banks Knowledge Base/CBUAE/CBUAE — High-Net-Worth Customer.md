# High-Net-Worth Customer

Defined term in the CBUAE Restricted Licence Banks Regulation setting the threshold test for a High-Net-Worth Customer: a natural person with net worth above AED 4,000,000 (excluding primary residence and loans secured on it) and liquid financial assets of at least AED 1,000,000. The definition matters because Restricted Licence Banks are barred from retail banking with natural persons unless the Central Bank grants a discretionary, condition-bound approval, and then only for persons the bank assesses as meeting this definition. Binds Restricted Licence Banks, including branches of foreign banks, when scoping permitted client segments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
