# Payment Token Service Provider

This node covers the defined term Payment Token Service Provider under the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation, the umbrella category for persons performing Payment Token Issuing, Payment Token Custody and Transfer, or Payment Token Conversion by way of business. It frames the Regulation's licensing and registration classes (Licensed Payment Token Service Providers, Registered Foreign Payment Token Issuers, Registered Foreign Payment Token Custodians and Transferors, Registered Payment Token Conversion Providers), the Central Bank's supervisory, reporting and quantitative limit-setting powers over volumes, values and customer onboarding, and the regime's objectives of safety and soundness, protection of the Reserve of Assets, risk-based licensing and consumer protection.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
