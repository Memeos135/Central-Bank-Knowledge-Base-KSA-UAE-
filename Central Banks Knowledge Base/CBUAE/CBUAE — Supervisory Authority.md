# Supervisory Authority

Defined collective term used in the CBUAE-led Guidelines for Financial Institutions adopting Enabling Technologies, covering the Central Bank of the UAE, the Securities and Commodities Authority, the DFSA (DIFC) and the FSRA (ADGM), each acting under its own enabling law. It identifies which regulator's expectations apply to an institution using enabling technologies (APIs, cloud computing, outsourcing) and to whom notification, access-to-information and materiality assessments are owed. It binds licensed institutions in each authority's jurisdiction, with the applicable authority determined by the institution's licensing regime rather than by the Guidelines themselves.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
