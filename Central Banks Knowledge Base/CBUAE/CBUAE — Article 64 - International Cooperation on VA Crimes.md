# Article 64 - International Cooperation on VA Crimes

A provision in Chapter Eight (International Cooperation and Asset Recovery) of the UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025 issuing the Executive Regulations of Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025 on AML/CFT and proliferation financing, addressing cooperation with foreign counterparts in relation to virtual-asset-related predicate and money laundering offences. It sits alongside the general cooperation duties (widest possible assistance, prioritisation, confidentiality, purpose limitation) that bind the Concerned Authorities rather than private sector entities. Note that the supplied context reproduces the surrounding general provisions rather than the full article text, so the operative wording should be read in the source.
