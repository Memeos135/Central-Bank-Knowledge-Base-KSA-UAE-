# Cabinet Resolution No. 7 of 2019 (Administrative Fines)

UAE Cabinet Resolution No. (7) of 2019 setting the framework for administrative fines imposed by the Insurance Authority under Federal Law No. (6) of 2007. It defines key terms (Company, Person, Insurance-Related Professions), sets the scope of application, empowers the Authority to levy the fines listed in the annexed schedule, doubles fines for repeat violations within one year up to a two million dirham cap, and provides a 15-day grievance route to the Board with a 60-day decision deadline. It binds insurance companies, licensed insurance-related professionals and any person committing a listed violation.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
