# Operational Risk Approaches

## Why this community

Calculation of the regulatory capital requirement for operational risk, and the menu of permitted measurement approaches a bank may use subject to supervisory eligibility conditions.

## How members connect

- Operational Risk is the head obligation; BIA, SA and ASA are alternative calculation methods ranked by sophistication, with movement between them typically requiring SAMA consent.
- BIA, SA and ASA all rely on the same defined financial indicator (gross income), so the definition and its exclusions drive the outcome under every approach.
- ASA is drafted as a variant of SA for specified business lines, meaning SA's conditions are read into it by reference.
- Approach selection is a supervisory permission question, not a free accounting choice — relevant to any capital adequacy filing.
