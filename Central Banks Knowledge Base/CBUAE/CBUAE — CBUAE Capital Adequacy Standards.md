# CBUAE Capital Adequacy Standards

This node covers the provisioning floor and capital interaction in Article 9 — where provisions fall short of the 1.50% of credit risk weighted assets floor, the shortfall may be held in a non-distributable 'impairment reserve-general' deducted from the capital base (Tier 1 for banks) — and the CBUAE's discretion to impose a higher floor where ECL model reliability or model management compliance is deficient. It also sets the dedicated methodology for Stage 3 wholesale provisions, computed net of eligible collateral after haircuts and documented recoveries, on a facility-specific basis rather than generic statistical LGD. The introduction confirms the standards are mandatory and enforceable alongside the Credit Risk Regulation for all LFIs, with board-level governance duties in Article 2.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
