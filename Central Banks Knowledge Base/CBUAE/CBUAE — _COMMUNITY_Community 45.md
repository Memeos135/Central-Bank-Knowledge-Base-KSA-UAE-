# Basel Risk Approaches

## Why this community

Non-market prudential risk regimes — operational risk, leverage, counterparty credit and the Pillar 2 supervisory review — covering how banks self-assess capital adequacy beyond minimum Pillar 1 charges.

## How members connect

- Pillar 2 / ICAAP is the integrating obligation: it pulls in risks not fully captured in Pillar 1 (concentration, IRRBB, liquidity, reputational/implicit support, securitisation supervisory review).
- Operational risk is a tiered approach hierarchy (basic indicator → standardised → alternative standardised), with supervisory permission and qualifying criteria governing movement up the tiers.
- The leverage standard chains from the exposure measure definition through derivative exposures into counterparty credit risk methodology — a shared measurement basis across two regimes.
- Multiple members cite the Basel Committee as the source framework, which is relevant to interpretation where SAMA text is silent or adopts Basel by reference.
- Reputational risk and implicit support cross-link this cluster to the securitisation framework.
