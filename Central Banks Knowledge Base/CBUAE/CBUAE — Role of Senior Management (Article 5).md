# Role of Senior Management (Article 5)

Article 5 makes Senior Management accountable for translating the Board-approved operational risk framework and Operational Resilience strategy into effective policies, processes, controls and systems across the LFI, and for monitoring the operational risk profile and material exposures. It requires timely, direct reporting to the Board (not a committee) of actual or expected breaches of Risk Appetite and Tolerance, thresholds and Risk Limits, maintenance of an internal control system with clear authority and accountability, periodic (at least annual for Banks and (Re)Insurance Companies) internal control assessment reported to the Board and the Central Bank, evaluation of ICT and cybersecurity risk management, adequate staffing, training and risk culture.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
