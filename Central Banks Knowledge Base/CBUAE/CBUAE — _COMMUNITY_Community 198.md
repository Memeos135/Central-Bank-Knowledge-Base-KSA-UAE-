# Insurance Ownership Ratios

## Why this community

Shareholding and ownership thresholds in licensed insurance companies — controlling/significant ownership limits and the supervisory approval consequences that attach to crossing them.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an Insurance Authority board decision setting ownership ratio parameters for insurance licensees.
- Connects legally to licensing and change-of-control obligations: ownership ratios are the trigger condition for prior non-objection and for ongoing shareholder suitability.
- Cross-regime relevance to governance/risk: ownership concentration feeds beneficial-ownership identification and control-person assessment.
