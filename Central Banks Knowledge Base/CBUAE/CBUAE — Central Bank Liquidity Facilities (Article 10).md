# Central Bank Liquidity Facilities (Article 10)

Article 10 of the CBUAE recovery planning regulation, requiring banks' recovery plans to contain an operational plan for accessing central bank standing liquidity facilities, including foreign central bank facilities where relevant. It specifies the minimum content of that plan (identification of facilities, eligibility assessment, eligible assets and drawing capacity, access processes and role allocation, and repayment-enabling recovery options), bars reliance on discretionary extraordinary central bank liquidity or public support in the plan, and mandates periodic operational testing with notification to the facility provider that transactions are tests. Binds licensed banks (Financial Institutions subject to the recovery planning regime) in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
