# Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy

## Why this community

Standardised motor insurance contract terms - the unified own-damage/loss policy wording and the regulatory decisions that mandate, amend and consolidate it.

## How members connect

- The unified policy is the mandated contract wording; the regulating Board Decision is its enabling instrument and defines when insurers must use it.
- Two later amending decisions alter specific policy terms; the consolidated version (VER2) reflects the amended wording and is the operative text for policies issued after its effective date.
- Legal effect is temporal: which version governs a claim depends on policy inception date relative to each amendment.
- Scope caution: Insurance Authority instruments, not SAMA-issued; treat as comparative material for KSA questions.
