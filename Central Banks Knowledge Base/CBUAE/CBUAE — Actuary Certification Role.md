# Actuary Certification Role

Concept node on the mandated role of the Actuary under the UAE life insurance and family takaful instructions (IA/CBUAE Decision No. 49 of 2019). It fixes the Actuary as the certifying party for the Protection Benefit Ratio calculation in product certification, as the person responsible for ensuring each savings product remains profitable across the policy term without front-loading cost onto the policyholder, for equitably allocating surrender charges, and for setting wakala fee levels on long-term products by reference to expense analysis and policyholder reasonable expectations. It binds licensed life/family takaful companies through their appointed actuary, and has consequences for product approval and pricing decisions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
