# Guarantees Recognition

Node covering the recognition of guarantees (and, alongside them, credit derivatives) as eligible credit risk mitigation under the CBUAE capital adequacy standard, including the operational conditions protection must satisfy and the effect of residual-maturity shortfalls on recognition. It sits within the broader CRM chapter that also sets out eligible financial collateral and the simple versus comprehensive approaches. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE when computing risk-weighted assets and regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
