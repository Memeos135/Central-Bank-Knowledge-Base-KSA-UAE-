# Safeguards

Section 9 of the CBUAE Regulatory Sandbox Conditions, setting the minimum safeguards a Participant must maintain throughout testing, framed around two protective aims: consumer protection and protection of the financial system. Required measures include complaint/compensation and dispute-resolution mechanisms notified to participating Consumers, segregation of Consumer funds or assets in an escrow arrangement with a Central Bank–licensed bank, data handling and protection arrangements, limits on which Consumers may be served plus express informed written consent, ongoing fitness and propriety of connected individuals, and AML/CTF measures under applicable legislation. It binds accepted Sandbox Participants, with the Central Bank reviewing ongoing compliance ad hoc and through agreed reporting, and able to impose additional case-specific safeguards.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
