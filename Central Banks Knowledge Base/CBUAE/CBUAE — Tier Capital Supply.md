# Tier Capital Supply

Refers to the Tier Capital Supply component of the CBUAE Standards for Capital Adequacy, which sets out eligible capital instruments and the composition of CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2, including the treatment of capital issued by subsidiaries to third parties (minority interest surplus) in consolidated capital and the deductions from regulatory capital. It is cross-referenced from the credit risk standardised approach, where exposures deducted under these standards receive specified risk-weight treatment. It binds banks subject to CBUAE capital adequacy requirements at solo and consolidated levels.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
