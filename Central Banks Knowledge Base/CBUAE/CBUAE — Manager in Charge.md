# Manager in Charge

Defines the Manager in Charge role for a Licensed Person under the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business: the senior individual accountable for the effective management of all the firm's activities. It sets prior-approval (no-objection) and fit-and-proper conditions for appointment, minimum qualification and experience thresholds by licence category, full-time/exclusivity and UAE-residency requirements, and notification plus replacement timelines when the post falls vacant. It binds the Licensed Person (and its board or owners/partners) rather than the individual directly.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
