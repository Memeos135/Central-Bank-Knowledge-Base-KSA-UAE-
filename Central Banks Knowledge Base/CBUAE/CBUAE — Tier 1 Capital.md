# Tier 1 Capital

Defined component of regulatory capital under the CBUAE capital adequacy framework, comprising Common Equity Tier 1 (ordinary shares and qualifying reserves, net of regulatory adjustments) plus Additional Tier 1 instruments. It carries its own minimum ratio requirement separate from CET1 and total capital, and non-qualifying legacy instruments are phased out over a defined horizon. Relevant to all banks calculating and reporting capital ratios to the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
