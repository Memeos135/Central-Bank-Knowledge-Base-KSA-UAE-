# Fit and Proper Process

Defined-term and duty node for the Fit and Proper Process: the assessment of expertise and integrity of proposed and serving board members, Senior Management, key control function holders and any other persons designated by the CBUAE, with detailed criteria set in the accompanying Standards. It obliges the Board to establish the process for both initial selection and continued assessment, and to maintain succession plans, performance standards and training. Referenced across several CBUAE instruments, it binds the company's board and, indirectly, the individuals being assessed.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
