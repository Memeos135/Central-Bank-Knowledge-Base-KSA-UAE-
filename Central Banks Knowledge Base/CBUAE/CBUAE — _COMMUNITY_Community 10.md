# Islamic Consumer Protection & Shari'ah

## Why this community

Retail consumer protection framework and its Shari'ah-governance overlay: responsible business and market conduct, responsible financing, disclosure of rates and fee caps, complaint handling, consumer data/asset protection, financial inclusion and accessibility, plus Shari'ah compliance duties for Islamic financial institutions.

## How members connect

- Law-to-regulation hierarchy: the central bank decretal law is the enabling statute cited by the Consumer Protection Regulation and by each of its standards/articles.
- The Regulation acts as a hub: each principle (responsible conduct, financing, complaints, data protection, education, inclusion, oversight) is a separate standard with its own obligations; the fee-and-commission annexure and interest/profit-rate provisions are the hard quantitative limits.
- Shari'ah layer: the Shari'ah governance standard and annual Shari'ah report guidance impose parallel compliance and board-reporting duties on Islamic financial institutions, so Islamic products must satisfy both conduct and Shari'ah requirements.
- Accessibility obligations import the People of Determination law into the inclusion standard; payment-data protection provisions interlock with general consumer data duties — flag overlap where a payments regime also regulates the same data.
