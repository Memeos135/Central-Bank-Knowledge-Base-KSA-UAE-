# Credit Risk & CVA Standards

## Why this community

Basel-derived credit risk capital treatment, covering risk weighting of exposures to multilateral development banks and the capital charge for credit valuation adjustment risk on derivative counterparty exposures.

## How members connect

- Members share a common source hierarchy: SAMA's capital adequacy framework transposes BCBS standards, so both MDB claims and CVA rules trace back to the same Basel text.
- The CVA charge is the parent obligation; the simple alternative approach is a permitted computational election within it, so scope and eligibility conditions must be read together.
- Weighted average maturity is an input parameter feeding the CVA calculation, making it a definitional dependency rather than a standalone obligation.
- MDB claims sit alongside CVA as exposure-classification rules determining the risk weight applied in the same capital ratio.
