# Commission Limit Rules

The commission-control provisions of IA Decision No. (49) of 2019, setting scope of application and caps on commissions payable to distribution channels for life and Family Takaful products. It fixes maximum commissions for pure protection and savings products (including single and ad-hoc premium), limits first-year indemnity commission to 50% with the remainder spread over the premium payment term, and imposes a minimum five-year commission claw-back; commission limits apply across all distribution channels as if there were only one. Binds companies, distribution channels and insurance-related professions for policies concluded after entry into force.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
