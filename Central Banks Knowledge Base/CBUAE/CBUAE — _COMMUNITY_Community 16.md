# SME Market Conduct

## Why this community

Market-conduct obligations owed to SME customers by licensed financial institutions, covering pre-contract disclosure, fair dealing in credit provision, complaint handling and Shari'ah-compliant product conduct.

## How members connect

- A single parent regulation acts as the hierarchy root; each article is an obligation branch (disclosure, responsible conduct, complaints, Shari'ah compliance) that inherits its scope and 'SME customer' definition from the parent.
- Responsible conduct links outward to the credit information agency, creating an affordability/creditworthiness assessment chain that ties conduct duties to credit-reporting inputs and permissible-purpose limits.
- Complaint management sits downstream of the disclosure and conduct duties: breaches of the earlier articles are what the complaints article must capture, escalate and remediate.
- Shari'ah compliance overlays the other articles for Islamic windows/products, so the same conduct duty can attract a second compliance test.
