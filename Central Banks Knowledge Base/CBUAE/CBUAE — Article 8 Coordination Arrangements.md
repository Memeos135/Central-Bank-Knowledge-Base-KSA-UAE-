# Article 8: Coordination Arrangements

Article 8 of the CBUAE Insurance Group Supervision Regulation sets out how the Central Bank, acting as group supervisor, cooperates with other domestic and foreign supervisory authorities to supervise an insurance Group. It allows the Central Bank to establish coordination mechanisms such as a Supervisory College and to invite supervisors of foreign insurance/reinsurance companies, banks and other financial institutions within the Group. It binds the Central Bank's own supervisory conduct rather than imposing direct duties on the Parent, though it frames the information exchange affecting Group entities; neighbouring provisions also address obstruction of inspections and designation of a Group reporting entity where the Parent is a Holding Company.
