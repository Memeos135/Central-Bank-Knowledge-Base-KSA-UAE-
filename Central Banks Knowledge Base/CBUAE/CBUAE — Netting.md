# Netting

Defined term and related operative provisions: netting means converting the various obligations owed to or by a Participant Person towards all other participants in a clearing and settlement system into a single net obligation. Where netting is effected in an LVPS meeting the designation conditions of the Central Bank Law, the System Operator and/or Settlement Institution must take account of the statutory netting treatment of insolvent or bankrupt parties' obligations. The Regulation does not limit the Central Bank Law provisions on preservation of rights in underlying transactions or a participant's duty to notify insolvency.
