# Resolution Authority

Node labelled "Resolution Authority" but the underlying text covers the establishment, composition, funding and powers of the Higher Shari'ah Authority attached to the UAE Central Bank under the 2025 Federal Decree-Law on the Central Bank and regulation of financial institutions. It sets Shari'ah rules, standards and governance requirements, supervises internal Shari'ah supervisory committees, opines on Central Bank and sovereign sukuk instruments, and may commission Shari'ah audits. Its resolutions and fatwas bind Islamic Financial Institutions, their internal Shari'ah committees and other entities seeking its opinion; those institutions also bear its costs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
