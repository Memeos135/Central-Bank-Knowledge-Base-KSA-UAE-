# Dormant Customer

Node on the CBUAE dormant accounts and unclaimed balances regime, setting the criteria by which a customer or account becomes dormant across banks, exchange businesses and insurance companies, including the three-year safe deposit box charge trigger, the one-year unclaimed funds rule for exchange businesses, and customer-level dormancy for insurance where no other active policy exists and the address is unknown. It also carries consumer protection duties: funds remain the property of the customer or heirs, interest continues to accrue per contract until transfer to the Central Bank, no debits or system-based charges may be applied, statement generation is suppressed but the customer is not excluded from routine communications. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs) and allocates certain responsibilities to customers on address updates and record-keeping.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, other
