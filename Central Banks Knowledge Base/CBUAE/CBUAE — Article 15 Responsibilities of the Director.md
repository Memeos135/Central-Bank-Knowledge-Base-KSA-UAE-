# Article 15: Responsibilities of the Director

Article 15 of the CBUAE Representation Offices Regulation defines the Director's mandate and accountability: representing the office and its foreign insurer/reinsurer before the Central Bank, courts and official bodies, carrying out the permitted activities set out in Article 3 and signing related documents, and receiving notices, warnings and judicial papers. The Director is fully responsible for all procedures and actions of the representation office, making this the primary accountability anchor for supervisory and service-of-process purposes.
