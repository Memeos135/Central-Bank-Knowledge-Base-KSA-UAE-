# Eligibility Criteria

Sets the eligibility criteria an Applicant must satisfy to enter, and continuously to remain in, the Regulatory Sandbox. The product, service, solution or business model must relate to financial products/services touching Central Bank objectives or constituting a Licensed Financial Activity, be genuinely innovative (new technology or novel use of existing technology), deliver benefit to Consumers or the financial services industry, show a real need for sandbox testing, and be intended for deployment in the State. Binds Applicants; assessment and admission remain at the Central Bank's discretion.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection
