# Appeals Committee

Defines the Appeals Committee established under the CBUAE Ombudsman Unit (Sanadak) Regulation as the second-instance body reviewing Determinations issued by the Ombudsman Unit in Complaints against Licensed Financial Institutions. It sets out the Committee's composition, quorum and majority decision-making, its freedom from strict court rules of evidence, its duty to apply Central Bank laws, regulations and instructions, its evidence-gathering powers, and its authority to uphold, partially uphold or reject a Complaint or to refuse an appeal that fails the admissibility conditions. It binds the Committee itself, the Ombudsman Unit's secretariat, and indirectly the Licensed Financial Institutions and Complainants who are parties to an appeal.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
