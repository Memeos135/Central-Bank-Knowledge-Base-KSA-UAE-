# Board Audit Committee

Node on the mandatory Board Audit Committee within the required committee structure (audit, risk, nomination, investment, compensation) of a CBUAE-licensed insurance/takaful company. It requires a written charter, access to resources and external expertise, a majority of Independent Members, an independent chair distinct from the board chair and other committee chairs, and collective experience in audit, financial reporting, accounting and risk; the audit committee may not be merged with the risk or any other committee. Internal audit and, with the risk committee, the actuarial function have reporting or direct access rights to it.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
