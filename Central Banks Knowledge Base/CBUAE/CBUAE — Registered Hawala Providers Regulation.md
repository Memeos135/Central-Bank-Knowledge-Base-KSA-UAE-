# Registered Hawala Providers Regulation

CBUAE Circular No. 24/2019 establishing the regulatory framework for hawala (money transfer) intermediaries operating in the UAE, issued under the Central Bank Law and the federal AML/CFT and counter-terrorism laws. It sets registration/certification conditions, undertakings, ongoing obligations (including agent due diligence, reporting to the Central Bank and AML/CFT compliance) and defines core terms such as Registered Hawala Provider and its agents. It binds registered hawala providers and their agents in the UAE; note this is a CBUAE instrument, not a SAMA rule, and is relevant only as comparative/cross-border material for KSA work.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
