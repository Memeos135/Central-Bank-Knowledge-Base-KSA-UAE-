# Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 (Central Bank Law)

Referenced primary statute: the UAE Central Bank Law governing the Central Bank and the organisation of financial institutions and activities. It is the enabling authority under which CBUAE issues regulations and standards (e.g. the Islamic Window standard) and contains the confidentiality provision relied on for defining confidential information. It binds licensed financial institutions and frames the Central Bank's supervisory and rule-making powers.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
