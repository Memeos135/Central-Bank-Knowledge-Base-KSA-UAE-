# Registered Hawala Providers and Alternative Remittance Systems

Section of the CBUAE TBML guidance addressing Registered Hawala Providers and other alternative remittance systems, which are vulnerable where they execute larger transfers for businesses conducting commercial or trade-related activity. It expects RHPs to establish the purpose and nature of transactions, to apply CDD/KYC to the underlying business where a customer acts on its behalf, and notes that such businesses must be UAE-registered and based to transact through the RHP. Binds RHPs and, by extension, LFIs servicing them.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
