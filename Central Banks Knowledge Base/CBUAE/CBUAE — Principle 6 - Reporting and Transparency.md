# Principle 6 - Reporting and Transparency

Principle 6 of the CBUAE/SFWG Principles for Climate Transition Planning (UAE), addressing reporting and transparency on climate transition plans. The retrieved context mainly carries the instrument's introduction and application section, which explains that the Principles complement the SFWG's earlier Risk Management Principles and the June 2024 Principles for Sustainability-related Disclosures, and support the UAE's Third Nationally Determined Contributions. Scope covers all UAE financial sector entities ('financial firms') regardless of business model, as guidance rather than binding rules.
