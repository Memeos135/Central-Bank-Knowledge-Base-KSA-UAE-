# BoD Decision 12/2018 - Insurance Consultants Regulation

Insurance Authority Board of Directors' Decision No. 12 of 2018, the regulation on licensing and registration of insurance consultants and the organisation of their operations, replacing the earlier 1985 ministerial framework. It prohibits practising insurance consultancy in the State without an IA licence and registration in the Register, bars insurance companies from dealing with unlicensed consultants, and sets the consultant's professional duties, permitted legal forms and fit-and-proper, qualification and experience conditions. Binds natural persons, sole proprietorships and corporate consultants, and indirectly insurance companies that engage them.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
