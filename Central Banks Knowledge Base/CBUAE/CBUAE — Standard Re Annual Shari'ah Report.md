# Standard Re Annual Shari'ah Report

CBUAE Standard on the Annual Shari'ah Report of the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, setting supervisory expectations and general requirements for issuing that report, together with a report template. It places responsibility for preparing the report on the ISSC, requires presentation to the general assembly, and requires prior submission to the Higher Shari'ah Authority for review and approval at least one month before the general assembly. Applies to all Takaful Insurance Companies licensed by the Central Bank and is read with HSA standards and resolutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
