# Actuary

Defined-term node for "Actuary" as a licensed and registered natural or corporate person performing actuarial functions for insurance companies. It captures scope and conflict rules: support staff are exempt from licensing but cannot sign actuarial reports or give actuarial advice; combining the actuary role with insurance agent, broker, consultant or surveyor/loss adjuster roles or other company positions is prohibited; caps apply on the number of insurance companies an actuary may serve (resident vs non-resident, natural vs corporate); appointment, contracting and dismissal require a board resolution and notification to the Authority. Binds actuaries and the insurance companies that engage them.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
