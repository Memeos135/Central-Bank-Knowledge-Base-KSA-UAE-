# Audit & Accounting Records

## Why this community

Books-and-records obligations and the control functions that assure their integrity — who must keep accounting records, and the internal audit, audit committee and external auditor roles that verify them.

## How members connect

- A single records provision anchors the cluster and points outward to each assurance actor, making record-keeping the obligation and audit the verification chain.
- Internal audit and the audit committee are linked as a reporting/oversight pair (function reports, committee oversees), sitting under board-level governance.
- External auditor duties sit at the top of the chain: independent attestation over the same accounting books the entity is required to maintain.
- Governance-regime cluster; note its interaction with AML/CTF and payments record-retention duties, which impose separate retention periods on the same underlying records.
