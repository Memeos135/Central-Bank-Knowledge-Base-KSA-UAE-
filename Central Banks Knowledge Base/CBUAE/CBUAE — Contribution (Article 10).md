# Contribution (Article 10)

Article 10 governs the Contribution: each Participant must pay it to the Takaful Insurance Fund on a Tabaru basis upon participation, in a lump sum or instalments depending on the product. The Company must set Contributions on technical and actuarial bases consistent with the nature, scope and duration of the risk, and for fund-accumulation products must split the Contribution transparently between the Fund and the Participants' Investment Account, with material investment information disclosed. Invested amounts in fund-accumulation products are owned by the Participant and subject to ownership rules including inheritance.
