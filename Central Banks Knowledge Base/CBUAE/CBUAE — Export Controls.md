# Export Controls

Section explaining export control regimes as a distinct control layer from sanctions, administered by authorities such as the EOCN, MOD, FANR, ICP and SIRA, and covering controlled items, technology and services. It expects LFIs to be familiar with national and international export control frameworks because they finance and settle international trade and may be prohibited from financing controlled exports, and it supplies maritime and shipping red-flag indicators. Addressed to Licensed Financial Institutions handling trade finance and cross-border payments.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk, payments
