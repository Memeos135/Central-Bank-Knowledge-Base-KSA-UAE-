# Transmittal Letter

Defines the Transmittal Letter as the Central Bank's formal written communication of examination findings and areas of non-compliance to a Licensed Person, issued after full-scope or fast-track examinations and structured by severity (major issues, violations, other observations). It creates enforceable response obligations: a written reply per finding with a detailed remediation action plan within the stated period or, absent one, thirty calendar days, signed by the authorised signatory and filed in hard copy with the Supervision & Examination Division. Also covers follow-up examinations and mandatory exit/follow-up meetings involving the Manager in Charge, Compliance Officer and Board/owners.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
