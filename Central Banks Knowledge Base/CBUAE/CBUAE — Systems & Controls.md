# Systems & Controls

This node covers the market risk governance framework requirement — the policies, processes, procedures, systems and controls a Bank must establish, implement and maintain to identify, assess, monitor, mitigate and control market risk — together with the defining terms that frame it (marking-to-market, marking-to-model, parent/subsidiary, residual risk, risk appetite, risk governance framework, risk limits, trading book). It fixes Board ownership: the framework must be documented, approved, maintained and overseen by the Board, integrated with the Bank's overall risk management, and supported by active Board and Senior Management involvement in market risk control. It binds Banks; institutions holding trading books carry an additional obligation to maintain separate trading book policy statements and risk frameworks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
