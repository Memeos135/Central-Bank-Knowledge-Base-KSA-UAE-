# TPA-Insurance Company Agreement

Node on the mandatory content and discipline of the agreement between the TPA and the insurance company, including insurer access to books and records, claim settlement and payment periods, authority to contract with medical service providers on the insurer's behalf, controls over contractual funds, and prescribed statistical reporting and record-keeping. It is paired with financial-conduct duties: separate accounts per insurer, no underwriting or reinsurance, no extra charges to insureds or beneficiaries, return of unused balances and of any unagreed commission or interest, and notification to the Authority of insurer payment delays beyond 15 days. Binds TPAs and, reciprocally, contracting insurers.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk
