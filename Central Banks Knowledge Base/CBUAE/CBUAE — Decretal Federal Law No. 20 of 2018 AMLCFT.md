# Decretal Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 AML/CFT

Reference node for Decretal Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism and Financing of Illegal Organisations and its Executive Regulations, cited as a legal basis for the Registered Hawala Providers Regulation alongside the Central Bank Law and the 2014 counter-terrorism law. Within this Regulation it operates as the primary AML/CFT statute that registered hawala providers, their customers and agents must comply with, including reporting violations to competent authorities. The underlying law binds UAE financial institutions and DNFBPs generally; here it is invoked as the enabling and compliance benchmark for hawala providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments
