# Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 (Establishment of Insurance Authority)

The federal statute establishing the Insurance Authority and regulating insurance business in the UAE; it is the enabling law cited as the legal basis for the insurance agents instructions and supplies core defined terms (Company, IA, Law). It binds insurers, intermediaries and related insurance-sector persons operating in the State. Open it for statutory authority, licensing perimeter and definitions rather than for detailed agent conduct rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
