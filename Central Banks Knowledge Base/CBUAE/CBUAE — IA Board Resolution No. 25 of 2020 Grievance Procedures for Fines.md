# IA Board Resolution No. 25 of 2020 Grievance Procedures for Fines

UAE Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 25 of 2020 setting the procedure for challenging administrative fines imposed by the Authority: an interested party may file a reasoned, fully documented grievance with the Board within 15 days of notification, and the Board must decide (reject, amend the fine, or cancel it) within 60 days. It binds any party fined by the Authority, including fines arising under the AML/CTF framework referenced in its preamble. Procedural only; it does not set the fine amounts, which sit in Cabinet Resolution No. 7 of 2019.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
