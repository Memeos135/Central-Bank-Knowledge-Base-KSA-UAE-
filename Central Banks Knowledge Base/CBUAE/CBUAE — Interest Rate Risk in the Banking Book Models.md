# Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book Models

Section 6 of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance on Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book models, treating conventional interest rate risk and Islamic profit rate risk alike. It supplements the CBUAE IRRBB Standards (notice 165/2018) and the 2016 Basel IRRBB Standards by setting modelling expectations for earnings and economic value metrics (NII, EVE), currency aggregation and offsetting for pegged currencies subject to basis risk capture, and treatment of non-performing assets. Governance, management, hedging and reporting for IRRBB sit in the separate CBUAE Standards, not here.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
