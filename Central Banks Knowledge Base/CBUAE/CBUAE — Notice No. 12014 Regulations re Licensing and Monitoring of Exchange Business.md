# Notice No. 1/2014 Regulations re Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business

Node for the predecessor licensing framework for exchange business (Notice No. 1/2014 on licensing and monitoring of exchange business), now to be read against the CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation issued by Circular 7/2025 to Exchange Houses. The current Regulation rests on the Central Bank Law, which lists currency exchange and money transfer among Licensed Financial Activities, and covers licensing, ongoing supervision, examination and sanctions, with binding accompanying Standards. It establishes four licence categories (I–IV) mapping to permitted activities such as Currency Exchange, Remittances and WPS salary processing, and binds any person carrying on Exchange Business in the UAE.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
