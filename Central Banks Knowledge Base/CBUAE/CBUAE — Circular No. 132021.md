# Circular No. 13/2021

Node covering CBUAE Circular No. 13/2021, the earlier Specialized Banks with Low Risk Regulation, which is expressly cancelled and replaced by the later Specialized Banks regulation (Circular 2/2022). Useful for version control: it marks the repeal point and sits alongside the successor's articles on regulatory reporting (financial year, outsourcing reporting), Islamic Specialized Banks standards and enforcement. Relevant to Specialized Banks and to anyone tracing which instrument applied on a given date.
