# Securitisation Standard

The CBUAE securitisation standard, based on the Basel revised securitisation framework, setting risk-based capital requirements for banking-book securitisation and resecuritisation exposures, including retained tranches, asset-backed investments, liquidity facilities, credit enhancements and credit risk mitigants. It includes key defined terms (originating bank, pool, implicit support, excess spread, early amortisation, resecuritisation), a prohibition on implicit support with punitive capital and public disclosure consequences, and specific resecuritisation parameters with a 100 percent risk weight floor. Applies to all UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
