# Article 9: Corporate Governance

Sets the corporate governance obligation for licensed Insurance Brokers: effective, robust and documented governance arrangements with a clear organisational structure and defined lines of responsibility, proportionate to nature, scale and complexity. Prescribes minimum content, including an organisation chart, conflict-of-interest and integrity controls, controls for compliance with Central Bank Laws and Regulations, confidentiality, monitoring and audit of governance arrangements, a code of conduct for Representatives and Staff, and a whistleblowing policy protecting staff from reprisals. Also requires a three-lines-of-defence structure (business lines; risk and compliance; independent internal audit).
