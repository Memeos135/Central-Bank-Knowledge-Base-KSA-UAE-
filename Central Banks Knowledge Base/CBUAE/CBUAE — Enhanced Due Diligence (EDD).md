# Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Concept node for Enhanced Due Diligence as treated in the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions, sitting inside the wider CDD programme (identification, profiling, acceptance, risk rating, monitoring, investigation, documentation). It explains the risk-based approach under the AML-CFT Law and Cabinet Decision, requiring intensified measures (e.g. source of funds, adverse-media and sanctions screening of customers, beneficial owners, beneficiaries and controlling persons) where higher ML/FT risk is identified, and contrasts this with SDD for low-risk, non-suspicious cases. It binds supervised financial institutions in the UAE, including those in financial and commercial free zones.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
