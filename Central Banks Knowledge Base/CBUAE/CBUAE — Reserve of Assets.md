# Reserve of Assets

Covers the Reserve of Assets backing Payment Tokens under the CBUAE regime: a documented stabilisation mechanism policy (asset type, allocation and composition; credit, counterparty, market and liquidity risk assessment; creation and destruction procedures; investment policy; redemption procedure and eligible redeemers). It imposes matching of token creation/destruction against the reserve, segregation from the issuer's own assets, a no-encumbrance/no-pledge rule, custody with a Bank or a Payment Token Custody provider within a short post-issuance window, prompt access for redemption, and independent bi-annual audit from White Paper approval. Binds Payment Service Providers issuing Payment Tokens.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
