# Solvency Margin and Minimum Guarantee Fund

Section 2 of the Financial Regulations, governing the Solvency Margin regime: Minimum Capital Requirement, Minimum Guarantee Fund, Solvency Capital Requirement, group capital adequacy, risk assessment and risk management systems, composition of Basic and Ancillary Own Funds, continuous maintenance duties and the limits on assets admissible for solvency. It binds insurance companies at all times, not only at reporting dates, and requires supervisory approval for certain Own Funds items such as subordinated liabilities and ancillary elements. Go here for capital adequacy, own-funds eligibility and solvency disclosure questions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
