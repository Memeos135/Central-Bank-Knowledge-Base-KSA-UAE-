# Standard re the Operational Model of Takaful Insurance

CBUAE Standard setting minimum requirements for the operational model of Takaful Insurance business and the efficient conduct of operations of both the Company and the Takaful Insurance Fund, reflecting the Central Bank's and HSA's Shari'ah and supervisory expectations. It covers establishment of the Fund, the mandatory Wakala-based operational model, contractual relationships, Wakala Fee and expenses, Qard Hasan for deficits, and Re-takaful policy, and defines terms such as Company, Takaful Insurance Fund, Wakala Contract and Insurance Surplus. It applies to all UAE-incorporated and licensed foreign Takaful Insurance Companies, including insurers housing Takaful Windows, for their Takaful activities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
