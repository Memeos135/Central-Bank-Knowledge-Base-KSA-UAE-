# No Objection Letter

Concept node for the CBUAE "No Objection Letter" application requirement: Licensed Financial Institutions must apply for a No Objection Letter for new or revised Financial Products and/or Services where the Central Bank prescribes it, with content and turn-around times set by the Central Bank. Related material covers the Central Bank's power to demand detailed product data, additional guidance from the Higher Shari'ah Authority for Islamic Financial Institutions, enforcement exposure for undue delays in filing, and the annexed caps on retail fees and commissions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
