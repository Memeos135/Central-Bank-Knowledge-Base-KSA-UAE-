# Article 35: Technology Risk & Information Security

Article 35 requires Payment Token Service Providers to establish an effective technology and cyber security risk management framework covering IT controls, cyber resilience, and the reliability, robustness, stability, availability and security of computer systems, proportionate to the nature, size, complexity and technologies of the business, and informed by recognised international standards. It mandates IT governance (IT function, technology risk management function, independent technology audit), Board-level responsibility for the risk framework, compliance with the UAE Information Assurance Standards, and specific policies on forking events, adverse ledger governance actions, redemption rights, and safeguarding self-held tokens. It applies to Licensees and Registrees, excluding Registered Foreign Payment Token Issuers.
