# Registered Hawala Provider

Defined-term node: a Registered Hawala Provider is a natural person holding a valid UAE residency visa, or a UAE juridical person, entered in the Central Bank's Hawala Providers Register under this Regulation, and the definition expressly extends to the provider's agent or agent network. A related definition captures the Registered Hawala Provider's Agent (any person acting for the provider by contract or under its direction). The term fixes the scope of who is bound by the Regulation's obligations, including responsibility flowing through to agents.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk
