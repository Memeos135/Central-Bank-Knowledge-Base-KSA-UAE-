# Complaint Management Function

Defines the required Complaint Management function: located in the UAE, capable of being combined with another suitable function based on business nature, size, Conflict of Interests potential and complexity, but independent of Retail Operations and able to review escalated Complaints and recommend redress. It lists the function's responsibilities (handling principles, channel monitoring, roles across Retail Operations/Risk/Compliance/Audit, service standards, governance, oversight and regulatory reporting) and the supporting process requirements, including written acknowledgment within 2 business days, a dedicated phone line and secure web portal, Senior Management–approved policies, Code of Fair Treatment of Consumers, and confidentiality of Complaint information. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
