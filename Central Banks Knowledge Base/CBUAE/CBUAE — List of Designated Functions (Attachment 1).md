# List of Designated Functions (Attachment 1)

Attachment 1 to the CBUAE Designated Functions framework, listing the senior roles (board members, CEO/deputy, CFO, CRO, chief internal auditor, CCO, AML compliance officer/MLRO, general counsel, CISO, Shari'ah committee and control/audit heads, and other executives reporting to the CEO/Board) that require prior CBUAE authorisation, presented separately for banks, insurance/reinsurance, finance companies, exchange houses, brokers/TPAs and other licensees. The list is indicative of substance rather than job title: licensees must map equivalent positions by duties and responsibilities, and are not required to maintain every listed function. It binds CBUAE licensees, which must run selection, qualification/experience verification, propriety checks and collective board suitability assessments, sign the prescribed forms, and track updates to the list.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, banking prudential
