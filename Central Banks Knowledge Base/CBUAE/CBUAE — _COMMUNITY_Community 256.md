# Re-takaful Insurance

## Why this community

Treatment of re-takaful arrangements — the conditions under which a takaful fund may cede risk and the constraints applying to that cession.

## How members connect

- A standalone provision within the takaful operational-model regime; it should be read against the fund-establishment and Wakala-model articles because cession affects the participants' fund.
- Its obligations bind the same licensed takaful operator addressed by the broader takaful standards.
