# Permitted Activities

This node covers Article 3 (and the adjacent Article 2 licensing provisions) of the Specialized Banks with Low Risk Regulation, which exhaustively lists the Licensed Financial Activities a Specialized Bank may perform: retail finance, mortgage finance, wholesale finance, issuance of debit/credit/prepaid cards under UAE-licensed schemes, stored value and electronic retail payment/digital money services, and agency distribution of third-party products with the relevant authority's approval. Activities are confined to UAE Residents and to UAE Dirham, and any activity beyond the list requires prior Central Bank approval. It binds licensed Specialized Banks; Islamic windows in conventional Specialized Banks are prohibited.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, governance/risk
