# Standard re Controls and Procedures for Establishing Takaful Insurance Fund

This is the CBUAE Standard setting minimum controls and procedures for establishing and managing a Takaful Insurance Fund as a legal entity separate from the licensed Company, together with its core defined terms (Company, Fund, Wakala Contract, Fund Charter). It articulates the Central Bank's and Higher Shari'ah Authority's Shari'ah and supervisory expectations for Fund set-up and administration. It binds all insurance companies incorporated in the UAE to conduct Takaful business, foreign licensed Takaful companies operating in the UAE, and conventional insurers/reinsurers operating Takaful windows (for their Takaful activities only), and must be read with other CBUAE and HSA standards and resolutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
