# Physical Risk

Concept node for Physical Risk as one of the three drivers of Climate-related Financial Risks in the CBUAE climate risk management standard/guidance for Financial Institutions. It matters mainly in measurement and in the traditional risk categories: geographic concentration metrics for assets, liabilities and collateral; operational risk and business continuity (including outsourcing and Critical Functions); and liquidity/funding effects such as withdrawal or credit-demand spikes after physical events. It binds licensed Financial Institutions (banks and insurance companies) as a supervisory expectation embedded in their existing risk management frameworks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
