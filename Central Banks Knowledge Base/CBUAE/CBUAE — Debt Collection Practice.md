# Debt Collection Practice

Governs how Licensed Financial Institutions and their authorised collection agents may pursue debts owed by Consumers. Requires written collection policies and procedures, documented discussion of financial difficulties before collection, enforcement of collateral/guarantees or litigation, engagement with the Consumer once an account is in Arrears and mandatory contact where Arrears persist 30 calendar days. Contact is restricted to 9 AM–8 PM with no unreasonable or excessive attempts, each communication must identify the institution/agent, department, contact number, working hours and caller, all communications must be recorded and retained for 5 years after settlement or write-off, and the Consumer must be told if a Third Party collector is appointed, what it may collect and the scope of its authority including receipt of payments.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
