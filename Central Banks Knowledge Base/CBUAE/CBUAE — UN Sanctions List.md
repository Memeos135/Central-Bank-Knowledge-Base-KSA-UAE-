# UN Sanctions List

Concept node for the UN Sanctions List regime — the consolidated lists maintained under UN Security Council resolutions and their Sanctions Committees (including the 1267/1989/2253, 1988, 1718 and 2231 regimes) — and the national procedure for proposing persons or organisations for designation. It covers how the Council gathers identifying information, coordinates with the relevant state and submits proposals through the Office, as well as requests for listing addressed to other countries under UNSCR 1373. It binds the Council, the Ministry, the Office, and indirectly all entities that must screen against and apply the published list.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
