# Payment Token Custody and Transfer

Defines Payment Token Custody and Transfer as one of the three Payment Token Services under the CBUAE regulation, and links to the related defined terms Payment Token Transfer (an act initiated by Payer, Payee or the Payment Token Issuer to transfer a token or an interest in it) and Payment Token Data. Carries the Central Bank's power to limit the number of customers a Payment Token Custodian and Transferor may serve or to restrict new on-boarding. Binds Licensed Payment Token Custodians and Transferors and Registered Foreign Payment Token Custodians and Transferors.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
