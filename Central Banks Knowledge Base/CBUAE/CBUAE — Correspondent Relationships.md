# Correspondent Relationships

A section of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions dealing with correspondent banking and similar relationships (including securities and funds-transfer arrangements), read against Article 25 of the AML-CFT Decision. It sets out the due diligence expected before and during such relationships: assessing the respondent's home regulatory and supervisory framework, ownership and management (including PEP exposure), the nature and scale of its business, and whether it offers downstream or nested correspondent services. It binds licensed financial institutions maintaining correspondent relationships with foreign or domestic institutions, and reiterates the prohibition on dealing with shell banks or institutions that permit shell-bank use of their accounts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
