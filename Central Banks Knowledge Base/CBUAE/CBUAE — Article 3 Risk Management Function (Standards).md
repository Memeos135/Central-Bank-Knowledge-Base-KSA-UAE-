# Article 3: Risk Management Function (Standards)

Article 3 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards governs the independent risk management function and the CRO: independence safeguards (no risk-taking-based remuneration, not the CEO or head of finance, compliance or internal audit), direct reporting to the board or board risk committee, and unfettered access including meetings without other executives. It lists the function's key activities, including identifying material and emerging risks, measuring exposures, monitoring against approved risk appetite and limits, operating early-warning and breach escalation, challenging material risk decisions and reporting to the board. It binds CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
