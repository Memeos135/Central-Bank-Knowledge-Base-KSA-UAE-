# Illicit Transshipment Risks

Section of the CBUAE guidance defining transshipment (goods routed through intermediate destinations before the final destination) and explaining how it is abused to disguise country of origin or end-user and to evade sanctions and export controls. It sets out recurring schemes such as re-export from non-sanctioned hubs, use of jurisdictions with weaker export restrictions, forged customs declarations, and third-jurisdiction front companies. Relevant to LFIs screening trade transactions for sanctions and export-control evasion risk.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
