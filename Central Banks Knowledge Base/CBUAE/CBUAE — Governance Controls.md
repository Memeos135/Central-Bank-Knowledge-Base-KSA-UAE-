# Governance Controls

Node covering governance and control-effectiveness expectations within the CBUAE risk-based approach guidance, setting out how an LFI should identify key AML/CFT/CPF controls (CDD/KYC, transaction monitoring, training, compliance auditing), define effectiveness criteria, measure performance, score and aggregate results, and adjust for control interdependencies. It frames governance as the mechanism linking inherent risk assessment to residual risk. Directed at licensed financial institutions' boards, senior management and compliance functions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
