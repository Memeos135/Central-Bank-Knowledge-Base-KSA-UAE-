# Prudent Valuation Guidance

Appendix-level guidance setting minimum expectations for prudent valuation of trading book positions, covering governance systems and controls (documented valuation policies, independent reporting lines up to a board executive director) and valuation methodologies beginning with at-least-daily marking to market at independently sourced close-out prices. It underpins supervisory assessment of fair value practices, including for complex or illiquid instruments. Binds banks holding fair-valued positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
