# Article 25: Obligations Towards Customers

Article 25 of the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation sets the general conduct duties owed by Licensees and Registrees to Customers: prudent, competent, responsible, honest and professional operation that does not adversely affect Customer interests. It expressly layers the Central Bank's Consumer Protection Regulation and Standards on top, with the consumer protection provisions prevailing in case of conflict, and adds distributed ledger duties (keeping a copy of the relevant ledger and treating forked versions of a payment token as equivalent).
