# Takaful Shari'ah Reporting

## Why this community

Internal Shari'ah governance architecture for Islamic financial institutions and takaful operators: constitution of the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, its charter, annual Shari'ah reporting, and management of Shari'ah non-compliance risk.

## How members connect

- Defined-term chain: Islamic Finance Institution -> Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee -> its Charter, each standard giving content to the next.
- The Annual Shari'ah Report standard is the output/disclosure obligation of the ISSC, cross-referencing both the governance standard and the charter standard.
- Shari'ah non-compliance risk provisions allocate identification, escalation and remediation duties to the ISSC, closing the loop back into the annual report.
- Hierarchy runs from the takaful/Islamic governance standard down to charter and reporting standards as implementing instruments.
