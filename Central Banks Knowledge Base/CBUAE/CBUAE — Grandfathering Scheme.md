# Grandfathering Scheme

Article 23 of the CBUAE Large Exposures regulation, setting the transitional grandfathering scheme for breaches arising solely from the move from Circular No. 32/2013 to the new framework. It fixes the grandfathered basis by reference to the position at 31 December 2022, ties it to a specific exposure and counterparty, and phases the relief down from 100% to 0% between 2023 and 31 December 2026, with full reporting plus supplementary disclosures and no relief for interbank exposures (full compliance by 31 December 2023). Binds banks relying on transitional relief.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
