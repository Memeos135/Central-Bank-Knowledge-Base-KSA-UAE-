# Enhanced Due Diligence for High-Risk CB

Section of CBUAE guidance on correspondent banking setting out enhanced due diligence expectations where a respondent institution presents elevated ML/TF/PF risk, and listing the triggering risk factors (nesting and payable-through accounts, high-risk jurisdictions of the respondent or its group, PEP involvement in ownership/management, higher-risk products, and a high-risk downstream customer base). It frames EDD as a scaled, risk-based overlay on the baseline correspondent due diligence required of all such relationships, alongside enterprise-wide risk assessment duties anchored in Article 4 of the AML-CFT Decision. It binds licensed financial institutions acting as correspondent institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
