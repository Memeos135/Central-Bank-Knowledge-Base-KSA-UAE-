# Cabinet Resolution No. 10 of 2019 (AML Executive Regulations)

Referenced instrument: the Executive Regulations implementing the UAE federal AML/CTF decree-law, setting out the operative CDD, KYC, enhanced due diligence, beneficial ownership and record-keeping obligations. In this context it is invoked as the source standard that insurance/Takaful companies and their compliance officers must satisfy, including PEP identification, senior management approval, source of wealth enquiry and identification of life/Family Takaful policy beneficiaries. It binds financial institutions and designated non-financial businesses subject to the federal AML regime, with sector supervisors verifying compliance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
