# Equity Investment Risk (Article 13)

Node for the equity investment risk article of the CBUAE Islamic banks risk management Standard, dealing with risks from profit-sharing and partnership-based investments (Mudaraba, Musharaka, Wakala). It requires Shari'ah-compliant risk mitigation, consistent and mutually agreed valuation methodologies, controls against misstated partnership earnings, and documented exit/extension/redemption strategies approved by the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, while recognising capital-impairment risk that resembles but is not conventional credit risk. Binds Islamic banks and banks with Islamic Windows; equity investment risk must also feature in the risk appetite statement and management reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
