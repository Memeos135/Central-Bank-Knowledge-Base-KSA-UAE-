# Wire Transfer Information Requirements

Sets the travel-rule style obligations for Licensed Persons (exchange houses) acting as ordering, intermediary and beneficiary institutions in cross-border and domestic money transfers, specifying the originator and beneficiary data that must accompany every transfer, verification of that data, and the fallback use of a unique transaction reference number where no account exists. It prohibits execution of transfers that fail these conditions, requires intermediaries to preserve accompanying information through the chain (or record it where technical limits intervene) and to detect transfers with missing information using straight-through-processing-compatible measures, with a three-business-day window for the ordering institution to make information available on request. Binds Licensed Persons under the CBUAE AML/CFT standards; record-keeping cross-refers to the retention paragraph.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
