# Principle 6 - Capital and Liquidity Adequacy

Principle 6 requires relevant financial firms to incorporate material climate-related financial risks into internal capital and liquidity adequacy assessment processes, evaluating liquidity, capital and solvency impacts over specified time horizons. Where a firm is required to perform an ICAAP, ILAAP, ORSA or similar process, climate risks must be integrated into those frameworks and any material impact recorded. Applies to CBUAE-supervised firms subject to such processes (banks and insurers in particular).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
