# Institutional Risk Assessment

Guidance node on how a Licensed Financial Institution should build and document its institutional (enterprise-wide) ML/TF/PF risk assessment, covering the expected methodology for identifying and rating inherent risk across customers, products and services, geographies and other factors. It expects consistency between the institutional assessment and the risk segments and rating methodology used in the day-to-day AML/CFT/CPF programme, express rationales where high-risk factors are excluded, and explicit acknowledgement where data gaps inflate the inherent risk rating. Addressed to LFIs supervised by the CBUAE (note: UAE, not SAMA, instrument).

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
