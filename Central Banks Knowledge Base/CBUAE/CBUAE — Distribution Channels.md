# Distribution Channels

Concept node on how the commission and cost rules apply across Distribution Channels (agents, brokers, banks) selling life and family takaful products. Commission caps and claw-back apply as if a single channel exists even where several channels are involved or the channel changes mid-term, costs of each channel must be allocated to clients within that channel, and separate treatment is set for credit life products, initial access fees and net-of-commission quoting. Binds insurers and every distribution channel in the sale chain.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
