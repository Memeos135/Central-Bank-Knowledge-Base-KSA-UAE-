# CBUAE Payment Message Field Specifications

Node drawn from the CBUAE retail payment services and card schemes instrument; despite the label, the supplied material is the definitions article setting out core payment terms — Payment Account, Payment Aggregation Service, Payment Data, Payment Initiation Service, Payment Instrument and its issuance, and Payment Service Provider. It also defines the Payment Token regime (issuing, buying, selling and exchange facilitation), including the Category I licence, white paper and prior Central Bank approval conditions for offering tokens to the public. Binds entities licensed or seeking a licence to provide retail payment services, including merchant aggregators and token service providers.

**Regimes:** payments, other
