# Takaful Insurance Fund

Node on the Takaful Insurance Fund under Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025: a Takaful, Re-Takaful or Insurance Company conducting Takaful business must establish a fund with independent legal personality and financial liability separate from the company, registered with and supervised by the Central Bank. Contributions (premiums) based on the concept of Tabaru' are deposited into the fund, which bears liability for compensation and benefits; Takaful business must comply with Islamic Shari'ah rules and the business models set by the Higher Shari'ah Authority, reflected in the company's constitutional documents. Related provisions address freeing up funds required to be maintained in the State when an insurer ceases a line of business.

### [[CBUAE — Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance|Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who must operate a segregated Takaful Insurance Fund and conduct business in conformity with Islamic Shari'ah principles, the scope is set by the Regulation's defined term for Company, which reaches takaful and re-takaful companies as well as conventional insurers and reinsurers operating a takaful window. The fund obligation therefore does not depend on the entity being wholly Islamic. The consequence is that a conventional insurer running a window is in scope for fund segregation and Shari'ah-conformity requirements for that business, and cannot treat them as applying only to standalone takaful operators.
