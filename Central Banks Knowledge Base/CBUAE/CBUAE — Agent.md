# Agent

Defined-term node for "Agent" under the CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation: a party a Finance Company may, with Central Bank non-objection, contract with to offer Short-Term Credit to Borrowers — either distributing credit funded by the Finance Company or granting credit fully funded by the Agent itself. The Finance Company must ensure the Agent complies with the applicable Part II articles except licensing, licence restrictions and minimum capital; Restricted Licence Finance Companies may not use Agents, so an Agent must partner with a Finance Company or a Bank. Agents are directly subject to the borrower disclosure and fair-conduct obligations (product features, Fees, late payment consequences, signed documentation, hardship assistance).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
