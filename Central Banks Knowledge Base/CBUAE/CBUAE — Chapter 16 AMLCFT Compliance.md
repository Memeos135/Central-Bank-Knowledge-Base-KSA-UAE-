# Chapter 16: AML/CFT Compliance

Chapter 16 is the AML/CFT compliance core of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business, setting minimum requirements for the AML/CFT Program, enterprise-wide ML/FT risk assessment, written policies and procedures, and their approval, annual review, distribution and group-wide application to majority-held branches, subsidiaries and affiliates. It also governs appointment, seniority, independence, resourcing, qualifications and duties of the Compliance Officer, including transaction monitoring, STR/SAR filing with the FIU, training, board reporting, record retention, gap analysis and bi-annual compliance reporting. It binds Licensed Persons carrying on exchange business, with some requirements scaled by licence category.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS

### [[CBUAE — Standards for Exchange Business in the UAE v1.20|Standards for Exchange Business in the UAE v1.20]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat Chapter 16 not as a standalone AML rulebook but as one chapter of the consolidated Standards for Exchange Business (v1.20), so its AML/CFT obligations are read subject to the scope, licensing conditions and definitions set in the parent instrument. The chapter's defined terms (such as the AML/CFT Program) and its addressee, the Licensed Person, derive from that parent framework. Practically, a compliance decision on an exchange house's AML programme must be justified by reference to both the chapter and the Standards' general provisions, and the applicable version of the Standards should be confirmed before relying on it.
