# Market Risk Regulation (Circular 164/2018)

A cross-reference node for the CBUAE Market Risk Regulation and accompanying Standards (Circular 164/2018), invoked within the Islamic banking risk governance framework as the baseline market risk regime that Islamic Banks must read together with their Shari'ah-specific requirements. In that setting it sits alongside the Risk Management Standards (153/2018) and frames board-level risk appetite, the three-lines-of-defence model, and market risk identification, pricing, valuation, income recognition, limits and MIS reporting, including for restricted investment account portfolios. It binds licensed banks, and in this context Islamic Banks and Islamic windows, with additional coverage of Shari'ah non-compliance, displaced commercial, rate of return and equity investment risk.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
