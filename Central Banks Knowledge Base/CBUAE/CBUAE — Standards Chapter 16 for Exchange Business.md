# Standards Chapter 16 for Exchange Business

Concept node for Chapter 16 (AML/CFT Compliance) of the CBUAE Standards accompanying the Regulations Regarding Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business, Version 1.20 (November 2021), amending Version 1.10 (February 2018). It sets sector-specific AML/CTF expectations for exchange houses, including compliance officer and alternate compliance officer appointment, compliance committee and corporate governance (Chapter 6), and sanctions obligations. It binds licensed exchange houses (LEH) supervised by CBUAE, applied with proportionality for smaller firms.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
