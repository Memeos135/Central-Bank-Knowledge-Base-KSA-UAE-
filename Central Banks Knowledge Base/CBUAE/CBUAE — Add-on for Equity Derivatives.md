# Add-on for Equity Derivatives

Sets out the equity derivatives add-on: entity-level effective notionals (single name or index) are scaled by supervisory factors, then combined at hedging-set level using supervisory correlations, with basis and volatility trades placed in separate hedging sets. Related context also addresses the separate market risk specific/general charges for equity positions and derivatives. Binds banks calculating counterparty credit and market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
