# Source of Funds and Wealth

Node addressing the requirement to take reasonable measures to establish the source of funds and source of wealth of PEPs, Related Customers and their beneficial owners as part of PEP enhanced due diligence. It explains the expected analysis: collecting declared net worth and principal income sources, corroborating with public or third-party information such as asset declarations or published salaries, and testing consistency of declared wealth against both declared income and actual financial behaviour, with deeper verification where risk is higher. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
