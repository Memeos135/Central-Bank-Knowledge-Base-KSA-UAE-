# Leverage Ratio Standard (CBUAE 1691)

The Leverage Ratio Standard sets a non-risk-based capital constraint: Tier 1 capital over a total exposure measure covering on-balance-sheet items, derivatives, securities financing transactions and off-balance-sheet commitments (with specified credit conversion factors). It fixes quarter-end calculation (more frequent averaging only with Central Bank approval), applies the same regulatory consolidation scope as the risk-based capital framework, generally bars recognition of collateral or other credit risk mitigation, and requires independent review and challenge of the calculations. It binds licensed banks, which must meet the minimum leverage ratio at all times.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
