# Local Lists

Concept node for the UAE domestic (Local) terrorist designation lists issued by the Cabinet on proposal of the Executive Office/Council, covering listing, re-listing, updating and de-listing procedures and the effective date and publication of those decisions. It also drives the operational duties that flow to obliged entities: registration for notifications, screening of customers, counterparties, potential customers and beneficial owners, and immediate freezing on a match. It binds the Council, Ministry of Presidential Affairs, the Cabinet, the Office, supervisory authorities, and financial institutions and DNFBPs that must screen and freeze.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
