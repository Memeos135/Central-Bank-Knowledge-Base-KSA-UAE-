# Establishing a Risk Profile

Guidance on constructing a customer risk profile using the same risk categories as the institution-level assessment — products and services, geography, and the customer's business, customers and counterparties — anchored in the obligation to understand the nature of the customer's business and the purpose of the relationship. It addresses UBO identification and verification timing (generally before the relationship starts, with a limited low-risk exception for completing verification afterwards) and the treatment of listed companies and their majority-owned subsidiaries where ownership data is available from reliable sources, while allowing institutions to prescribe higher-risk scenarios requiring full verification.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
