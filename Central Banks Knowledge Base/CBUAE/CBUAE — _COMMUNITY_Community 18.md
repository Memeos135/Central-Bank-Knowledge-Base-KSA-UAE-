# Finance Companies Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct regime for finance companies, including restricted-licence and short-term credit models, use of agents, Islamic finance company requirements and credit-information obligations.

## How members connect

- The Finance Companies Regulation is the parent instrument defining Finance Company and Restricted Licence Finance Company, with a circular operationalising it.
- Corporate-form requirements are imported from the Commercial Companies Law, creating a statute-to-regulation hierarchy.
- Product/scope limits link Restricted Licence Finance Company and Agent to Short-Term Credit, which constrains permitted activity and outsourced distribution.
- Islamic Finance Company adds a Shari'ah layer: Sharia'a Supervision Committee reporting into the Higher Sharia'a Authority.
- Credit Information Agency linkage creates data-reporting and enquiry duties relevant to affordability and credit-information rules.
