# Insurance Authority Regulations

## Why this community

Licensing, conduct and fee framework for insurance intermediaries and administrators under the former UAE Insurance Authority. Relevant as comparative material only; in KSA, insurance supervision sits outside this instrument set.

## How members connect

- Clear law-to-regulation hierarchy: Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 establishes the Authority and confers rule-making power, which the IA board resolutions and implementing regulations exercise.
- The insurance agents resolution derives its licensing and conduct obligations from both the establishing law and the 2009 implementing regulations, so agent duties must be read cumulatively.
- Cabinet resolutions form a separate fee layer, with the 2013 third-party health insurance administrator fees building on the earlier 2009 insurance fee schedule.
