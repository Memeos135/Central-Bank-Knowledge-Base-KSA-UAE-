# Corporate Governance Regulation (Circular 24/2022)

Node for the underlying CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation (Circular No. 24/2022), which the insurance Standards expand on and which is enforceable in the same manner. It frames Board responsibilities — setting Risk Appetite, approving key policies on liquidity, capital adequacy, technical provisions and solvency margin, overseeing the external auditor and Senior Management, approving annual financial statements — plus corporate culture and "tone from the top", group structure and intragroup conflict controls, and Shari'ah governance for Takaful companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
