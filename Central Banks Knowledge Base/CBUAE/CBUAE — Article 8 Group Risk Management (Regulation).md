# Article 8: Group Risk Management (Regulation)

Article 8 of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (153/2018) deals with group risk management, including local or regional application of group policies and procedures and the assessment of local/regional risks. Where the Central Bank is not the primary regulator of a bank belonging to a Group and elements of its risk management approach are controlled or influenced by another group entity, the bank's own risk governance framework must address risks arising from the group relationship and clearly identify linkages and material differences from the Group framework, whether its risk management function is wholly or partly derived from the Group, and the monitoring/reporting process to the Group. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks that form part of a wider group, including subsidiaries and branches of foreign groups.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
