# Minimum Capital Requirement

Concept node for the minimum capital requirement as defined in CBUAE's Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation: an ongoing, licensing-condition floor of fully paid-up capital (AED 2bn for UAE-incorporated Banks, AED 300m for Specialized Banks, AED 100m at branch level plus AED 2bn eligible capital at entity level for foreign bank branches). It also specifies quality criteria for branch allocated funds (irrevocable, unconditional, non-interest-bearing, in AED, with head-office shortfall undertaking) and confirms non-interaction with other capital regimes. Binds Banks and, at entity level, their foreign head offices.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
