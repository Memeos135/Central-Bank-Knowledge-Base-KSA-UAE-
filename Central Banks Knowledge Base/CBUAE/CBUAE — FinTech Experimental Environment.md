# FinTech Experimental Environment

Concept node for the FinTech experimental environment (sandbox) itself: eligible applicant categories, entry conditions and the safeguards applied during testing. Eligibility covers licensed Innovative Solutions Owners (whether or not the solution falls within their licence) and free-zone, national or foreign-branch FinTech companies, the latter only via partnership with an accountable licensed entity plus an acceptance letter. Testing conditions include customer disclosure of test status, AML/CTF controls, customer data protection and risk reporting.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk, other
