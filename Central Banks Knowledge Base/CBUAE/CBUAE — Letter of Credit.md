# Letter of Credit

Concept node on letters of credit as used in the CBUAE TBML guidance, focused mainly on LC-specific red flags: document discrepancies and mismatches between invoice, bill of lading and LC terms, repeated amendments or extensions, changes of beneficiary, payment location or country of origin, payment requests to third countries, willingness to waive discrepancies, and LCs routinely opened but left unutilised. Useful for LFIs reviewing presentations and deciding whether trade activity is consistent with the customer's profile.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
