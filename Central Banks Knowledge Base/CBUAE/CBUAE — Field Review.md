# Field Review

Article 8 of the CBUAE Shari'ah Compliance Function standard, requiring the IFI's SCF to carry out a field review of the institution using procedures built on a robust methodological basis, with further detail available in the standard's guidance note. It follows the planning and scoping stage (Article 7) and precedes issues and actions (Article 9) in the mandated review cycle. It binds Islamic Financial Institutions through their Shari'ah Compliance Function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
