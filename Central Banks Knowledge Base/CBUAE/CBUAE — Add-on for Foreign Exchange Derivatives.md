# Add-on for Foreign Exchange Derivatives

Covers the add-on calculation for foreign exchange derivatives under the CBUAE counterparty credit risk standard, where each currency pair forms a hedging set and the absolute effective notional is scaled by the FX supervisory factor and aggregated across hedging sets. Adjacent context also touches market risk treatment of net open FX positions (including gold, AED/USD relationship and the 8% charge). Applies to banks computing derivative exposure and FX capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
