# Record Retention

Sets the minimum five-year retention baseline for Licensed Persons across transaction records, KYC/CDD/EDD files, ongoing monitoring, correspondence and ID copies, measured from transaction completion, end of the business relationship or account closure. Extends the same period to AML training records and materials, transaction monitoring and investigation files, and STR/SAR documentation (running from the report date), with an extended trigger where litigation or an enforcement investigation is pending. Records include electronic and hard-copy material and must be sufficient to reconstruct individual transactions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
