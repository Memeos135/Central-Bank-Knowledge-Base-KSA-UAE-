# Maturity Ladder Approach (Commodity)

One of the two standardised alternatives for computing the commodity capital charge under CBUAE capital adequacy guidance, contrasted with the simplified approach and structured as a multi-step calculation across maturity time bands. Relevant where a bank wants a more granular treatment of basis, interest rate and forward gap risk than the flat simplified percentages. Applies to UAE-licensed banks calculating market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
