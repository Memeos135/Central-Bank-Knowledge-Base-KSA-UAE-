# Standards Article 9: Classification and Provisioning

Article 9 of the CBUAE Credit Risk Standards sets the classification and provisioning regime: each LFI must operate a classification process covering facility, obligor and portfolio-level creditworthiness review, internal rating refresh at least every 12 months, early identification of deterioration and timely past-due recording. It links restructuring outcomes and SICR findings into staging and provisioning decisions, and is the article referenced by internal audit, compliance and external audit scope obligations in Article 2. It binds LFIs and their control functions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee|Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For an Islamic financial institution applying the staging, classification and provisioning standards, the practical question is who signs off: the prudential rules set the minimum classification and provisioning outcome, while the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee must be established within the institution and governs the Shari'ah conformity of the underlying contracts and any restructuring or write-off mechanics used to reach that outcome. The two run in parallel — ISSC approval does not relax a prudential classification (including the transitional counting of months from issuance of the standards), and a prudential requirement does not authorise a Shari'ah-non-compliant instrument. In practice, provisioning decisions on Islamic exposures need both prudential justification and ISSC clearance of the contractual route taken; the reader should confirm the specific provisions in the primary text before relying on this allocation.
