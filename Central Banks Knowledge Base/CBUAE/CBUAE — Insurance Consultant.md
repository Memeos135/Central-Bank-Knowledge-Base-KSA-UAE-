# Insurance Consultant

Defined-term node for "Consultant" (Insurance Consultant) under BoD Decision 12 of 2018: a natural or corporate person who studies clients' insurance requirements, advises on suitable coverage, assists with claims preparation and is paid fees by the client (as distinct from commission-based intermediaries). The node also carries the licensing conditions and scope rules: consultants may be natural persons, sole proprietorships or companies, may be licensed for all or specific classes, and may not combine consultancy with any other insurance-associated profession. Sets the eligibility gate (capacity, good repute, no disqualifying convictions or disciplinary cancellations, and minimum academic and professional experience thresholds differing for Nationals and non-Nationals).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
