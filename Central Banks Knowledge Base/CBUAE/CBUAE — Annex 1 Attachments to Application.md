# Annex 1: Attachments to Application

Annex 1 lists the documents and statements that must accompany a licence application under Article 3, and is referenced together with the Central Bank's power to request further material when assessing an application. The related Article 3 and 4 context sets the assessment criteria (introducing new or high-demand products, increasing Premium retention in the State), possible liaison with a Foreign Insurance Company's home supervisor, and the sixty-business-day cure period after which an incomplete application lapses. Relevant to founders' committees and Foreign Insurance Companies applying for a UAE insurance Licence.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
