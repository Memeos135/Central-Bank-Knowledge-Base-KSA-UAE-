# Borrower

Defined-party node for the Borrower raising funds through a crowdfunding platform. It governs the crowdfunding company's Borrower-side obligations: due diligence and risk assessment, credit-bureau based information gathering and risk scoring/pricing, AML checks and identification of the ultimate beneficial owner, measures preventing borrowing for personal use, at least annual review of the Borrower's financial situation, escalation and action plans where risk increases, use of the Central Bank's default definition and methodology, and Borrower declarations of current and intended borrowing from other platforms and sources. It binds the licensed crowdfunding company and imposes disclosure duties on Borrowers via the Client Agreement.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
