# Tier 2 Capital

Defined supplementary layer of regulatory capital under the CBUAE capital adequacy framework, added to Tier 1 to produce Total Capital for the total capital adequacy ratio. Instruments that cease to qualify as Tier 2 (or as non-common equity Tier 1) are phased out gradually under the transitional rules, with eligibility criteria detailed in the accompanying Standards. Applies to all banks determining regulatory capital and ratio compliance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
