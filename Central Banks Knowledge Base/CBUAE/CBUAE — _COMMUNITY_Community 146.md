# Takaful Insurance Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing-level architecture for takaful insurance: the statutory basis for establishing and operating a takaful insurance fund, the standards that give it operational content, and the operational risk regime applied to the takaful operator.

## How members connect

- All instruments in the cluster derive authority from the same primary statute (the Central Bank Law), giving a law → standard → article hierarchy.
- The fund-establishment article operates as the trigger provision; the controls-and-procedures standard supplies the conditions a takaful operator must satisfy to comply with it.
- The operational-model standard and the operational risk regulation attach parallel conduct and governance duties to the same licensed takaful operator, so a fund cannot be assessed for compliance on segregation rules alone.
- Cross-regime point: takaful fund segregation obligations sit alongside generic prudential/operational-risk obligations; the two must be read cumulatively, not as alternatives.
