# Outsourcing Standards for Banks

The Outsourcing Standards for Banks (Circular 14/2021) are the enforceable elaboration of the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation, mirroring its article structure. They require an outsourcing-inclusive risk governance framework addressing operational and compliance risk from service providers, confirm the Board's ultimate responsibility for outsourced activities, keep outsourced activities within internal audit and compliance scope (including power to extend third-party audit scope), require management of cross-border provider and subcontractor risk, and set the minimum submission pack for obtaining Central Bank non-objection before outsourcing a Material Business Activity. Binds all Banks operating in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
