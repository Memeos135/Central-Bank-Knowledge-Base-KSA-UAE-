# Counterparty Credit Risk (SA-CCR)

Covers the counterparty credit risk framework (SA-CCR) and related capital topics, including exposure measurement for written and purchased credit derivatives, capping effective notional at maximum potential loss, and offsetting conditions requiring protection on a pari passu or junior reference obligation with same-or-more-conservative terms. The surrounding material links this to capital planning, leverage ratio and ICAAP material-risk identification, including credit concentration risk and IRRBB. It binds banks calculating derivative exposures and setting capital demand.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
