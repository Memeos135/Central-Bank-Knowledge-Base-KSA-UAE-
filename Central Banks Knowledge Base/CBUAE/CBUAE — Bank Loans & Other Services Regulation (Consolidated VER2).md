# Bank Loans & Other Services Regulation (Consolidated VER2)

Consolidated (version 2) text of the CBUAE regulation on bank loans and other services offered to individual (retail) customers, issued under Notice 29/2011 pursuant to the Union Law on the Central Bank, monetary system and banking organisation. It sets the conduct and product terms governing the relationship between banks (conventional and Islamic) and finance companies and their individual customers, covering loan definitions, instalment/salary-deduction caps (including a special regime for Armed Forces staff), bank account types, account closure timelines, and commissions, fees and charges. It binds all licensed banks and finance companies lending to individuals in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
