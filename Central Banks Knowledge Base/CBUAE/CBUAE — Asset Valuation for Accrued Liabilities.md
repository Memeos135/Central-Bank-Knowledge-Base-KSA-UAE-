# Asset Valuation for Accrued Liabilities

Sets the valuation rules an insurance company must apply when determining which assets count as Admissible Assets backing accrued insurance liabilities, including the prudent person principle, mark-to-model governance (senior management/Investment Committee awareness, model change control, periodic model review, risk function understanding of model weaknesses) and independent real estate revaluation thresholds. It also addresses assets held for life contracts where investment risk is borne by policyholders (unit-linked and index-linked benefits). Binds licensed insurance companies and branches of Foreign Insurance Companies in the UAE, with solvency-related consequences for asset admissibility.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
