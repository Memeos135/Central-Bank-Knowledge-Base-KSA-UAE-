# Correspondent Banking Risk

## Why this community

Due diligence regime for correspondent banking relationships: identifying risk factors, applying standard, specific and enhanced due diligence, and sustaining monitoring, governance and record-keeping.

## How members connect

- Risk mitigation is the obligation hub, chaining standard CDD, correspondent-specific due diligence, EDD for high-risk relationships, ongoing monitoring, independent audit and record-keeping.
- Risk factors (nesting, payable-through accounts, geography, ownership/management structure) are the trigger set that escalates a relationship to enhanced due diligence.
- FATF Recommendation 13 is the international source obligation; the Wolfsberg CBDDQ is the market-standard information-gathering instrument referenced to satisfy it.
- Standard CDD and record-keeping cross-refer to the general CDD/KYC and record-keeping guidance, showing hierarchy from general AML rules to the correspondent-banking overlay.
