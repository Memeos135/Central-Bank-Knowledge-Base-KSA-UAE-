# Contingency Funding Plan (CFP)

Sets out the expected characteristics of a bank's Contingency Funding Plan: proportionate to complexity and role in the system, with diversified contingency measures, identified funding sources and amounts, escalation and activation procedures, lead times, defined roles including authority to invoke, and a crisis team. The CFP must be integrated with ongoing liquidity risk analysis and with stress-test scenarios covering firm-specific and market-wide stress. Applies to banks as part of the qualitative liquidity risk management requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
