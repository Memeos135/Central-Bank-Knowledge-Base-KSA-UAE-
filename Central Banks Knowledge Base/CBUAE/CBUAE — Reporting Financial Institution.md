# Reporting Financial Institution

Defined term for the institution that carries the CRS due diligence and reporting obligations — in this instrument, insurance companies (including Takaful) issuing Cash Value Insurance or Annuity Contracts under Insurance Authority supervision. The node anchors who must identify Reportable Accounts, when reliance on self-certifications, documentary evidence or AML/KYC information is permitted, and the aggregation rules that apply to it.

**Regimes:** other, AML/CTF, data/credit info
