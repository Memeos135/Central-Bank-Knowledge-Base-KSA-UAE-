# Sanctions Evasion

Guidance expectation that LFIs detect and block attempts to evade, avoid or circumvent sanctions, not merely direct list matches. It flags typical evasion tactics (renaming, intermediaries, front companies, alternative financial networks) and positions CDD/EDD alongside screening as the mechanism for identifying complex evasion; it also sits next to the sanctions risk appetite and internal controls expectations (applicable regimes, 50% ownership/control treatment, unilateral and secondary sanctions exposure). Applies to all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
