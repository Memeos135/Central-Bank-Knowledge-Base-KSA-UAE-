# Licensee

Defined-scope node for "Licensee" under the Fitness and Propriety Regulation and Standards, covering collectively all Licensed Financial Institutions, Insurance Companies, Takaful Insurance Companies and Insurance-Related Professions. It frames the objective and reach of the fit-and-proper regime: requirements apply to the Licensee as a whole and to individuals involved in management, control or the exercise of significant power or responsibility, and UAE-established Licensees must extend compliance across subsidiaries, affiliates and international branches. Useful for resolving whether a particular entity or group structure falls within the regime.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Fit and Proper Process|Fit and Proper Process]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** The obligation to run the fit and proper process before appointing authorised individuals or material risk takers rests on the licensee, so the licensee is the accountable party even where assessment work is performed elsewhere in a group. The cross-reference also records a narrow relief: reliance on another jurisdiction's fit and proper requirements is possible, but only where the licensee satisfies the Central Bank that those requirements are adequate and are actually being met. The consequence is that cross-border or group-level vetting cannot be assumed to substitute for the local process; the exemption is discretionary and must be obtained, not self-certified.
