# UAE AML Framework

Node linking the dormant accounts regime to the UAE AML/CFT/CPF framework: institutions must keep customer profiles current in line with the AML federal decree-law, its executive regulation and CBUAE AML notices, and must monitor reactivated or claimed dormant accounts closely to ensure they are not misused. It sits at the intersection of dormancy/reactivation controls (ID re-verification, payment records, one-month claim settlement) and ongoing CDD obligations. Binds licensed financial institutions, including insurance companies, subject to CBUAE AML supervision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
