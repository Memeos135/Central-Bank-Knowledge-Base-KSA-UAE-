# Advances to Stable Resources Ratio (ASRR)

Concept node on the Advances to Stable Resources Ratio, the structural funding measure applied to banks that captures actual and likely uses of funds against the contractual maturity and behavioural profile of funding sources, to limit maturity mismatches and cliff effects, with requirements detailed in Central Bank BRF 7 reporting. It sits alongside the LCR and the NSFR (available stable funding over required stable funding, minimum 100%) in the liquidity ratio suite. Binds banks in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
