# Article 2: Governance and Risk Appetite

Article 2 sets the governance and risk appetite requirements for Climate-related Financial Risks: the Board and Senior Management must have adequate knowledge and a holistic view of the FI's exposures, embed these risks in governance and Risk Appetite frameworks, and review the climate component of risk appetite at least annually. Delegation of operational functions to Board committees or Senior Management is permitted but must be documented with clear responsibility, authority and escalation/reporting lines, and assigned functions must be adequately resourced and trained. It also requires a governance framework for green/sustainable/ESG-labelled assets and liabilities, including labelling criteria and due diligence and ongoing monitoring of customers and use of funds for green products.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
