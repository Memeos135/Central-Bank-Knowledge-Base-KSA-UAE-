# Article 8: Breaches of Minimum Capital Requirement

Provision governing actual or anticipated breaches of the minimum capital requirement by a Restricted Licence Bank: immediate notification to the Central Bank, submission of a remediation plan for Central Bank approval to restore capital, and Central Bank approval for any proposed profit distribution. Operates as the supervisory escalation and capital-preservation mechanism attached to Articles 4–7. Binds Restricted Licence Banks, including branches of foreign banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
