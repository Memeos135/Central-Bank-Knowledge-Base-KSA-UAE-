# Islamic Finance Institution

Concept node capturing the Islamic-finance-specific provisions (Article 14) of the CBUAE operational risk instrument, dealing with Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk as a category of operational risk. It requires an Islamic Financial Institution to maintain a Shari'ah governance and risk framework, test interlinkages of Shari'ah non-compliance with other risks across the contract life cycle, record and report non-recognised (purified) income, keep separate accounts for restricted/unrestricted investment accounts and Takaful funds, and refrain from circumventing contractually assumed risk. It binds licensed financial institutions conducting Islamic finance business, with determinative authority vested in the internal Shari'ah supervision committee and the Higher Shari'ah Authority's resolutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
