# Money Laundering

Concept node for money laundering as defined and criminalised under the UAE AML-CFT Law, as explained in the CBUAE AML/CFT and Illegal Organisations Guidelines for Financial Institutions. It covers the constituent acts (transferring/concealing/acquiring proceeds, assisting a perpetrator to escape punishment), the deliberately broad definition of "funds" (including virtual assets, securities, contracts, IP and physical property), and the international standard-setting architecture (UN/GPML, FATF Recommendations, FSRBs) that frames national obligations. It binds supervised financial institutions in the UAE and is relevant to KSA teams only as comparative/cross-border material.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
