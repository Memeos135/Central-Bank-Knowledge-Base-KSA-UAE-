# Circular 25/2005 Loans to Finance Purchase of Company Shares

Original text of CBUAE Circular 25/2005, addressed to banks, finance companies and investment companies, setting lending limits for loans used to purchase company shares against pledge of those shares. It caps advances by reference to nominal, book or market value depending on whether the borrower is a founder, an IPO subscriber or a holder of shares in an established company, extends a lower cap to GCC-incorporated company shares, requires collateral prioritisation by quality and liquidity, and penalises book-lending to share subscribers by forfeiture of the resulting interest.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
