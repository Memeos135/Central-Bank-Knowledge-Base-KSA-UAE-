# Federal Decree Law No. 20 of 2018 (AML/CFT)

This node captures the AML/CFT chapter of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business and its anchoring legislation — the UAE AML/CFT Decree Law of 2018 as amended in 2021, together with its implementing Cabinet Decision. It sets out what a Licensed Person's AML/CFT programme must contain: enterprise-wide ML/FT risk assessment, board- and senior-management-approved policies reviewed at least annually, group-wide application to majority-owned branches, subsidiaries and affiliates, and appointment of a senior, board-reporting and adequately resourced Compliance Officer.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
