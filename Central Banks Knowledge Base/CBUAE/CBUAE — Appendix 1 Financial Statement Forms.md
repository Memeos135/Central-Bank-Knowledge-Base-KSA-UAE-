# Appendix 1: Financial Statement Forms

Node covering the prescribed financial statement forms in Appendix (1) of the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies and the reporting articles that invoke them. It governs the format, signing, audit/limited review and submission deadlines for quarterly (45 days after quarter end) and annual reporting packs, including the board, actuary, auditor and management reports. Binding on insurance companies and branches of foreign insurers filing with the Authority; the appendix and addendums are an integral part of the regulations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
