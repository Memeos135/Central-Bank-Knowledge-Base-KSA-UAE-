# Rate of Return Risk

Rate of return risk is the Islamic-finance analogue of interest rate risk addressed by the IRRBB Regulation and Standards: Banks offering Islamic financial services must apply the same governance, measurement, limit-setting, stress testing and reporting principles to it, and the Regulation contains a dedicated Islamic banking article. Related defined concepts include Islamic Financial Services (covering Islamic Banks and Islamic Windows of conventional banks), displaced commercial risk, risk limits, risk governance framework and Senior Management. It binds Islamic Banks and conventional Banks with Islamic Windows.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
