# Reportable Account

Defined term for an account identified, through the CRS due diligence procedures, as held by a Reportable Person (or a Passive NFE with Reportable Person controlling persons) resident in a Reportable Jurisdiction. The node covers indicia-based classification, thresholds (including the USD 250,000 review threshold for Pre-existing Entity Accounts), change-of-circumstances triggers, and the duty to report undocumented accounts to the UAE Competent Authority.

**Regimes:** other, data/credit info
