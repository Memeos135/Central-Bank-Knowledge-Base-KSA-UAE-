# Federal Law No. 2 of 2015 (Commercial Companies)

Cross-reference node to UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2015 on Commercial Companies, cited as the corporate-form requirement for a loan-based crowdfunding company (partnerships and limited partnerships excluded) whose objects include e-finance or similar activities. Appears in the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation (Circular 7/2020), which is issued under Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 and applies to crowdfunding companies engaging in loan-based crowdfunding activities in the UAE except the Financial Free Zones. Useful for eligibility and licensing-scope questions rather than conduct obligations.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation|Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an applicant may be licensed to run loan-based crowdfunding, corporate-form and constitutional questions are decided under the commercial companies statute, not the crowdfunding rules alone. The Regulation's preamble invokes the Commercial Companies Law, so incorporation, shareholding, capital and corporate governance requirements for the applicant entity are read as a background layer that the licensing conditions sit on top of. Practically, a firm that satisfies the crowdfunding platform conditions but is not validly constituted or is in a form not permitted under company law still fails the gate, and both regimes must be evidenced in a licence file.
