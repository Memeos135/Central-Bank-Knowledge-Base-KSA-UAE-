# Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular No. 8-2020)

Node covering the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular No. 8-2020) and its accompanying Standards, with the extracted material centred on Article 6 (protection of consumer data and assets) and parts of Article 5 on suitability/affordability assessment and acting in the consumer's best interest. It requires licensed financial institutions to hold board-approved policies and controls over collection, confidentiality and authorised use of consumer data, to inform consumers in writing how their personal data is processed, to extend confidentiality to authorised agents and new-technology/social-media profiling and marketing, and to retain sales-profiling assessments for at least five years. Binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions (including licensed foreign bank branches) in their dealings with Consumers; confidentiality yields only to lawful disclosure orders or express consumer consent.
