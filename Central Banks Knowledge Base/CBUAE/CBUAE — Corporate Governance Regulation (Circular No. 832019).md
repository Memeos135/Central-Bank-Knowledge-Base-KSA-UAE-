# Corporate Governance Regulation (Circular No. 83/2019)

This node is the Corporate Governance Regulation (Circular No. 83/2019), the parent instrument that the Corporate Governance Standards elaborate on article by article. It frames obligations on board and senior management accountability, the Risk Governance Framework (including the three lines of defence and, for Islamic financial services, internal Shari'ah audit), group-level governance and internal control, code of conduct prohibiting fraud, sanctions breaches, money laundering, bribery and consumer rights violations, and related party controls. It binds all banks licensed by the Central Bank, with group-wide duties on a bank acting as Controlling Shareholder.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
