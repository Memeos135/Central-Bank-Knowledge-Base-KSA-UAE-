# Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks 83/2019

CBUAE Circular 83/2019 (Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks) and its accompanying Standards, issued under the Central Bank Law, establishing the overarching prudential framework and minimum acceptable standards for bank corporate governance. It covers board structure and committees, fit-and-proper and Central Bank no-objection for board appointments/removals, compensation caps for Senior Management and Material Risk Takers, and disclosure/transparency, while leaving risk management, internal control, compliance, audit and outsourcing to separate instruments. It binds all Banks and Groups operating in the UAE, with ultimate responsibility on the Board; this is a CBUAE, not SAMA, instrument.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
