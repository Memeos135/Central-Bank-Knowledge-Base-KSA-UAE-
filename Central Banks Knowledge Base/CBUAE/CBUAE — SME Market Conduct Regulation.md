# SME Market Conduct Regulation

This is the CBUAE Small to Medium Sized Enterprises (SME) Market Conduct Regulation (Circular 1/2021, dated 26/01/2021), issued under the Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018) and addressed to all Banks and Finance Companies. It sets market conduct, governance and oversight obligations for Licensed Financial Institutions when designing, promoting and selling Financial Products or Services to SME Customers, with the aim of improving SME access to finance. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, including Islamic Financial Institutions, and defines key terms such as Authorised Agent, SME size tiers, HSA and ISSC.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
