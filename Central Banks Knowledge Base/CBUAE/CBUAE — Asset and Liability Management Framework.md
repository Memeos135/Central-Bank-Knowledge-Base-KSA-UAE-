# Asset and Liability Management Framework

Requires an institution housing an Islamic Window to establish an Asset and Liability Management framework for Shari'ah-compliant assets and liabilities, including ring-fencing and demonstrable segregation from the institution's conventional book (separate product codes mapped to specific General Ledger accounts, with alternative methods only on CBUAE approval). Related provisions govern managing surpluses/deficits in a Shari'ah-compliant way, ISSC and CBUAE approval of the internal funding mechanism, treatment of non-Shari'ah-compliant income, a prohibition on converting or transferring Shari'ah-compliant assets to the conventional side, and separate branding of Islamic products.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
