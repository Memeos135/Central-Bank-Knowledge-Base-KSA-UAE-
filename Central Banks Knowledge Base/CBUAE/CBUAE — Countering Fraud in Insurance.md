# Countering Fraud in Insurance

Section of the CBUAE insurance standards requiring each Company to maintain policies, procedures and controls against internal and external insurance fraud across product development, client onboarding, staff hiring and dismissal, outsourcing, claims handling and dealings with Insurance Related Professions. It classifies fraud as internal, Insurance Related Professions' fraud and policyholder fraud, and mandates an independent audit of fraud controls, a whistleblowing policy, market fraud intelligence sharing, record-keeping and turnaround standards, board reporting, and staff/board training. Suspected or confirmed fraud must be reported immediately to law enforcement with notification to the Central Bank; the fraud strategy must be calibrated to the Company's Risk Profile.
