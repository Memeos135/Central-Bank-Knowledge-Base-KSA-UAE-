# Article 39: Amendment to Retail Payment & SVF Regulation

Article 39 resolves the perimeter overlap between the Payment Token Services Regulation and the existing Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes Regulation and the Stored Value Facilities (SVF) Regulation. At the end of the Transition Period, persons licensed under those regulations for Crypto-Asset, Virtual Asset Token or Virtual Asset activities cease to be licensed for those activities, and both regulations stop applying to those defined crypto/virtual asset categories and their service providers.

**Regimes:** payments, other
