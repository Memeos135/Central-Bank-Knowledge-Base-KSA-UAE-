# Separation of Persons/Fund and Property/Liability Insurance

Concept node for the separation requirement imposed on existing licensed composite insurers: full segregation between insurance of persons and fund accumulation operations and property and liability operations across procedures, systems, records and personnel, with the general manager as the only shared function. It also covers the class definitions cross-referenced to the Implementing Regulations and the separate investment strategy, bookkeeping and dual (consolidated plus per-type) reporting obligations. Useful when assessing whether a composite insurer's ring-fencing arrangements meet the standard.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, banking prudential
