# Threshold Deduction

The CBUAE treatment under which significant investments in the common shares of unconsolidated banking, financial and insurance entities and deferred tax assets arising from temporary differences are deducted from CET1 only above specified limits, with amounts below the limits aggregated and risk-weighted at 250% up to a further aggregate cap. The appendix explains the individual and aggregate limit calculations and the hypothetical CET1 base used for the aggregate test. Applies to banks computing regulatory capital under the CBUAE capital adequacy standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
