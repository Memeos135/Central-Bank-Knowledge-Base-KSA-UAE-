# Credit Information Agency

Defines the Credit Information Agency as Al Etihad Credit Bureau or any credit information agency established for UAE credit reporting, and frames the credit reporting duties tied to it. Restricted Licence Finance Companies and their Agents must supply Borrower credit information to the Agency on its terms and must obtain a credit report before extending credit of AED 5,000 or more; sharing or accessing Borrower credit information, including from employers, requires the Borrower's express consent, with disclosure to the Borrower and full compliance with data protection law.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, BNPL/finance, consumer protection
