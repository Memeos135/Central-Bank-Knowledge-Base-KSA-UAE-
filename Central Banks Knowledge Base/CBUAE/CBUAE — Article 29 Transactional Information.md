# Article 29: Transactional Information

Article 29 of the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation addresses transactional information — the information a Payment Token Service Provider must give Customers in connection with individual payment token transactions, following on from the pre-contractual and Customer Agreement content required by Article 28. The provided context captures mainly the Article 28 list preceding it, so the operative text of Article 29 should be read in the source document.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments
