# Article 31: Liability for Unauthorized Transfers and Refunds

Article 31 allocates liability for Unauthorized Payment Token Transfers and sets the refund duty. A Payment Token Custodian and Transferor is fully liable for fraudulent or unauthorised transfers it initiates, facilitates, effects or directs, or otherwise made from a Wallet it maintains, whether before or after the Customer reports suspected fraud, unless the Customer acted fraudulently or with gross negligence in failing to keep the Wallet safe; where liability attaches, the amount must be refunded. It binds custody-and-transfer providers vis-à-vis Customers and Tokenholders, and links to Customer Agreement disclosure duties on notification timelines and liability.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
