# Standard re External Shari'ah Audit for Takaful Insurance Companies

CBUAE Standard applying External Shari'ah Audit requirements to Takaful Insurance Companies, covering the Shari'ah reference basis, referral of unresolved issues to the company's ISSC, and Central Bank approval and fit-and-proper criteria for external Shari'ah audit firms. It also addresses the case where a company has no head of Internal Shari'ah Audit, permitting an approved external firm to perform that role for a capped term while prohibiting the same firm from also conducting the external Shari'ah audit. Applies to incorporated Takaful Insurance Companies, whether the appointment is voluntary or regulatorily required.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
