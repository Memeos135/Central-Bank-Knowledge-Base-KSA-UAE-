# Emirates Insurance Federation

Node associated with the insurance-sector industry body referenced in Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025; the available context sets out the surrounding defined terms for the insurance market rather than the federation's own provisions. Those definitions include Premium (Contribution in Takaful), Insurance Broker as an independent licensed intermediary remunerated by commission, Insurance Agent acting on an insurer's behalf, Technical Provisions, Solvency Margin, and the scope of Insurance-Related Professions (agent, broker, surveyor and loss adjuster, consultant, Actuary, health insurance third-party administrators, and others determined by the Central Bank). Open the source to confirm the federation's mandate and membership obligations, which are not evidenced in this extract.
