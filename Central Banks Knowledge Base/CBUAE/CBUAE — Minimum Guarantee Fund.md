# Minimum Guarantee Fund

Defined solvency floor under the CBUAE Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies: the Minimum Guarantee Fund must be at least one-third of the Solvency Capital Requirement and is also built up from per-line-of-business minimum amounts (an absolute minimum or a percentage of net earned contributions, whichever is higher, as set by the Authority). It is one of the three benchmarks (with Minimum Capital Requirement and Solvency Capital Requirement) whose largest value the Company must cover with Own Funds at all times, and it must be fully met by Own Funds (Basic plus only 50% of Ancillary). Binds licensed Takaful and Re-Takaful operators, with immediate notification to the Authority and a recovery plan on breach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
