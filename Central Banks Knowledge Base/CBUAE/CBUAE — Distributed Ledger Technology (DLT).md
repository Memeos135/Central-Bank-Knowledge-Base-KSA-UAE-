# Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)

Covers the Distributed Ledger Technology chapter of the Enabling Technologies Guidelines: governance of the ledger (ownership model, participant and validator rules, access approvals, key management, consensus protocol, off-chain procedures), roles and responsibilities of core groups, qualified users, participants and third parties, plus security, data standardisation/interoperability, retention and business continuity. Notably expects Personal Data to be kept off-chain and ledger pruning/key destruction to support retention and erasure. Applies to Institutions deploying DLT Applications, including via Outsourcing Service Providers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, other
