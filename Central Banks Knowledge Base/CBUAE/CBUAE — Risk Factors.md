# Risk Factors

A concept node setting out the risk-factor categories used in the business-wide ML/TF risk assessment — customers, products, services and transactions, delivery channels, geographic locations, and other relevant factors — and how inherent risk is identified, weighted (likelihood, timing, impact) and classified before controls are applied to derive residual risk. It stresses that risks typically arise from combinations of factors and that identified factors drive both risk categorisation and the calibration of mitigation measures and internal policies. Binds CBUAE-supervised financial institutions applying the risk-based approach.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
