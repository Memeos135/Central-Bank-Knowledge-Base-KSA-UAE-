# Section 2.1.4 Structured Investment Products Provisions

Disclosure regime for structured investment products and Shari'ah compliant structured products, applying in addition to the general provisions of Section 2.1.1 and subject to prior Central Bank approval before marketing or selling. It requires pre-contract disclosure of the issuing bank or entity and its contact details, allocation of responsibilities between the Consumer's institution and the issuer for sales, performance and management, identification of the product's regulator and any Third Party developer and whether it is UAE-regulated, plus quarterly statement content on net interest/profit and loss, itemised and cumulative fees, and complaint channels. IFIs must additionally explain the applicable Shari'ah concepts and investment account rights and obligations in plain language.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
