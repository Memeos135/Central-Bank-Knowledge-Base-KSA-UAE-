# Pillar 3 Market Disclosures

Concept node for Pillar 3 regulatory market disclosure requirements, aimed at market discipline through comparable public reporting of capital, RWAs and risk exposures. Covers scope (all UAE banks at top consolidated level and foreign bank branches, including Islamic financial services providers), publication as a standalone report on the bank's website with a five-year archive, disclosure frequency and signposting, periodic external audit of annual reports, a Board-approved disclosure policy with senior officer attestation, and the process for seeking Central Bank approval to withhold proprietary or confidential items.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
