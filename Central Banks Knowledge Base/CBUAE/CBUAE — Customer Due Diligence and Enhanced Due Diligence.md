# Customer Due Diligence and Enhanced Due Diligence

A concept node within CBUAE guidance for licensed financial institutions (LFIs) setting out how the risk-based approach and CDD/EDD components apply to customers from the dealers in precious metals and stones (DPMS) and real estate sectors. It covers customer identification and licensing checks, beneficial ownership identification at the 25% controlling-interest threshold with the senior managing official fallback, understanding the purpose of the account and nature of the business, and ongoing/transactional due diligence where a low-risk customer executes a high-risk sector transaction. It binds CBUAE-licensed LFIs (banks, exchange houses, finance companies, PSPs, hawala providers, insurers and brokers), not the DPMS/real estate firms themselves.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
