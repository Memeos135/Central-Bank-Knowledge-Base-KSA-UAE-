# IA Board Decision No. 25 of 2016 (Regulation of Unified Motor Policies)

Founding UAE Insurance Authority decision issuing the Regulation of Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies, under which the standard Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Loss and Damage is set. It fixes the mandatory contract terms between insurer and insured — general conditions, total-loss and depreciation-based compensation, salvage/towing and repair-quality obligations on the company, dispute handling over damage value, and an exhaustive list of exclusions (unlicensed drivers, racing, overloading, use outside the declared purpose, intentional traffic offences). Binds insurers writing motor own-damage cover in the UAE and defines the rights of insured vehicle owners.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
