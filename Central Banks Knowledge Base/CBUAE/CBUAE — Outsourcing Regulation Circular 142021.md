# Outsourcing Regulation Circular 14/2021

A defined-term/cross-reference node identifying the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation for Banks and its accompanying Outsourcing Standards (Circular 14/2021, as amended) as used in the Open Finance framework, alongside the definition of "Outsourcing" (an arrangement, onshore or offshore, including with related parties, for another party to perform on a continuing basis an activity the provider could perform itself). Its operative consequence here is that the API Hub must comply with the Outsourcing Regulation as if it were a "Bank", subject to any Central Bank exemptions and any additional outsourcing requirements or minimum international standards the Central Bank imposes. It binds the API Hub and, by definition, Open Finance Providers relying on outsourced arrangements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, banking prudential
