# Data and Customer Engagement

## Why this community

Expectations on data underpinning the transition plan and on engagement with customers/counterparties whose activities drive the institution's climate exposure.

## How members connect

- Standalone principle; supplies the informational basis that metrics, targets and strategy integration depend on.
- Potential interaction with consumer protection and data/credit information regimes where customer engagement involves collecting or using client-level data - scope limits of those regimes should be checked before acting.
