# Article 40: Transition Period

Article 40 establishes a one calendar-year Transition Period from commencement of the Regulation during which Article 2 (the licensing/registration and prohibition provisions) does not apply, extendable at the Central Bank's discretion. Despite the grace period, the Central Bank may order cessation of any in-scope business of a service provider it judges unlikely to be able to comply with Article 2 after the Transition Period. The accompanying articles designate the Regulatory Development Department as the interpretive authority and set publication and entry into force one month after Official Gazette publication.
