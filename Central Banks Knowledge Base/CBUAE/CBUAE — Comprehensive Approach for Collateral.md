# Comprehensive Approach for Collateral

Concept node for the comprehensive approach to collateral under the credit risk mitigation framework, under which collateral value is adjusted by haircuts rather than substituting the collateral's risk weight. It is the only approach permitted in the trading book, cannot be combined with the simple approach in the banking book, requires prior Central Bank approval, and is also used for counterparty risk charges on OTC derivatives and repo-style transactions. Binds banks calculating risk-weighted assets under CBUAE credit risk standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
