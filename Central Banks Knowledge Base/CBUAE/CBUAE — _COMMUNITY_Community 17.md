# Payment Token Issuance

## Why this community

Conduct and prudential obligations on licensed payment token issuers, covering disclosure, finality of transfers on distributed ledgers, and the risk-management and technology-security framework expected of the issuer.

## How members connect

- 'Payment Token Issuer' is the obligated person; the licence requirement and the dirham-denominated token definition set who is in scope and for what.
- The white paper article is the disclosure gateway, and the transfer-finality article cross-refers to it — settlement certainty representations must match what was disclosed.
- General risk management and internal controls is the parent obligation; technology risk and information security is its specialised limb, applied to the underlying DLT arrangements.
- The 'Virtual Asset' reference marks a scope boundary — tokens meeting the payment token definition are carved out of, or distinguished from, the virtual asset regime.
