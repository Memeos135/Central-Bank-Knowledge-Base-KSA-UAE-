# Aggregate Capital Funds

## Why this community

Minimum aggregate capital funds a licensee must hold and maintain on an ongoing basis — a prudential entry and continuing-authorisation condition rather than a conduct rule.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: no internal cross-references captured; it functions as a standalone prudential threshold.
- Practically it should be read against the licensing and enforcement provisions of whichever regime imposes it, since capital shortfall is typically a supervisory trigger.
- Check the source instrument before applying — the applicable figure and calculation basis are regime-specific (payments/SVF vs finance companies).
