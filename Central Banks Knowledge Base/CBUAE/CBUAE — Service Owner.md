# Service Owner

Defined term in the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation for a Licensee that holds an Account or Product for a User. The node sets the Service Owner's duties where a User consents to initiation through a Service Initiation Provider — secure communication, immediate provision of information needed to initiate the transaction, status display until completion, and parity of treatment with instructions received directly from the User — plus authentication duties (at minimum two-factor, with stronger measures in higher-risk cases) shared with Data Holders, and liability to the User for non-execution or defective execution subject to carve-outs. Binds UAE Licensees holding customer accounts or products.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
