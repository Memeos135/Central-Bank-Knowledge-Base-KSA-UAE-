# International Judicial Cooperation

## Why this community

Formal judicial cooperation with foreign states (mutual legal assistance, letters rogatory, evidence and procedural requests) in money laundering / terrorist financing matters.

## How members connect

- Complements the law enforcement exchange gateway: same cooperation chapter, but through judicial rather than administrative channels.
- Feeds the recognition-and-enforcement provisions, since assistance requests often precede or accompany foreign judgment execution.
- Conditioned by reciprocity and domestic procedural safeguards typical of this chapter.
