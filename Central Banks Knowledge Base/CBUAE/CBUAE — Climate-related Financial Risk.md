# Climate-related Financial Risk

Concept node for climate-related financial risk as used in the CBUAE Principles for the Effective Management of Climate-related Financial Risks. It frames climate risk not as a standalone risk type but as a driver that materialises through physical and transition (and possibly liability) channels within traditional risk categories — credit, market, operational, underwriting, reputational and liquidity — over short, medium and long horizons. It binds licensed financial firms in the UAE (banks, insurers and other CBUAE-supervised entities within the stated scope), which are expected to maintain a sound climate risk management framework with a board-approved risk appetite.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
