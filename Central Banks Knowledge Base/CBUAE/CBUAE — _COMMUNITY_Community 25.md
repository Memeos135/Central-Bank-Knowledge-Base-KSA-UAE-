# Insurance Marketing Regulation

## Why this community

Bancassurance-style distribution: the conditions under which insurance policies may be marketed and sold through banks, and the statutory bases (central bank, banking and electronic transactions legislation) that authorise and constrain that channel.

## How members connect

- The core Board Decision is the operative instrument; it draws authority from the central bank law and the banks/financial institutions law, and relies on the electronic transactions law to validate remote/e-signed sales.
- A later amending decision modifies the core decision, and a consolidated version merges original plus amendment - the consolidated text is the one to cite for current obligations.
- Hierarchy runs primary law -> regulator board decision -> amendment -> consolidation; the amendment has no standalone meaning outside the parent decision.
- Scope caution: these are Insurance Authority / federal-law instruments, not SAMA rules; use only for comparative analysis unless the KSA equivalent is separately identified.
