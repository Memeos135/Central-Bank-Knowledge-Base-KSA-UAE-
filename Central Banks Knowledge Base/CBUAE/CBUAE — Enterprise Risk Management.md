# Enterprise Risk Management

Concept node on Enterprise Risk Management as required of insurance companies under the CBUAE ERM Standards, read together with the Relevant Regulations (risk management and internal controls, corporate governance, recovery planning, group supervision and the 2014 financial regulations for conventional and Takaful companies). It covers the Risk Management function's support to the Board, risk committee and Senior Management across solvency, capital and liquidity planning, product and business planning, reinsurance/re-takaful strategy, ALM and investments, plus risk identification, interdependencies and correlation under stress. Takaful operators must additionally address Shari'ah non-compliance risk, fund segregation and use of conventional reinsurance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Takaful Insurance|Takaful Insurance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If the entity under review writes takaful business, the general enterprise risk management obligations apply with mandatory additions rather than in their baseline form. The ERM instrument expressly directs takaful companies to account for business-model-specific risks — Shari'ah non-compliance risk, risks from segregation of the takaful insurance fund, and risks from using conventional reinsurance — while the takaful instrument defines the actors and operations concerned. Practically, a risk framework that is adequate for a conventional insurer is not automatically adequate for a takaful operator, and gap assessments should test for these named risk categories explicitly.
