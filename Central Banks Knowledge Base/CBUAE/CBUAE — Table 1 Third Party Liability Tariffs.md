# Table 1: Third Party Liability Tariffs

Table 1 attached to the Motor Vehicle Insurance Tariffs Regulation, forming an integral part of it, setting the minimum and maximum AED premiums for Third Party Liability motor policies over a 13-month insurance period. Rates are banded by vehicle type and use (saloon private/commercial, four-wheel drive, etc.) and by engine cylinder count. It binds insurers pricing third-party liability motor cover, which must compete only within the stated floor and ceiling.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
