# Glossary of Terms

The defined-terms appendix and interpretive conventions of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions, giving the meanings of capitalised terms used throughout (for example Financial Institution, Financial Group, Financing of Terrorism, Financing of Illegal Organisations, FATF, FSRBs, Egmont Group). It also records the interpretive rule that references to financing of terrorism include financing of illegal organisations, and that where the guidelines' paraphrases conflict with the AML-CFT Law or Cabinet Decision, the primary instruments prevail. Useful as the entry point for scope and terminology questions affecting all supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
