# Permissionless DLT and Anonymity

Guidance addressing anonymity and pseudonymity risk in permissionless DLT: institutions building such applications are expected to ensure users are neither anonymous nor pseudonymous, given the exposure to tax evasion, bribery, money laundering and terrorist financing, and to consider chain analytics to trace transactions on pseudonymous chains. It sits alongside ongoing management and monitoring expectations (periodic review, code review and validation, vulnerability assessment and penetration testing, privacy and data protection impact assessments) and code confidentiality/integrity controls. Relevant where a financial institution's DLT use intersects with AML/CTF customer identification duties.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, data/credit info
