# Restricted Licence Finance Company

Defined-term node for "Restricted Licence Finance Company" under the CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation — a licensee permitted to offer only Short-Term Credit and barred from contracting with Agents. It sits within the same regulatory framework as Finance Companies (Article 3 application, including branches) and is subject to specific borrower-facing disclosure duties: key product features, total credit amount and expiry, repayment process, Fees in both amount and percentage terms, late-payment consequences and fees, enforceable signed contracts with a copy to the Borrower, and available hardship assistance. Binds the licensed entity toward its Borrowers and the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
