# Emiratization

Defined-term and scope node for Emiratization — employment of UAE Nationals as a national initiative — as used in the CBUAE penalties regulation. It frames the regulation's objective of monitoring compliance with approved Emiratization targets and identifies the violation types targeted, including falsifying Emiratization percentages, submitting inaccurate information to obtain NAFIS benefits, and circumventing Emiratization requirements. Applies across the banking, financial and insurance sectors to Licensed Institutions in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
