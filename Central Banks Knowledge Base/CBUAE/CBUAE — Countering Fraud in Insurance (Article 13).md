# Countering Fraud in Insurance (Article 13)

Node for the countering-fraud obligations (Article 13) within the CBUAE Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation and Standards for Insurance Companies (Circular 25/2022, effective framework supplementing Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 and related Insurance Authority decisions). It requires companies to deter, prevent, detect, report and remedy internal and external fraud through a Board-approved fraud risk management strategy, structure, policies and procedures, with integrity standards for Board and staff. It binds all insurance companies in scope of the Regulation, with further fraud requirements reserved to future CBUAE decisions.
