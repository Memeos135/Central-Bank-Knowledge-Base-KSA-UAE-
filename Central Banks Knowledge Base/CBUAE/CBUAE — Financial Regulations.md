# Financial Regulations

Reference node for the Financial Regulations for insurance companies and for takaful companies (Insurance Authority Board Decisions No. 25 and No. 26 of 2014), used as the financial/prudential baseline that the Corporate Governance Regulation cross-refers to. It matters mainly for financial reporting and external audit obligations — preparing statements under applicable accounting frameworks, publishing audited annual financial statements with an opinion from a Central Bank-approved external auditor, and submitting supervisory data under the 2020 Guidance Manual decision. Binds licensed insurance and takaful Companies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
