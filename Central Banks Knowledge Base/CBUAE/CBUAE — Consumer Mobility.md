# Consumer Mobility

Concept node labelled Consumer Mobility but whose extracted content covers the Licensed Financial Institution's duty to act in the Consumer's best interest — documented suitability, appropriateness and affordability assessments (retained five years, confidentiality-bound) — and the rules on blocking Consumer accounts or funds. The blocking rules set permitted grounds (Central Bank instruction, recovery of amounts owed, dishonesty/fraud/conviction, death), a 24-hour written notice duty with a financial-crime carve-out, and prohibitions on overdraft-generating fees, over-blocking, and blocking end-of-service payments for mortgage repayment.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
