# Central Bank Law (Union Law 10/1980)

The enabling-law and scope anchor of the CBUAE Market Risk Regulation, covering the objective, scope of application and Article 1 definitions (Affiliate, Bank, Board, Group, Subsidiary, Trading Book). It confirms that the Regulation and its accompanying Standards apply to all banks, on a solo and group-wide basis where significant group relationships exist, and must be read with the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation and Standards. Relevant to determining legal basis, addressees and defined-term scope before applying any substantive article.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
