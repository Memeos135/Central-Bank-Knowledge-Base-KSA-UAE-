# Responsible Business Conduct

Article 5 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation on responsible Business conduct, covering internal culture and behaviour across product design, promotion, sales, distribution and lending/financing. It requires Licensed Financial Institutions to act with due skill, care, diligence, fairness and honesty, to manage and disclose Conflicts of Interest, to maintain a staff code of conduct with training and complaint monitoring, to ensure customer-facing staff are competent and qualified, and to set remuneration and appraisal policies that do not incentivise mis-selling or unreasonable risk-taking. Institutions remain responsible for the acts of their staff.
