# ICAAP Guidance

Supervisory guidance elaborating CBUAE expectations for the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process, covering how the ICAAP is run, approved and reported, and what the ICAAP report must contain (including executive summary, capital composition, forward-looking and stressed capital positions). It binds all banks licensed by the CBUAE, with proportionality in supervisory evaluation but no exemption from full scope for smaller or less complex banks; for foreign bank branches, board-level references map to the Managing Director or the highest UAE committee. It also addresses methodological questions such as optional use of a foundation IRB comparison (PD floor of 0.03%, fixed 45% LGD, no own LGD estimates) and quantification of climate-related financial risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Pillar 2 Risks|Pillar 2 Risks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are deciding what risks must be captured in an ICAAP, this link defines the boundary: the ICAAP exists to cover risks that Pillar 1 does not address, or does not address fully, and to size capital to those risks. The obligation chain runs from the Pillar 2 risk framework into the ICAAP, so the ICAAP's risk inventory is not discretionary — omitting a recognised Pillar 2 risk is a gap in the assessment, not a scoping choice.
