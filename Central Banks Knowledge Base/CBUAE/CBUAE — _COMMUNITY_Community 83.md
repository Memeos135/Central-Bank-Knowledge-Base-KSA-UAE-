# Consumer Data Protection

## Why this community

Consumer data protection duties owed by supervised entities: safeguarding consumer data and assets, obtaining express consent before use or disclosure, and the narrow gateway for sharing with authorized agents.

## How members connect

- A single principal obligation (Article 6 on protection of consumer data and assets) anchors the cluster; the other members are its operative components.
- Consent is the legal precondition that conditions downstream disclosure — sharing with authorized agents is only lawful within the scope of the consumer's expressed consent.
- The data management and protection function is the governance/accountability leg: an internal control obligation that implements the Article 6 standard rather than a separate right.
- Cross-regime note: consent here is a consumer-protection concept and does not substitute for AML/CDD data-handling or credit-information disclosure permissions.
