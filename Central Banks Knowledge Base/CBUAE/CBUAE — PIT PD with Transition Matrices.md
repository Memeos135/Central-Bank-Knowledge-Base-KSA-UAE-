# PIT PD with Transition Matrices

Guidance on modelling point-in-time PD and the PD term structure, which directly affect provisioning outcomes. It describes the trade-offs between rating, time-step and segmentation granularity and sets minimum expected practices for three common methods — transition matrix, portfolio average/scaling, and the Vasicek credit framework — including adjusting a TTC transition matrix with forecast Credit Indices and pre-implementation coherence checks against long-term and current observed default rates. Methodology choices must be substantiated, documented and approved by the Model Oversight Committee, including where proposed by third-party consultants.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
