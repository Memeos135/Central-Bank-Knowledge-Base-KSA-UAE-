# Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation

The CBUAE Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation for insurance companies, setting out the required risk governance system, risk appetite and limits, board approval and documentation of material changes to the risk management system, group-level risk linkages where CBUAE is not the primary regulator, the Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA), stress testing of material risks, and the compliance function. It binds licensed insurance companies operating in the UAE, including their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
