# ML/FT Typologies

Concept node cataloguing ML/FT typologies and red flags in the CBUAE Guidelines, spanning layering and integration techniques (cross-border transfers, shell companies to hide beneficial owners, real estate and securities investment, stored-value products) and sector-specific abuse such as life and general insurance premium overpayment or early cancellation. It also points FIs to FATF guidance on virtual assets and VASPs and to UAE FIU trends and typologies reports. Used by UAE supervised FIs to calibrate risk assessment and transaction monitoring expectations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
