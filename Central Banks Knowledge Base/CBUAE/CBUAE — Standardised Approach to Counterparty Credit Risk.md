# Standardised Approach to Counterparty Credit Risk

A node labelled for the standardised approach to counterparty credit risk, but the surrounding text sits in CBUAE ICAAP/Pillar 2 guidance: banks must assess whether standardised credit risk weights reflect inherent risk, benchmark capital consumption under the Standardised Approach against a foundation IRB estimation (using IFRS one-year PDs where F-IRB PDs are not calibrated), and integrate material risk identification, capital planning and the leverage ratio into the ICAAP. Binds banks and their Boards/Senior Management, which must approve the capital-demand estimation framework. Open it for supervisory expectations on internal capital adequacy rather than for SA-CCR mechanics alone.
