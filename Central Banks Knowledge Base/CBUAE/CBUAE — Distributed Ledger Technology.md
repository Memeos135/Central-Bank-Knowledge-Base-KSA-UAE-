# Distributed Ledger Technology

Concept node for the Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) on which payment tokens are issued and transferred, as treated in the CBUAE Payment Token Services regulation. It carries the issuer's duties to keep a copy of the ledger, provide a redemption route independent of normal DLT operation in case of failure or disruption, handle 'forks' by redeeming one version as the original, specify the DLT-specific point of transfer of lawful power of disposal, and warn Customers about failed, reversed or unwound transfers and settlement timing. Binds Licensed Payment Token Issuers under CBUAE (UAE) rules.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
