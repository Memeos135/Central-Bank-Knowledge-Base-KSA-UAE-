# CVA Hedges

Sets the eligibility conditions for hedges to be recognised in the CVA capital calculation: the instrument must be an index CDS, single-name CDS, single-name contingent CDS or equivalent directly referencing the hedged counterparty, and it must form part of the bank's CVA risk management programme and be managed with the intent of mitigating the counterparty credit spread component of CVA risk. Recognised CVA hedges are carved out of the bank's market risk capital calculation, while non-eligible transactions are treated as ordinary instruments for regulatory capital. Binds UAE banks applying the CVA Standard.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
