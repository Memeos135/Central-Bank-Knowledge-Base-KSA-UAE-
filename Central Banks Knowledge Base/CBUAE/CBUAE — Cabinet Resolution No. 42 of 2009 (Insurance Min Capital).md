# Cabinet Resolution No. 42 of 2009 (Insurance Min Capital)

Original 2009 UAE Cabinet Resolution setting the minimum capital regime for insurance and reinsurance undertakings, issued under the Insurance Authority law (Federal Law No. 6 of 2007). It fixes paid-up capital floors, imposes a national/GCC ownership threshold, and subjects capital increases and reductions to Insurance Authority approval, with a three-year transition for existing companies. Binds insurance companies incorporated in the UAE and licensed foreign insurers operating through a branch or agent, including cooperative/Takaful and reinsurance companies, excluding free-zone companies.

**Regimes:** other
