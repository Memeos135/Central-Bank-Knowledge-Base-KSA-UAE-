# CBUAE Guidance on Risks Relating to Politically Exposed Persons

CBUAE guidance on ML/FT risks relating to Politically Exposed Persons, issued under the supervisory-guidance mandate in Article 44.11 of the AML-CFT Decision and read alongside the CBUAE AML/CFT Procedures and Guidelines. It sets definitions for PEPs, heads of international organisations and international organisations, and articulates supervisory expectations for identifying and managing PEP relationships. Addressed to CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; it states expectations rather than creating new legal obligations, with the underlying law prevailing on conflict.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Politically Exposed Person (PEP)|Politically Exposed Person (PEP)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a screening hit identifies a customer, or a beneficial owner behind a legal person or arrangement, as a politically exposed person, the decision is not whether to apply enhanced due diligence but how to calibrate it — EDD is triggered by status, not by transaction size or type. The PEP guidance sits alongside the defined term and the EDD trigger in the rulebook, so it supplies the supervisory expectation on identification, source-of-wealth and source-of-funds enquiry, and senior management approval, rather than creating a separate obligation. For the reader this means a PEP determination closes off any simplified or ordinary-course treatment and should be documented against the guidance's risk factors.
