# Operational Risk Management Framework (Article 2)

This node points to the operational risk management framework requirement within the CBUAE Operational Risk regulation, read alongside the Board, Senior Management and risk-function articles that give it effect. The framework (including the operational resilience strategy) must be Board-approved and translated by Senior Management into implemented policies, processes, controls and systems, with an independent operational risk function that challenges business units, maintains measurement policies and reports on material risks. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, their Boards and Senior Management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
