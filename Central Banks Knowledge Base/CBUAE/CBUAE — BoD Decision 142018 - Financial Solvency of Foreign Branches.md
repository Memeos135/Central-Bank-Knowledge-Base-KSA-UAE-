# BoD Decision 14/2018 - Financial Solvency of Foreign Branches

Insurance Authority Board of Directors' Decision No. 14 of 2018 applying the financial solvency requirements of Chapter Two of the Financial Regulations to branches of foreign insurance companies operating in the UAE, including Takaful and reinsurance branches. It prescribes how a branch calculates and discloses the parent company's net assets available to cover UAE risk exposures, with mandatory deductions (parent capital, additional paid-up capital, intangibles, reserves earmarked elsewhere, capital or guarantees pledged for solvency in other jurisdictions, and other unallocated obligations). It also requires annual filing of the parent's home-country solvency margin calculations, audited financial statements and supervisory capital adequacy documents, and a deficit report analysing the impact of any parent solvency shortfall on the branch.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
