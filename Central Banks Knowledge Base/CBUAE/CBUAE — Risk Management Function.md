# Risk Management Function

Defined-term and Article 3 node for the "Risk Management Function" under Risk Management Regulation 153/2018: the aggregate of systems, structures, policies, procedures and people that measure, monitor and report risk bank-wide and, where relevant, group-wide. It requires documented roles and responsibilities, policies ensuring all material risks are identified, measured, managed, mitigated and reported, contingency arrangements (business continuity, contingency funding), and adequately resourced compliance and internal audit functions providing independent assurance. Senior Management implements the board-approved framework; the Board remains ultimately accountable notwithstanding delegation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
