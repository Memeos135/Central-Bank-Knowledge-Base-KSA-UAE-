# CBUAE Standards on IRRBB

Refers to the CBUAE's "Interest rate and rate of return risk in the banking book Standards" (notice 165/2018) as the binding domestic IRRBB regime that institutions' models must be built to satisfy; the Model Management Guidance supplies modelling methodology only, while governance, management, hedging and reporting obligations remain in the separate CBUAE Standards. Under those Standards, interest rate risk (and, for Islamic products, profit rate risk) must be captured through both expected earnings and economic value of the balance sheet, driving the NII and EVE metric set and the identification of interest-sensitive positions. Binds CBUAE-supervised banks and financial institutions with banking book exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
