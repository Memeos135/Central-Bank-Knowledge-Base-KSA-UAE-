# Legal Arrangements

Covers the treatment of Legal Arrangements (trusts and equivalent structures) under the AML/CTF/CPF Executive Regulations: trustees and persons in equivalent positions must obtain, retain, verify and update adequate, accurate and up-to-date beneficial owner and basic information, keep records on supervised intermediaries and service providers, and retain data for at least five years after their involvement ends. Trustees must disclose their status to Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and VASPs when opening a business relationship or conducting an occasional transaction above threshold, and cooperate with Concerned Authorities, Law Enforcement and the FIU on request. The rules reach both domestic and foreign legal arrangements connected to or doing business in the State.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info
