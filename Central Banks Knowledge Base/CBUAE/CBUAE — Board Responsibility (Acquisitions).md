# Board Responsibility (Acquisitions)

Article 3 of the CBUAE Major Acquisitions Regulation (Circular 02/2020), setting the board's duty to adopt policies and procedures identifying, understanding and mitigating risks inherent in Major Acquisitions, including mandatory board approval and ongoing risk reporting to the board and senior management. It specifies the minimum content of the board's review, covering capital, income, liquidity, financial position and group-structure impacts. Binds the boards of all banks (and banking groups) licensed by the CBUAE; it sits alongside the separate 10% of Total Regulatory Capital cap on holdings in commercial companies and the prior-approval regime in Article 2.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
