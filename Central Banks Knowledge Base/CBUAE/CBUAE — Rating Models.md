# Rating Models

Section of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance addressing credit rating (score) models used to assess obligor or facility creditworthiness, including design and calibration expectations, replacement of persistently underperforming models, and the usage controls around rating assignment. It includes re-rating expectations after a new or recalibrated model (70% of in-scope obligors within six months, 95% within nine months), batch re-rating independent of business lines, archiving of ratings, and use of each model only on its documented obligor type. Guidance ('should') expectations for UAE institutions, read jointly with the MMS.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
