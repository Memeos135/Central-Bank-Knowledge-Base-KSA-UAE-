# Principle 8 - Review and Updates

Principle 8 of the CBUAE/SFWG Principles for Climate Transition Planning (UAE), requiring regular review and, where needed, update of transition objectives, targets, assumptions, strategies and implementation actions in light of scenario analysis, customer feedback and local/international standards. Updates should go to the financial firm's board for approval and be embedded in governance, policies and frameworks, with material outcomes communicated to internal and external stakeholders. Applies to UAE 'financial firms' as guiding principles; relevant to board oversight duties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
