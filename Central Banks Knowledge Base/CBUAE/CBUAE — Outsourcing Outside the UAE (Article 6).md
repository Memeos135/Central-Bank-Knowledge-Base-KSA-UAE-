# Outsourcing Outside the UAE (Article 6)

Article 6 of the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation governs cross-border outsourcing by Banks: the Master System of Record including all Confidential Data must be maintained and stored in the UAE (with a Central Bank–approved exception allowing foreign bank branches to hold a daily-updated copy locally), and customer Confidential Data may not leave the UAE without Central Bank approval plus prior written customer consent and acknowledgement of possible foreign legal access. It also requires compliance with applicable personal data protection law, dedicated policies, controls, monitoring, staff training, defined security requirements, subcontractor compliance, and contemplates Central Bank cooperation with foreign authorities. Binds Banks operating in the UAE.
