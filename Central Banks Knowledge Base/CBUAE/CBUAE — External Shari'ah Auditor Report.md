# External Shari'ah Auditor Report

A provision-level node within the CBUAE External Shari'ah Audit Standard for Takaful Insurance Companies dealing with the External Shari'ah Auditor's report: it must go to the general assembly only after Central Bank approval, with copies to the Board and the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC) before the ISSC issues its annual Shari'ah report, and must be published in the annual financial statements including any reservations, subject to transaction confidentiality. Related articles cover the Shari'ah reference hierarchy the auditor must rely on, escalation of unresolved issues to the ISSC, fit-and-proper/licensing criteria for approved external Shari'ah audit firms, and a recommended minimum audit frequency of once every three years absent a regulatory requirement. It binds Takaful Insurance Companies and the external Shari'ah audit firms they engage.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
