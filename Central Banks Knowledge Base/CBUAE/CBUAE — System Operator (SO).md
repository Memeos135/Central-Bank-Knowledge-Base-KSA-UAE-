# System Operator (SO)

Defines the System Operator (SO) as the person responsible for operating a Financial Infrastructure System, including comprehensive risk management across the system and ensuring operation complies with the RPS Regulation and other central bank regulations. The SO is a primary addressee of the retail payment systems licensing, designation and ongoing oversight regime, alongside Settlement Institutions. Relevant to determining who bears compliance and oversight obligations for a designated RPS.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
