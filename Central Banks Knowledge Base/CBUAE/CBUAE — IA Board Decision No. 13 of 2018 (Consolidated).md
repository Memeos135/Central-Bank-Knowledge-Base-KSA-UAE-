# IA Board Decision No. 13 of 2018 (Consolidated)

Consolidated version (as at 15/09/2019) of IA Board Decision No. 13 of 2018 on marketing insurance policies through banks, reflecting amendments made by IA Board Decision No. 42 of 2019. It covers the approval application file (including the bank–insurer agreement subject to IA approval and a continuing training plan), Designated Officer qualification conditions, IA decision timelines and appeal rights, grounds for cancelling approval, and a six-month rectification period for existing arrangements. Binds insurance and Takaful companies distributing through banks; the Arabic text prevails over this translation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
