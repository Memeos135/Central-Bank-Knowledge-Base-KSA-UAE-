# Compliance Officer

Covers the Compliance Officer function of a Licensed Person carrying on exchange business, principally as the head of the AML/CFT compliance function. It addresses prior CBUAE no-objection for appointment, consequences of rejection, notification of commencement and resignation to both the Banking Supervision and AML/CFT Supervision Departments, replacement deadlines, and independence/reporting lines to the board or owners. Obligations fall on the Licensed Person, with the officer's authority protected from interference by management.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
