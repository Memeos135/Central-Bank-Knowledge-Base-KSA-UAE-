# Qard Hasan (Article 18)

Article 18 of the CBUAE Takaful Insurance standard sets the regime for Qard Hasan: the Takaful Insurance Company must adopt a Board-approved, ISSC-endorsed deficit-management policy and must fund a Shari'ah-compliant interest-free loan from the Shareholders' Account to the Takaful Insurance Fund when the Fund cannot meet its obligations. It frames this as a regulatory (not contractual) obligation, excludes it from Wakala Fee pricing, permits recovery only out of future Surplus subject to solvency limits, requires annual assessment and full write-off three years after maturity, and bars granting Qard Hasan to ineligible persons. It binds licensed Takaful insurers, their Boards and Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
