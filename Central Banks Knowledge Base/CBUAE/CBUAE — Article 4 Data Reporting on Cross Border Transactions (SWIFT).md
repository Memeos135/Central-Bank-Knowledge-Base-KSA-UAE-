# Article 4: Data Reporting on Cross Border Transactions (SWIFT)

Article 4 imposes strict liability reporting duties on Licensed Financial Institutions for cross-border SWIFT payment transactions, supporting the central bank's balance of payments and international investment position statistics. LFIs must make the full published list of purpose-of-payment codes available to customers without merging or amending it and must capture purpose codes when initiating cross-border transfers; non-compliance attracts a fixed fine per violation.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk
