# Communication Plan (Article 12)

Article 12 of the CBUAE recovery planning regulation, requiring Financial Institutions to develop an adequate communication strategy for the public, financial markets, staff and other stakeholders to be used if the recovery plan is deployed. Surrounding context links it to governance requirements that communication plan procedures be embedded in, and consistent with, the recovery plan's governance and escalation arrangements, and to group/branch consistency rules. Binds Financial Institutions subject to recovery planning, including UAE branches of foreign institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
