# Claims Secured by Residential Property

Defines the standardised-approach asset class for loans secured by residential property (self-occupied or rented) where the property is fully mortgaged in favour of the bank, and sets out the loan-to-value measure and the rule that valuation is held at origination value unless the Central Bank requires a downward revision or an extraordinary idiosyncratic event permanently reduces value. Supervisory Q&A clarifies that customers holding more than four properties are reported as commercial property at 100% risk weight and that the 35% preferential risk weight applies only to completed, not under-construction, properties. It binds banks in capital adequacy reporting and risk weighting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
