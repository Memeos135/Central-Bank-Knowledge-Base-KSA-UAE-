# Guide to Regulatory Information, Data and Reports

The Guide to Regulatory Information, Data and Reports issued under the Insurance Authority framework, setting out what insurers and insurance-related professions must keep updated in the Authority's registers and electronic systems and what periodic reports they must file. It covers updating company, board, principal officer and staff/Emiratisation data (some changes requiring prior Authority approval), and mandates compliance officer reports, internal audit and external auditor reports on AML/CTF internal controls, plus biannual and annual AML/CTF self-assessment filings by set deadlines. Binds licensed insurance companies and related professions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
