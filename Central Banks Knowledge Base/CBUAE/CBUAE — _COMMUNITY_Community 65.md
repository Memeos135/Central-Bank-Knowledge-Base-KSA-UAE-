# Insurance Code of Conduct

## Why this community

Market-conduct and ethics regime for insurance companies and their intermediaries — pricing, policy documentation, and claims handling — as successively restated by regulator resolutions.

## How members connect

- Vertical consolidation chain: earlier ministerial resolution → the 2010 Code of Conduct → the 2019 consolidated Code adopted by IA Board Decision No. 40 of 2019; the later instrument supersedes/restates the earlier for currency purposes.
- The article-level nodes (pricing, the policy, claim procedures) are the substantive obligation set sitting inside the Code.
- Conduct duties here mirror general consumer-protection themes (disclosure, fair pricing, timely claim settlement) but are insurance-sector specific in scope.
