# Compliance Function

Concept node for the compliance function as a mandated, independent and permanent control function in banks. The board must ensure it exists and monitors and reports on observance of applicable laws, regulations and standards and on staff and board adherence to legal requirements, codes of conduct and conflict-of-interest policy; a board-approved compliance policy, adequate and competent staffing, primary reporting to the CEO with direct access to the board or its audit/risk committees, and a risk-based compliance programme subject to internal audit review are required. Binds banks and, where applicable, groups, including consistency of compliance policy across the group.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF
