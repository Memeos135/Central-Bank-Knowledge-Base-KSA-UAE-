# Article 7: Responsible Financing Practice

Article 7 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards, which governs responsible lending/financing and Credit Products offered to Consumers through any distribution channel, and must be read with Article 2 (Disclosure and Transparency) and Article 5 (Business Conduct). It subjects credit granting to the institution's credit risk policies, requires assessment of the consumer's ability to meet obligations and compliance with Central Bank Debt Burden Ratio limits, and imposes pre-contract consumer education including the offer document and Key Facts Statement. The source extract also carries the annexure of maximum fee and commission caps for retail customer services. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions dealing with Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
