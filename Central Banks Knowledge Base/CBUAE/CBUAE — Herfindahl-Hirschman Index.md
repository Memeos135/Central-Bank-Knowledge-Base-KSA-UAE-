# Herfindahl-Hirschman Index

Node labelled Herfindahl-Hirschman Index but drawn from CBUAE capital standard material on CVA capital calculation (index hedges, single-name decomposition, CVA RWA multiplier of 12.5, and the simple alternative approach) and on specific/general market risk charges for equity derivatives and index arbitrage strategies. Useful for concentration-metric and CVA/market risk questions; binds CBUAE-licensed banks. Check the source before relying on any HHI-specific formula, as the context is concentration/index-weighting rather than a defined HHI test.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
