# Internal Policies, Controls and Procedures

A concept node covering the requirement that financial institutions maintain documented internal AML/CFT policies, controls and procedures that mitigate the risks identified in their business-wide ML/TF risk assessment and reflect the National Risk Assessment and topical risk assessments. It sets expectations on senior management approval, periodic effectiveness review and updating, proportionality to risk, communication within the organisation, and group-wide application to branches, subsidiaries and majority-owned affiliates; it also extends to STR-related procedures and confidentiality controls. Binds financial institutions supervised by CBUAE under the UAE AML-CFT Law and Decision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
