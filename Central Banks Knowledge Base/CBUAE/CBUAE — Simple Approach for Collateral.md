# Simple Approach for Collateral

Concept node for the simple approach to collateral under the credit risk mitigation framework, available only in the banking book and mutually exclusive with the comprehensive approach. Capital relief requires the same minimum conditions as the comprehensive approach: legal certainty and enforceable right to liquidate or take possession on default or insolvency, no material positive correlation between collateral value and counterparty credit quality, and robust liquidation procedures. Binds banks recognising collateral for regulatory capital purposes under CBUAE credit risk standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
