# Credit Risk Mitigation

## Why this community

Recognition of collateral, guarantees and credit derivatives as credit risk mitigants, and the conditions and haircuts that limit how much capital relief a bank may claim.

## How members connect

- One parent CRM rule sets the eligibility gateway and then branches into two mutually exclusive computational routes — the simple and comprehensive approaches to collateral.
- Unfunded protection (guarantees, credit derivatives) shares the same recognition preconditions as funded collateral: legal certainty, eligible protection providers and defined credit events.
- Haircut and adjustment members (currency mismatch, maturity mismatch) act as mandatory reductions applied across both funded and unfunded protection.
- Basket products (first- and second-to-default derivatives) are carve-outs from the general credit derivative recognition rule with their own attribution logic.
