# Role-Based Training

A concept node within the CBUAE Best Practices on AML/CFT/CPF training covering role-based (including on-the-job) training tailored to the ML/TF/PF risk exposure of specific functions, alongside new-hire, annual enterprise-wide, group-mandatory, localised and board/senior-management training. It sets expectations that content, frequency and intensity be risk-based, that training be delivered to defined high-exposure groups across the three lines of defence, and that delivery, attendance, non-completion follow-up and corrective action be documented for audit and supervisory inspection. It is directed at CBUAE licensed financial institutions (banks, exchange houses, finance companies, PSPs, VASPs, hawala providers, insurers) and, through them, to employees and external staff performing AML/CFT/CPF functions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
