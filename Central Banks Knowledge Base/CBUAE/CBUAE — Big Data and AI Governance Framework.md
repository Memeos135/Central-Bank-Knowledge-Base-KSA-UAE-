# Big Data and AI Governance Framework

Concept node setting the governance architecture for Big Data Analytics and AI in the CBUAE enabling-technologies guidelines: materiality assessment of each application (use case, criticality, disruption impact), an approved and documented governance framework with policies covering design, development, monitoring and use, pre-launch validation, explainability to avoid 'black-box' models, a data governance framework addressing quality gaps and representativeness of training data, independent validation and testing, and the ability to suspend an application promptly. Binds CBUAE-licensed Institutions, with the Governing Body and Senior Management accountable for outcomes and automated decisions.
