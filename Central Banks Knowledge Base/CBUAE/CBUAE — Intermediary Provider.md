# Intermediary Provider

Addresses the Intermediary Provider role in a virtual asset transfer chain, where an intermediary receives or forwards a virtual asset between the originator's and beneficiary's VASPs. Obligations include confirming and verifying the regulatory status of both counterparty VASPs before facilitating a transfer, logging all transfers including rejections for non-compliance, retaining and onward-transmitting all originator and beneficiary information, applying risk assessment to the transfer, and reporting systemic information failures by other providers to the Supervisory Authority. Binds intermediaries operating in the UAE transfer chain, including in relation to foreign or unregulated counterparties.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
