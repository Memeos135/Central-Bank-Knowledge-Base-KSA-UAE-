# Governor of the Central Bank

Node covering the office of the Governor of the CBUAE under Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025: the Governor is the Central Bank's legal representative, runs day-to-day management, signs instruments and contracts, and implements the decree-law, Central Bank regulations and Board decisions, subject to Board and Chairman powers. Related provisions cover delegation to deputies/assistants (appointed by federal decree on the Chairman's proposal), full-time dedication of service and conflict-of-interest bans on other positions, board seats at licensed financial institutions and public-sector contracts, plus Board meeting, quorum and remuneration rules. It binds the Governor, his deputies and assistants, and also prohibits any person in the State, including in financial and non-financial free zones, from using the misleading title "Governor".
