# ICAAP Stress Test

Section requiring each bank to implement a stress testing framework serving both the ICAAP and wider risk management, covering enterprise-wide, internal portfolio-level and regulatory (supervisor-prescribed) stress tests. Tests must be integrated, forward-looking and at least annual, with more frequent execution where circumstances require, and the Central Bank may challenge key assumptions. The Board is accountable for implementation, with delegation to Senior Management and subject-matter experts under Board-approved policies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
