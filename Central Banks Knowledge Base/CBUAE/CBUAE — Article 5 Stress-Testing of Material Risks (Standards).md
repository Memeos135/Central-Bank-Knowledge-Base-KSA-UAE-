# Article 5: Stress-Testing of Material Risks (Standards)

Article 5 of the CBUAE Risk Management Standards requires a forward-looking stress testing programme covering credit, market and operational risk plus any other risk material to the bank's business (e.g. concentration, IRRBB, liquidity, currency, reputation and compliance, contagion, country and transfer, legal and strategic risk). It assigns the risk management function responsibility for recommending actions from results, requires communication to business lines and functional heads, and links results into contingency planning, liquidity and contingency funding, capital planning and the ICAAP on a bank-wide and where applicable group-wide basis. It binds CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
