# Takaful Shari'ah Audit

## Why this community

Shari'ah governance and external Shari'ah audit obligations for takaful insurers and Islamic financial institutions, sitting under the insurance and commercial transactions statutory framework.

## How members connect

- Law-to-standard hierarchy: the Insurance Law and Commercial Transactions Law are the statutory anchors cited by the Shari'ah audit standards.
- The Takaful Regulation is the operative regime the governance and external audit standards elaborate; both standards cross-refer to it.
- The takaful external Shari'ah audit standard parallels the generic Islamic financial institution standard, so scope-of-entity distinctions drive which applies.
- Governance standard (internal control, ISSC) and audit standard (independent external assurance) form a two-layer assurance chain.
