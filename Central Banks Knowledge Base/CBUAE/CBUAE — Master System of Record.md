# Master System of Record

Defined term in the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation for Banks: the full collection of data, including Confidential Data, needed to run all of a Bank's core activities — serving clients, managing risks, and meeting legal and regulatory obligations. Its significance is operative: the Master System of Record must be continuously maintained and stored inside the UAE, with a limited, approval-based copy alternative for foreign bank branches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, banking prudential
