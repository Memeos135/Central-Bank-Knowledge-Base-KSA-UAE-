# Screening Operations

Umbrella section of the CBUAE targeted financial sanctions guidance setting out how Licensed Financial Institutions must operationalise screening against the UN Consolidated List and the UAE Local Terrorist List, both on a regular basis and immediately upon notification of list changes. It covers the required scope of searches (customer databases, prospective customers, parties to transactions, beneficial owners, and persons with direct or indirect relationships) and the sub-topics of evasion detection, list maintenance, customer and name screening, and match adjudication. It binds LFIs supervised by CBUAE under Cabinet Decision 74.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
