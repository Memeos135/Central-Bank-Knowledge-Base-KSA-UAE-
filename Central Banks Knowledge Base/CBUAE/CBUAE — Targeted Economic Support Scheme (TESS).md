# Targeted Economic Support Scheme (TESS)

Concept node for the Targeted Economic Support Scheme, established by CBUAE Board Decision No. 49/3/2020 (5 April 2020) as the COVID-19 relief framework from which the accounting-provisions/capital transitional arrangements regulation flowed. Here it functions as the enabling policy context and effective-date anchor for the IFRS 9 prudential filter notified to banks and finance companies (Notice CBUAE/BSD/N/2020/2016, 22 April 2020). Relevant to anyone tracing why the 2020-2024 add-back phase-down exists and which entities it covered.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
