# Maximum Distributable Amount (MDA)

A concept node labelled Maximum Distributable Amount, but the attached context concerns the mandate-based approach (MBA) and look-through approach for risk-weighting bank investments in funds, applying conservative maximum-leverage and maximum-notional assumptions. Read it as capital-calculation material on conservative 'maximum permitted' assumptions rather than buffer-based distribution restrictions; distribution restriction material appears elsewhere in the same instrument. Binds UAE banks calculating RWA for fund exposures.
