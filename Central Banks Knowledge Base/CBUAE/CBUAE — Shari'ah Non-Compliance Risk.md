# Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk

Concept node drawn from the CBUAE Standard on the Shari'ah Compliance Function at Islamic Financial Institutions, addressing where responsibility for Shari'ah non-compliance risk sits: with the institution's risk management function rather than the Shari'ah Compliance Function, with an expectation of mutual information-sharing between the two. The surrounding articles frame the Standard as complementary to the Shari'ah Governance Standard and set out the stages the SCF's review process must cover. It binds licensed financial institutions conducting all or part of their business in accordance with Islamic Shari'ah, and the Central Bank reserves the ability to impose requirements beyond the stated minimums.
