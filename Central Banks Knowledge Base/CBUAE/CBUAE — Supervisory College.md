# Supervisory College

Concept node for the Supervisory College as a coordination arrangement the Central Bank, acting as Group supervisor, may establish to cooperate with domestic and foreign supervisory authorities, including supervisors of foreign insurers, reinsurers, banks and other financial institutions in the Group. The Central Bank facilitates and leads the college and may conclude coordination agreements allocating additional tasks; as host supervisor it may instead join colleges run by other authorities. Related provisions cover group-level reporting designation and enforcement, including sanctions such as restricting senior management powers or interim management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
