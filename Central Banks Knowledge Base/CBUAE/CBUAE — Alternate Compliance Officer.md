# Alternate Compliance Officer

Establishes the mandatory Alternate Compliance Officer role supporting the AML/CFT compliance function of a Licensed Person and ensuring continuity when the Compliance Officer is absent or the post is vacant. It sets employment, residency, reporting-line and independence conditions, limits on combining the role with other functions depending on licence category, prior no-objection for appointment, and notification and replacement timelines. Duties bind the Licensed Person; CBUAE retains removal powers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
