# Senior Management

Node covering the duties of Senior Management and the Board's oversight of it under the CBUAE insurance corporate governance framework. Senior Management is accountable to the Board for compliance, fair treatment of policyholders, record keeping and prudent day-to-day management, and must implement strategy, risk systems and internal controls consistent with the Board-set Risk Appetite while respecting the independence of risk, compliance, internal audit and (for Islamic business) Shari'ah functions. The Board must hold Senior Management accountable, review governance annually, meet regularly, challenge information provided, and plan succession.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other

### [[CBUAE — Model Oversight Committee|Model Oversight Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are allocating responsibility for a model failure, this link fixes the escalation path: the Model Oversight Committee performs the oversight role but answers upward, with Senior Management and the Board carrying the duty to ensure model risk is actually managed to the required standard. Establishing the committee is therefore delegation of function, not of accountability — a defect in model governance remains attributable to the Board and Senior Management. The consequence is that committee terms of reference, membership and reporting lines should be demonstrably approved and monitored at Board/Senior Management level, since that is where a supervisory finding will land.
