# Unauthorised Transaction Liability

## Why this community

Allocation of loss between provider and customer for transactions the customer did not authorise, including the thresholds and conduct (gross negligence, fraud, late notification) that shift liability.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster; its operative effect depends on the definitions of 'authorisation' and 'customer' used elsewhere in the same instrument.
- Chains to security and notification obligations: the provider's compliance with secure communication and strong authentication duties is the pivot for rebutting liability.
- Consumer-protection regime rule — outcomes are decided on burden of proof and evidence of the provider's controls, not on the customer's assertion alone.
