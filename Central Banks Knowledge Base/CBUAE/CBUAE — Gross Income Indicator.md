# Gross Income Indicator

Concept node explaining gross income as the exposure indicator for operational risk capital under the Basic Indicator Approach and the Standardised Approach, including its composition (net interest plus net non-interest income), the gross-of-provisions and gross-of-operating-expenses conventions, treatment of outsourcing fees, and exclusion of items such as securities sale gains and extraordinary income. It also covers how years of negative gross income are dropped from both numerator and denominator and the resulting potential distortions warranting supervisory attention under Pillar 2. Applies to banks calculating regulatory operational risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
