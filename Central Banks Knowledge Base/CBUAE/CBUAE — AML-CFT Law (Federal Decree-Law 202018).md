# AML-CFT Law (Federal Decree-Law 20/2018)

Alias/concept node for the AML-CFT Law (Federal Decree-Law 20/2018) as applied in the CBUAE Guidelines, focusing on its core obligations: continuous identification, assessment, documentation and updating of ML/FT risk; defining the scope of due diligence measures; internal controls and policies; suspicious-transaction indicators; and reporting to Competent Authorities. It anchors the risk-based approach that must be proportionate to each institution's exposure. Binds supervised financial institutions and their senior management and employees.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Record Keeping|Record Keeping]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If the question is what an institution must retain and for how long, the record-keeping obligation is not free-standing guidance but a restatement of duties imposed by the primary AML/CFT Law and its implementing Decision. The link is a hierarchy and obligation-chain relationship: the guidance categorises record types (transaction records among them) by reference to specific statutory and Decision articles, so the enforceable source is the law, not the guidance gloss. Practically, a retention decision should be justified against the cited primary provisions, and any gap in the guidance's categories does not excuse non-compliance with the underlying statutory duty.
