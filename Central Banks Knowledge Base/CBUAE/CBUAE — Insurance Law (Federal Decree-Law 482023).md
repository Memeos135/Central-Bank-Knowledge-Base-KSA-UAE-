# Insurance Law (Federal Decree-Law 48/2023)

A defined-term node identifying the "Insurance Law" as Federal Decree-Law 48/2023 regulating insurance activities together with its Executive Regulations and amendments, as referenced in the CBUAE Open Finance definitions (which also define Insurance Company, Insurance Broker, Insurance Intermediation and Insurance Underwriting). It matters for determining which insurance-sector entities fall within the Open Finance perimeter as Licensees/Data Holders. Binds insurance companies and brokers licensed in the UAE where the Open Finance regulation cross-refers.
