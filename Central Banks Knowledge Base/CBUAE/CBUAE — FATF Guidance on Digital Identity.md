# FATF Guidance on Digital Identity

An excerpt of CBUAE guidance drawing on the FATF Guidance on Digital Identity, explaining how licensed financial institutions should assess whether a digital ID system is sufficiently reliable and independent for CDD purposes. It sets out assurance-level concepts for the identity proofing and enrollment stage (presence, resolution, evidence, validation, verification, illustrated by NIST-derived tiers) without mandating any particular level, and describes enrollment-stage threats such as impersonation, counterfeit or stolen identity evidence, synthetic identities and IDSP compromise. It addresses institutions subject to CBUAE AML/CTF supervision (UAE regime, not SAMA), and is guidance-level expectation rather than a prescriptive rule.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, data/credit info
