# Suspicious Activity Report (SAR)

Concept node for the Suspicious Activity Report, one of several report types LFIs may file with the UAE FIU alongside the STR and information-request report types. The guidance treats SARs as covering suspicious activity (not only executed transactions), expects a structured narrative addressing who, what, when, where, why and how, and discourages defensive filings — filings made to avoid non-filing penalties on activity the institution does not genuinely find suspicious — as a sign of weak transaction monitoring and internal controls that may attract supervisory measures. Relevant to LFIs' reporting, quality-assurance and escalation decisions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
