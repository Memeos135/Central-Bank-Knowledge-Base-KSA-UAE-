# Bank Guarantee

Capital-adequacy-style security requirement in Chapter 2 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business: every Licensed Person must maintain a bank guarantee issued by a UAE-licensed bank in favour of the Central Bank, valued at the higher of 100% of the minimum paid-up capital for its licence category or 5% of the prior financial year's monthly remittance average value (capped at AED 75 million). It also sets the annual Certificate of Remittance Value filing, the duty to top up on Central Bank demand, transitional instalment deadlines, and the consequence of failure — capping monthly remittance volume at twenty times the existing guarantee or downgrading/surrendering the licence. Binds exchange houses (Category A/B/C Licensed Persons) and ties directly to the minimum paid-up capital thresholds.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk
