# Exchange Business Regulation

The CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation issued by Circular No. 7/2025 (16 June 2025) to exchange houses, made under the Central Bank Law and covering Article 65 licensed financial activities of currency exchange and money transfer. It sets the licensing regime (Category I–IV licences and permitted activities), ongoing supervisory, prudential (paid-up capital, bank guarantee or cash deposit in lieu), corporate-change (LNO for acquisition, merger, joint venture, subsidiaries, divestment) and enforcement requirements. It binds any Person carrying on Exchange Business in the UAE, and is supplemented by the legally binding Standards; the Central Bank may impose requirements beyond the stated minimums.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
