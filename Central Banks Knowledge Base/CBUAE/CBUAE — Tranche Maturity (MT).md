# Tranche Maturity (MT)

Concept node for tranche maturity (MT), a tranche's remaining effective maturity in years used as an input to securitisation risk weighting. Under the external ratings-based approach (SEC-ERBA) the risk weight is obtained by linear interpolation between the published one-year and five-year risk weights for the relevant rating, subject to a floor and to a separate thickness adjustment for non-senior tranches. Applies to banks using SEC-ERBA for rated securitisation exposures, including inferred-rating cases for lower-priority sub-tranches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
