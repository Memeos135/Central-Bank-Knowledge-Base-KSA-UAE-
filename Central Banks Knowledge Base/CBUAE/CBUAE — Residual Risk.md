# Residual Risk

Concept node for residual risk — the ML/TF/PF risk remaining after the effectiveness of the LFI's controls is applied to its inherent risk within the institutional risk assessment. It links the inherent-risk methodology (risk categories, granular factors, standardised segments) to control-effectiveness scoring, and is the output used to drive risk appetite and remediation decisions. Relevant to LFIs documenting the conclusions of their enterprise-wide assessment.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
