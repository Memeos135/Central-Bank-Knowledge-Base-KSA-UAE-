# Relevant Regulations

Defined-term node identifying the set of CBUAE instruments collectively called the "Relevant Regulations" in the ERM Standards: the Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation and Standards (Circular 25/2022), Corporate Governance Regulation and Standards (Circular 24/2022), Recovery Planning Regulation (Circular 4/2023), Insurance Group Supervision Regulation (Circular 4/2025), and Decisions No. 25 and 26 of 2014 on financial regulations for insurance and Takaful companies. These instruments impose the underlying obligations on risk identification and control, annual ORSA submission, forward-looking stress testing and recovery plans, and are the reference frame against which the ERM standards are read. Useful for tracing the legal basis of an ERM obligation to its source instrument.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
