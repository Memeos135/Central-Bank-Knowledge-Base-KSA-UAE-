# Fitness and Propriety Regulation (Circular No. 4/2024)

The Fitness and Propriety Standards issued under CBUAE Circular No. 4/2024, which expand on the Fitness and Propriety Regulation and are mandatory and enforceable in the same way as the Regulation, article by article. It defines the core terms of the regime (Applicant, Authorisation, Authorized Individual, Affiliate, Actuary and others) and sets procedural duties on Licensees, including liaison with foreign regulators where a person was previously authorised abroad, notification of appointment within seven business days of Authorisation, filling a vacant Designated Function within thirty days subject to Authorisation, and submission of a training plan where remediable knowledge or skills gaps are identified.
