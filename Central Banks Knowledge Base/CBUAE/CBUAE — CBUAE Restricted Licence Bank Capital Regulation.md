# CBUAE Restricted Licence Bank Capital Regulation

Instrument node for the CBUAE Restricted Licence Banks Regulation (Circular 23/2022), covering licensing, permitted activities, and the capital regime for Restricted Licence Banks. Core capital rules require continuous maintenance of a minimum paid-up capital as a licensing prerequisite, AED 1 billion fully paid-up for locally operating restricted banks (foreign branches follow the Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation), no capital reduction without prior Central Bank approval, and possible higher institution-specific minima at licensing. Binds all banks licensed by CBUAE to operate as Restricted Licence Banks in the UAE, including branches of foreign banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
