# Board & Senior Management Roles

## Why this community

Allocation of governance accountability between the board and senior management within a risk/compliance framework — who sets and approves strategy, policy and risk appetite versus who implements, monitors and reports.

## How members connect

- Both articles sit in the same governance chapter and form a single delegation chain: board approval and oversight above, senior management execution and escalation below.
- Article 5 is drafted by reference to Article 4, so management duties are defined as the operational counterpart of board-reserved matters.
- Shared defined terms (board, senior management, policies, reporting lines) mean a compliance gap at one level is usually a breach attributable at the other.
- Typical supervisory consequence: failure of implementation is assessed against board oversight adequacy, not treated as management-only.
