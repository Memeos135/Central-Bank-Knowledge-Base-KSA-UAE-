# Wages Protection System (WPS)

Concept node for the Wages Protection System (WPS), the central bank-hosted reconciliation mechanism for timely and secure payment of wages, and for the related defined terms WPS Payment Account and WPS Payment Account Holder. It governs eligibility of Payment Service Providers to apply for and be granted access to the WPS, the requirement for prior central bank approval, the ability to use an SVF scheme or Bank to hold credited wages, and the demonstration of stringent security measures; once admitted, PSPs may open WPS Payment Accounts. Binds licensed Payment Service Providers seeking or holding WPS access, and is relevant to employers/wage payers as end users of the system.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection

### [[CBUAE — Payment Service Provider|Payment Service Provider]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a decision on whether a provider can participate in wage disbursement infrastructure, note that the wages framework borrows the payment service provider definition from the retail payment services regime, so only entities properly licensed in that category are eligible applicants. Participation then adds a further, cumulative layer of access conditions — notably security and risk-mitigation requirements aimed at protecting the system itself. In practice a licence is necessary but not sufficient: the provider must satisfy the separate participation criteria, and breach of those conditions is an access/eligibility issue distinct from its underlying licensing status.
