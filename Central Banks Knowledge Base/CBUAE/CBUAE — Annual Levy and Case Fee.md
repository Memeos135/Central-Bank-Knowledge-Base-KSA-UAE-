# Annual Levy and Case Fee

Establishes the funding mechanism of the CBUAE Ombudsman Unit: Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies must pay an Annual Levy, any Supplementary Levy and a Case Fee within 30 calendar days of assessment, with administrative late-payment charges and possible escalation to the Central Bank. It also covers Appeals Fees (potentially refundable and waivable by the Chief Ombudsman), pro-rata levies for entities entering or leaving the Unit's jurisdiction, and exemptions or reduced levies where an institution undertakes not to serve Consumers within the Regulation's scope.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
