# Simplified Due Diligence

Node for Simplified Due Diligence (SDD) as treated in the CBUAE CDD/KYC guidance for LFIs — the reduced-intensity CDD option available for customers and relationships assessed as lower ML/TF/PF risk. Relevant to how an LFI justifies lighter information collection, less frequent review and higher monitoring thresholds, and how SDD sits against the EDD track within a risk-based programme. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; SDD is a permitted calibration, not a waiver of CDD.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
