# Al Etihad Credit Bureau

Concept node for Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB) as used in the CBUAE loan-based crowdfunding regime: AECB reports are a required input to borrower due diligence, risk scoring and loan pricing, and regular AECB checks are the expected means of monitoring whether borrowers are drawing loans from other platforms or sources. The same context sets borrower/lender due diligence duties (including AML checks and identifying the ultimate beneficial owner), annual review of borrower financial position, lending ceilings per retail client and market counterparty, a borrower ceiling of AED 10,000,000 per calendar year, single-platform listing per project, all-or-nothing loan release, and client information confidentiality. Binds crowdfunding companies; relevant to credit information sharing obligations elsewhere in the regulation.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, AML/CTF, consumer protection, other
