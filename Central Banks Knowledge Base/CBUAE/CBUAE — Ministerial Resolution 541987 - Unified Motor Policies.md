# Ministerial Resolution 54/1987 - Unified Motor Policies

Node for the UAE Ministerial Resolution No. 54 of 1987 on Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies, the predecessor instrument for standard-form motor cover. It is expressly repealed by the Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 25 of 2016 (together with any contrary decisions), so it is relevant mainly for legacy policies, historical comparison and transitional questions. It bound insurance companies underwriting motor business in the State; the surrounding context here concerns insurer obligations on repair, original parts and depreciation for injured third parties.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
