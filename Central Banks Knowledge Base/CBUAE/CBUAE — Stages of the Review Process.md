# Stages of the Review Process

Article 5 of the CBUAE Shari'ah Compliance Function standard, requiring the Shari'ah Compliance Function (SCF) of an Islamic Financial Institution to review and evaluate Shari'ah non-compliance controls, report on them and monitor remedial actions. The IFI must adopt internal policies and procedures covering at least six review stages: annual plan, planning and scoping, field review, issues and actions, draft reports and progress monitoring. It also delimits responsibilities: managing Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk sits with risk management, not the SCF, and the SCF reviews pre/at execution while Shari'ah audit reviews post-execution.
