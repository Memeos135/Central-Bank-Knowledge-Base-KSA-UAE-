# Regulations regarding Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business 2014

The Regulations regarding Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business (issued January 2014, as amended by Notice No. 269/2016) are the primary CBUAE instrument defining the scope of Exchange Business and the licensing framework that the Standards elaborate. They establish that exchange business may only be carried out under a written licence from the Governor, define permitted activities (foreign currency exchange, remittances, wage payments), and underpin the Central Bank's enforcement against activity outside licence scope. They bind all Licensed Persons and applicants for exchange business licences in the UAE.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
