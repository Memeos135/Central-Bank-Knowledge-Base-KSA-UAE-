# Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation (CBUAE 2809)

The CBUAE Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation (Circular 05/2020), issued under the Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018, Art. 95), sets minimum standards for changes in bank ownership. It requires prior written CBUAE approval for acquiring, increasing or divesting shareholdings at or above 5% of issued ordinary shares and convertible instruments, and for Significant or Controlling Shareholdings and equivalent voting rights, with suspension of voting rights and dividends on excess shareholdings held without approval. It binds banks (including foreign bank branches via parent-level changes), their boards, and any person acquiring or disposing of such interests.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
