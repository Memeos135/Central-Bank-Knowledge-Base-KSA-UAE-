# Takaful Regulation

Article 12 of the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies, together with the definition of "Takaful Regulation" (Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 4 of 2010, as amended). It requires companies offering takaful products to align their corporate governance framework with the Takaful Regulation — internal Shari'ah control review and Shari'ah governance reporting, controls protecting participants' rights, establishment of the ISSC, transparent financial reporting on participants' rights — plus compliance with Higher Shari'ah Authority directions, immediate notification to the Central Bank of matters affecting an ISSC member's fit-and-proper status or independence, and publication of an annual Shari'ah report. It binds insurance companies offering takaful and their boards.
