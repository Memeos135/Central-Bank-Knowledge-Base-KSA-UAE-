# Cooling-off Period

Cross-cutting concept in the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards giving Consumers a Cooling-off Period of 5 complete business days after signing a contract for credit, insurance/takaful, structured or other Central Bank-regulated products, with the longer period prevailing where other law or regulation prescribes one. Licensed Financial Institutions must inform the Consumer of the right at signing, hand over all related documentation and disclosures, and may accept a signed written waiver containing a warning about immediate commitment. Anchored in Article 2 (Disclosure & Transparency, clauses on documentation and waiver) and Article 5 (Business Conduct), with price-sensitivity warnings for investment and structured products and a Shari'ah-contract clause requirement for Islamic products.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
