# Insurance Administrative Fines

## Why this community

Administrative sanctions in the insurance regime — the fines schedule and the licensing, conduct and electronic-insurance rules whose breach triggers it.

## How members connect

- A Cabinet-level resolution sets the penalty regime and its annexed schedule enumerates specific breaches and amounts — law-to-schedule hierarchy.
- The licensing resolution for insurance producers is the main citing node, pulling in the companies law, the electronic insurance rules and the code of conduct as the substantive duties it enforces.
- The fines schedule cross-refers to the financial regulations for insurers, tying prudential/financial breaches to monetary penalties.
- Non-SAMA (UAE/IA) material: useful comparatively, but do not transpose fine amounts or article numbers into KSA insurance analysis.
