# Sharia'a Supervision Committee

Defines the Sharia'a supervision committee as the internal organ of an Islamic Finance Company responsible for confirming that its conduct, transactions and application methodologies comply with Islamic Sharia'a, with its formation and working method set out in the company's articles of association. The committee is accountable for its own Sharia'a decisions, views and opinions while the board retains ultimate governance responsibility and is expected to be guided by the committee; disputes with management must be escalated to the Higher Sharia'a Authority. Management must implement the committee's rulings, appointment and termination of members and chairmen follow Fit and Proper criteria, and operations manuals must be committee-approved.
