# Clearing and Settlement System for SVF

Explains how a payment system supporting a Stored Value Facility business and scheme is treated under the RPS designation framework: although such a system normally falls within the RPS definition, the regulator does not intend to designate a payment system run by an SVF licensee purely to support its own SVF business, since the SVF Regulation already governs its safety and soundness. Sits within the guidance on categories of RPS (including bank-owned systems and payment card systems) and the scope of designation. Relevant to SVF licensees assessing dual-regime exposure.

**Regimes:** payments
