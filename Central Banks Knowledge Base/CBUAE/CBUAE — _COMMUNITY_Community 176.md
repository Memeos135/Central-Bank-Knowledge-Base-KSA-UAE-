# Dormant Accounts & Unclaimed Funds

## Why this community

Treatment of dormant accounts, unclaimed balances and their eventual transfer to the central bank, sitting at the intersection of consumer protection (preserving the customer's claim) and AML/CTF customer due diligence obligations that persist over inactive relationships.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the dormancy article on unclaimed funds up to the Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Funds Regulation, which supplies the operative defined terms (Dormant Customer, Unclaimed Balances and Funds) and the transfer/reclaim obligation chain.
- Transfer of funds to the central bank and reclaim procedures are two halves of one obligation: extinguishing the institution's holding duty without extinguishing the beneficial owner's entitlement.
- AML linkage: dormancy does not suspend CDD — identification and verification standards (and the limits on third-party reliance) under the AML/CFT decision govern reactivation and any payout, so an unclaimed-funds reclaim is also a KYC event.
- Consumer protection duties (notification, fee limits, access to remedy) overlay the dormancy regime; note the cluster mixes a non-KSA (UAE) AML framework reference, so confirm jurisdictional applicability before relying on it for SAMA questions.
