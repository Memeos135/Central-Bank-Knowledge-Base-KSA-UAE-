# Personal Data & Cyber Security

## Why this community

Protection of personal data and cyber security: the duty to safeguard customer information and maintain information-security controls, sitting at the intersection of KSA data-protection law and SAMA prudential/operational-risk expectations.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster; it qualifies how record-keeping and customer-onboarding data may be stored, used, shared and disclosed.
- Cross-regime linkage: personal-data duties constrain data sharing with credit bureaus, outsourcing providers and third parties relied on for CDD, and interact with consumer-protection consent requirements.
- Breach and incident consequences typically escalate to the supervisor, connecting this article to supervisory powers and enforcement.
