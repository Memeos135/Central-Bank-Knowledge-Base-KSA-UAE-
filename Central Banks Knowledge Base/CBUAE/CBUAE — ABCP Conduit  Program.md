# ABCP Conduit / Program

Concept node for asset-backed commercial paper (ABCP) conduits and programmes: a special purpose vehicle issuing commercial paper against claims on underlying assets, and the programme of paper it issues. Under the CBUAE standard, short-term STC status for an ABCP conduit requires the appendix criteria to be met at both conduit and transaction level, with sponsor duties on transaction documentation and independent legal review, retention of a material net economic exposure to align interests, and limits on maturity transformation. Relevant to banks acting as sponsor, liquidity/credit support provider or investor, and to the originating-bank definition where sponsoring makes a bank an originator.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
