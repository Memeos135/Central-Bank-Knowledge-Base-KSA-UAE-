# Financial Institution (AML obligations)

Defined-term node: "Financial Institution" under CBUAE Board Decision 59/4/2019 means anyone licensed or registered by the Central Bank who conducts one or more of the financial activities or operations specified in Article 2 of the Executive Regulation, for or on behalf of a customer. This definition sets the perimeter of the AML/CTF compliance duty and of the Central Bank's inspection, information-gathering and sanctioning powers over the entity and its employees. Note the "for or on behalf of a customer" limb, which matters when assessing whether a given activity or counterparty relationship falls in scope.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
