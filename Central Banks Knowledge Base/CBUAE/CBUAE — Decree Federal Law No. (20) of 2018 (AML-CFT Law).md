# Decree Federal Law No. (20) of 2018 (AML-CFT Law)

Concept node for Decree Federal Law No. (20) of 2018, the primary UAE AML/CFT statute forming the legal basis of the CBUAE hawala guidance and of the AML-CFT Decision issued under it. In the surrounding text it anchors registration, FIU reporting (including goAML/IEMS enrolment), bank account and compliance programme obligations for Registered Hawala Providers. It binds all financial institutions and designated entities subject to the UAE AML/CFT regime.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
