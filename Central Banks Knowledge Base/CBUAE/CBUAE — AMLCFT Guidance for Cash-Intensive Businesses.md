# AML/CFT Guidance for Cash-Intensive Businesses

CBUAE guidance addressing money laundering and terrorist/illegal-organisation financing risks arising from cash-intensive business customers, including illustrative sectors (e.g. travel agencies, car dealers) and related instruments such as open- and closed-loop prepaid cards. It expects licensed financial institutions to define cash-intensive business using a documented, senior-management-approved methodology, understand expected activity, and apply risk-based CDD and ongoing monitoring against peer and profile baselines. Binding on CBUAE-licensed financial institutions (LFIs); note this is UAE, not SAMA, material and does not itself apply in KSA.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, payments

### [[CBUAE — Transaction Monitoring|Transaction Monitoring]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Where a customer is a cash-intensive business, the generic monitoring duty must be calibrated to that customer's specific risk profile rather than applied at baseline. The cash-intensive business guidance identifies the vulnerability — high legitimate cash volumes that mask illicit placement — which in turn informs what "anomalous from normal behaviour" means for that segment and which scenarios and thresholds are defensible. The consequence is that monitoring outcomes for such customers should be assessed against sector-specific expectations; generic thresholds that do not account for expected cash turnover are likely to be treated as inadequate coverage.
