# Takaful Wakala Operational Model

## Why this community

The Wakala operational model for takaful: how the operator's agency relationship with participants is constituted, documented, priced and accounted for, including contributions, wakala fees and participants' investment balances.

## How members connect

- The provisions form a single obligation chain: choosing and setting the operational model, then documenting the contractual relationship, then the membership policy, then the cash flows (contribution, fee).
- They share defined terms — Wakala Operational Model, Participants' Investment Account — so an interpretation of one article constrains the others.
- Fee and contribution articles both resolve into the Participants' Investment Account, making fund-separation and fee-entitlement questions interdependent.
- The membership policy is the disclosure vehicle through which fee terms must be made transparent to participants, linking conduct/consumer-facing duties to the structural model.
