# Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Third Party Liability

Node for the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Third Party Liability as annexed to IA-BOD-RES 25/2016 (later version). It fixes the compulsory cover terms and monetary limits: AED 200,000 per deceased person for a spouse, parent or child with disability compensation scaled to the disability percentage, payment of all treatment expenses and a letter of commitment to treatment providers, judicially awarded amounts and costs excluding fines, and AED 2,000,000 per accident for property damage. It also sets insurer duties on accident occurrence — repair to pre-accident condition, market value payment where damage exceeds 50% of market value, replacement vehicle or cash settlement at the injured party's option, and original-parts/depreciation rules. It binds insurers writing compulsory motor TPL in the State vis-à-vis insureds and injured third parties.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
