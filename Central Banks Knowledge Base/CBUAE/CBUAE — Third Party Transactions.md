# Third Party Transactions

Defines a third party transaction as one executed by a representative on behalf of a beneficial owner of funds, and establishes a general prohibition subject to narrowly listed exceptions. For natural-person representatives the exception requires a duly executed power of attorney or an in-person signed letter of authority (validity capped at two years) that specifies the transaction type, identification details of both parties and, for transfers, the beneficiary; signatures must be verified against passport or Emirates ID. Binds Licensed Persons; interacts with beneficial ownership identification, CDD/EDD and transaction monitoring rules that require special attention to third party activity.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
