# Data Management and Protection Function

Governance provision requiring the Board of a Licensed Financial Institution to assign responsibility and accountability for the Data Management and Protection function to a senior manager reporting directly to Senior Management, with oversight of the Data Management Control Framework and UAE/CBUAE data protection and privacy requirements. The function must maintain monitoring and preventive controls against unauthorised or accidental loss, misuse, modification, access, disclosure or destruction of Personal Data, verify legitimacy of collection and data integrity, keep monitoring records for five years, review the Framework annually, and investigate and report material data incidents to Senior Management.
