# Reputational Risk and Implicit Support

Node on reputational risk and implicit (non-contractual) support to securitisations under the CBUAE capital standards, including the duty to identify reputational risk sources when entering new markets or products, to reflect it in stress testing, and to quantify the support a bank might feel compelled to provide. It also sets out the consequence of providing implicit support and the distinction between contractual credit enhancement and implicit support. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks and their boards/senior management through Pillar 2 and ICAAP.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
