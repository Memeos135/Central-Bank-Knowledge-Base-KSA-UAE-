# Insurance Minimum Capital

## Why this community

Prudential entry requirements for insurers — minimum paid-up capital, its amendment over time, and related licensing conditions for investment limits and points of sale.

## How members connect

- The insurance law is the parent statute; the Cabinet Resolution on minimum capital is the implementing instrument, and board decisions sit below as supervisory detail — a clean law → resolution → decision hierarchy.
- Version control matters: the original 2009 resolution, the 2017 amendment and the consolidated text must be read together to identify the currently operative capital threshold.
- The commercial companies law is cited as the general corporate-form baseline that the insurance-specific capital floor overrides or supplements.
- Investment-limit and point-of-sale decisions attach continuing conditions to the entity whose capital adequacy the resolutions establish, linking entry capital to ongoing asset and distribution constraints.
