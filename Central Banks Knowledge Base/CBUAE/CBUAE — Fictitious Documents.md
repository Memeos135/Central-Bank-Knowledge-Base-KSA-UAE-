# Fictitious Documents

A TBML typology section within the CBUAE guidance on trade-based money laundering, describing how illicit actors fabricate or alter trade documents (invoices, shipping and customs papers, certificates of origin) because no uniform document standard exists. It sets the supervisory expectation that licensed financial institutions apply enhanced scrutiny where documents show clear discrepancies or visible alterations, and build familiarity with the document types their trade customers normally use. It binds LFIs handling documentary and open-account trade, including intermediary banks asked for supporting documentation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
