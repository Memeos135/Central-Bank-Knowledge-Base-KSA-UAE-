# PCI DSS

Concept node for the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard as a mandatory minimum information security baseline. It requires Card Schemes to apply and meet at least PCI DSS together with the UAE Information Assurance Standards as amended, to report compliance to the Board at least annually and to transmit that report to the central bank, and to notify the central bank of any Data Breach without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware. Binds licensed Card Schemes and their Boards.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
