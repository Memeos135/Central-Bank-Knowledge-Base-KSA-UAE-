# AI Ethics and Fairness

Concept node on fairness, reliability and post-deployment control of Big Data Analytics and AI applications under the CBUAE enabling-technologies guidelines: a documented framework for pre- and post-deployment review of algorithms, models and data; triggers for re-training, recalibration, redevelopment or discontinuation; review of outcomes for unintentional bias (e.g. false positive/negative rates); contingency measures including human intervention; error detection, rectification, escalation and model versioning records. Binds CBUAE-licensed Institutions using material AI/analytics applications, with accountability retained by the Governing Body and Senior Management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
