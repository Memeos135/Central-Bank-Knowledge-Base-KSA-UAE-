# Remuneration Framework

Covers the required remuneration framework and policy: Financial Institutions must develop, implement, monitor and regularly review a Board-approved remuneration policy that links pay to institutional performance, strategic objectives and risk-adjusted outcomes, and that does not incentivise inappropriate or excessive risk-taking, addressing the balance of fixed and variable components by role or Staff category. The Board must give final approval of total variable remuneration amounts and distribution on the remuneration committee's recommendation, ensure Control Functions Staff participate in designing and monitoring the policy, manage Conflicts of Interest (including for the ISSC on Shariah resolutions), and commission an annual independent remuneration review submitted to the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
