# Complaint Management and Resolution

Article 8 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation, setting the framework for Complaint management and resolution by Licensed Financial Institutions. It requires an independent complaint function reporting to Senior Management, a free and accessible complaint system, adherence to Central Bank-prescribed turnaround times and written responses, staff and Authorized Agent training, and consumer awareness of rights. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions in the UAE, including for complaints arising from products or services sold by their Authorized Agents.
