# Corporate Governance

## Why this community

Governance requirements imposed on the licensed entity - board and senior management responsibility, internal control and accountability arrangements as a licensing/ongoing condition.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in this graph; operates as the governance overlay on the conduct and contractual obligations elsewhere in the same instrument.
- Relevant cross-regime read-across to SAMA's general governance and risk-management frameworks for supervised institutions.
