# Expected Credit Loss (ECL)

Glossary node from the CBUAE Model Management Standards/Guidance defining Expected Credit Loss as the probability-weighted expected loss metric supporting IFRS 9 provisions for credit risk on banking-book facilities and bonds. It anchors the PD, LGD and EAD modelling expectations used throughout the instrument. Relevant to CBUAE-licensed institutions and their model development, validation and oversight functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
