# National Risk Assessment

Concerns the National Risk Assessment as the anchor of the risk-based approach in the AML/CTF/CPF Executive Regulations: Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and VASPs must identify, assess, document and mitigate their crime risks proportionate to nature and size, considering customer, geographic, product/service/transaction and delivery-channel factors, and taking account of NRA and sectoral assessment results. It also sets the National Committee's duties to update the NRA and national strategy at least every three years, disseminate it, and coordinate on legal-person and legal-arrangement risk. Binds supervised entities, the National Committee, the Registrar and Supervisory Authorities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
