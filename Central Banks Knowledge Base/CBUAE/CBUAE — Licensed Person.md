# Licensed Person

Defined term under the Exchange Business Regulation for the person holding a CBUAE licence to conduct Exchange Business; it is the primary obligation-bearer throughout the Regulation and Standards. Duties attaching to a Licensed Person include operating strictly within the scope of the licence and offering all licensed services continuously, maintaining the bank guarantee or substitute cash deposit on an ongoing basis, evidencing source of funds for that collateral, obtaining an LNO for acquisitions, mergers, joint ventures, subsidiaries, foreign offices and disposals, and the prohibition on lending money to any party (including customers, owners, directors, partners, shareholders) or holding current accounts for them.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential
