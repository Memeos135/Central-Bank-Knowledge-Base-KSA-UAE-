# Insurance Pricing Policy Revision

## Why this community

Insurance supervisory intervention in product pricing — revision of the pricing policy applied by insurers, sitting at the intersection of prudential adequacy of premiums and consumer/market-conduct fairness.

## How members connect

- Isolated node with no mapped cross-references in this graph; it should be read as a standalone regulator resolution and checked for later amendment or supersession by SAMA insurance instruments.
- Its practical effect is on pricing approval and actuarial justification obligations for insurers rather than on any AML or credit regime in the wider graph.
