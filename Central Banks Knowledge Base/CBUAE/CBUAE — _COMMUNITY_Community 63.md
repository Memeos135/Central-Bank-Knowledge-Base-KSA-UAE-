# Open Finance Data Sharing

## Why this community

The role taxonomy and customer-facing obligations of Open Finance: who is a data holder, data sharing provider, service initiation provider or service owner, and the consent, privacy and authentication duties attaching to each.

## How members connect

- Everything turns on defined roles: the Open Finance Provider is the umbrella term, split into data sharing and service initiation activities, each with a counterparty role (data holder, service owner) that bears reciprocal duties.
- Consent and data privacy requirements are the common gate for both activity types — no data sharing or service initiation is lawful without a valid, scoped customer consent.
- Authentication obligations bind the data holder and service owner side, allocating responsibility for verifying the customer at the point of access or initiation.
- The 'person deemed licensed' concept is the licensing bridge: it determines which entities may act as providers without a separate authorisation, and should be read against the framework and infrastructure cluster.
