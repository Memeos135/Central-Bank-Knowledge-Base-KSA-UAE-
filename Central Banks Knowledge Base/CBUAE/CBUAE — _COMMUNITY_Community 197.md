# Disciplinary Sanction Lapse

## Why this community

Time-limitation (lapse/expiry) of disciplinary sanctions imposed on supervised insurance-sector persons, i.e. when a recorded penalty ceases to carry forward legal effect for fit-and-proper, licensing or repeat-offence purposes.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone Insurance Authority board resolution operating as subordinate instrument under the insurance supervisory law and its implementing regulation.
- Links outward rather than internally — the lapse rule qualifies the disciplinary/penalty powers exercised elsewhere in the insurance enforcement regime.
- Relevant to governance and fit-and-proper assessments, where past sanctions are an input; the lapse period determines how long that input remains live.
