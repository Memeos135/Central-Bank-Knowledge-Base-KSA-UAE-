# Disclosure Regulation

## Why this community

Disclosure obligations under the Regulation — what the regulated entity must publish or report, to whom, and on what cadence, as a transparency counterpart to the governance and risk articles.

## How members connect

- Single Regulation-level node with no paired Standards article in the graph, so the requirement is read directly from the Regulation text without elaborating guidance in this cluster.
- Content of the disclosure is functionally supplied by the governance and group risk articles (Articles 7-8), which determine what information exists to be disclosed.
- Sequentially and thematically adjacent to Article 10, so scope carve-outs or additions for Islamic banking activity may qualify what must be disclosed.
