# Trading Book

This node carries the definition of the trading book — positions in financial instruments and commodities held with trading intent or to hedge other trading book elements — and the mandatory assignment criteria (short-term resale, profiting from short-term price movements, arbitrage, hedging such positions, and any further instruments determined by the regulator). It links that boundary to consequences: separate trading book policy statements and risk frameworks, and capital charges for interest rate, options and equity positions in the trading book, with equity exposure, foreign exchange, commodity and options risk calculated on entire positions and banking book interest rate risk addressed through the internal capital adequacy process. It binds Banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
