# Regulatory Capital Requirement

Sets the regulatory capital regime for Payment Token Service Providers under Articles 13 to 15, comprising initial capital as a licensing precondition and ongoing capital thereafter. Payment Token Issuers hold a fixed minimum plus an add-on calculated on the fiat face value of outstanding tokens (a higher fixed amount and 2% add-on where the alternative Reserve of Assets treatment under Article 22(3) applies); custody/transfer and conversion providers hold a minimum scaled to monthly average transfer values. The Central Bank may impose additional or increased capital requirements as a condition of licensing. Binds applicants and Licensed Payment Token Service Providers.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk
