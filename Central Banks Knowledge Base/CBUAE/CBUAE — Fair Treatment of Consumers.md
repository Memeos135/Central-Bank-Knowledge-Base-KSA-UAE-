# Fair Treatment of Consumers

Node covering the fair-treatment limb of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation and its supporting Standards (Article 5, section 5.2, read with the responsible-financing duties at 5.1.2). It requires Licensed Financial Institutions to treat all Consumers equitably, honestly and fairly at every stage of the relationship, embedding fair treatment in governance, business conduct and corporate culture, and it bars abusive contract terms, misleading or aggressive sales practices, tied selling and bundling, profiting from Errors, and coercive debt-collection pressure; it also requires monitoring of Borrowers/Financees in difficulty and provision of credit counselling. Binds all Licensed Financial Institutions (including Islamic Financial Institutions) in their dealings with Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
