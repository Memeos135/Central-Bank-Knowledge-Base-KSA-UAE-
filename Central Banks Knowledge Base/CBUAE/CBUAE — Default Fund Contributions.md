# Default Fund Contributions

Capital treatment of a bank's pre-funded contributions to a qualifying central counterparty's (QCCP) default fund, setting out the RWA formula and its inputs (the 20% risk weight subject to Central Bank uplift, the bank's contribution DFM, the total default fund DF, and the QCCP's aggregate clearing-member EAD). It imposes a floor requiring RWA of at least 2% of the bank's pre-funded contributions and specifies that member exposures are measured as bilateral counterparty credit risk trade exposure using a 10-day margin period of risk, net of collateral the QCCP can legally claim on default. Binds UAE banks that are clearing members of, or contribute to the default fund of, a CCP, on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
