# AML/CFT Reporting Requirements

The AML/CFT component of the reporting guide, including the internal audit report form and the verification checklist used to test an insurer's AML/CFT systems and controls. It addresses independence of the internal control unit, group-wide application of UAE AML standards to foreign branches (with notification to the Authority where host-country rules are weaker or compliance is impossible), CDD/EDD and ongoing monitoring, record keeping, training, compliance officer appointment and notification, tipping-off prohibitions, and STR/SAR filing exclusively to the UAE FIU via goAML with freezing of the relevant transactions. Binds insurance companies and related professions and their compliance and audit functions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
