# Standard Re. Shari'ah Governance for Islamic Financial Institutions

Node labelled as the Shari'ah governance standard for Islamic financial institutions; the extracted text is in fact Article 11 (Shari'ah compliance for financial services) of the Consumer Protection Standards plus Article 3 provisions on product data requests, "No Objection Letter" applications and enforcement for late filings. It requires IFI boards and senior management to ensure Shari'ah compliance, places responsibility on the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, mandates fair profit distribution with investment accountholders, embedding Shari'ah compliance in culture and conduct, independent oversight, and consumer education on conventional versus Islamic products; detailed Shari'ah governance rules sit in a separate CBUAE regulation. Binds Islamic financial institutions and Islamic windows; the Higher Shari'ah Authority may add guidance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, banking prudential
