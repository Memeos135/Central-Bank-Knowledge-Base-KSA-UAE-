# Article 16: Protection of Personal Data and Cyber Security

Article 16 obliges Insurance Brokers to maintain policies and procedures to prevent and resolve data security breaches and protect Personal Data, to store and back up Personal Data inside the State for the ten-year retention period, to limit processing to data necessary for brokerage services, and sets an exhaustive list of permitted disclosures (client consent or law, the Central Bank, other regulators with prior Central Bank approval, courts, authorised government bodies, and insurance counterparties for underwriting or Claim Settlement). It also requires cyber risk to be managed through the risk governance process and a cyber incident response and management plan. Binds licensed Insurance Brokers; UAE/CBUAE regime, not SAMA.
