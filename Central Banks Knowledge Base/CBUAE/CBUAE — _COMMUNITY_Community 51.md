# Bank Outsourcing Regulation

## Why this community

Prudential and conduct conditions under which banks may outsource functions to third parties, including board accountability, record-keeping of arrangements, protection of confidential customer data and cross-border outsourcing limits.

## How members connect

- Two-tier hierarchy: the outsourcing regulation sets the binding rule and the accompanying standards elaborate expected implementation; the articles are the individual obligation heads beneath the regulation.
- The governance article cross-refers to the general risk management regulation/standards, so outsourcing risk is a sub-set of the bank's enterprise risk framework rather than a standalone regime.
- Defined terms carry the substance: 'Confidential Data' fixes the scope of the data-protection duty and 'Master System of Record' conditions when cross-border outsourcing is permissible (data must remain retrievable/onshore-anchored).
- The outsourcing register and outsourcing agreements articles form a documentation chain evidencing that the governance, data and cross-border conditions were met — the usual supervisory inspection path.
