# Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Funds Regulation

Node linking the CBUAE Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Funds Regulation with Article 20 of the Exchange Business Regulation, which requires Licensed Persons to comply with that Regulation and to maintain board-approved (or owner-approved, for sole establishments) policies to assess, document, disclose and monitor unclaimed funds until paid to the customer or transferred to the Central Bank. 'Unclaimed Funds' is defined as money handed to a Licensed Person for onward delivery to a specific beneficiary that remains undelivered and whose rightful owner cannot be contacted within one year. Relevant where remittance proceeds or customer balances go unclaimed and for allocating internal roles and responsibilities over them.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, governance/risk
