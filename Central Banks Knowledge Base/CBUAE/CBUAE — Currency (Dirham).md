# Currency (Dirham)

Covers the currency regime: the State's exclusive issuance right exercised solely by the Central Bank, the prohibition on any person issuing or circulating currency or bearer-payable instruments resembling legal tender, legal tender status of notes, digital-form currency and coins (coins capped at fifty Dirhams per payment), and Board powers over denominations and specifications. It also governs withdrawal from circulation, exchange time limits, ten-year redemption at face value, destruction, non-liability for lost, stolen or counterfeit currency, and compensation for damaged notes and coins. Binds the Central Bank, Licensed Financial Institutions and any person handling or purporting to issue currency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, other
