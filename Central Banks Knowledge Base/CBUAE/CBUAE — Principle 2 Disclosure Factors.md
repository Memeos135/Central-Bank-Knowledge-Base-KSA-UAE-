# Principle 2: Disclosure Factors

Principle 2 of the UAE SFWG Disclosure Principles sets the factors Reporting Entities should consider when disclosing sustainability-related risks and opportunities; the node also carries the Application section explaining that the Principles are a shared minimum expectation and that each Authority decides scope, entity thresholds, required disclosures, frequency, voluntary/'comply or explain'/mandatory status and implementation timeline. Useful mainly for understanding the non-self-executing legal status of the Principles. UAE instrument, not SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
