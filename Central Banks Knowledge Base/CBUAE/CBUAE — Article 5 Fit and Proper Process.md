# Article 5: Fit and Proper Process

Defines and operationalises the Fit and Proper Process for natural persons proposed as Insurance Broker's Representatives, Senior Management, Specialised Employees and others designated by the Central Bank. Criteria cover qualifications, knowledge and experience, integrity, competence and financial soundness, sufficient time, absence of Conflict of Interest, compliance with the insurance law's personal-suitability requirements, and passing the Central Bank's assessment (including good conduct, no unrehabilitated moral-turpitude conviction or bankruptcy).
