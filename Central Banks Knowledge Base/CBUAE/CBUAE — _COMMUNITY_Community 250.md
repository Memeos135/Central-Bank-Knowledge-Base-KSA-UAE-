# Review and Updates

## Why this community

Principle imposing a periodic (and event-driven) review and update duty over the adopted framework or policy, keeping it current with regulatory change and the entity's risk profile.

## How members connect

- Singleton node; its legal force derives from the parent principles instrument rather than from cross-references to other nodes.
- Closes the loop with the implementation principle: what is implemented must be reassessed, with governance sign-off on updates.
- Commonly the hook supervisors use to test staleness of policies during inspections, so it interacts with board/senior-management responsibility provisions in the same instrument.
