# Authentication and Identity Lifecycle Management

A conceptual section of CBUAE digital ID guidance explaining the second stage of a digital ID system: authentication (confirming the claimant is the same person who was identity-proofed and onboarded) and ongoing identity lifecycle management, including binding and re-binding of authenticators. It classifies authentication factors (knowledge, possession, inherence), addresses assurance levels, step-up controls for high-risk account activities such as personal data changes, payee registration and high-value transfers, and describes continuous/behavioural authentication as a supplement to point-in-time login checks. It informs CBUAE licensed financial institutions (LFIs) applying CDD and ongoing monitoring obligations, and is expectation-setting guidance rather than standalone binding rules.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, payments
