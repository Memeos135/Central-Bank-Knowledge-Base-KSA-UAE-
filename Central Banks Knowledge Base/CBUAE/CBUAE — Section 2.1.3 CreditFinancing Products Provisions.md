# Section 2.1.3 Credit/Financing Products Provisions

Provisions governing Credit/Financing Products offered to Consumers, building on Section 2.1.1 and read with the responsible financing requirements. It restricts issuing or bundling a credit card with a Credit Product and automatic credit limit increases without expressed consent, requires a fresh Al Etihad Credit Bureau check plus consent before any limit increase, requires notice and reasons for limit decreases or card closure, and regulates bundling so the Consumer may refuse bundled products while retaining the original credit offer — with a carve-out where credit insurance/takaful is a condition. The related fee schedule caps charges on credit cards and home loans/financing (late payment, early and partial settlement, letters and certificates).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
