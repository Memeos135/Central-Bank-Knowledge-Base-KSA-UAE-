# Investment Account Holders (IAHs)

IAHs are the holders of investment accounts whose funds Islamic Banks invest, typically on a Mudarabah basis, across one or more investment pools that must be accounted for separately. The CBUAE Profit Equalization Standard governs how IAH returns may be smoothed (PER, IRR, Mudarib forgoing profit, Hibah) and imposes contractual and public disclosure duties toward IAHs on rights, loss-bearing, early withdrawal, reserve appropriation and fees; it binds all Islamic Banks and Islamic Windows in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, banking prudential
