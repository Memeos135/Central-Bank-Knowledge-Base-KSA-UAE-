# Retail Payment Systems Regulation

This is the Retail Payment Systems Regulation (Circular 10/2020), which sets the framework for licensing, designating and overseeing systemically important retail payment systems under the Central Bank Law. It covers which types of RPS fall in scope (including payment card systems and clearing and settlement systems), the designation criteria and process, ongoing obligations of designated systems, and the appeal mechanism for licensing/designation decisions. It binds System Operators and Settlement Institutions of licensed and/or designated RPS; it excludes Financial Free Zones unless expressly stated, and generally does not target bank-owned in-house systems serving only their own customers, or a Payment System run by an SVF Licensee for its own scheme.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
