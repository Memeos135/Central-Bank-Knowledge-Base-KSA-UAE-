# Compliance Officer Role

An inspection/verification checklist node describing the role, appointment and supervision of the AML/CTF Compliance Officer in UAE insurance companies and insurance-related professions. It covers evidence of appointment notified to the Insurance Authority, qualifications and ongoing training, suspicious transaction handling and freezing, exclusive reporting of STRs/SARs to the UAE FIU via goAML, tipping-off prohibitions, biannual reporting to senior management and the Authority, and independence of internal control. It binds licensed insurance companies and related professionals and their compliance function, and informs internal/external auditors reviewing them.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
