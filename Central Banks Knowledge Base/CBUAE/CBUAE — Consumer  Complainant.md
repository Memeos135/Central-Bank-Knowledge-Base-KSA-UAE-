# Consumer / Complainant

Addresses who qualifies as a Consumer or Complainant with free access to the Ombudsman Unit's Complaint resolution mechanism, and the admissibility conditions and exclusions for Complaints — including failure to raise the matter first with the institution, not allowing the institution 30 business days to give a final written response, time-barred matters, already-settled matters, and matters materially concerning the institution's risk management, internal pricing or anti-money laundering policies. It also covers multiple Complainants, filing format, and withdrawal with notification to the institution within 5 business days. It binds the Ombudsman Unit and, through the response obligation, Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, AML/CTF
