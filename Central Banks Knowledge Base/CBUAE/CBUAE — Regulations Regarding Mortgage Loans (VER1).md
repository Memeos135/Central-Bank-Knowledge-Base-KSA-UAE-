# Regulations Regarding Mortgage Loans (VER1)

Original (Version 1) text of CBUAE Circular No. 31/2013 setting minimum standards for granting mortgage loans in the UAE, issued under the Central Bank's powers in Union Law No. 10 of 1980. It covers definitions, risk-management and lending-policy requirements, key ratios (including debt burden and loan-to-value), disclosure and transparency, housing programmes, Shari'ah-compliant finance, reporting and supervision, and cancels an earlier notice. It binds banks, finance companies and other financial institutions providing mortgage loans, collectively 'mortgage loan providers', whether lending to nationals, GCC nationals or expatriates.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
