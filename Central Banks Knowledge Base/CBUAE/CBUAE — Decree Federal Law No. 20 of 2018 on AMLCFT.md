# Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 on AML/CFT

Instrument node for the UAE's primary AML/CTF statute, Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organisations, which forms the legal basis for the CBUAE guidance referencing it. It establishes the core criminalisation and preventive-measures framework, including the suspicious transaction reporting duty relied on in the guidance. It binds financial institutions, DNFBPs and other obliged entities in the UAE; the guidance itself does not add to or override it.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
