# Suspicious Transaction Report (STR)

Concept node covering Suspicious Transaction Reporting under the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions: internal escalation to the compliance officer, the officer's decision to file, and immediate reporting to the UAE FIU through its goAML system or other FIU-approved means. It also flags confidentiality/tipping-off controls, post-filing handling of the business relationship, and the criminal liability (fines and/or imprisonment) for failure to report, with no exemptions available to FIs. Binds all supervised FIs and their managers and employees, whether onshore or in free zones.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
