# Appropriateness Assessment

The second limb of the CBUAE digital ID expectation: having established a system's assurance levels, an LFI must separately determine whether that system is appropriate for CDD given its own business and risk profile, including financial-inclusion considerations where biometric authentication may fail for certain vulnerable groups. Like the assurance assessment, the appropriateness determination must be documented (within or outside the enterprise risk assessment) and updated periodically and on event triggers. Binds UAE LFIs adopting digital ID for customer identification and verification.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection
