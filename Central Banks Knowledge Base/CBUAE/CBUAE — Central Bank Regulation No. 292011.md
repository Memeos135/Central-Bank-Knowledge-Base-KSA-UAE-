# Central Bank Regulation No. 29/2011

Cross-reference node to CBUAE Regulations No. 29/2011 on Bank Loans and Other Services Offered to Individual Customers, invoked in the loan-based crowdfunding regulation as the source of the defined term "personal loan" (a loan to an individual borrower repaid from verifiable regular income from a well-defined source), as amended or superseded. It matters because crowdfunding borrowers must be UAE-registered companies or sole proprietors and platforms must prevent borrowing for personal use. Relevant to anyone mapping the boundary between retail consumer lending rules and crowdfunding activity.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, other
