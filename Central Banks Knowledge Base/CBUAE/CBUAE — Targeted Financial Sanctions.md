# Targeted Financial Sanctions

Definitional node for Targeted Financial Sanctions under the UAE AML/CFT framework: freezing funds and prohibiting their direct or indirect availability to persons or entities designated under Cabinet resolutions on Terrorist Lists or by UN Security Council Chapter VII resolutions on terrorism financing and proliferation financing. It sits alongside related definitions (Executive Office, Concerned Authorities, Law Enforcement Authorities, the Crime, Predicate Offence) and underpins the immediate-implementation duty placed on financial institutions, DNFBPs and VASPs. Useful for scoping who is designated, what conduct is prohibited, and which authority issues instructions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
