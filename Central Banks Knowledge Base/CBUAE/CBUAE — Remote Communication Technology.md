# Remote Communication Technology

Concept node on the defined term Remote Communication Technology and its use across the dispute process. It establishes that registration, notification, filing, appearance, publicity, pleading, witness and expert hearings, deliberation, decision-issuance and enforcement steps are valid whether performed wholly or partly by audiovisual remote means, while parties may request in-person hearings at any stage. It also covers notification of decisions (including by registered mail, licensed delivery office, email or remote means), the executory effect of decisions, and the 30-day window to challenge before the competent court of first instance.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
