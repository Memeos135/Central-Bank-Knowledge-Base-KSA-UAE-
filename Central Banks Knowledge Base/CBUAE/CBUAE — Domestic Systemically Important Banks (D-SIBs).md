# Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs)

Concept node for domestic systemically important banks within the CBUAE capital framework — the designation-driven layer of additional capital expectations sitting above the general minimum ratio and buffers. The surrounding material situates it in the supervisory review process, where the Central Bank calibrates capital to an individual bank's risk profile, control environment, stress testing and governance quality, and may set requirements above the stated minimums. Binds banks designated as D-SIBs by the Central Bank, and informs supervisory dialogue for all licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
