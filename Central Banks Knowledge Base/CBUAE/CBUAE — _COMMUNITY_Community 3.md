# Banking Book Rate Risk (IRRBB)

## Why this community

Quantitative risk regimes for the banking book — interest rate and rate of return risk, related market/credit risk categories for Islamic banks, and the model governance framework that validates the measurement models used for those risks.

## How members connect

- Regulation-then-standard hierarchy: circular-level regulations (risk management, market risk, IRRBB) are the binding layer; the IRRBB standards, Islamic-bank risk standards and model management standards/guidance elaborate them.
- Rate of return risk and displaced commercial risk are the Islamic-banking analogues of IRRBB, so the same measurement duty (EVE, NII, stress testing, option risk) is mapped onto Shari'ah-compliant balance sheets with an added Shari'ah non-compliance risk head.
- Model management standards and guidance supply the control chain over the measurement layer: model life-cycle, independent validation, performance monitoring and board/model oversight committee accountability, applied to rating, PD, LGD, macroeconomic, IRRBB and NPV models.
- Methodological nodes (PIT/TTC estimation, transition matrices, Vasicek, credit index, ECL, scenario forecasting) are not independent obligations but the techniques whose use must be justified and validated under the model standards and numerical thresholds.
- Basel IRRBB standards sit upstream as the international baseline that the national IRRBB standards transpose.
