# Payable-Through Accounts

Defines payable-through (pass-through) accounts, where a respondent institution's own customers transact directly on the respondent's correspondent account — for example by writing cheques — so the correspondent faces customers on whom it has performed no due diligence. Grouped with nested relationships as a heightened ML/TF/PF risk requiring enhanced due diligence; the node also carries the guidance's glossary entries for correspondent banking, correspondent institution, nested activity and de-risking. Applies to UAE LFIs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
