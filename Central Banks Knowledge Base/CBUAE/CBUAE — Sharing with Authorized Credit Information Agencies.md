# Sharing with Authorized Credit Information Agencies

Addresses mandatory reporting of Consumer Data to government-authorised Credit Information Agencies (e.g. Al Etihad Credit Bureau) and the duty to inform Consumers of this reporting and of the possible impact on future access to Financial Products and/or Services. Where reported information contains errors, omissions or inaccuracies, the Licensed Financial Institution must correct it within seven complete business days of becoming aware, and must request deletion of Personal Data that was unlawfully collected and reported.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection
