# Financial Sanctions & Reporting

## Why this community

Strict-liability administrative sanctions for breaches of specified prudential, operational and regulatory reporting duties - counterfeit currency handling, court-case data, cross-border and card transaction reporting, certificate issuance deadlines and periodic returns.

## How members connect

- Single parent instrument: the strict-liability sanctions regulation enumerates the listed articles as sanctionable violations, so each article is both an obligation and a penalty trigger.
- Liability is strict - no intent or harm element - so the analysis turns on whether the specified act or reporting deadline was met, not on culpability.
- Reporting articles converge on the Integrated Regulatory Reporting System as the prescribed channel, making timeliness and completeness of submission the operative test.
- Cross-reference to the bank loans and services regulation supplies the substantive customer-facing duty (certificate issuance within a fixed period) that the sanctions regime enforces.
