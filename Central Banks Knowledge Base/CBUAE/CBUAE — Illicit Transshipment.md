# Illicit Transshipment

Concept node explaining transshipment — routing goods through intermediate destinations before the final country of destination — and how it is abused to evade sanctions, export controls and trade restrictions. It sets out typical illicit schemes (re-export from a sanctioned origin with falsified country-of-origin documents, imports by entities in low-restriction or corruption-prone jurisdictions, front companies established in third jurisdictions) and connects these to commodity typologies such as construction materials. Relevant to LFIs financing or processing trade flows and to sanctions/export-control risk assessment.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
