# Standard re External Shari'ah Audit for Islamic Financial Institutions

CBUAE Standard setting the minimum requirements for the External Shari'ah Audit of Islamic Financial Institutions, including the Shari'ah reference hierarchy the external auditor must apply, referral to the institution's Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee where no provision exists, and fit-and-proper criteria for external Shari'ah audit firms (Central Bank approval, licensing, experience, resources, team composition and a formal methodology manual). It binds IFIs appointing external Shari'ah auditors and the audit firms themselves, which require Central Bank approval. UAE instrument, not SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
