# Nested Correspondent Banking

Addresses nested (downstream) correspondent activity, where indirect respondent institutions access a correspondent account through the direct respondent, adding a layer of intermediation that reduces the correspondent's visibility into the transacting parties. Sets expectations that correspondents require disclosure of nested relationships at onboarding and in ongoing reviews, review them at least annually, obtain lists of nested institutions and screen them (watchlist, adverse media, PEP, sanctions), assess the respondent's controls, and insist on transparent payment message formatting. Directed at UAE LFIs acting as correspondent institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
