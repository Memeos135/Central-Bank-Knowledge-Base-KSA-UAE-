# Beneficiary

Defined term and obligation cluster within the CBUAE virtual asset transfer (travel rule) instrument: the Beneficiary is the intended recipient of a Virtual Asset Transfer, and the node carries the duties attaching to the Beneficiary's VASP and to Intermediary Providers. It covers verification of originator/beneficiary information, retention of collected information under the Implementing Regulation's record-keeping article, domestic-transfer simplification where verified data is otherwise available, and reporting systemic failures by counterparty VASPs to the Supervisory Authority. It binds licensed Virtual Asset Service Providers (originator-side, beneficiary-side) and Intermediary Providers handling virtual asset transfers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
