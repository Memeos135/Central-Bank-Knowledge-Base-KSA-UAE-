# Solvency Requirements

## Why this community

Solvency adequacy for takaful operators — the prudential requirement to hold and maintain capital/technical resources sufficient to meet obligations to participants and creditors.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster: a standalone prudential obligation within the takaful regulatory framework, addressed to the licensed insurer/operator rather than to consumers.
- Operates alongside the wider takaful regulation as an ongoing licensing condition, with supervisory consequences where the solvency margin is breached.
- Interacts with governance/risk expectations and with the separation of participants' funds from shareholders' funds, which determines what resources count toward solvency.
