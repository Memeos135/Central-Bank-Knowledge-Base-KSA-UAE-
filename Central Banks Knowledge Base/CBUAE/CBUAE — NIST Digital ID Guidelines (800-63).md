# NIST Digital ID Guidelines (800-63)

Node covering the CBUAE guidance's use of the NIST 800-63 Digital Identity Guidelines as an illustrative benchmark for grading the identity proofing and enrollment stage across assurance tiers, alongside the two-route assurance assessment (self-assessment with IDSP due diligence, or expert-body audit/certification). Useful for understanding what evidence quality, validation and verification expectations look like in practice when judging a digital ID system's reliability. Non-mandatory reference; the operative obligation is the documented assurance and appropriateness assessment by the LFI.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
