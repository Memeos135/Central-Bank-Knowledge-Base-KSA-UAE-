# ATM and POS Provisions

Conduct-of-business provisions in the CBUAE Consumer Protection instrument governing debit card usage limits, ATM and POS service quality, and terminal security. It requires Licensed Financial Institutions to obtain Consumer consent to daily ATM/POS transaction limits at issuance or reissuance, give 60 calendar days' written notice before reducing limits or changing card terms, investigate Consumer complaints about incorrect or failed ATM/POS transactions, monitor merchant clients for unauthorised surcharging, and monitor and document evidence of ATM/POS fraud and tampering (including reversing previously authorised transactions). It also imposes physical and technical security duties on ATMs (PIN pad shields, anti-skimming devices, sensors, cameras, progressive upgrades). Binds Licensed Financial Institutions in the UAE; note this is a CBUAE instrument, not SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, governance/risk
