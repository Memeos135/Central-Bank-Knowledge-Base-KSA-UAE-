# Fuzzy Matching

Addresses fuzzy matching as a required element of LFI name-screening procedures, alongside the ownership/control rule and handling of weak or low-quality aliases, to prevent and detect sanctions breaches. It concerns the tolerance for approximate name matches against the UN Consolidated List and Local Terrorist List and the subsequent true-match versus false-positive disposition. Guidance-level expectation on CBUAE-supervised LFIs, not a system-specification.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
