# Insurance Pricing (Article 5)

Sub-node of the 2010 insurance code of conduct dealing with pricing and related conduct duties owed to clients. The surrounding provisions require clear, accurate and truthful information on products, cover terms and limits, and prohibit refusal of proposals, refusal to renew, or discrimination between insured persons on rates, terms and benefits unless justified on technical or actuarial grounds or the company's experience with the client. Binds insurance companies registered with the Insurance Authority and operating in the State.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
