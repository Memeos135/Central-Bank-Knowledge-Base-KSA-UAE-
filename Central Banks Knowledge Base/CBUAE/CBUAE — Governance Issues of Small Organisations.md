# Governance Issues of Small Organisations

Section 8.6 of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines addresses governance where an FI is small or mid-sized and cannot maintain full separation of duties. It confirms that small size gives no exemption from AML-CFT Law and Decision obligations, and expects that a compliance officer holding dual roles should not have day-to-day sales or customer relationship responsibility, with compensating measures for CDD, STR identification and reporting, high-risk country measures and sanctions/local list monitoring. It binds smaller CBUAE-supervised financial institutions, including exchange houses and other non-bank FIs.
