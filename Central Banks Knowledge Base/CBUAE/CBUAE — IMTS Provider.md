# IMTS Provider

Defined term for providers of instant money transfer services that partner with exchange houses; such providers must first be registered with CBUAE, which maintains a register, and must accept CBUAE examination and regulatory reporting on their IMTS activities with Licensed Persons. IMTS Providers must comply with the AML laws and regulations and other CBUAE-directed regulations for their UAE activities, and are accountable for failures to report or for false, misleading or inaccurate reporting, with deregistration as a possible consequence. A Licensed Person must obtain an LNO before entering an IMTS arrangement and must embed CBUAE's on/off-site examination, information and reporting rights in the contract.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk
