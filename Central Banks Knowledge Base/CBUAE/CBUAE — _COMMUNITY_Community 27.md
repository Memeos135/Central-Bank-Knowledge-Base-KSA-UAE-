# Credit Default & Provisioning

## Why this community

Prudential credit risk classification: when an exposure is in default, when credit risk has increased significantly, and the resulting staging, provisioning and capital consequences. Note the standards and cross-references here are Central Bank of the UAE instruments, so they are persuasive comparators rather than SAMA obligations.

## How members connect

- A definitional chain: the default definition anchors the SICR test, which in turn drives stage allocation and the provisioning obligation.
- Article-to-standards pairing — each substantive article has a corresponding Standards article that elaborates the supervisory expectation, a classic rule-to-implementing-standard hierarchy.
- Provisioning is the convergence point: restructuring treatment, credit risk mitigation recognition, capital adequacy and model governance all feed into or draw from the classification and provisioning obligation.
- The three-stage classification concept is the shared taxonomy that makes the default and SICR definitions operationally binding.
