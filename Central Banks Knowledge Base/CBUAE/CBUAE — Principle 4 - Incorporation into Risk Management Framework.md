# Principle 4 - Incorporation into Risk Management Framework

Principle 4 requires the board and senior management to oversee the embedding of climate-related financial risks into the firm's internal risk management framework, including policies and procedures to identify, assess, measure, mitigate, monitor and report exposures. It contains the materiality considerations referenced by Principle 2 and extends to group-wide coordination where a firm has significant relationships with subsidiaries, affiliates or international branches. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial firms.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
