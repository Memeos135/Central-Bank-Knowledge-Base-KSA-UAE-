# Article 3: Institutional Oversight

Article 3 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards establishes the institutional oversight and governance framework for consumer protection and conduct risk: organisational structure, corporate culture, qualified staff, documented policies and monitoring/control frameworks under board oversight. It requires the regulation's principles to be embedded in management, board governance of conduct and compliance risk (with an equivalent designated representative or committee for licensed foreign bank branches under the Corporate Governance Regulation), and supports the annual Consumer Protection & Conduct Risk Management report and product approval framework. Binds boards and senior management of all licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
