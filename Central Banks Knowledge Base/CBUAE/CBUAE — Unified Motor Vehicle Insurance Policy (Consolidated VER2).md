# Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy (Consolidated VER2)

Consolidated version of the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Loss and Damage (IA-BOD-RES 25/2016), containing the insurer's obligations chapter: repair, cash settlement or replacement on total loss, depreciation on new original parts, optional riders for additional cover, limits on refusing claims for late notification with acceptable excuse, and a prohibition on side agreements (including under fleet policies) that reduce cover or restrict the right to claim. It binds insurers issuing motor loss-and-damage policies and protects insureds and beneficiaries against contractual derogation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
