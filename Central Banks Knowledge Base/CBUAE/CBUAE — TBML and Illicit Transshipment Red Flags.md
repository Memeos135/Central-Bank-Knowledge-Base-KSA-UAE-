# TBML and Illicit Transshipment Red Flags

Node collecting TBML and illicit transshipment typologies and red flags in the CBUAE guidance, including transshipment through non-sanctioned intermediary countries, re-labelling of country of origin, diversion to prohibited end-users, exploitation of corruption-prone jurisdictions and use of third-jurisdiction front companies. It supports detection and suspicious activity reporting by licensed financial institutions, noting that no single typology is determinative and indicators should be used in combination. Introductory framing covers UAE free zone and trade-hub exposure to trade-based financial crime.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
