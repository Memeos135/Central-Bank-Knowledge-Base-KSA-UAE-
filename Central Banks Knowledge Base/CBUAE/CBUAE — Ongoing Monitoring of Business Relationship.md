# Ongoing Monitoring of Business Relationship

A concept node on continuous scrutiny of business relationships and transactions to confirm consistency with the customer's profile, source of funds and risk classification, and to keep CDD information current. It links customer/business-relationship risk assessment methodologies to the intensity of monitoring, and addresses the post-STR position: a reported relationship is by definition high risk, and if retained the decision and rationale must be documented and enhanced due diligence plus additional controls (added information requirements, product restrictions, transaction limits or approval gates) applied. Binds CBUAE-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
