# Repayment Installments (50% cap)

Node covering the regulation's ceiling on repayment instalments, i.e. the limit on deductions from a borrower's salary or regular income for all loans extended by banks and finance companies, set out in Article 7 and cross-referenced by the personal, car, overdraft and credit card articles. The consolidated text applies a fifty percent of gross salary ceiling to all instalments and facilities for Armed Forces personnel. Binds all banks and finance companies assessing borrower affordability for individual customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
