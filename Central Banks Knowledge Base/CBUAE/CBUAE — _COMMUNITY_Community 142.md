# Islamic Enterprise Risk Governance

## Why this community

Governance interface between conventional risk management and Shari'ah governance in Islamic financial activity.

## How members connect

- The governance article is the source obligation and cross-references both the enterprise risk management framework and the internal Shari'ah supervisory committee.
- The linkage makes Shari'ah compliance a governance input to risk management, not a parallel silo — the same governing body must accommodate both.
- Small bridging cluster: it connects the general ERM/solvency expectations to the Islamic-finance instruments governed elsewhere in the framework.
