# Recovery Planning

Concept node on recovery planning as an element of the CBUAE ERM standards for insurance companies, anchored in the Recovery Planning Regulation (Circular 4/2023) among the Relevant Regulations. It requires consistency between ORSA outcomes, stress testing results and the Recovery Plan, with the ORSA serving as a key input so that risk assessment and contingency planning are aligned. Binds insurance and Takaful companies and their Boards and Senior Management, which must develop plans and actions for periods of severe financial stress.
