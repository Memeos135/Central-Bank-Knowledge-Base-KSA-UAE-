# Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation

Concept node for the Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation, defined as the authority responsible for implementing targeted financial sanctions within the UAE. Its instructions on TFS must be implemented without delay by subject entities, alongside their broader preventive duties (CDD, prohibition of anonymous/fictitious accounts, approved internal policies and controls, record retention). Relevant when identifying the issuing authority for freezing obligations and the interface between TFS instructions and AML programme requirements.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
