# Pillar 3 Market Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 public disclosure obligations — the market-discipline leg of the Basel framework, requiring banks to publish prescribed quantitative and qualitative information on capital, risk exposures and risk assessment processes.

## How members connect

- Single-member community; its legal significance is derivative — it imposes disclosure duties over the computations produced under the Pillar 1 standards (credit, market, operational, securitisation, leverage) and the Pillar 2 process.
- Read alongside the Basel Risk Approaches and Securitisation clusters, since disclosure templates and frequency are keyed to the approach the bank actually applies.
