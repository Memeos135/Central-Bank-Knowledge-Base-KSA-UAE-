# Foreign Payment Token Registration

## Why this community

Treatment of payment tokens issued outside the jurisdiction and the registration pathway their issuers must follow before the token can be offered or used locally.

## How members connect

- The defined term 'Foreign Payment Token' sets the scope trigger; the registration obligation attaches only once a token falls within that definition.
- Sequencing matters: registration of the foreign issuer is the gating condition, so local distribution or service provision on an unregistered foreign token is a scope breach.
- Sits beneath the broader payment token services regime and inherits its licensing, capital and conduct expectations for domestic intermediaries.
