# Consumer Assessment for Suitability

Combines the business-conduct duty (5.1.1.13–5.1.1.16) to maintain policies, procedures, controls and training for consistently assessing consumers' needs, objectives and product appropriateness/suitability/affordability, with the Article 7 credit-specific suitability framework (7.1.3.3–7.1.3.5). It requires a free summary copy of any suitability/affordability assessment to the consumer, documented methods and clear approval authorities with independently reviewed exceptions, plus verification of income, purpose of credit and creditworthiness via the Credit Information Agency, and provision of General Advice. Profiling assessments must be documented, kept confidential and retained at least five years. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions and their sales and credit staff.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, data/credit info, governance/risk
