# Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones (DPMS)

Deals with dealers in precious metals and stones as a high-risk DNFBP category and with the related cross-border declaration controls. It links the AML/CFT enhanced due diligence duties for DNFBP customers (including PEP-linked beneficial owners and legal persons/arrangements) to the Customs Declaration Form regime for travellers carrying currency, precious metals or stones, or bearer negotiable instruments above AED 60,000, requiring the Licensed Person to collect and retain the original CDF and to reconcile partial exchanges against the declared value.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
