# CBUAE Sanctions Screening Testing Thematic Review

A reference to CBUAE supervisory work on sanctions screening testing, feeding the guidance's summary expectations and the annexed lessons learned from CBUAE supervision on screening effectiveness, list maintenance, risk appetite and evasion monitoring. It signals supervisory findings and examination focus areas for LFIs rather than creating new rules, and is read together with the binding TFS obligations under Cabinet Decision 74. Consult it to understand how the regulator assesses screening adequacy.
