# Federal Decree by Law 10 of 2025

Federal Decree by Law No. 10 of 2025 is the primary UAE statute on anti-money laundering and combating the financing of terrorism and proliferation financing, and the parent law of Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025. Its definitions carry directly into the Executive Regulations, which elaborate the categories of Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and VASPs and their obligations. It binds all persons falling within those defined categories, together with supervisory authorities and the Financial Intelligence Unit.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
