# Compensation System

This node covers the compensation (remuneration) requirements within the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks, sitting alongside articles on financial reporting/external audit and outsourcing. It requires compensation outcomes to be symmetric with risk outcomes, with deferral, malus and clawback mechanisms, fixed-only pay for Board members, predominantly fixed and independently determined pay for risk, compliance and internal audit staff, and performance-based components with reduction/reversal triggers for Senior Management and Material Risk Takers. It binds licensed banks, their Boards and Senior Management, with detailed control-function governance set out in separate CBUAE regulations and standards.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
