# Article 6: Quality of Capital to be Held

Provision specifying the quality of capital eligible to meet the minimum requirement: a Restricted Licence Bank's Article 5.1 minimum must be satisfied entirely with fully paid-up capital. Read together with Article 7, which requires the minimum to be calculated on a standalone basis, and with the Central Bank's power to set a higher minimum and define eligible capital quality at licensing. Binds Restricted Licence Banks operating in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
