# Retail Payment Service

The scoping concept of the Regulation: Retail Payment Services are the digital payment activities in the UAE that trigger a Central Bank licensing requirement, grouped into nine categories and split across licence Categories I to IV. The node's context includes the covering circular, which explains the licensing rules, the obligation on Card Schemes to be licensed, mandatory contractual arrangements between account-issuing PSPs and initiation/account-information providers, and additional ongoing requirements for PSPs seeking Wages Protection System access. Determines who falls inside the perimeter and must be licensed.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
