# Chief Ombudsman

Node on the Chief Ombudsman, the officer appointed by the CBUAE Board of Directors who runs day-to-day operations of the Ombudsman Unit under delegation from its Board. Duties include overseeing complaint acceptance, handling, review and resolution, ensuring staff, contractors, agents and consultants comply with the Unit's policies, terms of reference, code of conduct, conflict-of-interest rules, CBUAE regulations and the Decretal Law, and appointing and directing staff within delegated authority. Binds the Chief Ombudsman and, through supervision, Ombudsman Unit staff and engaged third parties.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
