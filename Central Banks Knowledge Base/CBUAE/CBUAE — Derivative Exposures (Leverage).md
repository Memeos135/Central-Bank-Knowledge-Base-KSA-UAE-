# Derivative Exposures (Leverage)

A sub-part of the CBUAE capital adequacy Standards dealing with how derivative positions enter the leverage ratio exposure measure, including client-clearing arrangements. It sets when a bank acting as clearing member may exclude client trade exposures (conditions on portability/segregation and non-reimbursement of client losses), requires guarantees of client performance to the CCP to be measured as if the bank had transacted directly, and treats trades with consolidated affiliates as proprietary exposures. It binds banks licensed in the UAE calculating leverage ratio exposure at the relevant level of consolidation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When computing the derivative leg of the leverage ratio exposure measure, the potential future exposure add-on is a mandatory component, not an optional refinement. The leverage provisions define the total exposure measure as an aggregate that includes derivative exposures, and those exposures are built from replacement cost plus the PFE add-on as specified in the counterparty credit risk methodology. For the reader, this means any dispute about PFE inputs is simultaneously a dispute about the reported leverage ratio, so the same add-on parameters should be applied consistently across both calculations.
