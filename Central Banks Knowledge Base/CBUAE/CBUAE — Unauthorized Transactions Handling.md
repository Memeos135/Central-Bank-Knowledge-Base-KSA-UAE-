# Unauthorized Transactions Handling

Concept node for the handling of Unauthorized Transactions: Licensed Financial Institutions must offer 24/7 digital and phone reporting channels for loss, theft, fraud or misuse of accounts, cards and digital instruments, with performance standards, call monitoring and one-year record retention. It sets the evidential rule that transactions are treated as authorized where proper secure validation was applied unless the Consumer shows prima facie reasonable doubt, and grants Consumers at least 30 business days to report after being properly informed. Adjacent provisions cover debit card daily limits (with 60 calendar days' written notice of reductions) and ATM/POS investigation, merchant surcharging monitoring and tampering reviews.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, governance/risk
