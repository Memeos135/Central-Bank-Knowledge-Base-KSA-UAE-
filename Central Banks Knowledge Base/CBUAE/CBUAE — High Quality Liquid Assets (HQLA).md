# High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)

Defines the eligible stock of High Quality Liquid Assets for the LCR, split into Level 1 (0% risk-weighted, no haircut, no cap, with a sub-limit on unrated 0% risk-weighted assets) and Level 2A/2B (capped at 40% and 15% respectively, subject to haircuts). It also sets expectations on unencumbered status, currency matching, diversification and sole use for liquidity management, and records national discretion for unrated UAE GRE/PSE issuers. Binding on banks computing the LCR.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
