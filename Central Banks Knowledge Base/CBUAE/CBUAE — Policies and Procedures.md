# Policies and Procedures

Sub-element of the SCP setting expectations for senior-management-approved sanctions policies and procedures: identification, escalation and reporting of suspicious activity, tipping-off prohibition regarding freezing or Other Measures, enhanced due diligence for customers and transactions assessed as high-risk for TF and PF, and detailed record-keeping. Requires consistent implementation across branches, subsidiaries and majority-held entities, communication to staff and outsourced providers, timely updates for emerging risks, at least annual formal review, and documented approval of any deviations.
