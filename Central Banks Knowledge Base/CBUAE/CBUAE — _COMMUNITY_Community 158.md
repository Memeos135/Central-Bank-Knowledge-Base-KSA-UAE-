# Strategic & Operational Decisions

## Why this community

Allocation of decision-making authority over strategic and operational matters — i.e. which decisions must be taken (or ratified) by the regulated entity's own governing body in the Kingdom rather than elsewhere in a group. The paired Regulation and Standards set the obligation and then its operative detail.

## How members connect

- Two-tier instrument pair on the same numbered article: the Regulation states the binding requirement, the Standards article expands it into supervisory expectations for the same subject matter.
- The Standards article expressly cross-refers back to the Regulation article, so the Standards cannot be read as a free-standing obligation — it takes scope and defined terms from the Regulation.
- Sits alongside the group risk management article (community 159): both govern the local entity/group boundary, so decision-authority and risk-control duties must be read together to avoid conflicting escalation lines.
