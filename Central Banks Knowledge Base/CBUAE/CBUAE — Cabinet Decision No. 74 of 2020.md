# Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020

An instrument node for Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020, cited among the legal bases on which the CBUAE proliferation-financing guidance is built (alongside Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 and the AML-CFT Decision). The surrounding guidance frames LFI duties to apply competent-authority directives implementing UN Security Council decisions without delay, to file STRs/SARs with the UAE FIU, and to face measures under Articles 13 and 14 of the AML-CFT Law for non-compliance. Binds all CBUAE-licensed and supervised entities, including banks, exchange houses, finance companies, payment and payment-token service providers, registered hawala providers and insurers.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
