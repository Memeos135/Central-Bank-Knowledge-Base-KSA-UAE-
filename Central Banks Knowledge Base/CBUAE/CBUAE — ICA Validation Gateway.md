# ICA Validation Gateway

Addresses the UAE government-supported validation channels — the online validation gateway of the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security, UAE Pass, or equivalent — that LFIs are expected to use to confirm identity information presented during digital onboarding matches reliable government sources. Sits within the proofing sequence of collection, validation, verification (including biometric/liveness checks), and enrollment/binding of credentials and authenticators. Binds UAE LFIs performing remote identity verification; the KSA analogue would be locally mandated national identity verification sources.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
