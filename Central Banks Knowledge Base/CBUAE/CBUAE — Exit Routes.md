# Exit Routes

The exit-routes element of the CBUAE Regulatory Sandbox Conditions, which (per the Overview at section 4) is one of four matters the Conditions regulate alongside eligibility, the application process and minimum safeguards. It covers how participation ends and what a Participant must do on exit, including the route toward applying for a Licence for market-wide deployment. It binds Participants (applicants accepted in full by the Central Bank) and clarifies the Central Bank's role and powers over them; related definitions (Participant, Regulations, the State) sit in section 3.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk
