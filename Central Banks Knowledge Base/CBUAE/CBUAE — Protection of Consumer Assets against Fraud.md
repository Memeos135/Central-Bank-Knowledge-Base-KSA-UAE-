# Protection of Consumer Assets against Fraud

Sub-section 6.2.1 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards setting conduct-based safeguarding duties over consumer assets, records and collateral, with accountability assigned to Senior Management and an internal control structure covering segregation of duties, operational risk mitigation, logical and physical access security, and documentation aligned with UAE AML/CTF guidance. It extends specific protections to unclaimed assets, Stored Value Facilities, digital money and dormant accounts, and requires collateral to be held and managed honestly, fairly and in the consumer's best interest. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions in the UAE and, indirectly, their staff, authorised agents and third parties with access to consumer assets.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF
