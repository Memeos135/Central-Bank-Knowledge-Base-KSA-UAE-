# Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 (Implementing Regulation)

Instrument node for Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019, the Implementing Regulation of Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018, as summarised in the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines. It operationalises the statutory AML/CFT obligations — risk identification and assessment, scope of due diligence, appointment of a qualified compliance officer, internal controls and MIS, suspicious-transaction indicators, and reporting/cooperation with Competent Authorities. It binds supervised institutions (financial institutions and DNFBPs) across the UAE, including free zones.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
