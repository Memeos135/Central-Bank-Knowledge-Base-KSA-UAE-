# Simple Alternative Approach for CVA

Node covering the simplified/alternative route to CVA capital within the CBUAE framework, set against the background of why a CVA charge exists (crisis-era mark-to-market losses on counterparty credit risk rather than outright defaults). It situates regulatory CVA as distinct from accounting CVA, notably excluding own-credit effects (DVA), and frames hedging recognition. Applies to banks determining CVA capital requirements for derivatives and in-scope SFT exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
