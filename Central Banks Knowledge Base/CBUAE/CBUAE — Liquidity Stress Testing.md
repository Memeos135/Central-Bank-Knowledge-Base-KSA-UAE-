# Liquidity Stress Testing

Concept node covering the qualitative liquidity risk management expectations derived from the BCBS 2008 sound liquidity principles, including stress-sensitive identification and measurement of liquidity risk across all jurisdictions and on/off-balance-sheet positions, and pricing of liquidity costs, benefits and risks into products and the new product approval process. It also anchors board responsibility for setting liquidity risk tolerance and senior management's role in implementation. Binding on banks' liquidity risk management and governance frameworks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
