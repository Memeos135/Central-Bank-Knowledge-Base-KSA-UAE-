# AML/CFT Guidance for LFIs Servicing Real Estate and Precious Metals Sectors

CBUAE AML/CFT guidance for Licensed Financial Institutions servicing the real estate and dealers in precious metals and stones (DPMS) sectors, explaining sector risk drivers and the regulatory perimeter. It clarifies when DPMS and real estate agents/brokers qualify as DNFBPs under Article 3 of the AML-CFT Decision (including the AED 55,000 transaction threshold for DPMS), who supervises them (Ministry of Economy in mainland/CFZs; FSRA and DFSA in financial free zones), and states that LFIs need not classify customers as DNFBPs but should establish that status through CDD. It also signals that CBUAE does not expect blanket de-risking of these sectors.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Risk-Based Approach|Risk-Based Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Sector-specific expectations for institutions servicing real estate and precious metals customers are not a separate rulebook; they are an application of the general risk-based approach. The guidance signals that the supervisor does not expect blanket de-risking of these sectors, and the risk-based approach section supplies the mechanism—customer risk rating, the institutional risk assessment and proportionate preventive measures for all customers, including legal persons and arrangements. The consequence is that a decision to exit or refuse a real estate or dealer relationship must be justified by a documented customer-level risk assessment rather than by sector classification alone.
