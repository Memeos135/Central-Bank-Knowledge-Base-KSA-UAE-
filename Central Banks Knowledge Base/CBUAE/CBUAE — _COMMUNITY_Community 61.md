# Politically Exposed Persons (PEP)

## Why this community

Enhanced due diligence triggered by customer status rather than transaction type: identifying politically exposed persons, their beneficial owners and related/associated customers, and establishing source of funds and wealth before and during the relationship.

## How members connect

- All members hang off the PEP definition; classification as a PEP (or a PEP's beneficial owner, family member or close associate) is the trigger that upgrades standard CDD into the EDD obligation chain.
- Screening is the detection step, EDD is the consequence: screening results feed senior-management approval, source of funds/wealth establishment and ongoing enhanced monitoring.
- The 'Related Customer' and 'Beneficial Owner' nodes extend PEP treatment through ownership and control layers, so EDD cannot be scoped to the named account holder alone.
- Sector variants (insurance EDD, DNFBP-facing EDD) show the same obligation applied under different supervisory regimes, so applicability depends on which entity type is in scope.
