# Responsibilities of Senior Management

Section 8.5 of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines sets out senior management involvement and accountability for AML/CFT compliance, cross-referenced to multiple AML-CFT Decision articles (including those on risk assessment, CDD approval, compliance officer appointment and reporting). It requires senior management to approve internal AML/CFT policies, controls and procedures, ensure they are reviewed, updated and applied across branches and majority-owned entities, and that they reflect NRA and topical risk assessment outcomes and be proportionate to the FI's own ML/FT business risk assessment. It binds boards and senior management of CBUAE-supervised FIs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
