# Remuneration Policy

Concept node covering the board-approved remuneration policy requirement under the CBUAE Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies. It requires the policy to link pay to institutional performance, strategic objectives and risk-adjusted outcomes, to avoid incentivising excessive or inappropriate risk-taking, to set the fixed/variable balance by role or staff category, to involve Control Function staff in design and monitoring, to address conflicts of interest (including for the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee), and to be subject to an annual independent review submitted to the Central Bank. It binds Banks and Insurance/Reinsurance Companies ("Financial Institutions"), with duties falling principally on the Board and its remuneration committee.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
