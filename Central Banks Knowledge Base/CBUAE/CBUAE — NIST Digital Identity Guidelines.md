# NIST Digital Identity Guidelines

A referenced external technical standard (NIST Digital Identity Guidelines) that the CBUAE digital ID guidance adapts, for illustration only, into a table of reliability factors — presence, resolution, evidence, validation, verification — mapped across no/high/very high assurance tiers for the identity proofing and enrollment stage. It is not itself binding: the guidance neither mandates nor recommends a particular assurance level, leaving licensed financial institutions to judge appropriate levels against their ML/TF, fraud and illicit finance risk. Relevant to UAE LFIs assessing whether a digital ID system is reliable and independent for CDD; it is persuasive reference material only for KSA-regulated entities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
