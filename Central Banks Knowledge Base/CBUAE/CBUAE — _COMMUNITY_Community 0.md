# Insurance AML & Solvency Rules

## Why this community

Insurance and takaful sector regulation covering licensing and conduct of intermediaries, composite-company separation, solvency and financial regulations, motor unified policies, life/family takaful pricing and distribution, surveyors/loss adjusters, plus the AML/CTF and targeted-financial-sanctions overlay applicable to insurers. Note that a substantial portion of these instruments are UAE Insurance Authority / federal instruments, so jurisdictional scope must be checked before applying them to a KSA question.

## How members connect

- Clear law → regulation → decision hierarchy: the establishing insurance law is the cited authority for nearly every Board/Administrative Decision in the cluster, and the Financial Regulations for Insurance/Takaful Companies is the shared prudential reference for solvency, composite separation and reinsurance decisions.
- Shared defined terms bind sub-groups: separation of persons/fund versus property/liability insurance links composite-company, reinsurance and takaful rules; Wakala and Mudaraba fees link the Shari'a committee, takaful regulations and life/family takaful instructions.
- Product-level chains: the unified motor vehicle policy decisions, their consolidated versions, the TPL and loss-and-damage policy documents, depreciation schedules and surveyor/loss-adjuster appointment for disputes form one continuous obligation chain from policy wording to claims settlement.
- AML/CTF sub-chain runs law → executive regulations → terrorist-lists regulations, and attaches to firm-level duties (compliance officer, risk assessment framework, reporting) and to the data/reporting guidance manuals used for supervisory submissions.
- Distribution and conduct cross-links: marketing insurance through banks, designated officer conditions, insurance consultant licensing, commission limits, free-look period and the code of conduct all govern the intermediary-to-policyholder relationship.
