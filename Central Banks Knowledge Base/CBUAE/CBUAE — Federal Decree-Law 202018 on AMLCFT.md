# Federal Decree-Law 20/2018 on AML/CFT

Node keyed to the UAE AML/CFT Decree-Law; the material provided is Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023 on Regulating the Beneficial Owner Procedures, issued under that Decree-Law and replacing the earlier Cabinet Decision 58/2020 regime. It defines beneficial ownership concepts (nominee board member, senior management, trustor/trustee/trust fund, complex ownership structure) and requires legal persons to maintain a beneficial owner register and a register of partners or shareholders filed with the registrar. It binds legal persons registered in the UAE and the registrars and concerned entities supervising them, and underpins CDD/beneficial-owner identification duties of financial institutions and DNFBPs.
