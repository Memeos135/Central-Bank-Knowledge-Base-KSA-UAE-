# AML-CFT Law (Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018)

This node is the referenced UAE primary AML/CFT statute — Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organisations, as amended by Decree Federal Law No. (26) of 2021 — as cited in the CBUAE payments guidance. Within this context it is invoked for the suspicious transaction reporting duty and for the obligation to apply targeted financial sanctions directives. It binds financial institutions and other reporting entities in the UAE; the node itself is a cross-reference within CBUAE guidance rather than the full statutory text.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
