# Restricted Licence Banks Regulation

CBUAE Circular No. 23/2022 (17/2/2022) establishing the Restricted Licence Banks Regulation, which sets the specific requirements applying to banks whose licences restrict them from onboarding natural persons as customers or conducting licensed financial activities with or for natural persons, and which bars accounts or facilities below AED 1,000,000 or equivalent. It covers minimum paid-up capital (AED 1 billion, with foreign branches following the Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation), capital quality, standalone calculation, breach notification and remediation, and approval for profit distributions. It binds all banks, including foreign bank branches, licensed to operate as restricted licence banks in the UAE; all other CBUAE regulations addressed to "all banks" continue to apply.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
