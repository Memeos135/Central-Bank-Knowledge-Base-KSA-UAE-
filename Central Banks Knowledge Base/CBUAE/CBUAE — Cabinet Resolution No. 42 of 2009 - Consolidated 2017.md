# Cabinet Resolution No. 42 of 2009 - Consolidated 2017

Consolidated version (as of 15/05/2017) of Cabinet Resolution No. 42 of 2009 on insurance company minimum capital, incorporating the amendments made by Cabinet Resolution No. 16 of 2017. It retains the capital-increase/reduction approval regime, the transitional rectification period and the Board's power to issue implementing instructions, while the shareholder-eligibility provision is now framed as a matter for a Board resolution. This is the operative text to cite for the post-2017 position applicable to insurers, Takaful operators and reinsurers.

**Regimes:** other
