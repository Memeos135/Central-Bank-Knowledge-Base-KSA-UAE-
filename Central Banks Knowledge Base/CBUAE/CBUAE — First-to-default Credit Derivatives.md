# First-to-default Credit Derivatives

Node on first-to-default credit derivatives, covering how specific risk capital is charged on basket protection (capped by the maximum possible credit event payment) and when a bank may offset the charge against a hedged reference credit instrument, including the rule limiting offset where multiple underlying positions are held. Related material addresses partial allowance where currency, maturity or asset mismatches exist between protection and the underlying. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks measuring market risk and credit protection recognition.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
