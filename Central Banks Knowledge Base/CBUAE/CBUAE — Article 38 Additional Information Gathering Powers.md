# Article 38: Additional Information Gathering Powers

Article 38 gives the CBUAE additional information-gathering powers, allowing it to require information it considers necessary from Licensees and Registrees and from virtual asset service providers that are neither licensed/registered by the Central Bank nor licensed or regulated by SCA or a Local Licensing Authority. It also permits the Central Bank to enter information-sharing agreements or memoranda of understanding with, or request information from, SCA, Local Licensing Authorities and other regulators to exercise its powers or meet the Regulation's objectives.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
