# Cancellation of Previous Notices (Article 19)

Article 19 is the repeal provision of the CBUAE Operational Risk and Operational Resilience Regulation, cancelling and replacing Circular No. 163/2018 (the Operational Risk Regulation and Operational Risk Standards of 29 August 2018). It sits alongside Article 18 (the Regulatory Development Department as interpretive authority) and Article 20 (publication in the Official Gazette in Arabic and English, effective one month after publication). Relevant to anyone checking which instrument governs an LFI's operational risk obligations for a given date.
