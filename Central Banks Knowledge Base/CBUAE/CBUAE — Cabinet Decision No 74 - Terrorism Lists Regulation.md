# Cabinet Decision No 74 - Terrorism Lists Regulation

UAE Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020 on the Terrorism Lists Regulation and implementation of UN Security Council resolutions on terrorism, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, abrogating Cabinet Decision No. 20 of 2019. It sets the procedures for listing, de-listing and re-listing persons on Local Lists and for proposing designations to the UN Security Council or a Sanctions Committee, and for publication, freezing and other measures, including grievance routes before the Competent Court. It binds the Supreme Council for National Security, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Executive Office, supervisory and law enforcement authorities, and, through freezing obligations, financial institutions and DNFBPs.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
