# Ownership/Control Rule

Concept node on the ownership and control test applied in sanctions screening: LFIs are expected to set policy on interests, assets or entities owned or controlled 50% or more by a Listed Person, and to assess control through criteria such as board appointment rights, dominant influence, voting-rights agreements, use of assets, unified management or joint liability. It sits within the sanctions risk appetite and name-screening expectations and determines whether non-listed entities are treated as within scope of a freeze.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
