# UAE Competent Authority

A defined term within the UAE Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 32 of 2017 on the Common Reporting Standard Regulations, identifying the Ministry of Finance (established under Federal Law No. 1 of 1972) as the UAE Competent Authority. It matters because the Competent Authority is the body that agrees common meanings under tax treaties or competent authority agreements and is the counterparty for automatic exchange of financial account information. It binds insurance and takaful companies in the UAE that issue or make payments under Cash Value Insurance Contracts or Annuity Contracts, which must report through this authority.
