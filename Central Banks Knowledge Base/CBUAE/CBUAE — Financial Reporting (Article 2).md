# Financial Reporting (Article 2)

Article 2 of a CBUAE instrument on financial reporting and external audit for (Takaful) insurance companies, placing responsibility on the Board and Senior Management for financial statements prepared under internationally accepted accounting policies, supported by record keeping, and published annually with an independent external auditor's opinion. It requires the Board audit committee to oversee financial reporting and significant accounting policies, mandates IFRS and Central Bank reporting instructions, and requires prior written no-objection from the Central Bank before distributing profits, reserves or other regulatory capital components. Binds licensed companies, their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
