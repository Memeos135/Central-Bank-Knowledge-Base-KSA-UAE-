# Supreme Council for National Security

Defined-term node for "the Council" under Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020, meaning the UAE Supreme Council for National Security. It is the body that assesses designation criteria, proposes listings and de-listings on Local Lists via the Ministry of Presidential Affairs to the Cabinet, proposes designations to the UN Security Council or Sanctions Committees, and decides on requests to lift freezing or other measures within set time limits. Understanding this role matters for identifying who makes designation decisions versus who merely publishes or implements them.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
