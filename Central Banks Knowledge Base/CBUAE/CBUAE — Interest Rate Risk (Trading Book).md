# Interest Rate Risk (Trading Book)

Node covering interest rate risk in the trading book under the CBUAE market risk capital standard, including the banking book/trading book boundary and the conditions for a position to receive trading book capital treatment. Requirements include a documented and senior-management-approved trading strategy, active management on a trading desk with monitored position limits, at least daily mark-to-market or model parameter assessment, senior management reporting, and policies governing transfers between books. Binds UAE banks holding trading positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** The practical decision is which regime captures a given interest rate exposure: the trading-book interest rate rules feed the market risk capital calculation, which must include interest rate derivatives and off-balance-sheet instruments sensitive to rate moves, whereas interest rate risk in the banking book is addressed through supervisory review rather than that Pillar 1 charge. Book classification therefore drives both the measurement methodology and the capital consequence. A misclassified position changes which standard's obligations bind, so classification decisions should be documented and tested against the primary text.
