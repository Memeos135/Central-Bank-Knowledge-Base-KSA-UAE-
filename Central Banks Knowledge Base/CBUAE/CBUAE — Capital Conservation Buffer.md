# Capital Conservation Buffer

A CET1 buffer required on top of the minimum CET1 ratio, phased in to its full level under the transitional table in the capital adequacy framework. Where a bank operates within the buffer range, constraints on distributions apply on a sliding scale by earnings, and the bank must have a plan to rebuild the buffer through its internal capital adequacy assessment process, restore it within a timeframe agreed with the Central Bank, and accept closer supervisory monitoring. Binds all banks subject to the Capital Adequacy Regulations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
