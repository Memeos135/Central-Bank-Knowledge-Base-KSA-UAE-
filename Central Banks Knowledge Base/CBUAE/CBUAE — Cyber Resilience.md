# Cyber Resilience

Node covering the cyber resilience provisions within the technology risk part of the CBUAE SVF Regulation: managing cyber security risk through the Licensee's technology risk management process, committing adequate resources to identify risk, protect critical services, contain incidents and restore services. It also addresses cyber threat intelligence (including subscribing to relevant services and industry information sharing), periodic assessment of the need for penetration and cyber-attack simulation testing scoped to the cyber risk profile and covering networks, applications, social engineering and emerging threats, timely remediation based on impact and risk exposure, and risks from internet-connected devices. Binds SVF Licensees (and banks conducting SVF business under Article 12).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
