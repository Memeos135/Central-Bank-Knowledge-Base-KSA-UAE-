# Securitisation Framework

Orientation node for the CBUAE securitisation capital framework, which sets out how banks in the UAE determine regulatory capital for banking-book securitisation exposures, closely tracking the Basel Committee's revised 2014/2016 standard. It defines what counts as a securitisation (tranching with differentiated loss absorption, payments driven by underlying pool performance rather than the originator's obligation), excludes simple pass-through structures, and identifies borderline cases such as real-estate cash-flow deals that may instead be specialised lending. It binds banks holding or originating securitisation exposures and requires consultation with the Central Bank where classification is uncertain; note this is UAE (CBUAE), not SAMA, material.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
