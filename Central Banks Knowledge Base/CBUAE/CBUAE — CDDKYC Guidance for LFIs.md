# CDD/KYC Guidance for LFIs

The parent node for the CBUAE guidance on customer due diligence / know your customer for Licensed Financial Institutions, which treats CDD and KYC as interchangeable terms for the ongoing, risk-based process of identifying customers and assessing their ML/TF/PF risk before and throughout the relationship. It sets out the trigger events for CDD (notably establishing a business relationship), expectations for correspondent banking relationships, and group-wide consistency — including applying UAE standards in foreign branches and majority-owned subsidiaries where host-country rules are less stringent. Binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions, including their overseas branches and majority-owned subsidiaries.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
