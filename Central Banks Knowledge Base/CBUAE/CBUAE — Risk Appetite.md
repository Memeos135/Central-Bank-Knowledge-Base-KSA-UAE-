# Risk Appetite

Defined-term node for Risk Appetite, the board-determined level of risk a company accepts, set against the competitive and regulatory landscape, long-term interests and actual capacity to manage risk. It anchors risk limits, early warning indicators, capital needs beyond regulatory minimums, mitigation decisions (reduce, transfer or cease the activity) and reporting, with any deviation requiring board review and approval. Operative for boards, senior management and control functions of insurance/Takaful companies and at group level.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

- **What this link tells you:** Read risk appetite not as a standalone statement but as the benchmark the risk management function is mandated to operationalise and police. The risk governance framework ties the two: the board articulates appetite, and the function sets entity-wide (or group-wide) risk and control strategies, independently challenges the business lines, and monitors and reports adherence.
