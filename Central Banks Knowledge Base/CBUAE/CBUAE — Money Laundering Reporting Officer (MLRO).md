# Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)

Node for the Money Laundering Reporting Officer role as used in the CBUAE risk-based approach guidance, which is issued under the Supervisory Authority mandate in Article 44.11 of Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019 implementing Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018. The context here is introductory and scope-setting rather than a standalone MLRO rulebook, so the node mainly locates the MLRO within the LFI's RBA and compliance programme. Relevant to licensed financial institutions and their appointed compliance officers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
