# Record Keeping

Chapter 9 of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines covers record-keeping obligations and required record types, referencing AML-CFT Law Article 16.1 and AML-CFT Decision Articles 7.2, 24, 36 and 37.3. It sets the minimum five-year retention period running from the latest of relationship termination, completion of an occasional transaction, completion of a supervisory inspection or the date of a final judgment, requires records to be organised so transactions can be reconstructed, and to be made available to Competent Authorities immediately on request; it also categorises transaction records and CDD records and addresses data protection, archiving, destruction of expired records and audit/quality assurance over record-keeping. It binds all CBUAE-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
