# Standards for Retention of Consumer Records

Sets retention and destruction standards for Consumer records: all Personal Data, documents, records and files must be securely retained for at least five years, running from the later of termination of the Business Relationship/account closure or completion of a casual transaction. Confidentiality and security standards continue after the relationship ends until destruction; data must not be processed or kept longer than necessary for its purpose, and after the mandatory period institutions must take reasonable steps to destroy or permanently delete it. Also covers the seven-business-day deadline to report Consumer-requested updates or corrections to Credit Information Agencies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
