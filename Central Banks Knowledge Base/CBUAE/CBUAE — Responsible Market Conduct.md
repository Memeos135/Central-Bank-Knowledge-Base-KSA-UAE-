# Responsible Market Conduct

Article 4 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation, setting responsible Market conduct duties for Licensed Financial Institutions. It requires upholding high ethical practices, prohibiting abusive sales, marketing and pricing practices, promoting disclosure, transparency and non-discriminatory competition, avoiding unreasonable barriers to consumer access, and ensuring independent control functions are competent in market conduct principles. Also carries adjacent business-conduct duties on staff ethics, competence, remuneration and institutional responsibility for staff actions.
