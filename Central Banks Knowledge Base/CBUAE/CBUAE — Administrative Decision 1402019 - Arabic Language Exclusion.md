# Administrative Decision 140/2019 - Arabic Language Exclusion

UAE Insurance Authority Administrative Decision No. 140 of 2019, issued under the Arabic-language requirement of Federal Law No. 6 of 2007, listing the classes of insurance policies exempt from being written in Arabic (marine and aviation hull, satellites/balloons/spaceships, oil insurance, and policies of an international nature requiring English). It also sets out the electronic product-licensing submission package insurers must file with the Authority for life/funds accumulation and property and liability products, including bilingual policy wordings, specification tables, application and claim forms, actuarial material and reinsurance arrangements. Binding on insurance companies operating in the State; notably it requires a certificate from the company's AML/CFT compliance officer assessing product-related money laundering risks and the internal controls managing them, plus an undertaking of legal compliance.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
