# Decree Law No. 20 of 2018

Instrument node for Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Financing of Illegal Organisations — the primary AML/CFT statute referenced throughout the CBUAE Guidelines, together with its defined terms (e.g. "Customer", "Decree-Law", "Decision"). It sets the minimum statutory duties on supervised institutions and underpins the criminal offences of ML, TF and financing of illegal organisations. Applies federally across the UAE, including financial and commercial free zones.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
