# IRRBB Regulation

The IRRBB Regulation is the CBUAE instrument requiring each Bank's Board to establish and oversee an interest rate and rate of return risk governance framework proportionate to the Bank's risk profile, nature, size, structure and complexity. It prescribes the framework's mandatory components — Board oversight, policies, ALCO or equivalent for larger/complex banks, measurement, monitoring and enforcement of limits, stress testing feeding decision-making, risk function oversight, internal audit assurance and reporting to Senior Management and Board — plus a Board-approved risk appetite statement and separation of duties between risk control and position-taking functions. It binds all Banks, with the Board bearing the onus of demonstrating an adequate approach; the accompanying Standards are enforceable in the same manner.
