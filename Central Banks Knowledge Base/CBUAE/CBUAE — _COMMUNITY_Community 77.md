# Payment Token Services

## Why this community

The payment token / stablecoin regime: licensing and permitted activities for token issuance, conversion, custody and transfer, with prudential capital, non-objection gating and outsourcing controls layered on top.

## How members connect

- The Payment Token Services Regulation is the parent instrument; conversion, custody/transfer and capital requirements are its dependent obligation sets, and algorithmic stablecoins are addressed as a defined (and scope-limited) category.
- Non-objection registration acts as a supervisory gate that conditions whether conversion activity may be performed — a prior-approval chain rather than a notification.
- Cross-reference to the Outsourcing Regulation imports the general third-party risk regime, so token service providers remain accountable for outsourced functions.
- Reference to a foreign regulator (UAE SCA) signals cross-border recognition/registration interaction and a jurisdictional scope limit that must be checked before relying on foreign authorisation.
