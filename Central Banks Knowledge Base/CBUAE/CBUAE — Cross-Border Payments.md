# Cross-Border Payments

Guidance section defining cross-border payments (originator and beneficiary in different countries, wholesale and retail) and the obligations of every institution in the payment chain, including intermediaries with no direct customer relationship, to screen, monitor and pass on originator and beneficiary information. It cites the AML-CFT Decision wire transfer articles and the AED 3,500 threshold for qualifying cross-border wire transfers, listing the minimum originator and beneficiary data elements, and notes stricter screening duties for exchange houses. Applies to UAE LFIs; relevant to KSA analysis only as a comparator on travel-rule style requirements.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS
