# Country and Transfer Risk Standards

CBUAE Country and Transfer Risk Standards (154/2018), a prudential instrument setting requirements for identifying, measuring, governing and provisioning for cross-border exposures. It requires a board-approved risk appetite statement addressing sovereign, contagion, currency, indirect country, liquidity and macroeconomic risk, a risk management function proportionate to exposure, stress-testing, information systems and internal and supervisory reporting. It binds banks supervised by CBUAE, with obligations scaled by materiality of country and transfer risk exposure.
