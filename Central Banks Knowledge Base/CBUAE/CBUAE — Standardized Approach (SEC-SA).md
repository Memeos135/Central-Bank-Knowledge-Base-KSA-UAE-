# Standardized Approach (SEC-SA)

Concept node for the Standardised Approach to securitisation exposures (SEC-SA) under the CBUAE securitisation capital standard, covering how a bank determines the exposure amount (including a 100% credit conversion factor for non-mitigant off-balance-sheet facilities) and then derives a risk weight, with the outcome driven by the parameters A, D and KA and a weighted-average formula where the attachment point falls below KA. It also carries the associated floors and constraints, notably the 15% risk-weight floor and the rule that an unrated junior exposure cannot attract a lower risk weight than the next more senior rated exposure. It binds CBUAE-licensed banks computing risk-weighted assets for securitisation positions held in the banking book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
