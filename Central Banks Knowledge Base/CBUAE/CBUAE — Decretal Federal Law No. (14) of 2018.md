# Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018

Reference node for the former UAE Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities, as amended), which established CBUAE's mandate, licensing and supervision of financial institutions and activities. It is cited in the recitals of Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 and is expressly repealed by that decree-law, alongside the 2023 Insurance Law. Relevant to anyone tracing legacy obligations of licensed financial institutions, transitional continuity of regulations/decisions/circulars, and the one-year position-reconciliation period.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, other
