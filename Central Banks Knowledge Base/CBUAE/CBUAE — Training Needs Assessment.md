# Training Needs Assessment

A concept node on documenting and assessing the AML/CFT/CPF training programme: an annual, board- or senior-management-approved training plan specifying participants, topics, delivery methods, objectives, minimum standards by department and role, frequency and post-training assessment. It also covers mandatory record-keeping of delivered training, tracking of attendance and pass/fail outcomes, follow-up and consequences for non-completion (up to termination), and measurement of training effectiveness. It applies to CBUAE-licensed financial institutions, including support functions such as IT and external staff (vendors, contractors, third parties).

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
