# Risk Assessment Framework

Sets out the self-assessment and risk-assessment reporting framework for UAE insurance companies and insurance-related professions, including the governance self-assessment form, the annual governance report, and the periodic risk and compliance self-assessment covering insurance, credit, market, operational, regulatory, related-party, financial crime, fraud and cyber risks. It also describes the AML risk-based approach: assessing ML/TF risk by customer type, products/services and technology, applying mitigation procedures, and classifying business relationship risk across at least four risk factors, plus minimum content of AML policies and procedures. Binds insurance companies and insurance-related professionals reporting to the Authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
