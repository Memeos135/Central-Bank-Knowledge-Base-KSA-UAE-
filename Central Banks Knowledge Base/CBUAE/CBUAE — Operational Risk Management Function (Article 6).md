# Operational Risk Management Function (Article 6)

Article 6 requires each LFI to maintain, within its risk management function, a dedicated Operational Risk management function that is adequately resourced, sufficiently independent, and for which the Chief Risk Officer is responsible and accountable. It sets the function's minimum mandate: forming an independent view of business units' material operational risks, controls and Risk Tolerance; challenging business-unit use of operational risk tools, measurement and reporting; owning operational risk policies and standards; monitoring and reporting the risk profile; identifying internal and external threats; assessing vulnerabilities of Critical Operations; and delivering training and awareness. The function must report its findings and recommendations to the Board regularly.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
