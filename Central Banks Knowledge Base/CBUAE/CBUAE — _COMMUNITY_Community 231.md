# Central Bank Reporting Duty

## Why this community

The standalone statutory duty to report specified matters to the central bank — a supervisory notification obligation distinct from AML suspicious transaction reporting to the FIU.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the article stands alone in the graph, with no internal cross-references extracted.
- Its practical significance is as a destination for obligation chains elsewhere (breach, incident, governance and dormancy events that must be escalated to the regulator rather than only recorded internally).
- When applying it, keep the recipient and trigger distinct from AML/CTF reporting duties, which run to the financial intelligence unit and carry tipping-off restrictions.
