# Displaced Commercial Risk

Displaced commercial risk is a defined term in the IRRBB Regulation: the market pressure on a Bank offering Islamic financial services to pay investment account holders a return exceeding the rate actually earned on the assets financing them, in order to attract or retain those funds. It is relevant to how Islamic Banks and Islamic Windows identify and manage rate of return risk within the IRRBB governance and measurement framework. The concept is definitional rather than a standalone obligation; the operative duties sit in the Regulation's governance, measurement and Islamic banking articles.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
