# STR Reporting

A concept node on the obligation of LFIs to file suspicious transaction reports with the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit where there are reasonable grounds to suspect that a transaction, attempted transaction or funds are linked to the proceeds of crime or to criminal use. It also flags non-exhaustive trigger scenarios (abandoned onboarding after CDD is explained, inability to evidence beneficial ownership or explain transactions) and, from the real-estate-focused source, indicator lists on payment means and transaction structure. Binding on CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; the reporting duty is statutory, not discretionary.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

### [[CBUAE — Transaction Monitoring|Transaction Monitoring]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Monitoring and suspicious-transaction reporting are two stages of one legal duty, so a gap in either breaks the chain. The monitoring material frames detection, alert triage and escalation as components of an end-to-end system whose output is an STR/SAR decision, and the reporting provisions anchor that duty in the AML/CFT Decision's requirement to monitor all customers for potentially suspicious behaviour and report it.
