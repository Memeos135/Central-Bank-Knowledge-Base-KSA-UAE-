# Best Practices for RBA and Institutional Risk Assessment

A CBUAE best-practices document on the risk-based approach and the institutional (enterprise-wide) AML/CFT/CPF risk assessment, setting expectations for how LFIs identify and rate inherent risk across customer, product/service, geographic, delivery-channel and other categories, and for consistency between the institutional assessment methodology and day-to-day risk rating. It addresses treatment of high-risk factors, granular risk factors, rationales for exclusions, and disclosure where system limitations (e.g. inability to capture exits for FCC reasons or STR/SAR volumes) elevate the inherent risk rating. It is guidance directed at CBUAE licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
