# IA Board Resolution No. 27 of 2020 - Licensing Insurance Producers

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 27 of 2020 issuing the Instructions for Licensing Insurance Producers, applying to all Insurance Producers operating in the UAE. It prohibits unlicensed/unregistered producer activity, bars companies from dealing with unlicensed producers, confines the producer role to marketing and lead referral (including via electronic links to the company or agent), restricts nationals/non-nationals by product class, prohibits combining the role with agent or broker activity or serving more than one company, and sets fitness, qualification, training and assessment conditions for licensing and registration.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
