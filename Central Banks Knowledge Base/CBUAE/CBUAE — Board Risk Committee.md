# Board Risk Committee

Node on the mandatory Board Risk Committee and the related Article 7 duties on risk management and internal controls. It requires a separate, non-merged committee chaired by an Independent Member with a majority of Independent Members experienced in risk management, and supports the company-wide (and group-wide) Risk Governance Framework, an independent risk management function with a direct reporting line to the Board or risk committee, and independent compliance and internal audit functions. It binds the Board, which owns design, approval and oversight of the framework and its alignment with Risk Appetite.
