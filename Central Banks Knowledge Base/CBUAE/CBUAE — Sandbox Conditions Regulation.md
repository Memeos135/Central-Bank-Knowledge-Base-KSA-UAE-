# Sandbox Conditions Regulation

CBUAE Sandbox Conditions Regulation (Circular 6/2023, effective 31/12/2023), issued under the Central Bank Law powers to exempt specified activities from the licensing requirement and to set conditions for supervised testing. It establishes the Regulatory Sandbox framework: eligibility, application process, minimum safeguards during testing, and exit routes, applied case by case under Central Bank discretion. Addressed to all Licensed Financial Institutions and to any person seeking to test innovative models, products or services relating to Licensed Financial Activities.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
