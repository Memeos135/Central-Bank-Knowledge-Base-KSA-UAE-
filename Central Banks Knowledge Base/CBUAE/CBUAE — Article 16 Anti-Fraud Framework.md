# Article 16: Anti-Fraud Framework

Article 16 requires a Licensed Person to maintain an anti-fraud framework covering prevention, detection, investigation and response to fraud incidents, in line with the Central Bank's Standards. Accountability is placed expressly on the Chief Executive Officer and the Board of Directors, or on the owner where the Licensed Person is a sole establishment. It applies to exchange houses licensed by the CBUAE and sits alongside the AML/CFT, internal audit and risk management obligations in the same regulation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
