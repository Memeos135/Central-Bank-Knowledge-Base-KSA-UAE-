# Article 22: AML/CFT Compliance

Article 22 requires a Licensed Person to comply with the applicable AML/CFT laws and regulations as amended, and to maintain comprehensive and effective internal AML/CFT policies, procedures and controls. It operates as the gateway provision linking the exchange business regime to the federal AML/CFT framework defined in Article 1 (the AML decree-law and its implementing cabinet decision, plus related instructions and guidance). It binds all CBUAE-licensed exchange businesses.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
