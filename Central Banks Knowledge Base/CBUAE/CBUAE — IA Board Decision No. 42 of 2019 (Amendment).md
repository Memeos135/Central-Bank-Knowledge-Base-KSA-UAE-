# IA Board Decision No. 42 of 2019 (Amendment)

Amending instrument (Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 42 of 2019) that modifies Board Decision No. 13 of 2018 on marketing insurance policies through banks, producing the consolidated bancassurance instructions as of 15/09/2019. The underlying regime conditions insurer–bank distribution arrangements: prior Authority approval of the marketing application and of the insurer–bank agreement, qualification and training conditions for the bank's Designated Officer, continuing training plans, and decision/appeal timelines and approval renewal. It binds licensed insurance companies (including Takaful) using banks as a distribution channel, and indirectly the participating banks and their designated staff.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk
