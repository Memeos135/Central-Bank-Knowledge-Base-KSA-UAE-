# Payment Systems & Shareholding

## Why this community

Licensing and ownership gatekeeping in payments and banking: authorisation of retail payment systems and stored-value facilities, settlement finality and netting protection, and regulatory control over significant or national shareholdings and minimum capital by licence type.

## How members connect

- Common legal anchor: the payment-system, stored-value, shareholding and restricted-licence instruments all cite the central bank law as their enabling authority.
- Designation as a gate: retail payment system designation triggers the regime under which settlement finality and netting protections operate.
- Ownership control chain: national shareholding rules cross-refer to the transfer of significant shareholding regime, so changes in control require prior regulatory clearance.
- Licence-scope calibration: restricted-licence banking sits against minimum capital and wholesale banking rules and is bounded by the high-net-worth customer definition, which limits permissible clientele.
