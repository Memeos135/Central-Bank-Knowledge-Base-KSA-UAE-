# Section 2.2 Transparency

Part of Article 2 (Disclosure & Transparency), covering advertising and marketing standards for Financial Products and/or Services and the product-specific advertisement rules that follow. It requires that forecasts of potential returns be labelled illustrative and non-indicative, be accompanied by a prominently displayed loss-risk warning and not be misleading when made; it exempts purely name-only or invitation-to-discuss material from the warning statements and obliges institutions to devise their own warnings where the prescribed ones do not fit their products. Deposit Product advertising quoting an interest/profit rate carries additional prescribed content; binds Licensed Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
