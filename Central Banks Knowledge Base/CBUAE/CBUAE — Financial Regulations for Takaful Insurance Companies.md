# Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies

The parent instrument: the Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies, issued by Insurance Authority Board Decision and now administered by CBUAE, covering investment of participants' rights, solvency margin and own funds, technical provisions, asset valuation, accounting policies and prescribed financial-statement and disclosure formats. Includes templates for gross and net contributions and claims, Re-Takaful cessions and accepted business. Applies to Takaful insurance companies incorporated in the State and foreign Takaful insurers licensed to operate there.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Insurance Authority|Insurance Authority]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the financial requirements to a takaful operator — solvency, provisions, asset and investment rules — the relevant obligations flow from decisions of the same Insurance Authority Board that governs the wider insurance regime, so the defined term for the authority is common across both instruments. That shared issuer means definitions, approval routes and reporting lines should be read consistently rather than as separate regimes. The consequence is that a takaful company's filings, approvals and remediation directions go to the same supervisor as conventional insurers, subject to verification of the current supervisory successor.
