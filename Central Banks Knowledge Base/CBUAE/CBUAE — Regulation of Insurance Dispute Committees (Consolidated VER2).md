# Regulation of Insurance Dispute Committees (Consolidated VER2)

Consolidated version 2 of the same regulation on committees for the settlement and resolution of insurance disputes, carrying the resolution reference IA-BOD-RES 33/2019. It restates definitions, general provisions (Arabic as the language of proceedings, interpreters, neutral hearing, evidentiary latitude), committee formation and membership terms, and competence/non-competence, and adds an exclusion for insurer subrogation claims against third parties causing loss. Relevant where a researcher needs the current consolidated text binding insurers and shaping complainants' procedural routes.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
