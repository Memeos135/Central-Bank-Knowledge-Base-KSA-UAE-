# Internal Shari'ah Control Division

Node covering the Internal Shari'ah Control Division or Section required in each IFI: a support function for the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, resourced in proportion to the institution's size and activities, technically supervised by the ISSC, headed by a person appointed to report to the Board. It must be organisationally separate from business divisions and kept distinct in reporting lines and staffing from the internal Shari'ah audit function, consistent with the three lines of defence. Binds IFIs, their boards and the heads of these functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
