# Money Laundering Phases

## Why this community

The conceptual core of the AML/CTF regime: what money laundering is, the predicate offences that generate criminal proceeds, the stages by which proceeds are laundered, and the adjacent offences of terrorist financing and proliferation financing.

## How members connect

- Definitional dependency: money laundering is defined by reference to proceeds of a predicate offence, so the predicate catalogue sets the perimeter of the offence and of reporting duties.
- The placement/layering/integration typology is the analytical frame used to characterise suspicion and to calibrate risk-based controls.
- Terrorist financing and proliferation financing sit alongside laundering as separate offences with overlapping detection and reporting obligations but different (often sanctions-driven) triggers.
- Falsely described goods and services links in as a trade-based red flag typology, principally for proliferation and sanctions evasion.
