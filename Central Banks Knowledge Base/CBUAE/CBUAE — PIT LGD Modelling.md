# PIT LGD Modelling

Section of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance requiring institutions to analyse the relationship between LGD and macroeconomic factors at an appropriate level of granularity and to let that analysis drive point-in-time LGD modelling strategy. It sets out four modelling options ranked by granularity (from macro-linked LGD parameters down to linking final LGD to obligor PD) and expectations on performance criteria, parameter justification, documentation and in-/out-of-sample evidence. Applies to institutions estimating IFRS 9 expected credit losses.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
