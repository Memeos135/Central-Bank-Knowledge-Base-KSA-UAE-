# Insurance Broker Definitions

## Why this community

Definitional scope for insurance brokerage, establishing who counts as an insurance broker and therefore who falls within the licensing and conduct obligations of the instrument.

## How members connect

- The definitions article is the gateway provision: the broker term takes its legal content from it, and all downstream duties in the instrument key off that status.
- Classification matters cross-regime, since broker status can trigger separate AML/CTF customer due diligence and consumer-protection conduct duties.
- Typical hierarchy: enabling insurance law -> implementing regulation whose Article 1 fixes the defined terms used throughout.
