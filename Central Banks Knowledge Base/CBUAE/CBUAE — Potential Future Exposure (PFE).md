# Potential Future Exposure (PFE)

A node within CBUAE prudential guidance keyed to Potential Future Exposure, but the surrounding material addresses conversion of off-balance-sheet items into exposure amounts: a mandatory 100% credit conversion factor for securitisation-related off-balance-sheet exposures (including undrawn servicer cash advances, where the Central Bank declined the Basel national discretion), the risk-weight treatment of overlapping exposures, and leverage ratio exposure-measure FAQs. It binds banks licensed by the UAE Central Bank when computing risk-weighted assets and the leverage ratio exposure measure. Useful for confirming how add-on/exposure components and CCFs are applied rather than for a definition of PFE itself.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
