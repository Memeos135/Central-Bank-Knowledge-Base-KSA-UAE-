# Wolfsberg Group

A reference/definition node identifying the Wolfsberg Group as an industry association of international banks that issues non-binding AML/CFT standards and guidance (e.g. correspondent banking questionnaires and due diligence principles), cited in the CBUAE Guidelines alongside FATF, FSRBs and the Egmont Group as an external benchmark. It creates no direct obligation; its relevance is interpretive and as a source of good-practice benchmarks for institutions and groups. Useful chiefly for definitional orientation and glossary look-ups.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
