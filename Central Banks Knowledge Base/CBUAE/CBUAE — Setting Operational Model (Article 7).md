# Setting Operational Model (Article 7)

Article 7 of the CBUAE Takaful Operational Model Standard requires any company applying to conduct Takaful business to set an operational model based on the Wakala model, covering property and liability insurance as well as insurance of persons and fund accumulation. The model must be approved in full by the Company's Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee before submission to the Central Bank and HSA, be known to relevant staff, be published on the Company's website, and set out contracting parties, contractual relationships and financial flows between Fund, Company, Participant and Beneficiary (feeding into Article 8). It binds Takaful licence applicants and licensed Takaful Insurance Companies.
