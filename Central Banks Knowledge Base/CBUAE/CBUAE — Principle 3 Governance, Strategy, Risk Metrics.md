# Principle 3: Governance, Strategy, Risk Metrics

Principle 3 of the UAE SFWG Disclosure Principles sets the expected minimum content of sustainability disclosures along the governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics and targets pillars, including board knowledge and oversight, senior management roles, transition plans, and short-, medium- and long-term effects on business model and financial results. Applies as an expectation to Reporting Entities under the member Authorities, subject to each Authority's implementation choices.
