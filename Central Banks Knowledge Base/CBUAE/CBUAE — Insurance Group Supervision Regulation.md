# Insurance Group Supervision Regulation

The CBUAE Insurance Group Supervision Regulation, which sets out when and how the Central Bank supervises insurance groups on a group-wide basis, including scope of application, identification of Holding Companies, group reporting, coordination arrangements and enforcement. It defines key terms (Company, Group, Parent, Subsidiary, Affiliate, Holding Company, External Auditor) by reference to the Central Bank Law and the Insurance Law. Binds Insurance and Reinsurance Companies incorporated in the State, their parents and holding companies, and other group entities required to supply data to the Parent.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other, banking prudential
