# Do Not Call Registry (DNCR)

Defines the Do Not Call Registry (DNCR) as the opt-out mechanism by which a Customer suspends receipt of telemarketing on a registered phone number. Licensed Financial Institutions are prohibited from telemarketing to any Customer who has opted out or rejected contact in any form, or whose number appears on the DNCR, and must publish an accessible guide on their official platforms explaining complaint and DNCR registration procedures. It also feeds disclosure duties during calls, where the Customer's DNCR right must be clearly stated.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
