# Standards for Exchange Business in the UAE v1.20

CBUAE Standards supplementing the 2014 Regulations on licensing and monitoring of Exchange Business, setting baseline requirements across licensing and continuing obligations, management and governance, risk management and security, AML compliance, and customer protection. Compliance is continuous, with non-compliance charges applied per the appendix schedule and ultimate responsibility placed on the board (or owner/partners) for adequate policies, systems and staffing. It binds all Exchange Houses (Licensed Persons) operating in the UAE, including new entrants.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk
