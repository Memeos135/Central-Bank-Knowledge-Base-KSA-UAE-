# Terms of Financing

An annexure schedule setting maximum permitted fees and commissions (in AED) that licensed institutions may charge retail customers across personal accounts, debit and credit cards, consumer, car and home loans/financing, remittances and term deposits. It caps items such as account closure, liability/no-liability letters, card replacement, late payment penalties, early/partial settlement (typically 1% subject to an absolute ceiling) and statement or certificate issuance. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions dealing with Consumers and operates as a hard pricing ceiling rather than guidance.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
