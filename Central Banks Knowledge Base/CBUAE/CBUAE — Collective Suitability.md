# Collective Suitability

Defines and imposes the Collective Suitability requirement: the Board as a whole must hold the combined experience, knowledge and skills to understand the Licensee's activities and risks, set strategy, exercise oversight and support constructive challenge, with diversity of skills, qualifications, background, age and gender factored into selection of Authorized Individuals. It binds Licensees (including insurance companies and insurance-related professions under the CBUAE fit-and-proper framework), which must support individual fit-and-proper assessments with a board-level suitability assessment and, where a deficiency is found, file a remediation action plan with the Central Bank. Related reporting duties require immediate notification of assessment results and of any circumstances that may adversely affect Collective Suitability.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
