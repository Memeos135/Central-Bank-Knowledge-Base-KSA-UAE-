# Federal Decree by Law No. 6 of 2025 Regarding the Central Bank

The full UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025 on the Central Bank, regulation of financial institutions and activities, and insurance business — the consolidated primary statute that replaces Decretal Federal Law 14/2018 and Federal Decree-Law 48/2023 (both expressly repealed), with a one-year transition for subject entities to reconcile their positions and continuity of existing regulations until replaced. It also addresses currency in digital form and confirms Virtual Assets are not Currency under the decree-law. Binds the Central Bank, all Licensed Financial Institutions and insurance-sector entities operating in the State.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, governance/risk, other
