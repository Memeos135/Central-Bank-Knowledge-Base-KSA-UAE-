# Independent Audit Function

Section 8.4 of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines, tied to AML-CFT Decision Article 20.6, covering the mandatory independent audit function that tests the adequacy and effectiveness of an FI's AML/CFT internal policies, controls and procedures. It addresses staffing, competence and proportionality to the FI's ML/FT risk, nature and size, and permits smaller FIs to source capability from qualified, regulated external providers while retaining internal oversight; it also sets out the expected audit scope (CDD policies, training adequacy, outsourced compliance functions, case management and STR processes). It binds all CBUAE-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
