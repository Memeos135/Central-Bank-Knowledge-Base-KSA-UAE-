# General CDD Measures (Insurance)

Node within CBUAE AML/CFT guidance for the insurance sector describing the sector's participants (insurers, reinsurers, tied agents, brokers, third-party administrators) and the general customer due diligence expectations applicable to them. It is important for scope: only life insurance and other investment-related (funds accumulation) products fall within the AML/CFT framework, while general property and liability insurance does not. Binds CBUAE-licensed insurance companies, reinsurers, agents and brokers conducting in-scope business.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
