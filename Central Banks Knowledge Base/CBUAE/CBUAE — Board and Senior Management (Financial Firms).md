# Board and Senior Management (Financial Firms)

Concept node for the roles of the board and senior management under the CBUAE climate risk Principles. It establishes that the board defines, approves and oversees climate risk appetite, remains ultimately accountable even where functions are delegated, and that both board and senior management must maintain sufficient knowledge, with training and capacity building for relevant staff. The Principles are structure-neutral: they do not mandate a particular board or management model, only clarity of roles and evidenced oversight.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
