# Protection Benefit Ratio

Defined metric used to test how much genuine protection a savings product delivers, calculated as the present value of protection benefit payouts divided by the present value of premiums over the policy term, and certified by the Actuary. Where the ratio falls below 10% for any age, gender, smoker status, premium payment term or policy term, the product may only be marketed with sufficient client warnings, including a bold red disclosure that protection benefit is limited or absent, signed by the client directly beneath it. Binds companies marketing savings products and their distribution channels.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
