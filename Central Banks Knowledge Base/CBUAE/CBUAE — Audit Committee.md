# Audit Committee

Concept node on the Audit Committee in the insurance governance framework, as referenced across the Financial Regulations and related CBUAE instruments. It is the board-level body to which an internal audit function must report independently, and which works with the board-level Investment Committee to determine the scope of audit procedures covering investment activities and identifying internal control and system deficiencies. It binds the boards of licensed insurance companies, with an equivalent body required in the governance structure of foreign insurance company branches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
