# Disclosure and Transparency

Section 13 of the CBUAE Corporate Governance Standards for Banks (83/2019), specifying the minimum content of a bank's annual corporate governance statement, including governance structures, major shareholdings and voting rights, Related Party Transactions, Board recruitment and diversity (including female representation), Board and Senior Management qualifications and individual compensation, committee composition and meeting frequency, risk exposures and strategies, and Shari'ah reporting for Islamic banking. The node also carries the associated defined terms (Related Parties, Related Party Transactions, Pillar 3, Material Risk Takers, Risk Appetite). It binds all banks subject to the Corporate Governance Regulation and Standards.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Consumer Protection Regulation|Consumer Protection Regulation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When drafting or reviewing a risk disclosure, you cannot treat the disclosure rule as self-contained: it expressly points back to the Consumer Protection Regulation as the benchmark for what protections the Consumer is being asked to waive. The disclosure obligation therefore borrows the defined term "Consumer" and the protective baseline from that regulation, making the two instruments a single obligation chain rather than parallel requirements. Practically, a disclosure that lists financial risks but does not summarise the specific regulatory protections being foregone is incomplete, and the adequacy test must be run against the consumer protection text itself.
