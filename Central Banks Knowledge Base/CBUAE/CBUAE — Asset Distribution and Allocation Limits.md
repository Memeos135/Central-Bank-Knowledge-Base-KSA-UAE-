# Asset Distribution and Allocation Limits

Sets quantitative concentration and asset allocation limits for insurance company investments, with carve-outs for Statutory Deposits and unit-linked funds, application to UAE-policy insurance fund assets for branches of Foreign Insurance Companies, and a rating benchmark for what counts as strong/very strong. It also fixes transitional compliance periods (three years for real estate limits, two years for other limits) and frames Investment Risk (market, credit quality, concentration, liquidity, derivatives) as a Board responsibility.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
