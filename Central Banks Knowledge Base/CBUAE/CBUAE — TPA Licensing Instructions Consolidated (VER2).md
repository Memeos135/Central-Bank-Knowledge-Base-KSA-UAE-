# TPA Licensing Instructions Consolidated (VER2)

Consolidated licensing instructions for Health Insurance Third Party Administrators (IA Board Resolution 9/2011, now within the CBUAE insurance supervisory perimeter), covering permitted TPA activities, licensing conditions, conduct and record-keeping duties, and the supervisory sanctions ladder. It binds licensed TPA companies and shapes the contractual allocation of duties between them and the insurance companies they act for, including joint responsibility toward third parties and liability for associates' fraud, forgery or abuse. Relevant to anyone assessing whether an outsourced claims administrator is properly licensed, what records it must keep (minimum five years), and what happens on suspension, cancellation or termination of the TPA agreement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
