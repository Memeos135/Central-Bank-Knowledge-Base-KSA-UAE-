# Stored Value Facilities Regulation

## Why this community

Prudential, governance and conduct regime for Stored Value Facilities (SVFs) as a licensed payment activity: what an SVF is, how customer float must be held and safeguarded, and the risk-management, technology, conduct and enforcement duties attaching to the licensee.

## How members connect

- Definitional spine: the SVF Regulation defines Stored Value Facility by reference to 'money's worth', which scopes every downstream obligation including clearing and settlement arrangements.
- Obligation chain within one instrument: the Regulation's articles cascade from general risk management and internal control, through information and accounting systems, float management and safekeeping, to technology and specific risk management.
- The technology risk article acts as a parent for the specific sub-disciplines — cyber resilience, payment security, business continuity and reputation risk — so each is read as a component duty, not a standalone rule.
- Cross-regime hooks: the Regulation pulls in AML/CFT procedures (customer due diligence and monitoring applied to SVF holders) and consumer-protection/business-conduct duties, with Article 15 supplying the supervisory enforcement and sanctions consequence for breach of any of the above.
