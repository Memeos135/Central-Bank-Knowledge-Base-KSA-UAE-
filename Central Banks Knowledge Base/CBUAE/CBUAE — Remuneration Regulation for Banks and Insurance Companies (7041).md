# Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies (7041)

A second version/record of the same CBUAE Circular No. 5/2026 Remuneration Regulation for Banks and Insurance Companies, with identical introduction, objective and scope provisions. It sets minimum requirements for remuneration frameworks that incentivise prudent risk management and impose consequences for poor risk outcomes, binding all Banks and Insurance/Reinsurance Companies. Open only if version comparison with the 7031 record is needed.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
