# Customer Complaints Process

Chapter 17 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets the minimum complaint-handling requirements binding on a Licensed Person (exchange house): documented resolution procedures, a dedicated complaints phone number and email displayed on receipts and in premises, and a designated head-office complaints officer who records, resolves and periodically reports complaints to the Manager in Charge. It allows the complaints role to be combined with another suitable function subject to conditions elsewhere in the Standards, creating a link to compliance-function segregation rules. The surrounding context also carries the KYC chapter's structure (CID, CDD, EDD tiers and the duty not to onboard or transact where due diligence cannot be completed).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF
