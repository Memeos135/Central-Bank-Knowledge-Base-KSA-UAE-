# CDD and EDD Measures

Covers customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence expectations in the counter-proliferation-financing context, particularly for correspondent banking relationships where the correspondent lacks direct knowledge of the respondent's underlying customers. It also treats operational risk in CPF compliance (staffing, screening and risk-rating systems, monitoring and CDD refresh backlogs, training gaps) and the requirement to establish mitigating policies, procedures and processes. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions, especially those offering correspondent services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk, banking prudential
