# AML-CFT Decision (Cabinet Decision 10/2019)

Concept node for Cabinet Decision 10/2019, the Implementing Regulation of Federal Decree-Law 20/2018, which converts the AML/CTF statute into operational obligations. It is the source of detailed requirements on CDD and beneficial ownership, the risk-based approach, appointment and independence of the compliance officer (Article 21), and application of targeted financial sanctions directives (Article 60). It binds licensed financial institutions and DNFBPs, and is cross-referenced throughout CBUAE sectoral guidance and Standards.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Risk-Based Approach|Risk-Based Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For any decision about how much due diligence or monitoring is enough, the risk-based approach is legally anchored in the AML-CFT Law and Decision rather than being a discretionary methodology the institution may adopt or drop. That means customer risk rating and the institutional risk assessment are the mechanism by which the Decision's identification-and-assessment obligation is satisfied, and the institution bears the burden of showing its risk judgements are documented and defensible. A firm cannot justify reduced measures by pointing to the RBA alone; it must show the RBA outputs trace back to the assessment duties in the primary text.

### [[CBUAE — Standard Customer Due Diligence|Standard Customer Due Diligence]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Standard CDD is the baseline the risk-based approach modulates, not an alternative to it: the Decision fixes both the required CDD elements and the timing (before or during establishment of the relationship, or before an occasional transaction). For a decision on whether onboarding may proceed, the risk rating can justify enhanced or simplified intensity but cannot displace the baseline measures or the timing rule set in the Decision. Any deviation therefore needs to be grounded in an express provision of the primary text, not in a general appeal to risk.
