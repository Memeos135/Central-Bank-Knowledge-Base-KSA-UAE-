# Quantifying Control Effectiveness

Methodological section of the CBUAE risk-based approach guidance setting out a six-plus step approach for LFIs to quantify AML/CFT/CPF control effectiveness: identify key controls, define measurable effectiveness criteria, measure performance, apply a scoring scale, aggregate (optionally weighted) scores, and adjust for interdependencies between controls. It is expectation-setting guidance on how residual risk is derived from inherent risk, not a prescriptive rule. Applies to licensed financial institutions conducting enterprise-wide ML/TF/PF risk assessments.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
