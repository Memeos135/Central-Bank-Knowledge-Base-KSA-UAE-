# Wolfsberg/ICC/BAFT Trade Finance Principles

A concept node citing the Wolfsberg Group, International Chamber of Commerce and BAFT trade finance principles as an external reference point in the CBUAE TBML guidance. It is used to establish that open account trade accounts for roughly 80 percent of international trade, framing the guidance's distinction between documentary and non-documentary trade finance and the differing controls expected by product type and LFI role. Persuasive industry standard material rather than a binding instrument, but informs supervisory expectations on LFIs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
