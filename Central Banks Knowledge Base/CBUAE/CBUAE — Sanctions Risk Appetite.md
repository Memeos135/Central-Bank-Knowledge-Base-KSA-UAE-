# Sanctions Risk Appetite

Concept node requiring LFIs to maintain a written, senior-management-approved sanctions risk appetite embedded in policies, procedures and screening parameters. It must state which sanctions regimes apply (UNSCR, OFAC, EU, UK), the treatment of interests owned or controlled 50% or more by a Listed Person, the approach to unilateral and extra-territorial/secondary sanctions exposure, and the handling of alias, vessel and paper-instrument names; deviations must be documented and approved. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions and shades into the internal controls requirement under Cabinet Decision 74.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
