# Determination

Defines the written outcome (upheld, partially upheld, or rejected) that the CBUAE Ombudsman Unit — and, on referral, the Appeals Committee or Insurance Dispute Resolution Committee — issues on a consumer Complaint, together with the grounds on which a Complaint may be upheld (breach of Central Bank regulations, deceptive/misleading/unfair conduct, improper motive or irrelevant grounds, inadequate explanation from the institution, prior unlawfulness finding, or breach of Consumer Protection Regulation principles). It also sets the appeal route: either party may refer a Determination for review within 30 complete business days, failing which the Determination becomes final and enforceable. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions, Insurance Companies and Complainants party to the dispute.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
