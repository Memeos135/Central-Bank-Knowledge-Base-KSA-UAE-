# AML/CFT Guidance for Licensed Exchange Houses

CBUAE guidance setting out supervisory expectations for Licensed Exchange Houses on their AML/CFT statutory obligations, issued under the Article 44.11 mandate of Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 (implementing Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018). It covers sector ML/FT risks, enterprise risk assessment, governance and compliance officer, CDD/EDD, transaction monitoring, sanctions, training, independent audit and record keeping, cross-referring to Chapter 16 of the Standards for the Regulations Regarding Licensing and Monitoring for Exchange Business. It is guidance, not new legislation: it does not override the legal framework, but LEH are expected to demonstrate compliance against it, subject to proportionality.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
