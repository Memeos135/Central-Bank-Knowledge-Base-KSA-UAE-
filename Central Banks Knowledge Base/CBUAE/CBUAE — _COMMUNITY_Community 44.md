# Beneficial Ownership Procedures

## Why this community

Identification, recording and registry-filing of beneficial owners of legal persons and legal arrangements as an AML/CFT control, and the parallel duties of registrars and of financial institutions doing CDD on corporate customers. Note the instruments here are UAE federal texts, so they are comparative/cross-border reference points rather than SAMA rules.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the primary AML/CFT decree-law down to Cabinet-level beneficial owner procedures and then to supervisory guidance for financial institutions serving legal persons and arrangements.
- All members turn on the same defined term, Beneficial Owner, plus the scoping terms Legal Arrangements and Registrar, which fix who must disclose and who must maintain the register.
- The later Cabinet decision on beneficial owner procedures supersedes/updates the earlier one while continuing to cite the AML/CFT decree-law as its enabling authority.
- Two obligation chains intersect: the entity/registrar disclosure-and-filing duty, and the institution's CDD duty to verify ownership and control - registry data supports but does not discharge CDD.
