# Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Regulation

Orientation node for the CBUAE Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Regulation and its Standards (161/2018), the framework governing a bank's internal control system and its second- and third-line functions. It covers the mandate, independence, competence and reporting lines of the internal audit function, the required charter content, coordination with the external auditor, and group-wide coverage of all activities and controlled entities. It binds banks operating in the UAE, their boards, board audit committees and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
