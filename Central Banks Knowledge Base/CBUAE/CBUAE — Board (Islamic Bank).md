# Board (Islamic Bank)

This node covers the Board's duties under the CBUAE Profit Equalization Standard for Islamic Banks: ensuring profit-equalization policies, procedures and controls exist, are periodically updated and are approved by the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee; approving investment and displaced-commercial-risk strategies including DCR limits and tolerance, profit-forgoing mechanisms and reserve appropriations; forming the independent reserves committee; and reporting periodically to Board committees, the ISSC and the Central Bank. It binds the boards of all Islamic Banks and banks housing Islamic Windows, on a solo and group-wide basis.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
