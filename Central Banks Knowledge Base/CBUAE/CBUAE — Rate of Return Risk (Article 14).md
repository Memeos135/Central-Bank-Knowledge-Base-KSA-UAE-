# Rate of Return Risk (Article 14)

Node for the rate of return risk article of the CBUAE Islamic banks risk management Standard, to be read together with the Interest Rate and Rate of Return Risk in the Banking Book Regulation and Standards (Circular 165/2018). It requires Islamic banks to maintain a comprehensive risk management and reporting process assessing how market factors affect returns on assets relative to returns expected by Investment Account Holders. Applies to Islamic banks and Islamic Windows; the risk must be covered in the risk appetite statement and reported to the Board and Senior Management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
