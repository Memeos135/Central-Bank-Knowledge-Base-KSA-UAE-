# Documentary Trade Finance

Defines documentary trade finance within the CBUAE TBML guidance: performance-based products such as letters of credit and documentary collections, and non-performance products such as guarantees and standby LCs, together with the document set (invoice, packing list, bill of lading, underlying contract, other transport documents) that LFIs collect and examine. The distinction matters because control expectations differ by product type, available information and the LFI's role in the transaction. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions calibrating trade-transaction controls.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
