# Foreign Judgment Execution

## Why this community

Recognition and execution in KSA of foreign judgments, including confiscation and asset-related orders, and the substantive conditions that must be satisfied before execution is granted.

## How members connect

- Article 68 expressly qualifies Article 67: the enabling power to execute is subject to the enumerated conditions - a classic rule/proviso pair.
- Both sit downstream of the judicial cooperation gateway; a foreign judgment typically arrives through those channels.
- Consequence for practice: assets frozen or seized on foreign request cannot be finally disposed of unless the Article 68 conditions are met.
