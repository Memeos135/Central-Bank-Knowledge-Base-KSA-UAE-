# Sanctions & Payments Screening

## Why this community

Sanctions and TFS screening obligations across the customer lifecycle and the payment message, including the lists to be screened against, disposition of false positives, and detection of evasion typologies.

## How members connect

- 'Screening Operations' is the umbrella obligation; customer screening, payments screening, false-positive verification and evasion detection are its component duties.
- List sources (UN Consolidated List, local terrorist list) define the screening universe — the scope of the lists determines the scope of the obligation, and omission of a list is itself a breach.
- Customer screening sits at the CDD/onboarding and ongoing-monitoring interface, while payments screening attaches to transaction execution; the same name-match logic produces different consequences (refuse relationship vs reject/hold transfer).
- False-positive verification is the control that makes discounting an alert defensible; evasion typologies extend the duty beyond literal name matching.
