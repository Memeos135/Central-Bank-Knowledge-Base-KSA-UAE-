# Annual Plan

Article 6 of the CBUAE Shari'ah Compliance Function standard, making the SCF responsible for developing the annual review plan ("Annual Plan"). The plan requires approval by both the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC) and the Board, establishing a dual governance sign-off. It binds Islamic Financial Institutions and their Shari'ah compliance functions, and sits as the first of the mandated review stages under Article 5.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
