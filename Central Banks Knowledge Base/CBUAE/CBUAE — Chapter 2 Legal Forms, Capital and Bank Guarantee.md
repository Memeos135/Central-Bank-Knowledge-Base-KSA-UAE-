# Chapter 2: Legal Forms, Capital and Bank Guarantee

Chapter of the Exchange Business Standards addressing permitted legal forms, licence categories (A, B and C, distinguished by permitted activities) and the related minimum paid-up capital and bank guarantee to be maintained in favour of the Central Bank, calculated by licence category, legal form and remittance volumes. The supplied extract also surfaces onboarding CDD/EDD requirements for legal persons and legal arrangements — beneficial owner identification, authorised signatories and representatives, expected activity and sanctions screening — so check which chapter the operative text sits in. It binds Licensed Persons and new licence applicants.
