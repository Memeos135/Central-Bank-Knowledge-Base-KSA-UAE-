# Mudaraba (Articles 12, 26)

Mudaraba appears twice in CBUAE Regulation 1/2026: Article 12, on applying mudaraba or investment wakala rules to investment deposits and investment accounts (Chapter Three), and Article 26, on banking mudaraba as a financing form (Chapter Four). Related deposit-side articles address commingling of investment deposit funds with institution funds, allocation of expenses, account statements and investment deposit assets. It binds Islamic financial institutions in structuring investment accounts and mudaraba-based financing, and is central to profit-sharing and loss-allocation questions (read with the defined terms for infringement/ta'addi and negligence/taqsir).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
