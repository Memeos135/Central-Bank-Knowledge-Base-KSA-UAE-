# Simple, Transparent, Comparable (STC) Securitisation

Concept node for Simple, Transparent and Comparable (STC) securitisation, the qualifying category that permits alternative (more favourable) regulatory capital treatment where the criteria in the standard's appendices are met. It sets out scope exclusions (synthetic securitisations, securitisations of revolving credit facilities and resecuritisations are not STC-compliant), the originator/sponsor disclosure duty, the investor's own obligation to assess STC status, and the requirement that the criteria be satisfied on a continuing basis. Binds originators, sponsors and investing banks under CBUAE supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
