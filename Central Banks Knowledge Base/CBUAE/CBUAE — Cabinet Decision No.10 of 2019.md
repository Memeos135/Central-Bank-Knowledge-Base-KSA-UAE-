# Cabinet Decision No.10 of 2019

Reference node for Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019, the Implementing Regulation of Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 on AML/CFT and financing of illegal organisations, as amended by Cabinet Decision No. (24) of 2022 (the "AML-CFT Decision"). It is cited as part of the legal basis for the CBUAE best-practices guidance, alongside Cabinet Decision 74 of 2020 (terrorism lists/UNSC TFS) and Cabinet Decision 58 of 2020 (beneficial owner procedures). It binds all UAE financial institutions and designated entities within scope of the AML-CFT Law, including CBUAE licensees.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
