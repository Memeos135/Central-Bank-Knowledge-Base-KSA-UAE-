# Product Approval Framework

Concept node for the Board-approved product approval framework and the associated product-level disclosure duties, with the extracted content focused on structured investment products and Shari'ah compliant structured products. It requires pre-sale disclosure of the issuing entity and its regulator, allocation of responsibilities between the Consumer's Licensed Financial Institution and the issuer, disclosure of Third Party involvement, suitability assessment of the target Consumer segment before advertising, and prescribed capital-loss warning wording in advertising. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, including Islamic Financial Institutions offering such products.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
