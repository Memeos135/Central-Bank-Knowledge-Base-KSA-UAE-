# Stored Value Facilities (SVF) Regulation

The Stored Value Facilities Regulation is the licensing and supervisory framework for SVF issuance, replacing the earlier stored value and electronic payment systems framework and grounded in the Central Bank Law provisions on licensed financial activities and supervisory powers. It sets definitions (including Money's Worth, device-based and non-device-based SVF, single-purpose SVF, Operating Rules, Senior Management) and licensing plus ongoing conduct, governance and AML-related obligations. It binds providers/issuers of stored value facilities in the State, excluding Financial Free Zones.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection
