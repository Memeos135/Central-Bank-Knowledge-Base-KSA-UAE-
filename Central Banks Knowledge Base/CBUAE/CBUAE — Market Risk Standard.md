# Market Risk Standard

Node covering the market risk elements of the CBUAE capital standards, including the separate specific-risk and general-market-risk capital charges for equity positions (8% of gross equity positions and 8% of the overall net position respectively), market-by-market netting, and the treatment of equity derivatives, stock indices and index arbitrage. It also sits alongside the Pillar 2 requirement that market risk be addressed in the bank's comprehensive risk assessment. Binds banks licensed in the UAE holding trading-book and equity-price-sensitive positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** The practical decision is which regime captures a given interest rate exposure: the trading-book interest rate rules feed the market risk capital calculation, which must include interest rate derivatives and off-balance-sheet instruments sensitive to rate moves, whereas interest rate risk in the banking book is addressed through supervisory review rather than that Pillar 1 charge. ### [[CBUAE — Pillar 2 - ICAAP|Pillar 2 - ICAAP]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Meeting the minimum market risk capital calculation does not close the assessment: risks addressed under supervisory review, including banking book interest rate risk and residual and concentration exposures, must also be captured in the institution's internal capital adequacy assessment. The market risk standard and the Pillar 2 framework operate as a chain, with Pillar 1 setting a floor and Pillar 2 requiring the institution to justify overall adequacy to the supervisor. Note also the sequencing: the Pillar 2 standard is described as already in effect while other standards phase in later, so the applicable obligation on any given date depends on the commencement position, which should be verified.
