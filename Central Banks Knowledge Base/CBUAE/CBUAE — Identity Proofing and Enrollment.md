# Identity Proofing and Enrollment

Section of the CBUAE digital ID guidance dealing with the identity proofing and enrollment stage: presence, resolution, evidence, validation and verification factors, illustrated against no/high/very high assurance tiers adapted from NIST digital ID guidelines. No specific assurance level is mandated; the LFI must assess and justify what level is appropriate given its ML, TF, fraud and other illicit financing risks. It also identifies the threat landscape — stolen or counterfeit identity evidence, impersonation and synthetic identities — that the proofing stage must withstand.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, data/credit info
