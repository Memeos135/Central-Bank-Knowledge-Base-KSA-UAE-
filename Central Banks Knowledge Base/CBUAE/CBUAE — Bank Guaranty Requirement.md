# Bank Guaranty Requirement

Sub-node within the TPA Licensing Instructions dealing with the financial entry conditions for health insurance third party administration, including corporate form, minimum paid-up capital of AED 5 million, exclusivity of business purpose, and the associated security/guarantee arrangements required of applicants. It binds companies seeking or holding a TPA licence, and failure to continue satisfying these conditions is an express ground for licence cancellation. Useful when checking ongoing eligibility rather than one-off authorisation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
