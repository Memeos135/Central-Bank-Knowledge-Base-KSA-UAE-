# Chapter 12: Counterfeit Currency Reporting

Chapter 12 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business obliges each Licensed Person to maintain procedures, equipment and training to detect counterfeit currency and to report incidents. Procedures must be approved by the Manager in Charge and reviewed annually; every licensed premises needs counterfeit detection machines programmed for at least five major currencies including the local currency, plus UV lamps and regular software updates, with counterfeit-detection training twice in an employee's first year and annually thereafter. Reporting duties run to the police of the relevant Emirate, to the FID as a fraud case via the STR system, and to the Banking Supervision Department on the Counterfeit Incident Reporting (CIR) Form — immediately for all local currency cases regardless of value, and for foreign currency cases at or above AED 36,000 per person (single or multiple transactions); a counterfeit register must be kept and reviewed annually.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
