# Operational Risk Circular

## Why this community

Transitional and instrument-hierarchy effects of the operational risk regime — how a circular issues the rules and what earlier supervisory notices it displaces.

## How members connect

- The circular is the issuing instrument; the cancellation article is the repeal clause inside the rules it issues, so they must be read together to fix the applicable text.
- The linkage answers a dating question: which obligations were superseded and from when, relevant to legacy policies and supervisory findings.
- Illustrates the law → regulation → circular hierarchy: prior notices remain relevant only for conduct before the cancellation takes effect.
