# Open Finance Provider

This node defines the Open Finance Provider, the holder of the new Open Finance Licence category created by the Regulation, permitting Data Sharing and/or Service Initiation (either or both). The licence is scope-limited: it does not authorise any other licensed activity, does not permit giving Advice, arranging or mediating Transactions in licensed activities, or holding customer funds, so separate licences must be obtained for those. It is distinguished from Persons Deemed Licensed (certain Licensee categories under Article 3) and from Technical Service Providers, who need no licence but cannot assume the provider's regulatory liability.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, governance/risk
