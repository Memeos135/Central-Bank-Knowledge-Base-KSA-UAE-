# Beneficial Owner

A defined-term node for Beneficial Owner as used across multiple CBUAE AML/CFT guidance documents, covering the duty to identify and verify customers together with beneficial owners, beneficiaries and controlling persons — whether natural persons, legal persons or legal arrangements — using reliable and independent sources. Timing, extent and method of verification vary with customer type and risk level, and enhanced measures (sanctions and adverse-media screening, more rigorous verification) apply to high-risk customers or beneficial owners. It binds supervised financial institutions and references the UAE beneficial-owner procedures resolution.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
