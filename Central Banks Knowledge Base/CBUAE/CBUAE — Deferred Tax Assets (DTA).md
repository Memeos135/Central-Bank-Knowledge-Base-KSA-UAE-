# Deferred Tax Assets (DTA)

Explains the regulatory capital treatment of deferred tax assets: those arising from net losses carried forward are deducted in full from CET1, while DTAs that rely on future profitability and arise from temporary timing differences fall under the threshold deduction regime rather than full deduction. Deductions are taken net of associated deferred tax liabilities where permitted. Applies to banks calculating regulatory capital and RWA under the CBUAE capital adequacy standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
