# AML/CFT/PF Federal Law

## Why this community

The primary AML/CFT/PF statutory framework: the criminalisation of money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, and the national governance bodies that set policy and assess risk.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy is explicit: the 2025 Decree Law is the parent instrument, repealing and replacing the 2018 Decree Law, and all offence articles and committees derive their authority from it.
- Definitional chain: the money laundering, TF and PF offence articles supply the predicate concepts that downstream CDD, reporting and sanctions obligations depend on.
- Institutional chain: the Supreme Committee and National Committee are creatures of the Law, with the National Committee owning the National Risk Assessment that drives the risk-based approach for supervised entities.
- Transitional issue: references in older guidance to the repealed 2018 Law must be read against the 2025 text before being relied on.
