# LCA Internal Controls (Article 7)

Article 7 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation requires adequate internal controls, segregation of duties within the organisational structure, and documented policies, authorities and procedures. It also subjects material outsourcing arrangements to prior Central Bank approval and defines materiality by reference to disruption, insolvency or detrimental impact on the ability to serve clients. Binds licensed crowdfunding companies conducting LCAs in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
