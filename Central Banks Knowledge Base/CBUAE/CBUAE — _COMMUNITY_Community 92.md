# Model Risk Management

## Why this community

Governance of models used for regulatory and risk measurement purposes, covering identification of model risk, independent validation, escalation of findings and remediation accountability.

## How members connect

- SAMA's model management standards are the governing instrument; model risk is the defined risk category the standards exist to control.
- The CRO is the named accountable function, creating a personal/functional ownership link between governance roles and model risk outcomes.
- Validation findings trigger a defined remediation chain — reporting, tracking and closure — making the two members sequential steps in one obligation.
- IRRBB is included as a substantive risk area whose measurement models fall expressly within the model management standards, showing cross-regime application.
