# Attachment and Detachment Points

Concept node for the attachment point (A) and detachment point (D) of a tranche, the inputs that define where a securitisation or CDO tranche sits in the loss waterfall. In the CBUAE standard these parameters drive SEC-SA risk weighting, the tranche-thickness adjustment under SEC-ERBA, the decomposition of tranched credit protection into sub-tranches, and the supervisory delta adjustment for CDO tranche derivatives under the counterparty credit risk calculation. Relevant to banks calculating capital for securitisation positions and for securitisation-related derivatives.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
