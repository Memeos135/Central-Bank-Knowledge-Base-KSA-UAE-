# Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs)

Covers Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions as a defined AML/CFT customer category (per the AML-CFT Decision) and as reporting entities to the UAE FIU. Under the Standards, a Licensed Person must be able to identify when a customer is a DNFBP and must apply enhanced due diligence before entering a business relationship or processing transactions, including verifying that the customer is supervised by the appropriate DNFBP supervisor and that it complies with applicable AML/CFT preventive measures. Relevant to KYB/customer-classification decisions for exchange houses.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
