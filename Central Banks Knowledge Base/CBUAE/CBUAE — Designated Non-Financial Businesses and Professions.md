# Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions

A defined category under the Executive Regulations covering Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions, which are subject to the same core AML/CFT obligations as Financial Institutions and VASPs. The Regulations require DNFBPs to apply customer due diligence to customers and pre-existing business relationships, prohibit establishing or continuing a relationship or executing a transaction where CDD cannot be applied, allow tipping-off-sensitive abstention with a suspicious transaction report, and bar dealings with shell banks. It binds non-financial designated sectors under the supervision of their respective Supervisory Authority.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
