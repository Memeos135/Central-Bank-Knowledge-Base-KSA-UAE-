# Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures

A concept/cross-reference node to the international Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (CPMI-IOSCO), which the CBUAE Open Finance regulation requires the API Hub to observe as to the relevant controls, alongside business continuity, data protection and data residency, and consent management duties. It functions as an imported external standard rather than a CBUAE instrument. Binds the licensed and supervised API Hub operator.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
