# Sanctions Screening

Covers targeted financial sanctions screening duties for Licensed Persons: written escalation and clearing procedures for potential matches, five-year retention of clearing logs from transaction receipt, and immediate (preferably automated) updating of the UN Consolidated List and the UAE Local Terrorist List in point-of-sale and core systems, with registration on the Executive Office portal for listing/de-listing alerts. On a confirmed match the Licensed Person must freeze funds immediately, without delay and without prior notice. Sits alongside transaction monitoring obligations and feeds reporting duties to the FIU, Central Bank and Executive Office.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
