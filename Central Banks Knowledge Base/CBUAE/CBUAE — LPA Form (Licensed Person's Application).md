# LPA Form (Licensed Person's Application)

Node for a Licensed Person application/authorisation form under the exchange-business Standards; the attached material describes the authorisation process (formal signed application letter, minimum qualification and competency requirements for the requested position, full disclosure duty, required documents, possible candidate interview and a 60-day notification of approval, conditional approval or refusal). The same extract also carries targeted financial sanctions reporting duties: Fund Freeze Reports for confirmed matches within two business days and Partial Name Match Reports for potential matches via goAML, with suspension maintained pending instructions. Binds Licensed Persons dealing with the Central Bank on approvals and on TFS reporting to the Central Bank and the Executive Office.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, sanctions/TFS, other
