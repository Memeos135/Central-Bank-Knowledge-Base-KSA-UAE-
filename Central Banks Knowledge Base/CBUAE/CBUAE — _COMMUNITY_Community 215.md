# Information Gathering Powers

## Why this community

Supervisory access rights allowing SAMA to require information beyond routine reporting, supporting ongoing monitoring and investigation.

## How members connect

- Feeds the enforcement article: information obtained under this power underpins any subsequent supervisory measure.
- Complements periodic reporting duties elsewhere in the instrument by adding an ad hoc, on-demand layer.
- Scope note: the power is exercisable against the licensed/registered entity, so outsourcing and third-party arrangements must be structured so the entity can still produce the information.
