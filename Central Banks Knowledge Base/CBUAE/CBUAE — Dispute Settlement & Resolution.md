# Dispute Settlement & Resolution

Concept node distinguishing Dispute Settlement (reconciliatory resolution between the parties) from Dispute Resolution (binding decision of the committee), and mapping the two-stage process. It covers the reconciliation phase, its fifteen-working-day deadline extendable by consent or by the committee chairman, attestation of a deed of reconciliation, escalation to resolution where reconciliation fails, and session procedures including consequences of a party's unexcused absence (deletion of the dispute, re-registration within thirty days, or decision in absentia).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
