# Specialized Banks with Low Risk Regulation

This is the CBUAE Specialized Banks with Low Risk Regulation (Circular 21/2022, effective 10/01/2022), issued under the Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018), creating a dedicated licensing and supervisory framework for a restricted-scope bank category. It sets out licensing, permitted activities, deposit-taking and funding caps, capital, liquidity, credit exposure, governance/AML/consumer-protection compliance and credit-reporting duties, aimed at protecting depositors and consumers and preserving financial stability. It binds all Specialized Banks, with all UAE branches treated as a single bank and single legal entity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, data/credit info
