# Over- and Under-Shipment

Addresses over- and under-shipment — misstating quantity, weight or type of goods relative to the invoice — as a TBML technique complementary to mis-invoicing, illustrated with commodity and construction-materials examples drawn from UAE FIU strategic analysis of SARs/STRs. It also links to transshipment manipulation used to evade sanctions and export controls. Relevant to LFIs conducting trade transaction review and red-flag analysis.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
