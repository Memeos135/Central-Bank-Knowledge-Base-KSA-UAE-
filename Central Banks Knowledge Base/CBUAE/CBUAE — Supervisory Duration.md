# Supervisory Duration

Explains the supervisory duration input used in the SA-CCR add-on calculation for interest rate derivatives, where adjusted notional equals trade notional multiplied by a prescribed duration formula based on the start and end dates of the referenced period. It sits within worked guidance and FAQs on trade classification, hedging sets, maturity buckets, supervisory delta, supervisory factors and correlations. It binds banks computing counterparty credit risk exposure on derivative portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
