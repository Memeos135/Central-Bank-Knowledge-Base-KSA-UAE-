# Category A License

Category A License is a defined CBUAE licence class under the Standards for Exchange Business, permitting the Licensed Person to conduct foreign currency exchange only (no remittances or WPS wage payments). The category drives downstream requirements, including minimum paid-up capital and bank guarantee calculations and the minimum experience threshold for the Compliance Officer. It is relevant to applicants and existing Licensed Persons assessing permitted activity scope and scaled compliance obligations.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, AML/CTF
