# Foreign Payment Token

The Foreign Payment Token is the non-AED token category under the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation; its issuers may operate towards Persons in the UAE only via a Foreign Payment Token Issuer Registration. The regulation limits certain services and the promotion of services relating to Foreign Payment Tokens and restricts their acceptance as a Means of Payment, and the Central Bank may cap volume, value or UAE customer numbers. Binds Registered Foreign Payment Token Issuers, Registered Foreign Payment Token Custodians and Transferors, and persons promoting or accepting such tokens in the UAE.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk, other
