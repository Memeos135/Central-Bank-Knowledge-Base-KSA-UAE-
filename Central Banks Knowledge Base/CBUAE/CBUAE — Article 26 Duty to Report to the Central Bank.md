# Article 26: Duty to Report to the Central Bank

Article 26 of the CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation sets the reporting duties owed to the Central Bank: timely submission of periodic compulsory returns (e.g. Exchange House Return Forms via the IRR system), pre-launch access to Central Bank reporting portals and to the FIU's suspicious transaction reporting channel, capacity to answer 'Search' and 'Search and Freeze' notices within the stated deadlines, and accurate provision of data and records on request. It also requires immediate notification of litigation or criminal investigations involving the Licensed Person, its owners/partners/shareholders (including holding and intermediate companies), directors, employees or subsidiaries, and obliges heads of regulatory compliance, financial crime compliance and internal audit to report concealed breaches directly, with protection against contractual confidentiality claims. Licensed Persons and their Senior Management are accountable for failures to report or for false, misleading or inaccurate submissions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments
