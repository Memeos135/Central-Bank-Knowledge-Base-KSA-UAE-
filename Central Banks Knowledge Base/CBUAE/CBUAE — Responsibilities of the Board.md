# Responsibilities of the Board

Article 4 of the CBUAE remuneration regulation, allocating ultimate responsibility to the board for approving and overseeing the financial institution's remuneration framework, including the remuneration policy and a standalone ISSC remuneration policy. It requires board approval of total variable remuneration on the remuneration committee's recommendation, involvement of Control Functions, conflict-of-interest safeguards, an annual independent remuneration review submitted to the Central Bank, and establishment of a remuneration committee. It binds boards of CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.
