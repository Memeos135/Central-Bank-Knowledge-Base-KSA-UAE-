# IA Board Resolution No. 08 of 2011 - Regulation of Insurance Agents Business

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 08 of 2011 sets the instructions governing the business of insurance agents: registration in the Agents Register, agency contract requirements, professional indemnity cover, handling of premiums through a dedicated bank account, and a code of conduct and ethics. It applies to all insurance agents in the UAE other than those in free zones (unless free-zone rules provide otherwise), and constrains insurance companies from contracting with unregistered agents. Relevant where questions concern agent licensing, single-principal restriction, non-assignment of the agency, or client-money handling.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
