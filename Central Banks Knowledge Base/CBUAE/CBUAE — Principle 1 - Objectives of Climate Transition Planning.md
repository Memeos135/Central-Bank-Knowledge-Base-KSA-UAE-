# Principle 1 - Objectives of Climate Transition Planning

Principle 1 of the Climate Transition Planning Principles deals with the objectives of a financial firm's climate transition planning — why the firm plans, what outcomes it targets, and alignment with UAE climate transition objectives. It frames the purpose-setting stage that the later principles on governance, strategy integration, metrics and reporting build on. Addressed to UAE financial firms as guidance-level expectations.
