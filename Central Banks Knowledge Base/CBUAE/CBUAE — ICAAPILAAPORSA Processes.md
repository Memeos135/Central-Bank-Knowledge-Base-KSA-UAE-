# ICAAP/ILAAP/ORSA Processes

This node addresses Principle 6 of the CBUAE climate principles: the incorporation of material climate-related financial risks into internal capital and liquidity adequacy assessment processes. Relevant financial firms are expected to evaluate liquidity, capital and solvency impacts over specified time horizons, including via transmission through traditional risk categories, and firms already required to run ICAAP, ILAAP, ORSA or equivalent must embed climate risk into those frameworks and record any material capital or liquidity impact. Adjacent content covers monitoring, internal reporting to the board and senior management, and group-level coordination.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
