# Credit Risk Mitigation & Covered Bonds

## Why this community

Prudential credit-risk measurement: how exposure values are determined and how eligible collateral, guarantees and netting reduce them, including the ring-fenced cover pool logic of covered bonds.

## How members connect

- Exposure measurement is the base obligation; credit risk mitigation operates as a recognised adjustment to that measured value, so the two are read in sequence.
- Eligibility, valuation and legal-certainty conditions for mitigants are shared definitions across both items.
- Covered bonds sit alongside as a structural form of collateralisation (cover pool/dual recourse), conceptually drawing on the same exposure and collateral-valuation concepts.
