# Risk-Based Approach (RBA)

Concept node for the risk-based approach as required of financial institutions under the UAE AML-CFT Law and Decision: FIs must identify, assess, document and continuously update their ML/FT risk assessment and make it available to the supervisory authority on request. It covers the business-wide (enterprise) risk assessment methodology — inherent risk across customers, products/services/transactions, delivery channels and geography, then controls and residual risk — as the basis for calibrating mitigation, risk appetite and resource allocation. Binds CBUAE-supervised FIs; conceptually parallel to the enterprise-wide ML/TF risk assessment expected under SAMA's AML/CTF regime.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** Where you must document how a risk-based approach was applied, the expected method is the institutional risk assessment sequence: measure inherent risk, assess the control environment, then derive residual risk. - **What this link tells you:** The business risk assessment is the evidentiary foundation for any risk-based decision, so a firm cannot justify differentiated CDD, monitoring or product controls without one that stands up. The two concepts are linked because the RBA presupposes an identified risk profile: the institutional assessment identifies exposures and tests how existing controls mitigate them, informed by but not limited to national and topical risk assessments.
