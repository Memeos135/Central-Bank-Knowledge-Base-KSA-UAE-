# Article 65 - Law Enforcement Information Exchange

Part of Chapter Eight of the UAE AML/CFT Executive Regulations (Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025) dealing with exchange of crime-related information involving law enforcement and other Concerned Authorities with foreign counterpart and non-counterpart authorities. It complements the supervisory-information-exchange duties (Article 63) and the general conditions on purpose limitation, consent and confidentiality. Obligations fall on state authorities, not on financial institutions, DNFBPs or VASPs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
