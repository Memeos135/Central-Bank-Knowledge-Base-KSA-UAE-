# Independent Validation

Sets the requirement for independent validation of all models as a mandatory life-cycle step and the basis for assessing Model Risk, applying the three-lines model (model owner, independent validator, internal audit) and the Effective Challenge principle. Validation must follow a defined process with responsibilities, metrics, limits and reporting per model type, cover both qualitative and quantitative dimensions, run at minimum frequencies set in Part II, and use monitoring outputs as inputs; it may be outsourced to a third party, but the institution retains ownership of and responsibility for acting on validation reports, with internal oversight where both internal and external validators are used. Binds institutions subject to the MMS, including for internally developed and vendor-acquired models.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
