# AML/CFT Sector Guidance

## Why this community

Sector-specific AML/CFT supervisory guidance sitting on top of the federal AML/CFT statutory framework, covering CDD/EDD, suspicious transaction reporting, sanctions screening and typologies such as shell/front company misuse and trade-based money laundering.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the AML-CFT Law and its implementing Cabinet Decision supply the enforceable obligations; the payment-sector, insurance and PEP guidance documents interpret them for defined licensee populations without creating new duties.
- Shared defined terms drive the linkage - 'Customer', beneficial owner, PEP, suspicious transaction - so a single CDD/EDD standard is applied with sector-specific risk overlays (correspondent relationships, enterprise risk assessment, payment flows).
- Obligation chain around reporting: transaction monitoring feeds STR filing, which triggers the statutory tipping-off prohibition; sanctions obligations run in parallel and are not discharged by STR filing.
- Typology entries (shell/front/shelf companies, TBML) are conceptually paired with the CDD/EDD entries as red-flag material informing risk rating rather than standalone obligations; FATF Recommendations are the external benchmark cited throughout.
