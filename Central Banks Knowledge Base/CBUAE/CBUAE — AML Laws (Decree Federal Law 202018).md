# AML Laws (Decree Federal Law 20/2018)

A defined-term node fixing the meaning of "AML Laws" for the CBUAE Open Finance framework: Federal Decree-Law 20/2018 on anti-money laundering, combating the financing of terrorism and financing of illegal organisations, Cabinet Decision 10/2019 (its implementing regulation), each as amended, plus related instructions, guidelines and notices. It does not itself create obligations but imports the whole AML/CTF regime into the Open Finance regulation by reference. Relevant to Applicants, Open Finance Providers, the API Hub and Licensed Financial Institutions subject to that regulation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments
