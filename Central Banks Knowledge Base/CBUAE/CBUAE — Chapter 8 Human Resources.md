# Chapter 8: Human Resources

Chapter 8 sets minimum human resources standards for Licensed Persons, requiring a Board-approved (or Owner/Partner-approved) Human Resource Policy covering recruitment and Know Your Employee (KYE), induction and training, job descriptions and KPIs, segregation of duties, staff rotation, working hours and overtime, leave, performance evaluation, employee rights and responsibilities and the disciplinary process, consistent with UAE law and reviewed periodically. It prescribes a minimum KYE process including CV screening, verification of academic qualifications, testing and interview, and employment history verification with previous employers. It binds Licensed Persons and interacts with function-combination limits referenced in the audit and AML/CFT chapters.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
