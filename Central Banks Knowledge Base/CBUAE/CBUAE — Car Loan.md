# Car Loan

Article 3 of the original regulation defining the Car Loan as financing extended by a bank or finance company for the purchase of a private vehicle, treated separately from the personal consumer loan. It caps financing at eighty percent of the financed vehicle's value, limits repayment to sixty months, requires security by mortgage over the car, and applies the same terms to car loans to sole proprietorships and companies secured by owner/partner salaries. Binds banks and finance companies offering vehicle finance to individuals.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
