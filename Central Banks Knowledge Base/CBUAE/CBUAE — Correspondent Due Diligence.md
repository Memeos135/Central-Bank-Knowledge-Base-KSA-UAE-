# Correspondent Due Diligence

Section of the CBUAE payment-sector AML/CFT guidance setting out correspondent banking due diligence duties: no relationships with shell banks, information gathering on the respondent's business and reputation, evaluation of its AML/CFT controls, senior management approval before onboarding, and clarity on each party's AML/CFT responsibilities. It applies these duties to relationships with payment-sector participants and stresses that in intermediated transaction chains each LFI remains responsible for monitoring transactions passing through it, with attention to nesting and indirect access risk. Binds LFIs providing correspondent or account services to payment firms.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
