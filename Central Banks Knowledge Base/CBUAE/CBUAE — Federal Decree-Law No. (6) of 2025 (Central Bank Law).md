# Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 (Central Bank Law)

This node is the enabling legislation — Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 on the Central Bank, Regulation of Financial Institutions and Activities, and Insurance Business — cited as the source of CBUAE's power to issue the Takaful Fund Standard. It establishes that licensed Takaful companies must comply with the Central Bank Law plus regulations, standards, resolutions and guidelines of the CBUAE and the HSA, and that CBUAE may impose requirements beyond the minimums stated in a standard. It binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions and insurance undertakings.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
