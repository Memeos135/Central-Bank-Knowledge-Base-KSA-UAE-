# Federal Decree No. 20 of 2018 (Anti-Money Laundering)

Reference node for the UAE federal AML/CFT decree-law on countering money laundering, terrorist financing and financing of illegal organisations, cited here as legal basis for an Insurance Authority decision on supervisory data, information and reporting by insurance companies and related professions. The surrounding context also cites the Cabinet resolutions containing the executive regulations and the terrorist-lists/UN sanctions implementation regime. It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs generally; in this instrument it anchors obligations on insurers, branches of foreign insurers, insurance agents and related professions operating in the State.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, other
