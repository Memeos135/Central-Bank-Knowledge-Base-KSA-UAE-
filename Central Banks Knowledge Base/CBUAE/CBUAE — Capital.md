# Capital

Article 5 of the CBUAE Market Risk Regulation, sitting alongside the valuation provisions of Article 4. It requires banks to hold capital at all times against unexpected losses arising from market risk exposures and to meet market risk capital requirements continuously, with related duties on independent model validation, prudent valuation adjustments for illiquid, concentrated or stale positions, and trading book valuation methodology. Binds all banks licensed by the CBUAE, on a solo and group-wide basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
