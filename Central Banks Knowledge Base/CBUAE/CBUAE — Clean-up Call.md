# Clean-up Call

Concept node on clean-up calls and related early amortisation features in securitisation transactions under the CBUAE capital standards, including the operational conditions determining whether an originating bank may exclude underlying exposures from risk-weighted assets. It sets out early amortisation triggers that subordinate or otherwise increase the originator's exposure, and lists structures (replenishment, term-mimicking, certain revolving structures) that may still qualify while capital is held against retained securitisation exposures. Binds originating banks structuring traditional or synthetic securitisations; the surrounding context also touches Additional Tier 1 call conditions requiring prior Central Bank approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
