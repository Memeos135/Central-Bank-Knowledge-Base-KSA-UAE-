# IA Board Decision No. 40 of 2019

Node for Insurance Authority Board of Directors Decision No. (40) of 2019, the amending instrument that produced the consolidated 16/09/2019 text of the Code of Conduct and Ethics (Resolution No. 3 of 2010). Its principal effect visible in the consolidated text is extension of the Code to insurance-related professions alongside insurance companies, with defined terms (Company, Client, Insured, Insurance Agent, Policy) retained. Consult it for version control and to establish which conduct obligations applied before versus after September 2019.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
