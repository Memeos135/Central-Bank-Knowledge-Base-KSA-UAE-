# Disclosure and Transparency (Article 2)

Article 2 of the SME Market Conduct Regulation sets disclosure and transparency duties owed to SME Customers, including proactive and timely information, plain language, warnings on key characteristics, risks, costs, fees, rates, penalties and lock-in terms, and effective disclosure at all stages of the relationship including changes to terms. It also requires reasons for application rejection to be given (subject to Financial Crime risk or legal prohibition), bans partial or biased disclosure to entice customers, mandates English and Arabic availability, accuracy of disclosures, and ongoing monitoring of disclosure effectiveness using complaint analysis and satisfaction surveys. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions dealing with SME Customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
