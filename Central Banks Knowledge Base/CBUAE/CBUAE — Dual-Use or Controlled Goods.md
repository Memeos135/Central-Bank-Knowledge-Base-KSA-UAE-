# Dual-Use or Controlled Goods

A concept node within the CBUAE proliferation-financing guidance covering dual-use and controlled goods — items with both civilian and WMD/military application that are subject to import/export control under Cabinet Resolution No. (50) of 2020 (the UAE Control List). It sets the supervisory expectation that licensed financial institutions screen trade-finance and international trade transactions against the Control List, consult the EOCN on whether commodities are controlled and whether valid permits exist, and use standardised product classification (Harmonized System codes) when recording and monitoring such transactions. It binds LFIs supervised by CBUAE offering trade finance or payment services connected to cross-border trade.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
