# Prudential Requirements (Article 4)

Article 4 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation sets the prudential floor for crowdfunding companies: minimum capital of AED 1,000,000 (Category 1) or AED 300,000 (Category 2), with an obligation to hold the higher of that amount or capital equal to 5% of outstanding lending volume. It binds licensed LCA crowdfunding companies on an ongoing basis and underpins the bank guarantee requirement in the licensing article. Comparative UAE material; not a SAMA requirement.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
