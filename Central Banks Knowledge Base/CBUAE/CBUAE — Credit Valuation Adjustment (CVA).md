# Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA)

Explains the regulatory credit valuation adjustment capital charge under CBUAE standards, including how regulatory CVA differs from accounting CVA (no debit valuation adjustment / own-credit effect) and which instruments qualify as eligible CVA hedges — index CDS, single-name and contingent CDS, equivalent instruments directly referencing the hedged counterparty, and CDS swaptions without knock-out clauses — with tranched and nth-to-default CDS excluded. Also touches the treatment of written and purchased credit derivatives in the leverage ratio exposure measure. Binds banks with derivative counterparty exposure calculating CVA and market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
