# Bi-Annual Compliance Report

Covers the Compliance Officer's bi-annual AML/CFT compliance report and its place in the Licensed Person's regulatory reporting calendar. The report must cover gap analysis against AML laws and standards, breaches raised in Central Bank examinations and audit reports, statistics on internal disclosures and STRs, policy changes, training coverage and gaps, resource requests, recommendations to the Manager in Charge and Board (or owner/partners), and a forward six-month action plan. Also situates related annual submissions such as audited financial statements and external auditor findings on AML/CFT under agreed-upon procedures, with specified filing channels and deadlines.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
