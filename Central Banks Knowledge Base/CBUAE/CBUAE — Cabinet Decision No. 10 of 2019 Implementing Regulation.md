# Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 Implementing Regulation

Instrument node for Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019, the Implementing Regulation of Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018, which operationalises the UAE AML/CTF statute. It contains the DNFBP scoping rules (Article 3), the ongoing transaction-monitoring duty referenced in the guidance (Article 7.1), the STR requirement (Article 17), and the mandate for supervisory authorities to issue guidance and feedback (Article 44.11). It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs, and prevails over CBUAE guidance in the event of any discrepancy.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
