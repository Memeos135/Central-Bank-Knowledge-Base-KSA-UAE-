# Insurance Authority Legal Instruments

## Why this community

Transitional and repeal mechanics for insurance-sector instruments: how a new Insurance Authority instrument extinguishes the legal force of earlier resolutions and what continues to apply in the interim.

## How members connect

- The cancellation article operates directly on the earlier resolution, so the two must be read as repealing instrument and repealed instrument.
- Linkage is hierarchical/temporal rather than substantive: the later instrument determines the effective date from which obligations under the 2013 resolution cease.
- Relevant to any compliance position that still cites the superseded resolution as its legal basis; grandfathering and saved provisions, if any, must be confirmed in the repealing text itself.
