# Chapter 15: Business Continuity Management

Chapter 15 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets the minimum business continuity management obligations for Licensed Persons (exchange houses), requiring identification and impact assessment of disruption risks and implementation of a Business Continuity Plan. It prescribes the mandatory content of the BCP, including crisis assessment, protection of the entity and staff, minimising suspension of operations, sensitive-data storage and recovery, and notification of the Central Bank and correspondents. It binds all licensed exchange businesses as a continuing operational-resilience requirement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
