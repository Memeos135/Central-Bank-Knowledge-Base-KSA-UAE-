# Telemarketing Regulation

This node is the CBUAE Telemarketing Regulation (Circular 3/2026), a consumer-protection instrument governing how Licensed Financial Institutions and their telemarketers may market financial products and services by phone and other communication channels. It imposes opt-out and Do Not Call Registry restrictions, a published telemarketing guide covering complaints and DNCR registration, mandatory telemarketing logs and record retention, disclosure standards prohibiting untrue or fraudulent statements, immediate termination on customer request, and limits on calling hours (9:00–18:00 State time) and contact frequency. It binds all Licensed Financial Institutions, including for conduct carried out through third-party telemarketers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
