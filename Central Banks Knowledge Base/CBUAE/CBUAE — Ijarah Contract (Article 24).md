# Ijarah Contract (Article 24)

Article 24 of CBUAE Regulation 1/2026 governs the ijarah contract, the longest of the financing-contract articles in Chapter Four on Islamic banking financing. It applies to Islamic financial institutions providing lease-based financing and should be read with the general contract provisions, the financing security article (Art. 30) and the undertaking to purchase assets (Art. 29). Relevant to allocation of ownership risk, maintenance and rental obligations in lease structures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
