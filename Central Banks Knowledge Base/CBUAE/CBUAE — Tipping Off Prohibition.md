# Tipping Off Prohibition

A concept node on the confidentiality duty attaching to suspicious transaction reporting — the prohibition on disclosing to a customer or third party that an STR has been or will be filed, or that an ML/FT investigation is underway. It sits alongside guidance on STR timing (report to the FIU without delay once suspicion is established) and on controls protecting STR data from unauthorised access. It binds financial institutions and their staff under the UAE AML-CFT Law and Decision, with breach exposure at both institutional and individual level.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
