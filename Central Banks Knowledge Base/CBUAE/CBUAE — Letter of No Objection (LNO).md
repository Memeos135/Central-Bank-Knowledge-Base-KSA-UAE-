# Letter of No Objection (LNO)

Defined-term node for the Letter of No Objection (LNO) under the CBUAE Exchange Business Regulation: a written (including electronic) no-objection letter issued by the Central Bank. The LNO operates as a prior-consent gate for specified corporate and business actions by a Licensed Person — including acquiring, merging with or entering a joint venture with another person (licensed or not), investing in or opening subsidiaries or offices inside or outside the UAE, selling/closing operations, and launching WPS payroll cards under a Category I licence. Relevant to any Licensed Person planning a structural, ownership or new-activity change.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
