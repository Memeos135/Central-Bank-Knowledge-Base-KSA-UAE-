# Outsourcing Agreements (Article 5)

Article 5 of the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation for Banks sets the mandatory contractual content of any outsourcing arrangement: retention of bank and customer data ownership, unfettered bank access to data during and after the contract, confidentiality and data-destruction terms, data-protection standards, limits and conditions on subcontracting, prohibition on onward disclosure of Confidential Data without bank or customer authorisation, Central Bank (and its agents') access and on-site inspection rights at the service provider, and prompt breach notification. It binds Banks licensed in the UAE, which must ensure all outsourcing is governed by a formal written contract; it operates indirectly on outsourcing service providers and their subcontractors through those terms.
