# Article 3: Court Cases Management System

Article 3 addresses the Court Cases Management System, the automated channel through which court orders issued against Licensed Financial Institutions by the participating courts and prosecution bodies are transmitted and monitored. It confirms the obligation to process such court orders within the prescribed time limits and sets a fixed fine per violation plus a daily accrual for each working day of continued delay, subject to a cap.
