# Option Risk

Covers optionality and related risk drivers in IRRBB modelling under the Model Management Guidance, including option risk and basis risk as granular inputs to EVE, and the treatment of currency aggregation of ∆EVE — no offsetting across currencies per Basel, with a conditional option to offset between pegged currencies (e.g. USD/AED) only where basis risk is demonstrably captured through dedicated stress scenarios. It also sets out the treatment of non-performing assets net of provisions: inclusion in EVE (via rate-sensitive discounting of recoveries and LGD, including rescheduled/forborne facilities), with NII treatment left to bank discretion. Binds CBUAE-supervised institutions computing IRRBB metrics.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
