# Use Test Principle

Articulates the use test for stress testing: stress tests must not be a standalone ICAAP exercise but must be embedded in business decision-making and risk management across the organisation, with Senior Management and the Board leading and approving assumptions, methodology and use of results. Requires that risk mitigations be considered in light of severity and likelihood, with documented justification where no action is taken, and sits alongside requirements for reverse stress testing to identify scenarios rendering the business model non-viable or breaching minimum capital requirements. Applies to all CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
