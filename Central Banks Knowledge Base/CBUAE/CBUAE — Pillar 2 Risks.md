# Pillar 2 Risks

Concept node for Pillar 2 risks: those material risks not covered, or not sufficiently covered, by Pillar 1 capital, which each bank must identify, measure, monitor and capitalise through its ICAAP. Sets expectations on material risk identification across the whole group (branches and subsidiaries), Board and Senior Management approval of the capital-estimation framework, continuous review of the applicable risk universe, and treatment of direct versus indirect risks; credit concentration risk and IRRBB are designated material. Applies to banks licensed by the UAE Central Bank and is assessed by the supervisor through SREP.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** If you are deciding what risks must be captured in an ICAAP, this link defines the boundary: the ICAAP exists to cover risks that Pillar 1 does not address, or does not address fully, and to size capital to those risks. The obligation chain runs from the Pillar 2 risk framework into the ICAAP, so the ICAAP's risk inventory is not discretionary — omitting a recognised Pillar 2 risk is a gap in the assessment, not a scoping choice. Consequence: gaps between the Pillar 1 calculation and the institution's actual risk profile must be explicitly identified and capitalised, and that reasoning must be documented.
