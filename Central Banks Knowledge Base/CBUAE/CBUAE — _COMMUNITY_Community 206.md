# Remittances & Foreign Exchange

## Why this community

Cross-border and domestic money transfer and foreign exchange activity, including the conduct and consumer-disclosure conditions attaching to remittance services.

## How members connect

- Standalone node in this graph: it links outward rather than internally, principally to AML/CTF wire-transfer and originator-information duties and to sanctions/TFS screening obligations.
- Also intersects with consumer protection disclosure duties (fees, exchange rate, execution time) and with licensing scope for money transfer businesses.
