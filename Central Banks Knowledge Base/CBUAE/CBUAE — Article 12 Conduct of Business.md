# Article 12: Conduct of Business

Conduct-of-business article binding Insurance Brokers, their Representatives and Staff: act honestly, fairly and transparently toward Clients, act in the Client's best interests when comparing products and selecting a Company, supply accurate underwriting information including Personal Data, and obtain written client authorisation on the CBUAE-approved form without restricting the Client's right to cancel. The supplied context also carries adjacent provisions on outsourcing (material outsourcing requires CBUAE non-objection, with the broker remaining fully liable for service providers) and on premiums, claim settlements and remuneration (direct payment between Clients and Companies; brokers prohibited from collecting premiums, claim settlements or refunds).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
