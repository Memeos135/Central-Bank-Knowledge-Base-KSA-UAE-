# Float Management

Concept node covering the safeguarding and management of the Float (customer funds/Money's Worth held against stored value) under the CBUAE SVF Regulation. Requires accurate and timely customer ledger reconciliation against total Float, segregation of Float assets from the Licensee's own funds and other business funds, internal controls against theft, fraud and misappropriation, and liquidity-focused Float management ensuring funds are always available for redemption; it also bars business models relying on investment returns from the Float. Binds SVF Licensees.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
