# Own Risk and Solvency Assessment

Concept node on the Own Risk and Solvency Assessment, the forward-looking self-assessment insurance companies must conduct at least annually as a core element of their ERM framework. The ORSA report must address current and projected financial soundness across scenarios, including year-end solvency and at least the following twelve quarters of projected solvency ratios, all material risks (underwriting, investment, credit, operational, liquidity), climate-related financial risks per the Climate-related Financial Risk Management Regulation, actual-versus-planned outcomes from the prior ORSA, risk policies and procedures, and quantitative analysis under normal and stress scenarios. It binds Companies on a solo and Group-wide basis.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
