# FATF Standards and Guidance

This node captures the FATF standards and guidance relied on as an interpretive source in CBUAE AML/CFT guidance materials, including FATF-derived definitions (for example nesting in correspondent banking) and risk-based approach expectations. It is not binding law in itself; it informs how supervisory expectations and national requirements are framed and applied by licensed financial institutions. Useful when tracing the origin of a defined term or a risk typology cited by the supervisor.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
