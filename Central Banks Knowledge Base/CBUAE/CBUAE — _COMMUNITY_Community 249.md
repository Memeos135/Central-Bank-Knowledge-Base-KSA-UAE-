# Implementation Strategy and Actions

## Why this community

Governance-style principle requiring a supervised entity to translate an adopted policy/framework into a concrete implementation strategy with assigned actions, owners and timelines. The compliance question is whether a board-approved policy alone satisfies the rule, or whether documented execution steps are separately enforceable.

## How members connect

- Singleton node: the principle stands alone in the graph but is structurally part of a numbered principles sequence, so it inherits the scope and definitions of its parent framework.
- Operates downstream of the earlier principles (policy adoption/approval) and upstream of the review principle, forming an obligation chain: set policy -> implement -> review.
- Typical hierarchy linkage: enabling law or SAMA regulation sets the duty; the principle text supplies the implementation expectation used in supervisory assessment.
