# AML/CFT Requirements

Article 24 of the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation, imposing AML/CFT obligations on "AML Obligors" in addition to and without prejudice to the UAE AML Law and other AML/CFT rules. It treats Payment Token Services as inherently high ML/TF risk (speed, anonymity, cross-border), and requires enterprise and customer risk assessment, risk-rated CDD and ongoing due diligence, internal policies and controls, compliance management including a compliance officer and MLRO, and STR filing to the FIU, with secrecy unavailable as a defence to reporting.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
