# Additional Tier 1 (AT1) Capital

A concept node on Additional Tier 1 capital, including its derivation as the difference between Tier 1 and CET1 and how AT1 issued by a subsidiary to third parties is partially included in consolidated group capital. The illustrative example shows parent holdings of a subsidiary's CET1, AT1 and Tier 2 and the resulting eligible amounts. Relevant to UAE banks computing consolidated capital ratios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
