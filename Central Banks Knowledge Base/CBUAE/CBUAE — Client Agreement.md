# Client Agreement

Concept node for the Client Agreement that a crowdfunding company must conclude with its Clients (Lenders and Borrowers) under the CBUAE crowdfunding regulation, including the mandatory terms set out in the appendices. It anchors conduct obligations such as disclosure of terms and conditions and subsequent updates, inclusion of dispute-handling and grievance-redress details, loan administration and default arrangements, applicable lending limits for Market Counterparties, and contingency/platform-failure arrangements. It binds licensed crowdfunding companies in their contractual dealings with Clients.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
