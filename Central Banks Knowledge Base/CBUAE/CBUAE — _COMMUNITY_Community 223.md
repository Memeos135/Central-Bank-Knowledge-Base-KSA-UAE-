# Central Bank Powers

## Why this community

Powers of the Central Bank: the supervisory and enforcement authority reserved to SAMA over licensed/registered entities - inspection, information demands, directions, and corrective or sanctioning measures.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster that operates as the enforcement backbone for every substantive obligation elsewhere in the instrument.
- Legal consequence chain: substantive duty (e.g. record-keeping, data protection) -> supervisory request or inspection -> direction or sanction under this article.
- Also the gateway for approvals and no-objection requirements that condition corporate events such as mergers and wind-downs.
