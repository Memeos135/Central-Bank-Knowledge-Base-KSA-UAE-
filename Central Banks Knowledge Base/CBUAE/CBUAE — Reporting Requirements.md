# Reporting Requirements

Article 7 of the CBUAE Market Risk Regulation on reporting, read together with the systems-and-controls duties requiring information systems that identify, aggregate, monitor and report market risk exposure to the Board and Senior Management. Related obligations cover market risk limits aligned to risk appetite, exception tracking and escalation, controls over risk models, trading book allocation policies, intra-day exposure limits, and submission of a trading book policy statement (and material changes) for Central Bank review. Applies to all CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
