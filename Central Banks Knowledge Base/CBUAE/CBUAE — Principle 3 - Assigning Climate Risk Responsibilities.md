# Principle 3 - Assigning Climate Risk Responsibilities

Principle 3 of the CBUAE climate risk Principles requires the board of a financial firm to assign climate-related financial risk management responsibilities across the organisation, to board committees or appropriate senior management. Assignments must be formally documented, with clear functions, accountability, governance structure, escalation and reporting lines, including for any dedicated climate roles or departments. Responsibilities must be reviewed regularly against the firm's business model, complexity and evolving climate risk methodologies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
