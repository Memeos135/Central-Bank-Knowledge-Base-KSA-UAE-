# FATF AML/CFT Standards

Concept node grouping the Regulation's AML/CFT compliance expectations for hawala providers: undertakings to comply with all applicable UAE laws including the federal AML/CFT laws and Central Bank regulations, to guarantee customer transfers, to notify competent authorities of violations, to hold a settlement account with a UAE bank, to exercise due diligence when approving agents and maintain a current agent/country list, and to supply data and statistics to the Central Bank on a confidential basis. The supplied context states these as domestic obligations; it does not itself reproduce FATF Recommendation text, so treat the FATF label as an interpretive framing rather than cited standards.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
