# Simplified Due Diligence (SDD)

A concept node on simplified due diligence as the lower-intensity end of risk-based CDD, available only where low risk has been identified and there is no suspicion of ML/FT. It sits within the CDD chapter alongside enhanced due diligence, and stresses that customer risk profiles are dynamic so institutions must be ready to escalate the type and level of due diligence. It binds financial institutions applying CDD measures under the AML-CFT Law and Decision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
