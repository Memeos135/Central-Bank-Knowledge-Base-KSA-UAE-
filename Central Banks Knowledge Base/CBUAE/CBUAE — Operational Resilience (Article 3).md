# Operational Resilience (Article 3)

Article 3 of the CBUAE Operational Risk / Operational Resilience regulation sets the resilience requirements that sit inside the wider operational risk framework, including the planning instruments a Licensed Financial Institution must maintain for Critical Operations (business continuity and disaster recovery planning for prolonged or permanent unavailability of assets). It frames tolerance for disruption and the expectation that resilience is managed on an ongoing, tested basis rather than as a static plan. It binds LFIs licensed by the CBUAE, with responsibility escalating to the Board and Senior Management under the following articles.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
