# Business Continuity Management

## Why this community

Operational resilience: the obligation to maintain business continuity arrangements so that payment/financial services and customer access are preserved and restored after disruption.

## How members connect

- Standalone article functioning as a specific-control obligation under the general risk management framework.
- Consequence chain: continuity failures engage both prudential supervision and consumer-facing service obligations (access to funds, service availability).
- Stay at obligation level — the article sets what must exist and be tested, not how systems are built.
