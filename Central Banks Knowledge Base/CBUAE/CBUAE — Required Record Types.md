# Required Record Types

Section of the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions dealing with record keeping: the categories of records FIs must retain (transaction records; CDD records covering customer and company information, third-party reliance, ongoing monitoring; and suspicious transaction reporting records) together with the retention and availability obligations. It sets a minimum five-year retention period running from the latest relevant trigger event (end of the business relationship or account closure, completion of an occasional transaction, completion of a supervisory inspection, or a final judgment), and requires records to be organised so transactions can be reconstructed and produced to Competent Authorities immediately on request. It binds financial institutions supervised under the UAE AML-CFT Law and its implementing Cabinet Decision, and expects supporting internal policies on archiving, destruction, data protection and audit testing.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, data/credit info
