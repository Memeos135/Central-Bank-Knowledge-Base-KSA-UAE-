# Proliferation Financing

Concept node within CBUAE's counter-proliferation-financing guidance, framing proliferation financing (PF) as the funding of WMD development, acquisition or delivery systems and the role of financial institutions in detecting and disrupting it. It maps the applicable UAE framework — Federal Decree Law No. (20) of 2018 and its Implementing Regulation (Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019 as amended by No. (24) of 2022), targeted financial sanctions, export controls and dual-use goods lists — alongside relevant FATF guidance and PF red-flag indicators. Binds LFIs, and by reference DNFBPs and VASPs, to implement TFS without delay and embed CPF into the AML/CFT programme.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — PF Risk (Threat, Vulnerability, Consequence)|PF Risk (Threat, Vulnerability, Consequence)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If the question is how to document a PF risk assessment, note that the threat–vulnerability–consequence framing is the assessment methodology, whereas proliferation financing is the underlying defined activity being assessed — the former is only meaningful once the latter is scoped. The guidance adopts the FATF risk-assessment methodology directly, so the three factors are the expected structure of analysis, not an optional analytical style.
