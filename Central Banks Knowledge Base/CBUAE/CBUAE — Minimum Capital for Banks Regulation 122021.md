# Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation 12/2021

CBUAE Circular 12/2021 (Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation), issued under Article 75 of the UAE Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law 14/2018), setting the minimum paid-up capital that licensed Banks must hold at all times as a licensing prerequisite. It fixes thresholds for UAE-incorporated Banks, Specialized Banks and branches of foreign banks, requires the requirement be met solely with fully paid-up capital, and states it operates independently of risk-based capital rules. It binds all Banks operating in the UAE, including foreign bank branches — note this is a UAE (CBUAE) instrument, not a SAMA rule.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
