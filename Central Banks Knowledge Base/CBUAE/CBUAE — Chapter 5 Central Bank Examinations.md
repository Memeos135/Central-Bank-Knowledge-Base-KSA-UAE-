# Chapter 5: Central Bank Examinations

Chapter 5 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business addresses Central Bank examinations of Licensed Persons — the supervisory inspection process through which compliance with the Standards is verified. It connects to obligations elsewhere in the Standards to keep files and records available or accessible at the Head Office for examiners, and to the supervisory powers and non-compliance charges set out in the appendices. It binds Licensed Persons conducting Exchange Business in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
