# Material Risk Takers

Concept node for the defined term "Material Risk Takers" — staff whose work is deemed to have a significant impact on the overall risk profile of the bank or group — as used across CBUAE governance and risk standards. It matters mainly for compensation design, risk-culture and Board oversight obligations, and for determining which staff fall within enhanced remuneration and accountability controls. Relevant to banks and their boards, compensation and risk committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
