# Secure Communication

## Why this community

Obligation on the service provider to ensure that communications and credentials exchanged with the customer are conducted through secure channels, protecting authentication data from interception or misuse.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster: the secure communication duty stands alone in the graph but sits in the payment/electronic services obligation chain alongside authentication and customer notification duties.
- Functionally linked to liability allocation rules, since failure to provide a secure channel bears on who carries the loss from a disputed transaction.
- Operates as a conduct/consumer-protection requirement rather than a prudential one, so breach is assessed against the provider, not the user.
