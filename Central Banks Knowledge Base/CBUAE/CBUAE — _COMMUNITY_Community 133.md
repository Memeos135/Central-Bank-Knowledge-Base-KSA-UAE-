# Trade Finance

## Why this community

Trade finance product structures - particularly non-documentary and open-account arrangements - where the financing institution has limited sight of underlying goods, creating heightened AML/CTF and credit-risk scrutiny expectations.

## How members connect

- Non-documentary trade finance is the organising node; factoring, forfaiting and structured trade finance are referenced as its product sub-types sharing the same reduced-documentation risk profile.
- Open account trade is treated as conceptually equivalent in risk terms, because settlement occurs without bank-intermediated documentary control.
- The absence of documentary evidence shifts the compliance burden onto customer-level CDD, beneficial ownership and transaction monitoring rather than document examination.
- Directly adjacent to the trade-based money laundering cluster: these products are the channels in which those typologies are expected to be detected.
