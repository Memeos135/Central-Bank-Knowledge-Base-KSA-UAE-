# Code of Conduct and Ethics for Insurance Companies (Res 3/2010)

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 3 of 2010, issuing instructions on the code of conduct and ethics for insurance companies operating in the UAE. It sets market-conduct duties including utmost good faith, disclosure and transparency, accurate and clear information to clients, non-discrimination in rates and terms except on technical or actuarial grounds, and rules on application forms, policy drafting and renewal. It binds companies registered with the Insurance Authority and operating in the State, including foreign insurers' branches, whether marketing directly or through agents or brokers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
