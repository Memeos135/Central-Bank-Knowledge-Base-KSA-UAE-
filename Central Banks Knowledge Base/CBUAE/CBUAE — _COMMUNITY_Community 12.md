# AML Controls Effectiveness

## Why this community

Institution-level AML/CTF risk assessment methodology: how a supervised entity measures inherent ML/TF risk, evaluates the quality of its control environment, and derives residual risk to drive a risk-based approach.

## How members connect

- Shared calculation chain: inherent risk minus assessed control effectiveness yields residual risk, which in turn calibrates the risk-based approach.
- The control-environment assessment is the hub node, decomposing into the mandated control pillars — CDD/KYC, training, independent testing, governance and internal controls.
- The MLRO is the accountable function linking the institutional risk assessment to governance and reporting obligations.
- External soft-law (Wolfsberg sanctions screening guidance) is cited to benchmark internal control adequacy, not as a binding SAMA obligation.
