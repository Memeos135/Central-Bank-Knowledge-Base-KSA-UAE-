# Letter of No Objection

Recurring prior-approval mechanism under the Standards for Exchange Business: a Licensed Person must obtain a written Letter of No Objection from the Banking Supervision Department before certain acts, including opening hedge or foreign accounts (via the Foreign Account Application form), offering any product or service outside the standard permitted activities (drawn from the Special Product Matrix), and appointing or re-appointing an External Auditor within the rotation limits. Functions as a regulatory gate on scope expansion and key appointments; binds all exchange houses licensed by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
