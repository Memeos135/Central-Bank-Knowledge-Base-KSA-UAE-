# Transfer of Fund and Investment Account (Article 26)

Node for Article 26 of the CBUAE Takaful Insurance Regulation; note the label here refers to transfer of the Fund and investment account, while the Regulation's table of contents titles Article 26 "Participant's Investment Account" — confirm the correct heading in the source. The retrieved context does not contain the operative Article 26 text; it instead carries Article 18, which sets the Wakala operating model and requires companies licensed for insurance of persons and fund accumulation to establish both a Participants' Investment Account (managed under Wakala Bi Al-Istithmar with the Participant) and a Takaful Insurance Fund (managed under Wakala with the Company), with revenues and expenses separately determined for the Fund, the Participants' Investment Account and the Shareholders' Account.
