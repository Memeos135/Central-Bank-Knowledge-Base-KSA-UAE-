# Risk Management Standards 153/2018

Parent node for the CBUAE Risk Management Standards 153/2018, the detailed companion to the Risk Management Regulation. It sets out expectations for the risk governance framework, the three-lines roles (board, senior management, risk management function/CRO, compliance, internal audit, business lines), risk identification and impact assessment, risk appetite and limit metrics, board-approved risk management policy, risk measurement and models, stress testing, and internal reporting. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE, on a bank-wide and where relevant group-wide basis.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
