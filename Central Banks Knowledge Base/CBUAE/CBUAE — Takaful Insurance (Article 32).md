# Takaful Insurance (Article 32)

Chapter Five (Article 32) of CBUAE Regulation 1/2026 is the takaful insurance provision, the sole article dedicated to takaful within a regulation otherwise focused on Islamic banking contracts. It sits alongside the Article 1 definitions of 'takaful insurance company' (licensed to conduct takaful activities per Shari'ah principles) and 'retakaful company' (a legal person licensed under the Central Bank Law and its implementing regulations). It binds licensed takaful and retakaful undertakings in the conduct of their commercial transactions.
