# Table 2: Loss and Damage Tariffs

This node is Table 2 annexed to the UAE Insurance Authority Board Decision No. (30) of 2016 on Motor Vehicle Insurance Tariffs, setting the premium rates for combined loss-and-damage plus third-party liability motor policies. It fixes, by vehicle category (saloon, 4WD, light and heavy trucks, buses by seating capacity and private/commercial use, equipment, motorcycles), a maximum rate percentage, a minimum premium, and fixed driver and passenger cover amounts for a 13-month insurance period. It binds insurance companies, agents and brokers writing motor cover in the UAE, which may not levy any additional charge without an Insurance Authority decision; note this is a UAE (not KSA) instrument and applies from 1 January 2017.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
