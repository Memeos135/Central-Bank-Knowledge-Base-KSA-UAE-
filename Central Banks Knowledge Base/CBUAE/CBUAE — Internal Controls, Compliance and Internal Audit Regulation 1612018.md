# Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Regulation 161/2018

Instrument node for CBUAE Circular 161/2018, the Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Regulation, issued to all banks. It requires a sound internal control framework and permanent, independent and effective compliance and internal audit functions, and addresses compensation of staff in those functions and group-wide consistency. It is read together with the accompanying Standards and with the Central Bank's corporate governance regulation, which supplies the overarching prudential framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks 832019|Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks 83/2019]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Any assessment of whether a bank's control functions are adequately structured has to be made against both instruments together: the internal controls, compliance and internal audit regulation sets the functional requirements, while the corporate governance regulation supplies the board and committee accountability framework those functions report into. Both are issued under the same banking-law authority, so the control-function duties are an application of, not an alternative to, the governance duties. Note the sequencing — the governance regulation is the later instrument — so where the two address the same board responsibility, check whether the later text supplements or displaces the earlier one before relying on a single provision.
