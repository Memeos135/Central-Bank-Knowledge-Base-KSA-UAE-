# Inherent Risk

Defines and elaborates the concept of inherent risk used in the institutional risk assessment: the ML/TF/PF exposure arising from an LFI's customer base, products and services, geographies and delivery channels before controls are applied. It sets expectations on granularity of risk factors within each category, standardised assessment approaches, and treatment of high-risk indicators such as exits for financial crime compliance reasons and STR/SAR volumes. Relevant to LFIs designing or defending the inherent-risk layer of their assessment.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
