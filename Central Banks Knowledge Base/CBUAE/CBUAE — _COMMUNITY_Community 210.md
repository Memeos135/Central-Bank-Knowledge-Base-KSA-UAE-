# Group Recovery Plans

## Why this community

Recovery planning at group level for financial institutions, addressing how a group-wide recovery plan must be prepared, scoped and maintained as a condition of prudential/resilience supervision.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the group recovery plan obligation sits as an article-level requirement whose scope term ('group') determines which entities and subsidiaries the plan must cover.
- Links outward rather than internally — it presupposes the entity-level recovery planning and governance regime (board approval, triggers, escalation) sitting above it in the hierarchy.
- Read as an enforceable planning duty with supervisory submission/approval consequences, not as guidance.
