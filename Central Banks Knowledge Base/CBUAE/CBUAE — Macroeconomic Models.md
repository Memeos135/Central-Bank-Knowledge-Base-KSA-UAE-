# Macroeconomic Models

Section 5 of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance (MMG), setting supervisory expectations for macroeconomic models — statistical constructions linking macro (independent) variables to risk or business metrics such as PD, Credit Index, LGD, cost of funds or revenues. It covers models used for Expected Credit Loss estimation and for internal and regulatory stress testing, and stresses that statistical performance must be balanced against business intuition, economic meaning and implementation constraints. Binds institutions within the MMS/MMG scope that choose to use such models, including UAE-incorporated groups' branches and subsidiaries.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
