# External Shari'ah Auditor

Concept node for the External Shari'ah Auditor: an independent, central-bank-approved external Shari'ah audit firm a Takaful company may appoint to assess Shari'ah compliance against central bank regulations and standards and HSA/ISSC resolutions. The auditor must meet the ISSC before finalising its report and must report to the board and ISSC on compliance status, Shari'ah non-compliance risks, the quality of the risk management system for those risks, and other significant matters. The related article also repeals the prior 2019 Insurance Authority decision on the Shari'a controller's role.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee|Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When the external Shari'ah audit encounters a matter not covered by the enumerated sources of Shari'ah provisions, the auditor cannot resolve it on its own authority and must escalate to the institution's Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee. This is an express obligation chain: the ISSC holds the resolution-issuing competence within the institution, and the external auditor's role is verification against those resolutions, not independent Shari'ah determination. Practically, an audit conclusion on a novel product or structure is only defensible if an ISSC resolution was first obtained, so the absence of one is itself a process defect.
