# Article 10: Financial Inclusion

Labelled as the financial inclusion node, but the attached material addresses access-related conduct rules: account closure by the institution with 60 calendar days' written notice and written reasons (subject to a financial crime compliance exception), and strict limits on blocking accounts, transactions, funds or privileges except under Central Bank request, applicable UAE law, or a court order, with record-keeping for Central Bank review. It also covers marketing conduct — no unsolicited marketing calls without express consent, restrictions on Exchange Houses selling Credit Products or facilitating bank account opening, and the rule that silence or inaction is never consent to a new or optional Financial Product, Service or Fee. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions, including Exchange Houses.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, AML/CTF, payments
