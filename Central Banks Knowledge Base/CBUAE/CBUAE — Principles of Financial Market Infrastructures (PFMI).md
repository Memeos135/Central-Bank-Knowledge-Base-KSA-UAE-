# Principles of Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMI)

This node is the defined concept of the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures — the CPMI-IOSCO international standards for payment systems, central securities depositories, securities settlement systems, central counterparties and trade repositories. Under the Retail Payment Systems Regulation, designated RPS must observe and comply with the relevant PFMI principles on an ongoing basis, in addition to the Regulation's own ongoing requirements, and the Central Bank may impose requirements higher than the PFMI on risk-specific or general policy grounds. It binds System Operators and Settlement Institutions of designated systems in their ongoing operations and reviews.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
