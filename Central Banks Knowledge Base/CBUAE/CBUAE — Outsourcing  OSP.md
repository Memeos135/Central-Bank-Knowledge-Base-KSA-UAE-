# Outsourcing / OSP

Node covering the outsourcing limb of the CBUAE Guidelines for Financial Institutions adopting Enabling Technologies, focused on outsourcing of cloud computing services. It sets supervisory expectations for a pre-engagement outsourcing risk assessment (materiality of the outsourced service, due diligence on the provider, benefits weighed against risks), a documented governance framework allocating roles between the institution and the provider, senior management accountability for reliance on providers for material services, and retention of documentation (strategy, materiality and risk assessments, due diligence, contracts) for review, audit and supervision. Addressed to licensed Institutions adopting these technologies; it is guidance-level expectation rather than a self-standing rulebook, and it operates alongside the outsourcing requirements of the relevant Supervisory Authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
