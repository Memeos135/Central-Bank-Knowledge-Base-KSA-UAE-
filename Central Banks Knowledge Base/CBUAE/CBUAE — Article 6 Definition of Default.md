# Article 6: Definition of Default

Article 6 sets the regulatory definition of Default for an Obligor, triggered by either non-payment or unlikeliness to pay. Non-payment arises where a material credit obligation is past due beyond 90 days, with a specific rule treating overdrafts as past due once a contractual or internal limit is breached or a lower limit is communicated; unlikeliness to pay arises where full repayment is improbable, and LFIs must document criteria and early warning signals using both financial and non-financial indicators. It binds all LFIs and feeds directly into SICR identification, classification and provisioning.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
