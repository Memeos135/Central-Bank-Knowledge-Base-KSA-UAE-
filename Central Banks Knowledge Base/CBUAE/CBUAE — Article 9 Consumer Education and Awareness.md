# Article 9: Consumer Education and Awareness

Article 9 requires Licensed Financial Institutions to establish a Consumer Education and Awareness (CEA) function delivering generic, minimally promotional educational programs for Consumers and the public, supporting the Central Bank law's financial literacy mandate. It mandates an annual summary report to the Central Bank by 31 January covering the prior year's activities and the coming year's proposed programme, permits collaboration with other organisations provided the institution's own consumer base has full access, and expects pre-release testing of initiatives. Adjacent material covers assessing Consumer needs, suitability and affordability, 5-year retention of profiling assessments, and the duty to act in the Consumer's best interest.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
