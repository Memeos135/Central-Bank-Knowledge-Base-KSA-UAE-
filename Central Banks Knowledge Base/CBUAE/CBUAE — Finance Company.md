# Finance Company

Defined-term node for "Finance Company" under the CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation, covering the entity licensed to carry out the listed financing activities and subject to the Regulation's funding, licensing and audit obligations. Key constraints include taking deposits and issuing certificates of deposit only from/to Juridical Persons (with a strict prohibition on individual deposits and accounts other than loan accounts), foreign funding capped at 25% of aggregate funding plus deposits with hedging, licence updates for new activities and a Central Bank no-objection for new product lines within licensed activities. It also carries external audit duties, including Central Bank pre-approval of the auditor, firm rotation every six years and audit-partner rotation every three years.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
