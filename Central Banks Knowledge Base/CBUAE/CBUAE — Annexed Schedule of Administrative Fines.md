# Annexed Schedule of Administrative Fines

The schedule annexed to Cabinet Resolution No. (7) of 2019, forming an integral part of it, listing each insurance-sector violation against a fixed dirham fine amount. Entries cover company-level breaches such as failure to pay contractual compensation on an insured event, non-compliance with investment rules, solvency margin and minimum guarantee fund, technical provisions, asset-liability matching and reinsurance criteria. Amounts are subject to doubling on repetition within a year and may be amended by the Cabinet.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
