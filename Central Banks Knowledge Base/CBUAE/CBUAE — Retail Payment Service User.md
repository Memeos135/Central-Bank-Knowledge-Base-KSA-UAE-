# Retail Payment Service User

Definitional and obligations node for the Retail Payment Service User, the person on whose behalf Payment Accounts, Payment Instruments and payment initiation or aggregation services are provided. It anchors the transparency duties owed to users: pre-contractual disclosure of terms in clear language in Arabic and English, disclosure to existing users on request, at least 30 calendar days' advance notice of changes, and a right to terminate free of charge if revised terms are not accepted. Binds Payment Service Providers in their dealings with each new and existing user.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
