# Article 5: Reporting of Intragroup Transactions

Article 5 obliges the Parent to report significant Intragroup Transactions to the Central Bank at the frequency and deadlines set by it. Transactions materially affecting the solvency or liquidity of the Group or of a participating Company are deemed significant, with a minimum list to be considered (investments, intercompany balances and cash-pooling, guarantees and letters of credit, derivatives, dividends and interest, reinsurance, service or cost-sharing arrangements, and asset purchase/sale/lease). The Central Bank sets thresholds based on solvency capital requirements and/or technical provisions and may designate always-reportable transaction types.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
