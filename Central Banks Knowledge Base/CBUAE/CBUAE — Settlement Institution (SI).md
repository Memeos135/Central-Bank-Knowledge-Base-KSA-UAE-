# Settlement Institution (SI)

Defines the Settlement Institution (SI) as an institution providing settlement services and settlement accounts (single- or multi-currency) to a Financial Infrastructure System, and in some cases granting participants access to intraday liquidity. The SI sits alongside the System Operator as an addressee of the RPS licensing, designation and ongoing requirements regime. Useful for allocating settlement-layer obligations and identifying who must comply with finality and oversight rules.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
