# Article 2: Licensing

Licensing article of the CBUAE Insurance Brokerage Regulation: it prohibits any person from carrying on Insurance Brokerage in the UAE without a Central Bank licence, and bars insurance/reinsurance companies from engaging unlicensed intermediaries. It sets the three licence categories (primary insurance, reinsurance, or both), incorporation and application conditions (including bank guarantee support), and a six-month window to obtain local licences and commence activity. Binds applicants and licensed Insurance Brokers, and indirectly the Companies that deal with them.
