# Company Board of Directors

Node on the composition, structure and governance duties of the Board of Directors of a CBUAE-licensed insurance/takaful company. It sets quantitative and qualitative requirements: board size and term limits, all-non-executive composition with a minimum proportion of Independent Members, female candidate quota, multiple-directorship caps, minimum meeting frequency, an independent board secretary, minutes, government shareholder representation, related-party exposure controls, and prior CBUAE non-objection for nomination, appointment, renewal and removal. It binds the company and its individual board members.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
