# Trading Book Interest Rate Risk

## Why this community

Market-risk capital treatment of interest rate exposures in the trading book, splitting the charge into issuer-specific risk and general market risk and prescribing the permitted measurement methodologies.

## How members connect

- Hierarchical decomposition: the trading book interest rate risk charge is defined as the sum of a specific risk component and a general market risk component.
- General market risk is operationalised through two alternative prescribed measurement approaches (maturity-band and duration-based), so the choice of method is an exercise of a permitted option, not a deviation.
- The generic interest rate risk node links the trading-book treatment to the wider rate-risk concept, marking the scope boundary between trading book capital charges and banking book rate risk.
- All members share the same defined terms used for capital adequacy reporting, so definitional drift between them changes the capital number.
