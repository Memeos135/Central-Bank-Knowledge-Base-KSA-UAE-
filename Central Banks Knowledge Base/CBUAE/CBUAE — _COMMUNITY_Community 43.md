# Proliferation Financing Sanctions

## Why this community

Targeted financial sanctions, particularly the proliferation-financing limb: the UN-to-national transmission of listings, the domestic competent authority, and the resulting freezing and screening duties, including in a trade-finance context.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from UNSC resolutions (including UNSCR 1540) down through the Cabinet Decision on TFS and the 2025 Executive Regulations, with the Executive Office as the designated implementing authority.
- Shared defined term: 'Targeted Financial Sanctions' links the general regime node to its trade-based money laundering application, so the same freezing/no-dealing obligation applies across product lines.
- FATF Recommendation 7 supplies the international standard the national instruments implement, which matters when interpreting scope and timeliness of freezing.
- Sanctions screening of trade transactions is the operational consequence of the listing obligations — an obligation to screen counterparties, goods and routes, not a discretionary control.
