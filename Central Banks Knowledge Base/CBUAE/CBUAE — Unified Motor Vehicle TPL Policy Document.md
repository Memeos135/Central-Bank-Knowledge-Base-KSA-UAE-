# Unified Motor Vehicle TPL Policy Document

Node for the standard-form Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Third Party Liability issued under the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies Regulation (version 1). It sets the mandatory policy wording: insurer obligations on repair and parts replacement, depreciation treatment, grounds for insurer recourse against the insured or driver (including intentional accidents, prohibited driving, undeclared trailers, off-road use, theft), termination and premium refund rules, and general conditions. It governs the contractual relationship between the insurer, the insured/driver and injured third parties for motor vehicles licensed in the State.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
