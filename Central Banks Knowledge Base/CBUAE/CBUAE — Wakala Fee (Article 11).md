# Wakala Fee (Article 11)

Article 11 regulates the Wakala Fee payable from the Takaful Insurance Fund to the Company for managing the Fund. The fee must be pre-determined under Central Bank rules, clearly disclosed, calculated by a known mechanism based on a technical study (lump sum or percentage of Contribution), stipulated in the Participation Membership Policy, balanced across the interests of the Company, Fund, Participants and Beneficiaries, approved annually by the ISSC, and capped at any maximum set by the Central Bank. It also addresses deduction of Wakala Fees for insurance and investment operations and related charges borne by the Fund and the Participants' Investment Account.
