# General Market Risk

Concept node covering the general market risk capital charge under the CBUAE market risk standard, i.e. the charge on a bank's overall net position in a given market (for equities, the net of longs and shorts, charged at 8%), as distinct from the specific risk charge on gross positions. It also frames how the charge is applied market-by-market and extended in modified form to equity derivatives, stock indices and index arbitrage. It binds banks calculating trading book capital requirements under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
