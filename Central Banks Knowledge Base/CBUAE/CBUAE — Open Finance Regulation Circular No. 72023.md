# Open Finance Regulation Circular No. 7/2023

This node is the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation issued under Circular No. 7/2023, which creates the Open Finance Licence for Data Sharing and Service Initiation, designates certain Licensees as Persons Deemed Licensed, and phases mandatory Open Finance access starting with banks (including foreign branches) and insurance companies. It clarifies that Technical Service Providers need no licence where limited to support services, that regulatory responsibility and liability cannot be outsourced to them or any third party, and that the Central Bank may levy fees to fund the Open Finance Framework and API Hub. It binds Licensees, Open Finance Providers, Persons Deemed Licensed and the API Hub.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, data/credit info, banking prudential
