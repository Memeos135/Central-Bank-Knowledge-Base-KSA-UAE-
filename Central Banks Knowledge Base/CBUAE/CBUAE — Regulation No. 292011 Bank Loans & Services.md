# Regulation No. 29/2011 Bank Loans & Services

Referenced instrument: the CBUAE regulation on bank loans and other services offered to individual customers, cited in the financial sanctions regulation as the source of the duty to close accounts without penalty after one year and to issue an appropriate certificate within seven days of application, alongside the standard account-opening agreement requirements. It applies to commercial banks dealing with individual (retail) customers. Here it functions as the underlying substantive rule that the strict-liability sanctions regime enforces.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential
