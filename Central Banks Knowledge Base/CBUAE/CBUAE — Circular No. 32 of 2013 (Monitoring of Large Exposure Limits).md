# Circular No. 32 of 2013 (Monitoring of Large Exposure Limits)

Reference node for the predecessor CBUAE regime on monitoring large exposure limits (Circular No. 32/2013 and its guidelines), superseded by the current Large Exposures regulation and relevant mainly as the comparison baseline for grandfathering. The surrounding context sets out the current core definitions and thresholds: a large exposure at 10% of Tier 1 capital, the 25% single-counterparty limit, the 15% G-SIB-to-G-SIB limit, treatment of UAE local government exposures, and the measurement rules for exposure values. Relevant to CBUAE-licensed banks assessing legacy versus current limits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
