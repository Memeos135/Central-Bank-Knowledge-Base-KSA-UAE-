# Guidance Note Re Shari'ah Compliance Function

Instrument node for the CBUAE Guidance Note on the Shari'ah Compliance Function at Islamic Financial Institutions, issued to support implementation of the corresponding binding Standard. It applies to all IFIs, but on a comply-or-equivalent basis: institutions may follow the guidance or apply equivalent criteria to meet the Standard's requirements. It is to be read together with the Standard and with Higher Shari'ah Authority standards and resolutions notified to IFIs, and it elaborates the SCF review cycle from annual planning through field review, issues, reports and progress monitoring.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
