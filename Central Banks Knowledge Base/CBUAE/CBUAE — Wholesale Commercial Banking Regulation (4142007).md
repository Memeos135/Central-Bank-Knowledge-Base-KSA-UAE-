# Wholesale Commercial Banking Regulation (41/4/2007)

Reference node for the superseded CBUAE Wholesale Commercial Banking Regulation (Board Resolution No. 41/4/2007), which is expressly repealed and replaced by the Restricted Licence Banks Regulation (Circular 23/2022), together with Board Resolution 21/2/88 and Board Circulars 495, 282 and 276. Licences previously issued under the 2007 wholesale banking regime are deemed to continue under the new Regulation from its effective date, with non-compliant banks required to file a compliance plan with the Central Bank within 90 days. Relevant to legacy wholesale/investment banks in the UAE and to anyone tracing the legal lineage of current restricted-licence requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
