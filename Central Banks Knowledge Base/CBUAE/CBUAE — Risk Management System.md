# Risk Management System

Defines Risk Management for insurance companies and sets expectations for integrating it into organisational structure and decision-making: Board-set risk appetite, entity-wide assessment across all risk types including emerging risks, timely and accurate management information, proportionality of systems and controls to nature/size/complexity, and regular review. It links to the solvency assessment framework by mapping main risk areas — life and non-life underwriting risk, market and liquidity (investment) risk and its sub-modules (interest rate, equity, and related sensitivities) — to Solvency Capital Requirement modules.
