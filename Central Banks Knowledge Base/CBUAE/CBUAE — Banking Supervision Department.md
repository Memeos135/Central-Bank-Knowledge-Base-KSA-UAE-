# Banking Supervision Department

Identifies the CBUAE Banking Supervision Department as the supervisory channel through which Licensed Persons obtain letters of no objection and file notifications — covering senior appointments (Manager in Charge, Compliance Officer, Alternate Compliance Officer), external auditor appointment, foreign and hedge account openings, and special products or services. It also receives resignation and vacancy notifications, often in parallel with the AML/CFT Supervision Department. Relevant for identifying the correct regulatory counterparty and filing deadlines.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
