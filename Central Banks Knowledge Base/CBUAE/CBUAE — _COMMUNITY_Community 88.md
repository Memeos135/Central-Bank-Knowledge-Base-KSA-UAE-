# Virtual Asset Travel Rule

## Why this community

Application of the FATF-style travel rule to virtual asset transfers: which participant (originating VASP, intermediary, beneficiary VASP) must collect, transmit, retain and screen originator/beneficiary identification data, and how anonymity-enhancing features such as privacy tokens constrain compliance.

## How members connect

- Shared defined terms chain the obligation: a Virtual Asset Transfer is framed by the Originator/Beneficiary pair, with the VASP as the obliged entity and the Intermediary Provider carrying forward the same data set along the payment chain.
- Hierarchy runs from the Cabinet Decision down to the UAE Virtual Assets Travel Rule instrument, which is the operative source of the data-transmission and record-keeping duties cited here (note: a non-KSA comparator, relevant only for cross-border/benchmarking analysis).
- Privacy Token links in as a scope/risk limitation - transfer mechanics that obscure originator or beneficiary data bear on whether the transmission obligation can be met and on the AML/CTF risk assessment.
- Cross-regime interaction with core AML/CTF CDD: travel-rule data is an overlay on customer identification, not a substitute for it, and intermediary reliance raises third-party CDD reliance questions.
