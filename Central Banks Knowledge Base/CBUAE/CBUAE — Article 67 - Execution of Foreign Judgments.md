# Article 67 - Execution of Foreign Judgments

Within Part Three of Chapter Eight of the UAE AML/CFT Executive Regulations, this article permits execution in the State of foreign judgments or judicial orders imposing seizure, freezing, other provisional measures, or confiscation in any form over criminal property or funds of equivalent value. It signals a relaxed procedural route for recognising such foreign orders. Directed at competent judicial authorities, with downstream effects on holders of the targeted assets.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, other
