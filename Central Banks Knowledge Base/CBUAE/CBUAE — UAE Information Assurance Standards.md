# UAE Information Assurance Standards

Concept node for the UAE Information Assurance Standards as the minimum technology and information security benchmark under the retail payments regime. Payment Service Providers must apply and meet at least these standards as amended, within a broader technology risk management framework covering IT governance, an independent technology audit function and Board-level responsibility; Card Schemes must meet these standards together with PCI DSS and report compliance. Binds Payment Service Providers, Card Schemes and their Boards or designated committees.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
