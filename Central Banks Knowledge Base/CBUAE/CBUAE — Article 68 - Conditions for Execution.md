# Article 68 - Conditions for Execution

A Chapter Eight provision of the UAE AML/CFT Executive Regulations concerning the conditions and limits applicable to executing international cooperation requests, read together with Article 60 (no refusal on grounds of fiscal or customs matters, institutional secrecy, or differences in authority status; notification of reasons for refusal or deferral; dual criminality only for coercive measures). It governs when assistance may be granted, deferred or conditioned. It binds the Concerned Authorities; the supplied context does not reproduce the article's own wording, so verify the operative text.
