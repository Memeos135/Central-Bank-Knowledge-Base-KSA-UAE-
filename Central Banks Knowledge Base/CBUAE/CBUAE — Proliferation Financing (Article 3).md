# Proliferation Financing (Article 3)

This node concerns proliferation financing under the 2025 UAE AML law, which treats it alongside money laundering and terrorism financing within the defined term "the Crime" and as a predicate offence. Related definitions capture funds used or allocated for proliferation financing, suspicious transactions referable to it, and targeted financial sanctions implemented by the Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation, including UNSC Chapter VII designations. Relevant to Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and Virtual Asset Service Providers for screening, reporting and freezing duties.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
