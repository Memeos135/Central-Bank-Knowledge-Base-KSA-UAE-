# Financial Institutions

A defined-scope concept: under Article 2 of the Executive Regulations, Financial Institutions are persons carrying out listed financial activities commercially for or on behalf of a Customer, including deposit-taking, lending and trade finance, financial leasing (excluding consumer-product leasing), money or value transfer services, issuance and management of means of payment including e-money, and guarantees and financial commitments. This classification determines who is subject to CDD, reporting, record-keeping and related AML/CFT duties, and is the trigger for risk-based supervision and inspection by the Supervisory Authority. Relevant for assessing whether financing, BNPL-type lending or payment-service activity falls inside the AML perimeter.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
