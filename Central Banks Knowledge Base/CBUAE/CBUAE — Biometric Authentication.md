# Biometric Authentication

A concept node from CBUAE digital ID guidance covering the authentication stage of a digital ID system: confirming that the claimant is the same person who was identity-proofed, enrolled and credentialed. It distinguishes knowledge, possession and inherence (biometric) authentication factors, addresses multi-factor authentication for high-risk customer activities such as personal-data changes, third-party payee registration and high-value transfers, and discusses phishing-resistant authenticators and continuous/behavioural biometric authentication. It informs how licensed financial institutions calibrate authentication controls for ongoing account access and fraud/ML risk.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, payments
