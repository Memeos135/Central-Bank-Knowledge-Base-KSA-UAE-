# Customer Consent Requirements

Sets the consent gateway for telemarketing: a Licensed Financial Institution must obtain the Customer's prior express consent before conducting Telemarketing, with the consent specifying preferred language, chosen channels, contact methods (including natural agents, AI-based agents or robocalls) and the Product/Service types covered. Terms and conditions for consent must be clear, unambiguous and in the Customer's preferred language, and robust age verification must be applied at the point of consent. Permitted consent methods include online sign-up with explicit written consent, signed physical forms, and Customer-initiated requests.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
