# TPA Licensing Instructions (Resolution 9/2011)

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 9 of 2011 issuing the Instructions for Licensing Health Insurance Third Party Administrators and regulating and supervising their business, made under the Federal Law No. 6 of 2007. It sets the definitions, licensing and registration regime, conduct obligations, supervisory inspection powers, joint liability with insurers, fees, and transitional rectification period for existing operators. It binds entities administering health insurance claims in the UAE and, in parts, the insurance companies contracting with them.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
