# National Committee

Concept node for the National Committee for AML/CFT/CPF under the UAE 2025 AML Decree Law, the operational coordination body that develops and implements national AML/CFT/CPF strategy, collects and analyses statistics to assess effectiveness, coordinates information exchange among represented entities, monitors the Mutual Evaluation process, and represents the State internationally. It is supported by a General Secretariat headed by a Secretary-General appointed by Cabinet, who also serves as Deputy Chairman and sits on the Supreme Committee. It binds government authorities and reports upward to the Supreme Committee; it is not a direct supervisor of financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
