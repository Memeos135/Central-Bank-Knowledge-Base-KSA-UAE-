# ML/FT Risk Assessment

A concept node on the enterprise-wide ML/FT risk assessment obligation for Licensed Exchange Houses under Article 4 of the AML-CFT Decision and Paragraph 16.2 of the Standards. It requires a documented methodology, documented findings (inherent risk, controls evaluation, residual risk, mitigation), a written risk appetite statement, and mechanisms to supply the assessment to the CBUAE on request; senior management/board must review and approve at least annually. The assessment must be proportionate to the LEH's nature, size and complexity, updated on material change, and must feed the AML/CFT program's policies, controls and training.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
