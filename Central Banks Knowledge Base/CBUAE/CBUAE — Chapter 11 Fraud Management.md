# Chapter 11: Fraud Management

Chapter 11 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets minimum requirements for an Anti-Fraud Framework that every Licensed Person must implement to prevent, detect, investigate and respond to fraud incidents. It classifies fraud as internal (by employees) or external (by outside parties) and gives a non-exhaustive list of conduct treated as fraud, including misappropriation, misrepresentation of financial and non-financial statements, corruption (bribery, illegal gratuities) and misconduct such as breaches of internal policy or of applicable laws, regulations, notices and the Standards.
