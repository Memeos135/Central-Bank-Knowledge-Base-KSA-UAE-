# Website Management Requirements

The website/smart-application obligations of the Electronic Insurance Regulations (Chapter Three, including Articles 7 and 10), covering governance and operational duties for the insurer's electronic channel. Requirements include establishing an IT department responsible for the official website, prior Authority approval before outsourcing website management, appointing a communication officer, scalability, 24/7 availability with limited maintenance windows and notification of outages, prohibited-content and legality checks, secure handling of sensitive user data, and security auditing. It binds insurance companies and insurance-related professions, and indirectly the outsourced website operator.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other, consumer protection
