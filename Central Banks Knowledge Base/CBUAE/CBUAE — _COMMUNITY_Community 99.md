# Governance & Complaint Management

## Why this community

Institutional governance and accountability: board and senior management responsibilities, board committees, conflicts and related-party controls, Shari'ah governance, SAMA examinations, and the consumer-facing complaint handling function.

## How members connect

- Governance hierarchy: board of directors delegates to senior management and mandates committees (audit, risk, compensation), each with its own defined remit under the corporate governance framework article.
- Integrity controls are linked definitionally — conflict of interest policy and related-party transaction rules address the same independence risk from different angles.
- Consumer-protection strand attaches to governance: the complaint management function and its article are anchored in the fair treatment of consumers principle, making complaint outcomes a board-oversight matter.
- Supervisory strand: the examinations chapter and transmittal letter route findings to the manager in charge and the board, closing the loop between governance duties and SAMA enforcement.
- Shari'ah governance (external Shari'ah audit, compliance with Islamic Shari'ah provisions) is a parallel assurance line reporting into the same senior management/board structure.
