# Cancellation of Previous Regulation (Article 28)

Article 28 is the repeal and continuity clause: this Regulation cancels and supersedes the 2022 CBUAE Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance, and confirms that Takaful Insurance Companies remain subject to all CBUAE prudential regulations, standards and instructions for insurance business unless the Central Bank states that particular provisions do not apply to them. The same passage carries Article 29 (violations may attract supervisory action and sanctions, including withdrawing or restricting Senior Management or Board powers, appointing interim management, or barring individuals from the UAE insurance sector) and Article 30 (the Regulatory Development Department is the reference for interpretation).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
