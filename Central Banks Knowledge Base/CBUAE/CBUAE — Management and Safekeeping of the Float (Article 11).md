# Management and Safekeeping of the Float (Article 11)

Node for Article 11 of the CBUAE SVF Regulation on management, protection and safekeeping of the Float. It requires accurate and timely maintenance of the customer ledger so aggregate balances always reflect the Float, segregation of Float assets (cash and bank deposits) from the Licensee's own funds and funds of other business lines, internal controls against theft, fraud and misappropriation, liquidity-focused management of the Float to ensure funds are always available for redemption, and a prohibition on business models relying on investment returns from the Float. Material non-compliance or unresolved reconciliation discrepancies must be reported immediately to the Central Bank with rectification measures.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
