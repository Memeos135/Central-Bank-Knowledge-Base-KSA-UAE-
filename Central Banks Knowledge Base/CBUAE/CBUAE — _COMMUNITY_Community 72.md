# Insurance Corporate Governance

## Why this community

Governance, risk management and internal control requirements applicable to insurance undertakings, including board and control-function duties, outsourcing accountability and fraud countermeasures.

## How members connect

- Clear instrument hierarchy: primary insurance law → corporate governance and risk/internal-control regulations issued by circular → detailed standards elaborating each regulation's articles.
- Paired structure: the governance standards and the risk management/internal controls standards are complementary, with control functions, the three-lines concept and board oversight defined once and relied on across both.
- Cross-references outward: the corporate governance regulation cites the financial (prudential) regulations and the securities-regulator governance rules for listed insurers, so a listed insurance company faces cumulative obligations.
- Scope-sensitive topics: outsourcing and insurance fraud are treated as governance responsibilities that cannot be delegated away, keeping accountability with the licensed entity's board and senior management.
