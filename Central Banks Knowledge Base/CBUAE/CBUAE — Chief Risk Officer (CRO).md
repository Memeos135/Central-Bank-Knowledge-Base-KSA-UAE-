# Chief Risk Officer (CRO)

Node on the Chief Risk Officer's responsibilities under the CBUAE Model Management Standards. The CRO must ensure that model design and usage across the entire institution — not merely the risk function — is appropriate for decision-making, and that Model Risk is identified, measured, monitored, reported and mitigated. This sits within a wider accountability chain: the Model Oversight Committee reports to Senior Management and the Board, which under the Risk Management Regulation 153/2018 remains ultimately accountable for model usage and must set the institution's model risk appetite.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
