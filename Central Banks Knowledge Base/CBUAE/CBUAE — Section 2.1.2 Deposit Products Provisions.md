# Section 2.1.2 Deposit Products Provisions

Product-specific disclosure and conduct requirements for Deposit Products, layered on top of the general provisions of Section 2.1.1. It addresses pre-contract disclosure of low-cost savings/current accounts, minimum balance and initial deposit conditions and consequences, expected annual interest/profit rate and payment frequency, all standard and optional fees, and account closure with 60 calendar days' advance written notice and reasons, subject to an exception permitting immediate closure or blocking where there are reasonable grounds of financial crime or fraud risk. Additional Shari'ah disclosure duties apply to IFIs offering compliant deposit products (mudarabah profit-sharing ratio, weights, distribution method, eligibility thresholds).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, AML/CTF
