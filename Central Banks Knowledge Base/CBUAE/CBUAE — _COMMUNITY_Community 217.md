# Accounting & Audit

## Why this community

Accounting and audit obligations on a licensed entity — maintaining proper books and records and submitting to independent audit as a supervisory assurance mechanism for SAMA.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster: the accounting/audit duty sits upstream of the reporting obligations in the neighbouring clusters, since audited records are the basis of what is reported to SAMA.
- Linked by shared addressee (the licensee) and by SAMA's supervisory power to inspect records and rely on external auditors.
