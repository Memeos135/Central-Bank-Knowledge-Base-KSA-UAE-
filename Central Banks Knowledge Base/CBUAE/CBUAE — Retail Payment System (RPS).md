# Retail Payment System (RPS)

This node is the defined concept of a Retail Payment System under the Retail Payment Systems Regulation — a system providing transfer, clearing or settlement of retail payment obligations, operated in the State or capable of handling obligations in the Currency, any currency or a Regulated Medium of Exchange. The definition determines which arrangements can be licensed and designated as systemically important, and expressly captures payment card systems and clearing and settlement systems supporting SVF schemes. Scope carve-outs matter: bank-operated systems serving only the bank's own customers are generally not designated, but may be if the bank provides RPS services to other payment service providers or financial institutions.

**Regimes:** payments
