# Price Comparison Websites Regulation

Chapter Four of the Electronic Insurance Regulations, governing insurers' and intermediaries' dealings with Price Comparison Websites. It prohibits companies and insurance-related professions from dealing with such websites except through Insurance Brokers, binds the insurer to the prices and coverage displayed, and imposes registration, corporate-purpose, activity-restriction and agreement-filing conditions on comparison sites (referral fee as lump sum only, no commission, no policy issuance or marketing, customer contact reserved to the broker), with escalating sanctions up to site blocking. It binds insurance companies, insurance brokers and the operators of registered comparison websites.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk
