# SCA Public Joint Stock Companies Governance Regulation

Reference node for the SCA Public Joint Stock Companies' Governance Guide (Chairman of the Authority's Board Resolution No. 3/Chairman of 2020) and the Commercial Companies Law (Federal Law No. 32 of 2021), which continue to apply to listed insurance Companies. The CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation and Standards are stated to be additional to those provisions, and an explicit conflict rule gives the Central Bank's requirements precedence where the SCA Regulation contradicts them. Useful for resolving dual-regulator governance questions for publicly listed insurers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
