# Zakat Fund Account

## Why this community

Article-level requirement concerning the zakat fund account within a takaful/insurance structure - its establishment, funding and the segregation of amounts held for zakat purposes from other accounts.

## How members connect

- Singleton article node; its meaning is fixed by the fund-segregation and surplus-distribution definitions of the same instrument.
- Links conceptually to the separation of participants' and shareholders' accounts, so it constrains how surplus and contributions may be applied.
- Cross-regime touchpoint: accounting and Shariah-governance expectations sit outside SAMA's article but shape how the account is operated and reported.
