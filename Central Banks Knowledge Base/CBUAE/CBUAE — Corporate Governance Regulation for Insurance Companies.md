# Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies

CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation for Insurance Companies (Circular 24/2022) with accompanying Standards, issued under the Central Bank Law and supplementing Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 and related Insurance Authority decisions. It sets the overarching prudential governance framework — board composition and independence, organisational structure, control environment, risk management responsibilities, compensation, financial reporting/external audit and disclosure, including a published corporate governance statement. It binds all licensed insurance Companies on a solo and Group-wide basis, applies proportionately to smaller Companies, and applies to branches of foreign Companies (with an exception for Article 5 and an option for equivalent arrangements).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
