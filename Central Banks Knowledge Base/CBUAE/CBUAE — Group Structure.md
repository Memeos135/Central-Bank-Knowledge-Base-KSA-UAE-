# Group Structure

Section 7 of the CBUAE Corporate Governance Standards for Banks (83/2019), setting out group-level governance duties owed by the Board of a Bank acting as Controlling Shareholder — a group corporate governance framework, group management and internal control structure, intragroup conflict-of-interest controls, approval policies for new legal entities, and information exchange across entities. It also allocates responsibility to subsidiary/branch boards for assessing compatibility of group policy with local legal and regulatory requirements, and requires the Board and Senior Management to avoid or mitigate risks from complex or non-transparent structures lacking economic substance. It binds banks licensed by the Central Bank that head or sit within a banking group, including foreign branches through equivalent local structures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
