# Three Lines of Defense

A concept node within the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines for Financial Institutions describing the three-lines-of-defence structure that an AML/CFT programme must cover: internal policies, procedures, controls and employee training (first line); a designated compliance function headed by a compliance officer/MLRO (second line); and independent audit testing programme effectiveness (third line). It is framed as guidance interpreting the UAE AML-CFT Law and Cabinet Decision, so the underlying statutory articles, not the guidance, carry the enforceable obligation. It binds licensed financial institutions in the UAE (banks, finance companies, exchange houses and MSBs, insurers and intermediaries, securities firms, VASPs), with calibration permitted for business nature, size and complexity.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When testing whether the compliance officer arrangement is adequate, place the role within the mandated three lines of defence rather than judging it in isolation.
