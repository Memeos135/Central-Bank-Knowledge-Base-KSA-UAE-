# Designated Infrastructure

Sets out how the Central Bank designates a Financial Market Infrastructure as systemically important ("Designated Infrastructure"), the qualifying conditions (operating in the State, Dirham clearing/settlement capacity, or retail/wholesale transfer capacity in any currency), and the notice, representation and decision timelines. It covers deemed designation for infrastructures established under Article 153, coordination where the FMI is licensed by another regulator, abroad or in a Financial Free Zone, and grievance rights before the Grievances and Appeals Committee. Binds FMI operators and their Settlement Institutions.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
