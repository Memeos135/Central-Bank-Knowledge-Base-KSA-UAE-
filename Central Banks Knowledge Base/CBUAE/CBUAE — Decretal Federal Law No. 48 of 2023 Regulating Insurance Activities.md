# Decretal Federal Law No. 48 of 2023 Regulating Insurance Activities

UAE primary legislation regulating insurance activities, cited as one of the two Central Bank Laws underpinning the CBUAE Representation Offices Regulation and referenced (with its Executive Regulations) as the "Insurance Law" in other CBUAE instruments. It provides the statutory basis for CBUAE licensing and supervision of insurance and reinsurance activity in the State, including the framework within which representation office rules operate. Binds insurers, reinsurers and related licensed entities operating in the UAE.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
