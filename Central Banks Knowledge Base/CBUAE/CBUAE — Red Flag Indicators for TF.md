# Red Flag Indicators for TF

Annex-style red-flag material in the CBUAE sanctions guidance covering terrorist financing, proliferation financing (drawn from FATF typologies) and sanctions circumvention indicators published by the Executive Office. Indicators span trade and shipping anomalies, shell and intermediary companies, linked counterparties, cash or precious metals use, and falsified documentation. Non-exhaustive detection aids for LFIs feeding monitoring, escalation and reporting obligations rather than standalone prohibitions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
