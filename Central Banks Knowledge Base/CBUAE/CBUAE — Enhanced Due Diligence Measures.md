# Enhanced Due Diligence Measures

Node grouping the additional/enhanced due diligence provisions (Articles 11–15) of the UAE AML/CTF Executive Regulations, including the exemption from identifying shareholders or beneficial owners of listed companies subject to adequate transparency-disclosure requirements and their controlled subsidiaries. It also sets due diligence and ongoing monitoring duties for beneficiaries of life and investment-linked insurance policies (naming, category/description, verification at payout, beneficiary as a risk factor), risk-based review of existing customers, and the prohibition on proceeding where due diligence cannot be applied. Binds Financial Institutions primarily for the insurance rules, and Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and VASPs for the general obligations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
