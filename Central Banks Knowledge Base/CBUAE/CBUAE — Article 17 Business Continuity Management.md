# Article 17: Business Continuity Management

Article 17 obliges a Licensed Person to identify and analyse risks that could disrupt its business and to maintain a business continuity management framework including a business continuity plan ensuring continuation, timely recovery or orderly scale-down of critical operations. The BCP must be tested at least annually, with senior management and responsible staff participating. It binds CBUAE-licensed exchange businesses and complements the risk management function requirements under the corporate governance article.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
