# Service Initiation

Defined activity under the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation: initiating, by electronic means, a Transaction relating to an Account or Product. The node covers the obligations attaching to the activity — it applies only to relevant Accounts and Products, requires the User's explicit consent and instructions, imposes information-provision, status-display and parity-of-treatment duties on the Service Owner, and allocates liability for non-execution, late or defective execution between the Service Initiation Provider and Service Owner. Relevant to licensed initiation providers and account/product-holding Licensees in the UAE.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
