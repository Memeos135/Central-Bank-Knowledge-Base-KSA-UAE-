# Shari'ah Compliance (Article 6)

Article 6 of the SME Market Conduct Regulation requires Islamic Financial Institutions (IFIs) to embed and uphold Shari'ah principles across reporting, business conduct, risk management and oversight, and to ensure staff dealing with SME Customers have sufficient knowledge of the Shari'ah aspects of the products and services offered. The node also carries the definitional framework for Shari'ah compliance, the Higher Shari'ah Authority (HSA) and the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC), whose fatwas and resolutions are binding. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions conducting all or part of their business in accordance with Islamic Shari'ah.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
