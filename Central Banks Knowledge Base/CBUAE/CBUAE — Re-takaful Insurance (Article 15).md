# Re-takaful Insurance (Article 15)

Article 15 requires the Takaful Insurance Fund to have an approved Re-takaful policy, prepared by the Company as agent of the Fund and approved by the Board and the ISSC, consistent with the business of the Company and Fund. Where risks are ceded to Re-takaful companies, the Fund must be the contracting party; use of conventional reinsurers is permitted only under specified controls, including prioritising Re-takaful capacity and demonstrating that takaful/re-takaful capacity is unavailable or insufficient. It binds Takaful companies, re-takaful companies and insurers/reinsurers operating takaful windows.
