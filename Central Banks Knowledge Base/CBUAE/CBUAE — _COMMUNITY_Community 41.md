# Customer Due Diligence Program

## Why this community

The preventive AML/CTF core: risk assessment, customer due diligence and record keeping obligations that a financial institution must build into its AML/CFT programme on a risk-sensitive basis.

## How members connect

- Obligation chain runs law/Cabinet Decision → Implementing Regulation → supervisory guidelines, with the July 2023 AML/CFT Guidelines citing the implementing regulation as its legal base (note this strand is UAE-sourced material; confirm jurisdictional fit before relying on it for KSA obligations).
- CDD is the hub definition: it drives customer risk profiling, ongoing monitoring, and the identification of Legal Persons and Legal Arrangements (beneficial ownership and control).
- The Risk-Based Approach conditions intensity of measures — Simplified Due Diligence and variants such as Customer Identification Diligence and Agent Due Diligence are calibrations of, not exceptions to, the CDD duty.
- Record Keeping and Required Record Types are the evidentiary back-end of CDD and monitoring, fixing what must be retained and made available to supervisors.
