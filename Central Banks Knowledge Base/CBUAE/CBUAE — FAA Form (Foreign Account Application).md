# FAA Form (Foreign Account Application)

Covers the Foreign Account Application (FAA) Form (Appendix 5), the prescribed application through which a Licensed Person seeks a Letter of No Objection from Banking Supervision before opening a hedge account with a financial institution inside or outside the country; hedge accounts may be used only to mitigate FX rate risk, never for speculation, with hedge accounting per IFRS and fully automated. The surrounding context sets out permitted remittance operations by licence category and the Designated Remittance Intermediate Account requirements. Binds Licensed Persons conducting FX and remittance business.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, governance/risk
