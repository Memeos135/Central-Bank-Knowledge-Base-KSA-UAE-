# Portfolio Scaling Approach

A section of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance describing the portfolio-level scaling approach as an alternative to transition matrices for modelling the term structure of point-in-time PD, based on modelling average PD across rating grades rather than every rating-to-rating transition. It is presented as suitable for portfolios with smaller data sets, subject to the institution justifying its suitability against alternatives and following the design and estimation decision process set out in the Model Management Standards. It binds banks and other CBUAE-licensed institutions developing IFRS 9/ECL credit models.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
