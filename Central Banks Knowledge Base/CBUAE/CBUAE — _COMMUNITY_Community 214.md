# Enforcement & Sanctions

## Why this community

Supervisory consequence layer: SAMA's power to act against a regulated entity for non-compliance with the instrument's obligations.

## How members connect

- Terminal node in the obligation chain — every substantive duty in the instrument is enforceable through this article.
- Draws its ultimate authority from the enabling statute (banking/payments law) that empowers SAMA to impose measures; the article is the instrument-level expression of that power.
- Operationally paired with the information-gathering power, which supplies the evidentiary basis for enforcement action.
