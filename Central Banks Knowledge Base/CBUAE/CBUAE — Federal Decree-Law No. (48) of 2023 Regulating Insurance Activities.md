# Federal Decree-Law No. (48) of 2023 Regulating Insurance Activities

Reference node for Federal Decree-Law No. (48) of 2023 Regulating Insurance Activities, the instrument that governed licensing and conduct of insurance and reinsurance business in the UAE and is the "Insurance Law" cross-referenced in CBUAE regulations. It is listed in the recitals of Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 and is repealed by that decree-law, which consolidates insurance business into the Central Bank law framework and carries over defined terms such as Insurance Company, Reinsurance Company, Takaful Insurance and Takaful Insurance Fund. Binds insurers, reinsurers, Takaful operators and related licensed persons.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
