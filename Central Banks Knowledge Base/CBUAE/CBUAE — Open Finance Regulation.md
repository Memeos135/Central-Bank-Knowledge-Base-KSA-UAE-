# Open Finance Regulation

The CBUAE Open Finance Regulation creates a dedicated Open Finance Licence for Data Sharing and/or Service Initiation, sets minimum requirements for Licensees mandated to provide Open Finance access, and identifies categories of Licensees treated as Persons Deemed Licensed. On-boarding of mandated Licensees is phased, starting with banks (including foreign bank branches) and insurance companies. Technical Service Providers supporting participants need no Open Finance Licence, but regulatory responsibility and liability cannot be transferred to them or any third party; the licence does not permit advice, arranging or mediating transactions, or holding customer funds, and the Central Bank may levy fees for the Open Finance Framework including the API Hub.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, governance/risk
