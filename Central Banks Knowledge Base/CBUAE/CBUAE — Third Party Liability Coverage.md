# Third Party Liability Coverage

Node covering the third party liability limits and insurer obligations under the UAE Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Third Party Liability (IA-BOD-RES 25/2016). It fixes compensation ceilings (notably AED 200,000 per deceased/disabled person for specified family members and certain drivers, and AED 2,000,000 per accident for property damage), obliges the insurer to meet judicially awarded amounts excluding fines, and to settle medical treatment costs directly with providers including via a letter of commitment. It binds insurance companies issuing compulsory motor cover in the UAE and confers direct claim rights on injured third parties.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
