# Product Checklist

Concept node for the appendix guidance on building a product review checklist for SCF reviews. It sets the coverage expected when checking a product for compliance with Islamic Shari'ah — the underlying structure, documentation templates, product manuals and workflows, accounting treatment, fees and charges, and the IT system's compatibility with the product's Shari'ah-compliant operation. It also indicates review frequency expectations for products, including a risk-based standard and a minimum review cycle.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
