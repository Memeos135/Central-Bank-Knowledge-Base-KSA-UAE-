# Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation

A cross-referenced CBUAE instrument, the Minimum Capital for Banks Regulation issued via Central Bank Circular No. 12/2021 (14/2/2021), which prescribes the capital that foreign bank branches licensed as restricted licence banks must maintain instead of the AED 1 billion paid-up capital floor applied to locally incorporated restricted licence banks. It is the governing reference for branch-level minimum capital under the Restricted Licence Banks Regulation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
