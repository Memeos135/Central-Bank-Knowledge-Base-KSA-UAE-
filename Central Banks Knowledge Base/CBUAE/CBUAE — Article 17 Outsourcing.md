# Article 17: Outsourcing

Article 17 governs Outsourcing by Insurance Brokers: contracts must bind service providers to the Regulation, the Broker stays fully liable for the provider's acts and for provider training and qualifications, material business activity Outsourcing needs Central Bank non-objection supported by a defined minimum file (materiality and risk assessments, due diligence outcome, internal audit and compliance sign-off, related arrangements, Representatives' approval), and no activity may be outsourced outside the State. The Central Bank decides case by case within twenty business days. Binds licensed Insurance Brokers; UAE/CBUAE regime.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
