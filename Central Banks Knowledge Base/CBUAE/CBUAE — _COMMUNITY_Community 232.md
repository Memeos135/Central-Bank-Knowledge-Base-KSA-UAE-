# Risk Management

## Why this community

Governance-level risk management obligation: the licensed entity must maintain an internal risk management function/framework proportionate to its activities, sitting within the prudential and governance regime rather than a single risk type.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster; the article operates as the umbrella risk obligation that more specific control articles (fraud, continuity, outsourcing) implement in practice.
- Legal linkage is hierarchical: framework-level duty set in the regulation, with board/senior management accountability for adequacy and review.
- Isolated in the graph, so cross-references must be traced outward to the specific-control articles rather than within the cluster.
