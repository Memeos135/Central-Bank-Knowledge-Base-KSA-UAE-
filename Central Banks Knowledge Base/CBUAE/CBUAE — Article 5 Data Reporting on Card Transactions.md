# Article 5: Data Reporting on Card Transactions

Article 5 sets strict liability data reporting requirements for card transactions, supported by definitions covering cashless payment modes (virtual wallets, digital cards, debit, credit, store and prepaid cards), ATMs, cash/cheque deposit machines, card BINs and the Integrated Regulatory Reporting System. Licensed Financial Institutions that fail to meet these card transaction reporting requirements face a fixed fine per violation.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk
