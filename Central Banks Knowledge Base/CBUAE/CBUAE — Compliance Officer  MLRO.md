# Compliance Officer / MLRO

Concept node for the Compliance Officer acting as the licensed financial institution's Money Laundering Reporting Officer under CBUAE AML/CFT guidance. It sets out the role's ownership of detecting ML/TF and illegal-organisation financing activity, reviewing and filing STRs/SARs with the FIU, taking post-filing actions (watchlisting, risk-rating changes, relationship measures), delivering staff training, and serving as primary liaison with the supervisor, FIU and law enforcement. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions through the designated individual and their accountability for an effective risk-based programme.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
