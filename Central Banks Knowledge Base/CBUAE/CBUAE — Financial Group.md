# Financial Group

A defined-term node from the UAE Cabinet Resolution issuing the Executive Regulations of the AML/CTF/CPF Decree-Law, sitting among the definitions of Financial Institutions, DNFBPs, Senior Management, and the originating/intermediary/beneficiary institutions in a funds-transfer chain. It matters mainly for group-wide AML/CFT obligations: supervisory authorities calibrate inspection frequency and risk assessment by reference to the characteristics and internal controls of Financial Groups, apply consolidated group-level supervision, and require additional measures (up to closure of operations) where a host country prevents proper implementation at foreign branches and majority-owned subsidiaries. It binds licensed Financial Institutions and their group structures, and the Supervisory Authority exercising oversight.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
