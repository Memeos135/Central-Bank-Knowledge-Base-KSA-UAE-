# IA Board Resolution No. 3 of 2010 (Code of Conduct and Ethics)

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 3 of 2010 issuing the Instructions on the Code of Conduct and Ethics to be observed by insurance companies operating in the UAE, as amended. It is cross-referenced as the conduct standard that Insurance Producers must follow when advertising, promoting and marketing insurance (electronic or ordinary). Binds insurance companies and, by incorporation, those marketing on their behalf.
