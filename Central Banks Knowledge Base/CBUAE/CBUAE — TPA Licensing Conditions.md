# TPA Licensing Conditions

Node covering the entry and maintenance conditions for a TPA licence: qualification and experience thresholds for the director and a specialist key officer, employment of a licensed and professionally independent physician to verify treatment cases, plus annual registration, renewal filings and audited financial statements. It also permits a TPA to serve multiple insurers and providers subject to separate records and bank accounts per insurer contract, and imposes identification and claim-payment conduct duties. Binds licence applicants and licensed TPAs vis-à-vis the Insurance Authority.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
