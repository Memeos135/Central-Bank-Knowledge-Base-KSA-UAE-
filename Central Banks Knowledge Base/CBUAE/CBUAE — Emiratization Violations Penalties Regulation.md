# Emiratization Violations Penalties Regulation

CBUAE Circular No. 6/2025 regulation setting administrative and financial penalties for Emiratization violations, issued under the Central Bank Law and the Insurance Law. It provides for collection of penalties (including deduction from funds held by the Central Bank if unpaid within ten working days), notification of NAFIS and judicial authorities, a fine range for fictitious Emiratization, and refund of incentives wrongly disbursed. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions, insurance companies and insurance-related professionals licensed in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
