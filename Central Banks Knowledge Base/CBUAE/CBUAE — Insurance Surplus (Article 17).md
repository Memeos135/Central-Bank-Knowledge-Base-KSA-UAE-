# Insurance Surplus (Article 17)

Article 17 requires the Takaful Insurance Fund to maintain an approved Insurance Surplus policy covering how surplus from insurance business is determined, the criteria for entitlement, and the basis and method of distribution. Surplus is defined as amounts remaining in the Fund at financial year end from total Contributions, investment return and other income after obligations. The surrounding provisions also cover co-insurance participation on behalf of the Fund, permitted only with Shari'ah-compliant risks and terms and with prior ISSC approval.
