# Licensed Institutions

Scope and enforcement node identifying "Licensed Institutions" — licensed financial institutions under Decretal Federal Law 14/2018, insurance companies and insurance-related professionals — as the addressees of the CBUAE Emiratization penalties regime (Circular 6/2025). It sets CBUAE's power to impose cumulative financial and administrative penalties for violations listed in Schedule (1), to require refund of incentives received from government bodies, and to compel payment of incentives to Nationals, while prohibiting any recovery from the National. CBUAE retains discretion to reduce, waive, increase or allow instalment payment of penalties.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
