# Enhanced Due Diligence for PEPs

Node covering the enhanced due diligence regime for PEPs under the AML-CFT Decision as explained in CBUAE guidance: senior management approval before establishing or continuing the relationship, reasonable measures to establish source of funds and wealth, and enhanced ongoing monitoring, on top of standard CDD. It distinguishes the automatic application of these measures to foreign PEPs and their Related Customers from the risk-triggered application to domestic PEPs and heads of international organisations and their Related Customers (only where the relationship is high risk). Binds licensed financial institutions; the guidance stresses that PEP status is not a basis for blanket de-risking.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
