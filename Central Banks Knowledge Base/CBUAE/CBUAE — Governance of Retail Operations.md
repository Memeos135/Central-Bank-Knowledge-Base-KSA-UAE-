# Governance of Retail Operations

Section 3.2 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation on governance of Retail Operations, plus supporting defined terms (Retail Operations, Senior Management, Person, Reducing Balance Method, Permissible, Regulation). It requires Licensed Financial Institutions to maintain strong governance and management oversight over design, development, promotion, sale, distribution and ongoing review of financial products and services, to assess consumer needs, suitability and affordability, and to protect consumer assets, information and data. Institutions offering Shari'ah-compliant products must also meet separate Shari'ah governance requirements.
