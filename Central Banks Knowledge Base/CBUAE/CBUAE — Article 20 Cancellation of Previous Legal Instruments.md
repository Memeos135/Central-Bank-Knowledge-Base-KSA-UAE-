# Article 20: Cancellation of Previous Legal Instruments

Article 20 of the CBUAE Insurance Brokers' Regulation (Circular 1/2024) repeals the predecessor Insurance Authority instruments governing insurance brokerage, namely the 2013 Board resolution on the Insurance Brokerage Regulation as amended, the 2013 Director General implementing decision, and the Insurance Authority work guide for internal controllers at brokerage companies. It sits alongside the enforcement article making breaches of the Regulation subject to CBUAE supervisory action and sanctions. Relevant to anyone determining which instrument applies to an Insurance Broker from the Regulation's application date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
