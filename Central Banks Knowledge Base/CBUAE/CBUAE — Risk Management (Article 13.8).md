# Risk Management (Article 13.8)

Places responsibility on the Board of Directors (or sole-establishment owner) for establishing a risk management function to identify, mitigate, monitor and report business risks, supported by board-approved policies, procedures, systems and controls that are periodically reviewed and proportionate to the nature, size and complexity of the business, with risk exposures aligned to strategy and business plan. Related provisions address group-level management structures and system capabilities for AML/CFT, transaction processing, reconciliation and regulatory reporting. Binds Exchange Business licensees, their boards and group management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments
