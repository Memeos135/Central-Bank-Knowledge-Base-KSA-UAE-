# Data Protection (Article 4)

Article 4 of the Outsourcing Regulation requires banks to comply with applicable UAE data legislation when managing and processing data under outsourcing, to retain ownership of all data given to a service provider while preserving customers' ownership of their own data (including Confidential Data), and to ensure customers can exercise related rights. Where subcontracting involves Confidential Data, the bank must ensure the subcontractor meets the same legal and regulatory requirements, and data must be secured against unauthorised access including by the provider's own staff. Read with the definition of Confidential Data and the contractual requirements in Article 5.
