# Emirates ID

Node covering the Emirates ID, the mandatory government-issued identity card for UAE citizens and residents, as an identity evidence source in CBUAE digital ID and AML/CFT guidance. It addresses use of the card's digital certificates and biometrics, and the expectation that LFIs validate the card through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security online gateway, UAE Pass or other government-supported solutions, and retain a copy plus the verification record. Applies to LFIs collecting and verifying the identity attributes required for CDD on natural persons.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info
