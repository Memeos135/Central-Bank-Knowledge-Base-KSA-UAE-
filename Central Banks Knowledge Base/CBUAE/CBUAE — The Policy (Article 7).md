# The Policy (Article 7)

Article 7 of the 2010 insurance code of conduct, governing how the insurance policy document must be arranged and presented. It requires clear legal and technical language, legible printing with nullification or forfeiture clauses and the arbitration clause set out conspicuously or separately, inclusion of all governing terms, secure binding and page numbering, accurate description of the subject matter and sum insured, a conspicuous non-payment termination clause, investment-reporting statements for life and unit-linked policies, prompt delivery of the policy and endorsements to the insured, an internal issuance mechanism, and that amendments are valid only by agreement of the parties. Binds insurance companies in scope of the instructions; read with Article 8 on renewal and disclosure of material facts.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
