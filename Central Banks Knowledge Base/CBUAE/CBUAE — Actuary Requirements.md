# Actuary Requirements

Sets out appointment, duties and professional obligations of the Actuary in relation to Technical Provisions and Mathematical Reserves: Board appointment of an Authority-registered actuary, notification of changes, review and approval of gross and net technical provisions, data quality assessment (accuracy remaining management's responsibility), professional liability, and an annual report to the Authority on current and future risks. It also constrains valuation methodology — consistent, non-arbitrary methods, discounting on AAA-rated sovereign securities (US$ proxy where AED long-term yields are unavailable), and treatment of unit-linked reserves for solvency.
