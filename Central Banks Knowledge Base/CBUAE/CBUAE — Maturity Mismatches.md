# Maturity Mismatches

Node dealing with maturity mismatches between a hedge and the underlying exposure for risk-weighted asset purposes, including the conservative definition of maturity on both legs, treatment of embedded call options, and the non-recognition rule where the protection has a short original maturity. Adjacent material covers sovereign guarantees and counter-guarantees and the separate maturity conventions used for securitisation tranches. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks applying credit risk mitigation and securitisation capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
