# Weighted Average Maturity Calculation

FAQ-style node on maturity and supervisory duration inputs used in the standardised counterparty credit risk and CVA calculations, alongside related clarifications on supervisory deltas and hedging sets. It confirms mandatory use of the standardised formulas for all options, treatment of hedging sets with or without a netting set, and CVA-specific points such as QCCP exclusions, non-netting of DVA, and hedge instruments creating new counterparty exposure. Directed at banks computing SA-CCR and CVA capital.
