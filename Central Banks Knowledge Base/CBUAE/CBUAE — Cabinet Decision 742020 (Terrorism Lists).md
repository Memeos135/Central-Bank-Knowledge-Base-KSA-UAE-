# Cabinet Decision 74/2020 (Terrorism Lists)

Reference node identifying UAE Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020 as one of the legal bases of the CBUAE transaction monitoring and sanctions screening guidance, alongside the AML-CFT Law (Federal Decree-Law 20/2018) and the AML-CFT Decision (Cabinet Decision 10/2019). It establishes the terrorism lists regime and implementation of UNSC resolutions on terrorism, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, with Article 21.2 cited as the source of LFI screening obligations. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions; the guidance itself sets supervisory expectations rather than new law.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
