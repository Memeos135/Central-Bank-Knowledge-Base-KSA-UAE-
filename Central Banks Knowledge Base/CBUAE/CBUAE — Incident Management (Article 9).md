# Incident Management (Article 9)

Article 9 of the CBUAE Operational Risk and Operational Resilience Regulation sets the incident management obligations of Licensed Financial Institutions (LFIs), requiring response and recovery plans that are regularly reviewed, tested and updated, with root-cause remediation of material incidents. It prescribes coverage of the full incident life cycle: severity classification against predefined criteria, response and recovery procedures linked to the Business Continuity Plan, allocation of roles for staff and external parties, and communication/escalation plans including reporting to the Central Bank and other authorities. It binds all LFIs supervised by the CBUAE, with accountability sitting with the Board and Senior Management through the operational risk framework.
