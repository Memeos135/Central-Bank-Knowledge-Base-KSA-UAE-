# Supreme Committee

Concept node for the Supreme Committee for the Supervision of the National Strategy for AML/CFT/CPF, established under the UAE Federal Decree by Law No. (10) of 2025 and affiliated with the Presidential Court, with its formation and rules of procedure set by Cabinet resolution. It sits above the National Committee: it evaluates national AML/CFT/CPF strategies, sets requirements for the National Committee and Concerned Authorities, directs inter-agency support, supervises the Mutual Evaluation process, and proposes legislation. It binds public-sector bodies (the National Committee, Concerned Authorities), not licensed institutions directly, though its resolutions shape the supervisory expectations those institutions face.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
