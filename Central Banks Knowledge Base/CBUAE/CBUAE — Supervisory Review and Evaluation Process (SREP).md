# Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process (SREP)

Node covering the Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process as the supervisory counterpart to the bank-run ICAAP: the CBUAE analyses banks' capitalisation levels and the ICAAP's internal view of capital adequacy. Where capital levels are judged unsatisfactory, the Central Bank may impose an adjusted Minimum Capital Adequacy ratio on the individual bank. Applies to all CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
