# International Cooperation and Asset Recovery

## Why this community

Cross-border mutual legal assistance and the recovery, freezing and repatriation of criminal proceeds - the outward-facing arm of the AML/CTF regime.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: functions as the anchor node for requests for assistance, information exchange and confiscation/asset-return mechanisms.
- Connects outward to predicate-offence, confiscation and financial-intelligence provisions rather than internally.
- Relevant to institutions chiefly as the legal basis for responding to foreign-origin freezing or information requests channelled through the competent authority.
