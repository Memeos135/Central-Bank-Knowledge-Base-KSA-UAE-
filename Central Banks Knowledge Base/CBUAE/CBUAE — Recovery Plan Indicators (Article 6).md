# Recovery Plan Indicators (Article 6)

Article 6 governs the recovery indicator and trigger framework: indicators must be calibrated to give early warning, and a trigger breach does not automatically require implementing recovery options but does start an escalation chain — immediate Senior Management notification and assessment, board notification within one business day, and Central Bank notification within two business days (or immediately on activation of the plan). It defines what activation means, requires integration into risk management and MIS, and mandates both quantitative indicators (capital, liquidity, profitability, asset quality, per the Annex) and qualitative indicators, with justification of the chosen calibration and described escalation governance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
