# Control Functions

Concept node for "Control Functions" under CBUAE governance standards — risk management, compliance and internal audit, plus Shari'ah compliance and audit for banks offering Islamic financial services. It anchors requirements on independence (including compensation set independently of the business lines monitored), fit-and-proper selection via the nomination committee, and the obligation on branches of foreign banks to maintain robust local Control Functions reporting to local management while accountable to group heads. Binds banks, their boards, nomination and compensation committees, and foreign bank branches in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF
