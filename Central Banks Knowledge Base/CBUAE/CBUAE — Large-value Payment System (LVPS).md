# Large-value Payment System (LVPS)

Defined term in the CBUAE Large-value Payment Systems Regulation: a clearing and settlement system designed mainly to process large-value and/or wholesale payments among financial market participants, or involving money market, foreign exchange or commercial transactions. Bilateral clearing and settlement arrangements that do not amount to a "system" are expressly excluded. The definition sets the perimeter for who needs a CBUAE licence and which arrangements can be designated and supervised.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
