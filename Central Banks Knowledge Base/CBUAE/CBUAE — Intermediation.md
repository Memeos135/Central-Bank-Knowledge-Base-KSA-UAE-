# Intermediation

Concept node in the CBUAE payment-sector guidance covering intermediation as an ML/FT risk driver: the insertion of multiple intermediaries (gateways, processors, aggregators, agents) between payer and payee that obscures who the underlying customer or merchant actually is. It is grouped with nesting and use of agents/affiliates as factors that degrade an institution's understanding of its merchant base, with heightened concern in cross-border chains and where CDD on merchants is outsourced to third parties. Directed at licensed financial institutions participating in or servicing the payment sector.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
