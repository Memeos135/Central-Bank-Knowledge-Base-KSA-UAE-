# Hawala Provider Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing and supervision of money-transfer intermediaries operating outside the banking channel, bringing hawala activity inside the AML/CTF perimeter through registration and transaction reporting.

## How members connect

- Status chain: the regulation creates the 'Registered Hawala Provider' status, evidenced by a certificate, which is the condition for lawfully conducting the activity.
- Registered status pulls the provider into the AML/CTF law as a reporting entity, with CDD, record-keeping and STR duties equivalent in substance to other transfer providers.
- Reporting obligation: periodic submission of remittance data to the supervisory reporting system is the ongoing supervisory hook attached to registration.
- Standards anchor: FATF recommendations on money or value transfer services supply the policy rationale and the benchmark against which the registration regime is assessed.
