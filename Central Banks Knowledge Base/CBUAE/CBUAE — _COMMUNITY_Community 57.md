# Derivative Add-On Calculations

## Why this community

The asset-class add-on mechanics that feed the potential future exposure component of SA-CCR, covering interest rate, FX, credit, equity and commodity derivatives.

## How members connect

- The Hedging Set is the common organising unit: each asset-class add-on is calculated within hedging sets, and hedging-set definition determines permitted offsetting.
- Each add-on is built from the same chain of prescribed inputs — Adjusted Notional Amount, Supervisory Delta Adjustment, Maturity Factor — so the inputs are shared obligations, not asset-class specific ones.
- Supervisory Factors and Correlations (Table 2) is the prescribed parameter table; the values are set by the supervisor and are not open to internal calibration.
- This cluster is the computational layer beneath the SA-CCR exposure cluster and should be read alongside it rather than in isolation.
