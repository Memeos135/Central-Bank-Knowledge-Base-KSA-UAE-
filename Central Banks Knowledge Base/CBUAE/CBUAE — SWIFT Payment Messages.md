# SWIFT Payment Messages

Explanatory background on the SWIFT messaging network as the infrastructure carrying most cross-border interbank payment instructions — a messaging layer only, with settlement occurring through correspondent accounts. It describes MT message series and how legacy formats (notably MT 103 and cover-payment practice) left intermediary institutions without full originator and beneficiary data, and notes the migration to ISO 20022 and retirement of the MT 1xx/2xx/9xx series from November 2025. Contextual/explanatory rather than obligation-creating; addressed to UAE LFIs.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF
