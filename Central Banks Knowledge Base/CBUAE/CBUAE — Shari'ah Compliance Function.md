# Shari'ah Compliance Function

Concept node describing the internal Shari'ah control division's functions in a Takaful company — Shari'ah consultation on contracts, products, marketing material and Shari'ah-related customer complaints; Shari'ah research and development; and the Shari'ah compliance function itself. The division cannot issue fatwas or resolutions and must refer matters to the ISSC, its staff cannot hold executive responsibility over activities they monitor, and Shari'ah compliance reports go to the ISSC before the CEO with a right of direct board access and only a dotted line to the company's general compliance head.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

### [[CBUAE — Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC)|Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Forming the ISSC does not by itself discharge the Shari'ah governance obligation; the framework is built on three lines of defence comprising the business line, the internal Shari'ah control function and the internal Shari'ah audit function, with the ISSC positioned as the supervisory authority over them. So assess the ISSC and the compliance/control and audit functions as one obligation chain: the committee's resolutions and oversight depend on the functions that execute and test compliance beneath it. A firm with an ISSC but no resourced internal Shari'ah control and audit functions is non-compliant even though the committee exists.
