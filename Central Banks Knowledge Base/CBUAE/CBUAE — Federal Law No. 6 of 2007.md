# Federal Law No. 6 of 2007

Node for UAE Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 (establishment of the Insurance Authority and organisation of insurance operations, as amended), the primary statute cited as the legal basis for Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 25 of 2014 (Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies) and referenced in related CBUAE instruments. It is the enabling law defining the regulator, licensing of insurers, and the scope for executive regulations and board decisions. It binds insurance companies incorporated in the UAE and licensed foreign branches, together with agents and brokers operating in the State; supervisory functions have since transferred to the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
