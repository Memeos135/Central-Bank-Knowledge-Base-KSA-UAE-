# Red Flag Indicators

An annex of red flag indicators and money laundering/terrorist financing typologies published by the UAE FIU (Biannual Financial Crime Trends and Typologies Report, Jan–Jun 2020) that Licensed Financial Institutions are expected to embed into their AML/CFT programmes, policies, detection scenarios and suspicion-identification criteria. The surrounding material also addresses STR/SAR filing timelines including expedited 24-hour filing, confidentiality and the prohibition on tipping off, responding to FIU information requests, and how to handle the customer relationship after filing. It binds LFIs supervised by the central bank and informs both detection and post-reporting conduct decisions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
