# Article 8: Restructuring

Article 8 obliges LFIs to have documented processes to identify, execute and manage Restructured Credit Facilities, with correct classification under Article 9 continuing throughout and after restructuring, including provisioning consequences. The restructuring process must be subject to robust internal oversight and periodic internal audit review; branches of foreign banks may rely on equivalent head-office oversight arrangements, subject to Central Bank supervisory review and approval. It distinguishes distressed restructuring (terms amended amid Obligor financial difficulty) from non-distressed restructuring.
