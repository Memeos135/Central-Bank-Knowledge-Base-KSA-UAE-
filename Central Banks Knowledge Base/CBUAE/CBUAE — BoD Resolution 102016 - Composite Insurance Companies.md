# BoD Resolution 10/2016 - Composite Insurance Companies

Insurance Authority Board of Directors Resolution No. 10 of 2016 regulating existing licensed composite insurance companies, i.e. those writing both insurance of persons and fund accumulation and property and liability insurance. It mandates complete operational separation of the two insurance types (technical, financial, technological, administrative, legal, systems and staff, except the general manager), separate investment strategies, books and records, and both consolidated and separate financial reporting. It binds composite insurers already licensed in the UAE.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, banking prudential
