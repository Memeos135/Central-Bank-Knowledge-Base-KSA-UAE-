# Fitness & Propriety Standards

## Why this community

Authorisation-stage and ongoing fitness and propriety of controllers, directors and senior/controlled-function holders across exchange business, open finance and insurance activities, including supervisory consent gateways for outsourcing and material changes.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: the Fitness and Propriety Regulation is issued by circular (4/2024) and operationalised by the Fitness and Propriety Standards, which sit beneath it as detailed criteria.
- Sector regulations (Exchange Business, Open Finance, Licensing and Monitoring of Exchange Business) each cross-refer to the fit-and-proper regime, making it a common gateway condition rather than a standalone regime.
- The Letter of No Objection is the shared consent instrument linking appointment/outsourcing decisions (Article 15) to the licensing framework — no LNO, no lawful change.
- AML/CTF law and the Insurance Law enter as integrity inputs: adverse AML findings or insurance-sector conduct feed the propriety assessment, creating a cross-regime interaction.
- Older and newer instruments coexist (Notice 1/2014 vs. current regulations), so supersession and transitional scope must be checked before relying on a provision.
