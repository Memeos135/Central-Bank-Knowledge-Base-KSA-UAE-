# Circular 468/1987 Foreign Company Public Subscription

UAE Central Bank Circular No. 468 of 1987 supplementing Circular 444/1987 for public subscriptions to the capital of companies established abroad. Banks operating in the UAE must additionally file an authenticated Arabic translation of the home-country regulator's approval permitting the company to offer shares abroad, and report the number of shares allocated to UAE subscribers versus those applied for; all approval applications must reach the Central Bank at least two weeks before the issue opens. Binds all banks acting as underwriters or intermediaries for foreign share offerings.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, other
