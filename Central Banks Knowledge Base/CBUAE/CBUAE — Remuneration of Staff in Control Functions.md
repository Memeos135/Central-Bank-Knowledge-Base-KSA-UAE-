# Remuneration of Staff in Control Functions

Article 8 of the CBUAE remuneration regulation, governing pay for staff in Control Functions (e.g. risk, compliance, audit). It requires direct board or board-committee oversight of their performance reviews and remuneration, a significant fixed proportion of total pay, performance assessment based on effectiveness and independence rather than financial or revenue contribution, and mitigation of conflicts of interest so variable pay does not compromise objectivity. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions and their boards.
