# Claims on Sovereigns

Node covering the sovereign exposure class under the CBUAE standardised approach to credit risk, including the condition for preferential (including 0%) risk weighting where a GCC sovereign exposure is both denominated and funded in that sovereign's domestic currency, and the default rule that all other sovereign exposures follow the sovereign's external rating. It also addresses the rating regime: only solicited ratings from eligible ECAIs may be used, with unsolicited ratings permitted only for the UAE federal government. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE when computing risk-weighted assets and may extend to collateral and guarantee treatment under credit risk mitigation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
