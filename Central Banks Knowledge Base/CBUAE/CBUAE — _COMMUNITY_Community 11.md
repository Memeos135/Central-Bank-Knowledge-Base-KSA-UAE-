# Electronic Insurance Regulation

## Why this community

Conduct and operational rules for selling insurance through electronic channels, including insurer websites, apps and price comparison platforms, and the identity-verification duties that attach when the contract is concluded remotely.

## How members connect

- The electronic insurance resolution is the hub instrument: it sets the permitted scope of electronic insurance operations and delegates detailed conduct duties to website management and price-comparison requirements.
- Remote onboarding creates a cross-regime link into AML/KYC customer identification — the 'Customer' being identified electronically is the same person as the insurance policyholder, so CDD standards ride on top of the insurance conduct rules.
- The e-commerce law supplies the enabling legal basis for electronic contracting, signatures and records that the insurance regulation presupposes.
- Price comparison websites and electronic insurance operations are conceptually parallel channels, both constrained by disclosure, data accuracy and intermediary-licensing limits.
