# Personal Loans Manual

## Why this community

Consumer/retail credit conduct for personal lending by supervised institutions — eligibility, disclosure, deduction limits and contract terms for individual borrowers.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the manual is a standalone consolidated conduct instrument for personal loans rather than part of an internal cross-reference chain.
- Its obligations sit downstream of the Finance Companies Control Law / Banking Control Law framework and interact with consumer-protection and responsible-lending rules elsewhere in the knowledge base.
- Relevant cross-regime check: the borrower here is a consumer, distinct from the AML notion of customer, so CDD obligations arise from separate instruments.
