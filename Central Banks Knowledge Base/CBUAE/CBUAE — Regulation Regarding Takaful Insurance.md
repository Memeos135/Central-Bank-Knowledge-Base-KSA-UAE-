# Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance

CBUAE regulation setting the licensing and governance framework for Takaful insurance in the UAE, including definitions of the Takaful Insurance Fund, Participation Membership Policy and Takaful Insurance Policy. It permits an optional (and potentially Central Bank-mandated) Takaful Insurance Fund Board of Trustees to protect fund interests, and conditions conventional insurers' conduct of Takaful business through a Takaful Insurance Window on prior Central Bank approval, a Shari'ah governance framework and an Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee. It binds insurance companies and Takaful companies licensed by the Central Bank, operating alongside standards issued by the Higher Shari'ah Authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
