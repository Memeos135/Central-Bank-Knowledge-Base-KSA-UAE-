# IA Board Resolution No. 12 of 2016 Elapse of Disciplinary Sanctions

A UAE Insurance Authority Board resolution (No. 12 of 2016) setting the periods after which the effect of a disciplinary sanction imposed on a person practising an insurance-related profession, or on their employee, lapses: 60 days after conditions are rectified for a warning, and 180 days from the decision lifting a suspension. It binds licensed insurance-related professionals (brokers, agents, loss adjusters and similar) and their employees, and is stated to operate without prejudice to penalties under other legislation. Useful only for the rehabilitation/expiry of disciplinary records, not for the grounds or process of imposing sanctions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
