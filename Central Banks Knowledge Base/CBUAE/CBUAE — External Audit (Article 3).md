# External Audit (Article 3)

Article-level node on the external audit requirements within the CBUAE Financial Reporting and External Audit framework (162/2018). It addresses selection of the audit firm via procurement at least every six years with firm rotation and a three-year cooling-off, three-yearly audit partner rotation, annual audit committee assessment of auditor quality and the auditor's internal quality control reporting, review of the representation letter and audit plan adherence, and approval of a non-audit services policy listing prohibited services. It binds the bank's board audit committee and senior management, with corresponding duties on the external auditor.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
