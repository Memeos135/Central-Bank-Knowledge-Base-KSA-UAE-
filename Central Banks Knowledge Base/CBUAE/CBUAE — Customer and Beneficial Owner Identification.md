# Customer and Beneficial Owner Identification

Guidance on the information licensed financial institutions should collect to identify customers and beneficial owners and to feed customer segmentation and risk rating, including purpose of account, source of funds and source of wealth, occupation or industry, channels, expected products, geographies and transaction volumes. It scales information demands to the assessed risk profile, with additional detail expected for higher-risk customers, and explains how the resulting baseline of expected activity supports ongoing monitoring and the SDD/EDD decision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
