# Internal Control System (Article 7)

Article 7 requires the operational risk framework to be underpinned by a strong control environment and an effective internal control system covering risk assessment, control activities, monitoring activities, and information and communication. The system must extend to all LFI activities, including those performed wholly or partly by Third-Party Service Providers, and consists of policies, processes, systems and control measures that mitigate, reduce likelihood of, or limit the impact of risks. The article also prescribes the content and quality of operational risk reporting, including risk profile indicators, Risk Limits and thresholds, residual risks and control deficiencies, non-compliance with tolerances, key and emerging risks, loss events with root cause analysis, external and regulatory developments, and audit/second-line recommendations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
