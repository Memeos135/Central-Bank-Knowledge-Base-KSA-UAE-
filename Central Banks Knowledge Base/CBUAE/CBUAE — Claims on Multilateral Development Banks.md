# Claims on Multilateral Development Banks

Node on exposures to multilateral development banks, which are generally treated like claims on banks but without the preferential short-term treatment, save for highly rated MDBs meeting specified criteria that qualify for a 0% risk weight. Eligibility follows the BCBS list of qualifying MDBs, which banks must consult and apply; the CBUAE takes no role in assessing individual MDB eligibility. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks in assigning risk weights to MDB exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
