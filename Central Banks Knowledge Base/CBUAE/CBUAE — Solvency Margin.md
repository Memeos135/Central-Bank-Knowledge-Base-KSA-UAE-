# Solvency Margin

Defines the Solvency Margin regime under the Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies: the obligation to hold Own Funds at all times against the largest of the Minimum Capital Requirement, Minimum Guarantee Fund and Solvency Capital Requirement, with prescribed composition (Basic vs Ancillary Own Funds) and disclosure tables. It also sets immediate reporting to the Authority on breach plus a recovery plan within thirty days and restoration within six months. Binds Takaful insurance companies incorporated in the State and licensed foreign Takaful branches.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
