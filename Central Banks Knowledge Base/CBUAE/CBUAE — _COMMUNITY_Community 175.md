# AML/CFT & Internal Audit

## Why this community

The assurance layer over AML/CTF compliance — independent internal audit testing of the AML/CTF programme, distinct from the compliance function's own first- and second-line duties.

## How members connect

- The internal audit provision expressly reaches the AML/CTF compliance article, creating an obligation chain: compliance function designs and runs controls, internal audit independently tests their adequacy and effectiveness.
- Three-lines separation matters legally: audit independence and reporting lines to the board/audit committee are conditions of a compliant programme, not good practice.
- Findings from audit feed back into the AML/CTF programme's required periodic review and into supervisory reporting expectations.
