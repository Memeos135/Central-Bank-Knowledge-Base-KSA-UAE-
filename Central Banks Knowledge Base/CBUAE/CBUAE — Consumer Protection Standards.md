# Consumer Protection Standards

CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards accompanying the Consumer Protection Regulation, covering Licensed Financial Institutions' duties on consumer data protection (collection, confidentiality, authorised use, profiling and data mining, disclosure by authorised agents) and on governance — board and senior management responsibility and resourcing of independent risk, compliance and audit control functions for retail operations. Obligations are framed as binding requirements on Licensed Financial Institutions, with confidentiality exceptions limited to lawful compulsion or express consumer consent.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
