# Materiality Assessment

This node covers the materiality assessment expectations under the risk management framework principle (Principle 4) of the CBUAE climate-related financial risk principles. It requires financial firms to assess how and to what extent climate risks affect their business, operations and portfolios across strategic, financial, operational and reputational dimensions, using defined materiality definitions and quantitative and/or qualitative thresholds covering both physical and transition risk exposure, and to update existing risk management frameworks on that basis. It also links to group-wide coordination and to the incorporation of material climate risks into capital and liquidity adequacy processes.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
