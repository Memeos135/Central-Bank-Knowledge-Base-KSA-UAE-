# Paris Agreement

Reference node for the Paris Agreement, the international climate treaty ratified by the UAE in 2016, cited as the policy anchor for the SFWG's creation and for domestic climate initiatives such as the UAE Green Agenda 2015-2030, the National Climate Change Plan 2017-2050 and the Net Zero by 2050 Strategic Initiative. It is contextual background rather than a source of direct financial-sector obligations; supervisory duties arise from the derived principles and each regulator's own instruments.
