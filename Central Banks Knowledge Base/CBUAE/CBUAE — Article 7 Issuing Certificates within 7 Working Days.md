# Article 7: Issuing Certificates within 7 Working Days

Article of the CBUAE strict-liability financial sanctions regulation requiring banks to issue certificates — including liability statements and clearance letters — to retail customers within seven working days of the customer's application. It ties this duty to the account-closure and certificate provisions of Regulation No. 29/2011 and to a Central Bank circular on normal banking practice, and expressly excludes corporate customers from scope. Binds banks as Licensed Financial Institutions, with a fixed fine per violation plus an escalating daily rate up to a stated cap.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, governance/risk
