# FATF

Concept node for the Financial Action Task Force standards as incorporated by reference across CBUAE instruments, including the FATF-derived definitions of Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers (exchange between virtual assets and fiat or between virtual assets, transfer, safekeeping/administration, and participation in an issuer's offer or sale). It anchors the requirement that providers of Payment Token Services — treated as high ML/TF risk because of speed, anonymity and cross-border reach — conduct risk assessment and apply mitigation consistent with the FATF risk-based approach guidance for virtual assets and VASPs. Useful for tracing where international AML/CTF standards become operative obligations on licensed providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
