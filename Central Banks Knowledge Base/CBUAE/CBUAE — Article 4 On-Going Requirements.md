# Article 4: On-Going Requirements

Sets continuing licence-maintenance obligations for Insurance Brokers: no combining brokerage with other Insurance-Related Professions, no partnership or agency with another broker, notification duties to the Central Bank on appointment, transfer, termination or vacancy of Specialised Employees (with replacement within 30 days), and adequate qualified staffing including at least one Specialised Employee per licensed line. Also requires prior written Central Bank approval before changing legal status, ownership, capital or premises.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
