# Monitoring & Reporting

## Why this community

Ongoing supervisory and internal monitoring of climate risk exposures and the associated reporting obligations to the board and the regulator.

## How members connect

- Stands as a single obligation node on data collection, metrics and escalation, downstream of the responsibility-allocation and risk-integration principles.
- Its outputs feed capital adequacy assessment and external disclosure duties in adjacent clusters.
