# Terrorism Lists Screening

## Why this community

Targeted financial sanctions arising from terrorism listing decisions: identifying a confirmed match against listed persons and the mandatory notification duty to the supervisor and the competent listing office. Note this cluster reflects a UAE instrument, so scope must be checked before analogising to the KSA/SAMA regime.

## How members connect

- The Cabinet Decision is the parent instrument; both the match-determination concept and the notification duty derive their definitions and triggers from it.
- Obligation chain: screening produces a potential hit → confirmation of the match → immediate freezing and reporting obligations crystallise, with strict timing.
- 'Confirmed match' is the defined trigger term — the notification duty is conditional on it, so its threshold governs whether a reporting breach exists.
