# Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)

Concept node for the Liquidity Coverage Ratio as applied in the CBUAE liquidity guidance: a 30-day stress metric covering bank-specific and market-wide stress, calibrated on the Basel III LCR standard with national discretions noted. It sits alongside ELAR, ASRR and NSFR as one of the quantitative liquidity ratios, with a phased compliance path rising from 70% to 100%. It binds banks that are approved to be assessed under the Basel III liquidity framework rather than the ELAR regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
