# Liquidity Risk Management

Node addressing liquidity and firm-wide risk management expectations within the CBUAE ICAAP framework, framed around the seven features of a rigorous capital assessment process, board and senior management oversight, risk appetite setting, MIS, and internal controls. It also confirms that Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 requirements are separate and cannot be netted. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks, with primary accountability on the board and senior management.
