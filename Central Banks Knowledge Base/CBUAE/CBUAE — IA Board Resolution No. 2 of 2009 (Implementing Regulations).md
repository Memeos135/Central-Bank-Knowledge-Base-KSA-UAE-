# IA Board Resolution No. 2 of 2009 (Implementing Regulations)

The Insurance Authority Board resolution issuing the Implementing Regulations of Federal Law No. 6 of 2007; it is cited as a legal basis for the 2011 insurance agents instructions and fills out the statutory framework for insurance business in the UAE. It binds licensed insurance companies and related regulated persons. Consult it for the executive-level rules underlying registration, appeals against rejection, and ongoing supervisory requirements.
