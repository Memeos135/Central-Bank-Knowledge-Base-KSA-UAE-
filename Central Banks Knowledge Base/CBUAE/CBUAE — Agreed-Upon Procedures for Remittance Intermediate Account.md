# Agreed-Upon Procedures for Remittance Intermediate Account

Appendix 6 sets the minimum agreed-upon procedures an External Auditor must perform on the Remittance Intermediate Account and report on, as required by the exchange business regulations and Chapter 4 of the Standards. Procedures include confirming a Designated Remittance Intermediate Account is held with a locally licensed bank, that customer remittance funds are deposited by the end of banking hours on the next business day, that the balances are ring-fenced against any use other than prefunding or settling foreign correspondent accounts, and risk-based sampling of ledger recording, transfers to correspondent accounts on payment-order transmission, and adequacy of correspondent prefunding. Binds External Auditors in their reporting duty and, indirectly, Licensed Persons holding a Category B or C licence.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
