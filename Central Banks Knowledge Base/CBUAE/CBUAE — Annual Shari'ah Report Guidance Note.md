# Annual Shari'ah Report Guidance Note

A CBUAE guidance note setting supervisory expectations for the Annual Shari'ah Report issued by the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC), complementing the federal central bank law and the Shari'ah Governance Standard. It fixes responsibility for preparing the report on the ISSC, requires prior review and approval by the Higher Shari'ah Authority, submission to the HSA within two months of financial year end, and presentation to the general assembly; a report template is annexed. Applies to all Islamic financial institutions, including Islamic windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an Islamic financial institution's Annual Shari'ah Report is validly issued, the answer turns on the status and composition of the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee, because the report is that committee's own annual disclosure on the institution's Shari'ah compliance. The guidance note on the report therefore depends on the ISSC framework for the defined term, the issuing authority and the underlying Shari'ah governance mechanisms the institution must maintain. In practice, a defect in the ISSC's formation, independence or mandate is also a defect in the report, so verify the committee's standing before relying on the report as evidence of compliance.
