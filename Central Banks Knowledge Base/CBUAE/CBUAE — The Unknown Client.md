# The Unknown Client

Defines "the Unknown Client" under the CBUAE Large Exposures regulation: where the counterparty behind an exposure cannot be identified, such as in structures that do not disclose holdings below a threshold, the exposure is assigned to a single notional client to which the large exposure limit applies. All such unidentified exposures are aggregated as one counterparty regardless of any actual relationship, and banks are barred from circumventing limits through multiple structures with identical underlying assets. Includes the look-through approach mechanics for structures and tranches; binds CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
