# Three Lines of Defence Model

This node covers the internal control framework in Article 2 of the CBUAE Standards 161/2018 and its three lines of defence model: business/client-facing units (first line, transaction-based), risk, compliance, legal and other control/support functions (second line, risk-based), and internal audit (third line, risk-based periodic assurance). It fixes minimum internal control content (organisational structure and delegation, accounting and reconciliation, four-eyes/segregation of duties, safeguarding assets and fraud prevention) and requires at least annual board or audit committee review of control effectiveness. Responsibility for internal control does not shift between lines; it binds licensed banks' boards, senior management and control functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
