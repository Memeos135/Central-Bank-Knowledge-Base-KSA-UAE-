# Article 5: Stress Testing of Material Risks (Regulation)

Article 5 of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation (153/2018) requires a Bank to run a stress-testing programme for material risks commensurate with the size of its risk exposures, and to feed the results on an ongoing basis into risk management, contingency planning and its internal capital and liquidity assessment. The internal capital and liquidity adequacy process must reflect the nature and level of risks taken and cover all material risks beyond those specified in the Central Bank's Capital Adequacy and Liquidity Regulations and Standards. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks.
