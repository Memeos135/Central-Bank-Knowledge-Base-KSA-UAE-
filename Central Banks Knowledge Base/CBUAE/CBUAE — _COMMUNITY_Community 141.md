# Climate Scenario & Capital Adequacy

## Why this community

Whether and how climate-related financial risks must be reflected in internal capital and liquidity adequacy, and the use of scenario analysis as the evidentiary basis for that assessment.

## How members connect

- Scenario analysis is the input obligation; the capital and liquidity adequacy principle is the output decision that must take those results into account.
- Both attach to the firm's existing ICAAP/ILAAP/ORSA processes rather than creating a separate capital regime.
- Reference climate scenarios are cited as the recognised methodological baseline for the analysis.
