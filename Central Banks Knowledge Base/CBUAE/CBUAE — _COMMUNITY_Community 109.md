# Card Scheme & PCI Security

## Why this community

Card scheme operator obligations: periodic regulatory reporting and adherence to recognised payment card and national information security standards.

## How members connect

- Card Scheme is the defined regulated entity; the other members are the duties attached to that status.
- Annex III sets the reporting content and cadence owed to the regulator by the scheme.
- PCI DSS and the national information assurance standards are incorporated by reference, converting external technical standards into supervisory expectations.
- Reads as the card-scheme branch of the retail payments regime, so it should be interpreted with the parent payments regulation and its security annex.
