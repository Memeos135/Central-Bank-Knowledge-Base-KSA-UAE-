# Article 13: Credit Risk Models

Article 13 of the CBUAE Credit Risk Management Regulation, requiring licensed financial institutions to hold methodologies and analytical approaches for measuring, analysing and categorising credit risk and computing provisions at facility, obligor, segment and portfolio level, including identification of concentration risk. Where models drive decision-making, LFIs must comply with separate CBUAE modelling standards and demonstrate that models are fit for purpose and adequately calibrated. Binds all LFIs in scope of the regulation; the surrounding context also carries the core definitions (Credit Facility, Credit Risk, CRM, CRO, DPD) and the Article 4–5 governance/underwriting duties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
