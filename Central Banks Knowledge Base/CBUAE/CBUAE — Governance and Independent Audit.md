# Governance and Independent Audit

Guidance on governance arrangements for correspondent banking AML/CFT/CPF programmes, including senior management and Board oversight, the option of a dedicated governance body to approve high-risk relationships and act as escalation point for suspicious activity and termination decisions. It maps responsibilities across the three lines of defence (frontline/relationship management, compliance, and internal or external audit) and expects representation from all three in oversight. Applies to LFIs offering correspondent banking services.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, banking prudential
