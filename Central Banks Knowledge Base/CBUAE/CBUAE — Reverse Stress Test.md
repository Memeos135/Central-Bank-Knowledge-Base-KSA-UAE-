# Reverse Stress Test

Sub-section setting the expectation that banks conduct reverse stress tests in addition to normal stress testing, starting from a predefined non-viability or breach-of-minimum-requirements outcome and working back to the scenarios and shocks that would produce it. It requires assessment of whether such outcomes are realistic and whether mitigating actions (including hedging viability under stressed market conditions) are warranted, and links to the use test requiring stress results to drive decision-making. It applies to banks within the ICAAP framework, with Board and Senior Management ownership of assumptions and methodology.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
