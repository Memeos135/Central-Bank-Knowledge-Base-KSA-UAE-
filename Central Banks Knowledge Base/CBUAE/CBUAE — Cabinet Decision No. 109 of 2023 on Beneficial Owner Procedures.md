# Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023 on Beneficial Owner Procedures

Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023 on the Regulation of Beneficial Owner Procedures, governing how legal persons register, identify and update Beneficial Owner information. It sets out registration and licensing data to be filed with the Registrar, the criteria for identifying a Beneficial Owner, exemptions for listed companies subject to disclosure requirements and their majority-owned subsidiaries, and a notification/enquiry process with 15-day response windows before details are entered in the Beneficial Owners' Register. It binds legal persons and their registrars, and underpins AML/CTF beneficial ownership transparency and KYB/CDD verification by financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
