# Circular 444/1987 Public Subscription to Company Capital

UAE Central Bank Circular No. 444 of 1987, consolidating earlier circulars, requiring all licensed banks and representative offices to obtain prior Central Bank (Banking Supervision) approval before accepting public subscriptions to the capital of new or existing companies, whether UAE or foreign. It defines when a bank acts as an underwriter or intermediary (advertising the issue or collecting application forms, as opposed to mere fund transfers), sets the content of approval applications, and imposes subscriber-protection duties on disclosure, acknowledgements, refunds, share certificate delivery, verification of founders' paid-up shares, and use of segregated subscription accounts subject to reserve requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, other
