# Insurance Dispute Committees

## Why this community

Adjudication of insurance disputes in KSA: the constitution, jurisdiction and procedure of the committees that settle disputes and violations arising under insurance contracts and supervision, including hearings conducted by remote means.

## How members connect

- The two versions of the Regulation of Insurance Dispute Committees sit in a version hierarchy — the consolidated text supersedes/amends VER1, so effective-date and transitional questions turn on which version governs a filed claim.
- Both versions constitute and empower the Committee for Settlement of Insurance Disputes, which is the operative defined body for jurisdiction, quorum and appeal routes.
- Remote Communication Technology appears as a procedural permission within the Regulation (service, hearings, pleadings), not as a standalone instrument.
- Dispute Settlement & Resolution is the umbrella concept linking committee procedure to broader consumer-protection and complaint-handling channels.
