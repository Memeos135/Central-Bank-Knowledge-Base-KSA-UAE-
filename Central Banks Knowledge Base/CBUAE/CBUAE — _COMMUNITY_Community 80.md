# Mortgage & Real Estate Lending

## Why this community

Prudential and transparency rules for mortgage and real-estate lending to individuals, including lending limits and disclosure of borrowing cost. This cluster is UAE Central Bank material and is only persuasive, not binding, in a KSA analysis.

## How members connect

- Vertical hierarchy from enabling statute (Union Law No. 10 of 1980) down through regulations, board resolutions and a notice, so the amending resolutions determine the currently operative text.
- Version control matters: the consolidated VER2 supersedes/incorporates VER1 as amended by the 2019 and 2020 board resolutions - answers should cite the consolidated text.
- Cross-regime overlap with retail conduct: the mortgage rules sit alongside the regulations on bank loans and other services to individual customers and the real-estate lending transparency regulations, so one facility can be subject to both prudential caps and disclosure duties.
