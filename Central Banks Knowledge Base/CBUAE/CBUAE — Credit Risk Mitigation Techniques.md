# Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques

Sets out which credit risk mitigation techniques may reduce exposure values in the large exposures framework — only unfunded credit protection and eligible financial collateral recognised under the standardised approach; IRB-only collateral is excluded and internally modelled haircuts are prohibited. It specifies the recognised amounts (protected portion, collateralised portion, CCR-recognised collateral, haircut-adjusted collateral) and requires substitution: any reduction against the original counterparty must be booked as a new exposure to the CRM provider. Treatment of maturity mismatches and enforceable loan/deposit netting follows the risk-based capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
