# Interest Rate and Rate of Return Risk in the Banking Book Standards

The IRRBB Standards are the detailed, mandatory elaboration of the CBUAE Interest Rate and Rate of Return Risk in the Banking Book Regulation, enforceable on the same footing as the Regulation itself. They set expectations on risk governance, staff competence, risk appetite and self-imposed exposure limits, and on measurement systems that capture repricing, yield curve, basis and option risk across both earnings and economic value, including embedded optionality. They bind all Banks licensed by the CBUAE, with banks offering Islamic financial services required to apply the same principles to rate of return risk; the article on definitions fixes terms such as IRR, IRRBB, option risk, Group, Parent and Subsidiary.
