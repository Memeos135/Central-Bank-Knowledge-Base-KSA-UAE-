# Market Risk Standards

## Why this community

Capital requirements for trading-book and market-risk exposures: equity, FX, commodities and options, plus the valuation standards that underpin the positions being measured.

## How members connect

- The Market Risk Standard is the parent instrument; each risk category (equity, FX, commodity) is a subordinate charge computed under it.
- Duplicate-labelled nodes (Market Risk / Market Risk Standard; Equity Position Risk / Equity Risk) reflect the same obligations expressed in different source instruments — treat as one obligation set when citing.
- Options treatment branches into alternative methods (simplified vs delta-plus), with eligibility to use the simpler method being a scope limit on the institution, not a free choice.
- Prudent Valuation Guidance conditions the inputs to every charge, so valuation adequacy is a precondition to a compliant market-risk calculation.
