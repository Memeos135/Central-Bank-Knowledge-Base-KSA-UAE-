# Remediation Process

Node setting out the CBUAE Model Management Standards requirements for reporting model findings and remediating model defects, including severity-based and Tier-based maximum remediation periods, the use of temporary tactical solutions (overlays, adjustments, scaling) with expiry dates, and the twelve-month outer limit for high severity findings. It also addresses the fallback where an institution has no internal validation team, with internal audit reviewing the accuracy of findings reporting. Applies to institutions subject to the MMS and their model owners, validators and audit functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** Where a model deficiency is identified, the question of who must act is answered by the same role framework that governs model oversight generally — both provisions sit in one instrument and rely on a common, mandatory allocation of roles (owner, developer, validator, user, data owner, and oversight committee). The remediation process is therefore not a separate workflow but an accountability chain running back to those named roles, with the oversight committee as the escalation and sign-off point. For a compliance decision, unremediated model findings are a governance failure attributable to identified individuals or the committee, not merely a technical shortcoming.
