# Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks (Circular 83/2019)

Cross-reference node for CBUAE Circular No. 83/2019 (Corporate Governance Regulation for Banks and accompanying Standards), cited in the Remuneration Regulation as one of the instruments it must be read alongside. It matters here because the remuneration requirements are stated to be additional to compensation requirements in the Central Bank's corporate governance framework, while the Remuneration Regulation takes precedence in the event of conflict. Relevant to banks; parallel governance instruments exist for insurance companies (Circular 24/2022).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
