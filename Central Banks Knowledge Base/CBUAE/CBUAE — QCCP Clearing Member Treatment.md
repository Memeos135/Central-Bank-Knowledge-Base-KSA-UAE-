# QCCP Clearing Member Treatment

Sets the risk-weighted asset treatment of exposures to qualifying central counterparties, distinguishing the bank's role as clearing member from its role as a client of a clearing member. Key rules include a 2% risk weight for trade exposures to a QCCP, a minimum 10-day margin period of risk for OTC derivative trade exposures to QCCPs, treatment of clearing member exposures to clients as bilateral trades (minimum 5-day MPOR), recognition of collateral passed on to the QCCP, coverage of multi-level client structures, and the option to treat exposures as if the CCP were non-qualifying where that produces lower RWA. Binds UAE-licensed banks with cleared derivative activity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
