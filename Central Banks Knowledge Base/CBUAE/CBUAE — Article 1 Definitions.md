# Article 1: Definitions

Article 1 sets the defined terms used throughout the Insurance Brokers' Regulation, including Applicant, Beneficiary, Central Bank, Central Bank Laws, Insurance Broker and its Representatives, Insurance Brokerage (solicit / negotiate / sell), Remuneration, and Internal Controls. These definitions determine the regulatory perimeter — notably what activity constitutes brokerage and therefore requires a licence. Read together with the Scope of Application and the proportionality principle.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
