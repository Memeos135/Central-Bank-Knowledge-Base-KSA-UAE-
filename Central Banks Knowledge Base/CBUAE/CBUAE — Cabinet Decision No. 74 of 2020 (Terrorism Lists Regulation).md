# Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020 (Terrorism Lists Regulation)

Reference node for UAE Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020 as the legislative framework for Targeted Financial Sanctions, establishing the Local Terrorist List and the mechanism for giving effect to the UN Consolidated List, and allocating the Executive Office's coordination and grievance-handling role. This node also carries the key defined terms used across the TFS regime, including Funds, Listed Person, Listing, and direct versus indirect relationship. It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs subject to UAE TFS obligations.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
