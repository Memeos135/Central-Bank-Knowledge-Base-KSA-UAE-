# Big Data Analytics and AI

Sets out the Big Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence chapter of the Enabling Technologies Guidelines: materiality assessment of each AI/analytics Application, governance framework, explainability to avoid 'black-box' outcomes, data governance and representativeness of training data, independent validation and testing before deployment, and the ability to suspend material Applications. Expectations scale with the Application's purpose, criticality and impact on decision-making. Addressed to Institutions using such models in financial services.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, other
