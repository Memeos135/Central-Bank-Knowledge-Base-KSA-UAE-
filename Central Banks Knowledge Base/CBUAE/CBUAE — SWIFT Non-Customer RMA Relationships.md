# SWIFT Non-Customer RMA Relationships

An explanatory segment of CBUAE correspondent banking guidance dealing with SWIFT Relationship Management Application (RMA) links established with counterparties that are not customers of the institution, illustrating typical non-customer RMA use cases (corporate reporting, relaying payment instructions, sub-custodian/global custodian flows, trade finance messaging). It sets out the minimum due diligence expected before key exchange and periodically thereafter — name and address collection, screening against internal, PEP and sanctions lists, and a risk assessment against red flags — drawing on Wolfsberg Group standards, and notes RMA Plus as a means of restricting permitted message types. It is addressed to licensed financial institutions operating SWIFT connectivity; the surrounding material also covers the ISO 20022 migration and MT message retirement.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
