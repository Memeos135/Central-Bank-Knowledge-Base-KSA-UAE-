# Wire Transfers

Node covering the wire transfer (travel rule) provisions (Division Three, Articles 28–29) of the UAE AML/CTF Executive Regulations. It sets the AED 3,500 threshold for verifying originator information on cross-border transfers, the originator and beneficiary data that must accompany transfers (including batch files and domestic transfers), a three working day response deadline for providing underlying data, the prohibition on executing non-compliant transfers, record retention, and the application of freezing and transaction-prohibition measures under Targeted Financial Sanctions; Article 29 sets intermediary institution duties where information is missing or lost in conversion. Binds originating, intermediary and beneficiary Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS
