# Fair Treatment of Financially Distressed Consumers

Sets the Board-level control framework for fair treatment of Consumers (ethics in selling, complaint handling, assisting People of Determination, equal treatment, confidentiality, conflicts, service standards, prompt correction of Errors) together with a Code of Fair Treatment of Consumers that must be distributed to Staff, trained on, annually reminded by Senior Management and used in performance appraisal. It then sets specific duties towards financially distressed Consumers: providing qualified debt/credit counselling, giving reasonable consideration to alternative repayment arrangements, proactively assisting at the first signs of payment irregularity, and ensuring counselling Staff are trained and treat Consumers with respect. Binds the Board, Senior Management and Staff of Licensed Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
