# Open Finance Framework

This node covers the Open Finance Framework established by the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation (Circular 03/2025), comprising the Trust Framework, the API Hub and Common Infrastructural Services for cross-sectoral data sharing and transaction initiation on behalf of Users. It binds Licensees (mandated participation, phased onboarding beginning with banks including foreign branches and insurance companies), Open Finance Providers, Persons Deemed Licensed and the API Hub. It also confirms that technical service providers need no Open Finance Licence but that regulatory responsibility and liability cannot be outsourced to them, and that the Central Bank may levy fees to fund framework operation.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, governance/risk
