# Unified Motor Vehicle Policy Against Third Party Liability

The unified standard-form motor third party liability policy: the compulsory cover protecting injured third parties, setting the scope of bodily injury and property damage cover, the treatment of passengers and excluded persons (insured, driver, certain employees), judicially awarded amounts including third-party legal costs but excluding fines, and the AED caps for death/disability per injured person and for property damage per accident. It also fixes repair obligations toward the injured third party, including agency repair in the vehicle's first year, parts quality and depreciation rules. Binds insurers writing compulsory motor cover in the State and determines third-party claimants' entitlements.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
