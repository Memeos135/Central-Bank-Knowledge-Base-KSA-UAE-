# Health Insurance TPA Licensing

## Why this community

Licensing and ongoing conduct regime for health insurance third-party administrators, covering entry conditions, financial security, and the contractual chain between TPA, insurer and medical service providers.

## How members connect

- The TPA licensing instructions define the regulated person ('Third Party Administrator') and impose the licensing conditions; the consolidated version is the operative text and supersedes the original where amended.
- The TPA–insurance company agreement is the mandated contractual instrument through which delegated claims/administration functions are performed — outsourcing responsibility remains with the licensed insurer.
- Medical service providers sit at the end of the obligation chain: TPA duties on network contracting and claims handling run through to them.
- The bank guaranty requirement is the financial-security condition tied to licence grant and renewal.
