# Data Protection

Guidance on protecting data across storage, transmission and use, principally in cloud computing arrangements: recoverability within pre-defined timeframes, secure backups, segregation in multi-tenancy or co-mingled environments, controls against unauthorised access and network attacks, cryptographic key management, encryption or tokenisation of personal data, identity and access management with multi-factor authentication, and logging of user access and activity. It connects to the data governance expectations for AI and analytics models (accuracy, completeness, consistency, security, timeliness and documented quality gaps). Binds financial institutions and shapes what they must require of outsourcing service providers.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, other
