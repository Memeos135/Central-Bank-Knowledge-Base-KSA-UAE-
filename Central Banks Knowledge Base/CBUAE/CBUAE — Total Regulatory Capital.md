# Total Regulatory Capital

Definition node from the CBUAE Major Acquisitions Regulation: Total Regulatory Capital is the sum of a bank's CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital, and functions as the denominator for the regulation's thresholds — over 5% for a Major Acquisition, over 1% (but below the Major threshold) for a Notification Acquisition, and the 10% cap on commercial company holdings. The surrounding context also defines Major Acquisition, Notification Acquisition, Purchase Price, Senior Management and Subsidiary, and sets out Article 2's prior written approval requirement and minimum application content. Relevant to any bank calculating whether a transaction triggers approval or notification.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
