# Claims on Corporates

Node on the corporate exposure class under the CBUAE standardised approach, which is the treatment applied to commercial counterparties including government related entities that operate competitively, risk-weighted by reference to the entity's external credit rating from an eligible ECAI. Only solicited ratings may be used for corporate exposures. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks when classifying and risk-weighting corporate and GRE counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
