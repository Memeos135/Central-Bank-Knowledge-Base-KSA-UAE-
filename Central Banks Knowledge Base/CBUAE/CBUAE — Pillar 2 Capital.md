# Pillar 2 Capital

Defines Pillar 2 capital as the additional capital each bank must determine and hold for risks not covered, or insufficiently covered, by Pillar 1 (credit, market, operational) capital under the CBUAE capital adequacy framework. Pillar 2 operates as both an internal process reported through the ICAAP and a supervisory evaluation, and requires banks to maintain capital consistent with risk profile and strategy, including stressed positions and Board-approved management actions. Binds all CBUAE-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
