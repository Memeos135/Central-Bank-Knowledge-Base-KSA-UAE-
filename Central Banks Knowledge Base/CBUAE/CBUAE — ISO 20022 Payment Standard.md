# ISO 20022 Payment Standard

Describes the ISO 20022 open, XML-based global messaging standard and its adoption by the SWIFT community for all cross-border payments, with a migration window from March 2023 and coexistence with MT messages until the MT 1xx/2xx/9xx series is retired in November 2025. Its compliance significance is richer structured originator and beneficiary data supporting screening and monitoring across the payment chain. Background context for UAE LFIs; not itself a rule text.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF
