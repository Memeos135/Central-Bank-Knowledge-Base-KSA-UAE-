# Senior Management Commitment

Sub-element of the SCP addressing governance accountability: senior management must review and approve the SCP, formally designate the persons responsible for its implementation, and ensure it is embedded in daily operations with adequate resources. It converts sanctions compliance from a control function matter into a board/executive responsibility within CBUAE-licensed institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
