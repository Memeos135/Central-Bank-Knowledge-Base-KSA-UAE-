# Insurance Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 48 of 2023)

A defined-term node for UAE Decretal Federal Law No. 48 of 2023 (Insurance Law), cited in the CBUAE Takaful External Shari'ah Audit Standard as the source of the Higher Shari'ah Authority's mandates and powers. It underpins the licensing and supervisory framework for insurance and Takaful business referenced by the Standard alongside the Executive Regulation and the Takaful Insurance Regulation. Relevant to insurers, Takaful companies and their Shari'ah governance functions in the UAE.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
