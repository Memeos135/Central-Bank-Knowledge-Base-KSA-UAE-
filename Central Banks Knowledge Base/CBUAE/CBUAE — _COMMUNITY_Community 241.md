# International VA Crime Cooperation

## Why this community

International cooperation specifically for virtual asset-related offences, addressing the jurisdictional gap created by borderless VA transfers.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster acting as the VA-specific counterpart to the general international cooperation and asset recovery provisions.
- Its operative effect depends on how virtual asset and VASP are defined elsewhere in the regime, so the scope limit must be read against those definitions.
- Practical consequence is at authority level (assistance, tracing, evidence-sharing) rather than a direct institutional compliance duty.
