# Advances to Deposits Ratio Circular

A 1986 UAE Central Bank circular (No. 394) amending the earlier 1977 advances-to-deposits ratio rule and re-anchoring it to liquidity measurement. It caps the ratio at 1:1 between loans, advances and interbank placements with over three months remaining life, on one side, and stable resources (free own funds, interbank deposits received with over six months remaining life, and stable customer deposits) on the other, with defined inclusions such as bank-issued CDs and certain refinancing. It binds all local and foreign banks for their UAE operations, with local banks having overseas branches also complying on a worldwide consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
