# Corporate Governance

Concept node for corporate governance as regulated by CBUAE for Banks: the required comprehensive approach covering strategy, organizational structure, control environment, risk management responsibilities and Board/Staff compensation. It includes bonus limits (individual bonuses capped relative to fixed pay with escalating Board and General Assembly approvals; aggregate staff bonus capped against net profit) and mandatory annual corporate governance disclosure in the annual report. The onus is on each Bank's Board to demonstrate compliance; banks may exceed the minimums.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
