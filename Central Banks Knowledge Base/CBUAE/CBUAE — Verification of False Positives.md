# Verification of False Positives

Addresses adjudication of screening alerts: LFIs must compare potential matches against the UN Consolidated List and Local Terrorist List to determine whether a hit is a true match or a false positive. Where a false positive is established, freezing and other sanctions measures are not required and the transaction or relationship may proceed; the surrounding material includes the control criteria used to judge whether an entity is controlled by a Listed Person. Applies to LFIs.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
