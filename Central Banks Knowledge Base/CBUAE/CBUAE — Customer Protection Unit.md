# Customer Protection Unit

Covers the customer protection chapter: banking confidentiality of customer data and transactions (surviving the end of the relationship, binding directors, Authorized Individuals, employees, experts and consultants), and the listed carve-outs for judicial and Central Bank powers, auditors, cheque certificates, legal disputes, business transfers or mergers, and AML/CTF obligations. It also requires the Board to issue customer protection regulations proportionate to the activities and products of Licensed Financial Institutions, and mandates an independent unit with legal personality to hear and decide customer complaints with binding resolutions. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions and persons with access to customer data.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, AML/CTF, governance/risk
