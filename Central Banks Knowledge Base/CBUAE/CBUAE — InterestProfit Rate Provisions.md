# Interest/Profit Rate Provisions

Provisions governing how Annual Interest/Profit Rate and Annual Percentage Rate must be disclosed in advertising of deposit and Credit Products, including fixed vs variable status, tenor and minimum amount conditions, payment frequency, calculation/compounding basis, and a worked example of total interest/profit and Fees (residential mortgages excepted). It also governs rate-change notification: where an increase applies to the margin over the variable Base Lending/Financing Rate, the institution must give 30 calendar days' notice with specified content, and must notify Consumers in Writing of missed payments and Arrears consequences. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions offering Credit Products to Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
