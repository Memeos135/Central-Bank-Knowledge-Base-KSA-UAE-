# Registered Hawala Provider (RHP)

Defined-term node for the Registered Hawala Provider: a natural or legal person holding a CBUAE-issued Hawala Provider Certificate and entered in the CBUAE Hawala Providers Register to carry on Hawala Activity (transfer and receipt of funds or equivalent value, settled through trade and cash). It fixes related defined terms (Originating/Beneficiary Hawala Provider, Hawala Activity, MVTS, beneficial owner, legal person) and the service perimeter distinguishing registered UAE providers from foreign hawala providers. Relevant to RHPs themselves and to LFIs assessing hawala-provider customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
