# Administrative Decision 122/2018 - Designated Officer Conditions

Insurance Authority Administrative Decision No. 122 of 2018 fixing the minimum qualification conditions for the Designated Officer under the bancassurance instructions (BoD Decision 13/2018). It requires thirty accredited training hours at an insurance company, three specified or Maharati-equivalent training courses with success within two years, submission of course content to the Authority, and enrolment in a continuous training plan on new products. It binds banks and insurance companies appointing Designated Officers for marketing insurance policies through banks.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection
