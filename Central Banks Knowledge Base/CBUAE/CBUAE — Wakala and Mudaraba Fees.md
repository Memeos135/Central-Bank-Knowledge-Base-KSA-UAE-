# Wakala and Mudaraba Fees

Addresses the fee models by which takaful operators are remunerated — wakala fees as a share of contributions and mudaraba fees as a share of investment income, or a wakala-only model — and their presentation in the prescribed financial statement forms. Sits in the accounting and reporting section of the CBUAE takaful financial regulations, affecting how operator income and participant-fund charges are disclosed. Binds licensed takaful companies preparing statutory financial reports for the Authority.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
