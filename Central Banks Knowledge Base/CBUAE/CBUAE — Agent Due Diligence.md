# Agent Due Diligence

A sub-section of the CBUAE hawala guidance setting expectations for Registered Hawala Providers (RHP) that use agents abroad to send or receive payment instructions on their behalf. It requires RHP to identify and assess the ML/TF risk arising from foreign agents and to know who those agents are, while confirming that agency arrangements inside the UAE are prohibited (no "nesting"), since an RHP must run its business personally. It binds RHP only; it sits alongside the customer EDD steps (management approval, source of funds/wealth, business information) described immediately before it.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
