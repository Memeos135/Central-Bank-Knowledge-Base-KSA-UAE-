# Cloud Computing & Continuity

## Why this community

Governance of cloud outsourcing: classifying cloud arrangements by materiality and ensuring the institution can continue operating or withdraw from a provider without disrupting regulated services.

## How members connect

- Cloud computing is the parent concept; materiality assessment is the gating step that determines the intensity of supervisory requirements (approval/notification, contractual terms, controls).
- Exit and resolution planning is a downstream obligation conditional on the materiality classification — material workloads require a documented, testable exit path.
- Business continuity planning is the overlapping general obligation: cloud dependencies must be reflected in continuity and recovery arrangements, so the two duties are read together rather than in isolation.
- Effectively a sub-regime of third-party/outsourcing risk, so concentration risk and non-delegable accountability of the institution carry across.
