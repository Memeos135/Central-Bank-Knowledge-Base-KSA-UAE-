# Shari'a Committee

A concept node covering the role of the Shari'a Committee within the CBUAE Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies. It identifies the matters that require Shari'a Committee sign-off alongside Authority approval — notably the Takaful operating model (Wakala/Mudaraba) and its limits, subsequent model changes, and the use of a capped share of the annual insurance/underwriting surplus for participants' funds (including the higher exceptional percentage supported by actuarial justification). It binds licensed Takaful insurance companies and their boards; approval is dual (internal Shari'a governance plus supervisory consent), so Shari'a clearance alone is not sufficient.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
