# Open Account Trade

Defines open account trade and non-documentary trade finance — where the exporter ships and payment falls due later, and the LFI finances or processes payment without collecting and inspecting underlying trade documents — and identifies these as among the highest-risk activities LFIs routinely engage in because of reduced visibility over parties and goods. Because document-based detection is unavailable, the guidance directs LFIs to rely on CDD/KYC, EDD and transaction monitoring for TBML indicators. Applies to Licensed Financial Institutions offering trade-related payment and financing services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
