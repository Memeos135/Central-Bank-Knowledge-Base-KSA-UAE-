# Credit Risk

A concept node on credit risk in the Pillar 2/ICAAP context, with emphasis on credit concentration risk (name and sectoral concentration) as a material risk the Central Bank expects all UAE banks to assess and capitalise. It links to the Large Exposures regime (the 10% of capital threshold) and to real estate exposure standards, while noting that Pillar 2 measurement should use a broader concentration concept than the regulatory large-exposure test. Binds banks in their risk identification, measurement and capital planning processes, with Board and Senior Management approval expectations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
