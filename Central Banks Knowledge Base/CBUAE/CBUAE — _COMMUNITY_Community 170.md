# ESG Disclosures

## Why this community

The factors that determine what environmental, social and governance information must be disclosed, i.e. the qualitative gate between a general disclosure duty and specific ESG content.

## How members connect

- Principle 2 supplies the criteria (disclosure factors) that scope and shape the ESG-related disclosure category.
- The ESG-Related Disclosures term is the defined output the principle governs, so the two are definition-to-obligation linked.
- Sits alongside the entity-level (Principle 3) and product-level (Principle 4) disclosure principles in the same framework.
