# Unauthorized Transfer Liability

## Why this community

Allocation of loss between provider and customer for unauthorised or incorrectly executed transfers, and the corresponding refund/remediation entitlement.

## How members connect

- Standalone provision in this graph; its practical effect depends on the customer agreement and disclosure provisions that define authorisation and notification duties.
- Determines burden of proof and timing consequences for a dispute decision - i.e. when the provider must refund and when customer conduct shifts liability.
