# Operational Risk & Resilience

## Why this community

The operational risk and operational resilience regime for licensed entities: the required risk management framework, its governance and control infrastructure, and the specific domains (ICT/cyber, incidents, continuity, third parties) it must cover.

## How members connect

- The operational risk management framework article is the hub; nearly every other provision is expressed as a requirement feeding into or implementing that framework.
- A second sub-chain runs from operational resilience into incident management and business continuity planning, i.e. resilience is the outcome obligation and those articles are its components.
- Governance layering: the dedicated risk function plus the internal control system are the accountability structures through which the framework must be discharged.
- Third-party risk management extends the framework to outsourced and vendor arrangements, so responsibility is not transferred with the activity; the annex of risk tools supplies the methodology the framework is expected to apply.
