# Payment Tokens & Crypto-Assets

## Why this community

Definitional perimeter for payment tokens and related crypto-asset activity: what counts as a payment token versus a broader crypto-asset, what backing must sit behind it, and what must be disclosed before it is offered.

## How members connect

- Anchored on the defined term 'Payment Token', which sets the scope boundary against the wider 'Crypto-Asset' category and thereby determines which licensing regime applies.
- 'Reserve of Assets' is the backing obligation attached to the token, i.e. an ongoing prudential/segregation duty rather than a one-off condition.
- 'White Paper' operates as the mandatory disclosure instrument, cross-referencing both the token's characteristics and its reserve arrangements.
- 'Payment Token Services' extends the perimeter from issuance to service provision, pulling intermediaries into the licensed activity list.
