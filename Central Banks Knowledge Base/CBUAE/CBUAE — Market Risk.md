# Market Risk

Node covering market risk as treated in the CBUAE ICAAP/Pillar 2 guidance, including expectations that banks calibrate market-risk analysis around key drivers (portfolios, asset classes, risk factors, geographies, products, tenors) and include stressed periods, and that amortised-cost portfolios be assessed for market-versus-book value differences under Pillar 2. It sits within the broader material-risk identification, measurement, monitoring and capital-planning duties owned by Senior Management and the Board. Binds banks in the UAE preparing ICAAP submissions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** For any judgement about whether an ICAAP submission is complete, market risk must be evidenced because it appears in the minimum risk scope the ICAAP guidance mandates, and the market risk section is the detailed expression of that requirement. ### [[CBUAE — Interest Rate Risk|Interest Rate Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** The link is a scoping one: both interest rate risk in the banking book and market risk appear in the same mandatory minimum list of risks a bank must cover in its internal capital adequacy assessment, so neither can be omitted and neither substitutes for the other. There is no hierarchy between them — IRRBB is a banking-book risk with its own measurement and supervisory outlier expectations, while market risk covers trading-book positions, and conflating the two would understate coverage. Practical consequence: your risk inventory must evidence both separately; because the two nodes were captured from an identical enumerating sentence, check the primary text for the distinct substantive requirements attaching to each.
