# ML Phases (Placement, Layering, Integration)

Concept node describing the three phases of money laundering — placement, layering and integration — as used in the CBUAE Guidelines to help FIs map where their products, channels or institution can be misused. It frames phase analysis as an input to enterprise-wide and relationship-level inherent ML/FT risk assessment, with examples such as commingling, structuring/breaking up amounts and foreign exchange purchases. Guidance-level material addressed to UAE supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
