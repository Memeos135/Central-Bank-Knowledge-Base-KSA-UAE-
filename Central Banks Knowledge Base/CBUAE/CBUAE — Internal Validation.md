# Internal Validation

This node sets the independence and validation requirements around internal market risk models: initial and ongoing validation by a risk management function independent of risk-taking, with more frequent validation after significant structural market changes, portfolio composition changes or material model changes; validation must go beyond profit-and-loss attribution and back-testing to test the model's assumptions. It also requires daily independent risk reporting to Senior Management, integration of model output into day-to-day risk management and trading limits, relevant pricing risk factors with proxies that track actual positions, and at least annual independent internal audit review of the market risk measurement system. It binds Banks using internal models.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
