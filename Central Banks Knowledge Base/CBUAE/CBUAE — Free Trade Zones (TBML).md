# Free Trade Zones (TBML)

A typology and red-flag cluster in CBUAE's TBML guidance addressing the abuse of free trade zones and special economic zones, where separate company formation regimes, limited ownership transparency and reduced customs controls facilitate transshipment, repackaging and re-export schemes. It lists associated red flags such as unjustified FTZ involvement, circuitous routing, circular payment flows and trade patterns inconsistent with a jurisdiction's economy. Directed at licensed financial institutions assessing trade transaction risk; CBUAE-issued, of comparative rather than binding value in KSA.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
