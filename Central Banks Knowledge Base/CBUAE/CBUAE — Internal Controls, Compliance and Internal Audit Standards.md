# Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Standards

Node for the CBUAE Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Standards (161/2018), which set out detailed expectations for the internal audit function: a written policy with conflict-of-interest and rotation cooling-off safeguards, staffing competence and training, confidentiality, the content of the internal audit charter, and the scope of audit coverage. Scope must extend to the whole bank including subsidiaries, branches, outsourced activities and matters of regulatory interest, and must evaluate internal control, risk management, compliance and governance. It binds banks, their boards and board audit committees, and heads of internal audit.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
