# Senior Management (RMIC)

Concept node covering the role and accountability of Senior Management (alongside the Board) under the CBUAE Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation and Standards for Insurance Companies (Circular 25/2022). It sets out Board oversight of Senior Management, group-wide risk coordination duties where a Company has subsidiaries, affiliates or international branches, the elements of an effective risk management system, and joint Board/Senior Management ownership of fraud risk management. It binds insurance companies licensed by the CBUAE and, through group policies, their subsidiaries, affiliates and foreign branches.
