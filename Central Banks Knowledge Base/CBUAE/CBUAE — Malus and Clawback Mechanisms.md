# Malus and Clawback Mechanisms

Node covering the CBUAE remuneration regulation's variable remuneration rules (Article 11) and the related remuneration policy content requirements, including identification of awards subject to reduction or forfeiture. It requires alignment of performance incentives with long-term value creation and the risk time horizon, at least 50% of Material Risk Takers' variable remuneration in shares or equivalent non-cash instruments with gradual vesting of the deferred cash portion, and substantial reduction of variable pay on negative financial performance through Malus or Clawback. It also preserves ISSC independence in the ISSC remuneration review. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.
