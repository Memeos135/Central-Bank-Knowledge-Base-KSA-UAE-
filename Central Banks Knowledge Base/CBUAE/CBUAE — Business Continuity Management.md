# Business Continuity Management

The business continuity management component of the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation, addressing vendor support obligations, reliance on external shared computing (e.g. cloud) for disaster recovery, senior management ownership of the continuity function, and an annual chief-executive statement to the board on the validity of recovery strategies and testing of the plan. It expects at least annual testing with participation of senior management and primary/alternate personnel, documented test plans and results, and a post-mortem review signed off by senior management. Binds SVF Licensees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
