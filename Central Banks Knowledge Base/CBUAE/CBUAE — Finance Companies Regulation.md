# Finance Companies Regulation

The CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation, issued under the Central Bank Law, establishing the licensing, permitted-activity, prudential, governance and conduct framework for Finance Companies and Restricted Licence Finance Companies in the UAE. Its stated aims are customer protection, protection of the companies themselves, and financial-sector stability; the Central Bank may impose requirements additional to those listed in any article. It applies to Finance Companies and Restricted Licence Finance Companies and their branches, with carve-outs for certain non-business inter-corporate short-term credit, direct instalment payment to vendors, and factoring/finance lease activity.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection, banking prudential
