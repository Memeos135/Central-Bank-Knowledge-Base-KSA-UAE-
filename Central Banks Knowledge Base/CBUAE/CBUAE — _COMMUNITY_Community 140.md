# Climate Risk Governance

## Why this community

Board- and management-level allocation of accountability for climate risk and its embedding into the firm's existing enterprise risk management framework, gated by materiality.

## How members connect

- Assignment of responsibilities is the precondition for the integration obligation: named accountable functions must then carry climate risk into the ERM framework.
- Materiality assessment operates as the scoping device determining the depth of integration required.
- Both principles are anchored to the Basel Committee's climate principles as the international benchmark they implement.
