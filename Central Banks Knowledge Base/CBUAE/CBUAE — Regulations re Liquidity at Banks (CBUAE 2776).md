# Regulations re Liquidity at Banks (CBUAE 2776)

CBUAE Regulations re Liquidity at Banks (Circular No. 33/2015, issued 27/05/2015), setting the prudential framework for controlling and monitoring bank liquidity in line with Basel Committee recommendations and international practice, supplemented by a later Guidance Manual. It imposes qualitative requirements for a liquidity risk management framework — including prudent management of liquidity risk and ultimate Board responsibility — alongside quantitative requirements, with supervisory review applied proportionately to bank size, scope of operations, interconnectedness and systemic impact. Binds all banks operating in the UAE at all times.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
