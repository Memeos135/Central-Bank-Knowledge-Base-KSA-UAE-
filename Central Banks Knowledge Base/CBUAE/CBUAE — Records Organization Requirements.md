# Records Organization Requirements

Governs the records an insurance company must organise, maintain and make available to the supervisory Authority: complete transaction records kept in original form, UAE-booked business held locally and accessible, segregated records where both persons/fund accumulation and property/liability licences are held, off-site backups, and validity of electronic records. It prescribes an enumerated list of record types (underwriting, claims, complaints, technical provisions, solvency, reinsurance, investments, related-party transactions, policyholder and shareholder funds, policies and procedures, actuarial reports) and a retention period of at least ten years, plus the Authority's examination and inspection powers and the waiver of actuary confidentiality.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
