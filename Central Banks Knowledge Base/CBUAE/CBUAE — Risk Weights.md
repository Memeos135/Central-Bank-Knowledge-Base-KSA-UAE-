# Risk Weights

Concept node linking risk weighting to the capital adequacy and ICAAP material-risk framework: banks must meet minimum capital plus buffers under business-as-usual and minimum capital under stress, plan capital including the leverage ratio, and identify, measure and cover both Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 risks across the group. It notes that credit concentration risk and IRRBB are treated as material, and that the gross-income-based Pillar 1 operational risk approaches may understate risk, so Pillar 2 capital must compensate. Binds banks, with framework approval sitting with Senior Management and the Board.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
