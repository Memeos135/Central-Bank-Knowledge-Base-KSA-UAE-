# CPMI Wholesale Payments Fraud Strategy

Concept node for the CPMI strategy on reducing the risk of wholesale payments fraud (seven elements covering prevention, detection, response and communication), which the regulation incorporates as a mandatory reference point. System Operators and Settlement Institutions of designated large-value payment systems must take the strategy and its amendments into account when implementing security measures. It sits alongside the PFMI principles applied to systemically important LVPS and is backed by administrative and financial sanctions for non-compliance.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
