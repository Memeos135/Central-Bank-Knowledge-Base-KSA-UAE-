# Liability Risk

Concept node for Liability Risk, the third component of Climate-related Financial Risks alongside Physical and Transition Risk, covering exposure to claims and reputational consequences arising from climate-related events and conduct. It must be captured in market risk identification, operational risk and business continuity assessment, and — for insurance companies — in liquidity impact assessment via increased liability claims and counterparty defaults. Binds CBUAE-supervised Financial Institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
