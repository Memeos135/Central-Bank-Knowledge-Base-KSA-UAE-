# United Nations Security Council

A concept node identifying the United Nations Security Council as the source of the global counter-proliferation framework — UNSCRs that impose targeted financial sanctions, activity-based and sectoral measures, which all Member States are obliged to implement domestically. The guidance notes uneven national implementation and enforcement, and that all UNSC sanctions regimes contain humanitarian exceptions whose jurisdictional requirements differ. Relevant to LFIs as the ultimate legal origin of the TFS obligations applied to them through UAE law and competent-authority directives.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
