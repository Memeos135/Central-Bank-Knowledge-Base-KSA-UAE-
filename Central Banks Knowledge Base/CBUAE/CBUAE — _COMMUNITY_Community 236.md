# Correspondent Banking Risk

## Why this community

Cross-border correspondent banking as a distinct higher-risk category under AML/CTF rules, where a Saudi institution provides account and payment services to a respondent foreign institution and inherits exposure to that respondent's own customer base.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the concept anchors the enhanced due diligence regime applicable to correspondent relationships (respondent institution assessment, senior management approval, understanding of respective AML/CTF responsibilities).
- Legally dependent on the AML Law and Implementing Regulation's EDD obligations rather than standing alone; read as a scope-triggered add-on to ordinary CDD.
- Interacts with the payable-through account and shell-bank prohibitions, and with sanctions/TFS screening of respondent traffic.
- Links outward to remittance and trade-finance clusters, which are common channels through which respondent risk materialises.
