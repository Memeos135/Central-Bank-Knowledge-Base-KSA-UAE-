# Customer Identification (CID) Process

Defines the Customer Identification (CID) Process as the lightest of the three KYC tiers that a Licensed Person (exchange house) must apply under the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business, based on ML/FT risk and transaction value. It sets out the identification steps and the value bands (notably lower-value foreign currency exchange by natural persons) at which CID alone suffices, with CDD/EDD layered on above the thresholds. Binds Licensed Persons; failure to complete CID/CDD/EDD triggers a prohibition on onboarding or transacting and consideration of an STR/SAR to the FIU.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
