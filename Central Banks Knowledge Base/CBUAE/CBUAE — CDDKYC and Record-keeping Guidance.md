# CDD/KYC and Record-keeping Guidance

Guidance on how licensed financial institutions should design and run a risk-based CDD/KYC programme, treating CDD and KYC as interchangeable terms for the ongoing process of identifying customers and assessing their ML/TF/PF and wider financial crime risk before onboarding and throughout the relationship. It addresses group-wide consistency — standardised global procedures, applying UAE standards in less stringent host jurisdictions to the extent permitted, standardised onboarding forms tailored by customer type — and record-keeping of the resulting CDD file. It also flags the expectation of sufficient CDD on customers with direct or indirect access to a correspondent bank account.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** For any question about evidencing compliance, read the CDD obligation as continuing into the record-keeping expectations: the duty is not only to perform due diligence but to retain the underlying materials, including source of wealth documentation, enhanced due diligence outcomes and onboarding approvals. The record-keeping guidance also fixes the accessibility standard, requiring customer records to be retrievable by competent authorities when needed. The practical consequence is that due diligence performed but not documented and retrievable will be treated as unevidenced, exposing the institution on both the CDD and record-keeping limbs.
