# Remittance Reporting System

Concept node for the Central Bank's Remittance Reporting System — the reporting channel through which applicants must undertake to submit remitter and beneficiary data and information electronically, on Central Bank forms, as a precondition to obtaining a Hawala Provider Certificate. It is framed as a licensing/registration condition and an ongoing data-submission duty, sitting alongside other conditions such as security arrangements and use of authorised cash transit providers. Binds hawala provider applicants and registered providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, data/credit info
