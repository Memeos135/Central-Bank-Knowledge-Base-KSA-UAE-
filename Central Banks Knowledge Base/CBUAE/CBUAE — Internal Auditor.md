# Internal Auditor

Sets out the internal audit function for a Licensed Person: scope covering all business activities, branches, core and non-core functions, reconciliation, liquidity and capital adequacy alongside AML and regulatory compliance. It requires adherence to professional auditing standards, access to staff and records, a board-approved annual audit plan developed with the audit committee, timely remediation of findings, and a dedicated, non-combined role with escalation of independence or conflict concerns. Adjacent provisions govern external auditor appointment subject to CBUAE no-objection and the regulator's power to appoint auditors itself.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
