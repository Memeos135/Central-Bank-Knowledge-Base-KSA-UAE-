# Registrar

Defines and sets the duties of the Registrar under the AML/CTF/CPF Executive Regulations regarding corporate transparency: obtaining, verifying, publishing and keeping up to date basic company information, nominee director/shareholder information, and beneficial owner information, and coordinating with the Supervisory Authority and National Committee on legal-person risk assessment. It also frames the corresponding company obligations (shareholder registers held in-State, 15-working-day updating of beneficial owner data, cooperation with FIs, DNFBPs and VASPs, and the prohibition on bearer shares). Binds the Registrar, registered companies, and indirectly the institutions that rely on such information for CDD.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
