# Training Controls

Sets expectations for AML/CFT/CPF training as a control: compulsory induction and periodic refresher training for all staff including the board and senior management, specialised role-based training (e.g. TBML and proliferation-financing awareness for trade finance teams), attendance and assessment record-keeping with follow-up on non-attendance or failures, and tailoring to the LFI's risk profile and local requirements. The same section introduces independent testing of the AML/CFT/CPF programme (suggested every 12–18 months or on significant change) and its autonomy. Binds LFIs and their governing bodies.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
