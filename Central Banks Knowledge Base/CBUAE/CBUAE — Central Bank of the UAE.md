# Central Bank of the UAE

Institutional node for the Central Bank of the UAE, the licensing, regulatory, supervisory and enforcement authority across the instruments in this corpus. Its powers derive from Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities, including licensing of activities such as stored value facilities and the imposition of supervisory action and administrative and financial sanctions. Binds all licensees and entities subject to CBUAE regulation; also the reference point for interpretation of its regulations.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF, consumer protection
