# Credit Reports / Al Etihad Credit Bureau

Node on the credit reporting obligations imposed on Specialized Banks: submitting borrower credit information to Al Etihad Credit Bureau (and any future UAE credit information agencies) at least monthly, and obtaining credit information before extending credit to an individual or corporate borrower. It sits in the same part of the regulation as cross-references to CBUAE corporate governance, AML/CFT (including STR reporting to the FIU) and consumer protection requirements. Binds licensed Specialized Banks.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, banking prudential, consumer protection, AML/CTF
