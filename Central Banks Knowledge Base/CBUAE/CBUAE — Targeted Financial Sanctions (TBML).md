# Targeted Financial Sanctions (TBML)

A concept node within the CBUAE trade-based money laundering guidance covering targeted financial sanctions duties as they arise in trade finance. It explains that licensed financial institutions must promptly give effect to UAE competent-authority directives implementing UN Security Council Chapter VII decisions, with Cabinet Decision 74 of 2020 supplying the domestic framework, including the UAE Local Terrorist List and the UN Consolidated List, and requiring appropriate risk management arrangements. It binds LFIs supervised by the CBUAE and sits alongside the CDD, EDD and ongoing monitoring expectations set out in the same guidance.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
