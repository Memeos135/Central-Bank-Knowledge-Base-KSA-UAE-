# Internal Control

A concept node for "internal control" as used across the CBUAE Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Standards 161/2018 — the system of policies, checks, reporting and oversight that internal audit must evaluate alongside risk management, compliance and governance processes. It anchors the requirement that internal audit coverage extend to the entire bank, its subsidiaries, branches, outsourced activities and matters of regulatory interest. Relevant to banks' boards, senior management and control functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
