# Risk Measurement & Models

## Why this community

Requirements on how institutions measure risk and govern the use of internal models, including validation and the limits of model reliance.

## How members connect

- Two-tier pairing: the Regulation article states the obligation; the identically numbered Standards article supplies the detailed expectations and is read as its implementing text.
- Model-measurement duties are the input layer to risk appetite monitoring and stress testing in the wider risk-management regime.
