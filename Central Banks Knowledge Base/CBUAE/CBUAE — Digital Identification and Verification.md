# Digital Identification and Verification

A sub-section of the CBUAE CDD/KYC and record-keeping guidance dealing with digital identity systems (electronic identity databases, digital credentials, biometrics, and identification APIs/platforms) as an alternative to documentary and non-documentary verification. It permits licensed financial institutions to identify and verify customers digitally provided the system is "reliable" and "independent" — meaning technology, governance and controls give adequate confidence in identity proofing, enrolment and authentication — while preserving the underlying principle that no relationship may be opened without a reasonable belief in the customer's true identity. It binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions (banks, exchange houses, finance companies, payment and payment token service providers, hawala providers, insurers) and cross-refers to the CBUAE's dedicated digital identification guidance; note this is UAE, not KSA, law.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
