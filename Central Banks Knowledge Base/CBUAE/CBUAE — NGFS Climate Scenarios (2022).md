# NGFS Climate Scenarios (2022)

This node refers to the NGFS Climate Scenarios (2022) and related NGFS/FSB materials listed in the Annex of the CBUAE climate-related financial risk instrument. These are external reference scenarios and supervisory guides, not binding rules; they are relevant because firms building scenario analysis capability may rely on existing or emerging global or regional climate scenarios while developing internal capabilities. Read alongside Principle 7 on scenario analysis and stress testing.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
