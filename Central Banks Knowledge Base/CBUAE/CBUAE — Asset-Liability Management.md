# Asset-Liability Management

Concept node covering asset-liability and liquidity risk management under the CBUAE ERM standards for insurance companies, requiring strategies, policies and processes so liabilities can be met as they fall due in normal and stressed conditions. Elements include liquidity stress testing, a portfolio of unencumbered liquid assets meeting stated quality criteria, a contingency funding plan, use of complaints data to ensure timely payment to Insureds and Beneficiaries, assessment of reinsurance programme effects on liquidity, and reporting to the Central Bank. Binds insurance and Takaful companies, with ALM support expected from the Risk Management function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
