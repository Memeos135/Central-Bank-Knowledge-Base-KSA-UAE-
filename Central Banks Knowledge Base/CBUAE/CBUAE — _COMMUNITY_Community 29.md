# Trade-Based Money Laundering

## Why this community

Trade-based money laundering: the use of commercial trade transactions, documentation and settlement to disguise illicit proceeds or move value across borders, and the typologies institutions are expected to detect during transaction monitoring and trade-finance review.

## How members connect

- Hub-and-spoke structure: the TBML concept is the parent node and each typology (invoicing and shipment manipulation, multiple invoicing, fictitious documents, back-to-back letters of credit, pass-through accounts) is a referenced red-flag category feeding the same suspicion-reporting duty.
- Shared legal consequence: identification of any typology engages CDD/EDD, source-of-funds enquiry, and the obligation to file a suspicious transaction report.
- Actor-based spokes (third-party intermediaries, surrogate shopping networks, registered hawala providers) link this cluster to beneficial ownership and MVTS obligations; jurisdictional spokes (free trade zones) link to country-risk and higher-risk geography assessment.
- Duplicate free-trade-zone nodes and the services-based variant show the same typology framing extended from goods to services, indicating the concept is applied by substance, not by documentary form.
