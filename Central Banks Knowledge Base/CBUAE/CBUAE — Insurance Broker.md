# Insurance Broker

Defined-term node for "Insurance Broker" under the CBUAE Insurance Brokers' Regulation: a licensed person that independently intermediates between an insurance or reinsurance client and a Company, and is remunerated by the Company with which the cover is placed. The definition anchors related terms (Insurance Brokerage as soliciting/negotiating/selling, Insurance Broker's Representatives, Internal Controls) and drives the Regulation's conduct rules, including the prohibition on brokers collecting Premiums, Claim Settlements or Premium refunds in primary insurance. Binds all entities licensed or seeking licence for insurance brokerage in the UAE, and the Companies dealing with them.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
