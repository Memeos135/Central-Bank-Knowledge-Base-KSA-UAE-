# Article 3: Responsibilities and Actions of the Parent

Article 3 makes the Parent responsible for Group-wide adherence to Central Bank Laws and Regulations, expressly including corporate governance and risk management requirements. It gives the Central Bank power to direct the Parent to act or refrain from acting, and to ensure other Group entities do likewise, and to require remediation within a set timeframe in addition to measures on individual Companies. Binds the Parent (or ultimate Parent) of an insurance Group supervised by CBUAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
