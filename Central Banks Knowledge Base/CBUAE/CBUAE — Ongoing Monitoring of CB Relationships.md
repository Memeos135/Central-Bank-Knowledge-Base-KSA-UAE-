# Ongoing Monitoring of CB Relationships

Guidance section on post-onboarding obligations in correspondent banking: continuous transaction monitoring of activity to, from or through the correspondent account against the expected purpose and profile of the respondent, plus periodic relationship review to confirm the relationship stays within risk appetite. It sets an expectation of at least annual ongoing due diligence for high-risk correspondent relationships, refreshed CDD/KYC on a risk-sensitive basis, and risk-mitigation levers such as product restrictions, volume limits, real-time monitoring and elevated senior management approval. Applies to LFIs providing correspondent services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
