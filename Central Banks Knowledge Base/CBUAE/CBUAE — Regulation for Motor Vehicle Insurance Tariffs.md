# Regulation for Motor Vehicle Insurance Tariffs

Insurance Authority Board of Directors' Decision No. 30 of 2016 issuing the Regulation for Motor Vehicle Insurance Tariffs (now within CBUAE's insurance mandate), effective 1 January 2017. It fixes minimum and maximum premium tariffs for third-party liability policies (Table 1) and for combined loss/damage and third-party liability policies (Table 2), sets a 13-month insurance period with pro-rata premium, requires tariffs to be inclusive of coverages, administrative charges, commissions and ambulance/medical transport loading, and prohibits insurers, agents or brokers from taking any additional amount without Authority approval. It binds insurance companies, agents and brokers writing motor insurance in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
