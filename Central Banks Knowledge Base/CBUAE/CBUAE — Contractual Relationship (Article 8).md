# Contractual Relationship (Article 8)

Article 8 of the CBUAE Takaful Insurance Standard sets the required contractual architecture between the Takaful Company, the Takaful Insurance Fund, the Participant and the Beneficiary, and requires that architecture to be stated plainly in the operational model, Fund Charter, Participation Membership Policy and Takaful Insurance Policy. It mandates the Wakala model for property and liability takaful (Company as agent of the Fund) and for persons/fund-accumulation business, with the Participant–Fund relationship built on Tabaru (donation) and the Participants' Investment Account governed by a Wakala Bi Al-Istithmar contract. It binds licensed Takaful companies (including takaful windows) and determines how the Beneficiary of the Fund is identified.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
