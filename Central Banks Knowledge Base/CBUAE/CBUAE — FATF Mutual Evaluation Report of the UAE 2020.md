# FATF Mutual Evaluation Report of the UAE 2020

This node is the FATF Mutual Evaluation Report on the UAE (April 2020) as relied upon in CBUAE guidance on dealers in precious metals and stones (DPMS) and the real estate sector; the MER's finding that both sectors are highly important in terms of ML/TF risk and materiality is the evidentiary basis for that guidance. It is not itself a binding instrument, but it drives risk-based expectations for Licensed Financial Institutions banking DPMS and real estate customers. The guidance it underpins applies to CBUAE-licensed/supervised entities including banks, foreign bank branches, exchange houses, finance companies, payment service providers, registered hawala providers and insurance companies, agencies and brokers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
