# Implicit Support

Concept node within the CBUAE capital standards covering non-contractual (implicit) support to securitisation structures, distinguished from contractual credit enhancement provided at inception (e.g. overcollateralisation, subordinated notes, spread accounts, clean-up calls above the 10% threshold). It is framed as a risk-management and capital-integrity issue: banks must recognise the likelihood that they would support their own securitisations under stress, measure that potential support as part of reputational-risk assessment and stress testing, and reflect it in the ICAAP and Pillar 2 capital. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE, in particular originators and sponsors relying on securitisation as a funding tool.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
