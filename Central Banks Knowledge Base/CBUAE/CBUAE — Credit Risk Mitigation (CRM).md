# Credit Risk Mitigation (CRM)

Concept node on credit risk mitigation — the conditions under which collateral, guarantees and credit derivatives reduce regulatory capital requirements, including treatment of currency, maturity and asset mismatches between protection and the underlying exposure, and the option to apply only the higher of the two capital charges where conditions are met. Recognition of CRM techniques for regulatory capital is conditional on meeting risk management and disclosure requirements subject to Central Bank supervisory review. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks claiming capital relief from credit protection or eligible collateral.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
