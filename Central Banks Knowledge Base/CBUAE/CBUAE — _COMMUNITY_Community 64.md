# Correspondent Banking & Wire Transfers

## Why this community

AML/CTF obligations attaching to cross-border correspondent banking and wire transfers, including the prohibition on dealing with shell banks, nesting/downstream relationship risk, and group-wide and supervisory oversight.

## How members connect

- Shared AML defined terms drive the obligations: Financial Institutions is the obligated-person definition, and Correspondent Banking Relationship, Wire Transfers, Shell Bank, Nesting and Intermediation set the scope of enhanced due diligence.
- Nesting and intermediation describe the indirect-access risk that makes respondent-side CDD and the shell bank prohibition operative in practice.
- Financial Group extends obligations beyond the single institution to group-wide programmes, branches and majority-owned subsidiaries.
- Supervisory Authority is the enforcement and reporting counterpart, confirming who supervises the obligated institutions and receives the relevant filings.
