# Safeguards & Reporting

## Why this community

Control environment obligations: the protective measures a regulated entity must have in place and the paired duty to test those measures and report outcomes to the supervisor or governing body.

## How members connect

- Direct obligation chain: safeguards create the control duty; testing and reporting create the assurance duty that evidences the first is effective.
- Cross-reference means a safeguard failure and a testing/reporting failure are separate breaches — remediation of one does not cure the other.
- Reporting element drives the notification trigger and timing that a compliance function must diarise.
