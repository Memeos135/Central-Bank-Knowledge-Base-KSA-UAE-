# Reinsurance

Concept node for "reinsurance" as defined and regulated under the 2019 reinsurance decision, covering the definitional vocabulary of the risk-transfer chain: cession, retrocession, retention, reinsurance ceded versus accepted by an insurance company, and the facultative, treaty and facultative-obligatory placement types. It also carries the permission for reinsurers to combine persons/fund-accumulation and property/liability lines, and for Takaful reinsurers to write family and general Takaful reinsurance, subject to constitutional-document and separation conditions. Relevant to insurers, reinsurers and Takaful reinsurers licensed or registered in the State.

**Regimes:** other
