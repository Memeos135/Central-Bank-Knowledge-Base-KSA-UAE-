# Suspicious Transaction Reports

Node covering the suspicious transaction reporting regime (Division Five, Articles 17–18) of the UAE AML/CTF Executive Regulations, plus the Financial Intelligence Unit's competences in handling such reports. It requires reporting entities to develop and continually update suspicion indicators and to report immediately to the Unit, without invoking banking or professional secrecy or contractual liability, any transaction, attempted transaction or funds suspected of being linked to a Crime, regardless of value, and to respond promptly to Unit requests. It binds Financial Institutions, DNFBPs and Virtual Asset Service Providers, with a narrow exemption for lawyers, notaries, other independent legal professionals and statutory auditors acting in defined legal-advisory or litigation contexts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
