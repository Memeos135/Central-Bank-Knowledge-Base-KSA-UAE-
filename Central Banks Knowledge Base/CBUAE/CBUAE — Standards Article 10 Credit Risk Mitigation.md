# Standards Article 10: Credit Risk Mitigation

Article 10 of the CBUAE Credit Risk Standards addresses credit risk mitigation — eligible collateral, haircuts and documented expected recovery cash flows — and is the reference point for deductions permitted when computing Stage 3 provisions. The surrounding context sets the credit risk management framework scope (group-wide view, all on- and off-balance-sheet exposures, concentration risk, climate-related credit risk) and the independent Credit Risk Management Function's powers over policy, measurement and defaulted facility review. It binds LFIs, their boards, CROs and credit risk functions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
