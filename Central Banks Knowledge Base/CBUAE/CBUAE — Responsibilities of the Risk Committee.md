# Responsibilities of the Risk Committee

Node covering Article 6 of the CBUAE remuneration regulation on the board risk committee's role in the remuneration framework, together with the Material Risk Taker identification provisions in Article 3. It requires annual review of the Material Risk Taker list by Control Functions and board approval on the remuneration committee's recommendation, formal notification of designated persons, and sets criteria for who must be designated (e.g. authority over material credit, underwriting, reinsurance or trading book exposures, or acceptance of large/non-standard risks). It binds boards, risk and remuneration committees of CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.
