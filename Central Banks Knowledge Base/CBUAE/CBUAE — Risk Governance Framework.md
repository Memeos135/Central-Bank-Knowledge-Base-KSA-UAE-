# Risk Governance Framework

Defined-term and obligation node for the "Risk Governance Framework" under the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation 153/2018 — the board- and management-level structure for setting strategy and risk approach, articulating and monitoring risk appetite and risk limits, and identifying, measuring, managing and controlling risk. It also carries the group dimension: where CBUAE is not the primary regulator and group entities control or influence risk management, the bank's framework must address group-relationship risks, linkages and differences with the group framework, dependence on group risk functions, and group monitoring/reporting lines. It binds banks licensed to take deposits in the UAE, with an associated public disclosure duty (annual report and website) on the framework and the nature and extent of risk exposures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
