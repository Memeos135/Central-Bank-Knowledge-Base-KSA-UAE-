# License

Concept node for the License requirement under the retail payment services regime, covering the four License categories (I–IV) mapped to specific Retail Payment Services, and the separate licensing of Card Schemes. It sets out that a Person intending to provide Retail Payment Services must apply for the appropriate category, that Card Schemes must be licensed before commencing operations in the State, the 90-day decision timeframe, the central bank's discretion to impose conditions or refuse, and its exclusive right to issue BINs. Binds Applicants, licensed Payment Service Providers and Card Schemes; exemptions may be granted and withdrawn by the central bank.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
