# Electronic Insurance Regulations (Resolution 18/2020)

Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 18 of 2020 (UAE), the Electronic Insurance Regulations, which govern insurance business conducted through websites, smart applications and other electronic/smart systems. It sets definitions (Company, Insurance-Related Professions, Insurance Proposer, Insurance Agent, Insurance Broker, Electronic Insurance Operations), the prior-approval regime including a board-approved action plan with volume projections and risk analysis (cyber, adverse selection, ML/TF, strategic risk), and defers to the federal electronic commerce and confidentiality laws for matters not specifically addressed. It binds licensed insurance companies (including branches of foreign insurers and Takaful companies), insurance-related professions, and bancassurance distribution to the extent relevant.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF
