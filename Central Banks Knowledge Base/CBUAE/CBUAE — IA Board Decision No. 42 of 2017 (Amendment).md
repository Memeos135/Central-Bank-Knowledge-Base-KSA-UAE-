# IA Board Decision No. 42 of 2017 (Amendment)

Amending Insurance Authority Board decision that modifies the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Loss and Damage originally issued under Decision 25/2016; its changes are carried into the consolidated policy text (as at 18/01/2021). Relevant mainly for tracing which policy conditions, definitions (e.g. Rider, Premium, Basic and Ancillary Deductible, Natural Disaster, Insurance Period) and coverage terms were altered and from when. Binds insurers issuing motor own-damage cover and affects insureds' contractual entitlements.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
