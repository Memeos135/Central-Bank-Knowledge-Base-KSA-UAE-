# Credit Risk Standardised Approach

## Why this community

Risk-weighting of banking-book credit exposures under the standardised approach: how exposures are bucketed by counterparty class and how external ratings and conversion factors drive the applicable risk weight.

## How members connect

- A single parent rule (the standardised approach) enumerates the exposure classes — sovereigns, PSEs, banks, corporates, regulatory retail, residential property-secured claims — each a defined term with its own eligibility conditions.
- Off-balance-sheet items enter the same calculation via credit conversion factors, linking the exposure classes to a common exposure-at-default measure.
- Rating inputs depend on recognised ECAIs, whose eligibility is tied to international standards (IOSCO CRA conduct code) and whose grades are mapped through rating scale correspondence tables.
- Preferential treatment of PSE claims is conditioned on inclusion in the published government-related entities list, a cross-reference that determines whether sovereign-equivalent weights apply.
