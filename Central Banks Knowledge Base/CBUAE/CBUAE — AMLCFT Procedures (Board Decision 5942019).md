# AML/CFT Procedures (Board Decision 59/4/2019)

CBUAE Board of Directors' Decision No. 59/4/2019 setting the supervisory and enforcement framework for anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing and financing of illegal organisations. It obliges Financial Institutions and their concerned persons to comply with Decree Federal Law No. 20 of 2018, Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019 (Executive Regulation) and CBUAE instructions/guidance; it grants the Central Bank powers of periodic and unannounced inspection, information requests, administrative sanctions, appeal rights and publication of sanctions. It binds all persons licensed or registered by the CBUAE that carry out the financial activities listed in Article 2 of the Executive Regulation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
