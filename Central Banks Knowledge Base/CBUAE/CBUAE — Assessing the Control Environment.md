# Assessing the Control Environment

Guidance on assessing and quantifying the effectiveness of an LFI's AML/CFT/CPF control environment, so that control strength can be applied against inherent risk. It sets out a stepwise approach: identify key controls, define effectiveness criteria, measure performance, score, aggregate (including weighting) and adjust for interdependencies among controls. Aimed at LFIs' compliance and internal audit functions preparing or validating the control-effectiveness component of the risk assessment.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
