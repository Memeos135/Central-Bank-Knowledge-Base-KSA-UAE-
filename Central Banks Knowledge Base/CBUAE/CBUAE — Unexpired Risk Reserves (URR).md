# Unexpired Risk Reserves (URR)

Supplementary technical provision required where the Unearned Contribution Reserve is judged inadequate to cover future liabilities: the Unexpired Risk Reserve is created at line-of-business level to cover the shortfall, with its calculation including cost of capital or other profit loadings. Unlike UPR it is created only as needed, but is subject to at least annual actuarial certification. Binds Takaful and Re-Takaful companies determining technical provisions for solvency and reporting purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
