# Financial Reporting and External Audit Regulation 162/2018

CBUAE Financial Reporting and External Audit Regulation (Circular 162/2018), issued under the Central Bank Law and to be read with the Corporate Governance Regulation and Standards for banks. It governs record-keeping, preparation of financial statements under IFRS and CBUAE instructions, publication of audited annual statements, and the appointment, rotation, independence and supervision of the External Auditor. It binds all banks licensed by the CBUAE, with duties also falling on boards, board audit committees and external auditors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
