# Higher Shari'ah Authority (HSA)

Defined-term node for the Higher Shari'ah Authority, the CBUAE's central authority for Islamic banking and financial activities. Its resolutions, fatwas, regulations and standards are the top tier of the Shari'ah compliance hierarchy; an institution's ISSC resolutions are valid only where they do not contradict HSA resolutions. The concept recurs across multiple CBUAE Islamic-finance instruments and binds all licensed institutions carrying on Shari'ah-compliant activities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
