# Health Insurance Third Party Administrator (TPA)

Defined-role node for the Health Insurance Third Party Administrator, the licensed entity that administers and settles health insurance claims on behalf of insurers and contracts with medical service providers on their behalf. The instructions circumscribe the role: no selling or marketing of health policies, no ownership or management stake in medical facilities or health insurers, segregated accounts for claims funds, and no service agreements with insurers not licensed in the UAE. Obligations attach to the TPA itself, with knock-on contractual duties for insurers and providers.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
