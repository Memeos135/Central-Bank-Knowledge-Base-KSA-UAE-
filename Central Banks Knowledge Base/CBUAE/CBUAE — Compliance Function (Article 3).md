# Compliance Function (Article 3)

Article-level node on the compliance function under the CBUAE Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Standards (161/2018). It sets the function's AML/CTF duties (examining suspicious transactions and reporting to the Central Bank's Financial Intelligence Department, bi-annual AML/CTF framework assessments sent to FID with senior management comments, ongoing ML/TF training), staff knowledge and training expectations, unrestricted access to staff, records and data, group-level organisation options, at least annual board-level performance assessment with an independent external quality assurance review every five years, and limits on outsourcing compliance tasks. It binds banks, their boards and board committees, and heads of compliance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
