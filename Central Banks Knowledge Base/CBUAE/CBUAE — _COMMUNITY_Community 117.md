# Emiratization Compliance

## Why this community

UAE labour-nationalisation (Emiratization) compliance for licensed financial institutions, including penalties for non-compliance and for artificial or fictitious hiring arrangements.

## How members connect

- Regulation-to-definition hierarchy: the penalties regulation supplies the operative sanctions and defines the regulated population (licensed institutions) and the prohibited conduct (fictitious Emiratization).
- NAFIS and the Tawteen system are the registration/reporting channels through which compliance is evidenced and breaches detected.
- Cross-jurisdiction flag: this is a UAE/CBUAE regime, not a SAMA obligation - use only for comparative purposes, not as KSA authority.
