# DNFBP & Real Estate AML

## Why this community

AML/CFT risk arising where financial institutions bank or service non-financial sectors — real estate and dealers in precious metals and stones — which are themselves designated obliged entities. Note: these members are UAE federal instruments and FATF material, not SAMA rules, and are relevant only as comparative or cross-border reference.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: the federal AML/CFT decree-law sets the primary obligations, the Cabinet implementing regulation defines DNFBP scope and detail, and the supervisory guidance operationalises both for institutions servicing these sectors.
- DPMS is a sub-category of the DNFBP definition, so the definitional node controls which businesses fall inside the regime.
- Real estate sector risk and DPMS risk are the typology inputs that justify the guidance's expectations on risk-based CDD, source of funds and monitoring of sector clients.
- The FATF mutual evaluation is cited as the evidentiary basis for the identified sector vulnerabilities and the resulting supervisory expectations.
