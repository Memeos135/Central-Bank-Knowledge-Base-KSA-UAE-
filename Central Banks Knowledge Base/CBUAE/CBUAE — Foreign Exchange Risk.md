# Foreign Exchange Risk

Node covering the foreign exchange component of the market risk capital framework: how a bank identifies net open positions in each foreign currency and in gold, converts them at spot into local currency, and derives an overall net open position (greater of aggregate net long or net short positions, excluding US dollar, plus the absolute gold position) to which an 8% capital charge applies. It also addresses consolidated measurement, including use of internal currency position limits as a proxy for marginal foreign branches/subsidiaries subject to ex-post monitoring. It binds banks subject to the central bank's capital adequacy/market risk standard, and links into the broader ICAAP-style comprehensive risk assessment expectations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
