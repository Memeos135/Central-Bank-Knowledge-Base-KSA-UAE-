# Money Laundering Offence (Article 2)

This node captures the money laundering offence under the 2025 UAE AML law: intentionally converting, transferring or transacting in proceeds to conceal their illicit origin; concealing or disguising the nature, source, location, disposition, movement or ownership of proceeds; acquiring, possessing or using proceeds; or helping the predicate offender escape punishment. It confirms ML as an autonomous offence, excluded from concurrence rules under the Crimes and Penalties Law, with no requirement of a predicate conviction and knowledge inferable from objective circumstances. It binds any person, natural or legal, and shapes how reporting entities frame suspicion.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
