# Conduct of Business

## Why this community

Conduct-of-business requirements governing how a licensed entity deals with customers and the market — fair dealing, disclosure and the consumer-protection interface with prudential licensing conditions.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster; connects outward to consumer protection principles and to the defined 'Customer'/'Consumer' population used elsewhere in the same instrument.
- Breach of conduct rules typically feeds the supervisory/enforcement chain rather than the reporting chain, so read alongside SAMA's general supervisory powers.
