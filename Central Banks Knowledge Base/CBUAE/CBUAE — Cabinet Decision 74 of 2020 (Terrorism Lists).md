# Cabinet Decision 74 of 2020 (Terrorism Lists)

Node situating Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020 on terrorism lists within the UAE's AML/CFT legal architecture, alongside Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 and Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019.
