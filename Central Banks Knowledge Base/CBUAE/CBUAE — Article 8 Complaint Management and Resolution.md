# Article 8: Complaint Management and Resolution

Article 8 establishes the end-to-end Complaint handling regime: a UAE-situated Consumer Complaint Management function independent of Retail Operations, Senior Management–approved policies, acknowledgment and turnaround-time standards, staff quality monitoring, and a free-of-charge Complaint Resolution Mechanism supervised by the Central Bank. It also requires ongoing monitoring and analysis of Complaint Data (received/closed/pending, substantiated, overdue, outside target time, before courts, escalated) for regulatory reporting. Binds all Licensed Financial Institutions in their dealings with Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
