# Three Lines of Defense (Shari'ah Governance)

Concept node applying the three lines of defence model to Shari'ah governance in IFIs: business lines, the internal Shari'ah control function supporting the ISSC, and the internal Shari'ah audit function, which must remain separate in reporting and human resources, with external Shari'ah audit providing annual independent assessment. It frames the definitions of Shari'ah supervision, internal and external Shari'ah audit, and compliance with Islamic Shari'ah (HSA resolutions plus non-conflicting ISSC resolutions). Binds IFIs and their boards in structuring Shari'ah control and audit functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
