# Transaction Screening

Concept node distinguishing transaction screening — screening a movement of value (funds, goods or assets) into, out of or through the institution between parties or accounts — from name screening of customer, employee, vendor and related-party reference data. It addresses which data elements are relevant to sanctions risk, data capture and reconciliation between core systems and screening systems, detection controls such as exception reporting and trend analysis, and root-cause escalation of data quality failures. Relevant to LFI compliance and operations functions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, data/credit info
