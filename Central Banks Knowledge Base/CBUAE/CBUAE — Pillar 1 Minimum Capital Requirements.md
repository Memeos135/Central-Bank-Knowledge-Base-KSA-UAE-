# Pillar 1 Minimum Capital Requirements

Concept node for Pillar 1 minimum capital requirements (credit, market and operational risk) as the baseline against which Pillar 2 add-ons are assessed in the CBUAE capital adequacy framework. The surrounding guidance explains that banks must cover Pillar 1 risks with capital under the Capital Adequacy Regulations and Standards, and then quantify residual risks not adequately captured by Pillar 1 as Pillar 2 capital, feeding capital planning, stress testing and management actions. Binds all banks licensed by the UAE Central Bank, at group and entity level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
