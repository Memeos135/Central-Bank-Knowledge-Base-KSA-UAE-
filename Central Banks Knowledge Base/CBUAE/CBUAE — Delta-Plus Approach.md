# Delta-Plus Approach

Node describing the delta-plus method for options, under which options enter each market risk category as a delta-weighted position (market value of the underlying times delta), with equity options fed into the equity charge, FX and gold options into the foreign exchange charge, commodity options into the simplified or maturity ladder approach, and interest rate/debt options slotted into time bands on a two-legged basis. Applies to banks that write options or otherwise cannot use the simplified option approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
