# Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 (Insurance Authority)

Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 on the Establishment of the Insurance Authority and Organisation of Insurance Operations (as amended), the primary UAE insurance statute cited as the legal basis for insurance regulations and resolutions, including the Electronic Insurance Regulations. Functions of the former Insurance Authority under this law were subsequently assumed by the Central Bank under Federal Law No. 14 of 2018. It binds insurance companies, reinsurers, insurance agents, brokers and other insurance-related professions operating in the State.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
