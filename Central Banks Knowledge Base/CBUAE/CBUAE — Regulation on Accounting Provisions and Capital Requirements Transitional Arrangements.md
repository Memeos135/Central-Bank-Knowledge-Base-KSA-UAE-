# Regulation on Accounting Provisions and Capital Requirements Transitional Arrangements

CBUAE Circular 04/2020 regulation creating a five-year 'prudential filter' that lets banks and finance companies add back part of the increase in IFRS 9 Stage 1 and Stage 2 ECL provisions to regulatory capital, applied retroactively from 1 January 2020 and fully unwound by 1 January 2025. It defines scope, the add-back calculation, offsetting adjustments to exposure values and Tier 2, use of adjusted capital in all capital metrics, and mandatory disclosure of both filtered and unfiltered ratios. Binds all banks (including foreign bank branches) and finance companies operating in the UAE; interpretation is reserved to CBUAE and breaches attract supervisory sanctions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
