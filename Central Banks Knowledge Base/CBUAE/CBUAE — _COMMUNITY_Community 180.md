# Unhosted Wallet Due Diligence

## Why this community

Treatment of virtual asset transfers involving wallets not controlled by a regulated intermediary, where counterparty information is absent and standard transfer-rule data cannot be obtained.

## How members connect

- A single definitional dependency: the enhanced due diligence duty applies only once a transfer is characterised as involving an unhosted wallet.
- The EDD requirement operates as a risk-mitigation substitute for counterparty identification that the travel-rule/transfer regime cannot supply.
