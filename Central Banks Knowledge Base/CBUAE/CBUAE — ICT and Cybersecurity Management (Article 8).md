# ICT and Cybersecurity Management (Article 8)

Article 8 sets ICT and cybersecurity risk management obligations for LFIs, requiring the Risk Appetite and Tolerance to expressly address ICT and cyber risk and resilience, and requiring infrastructure that supports business needs in normal and stressed conditions while preserving data and system integrity, confidentiality and availability. It mandates protection, detection, response and recovery programmes that are regularly tested, regular Board and Senior Management information on ICT and cyber exposures, incidents and identified weaknesses, and a policy covering governance, risk ownership and compliance, security controls (access, identity, Critical Information Asset protection, network and vendor security, vulnerability testing, information classification), data management, ICT operations and change/patch management, incident response and recovery, business continuity and disaster recovery plans, project management and systems development/acquisition. Processes must be reviewed and tested regularly against evolving threats and Risk Tolerance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
