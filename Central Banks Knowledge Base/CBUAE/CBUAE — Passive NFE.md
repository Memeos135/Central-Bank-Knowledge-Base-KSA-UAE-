# Passive NFE

Defined term for a Non-Financial Entity that is not an Active NFE, or certain managed Investment Entities in non-participating jurisdictions. Classification matters because a Passive NFE account becomes reportable where any Controlling Person is a Reportable Person; the node also sets out the Active NFE criteria and the evidence (self-certification, public information, AML/KYC data) used to establish status.

**Regimes:** other, AML/CTF
