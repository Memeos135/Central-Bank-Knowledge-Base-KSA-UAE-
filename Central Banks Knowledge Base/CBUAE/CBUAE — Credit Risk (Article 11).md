# Credit Risk (Article 11)

Article 11 of the CBUAE Islamic banks risk management Standard sets credit risk requirements for Islamic banks and Islamic Windows: a credit strategy including pricing and risk tolerance, oversight structure, credit policies, criteria, review processes, accepted risk mitigation and limits, measurement and analysis of exposures, and systems to monitor individual financings, handle remedial action and ensure adequate provisions. It requires a credit risk management and reporting framework covering all assets across the stages of Shari'ah-compliant products, investments and securitisation. It binds Islamic banks and banks housing an Islamic Window in addition to general CBUAE credit risk requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
