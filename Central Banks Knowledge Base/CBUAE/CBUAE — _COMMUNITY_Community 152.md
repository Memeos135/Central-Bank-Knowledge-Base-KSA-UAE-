# Risk Governance & Outsourcing

## Why this community

Governance, risk and control architecture of the licensed entity, including accountability for functions and activities transferred to third parties through outsourcing.

## How members connect

- Hierarchical linkage: the corporate governance article sets board and senior management accountability, which the risk governance/internal controls article operationalises as a framework requirement.
- The outsourcing article references the risk framework — outsourced activity is treated as in-scope risk, so the entity retains regulatory responsibility and control duties over the service provider.
- Together these articles supply the control environment that evidences compliance with the ongoing licence conditions and prudential soundness obligations in adjacent clusters.
- Cross-regime relevance: the same governance and outsourcing chain typically governs reliance on third parties for customer-facing and compliance functions, so delegation does not transfer liability.
