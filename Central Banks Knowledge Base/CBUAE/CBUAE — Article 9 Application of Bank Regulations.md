# Article 9: Application of Bank Regulations

Provision on how the Restricted Licence Banks Regulation interacts with the wider CBUAE rulebook: the Regulation sets only the requirements specific to Restricted Licence Banks, while all other Central Bank regulations, standards, guidelines and instructions that apply to "All Banks" remain fully applicable and enforceable to them. It also flags that restricted banks conducting activities supervised by another regulator must obtain the relevant licences from that regulator. Binds all banks, including foreign bank branches, licensed to operate as Restricted Licence Banks in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
