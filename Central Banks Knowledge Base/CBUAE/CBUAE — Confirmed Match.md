# Confirmed Match

Deals with the treatment of a screening hit confirmed as a true match against the UN Consolidated List or Local Terrorist List, as distinct from a false positive that requires no freezing action. It is read together with the ownership/control criteria for determining when a person, entity or arrangement is controlled by a Listed Person and therefore in scope of freezing and reporting obligations. Applies to CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
