# Large Value Payment Systems

## Why this community

Oversight of systemically important payment infrastructure: designation of large-value payment systems, the duties of system operators and settlement institutions, and alignment with international CPMI-IOSCO standards.

## How members connect

- Definitional core: the Large Value Payment Systems Regulation defines the LVPS and distinguishes it from a retail payment system, with obligations attaching to the defined System Operator and Settlement Institution roles.
- Designation as the trigger: being designated systemically important is the event that pulls a system into the heightened oversight regime.
- International standards incorporated: the PFMI, issued by CPMI and IOSCO, function as the substantive benchmark against which operators are assessed.
- Adjacent supervisory expectation: the CPMI wholesale payments fraud strategy extends operator responsibilities to endpoint security and fraud resilience within the LVPS.
