# ICAAP & Supervisory Review

## Why this community

The bank's internal capital adequacy assessment and its supervisory counterpart, covering how institutions must self-assess all material risks, plan capital, stress test, and how SAMA evaluates the result.

## How members connect

- ICAAP Guidance is the hub document; the individual risk headings (concentration, liquidity and funding cost, business model/strategic, climate-related financial risk) are mandated content chapters within it.
- The ICAAP output feeds SREP — the supervisory review determines whether additional capital is imposed, so ICAAP quality carries direct prudential consequence.
- Governance and the use test are cross-cutting conditions: the assessment must be board-owned and genuinely used in decision-making, not a compliance artefact.
- Stress testing and reverse stress testing are methodological obligations supporting capital planning; HHI is a prescribed measurement technique for concentration; ILAAP is the liquidity-side parallel process.
