# Article 37: Enforcement and Sanctions

Article 37 is the enforcement clause of the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation: breach of any provision of the Regulation, or commission of violations under the Central Bank Law, exposes a Licensee or Registree to administrative and financial sanctions and penalties at the Central Bank's discretion. It is the hook that makes the substantive obligations in the Regulation enforceable against licensed and registered payment token firms.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
