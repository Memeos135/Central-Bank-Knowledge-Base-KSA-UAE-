# Hawala and Alternative Remittance

A section of CBUAE proliferation-financing guidance dealing with hawala and other non-traditional remittance channels, distinguishing Registered Hawala Providers (registered under the Registered Hawala Providers Regulation, Circular No. 24/2019) from unlicensed/unregistered providers, which may not operate in the UAE. It treats RHPs — like MVTS and exchange houses — as inherently high risk for PF and expects enhanced controls, and directs Licensed Financial Institutions to have policies and procedures addressing exposure to unlicensed providers. It binds LFIs servicing or exposed to hawala activity, and indirectly frames registration duties for hawala operators.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments, governance/risk
