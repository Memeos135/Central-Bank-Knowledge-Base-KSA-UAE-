# Corporate Governance Regulation

Node labelled as corporate governance but the underlying material is a CBUAE circular issuing a Remuneration Regulation for banks and insurance companies (collectively "Financial Institutions"), setting minimum requirements for remuneration frameworks aligned to performance and risk outcomes. It sits alongside and expressly supplements the existing corporate governance, risk management, fitness and propriety, consumer protection and Shari'ah governance instruments, and prevails over them in case of conflict. It binds all licensed banks and insurance companies and is the standard CBUAE applies when assessing their remuneration policies, procedures and practices.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
