# Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee (ISSC)

Defined-term node for the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee: an independent committee that each Licensed Financial Institution conducting all or part of its business in line with Islamic Shari'ah must establish, staffed by specialists in Islamic finance jurisprudence. It supervises and approves the institution's businesses, products, services, contracts and documentation and sets their Shari'ah requirements, operating within the rules, principles and standards of the Higher Shari'ah Authority; its fatwas and resolutions bind the institution and must not contradict HSA resolutions. It sits alongside related definitions (HSA, Islamic Financial Institution, Licensed Financial Activities/Institutions) used across multiple CBUAE instruments.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
