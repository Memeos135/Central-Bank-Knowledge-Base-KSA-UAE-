# Takaful Participants' Risk Fund

## Why this community

Takaful fund mechanics: the participants' risk fund as a segregated pool and the interest-free loan mechanism used to cover its deficits.

## How members connect

- Qard Hasan is defined by reference to the Participants' Risk Fund — it is the remedy triggered when that fund is in deficit, creating a conditional funding obligation on the operator/shareholders' account.
- Both terms are definitional anchors that other takaful instruments (fund segregation, solvency, operator fee rules) rely on rather than standalone obligations.
- Linkage is hierarchical in practice: fund segregation rules create the deficit risk; the Qard Hasan obligation is the prescribed consequence, including repayment/recovery conditions from future surpluses.
