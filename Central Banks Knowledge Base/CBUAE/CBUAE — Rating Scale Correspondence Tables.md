# Rating Scale Correspondence Tables

Concept node on how external ratings map to risk weights and how banks must apply them: mappings are expressed on a scale closest to one major agency's notation for consistency with the Basel framework without endorsing any agency, and banks may choose among eligible ECAIs. It sets the operational rules for consistent use, no cherry-picking, exclusion of unsolicited ratings, treatment where two or three or more ratings diverge, and the bar on using a group entity's rating for affiliates. Binds banks applying the standardised credit risk and securitisation capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
