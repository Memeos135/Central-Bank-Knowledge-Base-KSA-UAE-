# Qard Hasan (Article 21)

Governs Qard Hasan: where Takaful Insurance Fund assets are insufficient to meet its liabilities, the Company must extend an interest-free loan to the Fund, capped at total shareholders' equity; the commitment is regulatory rather than contractual and must be disregarded by the ISSC when setting the Wakala fee. Recovery is only from future surplus, by general assembly decision with ISSC approval. Non-provision triggers notification to CBUAE, a fifteen-day cure period and possible suspension of business, so it is a solvency-support obligation on takaful companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
