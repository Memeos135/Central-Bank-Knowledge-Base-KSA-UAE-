# Crowdfunding Platforms

## Why this community

Crowdfunding intermediation: the licensed platform's contractual and disclosure duties toward the two counterparty classes it brings together, lenders and borrowers.

## How members connect

- The Crowdfunding Platform is the regulated actor at the centre; all other members are either its counterparties or its obligations.
- The Client Agreement is the instrument through which the platform's duties bite, defining rights and terms for both Lender and Borrower as defined client categories.
- Required Crowdfunding Company Disclosures attach to the platform as an ongoing transparency obligation running alongside the contractual layer.
- Definitional linkage matters: whether a participant is a Lender or Borrower determines which disclosure and agreement terms apply.
