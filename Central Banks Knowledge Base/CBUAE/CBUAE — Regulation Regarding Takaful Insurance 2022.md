# Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance 2022

Concept node for the CBUAE Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance issued in 2022, the predecessor instrument expressly cancelled and superseded by the current Takaful Insurance Regulation under Article 28. It is relevant mainly for historical/transitional analysis — e.g. assessing obligations arising before the new Regulation's application date, or comparing prior treatment of Takaful Insurance Windows, Shari'ah governance, the ISSC and the Higher Shari'ah Authority with the current requirements. It no longer binds Takaful Insurance Companies prospectively.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
