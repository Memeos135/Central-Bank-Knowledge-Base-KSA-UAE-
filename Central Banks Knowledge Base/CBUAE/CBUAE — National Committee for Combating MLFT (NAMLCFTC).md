# National Committee for Combating ML/FT (NAMLCFTC)

A concept node describing the UAE's national institutional AML/CFT coordination framework, including the National Committee for Combating Money Laundering and the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organisations, situated in the overview chapter of the CBUAE Guidelines for Financial Institutions. It explains how federal criminal and AML/CFT legislation applies across the State including the Financial and Commercial Free Zones, and how Competent and Supervisory Authorities divide implementation and enforcement. It is contextual rather than directly obligation-creating, but frames which authority binds a given institution.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
