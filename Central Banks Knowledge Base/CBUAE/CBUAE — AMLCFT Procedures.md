# AML/CFT Procedures

Concept node on the AML/CFT obligations applicable to SVF Licensees under the CBUAE SVF Regulation. Requires customer due diligence models calibrated to the customer profile and SVF product features, compliance management arrangements including Senior Management oversight and appointment of a Compliance Officer and Money Laundering Reporting Officer, comprehensive AML/CFT policies aligned with UAE AML/CFT law, an independent audit function, and risk assessment before launching new products, services or technology. Binds licensed SVF issuers/operators.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, payments
