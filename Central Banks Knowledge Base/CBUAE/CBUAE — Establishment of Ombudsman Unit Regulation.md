# Establishment of Ombudsman Unit Regulation

The CBUAE regulation establishing the Ombudsman Unit (Sanadak-type banking and insurance dispute resolution body), setting its governance structure, delegated powers from the CBUAE Board, funding mechanics (Annual Levy, Case Fee, Supplementary Levy) and oversight of performance. It creates institutional machinery for consumer complaint handling rather than direct conduct rules. It binds the Ombudsman Unit's board and staff, and indirectly Licensed Financial Institutions and Insurance Companies subject to its jurisdiction and levies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
