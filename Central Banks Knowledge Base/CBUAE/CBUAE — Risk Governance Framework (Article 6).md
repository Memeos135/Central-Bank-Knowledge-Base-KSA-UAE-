# Risk Governance Framework (Article 6)

Article 6 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation requires a proportionate risk governance framework identifying all material risks, with policies, processes, systems and controls to identify, measure, monitor and mitigate them, plus mitigating action plans. It expressly lists operational, conduct, employee fraud, cybercrime, money laundering, default management, mis-selling and terrorist financing risks; the board is ultimately accountable, the CRO reports directly to the board, and a detailed exit/wind-down plan protecting lender funds and loan administration is required. Binds licensed crowdfunding companies and their boards.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, BNPL/finance
