# Section 2.1.5 Remittances, Transfers and Foreign Exchange Provisions

Requirements for remittance, transfer and foreign exchange products and services, applying alongside the general provisions of Section 2.1.1 and the corresponding advertising rules in Section 2.3.5. It mandates clear disclosure of all applicable fees and buy/sell exchange rates, best-efforts disclosure (or an estimate plus disclaimer) of correspondent bank or disbursing provider charges, prominent in-branch display of "we buy"/"we sell" rates and remittance buy rates against AED, and prescribed warning statements in advertising about correspondent charges, FX rate exposure and penalties arising from customer error or incomplete information. Read with the business conduct rule that outward transfer fees must be a fixed amount, not a percentage.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
