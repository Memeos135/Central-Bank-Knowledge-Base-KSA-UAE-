# DLT Design Principles

Guidance on how DLT applications should be designed and secured, including optimisation to the intended use, and design principles for applications managing financial assets such as flexibility and traceability (with anonymity to be avoided). It extends to network integrity controls (node throughput limits, blocking abusive nodes/IPs, permissioned designs), selection of consensus methodology against performance, scalability, governance and resilience criteria, avoidance of clear-text personal data on-chain, and cryptographic key controls. Applies to financial institutions handling customer assets, personal data or restricted-participant arrangements on DLT.
