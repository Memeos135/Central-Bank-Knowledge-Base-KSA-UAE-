# Insurance Ombudsman & Hawala

## Why this community

Two regimes clustered together: governance and process of an insurance ombudsman body, and the non-KSA (UAE/CBUAE) AML-CTF and targeted financial sanctions framework applicable to hawala providers and licensed financial institutions.

## How members connect

- Governance chain: the Board of the Ombudsman Unit and Chief Ombudsman own the Complaint Resolution Process, which binds the insurance company and confers rights on the consumer/complainant.
- AML hierarchy: the AML-CTF Decree-Law sits above the implementing Cabinet Decision, with Cabinet Decision (74) of 2020 carrying TFS duties and Circular 24/2019 licensing/regulating hawala providers; the CBUAE Guidance interprets all of them.
- Obligation chain: transaction monitoring generates STR/SAR duties discharged through the goAML platform; freezing and reporting under TFS run to the same channel.
- Cross-regime link: LFIs and registered hawala providers are conceptually related because LFIs bank RHPs and must apply CDD/KYB to them — note the jurisdictional caveat that these are UAE instruments, not SAMA rules.
