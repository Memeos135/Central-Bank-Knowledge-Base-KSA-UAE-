# Cabinet Resolution 134 of 2025 Executive Regulations

Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025, the Executive Regulations implementing Federal Decree by Law No. 10 of 2025 on AML, CFT and proliferation financing; it replaces the 2019 executive regulations regime. It sets out definitions, the scope of Financial Institutions, DNFBPs, VASPs and NPOs, customer due diligence and suspicious transaction reporting duties, beneficial ownership and registrar obligations for companies, nominee arrangements, and supervisory authority powers. It binds regulated entities, companies and registrars, and supervisory authorities operating under the Decree by Law.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
