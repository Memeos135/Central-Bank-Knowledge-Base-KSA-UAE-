# Commercial Companies Law (Federal Decree Law 32/2021)

Refers to Federal Decree Law No. 32 of 2021 on Commercial Companies (as amended), one of the enabling statutes recited in the Finance Companies Regulation (Circular 3/2023) and a defined term in its glossary. It is the corporate-law baseline for finance companies, used in the Regulation for matters such as the number of founding shareholders.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, other
