# Article 7: Special Considerations for Holding Companies

Article 7 imposes notification and record-keeping duties on an identified Holding Company: immediate notice to the Central Bank of a 10% or greater increase or decrease in capital ownership or voting rights, changes or vacancies in board composition, planned mergers or acquisitions, and actual or likely insolvency, inability to meet obligations, or suspension of payments. Its board must keep minutes of meetings discussing insurance business and provide copies on request. Binds Holding Companies within a CBUAE-supervised insurance Group.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
