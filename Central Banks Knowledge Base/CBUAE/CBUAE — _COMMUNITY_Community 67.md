# Counterparty Exposure (SA-CCR)

## Why this community

The standardised approach for measuring counterparty credit exposure (SA-CCR): how a bank determines exposure at default for derivative transactions at netting-set level.

## How members connect

- EAD is the output obligation; it is defined as a function of Replacement Cost and Potential Future Exposure, so each input definition is legally load-bearing for the capital number.
- The Netting Set is the unit of account — recognition of netting determines whether exposures may be offset, and the PFE Multiplier and RC are both computed per netting set.
- Supervisory Duration and the PFE Multiplier are supervisor-prescribed parameters, leaving limited firm discretion; deviation requires SAMA acceptance rather than internal judgement.
- Duplicate-looking terms (Netting Set/Netting Sets, PFE/Potential Future Exposure) reflect parallel drafting across the standard and should be read as the same defined concept.
