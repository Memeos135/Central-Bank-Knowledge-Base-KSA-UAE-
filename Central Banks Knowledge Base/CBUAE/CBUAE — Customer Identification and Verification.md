# Customer Identification and Verification

Node on customer identification, verification and risk profiling under the CBUAE CDD/KYC guidance, reflecting Article 8 of the AML-CFT Decision's requirement to understand the nature and purpose of the customer relationship. It lists the baseline information expected at onboarding (purpose of account, source of funds and wealth, occupation or industry, expected channels, products, geographies and transaction volumes), scales additional information collection to the customer's assessed risk, and explains customer segmentation and initial risk rating as the basis for later monitoring thresholds, review cycles and SDD/EDD decisions. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** For a decision on remote onboarding, read digital identification as a method of discharging the existing customer identification and verification obligation, not as a separate or lighter regime.
