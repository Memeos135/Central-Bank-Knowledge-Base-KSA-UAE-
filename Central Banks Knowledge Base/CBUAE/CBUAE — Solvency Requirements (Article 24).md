# Solvency Requirements (Article 24)

Article 24 requires Takaful Insurance Companies to meet the solvency requirements of the applicable Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies, with regulatory solvency assessed at company level on a consolidated basis covering both the Shareholders' Account and the Takaful Insurance Fund. Consolidation does not dilute the separate financial position of the Fund (distinct assets and liabilities), which must be disclosed in the financial statements. Where the Fund is in recurring deficit or dependent on Qard Hasan, the Central Bank may require the Fund's solvency to be assessed on a standalone basis to protect Participants and Beneficiaries.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
