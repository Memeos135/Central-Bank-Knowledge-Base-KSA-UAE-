# Hawala and Remittance

## Why this community

Money or value transfer services outside conventional banking channels (hawala and similar alternative remittance arrangements) and their treatment under Saudi AML/CTF licensing and supervision requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster functioning as a definitional hook for MVTS/money remittance activity, which triggers licensing, registration and full CDD/recordkeeping duties.
- Obligation chain runs from the AML Law and its Implementing Regulation into the customer identification, wire-transfer information and STR-filing requirements.
- Cross-references the trade-based money laundering cluster, where registered and unregistered hawala providers appear as settlement mechanisms.
- Also intersects the payments/PSP regime where value transfer is conducted by non-bank operators.
