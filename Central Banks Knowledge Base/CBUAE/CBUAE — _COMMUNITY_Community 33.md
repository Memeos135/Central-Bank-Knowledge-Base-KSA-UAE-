# Loan-Based Crowdfunding Regulation

## Why this community

Authorisation and ongoing supervision of loan-based crowdfunding platforms: licensing gate, prudential floor, governance/risk/internal-control requirements, conduct of business toward lenders and borrowers, client money segregation, and reporting/record-keeping.

## How members connect

- Single parent regulation branches into article-level obligations; each article is a distinct, independently enforceable duty on the licensed platform.
- Obligation chain: licensing (Art. 3) is the precondition; prudential (Art. 4), governance (Art. 5–6) and internal controls (Art. 7) are continuing licence conditions; Art. 9 is the supervisory evidence layer.
- Conduct of business (Art. 8) carries the customer-facing duties and pulls in credit-bureau reporting/enquiry and client-money protection — money held for participants is not platform property.
- Cross-reference to Central Bank Regulation No. 29/2011 links crowdfunding lending to the pre-existing retail bank-lending conduct rules.
