# Article 10: Islamic Banking (Regulation)

Node for the Islamic banking article of the CBUAE Risk Management Regulation 153/2018, sitting alongside the Regulation's defined terms (Bank, Board, Group, Parent, Subsidiary, Islamic Financial Services) and the Article 9 public disclosure duty on the risk governance framework and risk exposures. It addresses how the risk governance requirements apply to Shari'a-compliant activity, including conventional banks operating Islamic Windows. It binds banks licensed by the CBUAE; note this is a UAE instrument, not a SAMA rule, and the captured context is mainly definitions plus the adjacent disclosure article rather than the full Islamic banking text.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
