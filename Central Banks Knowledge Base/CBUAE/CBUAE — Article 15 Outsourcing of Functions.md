# Article 15: Outsourcing of Functions

Article 15 of the CBUAE Exchange Business regulation sets a default prohibition on outsourcing by a Licensed Person, permitting it only where the Central Bank has issued a letter of no objection under the conditions and exceptions in the accompanying Standards. It further imposes an absolute bar on outsourcing the licensed exchange activities themselves (such as foreign currency dealing and money transfer acceptance or disbursement). It binds Licensed Persons carrying on Exchange Business, with the surrounding governance articles placing accountability on the board and senior management.
