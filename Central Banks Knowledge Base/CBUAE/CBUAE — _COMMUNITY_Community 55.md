# Enterprise Risk & Solvency

## Why this community

Prudential expectation that an insurer operate an integrated enterprise risk management framework linked to its solvency position, including forward-looking self-assessment and recovery planning.

## How members connect

- The ERM for solvency purposes standards is the parent instrument; enterprise risk management is the defined framework it imposes and from which the other obligations hang.
- ERM branches into substantive components — risk appetite and risk limits, asset-liability management, and capital adequacy — each a required element rather than a separate regime.
- The own risk and solvency assessment is the forward-looking output of the ERM framework and feeds recovery planning, creating a data and escalation link between routine assessment and stress response.
- A residual cross-reference to other relevant regulations means the standards are not self-contained and must be read with the wider prudential rulebook.
