# Alternative Standardised Approach

Node for the Alternative Standardised Approach (ASA) to operational risk capital under the CBUAE capital standards, an optional variant of the Standardised Approach subject to Central Bank permission. Eligibility is restricted: large diversified banks may not use it, and an applicant must show that retail or commercial banking accounts for at least 90% of income and that gross income is not a reliable exposure indicator. Sits within the operational risk standard defining operational risk (including legal and compliance risk, excluding strategic and reputational risk) and gross income, applicable to UAE banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
