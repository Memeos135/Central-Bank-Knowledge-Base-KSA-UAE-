# Article 5: Business Conduct

Node for Article 5 (business conduct) of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards; the extracted text is drawn largely from adjacent Article 3/4 provisions on compliance-culture monitoring, conduct risk mitigation and product oversight. It covers approved conduct risk frameworks with documented risk appetite and limits, mitigation strategies, independent assessment of retail business and control functions, annual reporting of conduct risk to the board, and a board-approved product approval framework. Binds licensed financial institutions' boards, senior management, control functions and retail business lines.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
