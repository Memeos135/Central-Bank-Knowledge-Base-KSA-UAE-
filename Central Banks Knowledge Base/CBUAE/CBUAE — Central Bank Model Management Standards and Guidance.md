# Central Bank Model Management Standards and Guidance

Concept node for the Central Bank Model Management Standards and Guidance as cross-referenced in the Pillar 2 framework, notably as the prescribed methodology for calculating and reporting interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB). It also anchors model risk treatment in ICAAP: banks must categorise models by risk, estimate potential model-driven losses through validation, benchmarking, sensitivity analysis and stress tests, and assess the quality of their model risk management. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks using models for risk, business or accounting purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
