# Customer Screening

Sets the expectation that screening occurs across the whole customer lifecycle rather than at onboarding only, distinguishing periodic name screening (triggered by changes to customer data or to the UN/Local lists), ad hoc screening (business need, competent authority request, or downstream institution feedback) and re-screening (e.g. high-risk jurisdiction identified in updated customer data). Adjacent guidance makes LFIs responsible for due diligence on external list vendors and for registering with the Executive Office for listing/de-listing notifications. Binds LFIs.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
