# Freezing Measures

Concept node for the substance of targeted financial sanctions: freezing of funds owned or controlled directly or indirectly by a Listed Person or persons acting on their behalf, funds derived from them, the prohibition on making funds or services available, and the treatment of accrued interest and contractual payments on pre-existing obligations. It also covers lifting freezing — authorised exemptions notified to the UN Security Council, and the false-positive mechanism (written request to the Office, thirty-day decision, immediate execution by the holding entity, and recourse to the Competent Court). It binds any person holding or controlling funds, financial institutions and DNFBPs, the Office, law enforcement and supervisory authorities, with bona fide third-party rights preserved.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
