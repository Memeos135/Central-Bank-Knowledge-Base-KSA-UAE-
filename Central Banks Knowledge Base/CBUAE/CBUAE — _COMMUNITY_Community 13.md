# Insurance Financial Regulations

## Why this community

The core prudential and financial-reporting rulebook for insurers and takaful operators: technical provisions, solvency and own funds, admissible assets and investment limits, actuarial and audit functions, and the governance wrappers (outsourcing, fraud, internal audit) around them. It also carries the actuary licensing regime and an insurance FinTech sandbox pathway.

## How members connect

- A single statutory root (the insurance law) supports the conventional and takaful financial regulations, which in turn define the calculation chain: technical provisions (UPR, URR, OSLR, IBNR, ALAE/ULAE, mathematical reserve) feed solvency margin, solvency capital requirement, own funds and the minimum guarantee fund.
- Asset-side rules link together: policyholders' rights investment basis, admissible assets, allocation limits and the investment committee are cumulative constraints on the same balance sheet, with AAOIFI standards substituting for takaful entities.
- Assurance and gatekeeping obligations interlock: the actuary (and its separate licensing regulation and professional indemnity condition), the external auditor with its duty to report to the Central Bank, and internal audit all validate the same prudential figures reported under IFRS on the prescribed financial statement forms.
- The chapter-level continuing obligations — licensing, accounts and audit, outsourcing, fraud management — and the sandbox decision operate as the governance and entry perimeter within which the financial regulations bite.
- Shared defined terms in the glossary are what make the conventional and takaful regimes comparable; differences are mainly in fund segregation and Shariah-compliant asset treatment, not in the solvency architecture.
