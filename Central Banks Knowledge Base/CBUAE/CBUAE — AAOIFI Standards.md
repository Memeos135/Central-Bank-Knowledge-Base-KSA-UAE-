# AAOIFI Standards

A concept node on the accounting and disclosure framework referenced in the CBUAE Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies, where AAOIFI standards operate alongside IFRS/IASB pronouncements and Authority-prescribed reporting templates. It frames the required content of financial statement notes (Takaful contract liabilities, Re-Takaful assets, accounting policies, related party and segment disclosures) and connects to actuarial reserving requirements for mathematical reserves. It binds Takaful insurance companies, their appointed actuaries and auditors for financial reporting purposes.
