# Federal Decree Law No.20 of 2018 (repealed)

Node covering the repeal and transition position of UAE Federal Decree by Law No. (20) of 2018 on AML/CFT and financing of illegal organisations, which is repealed by Federal Decree by Law No. (10) of 2025 (in force two weeks after publication; issued 30 September 2025). Executive Regulations, resolutions and circulars issued under the 2018 law remain effective to the extent they do not conflict with the new Decree Law until superseded, and conflicting provisions generally are repealed. Essential for dating obligations, citing the correct instrument, and assessing whether legacy secondary instruments still apply.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
