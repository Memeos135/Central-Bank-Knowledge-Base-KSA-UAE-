# Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular 8/2020)

Cross-reference node for the CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular 8/2020) as it interacts with the Regulatory Sandbox: sandbox Consumers may fall outside that Regulation's protections, so the Applicant must obtain express, informed written consent after clear, fair and not misleading disclosure of financial and other risks and a summary of the regulatory protections being foregone. Also anchors the sandbox safeguards on Consumer protection and financial-system protection, including complaints, compensation and dispute resolution, segregation of Consumer funds/assets in escrow with a Central Bank-licensed bank, data handling and protection, restrictions on which Consumers may be served, fitness and propriety of connected individuals, and AML/CTF measures. Binds Applicants and Participants.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, AML/CTF, other
