# Country Risk

Concept node for "country risk" as used in the CBUAE country and transfer risk requirements for banks, covering exposures arising from international funding, lending and investment activity. It anchors obligations to identify, measure, monitor and report such risk through the risk governance framework, set board-approved limits in the risk appetite statement, define materiality by reference to cross-border exposure size and nature, monitor developments and apply countermeasures, and ensure prudent provisioning. Binds banks supervised by the Central Bank, on a bank-wide and, where relevant, group-wide basis including intra-group exposures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
