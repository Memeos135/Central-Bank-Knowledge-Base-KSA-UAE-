# Ongoing Requirements

## Why this community

Continuing licence conditions — the obligations a licensed entity must satisfy on an ongoing basis after authorisation, as distinct from entry/licensing criteria.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster acting as the hinge between initial authorisation and the substantive conduct, solvency and governance duties elsewhere in the same regime.
- Functions as a chapeau obligation: breach of downstream requirements (financial soundness, governance, conduct) typically crystallises as a failure of on-going requirements and a supervisory/licensing consequence.
