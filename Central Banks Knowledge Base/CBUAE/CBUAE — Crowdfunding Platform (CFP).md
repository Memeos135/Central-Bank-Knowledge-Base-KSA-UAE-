# Crowdfunding Platform (CFP)

Definitions article of the CBUAE loan-based crowdfunding regulation, anchoring the term Crowdfunding Platform (CFP) as a web-based platform, social networking site or similar means used for crowdfunding, together with linked defined terms: Borrower (UAE-registered company or sole proprietor), Client (borrower or lender), Client Money, Pricing Platform, commitment period, cooling-off period of at least two full business days, funding goal, Loan, and Lender split into Retail Lender and Market Counterparty (net assets over AED 2,000,000 excluding primary residence, self-attested and verified by the CFP). It also sets the territorial trigger for being treated as conducting these activities in the UAE (incorporation, hosting, UAE correspondence address, or serving UAE-resident clients). Determines who falls within the licensing perimeter and how client categories drive downstream conduct duties.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, payments
