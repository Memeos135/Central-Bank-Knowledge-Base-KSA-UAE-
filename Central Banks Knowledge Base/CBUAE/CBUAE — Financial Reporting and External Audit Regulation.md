# Financial Reporting and External Audit Regulation

Orientation node for the CBUAE Financial Reporting and External Audit Regulation and its accompanying Standards (162/2018), covering how banks prepare and report financial information and how the external audit relationship is governed. It sets requirements on audit firm procurement and rotation, partner rotation and cooling-off, annual board audit committee assessment of auditor quality, communication of significant audit matters, and a board-approved policy on permitted and prohibited non-audit services. It binds banks (their boards, board audit committees and senior management) and, through engagement terms, their external auditors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
