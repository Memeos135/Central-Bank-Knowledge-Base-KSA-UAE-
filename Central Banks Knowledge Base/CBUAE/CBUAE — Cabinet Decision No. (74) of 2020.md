# Cabinet Decision No. (74) of 2020

Concept node for Cabinet Decision No. (74) of 2020, the UAE instrument governing targeted financial sanctions and the obligation to freeze without delay assets of UN- and UAE-listed persons. In this guidance it underpins the sanctions screening and immediate freezing duties imposed on Registered Hawala Providers, who may not process activity for listed persons absent authorisation. It binds RHP and, more broadly, UAE financial institutions and designated entities.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
