# Provisional Measures and Freezing

Sets out the FIU's provisional powers under the AML/CTF/CPF Executive Regulations: the Chief of the Unit may, without prior notice, suspend or halt a suspected transaction for up to ten working days and freeze suspected funds held by Financial Institutions, DNFBPs or VASPs for thirty days, based on analysis of STRs and other domestic or foreign information. Institutions must implement freezing orders without notice; the Attorney General is notified and may amend, revoke, direct, or extend the freeze. Relevant to reporting entities' obligations on receipt of such orders and to tipping-off constraints.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
