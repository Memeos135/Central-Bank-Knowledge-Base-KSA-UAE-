# Depreciation Percentage Schedules

Node containing the annexed schedules to the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Third Party Liability, setting depreciation percentages applied to replaced parts for private vehicles and for taxis, public transport and rental vehicles, the short-rate table of recoverable premium on early cancellation, and the list of safety-critical parts replaced new with no depreciation deduction. These schedules form an integral part of the policy contract and bind insurers when settling repair and refund claims.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
