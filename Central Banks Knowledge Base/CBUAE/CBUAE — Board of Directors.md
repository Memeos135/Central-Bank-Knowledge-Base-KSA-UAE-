# Board of Directors

Node on the Board's role under Risk Management Regulation 153/2018: the Board approves the risk appetite statement and risk limits, must have sufficient expertise to understand and oversee risk measurement systems including models, and remains ultimately accountable even where model approval or other responsibilities are delegated to management. It also carries escalation duties — exceptions to risk policies, procedures or limits must be addressed immediately by the appropriate management level or the Board, and the bank must notify the Central Bank immediately of significant deviations from the approved risk appetite or policies, or of a material risk not adequately addressed. Binds banks' boards of directors as defined in the Regulation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
