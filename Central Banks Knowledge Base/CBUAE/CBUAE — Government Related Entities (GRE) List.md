# Government Related Entities (GRE) List

Node on the CBUAE Government Related Entities list and the definition of GREs as commercial undertakings wholly owned or majority-owned by the federal or emirate governments. Such exposures must be classified as GRE and risk-weighted as corporate claims according to the entity's credit rating; banks must apply the latest published GRE list and notify the CBUAE of information supporting additions or removals. Internal audit/compliance functions of CBUAE-licensed banks must periodically verify PSE and GRE classification against the list.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
