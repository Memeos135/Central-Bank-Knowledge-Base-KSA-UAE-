# Section 2.1.1 General Provisions (Financial Products)

General product-level conduct and disclosure provisions applying across all Financial Products and/or Services under the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards, and the baseline section that the product-specific sections (deposits, credit, structured products, remittances) each incorporate by reference. It covers unsolicited marketing and promotional contact consent, the rule that silence or inaction is never affirmative consent to a new or optional product, fee or option, tying/bundling limits including the prohibition on Undue Pressure, and accountability and fit-and-proper due diligence for Authorized Agents; it also restricts Exchange Houses from soliciting credit products or assisting account opening. It binds Licensed Financial Institutions dealing with Consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
