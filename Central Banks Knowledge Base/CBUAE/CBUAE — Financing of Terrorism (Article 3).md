# Financing of Terrorism (Article 3)

This node is the terrorism financing offence and related definitions in the 2025 UAE AML law: intentionally providing, collecting or making available funds by any means, directly or indirectly — including via digital systems, virtual assets or cryptographic technologies — knowing they will be used for terrorist acts, by a terrorist or terrorist organisation, or to finance travel of individuals for terrorist purposes. Supporting definitions cover targeted financial sanctions, suspicious transactions, freezing and seizure. It binds any person and is the predicate for TFS freezing obligations on reporting entities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
