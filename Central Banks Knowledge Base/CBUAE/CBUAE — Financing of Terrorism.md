# Financing of Terrorism

Concept node for financing of terrorism (FT) under the UAE AML-CFT Law as presented in the CBUAE Guidelines for Financial Institutions, including its status as a criminal offence not subject to the statute of limitations and its treatment alongside financing of illegal organisations. It also situates FT within the Guidelines' scope statement (minimum supervisory expectations for identifying, assessing and mitigating ML/FT risk) and cross-refers the separate UN Targeted Financial Sanctions regime under Cabinet Decision No. 74 of 2020. Binds UAE supervised financial institutions; sanctions/TFS obligations sit in a distinct instrument.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
