# Leverage Ratio Standard

Node covering the leverage ratio component of the CBUAE capital standards: a non-risk-based Tier 1 capital measure (CET1 and/or AT1) over a total exposure measure, calculated at least quarter-end and required to meet or exceed the minimum ratio set in UAE regulations at all times. It sets the scope of regulatory consolidation (aligned to the risk-based framework), treatment of capital investments in entities outside consolidation, gross-accounting exposure measurement with limited recognition of collateral or credit risk mitigation, credit conversion factors for off-balance-sheet commitments, and independent review/audit of the calculation. Binds banks licensed and supervised by the CBUAE (UAE regime, not SAMA).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
