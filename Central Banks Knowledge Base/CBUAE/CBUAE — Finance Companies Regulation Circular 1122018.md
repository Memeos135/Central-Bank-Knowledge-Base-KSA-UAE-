# Finance Companies Regulation Circular 112/2018

Node for the CBUAE Finance Companies Regulation (labelled here as the earlier Circular 112/2018, with the supplied text corresponding to the replacement Circular 3/2023 addressed to all Finance Companies and Restricted Licence Finance Companies). It sets the licensing and prudential-conduct framework for finance and restricted-licence finance activity, with stated objectives of customer protection, protection of the licensees themselves, and financial-sector stability. It binds finance companies, restricted-licence finance companies and all their branches, is issued under the UAE Central Bank Law, and expressly allows the Central Bank to impose requirements beyond the minimum lists in individual articles; note it also cross-references the AML/CTF decree law and its implementing regulation, the Credit Information Law and the Commercial Companies Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF, data/credit info
