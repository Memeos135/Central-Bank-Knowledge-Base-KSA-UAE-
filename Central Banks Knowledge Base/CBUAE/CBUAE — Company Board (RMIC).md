# Company Board (RMIC)

A concept node on the board's role under the Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation: the board approves or delegates approval of models, oversees the forward-looking stress testing programme and internal capital and liquidity assessment, and is ultimately responsible for the compliance function and for a corporate culture of honesty, integrity and regulatory compliance. It also frames the board/senior management split in the risk governance system. It binds the boards of CBUAE-supervised insurance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
