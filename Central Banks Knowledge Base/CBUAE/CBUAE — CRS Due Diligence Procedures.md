# CRS Due Diligence Procedures

Concept node covering the general and special CRS due diligence requirements (Sections II–VII): the duty to establish, maintain and document procedures to identify Reportable Accounts, timing and valuation rules, permitted elections to apply stricter procedures, and the special rules on reliance on self-certifications and documentary evidence. It confirms that use of a service provider does not transfer the obligation, which remains with the Reporting Financial Institution.

**Regimes:** other, AML/CTF, governance/risk
