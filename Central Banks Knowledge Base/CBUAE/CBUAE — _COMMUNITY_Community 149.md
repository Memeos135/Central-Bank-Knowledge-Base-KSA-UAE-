# Fraud Protection & ATM/POS

## Why this community

Fraud risk at the consumer-facing channel level: safeguarding customer funds and card/account assets used through ATM and point-of-sale infrastructure, and the institution's duty to detect, investigate and remediate fraudulent transactions.

## How members connect

- The ATM/POS channel provisions set the operational service conditions that the anti-fraud asset-protection duty presupposes — the channel rules define where the exposure arises.
- The fraud detection and investigation obligation cross-references the consumer asset protection duty, creating an obligation chain: prevent, then detect, then investigate and make the consumer whole.
- All three sit in the consumer protection regime but interact with payments/PSP supervision, so liability allocation between issuer, acquirer and merchant should be read across both.
- Shared subject matter is the consumer's account/instrument, so the defined scope of 'consumer' (as opposed to AML 'customer') limits who can claim these protections.
