# UAE Data Protection and Privacy Framework

A concept node within CBUAE guidance on digital ID for AML/CFT purposes, flagging that digital identity systems collect and process personal data (including biometrics) and therefore sit under UAE data protection and privacy requirements alongside identity-proofing rules. It points to the relevant UAE federal instruments (cybercrime/rumours decree-law, electronic transactions and trust services decree-law), internet access management policy and Emirate-level rules, and to reliance on official identity infrastructure such as Emirates ID, the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship validation gateway, UAE Pass and Emirates facial recognition. Relevant to licensed financial institutions deciding whether and how to use digital ID for CDD, and to how they retain copies and verification records.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, AML/CTF, governance/risk
