# Article 66 - International Judicial Cooperation

Article 66 opens Part Three (International Judicial Cooperation) of Chapter Eight of the UAE AML/CFT Executive Regulations, requiring competent judicial authorities to provide prompt and effective mutual legal assistance to foreign judicial authorities under treaty or reciprocity in investigations, prosecutions and proceedings concerning the Crime. It authorises identification, tracing, valuation, seizure, freezing and confiscation of criminal funds or equivalent-value property (including where the accused is unknown or not criminally responsible), other investigative measures such as obtaining records from financial institutions, DNFBPs, VASPs and NPOs, searches, evidence gathering, covert techniques and controlled delivery, and surrender/recovery of persons and property. It binds judicial authorities but has direct consequences for reporting entities whose records may be compelled.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
