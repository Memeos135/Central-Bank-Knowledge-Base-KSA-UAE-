# Qualitative Requirements (Liquidity Risk Framework)

Article 2 of the CBUAE liquidity regulations setting the qualitative (non-ratio) limb of the Liquidity Risk Management Framework that every bank must maintain alongside the quantitative ratios. It allocates ultimate responsibility to the board (including articulating liquidity risk tolerance and having at least one board member with detailed liquidity risk understanding), requires senior management to implement strategies, policies and practices within the approved tolerance, continuous review with regular board reporting, embedding liquidity costs/benefits/risks in product pricing and approval for significant business activities, and sound identification, measurement, monitoring and control processes. Binds all banks, with supervisory review applied proportionately to size, scope of operations, interconnectedness and systemic impact; effective from 1 July 2015.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
