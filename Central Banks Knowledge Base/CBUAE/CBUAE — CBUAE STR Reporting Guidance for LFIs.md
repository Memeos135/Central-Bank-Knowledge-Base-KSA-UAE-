# CBUAE STR Reporting Guidance for LFIs

CBUAE guidance for Licensed Financial Institutions on reporting suspicious transactions and activity to the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit, covering why reports matter, report structure and narrative quality, submission via the FIU's reporting portal, report types, amendments, expedited filing, confidentiality and the tipping-off prohibition, responses to FIU information requests, and post-filing handling of the customer relationship. It binds LFIs supervised by the CBUAE and informs how compliance functions and senior management govern the filing process. Read with the AML-CFT Law and AML-CFT Decision, which are the enforceable source of the reporting duty.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
