# Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process

Node covering the internal adequacy assessment processes under Pillar 2 of the CBUAE capital framework, framed principally around the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP) and its liquidity counterpart. It requires each licensed bank to identify and quantify risks not fully captured by Pillar 1 (credit, market, operational), determine additional Pillar 2 capital, and evidence governance, risk appetite, MIS, stress testing and capital planning. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks and feeds the supervisory review and evaluation process, which may result in an adjusted minimum capital ratio.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
