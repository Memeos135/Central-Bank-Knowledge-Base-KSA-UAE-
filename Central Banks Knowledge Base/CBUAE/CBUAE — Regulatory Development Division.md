# Regulatory Development Division

Identifies the Central Bank's Regulatory Development Division as the authoritative interpreter of the Ombudsman Unit Regulation and related Central Bank Regulations, and provides that where the Ombudsman Unit refers an interpretive question to it, the Unit must not issue or proceed to a Determination inconsistent with, or while awaiting, that interpretation. Related provisions confirm that non-compliance with the Regulation by Licensed Financial Institutions or Insurance Companies is a regulatory violation exposing them to supervisory action, sanctions and penalties, and set publication and effective-date terms. It binds the Ombudsman Unit and the supervised institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
