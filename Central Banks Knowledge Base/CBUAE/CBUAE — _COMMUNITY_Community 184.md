# Takaful Insurance Regulation 2022

## Why this community

The 2022 takaful insurance regulation as the governing instrument, including its repeal of the prior regulation and the resulting transition in applicable rules.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the 2022 regulation is the parent instrument from which the operative articles (solvency, disclosure, fund transfer) derive their authority.
- The cancellation article expressly displaces the earlier regulation, so effective-date and grandfathering questions turn on this cross-reference.
- Defined terms used across the other clusters (participant, takaful fund, operator) are anchored in this instrument; scope limits should be read here first.
