# IA BOD Decision No. 49 of 2019 - Consolidated Version

Consolidated version (as of 20/03/2020) of Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 49 of 2019 issuing the Instructions for Life Insurance and Family Takaful Insurance, as amended by IA Board Resolution No. 15 of 2020; functions now sit with CBUAE. It governs product design, illustrations and disclosure, free look, commissions and claw-back, surrender values, wakala/mudaraba fees and actuarial certification for life and family takaful business, and contains the definitions chapter (Annualized Premium, Ad-hoc Premium, Actuarial Funding, Charges, Insurance Agent, Insurance Broker). Binds licensed life/takaful companies, agents, brokers and banks distributing these products; its preamble also invokes the federal AML/CTF decree and the terrorism-lists/UNSCR regime as part of its legal basis.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
