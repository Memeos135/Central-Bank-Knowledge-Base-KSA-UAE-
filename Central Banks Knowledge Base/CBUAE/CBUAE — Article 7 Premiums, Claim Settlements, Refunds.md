# Article 7: Premiums, Claim Settlements, Refunds

Sets money-flow rules: Premiums for primary insurance must be paid by Clients directly to Companies, brokers must instruct Clients in writing before inception and keep evidence of acknowledgment, and brokers are prohibited from collecting Premiums, Claim Settlements or Premium refunds in primary business. Reinsurance flows are left to the reinsurance brokerage agreement; brokers earn Remuneration from the Company on conclusion of an Insurance Policy, and brokers and insurers must maintain the means to operate these flows.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
