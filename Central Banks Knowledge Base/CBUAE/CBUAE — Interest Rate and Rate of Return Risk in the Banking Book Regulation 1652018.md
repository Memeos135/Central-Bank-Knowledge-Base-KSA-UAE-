# Interest Rate and Rate of Return Risk in the Banking Book Regulation 165/2018

CBUAE Regulation 165/2018 (with accompanying Standards) on Interest Rate and Rate of Return Risk in the Banking Book, issued under the Central Bank Law. It requires banks to maintain policies, processes, systems and controls to identify, measure, monitor, report, control and mitigate IRRBB, and defines related terms such as the trading book. It binds all banks licensed in the UAE; the Central Bank may impose requirements beyond the minimum items listed.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
