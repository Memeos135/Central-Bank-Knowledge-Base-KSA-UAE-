# Countercyclical Buffer

A macroprudential CET1 buffer that the Central Bank may activate within a defined range to address system-wide risk from excess credit growth, sitting alongside the capital conservation buffer in the transitional phase-in table. Breach consequences track the buffer regime generally, including restrictions on distributions and a requirement to restore the buffer. Applies to banks in the UAE, with the applicable rate set by the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
