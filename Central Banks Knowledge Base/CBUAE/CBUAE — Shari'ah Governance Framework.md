# Shari'ah Governance Framework

Concept node covering the Shari'ah governance architecture for Islamic Financial Institutions: defined terms (internal and external Shari'ah audit, Shari'ah supervision, compliance with Islamic Shari'ah) and the organisational requirements for the internal Shari'ah audit division, including board appointment of a Shari'ah controller and his eligibility criteria. It establishes a hierarchy of authority in which the Higher Shari'ah Authority's resolutions and fatwas prevail over those of an institution's Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee. It binds licensed Islamic financial institutions (and Islamic windows) and their boards, internal audit functions and external Shari'ah auditors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
