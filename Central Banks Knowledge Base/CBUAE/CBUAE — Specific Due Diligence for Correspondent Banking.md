# Specific Due Diligence for Correspondent Banking

Concept node on the correspondent-specific due diligence layer that sits above standard CDD for cross-border respondent relationships, including understanding the respondent's business, reputation and AML/CFT controls, documenting responsibilities in the correspondent agreement, and applying enhanced scrutiny where listed risk indicators are present (nesting, payable-through activity, high-risk jurisdictions, adverse ownership/management, high-risk products or customer base). Explains the correspondent/respondent construct and the service range involved. Binds CBUAE-licensed financial institutions acting as correspondents, on a risk-based, scaled basis.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
