# Committee for Settlement of Insurance Disputes

Concept node for the Committee for the Settlement and Resolution of Insurance Disputes as defined and constituted under the regulation. It covers the committees' administrative and financial reporting line to the Authority, seat locations and ability to sit elsewhere, composition (chairman, members, alternates, possible delegated judge), one-year renewable membership with hold-over until reappointment, judge-equivalent conduct obligations, quorum and majority decision-making, and freedom from ordinary civil procedure constraints. Directly relevant to assessing forum, standing and procedural fairness in insurance complaints against licensed companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
