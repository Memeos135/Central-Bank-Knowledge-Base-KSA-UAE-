# Standards Article 8: Restructuring

Article 8 of the CBUAE Credit Risk Standards governs the identification, approval and management of restructured credit facilities, splitting them into distressed restructuring (terms amended in a context of obligor financial difficulty, including post-90-day past due or unlikeliness to pay) and non-distressed restructuring (commercial or regulatory amendments absent distress). It requires separate retail and wholesale restructuring processes, a delegation-of-authority approval process mirroring new underwriting, eligibility and viability criteria, annual internal audit review, and treats a standstill exceeding 90 days as a default event. It binds LFIs, including those conducting Islamic financial services, which must also observe Shari'ah rules.
