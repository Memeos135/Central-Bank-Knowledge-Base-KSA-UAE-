# Parent

Parent node of a CBUAE regulation on insurance Group supervision, covering the scope provisions (Article 2) and the Parent's core duties (Article 3). It sets when the Central Bank supervises at Group level — a State-incorporated Company that is a Parent of at least one Company or foreign (re)insurer, or a Holding Company — permits supervision only at ultimate Parent level, allows case-by-case exclusion of negligible-risk entities, and obliges Group entities in the State to give the Parent the data it needs. It binds Parents, Holding Companies and Companies within an insurance Group under CBUAE jurisdiction (not a SAMA instrument).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
