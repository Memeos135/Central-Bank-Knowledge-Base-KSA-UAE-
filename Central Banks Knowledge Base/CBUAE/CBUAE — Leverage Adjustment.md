# Leverage Adjustment

Node on the non-risk-based leverage ratio as a backstop complementing risk-based capital requirements, including how the exposure measure is built up and adjusted. It confirms that total balance-sheet assets are the starting point, that derivative and SFT-related on-balance-sheet items move into their respective exposure components, that on-balance-sheet collateral remains in the exposure measure, and that unconditionally cancellable commitments to issue direct credit substitutes attract a 10% rather than 100% conversion factor. Applies to CBUAE-licensed banks calculating the leverage ratio.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
