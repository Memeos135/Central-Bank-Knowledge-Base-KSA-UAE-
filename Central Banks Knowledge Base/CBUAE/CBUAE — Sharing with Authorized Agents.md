# Sharing with Authorized Agents

Governs disclosure of Consumer Personal Data to Authorized Agents and Third Parties under outsourcing arrangements. Licensed Financial Institutions must apply a fit and proper standard to agents' data management, authorise access in writing, monitor it regularly and restrict it to the stated purpose; outsourcing contracts must contain confidentiality safeguards and prohibit unauthorised disclosure, with agents obliged to report significant Personal Data breaches to the institution's Data Management and Protection function. Liability is non-delegable: the institution's duty to protect Consumer Data covers the acts of all Authorized Agents, and on contract termination data must be retrieved or destroyed, with onward sharing by Third Parties contractually barred unless required by UAE law.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
