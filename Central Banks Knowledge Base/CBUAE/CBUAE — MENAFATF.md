# MENAFATF

A defined-term/background node identifying MENAFATF as the FATF-Style Regional Body for the Middle East and North Africa, referenced in the CBUAE AML/CFT Guidelines' glossary and its overview of the international AML/CFT architecture alongside the UN, FATF and other FSRBs. It is contextual rather than obligation-creating: it explains the standard-setting and mutual-evaluation environment that shapes national AML/CFT requirements. It binds no institution directly, but informs how FIs should read the origin and intent of UAE AML/CFT rules.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
