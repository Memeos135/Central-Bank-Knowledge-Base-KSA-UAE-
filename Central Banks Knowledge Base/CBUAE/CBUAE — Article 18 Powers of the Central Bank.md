# Article 18: Powers of the Central Bank

Article 18 lists the Central Bank's discretionary supervisory powers over Insurance Brokers: capping Remuneration for particular lines of business, liquidating bank guarantees where a Broker cannot meet liabilities, adjusting minimum capital and bank guarantee requirements case by case according to nature, scale and complexity, and raising the minimum UAE national shareholding percentage of paid-up capital. The node context also carries the Regulation's objectives and legal basis under the UAE central bank and insurance decretal laws. Binds Insurance Brokers indirectly by exposing them to these supervisory measures; UAE/CBUAE regime.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
