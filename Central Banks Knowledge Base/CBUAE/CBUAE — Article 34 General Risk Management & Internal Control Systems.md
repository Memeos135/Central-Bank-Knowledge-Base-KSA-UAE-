# Article 34: General Risk Management & Internal Control Systems

Article 34 sets the general risk management and internal control requirements for payment token businesses, sitting alongside Article 35 (technology risk) as the risk-policy limb assessed at licensing and covering matters such as record-keeping of Personal Data and Payment Data for five years. It is cross-referenced in the licensing chapter as one of the areas an applicant must demonstrate compliance with, together with capital, Reserve of Assets, conduct/customer protection and AML/CTF requirements. It binds Licensees and Registrees providing Payment Token Services.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, AML/CTF
