# Article 10: Risk Governance Framework and Internal Controls

Requires Insurance Brokers to maintain a Risk Governance Framework (policies, processes, systems and controls) to identify, measure, monitor, report and mitigate material risks on a timely basis, expressly covering operational risk, conduct risk, insurance fraud, cyber attacks and money laundering/terrorist financing. Specifies internal audit's assessment duties and obliges the heads of risk management, compliance and internal audit to report violations of Central Bank Laws and Regulations and Matters of Significance directly to the Central Bank, with good-faith protection. Also restricts dismissal of Control Function heads without CBUAE non-objection, requires notification of their resignation, keeps outsourced activities within Control Function scope, and conditions combining positions on CBUAE approval.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
