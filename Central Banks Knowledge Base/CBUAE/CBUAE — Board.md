# Board

Concept node covering the allocation of model governance accountability under the CBUAE Model Management Standards, at Board, Senior Management, Model Oversight Committee and Chief Risk Officer level. It sets out that the Board is ultimately accountable for the appropriate use and management of models (linking to the Risk Management Regulation), must own materially significant modelling decisions and define model risk appetite, while the CRO must ensure institution-wide identification, measurement, monitoring, reporting and mitigation of Model Risk and that material decisions reach the Board or Board Risk Committee. Binds licensed UAE institutions subject to the MMS and their governing bodies and senior officers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
