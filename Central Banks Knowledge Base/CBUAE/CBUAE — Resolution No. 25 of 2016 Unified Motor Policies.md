# Resolution No. 25 of 2016 Unified Motor Policies

A referenced instrument: Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 25 of 2016 issuing the Regulation of the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policies, which supplies the standard policy wordings and coverages that the motor tariff Regulation prices. Within the tariff Regulation it functions as the baseline: any coverage agreed beyond the Resolution No. 25 policies requires a separately stated additional premium, set out in clear wording in a different colour or by addendum. It binds insurance companies issuing motor policies in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
