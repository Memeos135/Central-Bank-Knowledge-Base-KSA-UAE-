# Testing and Reporting

Covers sections 10.3–12.1 of the CBUAE Regulatory Sandbox Conditions: grounds for discontinuing testing (purpose not achieved, risks outweighing benefits, breach of Central Bank requirements, voluntary exit, lapse of the period), the Central Bank's power to require immediate cessation and wind-down, the mandatory exit strategy (notifying Consumers, meeting Consumer obligations including compensation, reporting on implementation, secure disposal of Consumer information), the six-to-twelve-month testing duration with case-by-case extensions requested at least 30 calendar days before expiry, and the obligation to document and periodically report testing information. It binds Sandbox Participants and supports Central Bank supervision and review.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk
