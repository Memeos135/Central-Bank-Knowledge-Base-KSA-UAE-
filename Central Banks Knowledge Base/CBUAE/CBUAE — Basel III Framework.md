# Basel III Framework

Concept node for the Basel III framework as the international benchmark the Central Bank's capital standards are calibrated to, alongside Basel II's standardised approach implemented in 2009. It underpins capital adequacy, capital supply/instruments and leverage standards, with local adjustments such as a minimum total capital requirement of 10.5% excluding buffers and grandfathering arrangements for pre-2017 AT1 and Tier 2 instruments. Relevant to all licensed banks subject to the capital adequacy regulation and standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
