# Payments Screening

Component of the CBUAE screening guidance dealing with sanctions screening applied to payments and transactions, complementing customer-level name screening; the surrounding obligations require searching names of parties to any transaction and screening before conducting any transaction or entering a business relationship. It also ties to list maintenance from the UNSC and Executive Office sources and to name-screening controls such as ownership/control, fuzzy matching and weak aliases. Binds LFIs.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, payments, AML/CTF
