# Corporate Governance Framework (Article 2)

Node covering the overarching corporate governance framework requirement for insurance and takaful companies under the CBUAE Corporate Governance Regulation and accompanying Standards, including the Article 10 communications/disclosure duties and the Article 9 financial reporting and external audit duties. It requires each company (and, where relevant, its group) to adopt board-approved governance policies and processes spanning strategy, organisational structure, control environment, risk responsibilities and compensation, and to publish an annual corporate governance statement plus audited financial statements. It binds licensed insurance/takaful companies supervised by the CBUAE, and sits alongside separate regulations on risk management, internal control, compliance and outsourcing.
