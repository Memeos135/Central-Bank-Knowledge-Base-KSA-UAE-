# External Auditor Requirements

Concept node on External Auditor obligations under the Financial Regulations for Insurance Companies. It covers additional duties the regulator may impose (expanded audit scope, notification of financial violations, reservations on accounts or reserves, control and financial-statement deficiencies), protection of the auditor from breach-of-duty claims when performing those duties, strict independence criteria (no board, management, agency, kinship or competing interest), mandatory replacement if independence lapses, and signing/review requirements for quarterly (limited review, 45 days) and audited annual statements. It binds the insurance company and, through it, its appointed external auditor.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
