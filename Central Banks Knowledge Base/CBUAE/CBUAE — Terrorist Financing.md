# Terrorist Financing

Concept node covering terrorist financing as one limb of the UAE AML/CFT/CPF regime as treated in CBUAE's proliferation-financing guidance, where TF sits alongside ML and PF in institutional risk identification, TFS implementation and reporting duties. The source material focuses mainly on PF and its sanctions framework, with TF appearing as the adjacent obligation set under Federal Decree Law No. (20) of 2018 and its Implementing Regulation. Relevant to LFIs (and DNFBPs/VASPs) determining the scope of their combined ML/TF/PF risk assessments and sanctions screening duties.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
