# Responsible Financing Practices

## Why this community

Responsible lending to retail consumers: the pre-contractual assessment duties (affordability, suitability) and quantitative leverage limits that condition whether and on what terms credit may be extended.

## How members connect

- The general credit product provisions and the responsible financing article form the umbrella obligation; the suitability and affordability assessments are the operative duties that discharge it.
- The Debt Burden Ratio is the shared quantitative test referenced by the responsible financing article, the affordability assessment and the financing terms — it is the common binding constraint across the cluster.
- Suitability and affordability are distinct but paired tests (is the product right for this consumer vs. can this consumer repay); both must be satisfied before terms are set.
- Relevant to finance companies and BNPL providers, where the 'Consumer' definition and any exemptions from full affordability testing determine which of these duties actually bite.
