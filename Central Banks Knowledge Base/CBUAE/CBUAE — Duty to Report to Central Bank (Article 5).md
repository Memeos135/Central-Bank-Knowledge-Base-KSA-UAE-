# Duty to Report to Central Bank (Article 5)

Article 5 imposes a direct duty on external auditors to report promptly to the Central Bank any breaches of the Central Bank law, regulations or instructions and any Matters of Significance arising from the audit, with good-faith reporting protected from being treated as a breach of the auditor's obligations. Companies must notify the Central Bank of auditor resignation and reasons, and obtain non-objection before dismissing or changing the auditor; divergence of opinion is not valid grounds for dismissal. Material reservations must be reported before the audit is finalised, and a qualified opinion cannot be finalised without Central Bank non-objection; Article 6 permits appointment of an External Shari'ah Auditor for Takaful products.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
