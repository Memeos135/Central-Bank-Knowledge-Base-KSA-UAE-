# IA Board Resolution No. 26 of 2020 (Amendment)

Second amending instrument to the Unified Motor Vehicle Insurance Policy Against Loss and Damage (issued under Decision 25/2016), reflected in the consolidated version dated 18/01/2021. Used to establish the current in-force wording and the effective date of later changes to the standard motor own-damage policy. Binds UAE-licensed insurers and shapes policyholder rights under the unified wording.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
