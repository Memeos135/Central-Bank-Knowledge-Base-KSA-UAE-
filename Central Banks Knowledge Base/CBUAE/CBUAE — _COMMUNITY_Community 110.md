# Bank Capital Requirements

## Why this community

Minimum and ongoing capital requirements for restricted-licence banks: what counts as eligible capital, what happens on breach, how the wider bank prudential rules apply, and the supervisory consequences of non-compliance.

## How members connect

- Classic parent-regulation-to-article hierarchy: a single capital regulation is the anchor and every other member is one of its operative articles.
- Obligation chain runs from quality-of-capital criteria, through the breach provisions that set notification and remediation duties, to enforcement and sanctions as the consequence limb.
- The application article determines how far the general bank regulations are imported into the restricted-licence context - the key scope-limiting provision.
- The cancellation article performs the repeal function, so prior resolutions and circulars on this subject are superseded and should not be cited as live obligations.
- Jurisdictional flag: this is CBUAE material, not SAMA; do not transpose the capital thresholds into a KSA analysis.
