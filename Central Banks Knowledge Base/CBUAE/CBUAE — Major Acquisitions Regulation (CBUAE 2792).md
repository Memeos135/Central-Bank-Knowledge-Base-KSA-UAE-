# Major Acquisitions Regulation (CBUAE 2792)

CBUAE Major Acquisitions Regulation (Circular 02/2020, dated 24 March 2020), issued under the Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018) and addressed to all banks. It establishes a prudential framework governing how banks and banking groups propose and undertake major acquisitions, aiming at bank soundness, financial stability and retail customer protection, and places the onus on the board to demonstrate a comprehensive approach. Listed requirements are minimums — the Central Bank may impose additional requirements — and the regulation defines key terms including Major Acquisition, Notification Acquisition, Purchase Price, Senior Management and Subsidiary.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
