# Internal Controls & Audit

## Why this community

Governance architecture for internal control: the mandated control functions (compliance, internal audit, risk, actuarial), their independence and reporting lines, and board/senior-management accountability for an effective internal control framework across banks, finance companies and insurers.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Internal Controls, Compliance and Internal Audit Regulation and the Risk Management and Internal Controls Regulation sit above the defined 'Control Functions', which are then broken out into compliance, internal audit and actuarial roles.
- Shared organising concept: the Three Lines of Defense model links control functions to internal policies, controls and procedures, allocating ownership between business, oversight and assurance.
- Obligation chain upward: board (bank/insurer/finance company) and senior management carry non-delegable responsibility for approving, resourcing and reviewing the framework; control functions report into them.
- External standards incorporated by reference: internal audit obligations are benchmarked to IIA international standards; insurance-specific fraud-countering duties attach the same governance machinery to a sector control objective.
