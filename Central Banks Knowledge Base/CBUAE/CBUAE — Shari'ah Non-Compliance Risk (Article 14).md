# Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk (Article 14)

Article 14 of the CBUAE Operational Risk and Operational Resilience Regulation addresses Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk (SNCR), defined as the risk of financial loss or reputational harm from failure to comply with Shari'ah, and applies to Islamic Finance Institutions (including Islamic windows within its scope). It requires a Shari'ah governance framework aligned with Higher Shari'ah Authority resolutions, product and contract approval policies, dedicated systems, controls and limits for SNCR across the contract life cycle, proactive identification by the risk management function, and formal recording and reporting of non-recognised income to the internal Shari'ah supervision committee. The internal Shari'ah supervision committee holds the determinative authority on Shari'ah matters, and control frameworks must be reviewed and improved where SNCR materialises.
