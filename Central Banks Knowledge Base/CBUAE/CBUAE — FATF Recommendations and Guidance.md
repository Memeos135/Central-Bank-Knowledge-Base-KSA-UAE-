# FATF Recommendations and Guidance

Node representing FATF standards and guidance as the international reference underpinning CBUAE AML/CFT guidance issued under the Implementing Regulation of the AML/CTF Decree-Law, which tasks supervisory authorities with providing guidance to financial institutions. The associated guidance sets CBUAE expectations for demonstrating compliance without creating new legal obligations, and expressly defers to the in-force legal and regulatory framework in case of conflict; it includes FATF-derived typologies such as PEP red flags and indicators of suspicion. Relevant to LFIs and their supervisors when interpreting national AML/CFT requirements.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
