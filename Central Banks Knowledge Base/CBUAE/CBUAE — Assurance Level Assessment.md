# Assurance Level Assessment

Sets out the first limb of the CBUAE digital ID expectation: before adopting a digital ID system for CDD, an LFI must establish the system's assurance levels — its reliability and independence — either by performing the assessment itself or by relying on audit/certification from an expert body. Self-assessment triggers due diligence on the ID service provider, including its governance; reliance on an expert body (including a group entity or independent third party) requires a reasonable basis that the body correctly applies publicly disclosed assurance frameworks. The assessment must be documented and refreshed periodically and on trigger events; no specific internal function is prescribed to own it.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
