# Mergers & Liquidations

## Why this community

Mergers, liquidations and bankruptcies: how corporate restructuring, exit or insolvency of a supervised entity is regulated, including supervisory notification/approval and continuity of obligations toward customers and creditors.

## How members connect

- Single-article cluster; it presupposes the supervisor's approval and intervention authority, so it reads together with the Central Bank powers article.
- Continuity linkage: customer records, personal data and outstanding consumer/finance obligations must survive the corporate event, tying back to record-keeping and data-protection duties.
- Sits above general company/insolvency law as a sector-specific overlay - the supervisory consent step is additional, not a substitute.
