# Reporting Entities

"Reporting Entities" is the addressee concept in the CBUAE-endorsed ESG Disclosure Principles: entities expected to maintain internal systems, policies and procedures to monitor, escalate to board and senior management, and publicly report sustainability-related risks and information. The Principles deliberately leave it to each Authority to determine which entities, size or profile thresholds, frequency, and whether reporting is voluntary, comply-or-explain, or mandatory. Useful for scoping who is captured before assessing substantive disclosure duties.
