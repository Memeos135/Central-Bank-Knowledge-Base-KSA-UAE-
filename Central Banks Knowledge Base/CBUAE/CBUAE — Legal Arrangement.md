# Legal Arrangement

This node is the defined term "legal arrangement" — a contractual relationship between two or more parties that creates no separate legal personality, such as a trust — together with the CBUAE guidance on its ML/TF/PF risk profile, where ownership, control and enjoyment of funds may be split between different persons. For such customers LFIs must identify and verify the settlor, trustee (or equivalent), beneficiaries or class of beneficiaries, and any other controlling individuals, and must apply the same risk-based assessment factors used for legal persons. It binds LFIs onboarding or servicing trusts and similar arrangements, including those formed in the Mainland, DIFC and ADGM.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
