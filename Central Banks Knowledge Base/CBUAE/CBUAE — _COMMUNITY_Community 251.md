# Re-takaful Insurance

## Why this community

Article-level requirement governing re-takaful arrangements, i.e. the cession of takaful risk to re-takaful (or, where permitted, reinsurance) providers and the conditions attaching to such arrangements.

## How members connect

- Singleton article node within a takaful/insurance instrument; it depends on that instrument's defined terms (takaful operator, participants, surplus) rather than on other clustered nodes.
- Interacts with solvency, risk-retention and outsourcing-type controls elsewhere in the same regime, and with SAMA approval or notification requirements for counterparties.
- Sits at the regulation/rules tier beneath the insurance law, with supervisory circulars typically refining acceptable counterparty criteria.
