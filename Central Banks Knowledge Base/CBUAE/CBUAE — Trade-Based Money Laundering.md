# Trade-Based Money Laundering

Core concept node of CBUAE's TBML guidance: defines trade-based money laundering (following FATF) as manipulating domestic or international trade transactions to disguise criminal proceeds or the identity of trade participants, and situates it alongside financial-system and cash-movement laundering methods. It covers the UAE threat picture, the international trade and trade finance backdrop, and includes an annex of TBML and illicit transshipment red flags. Addressed to Licensed Financial Institutions for risk understanding, CDD and transaction monitoring purposes.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
