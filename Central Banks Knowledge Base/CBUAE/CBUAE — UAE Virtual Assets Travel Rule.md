# UAE Virtual Assets Travel Rule

This node is the UAE Virtual Assets Travel Rule issued under the CBUAE framework, setting the originator/beneficiary information requirements that must accompany virtual asset transfers and the definitions underpinning them (including Privacy Token, Unhosted Wallet, Virtual Asset, Receive, and STR). It imposes a risk-based regime covering collection and verification of transfer data, a daily aggregated AED 3,500 threshold for beneficiary identity verification, handling of transfers with missing information (reject, execute, delay, return, report, follow up), supervisory reporting of systemic failures, and record keeping aligned with the underlying AML Decision. It binds Virtual Asset Service Providers and Intermediary Providers operating within UAE federal, Emirate, Free Zone and Financial Free Zone territorial scope.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
