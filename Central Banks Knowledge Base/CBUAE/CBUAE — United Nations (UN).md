# United Nations (UN)

A contextual node covering the United Nations' role in the international AML/CFT framework as described in the CBUAE Guidelines, including the UNODC Global Programme against Money Laundering and the UN-derived targeted financial sanctions regime. It also appears in the Guidelines' useful-links annex. It creates no standalone obligation here; UN TFS listing obligations for UAE institutions are addressed under the separate Cabinet Decision No. (74) of 2020 framework referenced by the Guidelines.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
