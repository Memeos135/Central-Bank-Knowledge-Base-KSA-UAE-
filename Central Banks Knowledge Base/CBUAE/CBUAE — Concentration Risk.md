# Concentration Risk

Concept node defining risk concentration as a single exposure or group of correlated exposures (same counterparty, geography, industry or risk factor) large enough to threaten a bank's creditworthiness, core operations or risk profile, and requiring analysis at both legal entity and consolidated level. It sits within the Pillar 2 / ICAAP comprehensive assessment of risks, requiring aggregation of similar exposures across entities, asset types and risk areas, and routine reporting of concentrated positions to senior management. Binds banks in their internal capital adequacy assessment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
