# Supervisory Factors and Correlations (Table 2)

Concept node for the table of supervisory factors and supervisory correlation parameters used to convert effective notional amounts into asset-class and hedging-set add-ons for counterparty credit risk. Supervisory factors are differentiated by asset class and, for credit and equity, by rating or single-name versus index status; supervisory correlations drive the aggregation formulas for credit, equity and commodity hedging sets (energy, metals, agriculture, other). It binds banks applying the standardised counterparty credit risk approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
