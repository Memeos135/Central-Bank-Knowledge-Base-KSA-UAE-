# Commencement of Insurance Activities (Article 6)

Article 6 requires a registered insurance Company to begin insurance activities within sixty business days of Register entry, with the commencement date fixed as the date the first Insurance Policy is concluded; the Central Bank may grant one equivalent extension in duly justified circumstances. Failure to commence triggers a duty to inform the Central Bank and exposes the Company to suspension of registration or removal from the Register, with a defined route to re-registration. The surrounding context also sets out the classification of property and liability insurance classes and the licence application requirements (Article 3).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
