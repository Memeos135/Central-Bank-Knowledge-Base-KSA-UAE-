# Principle 7 - Scenario Analysis

Principle 7 of the CBUAE climate-related financial risk principles sets the supervisory expectation that relevant financial firms build and run climate scenario analysis and stress testing frameworks proportionate to their size, complexity, risk profile and activities. It covers objectives of the analysis, data inputs and metrics, coverage of physical, transition and liability risk pathways, use of short- and long-term horizons, and feeding results into risk management adequacy reviews and the internal capital and liquidity adequacy processes under Principle 6. It binds financial firms within the CBUAE's scope of application for this instrument, on a 'should' (supervisory expectation) basis, with acknowledgement that methodologies are still maturing.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
