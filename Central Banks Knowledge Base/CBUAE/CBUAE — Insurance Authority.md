# Insurance Authority

Institutional node for the Insurance Authority, established under Federal Law No. 6 of 2007 concerning the Establishment of the Insurance Authority and the Regulation of Insurance Business, and the issuing body for the insurance conduct and prudential instruments in this corpus (functions later assumed by the Central Bank). Its Board, Chairman and Director General are the decision-making organs referenced in the resolutions. Relevant to determining the issuing authority, scope of registration, and supervisory reach over insurers, agents and brokers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
