# Stress Testing (Article 5)

Article 5 of the IRRBB Regulation is the stress testing component of the interest rate and rate of return risk framework, sitting between the articles on measurement/models (Article 4) and information systems and internal reporting (Article 6). The governance framework requires stress testing of IRRBB and the use of its results in decision-making, alongside monitoring and enforcement of IRRBB limits. It applies to all Banks, including Islamic banks and windows applying the same principles to rate of return risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
