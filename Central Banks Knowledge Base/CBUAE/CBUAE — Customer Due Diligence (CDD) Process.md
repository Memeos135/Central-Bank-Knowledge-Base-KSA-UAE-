# Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Process

Sets the Customer Due Diligence tier of the KYC framework for Licensed Persons under the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business, covering identification and verification, source of funds and purpose of transaction capture, PEP screening, and periodic profile review and ongoing monitoring (at least annually or on ID expiry, and on any profile change). Also specifies minimum customer data to be recorded at point of sale and printed on transaction receipts. Binds Licensed Persons; CDD is mandatory for all legal persons/arrangements and for natural persons above the CID band.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
