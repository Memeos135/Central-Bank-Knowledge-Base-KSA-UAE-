- **What this link tells you:** If you are deciding whether beneficial ownership information collected for a corporate customer is sufficient, read the CDD obligation together with the UBO Resolution, which regulates real-beneficiary procedures and superseded the earlier resolution from its 2020 effective date. The Resolution supplies the definitional and registry-side expectations for identifying the real beneficiary, while CDD imposes the verification duty on the financial institution. Consequently, registry-sourced UBO data supports but does not discharge the firm's own verification and ongoing-update duties; treat divergence between register and customer-supplied information as a risk trigger.
