# Regulation 1/2026 on Islamic Commercial Transactions

CBUAE Regulation No. 1/2026 issued under Article 471 of Federal Decree-Law No. 50/2022 (Commercial Transactions Law), approved by the Higher Shari'ah Authority and the CBUAE Board, setting the controls and rules for the commercial transactions of Islamic financial institutions and takaful insurance companies. It is organised in six chapters covering definitions and scope, Shari'ah compliance and interpretation, general contract and obligation rules, promise (wa'd) and revocation of a binding promise, Islamic deposits and investment accounts, Islamic banking financing contracts (instalment sale, murabaha, istisna'a, salam, ijarah, mudaraba, investment wakala, musharaka, financing security, documentary credit), takaful insurance, and compliance with the regulation. It binds CBUAE-licensed Islamic financial institutions, takaful and retakaful companies, and is the reference framework for their contracts with customers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
