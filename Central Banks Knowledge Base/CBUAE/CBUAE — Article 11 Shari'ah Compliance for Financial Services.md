# Article 11: Shari'ah Compliance for Financial Services

Article 11 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards sets the conduct-side Shari'ah compliance obligations that apply when Islamic Financial Institutions offer Islamic Financial Products and/or Services, cross-referring to the separate Shari'ah Governance framework. It places duties on the Board, Senior Management and the Internal Shari'ah Supervision Committee covering Shari'ah compliance culture and independent oversight, fair profit distribution between shareholders and investment accountholders, consumer education on conventional versus Islamic products, disclosure of the legal consequences of contracts and consumer choices, access to internal Shari'ah functions, and limits on charity/late-payment obligations where a consumer is insolvent or bankrupt (not Mumatil). It also requires adherence to the Central Bank's Early Settlement Fee requirements and to permissible debt-retention limits under relevant HSA resolutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
