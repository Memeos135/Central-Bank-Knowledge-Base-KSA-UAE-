# Identifying the Customer (AML/KYC)

Article (16) of the Electronic Insurance Regulations on identifying the customer before selling or issuing a policy electronically, plus the adjacent selling rules in Article (17). It requires verification of customer identity and supporting documents, application of AML/CTF and counter-proliferation of illegal organisations legislation with reporting of suspicious activity through the electronic channel, retention of customer identity records for at least ten years, and creation of a protected electronic customer account (periodic password change, session re-authentication, email verification, board-approved two-factor login policy). It binds insurance companies and insurance agents, and insurance brokers when issuing insurance certificates.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other, consumer protection, data/credit info
