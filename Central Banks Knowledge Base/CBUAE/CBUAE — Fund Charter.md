# Fund Charter

This node sets the mandatory contents of the Charter of the Takaful Insurance Fund: the Fund's purpose, its separation from the Company's articles of association, its ownership of assets and liability for its own obligations with an independent financial position disclosed in the Company's financial statements, Participant/Beneficiary status and criteria, the Fund's capacity to contract with the Company, liquidation distribution to Beneficiaries, and a 100-year automatically renewable term. It also limits the Company's benefit from the Fund to the agreed Wakala Fee. It binds the Company drafting and maintaining the Charter.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
