# Standards Article 6: Definition of Default

Article 6 of the Credit Risk Standards defines default for all credit facilities and obligors, retail and wholesale, with Shari'ah-compliant facilities also meeting Shari'ah contract requirements. Default is triggered by non-payment (past due beyond 90 days on a material obligation, materiality set at more than 5% of the obligor's total funded exposure, with a consecutive DPD count across facilities for wholesale obligors) and/or unlikeliness to pay, for which indicative criteria are listed, including collateral deterioration, guarantee of a defaulted subsidiary, breach of facility terms, and for retail obligors loss of income sources or breach of the Debt Burden Ratio. Default must be assessed across all legal entities of a UAE banking group, including parent, branches and subsidiaries.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
