# RPS Designation Process

This node covers the designation process for a Retail Payment System, initiated by the Central Bank under the Central Bank Law where an RPS meets or is likely to meet the systemic importance criteria (monetary/financial stability, public interest or public order). It describes the Central Bank's power to require information and documents from any Person holding them, including the System Operator, Settlement Institution or a Participant Person, and the procedures for suspension/revocation of a licence or designation, including written representations, notification, and objection to the Grievances & Appeals Committee. Designation implies oversight, not endorsement, and revocation does not affect transactions already cleared and settled.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
