# Board Decision No. 22 of 2017 - Investment Limits

Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 22 of 2017 setting out how insurers apply the investment (asset distribution and allocation) limits contained in the Financial Regulations, and is read as complementary to those Regulations. It prescribes treatment of breaches and new investments outside the limits, distinguishing cases with and without a shortfall in Minimum Capital Requirement, Solvency Capital Requirement or Minimum Guarantee Fund, and requires portfolio analysis reporting or a detailed corrective plan as applicable. It binds insurance companies incorporated in the UAE and branches/agents of foreign insurers, including Takaful companies.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
