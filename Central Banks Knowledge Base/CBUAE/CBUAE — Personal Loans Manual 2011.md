# Personal Loans Manual 2011

CBUAE's 2011 Personal Loans Manual (issued Abu Dhabi, 28 April 2011) setting retail lending conduct and prudential-style caps for banks: debt-burden limits, maximum tenor, interest calculation method, fee and early-settlement charge rules, and restrictions on security. It binds banks lending to individuals in the UAE, distinguishing loans outstanding before 1 May 2011 from new or rescheduled facilities after that date. Note it is a UAE (CBUAE) instrument, not a SAMA rule, and is relevant mainly for cross-border comparison with KSA consumer finance/responsible lending requirements.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
