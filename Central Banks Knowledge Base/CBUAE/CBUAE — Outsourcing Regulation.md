# Outsourcing Regulation

Article 20 of the Payment Token Services Regulation, which applies the CBUAE Outsourcing Regulation for Banks (Circular No. 14/2021, as amended) to all Licensees as if each were a "Bank" under that regulation. The node also sits alongside the agency provisions, under which a Payment Token Service performed by an Agent is deemed performed by the principal Licensee, which remains responsible for agent training and qualifications.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
