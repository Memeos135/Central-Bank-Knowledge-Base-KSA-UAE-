# Governance (Article 5)

Article 5 requires Financial Institutions to have effective governance for producing, reviewing, approving, monitoring and implementing the recovery plan. Board approval is required before submission to the Central Bank; for branches of foreign institutions the branch Senior Management committee approves, with the Central Bank able to additionally require head office board approval, and the chief risk officer leads development alongside Senior Management. It also requires clear roles and responsibilities, governance for the communication plan, adequate information systems, consistency with a bank's liquidity contingency funding plan, and permits insurers to draw on ERM/ORSA tools.
