# Internal Shari'ah Supervisory Committee (ISSC)

Defined-term node for the Internal Shari'ah Supervisory Committee: a committee of Islamic finance scholars appointed by an Islamic Bank that independently supervises its transactions, activities and products for Shari'ah compliance and issues binding resolutions and fatwas, subject to not contradicting HSA resolutions. It is supported by the Internal Shari'ah Control Division/Section and the Shari'ah Audit function, all of which must be free from undue influence. In the liquidity context the ISSC must approve Shari'ah-compliant liquidity and hedging instruments and oversee non-commingling of funds and placements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

Practically, a liquidity framework that is prudentially adequate but lacks ISSC involvement is still deficient, and remediation runs through the Shari'ah governance channel as well as the risk function.
