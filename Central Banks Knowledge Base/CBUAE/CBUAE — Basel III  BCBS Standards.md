# Basel III / BCBS Standards

Concept node capturing how the CBUAE liquidity regulations incorporate Basel Committee (BCBS) standards by reference: qualitative terminology follows the 2008 Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management and Supervision, while LCR and NSFR definitions follow the January 2013 LCR and October 2014 NSFR Basel III texts. It also covers the Level 1 / Level 2 HQLA classification and run-off rates for cash outflows, which take their definitions and conditions from the Basel III LCR document, with full detail deferred to the Guidance Manual. Relevant to banks determining the substantive content of ratio definitions and to the ELAR definition (eligible liquid assets to total liabilities).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
