# Common Reporting Standard (CRS)

## Why this community

Automatic exchange of financial account information under the OECD Common Reporting Standard: which institutions report, what account-level due diligence they must run, and which accounts and controlling persons become reportable to the competent authority.

## How members connect

- The CRS Regulations are the domestic implementing instrument for the OECD standard, so the defined terms (Reporting Financial Institution, Reportable Account, Passive NFE) are standard-derived and should not be read against ordinary AML definitions.
- Obligation chain: entity classification as a Reporting Financial Institution triggers CRS due diligence procedures, which in turn determine Reportable Account status and the reporting flow to the competent authority.
- CRS due diligence expressly leans on existing AML/KYC records - a cross-regime interaction where AML customer identification and beneficial-ownership work is reused for tax-transparency purposes, but with different scope and thresholds.
- Passive NFE treatment is the look-through mechanism connecting entity accounts to controlling persons, mirroring but not identical to AML beneficial ownership.
- Note the competent-authority node is UAE-facing; confirm jurisdictional applicability before relying on it for a KSA question.
