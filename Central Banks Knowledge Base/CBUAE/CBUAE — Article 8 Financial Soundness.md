# Article 8: Financial Soundness

Node for the financial soundness (prudential) article of the CBUAE Insurance Brokers Regulation, issued under the UAE Central Bank Law and the 2023 insurance operations decree-law. It sits within the Regulation's prudential block covering financial soundness, risk management, internal controls, reporting and disclosure, and binds juridical persons licensed by the CBUAE as Insurance Brokers. Note that the supplied context is mainly the Regulation's preamble, objectives and definitions (including Fit and Proper Process and Insurance-Related Professions) rather than the operative soundness conditions, so the source should be opened for the actual requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
