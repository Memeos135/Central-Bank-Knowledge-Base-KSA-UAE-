# CVA Capital Calculation

## Why this community

Calculation of the credit valuation adjustment capital charge and the recognition of hedges against it, and how the resulting charge enters risk-weighted assets and the wider capital adequacy assessment.

## How members connect

- The CVA Capital Formula is the operative calculation; Single-Name Exposure and the Supervisory Discount Factor are its defined inputs.
- Hedge recognition is conditional: only Eligible CVA Hedges (including qualifying Index CDS Hedges) may reduce the charge, so eligibility criteria are the decisive legal test.
- Prescribed Risk Weights convert exposures into the charge, which then aggregates into Risk-Weighted Assets.
- Pillar 2 Capital sits at a different level — a supervisory assessment overlay on top of the Pillar 1 RWA outcome, not an input to the formula.
