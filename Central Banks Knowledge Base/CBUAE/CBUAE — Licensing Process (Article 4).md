# Licensing Process (Article 4)

Article 4 of the CBUAE Insurance Company Licensing Regulation, setting the procedural mechanics of the licensing decision: an application is formally received only when complete with all required documents, and deficiencies or clarification requests must be remedied by the applicant within sixty business days of notification, failing which the application lapses as null and void. It binds applicants (domestic companies and foreign insurers seeking branch licences) and frames the Central Bank's handling of incomplete files ahead of registration and commencement of insurance activities.
