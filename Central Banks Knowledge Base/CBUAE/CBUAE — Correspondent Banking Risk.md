# Correspondent Banking Risk

Concept node on correspondent banking as a PF/ML exposure within CBUAE's counter-proliferation-financing guidance, where an LFI processes transactions for a respondent institution's underlying customers with limited direct visibility. The surrounding material sets the risk-factor framework LFIs must apply — customer attributes and ownership, jurisdictional exposure, transactional behaviour including dual-use goods flows, watchlist and control-list hits, and geopolitical developments — feeding into customer risk ratings and risk categories. Relevant to LFIs setting risk appetite and due diligence intensity for cross-border banking relationships.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
