# Securities Financing Transaction Exposures

Covers the treatment of securities financing transactions (SFTs) in the exposure measure, including when cash payables and receivables with the same counterparty may be netted — same explicit final settlement date, legally enforceable set-off including on default/insolvency, and intent/mechanism for net or functionally net settlement, with failed securities legs split out and treated gross. It also requires a counterparty credit risk add-on to the adjusted gross SFT amount, and sits alongside credit conversion factor and failed-trade/non-DvP provisions. Binds banks measuring SFT and off-balance sheet exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
