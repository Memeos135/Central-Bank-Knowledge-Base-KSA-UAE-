# Exchange Business Licensing

## Why this community

Licensing and prudential entry conditions for exchange houses: permitted scope of exchange business by licence category, legal form, minimum capital and bank guarantee, with adjacent trade-finance and wage-remittance activities.

## How members connect

- Regulation-to-standards chain: the 2014 licensing and monitoring regulations are the legal base, with the Standards document elaborating requirements chapter by chapter.
- Chapter 1 fixes the activity perimeter (what 'exchange business' covers and what each licence permits); Chapter 2 attaches the entry conditions to that perimeter.
- Licence category determines permitted activities — e.g. WPS salary disbursement is tied to a specific category rather than available to all licensees.
- Bank guarantee functions as a continuing prudential condition of licence, distinct from its commercial use in documentary trade finance and letters of credit.
