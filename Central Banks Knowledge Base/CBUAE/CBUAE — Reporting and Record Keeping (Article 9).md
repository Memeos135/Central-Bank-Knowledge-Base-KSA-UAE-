# Reporting and Record Keeping (Article 9)

Article 9 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation setting periodic reporting duties to the Central Bank (quarterly for Category 1, semi-annual for Category 2, within 30 days of period end, plus annual audited financial statements) and the minimum content of those reports, including financial position, client money held, complaints and resolution status, loans arranged and defaults/near-defaults. It also imposes record-keeping obligations: adequate books and records at all times, availability for Central Bank inspection, a ten-year retention period for client money and completed transaction records, and a prescribed list of record categories (marketing and online content, internal policies, corporate/financial ledgers, client and regulator reports, client communications and classification confirmations, suitability assessments). Binds licensed loan-based crowdfunding companies operating in the UAE outside the Financial Free Zones; the surrounding text also places ultimate board responsibility for contingency loan-portfolio administration arrangements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
