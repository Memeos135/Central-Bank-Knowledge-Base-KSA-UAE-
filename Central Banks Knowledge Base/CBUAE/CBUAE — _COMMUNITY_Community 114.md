# Trade-Based Due Diligence

## Why this community

Risk-based customer due diligence applied to trade finance and cross-border goods flows, where the underlying typology is diversion, transshipment and dual-use/sanctions-evasion risk rather than conventional payment risk.

## How members connect

- Obligation chain: institution-level (enterprise-wide) risk assessment identifies trade/transshipment exposure, which in turn calibrates whether baseline CDD suffices or EDD is triggered.
- Shared risk factor: illicit transshipment indicators are the common reference point cited by both the enterprise-wide assessment and the heightened-risk EDD measures.
- Hierarchy of measures: CDD is the standing baseline; EDD is the escalated, case-specific overlay for trade relationships assessed as higher risk.
- Cross-regime interaction: trade due diligence sits at the AML/CTF and sanctions/TFS boundary, since goods-level red flags drive both suspicious-activity and sanctions-evasion analysis.
