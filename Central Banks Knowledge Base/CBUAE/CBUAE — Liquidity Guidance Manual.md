# Liquidity Guidance Manual

Concept node for the Guidance Manual that the Central Bank undertakes (under Article 10) to issue on compliance with ELAR, LCR and NSFR, including the definitions and assumptions for LCR/NSFR calculation aligned to Basel III and the detailed HQLA and cash-outflow treatment. Article 7 makes the Manual the source of reporting frequency and scope and requires banks to use its liquidity reporting templates; reports must be based on contractual data without behavioural assumptions, with the Central Bank applying homogeneous assumptions for micro- and macro-prudential analysis. Practically, it means the regulation cannot be read in isolation for calculation or reporting detail.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
