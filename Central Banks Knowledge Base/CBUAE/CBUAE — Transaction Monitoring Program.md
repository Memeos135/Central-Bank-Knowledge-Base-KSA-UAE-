# Transaction Monitoring Program

Concept node on the transaction monitoring programme: the control by which an institution detects, investigates and reports suspicious transactions and keeps customers and activity within risk appetite. It ties TM effectiveness to CDD/KYC quality and expected-activity baselines, requires risk assessment updates at least annually and on trigger events, and calibrates manual versus automated monitoring to institutional size, nature and complexity, with larger institutions expected to automate. Directed at LFIs' compliance function and senior management.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
