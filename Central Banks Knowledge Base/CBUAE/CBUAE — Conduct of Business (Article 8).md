# Conduct of Business (Article 8)

Article 8 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation governs how licensed crowdfunding companies deal with clients; the accompanying definitions and categories articles supply the operative concepts — pricing-model-only business, cooling-off period of at least two business days after the commitment period, funding goal, on-boarding, personal loan, and the Retail Lender versus Market Counterparty client classification (net assets above AED 2,000,000 outside primary residence, with self-attestation verified by the platform). Category thresholds turn on cumulative loans facilitated in a calendar year (AED 5,000,000 and above for Category 1). Binds crowdfunding companies intermediating between lenders and borrowers in the UAE outside the Financial Free Zones.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
