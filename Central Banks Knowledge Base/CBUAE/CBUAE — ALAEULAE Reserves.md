# ALAE/ULAE Reserves

A technical-provisions concept within the CBUAE Financial Regulations for Takaful Insurance Companies, covering reserves for loss adjustment expenses alongside the unearned contribution, unexpired risk and outstanding loss reserves. It sets how takaful operators must quantify liabilities for claims handling costs and unexpired exposure, including actuarial certification and minimum percentage floors for certain lines. Binds licensed takaful companies (and branches of foreign takaful operators) and their appointed actuaries when valuing liabilities and reporting to the Authority.
