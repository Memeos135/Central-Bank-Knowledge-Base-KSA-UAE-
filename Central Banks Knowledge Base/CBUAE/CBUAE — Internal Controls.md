# Internal Controls

Cross-document node on internal controls within CBUAE AML/CFT/CPF expectations, drawing on both the risk-based approach guidance and a further CBUAE instrument. It concerns the design, measurement and scoring of AML/CFT/CPF control frameworks — CDD/KYC procedures, transaction monitoring, staff training and compliance auditing — and how their aggregate effectiveness is evidenced. It binds licensed financial institutions and informs supervisory assessment of programme adequacy.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

### [[CBUAE — Sanctions Compliance Program (SCP)|Sanctions Compliance Program (SCP)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an institution's controls are adequate, the internal controls requirements and the sanctions compliance programme are two layers of the same obligation set and should be evidenced together. Internal controls are the generic policy, procedure and process discipline covering the AML/CFT/CPF programme, while the sanctions compliance programme is the specific build-out required to meet targeted financial sanctions obligations under the applicable Cabinet Decision and related directives. The consequence is that a sanctions programme cannot be treated as a standalone bolt-on: gaps in general internal controls will be read as gaps in sanctions compliance, and vice versa.
