# Assignment of Accounts (Article 10)

Article 10 of the CBUAE Standard on establishing a Takaful Insurance Fund, covering transfer of Fund management or of specific Fund accounts (assets, liabilities and Beneficiaries) to another Company or Fund. It requires the Fund Charter to permit such assignment, makes prior approval of the Central Bank and the Higher Shari'ah Authority mandatory, prohibits assignments contrary to Beneficiaries' interests, preserves a Beneficiary right to object, and requires protection of Participants' Investment Account rights. Binds the Company managing the Fund and any transferee Company or Fund.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
