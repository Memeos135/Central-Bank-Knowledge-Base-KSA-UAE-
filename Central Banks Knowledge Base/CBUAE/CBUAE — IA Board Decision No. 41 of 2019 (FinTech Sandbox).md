# IA Board Decision No. 41 of 2019 (FinTech Sandbox)

The Insurance Authority Board of Directors' Decision No. (41) of 2019 adopting the FinTech regulatory framework (supervisory rules for the experimental environment / sandbox) for the insurance sector. It adopts the annexed rules as integral, and empowers the Director General to amend them and to waive mandatory legislative requirements for participants within the scope and period of an acceptance letter. It binds insurance-sector licensees and FinTech firms seeking to test innovative solutions in the State.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
