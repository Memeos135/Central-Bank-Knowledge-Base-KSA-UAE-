# Commodities Risk

Node on commodities risk within the market risk capital framework, covering the maturity ladder treatment (gross-position charges per time band plus cumulative net-position charges and a final spread charge across all bands) and the alternative simplified approach for commodity positions. The surrounding context ties commodity exposures into the requirement that all material risks be captured in the bank's internal capital assessment, with board and senior management review of new products and firm-wide risk limits. Binds banks holding commodity or commodity-derivative positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
