# Customer Due Diligence

A concept node consolidating KYC, customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence obligations for UAE insurance companies and insurance-related professions, referenced to Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 and Cabinet Resolution No. 10 of 2019. It covers identification and verification for natural persons, legal persons and NGOs, third-party/agent transactions, beneficial owner and life/Family Takaful beneficiary identification, PEP screening with senior management approval and source of wealth checks, and record retention. It binds companies establishing or maintaining business relationships and is used as an audit/inspection benchmark.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
