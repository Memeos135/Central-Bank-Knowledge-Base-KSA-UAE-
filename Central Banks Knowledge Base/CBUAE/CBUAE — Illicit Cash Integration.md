# Illicit Cash Integration

A TBML typology describing how criminally derived cash is integrated into the financial system through trade, identified via three routes: use of non-bank financial institutions, offsetting or value-transfer arrangements, and infiltration of legitimate supply chains. It explains why cash characteristics (anonymity, fungibility, transportability) make it attractive in a trade context, and connects to adjacent material on sanctions and export-control evasion through re-export and origin disguise. Guidance for licensed financial institutions, including those with cash-intensive trade customers; CBUAE source.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
