# Cloud Exit and Resolution Planning

Guidance node within the CBUAE Guidelines for Financial Institutions adopting Enabling Technologies dealing with the cloud-computing lifecycle at its end stage: stressed exit scenarios and documented exit plans for outsourced cloud arrangements, sitting alongside materiality assessment, governance, auditability and business continuity expectations. It frames expectations (expressed as 'should') on institutions to anticipate disruption that continuity measures cannot absorb and to maintain the ability to withdraw from or transition away from an Outsourcing Service Provider. Binds CBUAE-licensed Institutions using cloud arrangements, with knock-on contractual obligations on the OSP and access rights for the relevant Supervisory Authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
