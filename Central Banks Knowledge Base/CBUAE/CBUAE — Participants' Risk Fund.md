# Participants' Risk Fund

Defines the Participants' Risk Fund (PRF) as the segregated pool of contributions from which takaful claims are met, and its role in the solvency architecture of the CBUAE takaful financial regulations. Solvency capital requirements are assessed across all PRFs and shareholders' funds, covering underwriting, market/liquidity, credit and operational risk, with shareholders required to backstop PRF deficiencies. Binds takaful companies, their boards and actuaries in fund segregation, solvency calculation and reporting.
