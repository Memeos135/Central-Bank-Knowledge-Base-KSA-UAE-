# Internal Audit Function

Concept node for the internal audit function, which banks must maintain as independent, permanent and effective, proportionate to size, nature of operations and organisational complexity. It provides independent assurance to the board and senior management on internal controls, risk management, compliance and governance; reports to the board or its audit committee; requires unrestricted access to staff, records and data; must follow the IIA international professional standards; and operates under a board-committee-approved internal audit charter and risk-based annual plan. Binds banks and, where relevant, group entities, subsidiaries, affiliates and international branches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[CBUAE — Risk Management Function|Risk Management Function]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When testing whether a governance structure is compliant, treat internal audit and risk management as separate lines that cannot be merged or made to report through one another. The link rests on the three-lines-of-defence construct: risk management, compliance and actuarial sit in the second line as control functions, while internal audit sits in the third line giving the Board and Senior Management independent assurance over the adequacy of those very controls. Practically, any arrangement in which audit designs, owns or operates risk controls destroys the assurance value the framework requires, and the reader should assess independence, reporting lines and scope of audit coverage together rather than instrument by instrument.
