# Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA)

Concept node on risk-weighted assets as the denominator of risk-based capital ratios within the capital planning and ICAAP expectations, including the requirement to look beyond risk-based ratios to the leverage ratio and to anticipate regulatory changes such as revised standardised approaches for credit, market and operational risk. It ties RWA-based Pillar 1 outcomes to the bank's own assessment of material risks and Pillar 2 add-ons. Binds banks subject to the Central Bank's capital adequacy and ICAAP framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
