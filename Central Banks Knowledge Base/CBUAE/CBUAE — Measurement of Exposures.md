# Measurement of Exposures

Specifies how exposure values are computed for large exposure purposes: exposures as defined under the risk-based capital framework, covering on- and off-balance sheet items in both banking and trading book and instruments carrying counterparty credit risk, valued at accounting value net of specific provisions. Counterparty credit risk instruments use exposure-at-default under the standardised approach; securities financing transactions use the comprehensive approach with standard supervisory haircuts; off-balance sheet items are converted via credit conversion factors with a 10% floor. Amounts already deducted from Tier 1 capital are excluded (except 1,250% risk-weighted exposures), and net short positions after offsetting need not be counted.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
