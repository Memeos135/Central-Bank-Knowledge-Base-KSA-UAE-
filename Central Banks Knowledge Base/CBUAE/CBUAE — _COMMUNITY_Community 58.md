# Acquisitions & Shareholding

## Why this community

Prior-approval regime for changes in ownership and large corporate acquisitions by licensed institutions - i.e. when a transaction triggers supervisory consent rather than mere notification. Note this cluster is UAE (CBUAE) material, not SAMA, and should not be cited as KSA authority.

## How members connect

- Two parallel instruments address the same control question from different angles: Major Acquisitions (the institution acquiring) and Transfer of Significant Shareholding (the institution being acquired into).
- Thresholds are definition-driven: whether a deal is a Major Acquisition turns on its size relative to Total Regulatory Capital; whether a holder is caught turns on the Significant Shareholder / Controlling Shareholder definitions.
- Obligation chain runs definition to trigger to consent: meeting a threshold produces a Requirement for Approval, with the board carrying non-delegable responsibility and the shareholder register serving as the evidentiary record.
