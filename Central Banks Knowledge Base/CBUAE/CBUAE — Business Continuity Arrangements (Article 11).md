# Business Continuity Arrangements (Article 11)

Article 11 of the CBUAE recovery planning regulation, setting business continuity requirements inside the recovery plan: assessing additional crisis-time requirements for maintaining Financial Market Infrastructure membership (e.g. pre-funding or collateralising positions) and putting in place contingency arrangements covering infrastructure, IT systems, key suppliers and key staff. It also requires institutions to ensure key service level agreements survive recovery events and that contracts permit transfer to third parties. Binds Financial Institutions (banks and insurance companies) preparing recovery plans under the regulation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
