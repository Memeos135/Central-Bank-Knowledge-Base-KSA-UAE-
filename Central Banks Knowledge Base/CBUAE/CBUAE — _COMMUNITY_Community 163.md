# Public Subscription Circulars

## Why this community

Historic SAMA circulars governing banks' involvement in public subscriptions to company share capital, including subscriptions to the capital of foreign companies. The problem-space is control over the marketing and receipt of subscription monies through the banking channel.

## How members connect

- Both are SAMA circulars from the same period addressing the same activity — banks acting as a channel for public subscription to company capital.
- The foreign-company circular narrows or qualifies the general subscription position by reference to the issuer's jurisdiction, so the two are read as a general rule plus a scope-specific overlay.
- Linkage is conceptual/subject-matter rather than an express cross-reference; neither should be assumed to amend the other absent explicit wording.
- Note temporal scope: pre-Capital Market Authority instruments, so applicability must be checked against later securities-offering regimes and supervisory perimeter.
