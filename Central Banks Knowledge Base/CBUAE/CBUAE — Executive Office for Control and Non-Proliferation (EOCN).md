# Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation (EOCN)

A concept node on the Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation (EOCN), the UAE national body responsible for implementing export controls and issuing counter-proliferation-financing guidance for FIs, DNFBPs and VASPs, alongside MOD, FANR, ICP and SIRA. It publishes the lists of controlled chemical and non-chemical goods and is the authority LFIs are expected to consult on whether commodities are controlled and whether permits are valid. Directed at CBUAE-licensed financial institutions involved in financing or settling international trade.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
