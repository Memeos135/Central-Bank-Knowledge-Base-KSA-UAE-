# Insurance Company

Node defining and regulating the Insurance Company (Insurer) under Federal Decree-Law No. (6) of 2025 — a juridical person licensed to carry on insurance business in the State — alongside related defined terms (Reinsurance Company, Takaful Insurance and Takaful Insurance Company/Fund, Insured, Beneficiary, Insurance Policy). Provisions cover separation of property/liability insurance from persons and fund-accumulation insurance (with Board power to require adjustment or restrict new combined policies), prohibitions on chairmen, board members and Authorized Individuals (competing management, conflicting activity, acting as agent or broker, taking commissions, representing shareholders), and the requirement that insurance or brokerage of property in the State be undertaken only by licensed Insurance Companies, while reinsurance may be placed inside or outside the State or in a Financial Free Zone.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
