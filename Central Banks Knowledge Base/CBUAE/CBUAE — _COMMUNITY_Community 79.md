# Sanctions & Beneficial Owner Screening

## Why this community

Screening obligations at the intersection of CDD and targeted financial sanctions: identifying the customer and the beneficial owner, then testing those identities and their transactions against sanctions and watchlists.

## How members connect

- Shared definitional spine: the customer/beneficial owner identified under CDD is the same subject fed into name screening, so the ownership/control test determines screening scope.
- Obligation chain: identify and verify -> screen names at onboarding and on list updates -> screen transactions -> resolve matches; list management is the upstream control that makes each step effective.
- Ownership/control rule creates derivative exposure - an unlisted entity can be in scope through a listed owner or controller - linking sanctions screening back to beneficial ownership findings.
- Fuzzy matching is a calibration parameter of the name screening duty, not a separate obligation; it bears on whether the screening obligation is adequately discharged.
