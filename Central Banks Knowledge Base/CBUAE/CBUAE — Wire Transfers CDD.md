# Wire Transfers CDD

Extracted material covering identity verification and documentation standards (powers of attorney, board resolutions, official registry extracts, court orders, certification of copies, Apostille, four-eyes review) alongside the third-party reliance section under Article 19 of the AML-CFT Decision. Despite the wire-transfer label, the context here is about how customer identity is verified and authenticated and how reliance on third parties for CDD must be controlled. It binds FIs conducting CDD on customers and authorised representatives.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
