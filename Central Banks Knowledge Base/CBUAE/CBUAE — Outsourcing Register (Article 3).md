# Outsourcing Register (Article 3)

Article 3 of the Outsourcing Regulation requires banks to maintain a comprehensive, up-to-date register of all outsourcing arrangements — material and non-material — on both a solo and group-wide basis. Minimum content includes service provider details, start and end dates, a service description, whether Confidential Data is involved, and whether the arrangement is material. Binds banks and supports supervisory oversight of the outsourcing portfolio.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
