# IA Board Resolution No. 20 of 2020 Extension of Financial Data Periods

UAE Insurance Authority Board Resolution No. 20 of 2020 granting a 45-day extension (ending 30/06/2020) to the reporting deadlines in the 2014 Financial Regulations for insurance and takaful companies for quarterly financial statements and other required periodic reports. It also delegates standing authority to the Director General to extend submission deadlines for financial statements and other required reports from insurance companies and insurance-related professions. Relevant mainly as a time-limited relief measure and as the source of the DG's delegated extension power.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
