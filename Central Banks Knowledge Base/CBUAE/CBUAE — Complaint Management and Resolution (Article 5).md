# Complaint Management and Resolution (Article 5)

Article 5 of the SME Market Conduct Regulation requires Licensed Financial Institutions to operate a fair, accessible, free-of-charge complaint handling process, supported by an independent complaints management function reporting directly to Senior Management, documented policies and a complaint management system. It also requires customer awareness of rights, staff and Authorised Agent training, institutional responsibility for complaints about Authorised Agents' activities, root cause and trend analysis, quarterly SME Complaints Data reporting to Senior Management, and participation in any external complaints resolution or ombudsman mechanism. It binds banks and finance companies in their dealings with SME Customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
