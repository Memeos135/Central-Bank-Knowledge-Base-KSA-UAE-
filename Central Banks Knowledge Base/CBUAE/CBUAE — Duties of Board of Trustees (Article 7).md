# Duties of Board of Trustees (Article 7)

This node concerns the duties of the Board of Trustees in protecting the interests of the Takaful Insurance Fund and its Participants, read together with the composition, tenure, meeting and remuneration rules for that Board. The supplied context largely reproduces the Article 6 provisions on constitution, dismissal, resignation replacement, term length and meeting frequency, so the substantive duty list should be verified in the source. It binds the Board of Trustees members and the Company that appoints and supports them.
