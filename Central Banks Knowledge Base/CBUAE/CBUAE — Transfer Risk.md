# Transfer Risk

Defined-term node for "transfer risk": the risk that a borrower cannot convert local currency into foreign exchange and therefore cannot service debt in foreign currency. It is treated jointly with country risk in the bank's risk governance framework, risk management function, limit-setting, provisioning and stress testing (including scenarios such as imposition of capital or exchange controls in relevant foreign jurisdictions). Applies to banks with exposures to such risks, at bank and group level.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
