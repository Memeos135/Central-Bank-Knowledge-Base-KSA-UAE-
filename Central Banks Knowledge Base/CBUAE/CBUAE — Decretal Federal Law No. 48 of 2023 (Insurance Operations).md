# Decretal Federal Law No. 48 of 2023 (Insurance Operations)

Reference node for Decretal Federal Law No. 48 of 2023 Concerning the Organization of Insurance Operations, the sector statute under which (together with the 2018 Central Bank Law) the CBUAE issued the Insurance Brokers' Regulation. It underpins licensing, conduct and supervision of insurance and reinsurance intermediation in the UAE. Consult when identifying the primary insurance legislation behind broker obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
