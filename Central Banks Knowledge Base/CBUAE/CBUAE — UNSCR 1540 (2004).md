# UNSCR 1540 (2004)

A concept node on UNSCR 1540 (2004), described as the overarching global obligation on proliferation financing: it is activity-focused rather than list-based, imposing no asset-freeze designations. It requires states to refrain from supporting non-state actors seeking to develop, acquire, possess, transport, transfer or use WMD and their delivery means, and to adopt and enforce corresponding domestic laws. It binds states directly; LFIs encounter it indirectly through UAE implementing legislation and CBUAE counter-proliferation expectations.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
