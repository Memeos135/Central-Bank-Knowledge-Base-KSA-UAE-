# Federal Law No. 10 of 1980 (Central Bank)

UAE Federal Law No. 10 of 1980 on the Central Bank, monetary system and organisation of banking, cited in the bancassurance instructions as the statute establishing the Central Bank. It matters here because the defined term "Bank" in those instructions means a bank or finance company licensed by the Central Bank to operate in the State. Referenced as an institutional/definitional anchor; this node is not the source of the marketing conduct rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
