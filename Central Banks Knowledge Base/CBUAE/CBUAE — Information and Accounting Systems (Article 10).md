# Information and Accounting Systems (Article 10)

Node for Article 10 of the CBUAE SVF Regulation, setting requirements for robust information and accounting systems: timely and accurate recording of business activities, quality management information, an appropriate audit trail, proper books and accounts, and financial statements/returns meeting applicable reporting and accounting standards, plus record-keeping policies. Scope limit: this Article does not apply to licensed banks carrying out SVF business, which instead follow the Central Bank's banking regulations in these areas. Related material addresses information ownership, classification, storage, transmission, processing and retention of customer data collected through SVF registration and payment transactions.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, data/credit info
