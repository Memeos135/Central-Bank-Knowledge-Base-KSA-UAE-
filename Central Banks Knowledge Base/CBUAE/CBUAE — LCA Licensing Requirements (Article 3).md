# LCA Licensing Requirements (Article 3)

Article 3 of the CBUAE Loan-based Crowdfunding Activities Regulation (Circular 7/2020) sets the licensing gateway for crowdfunding companies wishing to conduct LCAs in the UAE (outside the Financial Free Zones), including application form and language/currency requirements, selection and justification of Category 1 or Category 2 status, upgrade/downgrade conditions, Central Bank powers to require further information or inspections, and a bank guarantee in favour of the Central Bank equal to required paid-up capital. It binds companies applying for or holding an LCA licence, including those whose platforms are hosted abroad but which serve UAE-resident clients or use a UAE correspondence address. Note this is a CBUAE instrument, not SAMA, so it is comparative rather than directly applicable to KSA-licensed entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, other
