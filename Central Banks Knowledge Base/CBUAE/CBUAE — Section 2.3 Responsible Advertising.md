# Section 2.3 Responsible Advertising

Section 2.3 of the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards (Article 2: Disclosure & Transparency) sets the responsible advertising regime for Licensed Financial Institutions, applying to every promotional channel — branches, websites, apps, ATMs, telephone banking, statements, social and public media in audio, visual or print form. It requires balanced presentation of potential returns (illustrative-only forecasts plus prominent loss-risk warnings), permits omission of warnings in name-only or invitation-to-discuss material, and obliges institutions to craft their own warnings where prescribed ones do not fit their products. Sub-sections add specific content requirements for advertising Deposit Products and Credit Products, and the section must be read with Article 5 (Business Conduct).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
