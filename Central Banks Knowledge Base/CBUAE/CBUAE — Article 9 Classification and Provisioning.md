# Article 9: Classification and Provisioning

Article 9 requires each LFI to assess, monitor and classify every credit facility or portfolio by current and expected creditworthiness, using the Stages set in the accompanying standards or a system mapped to them, with the classification documented under a Board-approved (or head-office/branch senior management committee-approved) provisioning policy. It also requires a documented, resourced process to estimate provisions for all facilities across all Stages and portfolios, approved by Senior Management. Classification under this Article is the reference point for restructuring treatment under Article 8 and for SICR outcomes under Article 7.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
