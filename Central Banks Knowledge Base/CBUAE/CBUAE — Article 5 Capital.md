# Article 5: Capital

Article 5 requires Financial Institutions to develop processes to evaluate the capital and solvency impact of Climate-related Financial Risks arising within relevant planning horizons. Such risks must be considered in the internal capital adequacy assessment, including through their effect on traditional risk categories, with banks incorporating them into ICAAP and insurance companies into their Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA). The adjacent Article 6 confirms that breaches may attract supervisory action and administrative and financial sanctions from the Central Bank.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
