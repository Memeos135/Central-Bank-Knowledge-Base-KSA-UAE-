# Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018)

Defined-term node for the Central Bank Law, Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Services, as amended or substituted. It is the enabling statute underpinning CBUAE licensing and supervision, including the licensing regime for money transfer, electronic retail payments and digital money services and the incorporation requirements referenced for licence applicants. It is cross-referenced across multiple CBUAE regulations and binds all entities carrying on licensed financial activities in the UAE.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, banking prudential, other
