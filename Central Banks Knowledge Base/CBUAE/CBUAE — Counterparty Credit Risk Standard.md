# Counterparty Credit Risk Standard

A CBUAE capital adequacy standard setting out how banks must calculate risk-weighted assets for counterparty credit risk arising from derivatives and long-settlement transactions, replacing earlier CCR measurement approaches for regulatory capital purposes. It follows the Basel standardised approach for CCR, but is applied against the UAE's higher minimum capital requirement, which caps the effective charge on a single exposure. It binds all banks licensed in the UAE and applies on a consolidated basis, sitting alongside the credit risk, securitisation, equity-investments-in-funds and CVA standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
