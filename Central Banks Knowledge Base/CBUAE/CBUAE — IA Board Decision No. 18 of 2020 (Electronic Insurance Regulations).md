# IA Board Decision No. 18 of 2020 (Electronic Insurance Regulations)

Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 18 of 2020 on the Electronic Insurance Regulations, cited as a legal basis for the Insurance Producer licensing instructions. It governs the conduct of insurance business through electronic channels, websites and smart applications, and is the reference regime for producers whose activity is limited to routing electronic insurance applicants to the company's or agent's website. Binds insurance companies, agents and related professions distributing insurance electronically in the UAE.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
