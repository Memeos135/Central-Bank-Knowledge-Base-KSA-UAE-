# Fitness and Propriety Standards

The Fitness and Propriety Standards that accompany and supplement the CBUAE Fitness and Propriety Regulation, elaborating the minimum criteria and the Fit and Proper Process for assessing persons before appointment and on an ongoing basis. They detail licensee responsibilities for assessment and reporting, and carry the list of positions subject to Central Bank Authorisation (Attachment 1). They apply to the same population as the Regulation: all Licensed Financial Institutions, Insurance Companies, Takaful Insurance Companies and Insurance-Related Professions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
