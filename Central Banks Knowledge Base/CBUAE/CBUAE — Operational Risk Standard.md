# Operational Risk Standard

Node covering operational risk under the CBUAE capital standards: qualifying criteria for the operational risk management framework (use in risk and management reporting, regular reporting of exposures and material losses to business units, senior management and the board, documented policies and treatment of non-compliance, independent validation and review by external auditors or the Central Bank) and additional conditions for the Alternative Standardised Approach (ASA), which is closed to large diversified banks and requires retail/commercial banking to represent at least 90% of income. Binds banks licensed by the CBUAE seeking or using these approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
