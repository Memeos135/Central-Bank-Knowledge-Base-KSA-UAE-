# Regulation Regarding Takaful Insurance (2022)

Node for the 2022 Takaful Insurance Regulation as the defining instrument referenced by the Shari'ah governance standard, together with the standard's defined terms (Takaful Insurance Company, Higher Shari'ah Authority, Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk, Confidential Information) and the general requirement of continuous Shari'ah compliance across all objectives, activities, operations and codes of conduct. The Regulation is the source of the HSA's mandate and of the incorporation and business conditions for Takaful companies; the standard supplements rather than replaces it.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
