# Lender

Defined-party node for the Lender on a crowdfunding platform, i.e. the Client providing funds to Borrowers through the CFP. It governs the crowdfunding company's duties toward Lenders: onboarding care, suitability assessment and risk understanding, identity and address verification (in addition to AML checks), communication of Borrower due diligence and risk scoring, and the Lender-specific Client Agreement terms on loan administration, default steps, applicable lending limits for Market Counterparties and contingency arrangements. It binds the licensed crowdfunding company; it also frames the Lender's own compliance with lending limits.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk
