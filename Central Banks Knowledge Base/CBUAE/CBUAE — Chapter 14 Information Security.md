# Chapter 14: Information Security

Chapter 14 of the CBUAE Standards for Exchange Business sets minimum information security measures for Licensed Persons, framed as illustrative rather than exhaustive, with additional controls expected in line with business size and complexity and the findings of penetration tests and external IT audits. It mandates independent corporate email systems (public webmail is prohibited), a dedicated CBUAE-facing email address notified in writing to the Banking Supervision Department by an authorized signatory — the Central Bank will correspond only through it — and a ban on employees using office systems for personal email, social media or non-business sites. It also requires an Information Security Policy governing the protection of customer information and transaction data.
