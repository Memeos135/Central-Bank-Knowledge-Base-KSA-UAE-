# Holding Company

Defined term and determination process for a "Holding Company": a juridical person incorporated in the State, other than a Company, identified by the Central Bank under the Insurance Law and Article (6) of the Insurance Group Supervision Regulation. Article (6) sets the criteria (including insurance revenue measured proportionally to capital held and audited consolidated revenue), the entity's duty to supply requested information, and the Central Bank's power to issue and revoke the determination, including where a criterion is met only temporarily. Determination brings the entity within group supervision and potential group-level reporting and sanctions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
