# Liquidity Regulations Guidance Manual (Circular 33/2015)

Guidance manual accompanying the UAE liquidity regulations (Circular 33/2015), setting out qualitative liquidity risk management expectations drawn from the Basel Committee's 2008 Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management, plus the quantitative ratio framework. It assigns ultimate responsibility for liquidity risk and risk tolerance to the board, requires pricing of liquidity costs, benefits and risks into products and new product approval, and requires identification, measurement, monitoring and control of liquidity risk across all jurisdictions and on/off-balance-sheet positions. Binds banks operating in the UAE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
