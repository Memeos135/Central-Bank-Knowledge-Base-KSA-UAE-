# Annex I: Retail Payment Services

Annex I lists the Retail Payment Services that a licensed Payment Service Provider may provide, subject to the Regulation's requirements; it is the operative catalogue behind the licensing categories. Read with the definitions and the covering circular, it delimits activities such as payment account and payment instrument issuance, merchant acquiring, payment aggregation, domestic and cross-border fund transfers, payment token services and payment initiation/account information services. Consult it to confirm whether a specific business model falls within a licensable activity and under which permission.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
