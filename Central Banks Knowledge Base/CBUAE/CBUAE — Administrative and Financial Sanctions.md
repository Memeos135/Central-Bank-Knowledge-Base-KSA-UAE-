# Administrative and Financial Sanctions

Sets out the Central Bank's enforcement toolkit: grounds for full or partial withdrawal, revocation or suspension of a licence (including failure to meet capital, liquidity or solvency requirements, non-cooperation with examiners, bankruptcy, cessation of activity), and the administrative and financial sanctions available for violations. Sanctions include restitution of customer funds, multiple-of-benefit fines, fines up to one billion Dirhams on institutions, fines and function bans on Authorized Individuals, delinking from Financial Market Infrastructures, licence conditions or revocation, and minimum fines for unlicensed activity or unlicensed infrastructure operation. Binds Licensed Financial Institutions, Authorized Individuals and any person carrying on or promoting financial activity without a licence.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
