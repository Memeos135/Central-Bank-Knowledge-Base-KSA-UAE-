# Business Continuity Planning (Article 11)

Article 11 of the CBUAE Operational Risk and Operational Resilience Regulation requires LFIs to maintain Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plans for their Critical Operations, integrated with the operational risk and resilience framework and built on the mapping of critical operations and assets. It mandates disruption scenario analysis with quantitative and qualitative business impact assessment, clear activation triggers, Board-approved recovery time and recovery point objectives, stakeholder communication arrangements, and at least annual review and testing for Critical Operations (including key Third-Party Service Providers), with results reported to the Board and Senior Management. Second and third lines of defence must review the plans; the obligation binds all CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.
