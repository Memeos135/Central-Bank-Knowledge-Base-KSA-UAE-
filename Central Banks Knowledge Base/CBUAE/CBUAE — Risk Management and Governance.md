# Risk Management and Governance

Governance and risk-management provisions of the CBUAE Large Exposures regulation (Article 17 and related credit risk mitigation articles), requiring banks to identify and aggregate concentration risk, set risk-appetite thresholds, report material concentrations to the board, stress test concentrations and cover them in ICAAP and ILAAP. It also fixes senior management duties to monitor limits and escalate breaches immediately without board pre-approval, and sets out how eligible credit risk mitigation reduces the original exposure while creating a recognised exposure to the protection provider. Binds CBUAE-licensed banks and their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
