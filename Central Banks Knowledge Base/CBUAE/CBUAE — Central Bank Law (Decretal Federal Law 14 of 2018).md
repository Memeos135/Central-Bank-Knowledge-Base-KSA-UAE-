# Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law 14 of 2018)

Node for the UAE Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities, as amended), the enabling statute under which CBUAE issues licensing and supervisory regulations. In the cited context it supplies the legal basis and defined terms (e.g. "Bank" as a juridical person licensed primarily to take deposits and carry on other Licensed Financial Activities) for the Specialized Banks with Low Risk framework. It binds all persons carrying on licensed financial activities in the UAE and grounds CBUAE's powers to license, supervise and sanction.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
