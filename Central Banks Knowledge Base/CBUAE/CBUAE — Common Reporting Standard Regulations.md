# Common Reporting Standard Regulations

UAE Insurance Authority regulations implementing the Common Reporting Standard for automatic exchange of financial account information. They set out general provisions, the persons bound (Reporting Financial Institutions under the Authority's supervision, Account Holders/policyholders of Reportable Accounts, and other covered persons), the Authority's inspection and information-gathering powers, and due diligence record-keeping with a five-year retention period after reporting. Binds insurance-sector Reporting Financial Institutions and, for information requests, their Account Holders.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, other
