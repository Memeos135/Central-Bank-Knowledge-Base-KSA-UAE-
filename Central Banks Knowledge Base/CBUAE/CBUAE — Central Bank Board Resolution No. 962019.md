# Central Bank Board Resolution No. 96/2019

An amending instrument of the CBUAE Board of Directors that modifies the Regulations Regarding Mortgage Loans (C 31/2013); it is referenced as one of two amendments folded into the consolidated text dated 8 April 2020. It is not a free-standing regime but changes provisions on mortgage lending, and binds mortgage loan providers (banks, finance companies and other financial institutions offering mortgage loans). Open it to confirm which articles (e.g. risk management, LTV, income verification) were altered and the effective date of the change.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
