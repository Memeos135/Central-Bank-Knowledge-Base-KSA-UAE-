# Insurance Dispute Resolution Committee

Defines the Insurance Dispute Resolution Committee as the appeal body for Complaints against Insurance Companies, mirroring the Appeals Committee's role for Licensed Financial Institutions, with the same 30-business-day appeal window and referral procedure. It sets out delivery of the Determination to both parties, the institution's duty to comply with any direction or recommendation within the specified or extended period and to notify compliance in writing within 14 business days, and the escalation of non-compliance to the Central Bank. It binds Insurance Companies, the Committee and the Ombudsman Unit.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
