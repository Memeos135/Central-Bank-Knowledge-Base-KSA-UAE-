# Business Continuity Management

## Why this community

Business continuity and operational resilience obligations — maintaining critical financial services through disruption, with continuity planning and testing as a supervisory expectation.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone rulebook chapter that sets the continuity obligation without dense internal cross-referencing in this graph.
- Reads naturally against the adjacent risk management, general security and information security chapters (community 22), which supply the risk-assessment and incident foundations continuity planning depends on.
