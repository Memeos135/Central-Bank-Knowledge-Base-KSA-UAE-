# Group of Connected Counterparties

Defines when two or more counterparties must be aggregated as a Group of Connected Counterparties — through a control relationship (direct or indirect) or economic interdependence (financial distress in one likely causing funding/repayment difficulty in the other) — and requires banks to assess relationships against prescribed criteria. The node also links to the stricter related-party regime, including individual and aggregate caps on exposures to 5%+ shareholders and their connected groups, plus board approval, immediate board notification where an exposure becomes large, and enhanced monitoring across credit, legal, compliance, market and interest rate risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
