# Market Risk (Article 12)

Node for the market risk article of the CBUAE Standard on risk management for Islamic banks, which supplements the Risk Management Regulation (Circular 153/2018). Market risk is one of the material risk categories that an Islamic bank's Shari'ah-compliant risk governance framework must address, alongside credit, liquidity, operational, displaced commercial, equity investment and rate of return risk. It binds licensed Islamic banks and conventional banks housing an Islamic Window (the latter in respect of their Shari'ah-compliant business), with board and Risk Committee accountability for the framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
