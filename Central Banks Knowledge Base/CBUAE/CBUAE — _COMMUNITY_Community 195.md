# Human Resources

## Why this community

Human resources and staffing requirements within an institution's governance framework - fit and proper staffing, training and internal accountability as a control function input.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster with no internal cross-references; it is an isolated chapter node rather than a linked obligation chain.
- Its regulatory weight derives from the parent manual or rulebook it sits in, so establish that parent before relying on it.
- Practically it interacts with AML/CFT training and compliance-officer competence duties and with governance/fit-and-proper regimes, though those links are not captured in this graph.
