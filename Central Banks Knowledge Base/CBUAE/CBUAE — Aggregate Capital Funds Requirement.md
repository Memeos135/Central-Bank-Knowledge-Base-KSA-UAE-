# Aggregate Capital Funds Requirement

Capital/financial resources node under the CBUAE SVF Regulation requiring a Licensee to hold Aggregate Capital Funds of at least 5% of total Float received from Customers, alongside a minimum capital amount in approved currency. It defines the composition of Aggregate Capital Funds (paid-up capital, reserves excluding revaluation reserves, retained earnings) and the deductions (accumulated losses, goodwill), and requires demonstration of sufficient resources for sustainable operation, disclosure of source of funds, and capacity for an orderly wind-down. Binds SVF applicants and Licensees.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, governance/risk
