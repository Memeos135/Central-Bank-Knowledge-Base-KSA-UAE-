# Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018 (Central Bank Law)

This node is the UAE Central Bank Law (Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 on the Central Bank and Organization of Financial Institutions and Activities), cited here as the enabling authority under which the Central Bank issues binding regulations and standards — in this context the Standard on Liquidity at Islamic Banks forming part of the Regulations re Liquidity at Banks. It supplies the legal basis for supervisory expectations to be legally enforceable, and also underpins defined terms such as Bank and Confidential Information (disclosure restrictions under Article 120). It binds licensed financial institutions generally, including licensed banks conducting all or part of their activities in accordance with Islamic Shari'ah.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
