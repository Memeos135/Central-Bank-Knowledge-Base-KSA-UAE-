# Vasicek Credit Framework

A section of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance addressing use of the Vasicek one-factor credit model (and derivations) to build point-in-time PD term structures for ECL. It warns that the model was designed for portfolio-level economic capital and tail losses rather than obligor-level expected loss, that asset correlation to a non-observable systemic factor is hard to calibrate and highly influential, and that combining it with macroeconomic regressions raises further identification issues. Directed at institutions choosing and justifying ECL modelling methodologies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
