# Qard Hasan

Covers the Shari'ah-based interest-free loan from shareholders' funds used to remedy a deficit in the Participants' Risk Fund under the CBUAE takaful financial regulations. It requires the entire shareholders' funds to be available for this purpose, and assets earmarked for qard hasan to be separately accounted for and valued under the asset-valuation rules for solvency. Relevant to takaful operators, their boards and actuaries assessing solvency capital adequacy.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
