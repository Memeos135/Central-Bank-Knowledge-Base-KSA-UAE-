# Federal Decree Law No.20 of 2018 (AML-CFT Law)

Reference node for Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organizations, as amended by Federal Decree-Law No. 26 of 2021 (the "AML-CFT Law"). It is the primary statutory foundation for the CBUAE best-practices documents on training and on risk-based institutional risk assessment, and training must make employees aware of these legal obligations and the consequences of non-compliance. It binds all UAE natural and legal persons subject to the AML/CFT regime, including CBUAE-licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
