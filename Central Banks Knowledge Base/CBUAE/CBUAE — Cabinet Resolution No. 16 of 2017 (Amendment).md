# Cabinet Resolution No. 16 of 2017 (Amendment)

Amending instrument node: Cabinet Resolution No. 16 of 2017, which amended the 2009 insurance minimum capital regulations and produced the consolidated text effective 15/05/2017. Its visible effect in the consolidated version is on the shareholder/ownership provision, which is delegated to a Board of Directors resolution setting the conditions for natural and juridical persons to hold shares in an insurance company. Use it to establish the date and scope of change when comparing pre- and post-2017 obligations.
