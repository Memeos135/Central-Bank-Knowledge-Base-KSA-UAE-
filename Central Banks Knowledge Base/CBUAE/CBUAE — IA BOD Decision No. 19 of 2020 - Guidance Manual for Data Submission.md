# IA BOD Decision No. 19 of 2020 - Guidance Manual for Data Submission

Insurance Authority Board Decision No. 19 of 2020 adopting the Guidance Manual (and attached forms) for insurance companies and insurance-related professions on submitting data, information and supervisory reports. It applies to UAE-established insurers, branches and agents of foreign insurers, and related professions, requiring submission within periods set by the Director General; the manual and forms are an integral part of the decision. Its recitals tie the reporting framework to the federal AML/CTF decree-law and its executive regulations and to the terrorist-lists/UN sanctions regulations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, other
