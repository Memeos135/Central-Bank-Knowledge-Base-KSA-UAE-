# Article 21: Liability for Unauthorised Transactions

Article 21 of the CBUAE Open Finance Regulation allocates liability to Users for unauthorised access to or loss of User Data, and for non-execution, late or defective execution of initiated Transactions. Open Finance Providers bear data-breach liability; Service Initiation Providers bear execution liability including failures of authorisation, authentication, accurate recording or secure communication, and carry the burden of proving correct processing in a dispute; Service Owners are liable for execution failures subject to carve-outs for acts or omissions of others. Related provisions expose Open Finance Providers to Central Bank administrative and financial sanctions for security breaches affecting Personal Data.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
