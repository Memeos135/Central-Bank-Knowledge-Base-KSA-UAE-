# Risk Management Principles (Nov 2023)

Refers to the SFWG's Principles for the Effective Management of Climate-related Financial Risks, issued November 2023, which address board and senior-management oversight and allocation of responsibility for climate-related financial risks, their integration into strategy-setting, risk management frameworks, capital and liquidity planning, and scenario analysis. It is the risk-management companion instrument that the Climate Transition Planning Principles must be read with. It applies to UAE financial firms as supervisory guidance on climate risk.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
