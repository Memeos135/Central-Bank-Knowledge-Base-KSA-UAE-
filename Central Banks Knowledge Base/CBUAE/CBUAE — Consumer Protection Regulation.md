# Consumer Protection Regulation

The CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation (Circular 8/2020), issued under Article 121 of Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018, establishing the principles-based conduct framework for the protection of Consumers of financial products and services, supported by detailed Standards. It covers disclosure and transparency, institutional oversight, market and business conduct, protection of consumer data and assets, responsible financing, complaints handling, consumer education, financial inclusion, and Shari'ah compliance of financial services. Binds all Licensed Financial Institutions, including those operating wholly or partly in compliance with Islamic Shari'ah, and their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info
