# Regulation re Liquidity at Banks

Cross-reference node for the CBUAE Regulation re Liquidity at Banks, the parent instrument of which the Islamic Banks liquidity Standard forms part and with which it must be read. It sets the baseline qualitative, quantitative and reporting liquidity obligations for licensed banks, including banks housing an Islamic Window, which comply with this Regulation rather than the Islamic ratios. Useful for identifying the general liquidity requirements that the Islamic Standard supplements or adapts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
