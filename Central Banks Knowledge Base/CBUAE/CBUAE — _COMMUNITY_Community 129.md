# Motor Insurance Tariffs

## Why this community

Mandatory motor insurance pricing: the regulatory framework setting permitted tariffs for compulsory third-party liability and optional loss-and-damage cover, tied to the unified motor policy wordings.

## How members connect

- The tariffs regulation is the parent instrument; the two tariff tables are annexed schedules it incorporates by reference and are enforceable as part of it.
- The regulation cross-refers to the resolution approving the unified motor policies, linking permitted pricing to the standard coverage terms being priced.
- Split by product line: one table governs compulsory third-party liability rating, the other governs own-damage (comprehensive) rating, so the applicable table depends on the policy type issued.
