# Product-Level Disclosures

## Why this community

Disclosure obligations at the level of individual financial products rather than the entity, covering how a product's sustainability characteristics must be presented.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in this graph; its legal meaning derives from the wider principles set, where entity-level disclosure is addressed separately.
- Read together with the disclosure-factor criteria, which determine what product information is disclosure-relevant.
- Distinguish the addressee: product-level duties bite on offering/marketing of a product, engaging consumer-protection style mis-selling concerns, not only periodic reporting.
