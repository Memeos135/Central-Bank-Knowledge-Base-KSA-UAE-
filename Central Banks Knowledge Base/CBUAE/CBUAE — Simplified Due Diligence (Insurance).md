# Simplified Due Diligence (Insurance)

A node within CBUAE's AML/CFT guidance for the insurance sector (UAE instrument, persuasive comparator only for KSA work) dealing with simplified due diligence in insurance. The surrounding material scopes the sector — conventional and Takaful insurers, reinsurers, tied agents, brokers and third-party administrators — and limits AML/CFT application to life and other investment-related insurance products, general insurance falling outside the UAE AML-CFT Decision. Any SDD relief must therefore be read against that product scope and against the operator's own ML/FT risk assessment; the extract supplied here is sector definition, so the SDD conditions themselves must be checked in the source.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
