# Significant Shareholder

Concept node on the Significant Shareholder under the CBUAE Transfer of Significant Shareholding Regulation, read with Article 8 (duty to report). Banks must immediately notify the CBUAE of any change or proposed change in a Significant or Controlling Shareholder/Shareholding and of material information affecting their suitability, collect required information from such shareholders annually, and look through nominees to beneficial shareholders; fit-and-proper status is a continuing requirement and approval may be revoked. Non-compliant shareholders must divest excess shareholding above 5% and lose related voting rights and dividends.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
