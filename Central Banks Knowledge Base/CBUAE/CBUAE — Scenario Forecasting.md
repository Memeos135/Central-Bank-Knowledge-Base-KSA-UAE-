# Scenario Forecasting

Section 5.11 of the CBUAE Model Management Guidance sets expected practice for macroeconomic scenario forecasting used in regular expected credit loss (ECL) estimation, requiring unbiased, probability-weighted, forward-looking forecasts extending to the longest portfolio maturity. It addresses governance of internally built versus third-party scenarios (review by the Model Oversight Committee and the ECL oversight committee, internal validation of vendor scenarios), consensus-based and economically coherent base/upside/downside narratives, use of the most recent PD and LGD models, and encouraged disclosure of scenario values and weights over three years. It also draws a boundary: ICAAP, recovery and resolution, and regulatory stress-testing scenarios (including CBUAE-supplied macro scenarios) may serve only as distant comparables, not as ECL inputs. Binds institutions supervised by the CBUAE that compute ECL.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
