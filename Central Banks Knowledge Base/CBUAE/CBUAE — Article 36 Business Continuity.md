# Article 36: Business Continuity

Article 36 of the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation sets operational resilience duties for Payment Token Service Providers, requiring a business continuity management programme built on business impact analysis, documented and tested recovery strategies, and a business continuity plan covering recovery procedures, escalation and crisis management (including timely Central Bank reporting), customer and media communication, key-personnel contacts and primary/alternate recovery roles. It also mandates protection and timely restoration of business and Customer records, Customer access to their records, notification of record loss through operational failure or theft, at least annual testing with Senior Management participation, formal test documentation and a post-mortem signed off by Senior Management. Separately it requires a viable business exit plan for an orderly wind-down so failure or exit does not disrupt Customers or UAE payment systems.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
