# Legal Person

This node is the defined term "legal person" — any non-natural entity that can establish a customer relationship or own property (companies, bodies corporate, foundations, partnerships, associations) — and the CBUAE guidance on the ML/TF/PF risks such customers present through opaque ownership structures. It sets out the risk factors LFIs must weigh (legal form, formation controls, beneficial ownership reporting, recordkeeping, supervision) and the mandatory customer identification data set, plus identification of controlling owners and of the natural person authorised to act for the entity. It binds LFIs applying a risk-based approach and CDD to corporate customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
