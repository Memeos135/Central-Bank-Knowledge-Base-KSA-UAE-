# Federal Decree Law No. 20 of 2018 (AML-CFT Law)

Federal Decree Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Illegal Organisations is the UAE's primary AML/CFT statute, cited as the legal basis with which a Licensed Person's AML/CFT Program must align, together with its amendment by Federal Decree Law No. 26 of 2021. Within this node's context it functions as the governing law referenced by the Standards for Exchange Business rather than as text reproduced in them. It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs in the UAE, including licensed exchange houses supervised by the CBUAE.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
