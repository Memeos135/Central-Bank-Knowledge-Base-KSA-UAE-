# IOSCO

Reference concept: the International Organization of Securities Commissions, whose Technical Committee jointly with CPMI issued the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures. Within the Regulation, IOSCO appears only as a source of the PFMI standards that designated LVPS are required to comply with; the Regulation also identifies the Securities & Commodities Authority alongside the Central Bank as the domestic Regulatory Authorities.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
