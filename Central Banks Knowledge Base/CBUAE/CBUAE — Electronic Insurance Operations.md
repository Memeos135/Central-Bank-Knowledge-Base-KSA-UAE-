# Electronic Insurance Operations

The defined term and scope concept of "Electronic Insurance Operations" – business carried out by an insurance company through electronic and smart systems – together with the related definitions and the Article (2) scope and Article (3) approval conditions. It determines which activities trigger the regulations and the prior Authority approval with a board-approved action plan covering projected volumes and risk mitigation. It binds licensed insurance companies and insurance-related professions conducting business electronically.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, AML/CTF
