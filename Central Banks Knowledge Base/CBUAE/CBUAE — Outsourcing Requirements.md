# Outsourcing Requirements

Node covering the outsourcing provisions of the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities (SVF) Regulation, permitting a Licensee to outsource activities and processes to third parties or intra-group companies only with Central Bank approval. It fixes the Licensee as ultimately accountable for the adequacy, service levels, quality, security and continuity of outsourced activities and for the integrity and protection of information held by providers, and requires a comprehensive independent risk assessment plus due diligence on the provider's financial soundness, reputation, management, technical/operational capacity and regulatory capability before outsourcing. It binds SVF Licensees (and is relevant to controlling shareholder / fit-and-proper assessment sitting alongside it in the same chapter).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
