# Stored Value Facilities Regulation

Instrument reference to the CBUAE Stored Value Facilities Regulation (Circular No. 6/2020, Notice 4834/2020), cited in the payment-sector AML/CFT guidance as the licensing framework for SVF providers. Its Article 14 is flagged as requiring all SVF licensees to meet CBUAE AML/CFT legal and regulatory obligations and to mitigate ML/FT risk through preventive measures. Binds SVF licensees (prepaid card and e-wallet issuers) as a subset of licensed financial institutions.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk
