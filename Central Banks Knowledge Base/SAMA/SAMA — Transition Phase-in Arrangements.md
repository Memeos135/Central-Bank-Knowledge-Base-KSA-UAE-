# Transition Phase-in Arrangements

A node on transitional and phase-in arrangements in SAMA's capital framework, covering capital instruments subject to phase-out (caps on CET1, AT1 and Tier 2 legacy instruments and amounts excluded), caps on provisions included in Tier 2, and the linear phase-in for the removal of the IRB approach for equity exposures. It also explains how banks must disclose exposures during transitional periods. Applies to banks completing the composition-of-capital and credit risk disclosure templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
