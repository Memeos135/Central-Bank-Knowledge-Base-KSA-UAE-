# BCM Testing

A component concept within SAMA's Business Continuity Management (BCM) Framework, which sets mandatory business continuity requirements (governance, strategy, plans and their exercising/testing) for Member Organizations. The surrounding framework defines BCM, IT Disaster Recovery and Maximum Acceptable Outage, and fixes board-level accountability, a mandated BCM committee, a BCM function and qualified BCM manager. It binds all SAMA-supervised Member Organizations, including banks operating in Saudi Arabia, their banking subsidiaries and subsidiaries of foreign banks in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
