# IT Incident Management

Provision of the SAMA IT Governance Framework requiring an IT incident management process to identify, respond to and handle incidents affecting information assets, and to report relevant incidents to SAMA under a defined communication protocol. It specifies a designated incident team, communication and notification plans, trained staff, prioritisation and classification, evidence and log protection, root-cause analysis, lessons learned, logging of user requests and incidents, and periodic effectiveness evaluation. Binds Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
