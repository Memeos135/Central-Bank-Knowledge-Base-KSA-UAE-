# Object Finance (OF)

Defines object finance (OF) as funding the acquisition of equipment (e.g. ships, aircraft, satellites, railcars, fleets) where repayment depends on cash flows from the specific financed assets pledged or assigned to the lender. Where no issue-specific external rating is available, OF exposures are risk-weighted at 100% under the standardized approach. Binds banks classifying and risk-weighting specialized lending exposures under SAMA's credit risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
