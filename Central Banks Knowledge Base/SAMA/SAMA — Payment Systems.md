# Payment Systems

Framework sub-domain requiring Member Organizations to define, approve, implement and monitor a cyber security standard for payment systems, with periodic effectiveness measurement, aimed at protecting the confidentiality and integrity of shared banking systems. Rather than setting detailed controls, it cross-refers to the SARIE Information Security Policy and the mada Rules and Standards Technical Book (security framework, HSM requirements, SAMA CA IPK certificate procedures). Binds Member Organizations participating in these shared payment infrastructures.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
