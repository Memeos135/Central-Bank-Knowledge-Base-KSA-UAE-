# IMA Capital Requirement Aggregation

Concept node for how the components of the market risk internal models approach are aggregated into a total capital requirement — combining the modelled ES-based charge, the non-modellable risk factor add-on, default risk capital, any amber-zone trading desk surcharge, and the standardised approach charge for desks ineligible for the IMA. The surrounding source is SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework, including the fixed-format templates and capital distribution constraint disclosures. Relevant to banks calculating and disclosing market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
