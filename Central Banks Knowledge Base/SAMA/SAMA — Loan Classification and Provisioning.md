# Loan Classification and Provisioning

Sets the DTFC loan classification and provisioning regime: at least quarterly review and classification of the loan portfolio per Appendix C, contagion treatment where one loan to a borrower is non-performing, rules for renegotiated/restructured and twice-restructured facilities, suspension and reversal of profit on non-performing exposures, return to accrual only on full settlement, and minimum provisioning percentages for Normal, Watch, Substandard, Doubtful and Loss grades, including where IFRS impairment falls short. Binds every deposit-taking finance company.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
