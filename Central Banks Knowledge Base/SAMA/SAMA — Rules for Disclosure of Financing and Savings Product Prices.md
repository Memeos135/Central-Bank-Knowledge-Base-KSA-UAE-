# Rules for Disclosure of Financing and Savings Product Prices

Updated SAMA rules on disclosure of pricing for financing and savings products, replacing the earlier circulars on the same subject. They require standardised disclosure of APR/AER and related terms (finance amount, maturity, monthly instalment, credit card purchase rate and minimum payment, LTV, early repayment charges, deposit amount) using prescribed disclosure tables for individuals and for small and micro enterprises, to enable price comparison. They bind all banks and finance companies licensed and supervised by SAMA, with a short implementation window and a confirmation-of-compliance obligation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
