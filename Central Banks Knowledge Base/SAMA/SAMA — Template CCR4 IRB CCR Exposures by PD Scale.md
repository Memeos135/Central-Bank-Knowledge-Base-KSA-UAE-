# Template CCR4: IRB CCR Exposures by PD Scale

Template CCR4 is a Pillar 3 disclosure requiring banks that use F-IRB or A-IRB approaches for counterparty credit risk to disclose CCR exposures and model parameters (EAD post-CRM, average PD, obligor numbers, average maturity, RWA) by portfolio and PD band. Separate templates must be produced where both F-IRB and A-IRB are used, with narrative on model scope and RWA coverage; CVA charges and CCP-cleared exposures are excluded. Mandatory semiannually for IRB banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
