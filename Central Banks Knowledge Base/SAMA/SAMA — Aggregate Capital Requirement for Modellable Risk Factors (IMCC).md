# Aggregate Capital Requirement for Modellable Risk Factors (IMCC)

Concept node on the aggregate capital requirement for modellable risk factors (IMCC) within the market risk internal models approach, situated alongside rules on the risk factor eligibility test (RFET), non-modellable risk factors (NMRF), and principles SAMA applies when judging data quality and modellability. The same instrument confirms that all banks, D-SIB and non-D-SIB, must calculate market risk capital under the standardised approach, and addresses recognition of internal CVA risk transfers. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
