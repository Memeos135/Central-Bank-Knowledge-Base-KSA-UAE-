# Real Estate Finance Debt Transfer Timelines

A circular reaffirming SAMA's rules on the time limits for issuing clearance (release) letters and transferring accounts and indebtedness, as applied to real estate finance, and confirming that mortgage contracts between real estate financiers and individual customers fall under SAMA's finance laws and instructions. Real estate financiers must execute customer requests to transfer Murabaha-based real estate finance debt within the prescribed timelines, comply with the rules on purchase and transfer of real estate finance debt between financiers, exclude from the timelines business days linked to mortgage release procedures where the delay is caused by an external party, and update internal policies accordingly. Binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
