# Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority (SAMA)

A node identifying the Saudi Central Bank (formerly Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority) as issuer and supervisor of the Cyber Security Framework (v1.0, May 2017), including its IT Risk Supervision function under the Deputyship of Supervision. It covers SAMA's role in receiving framework update and waiver requests from member organizations (submitted to the Deputy Governor, with CISO and cyber security committee approval) and in expecting board and senior management oversight of implementation. Relevant to banks, insurance and financing companies regulated by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
