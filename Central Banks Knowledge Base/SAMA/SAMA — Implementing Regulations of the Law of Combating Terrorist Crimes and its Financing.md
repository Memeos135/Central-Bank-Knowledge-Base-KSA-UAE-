# Implementing Regulations of the Law of Combating Terrorist Crimes and its Financing

Implementing Regulations to the Law of Combating Terrorist Crimes and its Financing, which flesh out the Law's definitions and operational duties — notably the list of financial activities and of commercial/professional (DNFBP) activities that bring a person within scope, and the minimum content of CTF policies, procedures and rules (risk management before completing customer verification, STR reporting, a senior-level compliance officer, employee screening and training, independent audit, and control of foreign subsidiaries). It binds financial institutions, designated non-financial businesses and professions, and non-profit organisations, with supervisory authorities empowered to add measures. Also imposes group-wide application where a host country's CTF standards are weaker.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
