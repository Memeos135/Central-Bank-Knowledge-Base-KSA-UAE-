# Template CCR8: Exposures to Central Counterparties

Template CCR8 requires banks to disclose all exposures to central counterparties — trade exposures, initial margin (segregated and non-segregated) and pre-funded/unfunded default fund contributions — with EAD post-CRM and RWA, split between qualifying CCPs and non-qualifying CCPs. It is mandatory for all banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, semiannual, fixed format, with narrative on material changes. The extracted context also includes Table CRB-A on prudential treatment of problem assets (non-performing and forborne exposure definitions).
