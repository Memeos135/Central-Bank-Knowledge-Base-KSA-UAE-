# Circular: Accounting Standards Used by External Auditors

Short SAMA Banking Supervision circular to all money exchange businesses in the Kingdom, grounded in the requirement (Article 18 of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business) to appoint a SAMA-accepted licensed external auditor for annual audit. It directs each exchanger to obtain and report to SAMA the accounting standards its external auditor applies when auditing the financial statements. It binds licensed money exchange companies and establishments.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
