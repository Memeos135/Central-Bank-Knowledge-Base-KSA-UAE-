# Remuneration Compliance Report

Reporting and supervisory-review provisions of SAMA's bank remuneration rules, requiring banks to file a semiannual Compliance Report assessing alignment with the rules, with gap analysis and action plans, plus remuneration breakdowns and data on the top 12 highest-paid employees. It sets filing deadlines (before 31 March and 31 August), submission of the annual independent remuneration review results, and SAMA's powers to require rectification, additional capital/liquidity, penalties or limits on total remuneration. Binds banks, their boards and Nomination and Remuneration Committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
