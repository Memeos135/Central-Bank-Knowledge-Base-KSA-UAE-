# Profit Sharing Investment Accounts Rules

SAMA rules governing Profit Sharing Investment Accounts (PSIA) offered by banks and Islamic windows, defining PSIA, restricted vs unrestricted accounts, Investment Account Holders (IAH), and reserves (PER, IRR). They cover how PSIA funding may be raised via Mudarabah and Wakalah contracts, loss-bearing by IAH absent bank negligence/misconduct/fraud/breach, product awareness disclosures, and product governance including contract terms and conditions. Binds banks in KSA operating Shari'ah-compliant investment accounts and their Shari'ah Committees.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection
