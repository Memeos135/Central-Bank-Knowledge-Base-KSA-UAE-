# Cash Transport Compliance

## Why this community

Operational and conduct obligations around physical cash movement: licensed transport of cash and valuables, and the duty to warn customers of the risks of carrying cash themselves.

## How members connect

- Both circulars sit under the cash transport regulatory framework and address the same risk (loss, theft, AML exposure from untraced cash movement) from two angles: institutional compliance and customer-facing awareness.
- Obligation chain: the transport regulations bind institutions to use licensed arrangements, while the awareness circular pushes a disclosure/advisory duty onto the customer relationship.
- Cross-regime interaction with AML/CTF — large physical cash handling is a recognised higher-risk channel and feeds CDD and reporting expectations.
