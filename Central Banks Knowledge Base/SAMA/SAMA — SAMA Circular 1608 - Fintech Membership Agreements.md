# SAMA Circular 1608 - Fintech Membership Agreements

A SAMA circular easing the prior requirement (under an earlier circular) for written SAMA no-objection before credit bureaus conclude membership agreements with non-SAMA-supervised entities. Credit information companies may now sign membership agreements with fintech firms that hold or have applied for a SAMA or CMA licence/permit, subject to conditions: verifying the licence application before signing, the fintech's activity involving a credit relationship with consumers, no activation of access to consumer credit records until a copy of the licence is obtained, and, on cessation or expiry of the licence, limiting the firm to updating records of its own customers without new enquiries. Responsibility for compliance rests entirely with the credit information companies, which remain bound by the Credit Information Law and its implementing regulation.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, payments, governance/risk
