# Business Viability Analysis

Node on the qualitative counterpart to financial analysis: assessing the borrower's business model, business plan and medium-to-long-term projections, distinguishing sustainable from unsustainable debt, and checking alignment with existing credit terms. It also addresses validation of assumptions and sensitivity analysis, review of documentation features such as cross-default and notification obligations, the option to appoint a financial advisor for an independent business review, and verification that collateral remains formalised, valid and enforceable. Guides bank Workout Units in identifying commercially sustainable restructuring options.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
