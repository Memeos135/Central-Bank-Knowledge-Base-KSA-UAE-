# KYC Verification for POS Sales

SAMA circular to payment service providers reaffirming that effective customer verification and Know Your Customer procedures must be completed before selling or activating POS terminals, and that any third party marketing or promoting such devices must itself be SAMA-licensed (marketing/promotion of payment services being a licensable activity under the PSP Rules). It also requires PSPs to ensure internal policies and procedures cover all customer (merchant) categories, identify and monitor risks with reporting, and to verify that the POS terminal is linked only to the merchant customer's own bank account and not diverted to another account.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF
