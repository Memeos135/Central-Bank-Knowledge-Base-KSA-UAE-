# Complaints Handling

Covers Principle 7 and the associated rules requiring financial institutions to operate a clear, effective complaints mechanism and to resolve complaints fairly and without delay. Institutions must offer multiple submission channels (toll-free number, branches/website, mobile app, email), publish the handling mechanism, acknowledge complaints with a reference number and stated handling period by SMS, identify the responsible department, and retain communication records for at least five years. Also touches consumer education duties on debt, default and fraud.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
