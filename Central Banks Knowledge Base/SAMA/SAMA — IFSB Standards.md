# IFSB Standards

A referenced external standard-setting body (Islamic Financial Services Board) whose standards SAMA cites as the basis for the 2022 update of the Related Parties Rules for Banks; the covering circular states the updated rules were aligned to IFSB standards and supersede the earlier version. IFSB standards are not directly binding in themselves here — the binding instrument is SAMA's Related Parties Rules, which apply to all local banks. Relevant when tracing the international-standards provenance of related-party definitions, control tests and exposure limits.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
