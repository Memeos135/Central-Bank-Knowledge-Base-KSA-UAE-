# Systemically Important Payment Systems Standards

SAMA standards (1436H) setting out the criteria for designating payment systems as systemically important in the Kingdom, on the basis that SAMA is the owner, operator and sole regulator of such systems and is responsible for their safety and soundness. A system is systemically important if it handles interbank or customer payments posing financial-stability risk, is the principal system by daily volume and value, is used to settle another systemically important financial market infrastructure, or is a central payments infrastructure with no substitutes. It designates SARIE, the Saudi Payments Network (SPAN) and SADAD as systemically important.
