# Late Payment and Default Provisions

The late payment and default provisions (Article 8, with related termination steps in Article 9 and breach provisions) of SAMA's mandatory standard consumer finance contract for individuals. They set the consequences of delayed or defaulted instalments and the financier's procedure for exercising termination/enforcement, with the contract summary requiring these effects to be highlighted to the beneficiary. They bind banks and finance companies as financiers and determine the consumer's exposure on arrears.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
