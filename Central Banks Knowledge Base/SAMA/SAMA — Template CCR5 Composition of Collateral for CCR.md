# Template CCR5: Composition of Collateral for CCR

Template CCR5 requires disclosure of the composition of collateral posted and received in support of counterparty credit risk exposures arising from derivatives and securities financing transactions, including CCP-cleared trades, split by collateral type and segregated/unsegregated status. Fair values of both legs of a transaction must be reported. Mandatory for all banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, on a semiannual basis with narrative explanation of material changes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
