# Other Physical Collateral

A node covering non-financial/"other" collateral within SAMA's credit risk mitigation and Pillar 3 disclosure material, including the CCR5 template breakdown of collateral posted and received for derivative and securities financing transaction exposures, and the CR3 illustrations allocating secured exposures between collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives. It sets what collateral categories must be reported at fair value or carrying value and how multi-secured exposures are split. It binds all banks subject to SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk and the Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
