# Internal Risk Transfer

Defines internal risk transfer as an internal written record of a risk transfer within the banking book, between banking and trading book, or between trading desks, and sets the conditions for capital recognition. Key requirements include exact matching between the internal transfer and an external hedge, use of a SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk for general interest rate risk, and the exclusion of trading-to-banking-book transfers from capital recognition. Binds banks applying SAMA's market risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
