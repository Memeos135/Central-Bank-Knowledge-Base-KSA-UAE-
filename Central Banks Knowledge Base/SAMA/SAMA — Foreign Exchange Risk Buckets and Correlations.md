# Foreign Exchange Risk Buckets and Correlations

A segment of SAMA's market risk capital rules addressing the FX risk class under the sensitivities-based standardised approach: identification of FX risk factors (currency pairs against the reporting currency), bucket structure, risk weights and correlations, alongside the simplified standardised approach treatment of FX risk. Binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating market risk capital requirements, including FX risk arising in the banking book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
