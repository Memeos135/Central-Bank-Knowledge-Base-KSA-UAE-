# Article 23: Rules for Dispute Resolution

Article 23 (Section Seven) of SAMA's consumer financing rules defines "account statement error/dispute" and sets the Creditor's handling duties: investigate and apply the disclosed dispute procedures within 60 calendar days and in no case beyond 90, correct confirmed errors and refund the disputed amount with related Term Cost and charges, and notify the Borrower by Guaranteed Communication. Pending satisfactory resolution the Borrower need not pay the disputed portion and the Creditor may not pursue collection, nor make or threaten an improper credit-standing report or delinquency report based on non-payment of the disputed amount. Binds Creditors (banks/finance companies) toward individual Borrowers under Consumer Financing.
