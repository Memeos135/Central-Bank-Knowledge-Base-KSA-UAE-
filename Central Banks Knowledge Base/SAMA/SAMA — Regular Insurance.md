# Regular Insurance

A concept node on conventional (regular) insurance as an external risk financing mechanism for bank operational risk, contrasted with self-insurance and alternative vehicles such as retention groups, captives and pools. It addresses insurer/reinsurer selection, broker relationships and remuneration, coverage enhancement and multi-year placements as matters of prudent management judgement. Addressed to Saudi banks' senior management in framing an integrated operational risk financing programme.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
