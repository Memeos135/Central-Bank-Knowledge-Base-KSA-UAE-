# Amendments to International Accounting Standards

A SAMA circular to finance companies transmitting SOCPA-endorsed amendments and annual improvements issued by the IASB to international accounting and financial reporting standards, and directing compliance from the date the instructions were emailed. The covered changes touch IFRS 1 (first-time adoption — cumulative translation differences for subsidiaries), IFRS 9 (fees in the 10% derecognition test for modified/exchanged debt), IAS 41 (cash flows in fair value measurement), IAS 37 (cost of fulfilling an onerous contract), IAS 16 (proceeds before intended use), and IFRS 3 (business combinations). It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and points them to SOCPA's international standards page for the texts.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
