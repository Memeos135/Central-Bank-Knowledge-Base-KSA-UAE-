# Circular on Outsourcing Debt Collection to Licensed Entities

SAMA circular revising the framework for outsourcing collection of bank and finance company debts. It cancels the earlier circulars that had confined such outsourcing to debt collection entities licensed by SAMA and the related transitional deadlines, and instead permits contracting with entities meeting the requirements set out in the applicable laws, regulations and instructions, subject to obtaining SAMA's no-objection letter and without prejudice to the outsourcing rules and other relevant instructions. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom and SAMA-licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
