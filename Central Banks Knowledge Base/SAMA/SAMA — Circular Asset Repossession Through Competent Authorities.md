# Circular: Asset Repossession Through Competent Authorities

SAMA circular confirming that repossession of movable assets from a lessee, where the contract gives the lessor a repossession right, may occur only through the competent authorities, based on Article 25(3) of the Finance Lease Law and Ministry of Interior instructions on vehicle repossession. Until the Ministry of Justice licenses specialised enforcement companies, financiers must follow the Ministry of Interior instructions; staff who repossess without recourse to the competent authorities face legal liability. It binds banks and finance companies licensed for finance leasing activity.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
