# Instructions on Purchase of Consumer Finance Debts Between Finance Companies

SAMA instructions (Arabic, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law) regulating the purchase/transfer of individual consumer finance debts between finance companies, i.e. debt buyout or refinancing of a customer's existing exposure by another lender. They bind both the acquiring finance company (which must hold a consumer finance licence, disclose new finance terms and costs and obtain documented customer consent, use a dedicated application form, settle the outstanding debt through the SARIE system with prescribed payment data, and withhold surplus funds until a clearance letter is received) and the selling finance company (which must issue liability statements and debt confirmation letters, effect transfer, and notify settlement within one business day). No separate SAMA approval is required for each transaction, but consumer protection instructions and limits on fees (only early settlement or administrative fees) apply.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, payments
