# Acceptance of Family Record from Absher and Tawakkalna

A circular extending SAMA's earlier acceptance of the Absher/Tawakkalna digital identity to the electronic Family Record (سجل الأسرة) document for citizens, viewable via QR code. It obliges financial institutions to accept the digital Family Record in processes that do not require taking a copy of the document, with the same rules and provisions applying as to printed copies. Binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, consumer protection, other
