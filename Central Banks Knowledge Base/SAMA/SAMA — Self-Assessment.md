# Self-Assessment

"Self-Assessment" is a defined term in SAMA's Oversight Framework for Payment Systems and their Operators: an assessment carried out by the payment system operator itself to determine its degree of compliance with the PFMIs. It is one of the supervisory tools set out in the framework's supervisory methodology, alongside supervisory assessment. It applies to operators of payment systems licensed by SAMA.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
