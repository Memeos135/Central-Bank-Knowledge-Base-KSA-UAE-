# Template OV1 - Overview of RWA

Template OV1 is the Pillar 3 overview of risk-weighted assets, breaking RWA down by risk type (credit risk, counterparty credit risk, CVA, securitisation, market risk, operational risk) and by whether modelled or standardised approaches are used. It carries the row definitions and instructions that also drive the comparison template CMS1, including treatment of equity investments in funds, settlement risk, trading/banking book switches and amounts below deduction thresholds. It applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
