# National Drug Prevention Project (Nebras) Circular

A SAMA circular (Arabic) from the General Department for Supervision of Finance Companies transmitting a Ministry of Finance letter and a Crown Prince/National Committee for Drug Control cable on the launch of the National Drug Prevention Project (Nebras). It asks supervised entities to coordinate with the General Secretariat of the National Committee for Drug Control and to place any similar preventive programmes under the Nebras banner to unify and integrate efforts. Addressed to banks, insurance companies, finance companies and SIMAH; it is a coordination/branding instruction rather than a prudential or AML obligation.

**Regimes:** other
