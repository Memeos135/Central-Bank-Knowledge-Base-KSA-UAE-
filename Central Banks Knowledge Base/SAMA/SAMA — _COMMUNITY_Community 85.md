# Standardized Credit Risk Mitigation

## Why this community

Standardized approach mechanics for credit risk: how banks may use external ratings to risk-weight exposures (notably corporates) and the conditions for recognising credit risk mitigation such as on-balance sheet netting.

## How members connect

- Shared gatekeeping definition: eligible ECAIs and the supervisory mapping of their assessments to risk weights condition every rating-based determination in the cluster.
- Rule chain on rating use: eligibility feeds the multiple-ratings rule, the issue-specific vs issuer rating rule, and the short-term/long-term rating interaction, which then drive the corporate exposure risk weights.
- CRM provisions cross-refer to the same rating rules when determining the treatment of collateral, guarantees and protection providers, and set operational conditions (legal enforceability, netting agreements) for capital recognition.
- Corporate exposure nodes sit downstream as the main application case, including unrated/general corporate treatment where external ratings are unavailable.
