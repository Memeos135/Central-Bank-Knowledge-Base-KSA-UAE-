# Template CVA4 - RWA Flow Statements of CVA Risk Under SA-CVA

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring an RWA flow statement for CVA risk calculated under the standardised approach (SA-CVA), showing movements in RWA and the associated minimum capital requirement between reporting dates. The surrounding instructions explain how CVA RWA and capital charges relate to credit risk, counterparty credit risk and other RWA categories reported elsewhere in the framework. It binds banks required to disclose under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
