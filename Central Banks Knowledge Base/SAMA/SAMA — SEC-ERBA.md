# SEC-ERBA

Concept node for the Securitisation External Ratings-Based Approach, which risk-weights securitisation exposures by reference to external credit assessments, subject to operational requirements for using ratings and for inferred ratings, with an alternative treatment for term and short-term STC securitisations. It ranks below SEC-IRBA in the hierarchy and its outputs feed the Pillar 3 securitisation disclosure templates. It binds banks holding rated (or inferably rated) banking book securitisation positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When a bank holds equity in a fund and looks through to the underlying holdings, any securitisation exposures inside that fund must be capitalised under the securitisation framework rather than as plain equity. The fund provisions cross-refer to the securitisation approach hierarchy, under which SEC-ERBA is the external-ratings-based approach applied where the bank does not use (or is not permitted to use) SEC-IRBA or SEC-IAA. The practical consequence is that the look-through result is only as good as the securitisation approach selected for each underlying position, and the eligibility conditions for that approach must be met and evidenced.
