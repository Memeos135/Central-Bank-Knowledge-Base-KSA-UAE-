# SWOT Analysis

Concept node covering the viability and negotiation analysis a bank's workout unit should perform before agreeing a rehabilitation plan, including key financial ratios (liquidity, solvency/leverage, profitability), balance sheet composition and total debt burden, and cash flow analysis as the test of financial viability. It also frames SWOT analysis as a tool to identify account strengths and weaknesses and build a negotiating strategy, including educating borrowers who do not understand the restructuring. The analysis must be conducted by workout staff not involved in the original loan approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
