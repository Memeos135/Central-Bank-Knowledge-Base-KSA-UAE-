# Circular: Client Awareness of Cash Carrying Risks

SAMA circular to all banks operating in the Kingdom reinforcing earlier circulars on customer awareness when dealing in cash. Following reports of thefts from customers carrying large cash sums, banks must activate the prior circulars, educate customers on the risks of carrying large amounts of cash, encourage use of electronic payment channels, and inform them of licensed cash and precious metals transport companies as a safe alternative.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, AML/CTF
