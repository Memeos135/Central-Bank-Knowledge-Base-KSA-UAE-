# Unsecured Wholesale Funding Run-off

LCR guidance node defining unsecured wholesale funding (liabilities from non-natural persons not secured on designated assets, excluding derivative obligations) and the run-off factors applied by counterparty type within the 30-day horizon. It covers treatment of non-financial corporates, sovereigns, central banks, multilateral development banks and PSEs (20% or 40% depending on deposit insurance or equivalent public guarantee) and other legal-entity customers including banks, securities firms, insurers and fiduciaries at 100%. Applies to banks in the Kingdom reporting the amended LCR return; SAMA may also impose higher run-off on qualifying retail term deposits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
