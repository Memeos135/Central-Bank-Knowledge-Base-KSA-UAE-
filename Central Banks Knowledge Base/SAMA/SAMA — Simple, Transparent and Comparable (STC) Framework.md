# Simple, Transparent and Comparable (STC) Framework

Sets the scope and conditions for the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) framework, which allows alternative (lower) capital treatment for qualifying traditional term securitisations and for ABCP conduits and conduit-financed transactions. It lists the lettered STC criteria (asset nature, performance history, underwriting consistency, disclosure, alignment of interest, etc.), requires originator/sponsor disclosure and an independent investor assessment, and requires continuous compliance. Binds originators, sponsors and investing banks claiming STC capital treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
