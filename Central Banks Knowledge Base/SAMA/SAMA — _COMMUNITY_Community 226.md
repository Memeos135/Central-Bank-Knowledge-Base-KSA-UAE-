# G-SIB Indicators Template

## Why this community

Disclosure of the indicators used to assess global systemic importance of banks, feeding the G-SIB identification and higher loss-absorbency assessment methodology.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a reporting/disclosure template whose legal force comes from the systemic-importance assessment framework and the Pillar 3 disclosure obligation that requires its publication.
- Indicator definitions (size, interconnectedness, substitutability, complexity, cross-jurisdictional activity) are imported from the G-SIB methodology; the template adds no substantive definitions.
- Scope limit worth flagging: applicability depends on the size/activity thresholds that trigger indicator reporting, so not all banks in the KSA sector are in scope.
