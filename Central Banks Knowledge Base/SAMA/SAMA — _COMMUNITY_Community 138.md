# BNPL Licensing

## Why this community

Authorisation gateway for Buy Now Pay Later activity: who may apply, the procedural steps to obtain a BNPL licence, and the documentary evidence SAMA expects at application.

## How members connect

- The guidelines act as the umbrella instrument and cross-refer to both the procedural steps and the required documents, forming a single application-stage obligation chain.
- Procedures and documents are mutually referencing: each procedural stage triggers a defined evidentiary submission.
- Scope turns on the BNPL activity definition and the 'Consumer' concept used in the BNPL regime, which is narrower than the AML 'Customer' notion applied to the same entity once licensed.
- Licensing conditions here are the precondition for the ongoing conduct, consumer protection and AML obligations that attach post-authorisation.
