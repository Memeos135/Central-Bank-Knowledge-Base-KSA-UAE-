# Cyber Risk Management

## Why this community

The cyber risk management lifecycle expected of SAMA-supervised member organisations: identifying, analysing, responding to and continuously monitoring cyber security risk as a defined sub-discipline of enterprise risk governance.

## How members connect

- A parent cyber security risk management obligation sets the framework and cross-references each lifecycle stage as a mandatory component rather than an optional practice.
- The stages form an obligation chain: identification feeds analysis, analysis drives risk response (accept/mitigate/transfer/avoid), and monitoring and review closes the loop back to identification.
- Shared defined terms (cyber security risk, risk appetite, member organisation) carry through all members, so scope and accountability sit with the same governance body across stages.
- Sits in the governance/risk regime and interacts with board-level oversight and periodic reporting duties to SAMA.
