# Credit Risk Stage Classification

Sets the regulatory staging framework for credit risk exposures of finance companies, including the bifurcation of Stage 2 into sub-tiers for quarterly prudential reporting only (not for accounting). It prescribes minimum documented eligibility conditions for re-staging Stage 2 exposures back to Stage 1, including no arrears beyond 30 days past due, a 90-day cure period for non-retail borrowers (60 days for retail), and evidence that the drivers of significant increase in credit risk have disappeared. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
