# Forgery

Sub-element of criminal risk in SAMA's operational risk framework covering forgery, including cheque forgery and forgery of negotiable instruments such as letters of credit, historically the second largest reported operational loss category for Saudi banks. It links losses to failures by staff to verify document authenticity and warns that technology raises the sophistication and value of forgeries, so bank risk policies and procedures should address prevention of forgery by electronic means, including in electronic signature verification and payment systems. Directed at banks' boards, management and control functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments
