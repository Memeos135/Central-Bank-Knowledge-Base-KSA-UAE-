# Premium Residency Acceptance

## Why this community

Treatment of Premium Residency holders as an accepted identity/status category for banking relationships, bearing on customer acceptance and identification eligibility.

## How members connect

- Single circular; its legal effect is to extend acceptable customer status, which interacts with CDD/identification rules in the AML regime even though those instruments are not in this cluster.
