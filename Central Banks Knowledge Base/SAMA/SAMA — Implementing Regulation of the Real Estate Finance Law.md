# Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law

The Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law, setting out definitions, SAMA's regulatory powers over the real estate finance sector, contract requirements between real estate finance entities and borrowers, product no-objection requirements, insurance, and the framework for the Real Estate Refinance Company and secondary-market transfers of contractual rights. It binds commercial banks and licensed real estate finance companies acting as real estate finance entities, originators, and the licensed refinance company, with supervision by SAMA.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
