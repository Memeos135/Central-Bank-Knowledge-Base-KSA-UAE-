# Private Associations Account Rules

Covers accounts of private associations and private foundations under SAMA's Rules for Bank Accounts, including branch expense sub-accounts: MHRSD and supervisory authority approvals, joint-signature authorised managers who cannot be changed without MHRSD approval, bank compliance manager sign-off, one expense sub-account per branch at the same bank as the main account, funding only by periodic advances from the main account, and disbursement limited to first-beneficiary cheques and domestic transfers to support beneficiaries. The context also carries the general dormant account rules. It binds licensed banks and constrains the associations/foundations concerned.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
