# Fraud Response Plan

Node within the Respond domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework setting the requirement for Member Organisations to define, approve, implement and maintain a documented Fraud Response Plan governing how actual or suspected fraud incidents are detected, escalated, investigated and resolved, with root-cause review afterwards. It prescribes minimum content (alerting and reporting channels for customers, employees and third parties; roles and responsibilities; escalation and decision-making authority) and requires monitoring of compliance and periodic effectiveness measurement. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organisations and is expected to align with the enterprise incident management process; it is a fraud-governance obligation distinct from AML suspicious-transaction reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
