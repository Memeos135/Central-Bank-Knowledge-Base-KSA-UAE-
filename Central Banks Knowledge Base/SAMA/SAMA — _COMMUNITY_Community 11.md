# Cyber Security Framework

## Why this community

Cyber and IT risk governance for SAMA-supervised entities: the Cyber Security Framework as the umbrella obligation set, extended into third-party/cloud arrangements, change and project controls, and alignment with external technical standards.

## How members connect

- The Cyber Security Framework sits at the top of the hierarchy and is the instrument that incorporates or aligns with external standards (NIST, PCI DSS, SWIFT CSCF), so compliance with those standards is read as evidence of framework compliance rather than as freestanding KSA obligations.
- Domain nodes (third-party cyber security, cloud computing controls, IT risk management, change management, system development, project management, quality assurance) are subordinate control areas that cite back to the framework for their mandatory baseline.
- Outsourcing/cloud linkage creates an obligation chain: the supervised entity remains accountable for cyber controls exercised by service providers, connecting this cluster to outsourcing and operational-risk regimes.
- The General Department of Cyber Risk Control is the supervisory counterpart, relevant to notification, approval and examination touchpoints.
