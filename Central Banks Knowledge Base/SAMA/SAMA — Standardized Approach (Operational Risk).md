# Standardized Approach (Operational Risk)

Node covering the standardised approach as a prescribed regulatory capital calculation method, including its use for operational risk and, in the disclosure material drawn on here, the standardised approach to credit risk and how standardised versus modelled (IRB/slotting) portfolios are split across Pillar 3 template rows and columns. It fixes which exposures and RWA belong in the standardised bucket, the treatment of credit risk mitigation (simple versus comprehensive approach with supervisory haircuts), and the phase-in for equity exposures leaving IRB. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating and disclosing minimum Pillar 1 capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
