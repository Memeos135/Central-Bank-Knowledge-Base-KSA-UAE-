# Consumer Protection Checklist

Covers the consumer-fairness design considerations and the submission checklist (Annexure 1) under SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation. It requires customer appropriateness and fairness assessments before approval, avoidance of mis-selling, unfair terms and conflicts of interest, proportionate product-risk control across the value chain, phased launch, and cross-compliance with instruments such as the Responsible Lending Principles, Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules and the advertising rules; the checklist mandates a CCO letter, application form, statement of compliance, a signed consumer protection checklist for retail products, contracts/SLAs/NDAs for third parties, a multi-risk assessment report (including AML/CFT, fraud, cyber, data privacy) and Shari'ah Committee approvals where relevant. Binds banks notifying SAMA or seeking no-objection for new retail and other products.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF
