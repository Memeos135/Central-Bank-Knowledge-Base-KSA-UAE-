# Restructuring KPIs

A node within SAMA's guidance on loan restructuring/workouts for banks, sitting alongside the restructuring principles appendix and the guidance on documenting a restructuring agreement. It addresses how banks should frame, measure and monitor restructuring activity (including expected conduct during the life of a restructuring agreement and the terms captured in it), rather than imposing licensing conditions. It is directed at banks managing distressed and MSME borrowers; the appendix material is framed as encouraged practice rather than hard obligation, so the binding weight of each item should be checked in the source.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
