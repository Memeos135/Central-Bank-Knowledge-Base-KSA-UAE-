# Scenario Approach for Options

Describes the scenario (grid simulation) alternative for measuring market risk capital on options portfolios, where the general market risk charge is set by the scenario combination of underlying price and volatility changes producing the largest loss. Specific risk is calculated separately by applying specific risk weights to each option's delta-equivalent, as under the delta-plus method. Applies to banks using the simplified/standardised options treatment, subject to SAMA supervisory acceptance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
