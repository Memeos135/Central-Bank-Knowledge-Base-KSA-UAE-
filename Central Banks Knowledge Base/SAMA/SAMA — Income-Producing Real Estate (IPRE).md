# Income-Producing Real Estate (IPRE)

Covers income-producing real estate — under IRB an SL sub-class, and under the standardized approach the real estate exposures classified as "materially dependent on cash flows generated by the property" (IPRRE for residential, IPCRE for commercial). It sits within the real estate exposure class, which also distinguishes regulatory versus other real estate and land acquisition, development and construction (ADC) exposures, and feeds Pillar 3 disclosure row definitions. Binds banks assigning risk weights to real-estate-secured exposures under SAMA's credit risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
