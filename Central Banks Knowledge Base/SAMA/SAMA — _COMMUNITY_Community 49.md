# Targeted Financial Sanctions

## Why this community

The targeted financial sanctions (TFS) regime: obligations on financial institutions to screen against designation lists, handle alerts, and execute freezing/unfreezing without delay, aligned to FATF Recommendations 6 and 7.

## How members connect

- The TFS Rules are the parent instrument; screening, list management, alert handling and freezing/unfreezing are its component obligation chains, each cross-referenced from the Rules.
- A sequential obligation chain runs list ingestion → screening → alert triage/escalation → freeze and reporting, so a defect at any upstream step is a breach of the downstream duty.
- FATF Recommendations 6 and 7 are cited as the international standard the Rules implement, relevant to interpreting 'without delay' and designation scope.
- Note the duplicate node: treat it as the same instrument, not a separate legal source, when citing.
