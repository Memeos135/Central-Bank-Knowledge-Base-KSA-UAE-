# Compliance Principles for Finance & Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA's Compliance Principles establishing the compliance function/unit, its officer and staff, and its duties — including monitoring the full body of applicable laws and SAMA instructions (AML/CTF, terrorism financing, BNPL rules, anti-fraud, consumer protection, debt collection, governance and conduct), training, compliance-risk measurement and maintaining an instructions database. The Unit reports directly to the Audit Committee and must escalate irregularities and violations to both SAMA and the Committee, and periodically review the customer care department and debt collection function or outsourced collector (excluding real estate refinance companies). They bind finance and real estate refinance companies and guide finance support and finance lease contract registration companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, consumer protection

- **What this link tells you:** For any question on whether a finance company must maintain an independent compliance function, read these Compliance Principles as derivative obligations flowing from the Finance Companies Control Law rather than as optional standards. The instrument states it was issued under powers vested in SAMA by that Law, so the parent Law supplies both the licensing perimeter (who is caught) and the enforcement route for breaches. The consequence is that compliance-function failings can be escalated as breaches of the statutory regime, and the Principles should be read together with the Law's Implementing Regulation where the two address the same subject.
