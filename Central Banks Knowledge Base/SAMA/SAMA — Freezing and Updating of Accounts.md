# Freezing and Updating of Accounts

Chapter of the DTFC Regulations requiring that customer relationships operate only under valid identification, with mandatory freezing of General Accounts of juristic persons 90 days after expiry of their licence or commercial registration, or where opening documents carry no validity date. It also requires monitoring of directors' and authorised signatories' IDs (suspending operating powers rather than the account), contractual disclosure of the freezing right, customer notification before freezing, and related disclosure/blocking of accounts where SAMA so instructs. Binds all DTFCs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, BNPL/finance
