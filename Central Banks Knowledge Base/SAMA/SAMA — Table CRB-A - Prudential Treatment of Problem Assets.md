# Table CRB-A - Prudential Treatment of Problem Assets

Table CRB-A is a mandatory annual, flexible-format Pillar 3 disclosure supplementing the quantitative credit-quality templates with information on non-performing and forborne exposures. Banks must state their own definitions of non-performing and forborne exposures, say whether they follow SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Assets (No. 41033343, January 2020), and explain materiality thresholds for past due, exit criteria and probation periods, including any corporate/retail differences. It binds all banks reporting under the framework, using carrying values on the regulatory scope of consolidation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
