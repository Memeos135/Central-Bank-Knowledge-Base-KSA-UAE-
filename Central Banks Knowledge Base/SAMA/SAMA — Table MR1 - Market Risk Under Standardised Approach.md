# Table MR1 - Market Risk Under Standardised Approach

A Pillar 3 quantitative template disclosing market risk capital requirements computed under the standardised approach, broken down by risk class (including general interest rate, equity, FX, commodity and credit spread risk) and by default risk and residual risk add-on components. It complements the qualitative Table MRA and excludes counterparty credit risk on the same exposures. Applies to banks reporting under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
