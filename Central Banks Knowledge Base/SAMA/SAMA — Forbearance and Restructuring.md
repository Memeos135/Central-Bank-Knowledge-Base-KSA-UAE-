# Forbearance and Restructuring

Defines restructuring for finance companies and splits it into forbearance (concession granted because of borrower financial difficulty) versus renegotiation/refinancing/rescheduling (borrower convenience, no deterioration). Forborne exposures may only be reported in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B, and exit from Stage 3 requires cure periods (12 months non-retail, 6 months retail, with shorter steps between sub-stages), restarting if the exposure re-defaults, plus specified repayment conditions for first- and second-time restructurings. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
