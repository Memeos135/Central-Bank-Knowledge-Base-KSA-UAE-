# Kill Chain

Glossary/concept node for the kill chain as referenced in SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles: a military-derived model (developed by Lockheed Martin) breaking a cyber attack into sequential phases from reconnaissance through actions on objectives. The Principles expect Member Organizations to define and taxonomise attack phases using such recognised frameworks, including unified kill chain variants. Applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations as supervisory expectation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
