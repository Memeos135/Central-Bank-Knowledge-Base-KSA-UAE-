# Unifying Financial Notification Elements

SAMA circular unifying the minimum content of transaction notification messages sent to customers of financial institutions, extending earlier requirements for mada card notifications. Addressed to banks, payment service providers and category (A) exchange companies, it requires notifications for all financial transactions containing at least the data elements listed in the annexed tables (by transaction type: domestic/international purchases, ATM withdrawals, deposits, cheques, domestic and cross-border transfers, internet purchases, bill payments, financing drawdowns and instalments, fees, refunds/reversals, mobile-app and e-wallet transactions), along with a standardised Arabic/English terminology list, implementation within 15 days and submission of an implementation plan within two weeks.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments
