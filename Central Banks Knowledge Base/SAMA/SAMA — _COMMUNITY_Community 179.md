# Disability Emergency Evacuation

## Why this community

Accessibility and safety expectations for persons with disabilities in supervised entities' premises, framed as emergency evacuation preparedness rather than prudential or conduct-of-business regulation.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: guidance-level material with no internal cross-references in the graph.
- Legally it attaches to consumer protection and fair-treatment duties owed to customers with disabilities accessing branches and service points.
- Sits below binding rules in the hierarchy — read as expected practice supporting broader accessibility obligations, not as a standalone enforceable regime.
