# Cash Variation Margin Treatment

Sets the cumulative conditions under which cash variation margin exchanged on derivatives may be treated as a settlement payment rather than collateral — including non-segregation, at-least-daily exchange based on mark-to-market, eligible currency, full mark-to-market amount, and coverage by a single legally enforceable master netting agreement. Where met, cash variation margin received reduces replacement cost and margin provided may be deducted from the leverage ratio exposure measure. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks with derivative portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
