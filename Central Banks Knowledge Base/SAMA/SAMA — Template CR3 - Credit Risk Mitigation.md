# Template CR3 - Credit Risk Mitigation

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's market disclosure framework requiring banks to present an overview of exposures covered by credit risk mitigation, split between unsecured carrying amounts and amounts secured by collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives. It sits in the credit risk section alongside the qualitative CRM table (CRC) and is supported by worked allocation examples for multi-secured and over/under-collateralised exposures. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements; scope excludes securitisation and counterparty credit risk positions, which are disclosed elsewhere.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
