# Reporting of Suspicious Transactions

This node sets out suspicious transaction reporting obligations in SAMA's AML/CTF guide, requiring board-approved documented reporting procedures covering internal escalation, investigation stages, approval/closure levels, the designated reporting officer, confidentiality and no tipping-off. Financial institutions must report immediately and directly to the SAFIU on suspicion or reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF, including attempted transactions regardless of amount, and must respond to SAFIU requests for further information. It also intersects with targeted financial sanctions, requiring notification to SAMA of accounts, relationships or transactions linked to UN Security Council and national designated-persons lists.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
