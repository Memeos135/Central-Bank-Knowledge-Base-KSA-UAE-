# Debt/EBITDA Ratio

A concept node on the Debt/EBITDA leverage ratio used as a proxy indicator in the initial viability assessment of distressed borrowers, sitting within the broader set of illustrative financial ratio categories (liquidity, solvency/leverage, profitability) that Workout Units apply. The guidance is indicative rather than prescriptive, and directs banks to cash-flow based analysis for MSME borrowers where audited financials are unavailable. The analysis must be performed by Workout Unit staff not previously involved in the original loan approval.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
