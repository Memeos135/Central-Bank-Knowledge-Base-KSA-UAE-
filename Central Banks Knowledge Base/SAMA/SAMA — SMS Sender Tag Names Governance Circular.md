# SMS Sender Tag Names Governance Circular

A SAMA circular relaying a communication from the Communications and Information Technology authority on governance of bulk SMS sender IDs (Tag Names) through a unified electronic portal for SMS service providers, aimed at preventing misuse of text messages. It directs supervised entities to cooperate promptly with their contracted SMS providers and complete registration within the transitional period set by the authority. Addressed to payment sector entities supervised by SAMA's Payment Systems and Companies Control Department.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk
