# Trading Book Securitisation Template

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of securitisation exposures held in the trading book, broken down by bank role and exposure type.

## How members connect

- Part of the SEC quantitative template series; the trading-book counterpart to the banking-book templates, split by where the exposure is booked.
- Depends on the same securitisation definitions and role classifications set out in the qualitative securitisation table.
- Content is fixed-format: the disclosure obligation is satisfied only by completing the prescribed rows and columns.
