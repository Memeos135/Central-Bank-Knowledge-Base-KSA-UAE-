# Credit Card Rules

## Why this community

The credit card regime: issuance, operation, disclosure, fee and profit-rate treatment, dispute handling and cardholder protections for issuers and operators.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy — the Rules are the binding instrument; the FAQ is interpretive guidance that references and explains them and does not create new obligations.
- Both share the defined terms for cardholder, issuer and card product, so scope questions resolve against the Rules' definitions.
- The FAQ is the practical route to SAMA's reading of ambiguous provisions (billing, grace periods, fee caps, consent to limit changes), useful where the Rules are terse.
