# Money Transfer Controls

## Why this community

Controls governing how licensed currency exchange businesses may receive inbound money transfers, sitting at the intersection of the money exchange licensing regime and AML/CTF source-of-funds and counterparty due diligence expectations.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the control set stands alone as a conduct requirement addressed to currency exchange licensees rather than to banks or PSPs.
- Obligation logic runs from the money exchange licensing/permitted-activities perimeter to specific operational limits on receiving transfers, with AML/CTF customer and counterparty verification as the implicit backstop.
- Read together with the broader AML/CTF regime where the transfer originator or correspondent is treated as a customer or counterparty requiring CDD.
