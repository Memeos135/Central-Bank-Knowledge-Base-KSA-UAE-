# ATM Cash Feeding & Transport Hours Ramadan 1447H

Circular setting, for Ramadan 1447H, the permitted hours for cash transport to branches, cash centres, remittance centres, exchange centres and retail (09:00–22:00) and for ATM cash replenishment and maintenance (24 hours in listed major cities, malls, airports and train stations; 07:00–22:00 elsewhere), subject to security authorities' directions. It reiterates compliance with the security safeguards in the Law on Transport of Cash, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents, its implementing regulation and related SAMA circulars. Binds banks and exchange centres operating in the Kingdom.
