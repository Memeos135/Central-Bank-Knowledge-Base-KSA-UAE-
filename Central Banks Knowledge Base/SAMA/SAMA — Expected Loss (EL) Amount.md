# Expected Loss (EL) Amount

Node covering the expected loss (EL) amount concept in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (Basel III credit risk framework): EL multiplied by exposure at default, aggregated across all IRB exposures to give a total EL amount that is then compared with eligible provisions for capital purposes. It sets how EL is derived (PD x LGD for non-defaulted corporate, sovereign, bank and retail exposures; best-estimate expected loss or supervisory LGD for defaulted exposures; slotting-based EL for specialised lending), and excludes securitisation exposures. Binds SAMA-licensed banks that have been permitted to use the internal ratings-based approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
