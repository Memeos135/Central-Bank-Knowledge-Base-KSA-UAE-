# Rules for Regulating Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA circular approving the Rules for Regulating Real Estate Refinance Companies, issued under the Real Estate Finance Law. The rules define the refinance company, real estate financiers, primary and secondary markets and refinance activity, and set requirements on capital adequacy and liquidity, governance, permitted activities, internal organisation, outsourcing, risk management, compliance, internal audit, supervision and secondary-market operations. They bind joint-stock companies licensed by SAMA to conduct real estate refinance activity, which must direct services to real estate financiers unless SAMA issues a no-objection.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
