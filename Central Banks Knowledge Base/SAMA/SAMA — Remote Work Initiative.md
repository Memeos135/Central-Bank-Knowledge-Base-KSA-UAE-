# Remote Work Initiative

SAMA circular on the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development "remote work" initiative, addressed to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA. It requires that remote customer-service staff be recorded in official registers and either enrolled in social insurance or have their contracts documented on the Ministry's designated portal, including where the worker is engaged directly or supplied through an intermediary, contractor or outsourcing provider.
