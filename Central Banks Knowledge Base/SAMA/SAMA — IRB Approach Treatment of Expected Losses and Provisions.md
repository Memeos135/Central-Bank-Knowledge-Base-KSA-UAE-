# IRB Approach: Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions

Concept node for the IRB treatment of expected losses (EL) and provisions, covering calculation of EL amounts, calculation of eligible provisions, and the comparison of the two for capital purposes (shortfall deduction or surplus inclusion). It sits within SAMA's credit risk capital framework alongside the IRB risk components and supervisory slotting rules. It binds banks approved to use IRB approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** If an exposure is risk-weighted under the supervisory slotting criteria rather than by internal parameters, the expected loss calculation and the provisions comparison must follow the slotting-specific route, so the two chapters cannot be applied in isolation. The expected loss chapter governs the general IRB method for computing EL and reconciling it with provisions, while the slotting approach sets separate EL outcomes for specialized lending categories, and the general chapter cross-refers to that treatment. For a decision, first classify the exposure (specialized lending under slotting versus other IRB exposures), because that classification determines which EL figure feeds the provisions shortfall or excess adjustment to capital.
