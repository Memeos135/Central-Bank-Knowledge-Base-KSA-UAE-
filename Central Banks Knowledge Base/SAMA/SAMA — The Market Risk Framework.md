# The Market Risk Framework

Concept node on the market risk capital framework, focusing on internal risk transfers between the banking book, trading book, the SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk and the CVA portfolio, and the conditions for regulatory capital recognition (including exact matching of external hedges and documentation of hedged CVA risk). It also states that all banks, D-SIB and non-D-SIB, must calculate market risk capital using the Standardised Approach, with RWA derived by a 12.5 multiplier. Binds SAMA-licensed banks with trading book or FX/commodities banking book exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
