# Real Estate Financing Rules

## Why this community

Prudential and conduct rules for real estate finance products, covering permitted product structures and the treatment of appraisal costs within financing contracts.

## How members connect

- Both apply to banks and finance companies offering real estate finance, under the same product-approval and contract-terms framework.
- Hierarchy link: product instructions set what may be offered and on what terms; the appraisal-fee rule constrains a specific charge that must appear in — and be limited by — those contract terms.
- Consumer-protection chain: fee transparency and cost-recovery limits interact with disclosure and total-cost-of-financing requirements for the borrower.
- Appraisal outcomes also feed collateral valuation and LTV-type prudential considerations, connecting conduct rules to credit-risk treatment.
