# Standardized Approach for Credit Risk

This node concerns the standardised approach (SA) for credit risk as applied in SAMA's Basel-aligned capital rules and its reporting/disclosure treatment. It defines what falls within SA risk-weighted assets, how credit risk mitigation is recognised (simple vs comprehensive approach with supervisory haircuts), the carve-outs for counterparty credit risk, equity, settlement risk and securitisation, and the phase-in of the prohibition on IRB for equity exposures. It binds banks calculating Pillar 1 credit risk capital and completing the related RWA templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

### [[SAMA — Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk|Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat the standardised approach as a component of, not an alternative to, the minimum credit risk capital obligation: the enforceable requirement is to hold minimum capital against credit risk, and the standardised approach supplies the default method and shared defined terms (including the risk-class taxonomy) for computing it. Exposures for which the bank lacks supervisory approval for an internal model fall back into this approach by operation of the minimum-capital rules. For a compliance decision this means standardised-approach outcomes must be read against the overarching minimum ratio requirement, and any approach change or scope carve-out needs to be justified under that parent obligation.
