# Off-Balance Sheet Items Treatment

Addresses on-balance sheet and off-balance sheet items within SAMA's leverage ratio exposure measure, including fiduciary assets, trade/settlement date accounting and delivery-versus-payment, cash pooling arrangements, and the conversion of commitments, direct credit substitutes, acceptances and letters of credit via credit conversion factors. It also flags SAMA's supervisory concern with structures designed to minimise the exposure measure (agency-model arbitrage, collateral swaps, off-balance sheet transfers) and confirms that central bank reserves are included absent a temporary SAMA exemption. Binds SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
