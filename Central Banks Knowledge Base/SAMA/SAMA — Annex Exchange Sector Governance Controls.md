# Annex: Exchange Sector Governance Controls

Annex to SAMA's Key Principles of Governance in Financial Institutions (3rd edition, Dhul-Qi'dah 1442H / June 2021) setting sector-specific governance controls for the money exchange sector. It adapts the general board, committee and disclosure principles to exchange companies and establishments, which typically have simpler structures than banks. Binds licensed exchange companies and establishments operating in the Kingdom; the main body of the Principles applies to the wider set of SAMA-supervised financial institutions.
