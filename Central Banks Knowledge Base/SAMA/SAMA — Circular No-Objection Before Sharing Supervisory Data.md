# Circular: No-Objection Before Sharing Supervisory Data

SAMA circular from the General Department for Supervision of Finance Companies requiring finance companies to obtain SAMA's no-objection before providing governmental or non-governmental bodies with the periodic supervisory data submitted to SAMA or with unpublished statistical data. It reflects SAMA's control over the integrity and publication of sector data and binds all finance companies operating in the Kingdom.
