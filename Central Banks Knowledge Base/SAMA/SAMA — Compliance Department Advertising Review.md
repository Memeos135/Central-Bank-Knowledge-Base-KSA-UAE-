# Compliance Department Advertising Review

A segment of SAMA's advertising rules covering conduct and control obligations in Articles 8–14: protecting sector reputation and fair competition, prohibiting use of customer personal data in advertising without written consent, banning insurance/credit advertising to under-18s, requiring opt-in consent and easy permanent opt-out for receiving ads, and making advertised offers and specifications contractually binding. It also requires use of official or licensed third-party channels with the institution retaining responsibility for third-party non-compliance, and mandates an internal advertising policy aligned with SAMA rules. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
