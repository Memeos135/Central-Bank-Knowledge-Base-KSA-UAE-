# Late Payment, Default and Contract Termination Provisions

The provisions of the mandatory individual vehicle Ijara template governing late payment of rental instalments, breach and termination of the contract, lessee withdrawal, and expiry without the lessee acquiring the asset. They fix the consequences of arrears and the lessor's remedies, together with treatment of guarantees where their value declines. Binding on lessors (banks and finance companies) in dealings with individual lessees.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
