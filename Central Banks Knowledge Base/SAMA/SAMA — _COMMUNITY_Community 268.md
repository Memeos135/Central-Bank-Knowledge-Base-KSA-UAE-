# IRB Models Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure for banks permitted to use internal ratings-based approaches: model scope, supervisory approval status, rating system design, governance and control of the estimation process.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster acting as the narrative gateway to the IRB quantitative templates — it explains the models whose outputs those templates quantify.
- Applies only to banks with supervisory permission to use IRB, so the disclosure obligation is conditional on the approval regime rather than general.
- Links governance/model-risk expectations to the capital framework: the qualitative account must be consistent with the approved model perimeter.
