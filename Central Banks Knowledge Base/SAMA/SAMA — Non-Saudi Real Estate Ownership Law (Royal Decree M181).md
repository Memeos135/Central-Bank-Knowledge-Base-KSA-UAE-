# Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law (Royal Decree M/181)

Concept node for the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law (Royal Decree M/181) and its Implementing Regulation issued by Council of Ministers decision, which define the categories of non-Saudi natural and legal persons permitted to own property or acquire other real rights in the Kingdom. For SAMA purposes it is the eligibility gateway: persons covered by this Law form the customer category for which the Bank Account Rules were amended to allow non-resident account opening, subject to prescribed documentation and restrictive operating controls.

**Regimes:** other, AML/CTF, banking prudential
