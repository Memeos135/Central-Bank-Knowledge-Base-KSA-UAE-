# Case Management System

Requires Member Organisations to implement and maintain a Case Management System as the database and workflow for responding to fraud, recording suspected fraud alerts, internal and external reports, and investigations from initial assessment through to resolution. Sets minimum capabilities including restricted user access, configurable workflow aligned to the operating model and Fraud Response Plan, case ownership allocation, categorisation for trend analysis and reporting, tracking, recording of investigative steps, and retention of all case evidence. Sits alongside response actions such as transaction reversal, customer contact and identity verification before reinstating frozen services; binds Member Organisations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
