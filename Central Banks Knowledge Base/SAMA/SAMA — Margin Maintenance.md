# Margin Maintenance

Margin provisions of the SAMA-approved Master Repurchase Agreement, setting out when a party may call for a Margin Transfer based on Net Exposure, how Net Exposure and Net Margin are computed across Transaction Exposures, the currency and composition of margin, and the related defined terms (Margin Securities, Margin Percentage, Market Value). It establishes the contractual collateralisation mechanics for repo exposures. It binds the two counterparties to each repo transaction under the mandated MRA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
