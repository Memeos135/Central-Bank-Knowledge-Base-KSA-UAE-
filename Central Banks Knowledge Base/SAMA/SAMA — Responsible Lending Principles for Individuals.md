# Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals

SAMA circular and annexed Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals, issued under SAMA's supervisory powers and read alongside the Banking Control Law, Finance Companies Control Law, Real Estate Finance Law, Finance Lease Law and the consumer/finance customer protection principles. It defines core terms (Financer, Customer as a natural person, amount of finance, term cost, total amount payable, gross salary, total monthly income, monthly credit obligations) and sets creditworthiness-assessment and affordability duties before granting individual finance. It binds banks and finance companies licensed and supervised by SAMA that lend to natural persons.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

### [[SAMA — Real Estate Finance Law|Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Where responsible lending assessments concern real estate finance to individuals, the affordability and conduct expectations of the Responsible Lending Principles operate alongside the specific statutory duties in the Real Estate Finance Law. That law imposes its own obligations on the real estate finance entity, including verifying the title deed and the absence of in rem rights before contracting, which is a security and validity check distinct from borrower creditworthiness. For a decision, a lender cannot treat a satisfactory affordability assessment as completing its pre-contract obligations; the title verification duty applies independently.
