# Implementing Regulation to the Anti-Money Laundering Law

Implementing Regulation to the Anti-Money Laundering Law, setting out the detailed rules behind the Law's articles: definitions of supervisory and competent authorities, the acts that constitute conducting a transaction or business relationship, the written ML risk assessment duty, group-wide AML policies and intra-group information sharing, treatment of jurisdictions with weaker AML requirements, and the triggers and scope of suspicious transaction reporting. It binds financial institutions, DNFBPs and NPOs, and frames the reporting interface with the FIU and supervisors. Useful for scoping questions on who is covered and what documentation supervisors may demand.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
