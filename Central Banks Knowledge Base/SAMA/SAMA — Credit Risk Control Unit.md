# Credit Risk Control Unit

Sets the qualitative model-governance conditions for using internal models to measure counterparty credit risk exposure (IMM) and market risk (IMA): an independent risk control unit reporting to senior management, initial and ongoing independent validation separate from model development, regular backtesting and profit-and-loss attribution at trading desk level, active board and senior management involvement, integration of model output into day-to-day risk management and trading/exposure limits, and documented policies and procedures. It binds banks seeking or maintaining SAMA permission to use internal models for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
