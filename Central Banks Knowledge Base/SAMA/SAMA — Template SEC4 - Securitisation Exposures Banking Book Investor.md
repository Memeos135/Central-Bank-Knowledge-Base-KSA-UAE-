# Template SEC4 - Securitisation Exposures Banking Book Investor

A Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure template for banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as investor, reporting exposure amounts, approaches used, risk weight bands, RWA and capital requirements. Like SEC3, it is limited to positions subject to the securitisation regulatory framework, so no double-counting arises with the broader SEC1/SEC2 templates. It applies to banks within the scope of SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
