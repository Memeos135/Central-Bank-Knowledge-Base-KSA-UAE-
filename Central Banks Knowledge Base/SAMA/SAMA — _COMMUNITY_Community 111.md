# Dormant & Term Accounts

## Why this community

Account lifecycle rules for bank accounts — opening and operating general and term deposit accounts, and the consequences of customer inactivity, including reclassification as dormant, freezing, and reactivation/data update requirements.

## How members connect

- The general account rules set the baseline definitional and operating conditions; term deposit rules and the inactivity/dormancy rules are built as references back to that baseline.
- Freezing and updating obligations are the enforcement consequence of failing to refresh customer identification data, which is where this consumer/account regime intersects with AML/CTF ongoing CDD duties.
- Inactive versus dormant are distinct defined statuses with different permitted operations and different customer-notification and reactivation triggers — do not conflate them.
- Freezing provisions apply across both general and term deposit accounts, so the account type does not displace the update obligation.
