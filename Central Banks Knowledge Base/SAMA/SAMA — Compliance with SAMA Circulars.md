# Compliance with SAMA Circulars

A SAMA circular (re-issued by the Finance Companies Supervision Department to finance companies operating in the Kingdom) confirming that financial institutions remain bound by SAMA instructions communicated through official written correspondence or the official email of SAMA's administrative units. It clarifies that publication of circulars on SAMA's public portal does not relieve any supervised institution of its compliance obligations; compliance is required from the date of email transmission.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, other
