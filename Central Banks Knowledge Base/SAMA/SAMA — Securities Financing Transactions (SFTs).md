# Securities Financing Transactions (SFTs)

Covers the counterparty credit risk treatment of securities financing transactions and repo-style transactions, including eligible collateral and haircuts in the trading book, and the separate regime of minimum haircut floors for certain non-centrally cleared SFTs. It specifies scope and exemptions (central bank SFTs, qualifying cash-collateralised securities lending, collateral upgrade transactions where re-use is precluded) and sets the haircut floor percentages. Binds banks entering SFTs with in-scope counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
