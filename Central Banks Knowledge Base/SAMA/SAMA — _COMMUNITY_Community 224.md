# SA-CVA Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure of the standardised approach to credit valuation adjustment (SA-CVA) risk, covering how a bank explains its CVA risk governance, scope and methodology rather than numeric outcomes.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a disclosure table sitting in the market/counterparty credit risk branch of the Pillar 3 hierarchy (capital framework rules -> disclosure requirements -> prescribed table).
- Its content depends on defined terms and eligibility conditions for SA-CVA use set out in the minimum capital requirements for CVA risk; the table is the reporting consequence of that approval-based regime.
- Qualitative narrative is meant to be read alongside the quantitative CVA disclosures, so completeness is assessed against the underlying capital computation obligations.
