# Expired ID Handling

## Why this community

How supervised entities must treat customers whose National ID or Iqama has expired — whether accounts and services may continue, and what re-verification is required.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster addressing the identification-document currency element of ongoing due diligence and account maintenance.
- Bridges two regimes: AML/CTF requirements to keep customer identification data current, and consumer-protection limits on suspending or restricting access to funds.
- Operationally determines the trigger point for account restriction and the conditions for reinstatement once documents are renewed.
