# IRB Exposure Classes

## Why this community

Internal Ratings-Based approach to credit risk: how exposures are classified into asset classes and how risk-weighted assets are derived under the foundation versus advanced variants, including the special case of purchased receivables.

## How members connect

- The IRB overview node is the definitional parent: corporate, sovereign and bank exposure definitions determine which risk-weight function and parameter regime applies.
- Choice of F-IRB versus A-IRB governs which parameters a bank may estimate itself, and both routes feed the same default-risk RWA calculation.
- Purchased receivables form a nested regime that inherits the asset-class definitions but adds a separate dilution-risk capital charge alongside default risk.
- Recognition of credit risk mitigants is the cross-reference out of this cluster into the CRM/collateral rules, so eligibility conditions are resolved elsewhere.
