# Financial Entities Ethical Red Teaming Framework (F.E.E.R.)

SAMA's Financial Entities Ethical Red Teaming (F.E.E.R.) Framework, setting out a four-phase intelligence-led cyber attack simulation process (preparation, threat intelligence/scenario development, execution, lessons learned) with defined roles for the Green, White, Red and Blue Teams. It also specifies selection and procurement requirements for external Red Teaming Providers, including experience, references, certified staff, methodology, governance and risk management. Applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations directed to undertake red teaming exercises.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
