# Eligible Commercial/Residential Real Estate Collateral

Sets out when commercial and residential real estate qualifies as recognised collateral for capital purposes, including the operational conditions of legal enforceability and perfected lien, objective market valuation, and at least annual revaluation, and excludes income-producing real estate falling in the specialised lending asset class from recognition against corporate exposures. It also frames the real estate exposure class (regulatory residential/commercial, other real estate, ADC) used in the standardised approach. Binds banks under SAMA supervision when computing risk-weighted assets and applying credit risk mitigation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
