# PFMI Principles

This node covers SAMA's adoption of the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMIs) as the substantive benchmark in its Oversight Framework for Payment Systems and their Operators. It explains that SAMA supervises on a principles-based approach, applying both the regulatory framework and the PFMIs (April 2012 and later amendments), supported by the CPSS-IOSCO Disclosure Framework and SAMA's guidance manual on payment system compliance with the PFMIs. It binds licensed payment system operators (PSOs) of both systemically important payment systems (SIPS) and non-classified systems operating in or across the borders of the Kingdom.
