# Head of Counter-Fraud

Node covering the counter-fraud function's leadership and organisational placement under SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, including the requirement that Member Organisations establish and maintain a Counter-Fraud Department owning day-to-day running of the Counter-Fraud Programme. Its listed duties span policy compliance monitoring, enterprise fraud risk assessment, control design, intelligence sharing with SAMA and the sector, system tuning, investigations and root-cause analysis, risk appetite breach escalation, alignment with cyber security and financial crime functions, and periodic senior management reporting. It also frames reporting-line choice and the maturity model against which the function's effectiveness is graded, binding all Member Organisations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
