# Standing Payment Order Controls for Finance Entities

Second edition (November 2021) of SAMA's Controls on the Standing Payment Order in Favour of a Financing Entity, replacing the earlier real-estate-finance-specific controls; it now covers all financing products and extends the definition of Financing Entity to government financiers including National Development Fund banks and funds. It defines the service (periodic transfers from a customer's account to the financing entity to repay financing) and sets minimum obligations on banks — verifying stable monthly income, taking the customer's acknowledgement, notification of set-up and of non-execution, execution timing, and requiring the beneficiary financier's consent or a release letter before amendment or cancellation — plus obligations on the beneficiary financing entity regarding rescheduling and issuing release letters within set deadlines. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom and applies without prejudice to responsible retail finance principles and retail collection rules.

**Regimes:** payments, BNPL/finance, consumer protection
