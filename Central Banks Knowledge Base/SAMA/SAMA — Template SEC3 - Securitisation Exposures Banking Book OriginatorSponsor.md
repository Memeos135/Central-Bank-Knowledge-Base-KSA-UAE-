# Template SEC3 - Securitisation Exposures Banking Book Originator/Sponsor

A Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure template for banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as originator or sponsor, showing exposures, regulatory approaches applied, risk weight bands, RWA and capital requirements. Only exposures the bank treats under the securitisation framework are reported here, which for originators presupposes that the significant risk transfer recognition criteria are satisfied. It binds banks reporting under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
