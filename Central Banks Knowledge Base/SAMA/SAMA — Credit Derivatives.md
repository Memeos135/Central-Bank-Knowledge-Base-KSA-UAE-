# Credit Derivatives

Covers the capital treatment of credit derivatives under SAMA's credit risk framework, both where a bank sells protection (first-to-default, second-to-default and nth-to-default derivatives, with risk weights aggregated up to a 1250% cap) and where it buys protection as credit risk mitigation. Only credit default swaps and total return swaps providing guarantee-equivalent protection are eligible mitigants; nth-to-default derivatives give no capital relief to the protection buyer. Binds banks calculating RWAs under SAMA's capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
