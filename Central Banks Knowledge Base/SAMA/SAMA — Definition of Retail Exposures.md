# Definition of Retail Exposures

Sets out how exposures qualify as retail for credit risk capital, including the regulatory retail criteria (low individual value with an aggregate SAR 4.46 million cap per counterparty and a 0.2% granularity limit), the "transactor" concept for credit/charge cards and overdrafts, and "other retail". Drives the applicable risk weights (45% for transactor regulatory retail, 75% for other regulatory retail, 100% for other retail) and the IRB retail asset class. Binds banks subject to SAMA's credit risk capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
