# Update to Senior Leadership Appointment Requirements

SAMA circular announcing an update to the Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions in SAMA-supervised financial institutions (second edition, previously issued under circular 17/1594), extending their scope to cover "activities supporting finance activity". The updated version is published on SAMA's website and applies from the circular's date. It binds banks, finance and real estate finance companies and finance-support companies, credit information companies, exchange houses, and payment companies/payment systems operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, payments, data/credit info
