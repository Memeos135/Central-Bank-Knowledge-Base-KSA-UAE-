# Standardized Credit Risk Assessment Approach (SCRA)

This node covers the Standardized Credit Risk Assessment Approach within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, applied by banks to bank counterparty exposures that lack an eligible external rating. It requires classification of such exposures into Grades A, B or C with corresponding base and short-term risk weights, subject to conditions (including CET1 and Tier 1 leverage thresholds for a preferential unrated treatment) and to due diligence that may only push risk weights higher, never lower. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk RWAs under the standardized approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
