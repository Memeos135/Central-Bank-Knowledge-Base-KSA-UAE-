# Customer Income Verification

## Why this community

Verification of a customer's income as part of due diligence — relevant both to affordability/creditworthiness assessment in consumer finance and to source-of-funds/wealth understanding under CDD.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the obligation to obtain and verify income evidence rather than rely on customer self-declaration.
- Sits at the intersection of two regimes — responsible-lending affordability testing and AML/CTF customer due diligence — so the same evidence can serve different legal purposes with different retention and refresh triggers.
- Consequence for a decision: an unverified income figure undermines both the credit decision's defensibility and the CDD risk rating.
