# Instructions on Providing Account Info to Government Bodies (SAMA 5708)

SAMA instructions governing when banks, banking institutions and licensed exchange businesses may release customer account documents, information and data to governmental and non-governmental bodies, framed around banking secrecy and the prior no-objection requirement. It sets out categories where direct disclosure is prohibited and requests must be routed through SAMA — criminal matters (via the Public Prosecution under the Law of Criminal Procedure implementing regulation), civil matters (via judicial authorities under the Enforcement Law and Law of Civil Procedure), and statutory information powers of certain other government agencies, which SAMA assesses against reasoned objective grounds — and identifies limited exceptions where direct disclosure is permitted without SAMA's no-objection. Non-compliant direct requests must be declined in writing citing these instructions.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, consumer protection, other
