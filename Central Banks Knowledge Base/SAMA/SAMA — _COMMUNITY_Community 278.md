# Simplified Market Risk Approach

## Why this community

Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of market risk capital requirements for banks eligible to apply the simplified standardised approach.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a template whose applicability is defined by eligibility for the simplified standardised approach, an exemption-style scope limit.
- Alternative branch to the IMA and full standardised disclosure tables — mutually exclusive by approach used.
- Disclosure obligation derives from the market risk capital calculation rules; the table reports outcomes, it does not set the methodology.
