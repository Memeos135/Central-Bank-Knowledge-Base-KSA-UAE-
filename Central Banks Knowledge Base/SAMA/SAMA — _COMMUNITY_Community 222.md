# Commodity Risk Buckets

## Why this community

Bucketing and correlation parameters for commodity exposures used to aggregate risk-class sensitivities into a capital charge.

## How members connect

- Isolated parameter node: it supplies the calibration inputs relied on by the commodity risk calculation rather than imposing a standalone obligation.
- Meaning depends entirely on the upstream commodity risk provisions that define which exposures fall into which bucket.
