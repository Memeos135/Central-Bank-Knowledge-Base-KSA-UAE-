# Rules Governing Calculation of Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

SAMA Rules Governing the Calculation of the Annual Percentage Rate (APR), setting definitions (APR, total amount payable, total cost of finance, amount of finance), scope, the net present value calculation method, and mandatory use of SAMA's Excel-based APR Calculator. Finance providers must align policies, internal controls and audit mechanisms, validate any automated calculator against SAMA's version, and keep customer-facing website calculators updated. They bind banks and finance companies licensed for retail lending, and apply to advertising, offer stage, contracts, periodic statements and any other APR disclosure; they are read with the Finance Companies Control Law, consumer finance regulations and related circulars.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
