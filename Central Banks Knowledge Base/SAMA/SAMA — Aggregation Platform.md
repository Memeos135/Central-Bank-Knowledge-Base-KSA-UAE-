# Aggregation Platform

Defines "Platform" under SAMA's Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity as any electronic means (websites, applications) through which a licensed aggregation entity carries out the activity, and carries the associated operational duties attached to it. These include confidentiality of consumer data obtained via the platform, no disclosure to external parties without prior SAMA approval, security/integrity/availability of information, record retention for at least 10 years after the relationship ends, conflict-of-interest policy, and on-platform disclosure of terms, security instructions and payment methods. It binds entities licensed by SAMA to practice aggregation activity (a finance support activity).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
