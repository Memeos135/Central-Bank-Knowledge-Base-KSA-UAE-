# Scope of Application of Basel Framework

Guidance note setting out SAMA's policy on the scope of application of its Basel Framework in Saudi Arabia, confirming that local banks apply the risk-based capital, leverage, liquidity, large exposure, Pillar 2 and Pillar 3 requirements on both a standalone and consolidated basis, including at holding-company level and every tier of a banking group. It also covers prudential reporting alignment and disclosure templates (e.g. reconciliation of accounting versus regulatory scopes of consolidation). Binds locally incorporated banks and their parent/holding entities supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
