# Finance Support License Guidelines

## Why this community

Entry requirements for firms seeking authorisation to carry on finance support activities, i.e. the documentation, form and substantive conditions SAMA expects in a licence application.

## How members connect

- Single-member community; the guidelines are procedural and operate downstream of the finance-sector licensing framework, so the governing law/regulation for finance support activities must be read alongside them.
- Relevant at the perimeter question: whether a proposed business model falls within 'finance support activities' determines whether this application route, rather than a finance company or payments licence, applies.
