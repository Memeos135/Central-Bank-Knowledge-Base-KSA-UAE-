# IRB Disclosure Templates

## Why this community

Internal ratings-based (IRB) credit risk disclosure and the underlying IRB treatment of corporate, specialised-lending and equity exposures, including model validation evidence.

## How members connect

- The IRB approach rules sit above the exposure-class provisions (corporate, equity) and are the substantive source the CR6, CR9 and CR10 templates report against.
- PD backtesting disclosure is tied to the exposure template, linking model outputs to reported risk parameters - a validation obligation chain, not just a reporting one.
- Equity-exposure treatment and subordinated debt/capital-instrument treatment share definitional boundaries, which determines which template and risk-weight regime applies.
- Slotting disclosure applies where IRB parameter estimation is not permitted, so template choice follows the approach-eligibility rules.
