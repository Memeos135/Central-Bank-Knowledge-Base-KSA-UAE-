# KYC and Deregistered Companies Circular

SAMA circular reminding payment companies of their know-your-customer duties, requiring them to verify the legal/regulatory status of customers served, including validity of official identification documents, and to reflect this in service policies and agreements (e.g. obliging the contracting party to notify changes in its legal status). It invokes the PSP Regulatory Rules provision allowing a payment service provider to refuse payment orders where execution would breach its legal or regulatory obligations, and transmits lists of entities with cancelled/deregistered commercial registers for re-assessment and appropriate action. Binds licensed payment service providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
