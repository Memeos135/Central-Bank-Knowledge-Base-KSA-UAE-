# IRRBB Quantitative Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), requiring banks to publish standardised quantitative measures of economic value and earnings sensitivity under prescribed interest rate shock scenarios.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a fixed-format disclosure template rather than a rule text, so its obligation content is derivative of the IRRBB standard and the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements that mandate its publication.
- Depends on definitions and measurement conventions (banking book scope, shock scenarios, economic value and net interest income metrics) set in the underlying IRRBB framework, not in the template itself.
- Sits at the bottom of the hierarchy: prudential standard -> disclosure framework -> template, with frequency, scope of application and format fixed by the parent disclosure rules.
