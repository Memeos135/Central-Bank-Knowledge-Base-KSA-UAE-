# Non-profit Sector Account Rules

A node grouping non-profit sector account provisions in SAMA's Rules for Bank Accounts with adjacent general account-status rules. The retrieved context covers dormant account treatment (24 months since last customer debit or documented contact, dual supervision for reactivation, permitted incoming credits without changing status, pre-five-year customer contact) and high-risk classification plus conditions for accounts of expatriates on temporary 90-day work visas, including employer undertakings, freezing and closure. It binds licensed banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection
