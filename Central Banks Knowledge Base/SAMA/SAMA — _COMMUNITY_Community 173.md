# PCI-DSS Standards Compliance

## Why this community

Mandated adherence to external (inter)national industry standards, with payment card data security (PCI-DSS) as the named instance relevant to card issuers, acquirers and payment service providers.

## How members connect

- The general obligation to comply with recognised industry standards cross-references PCI-DSS as a specific applicable standard, creating an incorporation-by-reference structure.
- Legal consequence: a third-party/private standard becomes supervisorily enforceable through the SAMA requirement that points to it, including its certification and attestation cadence.
- Cross-regime relevance to the payments/PSP regime and to cardholder data handling, which also engages consumer protection and data confidentiality duties.
