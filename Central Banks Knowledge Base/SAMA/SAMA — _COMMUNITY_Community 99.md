# SAMA Finance Circulars

## Why this community

Supervisory circulars addressed to finance companies covering conduct and prudential expectations across the credit lifecycle: granting policies, outsourced debt collection, real estate secondary-market duties, and SME financial awareness.

## How members connect

- Common addressee and scope: entities licensed under the finance companies regime, so each circular layers onto the same licensing and consumer-protection base.
- Obligation chain follows the credit lifecycle — origination and credit-granting policy, ongoing servicing and disclosure, then collection and transfer/assignment in the secondary market.
- Outsourced collection engages third-party/outsourcing accountability: the financier remains responsible for the service provider's conduct toward the borrower.
- Circulars are the lowest tier — they refine, not replace, the underlying finance regulations; read them as supplemental obligations.
