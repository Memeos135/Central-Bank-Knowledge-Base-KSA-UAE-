# SMS Sender Tag Governance

## Why this community

Governance of SMS sender tag (sender name) usage by supervised entities — controlling impersonation and spoofing risk in customer-facing messaging channels.

## How members connect

- Standalone SAMA circular; its obligations run to supervised entities' registration and controlled use of sender identifiers.
- Legally adjacent to consumer-protection and fraud-prevention duties, and to the unified SMS notification requirements governing message content.
