# Audit Committee Compliance Responsibilities

Node for the Audit Committee's duties and responsibilities toward compliance (Principle Two) under SAMA's Compliance Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies. It matters because the compliance Unit reports directly to the Audit Committee, making the Committee the oversight body for the effectiveness, independence and resourcing of the compliance function. It binds audit committees of SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.
