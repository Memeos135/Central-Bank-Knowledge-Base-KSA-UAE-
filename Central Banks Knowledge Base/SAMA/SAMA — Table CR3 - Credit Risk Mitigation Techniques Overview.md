# Table CR3 - Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques Overview

Template CR3 is a mandatory, fixed-format semiannual Pillar 3 template giving an overview of the extent to which banks use credit risk mitigation, splitting carrying values of loans and debt securities into unsecured exposures and exposures secured by collateral, financial guarantees or credit derivatives, including a defaulted sub-line. All CRM techniques used to reduce capital requirements and all secured exposures must be captured, regardless of whether the standardised or IRB approach is used. It binds all banks, with narrative commentary required on significant period-on-period changes and disclosure of the allocation method for multi-secured exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
