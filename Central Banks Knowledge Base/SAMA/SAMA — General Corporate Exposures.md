# General Corporate Exposures

This node isolates the general corporate exposures subcategory of the corporate exposure class under SAMA's standardized approach for credit risk, i.e. corporate exposures other than specialized lending. It is the residual bucket for entities that do not qualify as bank, sovereign, PSE, retail or real estate exposures and do not meet the specialized lending criteria. It binds banks assigning risk weights to corporate counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
