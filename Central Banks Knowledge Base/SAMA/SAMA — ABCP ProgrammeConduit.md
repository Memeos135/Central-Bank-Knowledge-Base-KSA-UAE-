# ABCP Programme/Conduit

Covers the asset-backed commercial paper conduit and programme, including the defined roles of conduit, programme, assets/asset pool, investor, obligor, seller, sponsor and transaction, and the requirement that short-term STC criteria be satisfied at conduit level and transaction level as applicable. It determines eligibility for the simple, transparent and comparable short-term securitization capital treatment and the treatment of programme-wide credit enhancement and liquidity support. It binds banks acting as sponsor, investor or liquidity provider to ABCP structures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
