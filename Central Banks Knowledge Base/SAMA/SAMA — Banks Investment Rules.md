# Banks Investment Rules

SAMA's Banks Investment Rules, setting the regulatory framework for banks' investment activity, including defined terms distinguishing Local Investment from International Investment (based on where the investment takes place, regardless of currency or issuer residency). The rules impose prudential expectations on concentration, liquidity, documented investment rationale, alignment with the bank's investment strategy, diversification, annual portfolio stress testing, and adequate resources and expertise. They also set approval controls, requiring SAMA non-objection for investments needing regulatory approval under the Banking Control Law and the relevant Ministerial Decree, and conditions on channels such as money market funds licensed by the CMA. They bind banks licensed and operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** To determine whether an entity is in scope of the Banks Investment Rules, apply the Banking Control Law licensing test rather than any commercial notion of 'bank' — the Rules expressly limit themselves to banks licensed under that Law and to investments reported as net investments on the balance sheet. The parent statute therefore fixes both the perimeter and the enforcement basis. Consequently, non-bank financiers or SAMA-licensed entities under other regimes are not captured here, and the reader must confirm licensing status and balance-sheet classification before applying the investment limits.
