# Crisis Management Plan

Node covering the Crisis Management Plan requirement of the SAMA BCM Framework: an approved, monitored plan for responding to major incidents, including criteria for declaring a crisis, a command centre for centralised management, and rapid internal and external stakeholder communication. It connects to the testing requirements, which expect crisis management team activation to be exercised in BCP and DR tests. Binds all SAMA member organizations.
