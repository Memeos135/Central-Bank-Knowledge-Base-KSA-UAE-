# SEC-ERBA STC Risk Weights

This node concerns the alternative SEC-ERBA capital treatment for term and short-term securitizations that meet the STC (simple, transparent and comparable) criteria for capital purposes, sitting within the securitization chapters of SAMA's credit risk capital framework and connected to the Pillar 3 securitization disclosure templates (SEC1–SEC4, Table SECA). It determines preferential risk weights where STC eligibility conditions are met and how such exposures are reported. It binds banks acting as originators, sponsors or investors in securitizations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
