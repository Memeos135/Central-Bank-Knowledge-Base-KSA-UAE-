# Treatment of Clearing Services

Sets the prudential treatment of exposures arising where a bank acts as clearing member providing client clearing services, or as a client clearing through a member or CCP, including capitalisation of client exposures as bilateral trades, the reduced five-day margin period of risk, recognition of client collateral passed to the CCP, and conditions for applying CCP treatment to client-leg exposures. Also covers multi-level client structures and defines initial margin, variation margin and trade exposures. Binds SAMA-licensed banks acting as clearing members or clearing clients.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments
