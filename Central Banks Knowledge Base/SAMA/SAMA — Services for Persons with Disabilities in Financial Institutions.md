# Services for Persons with Disabilities in Financial Institutions

SAMA circular and instructions (January 2020) on services provided to persons with disabilities by financial institutions, aimed at financial inclusion, equal access and removal of physical and behavioural barriers so that such customers can manage their finances independently and privately. Requirements cover establishing a dedicated organisational unit, accessible premises and branches, accessible self-service devices and electronic channels, transparency and fairness in dealings, and financial education. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions, including banks, finance companies, insurers/reinsurers, credit bureaus, exchange businesses and payment companies, with a compliance plan required to SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
