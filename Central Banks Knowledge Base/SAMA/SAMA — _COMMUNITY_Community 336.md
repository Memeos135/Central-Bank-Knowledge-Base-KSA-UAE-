# Cyber Security Review

## Why this community

Periodic cyber security review — the requirement that a member organisation's cyber security posture, controls and framework be subject to independent or internal review and reported to governance bodies.

## How members connect

- Standalone node acting as the assurance counterpart to the cyber risk management lifecycle; it is the mechanism by which control effectiveness is tested rather than a control itself.
- Consequence for decisions: review findings drive remediation timelines and feed supervisory reporting, so gaps here are treated as governance failures, not technical ones.
