# Patch Management

Requires Member Organizations to define, approve, implement and communicate a patch management process so that applicable functional and non-functional patches are installed in a timely manner, reducing technical failures and security breaches arising from unremediated vulnerabilities. Effectiveness of the process must be measured and periodically evaluated. It forms part of the System Change Management chapter and interacts with IT risk management and configuration management controls.
