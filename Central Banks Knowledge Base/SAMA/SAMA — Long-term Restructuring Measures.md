# Long-term Restructuring Measures

Identifies the long-term/permanent restructuring toolkit (arrears and interest capitalisation, interest rate reduction, maturity extension, debt consolidation, rescheduled payments, covenant alterations, sale by owner and similar) as distinct from short-term temporary relief, and situates them within the restructuring options taxonomy. It is paired with guidance on documenting the outcome in a restructuring agreement, whether as a new agreement or an amendment. Binds banks selecting a permanent workout solution matched to the borrower's sustainable repayment capacity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
