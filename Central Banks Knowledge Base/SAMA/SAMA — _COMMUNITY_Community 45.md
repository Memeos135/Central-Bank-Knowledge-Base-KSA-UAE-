# RWA & CVA Disclosure Templates

## Why this community

The aggregation layer of Pillar 1: CVA capital methodologies, total RWA build-up across credit, market, operational and CCR risk, the output floor constraint on internally modelled RWA, and the Pillar 3 disclosure templates that evidence all of it.

## How members connect

- Methodology hierarchy: the CVA framework branches into BA-CVA (reduced and full versions) and SA-CVA, with stand-alone CVA capital, eligible hedges, regulatory CVA and supervisory risk weights as shared defined inputs.
- RWA for credit, market and operational risk are sourced from the respective SAMA minimum capital rulebooks (SCRE, SCCR, SMAR, SOPE) and then aggregated for the output floor calculation.
- The output floor operates as a binding constraint on modelled approaches, which is why the CMS templates compare modelled against standardised RWA.
- The CR, CCR, MR, CVA and OV templates and CRB tables are the disclosure obligations that mirror the underlying calculation rules; defined terms must align between calculation and template.
- Central clearing exposures (clearing member capital requirement, CCP hypothetical capital) attach to the same CCR/credit risk chain covered by the counterparty credit risk cluster.
