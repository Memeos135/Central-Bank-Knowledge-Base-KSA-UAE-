# Workout Plan

Node covering the workout function under SAMA's non-performing loan management guidance: the requirement for banks to maintain a dedicated, permanent Workout Unit segregated from loan origination and reporting into Risk Management, and the principles for selecting and tailoring restructuring options for defaulted borrowers. It sets the pre-conditions a borrower must meet for a restructuring plan (sustainable projected cash flows and cooperative behaviour) and favours out-of-court settlement where documented recovery exceeds foreclosure or bankruptcy outcomes. Binds licensed banks; relevant to their risk governance, staffing/training of workout officers, and to syndicated/multi-creditor situations where dissenting lenders may fall back on the Bankruptcy Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
