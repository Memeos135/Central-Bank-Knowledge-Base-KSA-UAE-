# Non-performing Loan Securitization

This node defines a non-performing loan (NPL) securitization by reference to the delinquency variable W being at or above 90% at origination cut-off and on any subsequent asset addition/removal, limits eligible underlying exposures to loans and loan-equivalent instruments, and bars resecuritization. It reserves SAMA's discretion to impose a stricter definition, precludes SEC-IRBA where the foundation approach is used to compute KIRB, and applies a risk weight floor to NPL securitization exposures. It binds banks holding exposures to NPL securitizations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
