# Expatriate Bank Account Rules

This node covers accounts for expatriates in Saudi Arabia, including the regime for holders of a temporary 90-day work visa with no Iqama: opening on an official employer request specifying employment, purpose and source of funds, in-person meeting with the account holder, employer notification on final exit, and immediate freezing with subsequent treatment under the account-freezing rule. It classifies such accounts as high risk and connects to the dormancy rules (24 months without customer-initiated debit activity). It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, consumer protection
