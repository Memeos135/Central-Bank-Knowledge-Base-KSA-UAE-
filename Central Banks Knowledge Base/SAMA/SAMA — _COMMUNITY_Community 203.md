# ATM Receipt Requirements

## Why this community

Consumer-protection style requirements on the content, availability and retention of ATM transaction receipts issued to customers.

## How members connect

- Standalone initiative-based requirement directed at banks/ATM operators as part of disclosure and transaction-evidence duties.
- Reads across to consumer protection and payments regimes on transaction records and dispute evidence, though not cross-referenced here.
- Obligation is operational and customer-facing: what must be printed/offered, and when.
