# Unified Number Registration

## Why this community

Migration of entity identification in KSA from commercial registration numbers to the Unified Number (including the professional/licensing equivalent for law firms), and the consequence for onboarding, KYB and record-keeping fields used by financial institutions.

## How members connect

- Both items address the same identifier reform: the Unified Number becomes the authoritative entity identifier that supersedes or sits alongside the commercial registration number.
- The lawyers' item is a sectoral application of the general rule, so it should be read as a downstream extension of the replacement instruction rather than a separate regime.
- Practical linkage is through customer/entity due diligence: identification and verification data captured for legal-person Customers must reflect the new identifier, and legacy records must remain reconcilable.
- Explicit cross-reference exists between the two members, confirming hierarchy: general instruction first, profession-specific instruction second.
