# Rating System Design

Concept node defining what constitutes a "rating system" (methods, processes, controls, data and IT supporting credit risk assessment and default/loss estimation) and the design standards it must meet. It requires two distinct rating dimensions for corporate, sovereign and bank exposures (borrower default risk and transaction-specific factors), prohibits cherry-picking between multiple rating systems, and imposes documentation duties covering rating criteria, exceptions, governance, default and loss definitions, and statistical model methodology. Binds banks using IRB approaches under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
