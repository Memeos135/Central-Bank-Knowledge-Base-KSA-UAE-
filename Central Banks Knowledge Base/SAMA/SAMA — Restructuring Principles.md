# Restructuring Principles

Appendix of generic restructuring principles that SAMA encourages banks to adopt as part of their credit culture, covering good faith, transparency and disclosure between bank and borrower, prompt response to borrower proposals, reasonable borrower response time, sustainable rather than repeated short-term solutions, and confidentiality. It also explains the role and drafting of the restructuring agreement, including whether the workout replaces or amends the original loan documentation (especially relevant for MSMEs with simplified documentation). Addressed to banks dealing with distressed borrowers; the principles are hortatory ('encouraged', 'should'), not a closed list.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection
