# Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures Compliance

This node covers the payment-systems rulebook provisions requiring payment system operators to assess and disclose compliance with the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures, including periodic self-assessment by operators of non-designated (unclassified) payment systems on terms agreed with SAMA under their licence. Adjacent articles set financial-resource and capital adequacy expectations (for unclassified systems, resources covering at least six months of highest operating cost plus SAR 10 million paid-up capital) and SAMA's powers to demand information, documents, auditor reports and operating rules from operators and their members. It binds licensed payment system operators (both systemically important and unclassified) and, indirectly, their participants/members, who must cooperate with information requests.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential
