# Written Credit Derivatives

Defines written credit derivatives broadly as any instrument through which a bank effectively sells credit protection, and sets the conditions for offsetting effective notional amounts against purchased credit protection (identical reference name, pari passu or junior reference obligation, same or more conservative material terms, strike price test). Determines the amount added to the leverage ratio exposure measure for sold credit protection. Binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating leverage and counterparty credit exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
