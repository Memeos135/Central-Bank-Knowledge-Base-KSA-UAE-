# Supervisory Haircuts

This node covers the supervisory haircut mechanics within the comprehensive approach to credit risk mitigation under SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk framework, including how exposure and collateral values are volatility-adjusted and how haircut size varies with the prescribed holding period, instrument type, transaction type, residual maturity and remargining/mark-to-market frequency. Adjacent material covers rating-assignment criteria under the IRB supervisory slotting approach and mapping internal grades to the five supervisory categories. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating regulatory capital for credit risk.
