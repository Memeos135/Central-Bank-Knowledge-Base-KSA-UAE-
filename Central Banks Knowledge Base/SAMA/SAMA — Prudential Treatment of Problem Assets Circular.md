# Prudential Treatment of Problem Assets Circular

A SAMA circular to all banks operating in the Kingdom directing adherence to the Basel Committee's guidelines on the prudential treatment of problem assets, covering non-performing loans and forbearance. It addresses harmonised definitions and criteria for asset quality across supervisory reporting, internal ratings-based credit risk management, Pillar 3 disclosure and published asset-quality indicators. It binds banks as a supervisory expectation rather than setting out its own detailed criteria.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
