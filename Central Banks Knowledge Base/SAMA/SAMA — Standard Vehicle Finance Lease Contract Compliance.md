# Standard Vehicle Finance Lease Contract Compliance

A June 2020 circular from SAMA's Finance Companies Supervision Department reinstating the compliance deadline for the standard (model) form of the individual vehicle finance lease contract, originally mandated by circular 41038534 and suspended in April 2020 as a COVID-19 relief measure. Lessors must adopt the model form and complete internal procedures by 30/11/1441H (21 July 2020), and may not conclude contracts that depart from or amend that form. Binds finance companies acting as lessors to natural persons.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
