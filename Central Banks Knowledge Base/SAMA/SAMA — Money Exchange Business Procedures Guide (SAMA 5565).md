# Money Exchange Business Procedures Guide (SAMA 5565)

SAMA's Money Exchange Business Procedures Guide (First Edition, February 2015), issued as a complement to the Ministry of Finance Rules Governing Money Exchange Business and to SAMA's AML/CTF rules for banks and money exchangers. It structures the operational and compliance framework for licensed exchange houses: customer categories (membership customers vs. walk-in/transient customers), customer acceptance policies, KYC and CDD, permitted exchange activities, inbound/outbound domestic and cross-border remittances, bank cheques, cross-border transport of cash and bearer negotiable instruments, correspondent banking relationships, physical security systems, staffing and training, plus monthly reporting annexes on FX sales/purchases and Category (A) remittance flows. It binds Category (A) and Category (B) money exchangers licensed by SAMA; deposit-taking and banking business remain prohibited to them.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
