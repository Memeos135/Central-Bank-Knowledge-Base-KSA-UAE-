# Muslim World League Account Rules

A sub-rule within SAMA's Rules Governing the Opening of Bank Accounts and General Operational Guidelines, addressing accounts for the Muslim World League as a specified international/charitable-type juristic person. It sets the documentary and authorisation conditions under which a bank may open and operate such an account, and how it is to be named, operated and monitored. It binds SAMA-licensed banks conducting account opening and ongoing due diligence, and interacts with AML/CTF customer due diligence and charitable-funds controls.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
