# Full Version of BA-CVA

Defines the full version of BA-CVA, which recognises eligible counterparty credit spread hedges and is intended for banks that actively hedge CVA risk; it incorporates the reduced BA-CVA result to limit hedge recognition. Related rules distinguish eligible from ineligible external and internal CVA hedges and their allocation between the CVA portfolio and the trading book for market risk capital, and permit capping the IRB maturity adjustment factor at 1 for netting sets in the CVA charge. Binds banks using BA-CVA under SAMA's capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
