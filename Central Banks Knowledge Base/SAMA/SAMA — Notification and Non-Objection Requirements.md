# Notification and Non-Objection Requirements

Sets the regulatory gateway before launch: banks meeting the required maturity levels under the Cyber Security, Counter-Fraud, Business Continuity and IT Governance Frameworks (independently validated annually) may proceed by notifying SAMA, while home loans, financial lease, financial derivatives and products not covered by existing SAMA rules require prior non-objection. Both routes carry a 12-month launch window, re-submission on lapse, re-notification/new non-objection for products discontinued over 12 months, SAMA's right to request further information and its power to prohibit a product on safety-and-soundness grounds. It also covers the annual CEO-signed product report to SAMA and documentation/retention duties. Binds licensed banks, with submissions channelled through the compliance function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
