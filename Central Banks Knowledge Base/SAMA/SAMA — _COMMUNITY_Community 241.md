# Securitisation Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Qualitative narrative disclosure on a bank's securitisation activity - roles undertaken (originator, sponsor, investor, servicer), objectives, risk retention and accounting treatment.

## How members connect

- Acts as the qualitative anchor for the securitisation disclosure family: the narrative table that gives context to the quantitative securitisation templates.
- Shares the securitisation defined-term set (originator/sponsor/investor, banking book vs trading book, resecuritisation) with the SEC quantitative templates.
- Hierarchy: Pillar 3 disclosure requirements set the obligation; this table prescribes the minimum narrative content and management judgement to be explained.
