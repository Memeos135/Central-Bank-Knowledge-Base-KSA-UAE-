# Circular: Definition of Commercial Real Estate Finance

A SAMA circular standardising the definition of "commercial real estate finance" as credit granted to a natural or legal person to purchase, develop or build real estate, where at least one of four criteria is met: the beneficiary is a legal person; the financed property has five or more residential units; the property is mainly used for non-residential purposes; or the property is income-generating. Entities must align policies, procedures and regulatory reporting with this definition from the stated reporting period. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential
