# Promissory Notes and Payment Orders

## Why this community

Debt instruments and recurring payment authorisations used by finance entities: how promissory notes may be taken, held and enforced against borrowers, and the controls governing standing payment orders on customer accounts.

## How members connect

- Both address instruments by which a finance entity secures or collects repayment from a Consumer/borrower, and both sit under SAMA's supervisory instructions for finance entities.
- Shared obligation chain: disclosure and consent at contracting, limits on the amount and use of the instrument, and constraints on enforcement/deduction without fresh customer authorisation.
- Cross-regime interaction with consumer protection principles (fair treatment, transparency) and with finance company conduct rules on collection practices.
