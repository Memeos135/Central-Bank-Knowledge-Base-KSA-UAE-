# Agent Registry (Article 29)

Article 29 of SAMA's Agent Banking regulation establishes an electronic agent registry maintained by SAMA into which banks must enter prescribed details for every agent acting on their behalf, including relationship start date, agent name and business name, address and geographic coordinates, contact details, core business and years of operation, and the agent banking contract. Registration is a precondition to conducting agent banking: unregistered activity exposes the bank and agent to SAMA enforcement, and banks must refresh changed agent data within ten business days. The obligation binds licensed banks, which remain responsible for the agent's compliance with the regulation, including consumer protection requirements; the adjacent Article 30 requires an annual board-approved report to SAMA covering transaction volumes, ML/TF cases, fraud/theft incidents, monitoring visit results and customer complaints.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF, consumer protection
