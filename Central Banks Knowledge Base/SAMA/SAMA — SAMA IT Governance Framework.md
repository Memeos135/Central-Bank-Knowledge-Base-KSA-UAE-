# SAMA IT Governance Framework

Node anchoring the Governance domain of the Counter-Fraud Framework and its interaction with SAMA's wider governance instruments: the Board and executive leadership are accountable for the Counter-Fraud Programme, comprising a Counter-Fraud Strategy, Counter-Fraud Policy and a Governance Structure proportionate to the organisation's size and complexity. It requires a dedicated Counter-Fraud Governance Committee chaired by an Executive Committee member, with minimum representation including the head of counter-fraud, CRO, COO and head of digital. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organisations and should be read with SAMA's IT governance and cyber security frameworks.
