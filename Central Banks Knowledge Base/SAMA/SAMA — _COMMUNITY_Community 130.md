# Electronic Notarization Circulars

## Why this community

Acceptance of digitised and electronically authenticated legal instruments — notarised deeds, electronically notarised company formation contracts, and the removal of wet-stamp formalities — in onboarding and documentation by supervised entities.

## How members connect

- All three circulars address the same evidentiary question: what documentary form a financial institution must accept as valid proof of legal capacity or authority.
- They are conceptually chained rather than hierarchical — each progressively relaxes a formality (licensed-notary deeds, e-notarised LLC contracts, abolition of the official stamp).
- Direct consequence for KYB/customer due diligence and corporate onboarding: an entity may not refuse a document solely for lacking a traditional physical formality covered by these circulars.
- Scope should be read narrowly to the document types named; they do not displace underlying identification and verification obligations.
