# Calculation of the Output Floor

This node covers SAMA's Output Floor Requirements and the mechanics of computing the floor, which caps the capital relief banks can obtain from internal models by referencing capital requirements derived under standardised approaches. It sets out the instrument's legal basis (Central Bank Law and Banking Control Law), scope, effective date of 1 January 2023 with transitional phase-in, quarterly reporting to SAMA within 30 days of quarter-end, and the row-by-row composition of risk categories feeding the floor calculation. It binds all domestic banks on both a consolidated and standalone basis, and expressly excludes branches of foreign banks operating in the Kingdom, which follow their home regulator's capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
