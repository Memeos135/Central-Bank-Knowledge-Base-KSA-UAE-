# IRB Retail Exposures

This node covers the retail exposure class within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk, setting out how retail exposures are defined and segmented into regulatory retail (transactor and non-transactor) and other retail. It fixes the qualifying criteria — product type, an absolute aggregated exposure ceiling per counterparty, and a granularity limit relative to the overall regulatory retail portfolio — and the corresponding risk weights; real estate exposures sit outside the class and non-qualifying MSME exposures fall back to corporate treatment. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating risk-weighted assets for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
