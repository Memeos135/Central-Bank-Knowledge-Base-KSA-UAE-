# FATF

A defined-term node for the Financial Action Task Force in the Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers, described as the inter-governmental standard-setter for AML, CTF and proliferation financing measures. Operationally the Guide uses FATF as a risk trigger: enhanced scrutiny of funds to or from countries subject to FATF warnings, treatment of FATF-listed non-compliant jurisdictions as high risk for due diligence, and contractual obligations on transfer providers to observe FATF and UN warning notices. It is relevant to banks' remittance centers, licensed remittance companies and their contracted transfer services providers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments
