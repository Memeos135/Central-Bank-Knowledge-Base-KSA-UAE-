# Internal Audit Compliance Responsibilities

Node for the internal audit department's responsibilities toward compliance (Principle Seven) under SAMA's Compliance Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies. It addresses how internal audit provides independent assurance over the compliance function and the company's adherence to laws and SAMA instructions, delineating third-line assurance from the second-line compliance Unit. It binds internal audit departments of SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.
