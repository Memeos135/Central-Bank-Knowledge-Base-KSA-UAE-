# Currency Reproduction Controls

## Why this community

Restrictions on reproducing, imitating or depicting Saudi currency (images, advertising, souvenirs, digital renderings) to protect currency integrity and prevent counterfeiting confusion.

## How members connect

- Standalone control set: obligations attach to any person or entity reproducing currency, not only SAMA-supervised institutions.
- Operates alongside criminal counterfeiting law; the controls set permission and format conditions rather than penalties.
- No internal cross-references in this cluster — assess it as a discrete compliance duty for marketing and communications activity.
