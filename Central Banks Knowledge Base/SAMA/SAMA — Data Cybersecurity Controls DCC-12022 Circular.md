# Data Cybersecurity Controls DCC-1:2022 Circular

SAMA circular directing supervised financial institutions (issued via the payment systems and companies supervision channel) to adopt the National Cybersecurity Authority's Data Cybersecurity Controls (DCC-1:2022), which extend and complement the Essential Cybersecurity Controls. It relies on the SAMA Cyber Security Framework provisions on regulatory compliance and compliance with (inter)national industry standards, and requires continuous compliance, a self-assessment using the attached assessment/compliance-measurement tool, and full implementation by end-September 2023. Binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA; queries routed to SAMA's cyber risk supervision function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
