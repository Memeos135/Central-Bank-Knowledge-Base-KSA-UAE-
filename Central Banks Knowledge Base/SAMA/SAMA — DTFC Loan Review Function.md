# DTFC Loan Review Function

Chapter 6 (Asset Quality) requirement that each DTFC operate a loan review function assuring that lending conforms to a board-approved written lending policy, that management and the board are properly informed of credit risk and control effectiveness, that problem accounts are identified and classified on time, and that adequate provisions are maintained. It links directly to the quarterly review/classification duty and the Appendix C classification criteria. Binds every deposit-taking finance company and its board.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, BNPL/finance
