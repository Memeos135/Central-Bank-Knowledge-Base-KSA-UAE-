# Template REM2 - Special Payments

Template REM2 is the quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of special payments made during the financial year — guaranteed bonuses, sign-on awards and severance payments — split by number of employees and total amount for senior management and other material risk-takers. Employee categories must match those described in Table REMA. Mandatory for all banks, annual, flexible format, with optional narrative on significant movements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
