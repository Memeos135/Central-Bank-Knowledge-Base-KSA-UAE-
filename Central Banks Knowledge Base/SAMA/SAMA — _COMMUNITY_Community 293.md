# Electronic Bank Document Verification

## Why this community

Rules on issuing bank documents electronically and enabling reliable verification of their authenticity by recipients. The regulatory concern is document fraud, evidentiary reliability and customer convenience in dealings with third parties and government bodies.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an operational instruction to banks on the form and verifiability of documents they issue (certificates, statements, confirmations).
- Connects to consumer protection principles on accurate, accessible and timely provision of records to customers.
- Touches the evidentiary/authentication dimension relied on by counterparties, courts and authorities, and so indirectly supports information-request and verification duties elsewhere in the framework.
