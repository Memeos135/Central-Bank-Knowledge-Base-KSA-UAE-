# Non-resident Juristic Persons Rules

Section 300.2 of SAMA's bank account opening rules, setting the categories and conditions for accounts of non-resident juristic persons — including GCC non-banking companies, non-GCC companies with or without contracts or projects in Saudi Arabia, non-resident banks, international investment companies and funds, insurance companies, money changers and foreign payment card companies. Each sub-category carries distinct permitted account purposes and documentary requirements. It binds SAMA-licensed banks and shapes cross-border customer onboarding and correspondent-type relationships.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, governance/risk
