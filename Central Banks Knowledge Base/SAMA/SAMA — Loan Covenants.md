# Loan Covenants

Part of the restructuring toolkit guidance describing loan restructuring options and additional measures available to banks, including loan splitting, loan-to-asset swaps, interest rate reduction, maturity extension and rescheduled payments (partial, balloon/bullet, step-up), together with cautions on new money and roll-overs and the use of independent sector experts to validate business plans and cash-flow projections. Additional measures are presented as supplements that do not on their own reduce the exposure. Relevant to banks structuring workout terms and the conditions/covenants attached to them.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
