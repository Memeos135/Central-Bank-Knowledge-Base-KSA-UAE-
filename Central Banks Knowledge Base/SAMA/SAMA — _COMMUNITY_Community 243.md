# Asset Encumbrance Template

## Why this community

Disclosure of encumbered versus unencumbered assets, relevant to assessing how much of a bank's balance sheet is available to unsecured creditors.

## How members connect

- Single isolated template; it operates as a standalone Pillar 3 disclosure item with no internal cross-references captured here.
- Read alongside the main Pillar 3 disclosure framework, which sets the mandate, format and timing for this template.
