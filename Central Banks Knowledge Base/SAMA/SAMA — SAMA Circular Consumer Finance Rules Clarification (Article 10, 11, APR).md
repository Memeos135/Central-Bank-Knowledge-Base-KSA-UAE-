# SAMA Circular: Consumer Finance Rules Clarification (Article 10, 11, APR)

SAMA circular (Arabic) clarifying three points in the Updated Consumer Finance Rules: the consumer's right to terminate or withdraw from the finance agreement (Article 10-1), the initial disclosure requirement (Article 11-2), and the method for calculating the Annual Percentage Rate. It states that the withdrawal/termination right ends within ten days of contract signature where the consumer has already benefited from the financing — for example by drawing any part of the cash finance amount, having shares deposited in the consumer's portfolio, or taking ownership or use of the financed asset — and requires use of SAMA's published initial-disclosure form and APR guidance manual. Addressed to banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
