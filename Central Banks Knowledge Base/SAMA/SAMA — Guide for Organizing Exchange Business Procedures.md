# Guide for Organizing Exchange Business Procedures

A SAMA circular issuing a guide organising the operating procedures of money exchange businesses, grounded in the Rules Regulating the Practice of Exchange Business (Ministerial Decision 1350) and the SAMA licensing requirement. It distinguishes Category A exchangers (permitted, where previously licensed, to carry out domestic and cross-border money transfer) from Category B (purchase and sale of foreign currency, travellers' cheques and bank cheques), and requires compliance with related SAMA instructions and the AML/CTF rules. It binds all licensed Category A and B exchange houses in the Kingdom from two months after the circular date.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk
