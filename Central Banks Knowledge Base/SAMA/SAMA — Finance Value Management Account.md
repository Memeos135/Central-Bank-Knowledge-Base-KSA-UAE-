# Finance Value Management Account

A defined-term node for the "Finance Value Management Account" – the pooled account holding participants' funds at a debt-based crowdfunding entity, which SAMA requires to be named "Finance Value Management Account – [name of the debt-based crowdfunding entity]". Its purpose is restricted to managing the finance value, it must be segregated from the entity's own funds and free of any charge or claim arising from the entity's own obligations, and its opening or closing must be notified to SAMA. Operation is subject to a written policy, a board-approved authority matrix, multi-level control, senior management reporting and documented reconciliation.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
