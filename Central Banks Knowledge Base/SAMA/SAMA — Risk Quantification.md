# Risk Quantification

A prudential node combining market-risk framework material on internal risk transfers (the conditions under which banking-book/trading-book and inter-desk hedges receive regulatory capital recognition, including the SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk) with the market risk glossary of quantification concepts such as risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, risk class, sensitivity and delta risk. It governs how banks identify and measure the drivers of value change used in market risk capital requirements. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating minimum capital requirements for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
