# Template CDC - Capital Distribution Constraints

Template CDC is a mandatory Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to publish the capital ratio thresholds at which capital distribution constraints are triggered, covering risk-based CET1 (including buffers, G-SIB surcharge, countercyclical buffer and Pillar 2 add-ons), TLAC-related requirements, and the leverage ratio inclusive of its buffer. It is annual, fixed-format, and requires narrative description of any constraints actually imposed plus a link to SAMA's published buffer/stacking framework. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
