# Market Risk Stress Testing

Addresses market risk stress testing under SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing: banks must test the resilience of on- and off-balance sheet positions exposed to adverse movements in interest rates, FX rates, equity prices, credit spreads and commodity prices. It breaks market risk into component factors, notably interest rate risk (re-pricing, basis, yield curve and options risk), with duration GAP or equivalent models as possible techniques. FAQ material clarifies that SAMA will not supply a covariance matrix or common multifactor methodology; banks choose techniques suited to their own risk profile, and stress effects are applied as at the reporting date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
