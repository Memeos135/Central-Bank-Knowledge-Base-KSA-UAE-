# POS Devices 4G Technology Upgrade

SAMA circular requiring the upgrade of point-of-sale (POS) terminals operating in the Kingdom to at least 4G connectivity, following CITC's decision to sunset 2G/3G networks (3G end-2022; 2G during Q4 2024). It obliges POS suppliers to supply 4G-capable devices and obtain technical certification from Saudi Payments, requires all new and replacement installations to be 4G-capable from January 2023, and sets a minimum monthly replacement rate for existing terminals with progress reporting to SAMA and Saudi Payments. It binds banks, payment service providers/sponsoring entities and Saudi Payments.

**Regimes:** payments
