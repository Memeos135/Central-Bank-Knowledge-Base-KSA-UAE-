# NPL Strategy

Requires banks to develop and implement a Board-approved NPL strategy setting the bank's approach, objectives and annual quantitative targets across short, medium and long-term horizons, acting as a roadmap for resource allocation and controls. The strategy must consider all resolution options (hold/restructure, portfolio sales, write-offs of unrecoverable provisioned NPLs, taking collateral onto balance sheet, legal and out-of-court routes) applying proportionality and materiality, and be subject to regular review and integration into the risk management framework, with unit and team incentives aligned to its targets.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
