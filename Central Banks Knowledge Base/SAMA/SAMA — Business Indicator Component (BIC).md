# Business Indicator Component (BIC)

The Business Indicator Component (BIC) is the Business Indicator transformed by the regulatory marginal coefficients and is the base figure for the standardised operational risk capital requirement. It is also the benchmark against which the Loss Component is compared to derive the Internal Loss Multiplier, and banks lacking sufficient high-quality loss data must calculate capital on the BIC alone. It applies to SAMA-supervised banks for both capital calculation and annual Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
