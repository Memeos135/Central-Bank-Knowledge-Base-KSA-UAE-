# Internal Models Approach Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure specific to banks permitted to use the internal models approach (IMA) for market risk capital, covering model scope, validation and supervisory approval.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a qualitative table that applies only to banks with supervisory permission to use internal models, so its scope is narrower than the general market risk disclosure.
- Conditional obligation chain: model approval under the capital adequacy/market risk rules triggers the enhanced disclosure duty in this table.
- Cross-references the general market risk qualitative disclosure, which it supplements rather than replaces.
- Relies on defined terms for model scope and validation drawn from the market risk capital requirements.
