# Publication of Banking Penalties Instructions (SAMA 5544)

SAMA circular (29/03/1439H) issuing Instructions on Publication of Banking Penalties, requiring banks to disclose in their annual reports, under a dedicated heading for SAMA penal decisions, the violations for which penal decisions were issued — showing the violation subject, number of decisions and total fines in SAR for the current and prior financial year (current year only in the first year of application). Violation subjects must be stated using the attached classification guide, which maps specific penal-decision grounds to published categories such as breach of supervisory instructions, consumer protection instructions, due diligence, AML/CTF due diligence, and ATM/POS performance standards. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom; SAMA will republish the disclosed data on its website.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF, banking prudential
