# Basel III: Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Monitoring Tools Jan 2013

The BCBS January 2013 standard "Basel III: The Liquidity Coverage Ratio and liquidity risk monitoring tools", used as the source text that SAMA's amended LCR guidance mirrors and cites by paragraph. It sets the eligibility criteria and haircuts for Level 1, Level 2A and Level 2B assets (including corporate debt, covered bonds and qualifying common equity) and the rules for jurisdictions with insufficient HQLA. It binds Saudi banks indirectly, as adopted and modified through SAMA's circular — notably SAMA currently does not recognise Level 2B assets in LCR computation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
