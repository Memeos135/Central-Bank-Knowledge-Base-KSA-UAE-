# Backtesting Green/Amber/Red Zones

Concept node for the backtesting traffic-light classification (green, amber/yellow, red zones) used to assess internal model performance against realised outcomes and to drive multiplier add-ons or model use restrictions. The retrieved context is dominated by credit risk mitigation disclosure material (Template CR3), so the backtesting zone provisions themselves must be confirmed in the underlying market risk/internal models sections. Applies to banks with SAMA-approved internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
