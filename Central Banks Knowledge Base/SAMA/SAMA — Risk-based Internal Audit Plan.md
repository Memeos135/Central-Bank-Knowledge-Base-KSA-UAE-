# Risk-based Internal Audit Plan

Refers to the eighth principle of SAMA's Internal Audit Principles, on the internal audit plan (with related provisions on department reports and working policies/procedures). It governs how audit coverage is planned, approved and reported for a finance or real estate refinance company. Binds licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies, with approval/oversight sitting at board and audit committee level.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

- **What this link tells you:** In judging whether an institution's audit coverage is sufficient, the risk-based audit plan provisions are the operative standard against which the internal audit department's work is measured. The plan requirements are drafted by reference to the department's mandate in the same instrument, so the department's scope, resourcing and independence determine whether a credible risk-based plan can be delivered. Practically, a plan that omits material risk areas, or a department without the resources to execute the approved plan, is a defect in the internal audit function as a whole and should be escalated for governance-level approval of any deviation.
