# Attachment (A) and Detachment (D) Points

This node defines the attachment point (A) and detachment point (D) used to locate a tranche in the loss waterfall and thereby determine its risk weight under the securitization internal-ratings-based approach (SEC-IRBA), with worked tranche examples including dilution-risk scenarios. It governs how tranche thickness and seniority feed into RWA calculation. It applies to banks using SEC-IRBA for securitization exposures under SAMA's credit risk capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
