# Circular: Definition of Small and Medium Enterprises

A Banking Policies Department circular adopting the unified definition of micro, small, medium and large enterprises based on the decision of the Small and Medium Enterprises General Authority (Monsha'at), using revenue bands as the primary classification criterion and full-time headcount where revenue data is unavailable (e.g. new entities). It provides the common classification used for SME-related reporting and credit categorisation. It binds banks, finance companies operating in the Kingdom and the licensed credit information companies (SIMAH and Bayan).
