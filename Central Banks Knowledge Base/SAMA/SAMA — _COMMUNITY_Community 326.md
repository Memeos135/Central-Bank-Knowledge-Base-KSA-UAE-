# Cyber Security Audits

## Why this community

Independent assurance over cyber security: periodic audit and review of the member organisation's cyber security controls, with findings reported to the audit committee/board and remediated within defined timelines.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the audit function sits in the assurance layer of the SAMA cyber security governance regime, distinct from first-line operations and second-line risk/compliance.
- Linked upward to board and audit committee oversight duties and to SAMA's supervisory reporting expectations on cyber risk.
- Downstream dependency on the control domains it tests (asset, HR, physical and access controls), so audit scope inherits those domains' defined terms.
