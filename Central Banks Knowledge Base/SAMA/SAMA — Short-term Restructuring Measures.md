# Short-term Restructuring Measures

Covers short-term (temporary) restructuring measures — relief granted for an identified, evidenced short-term cash-flow disruption or to give creditors time to assess the situation — and the expectation that the borrower resumes the existing payment schedule at the end of the period, with a tenor cap (24 months, shorter in certain cases). It also addresses the restructuring agreement as the governing workout document, including the choice between a replacement agreement and an amendment to the original facility. Applies to banks restructuring borrower exposures, with simplified documentation contemplated for MSMEs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
