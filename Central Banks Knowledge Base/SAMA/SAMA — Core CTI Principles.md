# Core CTI Principles

The foundational tier of SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, covering the planning, production, processing and dissemination of threat intelligence that underpins the strategic, operational and technical/tactical tiers. It includes duties such as classifying shared information under the Traffic Light Protocol per SAMA's Cybersecurity Communication Protocols, delivering actionable intelligence with defined actions/owners/timeframes, internal sharing with SOC and IT, and continuous improvement. Applies to Member Organizations, including where the CTI capability is outsourced; phasing of implementation is at the organization's discretion.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
