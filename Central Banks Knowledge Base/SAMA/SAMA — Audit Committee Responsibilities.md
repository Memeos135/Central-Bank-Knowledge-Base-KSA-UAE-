# Audit Committee Responsibilities

A node within SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies (issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation), covering the second principle: the audit committee's duties toward the internal audit function. It addresses the committee's oversight role — including matters such as approving/recommending audit policy, plan and reporting lines, and safeguarding the department's independence and objectivity. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies at board/committee level.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

- **What this link tells you:** For decisions about internal audit independence, reporting lines, resourcing or appointment and removal of the head of internal audit, the audit committee's responsibilities and the internal audit department's requirements form a single obligation chain and must be applied together. The committee provisions place oversight duties over the function, while the department provisions set what the function itself must do, so compliance cannot be demonstrated from one side alone. Consequently, an internal audit arrangement that reports functionally to management rather than to the audit committee is a governance defect under the committee provisions even if the department's own mandate appears satisfied.
