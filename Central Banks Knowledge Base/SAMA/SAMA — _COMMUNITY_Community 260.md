# CCR RWA Flow Statements

## Why this community

Flow-statement disclosure of movements in risk-weighted assets for counterparty credit risk exposures calculated under the Internal Model Method, aimed at explaining period-on-period RWA changes.

## How members connect

- Isolated template whose obligation depends on the bank having supervisory approval to use IMM, making scope conditional on model permission rather than on other templates.
- Shares RWA definitions and reconciliation logic with the wider capital adequacy disclosure hierarchy (framework requirement to template).
