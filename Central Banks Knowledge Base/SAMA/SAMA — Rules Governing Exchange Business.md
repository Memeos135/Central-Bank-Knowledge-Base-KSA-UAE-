# Rules Governing Exchange Business

This node concerns the Rules Governing Exchange Business (issued by Ministerial Decision) as invoked by a SAMA circular updating the supervisory return templates for money exchange centres. It relies on the Rules' powers requiring an exchange centre to supply SAMA with any data or reports it requests at the times it specifies, and allowing SAMA to issue instructions necessary to apply supervisory requirements to exchange business. The circular binds licensed exchange centres in the Kingdom to submit monthly (sales/purchases), quarterly (currencies, largest customers, counterfeit currency, staff data, financial statement comparison) and annual returns through SAMA's Returns Management System within set deadlines, on pain of enforcement action.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other, AML/CTF
