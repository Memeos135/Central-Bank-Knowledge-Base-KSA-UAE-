# Clearing Member Default Fund Capital (KCMi)

Sets the risk-sensitive capital requirement (KCMi) that a clearing member bank must hold against its default fund contribution to a qualifying CCP, calculated in two steps: first the CCP's hypothetical capital requirement (KCCP), then the allocation to the individual clearing member. The risk weight reflects the size and quality of the CCP's financial resources, its counterparty credit risk exposures, and the operation of its loss-bearing waterfall on member default. Binds SAMA-supervised banks acting as clearing members of QCCPs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
