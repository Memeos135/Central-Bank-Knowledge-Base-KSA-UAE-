# Circular: Providing Financial Statements and Internal Control Reports

SAMA circular to money exchange centres setting out reporting obligations under Articles 15(c), 17 and 19 of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business: quarterly financial position within 15 days of the following quarter plus externally reviewed quarterly statements within 30 days, and audited annual balance sheet within one month with audited annual financial statements within two months of financial year-end. It also caps the external auditor's appointment at five years, requires an annual external auditor assessment of internal control systems submitted separately from the financial statements within two months of year-end, and prescribes submission channels. Binds licensed money exchange centres from financial year 2014, with SAMA reserving enforcement action for breaches.
