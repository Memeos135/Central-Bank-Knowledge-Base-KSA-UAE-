# IRB Approach: Risk Weight Functions

Concept node for the IRB risk weight functions — the prescribed formulas converting risk components into risk weights and RWA for exposures treated under the IRB approaches. In the Pillar 3 context it underlies the modelled-RWA figures disclosed in the overview and credit risk templates, including the equity-exposure phase-in floor where the IRB-derived risk weight is compared against the standardised phase-in weight. Relevant to SAMA-licensed banks computing IRB credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
