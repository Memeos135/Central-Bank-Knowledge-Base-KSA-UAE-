# Default Fund Exposures

Covers exposures arising from a bank's pre-funded and unfunded contributions to a CCP's default fund, and their capitalisation and disclosure separately from trade exposures and initial margin. The CCR8 template requires segregated reporting of these contributions split between qualifying and non-qualifying CCPs. Binds SAMA-supervised banks that are clearing members contributing to CCP mutualised loss-sharing arrangements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
