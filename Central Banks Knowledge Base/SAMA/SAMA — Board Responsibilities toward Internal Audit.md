# Board Responsibilities toward Internal Audit

Node for the Board's duties and responsibilities toward the internal audit function (Principle One) under SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies. It covers the Board's role in approving the internal audit charter and safeguarding the function's independence, resourcing and standing relative to the Audit Committee and executive management. It binds boards of SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.
