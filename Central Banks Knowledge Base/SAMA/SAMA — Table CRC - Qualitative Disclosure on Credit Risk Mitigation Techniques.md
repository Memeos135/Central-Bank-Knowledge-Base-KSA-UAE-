# Table CRC - Qualitative Disclosure on Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques

Table CRC is the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on credit risk mitigation techniques, forming the CRM block of the credit risk section together with Template CR3. It requires narrative explanation of how the bank uses collateral, guarantees and credit derivatives to reduce credit risk and capital requirements, complementing the quantitative CRM figures. It applies to banks disclosing under the SAMA Pillar 3 framework; related instructions distinguish the simple and comprehensive approaches to CRM under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
