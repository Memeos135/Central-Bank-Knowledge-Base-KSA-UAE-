# General Interest Rate Risk (GIRR)

Concept node for General Interest Rate Risk (GIRR), one of the defined market risk classes used to calculate market risk capital requirements and a key reference point in the internal risk transfer rules between banking book and trading book. It governs how interest-rate-driven risk positions are identified, allocated to a dedicated SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk, and recognised for regulatory capital. Binds banks calculating minimum market risk capital under the SAMA framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
