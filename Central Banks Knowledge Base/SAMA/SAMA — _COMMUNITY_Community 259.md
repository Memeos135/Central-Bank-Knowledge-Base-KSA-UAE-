# Credit Derivatives Exposures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 counterparty credit risk disclosure for credit derivative positions, requiring banks to present protection bought and sold separately so supervisors and market participants can assess CCR concentration and transfer of credit risk.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster sitting within the counterparty credit risk (CCR) disclosure series, so its linkage is hierarchical: framework requirement down to prescribed reporting template.
- Relies on CCR definitions and exposure measurement concepts shared across the CCR template family rather than on internal cross-references.
