# Compliance with Advertising and Marketing Instructions

Short SAMA circular reaffirming the existing advertising and marketing instructions, which require promotional material for products and services to be clear and free of wording that directly or indirectly misleads customers. Issued after SAMA observed non-compliant promotional advertisements, it directs financial institutions to review all promotional advertising for conformity and warns that statutory action will be taken against violators. It binds banks, finance companies and payment companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
