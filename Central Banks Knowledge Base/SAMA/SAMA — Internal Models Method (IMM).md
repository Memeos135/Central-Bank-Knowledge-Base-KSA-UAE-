# Internal Models Method (IMM)

Concept node on the Internal Models Method for measuring exposure/EAD for counterparty credit risk, and on internal models governance more generally. Use of IMM requires prior SAMA approval, satisfaction of the stated qualitative and quantitative requirements, and application across all CCR exposures (or all exposures within an approved category such as OTC derivatives or SFTs), with a transition plan for any portion still on the standardised approach. Related model-governance duties (independent risk control unit, backtesting/P&L attribution, independent model validation, board and senior management involvement) bind banks using internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
