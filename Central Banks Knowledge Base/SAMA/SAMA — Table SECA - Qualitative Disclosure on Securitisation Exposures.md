# Table SECA - Qualitative Disclosure on Securitisation Exposures

The qualitative disclosure table opening the securitisation section of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, requiring banks to describe their securitisation activities, roles (originator, sponsor, investor, servicer) and related risk management and accounting policies. It covers all securitisation exposures, not only those treated under the securitisation capital framework, and excludes trading book securitisation capital charges reported elsewhere. Binds SAMA-supervised banks with securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
