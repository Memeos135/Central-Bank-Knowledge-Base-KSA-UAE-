# SAMA Authorization of DTFCs

Node within the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020 keyed to SAMA's authorization of DTFCs, with the retrieved material drawn from the account-opening and Know Your Customer chapters. It covers KYC programme design (customer acceptance policy, identification, ongoing monitoring of high-risk accounts), prohibitions on anonymous, numbered, fictitious or inaccurate-name accounts, refusal to open or transact where KYC doubts exist with referral to SAMA's Financial Investigation Unit, account-holder acknowledgements on source of funds and data updating, and the bar on fully remote opening of a first General Account. It binds licensed deposit-taking finance companies and, indirectly, their customers through the account agreement.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
