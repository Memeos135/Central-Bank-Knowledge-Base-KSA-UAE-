# Off-Balance Sheet Items

Addresses the capital treatment of off-balance sheet items, including the application of credit conversion factors (CCFs) under the standardized approach and the estimation of exposure at default (EAD) for such items under the advanced IRB approach. Sets out that EAD must be estimated as the expected gross exposure at obligor default with a margin of conservatism, and specifies when own estimates versus foundation CCFs apply. Binds banks subject to SAMA's credit risk capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

The sequencing matters because eligible protection is applied to the converted exposure amount, not to a notional or unconverted figure, and a credit derivative that fails the operational conditions gives no capital relief.
