# Finite Risk Insurance

A concept node describing finite risk insurance as an external risk financing option that blends conventional risk transfer with self-insurance and carries an upper limit on the insurer's exposure. It sits alongside captives, retention groups and pools in the guidance's catalogue of alternatives to pure conventional cover. Relevant to bank senior management choosing operational risk financing structures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
