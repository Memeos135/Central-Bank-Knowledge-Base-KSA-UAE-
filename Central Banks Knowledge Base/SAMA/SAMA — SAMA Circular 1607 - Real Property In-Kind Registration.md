# SAMA Circular 1607 - Real Property In-Kind Registration

A SAMA circular relaying the General Real Estate Authority's designation of Al-Falah district in Riyadh as the first real estate zone under the Real Estate In-Kind Registration Law (Royal Decree M/14) and its implementing regulation. It instructs financing entities holding rights in a designated zone to treat the ownership registration deed issued by the General Real Estate Authority as the authoritative title document there, and places on them an ongoing duty to monitor subsequent Authority announcements. It is addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other
