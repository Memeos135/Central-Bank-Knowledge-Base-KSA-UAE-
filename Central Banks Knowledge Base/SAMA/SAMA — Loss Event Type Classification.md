# Loss Event Type Classification

Covers how operational loss events are identified, classified and treated for inclusion in the standardized approach loss data set under SAMA's operational risk capital framework. It addresses grouping of losses arising from a common or related operational risk events, allocation across accounting years, the accounting-date reference for legal loss events, and the required policies and procedures for classification. Binding on domestic banks; removal of events from the loss component requires strong justification and SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
