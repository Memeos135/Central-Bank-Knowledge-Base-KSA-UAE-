# Circular: Registering White Land Fee Properties

A SAMA circular issued by the General Department for Supervision of Finance Companies directing banks and licensed finance companies to register land plots subject to the White Land Fees Law within the prescribed registration window, so that beneficial/real owners are not exposed to financial penalties. It is an operational compliance instruction tied to the White Land Fees Law and its Implementing Regulation rather than a substantive prudential rule. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other
