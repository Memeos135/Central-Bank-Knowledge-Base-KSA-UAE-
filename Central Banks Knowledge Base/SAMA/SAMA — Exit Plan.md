# Exit Plan

A licensing-application component of SAMA's additional requirements for Digital-only Banks, requiring an applicant to submit an exit plan setting out how customer funds, ongoing business, assets and liabilities would be handled if the targeted business objectives are not achieved (e.g. migration of accounts and continuity of service). It sits alongside the guidelines' adjacent expectations on prudential/supervisory treatment (same prudential requirements as other banks, plus electronic data accessible for supervision) and consumer protection (SAMA's Banking Consumer Protection Principles apply, with adequate customer support channels across the banking life cycle). It binds applicants for, and holders of, a Digital-only Bank licence in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection
