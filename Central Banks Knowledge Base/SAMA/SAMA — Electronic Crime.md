# Electronic Crime

Concept node covering electronic and computer crime as an operational risk element and its treatment in insurance-based risk transfer under SAMA's operational risk guidance. It describes Electronic and Computer Crime (ECC) cover — responding to third-party fraud or unexplained loss involving computer and telecommunications systems, and available only alongside a Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond — and situates it among D&O and professional indemnity covers. Relevant to banks' operational risk managers and boards deciding on retention versus transfer of e-crime exposure.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments
