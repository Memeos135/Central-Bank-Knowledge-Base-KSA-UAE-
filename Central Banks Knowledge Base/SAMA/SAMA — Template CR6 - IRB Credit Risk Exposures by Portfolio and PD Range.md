# Template CR6 - IRB Credit Risk Exposures by Portfolio and PD Range

A Pillar 3 template requiring semiannual disclosure of IRB credit risk exposures broken down by regulatory portfolio and PD scale, with the key parameters driving RWA (EAD post-CRM, average PD, number of obligors, average LGD, average maturity, RWA and expected loss). Portfolio rows reflect the exposure categories set by SAMA for local IRB implementation; a parallel structure applies to counterparty credit risk under CCR4. It binds banks approved to use F-IRB or A-IRB approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
