# Template CMS1 - Modelled vs Standardised RWA

Pillar 3 'capital floor / comparison of modelled and standardised' template requiring banks to disclose, by risk category, RWA arising from internally modelled approaches that SAMA has approved, RWA from standardised approaches, total actual RWA, and the RWA that would result if the full standardised approach were applied to all exposures. It covers credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation, market risk, operational risk and residual RWA. Relevant to banks using IRB or other model-based approaches under SAMA approval and subject to the output/capital floor.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
