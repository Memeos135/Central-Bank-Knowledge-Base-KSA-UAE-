# Complaint Management System Phase 1

A SAMA circular launching Phase 1 of the centralised Complaint Management System for all financial institutions under SAMA supervision, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law and SAMA's consumer-protection mandate. It requires complaints to be handled end-to-end through the system within fixed periods (five business days at level one, three business days for customer-escalated level-two complaints, and two business days to respond to SAMA requests on accepted escalations), and sets quarterly KPIs covering complaint handling quality, escalation rates, timeliness, response quality, repeat complainants and requests for missing documents. Institutions must resource and empower their customer care units, align internal policies, service level agreements and escalation mechanisms; SAMA will monitor compliance and take action against non-compliant institutions from Q4 2025.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
