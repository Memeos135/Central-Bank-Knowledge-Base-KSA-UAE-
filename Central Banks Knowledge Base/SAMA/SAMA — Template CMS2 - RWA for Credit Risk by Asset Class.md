# Template CMS2 - RWA for Credit Risk by Asset Class

Companion Pillar 3 template presenting credit risk RWA split by asset class, comparing amounts produced under modelled (IRB/slotting) approaches with those that would arise under the standardised approach for credit risk. It supports assessment of the capital floor impact and of variability between internal models and the standardised framework. Applies to banks using SAMA-approved internal approaches for credit risk when making Pillar 3 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
