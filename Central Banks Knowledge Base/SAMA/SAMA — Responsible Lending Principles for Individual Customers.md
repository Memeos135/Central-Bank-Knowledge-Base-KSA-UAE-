# Responsible Lending Principles for Individual Customers

SAMA's Responsible Lending Principles for Individual Customers (Circular 46538/99 of 1439H, as amended), setting definitions and general provisions for consumer credit granted by banks and finance companies. It applies to all creditors and all finance activities directed at consumers — personal finance, vehicle finance, credit cards and real estate finance — and requires internal controls, documentation, staff awareness, and oversight of any third parties performing related work.
