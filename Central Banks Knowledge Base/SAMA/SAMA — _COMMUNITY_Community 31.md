# Counterparty Credit Risk Mitigation

## Why this community

Credit risk mitigation (CRM) for banking-book and counterparty exposures: when collateral, netting, guarantees and credit derivatives may reduce regulatory exposure, and the haircuts, eligibility and maturity conditions attached to that recognition.

## How members connect

- A single eligibility gate feeds everything: eligible financial collateral and eligible protection providers determine whether the simple or comprehensive approach, or substitution via guarantee/credit derivative, may be applied.
- Collateralized transactions act as the hub node, branching to the two alternative computation routes (simple vs comprehensive with supervisory haircuts) and to the netting treatment for securities financing transactions.
- Cross-cutting conditions override both routes: maturity mismatch rules and the CCR framework constrain recognition regardless of which technique is used; off-balance-sheet and credit derivative items are pulled in as exposures needing mitigation treatment.
- Sovereign guarantees and counter-guarantees sit as a preferential carve-out within the general guarantee rules, so the general rule must be read before the exception.
