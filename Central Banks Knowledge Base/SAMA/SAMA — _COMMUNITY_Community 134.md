# Digital Banking Authentication

## Why this community

Authentication and access control for customer-facing electronic banking channels — who may access an account, on what evidence of identity, and what strength of authentication the supervisor expects.

## How members connect

- Identity and access management supplies the controlling concept (identification, authorisation, entitlement review) that the multi-factor authentication requirement implements.
- Electronic banking services define the scope trigger: the channel's risk profile determines when stronger authentication becomes mandatory rather than optional.
- Cross-regime relevance: authentication evidence overlaps with CDD/customer identification under AML/CTF and with consumer protection expectations on unauthorised transactions.
