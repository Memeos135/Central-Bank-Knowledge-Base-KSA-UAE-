# Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity

SAMA's Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity (May 2023), issued under the Finance Companies Control Law, setting minimum standards and procedures for entities licensed by SAMA to operate finance-comparison/aggregation platforms. It defines key terms (Activity, Entity, Financier, Consumer, Platform, Documented Channels), requires a licence under the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities, and covers technological capability, standard interfaces with financiers, verification of consumer identity from reliable and independent sources, accuracy of finance offers, and electronic consumer records under the Electronic Transactions Law. It binds licensed aggregation entities and operates without prejudice to other SAMA instruments, including the Business Continuity Management Framework and the Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info
