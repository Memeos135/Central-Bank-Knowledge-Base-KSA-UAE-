# Integrated Logistics Zone Entities Circular

SAMA circular on payment services provided to entities licensed under the Integrated Logistics Zone regime (established by Royal Order No. 17/1 dated 01/02/1440H). It requires payment sector companies to take account of the special nature of these entities, update their policies and service agreements accordingly, and verify their identity using the commercial register issued by the General Authority of Civil Aviation, the articles of association and annexes, and identification of persons authorised to open and manage accounts. Binds companies operating in the payments sector when onboarding ILZ-licensed entities.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF
