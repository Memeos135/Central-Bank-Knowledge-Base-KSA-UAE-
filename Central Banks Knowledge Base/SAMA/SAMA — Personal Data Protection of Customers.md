# Personal Data Protection of Customers

A SAMA supervisory circular reaffirming that all SAMA-supervised financial institutions must fully comply with customer personal data protection requirements, following SAMA's earlier circular on the Personal Data Protection Law and issued data governance policies, controls and rules. It responds to observed practices of requiring individual customers to disclose personal data not necessary for the service or product, whether directly or via a third party. Institutions must align with SDAIA and NDMO requirements and SAMA instructions, review existing disclosure practices, put in place controls ensuring data security and purpose limitation, and report the remedial steps taken to SAMA by the stated deadline.
