# Saudization Children Counting

## Why this community

Workforce nationalization (Saudization) treatment of dependants — whether the children of a Saudi citizen may be counted toward a supervised entity's Saudization ratio. Sits in the HR/licensing-condition space rather than a prudential or conduct regime.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the rule stands alone as an interpretive position on how Saudization headcount is calculated for entities SAMA supervises.
- Legal consequence attaches to licensing and ongoing-compliance conditions (nationalization targets), not to AML/CTF or consumer-protection duties.
- No internal cross-references in the graph; linkage to other regimes would be indirect, via fit-and-proper/governance and staffing obligations.
