# Corporate MSME Exposures

This node concerns the corporate MSME exposure subcategory within the corporate exposure class of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. It marks the boundary between MSME exposures that meet the regulatory retail criteria (treated as retail) and those that do not (treated as corporate MSMEs), and sits within the broader corporate class covering incorporated entities, partnerships, trusts, funds, insurers, securities firms and other financial institutions, but not individuals. It binds SAMA-supervised banks in classifying counterparties and assigning risk weights.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
