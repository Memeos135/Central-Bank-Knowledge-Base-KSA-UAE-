# AML Compliance Function

## Why this community

The internal AML/CTF control architecture of a supervised institution: who must monitor transactions, who escalates, and how suspicion converts into a mandatory external report. It links ongoing monitoring duties to the STR obligation owed to the Financial Intelligence Unit and to accountability at senior management level.

## How members connect

- Obligation chain: ongoing monitoring of transactions and activities generates the trigger for suspicion, which activates the reporting duty; reporting is not discretionary once the threshold is met.
- Shared institutional actor: the AML/CTF compliance function is the designated owner of both monitoring outputs and the reporting channel to SAFIU, making it the addressee of most duties in this cluster.
- Hierarchy of responsibility: senior management responsibilities sit above the compliance function — resourcing, independence and approval of the AML programme — so failures in monitoring or reporting escalate as governance breaches.
- External reference point: SAFIU is the statutory recipient, which also engages tipping-off restrictions and confidentiality constraints around any filed report.
