# Asset Management

## Why this community

Identification, ownership, classification and lifecycle handling of information and technology assets, which is the prerequisite for scoping every other security and resilience obligation.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster; functions as the foundational control domain on which access, physical and audit requirements depend.
- Classification outputs drive downstream handling, retention and confidentiality duties, including customer data protection obligations.
- Asset inventory completeness is a standing audit and supervisory test point, linking this domain to the assurance layer.
