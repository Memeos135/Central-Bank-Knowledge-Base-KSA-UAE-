# Due Diligence on Customer Income Verification

A SAMA circular addressed to finance companies operating in the Kingdom, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51) and its Implementing Regulation. It reiterates the obligation to exercise due diligence before granting finance, proportionate to the potential risks, including verifying the authenticity of income-supporting documents submitted by the customer and strengthening internal procedures to that effect. It is a supervisory reminder rather than a new rulebook, but it is enforceable against finance companies as part of responsible-lending and affordability obligations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
