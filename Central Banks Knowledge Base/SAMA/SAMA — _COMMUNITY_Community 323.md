# Social Responsibility Day

## Why this community

A standalone SAMA communication on participation in Social Responsibility Day by supervised financial institutions, sitting in the conduct/corporate-citizenship space rather than a prudential or AML regime.

## How members connect

- Single-member community with no internal cross-references; it does not amend or implement a higher-level regulation in the provided context.
- Read as a participation/communication instrument — check the wording carefully before treating it as an enforceable obligation rather than an expectation or invitation.
