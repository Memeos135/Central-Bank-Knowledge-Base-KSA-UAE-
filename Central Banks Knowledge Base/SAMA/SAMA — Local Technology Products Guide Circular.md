# Local Technology Products Guide Circular

Short SAMA circular (Payment Systems and Companies Control) forwarding the CST/Communications, Space and Technology Commission's Local Technology Products Guide, an indicative list of locally developed technology products serving sectors including banking, insurance and finance. It is informational rather than a new obligation, but expressly reiterates that supervised entities must continue to comply with SAMA's existing rules, particularly those on outsourcing to third parties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
