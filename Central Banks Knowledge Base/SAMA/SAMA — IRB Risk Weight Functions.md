# IRB Risk Weight Functions

This node relates to the IRB risk-weight functions that convert risk components into risk-weighted assets, including treatment of defaulted exposures and of corporate, sovereign and bank exposures, alongside the purchased receivables chapter's rules on recognising credit risk mitigants. It explains how guarantees and collateral covering default risk, dilution risk or both are substituted or apportioned, including where mitigants provide first-loss protection interacting with the securitisation IRB approach. It binds SAMA-supervised banks computing IRB credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
