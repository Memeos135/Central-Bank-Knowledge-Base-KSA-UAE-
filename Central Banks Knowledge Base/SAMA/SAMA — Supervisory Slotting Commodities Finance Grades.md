# Supervisory Slotting: Commodities Finance Grades

Concept node for the supervisory slotting criteria applied to commodities finance (CF), a specialized lending sub-class, setting the assessment factors and characteristics used to assign exposures to the five supervisory categories and their associated risk weights and expected loss amounts. It connects to the IRB minimum requirements on rating structure, information quality and the mapping of internal grades to supervisory categories. Binds SAMA-supervised banks applying the slotting approach to specialized lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
