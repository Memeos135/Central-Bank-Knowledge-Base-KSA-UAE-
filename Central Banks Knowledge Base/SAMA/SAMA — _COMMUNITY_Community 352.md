# Unified SMS Notifications

## Why this community

Standardisation of the mandatory content elements of customer SMS notifications, so that transaction and account alerts convey a consistent minimum information set.

## How members connect

- Standalone SAMA circular creating a disclosure-content obligation within the consumer-protection regime.
- Complements sender-identity governance: one instrument controls who the message appears to come from, the other what it must contain.
- Consequence for decisions: notification content is a compliance artefact, relevant to dispute handling and unauthorised-transaction claims.
