# CCR Collateral Composition

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of collateral used to mitigate counterparty credit risk, split between collateral for derivative transactions and for securities financing transactions, and between segregated and unsegregated, received and posted.

## How members connect

- Single-member community that supports the exposure templates by evidencing the credit risk mitigation assumed in the reported EAD figures.
- Relies on collateral eligibility, haircut and segregation concepts defined in the capital adequacy and CRM rules; the template only prescribes presentation.
- Segregation categories interact with the CCP disclosure regime, since client collateral treatment differs for cleared exposures.
