# Financial Viability Analysis

Node describing the financial viability assessment a bank's Workout Unit must perform on a distressed borrower before choosing between restructuring, debt swap or legal proceedings, and which must be carried out by staff not involved in the original loan approval. It covers indicative ratio categories (liquidity, solvency/leverage, profitability), balance sheet review of total debt burden and priority claims such as wages and taxes, and cash flow analysis as the defining test of viability, with a cash-flow-based approach for MSMEs lacking reliable audited financials. Binds banks conducting NPL workouts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
