# SMS Services Subscription

## Why this community

Customer notification by SMS — the terms on which supervised entities subscribe customers to SMS alert/notification services, and the consequences for consent, fees and transaction transparency.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster addressing the conduct/consumer-protection dimension of customer communications.
- Obligation chain runs from the entity to the individual customer: enrolment, disclosure and continuity of alerting.
- Interacts indirectly with fraud-detection and transaction-advice duties, but the rule itself is a service-terms instruction.
