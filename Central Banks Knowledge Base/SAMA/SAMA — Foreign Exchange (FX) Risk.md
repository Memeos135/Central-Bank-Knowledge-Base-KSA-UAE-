# Foreign Exchange (FX) Risk

Concept node for Foreign Exchange (FX) risk, a defined market risk class capturing losses from exchange rate movements on trading book and certain banking book positions. It appears in the internal risk transfer recognition rules (FX and commodity risk in the banking book fall within the market risk capital scope) and as a standalone capital component in the simplified standardised approach, including non-delta option risk add-ons. Binds banks calculating market risk capital requirements under the SAMA framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
