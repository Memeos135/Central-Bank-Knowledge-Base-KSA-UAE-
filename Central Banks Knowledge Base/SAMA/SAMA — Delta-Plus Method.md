# Delta-Plus Method

A concept node on the delta-plus method for option positions under the simplified standardised approach to market risk. It explains that delta-equivalent positions feed into the general market risk charges for interest rate, equity, FX/gold and commodity categories, with separate capital requirements applied to gamma and vega risk, and specific risk determined by applying specific risk weights to delta-equivalents; it sits alongside the simplified and scenario approaches. It binds banks eligible to use the simplified standardised approach rather than the full standardised or internal models approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
