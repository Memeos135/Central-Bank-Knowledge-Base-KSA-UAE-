# Recognition of Risk Transference

Concerns when a transfer of risk is recognised for regulatory capital purposes: in the securitization context whether the originator may treat exposures as transferred, and in the market risk context the conditions for recognising internal risk transfers between the banking book, trading desks, the approved internal risk transfer desk and the CVA portfolio, including exact-match external hedge and documentation conditions. Non-recognition means the bank must hold capital as if no transfer or protection occurred. It binds banks calculating credit risk and market risk minimum capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
