# Lending Application Special Controls

A discrete set of additional cyber/fraud controls that SAMA imposes on lending platforms on top of the general registration, onboarding and transaction controls in the same circular, covering verification that the disbursement IBAN belongs to the loan applicant, use of an authorised digital signature provider, and creation and custody of promissory notes through a national trusted party. It binds Member Organizations under SAMA supervision that offer lending products via digital applications (including fintech/finance company models). Relevant to loan origination integrity and fraud prevention rather than credit underwriting or consumer-disclosure duties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
