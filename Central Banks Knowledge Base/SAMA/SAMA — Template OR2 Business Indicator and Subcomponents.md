# Template OR2: Business Indicator and Subcomponents

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to publish the Business Indicator (BI) and its interest/lease/dividend, services and financial subcomponents, together with the resulting Business Indicator Component used in the standardised operational risk capital calculation. It is mandatory for all banks, fixed-format and annual, with full prior-period disclosure required from the implementation date (the usual retrospective-disclosure relief does not apply). Narrative commentary on significant changes is expected, and additional narrative is required where SAMA has approved exclusion of divested activities from the BI.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
