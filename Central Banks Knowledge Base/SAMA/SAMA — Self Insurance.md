# Self Insurance

A concept node describing self-insurance as an internal risk financing option for bank operational risk, covering unfunded and funded retention, single-parent captives and contractual risk transfer, and treating risk retention groups, group captives and pooling arrangements as economically equivalent to self-insurance. It frames retention decisions as governed by loss retention guidelines set within the bank's Operational Risk Management Plan. Relevant to bank boards and senior management deciding how much operational loss to retain versus transfer.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
