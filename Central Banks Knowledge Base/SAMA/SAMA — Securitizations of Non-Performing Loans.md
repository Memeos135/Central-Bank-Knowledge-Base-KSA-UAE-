# Securitizations of Non-Performing Loans

This node covers the capital treatment of securitizations of non-performing loans within SAMA's credit risk framework, and connects to the prudential definition of non-performing and forborne exposures (SAMA Rules on Management of Problem Loans No. 41033343, January 2020) and the related Pillar 3 disclosure table CRB-A. It governs how NPL securitization exposures are risk-weighted and how problem assets are defined and disclosed. It binds SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
