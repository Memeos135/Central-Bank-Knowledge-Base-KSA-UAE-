# Finance Reporting Guidelines

## Why this community

Supervisory data and reporting requirements for the finance sector, covering how lenders classify exposures, report interest rates on assets and liabilities, and observe timelines in real estate finance debt transfer.

## How members connect

- Shared reporting taxonomy: the interest rate reporting guidelines rely on ISIC4 as the classification standard for categorising counterparties and exposures, an explicit cross-reference.
- Real estate finance debt transfer timelines and rate reporting are conceptually linked through the same underlying credit exposures, which must be consistently identified and reported across returns.
- Hierarchy runs from the finance regime's prudential and reporting powers down to guideline-level instructions on classification and submission content.
- Practical consequence: misclassification under ISIC4 propagates into rate reporting and into the accuracy of supervisory views on real estate finance portfolios.
