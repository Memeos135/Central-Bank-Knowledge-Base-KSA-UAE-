# Corporate Governance and Risk Management

Chapter 3 of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020, setting SAMA's internal governance and risk management requirements for DTFCs: proportionality to nature, scale and complexity; clear organisational structure; job descriptions for senior managers tied to fit-and-proper pre-approval; a Management Responsibilities Description Document; and segregation of functions. The excerpt also carries AML/KYC-related duties placed on DTFCs, including the compliance officer's access rights, scrutiny of nominees/trustees/authorised representatives, ongoing transaction monitoring, risk classification of accounts and STR reporting to the FIU with notification to SAMA.
