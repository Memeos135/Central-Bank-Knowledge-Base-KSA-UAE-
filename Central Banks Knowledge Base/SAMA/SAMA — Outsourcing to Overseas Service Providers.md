# Outsourcing to Overseas Service Providers

A section of SAMA's outsourcing rules for banks addressing arrangements with third-party service providers located outside the Kingdom, including the additional country, legal, confidentiality and data-access risks such arrangements create. It requires banks to obtain SAMA's written no-objection before entering cross-border outsourcing and to submit specified information (function, materiality classification, rationale for not performing it in KSA, provider details, data nature and disposal). It also sits alongside requirements on business continuity/contingency planning for material outsourcing, unrestricted SAMA and auditor access to outsourcing records and data, and ongoing monitoring of the provider relationship. It binds banks licensed in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, data/credit info
