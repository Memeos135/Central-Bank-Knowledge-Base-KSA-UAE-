# Company Obligations

The insurer-facing duties under the Bancassurance Rules: the Company must obtain SAMA approval before any material amendment to the Agreement with the bank, file an annual training plan for Authorised Employees covering marketing and distribution techniques, product workshops and AML/CTF training, keep bank and Client data confidential, periodically review the bank's conduct of Bancassurance Activities across electronic and non-electronic channels, and pay the agreed commission within the Agreement period and within SAMA's limits. A related prohibition bars tying insurance to banking products (or vice versa) unless coverage is mandated by a competent authority. It binds licensed insurance companies distributing through banks.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
