# Table LIQA - Liquidity Risk Management

Table LIQA is a mandatory annual Pillar 3 disclosure table on liquidity risk management, requiring qualitative and quantitative narrative on liquidity governance, risk tolerance, funding strategy and diversification, mitigation techniques, stress testing, contingency funding plans, concentration limits and entity-level liquidity exposures. Format is flexible, so banks select content proportionate to their business model and liquidity risk profile. It binds all banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework and sits alongside the risk-management overview table (OVA) in the same instrument.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
