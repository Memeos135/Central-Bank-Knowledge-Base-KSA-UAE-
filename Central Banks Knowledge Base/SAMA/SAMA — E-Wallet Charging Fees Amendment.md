# E-Wallet Charging Fees Amendment

SAMA circular amending the pricing applied to e-wallet top-up transactions made with mada cards through the e-commerce route, replacing percentage-based pricing with fixed caps: a Merchant Service Charge capped at a fixed riyal amount and a fixed Interchange Fee. It restricts these rates to e-wallet charging transactions only and requires banks and PSPs to disclose all fees in new Merchant Service Agreements, update existing agreements, and obtain merchant acceptance before signing; effective from the stated date.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
