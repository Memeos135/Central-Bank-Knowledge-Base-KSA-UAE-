# Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk

Concept node for SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (SCRE), covering how risk-weighted assets and Pillar 1 capital requirements (normally RWA × 8%) are computed under the standardised approach, F-IRB/A-IRB and supervisory slotting, and how they are broken out in Pillar 3 overview templates separately from counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity, settlement and securitisation exposures. It binds banks calculating regulatory capital under SAMA's adoption of the Basel III post-crisis reforms.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
