# Minimum Requirements for Exchange Center Premises

SAMA circular to licensed money exchangers (categories A and B) attaching the minimum requirements for exchange centre premises, issued under the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, with a one-year period to bring existing premises into compliance. It sets conditions on location (main commercial street, mall, airport/border crossing), minimum floor areas and counter setbacks for head offices and branches (with special thresholds for the Central Area of the Two Holy Mosques), 3D engineering designs consistent with the financial-sector security and safety manual, fit-out and signage, insurance of premises, complaint handling, and technical requirements including an automated accounting/reporting system supporting AML obligations and screening against international prohibition lists in Arabic and English, off-site data backup and restrictions on third-party access to data. SAMA no-objection is required before leasing premises and before executing designs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
