# Securitization Due Diligence Requirements

Sets the ongoing due diligence condition for using the securitization risk weight approaches and, more broadly, for assigning counterparty risk weights: banks must understand the risk profile and performance of underlying exposures and counterparties at origination and at least annually, with sophistication proportionate to activity. Failure to obtain the required information results in a 1250% risk weight on the securitization exposure; banks must also maintain policies, processes and controls and demonstrate adequacy to SAMA. It binds all banks calculating credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
