# Rules for Advertising Products and Services (1722)

SAMA's Rules for Advertising Products and Services, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law, setting minimum content, language and conduct requirements for any advertisement of a product or service by a SAMA-supervised entity. It defines Financial Institution, Customer, Consumer, Products and Services and Advertising, and imposes due-diligence duties against misleading, tempting or exaggerated messaging, mandatory Arabic, and disclosure of fees, VAT, key terms, target audience, and APR/finance term (including whether real estate finance term cost is fixed or variable). It binds all financial institutions supervised and regulated by SAMA when advertising.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
