# Template GSIB1 - Disclosure of G-SIB Indicators

A Pillar 3 template setting out the twelve indicators feeding the Basel methodology for assessing global systemic importance. Under SAMA's framework it is mandatory only for banks previously classified as G-SIBs, those exceeding the EUR 200 billion leverage ratio exposure threshold, or those in the assessment sample by supervisory judgment; SAMA states it need not be completed unless SAMA identifies the bank as a G-SIB. Disclosure is annual, flexible in format, in the bank's own currency, with accompanying narrative and links to prior assessment disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
