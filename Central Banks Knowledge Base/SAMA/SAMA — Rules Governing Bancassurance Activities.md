# Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities

SAMA's Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities regulate the marketing and distribution of insurance products by banks to their Clients under a written Agreement with a licensed insurance Company. They set definitions (Bancassurance Activities, Agreement, Authorised Employee, Client), scope, objective, minimum Agreement contents, conduct standards and the respective obligations of bank and insurer. They bind banks licensed under the Banking Control Law and insurance companies licensed under the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law; the Rules are supplemented by those laws and other SAMA instructions on matters not expressly addressed.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential, other
