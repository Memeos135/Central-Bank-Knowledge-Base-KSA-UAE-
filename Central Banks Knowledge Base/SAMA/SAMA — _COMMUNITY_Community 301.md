# Unified Financial Notifications

## Why this community

Standardisation of the content and format of financial notifications sent to customers, so disclosures about transactions and account events are consistent, complete and comprehensible.

## How members connect

- Consumer-protection regime: converts general transparency/disclosure duties into prescribed notification elements financial institutions must include.
- Links to transaction-execution and fee-disclosure obligations, since the notification is the evidentiary touchpoint for what the customer was told and when.
- Supports dispute and complaint handling, as notification timing and content determine customer awareness and objection periods.
