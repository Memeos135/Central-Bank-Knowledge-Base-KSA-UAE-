# QR Code Payments Circular

A SAMA circular on QR Code payments issued under the SAMA Charter (M/36) and Council of Ministers Resolution No. 226, confirming SAMA as the competent authority for operating and supervising payment and financial settlement systems in the Kingdom. It requires entities that have already launched QR-based payment services to comply with the technical and commercial requirements and standards of the associated payment environment and to complete technical certification/testing with Saudi Payments by end-2021. It binds banks and licensed fintech/payment participants offering QR code payment services.

**Regimes:** payments
