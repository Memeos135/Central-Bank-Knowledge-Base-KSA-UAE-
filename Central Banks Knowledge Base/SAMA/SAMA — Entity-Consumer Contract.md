# Entity-Consumer Contract

Covers the mandatory contract between a SAMA-licensed aggregation entity and the Consumer, together with the entity's pre-contractual conduct duties (clear, non-misleading product information, reasons for declined finance applications, notification of approval via documented channels, complaint channels). The contract must be documented with a copy to each party and state, at minimum, parties, scope, term, desired finance type and requirements, rights and obligations, pricing and fees, withdrawal, dispute resolution, and termination/expiry. Binds the licensed aggregation entity in its dealings with consumers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
