# Agent Due Diligence (Article 14)

Article 14 sets the Bank's ongoing agent due diligence obligations: documented procedures with periodic reviews, early warning indicators and corrective actions, clear allocation of agent-management roles across internal functions, and AML/CFT, embezzlement and fraud monitoring of Agents with regular compliance checks. Minimum due diligence content covers legal status and address verification, moral/business/professional suitability, adverse credit bureau or other information, criminal records touching ML/TF, fraud or integrity, experience, and funding sources. Note this is diligence on the Agent (entity-level, KYB-style), distinct from the customer due diligence duties in Article 15.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential, data/credit info
