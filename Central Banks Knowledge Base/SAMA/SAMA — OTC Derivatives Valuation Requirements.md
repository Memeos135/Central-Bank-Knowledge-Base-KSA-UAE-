# OTC Derivatives Valuation Requirements

Valuation provisions within the risk mitigation section for non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives: banks must be able to value transactions internally, corroborate counterparty or third-party valuations, use mainstream-accepted models (justifying any new methodology), and periodically review and document the agreed valuation process. Banks must also agree and document fallback valuation approaches where inputs fail, procedures for modifying the process, and how valuation disputes are resolved. Binds banks that are contracting parties to non-centrally cleared OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
