# Template SEC1: Securitisation Exposures Banking Book

Template SEC1 is the quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of securitisation exposures held in the banking book, covering all such exposures regardless of whether risk transfer recognition criteria are met, split by the bank's role as originator, sponsor or investor. Because it is not limited to exposures treated under the securitisation capital framework, it can overlap with exposures reported elsewhere in the Pillar 3 report and is intended to give a comprehensive picture of securitisation activity. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
