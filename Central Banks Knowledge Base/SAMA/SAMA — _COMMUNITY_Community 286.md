# Remuneration Policy

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure of a bank's remuneration policy — the governance, design and risk-alignment features that must be described publicly.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone Pillar 3 narrative table whose obligation is disclosure content rather than conduct.
- Conceptually paired with the quantitative remuneration templates; the policy narrative is the interpretive frame for the figures disclosed.
- Sits within the governance/risk regime, linking remuneration design to risk-taking incentives and board oversight.
