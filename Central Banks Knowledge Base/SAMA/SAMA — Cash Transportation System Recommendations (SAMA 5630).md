# Cash Transportation System Recommendations (SAMA 5630)

A SAMA circular transmitting the security committee recommendations on cash, precious metals and valuables transportation, addressed to banks and licensed cash-in-transit companies. It requires banks to strengthen oversight of contracted CIT firms inside cash centres and enforce receipt/delivery procedures, mandates separation of cash transport duties from ATM replenishment (three-member armoured vehicle crew with two armed, plus a separate two-member replenishment team in a civilian escort vehicle), dual-control sealing of ATM cassettes under CCTV, independent reconciliation of returned cash, upgraded tracking/self-destruct containers, use of SIMAH credit records in vetting CIT job applicants, and reporting of monitoring procedures to SAMA. It also signals future licensing of company-operated cash processing units.
