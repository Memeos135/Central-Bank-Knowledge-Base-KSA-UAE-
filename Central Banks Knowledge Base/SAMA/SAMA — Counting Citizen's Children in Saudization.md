# Counting Citizen's Children in Saudization

SAMA circular to exchange centres operating in the Kingdom (categories A and B) clarifying an earlier circular that restricted all jobs in the exchange sector to Saudis. It confirms that categories treated as Saudis for labour purposes — including the child of a Saudi mother — count toward Saudization ratios in line with decisions of the Ministry of Labour and Social Development, and that this must be disclosed in data submitted to SAMA. Exchange centres must continue to track and comply with whatever the Ministry issues on the matter.
