# SAMA Rules on Large Exposures of Banks

Cross-reference to SAMA's Rules on Large Exposures of Banks, which supply the measurement methodology and group-of-connected-counterparties concept used to size related-party exposures under the Related Parties Rules. In this context it drives hard caps (5% of eligible capital base for a non-bank related party, 10% for Saudi-listed non-bank related parties, up to 25% for non-banking financial-sector subsidiaries, 50% cumulative), immediate breach notification to SAMA with a remediation plan, security requirements under Article 9 of the Banking Control Law, and quarterly reporting of exposures above 5%. Binds all local banks; effective from 1 September 2022.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
