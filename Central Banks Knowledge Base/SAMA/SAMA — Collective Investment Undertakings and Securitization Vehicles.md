# Collective Investment Undertakings and Securitization Vehicles

This node governs how banks attribute exposures held through funds, securitisation vehicles and other structures with underlying assets for large exposure purposes. It permits treating the structure itself as a distinct counterparty where each underlying exposure is below 0.25% of the eligible capital base, otherwise requiring identification of the underlying counterparties, and obliges banks to identify third parties (originator, fund manager, liquidity provider, protection seller) that constitute an additional risk factor and may form a group of connected counterparties. It binds SAMA-supervised banks investing in such structures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
