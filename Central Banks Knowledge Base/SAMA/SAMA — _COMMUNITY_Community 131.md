# Real Estate Finance Circulars

## Why this community

Scope and limits of real estate finance: what counts as real estate finance (including land financing and commercial real estate finance) and the maximum financing ratio available for a first home.

## How members connect

- Shared defined term 'real estate finance' - each circular either widens the perimeter (land financing) or carves out a sub-category (commercial real estate finance).
- Scope determinations drive applicability of the LTV/maximum-financing cap, so the definitional circulars are conditions precedent to the first-home limit circular.
- Explicit cross-reference: the land financing circular points to the first-home maximum circular; the commercial definition circular is read alongside it.
- All three are circular-level instruments implementing the Real Estate Finance Law and its implementing regulation.
