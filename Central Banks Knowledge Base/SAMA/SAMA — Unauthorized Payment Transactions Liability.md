# Unauthorized Payment Transactions Liability

This node covers the treatment of unauthorised payment transactions under SAMA's Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services — authorisation and consent, the Customer's notification duty, refund/rectification obligations and the allocation of loss between licensee and Customer (including cases of gross negligence or fraud). It is the operative liability rule behind disputed and fraudulent payment claims. It binds SAMA-licensed payment service providers in their dealings with Customers and Consumers.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
