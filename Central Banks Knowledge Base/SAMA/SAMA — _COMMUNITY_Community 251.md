# SRC Residential Real Estate Purchase

## Why this community

Permission for the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company to acquire residential real estate finance portfolios, touching secondary-market mortgage liquidity and the scope of permitted acquisitions by a SAMA-supervised entity.

## How members connect

- Isolated circular acting as a targeted authorisation/carve-out layered on the real estate finance and finance company regulatory framework.
- Consequences run to assignment and transfer of consumer real estate finance contracts and the continuity of borrower protections post-transfer.
