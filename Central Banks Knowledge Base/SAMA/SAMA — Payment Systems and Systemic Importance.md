# Payment Systems and Systemic Importance

This node covers Part Nine of SAMA's Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services, dealing with the classification of important payment systems, application of the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures, SAMA's supervisory powers over systems and members, and settlement finality and insolvency. It requires periodic self-assessment against the PFMIs and adequate financial resources, including a stated minimum paid-up capital and operating-cost coverage for unclassified payment systems. It binds payment system operators and, through them, system members, who must supply information and reports on SAMA's request.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential
