# Shari'ah Non-compliance Risk (SNCR)

Defined term and substantive requirement (Section 9) on Shari'ah Non-compliance Risk: the risk arising from failure of a bank's Shari'ah governance mechanism to ensure compliance with Shari'ah rules and principles determined by its Shari'ah Committee. Banks must manage SNCR as part of operational risk, maintain policies to measure, mitigate and monitor it, treat Shari'ah compliance as a higher-priority risk given reputational consequences, ensure contract documentation is Shari'ah compliant, and track income arising from non-compliant sources. Binds full-fledged Islamic banks and Islamic Windows of conventional banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
