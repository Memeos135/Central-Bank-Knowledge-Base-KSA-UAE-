# Leverage Adjustment

Leverage adjustment covers two related concepts in the SAMA framework: the leverage adjustment applied when risk-weighting equity investments in funds, and the reconciling adjustments between published accounting assets and the leverage ratio exposure measure disclosed in Pillar 3 template LR1 (regulatory consolidation, securitised exposures, temporary central bank reserve exclusions, fiduciary assets, regular-way purchases/sales, cash pooling). It binds banks calculating the leverage ratio and preparing quarterly leverage disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
