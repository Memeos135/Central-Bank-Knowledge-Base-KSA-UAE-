# Prudential Data Reporting

## Why this community

Periodic prudential and financial reporting to the supervisor — the content, form and timing of financial statements and prudential returns submitted by supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster covering the reporting obligation chain: preparation, internal approval, and submission to SAMA within prescribed deadlines.
- Underpins off-site supervision; accuracy and timeliness of returns are enforceable in their own right, separate from the prudential ratios they report.
- Cross-reads to governance clusters, since responsibility for the integrity of submitted data typically rests with board and senior management.
