# Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law

A referenced instrument rather than a standalone node: the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law is the licensing statute under which the insurance "Company" in the Bancassurance Rules is authorised to conduct insurance business in the Kingdom. Within the Bancassurance Rules it fixes who qualifies as a Company and, together with its Implementing Regulation, the Banking Control Law and other SAMA and competent-authority rules, governs any matter the Bancassurance Rules do not expressly address. It binds licensed insurers; banks encounter it indirectly through the counterparty eligibility requirement.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
