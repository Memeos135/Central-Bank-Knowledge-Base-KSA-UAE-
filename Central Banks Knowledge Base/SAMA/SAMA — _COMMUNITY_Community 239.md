# Banking Book Securitisation Template

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of banking-book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as investor, together with the associated capital requirements.

## How members connect

- Sits in the SEC quantitative series alongside the trading-book template, distinguished by book (banking book) and by the bank's role (investor rather than originator/sponsor).
- Uses the role and exposure definitions established in the qualitative securitisation table, and links exposure amounts to the securitisation capital treatment.
- Hierarchy: disclosure framework obligation to the specific template; figures disclosed must reconcile with the capital requirements calculated under the securitisation rules.
