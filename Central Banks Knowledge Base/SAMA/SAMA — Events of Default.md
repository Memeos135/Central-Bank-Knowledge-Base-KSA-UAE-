# Events of Default

Default-related provisions of the SAMA-approved Master Repurchase Agreement annexed to the Repo Guidelines, covering service of a Default Notice, the effectiveness of notices by various delivery channels, the Early Termination Date, and close-out netting into a single Cash Settlement Amount. It governs how the non-Defaulting Party establishes Default Market Values and takes an account of amounts due between the parties. It binds counterparties to repo transactions documented under the mandated MRA, which is governed by Saudi law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
