# Table CCRA: Qualitative CCR Disclosure

The qualitative disclosure table for counterparty credit risk in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, opening the CCR section that covers all banking book and trading book exposures subject to a CCR charge, including exposures to central counterparties. It is the narrative companion to the quantitative CCR templates (CCR1 and CCR3 to CCR8). Mandatory for banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
