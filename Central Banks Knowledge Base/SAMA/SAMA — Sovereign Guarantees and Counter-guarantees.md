# Sovereign Guarantees and Counter-guarantees

A concept node on preferential risk weighting of exposures guaranteed by a sovereign or central bank, including the extension of the 0% risk weight for Saudi sovereign/SAMA exposures denominated and funded in Saudi Riyal to guaranteed portions. It sets the three conditions under which an indirect sovereign counter-guarantee may be treated as a direct sovereign guarantee, subject to SAMA's satisfaction as to robustness. It binds banks applying the standardised credit risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
