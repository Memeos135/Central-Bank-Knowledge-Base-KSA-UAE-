# Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO)

Node covering the recommended asset and liability committee within SAMA's liquidity risk management rules for finance companies. It describes ALCO composition (CEO, risk management, treasury, financial control and other business areas affecting the liquidity profile), the Board's duty to define its mandate for planning, directing and managing liquidity risk, and the committee's role in ensuring the liquidity risk framework measures exposure and produces timely reporting to senior management, the Board and SAMA (e.g. quarterly risk reports on cash flow mismatches, maturity gaps, funding concentration and collateral availability). Binds finance companies licensed under the Finance Companies Control Law; ALCO establishment is recommended rather than mandatory, but reporting expectations sit alongside it.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
