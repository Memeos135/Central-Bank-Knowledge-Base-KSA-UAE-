# Collateral Management

Sets collateral management requirements applying throughout the credit process regardless of loan performance: Board-approved (or delegated) collateral policies and procedures reviewed at least every three years and aligned with the risk appetite statement, governance over internal and external valuers, and regular internal audit review of policy quality, valuer independence and valuation appropriateness. Banks must document accepted collateral types (financial collateral, lease exposures, other funded credit protection, guarantees) and ensure documentation, legal enforceability across all areas of operation including foreign branches and subsidiaries, and mitigation of valuation, termination and concentration risks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
