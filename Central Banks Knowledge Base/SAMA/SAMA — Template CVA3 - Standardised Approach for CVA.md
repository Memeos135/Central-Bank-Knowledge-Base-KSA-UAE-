# Template CVA3 - Standardised Approach for CVA

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering the standardised approach for credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk, with accompanying definitions and completion instructions for rows/columns and the treatment of standardised versus SAMA-approved modelled approaches. It is a reporting/disclosure obligation rather than a substantive capital rule, and sits alongside related comparison disclosures of actual RWA versus RWA under full standardised approaches. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime, at the frequency and format fixed for the template.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
