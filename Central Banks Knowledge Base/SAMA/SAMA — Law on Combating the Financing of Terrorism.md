# Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism

The Saudi Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and their Financing, setting out definitions (Terrorist Crime, Terrorism Financing Crime, Terrorist), offences and penalties, and the institutional architecture for enforcement. It covers mutual legal assistance and extradition, the Permanent Committee's role in implementing UN Security Council resolutions on terrorism and its financing, and the General Directorate of Financial Intelligence's mandate to receive, analyse and disseminate suspicious transaction reports. Binds persons in the Kingdom generally and imposes reporting and information-provision duties on financial institutions, with requests routed through the supervisory authority.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
