# Template CR8 - RWA Flow Statements of Credit Risk Exposures Under IRB

A Pillar 3 flow statement template reconciling movements in IRB credit risk RWA between reporting periods, attributing changes to drivers such as asset size, asset quality, model updates, methodology and policy, acquisitions and disposals, and foreign exchange movements. It covers credit risk RWA under IRB approaches, excluding counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity positions, settlement risk and securitisation, which are reported elsewhere. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using IRB approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
