# Table ORA: Operational Risk Framework

The general qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table on a bank's operational risk management framework, sitting within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework alongside the quantitative operational risk templates such as historical losses and business indicator data. It governs narrative disclosure of how operational risk is identified, measured, governed and capitalised under the standardised operational risk capital regime. Binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to Pillar 3 reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
