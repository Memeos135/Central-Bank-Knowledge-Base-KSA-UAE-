# Netting Set

Defined unit of aggregation in SAMA's counterparty credit risk capital framework: the group of transactions with a single counterparty that may be netted for exposure measurement. The material sets out how replacement cost and PFE add-ons are computed at netting set level, how several netting sets under one margin agreement are aggregated (with unmargined PFE treatment where collateral is exchanged on a net basis across netting sets), and how collateral taken outside a netting set is attributed. Binds banks calculating regulatory exposure at default for derivatives and other CCR exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
