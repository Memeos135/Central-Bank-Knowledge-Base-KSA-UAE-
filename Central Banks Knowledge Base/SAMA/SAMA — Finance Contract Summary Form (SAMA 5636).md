# Finance Contract Summary Form (SAMA 5636)

A SAMA circular issuing the Finance Contract Summary Form, grounded in Article 79 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 5(1) of the updated Consumer Finance Regulations. It requires the summary form to appear at the front of every finance contract subject to that Law and those Regulations, disclosing beneficiary identity, total cost of finance, term cost, administrative and real-estate valuation fees, number and amount of instalments, first and last instalment dates, total amount payable, and cross-references to key contract clauses (including collateral shortfall handling and termination rights), with a note that the summary does not substitute for reading the contract. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
