# Banking Disputes Committee Requests

## Why this community

Duties of supervised financial institutions to respond to requests and correspondence from the Committee for the Settlement of Banking Disputes, including timeliness and completeness of submissions.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the requirement is a procedural cooperation obligation owed to an adjudicatory body rather than a prudential rule.
- It links conceptually to consumer protection and complaint-handling regimes, since disputes escalated by customers become matters requiring institutional response and documentary support.
