# Outsourcing Rules for Finance Companies

Rules on outsourcing for finance companies, approved by Governor's decision and circulated in 1440H (Dec 2018), issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. They define outsourcing, external service provider and material functions, and impose board-approved outsourcing policy, annually updated, risk assessment at contracting and renewal, due diligence on the provider's licensing and capability, non-delegable responsibility for provider compliance, record retention, protection/retrieval or destruction of beneficiary data, and SAMA/external auditor access rights. They bind finance companies licensed by SAMA.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, data/credit info
