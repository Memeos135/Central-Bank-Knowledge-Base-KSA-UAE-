# Cyber Operations & Incident Management

## Why this community

Operational and cyber resilience expectations for SAMA-supervised entities: running security operations, detecting events, escalating incidents, and managing third-party and cloud dependencies.

## How members connect

- Cyber Security Operations and Technology is the parent domain; SOC capability, event management, incident management and threat management are its component control areas.
- There is a clear escalation chain: monitoring/detection (SIEM, SOC) feeds event management, which feeds incident management, which in turn triggers notification obligations to SAMA.
- IT Incident Management and Cyber Security Incident Management overlap substantially but are distinct control tracks — do not assume one satisfies the other's requirements.
- Outsourcing and Cloud Computing connect the internal control set to external dependencies, where the regulated entity retains accountability toward SAMA regardless of delegation.
