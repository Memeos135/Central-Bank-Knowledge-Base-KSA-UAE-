# Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48)

Concept node for the Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48) and its Implementing Regulation, which underpin SAMA's authority over finance leasing, lessors and licensed finance lease contract registration companies. In the cited source it is the legal basis for SAMA's controls and procedures on requesting and issuing extracts of the executive instrument (writ of execution) for a registered finance lease contract — covering repossession of movable assets, delivery of immovable assets, and financial execution certificates — which are treated as enforceable instruments under the Enforcement Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, other
