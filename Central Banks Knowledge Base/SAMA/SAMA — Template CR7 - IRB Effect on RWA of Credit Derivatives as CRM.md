# Template CR7 - IRB Effect on RWA of Credit Derivatives as CRM

A mandatory semiannual Pillar 3 template showing, for each IRB portfolio, RWA calculated without recognising credit derivatives as a credit risk mitigant against actual RWA reflecting that mitigation, so users can gauge the capital impact of credit derivative CRM. Rows split sovereign, bank, corporate, specialised lending, retail sub-classes, equity and purchased receivables between F-IRB and A-IRB. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using A-IRB and/or F-IRB for some or all exposures, with narrative commentary expected.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
