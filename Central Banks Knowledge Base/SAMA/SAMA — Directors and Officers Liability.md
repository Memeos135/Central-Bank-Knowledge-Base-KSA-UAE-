# Directors and Officers Liability

Concept node distinguishing directors' and officers' (D&O) liability from the bank's own professional liability within SAMA's operational risk guidance. It explains that personal assets of executive and non-executive directors and senior officers may be exposed to claims of negligent or imprudent acts and omissions, that D&O cover is purchased by the bank to indemnify those individuals (including where the bank cannot legally indemnify them), and that its scope of wrongful acts is broader than professional indemnity while covering fewer persons. Sources of exposure include shareholder actions, client services, employment practices and environmental claims.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
