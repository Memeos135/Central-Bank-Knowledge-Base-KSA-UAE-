# Financial Inclusion and Priority Areas

The definitional and policy-content provisions of the Branch Network Regulation dealing with financial inclusion, including the zone classification of locations by population and the designation of Zone 3 (small cities) and Zone 4 (rural areas) as Priority Areas, plus definitions of remittance centers, ATMs, digital modes and Customer. Banks' Branch Network Policies must address outreach, digital delivery channels and serving priority areas as corporate social responsibility. It binds banks subject to the Branch Network Regulation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, payments
