# Three-Stage Credit Risk Classification

Concept node on the three-stage classification of credit exposures required of finance companies: Stage 1 (Regular, no significant increase in credit risk), Stage 2 (Special Monitoring/Substandard) and Stage 3 (Doubtful/Loss), assessed individually or collectively against credit risk at origination. It sets cure-period and re-staging conditions for moving exposures from Stage 2 back to Stage 1 (90 days generally, 60 days for retail moving from Stage 2B) and requires bifurcated Stage 2 reporting in quarterly prudential returns. It binds finance companies in both classification and regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
