# Record Keeping

This node sets the record-keeping regime in SAMA's AML/CTF guide, requiring retention for at least ten years from the end of the business relationship, conclusion of the transaction, account closure, or completion of an occasional customer transaction, with extension where the competent authority instructs. It requires approved internal retention procedures, resourced and protected archiving, at least annual effectiveness testing, and retrievable auditable records available to SAMA and competent authorities. Where record keeping is outsourced or held outside Saudi Arabia, the institution remains responsible and must secure contractual access, confidentiality and inspection rights.
