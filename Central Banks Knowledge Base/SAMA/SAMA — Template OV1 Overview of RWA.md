# Template OV1: Overview of RWA

Pillar 3 node for template OV1, the overview of risk-weighted assets by risk type (credit, counterparty credit, securitisation, market, operational and residual RWA), distinguishing modelled approaches approved by SAMA from standardised approaches and showing RWA as if the full standardised approach applied. It includes the definitions and row-by-row instructions that determine how each RWA component is allocated, including output-floor comparison figures. Applies to banks under SAMA prudential supervision subject to Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
