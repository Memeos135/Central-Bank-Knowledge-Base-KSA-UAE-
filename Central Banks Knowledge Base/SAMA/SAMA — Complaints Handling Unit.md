# Complaints Handling Unit

A mandated sub-unit of the Customer Care Department that banks must establish under SAMA's Rules on Establishing the Customer Care Department in Banks (second edition, effective December 2022, replacing the earlier complaint-unit rules). It handles customer complaints as defined in the rules (any expression of dissatisfaction, written or oral, justified or not) and must offer multiple easily accessible intake channels published prominently on the bank's website and platforms, at minimum toll-free phone, website, mobile apps, email and branches. Binds banks licensed by SAMA to operate in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
