# Corporate Governance & Compliance

## Why this community

Institutional governance architecture for SAMA-supervised entities: board composition and duties, mandated board committees, the compliance and internal audit functions, conduct/ethics obligations, disclosure, and remuneration design including risk-adjustment mechanisms.

## How members connect

- The core governance principles act as the parent instrument; board qualification, board responsibility, committee and sector-annex items are subordinate provisions citing back to it.
- An obligation chain runs board → audit/risk/nomination-and-remuneration committees → compliance unit and internal audit, allocating oversight, reporting lines and independence requirements.
- The code of conduct links governance to substantive regimes — AML/CTF, confidentiality and conflict of interest — showing conduct duties as the delivery mechanism for those regimes at firm level.
- Remuneration rules depend on governance definitions (independent member, nomination and remuneration committee) and add risk-sensitive controls such as malus, clawback, misconduct-risk adjustment and compliance reporting.
- Sector-specific overlays (exchange sector annex; finance and real estate refinance compliance principles) apply the general principles proportionately to particular licence types.
