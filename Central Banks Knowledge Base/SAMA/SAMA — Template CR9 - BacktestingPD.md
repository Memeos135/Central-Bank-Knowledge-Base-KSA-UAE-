# Template CR9 - Backtesting/PD

Specification for Pillar 3 Template CR9, the annual disclosure backtesting internal probability-of-default estimates against realised obligor default rates for each regulatory portfolio, using at least a five-year average annual default rate. It is mandatory for banks on the foundation and/or advanced IRB approaches, requiring separate portfolio breakdowns where both approaches are used, coverage of key SAMA-approved group-wide PD models, and narrative explaining model scope and the share of RWA covered. Binds SAMA-licensed banks using IRB for credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
