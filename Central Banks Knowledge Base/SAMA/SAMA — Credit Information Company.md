# Credit Information Company

Defined term and licensing subject of the Implementing Regulation of the Credit Information Law: a credit information company licensed by SAMA to collect, maintain and supply consumer credit information to Members on request. Licensing conditions include being a joint stock company headquartered in the Kingdom, minimum paid-up capital of SAR 50 million, SAMA approval of constitutive documents and the credit information system, fees, a five-year renewable licence, and prior written SAMA approval for branches, mergers/acquisitions and constitutional amendments. Binds founders and licensed credit bureaus operating in KSA.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk
