# Blue Team

Defines the Blue Team as the Member Organization's cyber security monitoring function (e.g. SOC) that monitors and analyses security alerts, is expected to detect the Red Team's simulated attacks and invoke incident response procedures, and is generally unaware that a test is underway. In the lessons learned phase the Blue Team produces its own report of alerts detected, actions taken and recommendations, and participates with the Red Team in the chronological replay exercise. Its performance is the primary measure of detection and response capability assessed by the exercise.
