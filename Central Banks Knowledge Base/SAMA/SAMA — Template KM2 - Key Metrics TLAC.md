# Template KM2 - Key Metrics TLAC

Pillar 3 disclosure template KM2 setting out key metrics on total loss-absorbing capacity (TLAC) available and TLAC requirements measured at resolution group level, under single point of entry and multiple point of entry resolution approaches. It specifies quarterly, fixed-format quantitative disclosure of TLAC ratios against RWA and leverage exposure, with narrative explanation of material changes. Mandatory only for resolution groups of G-SIBs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
