# Quarterly and Annual Financial Statements Comparison Form

A SAMA supervisory reporting template issued by the Exchange Centers Supervision Division (version 2.0) for licensed money exchange centers to submit comparative quarterly and annual financial statements. It fixes the line items to be reported across the balance sheet (e.g. foreign currency inventory, amounts due from/to related parties for cash withdrawals, credit sales and cash support, zakat and end-of-service provisions, owner's equity and retained earnings) and the income statement (revenues, salaries, general and administrative expenses, zakat), with income-statement data covering three months for quarterly filings and a full year for annual filings. It binds SAMA-licensed exchange houses as a periodic prudential/supervisory submission format rather than a substantive conduct rule.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
