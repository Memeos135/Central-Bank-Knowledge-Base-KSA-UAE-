# Template CR10 - IRB Specialised Lending Under Slotting Approach

Pillar 3 disclosure template CR10 in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, covering specialised lending (and equity, where relevant) exposures risk-weighted under the supervisory slotting approach rather than full F-IRB/A-IRB modelling. It binds banks that use IRB-family approaches and apply supervisory slotting categories, sitting alongside the standardised-approach and IRB credit risk templates. Relevant when identifying which credit exposures fall outside modelled PD/LGD disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
