# AML/CTF Compliance Function

This node describes the AML/CTF compliance function required by SAMA's guide: an independent, specialised unit headed by a senior-management-level officer who reports administratively and technically to the board and its committees, with timely access to customer data, transaction records and any information needed. It lists the officer's mandated duties, including maintaining the AML/CTF program, policy review and monitoring, internal STR handling and SAFIU reporting, sanctions-list notifications to SAMA, branch inspections, and periodic board reporting. It also sets board and senior management responsibilities for risk identification in new products and channels, at least annual policy review, training, and adequate staffing, systems and budget; staffing of the unit is restricted to Saudi nationals.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
