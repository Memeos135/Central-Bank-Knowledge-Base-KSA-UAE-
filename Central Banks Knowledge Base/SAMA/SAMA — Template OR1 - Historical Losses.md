# Template OR1 - Historical Losses

Template OR1 requires annual disclosure of aggregate operational risk losses over the past ten years, net of recoveries and of excluded losses, at loss event thresholds of SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000, plus information on whether losses feed the internal loss multiplier (ILM). Scope is mandatory for banks in the second or third business indicator (BI) bucket and for first-bucket banks with SAMA approval to use internal loss data. Narrative commentary on new loss exclusions and other material information is expected; the general retrospective-disclosure carve-out does not apply.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
