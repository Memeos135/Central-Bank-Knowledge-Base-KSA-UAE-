# CVA Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure of credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk — how a bank identifies, measures and manages counterparty credit valuation risk and which CVA approach it applies.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a qualitative table in the CVA section of the Pillar 3 disclosure framework.
- Depends on CVA and counterparty credit risk definitions set in the capital adequacy rules; the table imposes a disclosure duty only.
- Interacts with counterparty credit risk and market risk disclosures, since CVA capital treatment and hedging sit across both regimes.
- Hierarchy: Basel standard as adopted by SAMA → disclosure requirements → prescribed table format and frequency.
