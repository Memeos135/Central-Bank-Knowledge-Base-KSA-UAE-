# Credit Derivative Offsetting Rules

A concept node on offsetting and hedge recognition for credit derivatives in the specific risk capital calculation, covering full allowance (an 80% specific risk offset on the higher-requirement leg where long and short positions fully offset, with zero on the other side), partial allowance where asset, currency or maturity mismatches exist, and the capital treatment of first-to-default and nth-to-default basket credit derivatives, including when offset against underlying reference credit instruments is permitted. It binds banks calculating specific risk capital for credit derivative and hedged cash positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
