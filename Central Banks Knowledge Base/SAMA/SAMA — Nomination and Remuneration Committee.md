# Nomination and Remuneration Committee

Concept node for the Nomination and Remuneration Committee as a board committee under SAMA's Key Principles of Governance for financial institutions, supplemented by related SAMA governance/remuneration instruments. It concerns nomination of board and executive candidates, fit-and-proper and independence assessment, board composition and succession, and the design and review of remuneration policy. Binds the boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions within the governance framework's scope (excluding insurance and reinsurance companies).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
