# Circular: Deeds Issued by Licensed Notaries

A Banking Policies Department circular confirming that deeds and documents issued by notaries licensed by the Ministry of Justice (including powers of attorney and their revocation, company contracts and amendments) are to be accepted within the notaries' scope of competence and treated as enforceable instruments under the Enforcement Law. It also notes that the validity of a notary's licence can be verified through the Ministry of Justice notaries portal. It binds banks, insurance and reinsurance companies, finance companies, credit information companies and money exchange businesses.
