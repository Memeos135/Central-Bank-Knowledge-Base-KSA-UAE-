# BNPL Licensing Procedures

The procedural part of SAMA's BNPL licensing guidelines, setting out how the Applicant submits the completed application with checklist attachments and how SAMA notifies completeness and processes the request. It reinforces submission rules (no modification of the form, submission by founders or an authorised representative, soft copies with originals retained for SAMA on demand) and the consequences of inaccurate or incomplete disclosure. It binds founders/shareholders seeking SAMA approval to incorporate and licence a BNPL company.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance
