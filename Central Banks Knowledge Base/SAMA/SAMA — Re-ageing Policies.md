# Re-ageing Policies

Requires banks to maintain documented re-ageing policies governing the counting of days past due and the granting of extensions, deferrals, renewals and rewrites, with mandatory minimum content (approval authorities, minimum facility age, eligible delinquency levels, maximum re-ageings, reassessment of repayment capacity) applied consistently and consistent with the use test. Adjacent material covers overdraft limits and days-past-due triggers, and work-out/monitoring systems for purchased receivables. Binds banks using the IRB approaches for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
