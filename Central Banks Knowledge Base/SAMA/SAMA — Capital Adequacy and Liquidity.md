# Capital Adequacy and Liquidity

Chapter II of the Real Estate Refinance Rules imposes prudential requirements on the licensed refinance company: compliance with SAMA-determined capital adequacy ratio, leverage ratio and minimum liquidity ratio, and prior written SAMA approval for any capital increase or decrease. The surrounding provisions also confirm SAMA's power to control and restrict service pricing and list the activity prohibitions (e.g. no direct finance to borrowers, collateral transfer conditions, restrictions on asset acquisition and liquidation). Binds the licensed real estate refinance company and its board/senior management.
