# Risk Management Framework

Sets the risk management obligations of a Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company: a board-approved, annually updated written risk policy covering an enumerated risk taxonomy (credit, market, operational, legal, reputational, technology, fraud, information security, cyber, ML/TF, outsourcing, platform cessation, and Participant-protection/default risk), plus procedures for identification, assessment, monitoring, escalation and reporting. Adjacent provisions cover external auditor appointment with SAMA non-objection, business continuity, 10-year record retention, staffing, and compliance with AML/CTF laws and SAMA information-security and financial-crime instructions. Binds the licensed DBC company's board and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, BNPL/finance
