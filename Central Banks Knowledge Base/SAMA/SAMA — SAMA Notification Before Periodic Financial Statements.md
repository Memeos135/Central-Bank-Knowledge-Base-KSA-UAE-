# SAMA Notification Before Periodic Financial Statements

SAMA circular issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation requiring finance companies listed on the Saudi Exchange to notify SAMA before publishing periodic financial statements on Tadawul's website, and to provide SAMA with the draft financial-results announcement in Arabic and English five business days before publication. It is without prejudice to other applicable laws and instructions, and binds only listed finance companies.
