# Early Repayment Provisions

The early repayment article (Article 10) of SAMA's mandatory standard consumer finance contract for individuals, flagged in the contract summary as a term the beneficiary must be pointed to. It governs the beneficiary's right to settle the financing ahead of schedule and the resulting treatment of term cost/profit and amounts due, within the uniform template that financiers may not vary. It binds banks and finance companies contracting with individual beneficiaries.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
