# Publication of Finance Company Financial Statements

A circular reaffirming the disclosure duties in Articles 72 and 73 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law regarding audited annual and quarterly financial statements, the auditor's report and the board report. It requires finance companies to publish those reports and statements on their own websites within ten business days after the end of the prescribed submission period. Binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company.
