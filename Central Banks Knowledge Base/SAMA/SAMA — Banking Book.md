# Banking Book

A concept node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk covering the banking book as the counterpart to the trading book, specifically how banking book credit, equity and interest rate exposures are treated when hedged via internal risk transfers with the trading book. It sets the conditions under which a banking book exposure is deemed hedged for capital purposes, and how unrecognised short credit/equity positions fall into the market risk rules instead. Binds banks subject to SAMA's capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
