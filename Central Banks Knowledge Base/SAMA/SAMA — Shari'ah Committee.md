# Shari'ah Committee

Definitional node from SAMA's Profit Sharing Investment Accounts Rules setting out the Shari'ah Committee as the body supervising compliance with Shari'ah principles in the bank, with "Shari'ah Compliant" defined by reference to that committee's decisions. It sits alongside the Rules' other defined terms (Islamic Window, IAH, PSIA, Mudarabah, Musharakah, Wakalah) and the Rules' legal basis, objectives and scope. Applies to local Saudi banks licensed under the Banking Control Law that conduct Shari'ah compliant banking.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
