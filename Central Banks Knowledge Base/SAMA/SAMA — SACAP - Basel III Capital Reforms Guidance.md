# SACAP - Basel III Capital Reforms Guidance

Reference points into SAMA's capital framework (SACAP) used by the Pillar 3 composition-of-capital disclosures: the regulatory adjustments and deductions applied to CET1 (prudent valuation, goodwill and intangibles, DTAs, cash flow hedge reserve, provision shortfall, securitisation gain on sale, own credit risk, pension assets, own shares, reciprocal cross-holdings, threshold deductions) and the parallel Tier 2 deductions covering capital instruments and other TLAC liabilities. Includes the 10% and 5% threshold treatments, with distinct conditions for G-SIBs versus non-G-SIBs. Binds banks calculating regulatory capital and completing Template CC1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
