# CCTV Specifications for the Financial Sector

SAMA's CCTV Specifications for the financial sector (v1.0, July 2019), setting minimum physical-security surveillance requirements for supervised financial institutions' premises. It covers operational deployment (restricted and logged access to the CCTV/control room, camera siting and relocation approvals, daily system checks, reporting of overwritten or missing footage, cooperation with law enforcement through bank procedures) and references international standards for security surveillance systems. It binds banks and other financial organisations in respect of head offices, branches, cash centres and ATMs, and carries footage-retention duties that matter for customer complaints and disputed claims.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
