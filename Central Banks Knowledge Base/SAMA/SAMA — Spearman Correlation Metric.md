# Spearman Correlation Metric

Sets out the Spearman rank correlation metric, one of the two PLA test statistics, which ranks the most recent 250 trading days of HPL and RTPL observations and measures the correlation between the two rank series. The resulting coefficient feeds the green/amber/red zone classification that determines a trading desk's continued eligibility for the internal models approach. Applies to banks calculating market risk capital under IMA supervision by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
