# Financial Lease Contract for Individuals

SAMA circular issued under the Banking Control Law, Finance Companies Control Law and Finance Lease Law (and Article 10(2) of its Implementing Regulation) mandating a standard-form vehicle finance lease (Ijara) contract for individuals. Lessors must use the annexed template, complete internal procedures by the stated deadline, and may not conclude contracts that deviate from or amend the standard form. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom acting as lessors to individual lessees.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
