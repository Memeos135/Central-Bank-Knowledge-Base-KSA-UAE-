# General Department of Cyber Risk Control

Node for the SAMA organisational unit referenced in connection with this IT governance/IT controls framework; the surrounding text concerns the IT risk management chapter (risk identification and analysis, risk register content, risk treatment options of accept/avoid/transfer/mitigate, and risk reporting, monitoring and profiling). It is useful for locating the supervisory owner of the framework and the IT risk governance expectations imposed on Member Organizations, including reporting to business owners, senior management, the risk committee and the ITSC. Substantive obligations in the surrounding context bind Member Organizations rather than the department itself.
