# Market Risk Standardised Approach

## Why this community

Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of market risk capital requirements calculated under the simplified standardised approach, for banks not using internal models.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a fixed-format quantitative template reporting capital charges by risk class.
- Scope is mutually exclusive with the internal models disclosures — applicability turns on which calculation approach the bank is permitted to use.
- Figures must reconcile to the market risk capital requirement computed under the capital adequacy rules; the template does not create the calculation, only the disclosure duty.
- Read alongside the market risk qualitative table, which explains the approach behind the reported numbers.
