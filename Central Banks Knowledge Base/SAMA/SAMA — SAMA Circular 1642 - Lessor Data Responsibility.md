# SAMA Circular 1642 - Lessor Data Responsibility

An Arabic-language SAMA circular reaffirming that lessors are responsible for the accuracy of the data they submit when applying to a licensed finance lease contract registration company for issuance of executive deed (writ of execution) extracts for a registered finance lease contract. It is issued under the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation and refers back to the earlier controls and procedures circular governing requests for and issuance of such extracts. It binds lessors (banks and finance companies) for data accuracy and any prejudice to lessee rights, while preserving registration companies' own duty to verify the lessor's entitlement and obtain the required undertakings before issuing extracts.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
