# Operational CTI Principles

The operational tier of SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, covering identification of threat actors' modus operandi, behaviours and techniques. It complements the Core, Strategic and Technical/Tactical tiers within a holistic CTI practice. Applies to Member Organizations, including where CTI is outsourced, with phased implementation left to their discretion.
