# Board of Directors Responsibilities

Section 1 of the governance chapter of SAMA's AML/CTF guide, setting out the board of directors' non-exhaustive responsibilities for AML/CTF compliance: ensuring an accurate institution-wide ML/TF risk assessment, adopting risk-mitigation policy, ensuring independent audit mechanisms, addressing branches or subsidiaries in jurisdictions that cannot apply the requirements, and receiving regular reports on ML/TF risks, audit findings, SAMA inspection results and targeted financial sanctions implementation. Oversight must align with international best practice and SAMA Governance Guidelines, and be documented (e.g. board or committee minutes). It binds boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
