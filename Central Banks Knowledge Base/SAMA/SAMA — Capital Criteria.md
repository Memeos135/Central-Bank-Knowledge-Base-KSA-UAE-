# Capital Criteria

The capital element of SAMA's minimum bank licensing criteria: SAMA assesses capital adequacy case-by-case against the scale, nature and complexity of operations proposed in the applicant's business plan. Foreign bank branches are not required to maintain capital in KSA, though SAMA may impose capital requirements on them case-by-case, for example for high-risk businesses or specialised business lines. Applies to all banking licence applicants and sits within the broader expectation of compliance with the BCL and prudential requirements from commencement of operations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
