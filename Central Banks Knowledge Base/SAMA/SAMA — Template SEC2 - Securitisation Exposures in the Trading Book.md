# Template SEC2 - Securitisation Exposures in the Trading Book

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering securitisation exposures in the trading book where the bank acts as originator, sponsor or investor. It sits in the securitisation section (alongside Table SECA and template SEC1), which captures all securitisation exposures regardless of whether risk transfer recognition criteria are met, as opposed to SEC3/SEC4 which are limited to banking book exposures treated under the securitisation framework. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime at the regulatory scope of consolidation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
