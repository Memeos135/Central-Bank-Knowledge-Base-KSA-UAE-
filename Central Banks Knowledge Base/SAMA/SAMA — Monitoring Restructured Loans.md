# Monitoring Restructured Loans

Concept node on ongoing monitoring of borrowers showing increased credit risk and of restructured loans, covering early warning signals, watch-list classification at a lower rating, corrective action plans (additional collateral, site visits, credit committee updates, revised forecasts) and defined timelines proportionate to loan size and risk. It extends the early warning system to restructured as well as never-defaulted performing loans, and addresses bank oversight of borrower cash-flow management and expenditure controls during a workout within the rights conferred by the restructuring agreement. Binds banks' business, risk and credit committee functions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
