# Malus and Clawback

Defines and mandates ex post remuneration adjustment tools: malus (reducing unvested deferred remuneration before vesting) and clawback (recovery of variable remuneration already paid or vested). Banks must document such mechanisms in policies and procedures and apply them where risks, misconduct, material error, performance downturn or risk-management failure subsequently emerge, including clawback of unvested amounts on negative contributions during the vesting period. Binds banks' Boards and remuneration governance, with justifications for reductions submitted to SAMA with the compliance report.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
