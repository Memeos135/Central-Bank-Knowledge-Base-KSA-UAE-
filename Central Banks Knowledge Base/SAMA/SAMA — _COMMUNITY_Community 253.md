# IT Steering Committee

## Why this community

The governance body accountable for IT strategy, prioritisation and oversight within a supervised entity, and the board-delegated mandate that makes IT decisions attributable to named accountable persons.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the committee is the accountability node to which IT governance obligations (strategy approval, resource allocation, escalation) attach.
- Connects upward to board responsibility and downward to individual IT control requirements in the same framework.
