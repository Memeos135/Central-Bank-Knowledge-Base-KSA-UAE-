# EAD Estimation

Specifies the standards for own estimates of exposure at default under the advanced IRB approach: EAD as expected gross exposure at default, floors at current drawn amount for on-balance sheet items, focus on off-balance sheet conversion factors, long-run default-weighted averages with a margin of conservatism, and data-quality expectations (no capping of reference data, handling of the ULF instability region). Binds banks using A-IRB for credit risk capital, subject to SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
