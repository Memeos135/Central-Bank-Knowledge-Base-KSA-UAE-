# Definition of Default

Covers the regulatory reference definition of default and its use in credit risk disclosure, including the standardised-approach anchor of exposures past due more than 90 days and the flows into and out of defaulted status (new defaults, cures, write-offs). It also frames PD backtesting against observed default rates for IRB models. Relevant to all SAMA-supervised banks for credit risk measurement and Pillar 3 disclosure; the backtesting element binds F-IRB/A-IRB banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
