# Trading Desk

Defines the trading desk as the unambiguously defined group of traders or trading accounts that forms the basic unit for market risk capital and model approval. Sets out required attributes: a head trader with direct oversight, single-desk assignment of trading accounts, a defined risk scope, a documented business strategy with budget and management information, and a clear risk management and trading limits structure. Desk structures require SAMA approval; internal operational subdesks do not, but are not used in the capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
