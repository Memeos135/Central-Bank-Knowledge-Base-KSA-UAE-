# Internal & Shariah Audit

## Why this community

Third-line assurance architecture for SAMA-supervised entities: the mandate, independence and reporting lines of internal audit, and the parallel Shariah compliance and Shariah audit functions required of banks, Islamic windows and finance companies.

## How members connect

- Obligation chain by organ: board and audit committee set and approve the internal audit charter/policy and risk-based plan; executive management must enable access and remediation; the internal audit department executes — each node cross-references the others.
- Shariah governance mirrors this hierarchy: the Shariah Committee issues resolutions, a Shariah compliance function monitors first/second-line adherence, and internal Shariah audit provides independent assurance, typically housed within or reporting alongside internal audit.
- Sectoral split of the same concept: the Shariah Governance Framework for Local Banks and the Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies impose analogous duties on different licence types; product development and Islamic window operations are the in-scope activities.
- The Three Lines of Defense model is the shared conceptual spine linking internal audit, compliance principles for banks and conduct/ethics rules for exchange centres.
