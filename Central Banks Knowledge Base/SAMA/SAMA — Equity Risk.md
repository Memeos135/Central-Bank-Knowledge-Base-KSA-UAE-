# Equity Risk

Concept node for equity risk as a defined market risk class and as a separately reported exposure category in the capital/RWA overview templates, where equity positions are excluded from general credit risk rows and reported under the simple risk weight approach or internal models method. It governs how equity positions attract sensitivity-based (delta/vega) market risk capital and how they are disclosed alongside credit and counterparty credit risk RWA. Binds banks subject to SAMA minimum capital and Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
