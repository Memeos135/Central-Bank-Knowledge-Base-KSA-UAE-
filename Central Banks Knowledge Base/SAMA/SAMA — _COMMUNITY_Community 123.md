# Operational Risk Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 operational risk disclosures: the loss history, the Business Indicator and its components, and the resulting minimum operational risk capital requirement.

## How members connect

- Obligation chain: historical loss data (OR1) and the Business Indicator with its subcomponents (OR2) are the two inputs that feed the capital figure disclosed in OR3.
- Both OR1 and OR2 are cross-referenced by OR3, making OR3 the reconciliation point where the standardised operational risk calculation is evidenced.
- All three share the defined terms (Business Indicator, loss component, internal loss multiplier) fixed in the operational risk capital rules; a change in scope or bucket thresholds there changes all three templates.
- Read together with the qualitative operational risk framework table, which explains the governance behind these numbers.
