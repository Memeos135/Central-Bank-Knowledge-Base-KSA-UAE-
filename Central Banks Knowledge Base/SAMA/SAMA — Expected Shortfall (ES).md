# Expected Shortfall (ES)

Concept node for Expected Shortfall (ES), the internal models approach risk measure used to set market risk capital requirements, disclosed in both unconstrained form and in the constrained form that applies supervisory limits on cross-risk-factor correlations (summed as partial ES across interest rate, equity, FX, commodity and credit spread classes). The node sits within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure material, where ES appears alongside non-modellable risk factor charges, default risk capital and the amber-desk surcharge in the aggregation of IMA capital requirements. It binds banks authorised to use the IMA for market risk and drives what they must report publicly.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
