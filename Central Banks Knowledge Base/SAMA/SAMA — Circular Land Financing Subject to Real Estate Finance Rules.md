# Circular: Land Financing Subject to Real Estate Finance Rules

Urgent SAMA circular confirming, in response to industry queries, that financing of land for individuals falls under Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law. It relies on SAMA's powers under the Real Estate Finance Law and the Finance Companies Control Law. Binds all real estate financiers — banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
