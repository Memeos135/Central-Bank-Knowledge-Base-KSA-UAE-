# Cyber Security Review

Sub-domain 3.2.4 of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, requiring periodic cyber security reviews of the Member Organization's information assets to confirm that controls are securely designed, implemented and monitored for effectiveness. Reviews focus on critical information assets and customer/internet-facing services. Results feed the Framework's maturity model, under which level 3 and above require demonstrable, formalized and reviewed controls.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
