# Basel Committee Banking Supervision

A concept node marking the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision framework as the source standard underlying SAMA's recalibrated IRRBB shock scenarios. It is not itself a SAMA instrument but signals that the prescribed six-scenario EVE / two-scenario NII approach, currency-specific shock magnitudes and shaping formulas follow the Basel IRRBB standard as adopted and adjusted by SAMA. Relevant to banks reconciling SAMA requirements with international prudential standards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
