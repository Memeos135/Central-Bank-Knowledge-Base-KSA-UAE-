# Supervisory Parameter p

This node concerns the supervisory parameter p used in the securitization internal-ratings-based approach, which drives the capital surcharge applied to securitization tranches above the underlying pool's capital requirement; the surrounding material also covers other supervisory-set parameters (supervisory delta, supervisory factor, maturity factor) in the market and counterparty credit risk calculations. It governs how regulator-prescribed inputs enter RWA formulas. It binds banks computing capital under SAMA's credit and market risk frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
