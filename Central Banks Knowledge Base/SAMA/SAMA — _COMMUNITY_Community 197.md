# Martyrs' Cheque Exemption

## Why this community

Protection of specified beneficiary payments (martyrs' entitlements) from enforcement action, limiting banks' ability to apply judicial seizure or set-off to those amounts.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an exemption instruction that carves out a defined category of funds from general attachment/seizure execution duties.
- Legal linkage runs to enforcement-court cooperation obligations and account-freezing procedures; the exemption operates as a scope limit on them.
- Consequence for decision-making: banks must identify and ring-fence the exempt amounts before executing a seizure order.
