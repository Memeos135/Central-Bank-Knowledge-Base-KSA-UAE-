# Remuneration Awarded Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of remuneration actually awarded in the financial year, by category and instrument type.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a fixed-format disclosure template, so the obligation is completeness and format fidelity rather than substantive conduct.
- Reads together with the qualitative remuneration policy table, which supplies the definitions and policy context for the reported amounts.
- Part of the broader Pillar 3 disclosure framework and its periodic publication timing requirements.
