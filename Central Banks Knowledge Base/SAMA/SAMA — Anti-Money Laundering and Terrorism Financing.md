# Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing

This is the anti-financial-crime strand of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics for Financial Institutions (circular, August 2019), sitting under the heading on combating financial and administrative corruption crimes alongside anti-bribery. It sets conduct-level expectations that staff avoid, detect and escalate money laundering, terrorist financing and suspicious transactions, complementing (not replacing) the AML/CTF Law and SAMA's AML/CTF rules. It binds all persons associated with a supervised financial institution — board and committee members, executives, permanent and contract employees, consultants and third-party-supplied staff — in banks, insurance/reinsurance and free-zone insurance firms, finance and finance-lease registration companies, and credit information companies, with the board required to adopt a conforming internal code.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
