# Independent Audit Function

## Why this community

The requirement for an independent audit function to test the adequacy and effectiveness of the AML/CTF programme, separate from the compliance function that operates it.

## How members connect

- Standalone node: its legal significance is as the third line of defence — independence from the function being tested is the operative requirement, not the audit methodology.
- Cross-regime linkage: findings feed governance/risk reporting lines and, in practice, the senior management and compliance-function duties in the AML compliance cluster.
