# Default and Contract Termination

A clause-level node covering breach, late payment consequences and termination of the standard individual vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract mandated by SAMA. It links to the contract's late-payment provisions and the summary disclosure of consequences of missed rental payments, and governs when the lessor may terminate and what the lessee owes. Binding on banks and finance companies acting as lessors.
