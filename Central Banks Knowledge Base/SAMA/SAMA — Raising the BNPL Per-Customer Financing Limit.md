# Raising the BNPL Per-Customer Financing Limit

SAMA circular raising the cap in Article 22(1) of the Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies on total outstanding finance per individual customer from SAR 5,000 to SAR 10,000, exercising the express power in that provision to increase or decrease the amount. The circular confirms that all other provisions and requirements continue to apply, including the debt-burden ratios under the Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals. It binds banks, finance companies and BNPL companies operating in the Kingdom.
