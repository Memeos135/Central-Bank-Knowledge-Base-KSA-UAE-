# Template MR1: Market Risk Standardised Approach

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering market risk capital requirements computed under the standardised approach, reported alongside the wider RWA breakdown (credit risk, counterparty credit risk, CVA, securitisation, settlement risk) and the minimum capital requirement measure. It fixes the rows, definitions and instructions banks must follow when publishing these figures. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the Pillar 3 regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
