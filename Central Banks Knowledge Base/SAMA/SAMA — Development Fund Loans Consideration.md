# Development Fund Loans Consideration

SAMA circular requiring banks and finance companies to take into account loans granted to individual customers by development funds and banks affiliated with the National Development Fund when assessing affordability. It builds on the Responsible Lending Principles requirement to count government-provided loans within the customer's monthly obligations, and directs lenders to rely on reports from licensed credit bureaus and to include the development-fund deduction in the customer's monthly debt-burden ratio before granting new financing.
