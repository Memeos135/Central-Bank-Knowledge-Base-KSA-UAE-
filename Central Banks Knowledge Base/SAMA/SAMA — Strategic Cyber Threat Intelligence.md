# Strategic Cyber Threat Intelligence

Domain 2 of the SAMA CTI Principles, covering strategic cyber threat intelligence focused on the objectives, motivations and intent of threat actors, attribution, links between cyber events and the financial sector ecosystem. Principle 12 requires Member Organizations to identify the cyber threat landscape relevant to their organisation and operations, including vulnerable assets, threats, risks, threat actors, observed trends and events that can shift the sector's threat landscape. Strategic output is directed to executive management (e.g. the CISO) for onward relay to IT management and business leaders.
