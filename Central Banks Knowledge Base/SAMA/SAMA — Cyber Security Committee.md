# Cyber Security Committee

Concept node for the cyber security committee referenced in the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, sitting within the framework's governance layer alongside board-level accountability, the cyber security policy and defined roles and responsibilities. It is the body to which matters such as accepted cyber security risk (risk within appetite and not conflicting with SAMA rules) are reported, and it anchors oversight of policy approval, resourcing and control ownership. It binds Member Organizations (entities affiliated with SAMA) and their boards and senior management, not customers or third parties directly.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
