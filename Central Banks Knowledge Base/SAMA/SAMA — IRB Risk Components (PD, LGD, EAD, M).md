# IRB Risk Components (PD, LGD, EAD, M)

Concept node covering the four IRB risk components — probability of default (PD), loss given default (LGD), exposure at default (EAD) and effective maturity (M) — as used in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk to drive the IRB risk-weight functions. It distinguishes foundation IRB (supervisory parameter values) from advanced IRB (own estimates) across corporate, sovereign, bank, retail, equity and purchased receivable asset classes, and covers how credit risk mitigants such as guarantees and collateral feed into the parameters. It binds SAMA-supervised banks that have adopted, or seek approval for, an internal ratings-based approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
