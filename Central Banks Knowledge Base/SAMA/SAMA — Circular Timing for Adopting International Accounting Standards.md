# Circular: Timing for Adopting International Accounting Standards

SAMA circular setting the timetable for finance companies to begin applying international accounting standards under Article 71 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. Applicants licensed as joint stock companies start from the beginning of 2015 and submit IFRS-based quarterly statements from Q1 2015; applicants licensed as limited liability companies start from the date of the Minister of Commerce and Industry's decision converting them into a joint stock company, with quarterly statements from the first quarter of their first financial year as a JSC or Q1 2015, whichever is later, in line with Article 72. Binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.
