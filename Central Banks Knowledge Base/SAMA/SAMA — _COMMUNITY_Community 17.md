# Ethical Red Teaming

## Why this community

SAMA's intelligence-led ethical red teaming regime for financial entities: mandated adversarial testing of live production systems, the roles of the parties involved, and the reporting and remediation duties that follow a test.

## How members connect

- The F.E.E.R. framework is the parent instrument; it defines each team role (Red, Blue, White, Green) and the qualification conditions for an external Red Teaming Provider.
- Obligation chain runs test → findings → reports: the Red Teaming Provider issues the Red Team Evaluation Report and the defending Blue Team issues the Blue Team Report, both of which feed a mandatory Remediation Plan owned by the White Team.
- Shared defined terms (Threat Intelligence, Cyber Kill Chain Methodology) fix the required methodology and scope of the exercise.
- Penetration Testing is distinguished from red teaming in scope and intrusiveness — a scope boundary that matters for whether an entity has met the framework's requirement.
