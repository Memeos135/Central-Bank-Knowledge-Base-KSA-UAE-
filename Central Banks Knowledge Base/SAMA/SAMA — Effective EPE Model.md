# Effective EPE Model

Concept node for the Effective EPE (expected positive exposure) model used under the internal models method to determine counterparty credit exposure at netting-set level. It sets out model validation, backtesting, benchmarking of pricing models, parameter-update frequency and ongoing performance assessment expectations, including SAMA's power to require correction of model or data deficiencies and to impose additional capital, and the option to use a more conservative measure than alpha times Effective EPE with prior SAMA approval. Binds IMM-approved banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
