# Professional Liability Risk

Sub-element of professional risk addressing liability losses arising from shareholder suits, customer claims, regulatory non-compliance fines and negligent or imprudent acts of directors, officers and employees. The guidance treats such litigation risk as controllable through product risk analysis, staff training, careful contractual indemnities with customers, and professional indemnity insurance, and distinguishes liability attaching to the bank from that attaching to directors and officers. Aimed at bank management and boards setting the operational risk management plan.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
