# Senior Management Responsibilities

Section 2 of the governance chapter of SAMA's AML/CTF guide, allocating to senior management continuous implementation and monitoring of AML/CTF compliance, complementing the board's oversight role and requiring that AML/CTF policies, procedures and controls be grounded in ML/TF risk assessment results and integrated into overall risk management. Related provisions require approval at senior management level of transaction-monitoring measures and senior management sign-off for high-risk customer relationships. It binds senior management of SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
