# Market Risk

Section 8 (Principle 3.0) of the same SAMA framework, requiring banks to maintain a market risk management framework, strategy, limits and independent middle-office monitoring covering all assets held, including illiquid or highly volatile ones. It addresses valuation and income recognition policies, stress-tested quantification of net open positions, product-specific risks (Salam, Parallel Salam, Sukuk, Murabahah assets), FX risk on cross-border and foreign-currency balances, and escalation when limits are approached or breached. Binds banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
