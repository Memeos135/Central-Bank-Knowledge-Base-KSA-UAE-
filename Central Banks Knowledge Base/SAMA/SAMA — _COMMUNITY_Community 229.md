# Liquidity Coverage Ratio Template

## Why this community

Standardised public disclosure of the Liquidity Coverage Ratio - high-quality liquid assets against stressed net cash outflows over a 30-day horizon.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a fixed-format template giving effect to the disclosure limb of the LCR standard; the substantive calibration, haircuts and run-off assumptions sit in the LCR rules, not here.
- Uses the LCR standard's defined terms (HQLA categories, net cash outflows, weighted/unweighted values) and its averaging and observation-period conventions.
- Complements the qualitative liquidity risk management disclosure, forming the quantitative half of the liquidity transparency package.
