# Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

Covers the requirement for Member Organisations to define, approve and monitor Key Risk Indicators measuring position against the agreed Fraud Risk Appetite and giving early warning of rising fraud exposure. KRIs must rest on a documented methodology tied to the risks identified in the Fraud Risk Assessment. Binds Member Organisations as part of the Counter-Fraud Programme's governance layer.
