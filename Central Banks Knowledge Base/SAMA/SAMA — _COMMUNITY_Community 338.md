# Cyber Security Architecture

## Why this community

Cyber security architecture as a governance-level control domain: the requirement that a regulated entity's security design be defined, approved and maintained as part of its overall cyber security framework rather than assembled ad hoc.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the architecture requirement sits at the design layer of the SAMA cyber security framework and is the anchor other technical domains (infrastructure, application, cryptography, access) are expected to conform to.
- Legally it operates as an obligation on the member organisation's governance and risk function, not as a standalone technical standard.
