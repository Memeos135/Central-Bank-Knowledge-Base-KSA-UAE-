# CVA Risk General Disclosure

## Why this community

General qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk — how the bank identifies, measures, governs and hedges counterparty-credit valuation risk.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the entry-point qualitative disclosure applying to all banks subject to the CVA risk capital framework, irrespective of approach chosen.
- Acts as the parent to approach-specific CVA disclosures (e.g. SA-CVA), which add obligations on top of this baseline.
- Relies on defined terms from the counterparty credit risk and derivatives framework; scope follows the population of in-scope derivative and SFT exposures.
