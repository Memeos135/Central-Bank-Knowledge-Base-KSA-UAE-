# Repurchase Agreement (Repo)

Defined-term node for a repurchase agreement (repo) under SAMA's repo guidelines, covering the sale of eligible securities with an agreement to repurchase equivalent securities at an agreed future date or on demand; the term is used to cover repo and reverse repo equivalently unless stated otherwise. Related defined roles include Repo Buyer (cash provider) and Repo Seller (securities provider), plus Custodian, Held-in-Custody and Eligible Security. Relevant to any SAMA-supervised institution or eligible counterparty transacting SAR-denominated repo in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
