# IT Governance Framework Glossary

An orientation node for the structure and assessment mechanics of SAMA's IT Governance Framework: how it is organised into domains and subdomains, each with a principle and numbered control requirements, and how compliance is measured. It also covers the periodic self-assessment questionnaire reviewed and audited by SAMA, the six-level IT governance maturity model (0-5, cumulative), and the option of compensating controls plus internal risk acceptance and a formal SAMA waiver where a control cannot be met. Relevant to all Member Organizations subject to the Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
