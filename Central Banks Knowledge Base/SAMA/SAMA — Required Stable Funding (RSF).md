# Required Stable Funding (RSF)

Defines Required Stable Funding — the amount of stable funding a bank must hold against its assets and off-balance-sheet exposures, measured by assigning carrying values to categories and applying RSF factors, with definitions mirroring the LCR unless stated otherwise. Allocation depends on residual maturity or liquidity value, assuming investors exercise maturity-extension options and allowing SAMA to weigh reputational constraints on bank-held options. It governs the NSFR denominator for banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
