# Total Net Cash Outflows

Defines the LCR denominator — total net cash outflows as total expected outflows less total expected inflows over the 30-day stress horizon, including interest expected to be received and paid. It sets the run-off and roll-over factors for deposits, operational and cash management balances, secured funding by collateral type and counterparty, and additional requirements such as 100% treatment of net derivatives cash outflows. It binds Saudi banks in computing and reporting the LCR.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
