# Finance Company Web Updates

## Why this community

Obligations on finance companies to keep public-facing channels — websites and social media — accurate and current, chiefly as a consumer disclosure and fair-dealing requirement.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the duty derives from consumer protection principles applied to finance companies' advertising and product information.
- Cross-reads with product disclosure and certification requirements: published terms must match approved product documentation.
