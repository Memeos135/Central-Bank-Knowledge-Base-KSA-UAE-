# IRB Approach for Credit Risk

Concept node for the internal ratings-based (IRB) approach to credit risk in SAMA's credit risk capital framework, covering asset class definitions (corporate, sovereign, bank, retail, equity, purchased receivables), foundation versus advanced approaches, risk-weight functions, risk components (PD/LGD/EAD/M), supervisory slotting for specialised lending, RWA for purchased receivables including dilution risk, treatment of expected loss and provisions, and the minimum requirements for IRB use. It applies to banks seeking or holding SAMA permission to use IRB approaches for regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
