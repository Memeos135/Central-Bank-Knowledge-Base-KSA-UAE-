# Short-term/Long-term Ratings

Governs use of short-term external ratings in standardised-approach risk weighting: short-term ratings are issue-specific, limited to short-term bank and corporate exposures, and can never support a risk weight for an unrated long-term exposure. It sets the short-term risk weight table, contagion rules where a rated facility attracts 50% or 150%, the interaction with the general preferential treatment for interbank exposures up to three months, the bar on using one group entity's rating for another, and the prohibition on unsolicited ratings. Binds banks calculating credit risk RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
