# PCI-DSS

A concept node for the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard as referenced in SAMA's Cyber Security Framework (v1.0, May 2017), where it appears both as a glossary term for the card-scheme data security standard and as a mandatory external industry standard. Under the Framework's compliance section, member organizations are required to comply with PCI-DSS alongside the EMV technical standard and the SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework, and to maintain a process for identifying and meeting regulatory and industry-standard obligations. It binds SAMA-regulated banks, insurance and financing companies subject to the Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, banking prudential
