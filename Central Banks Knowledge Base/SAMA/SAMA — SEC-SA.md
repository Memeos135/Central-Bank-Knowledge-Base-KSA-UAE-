# SEC-SA

Node on the Securitisation Standardised Approach (SEC-SA), the fall-back method for risk-weighting securitisation exposures where SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA or SEC-IAA cannot be used. It covers the KA/W inputs, the 15% risk-weight floor, inference of risk weights for market-risk hedges, and adjustments for resecuritisation exposures (including the p parameter of 1.5). Binds banks computing securitisation RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
