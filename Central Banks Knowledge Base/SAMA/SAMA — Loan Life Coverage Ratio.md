# Loan Life Coverage Ratio

A concept node (Appendix 2 of the problem loans guidelines) defining and computing the Loan Life Coverage Ratio: the present value of cash flows available for debt service over the loan life plus debt reserve, divided by outstanding loan at assessment date, discounted at the weighted average cost of capital. It is positioned as a multi-period alternative to DSCR and is to be used by Workout teams for project finance and other multi-year loans, with 1.4 cited as the indicative benchmark. Addressed to banks assessing long-term borrower viability.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
