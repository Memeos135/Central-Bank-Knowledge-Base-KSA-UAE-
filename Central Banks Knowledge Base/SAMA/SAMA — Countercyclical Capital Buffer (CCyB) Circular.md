# Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB) Circular

SAMA circular on the Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB), issued under the Central Bank Law and the Banking Control Law, setting the buffer requirement applicable to banks' relevant credit exposures in the Kingdom. It is a macroprudential capital add-on instrument; the source text is heavily degraded, so the effective rate and implementation date should be read from the original document. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
