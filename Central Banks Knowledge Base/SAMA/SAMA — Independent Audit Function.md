# Independent Audit Function

Section 10 of SAMA's AML/CTF guidance/rules for financial institutions, setting the requirement for an independent audit function to test the adequacy and effectiveness of AML/CTF internal controls, policies and procedures on a risk-based basis. It requires resourcing, competence and independence of the auditor from the AML/CTF function, permits use of the external auditor where appropriate, and places responsibility on senior management to remediate identified weaknesses. It binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA and links back to the AML Law and Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing implementing regulations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
