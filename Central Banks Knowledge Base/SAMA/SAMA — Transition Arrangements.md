# Transition Arrangements

Transition node within SAMA's Basel III-aligned capital adequacy/risk-weighted assets framework, covering phase-in arrangements (notably for the standardised treatment of equity exposures and the removal of the IRB approach for equity exposures). Surrounding material in the source addresses credit conversion factors for off-balance sheet commitments and eligibility conditions for external credit assessment institutions (ECAIs). It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia in computing regulatory capital during the phase-in periods.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
