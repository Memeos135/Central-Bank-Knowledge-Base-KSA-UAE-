# IRB Approach: Minimum Requirements

Concept node on the minimum requirements and adoption conditions for the Internal Ratings-Based approach to credit risk, including the distinction between standardised, F-IRB, A-IRB and supervisory slotting treatments and what RWA is reported under each. It covers SAMA-agreed phased rollout across asset classes and business units, the required implementation plan, the bar on capital-minimisation motives, and the prohibition on IRB for equity exposures subject to phase-in. Binds banks seeking or using SAMA approval for internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
