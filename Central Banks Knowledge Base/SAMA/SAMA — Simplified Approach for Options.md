# Simplified Approach for Options

Sets the carve-out treatment under which option positions and their associated cash or forward hedges are excluded from the standardised methodology and given separately calculated capital requirements covering both general and specific market risk, with the resulting figures added back to the relevant risk category (interest rate, equity, FX, commodity). Prescribes position-by-position treatments such as long cash with long put and standalone long call/long put. Relevant to banks with limited options activity; banks with significant trading activity are expected to use the standardised or internal models approaches instead.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
