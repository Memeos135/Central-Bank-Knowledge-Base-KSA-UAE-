# Delta Risk

Delta risk is one of the three components of the sensitivities-based method within SAMA's standardised approach to market risk capital, capturing linear sensitivity of instruments to prescribed risk factors (GIRR, credit spread, equity, FX, commodity) before aggregation with vega and curvature charges. The node sits in the market risk capital framework that SAMA mandates for all banks, D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs alike, and its risk weights also feed the curvature calculation. It binds licensed banks computing Pillar 1 market risk RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
