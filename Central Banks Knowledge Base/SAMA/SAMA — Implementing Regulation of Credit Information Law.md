# Implementing Regulation of Credit Information Law

The Implementing Regulation of the Credit Information Law (Royal Decree M/37, 1429H), issued in Arabic, covering definitions, licensing of credit information companies, scope of credit information services, governance, the credit record, supervisory requirements, confidentiality, member obligations, objections/complaints, and the disputes committee. It prohibits any person from conducting credit information activity without a SAMA licence and sets capital, corporate form, fee, approval and licence-duration conditions. Binds licensed credit bureaus and their Members (public and private entities exchanging credit information), and protects Consumers (natural or legal persons with credit dealings).

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection, governance/risk
