# Expected Credit Loss (ECL)

Concept node on Expected Credit Loss under the SAMA provisioning Rules: the IFRS 9 estimate of losses from credit events, measured as 12-month or lifetime ECL depending on staging. The Rules place responsibility on the board of directors of a finance company to keep ECL provisions adequate and to oversee credit risk practices, policies, model validation and data integrity. It prescribes minimum content for the ECL framework, including defined terms (default, SICR), roles and responsibilities, portfolio grouping, methodology choice and documentation of inputs and assumptions.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
