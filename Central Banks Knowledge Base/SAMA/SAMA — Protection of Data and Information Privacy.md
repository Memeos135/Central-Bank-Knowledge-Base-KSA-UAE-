# Protection of Data and Information Privacy

Principle 6 of the consumer protection framework, requiring financial institutions to put in place mechanisms protecting the privacy of consumers' financial, credit, insurance and personal information consistent with applicable regulations and the Personal Data Protection Law, including all rights conferred by that law. It also requires high-level control systems that specify the purposes for which data is collected. Relevant where consumer protection intersects with data protection and credit information regimes.
