# Supervisory LGD and EAD Estimates

Addresses when banks must fall back on supervisory LGD and EAD values instead of own estimates: for corporate, sovereign and bank exposures, banks that do not meet the minimum requirements for own-estimates of LGD or EAD must use the supervisory parameters, with the standards for their use set out in the foundation-approach requirements. The surrounding material sets the own-estimate benchmark, including EAD defined as expected gross exposure at obligor default, floored at the current drawn amount for on-balance sheet items, and long-run default-weighted average EAD with conservatism for off-balance sheet items. Binds SAMA-supervised banks applying the IRB approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
