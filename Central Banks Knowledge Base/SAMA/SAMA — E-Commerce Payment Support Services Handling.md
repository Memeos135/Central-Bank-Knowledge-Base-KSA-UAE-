# E-Commerce Payment Support Services Handling

A SAMA circular (superseding an earlier circular on the same subject) clarifying that technical connectivity and support services for e-commerce payment transactions are ancillary/technical services falling outside the definition of related payment services under the Payment Services Implementing Regulation, and therefore do not currently require a SAMA licence. Such providers must obtain the necessary technical approvals from Saudi Payments and must not contract directly with merchants, onboard them, perform KYC/merchant verification, AML/CTF or anti-fraud checks, or complete settlement and deposit of funds into merchant accounts. It binds banks and licensed payment service providers, which may only deal with licensed PSPs for payment services, must notify SAMA of unlicensed activity, and must apply outsourcing rules and mada operational, technical and fee requirements when contracting technical support firms.
