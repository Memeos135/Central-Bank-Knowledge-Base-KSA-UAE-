# Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules

SAMA's Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules, setting the overarching conduct framework that financial institutions must observe in all dealings with consumers. It combines high-level principles (fair treatment, disclosure and transparency, education, conduct, data privacy, complaints handling, competition, outsourcing) with detailed general conduct rules on contracts, initial disclosure forms, fee and commission changes, notification timelines, application processing and complaint channels. Binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions across their consumer-facing products and services.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info
