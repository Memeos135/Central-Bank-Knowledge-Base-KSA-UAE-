# Finance Consumer Fees

## Why this community

Limits and disclosure duties on fees and charges imposed on finance consumers — service fees, administrative fees on consumer finance, and how these interact with consumer protection principles and finance company licensing.

## How members connect

- The service fee guide sits downstream of the consumer protection principles (fairness, transparency, disclosure) and operationalises permissible fee types and caps.
- Administrative fee controls on consumer finance are conceptually anchored in the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, which supplies the licensing perimeter and product definitions.
- Micro finance / micro consumer finance rules and licensing requirements inherit from the same Implementing Regulation, so fee ceilings must be read against the specific licence category and product.
- Beware the 'Consumer' definition: fee protections attach to consumers, not all customers, which narrows scope relative to AML-style 'customer' duties.
