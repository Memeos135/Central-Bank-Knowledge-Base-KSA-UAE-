# Clean-up Call

Sets the conditions under which a clean-up call is acceptable, notably that it must be at the originator's discretion, must not be structured to avoid loss allocation or to provide credit enhancement, and must be exercisable only when 10% or less of the original portfolio or reference portfolio remains. Failing those conditions triggers capital consequences: traditional securitizations are treated as unsecuritized with no gain-on-sale recognition, and synthetic protection purchasers must capitalise the full exposures; an exercised call serving as credit enhancement is treated as implicit support and deducted from capital. It binds originating and protection-purchasing banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
