# Circular: Accepting Electronically Notarized LLC Formation Contracts

A SAMA circular instructing finance companies to accept electronically notarised formation contracts of limited liability companies that carry only the Ministry of Commerce and Investment seal and no notary public seal, following the Ministry's move to electronic notarisation for LLCs owned by Saudi partners (non-minors), a Saudi individual or a registered resident. Companies must still verify the contract data through the existing verification service used by banks and financial institutions or the Ministry's business record portal. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and is relevant to corporate customer documentation and identification/KYB practice.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF
