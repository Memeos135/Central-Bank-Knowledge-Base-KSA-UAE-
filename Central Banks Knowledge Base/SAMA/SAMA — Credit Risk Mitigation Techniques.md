# Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques

Governs which credit risk mitigation techniques a bank may recognise when measuring exposure values for large exposure purposes: only unfunded credit protection and eligible financial collateral recognised under the standardised approach, with equities, convertible bonds and UCITS excluded, and IRB-only collateral not eligible. It also requires consistency (a CRM technique used for risk-based capital must be recognised for LEX) and sets treatment of maturity mismatches and on-balance sheet netting. Binds banks calculating exposure values under the LEX framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
