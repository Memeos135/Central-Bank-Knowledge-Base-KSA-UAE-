# Payment Systems

## Why this community

The payment systems perimeter — operation of and participation in payment and settlement infrastructure, and the licensing/oversight consequences that follow from falling within it.

## How members connect

- Single-node anchor concept that scopes the payments regime and determines which entities are supervised as operators or participants.
- Feeds definitional linkage into PSP licensing, settlement finality, and operational resilience obligations.
- Cross-regime relevance: payment flows are the transactional substrate for AML/CTF monitoring, wire transfer information rules and TFS screening.
