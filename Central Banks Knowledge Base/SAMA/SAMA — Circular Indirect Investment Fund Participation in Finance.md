# Circular: Indirect Investment Fund Participation in Finance

SAMA circular (Finance Companies Control General Department) setting out the activities licensed finance companies may conduct with persons authorised by the Capital Market Authority, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and coordinated with the CMA. It permits sale of finance portfolios to investment funds, loan participation agreements with funds for co-financing, and fund investment arrangements provided the credit-granting decision remains with the finance company. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and does not override the Finance Companies Control Law or its Implementing Regulation.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
