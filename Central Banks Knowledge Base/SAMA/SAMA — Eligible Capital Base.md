# Eligible Capital Base

Sets out the LEX Rules definitions — including Eligible Capital Base as the effective amount of Tier 1 capital under Basel III — which is the denominator for identifying a Large Exposure (10% or more) and for all exposure caps. It also carries the related caps applied against that base: 5% for individuals/sole proprietorships/partnerships, 25% bank-to-bank (15% where a D-SIB or G-SIB is involved), 25% for commercial undertakings majority owned by the Saudi Government, and an aggregate large exposure ceiling of 6 times eligible capital. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Large Exposure Rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
