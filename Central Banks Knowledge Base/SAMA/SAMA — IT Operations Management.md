# IT Operations Management

A cluster of provisions from SAMA's IT Governance Framework covering IT operations and, in the adjacent sections, the IT risk management lifecycle (identification, analysis and classification, treatment, reporting, monitoring and profiling). It sets principle-plus-control-requirement expectations that IT risk processes be defined, approved, implemented, aligned to enterprise risk management, and reported to business owners, senior management, the risk committee and the IT Steering Committee, covering information assets, people and third-party relationships. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
