# Template CCR1 - Analysis of CCR Exposures by Approach

Template CCR1 requires banks to disclose counterparty credit risk exposures broken down by the method used to determine exposure at default (e.g. standardised and internal models approaches), with associated EAD and RWA. It applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework holding derivative, SFT or other CCR-bearing positions in the banking or trading book. It is the summary-level CCR template that the more granular CCR3–CCR8 templates decompose.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
