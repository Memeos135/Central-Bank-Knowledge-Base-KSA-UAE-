# Stressed Expected Shortfall (ESR,S)

Covers the stressed expected shortfall measure and the data principles governing it — in particular that inputs must reflect prices observed or quoted during the identified stress period and be sourced directly from that historical window where possible, with documented handling where current instruments differ from stress-period instruments. It sits alongside data update-frequency and calibration expectations and feeds the internal models approach capital calculation. Binds banks approved to use the IMA for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
