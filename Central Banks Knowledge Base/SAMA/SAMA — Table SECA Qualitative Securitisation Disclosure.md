# Table SECA: Qualitative Securitisation Disclosure

Table SECA is the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure requirement on securitisation, requiring banks to describe their objectives, risk management policies and the main features of securitisation and re-securitisation activity, including the extent of credit risk transfer, risks assumed versus retained, sponsored special purpose entities and affiliated entities that invest in or manage securitisations. Where positions sit in both the regulatory banking book and trading book, the description must be given separately for each book. It is mandatory for all banks with securitisation exposures, disclosed annually in a flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
