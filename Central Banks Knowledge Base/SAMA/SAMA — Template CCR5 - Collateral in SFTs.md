# Template CCR5 - Collateral in SFTs

A mandatory Pillar 3 template requiring banks to disclose the composition of collateral supporting counterparty credit risk, broken down by collateral type and by whether it is posted or received, segregated or unsegregated, and used in derivatives or securities financing transactions (SFTs), including CCP-cleared trades. Disclosure is semiannual, with fixed columns, flexible rows and a narrative explaining material period-on-period changes. Applies to all banks within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
