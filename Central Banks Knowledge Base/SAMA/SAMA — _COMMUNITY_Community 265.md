# Credit Risk General Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure on the bank's overall credit risk profile: business model drivers of credit risk, risk appetite and limits, governance and reporting structures, and the definitions used for past-due and impaired exposures.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a qualitative narrative table anchoring the quantitative credit risk templates that follow it in the Pillar 3 set.
- Relies on definitions drawn from the capital adequacy framework and the credit classification/provisioning rules (default, past due, impairment).
- Hierarchy: Basel-aligned capital framework adopted by SAMA -> Pillar 3 disclosure requirements -> general qualitative credit risk table.
- Interacts with governance and risk-management expectations, since disclosed content must reflect the board-approved credit risk appetite and control framework.
