# Interest Rate Risk

A concept node on interest rate risk within SAMA's market risk capital framework, covering general interest rate risk (GIRR) as a defined risk class and the supporting definitions (risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, risk class, sensitivity, delta), plus the conditions for internal risk transfers of banking book interest rate risk into the trading book through a SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk. It binds banks calculating regulatory capital for market risk and determines when a banking book hedge and its trading book leg receive capital recognition.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
