# Detect Domain

This is the Detect domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, addressing risk-based identification of actual or attempted fraud where preventive controls fail. It requires defined, approved and maintained fraud detection standards covering internal and external fraud, specifying data sources, detective controls (including Wholesale Payment Endpoint Security monitoring), systems, roles and the rationale for proportionality, with enhanced measures where inherent fraud risk is higher. It binds Member Organisations and requires periodic review and effectiveness evaluation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other
