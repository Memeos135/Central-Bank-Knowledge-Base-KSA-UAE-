# Bankruptcy Law

Node dealing with the interaction between the SAMA Bank Account Rules and the Bankruptcy Law: conditions and documents for opening liquidation / financial restructuring accounts (court order commencing liquidation or administrative liquidation, appointment of the bankruptcy officeholder or committee, prescribed account naming, identification of the entity and officeholder), and the duty to freeze an account on death, loss of legal competence, or commencement of liquidation under the Bankruptcy or Companies Law. Also covers priority of SAMA-directed compulsory deductions. Binds banks holding accounts of debtors under insolvency proceedings.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
