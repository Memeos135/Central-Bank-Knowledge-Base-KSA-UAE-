# Executive Committee

Sub-node covering the Executive Committee established under the Terms of Reference for Finance Company Committees, issued pursuant to the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 98 of its Implementing Regulation. The Terms of Reference set out its composition, nomination and exemption of members, meetings and quorum, agenda, minutes and reporting lines, and its responsibilities within the finance-sector committee structure. Relevant to finance companies whose representatives sit on or report to this committee.
