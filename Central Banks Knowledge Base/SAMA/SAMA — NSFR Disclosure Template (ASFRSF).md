# NSFR Disclosure Template (ASF/RSF)

This is the line-by-line NSFR disclosure template with its instructions, mapping each funding source to an ASF factor (regulatory capital, stable and less stable retail and small business deposits, operational deposits, other wholesale funding, derivative liabilities, interdependent liabilities) and each asset to an RSF factor (HQLA, performing loans, residential mortgages, non-HQLA securities, traded commodities, initial margin and default fund contributions, derivative assets). It cross-references the SAMA NSFR and LCR rulebook sections for each factor. It binds banks subject to the NSFR standard and Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
