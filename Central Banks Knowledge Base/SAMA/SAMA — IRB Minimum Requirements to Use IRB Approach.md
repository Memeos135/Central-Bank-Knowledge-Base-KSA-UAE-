# IRB: Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach

Node for the minimum requirements a bank must satisfy to use the internal ratings-based approach under SAMA's credit risk capital rules, including rating system design, corporate governance and oversight, use test, risk quantification, validation and data maintenance, plus the rules on adopting IRB by asset class. It also covers phased rollout: SAMA may allow staged adoption across asset classes and business units against an agreed, realistic implementation plan, but once IRB is applied to an asset class in a business unit all exposures in that class and unit must be treated on IRB, and no capital relief is permitted for intra-group transactions designed to reduce the group charge during rollout. Binds banks seeking or holding SAMA approval for foundation or advanced IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
