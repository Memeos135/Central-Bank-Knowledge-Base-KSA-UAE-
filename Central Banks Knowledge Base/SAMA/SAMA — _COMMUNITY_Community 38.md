# Consumer Finance APR Rules

## Why this community

Consumer finance conduct regime: cost-of-credit transparency (APR), contract documentation, borrower treatment and dispute handling, plus debt buyout/transfer between finance companies and adjacent mortgage and licensing requirements.

## How members connect

- The Regulations for Consumer Financing are the anchor instrument; the APR rules, annex methodology and SAMA calculator prescribe the single mandatory disclosure metric, and fee/administrative-cost circulars clarify what must be included in it.
- Circulars (signing all contract pages, equality in financing products and insurance coverage, fee clarification) operate as supervisory refinements layered on the regulation — they narrow discretion rather than create a separate regime.
- Debt buyout instructions and clearance-letter/transfer timeframes form a procedural obligation chain with deadlines running between the transferring and acquiring finance companies, with consumer-protection effect if breached.
- Borrower-dealing and dispute-resolution articles, the finance contract summary form, mortgage documentation and valuation-client obligations attach to the same Consumer/borrower defined term, while licensing guidelines set who may undertake the activity at all.
