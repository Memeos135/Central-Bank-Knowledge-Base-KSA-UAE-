# Identity and Access Management

A control domain of SAMA's Cyber Security Framework (section 3.3.5) requiring Member Organizations to restrict access to information assets on need-to-have and need-to-know bases through a defined, approved and implemented identity and access management policy. It addresses joiner/mover/leaver handling for internal staff (driven by HR) and for third parties (driven by an accountable party), formal approval of access requests, and monitoring and periodic evaluation of control effectiveness. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations and interacts with the framework's cyber security risk management expectations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
