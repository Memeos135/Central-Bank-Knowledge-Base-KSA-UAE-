# Customer Due Diligence Measures

Section 9.6 of the remittance centers guide sets customer due diligence measures: understanding and monitoring customers' transactions, identifying and verifying beneficial owners (real beneficiaries) through reliable independent sources, applying a risk-based approach, keeping identification data current on an ongoing basis, tracking identity changes, and making identification records available to AML/CFT compliance officers. CDD applies to all permanent and temporary customers including beneficial owners, and must be continuous and proportionate to the risk of the business relationship and transactions. Binds banks' remittance centers and licensed remittance companies.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
