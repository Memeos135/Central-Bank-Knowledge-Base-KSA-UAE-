# Real Estate Exposure Class

Sets out the real estate exposure class in the standardised approach: what counts as real estate (immovable property), and the split into regulatory real estate, other real estate, and land acquisition, development and construction (ADC) exposures. It further splits regulatory real estate into residential/commercial and materially-dependent/non-dependent on property cash flows, and gives risk weights for commercial materially-dependent exposures (70/90/110% by LTV band) and for other real estate (counterparty risk weight, or 150% where materially cash-flow dependent). Binds banks computing credit risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
