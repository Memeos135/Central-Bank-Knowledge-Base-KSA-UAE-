# KYC Deregistered Companies

## Why this community

Ongoing due diligence where a corporate customer's commercial registration has lapsed or the entity has been struck off — how deregistration affects the continuation, restriction or termination of the business relationship.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster operating inside the AML/CTF regime, applying ongoing CDD and record-updating duties to a specific trigger event (loss of legal status).
- Depends on the defined terms for Customer and Beneficial Owner and on the requirement to keep identification data current throughout the relationship.
- Cross-regime consequence: deregistration bears on contractual capacity and on account/service restriction decisions, so it interacts with consumer protection and finance-contract obligations.
