# Sarie Instant Payments

## Why this community

Saudi instant payment rail infrastructure (Sarie) as a designated payment system, relevant to settlement finality, participant obligations and payment-service scope.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the system itself is the anchor entity rather than a rule family, so linkage runs outward to PSP licensing, operational/fraud controls and consumer protection for instant transfers.
- Its regulatory weight derives from the payments regime (system operator/participant duties) rather than from internal cross-references.
