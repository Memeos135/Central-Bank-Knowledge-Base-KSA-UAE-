# Debt Management Office (DMO)

Identifies the Debt Management Office as the counterparty that appoints Primary Dealers and signs the undertaking agreement with Bank-PDs, and as a recipient of regulatory returns alongside SAMA. Under these Rules, Bank-PDs must report to SAMA any breach of the DMO undertaking agreement, subject PD transactions and DMO/SAMA returns to concurrent annual audit, and note that the SAMA no-objection requirement does not apply to banks already appointed by the DMO before the effective date. Relevant to banks engaged in primary dealership of Saudi Government securities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
