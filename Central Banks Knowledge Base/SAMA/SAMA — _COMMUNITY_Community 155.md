# Qawaem Program Mandate

## Why this community

SAMA-mandated use of the Qawaem program for financial statement submission/verification by supervised entities, i.e. a reporting and disclosure compliance obligation.

## How members connect

- Circular-level instrument issued under SAMA's supervisory authority; the obligation flows from regulator to the addressed institutions.
- The SAMA node anchors the hierarchy (enabling law → SAMA supervisory power → circular) and identifies the recipient of the resulting submissions.
