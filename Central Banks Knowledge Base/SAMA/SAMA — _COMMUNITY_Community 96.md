# Seasonal Operating Hours

## Why this community

SAMA's seasonal operational instructions setting working hours, service readiness and cash-logistics arrangements for Ramadan, Eid and Hajj across banks, exchange centres, ATMs and the SARIE settlement system.

## How members connect

- All members are circular-level operational directives issued for the same Hijri season and applied to overlapping supervised populations (banks, exchange houses, payment infrastructure).
- Obligation chain: general working-hours/Eid holiday instruction sets the calendar; the SARIE and ATM/cash-transport notices tailor it to settlement windows and cash availability.
- Shared compliance consequence — service continuity and customer access duties under consumer protection rules must still be met on reduced-hour days.
- Exchange-centre readiness for Hajj extends the same continuity logic to pilgrim-serving FX and remittance demand.
