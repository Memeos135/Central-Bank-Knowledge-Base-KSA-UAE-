# Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company

Defines the Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company as a joint-stock company licensed by SAMA to raise funds from Participants via a platform for lending to an Institutional Beneficiary, and sets out core operating duties attaching to that licensed entity. Obligations covered include segregation of Participant funds in a dedicated Finance Amount Management Account, electronic-only repayment (cash repayment prohibited), consent controls for auto-participation, and platform disclosure of business nature, risks and party relationships. It binds licensed DBC companies under the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding, read with the Finance Companies Control Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
