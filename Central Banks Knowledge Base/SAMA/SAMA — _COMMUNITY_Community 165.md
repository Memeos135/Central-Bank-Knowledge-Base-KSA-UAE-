# Payment Fraud and Fees

## Why this community

Card-payment integrity and cost allocation in the domestic scheme environment: preventing misuse of preauthorization holds and controlling what fees may be charged in connection with mada claims/chargeback handling.

## How members connect

- Both sit in the payments/card-scheme regime and bind the same actors — acquirers, issuers and merchants operating under mada rules.
- Shared obligation chain: proper authorization/preauthorization handling is the control that determines when a claim (dispute/chargeback) arises, and the fee rules then govern who bears the cost of that claim.
- Consumer-protection overlay: both constrain charges and conduct toward the cardholder, so fraud-loss allocation cannot be passed on as an unapproved fee.
- Fraud-related preauthorization misuse also engages AML/fraud reporting duties, a cross-regime link to suspicious-activity obligations.
