# Due Diligence

Node requiring Member Organisations to set, approve and apply fraud-focused Due Diligence standards covering employees (including contractors), customers and third parties, so relationships outside fraud risk appetite are not established and fraud risk is managed for the life of the relationship. It includes Employee Due Diligence (identity confirmation, criminal, conflict-of-interest, qualification and prior-employment checks, at hiring, on role change and periodically on a risk basis) and Customer Due Diligence aimed at capturing and validating customer identity against external fraud. Standards must be aligned to the Fraud Risk Assessment, periodically reviewed and effectiveness-tested; note this is fraud-risk diligence and sits alongside, not in place of, AML/CTF CDD obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
