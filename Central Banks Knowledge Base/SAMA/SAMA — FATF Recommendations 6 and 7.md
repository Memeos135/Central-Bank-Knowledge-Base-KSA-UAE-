# FATF Recommendations 6 and 7

Concept node for FATF Recommendations 6 and 7, the international standards on targeted financial sanctions for terrorism/terrorist financing (R.6) and for proliferation of weapons of mass destruction and its financing (R.7). They require countries and their financial institutions to implement UN Security Council designations without delay through asset freezing and prohibitions on providing funds or services to listed persons. They are not directly binding in themselves; they are given domestic force through the Kingdom's UNSCR implementation mechanisms and SAMA's TFS Rules.
