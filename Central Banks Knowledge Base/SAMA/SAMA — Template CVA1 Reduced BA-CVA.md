# Template CVA1: Reduced BA-CVA

A mandatory semiannual Pillar 3 template reporting the components of RWA under the reduced basic approach for CVA risk — the systematic and idiosyncratic aggregations and the total — with a required narrative on hedge types used even where not recognised under the reduced approach. It links to the overall RWA overview template. It binds banks measuring any part of their CVA RWA under the reduced BA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
