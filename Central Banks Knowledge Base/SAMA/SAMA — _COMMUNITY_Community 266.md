# Credit Risk Mitigation Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure on credit risk mitigation: use of collateral, netting, guarantees and credit derivatives, together with valuation, management and concentration considerations arising from mitigants.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a qualitative table that explains the policies behind the quantitative credit risk mitigation templates.
- Depends on the eligibility criteria and recognition conditions for collateral, guarantees and netting set in the capital adequacy framework's credit risk mitigation rules.
- Directly complements the general credit risk qualitative table, sharing scope, exposure definitions and governance narrative.
- Hierarchy: capital adequacy framework -> Pillar 3 disclosure requirements -> credit risk mitigation qualitative table.
