# Compliance Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA-issued Compliance Principles for finance companies and real estate refinance companies, adopted under the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulation by Governor's decision and effective a set period after publication on SAMA's website. They allocate compliance responsibilities across the board, audit committee, executive management, the compliance unit and its officer, company staff and internal audit, and set minimum requirements for the unit's independence, mandate and effectiveness, together with a compliance-risk definition covering legal sanctions, financial loss and reputational harm. They bind SAMA-licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies and are stated to be without prejudice to other applicable regimes (including AML and counter-terrorism financing laws, BNPL rules, fraud-prevention rules, governance principles, conduct rules and consumer protection principles).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection
