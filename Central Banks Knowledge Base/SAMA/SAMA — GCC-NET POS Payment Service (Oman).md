# GCC-NET POS Payment Service (Oman)

A SAMA circular activating GCC-NET point-of-sale acceptance between Saudi Arabia and the Sultanate of Oman from a specified date, supplementing earlier circulars on the GCC payment network POS service and its tariff. It obliges banks to make the technical and operational arrangements to accept and process GCC-NET POS transactions, apply approved pricing with cardholders and merchants, notify and educate GCC-network cardholders that Omani purchases route via Oman's national network linked to GCC-NET, and accept GCC-network branded cards on their POS terminals.
