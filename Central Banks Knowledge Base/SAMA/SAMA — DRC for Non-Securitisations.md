# DRC for Non-Securitisations

Sets out the default risk capital requirement for non-securitisation exposures under SAMA's standardised approach to market risk: gross and net JTD determination including LGD ratios, bucketing by obligor type, credit-quality-based risk weights, the hedge benefit ratio, and the rule that bucket-level requirements are simply summed with no cross-bucket hedging or diversification benefit. Binds SAMA-licensed banks with trading book credit and equity exposures, including the treatment of sovereign, PSE, MDB and fund exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
