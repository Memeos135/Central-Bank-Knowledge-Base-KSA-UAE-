# Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) Circular

A SAMA circular requiring financial institutions under its supervision, their subsidiaries and branches of foreign financial institutions operating in KSA to hold a valid and up-to-date Legal Entity Identifier issued by a GLEIF-accredited operating unit, building on an earlier SAMA circular on LEI adoption. It also permits those institutions to enter an agreement with SIMAH, as the accredited local operating unit (LOU), to issue, update and renew LEIs on behalf of existing and prospective corporate customers. Rationale is risk management, financial stability, market transparency and KSA's G20-related international commitments.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, banking prudential
