# Stage 1: Application Submission

Describes the first stage of SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox process: the innovator submits the application form and SAMA assesses its completeness against the Sandbox framework's eligibility criteria, returning a final decision within 60 business days. Success at this stage yields an initial approval letter permitting progression to operational readiness, not authorisation to operate. It binds FinTech applicants seeking temporary permission to test in the KSA market, and sets SAMA's own processing timeframe.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
