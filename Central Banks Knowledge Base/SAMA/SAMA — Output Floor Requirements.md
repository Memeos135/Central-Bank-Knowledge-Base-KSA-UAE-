# Output Floor Requirements

SAMA's Output Floor Requirements, issued under the Central Bank Law (M/36) and the Banking Control Law, cap the benefit of internal models by floor-setting RWAs at a percentage of the standardised-approach outcome, to limit RWA variability and improve comparability of capital ratios. Effective 1 January 2023 with transitional phase-in, and paired with quarterly reporting to SAMA within 30 days of quarter-end. Applies to all domestic banks on consolidated and standalone bases; branches of foreign banks in KSA are out of scope and follow home-regulator capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
