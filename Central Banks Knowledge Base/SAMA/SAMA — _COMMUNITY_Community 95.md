# Regulatory Data Forms

## Why this community

Periodic supervisory reporting: the prescribed returns, forms and reporting cycles through which supervised entities submit financial, prudential and customer-concentration data to SAMA.

## How members connect

- Hierarchical linkage: the guidance manual explains submission of the returns system, while each form is a specific prescribed return governed by that manual.
- Shared reporting obligation chain — designated submitting entity, prescribed template, fixed periodicity (quarterly/annual), and defined submission deadlines.
- Common defined data fields and consistent classification across forms, so that quarterly figures reconcile with annual and comparative financial statement submissions.
- Non-submission or inaccurate submission is a supervisory breach in its own right, independent of the underlying prudential position being reported.
