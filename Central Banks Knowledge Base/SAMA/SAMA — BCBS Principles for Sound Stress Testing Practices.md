# BCBS Principles for Sound Stress Testing Practices

A concept node on the Basel Committee's 2009 Principles for Sound Stress Testing Practices and Supervision as incorporated into SAMA's stress testing regime: SAMA circulated the Principles to banks in 2009 and requires banks to take them into account, in addition to the Rules, when designing and implementing stress testing programmes. Where the Rules and the BCBS Principles conflict, banks must seek SAMA guidance rather than resolve the inconsistency themselves. Also relevant for the Rules' effective date and the deadline for having a compliant framework in place.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
