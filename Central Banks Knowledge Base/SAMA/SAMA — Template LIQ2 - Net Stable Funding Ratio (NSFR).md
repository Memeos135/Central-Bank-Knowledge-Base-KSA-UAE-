# Template LIQ2 - Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)

Template LIQ2 is the Pillar 3 disclosure of the Net Stable Funding Ratio, presenting available and required stable funding components. In the source instrument it appears with the quarterly KM1 key prudential metrics template and the CDC capital distribution constraints template, which together frame a bank's capital, leverage and liquidity disclosure set. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
