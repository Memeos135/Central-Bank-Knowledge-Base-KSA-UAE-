# Cyber Security Risk Management

Covers the cyber security risk management and compliance domain of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, requiring a defined, approved and implemented cyber risk management process aligned with the Member Organization's enterprise risk management. Its objective is protection of confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets, with compliance subject to periodic review and audit. Applies to Member Organizations under SAMA supervision and is graded against the six-level maturity model.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
