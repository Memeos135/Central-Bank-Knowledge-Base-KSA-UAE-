# Large Exposure (LEX) Rules for Banks

SAMA's Large Exposure (LEX) Rules governing concentration risk for banks: board-level responsibility and policies, stress testing, systems to identify, measure and monitor exposures, maximum exposure limits by counterparty type, exemptions, credit risk mitigation treatment, and quarterly regulatory reporting before and after CRM. Limits are measured against the eligible capital base disclosed in the latest published quarterly financial statements. The rules bind SAMA-licensed banks and their boards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
