# Firm-size Adjustment for MSMEs

Concept node for the firm-size adjustment applied to the corporate IRB risk-weight formula for micro, small and medium-sized enterprise borrowers. It defines MSME borrowers by consolidated group revenue thresholds in SAR, sets the adjustment formula and the revenue floor used in it, and allows SAMA to permit substitution of total assets where revenue is not a meaningful size indicator. It binds banks using the IRB approach for corporate credit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
