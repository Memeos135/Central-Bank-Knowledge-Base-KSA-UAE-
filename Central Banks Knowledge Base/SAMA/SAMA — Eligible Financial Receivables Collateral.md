# Eligible Financial Receivables Collateral

Defines eligible financial receivables as collateral — claims with original maturity of one year or less repaid from commercial or financial flows of the borrower's underlying assets — and excludes receivables tied to securitisations, sub-participations or credit derivatives. It imposes legal certainty conditions: robust security mechanism, perfected first-priority claim, registration where required, binding and enforceable documentation supported by legal review. Relevant to banks using the foundation IRB approach (and the wider CRM framework) to reduce LGD or exposure values.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
