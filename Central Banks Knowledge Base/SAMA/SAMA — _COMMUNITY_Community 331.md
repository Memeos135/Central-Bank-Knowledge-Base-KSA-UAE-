# Human Resources

## Why this community

People-side cyber and operational risk controls across the employment lifecycle: pre-employment screening, contractual security and confidentiality terms, awareness training, and controlled exit/revocation of access.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster representing the human resources control domain within the cyber security control framework.
- Obligation chain into access management and asset return on termination; also intersects fit-and-proper/governance requirements for staff in sensitive roles.
- Supports AML/fraud regimes indirectly, since screening and awareness obligations underpin insider-risk and confidentiality duties.
