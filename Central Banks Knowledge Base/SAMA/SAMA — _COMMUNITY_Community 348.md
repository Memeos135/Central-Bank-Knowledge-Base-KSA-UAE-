# Real Estate Broker Compliance

## Why this community

Obligations attaching to licensed real estate brokerage activity, including its status as a designated non-financial business and profession for AML/CTF and licensing-conduct purposes.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the licensed real estate broker is the obligated person, so linkage runs outward to AML/CTF customer due diligence and reporting duties rather than between members.
- Relevance depends on whether the broker falls within the DNFBP scope definition and any transaction thresholds set in the applicable regime.
- No internal cross-references are present in the graph; treat as a scope/definition anchor pending linkage to the AML law and implementing regulation.
