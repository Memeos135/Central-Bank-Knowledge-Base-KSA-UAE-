# Credit Card Rules FAQ

A SAMA-issued FAQ document accompanying the Rules for Issuing and Operating Credit Cards, circulated to banks and finance companies as non-binding guidance. It explains, in question-and-answer form, issuance conditions (documented customer request, notification timelines, re-issued and supplementary cards, minimum age), and fee/cost treatment (annual fees only after activation, late payment fees, international purchase fees, no term-cost increase on default, grace period, treatment of cash advances, transfers and e-wallet top-ups). It is interpretive only and yields to the Rules and other applicable regulations in case of conflict.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, BNPL/finance
