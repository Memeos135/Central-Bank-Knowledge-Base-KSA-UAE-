# Project Finance (PF)

Defines project finance (PF) as funding where the lender looks primarily to a single project's revenues as both source of repayment and security, typically via an SPV, and sets the unrated standardized-approach risk weights: 130% pre-operational, 100% operational, and 80% for high-quality operational-phase exposures meeting the stated conditions. It also defines "operational phase" (positive net cash flow sufficient to cover remaining contractual obligations plus declining long-term debt) and the high-quality criteria. Binds banks risk-weighting PF exposures under SAMA's credit risk capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
