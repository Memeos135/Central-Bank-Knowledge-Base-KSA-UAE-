# SAMA Property and Fintech Circulars

## Why this community

Discrete SAMA circulars addressing narrow supervisory expectations — treatment of real property contributed in kind and arrangements around fintech membership agreements.

## How members connect

- Same instrument type and issuing authority (SAMA circulars), linked conceptually rather than by textual cross-reference.
- Both act as supervisory overlays that qualify how licensed entities document and register specific asset or contractual arrangements.
- Each must be read against the underlying licensing/prudential framework it modifies; neither is self-standing authority.
