# Rules on Management of Problem Loans

SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans, issued under the SAMA Charter and Banking Control Law, set enforceable requirements for how banks identify, monitor, restructure and resolve loans showing stress or already non-performing. It sets out objectives (rehabilitation of viable borrowers, fair customer treatment especially for MSMEs, adequate controls over NPL and restructuring processes), scope, and the defined terms used throughout (problem loans, non-performing loans, watch-list, early warning signals, cooperating/viable borrower, viability assessment, covenant, KPIs). It binds all banks licensed under the Banking Control Law and is read together with SAMA's Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning and the accompanying Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
