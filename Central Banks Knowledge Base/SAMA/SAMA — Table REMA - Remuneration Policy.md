# Table REMA - Remuneration Policy

Table REMA is the qualitative Pillar 3 remuneration policy disclosure in SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, describing governance bodies overseeing remuneration, design of remuneration processes, risk adjustment, and identification of senior managers and material risk-takers. It is mandatory for all banks, annual, flexible format, and anchors the employee categories used in Templates REM1–REM3. Disclosure must be published annually and, if not concurrent with the annual report, as soon as possible thereafter.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
