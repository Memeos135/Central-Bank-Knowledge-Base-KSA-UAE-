# Three Lines of Defense Model

Concept node on the three-lines model as reflected in SAMA guidance: first-line business units that own and manage the risks of their activities within tolerances, second-line control/compliance and risk units, and internal audit as the independent third line. The thirteenth principle of the Internal Audit Principles governs the audit department's relationship with first- and second-line units, preserving audit independence from operational and control functions. Relevant to finance companies, real estate refinance companies and, via the second cited instrument, broader supervised entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
