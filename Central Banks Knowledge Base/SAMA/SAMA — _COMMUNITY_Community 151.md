# Counterparty Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of CCR exposures under the standardised approach and of exposures to central counterparties, covering risk-weight and counterparty-type breakdowns and the qualifying/non-qualifying CCP distinction.

## How members connect

- The two templates are cross-referenced: CCP-related exposures disclosed in CCR8 are carved out of, or reconciled against, the standardised-approach exposures reported in CCR3.
- Both rely on the same defined terms for exposure at default, netting set and risk weight drawn from the capital adequacy framework.
- Together they form an obligation chain from measurement rules in the framework down to a fixed-format periodic disclosure to SAMA and the market.
