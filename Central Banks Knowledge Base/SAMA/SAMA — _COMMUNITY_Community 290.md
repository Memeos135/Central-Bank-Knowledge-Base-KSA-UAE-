# IMA Capital Requirements

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of market risk capital requirements calculated under the Internal Models Approach. The compliance question is scope and eligibility: only banks with SAMA permission to use internal models populate this template.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the template implements the disclosure leg of the market risk capital framework; the substantive calculation and model-approval conditions live in the parent market risk rules.
- Its applicability is conditional on prior supervisory approval of the internal model — an approval-gated obligation rather than a general one.
- Sits alongside the standardised-approach market risk templates in the same disclosure suite, so the correct template depends on the approach the bank is authorised to use.
