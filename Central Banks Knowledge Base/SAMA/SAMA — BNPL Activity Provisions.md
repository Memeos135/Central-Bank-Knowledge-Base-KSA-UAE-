# BNPL Activity Provisions

This node covers the definitional, scope and licensing architecture of SAMA's Rules Governing BNPL Companies, including the defined terms (BNPL Activity, BNPL Company, Consumer, Stores), the prohibition on carrying on BNPL activity without a SAMA licence, and the licensing lifecycle (initial approval and commercial register timelines, verification visits, five-year licence term and renewal, revocation on request, and issuance/renewal fees). It binds joint stock companies licensed or seeking licence by SAMA to conduct BNPL activity, and is expressly subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law and the senior-appointments requirements.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
