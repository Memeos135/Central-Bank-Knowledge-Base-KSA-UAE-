# Contingency Funding Plan (CFP)

Node on the Contingency Funding Plan required of finance companies to handle liquidity crises and cash flow shortfalls under stress. It specifies the CFP's minimum content: a quantitative and qualitative definition of a liquidity crisis event, early warning indicators with assigned monitoring responsibility and frequency, options for different time horizons, designated roles and activation authority, escalation procedures, plans to modify balance sheet composition and maturities, a prioritised list of alternative funding sources, and periodic testing and updating. Binds finance companies; the CFP is subject to Board approval as part of the liquidity risk policy set.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
