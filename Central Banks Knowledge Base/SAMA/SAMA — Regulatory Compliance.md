# Regulatory Compliance

Sub-domain 3.2.2 of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, requiring each Member Organization to maintain a defined process to identify, communicate and comply with the cyber security implications of applicable regulation. The process must run periodically and on entry into force of new requirements, involve key business areas, and feed changes back into cyber security policy, standards and procedures. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations (banks, insurers and financing companies) and sits alongside industry-standards compliance, cyber security review and audit provisions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
