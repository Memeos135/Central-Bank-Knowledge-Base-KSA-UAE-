# Model Real Estate Contracts

## Why this community

Standardised documentation for residential real estate finance: SAMA-mandated model contracts for Murabaha and Ijarah financing extended to consumers, fixing minimum terms, disclosures and consumer-protection clauses that lenders cannot vary to the borrower's detriment.

## How members connect

- A SAMA circular sits at the top of the hierarchy and gives the two model contracts their binding force, directing banks and finance companies to adopt them for real estate finance.
- The circular expressly references both model forms, so each contract's clauses are read as the mandated baseline rather than negotiable drafting.
- The Murabaha and Ijarah models are parallel instruments covering the same consumer real estate finance regime through different Shariah structures; they share common disclosure, pricing, early repayment and default-handling content, making differences between them the analytically important point.
- Both operate within the Real Estate Finance Law and consumer finance protection framework, so obligations in the model text should be traced back to the underlying statutory and regulatory duties rather than treated as pure contract terms.
