# Secure Information Disposal

## Why this community

End-of-life handling of information assets, covering the duty to render data irrecoverable at disposal, decommissioning or media reuse.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster representing the terminal stage of the information asset lifecycle obligation chain (classify, protect, retain, dispose).
- Cross-references record retention duties: disposal may not occur before statutory/regulatory retention periods expire, notably for AML/CTF records.
- Consequential for outsourcing and vendor exit, where custody of assets passes outside the member organisation.
