# Benefiting from General Authority for Awqaf Services for Beneficial Owner Verification

A SAMA circular instructing supervised financial institutions to use the General Authority for Awqaf's channels — technical integration with the Authority's systems, requests to its Data Management Office by email, the waqf certificate verification service, and the public register of endowments — when identifying and verifying beneficial owner data for awqaf (endowments). It is grounded in the AML Law and its Implementing Regulation's customer due diligence obligations, and additionally requires institutions to notify the Authority, via its designated form, of any discrepancies between Authority data and CDD findings, without prejudice to suspicious transaction reporting duties or the Personal Data Protection Law.
