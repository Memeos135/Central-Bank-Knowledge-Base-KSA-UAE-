# AML/CTF Training

Covers the AML/CTF training obligation and the surrounding compliance-function governance duties in SAMA's AML/CTF rules: ongoing training budgets and programs for senior management and staff, specialized annual training for AML/CTF compliance staff, and board/senior management responsibilities for policies, periodic review and resourcing. It also sets out the requirement for an independent, adequately resourced AML/CTF unit headed by a senior-level officer (Saudi nationals only) reporting to the board, with access to customer due diligence and transaction records. Binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
