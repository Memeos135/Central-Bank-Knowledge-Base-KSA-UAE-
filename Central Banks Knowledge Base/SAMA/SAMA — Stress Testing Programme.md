# Stress Testing Programme

This node covers the stress testing and backtesting expectations attached to banks using the internal models approach (IMA) for market risk capital under SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk. It requires a rigorous stress testing programme at both trading desk and bank-wide level, combining SAMA-prescribed and bank-specific scenarios, with quantitative and qualitative elements and reporting to senior management and the board; the linked backtesting material sets out the green/amber/red zone consequences, including capital add-ons, an increased multiplication factor, or withdrawal of model permission. It binds SAMA-licensed banks permitted to use internal models for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
