# Central Counterparty (CCP)

Defines central counterparty (CCP), clearing member and client for counterparty credit risk purposes, including the treatment of a linked second CCP as a clearing member of the first. It also sets the Pillar 2 expectation that a bank remains responsible for holding adequate capital against CCP exposures regardless of QCCP status, and must stress test and report CCP exposures to senior management and the board. Applies to banks dealing with CCPs as clearing members or clients.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
