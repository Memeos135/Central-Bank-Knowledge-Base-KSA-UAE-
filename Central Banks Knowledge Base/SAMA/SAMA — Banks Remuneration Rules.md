# Banks Remuneration Rules

SAMA's Banks Remuneration Rules set minimum requirements and supervisory guidance for bank remuneration policies, procedures and practices, aimed at managing risks (including misconduct risk) rather than fixing absolute pay levels. They supersede the 2010 Rules on Compensation Practices and require compliance with any regulatory caps set by SAMA or other authorities. They bind all locally incorporated licensed banks, with group-level application to majority-owned financial-sector subsidiaries in and outside KSA (subject to host-country law).
