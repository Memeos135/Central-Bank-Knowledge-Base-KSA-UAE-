# Table LIQA: Liquidity Risk Management

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure node for Table LIQA, covering a bank's liquidity risk management framework: governance, funding strategy, liquidity risk tolerance, stress testing and contingency funding planning. It is the liquidity counterpart to the general risk management table (OVA) within SAMA's disclosure framework, and is narrative/flexible in format. Binding on banks subject to SAMA Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
