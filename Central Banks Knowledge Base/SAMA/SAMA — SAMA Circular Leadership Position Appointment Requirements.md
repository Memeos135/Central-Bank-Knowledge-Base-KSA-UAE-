# SAMA Circular: Leadership Position Appointment Requirements

Arabic SAMA circular amending Annex (1) of the second edition of the Requirements for Appointment to Leading Positions in SAMA-supervised financial institutions, adding a further senior role (customer-facing/customer care management) to the list of posts needing SAMA's prior written no-objection. The annex enumerates board and senior-management positions (chair, board members, committee heads, CEO, CFO, CRO, internal audit, compliance, AML/CTF, anti-fraud, anti-financial-crime, credit, operations, legal, governance/board secretary, information security/cyber, IT, HR, retail and corporate banking, treasury ALM/SAIBOR setter, financial enforcement unit head, and any executive reporting directly to the CEO). It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and applies to appointments made from the circular's date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
