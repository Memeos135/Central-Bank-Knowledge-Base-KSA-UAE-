# SAMA Circular on Real Estate Financier Obligations in Secondary Market

A SAMA circular (dated 03/09/1443H) reaffirming that a real estate financier remains bound by all statutory, contractual and supervisory obligations after transferring its rights in the secondary market (refinancing through licensed real estate refinance companies) or outsourcing collection to a third party. It stresses continuing duties for the full contract term, including prompt updating of customers' credit records within the period set in the Credit Information Law's implementing regulations, and correct handling of early repayment and debt rescheduling. Addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, with SAMA reserving enforcement action for non-compliance.
