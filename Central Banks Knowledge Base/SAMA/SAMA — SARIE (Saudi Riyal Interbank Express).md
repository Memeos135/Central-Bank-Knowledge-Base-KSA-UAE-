# SARIE (Saudi Riyal Interbank Express)

A concept node identifying SARIE (Saudi Arabian Riyal Interbank Express) as a permitted modern transfer system, alongside SWIFT, for outbound and inbound domestic and international remittances under the Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers. For domestic transfers executed exclusively through SARIE, remittance centers must capture and store the transferor's name and account number for rapid retrieval by competent authorities, and verify the inbound beneficiary's identity per the Rules Governing the Opening of Bank Accounts. It binds banks' remittance centers and licensed remittance companies.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
