# Escrow Account for Real Estate Development

A special-purpose account category within SAMA's Rules for Bank Accounts governing escrow accounts for off-plan real estate development projects. It sets conditions for opening and activating the account (Off-Plan Sales/Rent Committee licence, tripartite agreement among developer, bank, consulting office and certified accountant, naming convention, one account per project with defined sub-accounts), deposit restrictions (no cash; buyers and financiers only), a 5% retention or equivalent bank guarantee, and prohibitions on set-off, transfers out, card issuance and investment of project funds. The adjacent provision covers segregated collection accounts holding payment companies' client funds. It binds banks licensed in Saudi Arabia, with consequential obligations on real estate developers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection
