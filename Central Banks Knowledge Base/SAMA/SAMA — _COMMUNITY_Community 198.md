# Fire Safety Compliance

## Why this community

Physical safety and premises-compliance duties of supervised financial institutions, specifically appointing and empowering a fire safety officer in line with civil-defence requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a compliance instruction transposing a national fire-safety regulation into SAMA-supervised entities' obligations.
- Legally sits alongside operational-risk and business-continuity governance rather than prudential or conduct regimes.
- Accountability point is an appointed responsible person, creating a named-role obligation verifiable on inspection.
