# SAMA

The root node for SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (version 2.1, December 2022), the Kingdom's Basel-aligned credit risk capital rulebook. It covers scope and implementation timing, SAMA reporting, due diligence, the standardised approach (individual exposure classes, external ratings, credit risk mitigation), the full IRB framework (asset classes, risk-weight functions, risk components, supervisory slotting, purchased receivables, expected loss and provisions, minimum qualifying requirements), transition arrangements and the securitisation framework. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia for regulatory capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
