# Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA) Requirements

This node covers SAMA's Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) framework circular of August 2014 and, specifically, the Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA) capital add-on. It sets out the indicator-based scoring (size, interconnectedness, substitutability, complexity), a cut-off score with five buckets, and the requirement that designated D-SIBs hold HLA capital fully in CET1 on top of the SAMA-set target capital adequacy ratio, with scope for supervisory judgment and further measures such as recovery and resolution plans. It binds all banks licensed in Saudi Arabia, addressed to managing directors, CEOs and general managers, effective from 1 January 2016.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
