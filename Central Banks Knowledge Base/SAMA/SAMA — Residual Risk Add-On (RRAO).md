# Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO)

The Residual Risk Add-On is a supplementary standardised market risk charge for exotic underlyings and other residual risks (e.g. behavioural prepayment features, securitisation tranches with embedded options) not captured by delta, vega, curvature or DRC. It is also relevant to whether internal CVA risk transfers subject to RRAO can receive capital recognition, which requires an exactly matching external hedge. Applies to banks under SAMA's standardised market risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
