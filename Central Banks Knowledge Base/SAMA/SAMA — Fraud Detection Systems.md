# Fraud Detection Systems

Sets out the Detect domain requirement for defined, approved, implemented and maintained fraud detection standards covering both internal and external fraud, proportionate to the Fraud Risk Assessment outcome and risk appetite. Specifies minimum content: data sources, detective controls (including Wholesale Payment Endpoint Security measures such as counterparty white-lists and real-time payment blocking), enabling systems, roles and responsibilities for calibration, triage and escalation, and rationale for adequacy; with periodic review, compliance monitoring and effectiveness measurement. Binds Member Organisations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, AML/CTF
