# Counter-Fraud Maturity Model

This node sets out the Counter-Fraud Maturity Model used to measure a Member Organisation's counter-fraud maturity across six levels (0 non-existent, 1 ad-hoc, 2 repeatable but informal, through 3, 4 and 5), where higher levels require satisfaction of all preceding levels. Level 3 turns on documented policies, standards and procedures with embedded controls and KPI-monitored compliance; level 4 requires effectiveness measurement via KRIs with defined thresholds; level 5 requires continuous improvement. It provides the assessment yardstick applied to Member Organisations under the Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
