# Representations and Warranties

The representations and warranties clause of the SAMA securities sale and purchase master agreement, given by each party on execution and deemed repeated on each transaction and transfer date. It covers capacity and authorisation, acting as principal, regulatory approvals, unencumbered title to transferred securities, and — notably — a party's own responsibility for satisfying itself as to Shari'ah Compliance, with a waiver of Shari'ah-based challenge and non-reliance on the counterparty's fatwa or opinions. Breach of these representations is itself an Event of Default, so the clause drives close-out risk allocation between the counterparties.
