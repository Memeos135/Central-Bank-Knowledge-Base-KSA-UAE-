# SAMA Standard Consumer Finance Contract 1611

SAMA circular issuing the mandatory standard-form consumer finance contract for individuals, based on SAMA's Law and the Banking Control Law, to reduce divergence between financiers' contracts and limit disputes. All financing entities must adopt the template from the stated compliance date and may not conclude contracts that depart from or amend it; the document includes a contract summary (APR, term cost, instalments, administrative fees, total amount payable) and articles covering scope, definitions, formation, financed asset, financial obligations, instalments, payment methods, late payment/default, termination, early repayment, fees, guarantees, undertakings, breach, death or incapacity, notices and governing law. It binds banks (local, foreign branches, digital banks) and finance companies dealing with individual beneficiaries.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
