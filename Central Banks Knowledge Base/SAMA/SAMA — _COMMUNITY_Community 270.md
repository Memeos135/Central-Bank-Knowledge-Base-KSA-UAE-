# SA-CVA Qualitative Disclosures

## Why this community

Approach-specific Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure for banks using the standardised approach for CVA risk (SA-CVA), covering governance, eligible hedges and model/inputs controls.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: obligation conditional on supervisory permission to use SA-CVA rather than the basic approach.
- Layered on the general CVA qualitative disclosure — the general table is the baseline, this adds approach-specific content.
- Cross-refers to CVA hedging eligibility and capital calculation rules in the substantive CVA framework; disclosure scope mirrors the permission's scope.
