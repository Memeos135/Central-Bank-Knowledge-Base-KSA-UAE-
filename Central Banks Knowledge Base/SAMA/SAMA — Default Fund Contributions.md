# Default Fund Contributions

Governs the capital treatment of clearing member banks' prefunded default fund contributions to a QCCP, using a risk-sensitive two-step formula based on the CCP's hypothetical capital requirement (KCCP) and the member's share of the default waterfall, with a floor. It also imposes data and calculation duties: KCCP must be computed at least quarterly (more often if SAMA requires) and sufficient information shared so each clearing member can compute its requirement and SAMA can verify it.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
