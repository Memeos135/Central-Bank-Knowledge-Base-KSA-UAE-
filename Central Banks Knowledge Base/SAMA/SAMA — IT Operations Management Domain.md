# IT Operations Management Domain

Domain 3.3 of the IT Governance Framework addresses day-to-day IT operations for Member Organizations, including service level agreement management, availability and capacity, data centre management, backup, interdependency management, and controls over virtualized environments and system-level objects. It states principles with mandated control requirements for operational resilience and monitoring of IT services. It applies to all SAMA-regulated Member Organizations.
