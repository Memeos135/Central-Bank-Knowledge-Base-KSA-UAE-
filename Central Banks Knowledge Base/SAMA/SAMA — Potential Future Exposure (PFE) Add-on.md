# Potential Future Exposure (PFE) Add-on

Concept node for the potential future exposure (PFE) add-on component of counterparty credit risk exposure on derivatives, as used in SAMA's adopted Basel-based capital and leverage ratio framework. It governs how banks quantify and report the forward-looking element of derivative exposures (alongside replacement cost, with the prescribed alpha factor) in the leverage ratio exposure measure and related Pillar 3 templates. Binds banks subject to SAMA's minimum capital and disclosure rules on a regulatory consolidation basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
