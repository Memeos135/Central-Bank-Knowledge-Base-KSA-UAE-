# Basel Risk-Based Capital Framework

Anchors the LEX Rules to the Basel risk-based capital framework: banking book, trading book, exposure scope (on- and off-balance sheet plus counterparty credit risk instruments), eligible capital and CRM recognition all take their meaning from that framework and SAMA's Basel guidance documents. It is a definitional/cross-reference node rather than a standalone obligation, but it determines how banks measure exposures and capital for large exposure compliance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
