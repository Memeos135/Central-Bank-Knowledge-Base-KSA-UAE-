# Mudarabah Contract

Defined-term node within the PSIA Rules describing Mudarabah as a partnership contract where the capital provider (Rabb-ul-Mal / IAH) supplies capital managed by the Mudarib (the bank), with profits shared on a pre-agreed ratio and losses borne by the capital provider unless caused by the bank's negligence, misconduct, fraud or breach. It underpins how banks may raise PSIA funding and how loss allocation and reserves (PER) operate. Relevant to banks and Islamic windows offering Shari'ah-compliant investment accounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
