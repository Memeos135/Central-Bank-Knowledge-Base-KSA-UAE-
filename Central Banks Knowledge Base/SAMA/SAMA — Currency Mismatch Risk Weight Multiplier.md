# Currency Mismatch Risk Weight Multiplier

A node covering the risk weight multiplier applied to certain unhedged exposures where the borrower's income currency differs from the currency of the loan, within the standardized approach chapter of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. Binds banks calculating standardized-approach RWAs. The extracted context here instead covers short-term versus long-term external ratings and the treatment of credit risk mitigants for dilution risk, so verify the operative paragraph before citing.
