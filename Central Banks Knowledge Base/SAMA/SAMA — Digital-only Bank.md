# Digital-only Bank

Defines the "Digital-only Bank" as a bank conducting banking business mainly through digital channels such as web and mobile applications, and scopes the additional licensing criteria applicable to it. Core conditions include local incorporation as a joint-stock company, qualified promoters, a physical principal place of business in KSA without an expectation of branches (SAMA may require customer service centres), and the same corporate governance principles as conventional banks. It binds digital-only bank licence applicants, alongside the general banking licensing criteria.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF
