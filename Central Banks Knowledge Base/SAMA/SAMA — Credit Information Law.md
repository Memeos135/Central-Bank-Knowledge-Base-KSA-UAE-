# Credit Information Law

The Credit Information Law establishes the principles and controls for collecting, exchanging, protecting and correcting consumer credit information, and defines key terms including Credit Information, Consumer, Member, Companies, Credit Record and Public Records. It requires licensing of credit information companies by SAMA, consumer consent for a member to obtain a credit record, confidentiality duties on members, companies and their staff, and lists the acts deemed violations (unlicensed activity, unlawful disclosure or use, delayed updating, false data). It binds credit information companies, members (government and private entities party to exchange contracts) and other entities maintaining credit information.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection
