# Banking Control Law

The Banking Control Law (Royal Decree M/5) — the primary statute governing the licensing, governance, supervision and prudential control of banks in Saudi Arabia, together with its Implementation Rules and the SAMA circulars issued under it. Key provisions addressed in the linked material include board and senior appointments, conduct of banking business, periodic reporting to SAMA, inspections, and contraventions and penalties. It binds all banks operating in the Kingdom and underpins much of SAMA's supervisory authority over them.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
