# Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) Test Metric

Sets out the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distributional test metric, the second PLA test statistic, measured as the largest absolute difference between the empirical cumulative distribution functions of RTPL and HPL. Together with the Spearman metric it drives allocation of a trading desk to the PLA green, amber or red zone, with red-zone desks losing IMA eligibility and reverting to the standardised approach until they return to the green zone and clear backtesting exception requirements for twelve months. Binds SAMA-supervised banks using internal models for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
