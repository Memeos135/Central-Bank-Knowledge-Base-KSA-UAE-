# Regulation of Agent Banking

The Regulation of Agent Banking sets the minimum standards for banks that wish to deliver banking products and services through contracted Agents, covering agent eligibility, fit-and-proper assessment, SAMA non-objection, permissible activities, the agency contract, customer due diligence and AML/CFT, information security and confidentiality, consumer protection, and supervision of agents. It defines key terms such as Agent, Exclusive Agent, Multiple Agent, Customer and Conflict of Interest. It applies to all banks contracting with an Agent; the bank's board is accountable for compliance and the bank remains fully liable for its Agents' acts and omissions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, banking prudential

### [[SAMA — Rules on Outsourcing|Rules on Outsourcing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If a bank appoints or renews a banking Agent, assess the arrangement under the Rules on Outsourcing as well as the Regulation of Agent Banking, since agency is a form of delegating activity to a third party. The Outsourcing Rules require board (or delegated authority) approval of an outsourcing policy covering Material business activities and a framework for managing all outsourcing arrangements, which supplies the governance layer the agent banking regime presupposes. Consequently, the periodic reassessment before contract renewal should be evidenced at the governance level required by the Outsourcing Rules, and an agent arrangement that is Material attracts the fuller approval and oversight obligations.
