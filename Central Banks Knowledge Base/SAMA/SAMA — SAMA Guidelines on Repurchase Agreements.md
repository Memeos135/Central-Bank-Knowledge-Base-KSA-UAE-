# SAMA Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements

Orientation node for SAMA's Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements, issued under SAMA's Charter and the Banking Control Law, setting binding policy requirements for the Saudi repo market. They cover scope (SAR-denominated repo/reverse repo in eligible securities), definitions, mandatory use of the SAMA-approved Master Repurchase Agreement, eligible counterparties, eligible collateral and risk management expectations, and are to be read with CMA rules and the Bankruptcy Law. They bind all repo market participants defined as eligible counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
