# Internal IT Audit

Sets out the internal audit function's IT-specific duties under the SAMA IT Governance Framework — identifying auditable IT areas, performing IT risk assessment during audit planning, and conducting IT audits — alongside adjacent obligations on IT regulatory compliance and service level agreement management. It binds Member Organizations' internal audit and IT functions, and extends to third-party/outsourcing arrangements via SLA and risk-assessment requirements.
