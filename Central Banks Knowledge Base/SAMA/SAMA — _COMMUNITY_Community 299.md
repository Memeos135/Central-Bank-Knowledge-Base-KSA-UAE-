# Development Fund Loans

## Why this community

Treatment of government development fund loans in banks' dealings with customers, including how such financing is to be considered in credit assessment and customer accommodation.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the instruction is directed at lenders' credit and customer-treatment practices in relation to state-backed development financing.
- It intersects with credit-granting and consumer/SME finance obligations, particularly affordability and obligation-to-income assessment where subsidised loans form part of the customer's exposure.
