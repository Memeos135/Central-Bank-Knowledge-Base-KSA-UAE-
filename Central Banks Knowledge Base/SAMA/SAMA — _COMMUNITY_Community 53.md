# DTFC Regulations

## Why this community

The prudential and governance regime for finance companies licensed to take deposits, covering authorization, capital adequacy, liquidity and board-level governance and risk management.

## How members connect

- The 2020 DTFC Regulations act as the parent instrument; the other members are its constituent obligation pillars referenced from it.
- Authorization is the gateway condition — capital, liquidity and governance requirements are ongoing licence conditions whose breach affects continued authorization.
- Shared defined term 'Deposit Taking Finance Company' delimits scope: these duties sit above the ordinary finance company regime but below the banking law regime.
- Governance and risk management provisions interact with SAMA's wider corporate governance, internal control and AML/CTF expectations for supervised entities.
