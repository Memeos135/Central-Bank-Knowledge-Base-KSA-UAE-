# Eligible Counterparties

This node defines who may transact repos in Saudi Arabia: SAMA-licensed financial institutions (banks, insurers, finance companies), CMA-approved capital market institutions, KSA-incorporated corporates assessed for sophistication, and non-domiciled corporates subject to maturity restrictions (no securities with under one year to maturity; foreign financial corporates' collateral must mature at least three months beyond the repo). At least one principal to every repo must be a SAMA-licensed bank. It also carries confidentiality of counterparty identity (except regulatory reporting to SAMA) and legal-risk requirements on full transfer of title and adequate documentation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
