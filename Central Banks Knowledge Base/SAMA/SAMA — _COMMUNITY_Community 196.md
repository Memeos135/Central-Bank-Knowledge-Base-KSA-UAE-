# Seasonal Currency Readiness

## Why this community

Operational readiness of currency supply and exchange services during peak demand periods (Ramadan and Hajj), when cash, foreign-currency and pilgrim-servicing volumes spike.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone seasonal instruction directed at banks and money exchangers rather than a rule family.
- Sits at circular/instruction level under SAMA's supervisory authority over currency issuance, cash handling and exchange activity.
- Interacts with business-continuity and branch-service obligations; non-compliance is a supervisory conduct issue, not a licensing one.
