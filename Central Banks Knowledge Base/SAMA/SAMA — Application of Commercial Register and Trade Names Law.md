# Application of Commercial Register and Trade Names Law

SAMA circular requiring supervised financial institutions to apply the new Commercial Register Law and Trade Names Law, including rectifying their own commercial registers within the transitional grace period, urging customers to do the same, and verifying that customers' CR entries remain valid. It directs that a suspended commercial register be treated the same as an expired one, and reiterates the money-changing rules requirement that a party wishing to conduct activities other than exchange business establish a company rather than a sole proprietorship (or liquidate the other activities). It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
