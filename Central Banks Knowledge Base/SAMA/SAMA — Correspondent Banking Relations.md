# Correspondent Banking Relations

The correspondent banking section of SAMA's Money Exchange Business Procedures Guide, covering the relationships Category (A) exchange houses maintain with correspondents for issuing and receiving remittances, within the broader chapter on exchange business activities. It sits alongside the guide's KYC/CDD, remittance and cross-border cash rules and is read with SAMA's AML/CTF rules for banks and money exchangers. It binds SAMA-licensed money exchangers; only Category (A) houses may issue or accept remittances through correspondents.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
