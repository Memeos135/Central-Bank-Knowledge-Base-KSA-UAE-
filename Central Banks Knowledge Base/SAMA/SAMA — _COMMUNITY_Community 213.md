# GCC-NET POS Payments

## Why this community

Regional card acceptance at point of sale through the GCC-NET switch, covering the payments regime for cross-border GCC transactions processed at KSA POS terminals.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster within the payments/PSP regime; touches acquirer and merchant obligations and the operating rules of the national switch.
- Relevant to AML/CTF and sanctions screening of cross-border card flows, and to consumer protection on fees and transaction disclosure.
