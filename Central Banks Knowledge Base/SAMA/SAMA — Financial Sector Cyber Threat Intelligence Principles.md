# Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles

SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Principles, structured into domains covering core CTI lifecycle principles and strategic CTI, expressed as numbered Principles addressed to Member Organizations. It sets expectations for building an in-house CTI capability (roles, dedicated team, resources, budget, internal and external communication channels) and for identifying an organisation-specific cyber threat landscape including threat actors, vulnerable assets, risks and trends. It applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations in the KSA financial sector, with outputs directed at executive management such as the CISO.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
