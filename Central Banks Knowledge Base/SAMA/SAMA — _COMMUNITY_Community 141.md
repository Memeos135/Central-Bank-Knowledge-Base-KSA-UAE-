# Complaint and Fund Distribution

## Why this community

Consumer-facing conduct duties for financial service delivery: handling and escalating customer complaints, and the conduct/distribution conditions attached to selling investment fund units through electronic channels.

## How members connect

- Linked by a shared consumer-protection premise: any electronic distribution channel must be backed by an accessible complaint-handling route with defined turnaround and escalation.
- Complaint-management requirements operate as a cross-cutting obligation layered onto product-specific distribution rules, not as a standalone regime.
- Phased implementation of complaint systems sets the compliance timeline against which channel-specific offerings must be assessed.
