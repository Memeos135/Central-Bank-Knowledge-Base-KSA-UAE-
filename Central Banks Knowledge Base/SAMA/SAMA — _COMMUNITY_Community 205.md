# Money Exchange Statutory Reserve

## Why this community

Prudential reserve requirement imposed on money exchange centres — the amount and maintenance of statutory reserve balances held with SAMA.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular; its linkage is to the money-exchange licensing regime rather than to other nodes in this graph.
- Relevant as a prudential/supervisory condition of licence, distinct from AML/CTF obligations that also bind exchange centres.
