# Companies Law

Node covering account opening for juristic persons under the Companies Law within the SAMA Bank Account Rules — documentary requirements for joint-stock companies (including companies under formation and formation-expenses accounts requiring Ministry of Commerce approval), limited liability companies, general and limited partnerships, GCC and non-resident companies, and high-risk classification where endowments, private foundations or cooperative associations hold more than 50% of capital. Binds banks opening and maintaining corporate accounts.
