# Internal Models Approach (IMA)

Concept node for the Internal Models Approach (IMA), the SAMA-approved alternative to standardised methods for computing market risk capital, built on expected shortfall (ES), default risk capital (DRC) and stressed ES for non-modellable risk factors, applied at trading desk level. It also connects to the modelled-approach columns of the capital/RWA overview templates, which distinguish RWA from approaches a bank has SAMA approval to use from standardised-approach RWA. It binds SAMA-licensed banks that have obtained supervisory approval to use internal models, including group-wide disclosure duties (Table MRB qualitative disclosures).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** The two approaches are alternatives at desk level but not mutually exclusive at bank level, so the decision is which portfolios sit where. The framework permits a choice between the standardised approach and the IMA, while requiring that portfolios in desks not eligible for or not using the IMA be capitalised under the standardised approach. ### [[SAMA — Trading Desk|Trading Desk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** The trading desk is the unit at which IMA eligibility is granted and therefore the unit at which the capital and disclosure obligation is decided. Desks that are not eligible for the IMA must be capitalised under the standardised approach, and a bank using the IMA must additionally compute a standalone standardised figure for each eligible desk with no offsetting across desks. The consequence is that IMA permission is never firm-wide in effect: the reader must map each desk's eligibility status before determining which capital requirement and which reported figure applies.
