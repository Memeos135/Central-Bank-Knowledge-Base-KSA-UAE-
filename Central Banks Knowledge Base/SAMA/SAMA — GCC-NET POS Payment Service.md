# GCC-NET POS Payment Service

A SAMA circular activating the GCC-NET point-of-sale payment service for acceptance of transactions in Qatar as of a stated go-live date, building on earlier circulars on GCC-NET POS and its tariff. It requires banks to make the technical and operational changes needed to accept and process GCC-NET card transactions on their systems and POS terminals, to arrange commercial terms with GCC cardholders and acquiring merchants so transactions are processed at approved pricing, to inform and educate cardholders that purchases in Qatar route via the Qatari national network linked to GCC-NET, and to accept GCC-NET branded cards. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments
