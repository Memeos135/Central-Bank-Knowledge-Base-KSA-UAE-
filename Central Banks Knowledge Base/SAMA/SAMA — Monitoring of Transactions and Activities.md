# Monitoring of Transactions and Activities

This node covers ongoing monitoring of transactions and activities under SAMA's AML/CTF guide, grounded in the AML Law and the counter-terrorism financing law duty to check that transactions, documents and data remain consistent with what the institution knows about the customer. It requires documented, senior-management-approved monitoring procedures calibrated by risk assessment outcomes, with enhanced scrutiny for high-risk customers and simplified treatment for low-risk ones, and treats manual monitoring alone as insufficient. It also requires periodic (at least annual) documented testing of monitoring tools and ongoing review, updating and reclassification of customer information.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
