# Money Changing Business Rules

## Why this community

Licensing and ongoing conduct regime for money changers in KSA, covering who may carry on money changing business and the AML/CTF controls that attach to that licence.

## How members connect

- The Rules Regulating Money Changing Business act as the parent instrument, cross-referring both to the licensing conditions and to the AML/CTF obligations.
- Licensing is a gateway control: authorisation conditions are the hook through which AML/CTF and supervisory requirements become enforceable on the money changer.
- Cross-regime interaction: money changers are DNFBP-adjacent financial institutions, so the AML/CTF Law and Implementing Regulations apply in parallel with these sectoral rules (CDD, record keeping, STR reporting, TFS screening).
