# Exchange Center Premises Requirements

## Why this community

Licensing and operational prerequisites for the physical premises of money exchange centres, covering minimum standards a licensed exchanger's branch must satisfy.

## How members connect

- Standalone premises-standard instrument within the exchange/money-changing licensing regime; it conditions licence grant and continued authorisation.
- Connects conceptually to AML/CTF and security expectations for cash-handling businesses, since premises controls support customer identification and safe handling at the point of service.
- Read as a supervisory requirement layer beneath the exchange-business licensing rules rather than as an independent regime.
