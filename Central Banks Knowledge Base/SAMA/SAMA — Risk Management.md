# Risk Management

Chapter VII of the Real Estate Refinance Rules requires a board-approved and annually updated business strategy and risk management policy covering all risk types including outsourced processes, plus procedures to identify, assess, manage, monitor and report risks within a comprehensive framework. Related provisions cover separation of executive and supervisory functions, adequacy of IT systems and data integrity/confidentiality, business continuity planning, and periodic risk reporting to SAMA covering market risk, collateral values, term cost mismatch, delinquencies, concentration and securities exposures. Binds the licensed real estate refinance company, its board, risk and credit management committee and senior management.
