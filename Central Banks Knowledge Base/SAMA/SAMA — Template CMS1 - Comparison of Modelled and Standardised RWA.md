# Template CMS1 - Comparison of Modelled and Standardised RWA

Template CMS1 requires banks to compare actual RWA (including portfolios where SAMA has approved modelled approaches) against RWA that would result from applying the full standardised approach across credit, counterparty credit, securitisation, market and operational risk, plus residual RWA. The instructions define each row's standardised-approach baseline, including credit risk mitigation treatment and the phase-in for equity exposures under IRB. It binds banks using internal models under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
