# Table MRA - General Qualitative Disclosure on Market Risk

A qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table requiring banks to describe their market risk management strategy, governance, reporting and measurement arrangements under the standardised approach. It sits at the head of the market risk section, which covers trading book and banking book exposures subject to market risk capital requirements plus trading book securitisation positions, but excludes counterparty credit risk (reported separately). It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
