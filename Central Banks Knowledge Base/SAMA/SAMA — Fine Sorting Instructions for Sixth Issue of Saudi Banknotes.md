# Fine Sorting Instructions for Sixth Issue of Saudi Banknotes

SAMA instructions establishing unified technical fitness-sorting standards for the sixth issue of Saudi banknotes, covering counting and sorting controls, counterfeit detection, material and quality defects, fitness rejection levels, density measurement, quality control, machine sensors and monitoring. They apply to financial institutions as defined for this purpose — banks, cash centres and exchange centres supervised by SAMA — with staggered compliance dates by denomination and a requirement to coordinate with SAMA to obtain specimen notes for machine programming.
