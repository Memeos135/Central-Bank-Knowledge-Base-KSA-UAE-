# Template CMS1 - Modelled vs Standardised RWA at Risk Level

Pillar 3 template CMS1 requiring comparison of risk-weighted assets calculated under SAMA-approved internal/modelled approaches against RWA that would result from the corresponding standardised approaches, presented at the level of each risk category. It defines which exposures fall in each row (credit risk, counterparty credit risk, CVA, securitisation, equities, settlement risk) and how the standardised comparison is computed. Applies to banks using modelled approaches with SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
