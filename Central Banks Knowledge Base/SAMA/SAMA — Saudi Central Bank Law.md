# Saudi Central Bank Law

The Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36, 2020), the constitutive statute establishing SAMA as a financially and administratively independent legal person reporting to the King. It sets the Bank's objectives (monetary stability, financial sector stability and confidence, economic growth) and its powers, including currency issuance, supervision and regulation of financial institutions, issuing regulations and directives, monetary and FX policy, reserve management, acting as the government's bank and adviser, operating and licensing payment/settlement/clearing systems and fintech platforms, consumer protection directives, prudential policy, and crime-prevention measures. It binds SAMA itself and, through its supervisory mandate, every natural or legal person defined as a Financial Institution subject to SAMA's supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, consumer protection, other
