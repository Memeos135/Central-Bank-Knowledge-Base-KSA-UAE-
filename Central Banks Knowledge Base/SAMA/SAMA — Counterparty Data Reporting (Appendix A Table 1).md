# Counterparty Data Reporting (Appendix A Table 1)

Appendix A Table 1 of the SAMA OTC derivatives reporting requirements, specifying the counterparty-identification fields a reporting bank must populate when submitting reportable transactions to the SAMA-authorised trade repository (reporting and other counterparty IDs, country of counterparty, broker and clearing member IDs). It sits within the reporting regime that obliges banks to report directly (no agents or outsourcing), to sign a reporting service agreement including a data-consent clause, and to pass user testing before live reporting. Binds SAMA-licensed reporting banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, data/credit info
