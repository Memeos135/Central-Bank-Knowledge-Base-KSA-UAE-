# Contracting with Cash Transport Companies Circular

A SAMA Currency Department circular (Arabic) implementing Ministry of Interior recommendations requiring large companies and commercial outlets to contract only licensed cash transport companies for moving cash, and to use security bags/containers approved by SAMA — including containers permitting automatic tracking and self-destruction of notes if the planned route is changed. It binds banks, exchange companies, licensed cash transport companies and operators of licensed consolidated cash centres, and notes that SAMA will conduct field inspections and impose penalties on violators.
