# Traditional Securitization

Defines traditional securitization as a structure where cash flows from an underlying exposure pool service at least two stratified/tranched risk positions, with investor payments depending on pool performance rather than the originator's obligation, and distinguishes it from ordinary senior/subordinated debt. It sits within the securitization framework's scope provisions, which require capital treatment based on economic substance rather than legal form, and define securitization exposures, resecuritization and originator status. Binds banks determining regulatory capital for securitization exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
