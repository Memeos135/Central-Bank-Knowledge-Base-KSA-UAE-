# Template MR2: Market Risk IMA per Risk Type

A Pillar 3 template disclosing market risk capital under the internal models approach broken down by component — unconstrained and constrained expected shortfall, non-modellable risk factors, default risk charge, amber-desk surcharge and standardised amounts for IMA-ineligible desks. Accompanying text addresses internal risk transfers between banking and trading books and their regulatory capital recognition. It binds banks with SAMA approval to use the IMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
