# Marketing & Complaints Compliance

## Why this community

Consumer-protection conduct duties governing how financial institutions market to, communicate with, and resolve complaints from customers, including accessibility obligations toward persons with disabilities.

## How members connect

- Common consumer-protection base: advertising/marketing content standards, complaint-handling standards and accessibility duties all attach to the same customer-facing conduct obligation set.
- Accessibility requirements cross-reference marketing and disclosure rules, so advertising material and channels must meet disability-access standards as well as fairness/clarity standards.
- The unified complaint response template operationalises complaint-handling obligations and supplies the standardised output SAMA expects, making it the procedural endpoint of breaches of marketing or accessibility duties.
- Hierarchy runs from consumer-protection principles down to instructions and then to the standardised template/form level.
