# Government Entities Account Rules

Section 500 of SAMA's bank account opening rules, setting how banks may open and operate riyal accounts for Saudi ministries and government entities, including donation-receipt accounts. It requires Ministry of Finance application and SAMA notification, prohibits accounts in a natural person's name or job title, restricts foreign-currency accounts, prohibits loans, facilities or overdrafts to government entities absent Council of Ministers approval, controls transfer and closure of accounts, and limits signatories to Saudi nationals. It binds SAMA-licensed banks and constrains their credit exposure to government counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
