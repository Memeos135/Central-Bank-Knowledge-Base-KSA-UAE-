# Circular: Cancel Official Stamp Requirement

A SAMA circular prohibiting licensed finance companies from requiring the official stamp of establishments and companies on documents and papers submitted in financing transactions, following Ministry of Commerce and Investment decisions and a Royal Order aimed at improving the investment environment. The prohibition applies where no statutory requirement for a stamp exists and is expressly without prejudice to Know Your Customer requirements. It binds licensed finance companies with effect from the circular's date.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, other
