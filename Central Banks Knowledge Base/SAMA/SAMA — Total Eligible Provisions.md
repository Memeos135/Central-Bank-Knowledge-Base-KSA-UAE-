# Total Eligible Provisions

This node defines total eligible provisions for IRB purposes as the aggregate of specific provisions, partial write-offs and portfolio-specific general provisions attributed to IRB exposures, plus permitted discounts on defaulted assets, while excluding provisions held against securitisation exposures. It also sets out how banks using both standardised and IRB approaches allocate general provisions between the two treatments, including pro rata attribution and SAMA discretion to accept internal allocation methods. It binds banks using IRB for all or part of their credit risk exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
