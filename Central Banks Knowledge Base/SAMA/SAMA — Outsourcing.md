# Outsourcing

Chapter VI of the Real Estate Refinance Rules requires a board-issued, annually updated outsourcing policy (board/senior management responsibilities, functions that may not be outsourced, provider eligibility, risk identification and hedging) and mandates contractual audit/access rights for SAMA, the external auditor and the company. The company remains liable for the outsourcing provider's non-compliance, and a SAMA no-objection letter is required before outsourcing that could materially affect activities, reputation or financial position, or that involves transferring, processing or storing borrower data — with subcontracting likewise conditional on SAMA non-objection. Binds licensed real estate refinance companies; read with SAMA's wider outsourcing framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
