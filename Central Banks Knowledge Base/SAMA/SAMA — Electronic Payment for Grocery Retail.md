# Electronic Payment for Grocery Retail

A SAMA circular implementing the national mandate (with the Anti-Commercial Concealment Programme, Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs and Ministry of Commerce) that all groceries and supply stores in the Kingdom must offer electronic payment means from 17 Ramadan 1441 / 10 May 2020. It directs banks and payment service providers to be ready to open bank accounts and e-wallets for merchants in that sector, to receive and process requests for SAMA-authorised payment instruments (POS terminals, QR) through all channels, and to apply the correct merchant category code 5411. It binds all banks and PSPs operating in KSA, including an internal-communication duty to staff.

**Regimes:** payments, other
