# SAMA Circular on Updating Credit Granting Policies for Finance Companies

A short SAMA circular, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulation, directing finance companies to update their credit granting policies and procedures to keep pace with developments in the finance sector and the activities being financed, and to adopt prudential measures to limit the rise in non-performing loans. Firms must also ensure provisions and collateral are commensurate with the risks of the activity. Distribution covers finance companies operating in the Kingdom and SAMA-licensed crowdfunding entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
