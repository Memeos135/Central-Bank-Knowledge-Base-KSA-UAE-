# Money Exchange Procedures

## Why this community

The prudential, AML/CTF and operational rulebook for licensed money exchangers, spanning customer identification, correspondent relationships, physical cash movement (including cross-border and causeway traffic) and Saudization/employment conditions.

## How members connect

- The Money Exchange Business Procedures Guide is the hub instrument: it incorporates KYC and CDD requirements and correspondent banking conditions by reference, and cites the nationals-employment/recruitment requirements.
- A clear hierarchy exists - the later updating circular amends or supplements the Guide, so the Guide must be read as amended rather than standalone.
- KYC and CDD form a single obligation chain (identify and verify the Customer, then apply risk-based ongoing due diligence), which in turn conditions whether an exchanger may open or maintain a correspondent relationship.
- Cash-movement instruments (causeway entry/exit, cash transportation, cross-border reporting) connect conceptually: the same physical currency flows trigger both transport-security controls and declaration/reporting duties under the AML/CTF regime.
