# Liquidity Risk Management

## Why this community

Qualitative disclosure of a bank's liquidity risk management framework - governance, funding strategy, stress testing, contingency funding planning and risk tolerance.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a narrative (table) disclosure whose content requirements are tied to the liquidity risk governance and management expectations set out in the prudential liquidity framework.
- Provides the qualitative context for the quantitative liquidity metrics disclosed elsewhere, so it is read together with the LCR/NSFR templates rather than standalone.
- Links governance/risk-management obligations to transparency obligations: the same internal framework the bank must maintain is what it must describe publicly.
