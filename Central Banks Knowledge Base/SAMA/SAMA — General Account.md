# General Account

Rules within the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations governing the General Account, a purpose-limited account whose sole function is to support the operation of Term Deposit Accounts. It sets permitted credit sources (only the accountholder's own bank accounts or their linked Term Deposit Account), name-matching and return-of-funds duties, mandatory record fields for credit postings, and withdrawal rules requiring funds to be remitted back to a source account of the same accountholder; transfers between General Accounts are allowed only in the same name. Binds DTFCs licensed in KSA in relation to their non-individual depositors.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, governance/risk
