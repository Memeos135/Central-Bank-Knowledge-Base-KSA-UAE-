# Professional Risk

The second main category of operational risk in SAMA's guidance, covering professional errors and omissions, professional liability, and contingent client-related liability, as distinct from criminal risk. It sits within the framework requiring banks to identify and analyse operational risks and to select integrated control and financing techniques, including training, product risk analysis, contractual indemnities and professional indemnity insurance. It binds banks' senior management, the Operational Risk Manager and the board through the operational risk management plan.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
