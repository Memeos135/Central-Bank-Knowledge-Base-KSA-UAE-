# Rules for Deposits in Bank Accounts

Node addressing deposit handling under the SAMA Bank Account Rules, including which credits may be received into an account whose status is restricted — dormant accounts may continue to accept deposits, domestic and international transfers and dividends from third parties without the status reverting to active. It binds banks operating customer accounts and is relevant to disputes over whether incoming credits reset dormancy periods.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, banking prudential
