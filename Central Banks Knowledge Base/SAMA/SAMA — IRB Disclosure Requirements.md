# IRB Disclosure Requirements

A node on the Pillar 3 disclosure obligations applicable to banks using foundation or advanced IRB approaches, including template CR7 showing the RWA effect of credit derivatives used as CRM, and the overarching disclosure principles (meaningfulness, consistency over time, comparability, proportionality) and assurance requirements. Templates are mandatory for banks applying A-IRB and/or F-IRB to any exposures, with semiannual frequency and accompanying narrative commentary. Requires a formal board-approved disclosure policy.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
