# Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB)

SAMA's January 2017 circular adopting the Basel Committee's revised standards on Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book, covering identification, measurement, monitoring, control and disclosure of IRRBB, including interest rate shock scenarios, behavioural/modelling assumptions, an updated standardised framework and a tighter outlier test (15% of Tier 1 capital, down from 20% of total capital). It is addressed to domestic banks only and expressly does not apply to foreign bank branches, and is accompanied by annexures on regulatory returns and FAQs including national discretions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
