# Global Master Repurchase Agreement (GMRA)

This node covers the legal documentation layer of SAMA's repo guidelines: the scope of application (SAR-denominated outright sale/repurchase of eligible securities among eligible counterparties), the key defined terms (Custodian, Eligible Security, Held-in-Custody, Repo Buyer/Seller, Saudi Repo Market, SARIE, Tadawul), and the requirement that Saudi repo transactions be documented under SAMA's approved standard Master Repurchase Agreement (2020 version), governed by Saudi law, with variations confined to annexures and mutually agreed. It clarifies that existing foreign-currency repos documented under the GMRA are not prohibited, subject to applicable KSA rules. It binds all repo market participants, with a bank licensed by SAMA required as a principal.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
