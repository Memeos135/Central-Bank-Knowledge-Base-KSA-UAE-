# Write-off Rules

Prescribes when finance companies must de-recognise uncollectible credit exposures, requiring write-off after collection and recovery options are exhausted and internal audit review, with maximum time limits from Stage 3 classification: 360 days unsecured, 720 days secured, and 1,080 days for mortgages and corporate exposures (more conservative internal policies permitted). It confirms write-off does not extinguish recovery rights and that multiple exposures to one counterparty are treated at counterparty level. Also clarifies that partial write-off or collateral repossession does not by itself restore a forborne exposure to performing.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
