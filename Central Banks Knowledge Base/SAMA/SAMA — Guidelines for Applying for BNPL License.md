# Guidelines for Applying for BNPL License

SAMA guidance document explaining how to apply for a licence to conduct Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) activity under the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules Regulating BNPL Companies. It covers application instructions, defined use of "Applicant" (founders/shareholders), required attachments and the licensing process, and warns that false or omitted material information breaches the Finance Companies Control Law and may cause rejection or cancellation. It binds prospective BNPL companies and their founders/authorised representatives.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance
