# Rating Overrides

Concept node addressing constraints on overriding or selectively applying ratings when assigning risk weights: banks must apply recognised ECAI ratings consistently and may not cherry-pick among agencies, and the rules dictate which rating governs where multiple, issuer-level or issue-level ratings exist. Low-quality issuer or issue ratings are pushed onto pari passu or subordinated unassessed exposures, and limited-class ratings (e.g. deposit or counterparty risk ratings) may only be used within that class. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
