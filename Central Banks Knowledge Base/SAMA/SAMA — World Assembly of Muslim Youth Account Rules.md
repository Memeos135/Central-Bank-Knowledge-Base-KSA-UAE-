# World Assembly of Muslim Youth Account Rules

A sub-rule of SAMA's bank account opening rules governing accounts for the World Assembly of Muslim Youth, treated as a designated international Islamic organisation. It specifies the approvals, supporting documents, account naming and operating authorities required before a bank may open or maintain the account. It binds SAMA-licensed banks and connects to AML/CTF due diligence expectations for non-profit and charitable funds flows.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
