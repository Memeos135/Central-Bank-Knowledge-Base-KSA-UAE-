# Replacing Commercial Registration with Unified Number

SAMA circular and annexed instructions requiring supervised entities to substitute the Unified Number (issued by the National Information Center, beginning with "7") for the Commercial Registration number and licence numbers as the digital identifier of non-governmental entities, while retaining the existing CR field and linking the two so a search on either returns both. It extends the migration deadline, replaces all earlier circulars on the subject, and requires entities to inventory affected customers, licensed non-CR entities (e.g. lawyers, non-profit associations) and business partners, and to assess risk impacts on credit, blocks/freezes, dormant accounts, local and UN lists, KYC generally and beneficial-owner verification. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision and all credit information companies.
