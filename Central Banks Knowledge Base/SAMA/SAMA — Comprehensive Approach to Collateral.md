# Comprehensive Approach to Collateral

Covers the comprehensive approach to financial collateral, under which exposure and collateral values are adjusted by supervisory or own-estimate haircuts rather than substituting risk weights, and relates to disclosure of collateral composition for counterparty credit risk exposures. Relevant to banks using the standardised approach for credit risk mitigation and to Pillar 3 collateral reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
