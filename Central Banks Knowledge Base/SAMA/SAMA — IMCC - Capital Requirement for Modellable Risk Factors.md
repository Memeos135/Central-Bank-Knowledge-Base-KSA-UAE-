# IMCC - Capital Requirement for Modellable Risk Factors

Concept node for IMCC, the internally modelled capital charge covering modellable risk factors under the market risk internal models approach, built from the constrained and unconstrained expected shortfall figures and feeding the aggregate IMA capital requirement. The surrounding context is SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework, which sets out RWA and minimum capital requirement templates (including the standard RWA*8% Pillar 1 relationship) and capital distribution constraint disclosures. Relevant to banks using the IMA and to their public disclosure obligations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
