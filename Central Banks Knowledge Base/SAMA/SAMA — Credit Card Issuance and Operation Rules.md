# Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules

SAMA's Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules, issued under the SAMA Law and circulated to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, replacing the earlier Credit and Charge Card Regulations. It sets the full lifecycle framework for credit cards: definitions (Issuer, Customer, Credit Limit, Amount Due, Minimum Payment, Due Date, APR, Grace Period, Term Cost, Late Payment Fee, Initial Disclosure), issuance conditions, the cardholder agreement, repayment, statements, transactions, liability for unauthorised use, and fees and charges, plus disclosure form annexes. It binds all SAMA-supervised entities licensed to issue and finance credit cards, with a general 90-day compliance window and a longer deferred date for two specified provisions on repayment and on card fees/costs.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, data/credit info, AML/CTF
