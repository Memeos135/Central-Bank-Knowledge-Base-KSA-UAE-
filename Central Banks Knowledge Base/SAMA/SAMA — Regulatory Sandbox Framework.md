# Regulatory Sandbox Framework

The SAMA Regulatory Sandbox framework, established in November 2019, permitting local and international firms to test new digital financial solutions in a live KSA environment ahead of full deployment. In the cyber context it is the gateway population for the Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements: sandbox entities must demonstrate compliance, and SAMA may block graduation or licensing if they cannot. Relevant to firms seeking authorisation and to supervisors assessing readiness for a full licence.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, payments
