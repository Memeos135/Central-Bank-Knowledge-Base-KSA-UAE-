# Supervisory Assessment

"Supervisory Assessment" is a defined term in SAMA's Oversight Framework for Payment Systems and their Operators: an independent assessment conducted by SAMA to measure a payment system's compliance with the applicable regulatory frameworks and relevant international principles. It is the regulator-side counterpart to the operator's self-assessment within the framework's supervisory methodology. It applies to payment systems and their operators subject to SAMA oversight.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
