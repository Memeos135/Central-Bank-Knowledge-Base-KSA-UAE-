# Founding Day Holiday 1447H

SAMA circular designating Founding Day (22 February) for 1447H as an official holiday for supervised financial institutions, fixing the corresponding Sunday date. It also requires banks, exchange centres and payment service providers to keep a number of branches open at airports and border crossings for defined periods during the holiday and to notify SAMA of those branches and their hours, with public announcement. Binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** other, payments
