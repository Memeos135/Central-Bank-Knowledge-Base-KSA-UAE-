# Advanced IRB Treatment

This node concerns the advanced IRB treatment, under which a bank uses own estimates of loss given default and exposure at default in addition to probability of default, where SAMA permits such use. It is framed by the roll-out provisions allowing a staged move from the foundation to the advanced approach across asset classes and business units, subject to an implementation plan agreed with SAMA and a prohibition on cherry-picking to minimise capital. It applies to SAMA-approved IRB banks and their banking groups.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
