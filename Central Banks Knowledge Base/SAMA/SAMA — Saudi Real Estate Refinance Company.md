# Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company

Concept node for the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company, the SAMA-supervised secondary-market entity that purchases residential real estate finance portfolios from banks and finance companies. Related instruments set the conditions for such purchases, including minimum seasoning before sale, the length of the originator's recourse obligation, and relief from the prior no-objection requirement for disposals of finance assets (subject to post-transaction notification to SAMA).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential
