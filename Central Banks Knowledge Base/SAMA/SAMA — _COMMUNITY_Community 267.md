# External Credit Ratings Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure on how a bank uses external credit assessment institutions (ECAIs) when assigning risk weights under the standardised approach to credit risk. It addresses the mapping and consistency of nominated ratings to exposure classes, not the computation itself.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the table sits at the qualitative layer of the disclosure hierarchy (Basel/SAMA capital framework rules on ECAI recognition, then the standardised approach rules, then this disclosure obligation).
- Its content is the narrative counterpart to the standardised-approach quantitative templates, explaining the rating inputs that drive the risk-weight buckets reported elsewhere.
- Obligation is periodic disclosure by the bank; scope is limited to exposure types where a nominated ECAI rating is actually used.
