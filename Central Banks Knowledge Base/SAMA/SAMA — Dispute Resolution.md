# Dispute Resolution

Requires banks to agree and document with counterparties the mechanism for identifying when discrepancies in material terms or valuations constitute disputes and how such disputes are resolved as soon as practicable, with material disputes escalated to senior management and the Board under clear criteria. Sits within Section B risk mitigation alongside reconciliation, compression and governance provisions. Binds banks that are parties to non-centrally cleared OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
