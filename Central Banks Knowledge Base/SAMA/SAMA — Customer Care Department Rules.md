# Customer Care Department Rules

SAMA rules (second edition) requiring banks to establish a Customer Care Department, replacing the earlier complaint-unit rules and setting the minimum standards for customer care, complaint handling and financial education. The department must be created by board decision, report directly to the bank's most senior executive, be independent of other departments, be adequately resourced, and contain at least three units: complaint handling, quality and performance analysis, and financial awareness and education. Applies to banks licensed to conduct banking business in the Kingdom, effective from the date stated in the circular.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
