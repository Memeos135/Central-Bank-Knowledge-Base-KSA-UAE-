# Electronic Authorization for Judicial Notifications Circular

SAMA circular urging financial institutions to use the Ministry of Commerce "electronic authorisation" service to appoint a delegate to receive judicial notifications filed against them, and to monitor judicial orders for and against them through the Najiz portal. It builds on the Royal Order approving electronic means of judicial notification (verified mobile SMS, email, notification via registered government system accounts) and on the Enforcement Law and its Implementing Regulation provisions on notification timing, so institutions can settle claims within the notification period and avoid compulsory enforcement measures. Addressed to SAMA-supervised financial institutions, including payment companies.
