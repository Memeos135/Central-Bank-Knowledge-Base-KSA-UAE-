# Minimum Haircut Floors for SFTs

Sets the minimum haircut floors regime for non-centrally cleared securities financing transactions with in-scope counterparties, defining which SFTs are captured, the exemptions (central bank SFTs, certain cash-collateralised securities lending, non-re-usable collateral upgrade trades) and the floor percentages by collateral type and residual maturity. Also frames the consequence of breaching a floor for capital treatment. Applies to banks conducting SFTs under SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit/counterparty risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
