# Correspondence Relationship

Section 13 of SAMA's AML/CTF rules on correspondent (correspondence) relationships, requiring pre-onboarding information gathering on the respondent institution's business, reputation, supervision and AML/CTF controls, clear allocation of responsibilities between parties, ongoing monitoring of correspondent accounts, and senior management approval with input from the AML/CTF compliance officer. It prohibits entering or continuing relationships with shell banks or with institutions that allow shell bank use of their accounts. Also touches employee screening/background verification and leave-rotation controls in the preceding section.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
