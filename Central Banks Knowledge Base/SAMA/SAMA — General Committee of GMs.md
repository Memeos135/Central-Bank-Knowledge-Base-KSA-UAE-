# General Committee of GMs

Sub-node covering the General Committee of General Managers of finance companies, established by Governor's decision and governed by the Terms of Reference for Finance Company Committees issued under Article 98 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. The Terms of Reference address its formation, nomination and removal of members, meetings and quorum, agenda/minutes/reporting, and responsibilities. It binds participating finance companies and their general managers as a sector-representative body channelling proposals to SAMA.
