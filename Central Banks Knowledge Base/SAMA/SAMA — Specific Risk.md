# Specific Risk

A concept node on specific risk (issuer-specific, as distinct from general market risk) within SAMA's market risk capital framework, including its interaction with internal risk transfers between banking book and trading book and the conditions under which hedges and their trading book legs receive regulatory capital recognition. It also underpins the treatment of short credit and short equity positions created by internal risk transfers. It binds banks calculating market risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
