# IRB Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure for IRB credit risk: exposures and risk parameters by portfolio and PD band, and back-testing of realised defaults against estimated PD to evidence model performance.

## How members connect

- Shared defined terms and segmentation — portfolio, PD range/band, obligor grade — so the two templates must be populated on a consistent basis.
- Data dependency: the PD estimates and portfolio buckets disclosed in CR6 are the estimates tested against realised outcomes in CR9.
- Both are conditional on IRB permission and take their model scope and governance narrative from the IRB qualitative disclosure.
- Back-testing disclosure operationalises the supervisory expectation of ongoing model validation, creating a consequence chain from model underperformance to supervisory challenge.
