# Basle Committee on Banking Supervision

Concept node for the Basle (Basel) Committee on Banking Supervision as the issuer of the "Sound Practices for Banks' Interactions with Highly Leveraged Institutions" paper transmitted by SAMA to all banks in March 1999 following the near-collapse of LTCM. The paper promotes prudent assessment, measurement and credit risk management of exposures to HLIs and hedge funds, complementing general credit risk principles. SAMA expects banks with such exposures to manage them consistently with SAMA's Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks and their own internal credit policies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
