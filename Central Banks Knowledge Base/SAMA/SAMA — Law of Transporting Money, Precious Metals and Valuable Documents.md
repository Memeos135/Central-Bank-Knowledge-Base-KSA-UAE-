# Law of Transporting Money, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents

The Law on Practising the Transport of Money, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents, issued by Royal Decree and approved by Council of Ministers resolution. It defines money, precious metals, valuable documents and the licensed activity, sets the licensing regime administered by the Ministry of Interior/General Security (three-year renewable licences, fees, security-department requirement), mandates insurance cover for transported items, and requires advance notification and route coordination for consignments above a stated value. It binds financial institutions (banks, exchange houses, investment and insurance firms), precious-metals factories and outlets, and licensed private security companies carrying out such transport.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
