# Rating Refresh and Review

Concept node on the use of external credit assessments (ECAIs) for standardised-approach risk weighting: consistent use of SAMA-recognised ECAIs, prohibition on cherry-picking or arbitrarily switching ECAIs, use of global rating scales, and the multiple-ratings hierarchy (higher risk weight where two ratings differ; second-lowest rule where three or more). It also governs when issue-specific versus issuer ratings may be relied on and bars double-counting of credit enhancement already reflected in a rating. Binds banks determining risk weights for rated and unrated exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
