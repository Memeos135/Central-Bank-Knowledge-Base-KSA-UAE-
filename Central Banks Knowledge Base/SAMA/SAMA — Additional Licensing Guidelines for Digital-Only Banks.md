# Additional Licensing Guidelines for Digital-Only Banks

SAMA's Additional Licensing Guidelines and Criteria for Digital-Only Banks, issued under the SAMA Charter and the Banking Control Law, layered on top of the general Banking Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria. It sets licensing conditions (locally incorporated joint-stock company, promoter experience and financial capacity), business plan content, ICAAP/ILAAP submission, physical head-office presence, governance parity with conventional banks, risk management, AML/CFT in a fully digital environment, technology/cybersecurity, independent assessment, outsourcing, exit plan, prudential/supervisory requirements and consumer protection. It binds applicants for a digital-only banking licence in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection
