# Stress Testing of Counterparty Credit Exposures

This section of the HLI sound practices guidance covers stress testing of counterparty credit exposures and its link to limit setting, requiring banks to model large market moves, liquidation effects and liquidity impacts on positions and pledged collateral rather than relying on higher confidence intervals or longer horizons for potential future exposure (PFE). It also sets expectations that overall credit limits be established per counterparty on an aggregated, comparable basis, be binding, and be monitored with clear procedures for reducing exposure. It applies to banks with material exposures to highly leveraged institutions and, by extension, to counterparty credit management generally.
