# Company Incorporation Contracts

## Why this community

Formation-stage documentation for companies: how incorporation contracts (articles of association / memoranda) are treated for regulatory and onboarding purposes, including their evidential role in establishing legal existence and authorised signatories.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a SAMA circular that sits below the Companies Law and Ministry of Commerce registration framework and directs supervised entities on how incorporation instruments are to be accepted or relied upon.
- Feeds the KYB limb of customer due diligence — constitutive documents are the source for legal form, ownership chain and representation powers used in beneficial-ownership identification.
- Cross-regime relevance: the same documents underpin account-opening decisions under AML/CTF rules and contractual capacity questions under consumer/finance regimes.
