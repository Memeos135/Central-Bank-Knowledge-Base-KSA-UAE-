# Credit Information Data Sharing Circular

An Arabic-language SAMA circular addressed to credit information companies, issued under the Credit Information Law and its Implementing Regulations. It requires those companies to obtain SAMA's prior no-objection before supplying any data or statistics to governmental or non-governmental entities, carving out only what is already covered by member agreements concluded under that Law and its Regulations. It binds licensed credit bureaus and constrains their data-sharing and disclosure practices.

**Regimes:** data/credit info
