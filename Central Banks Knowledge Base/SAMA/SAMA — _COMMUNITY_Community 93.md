# Cyber Security Leadership

## Why this community

Cyber security governance obligations under the SAMA Cyber Security Framework — board-level ownership, the CISO role and committee structure, and the maturity model used to assess compliance.

## How members connect

- The Cyber Security Framework is the parent instrument; the leadership and governance domain and the maturity model are its referenced components, with maturity levels functioning as the assessment benchmark for whether obligations are met.
- Role obligations converge on the CISO and the Cyber Security Committee as the required governance structures.
- A SAMA circular on leadership position appointments adds fit-and-proper/appointment conditions layered onto the CISO role defined in the framework.
- The IT Governance Maturity Model is the structural analogue of the cyber maturity model, so findings under one are read consistently with the other.
