# Stress Tests in Capital Adequacy Assessment

Node covering the requirement that an IRB bank maintain sound stress testing processes for assessing capital adequacy, identifying events or changes in economic conditions that could adversely affect credit exposures and testing the bank's ability to withstand them (examples include economic or industry downturns, market-risk events and liquidity conditions). The surrounding context also covers IRB data maintenance for corporate and retail exposures and, separately, the securitisation internal assessment approach requiring prior SAMA notification and approval. Binds SAMA-licensed banks on the IRB approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
