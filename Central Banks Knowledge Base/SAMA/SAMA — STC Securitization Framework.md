# STC Securitization Framework

Defines the scope of, and gateway conditions for, the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) securitization framework: only traditional securitizations, including ABCP conduits and transactions financed by them, fall within scope, and STC-compliant exposures may attract alternative (preferential) capital treatment under SEC-ERBA, SEC-SA and SEC-IRBA. It allocates duties: the originator/sponsor must disclose transaction-level information sufficient for investors to assess STC status, the investor must make its own assessment before applying the alternative treatment, and for retained positions with significant risk transfer the determination rests with the retaining originator. Criteria must be met on an ongoing basis, with some checks required only at origination. Binds SAMA-supervised banks acting as originators, sponsors or investors.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
