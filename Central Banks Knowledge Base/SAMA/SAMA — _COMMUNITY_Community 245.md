# Finance Company Registration Fees

## Why this community

Licensing and authorisation administration for finance companies: the fees payable to SAMA in connection with registration/licensing under the finance companies regime.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular acting as an implementing instrument under the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulation.
- Relevance is conditional on entity classification as a licensed finance company, so scope turns on definitions in the finance regime rather than on payments or banking rules.
- Compliance consequence is administrative (fee payment as a condition of registration), distinct from prudential or conduct obligations.
