# Margin Requirements for Non-centrally Cleared Derivatives

SAMA's margin framework for non-centrally cleared derivatives, setting out instrument scope (all such derivatives except physically settled FX forwards and swaps, with special treatment for the principal exchange leg of cross-currency swaps) and entity scope. Covered entities are financial firms and systemically important non-financial entities (aggregate month-end average notional for March–May exceeding SAR 30 billion group-wide, excluding inter-affiliate trades); central banks, sovereigns, MDBs, the BIS and non-systemic non-financial firms are excluded. Requirements to exchange initial and variation margin apply only to transactions between two covered entities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
