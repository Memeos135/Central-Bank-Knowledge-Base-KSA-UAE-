# General Market Risk

A concept node on general market risk within SAMA's market risk capital framework, covering the general provisions and structure of the standardised approach — including the requirement that all banks (D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs) calculate the market risk capital charge using the standardised approach, the 12.5 multiplier to convert capital requirements into risk-weighted assets, and SAMA's power to demand standardised-approach figures. It also frames internal risk transfers (including CVA risk transfers) that fall within the scope of the market risk capital requirement. It binds all SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
