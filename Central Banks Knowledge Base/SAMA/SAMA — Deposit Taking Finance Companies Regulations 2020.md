# Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020

The parent instrument: SAMA's 2020 Regulations for Deposit Taking Finance Companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51) and its Implementing Regulation. It sets the conditions for exercising deposit-taking activity by finance companies authorised to mobilise savings and time deposits from non-individual customers and to lend from them, covering corporate governance, depositor record-keeping (five-year retention), liquidity and prudential returns, and account operation rules. Applies to all DTFCs in KSA, in addition to the Finance Companies Control Law and general SAMA finance company regulations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF
