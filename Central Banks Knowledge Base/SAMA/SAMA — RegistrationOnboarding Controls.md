# Registration/Onboarding Controls

Section 3 of SAMA's cyber controls document for member organizations providing e-wallets, lending products, crowdfunding or other fintech models, setting registration and onboarding requirements. It mandates one registration per phone number or National ID/Iqama per application, a secure user validation process, strong authentication via an independent licensed trusted party (e.g. National Single Sign-On for e-wallets), OTP verification to a verified number, SMS notifications on status changes, and secure deactivation/reactivation and device re-registration processes.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
