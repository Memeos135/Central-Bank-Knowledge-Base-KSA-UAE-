# Internal Audit Department

Defines and regulates the Internal Audit Department in a finance or real estate refinance company — its key attributes, the head of department's and the department's duties, external quality assessment, record retention, and its relationship with first- and second-line units. Also anchors the defined terms independence, objectivity, conflict of interest and the internal audit function as an independent assurance and advisory activity over internal control, governance and risk management. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
