# Circular on Adopting Ownership Document from Economic Cities Authority

A SAMA circular (Arabic text) confirming earlier circulars that the ownership document issued by the Economic Cities Authority must be accepted for any property or land owner inside the economic cities as an official, recognised title document standing in place of the title deed used outside those cities. It removes doubt about collateral and property documentation within economic cities. It is addressed to banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential
