# ML/TF Risk Assessment

The risk assessment section of the SAMA AML/CTF Guide requires financial institutions to identify, understand and document ML/TF risks across customers and beneficial owners, products and services, geography and delivery channels, as the foundation of the risk-based approach. It mandates customer risk classification (e.g. high/medium/low), regular updating of customer risk profiles, pre-launch assessment of new products, services, practices and technologies, refresh of the risk-based approach at least every two years or on change of risk factors, and provision of risk assessment information to SAMA on request. It also discourages wholesale de-risking in favour of proportionate mitigation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
