# Regulation on Branch Network

SAMA regulation establishing the licensing and supervisory framework for banks' physical branch networks, covering opening, closing and relocating branches, service centers and remittance centers, including zone classification, use of inactive licenses, SAMA prior approval requirements and approval for branches abroad. It replaces the Annual Branch Expansion Plan with a board-approved Branch Network Policy. It applies to all locally incorporated banks and to branches of foreign banks licensed in the Kingdom that plan to expand beyond three branches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection
