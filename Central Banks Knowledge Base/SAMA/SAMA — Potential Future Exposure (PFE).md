# Potential Future Exposure (PFE)

This node concerns potential future exposure as the loan-equivalent measure used to aggregate derivatives exposures to a counterparty across products and instruments. It expects peak exposure measures that reflect netting of long and short positions, portfolio effects across products, risk factors and maturities, multiple time horizons, and industry convergence on confidence intervals and volatility assumptions; PFE should also inform judgments about whether a counterparty can meet implied margin calls. It binds banks measuring and limiting counterparty credit risk on derivatives, particularly with HLIs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
