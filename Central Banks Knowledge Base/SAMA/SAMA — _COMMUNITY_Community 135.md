# LEI and Data Privacy

## Why this community

Entity identification requirements for financial-sector participants alongside bank secrecy constraints on disclosing customer data — the tension between mandated identifier registration and confidentiality duties.

## How members connect

- The LEI Circular imposes the obligation to obtain and maintain a legal entity identifier; the SIMAH issuance agreement designates the local issuing channel through which that obligation is discharged.
- Identifier registration involves transmitting entity and, potentially, customer-linked data to a third party, which engages the confidentiality limits in the non-disclosure circular.
- Read together they define the permissible boundary: a mandated disclosure to an authorised issuer does not displace the general prohibition on disclosing customer banking data.
