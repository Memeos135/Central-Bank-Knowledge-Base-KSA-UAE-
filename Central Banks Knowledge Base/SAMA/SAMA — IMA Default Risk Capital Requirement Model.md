# IMA Default Risk Capital Requirement Model

Concept node for the Default Risk Capital (DRC) requirement under the market risk internal models approach — the modelled measure of jump-to-default risk on trading book positions (including sovereign, equity and defaulted debt exposures) other than those on desks subject to standardised capital requirements. It is one of the IMA components banks must describe and quantify in SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosures, entering the aggregate capital requirement alongside ES-based charges. Binds banks with IMA-approved trading desks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
