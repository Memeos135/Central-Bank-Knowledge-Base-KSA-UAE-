# Account Closure Rules

Node on termination and restriction of banking relationships under the SAMA Bank Account Rules, covering bank-initiated closure of low-balance accounts (SAR 1,000 or less) subject to advance and closing notification and record retention, and the obligation to search and report across all relationships — active, closed, suspended and inoperative accounts, deposits, safe boxes, credit cards and remittance memberships — when SAMA requests disclosure or enforcement (blocking, compulsory deduction, check issuance, transfer). It binds banks and stresses that such requests come only through SAMA and are strictly confidential.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, data/credit info, banking prudential
