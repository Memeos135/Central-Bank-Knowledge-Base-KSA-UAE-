# Cash Transportation Security Guide Circular

SAMA (then the Saudi Arabian Monetary Agency) circular to money exchange centres reaffirming compliance with the rules and instructions governing the movement of cash and with the Security Safety Guide. It consolidates earlier circulars on contracting with cash-in-transit companies, use of specification-compliant security bags, organisation of cash movement for local banks and exchange houses, and the Law on Dealing in Money and Precious Metals, including requirements for armoured vehicles when transporting cash and valuables above the stated threshold. Binds all exchange centres operating in the Kingdom.
