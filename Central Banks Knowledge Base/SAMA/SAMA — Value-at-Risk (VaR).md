# Value-at-Risk (VaR)

A concept node covering value-at-risk and the associated defined vocabulary of SAMA's market risk capital framework — risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, risk class, sensitivity/delta, look-through approach, embedded derivative. VaR appears chiefly as a benchmark in backtesting and exception analysis rather than as the primary capital measure (which is expected shortfall). Relevant to banks calculating market risk capital and to supervisory review of internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
