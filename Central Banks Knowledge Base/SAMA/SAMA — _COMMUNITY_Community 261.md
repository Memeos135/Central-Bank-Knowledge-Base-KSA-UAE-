# CCR Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on counterparty credit risk, covering the bank's objectives, policies and management approach for CCR exposures.

## How members connect

- Single-table cluster providing the narrative counterpart to the quantitative CCR disclosure templates.
- Draws on defined terms from the counterparty credit risk measurement rules (exposure measurement methods, collateral and wrong-way risk concepts).
- Governance linkage: the disclosure obliges the bank to describe risk management, limit-setting and collateral policies set under its board-approved risk framework.
