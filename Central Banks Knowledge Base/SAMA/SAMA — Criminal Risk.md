# Criminal Risk

A concept node within SAMA's Operational Risk Management Guide covering crime as a category of operational risk — losses from fraud, counterfeiting, forgery and similar criminal activity — alongside system outage, professional liability, regulatory non-compliance and reputational losses. The Guide treats crime as one of the risk categories that the Board-approved Operational Risk Management Plan must expressly address, through internal controls, retention or risk transfer; it is guidance for bank directors and senior management, not an AML/CTF obligation in itself.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
