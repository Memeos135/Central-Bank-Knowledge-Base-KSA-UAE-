# Standardised Approach for Counterparty Credit Risk (SA-CCR)

This node identifies the measurement basis for exposures that carry counterparty credit risk under SAMA's Large Exposure Rules, requiring exposure at default computed under the standardised approach for counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR) as adopted by SAMA circulars, with separate treatment for banking book non-derivative assets and securities financing transactions. It also flags the interim SFT measurement position pending SAMA's adoption of the revised comprehensive approach, and the eligibility boundaries for credit risk mitigation in the large exposures context. It binds SAMA-licensed banks measuring counterparty exposures for the large exposure limit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** Where a bank capitalises an equity investment in a fund using the mandate-based approach, derivative positions permitted by the fund's mandate must still be valued for exposure purposes under SA-CCR, so the counterparty credit risk rules feed directly into the fund look-through calculation. Both sit within the same capital adequacy framework, and the MBA provisions rely on the SA-CCR exposure-at-default definition rather than a separate measure. The practical consequence is that a bank cannot avoid SA-CCR mechanics by holding derivatives indirectly through a fund; the mandate ceiling is converted into exposure using the same methodology.
