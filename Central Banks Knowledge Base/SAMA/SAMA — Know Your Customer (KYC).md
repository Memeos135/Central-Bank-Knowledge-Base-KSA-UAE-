# Know Your Customer (KYC)

KYC in the agent banking context is a mandatory component of the bank's Customer Due Diligence programme, alongside information security and data privacy/confidentiality. Agents must establish customer identity by means the bank deems appropriate and verify the purpose and nature of the banking activity or relationship; where an agent doubts the credibility of customer information it must cease dealing with the customer and report to the bank's Money Laundering Reporting Officer. The obligation rests on the bank, which must ensure its agents comply with the CDD programme and remains liable for their conduct.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk
