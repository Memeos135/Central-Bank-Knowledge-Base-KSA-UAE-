# Backtesting Requirements

Sets out backtesting obligations for market risk internal models, including bank-wide green/amber/red zone interpretation, the resulting multiplier add-ons or withdrawal of model permission, documentation and explanation of every exception, and daily trading-desk-level backtesting. Also covers the narrow allowance to disregard a desk-level exception driven by an NMRF with sufficient SES capital, subject to notification and no SAMA objection, and quarterly updating of the backtested portfolio. Binds banks using internal models for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
