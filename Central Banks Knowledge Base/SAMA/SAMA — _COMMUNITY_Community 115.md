# Counterparty Data Reporting

## Why this community

Regulatory reporting of counterparty identity and transaction data, including the identifier standards and the rules determining who must report what and when.

## How members connect

- Appendix A Table 1 sets the counterparty data fields to be reported, and fixes entity identification by reference to the ISO 17442 LEI standard.
- Appendix C carries the reporting obligation itself (scope, reporting party, timing) and cross-refers to the Table 1 data set as the content of that obligation.
- Effectively a hierarchy: obligation rule -> prescribed data schedule -> incorporated external identifier standard; the LEI reference makes an external standard enforceable through the reporting rule.
- Practical consequence: a data-field defect in Table 1 terms is a breach of the Appendix C obligation, not a mere technical issue.
