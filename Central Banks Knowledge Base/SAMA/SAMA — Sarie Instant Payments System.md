# Sarie Instant Payments System

A Governor's circular to banks operating in the Kingdom announcing the launch of the Sarie instant payments system and setting compliance expectations for participants. It requires members to sign the system participation agreement and follow the technical, operational and service-level documents, observe transaction caps (single transaction limit and quick transfer limit), apply the capped customer transfer fees, and follow the Sarie brand and customer journey guidance across channels and staff training. It binds banks participating in the national payments infrastructure.

**Regimes:** payments
