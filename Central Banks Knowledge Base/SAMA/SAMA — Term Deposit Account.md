# Term Deposit Account

Provisions on opening and operating Term Deposit Accounts at Deposit Taking Finance Companies, including the precondition of an active General Account in the same customer's name, acceptance of a Term Deposit Deal, and permanent linkage of each Term Deposit Account to one General Account. It fixes a minimum six-month tenor, one-deal-per-account and no-recycling rules, naming conventions, and the minimum deal data (currency, dates, day-count, principal, profit rate, status) plus Sharia-compliant commodity details where applicable. Binds DTFCs taking savings and time deposits from non-individual customers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
