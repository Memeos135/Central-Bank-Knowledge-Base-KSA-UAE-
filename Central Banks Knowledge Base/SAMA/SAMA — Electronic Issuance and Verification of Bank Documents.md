# Electronic Issuance and Verification of Bank Documents

SAMA rules (first issue, Arabic) on the electronic issuance and verification of bank documents, defining "bank documents" as certificates or statements issued at a customer's request and requiring banks to process all such requests through technical systems, issue electronically in Arabic (English on request), and provide an electronic verification channel for every document issued, whether electronic or paper. It sets a minimum list of documents that must be available electronically (bank certificate, proof of indebtedness, account statement, clearance letter, IBAN certificate), alignment with the Personal Data Protection Law, Electronic Transactions Law and SAMA's information security and business continuity frameworks, issuance-date inclusion, customer awareness and adherence to SAMA's prescribed issuance timeframes. Mandatory for banks licensed under the Banking Control Law from the stated effective date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info
