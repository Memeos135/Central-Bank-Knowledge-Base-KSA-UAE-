# Application & Infrastructure Security

## Why this community

Technical control obligations over the entity's own systems estate — securing applications and infrastructure, identifying and remediating weaknesses, and controlling changes so that remediation and deployment do not themselves create exposure.

## How members connect

- Vulnerability management is the common obligation chain endpoint: both application and infrastructure security requirements feed identified weaknesses into it.
- Change management acts as the procedural gate — fixes and releases arising from the other three domains must pass through approved change controls.
- All four are implementation-layer domains subordinate to the entity's approved cyber security architecture and governance framework.
