# Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules

SAMA's Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (first edition, 2025), issuing minimum mandatory obligations for implementing UNSC-based TFS regimes on terrorism/terrorist financing and proliferation of weapons of mass destruction and its financing. It defines Financial Institution, targeted financial sanctions, sanctions lists, designated persons and funds/assets, and structures obligations across governance, screening, list management, alert escalation, freezing and unfreezing, recordkeeping, and training. Binds all financial institutions subject to SAMA supervision, requires board-level presentation and allocation of responsibilities, and replaces the earlier SAMA circulars on UNSC implementation mechanisms.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk
