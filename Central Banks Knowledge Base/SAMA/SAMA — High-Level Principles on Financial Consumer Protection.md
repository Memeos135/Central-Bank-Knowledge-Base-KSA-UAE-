# High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection

A concept node for the international High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection, cited in the preamble of SAMA's Customer Care Department rules as the policy basis for accessible complaint-redress mechanisms, financial education, and clear disclosure of customer protections, rights and responsibilities. It is referenced as a standard informing SAMA's requirements rather than as a directly binding instrument; the binding obligations flow from the SAMA rules themselves on licensed banks.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
