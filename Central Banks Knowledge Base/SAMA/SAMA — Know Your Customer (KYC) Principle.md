# Know Your Customer (KYC) Principle

The Know Your Customer principle within SAMA's Money Exchange Business Procedures Guide. It requires an exchange house to form a reliable picture of each customer's true identity and the types of business and transactions the customer is likely to conduct, including identifying and verifying beneficial owners from reliable and independent sources, risk-rating customer types and applying enhanced identification where warranted, keeping identification data updated, tracking changes in customer and beneficial-owner identity, and making identification records available to the AML/CTF compliance officer and relevant officers. It binds licensed money exchangers in relation to both membership customers and transient customers, and is applied together with the transaction-threshold rules for currency exchange.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
