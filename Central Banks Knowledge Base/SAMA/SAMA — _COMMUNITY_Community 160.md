# Standardised Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of standardised-approach credit risk: exposure amounts before and after credit conversion factors and credit risk mitigation, and the resulting distribution across asset classes and risk weights.

## How members connect

- Both templates share the same defined perimeter (standardised-approach exposures, asset classes, CRM techniques), so definitional consistency across them is an enforceable reconciliation point.
- Direct cross-reference: post-CRM, post-CCF exposure reported in CR4 is the base that CR5 disaggregates by risk weight.
- They depend on the ECAI/rating disclosures for the inputs that determine the risk-weight bands.
- Sit below the capital adequacy rules in the hierarchy: the rules set the measurement, these templates set the disclosure form and frequency.
