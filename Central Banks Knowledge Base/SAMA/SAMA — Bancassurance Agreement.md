# Bancassurance Agreement

The Agreement is the defined contract by which an insurance Company and a bank agree that the bank will carry out Bancassurance Activities, and it is the legal basis on which the bank markets and distributes the insurer's products to its Clients. The Rules prescribe minimum contents: term and termination procedures, the electronic and non-electronic marketing and distribution channels used, the Authorised Employee training plan, KYC and compliance procedures, insurance classes and products covered, marketing/distribution and premium-collection procedures, commission and its calculation, due date and collection, client care and complaints resolution, and claims receipt and transfer to the Company. It binds both contracting parties; material amendments require SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
