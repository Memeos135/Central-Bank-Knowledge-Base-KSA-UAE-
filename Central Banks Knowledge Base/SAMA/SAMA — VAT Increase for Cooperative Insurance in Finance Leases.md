# VAT Increase for Cooperative Insurance in Finance Leases

A July 2020 SAMA circular clarifying who bears the increase in VAT from 5% to 15% (effective 1 July 2020) on cooperative insurance contracts linked to finance lease agreements. SAMA confirms the lessor must absorb the increase and may not pass it on to the lessee, relying on Article 9(3) of the Finance Lease Law, under which the lessor bears cooperative insurance on the leased asset and cannot stipulate it on the lessee. Binding on finance companies acting as lessors.
