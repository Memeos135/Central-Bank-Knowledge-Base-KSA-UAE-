# Verification of Total Economic Loss for Leased Vehicles

A SAMA circular confirming obligations of finance entities conducting vehicle finance leasing for individuals when determining total economic loss of a leased vehicle. Determinations must rely on reports issued by appraisal firms licensed by the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, applying the total-loss provision of the Finance Lease Law together with the comprehensive insurance controls for individually finance-leased vehicles and the standard vehicle finance lease contract form; on notice of damage to the leased asset, the finance entity must exercise due care with all relevant parties to preserve the lessee's continued use before treating the vehicle as a total economic loss. Binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
