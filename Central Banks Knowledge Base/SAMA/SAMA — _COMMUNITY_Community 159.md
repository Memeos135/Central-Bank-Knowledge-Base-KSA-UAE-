# Counterparty Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Granular Pillar 3 CCR disclosure where exposure is decomposed by risk weight and by counterparty type, covering both standardised-approach bank book counterparties and exposures to central counterparties (qualifying and non-qualifying CCPs).

## How members connect

- Both templates disaggregate the same aggregate CCR exposure measure disclosed at summary level, so figures must reconcile across the template set.
- CCP exposures are carved out of the general standardised-approach portfolio breakdown, creating an explicit cross-reference/exclusion relationship between the two templates.
- Shared defined terms: qualifying CCP, default fund contribution, trade exposure, risk-weight bucket — each drives a different capital treatment.
