# Liquidity Risk

Concept node for liquidity risk under SAMA's Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, defined as potential loss from inability to meet obligations or fund assets, and requiring banks to maintain and periodically review liquidity risk profiles and liquidity management policies as market conditions change. It is embedded in the framework's board/senior management oversight and general risk identification, measurement, mitigation, monitoring and reporting duties. Applies to banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking, including Islamic Windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
