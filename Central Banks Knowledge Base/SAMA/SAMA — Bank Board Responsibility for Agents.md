# Bank Board Responsibility for Agents

Sets out the accountability of the bank's board of directors for agent banking: approving comprehensive policies and procedures reflecting SAMA requirements, ensuring mechanisms exist for relevant departments to implement them, and ensuring agent monitoring is proportionate to the risk posed to the bank. Banks are fully liable for their Agents' actions and compliance and must maintain adequate systems for risk management, consumer protection, AML/CFT and combating embezzlement and fraud, including independent testing of the agent's AML/CFT programme, integration of agent transactions into core banking with instant customer confirmation, clear branding, agent disclosure on the bank's website, and protection of customer information (unauthorised disclosure being punishable under the Banking Control Law). It binds the board and senior management of each bank using agents.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, banking prudential
