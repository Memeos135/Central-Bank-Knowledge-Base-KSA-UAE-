# Emergency Evacuation Guide for Persons with Disabilities

A guide issued by the Authority of Persons with Disabilities on emergency evacuation planning for persons with disabilities, covering definitions (evacuation, emergency, safe areas, escape routes), disability categories, building hazards, self-preparedness, evacuation planning steps, and accessible alerting, communication and assistive listening, visual alarm, tactile warning surface and access-control arrangements. It is a safety/accessibility guidance document, not a SAMA financial regulation, but is relevant to premises and branch accessibility obligations of regulated entities. It is addressed to entities operating buildings serving employees and visitors.

**Regimes:** other
