# Definition of Sovereign Exposures

The IRB asset-class definition of sovereign exposures within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, used to categorise exposures for internal ratings-based capital calculation and for the related standardised-approach treatment of sovereigns. It sits alongside the parallel definitions of corporate, bank and retail exposures that drive asset-class segmentation. Binds banks subject to SAMA's credit risk capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
