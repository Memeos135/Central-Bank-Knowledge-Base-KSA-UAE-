# Fraud Risk Appetite

Defines the obligation to set a Fraud Risk Appetite stating the level of fraud risk the Member Organisation will tolerate, derived from the Fraud Risk Assessment and aligned with overall enterprise risk appetite. Requires measures, thresholds and limits addressing impact on both the institution and its customers, Board endorsement and at least annual review, updates on material business model change, and, where a customer-impacting limit is breached, escalation to Senior Management plus a CEO-involved crisis management process meeting at least weekly until resolved. Binds Member Organisations and their boards.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
