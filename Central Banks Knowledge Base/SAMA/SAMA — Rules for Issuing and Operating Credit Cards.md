# Rules for Issuing and Operating Credit Cards

The underlying SAMA Rules for Issuing and Operating Credit Cards (notified by earlier SAMA circular), which govern how issuers—banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom—may issue, re-issue and operate credit cards and charge fees. Substantive points reflected here include the need for a documented customer request, notice of acceptance/rejection with reasons and objection route, supplementary cardholder identification and verification, an 18 (Hijri) year minimum age, restrictions on annual fees before activation, a minimum statement grace period, and prohibitions on increasing the agreed term cost due to late payment or default. The FAQ node is guidance on these Rules; the Rules themselves are the binding reference.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, BNPL/finance
