# Credit Risk RWA Flow Statements

## Why this community

Periodic disclosure explaining movements in credit risk RWA under the IRB approach between reporting dates.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster forming part of the mandatory Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure set for IRB banks.
- Obligation chain: IRB approval and capital adequacy rules generate the RWA figures that this flow statement must reconcile period-on-period.
- Cross-reads with the other IRB credit-risk templates, which supply the opening and closing RWA balances whose drivers are explained here.
