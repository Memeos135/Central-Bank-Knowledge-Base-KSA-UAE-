# Unified SMS Notification Elements Circular

A follow-up circular to SAMA's earlier instruction on standardising the elements of SMS notifications sent to financial institutions' customers, intended to raise customer awareness of transactions on their accounts, memberships and e-wallets. It attaches the mandatory core notification elements for newly introduced mada transaction types and sets a compliance deadline of the end of Q1 2022, with an implementation plan due to SAMA by 4 November 2021. Binds financial institutions/payment service providers supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
