# Liquidity Risk Tolerance

Node on liquidity risk tolerance and Board governance duties under SAMA's liquidity risk rules for finance companies. It sets out that the Board is ultimately responsible for liquidity risk, must establish a risk tolerance aligned with business strategy, approve the liquidity risk strategy and policies (including the contingency funding plan and stress testing framework) at least annually, monitor the liquidity profile through regular and immediate material-change reporting, and ensure senior management identifies, measures, monitors, controls and reports liquidity risk. Tolerance must be reviewed at least annually and may be expressed through quantitative measures such as liquid asset holdings, maturity mismatches, funding concentration and contingent liquidity obligations.
