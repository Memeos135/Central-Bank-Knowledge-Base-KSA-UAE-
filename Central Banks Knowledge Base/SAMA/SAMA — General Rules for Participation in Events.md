# General Rules for Participation in Events

Second edition of SAMA's General Rules for Participation in Events (Arabic), issued by circular as an update to the earlier rules on holding and sponsoring events. It sets minimum conduct, governance and risk requirements for participation in conferences, forums, seminars and similar gatherings — inside or outside the Kingdom, directly or through third parties — including a board-approved participation policy, documentation and annual reporting to the board, careful selection and professional conduct of representatives, confidentiality, and event risk assessment. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA and notably extends scope to presence via "temporary booths" for institutional promotion, which are exempted from the SAMA no-objection requirement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
