# Permissible Activities (Article 19)

Article 19 lists the banking services a Bank may, after SAMA non-objection, delegate to an Agent — including account opening, loan application intake, account statements, account activation, domestic and international transfers, currency exchange, card and cheque issuance subject to Bank approval, cheque encashment, SME banking services, POS terminal applications, and sales and marketing. Services must be specified in the agent contract, displayed at the Agent's premises and published on the Bank's website with a full agent list; the Bank decides each Agent's permitted scope based on agent risk assessment. Related Article 18 requires pre-commencement and ongoing training of Agent employees covering KYC, AML procedures, customer information protection, complaints and fraud detection.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk
