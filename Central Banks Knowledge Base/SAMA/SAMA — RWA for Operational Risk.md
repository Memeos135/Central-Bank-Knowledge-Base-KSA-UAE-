# RWA for Operational Risk

This node covers the operational risk component of risk-weighted assets as reported in SAMA's capital and output floor templates, forming part of the total RWA against which the Pillar 1 minimum (normally 8%) is applied. Because operational risk under the finalised Basel framework is computed on a single standardised basis, it feeds identically into both the modelled and standardised legs of the floor comparison. It applies to banks within scope of SAMA's minimum risk-based capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
