# Standardised Approach

The default methodology all SAMA-supervised banks must use to calculate market risk capital, with risk-weighted assets derived by multiplying the capital requirement by 12.5. Use of any alternative (internal models approach or the simplified alternative) requires prior SAMA approval, and IMA-approved banks must still compute and report standardised approach figures across all trading desks. SAMA may also demand a standardised approach calculation at any time.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[SAMA — Sensitivities-Based Method|Sensitivities-Based Method]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
