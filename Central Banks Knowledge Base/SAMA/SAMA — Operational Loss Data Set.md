# Operational Loss Data Set

Part of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk, setting the criteria banks must follow to build and maintain the internal operational loss data set used in the standardized approach. It fixes definitions of gross loss, net loss and recoveries, mandatory inclusions (direct P&L charges, consequential costs, provisions, pending and timing losses) and exclusions (general maintenance, post-event business enhancements, insurance premiums), plus use of the date of accounting and allocation of related losses across years. It binds domestic banks on both a consolidated and standalone basis and makes exclusion of loss events subject to SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
