# Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities

Amended SAMA Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities, issued under the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. They define "finance support activities" (support, complement or enable finance activity, expressly including digital brokerage for finance entities but excluding debt collection for finance entities), and set licensing application requirements, minimum capital (SAR 2 million for digital brokerage), fit-and-proper/management conditions, conduct-of-activity obligations, supervision and compliance. They bind any person licensed by SAMA to carry on a finance support activity, notably licensed digital brokerage companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
