# National Address Postal Services Requirement

A SAMA circular (1447) requiring financial institutions under SAMA supervision to supply the customer's approved National Address (as registered with Saudi Post – SPL) to postal service providers when requesting delivery of documents relating to financial products and services. It follows a General Transport Authority decision obliging postal operators to refuse shipments not addressed by National Address from 1 January 2026, and confirms that the National Address is the primary and fundamental reference for identifying customer addresses. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA and applies alongside related SAMA instructions on residence and work address data.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
