# E-Wallet Security Controls

The onboarding and general security control set applied to e-wallet and similar fintech applications under SAMA supervision, covering one-application-per-phone-number/National ID registration, strong authentication via an independent trusted party, multi-factor authentication at login, OTP triggers for wallet-to-wallet, bill, marketplace, IBAN and international transfers, and out-of-band OTP for higher-value or high-risk transactions. It also addresses terms and conditions/data privacy consent, inactive account policy, fraud handling, SMS notification content, and board-approved internal policies. Binds Member Organizations providing e-wallets, lending, crowdfunding or other fintech models.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
