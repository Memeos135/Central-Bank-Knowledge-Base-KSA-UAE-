# IT Governance Framework

## Why this community

SAMA's IT Governance Framework: the mandatory domain structure and governance roles that supervised entities must implement to direct, resource and control technology, including change management and operational resilience linkages.

## How members connect

- Single parent instrument: the IT Governance Framework is the anchor, with each domain (governance and leadership, IT risk, IT operations, system change management) a referenced sub-obligation.
- Role allocation within the leadership domain: the IT Steering Committee, CIO and an independent internal IT audit function form the accountability chain.
- Cross-regime link: IT operations management refers out to the SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework, so continuity obligations are read together with IT governance rather than in isolation.
- Overlapping labels (domain vs. topic nodes) reflect the same requirement expressed at framework and detail level.
