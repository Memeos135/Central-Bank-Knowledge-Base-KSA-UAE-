# Operational Risk Capital

## Why this community

Pillar 1 minimum capital for operational risk under SAMA's adoption of the Basel III post-crisis reforms, i.e. how a bank must compute its operational risk capital charge and report it.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: SAMA's operational risk capital rules implement the Basel III post-crisis reform standard, then cascade into the prescribed calculation method and reporting template.
- Obligation chain within the calculation: Business Indicator feeds the Business Indicator Component, which combines with the Internal Loss Multiplier to produce Operational Risk Capital.
- Scope of Application fixes which entities and consolidation levels the Standardised Approach binds; the Simplified Alternative is a carve-out/derogation from that default method.
- The Q17 template is the supervisory reporting consequence of the same defined inputs, so definitions must be read consistently across calculation and reporting.
