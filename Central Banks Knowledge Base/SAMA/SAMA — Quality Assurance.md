# Quality Assurance

Requires Member Organizations to establish an approved, communicated quality assurance process that independently verifies that changes or developments to information assets meet business/user and applicable regulatory requirements before release to production. It prescribes clear QA roles, minimum quality requirements, and handling of quality records, and requires the QA function to be independent in existence and reporting so it can give objective evaluation and report results to stakeholders with improvement recommendations.
