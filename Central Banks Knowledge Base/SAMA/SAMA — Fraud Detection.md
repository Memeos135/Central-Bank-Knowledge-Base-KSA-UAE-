# Fraud Detection

Node covering the fraud detection limb of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, sitting within the wider counter-fraud governance and control requirements and supported by the framework's defined terms (Fraud, Fraud case, External Fraud, Fraud Landscape, Electronic Money, Employee). It requires Member Organizations to maintain systems and controls capable of identifying fraud as part of a risk-based Counter-Fraud Programme overseen by the Board and the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, with performance tracked through indicators. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations, including payment service providers and electronic money institutions within scope.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, AML/CTF
