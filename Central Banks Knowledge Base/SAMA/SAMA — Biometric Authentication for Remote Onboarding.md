# Biometric Authentication for Remote Onboarding

A SAMA circular mandating biometric authentication when a customer relationship is initiated or established remotely, supplementing existing requirements to identify and verify an individual customer's identity using documents, data or information from a reliable and independent source. Supervised institutions must adopt the biometric verification feature at a verification assurance level of high or above and complete integration with the approved service provider by the stated deadline, notifying SAMA and raising any challenges beforehand. It binds banks (local banks, foreign bank branches, digital banks), finance companies and payment companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
