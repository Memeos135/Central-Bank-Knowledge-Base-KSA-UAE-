# IRB Credit Risk Requirements

## Why this community

The qualifying conditions a bank must satisfy before SAMA will permit use of the IRB approach — rating system design and operation, governance, data, parameter estimation, collateral recognition, validation and Pillar 3 disclosure.

## How members connect

- Everything is subordinate to a single gateway obligation: the minimum requirements that condition supervisory permission to use internal estimates for regulatory capital.
- Obligation chain from definitions outward — the default and economic loss definitions anchor PD, LGD and EAD estimation, which in turn depend on data maintenance and validation duties.
- Governance layer (board and senior management oversight, an independent credit risk control unit, internal and external audit, the use test) is the control counterpart to the quantification requirements.
- Credit risk mitigation members (real estate, receivables, other physical collateral, leasing and residual value) qualify only where the recognition conditions are met, otherwise supervisory LGD/EAD values apply; SAMA's Basel III guidance circular sits above these as the local implementing instrument.
