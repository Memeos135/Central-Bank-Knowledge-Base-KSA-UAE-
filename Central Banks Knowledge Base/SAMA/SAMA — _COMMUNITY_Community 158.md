# Stress Testing Governance

## Why this community

Allocation of accountability for the stress testing programme between the board and senior management — who owns approval, oversight, and execution.

## How members connect

- Both members derive from the same governance provisions of the stress testing rules and split a single obligation set: board-level approval and oversight versus management-level design, execution and escalation.
- They form a hierarchy: senior management's duties are discharged under, and reported up to, the board, so a gap at one level is a compliance failure at the other.
- The pairing is the governance entry point into the wider stress testing cluster (community 20) — programme adequacy is assessed against these responsibilities.
