# Indirect Investment Fund Participation

## Why this community

Treatment of finance companies' or financiers' indirect participation in investment funds used to fund or hold finance exposures, and whether such indirect holdings fall within finance-activity and exposure limits.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA circular clarifying the regulatory characterisation of indirect fund participation.
- Connects conceptually to finance companies' investment/exposure limits and to look-through treatment of fund holdings.
- Circular-level guidance interpreting the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulation.
