# Stand-alone CVA Capital (SCVA)

This node addresses the CVA risk capital requirement, including the treatment of eligible and ineligible internal and external CVA hedges and their interaction with the market risk framework. It sets out the scope of covered transactions (derivatives other than those with a QCCP, and fair-valued SFTs where CVA loss exposure is material), the standalone CVA portfolio concept, and the choice between BA-CVA (default) and SA-CVA (requires SAMA approval). It binds all SAMA-supervised banks with covered transactions in either the banking or trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
