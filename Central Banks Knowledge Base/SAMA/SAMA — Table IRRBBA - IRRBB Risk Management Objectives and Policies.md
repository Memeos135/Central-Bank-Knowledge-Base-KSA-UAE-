# Table IRRBBA - IRRBB Risk Management Objectives and Policies

A qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework requiring banks to describe their objectives, policies, governance, measurement and mitigation approach for interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), including shock and stress scenarios, modelling assumptions and how IRRBB is defined for control purposes. It is the qualitative companion to Template IRRBB1 and binds all banks within the IRRBB scope of application, with annual, flexible-format disclosure. Banks are directed to use their own internal measurement system, referencing the SAMA IRRBB circular and the Basel supervisory review process standard.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
