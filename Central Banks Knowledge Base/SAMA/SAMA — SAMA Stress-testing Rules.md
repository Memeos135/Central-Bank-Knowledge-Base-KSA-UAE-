# SAMA Stress-testing Rules

The stress-testing element of SAMA's ILAAP Guidelines, including the annexure on stress-testing and the Contingency Funding Plan. It requires banks to run regular institution-specific, market-wide and combined scenarios to identify sources of liquidity strain, confirm exposures remain within the board-set liquidity risk tolerance, and assess impacts on cash flows, liquidity position, profitability and solvency; it also calls for a defined stress-testing programme across normative and economic perspectives, reverse stress-testing on a proportionate basis, at least annual review, and internal review/independent validation of the ILAAP. Binding on SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
