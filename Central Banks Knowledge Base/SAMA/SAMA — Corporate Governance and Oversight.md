# Corporate Governance and Oversight

Despite the label, the retrieved content concerns the standardised approach to credit risk: the mandatory due diligence banks must perform on external ratings (with the requirement to notch up a risk-weight bucket where due diligence shows higher risk, and the prohibition on notching down), and the definition and risk weighting of exposure classes including covered bonds, securities firms and other financial institutions, and corporates (general, specialised lending, MSMEs, unrated exposures). It binds banks calculating minimum credit risk capital requirements, including their overseas operations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
