# Cyber Security Risk Management and Compliance

This node sets out the cyber security risk management and compliance domain: a defined, approved and implemented risk management process covering identification, analysis, response, and monitoring/review, aligned with the organisation's enterprise risk management process and aimed at confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets. It also requires periodic review and audit of compliance with cyber security controls. It binds Member Organizations, with maturity assessed against the framework's maturity model.
