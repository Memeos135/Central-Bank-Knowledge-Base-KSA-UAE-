# Judicial Authorities Response

## Why this community

The duty of supervised financial institutions to respond promptly and completely to requests, orders and enquiries from judicial and investigative authorities. It is a cooperation/conduct obligation that cuts across banking secrecy, account freezing and law-enforcement support.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a circular-level instruction that operationalises statutory cooperation duties owed to courts, prosecution and enforcement bodies.
- Interacts with the AML/CTF and sanctions regimes, where similar duties to act on official orders (freezing, information production) arise from a different legal base.
- Constrains the confidentiality/bank secrecy regime — lawful judicial requests are a recognised gateway, so the two must be read together.
- Timeliness is the enforceable element: delay itself is the breach, independent of the substantive answer given.
