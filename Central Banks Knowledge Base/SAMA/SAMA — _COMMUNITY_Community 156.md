# Debt Restructuring Coordination

## Why this community

Multi-creditor workout mechanics: how banks hold off enforcement and coordinate while a restructuring is negotiated.

## How members connect

- The standstill agreement supplies the contractual moratorium within which the coordinating bank operates.
- The multi-bank coordinator is the conduit for information sharing and creditor decision-making during the standstill period.
- Both concepts sit upstream of the restructuring documentation cluster and shape when a modified exposure crystallises.
