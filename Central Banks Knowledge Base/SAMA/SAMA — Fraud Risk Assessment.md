# Fraud Risk Assessment

Node on the enterprise-wide Fraud Risk Assessment: identifying fraud risks affecting the organization and its customers, calibrating controls to inherent risk by product and service, testing control effectiveness and determining residual risk. It must be refreshed periodically and on material change in the internal or external fraud risk environment (new products, digital platforms, acquisitions, newly identified threats), and links to Fraud Risk Appetite and KRI monitoring. Binds SAMA Member Organizations and underpins the risk-based scaling of counter-fraud controls.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
