# Real Estate Financing Product Instructions

SAMA instructions setting the minimum requirements financiers must meet when offering retail (individual) real estate finance products, reissued to amend paragraph 3 so that the financing offer may be delivered electronically at the customer's choice; they supersede the earlier instructions. Requirements include affordability/credit-capacity assessment before any offer, a documented plain-language explanation of terms and risks, an offer valid at least fifteen working days containing the finance contract, the disclosure form and (for variable-rate products) a credit-risk acknowledgment, plus a mandatory session with an independent qualified credit advisor. It binds banks and real estate finance companies supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
