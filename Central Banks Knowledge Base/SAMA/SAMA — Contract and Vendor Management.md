# Contract and Vendor Management

A third-party cyber security control requiring Member Organizations to define, approve, implement and monitor cyber security requirements inside their contract and vendor management processes, so that requirements are settled before contract signature and compliance is monitored across the contract life-cycle. It covers when the cyber security function must be involved (e.g. due diligence), baseline security requirements applicable in all cases, and rights to periodic cyber security review. It binds Member Organizations dealing with third parties, defined broadly to include service providers, outsourcing and cloud providers, vendors, suppliers and government agencies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
