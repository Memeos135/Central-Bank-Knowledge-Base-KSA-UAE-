# Loss Component (LC)

The Loss Component (LC) equals fifteen times average annual operational risk losses over the preceding ten years and is the loss-sensitive input to the Internal Loss Multiplier. Its calculation rests on loss data quality standards, net-of-recovery loss amounts, loss event thresholds (SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000) and any SAMA-approved exclusions such as divestitures, all of which feed prescribed annual disclosure rows. It binds SAMA-supervised banks computing standardised operational risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
