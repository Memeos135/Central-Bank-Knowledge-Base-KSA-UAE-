# Business Indicator (BI)

The Business Indicator (BI) is the financial-statement-based volume proxy that drives the standardised operational risk capital charge, built from the interest, lease and dividend component, the services component and the financial component, each averaged over three years. Its subcomponents and any exclusion of divested activities (which requires SAMA approval) must be disclosed annually in the fixed Pillar 3 template with supporting narrative. It binds banks supervised by SAMA that compute operational risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
