# LCR and NSFR Liquidity Requirements

Sets the prudential treatment of PSIAs: banks must apply SAMA's regulatory capital requirements to risk-weight assets funded by PSIAs and correctly reflect PSIA funds in the Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Net Stable Funding Ratio. PSIA funds and PER/IRR reserves cannot count toward regulatory capital, concentration risk must be managed under the Large Exposures Rules, and outsourced management of IAH funds must comply with SAMA outsourcing rules. SAMA retains discretion to direct whether a given mudarabah, musharakah or wakalah arrangement is treated as a PSIA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
