# Crowdfunding Pooled Accounts

## Why this community

Segregation and handling of participant money in debt-based crowdfunding, where platform operators hold subscription and repayment funds that are not their own assets.

## How members connect

- The circular sets the operating condition for pooled client-money accounts, and the finance-value management account is the defined vehicle through which those balances are held and reconciled.
- Linkage is definitional and operational: the account construct gives effect to the circular's segregation/no-commingling obligation owed to beneficiaries and lenders.
- Both sit downstream of the crowdfunding licensing regime, so scope depends on being a licensed debt-based crowdfunding company.
