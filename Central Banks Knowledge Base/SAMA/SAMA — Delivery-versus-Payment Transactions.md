# Delivery-versus-Payment Transactions

This node covers the capital treatment of unsettled transactions and failed trades, distinguishing delivery-versus-payment (DvP) settlement — where exposure is limited to the positive current exposure and capital is required once payment is five business days past settlement date — from non-DvP free deliveries, where the full amount paid or delivered is at risk. It also touches maturity-floor exemptions for short-term collateralized and self-liquidating trade transactions. It binds banks with unsettled securities, commodities and FX transactions, requiring monitoring of failed trades from the first day of failure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
