# Liquidity & Counterparty Risk

## Why this community

Bank risk management for liquidity and counterparty credit exposures, including exposure to highly leveraged institutions (HLIs). Covers how banks must measure, limit, collateralise, stress-test and monitor exposures, drawing on Basel Committee sound-practice standards.

## How members connect

- SAMA circulars and guidance adopt or cite Basel Committee sound-practice papers, so the Basel text supplies the substantive standard while the SAMA instrument supplies the enforceable expectation for KSA-licensed banks.
- The HLI sound-practices document acts as the hub, referencing the operational building blocks: exposure measurement, limit setting, collateral and early termination terms, stress testing and ongoing monitoring.
- Exposure measurement (including potential future exposure) is the input to limit setting — limits are meaningless without the measurement convention, so read them together.
- The internal control guidelines supply the governance and independent-oversight layer sitting above these technical risk measures.
- Liquidity risk and counterparty credit risk are linked through collateral and early-termination provisions, which can convert a counterparty event into a funding event.
