# Trade Exposures to CCPs

Concerns trade exposures arising from transactions cleared through a CCP — current and potential future exposure and variation margin — excluding initial margin and default fund contributions, and their EAD/RWA treatment and disclosure. The CCR8 disclosure template requires banks to break these down by QCCP versus non-QCCP and by product type (OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, SFTs, and cross-product netting sets). Mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks, reported semi-annually with narrative explanation of material changes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
