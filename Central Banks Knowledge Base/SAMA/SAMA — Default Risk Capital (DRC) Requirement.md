# Default Risk Capital (DRC) Requirement

The Default Risk Capital (DRC) requirement is the standardised market risk component covering jump-to-default losses on trading book exposures, sitting alongside the sensitivities-based method and the residual risk add-on and reported in Pillar 3 RWA and capital disclosure templates. This node is anchored in SAMA's disclosure/capital-requirement instruments, where DRC amounts feed minimum capital and RWA reporting. It binds banks computing and disclosing Pillar 1 market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
