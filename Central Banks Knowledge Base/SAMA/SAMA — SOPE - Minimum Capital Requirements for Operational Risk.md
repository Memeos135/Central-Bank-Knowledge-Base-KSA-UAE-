# SOPE - Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk

Node for SAMA's operational risk minimum capital requirements (SOPE) within the Basel-aligned capital and Pillar 3 disclosure framework. It is the operational risk limb of the Pillar 1 capital calculation feeding RWA totals, capital ratios and the disclosure templates on capital adequacy and distribution constraints. Applies to banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
