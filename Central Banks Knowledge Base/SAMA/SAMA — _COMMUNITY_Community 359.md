# Data Protection Law Amendment

## Why this community

Amendments to the KSA Personal Data Protection Law, setting the statutory baseline for lawful processing, data subject rights and controller obligations that financial institutions must observe alongside SAMA-specific data, credit information and outsourcing rules.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a primary instrument (Royal Decree-level law amendment) that sits at the top of the hierarchy and is referenced by, rather than derived from, SAMA circulars and guidance.
- Interacts cross-regime with credit information, AML/CTF record-keeping and consumer protection duties, where processing grounds and retention periods must be reconciled.
- Defined terms (personal data, controller, processor, sensitive data) govern how downstream SAMA obligations on customer data and disclosure are read.
