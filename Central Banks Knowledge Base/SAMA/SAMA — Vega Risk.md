# Vega Risk

Vega risk is the optionality-related component of the sensitivities-based method, capturing sensitivity to implied volatility; only instruments whose cash flows cannot be written as a linear function of notional (i.e. instruments with optionality) attract vega and curvature charges. It is aggregated with delta and curvature to form the sensitivities-based capital requirement under SAMA's standardised market risk rules. Applies to all banks calculating market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
