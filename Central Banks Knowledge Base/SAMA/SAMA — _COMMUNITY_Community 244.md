# Transition Phase-in Arrangements

## Why this community

Timing rules that phase new prudential requirements in over a defined period, governing what applies to a firm at a given date and how legacy positions are treated during the run-in.

## How members connect

- Single node that functions as a temporal scope limit across otherwise separate capital and liquidity requirements.
- Legal effect is to modify, not remove, the underlying obligation - compliance must be measured against the schedule applicable on the relevant reporting date.
- Typically set out in the parent framework and refined by subsequent circulars, so hierarchy and effective dates must be read together.
