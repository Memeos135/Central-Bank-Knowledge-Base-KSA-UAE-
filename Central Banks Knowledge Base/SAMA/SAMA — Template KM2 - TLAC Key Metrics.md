# Template KM2 - TLAC Key Metrics

Fixed-format quarterly Pillar 3 template disclosing total loss-absorbing capacity (TLAC) available, TLAC ratios against RWA and leverage exposure, and related buffer requirements, reported at resolution group level for single point of entry and multiple point of entry structures. It is mandatory only for resolution groups of global systemically important banks and takes effect from the TLAC conformance date; SAMA's framework states TLAC disclosures need not be completed unless SAMA specifies otherwise. Requires a narrative explaining material period-on-period changes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
