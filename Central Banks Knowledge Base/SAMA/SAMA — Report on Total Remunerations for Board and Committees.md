# Report on Total Remunerations for Board and Committees

An annex disclosure template for reporting total remuneration paid to board of directors and board committee members, broken down by member name, membership type (executive, non-executive, independent), fixed amounts, attendance allowances, in-kind benefits, profit share, granted shares, chairman/managing director/secretary remuneration, and other items, all in thousands of riyals. It is a reporting format supporting governance and remuneration transparency obligations rather than a substantive rule.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
