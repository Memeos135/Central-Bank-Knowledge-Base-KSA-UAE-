# Template CR6 - IRB Credit Risk Exposures by PD Range

Template CR6 is the IRB disclosure presenting credit risk exposures broken down by portfolio and PD range, including key model parameters and resulting RWA. It sits with the IRB-related Pillar 3 disclosures, which banks complete only where SAMA has approved use of the internal ratings-based approach (F-IRB, A-IRB or supervisory slotting). The accompanying instructions distinguish modelled from standardised portfolios and exclude securitisation and counterparty credit risk positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
