# Pooled Accounts for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Circular

A SAMA circular (Arabic) to debt-based crowdfunding entities operating in the Kingdom on pooled accounts used to manage finance amounts, issued under the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law and read with the Rules for Practising Debt-Based Crowdfunding. It requires that pooled accounts be used solely for managing finance value and segregated from the entity's own funds, be named "Finance Value Management Account – [entity name]", and that SAMA be notified on opening or closing such accounts with banks in the Kingdom. It also mandates a written policy on account operation (board-approved authority matrix, multi-eyes control, reporting to senior management, no encumbrance of the account by the entity's obligations), documented reconciliation against executed financing transactions, and bank account agreements reflecting the regulatory requirements and allocating responsibilities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
