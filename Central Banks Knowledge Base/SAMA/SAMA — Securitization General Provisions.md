# Securitization: General Provisions

General provisions of the securitisation chapter of SAMA's capital framework: it defines securitisation and resecuritisation exposures, the underlying pool, and roles such as originator, and sets scope, risk-transference recognition, due diligence, RWA calculation, caps and credit risk mitigation treatment. It binds banks calculating risk-based capital for exposures to traditional and synthetic securitisations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
