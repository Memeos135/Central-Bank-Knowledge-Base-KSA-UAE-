# Capital Treatment of Unsettled Transactions and Failed Trades

This node covers the capital treatment of unsettled securities, commodities and foreign exchange transactions and failed trades, requiring recognition of exposure from trade date irrespective of booking or accounting treatment, with escalating capital requirements after five business days. It excludes instruments in the counterparty credit risk/CVA framework and permits SAMA to waive requirements during system-wide settlement failures. It binds banks calculating credit RWA and their related Pillar 3 settlement risk disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
