# IMM Stress Testing Program

Sets the minimum content and frequency of a bank's counterparty credit risk stress testing programme: complete trade capture and counterparty-level aggregation, at least monthly single-factor exposure stress tests, at least quarterly multifactor and non-directional stress tests, joint stressing of exposure and counterparty credit quality, reverse stress testing, and senior management ownership with results measured against risk appetite. Binds banks measuring counterparty credit risk, particularly those using internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
