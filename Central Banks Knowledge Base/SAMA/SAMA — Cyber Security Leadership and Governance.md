# Cyber Security Leadership and Governance

The cyber security leadership and governance domain of SAMA's cyber requirements, setting board- and management-level obligations: a resourced governance structure, approved and communicated cyber security policies, procedures and supporting standards, periodic review against the evolving threat landscape, embedding cyber and fraud risk considerations in the business operating model, password controls, and documented, periodically reviewed IT and cyber risk assessments held in a central register. It binds the entities in scope of the relevant SAMA cyber instrument and is directed at senior/executive management, information asset owners and heads of cyber security.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
