# Operational Risk Capital (ORC)

Operational Risk Capital (ORC) is the Pillar 1 minimum capital requirement for operational risk under the standardised approach, derived from the Business Indicator Component multiplied by the Internal Loss Multiplier and converted into RWA for the overall capital ratio. It sits alongside credit, counterparty credit, market and other Pillar 1 charges in the RWA overview and feeds ratio-based consequences such as capital distribution constraints. It binds SAMA-supervised banks for calculation, reporting and Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
