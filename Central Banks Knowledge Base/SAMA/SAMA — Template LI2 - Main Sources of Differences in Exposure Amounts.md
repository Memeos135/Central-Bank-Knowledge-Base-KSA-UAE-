# Template LI2 - Main Sources of Differences in Exposure Amounts

Pillar 3 disclosure template LI2 under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, requiring banks to explain the main drivers of difference between accounting carrying values (under the regulatory scope of consolidation) and the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes, broken down by credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation and market risk frameworks. Differences arising purely from scope of consolidation belong in Template LI1; LI2 covers off-balance sheet amounts, valuation and netting differences, provisions and prudential filters. Mandatory for all banks, annual, flexible format, with accompanying narrative in Table LIA. Context also carries Table CRB-A on prudential treatment of problem assets (non-performing and forborne exposure definitions).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
