# Ministry of Interior Computer Number

An appendix-level concept within SAMA's bank account rules explaining the ten-digit Ministry of Interior computer number and how its leading digit signals identity type (1 for Saudi nationals, whose number is the national ID and also the identifier of a sole establishment owned by them; 2 for resident foreigners, matching the Iqama number). The surrounding appendices also list government entities and public-sector legal entities, and show where a ministry-issued computer number (e.g. from the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah) must appear in account-opening documentation. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom when identifying and verifying customers and authorised signatories at account opening, reactivation and ongoing maintenance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, other
