# Electronic Payments for Charities

A SAMA circular (No. 45040399, dated 25/06/1445H) permitting charities and non-profit associations to be provided with all SAMA-authorised electronic payment solutions, including GPRS mobile POS terminals, conditional on the entity obtaining approval from the National Center for Non-Profit Sector Development. It relaxes an earlier SAMA instruction that limited such entities to fixed-line POS terminals, and should be read with the circular on upgrading POS devices to 4G. It binds banks and payment service companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** payments, other
