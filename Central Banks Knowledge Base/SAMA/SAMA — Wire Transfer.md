# Wire Transfer

Section 14 of SAMA's AML/CTF rules governing cross-border and domestic wire transfers in any currency (including serial and cover payments) sent, received or processed in Saudi Arabia, including card, prepaid and mobile-initiated transfers. It sets mandatory originator and beneficiary information, a customer declaration on the legitimacy of the transfer, the domestic-transfer exemption with a three-working-day information-provision duty, and policies for handling incoming transfers with missing information (execute, reject, suspend, restrict or terminate). It also imposes currency restrictions on inbound transfers destined for beneficiaries at foreign institutions and confidentiality limits on information shared with correspondent or intermediary institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
