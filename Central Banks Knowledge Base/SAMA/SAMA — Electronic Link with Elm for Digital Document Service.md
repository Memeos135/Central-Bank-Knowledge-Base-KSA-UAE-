# Electronic Link with Elm for Digital Document Service

SAMA circular directing supervised financial institutions to establish technical/electronic linkage with Elm in order to use the Ministry of Interior's "Digital Document" service, which allows an electronic copy of the digital ID to be taken and the digital identity, its validity date and an inquiry reference number to be verified (via QR reader or entry of the National ID/Iqama number). It supplements an earlier SAMA circular on accepting the digital identity for existing customers and on finding a mechanism to capture an ID copy electronically where a copy is required. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA, which must coordinate directly with Elm, notify SAMA of implementation difficulties, comply with applicable laws and instructions, and preserve the customer's freedom to choose between presenting the physical or the digital ID.
