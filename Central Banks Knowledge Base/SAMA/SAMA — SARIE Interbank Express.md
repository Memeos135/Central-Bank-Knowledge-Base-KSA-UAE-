# SARIE Interbank Express

Defines SARIE (Saudi Arabian Riyal Interbank Express, or any future instant payments system) within the Repo Guidelines and anchors the settlement rules requiring cash legs of SAR repo transactions between licensed agents to settle through SARIE, with securities legs through Tadawul or SAMA depending on the issuer. Where a counterparty is not a licensed agent or the currency is not SAR, other mutually agreed systems may be used. Relevant to banks and other repo market participants settling repos in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, other
