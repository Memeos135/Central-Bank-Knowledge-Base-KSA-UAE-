# Guarantees and Protection Providers

This node addresses eligibility and capital treatment of guarantees and credit derivatives as credit protection, including the exclusion of nth-to-default derivatives, conditions for total return swaps, substitution of the protection provider's risk weight for the protected portion, and the 1250% treatment of materiality thresholds. It also covers recognition of guarantees and tranched/pro rata credit protection in the securitization framework, including the exclusion of SPEs as eligible guarantors. It binds banks computing regulatory capital under SAMA's credit risk rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** Where reliance is placed on a guarantee or credit derivative, the CRM framework routes the assessment to the guarantee provisions, which govern eligibility of the protection provider, the operational conditions for recognition, and substitution of the protection provider's risk weight for the obligor's. This is an internal obligation chain within the same standardised-approach instrument, so the general CRM conditions and the guarantee-specific conditions apply cumulatively. The consequence is that unrecognised or non-conforming protection leaves the original obligor's risk weight in place, regardless of commercial expectations.
