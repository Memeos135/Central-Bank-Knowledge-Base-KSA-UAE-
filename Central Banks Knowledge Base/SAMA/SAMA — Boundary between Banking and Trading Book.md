# Boundary between Banking and Trading Book

Concept node on the regulatory boundary between the banking book and the trading book under SAMA's market risk capital standard, as applied through the internal risk transfer rules. It determines which leg of a hedge is capitalised under banking book rules versus market risk rules, including the asymmetric treatment that transfers from trading book to banking book get no capital recognition. Relevant to banks calculating regulatory capital and to desk-level allocation of exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
