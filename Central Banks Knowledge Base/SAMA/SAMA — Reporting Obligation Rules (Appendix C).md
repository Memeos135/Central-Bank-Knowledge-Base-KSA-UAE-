# Reporting Obligation Rules (Appendix C)

The operative reporting rules for OTC derivative contracts, covering the manner, timing, correction and record-keeping of submissions to the SAMA-authorised trade repository (TR). Key obligations: banks must report directly (no agents or outsourcing to third-party service providers), must sign a reporting service agreement containing counterparty-consent wording, must complete system linkage and user testing before acceptance, must report all template fields, must apply a life-cycle approach to business events (including re-reporting novated trades as early termination with a Linked UTI), and must report by 23:59:59 on T+1 (excluding Fridays, Saturdays and KSA public holidays). It binds reporting banks; limited relief exists where a trade is cancelled or fully terminated within the T+1 window, except where cancellation is for central clearing.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
