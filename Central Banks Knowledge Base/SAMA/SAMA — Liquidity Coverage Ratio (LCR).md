# Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)

SAMA's amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio package (guidance notes, prudential return, FAQs and national discretions) implementing the Basel III LCR standard, cross-referenced paragraph-by-paragraph to the BCBS January 2013 text. It requires banks to hold unencumbered high-quality liquid assets at least equal to total net cash outflows over a 30-day stress horizon, with a 100% minimum absent financial stress, and permits temporary drawdown below 100% in stress subject to SAMA assessment. It binds Saudi banks for LCR reporting and monitoring, alongside continued NSFR reporting under SAMA's 2012 circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
