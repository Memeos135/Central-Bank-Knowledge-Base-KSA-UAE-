# Template CCR3 - Standardised Approach CCR Exposures by Portfolio and Risk Weights

Template CCR3 requires banks using the standardised approach for credit risk to disclose counterparty credit risk exposures (post-CRM, post-CCF) broken down by regulatory portfolio and risk weight band. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 framework whose CCR exposures are risk-weighted under the standardised approach, and is the standardised counterpart to the IRB template CCR4.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
