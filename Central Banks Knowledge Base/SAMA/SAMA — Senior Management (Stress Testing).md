# Senior Management (Stress Testing)

Specifies senior management's obligations under SAMA's Stress Testing Rules: designing, developing and implementing the stress testing framework, establishing organisational structure, resources, processes and supporting infrastructure, overseeing scenario definition and methodology selection, ensuring clear compilation and communication of results to the board, committees, business areas and SAMA, preparing action plans, and periodically reassessing the framework with board approval for changes. Read together with the board's oversight role in the same section.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
