# Template CR2 - Changes in Stock of Defaulted Loans

Pillar 3 disclosure template CR2 within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, requiring banks to present a flow reconciliation of defaulted loans and debt securities from opening to closing stock (new defaults, returns to non-default status, write-offs, other changes). It fixes the reporting definitions, notably that defaulted exposures are reported net of write-offs and gross of allowances, and for standardised-approach banks correspond to exposures past due more than 90 days. Mandatory for all banks, semiannual, fixed format, with narrative explanation of significant movements and whether off-balance sheet items are included.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
