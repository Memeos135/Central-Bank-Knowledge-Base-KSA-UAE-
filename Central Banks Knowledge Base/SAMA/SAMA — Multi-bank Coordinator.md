# Multi-bank Coordinator

Guidance on handling borrowers with exposures to more than one bank: the coordinator role should be taken by the bank with the largest exposure, with duties covering setting goals, deadline compliance, transparent communication with all creditors, minuting creditor meetings, assessing the need for external financial or legal advisers, drafting the proposed restructuring solution, and possibly acting as agent post-restructuring (including the right to withdraw). It also covers use of external advisers, borrower counselling/mediation, and keeping guarantors informed and re-confirming guarantees when loan terms change. Directed at banks; negotiated out-of-court restructuring is stated as preferable to court proceedings.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
