# Capital Market Law

Concept node for the Capital Market Law and the Capital Market Authority regime as cross-referenced in SAMA instruments — for example, securities and securities-related trading activities that fall in AML/CTF scope, and, in the finance lease context shown here, issuance of securities against a lessor's rights under CMA rules while SAMA disclosure and supervisory obligations continue to apply. Relevant mainly for regulatory-perimeter and dual-supervision questions between SAMA and the CMA.

**Regimes:** other, BNPL/finance, AML/CTF

### [[SAMA — Real Estate Finance Law|Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat this as a reminder that a real estate finance transaction can have two separate legal touchpoints: perfection of the security/ownership position at the real estate registration bodies, and capital-market consequences where the resulting contractual rights are packaged or traded. The Real Estate Finance Law obliges the finance entity to verify the title deed and confirm the absence of in rem rights before contracting, which is the factual precondition for any later assignment or securitisation governed by capital-market rules. Note that the excerpt attached to the Capital Market Law node concerns registration of ownership arising from finance lease contracts rather than securities regulation, so the link may be indirect; verify the primary texts before relying on it to allocate responsibility between SAMA-regulated finance activity and CMA-regulated market activity.
