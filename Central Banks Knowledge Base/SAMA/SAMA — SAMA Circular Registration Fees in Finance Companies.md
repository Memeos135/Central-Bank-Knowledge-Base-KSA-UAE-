# SAMA Circular: Registration Fees in Finance Companies

An Arabic-language SAMA circular (from the Deputy Governor for Supervision) confirming that registration fees charged by the contracts registration company fall within the fees, commissions and administrative service costs capped by Article 83 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. It confirms such fees cannot be passed to the customer outside that cap (the lower of 1% of the finance amount or SAR 5,000), reinforcing an earlier circular that the cap covers all unavoidable charges beyond the finance amount and term cost. It binds banks and finance companies operating in KSA (and is copied to the Saudi Finance Lease Contract Registration Company).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
