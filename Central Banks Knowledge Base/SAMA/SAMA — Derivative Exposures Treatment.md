# Derivative Exposures Treatment

This node sets out the treatment of derivative exposures in the Leverage Ratio exposure measure, comprising replacement cost and potential future exposure, with special rules for eligible bilateral netting and for written credit derivatives (including when a purchased credit derivative on the same reference name may offset, subject to same-or-more-conservative material terms). It also covers conditions under which cash pooling arrangements may be treated on a combined rather than account-by-account basis. It binds SAMA-supervised banks computing the leverage ratio.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
