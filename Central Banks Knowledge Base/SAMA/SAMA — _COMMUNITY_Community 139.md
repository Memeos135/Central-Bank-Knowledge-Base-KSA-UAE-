# QR Code Payment Regulations

## Why this community

Regulation of QR-code-based payment acceptance in KSA — standardising how payment service providers and merchants deploy QR instruments, and anchoring SAMA's instruction in its statutory mandate over the payments system.

## How members connect

- Vertical instrument hierarchy: the royal decree supplies the legislative basis, the Council of Ministers resolution operationalises the approved arrangement, and the SAMA circular imposes the operative supervisory requirement on licensed entities.
- The circular's binding force derives by cross-reference to the higher instruments; scope and addressees of the circular should be read against the authority those instruments confer.
- Cross-regime touchpoint: QR acceptance rules sit in the payments/PSP regime but pull in merchant onboarding (KYB) and consumer-protection disclosure duties at the point of acceptance.
