# Security Surveillance Camera System Compliance

SAMA circular reaffirming that supervised financial institutions must apply the Law on the Use of Security Surveillance Cameras (Royal Decree M/9), its Implementing Regulation issued by the Ministry of Interior, and the accompanying document of security conditions and requirements for installing surveillance systems and the general technical specifications for camera equipment. It notes that financial institutions are classified among high-sensitivity sites, so camera installation must follow the site-sensitivity classification, and also requires continued compliance with SAMA's Security and Safety Guide for the financial sector. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
