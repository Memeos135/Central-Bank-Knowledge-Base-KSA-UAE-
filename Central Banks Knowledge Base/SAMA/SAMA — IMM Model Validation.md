# IMM Model Validation

Sets the ongoing validation expectations for EPE models and related risk measures used under the Internal Models Method, including backtesting against realised exposures, independent benchmarking of pricing models, assessment of parameter update frequency, validation out to horizons matching trade maturity, and coverage of all relevant counterparties. Also addresses SAMA's power to require correction or additional capital where model performance is deficient, and prior SAMA approval for using a more conservative measure in place of alpha times Effective EPE. Binds banks with IMM approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
