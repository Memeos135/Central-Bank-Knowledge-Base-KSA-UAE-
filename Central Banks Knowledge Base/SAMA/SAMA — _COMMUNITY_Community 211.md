# Finance Product Digital Certification

## Why this community

Certification/approval of finance products in digital form, addressing how finance companies evidence that a product and its documentation have been cleared for offering to customers.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an instruction directed at licensed finance entities, operating as a compliance condition on product offering rather than a substantive product rule.
- Interacts with finance-sector consumer protection and disclosure obligations, since certification status affects what may lawfully be marketed.
