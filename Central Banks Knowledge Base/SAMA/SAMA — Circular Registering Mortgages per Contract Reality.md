# Circular: Registering Mortgages per Contract Reality

SAMA circular requiring banks and finance companies to document real estate mortgages according to the true nature of the contract and to stop procedures that transfer ownership of the property instead of registering a mortgage over it. It also requires rectification of properties currently registered in the name of the bank or finance company within a stated period, customer awareness of that process, and reporting to SAMA of cases where notaries refuse to register a mortgage in the financier's favour. Issued under the SAMA Law, Banking Control Law, Finance Companies Control Law and the Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law; effective immediately.
