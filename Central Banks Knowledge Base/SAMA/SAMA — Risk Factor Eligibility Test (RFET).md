# Risk Factor Eligibility Test (RFET)

Defines the Risk Factor Eligibility Test, the quarterly quantitative gate (real price observation counts and gap conditions) determining whether a market risk factor may be modelled in the expected shortfall model or must be treated as non-modellable. It also sets bucketing, parametric-curve and systematic-versus-idiosyncratic allocation rules, plus qualitative modellability principles that SAMA may apply to reject data. Binds banks using the IMA for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
