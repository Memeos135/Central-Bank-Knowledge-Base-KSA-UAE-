# SCRE - Credit Risk Capital Requirements

Cross-references to SAMA's credit risk capital standard (SCRE) as used in the Pillar 3 overview of RWA: which exposures fall under the standardised approach, foundation and advanced IRB, and supervisory slotting, and which are carved out to counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity, settlement, securitisation and threshold-deduction rows. Also covers Template CDC on capital ratio levels triggering capital distribution constraints (risk-based buffers, G-SIB surcharge, countercyclical buffer, Pillar 2 and leverage buffer). Binds banks calculating credit risk RWA and making Pillar 3 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
