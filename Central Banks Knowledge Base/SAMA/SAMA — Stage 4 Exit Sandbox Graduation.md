# Stage 4: Exit Sandbox Graduation

Describes the final Sandbox stage, exit or 'graduation', following successful completion of live testing. It is the transition point from temporary Sandbox permission towards application for a full SAMA licence under the applicable regulatory framework. Relevant to FinTechs concluding testing and to assessing whether an entity's permissions are transitional or permanent.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
