# NSFR Guidance Document (Basel III)

SAMA's guidance document implementing the Basel III Net Stable Funding Ratio in Saudi Arabia, setting the standard, its calibration, scope and implementation timeline. It covers frequency of calculation and reporting (at least quarterly, within Basel capital-standard reporting lags), consolidated application to internationally active banks with discretion to extend to other banks and entity subsets, and detailed treatment of items such as derivative assets, netting and cash variation margin. It binds banks supervised by SAMA and operates alongside the Sound Principles for liquidity risk management at legal entity, branch, subsidiary and group level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
