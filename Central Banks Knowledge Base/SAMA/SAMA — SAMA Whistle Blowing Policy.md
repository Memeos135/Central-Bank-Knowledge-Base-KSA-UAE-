# SAMA Whistle Blowing Policy

This node covers the whistle-blowing obligation within SAMA's counter-fraud rules for Member Organisations, which must build and maintain multi-channel reporting processes allowing employees and third parties to raise suspected fraud without retaliation, aligned to SAMA's Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions. It sits alongside internal-fraud controls (employee access audit trails, staff activity logging, monitoring of unusual behaviour, oversight of payments to staff accounts) and the Counter-Fraud Policy requirements on ownership, board intent, three-lines-of-defence responsibilities and breach escalation. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organisations, including their branches and majority-owned subsidiaries.
