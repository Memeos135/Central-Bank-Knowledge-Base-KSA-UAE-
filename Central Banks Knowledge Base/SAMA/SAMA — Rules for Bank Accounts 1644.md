# Rules for Bank Accounts 1644

The SAMA Rules for Bank Accounts, the consolidated account-opening and account-operation framework for banks licensed in KSA. It covers account types and opening requirements for natural and juristic persons and specific entity categories (e.g. government bodies, courts, societies, correspondent banks, non-resident financial institutions), inoperative account lifecycle (dormant, unclaimed, abandoned), internal audit and SAMA reporting, and disclosure of and enforcement on bank balances, accounts and relationships. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and interacts closely with AML/CTF customer due diligence obligations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info
