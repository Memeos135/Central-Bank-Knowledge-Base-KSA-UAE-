# Basel III Capital Standards

SAMA circular to all banks applying the Basel III countercyclical capital buffer (CCyB) in Saudi Arabia, addressed to senior bank management. It sets out how the bank-specific buffer is calculated as a weighted average of jurisdictional buffer rates based on the ultimate-risk geographic location of private-sector credit exposures (excluding interbank and public-sector exposures, including non-bank financial sector), with a 2.5% default where no rate is published and SAMA's discretion to impose more prudent rates. It also fixes the 1 January 2016 start date and SAMA's pre-announcement practice (increases effective after one year; decreases immediate).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
