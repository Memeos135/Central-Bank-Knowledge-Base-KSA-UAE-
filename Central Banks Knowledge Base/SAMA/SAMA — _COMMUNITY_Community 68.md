# CVA & Market Risk Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of market risk and credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk capital, and their consolidation into the bank's overall risk-weighted assets picture.

## How members connect

- All templates feed into the RWA overview template, which acts as the reconciliation hub: figures disclosed in the market risk and CVA templates must tie back to the aggregate RWA statement.
- The CVA templates form a tiered set reflecting the permitted approaches (reduced basic, full basic, standardised) plus an RWA flow statement, so which template applies is determined by the approach the bank is authorised to use.
- Market risk disclosures split by approach: standardised versus internal models, with the internal models template cross-referring to both the standardised template and the modelled-versus-standardised comparison, supporting supervisory output-floor and benchmarking scrutiny.
- Membership is governed by the same underlying capital adequacy framework definitions (trading book boundary, covered positions, eligible hedges), so a change in classification propagates across multiple templates.
