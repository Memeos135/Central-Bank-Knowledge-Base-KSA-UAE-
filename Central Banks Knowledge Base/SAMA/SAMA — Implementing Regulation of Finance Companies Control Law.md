# Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law

SAMA circular and the amended Implementing (Executive) Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law (Rajab 1447H / December 2025), issued under the Law promulgated by Royal Decree M/51. It sets the definitional and supervisory framework for the finance sector — defining finance activities, finance contract, instalment, term cost, total cost of financing, finance amount, annual percentage rate, board and senior management, exposure and large exposure, and influential shareholding — and confirms SAMA's mandate to regulate and supervise finance companies. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom, SAMA-licensed digital brokerage companies, and licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
