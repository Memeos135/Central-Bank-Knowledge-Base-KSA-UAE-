# Cash Entry/Exit via King Fahd Causeway (SAMA 5513)

A SAMA Currency Department circular setting the agreed mechanism with Customs for the inbound and outbound movement of Saudi and foreign banknotes through King Fahd Causeway customs. It covers electronic financial declaration forms, sealing and faxing of consignment details to SAMA's Dammam branch, deposit matching and counting, mandatory reporting of discrepancies (declared vs delivered amounts, condition, non-arrival, delay) and of counterfeit notes, and conditions for GCC cash-in-transit vehicles entering Saudi territory (armoured specification compliance and a licensed armed escort). Binds all banks and exchange companies operating in the Kingdom, with SAMA's Dammam branch and Customs as counterparts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other, governance/risk
