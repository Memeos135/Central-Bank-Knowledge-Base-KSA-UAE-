# Cyber Security Operations and Technology

This node covers the operational and technical control domain of SAMA's cyber security requirements, sitting alongside leadership/governance and resilience domains, and drawing on defined terms such as cyber security governance, cyber security incident, cyber security policy and cyber security risk. It sets expectations for governance structures, approved and periodically reviewed policies, standards and procedures, embedding cyber and fraud risk considerations in business operating models, password/access controls, and documented IT and cyber risk assessments held in a central register. It binds entities subject to the Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements (notably newly established/early-stage and sandbox-type firms) and, via the parallel framework, Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
