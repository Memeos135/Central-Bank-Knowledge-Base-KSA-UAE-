# Template OR1: Historical Losses

A fixed-format annual Pillar 3 template requiring disclosure of aggregate operational risk losses net of recoveries over the past ten years, including excluded losses, at loss-event thresholds of SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000, plus details of whether losses feed the internal loss multiplier. It is mandatory for banks in the second and third business indicator buckets and for first-bucket banks with SAMA approval to use internal loss data, with narrative commentary expected on new loss exclusions. Binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating operational risk capital; retrospective disclosure of prior periods is required unless SAMA permits fewer years transitionally.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
