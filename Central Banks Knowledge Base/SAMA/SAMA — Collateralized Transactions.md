# Collateralized Transactions

Covers the capital treatment of collateralised and repo-style transactions — OTC derivatives, margin lending, repos/reverse repos and securities lending/borrowing — including exemptions from the one-year maturity floor for short-term, daily-remargined and self-liquidating trade exposures, and the trading-book rules on eligible collateral and counterparty credit risk charges. It also introduces minimum haircut floors for non-centrally cleared securities financing transactions. Applies to banks under SAMA's credit risk and counterparty credit risk frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
