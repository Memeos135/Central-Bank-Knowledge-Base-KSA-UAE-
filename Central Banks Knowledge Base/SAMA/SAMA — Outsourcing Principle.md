# Outsourcing Principle

Principle 9 of the consumer protection framework, addressing outsourcing of consumer-facing services. The financial institution must ensure outsourced service providers comply with the applicable Principles and Rules and act in consumers' interests, and it remains responsible for consumer protection even where the provider fails to comply. Relevant to third-party arrangements such as collection, servicing, distribution and call-centre functions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
