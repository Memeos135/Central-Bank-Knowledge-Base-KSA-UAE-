# Securitization Framework

## Why this community

The Basel-aligned securitisation capital framework as adopted by SAMA: how banks recognise risk transference, classify exposures, choose a capital treatment, and meet due diligence, STC and disclosure obligations.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy of instruments: the general provisions and defined terms (traditional/synthetic securitisation, SPE, resecuritisation, sponsor, ABCP conduit, clean-up call) feed the operational recognition-of-risk-transference tests, which gate any capital relief.
- Obligation chain on calculation: due diligence requirements are a precondition to using the approach hierarchy (SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA, SEC-IAA, SEC-SA), with shared inputs (KIRB, attachment/detachment points, supervisory parameter p) and caps/floors.
- External rating dependency: SEC-ERBA, inferred ratings and the IAA all rely on ECAI eligibility and mapping, linking this cluster to the standardized credit risk cluster.
- Special regimes branch off the core: STC term and short-term (ABCP) criteria give preferential treatment, while non-performing loan securitisations and dilution risk carry bespoke rules; Pillar 3 templates SEC1 and SEC3 close the loop as disclosure obligations.
