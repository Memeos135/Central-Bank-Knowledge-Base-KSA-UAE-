# Regulatory Sandbox FAQ

A bilingual FAQ document supporting applicants to SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox, explaining its purpose, benefits, eligibility and the nature of exemptions granted. It clarifies that the Sandbox is reserved for new business models or concepts for which no regulatory framework or licensing path already exists, that testing occurs live within pre-defined limits (customer numbers, transaction volume and value), and that exemptions are assessed case by case per the published Sandbox Framework. It is guidance for prospective applicants rather than a binding rulebook.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
