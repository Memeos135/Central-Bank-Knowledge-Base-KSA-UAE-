# Net Stable Funding Ratio

## Why this community

Standardised public disclosure of the structural funding metric: available stable funding against required stable funding over a one-year horizon.

## How members connect

- Quantitative Pillar 3 template implementing the NSFR minimum standard; disclosure obligation layered on top of the prudential ratio requirement.
- Uses the same balance-sheet categorisation and weighting definitions (ASF/RSF factors, residual maturity buckets) as the NSFR rules.
- Paired with the LCR template and explained by the qualitative liquidity risk management table.
