# SAMA Circular Compliance

## Why this community

The baseline duty of supervised entities to track, acknowledge and comply with SAMA circulars as binding instruments, and the official channel through which those circulars are issued.

## How members connect

- A compliance obligation paired with its authoritative source-of-truth channel: the portal fixes what counts as validly issued guidance for compliance purposes.
- Cross-cuts every other regime, since circulars amend or supplement regulations across AML, payments, finance companies and consumer protection.
