# Fit & Proper Assessment (Article 13)

Article 13 requires the Bank to run a fit and proper assessment on the prospective Agent, its proprietors and the individuals managing the agent banking business, testing competence, integrity, adequacy of management structures and sources of funding. It is the substantive standard referenced by the Article 9 eligibility gate and feeds the Bank's declaration to SAMA. The obligation sits with the contracting Bank; SAMA vets and approves Agents and connected individuals.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF
