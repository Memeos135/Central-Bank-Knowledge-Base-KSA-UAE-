# Circular: Compliance with Cash Transport Regulations

SAMA Banking Supervision circular reminding all money exchange companies and establishments of their obligation to comply with the rules on physical cash movement/transport and the security safety guide, referencing earlier circulars and the Law on Transport of Money and Precious Metals. It warns that transporting cash unlawfully, or receiving cash unlawfully transported from other exchangers, exposes the party to statutory penalties under the Banking Control Law. It binds licensed money exchange companies and establishments.
