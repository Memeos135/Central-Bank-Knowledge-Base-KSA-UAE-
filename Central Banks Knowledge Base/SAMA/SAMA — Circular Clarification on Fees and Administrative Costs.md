# Circular: Clarification on Fees and Administrative Costs

SAMA circular clarifying the fee cap in Article 83 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 9 of the Consumer Finance Regulations (the lower of 1% of the finance amount or SAR 5,000). It confirms that the cap covers every amount the beneficiary must pay other than the finance amount and the term cost — i.e. all unavoidable fees such as application study, property valuation, property inspection and registration fees — and that the financier alone must deal with and pay third parties (valuers, registration bodies) rather than instructing the customer to do so; all such fees must be reflected in the APR. Binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from the circular date.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
