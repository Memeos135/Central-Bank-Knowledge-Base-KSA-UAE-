# Cyber Security Leadership and Governance Domain

This node is the leadership and governance domain of SAMA's cyber security framework, setting principles, objectives and control considerations for cyber security policy content and for the allocation of cyber security roles and responsibilities across a Member Organization. It places ultimate accountability on the Board of Directors (including budget allocation and approval of key cyber security documents) and requires a policy addressing scope, information classification and ownership, risk assessment, awareness, regulatory and contractual compliance, breach reporting and business continuity. Compliance is assessed against the framework's six-level cyber security maturity model, with formal definition, approval and demonstrable implementation required from level 3.
