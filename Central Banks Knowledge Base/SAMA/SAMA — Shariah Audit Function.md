# Shariah Audit Function

The Shariah audit element within SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies, forming part of the Shariah supervision/control architecture alongside the Shariah Committee and compliance duties. It concerns independent review and assurance that the finance company's financing activities and products conform to Shariah rulings and Committee decisions, and reporting of findings within the governance chain. It binds licensed finance companies within the instructions' scope.
