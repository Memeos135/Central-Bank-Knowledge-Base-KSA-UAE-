# Qualifying CCP (QCCP)

Sets out the qualifying CCP (QCCP) concept: a CCP licensed and supervised under rules consistent with the PFMI, with SAMA able to determine QCCP status where the home regulator does not apply the Principles, and subject to the default fund calculation conditions being met. QCCP status determines the preferential capital treatment available to clearing member banks and their clients, but does not remove the bank's Pillar 2 responsibility.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
