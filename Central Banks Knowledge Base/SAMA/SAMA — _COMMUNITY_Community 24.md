# Options & Simplified Market Risk

## Why this community

Market risk capital measurement under the simplified/standardised regime: how a bank computes charges for interest rate, equity, FX and commodity exposures, including the specific treatments available for option positions.

## How members connect

- A single scope gateway (the Simplified Standardised Approach) determines eligibility and then routes the bank into the four risk-class charges, which explains the hub-and-spoke reference pattern.
- Shared defined terms — general market risk, delta, maturity ladder — carry across risk classes, so the ladder and delta concepts are reused rather than redefined for each exposure type.
- The three option methods form a tiered obligation chain by complexity of the book: simplified for purchased-option-only profiles, delta-plus for written positions, scenario for more complex portfolios; choosing one constrains the rest of the calculation.
- Hierarchy runs from the framework's scope provisions down to risk-class calculation rules and then to method-level annex detail; the SSA-prefixed members are the scoped-down variants of the general risk-class rules.
