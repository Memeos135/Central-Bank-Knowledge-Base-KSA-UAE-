# CCR Exposure Analysis Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of counterparty credit risk (CCR) at the entry point: the summary view of CCR exposures broken down by the measurement approach used (e.g. standardised vs internal models). It frames how a bank must publicly evidence the basis on which its derivative and SFT exposures are measured.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the CCR1 template is the gateway disclosure from which the more granular CCR templates inherit their exposure measure and scope.
- Obligation chain runs from the capital adequacy/Pillar 3 disclosure framework (law/regulation level) down to the prescribed template format banks must complete and publish.
- Defined terms (exposure at default, replacement cost, potential future exposure, approach used) are set at this level and carry into the portfolio-level templates.
