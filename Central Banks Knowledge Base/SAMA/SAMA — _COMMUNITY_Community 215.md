# Financial Statement Publication

## Why this community

Disclosure and transparency obligations requiring finance companies to publish financial statements, supporting supervisory oversight and market/consumer visibility of licensee financial condition.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster addressed to finance companies as a licensee class under the finance companies regime.
- The obligation chain runs from the finance law and implementing regulation's accounting and audit requirements to the specific publication duty (what, when, and in what form).
- Interacts with governance and supervisory reporting duties: published statements are the public-facing element of a wider prudential reporting stack.
