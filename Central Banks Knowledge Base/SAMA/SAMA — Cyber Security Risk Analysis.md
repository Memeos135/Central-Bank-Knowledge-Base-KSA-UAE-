# Cyber Security Risk Analysis

Sub-node addressing cyber security risk analysis — determining the likelihood of identified cyber risks materialising and their resulting impact — as the second stage of the framework's risk cycle. It feeds the risk response step, where treatment options and risk acceptance limits are assessed against risk appetite. Applies to Member Organizations and is scored against maturity levels.
