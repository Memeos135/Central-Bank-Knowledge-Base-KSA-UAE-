# BNPL & Responsible Lending

## Why this community

Consumer credit affordability and conduct across BNPL and finance-lease activity: who may be licensed, how credit capacity must be assessed before granting, and what disclosure, advertising and consumer-protection duties attach.

## How members connect

- The BNPL rules act as the hub instrument and expressly pull in responsible lending principles, debt burden ratio caps, advertising controls and their own licensing, activity and consumer-protection chapters — a rulebook-to-annex hierarchy.
- Shared affordability chain: responsible lending principles set the assessment duty, debt burden ratios set the quantitative ceiling, and BNPL activity provisions apply both to deferred-payment exposures.
- Finance Lease Law anchors the leasing side; the total-economic-loss verification instruction is a downstream operational rule interpreting lessee/insurer outcomes under that law.
- Definitional caution: the BNPL regime's 'Consumer' is narrower than the AML/CTF 'Customer', so CDD, merchant-as-customer and KYB duties are not satisfied by BNPL consumer-protection compliance alone.
