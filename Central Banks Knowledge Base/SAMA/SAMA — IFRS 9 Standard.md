# IFRS 9 Standard

Part of SAMA's Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning, setting out IFRS 9-based definitions and staging used for credit risk classification: probability of default, exposure at default, loss given default, financial asset, expected credit loss (12-month and lifetime), and Stage 1/2/3 exposures. It adds regulatory-only sub-categories (Stage 2A special monitoring and 2B substandard; Stage 3A doubtful and 3B loss) for reporting purposes, maps them to earlier SAMA provisioning categories, and defines past due and default (including the 90 days past due trigger). Binds SAMA-supervised credit-granting institutions for classification, provisioning and regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
