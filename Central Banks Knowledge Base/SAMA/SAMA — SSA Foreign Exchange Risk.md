# SSA Foreign Exchange Risk

Sets out the foreign exchange risk element of the simplified standardised approach for market risk, within the structure where total capital is the arithmetic sum of interest rate, equity, FX and commodity risk charges plus non-delta option risks, converted to RWA by multiplying by 12.5. It also covers internal risk transfers involving FX and commodities risk in the banking book and their capital recognition. Applies to banks using the simplified standardised approach for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
