# Wage Protection and Mudad Platform Circular

A circular responding to the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development's concerns about integration failures and technical outages between the Mudad platform's payroll management system and partner financial institutions, which delay wage payments and employers' compliance with the Wage Protection programme. SAMA stresses adherence to the service level agreement with Mudad and due care in resolving technical issues. Directed at payment companies and financial institutions integrated with Mudad.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
