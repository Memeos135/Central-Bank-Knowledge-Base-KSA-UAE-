# SSA Interest Rate Risk

Part of SAMA's market risk framework covering general interest rate risk under the simplified standardised approach, together with the defined vocabulary used across the market risk rules (risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, risk class, sensitivity, delta, look-through, embedded derivative). It also carries the rules on internal risk transfers of interest rate risk between the banking book and a SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk, and when such transfers get capital recognition. Binds banks calculating minimum market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
