# Electronic Payment for Retail Sector

SAMA circular requiring banks and payment service providers to be operationally ready to receive and process requests from retail-sector merchants for electronic payment acceptance, following the national decision mandating all retail activities to offer electronic payment from the stated date. Obligations cover readiness to open merchant accounts and e-wallets, to install SAMA-authorised payment means (mada POS, e-wallets) through all customer channels, accurate assignment of Merchant Category Codes, and internal communication to branches and customer service. It binds banks, PSPs and Saudi Payments.
