# CCP Hypothetical Capital (KCCP)

This node covers the hypothetical capital requirement of a central counterparty (KCCP) used as the first step in determining a clearing member bank's risk-sensitive capital charge for its default fund contributions to a qualifying CCP. It sits within SAMA's counterparty credit risk framework and explains that KCCP is a notional, consistently computed figure for capitalizing default fund contributions only, not the CCP's own regulatory capital. It binds SAMA-supervised banks acting as clearing members, and also frames Pillar 2 expectations to hold capital beyond the minimum where CCP dealings are riskier or QCCP status is unclear.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
