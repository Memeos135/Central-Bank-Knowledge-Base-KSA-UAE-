# Counter-Fraud Governance Committee (CFGC)

Node defining the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, a senior-management-owned governance structure responsible for oversight and control of all aspects of the organisational Counter-Fraud Programme. The framework requires each Member Organization to establish and maintain a CFGC meeting at least quarterly, with minimum responsibility for approving, supporting, communicating and monitoring the Counter-Fraud Programme, Counter-Fraud Policy and management information, sitting beneath ultimate Board responsibility. Relevant when assessing governance adequacy and accountability allocation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
