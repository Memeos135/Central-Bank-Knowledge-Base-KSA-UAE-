# Template MR2 - IMA Capital Requirements

A Pillar 3 market risk template covering capital requirements determined under the internal models approach (IMA), sitting within SAMA's suite of quantitative disclosure templates for banks. The surrounding framework material addresses related capital-composition and distribution-constraint disclosures (e.g. CET1 triggers, buffers, Tier 2 deductions), indicating the template's placement in the capital adequacy disclosure chain. Relevant to banks with SAMA approval to use internal models for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
