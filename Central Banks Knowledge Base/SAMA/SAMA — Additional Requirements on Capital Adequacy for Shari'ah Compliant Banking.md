# Additional Requirements on Capital Adequacy for Shari'ah Compliant Banking

SAMA rules issued under the SAMA Charter (M/36) and the Banking Control Law imposing additional capital adequacy requirements for Shari'ah compliant banking, to be read alongside SAMA's Basel framework (circular 44047144). They set out how Shari'ah compliant contracts (e.g. Murabahah with binding and non-binding purchase orders) are mapped to credit risk and market risk treatment across the stages of the contract. They bind all domestic banks licensed by SAMA that conduct Shari'ah compliant banking, including their foreign subsidiaries and branches conducting such business.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
