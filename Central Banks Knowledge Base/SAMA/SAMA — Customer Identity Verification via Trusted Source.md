# Customer Identity Verification via Trusted Source

SAMA circular strengthening KYC by requiring financial institutions to verify that the mobile number linked to a customer account or membership belongs to the same identity holder, matched against the national trusted source (the "Tahaqaq" verification service). Addressed to banks, finance companies, payment service providers and category (A) exchange centres, it requires assessment of existing non-matching accounts, submission of results and a remediation plan to SAMA, adoption of the verified mobile number in onboarding for new accounts/memberships (in-person and remote), implementation within 45 days, and continued compliance with the Rules for Bank Account Opening.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
