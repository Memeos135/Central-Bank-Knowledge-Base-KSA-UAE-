# Treatment of Overdrafts

A node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk dealing with how overdraft facilities are treated for capital purposes (exposure recognition and risk weighting). The material retrieved around it sits in the standardised-approach chapters, covering use of external ratings for bank exposures (short-term rating interaction with the preferential treatment for exposures of up to three months, the bar on unsolicited ratings and on using a group entity's rating for affiliates) and offsetting of matched positions. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating regulatory credit risk capital; open the source to confirm the specific overdraft provisions.
