# SAMA General Guidance concerning Amended LCR

SAMA guidance and prudential return package for the Amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio, aligned to the January 2013 BCBS LCR standard and cross-referenced to that document's paragraph numbering, with general and specific guidance, FAQs and national discretions. It governs HQLA composition, diversification and concentration limits, the 30-calendar-day grace period for assets that become ineligible, and reporting mechanics (monthly submission due 30 days after month end, with transitional relief for the first submission). Binds Saudi banks; foreign bank branches are excluded from the monthly return requirement.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
