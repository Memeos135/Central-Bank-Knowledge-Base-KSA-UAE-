# Cyber Threat Intelligence

## Why this community

Cyber threat intelligence expectations for supervised financial entities: what intelligence capability must exist, at which levels (strategic, operational, technical/tactical), and how it feeds broader cyber resilience and threat-led testing.

## How members connect

- A central principles document anchors the cluster; the core and level-specific CTI items are subordinate elaborations that reference it.
- Cross-framework linkage: the CTI principles and the ethical red teaming framework both reference the Cyber Security Framework, forming a framework-to-framework obligation chain (governance baseline, intelligence capability, threat-led testing).
- Methodology references (kill chain, adversary technique taxonomies) are incorporated as analytical standards for operational CTI rather than as independent obligations.
- Practical consequence: intelligence outputs are expected to be usable inputs to red teaming scope and to board-level risk reporting.
