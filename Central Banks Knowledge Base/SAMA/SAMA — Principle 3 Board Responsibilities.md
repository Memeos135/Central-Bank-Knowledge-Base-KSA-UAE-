# Principle 3: Board Responsibilities

Principle 3 of SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, setting out the responsibilities of the board of directors of financial institutions supervised by SAMA, read together with Principle 4 on executive management and Principle 5 on board committees. Used to identify where accountability for strategy, internal control, risk and compliance oversight sits at board level. Binds boards of supervised institutions other than insurance and reinsurance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
