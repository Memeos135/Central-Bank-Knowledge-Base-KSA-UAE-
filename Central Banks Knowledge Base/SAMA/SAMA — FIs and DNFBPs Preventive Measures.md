# FIs and DNFBPs Preventive Measures

This node covers the AML Law's core preventive obligations on financial institutions and DNFBPs: risk-based customer due diligence (with enhanced measures for higher risk), PEP identification and additional measures for domestic, foreign and international-organisation PEPs, cross-border correspondent banking safeguards and the shell-bank prohibition, and wire transfer originator/beneficiary information and recordkeeping requirements (including refusing transfers where information cannot be obtained). It also touches supervisory authority powers to inspect, compel information, assess sector risk and issue instructions. It binds all FIs and DNFBPs subject to the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[SAMA — Due Diligence Measures|Due Diligence Measures]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Before allowing a financial institution to rely on a third party's CDD, check that the reliance sits within the statutory CDD duty, not outside it. The Guide's third-party reliance provision operates against the AML Law obligation on financial institutions and DNFBPs to apply customer due diligence, with the Implementing Regulation specifying when and what measures apply. The consequence is that reliance shifts execution but not accountability: the relying institution remains answerable for the adequacy of the measures and for obtaining the underlying information, and the permitted class of third parties is limited to other FIs and DNFBP owners.
