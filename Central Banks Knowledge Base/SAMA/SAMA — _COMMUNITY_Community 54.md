# Outsourcing for Finance Companies

## Why this community

Governance of outsourcing arrangements by finance companies, principally the boundary between functions that may be delegated and those requiring heightened control and SAMA visibility.

## How members connect

- The Rules are the single governing instrument; the other members are its defined terms and required artefacts.
- 'Material Functions' is the classification trigger — it determines the intensity of the approval, oversight and contractual obligations that attach to an arrangement.
- 'External Service Provider' fixes the counterparty scope, including whether intra-group and cross-border providers fall in.
- A board-approved Outsourcing Policy is the standing compliance obligation that must exist before individual arrangements are entered into.
