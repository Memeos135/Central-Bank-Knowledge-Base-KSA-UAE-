# Standardized Approach: Use of External Rating

A component of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk dealing with the use of external (or inferred) credit ratings to set risk weights, including the securitization external-ratings-based approach (SEC-ERBA) where risk weights turn on rating grade, seniority, tranche maturity and, for non-senior tranches, tranche thickness. It sets operational criteria that must be met before external or inferred ratings may be relied upon for capital purposes. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Basel-aligned credit risk capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
