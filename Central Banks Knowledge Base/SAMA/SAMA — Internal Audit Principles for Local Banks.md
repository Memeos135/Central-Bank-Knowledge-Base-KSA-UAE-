# Internal Audit Principles for Local Banks

First edition of SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for local banks, issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law and effective from the stated implementation date. It sets out the board's, audit committee's and senior management's duties toward internal audit, and the independence, objectivity, professional competence, ethics, charter/policy, organisation, reporting lines, risk-based audit planning, quality assurance and documentation requirements for the internal audit unit as third line of defence, including SAMA no-objection for appointing or changing the head of the unit and coverage of subsidiaries. It binds locally licensed banks operating in the Kingdom and their governance bodies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
