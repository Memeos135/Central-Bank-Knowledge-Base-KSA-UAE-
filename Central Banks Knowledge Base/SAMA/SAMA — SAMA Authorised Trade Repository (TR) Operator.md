# SAMA Authorised Trade Repository (TR) Operator

Describes the role of the SAMA-authorised Trade Repository Operator as the mandatory destination for OTC derivative trade reports and the contractual/operational conditions attaching to it. Reporting banks must report directly (no agents or outsourcing to third-party providers), sign a reporting service agreement containing consent for counterparties to report their trade data, complete electronic system linkages and user testing, and use the Trade Repository's prescribed templates. Binds all banks in KSA with reportable OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, data/credit info
