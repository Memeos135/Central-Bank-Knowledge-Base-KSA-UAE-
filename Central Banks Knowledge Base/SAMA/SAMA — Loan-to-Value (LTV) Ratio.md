# Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

A concept node on the loan-to-value ratio as one of the two screening criteria (alongside financial/cash-flow ratios) in the initial viability assessment of non-performing loans. LTV indicates the quantitative level of collateral coverage against the outstanding loan and is drawn from information already in the loan file, supporting early selection among workout strategies. Relevant to banks' Workout Units and credit risk functions under the problem loans guidelines.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
