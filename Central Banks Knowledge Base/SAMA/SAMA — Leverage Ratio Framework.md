# Leverage Ratio Framework

A framework-level node on the leverage ratio as a non-risk-based capital constraint, including the leverage ratio buffer and its role as a trigger for capital distribution constraints, and its mandatory appearance in Pillar 3 key metrics and reconciliation templates. It covers disclosure frequency, format and narrative expectations as well as the ratio's interaction with risk-based buffers. It binds all banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy and Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
