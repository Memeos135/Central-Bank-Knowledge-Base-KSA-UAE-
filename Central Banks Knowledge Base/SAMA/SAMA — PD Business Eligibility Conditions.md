# PD Business Eligibility Conditions

The eligibility criteria and operating conditions for banks wishing to act as Primary Dealers (Bank-PDs) in Saudi Government dated securities, requiring the ICAAP-set minimum capital adequacy ratio, adequate trading and risk management capability, infrastructure and skilled staff, and SAMA's no-objection. It also imposes ongoing obligations: a board-approved investment policy covering PD activity, prudential exposure limits, held-for-trading valuation treatment, liquidity ratio counting, and reporting of undertaking-agreement violations. It binds banks licensed in the Kingdom that undertake or seek to undertake PD business.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
