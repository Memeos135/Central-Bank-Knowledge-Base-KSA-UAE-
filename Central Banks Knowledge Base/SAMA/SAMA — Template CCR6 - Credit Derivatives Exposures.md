# Template CCR6 - Credit Derivatives Exposures

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to report credit derivative exposures (protection bought and sold) as part of the counterparty credit risk disclosure section of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. It is one of the CCR-series templates (CCRA, CCR1, CCR3–CCR8) covering banking and trading book exposures subject to a CCR capital charge. Applies to banks supervised by SAMA under the Pillar 3 regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
