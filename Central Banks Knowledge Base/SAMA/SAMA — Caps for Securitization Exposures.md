# Caps for Securitization Exposures

Concept node for the caps that limit capital requirements on securitisation exposures, including the maximum look-through style cap by reference to the capital that would apply to the underlying pool and the cap available to originators and sponsors. It operates as an overlay on whichever approach in the hierarchy a bank applies, and interacts with definitions of securitisation/resecuritisation exposure and originator status. It binds banks calculating risk-based capital for securitisation positions, with originator-specific conditions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
