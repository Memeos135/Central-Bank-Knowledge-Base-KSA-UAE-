# Consumer Finance Debt Buyout Instructions Between Finance Companies

SAMA instructions (Nov 2021, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law) regulating the buyout of individual consumer finance debts between finance companies. They set obligations on the purchasing company (consumer finance licence required; no separate SAMA approval per transaction; disclosure of new financing terms and costs with documented customer consent; a standard buyout application form; settlement of the existing debt through SARIE with prescribed payment data; no transfer fees beyond early-settlement or administrative fees; no release of residual funds to the customer before a clearance letter and required security are obtained) and on the selling company (issuing liability statements and debt-proof letters, facilitating transfer, and notifying the customer, each within one business day). Binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

- **What this link tells you:** For a debt buyout between finance companies, do not read the buyout instructions as a self-contained regime: the underlying exposure remains a Consumer Financing product and the Regulations for Consumer Financing continue to govern it. The buyout instructions operate as the specific rules for the transfer mechanics, while the general regulation supplies the defined terms and the creditor's standing duties on disclosure, pricing, code of conduct for sales and collections, and consumer treatment. Practically, the acquiring creditor inherits full consumer-protection obligations from the transfer date and must satisfy both instruments; where the buyout text is silent, default to the general regulation and verify the express cross-reference in the primary text.
