# Material Outsourcing

Concept node for "Material" outsourcing under SAMA's outsourcing rules — the classification that triggers heightened obligations: board-approved policy coverage, documented business case and risk-impact assessment, separate contingency plans, regular internal audit reporting to the Board or Audit Committee, possible external expert assessment at SAMA's request, enhanced register content, and immediate notification to SAMA on termination with transition arrangements. Binds banks and their boards, which must record the materiality assessment and its date for each arrangement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
