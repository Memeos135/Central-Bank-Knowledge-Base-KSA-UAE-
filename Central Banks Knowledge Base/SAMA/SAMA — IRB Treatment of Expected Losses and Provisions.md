# IRB: Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions

This node covers the IRB chapter on expected losses and provisions, explaining that risk-weight functions capitalise unexpected loss while expected loss is addressed separately through comparison with eligible provisions. It links the calculation of expected losses, the calculation of provisions, and the capital consequences of any shortfall or excess. It binds banks approved by SAMA to use the IRB approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
