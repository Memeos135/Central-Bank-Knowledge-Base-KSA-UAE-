# IRB Specialised Lending Slotting

## Why this community

Disclosure of specialised lending exposures risk-weighted under the IRB supervisory slotting criteria rather than a bank's own parameter estimates.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster anchored in the IRB sub-class of specialised lending and its prescribed supervisory categories.
- Applies where a bank cannot meet the requirements for own estimates of IRB risk parameters, so the slotting approach substitutes supervisory risk weights.
- Scope is defined by the capital adequacy framework's exposure-class taxonomy, which determines which facilities fall into this template versus the general corporate IRB templates.
