# Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB)

A SAMA circular to all banks implementing the Basel III countercyclical capital buffer in Saudi Arabia, effective for calculations from 1 January 2016. It sets the 0%–2.5% range, reciprocity based on published jurisdictional buffer rates, a default 2.5% where no rate is published, the exposure scope (private sector credit exposures on an ultimate-risk basis, excluding interbank and public sector but including non-bank financial sector), and the weighted-average calculation. SAMA pre-announces the KSA rate at least one year ahead, with increases effective after one year and decreases effective immediately. Binds all banks in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
