# Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers

SAMA's Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers sets the operating framework for bank-owned remittance centers and licensed remittance companies, covering definitions (Centers, Bank, Remittance Membership, SAFIU, FATF), working hours and days, general compliance provisions, disclosure and transparency, complaints handling, in-branch services, and customer acceptance/KYC/CDD expectations. It binds banks operating remittance centers and licensed remittance companies in the Kingdom, and cross-references the Rules Governing the Opening of Bank Accounts, the Banking Consumer Protection Principles and SAMA's AML/CFT circulars.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection, governance/risk
