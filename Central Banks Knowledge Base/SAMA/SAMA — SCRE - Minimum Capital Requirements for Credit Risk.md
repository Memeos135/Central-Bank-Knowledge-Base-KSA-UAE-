# SCRE - Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk

SCRE is the SAMA-adopted credit risk standard of the Basel framework, referenced throughout the capital and output floor reporting rules as the source for standardised approach risk weights (SCRE5–SCRE9), IRB approaches (SCRE10–SCRE16), supervisory slotting (SCRE13), equity treatment and related provisions. It functions as the definitional anchor for which exposures and approaches populate each credit risk reporting row. It binds banks calculating regulatory capital for credit risk in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
