# Template IRRBB1: Quantitative Information on IRRBB

The quantitative Pillar 3 template for IRRBB, requiring disclosure of measured changes in economic value of equity (ΔEVE) and net interest income (ΔNII) under the prescribed supervisory interest rate shock scenarios, computed using the bank's own internal measurement system. It pairs with Table IRRBBA and cross-refers to the Basel supervisory review process IRRBB standard and SAMA's IRRBB circular. It binds banks within the IRRBB scope of application.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
