# Agent Appointment Provisions

Part of the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services (issued 1446H, replacing the 2020 Rules Regulating Payment Service Providers), specifically the chapter on agents and e-money distributors. It governs when and how a licensee may appoint agents to act on its behalf, and the licensee's continuing responsibility for agent conduct. Binds SAMA-licensed payment service providers and payment system operators, with a transitional remediation plan expected within six months of entry into force.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
