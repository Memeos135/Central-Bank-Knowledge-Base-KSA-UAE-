# Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) Approach

This node covers the internal ratings-based (IRB) approaches to credit risk — foundation IRB, advanced IRB and the supervisory slotting approach — as reflected in SAMA's capital framework and RWA disclosure instructions. It addresses which exposures may be modelled with SAMA approval, the boundary with the standardised approach, the equity-exposure IRB prohibition phase-in, and IRB interactions with the securitisation framework (SEC-IRBA, KIRB) and equity investments in funds. It binds banks that have, or seek, SAMA permission to use internal models for credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
