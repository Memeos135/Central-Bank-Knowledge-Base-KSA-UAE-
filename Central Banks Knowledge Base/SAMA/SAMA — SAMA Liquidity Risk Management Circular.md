# SAMA Liquidity Risk Management Circular

A SAMA Banking Supervision circular addressed to all banks (CFOs and CROs) revisiting SAMA Circular BCS 311 of 5 December 2008 on Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management and Supervision. It requires banks to conduct an internal audit assessing their implementation of those principles and to submit the findings report to the Director of Banking Supervision by 31 March 2015. It binds all SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
