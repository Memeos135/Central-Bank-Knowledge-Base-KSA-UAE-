# Finance Company Committees

## Why this community

Governance architecture for finance companies: the sector-level committee structure (terms of reference, mandates, escalation) that channels how finance companies coordinate with each other and with SAMA on industry matters.

## How members connect

- A single constitutive instrument (the Terms of Reference) defines and confers mandate on each committee body, so the other members exist only as creatures of that document.
- Hierarchical obligation chain: the General Committee of GMs sits above the Executive Committee, which in turn constitutes and oversees Subcommittees, with reporting and escalation running back up.
- Shared defined terms (membership, quorum, voting, mandate scope) are set once in the Terms of Reference and inherited by each tier, so scope limits on one tier read across to the others.
- Governance/risk regime only — these are internal sector-coordination organs, not a supervisory delegation; they cannot displace SAMA's direct licensing or prudential powers over individual finance companies.
