# Financing Price Disclosure

## Why this community

Consumer-facing price transparency and contractual fairness in finance and savings products: how cost of credit must be calculated, disclosed pre-contract, and reflected in standard contract terms on early repayment, late payment and default.

## How members connect

- The disclosure rules supply the calculation and presentation standard (APR) that the standard consumer finance contract must embed, creating a disclosure-to-contract obligation chain.
- Early repayment, late payment and default clauses are components of the same standard contract instrument, so their limits are read as mandatory contractual content, not optional terms.
- The lessor data circular attaches data-accuracy/reporting responsibility to the financier, linking pricing disclosure to credit information and consumer protection duties.
- Hierarchy runs from rules/regulations down to the standard contract template and then to clarifying circulars.
