# Aggregation Entity

Defines the licensed aggregation Entity and sets out its ongoing obligations under SAMA's aggregation instructions: platform cybersecurity and immediate breach notification to SAMA, restricting the electronic link with financiers to aggregation purposes only absent SAMA approval, an in-platform complaints/feedback channel with recordkeeping, pop-up consumer consents and acknowledgements, informing consumers through documented channels of rejection reasons or additional document requests, clear and non-misleading disclosure, consumer risk-awareness materials, a list of participating financiers, a prohibition on handling funds for financiers without SAMA approval, and restrictions on marketing for individual financiers. Binds SAMA-licensed aggregation entities; note the overlap with consumer-protection and outsourcing/technology requirements elsewhere.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
