# SAMA Circular: Amended LCR Prudential Returns & NSFR

SAMA circular (2013) transmitting the Amended LCR package — guidance documents, prudential returns, FAQ responses and national discretions — and setting reporting mechanics for banks. It requires monthly LCR prudential returns from month-end 30 June 2013 within 30 days of each month end (with a one-off extension to 30 September 2013 for the first submission), excludes foreign bank branches from that requirement, and confirms NSFR reporting continues unchanged under the February 2012 circular pending BCBS alignment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
