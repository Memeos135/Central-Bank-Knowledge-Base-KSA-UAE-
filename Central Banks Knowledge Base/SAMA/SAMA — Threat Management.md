# Threat Management

A control area of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework requiring Member Organizations to define, approve and implement a threat intelligence management process, and to measure and periodically evaluate its effectiveness. It prescribes the sources (internal logs, SIEM, support functions; external feeds including SAMA and government agencies), an analysis methodology, the threat attributes to capture, actionability for follow-up, and sharing of relevant intelligence with stakeholders such as SAMA and BCIS members. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA and sits within the wider cyber security risk management and compliance regime.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
