# Board and Senior Management Responsibilities

This part allocates accountability for the new-products regime between the Board and senior management: the Board oversees that policies and procedures exist, that product risks are managed and consumer needs and rights are addressed, and reviews consistency with (and any revision of) the approved risk appetite. Senior management owns design, implementation and compliance, periodic review of the internal policies, reputational risk mitigation, and full operational readiness plus any other authorities' approvals before launch. It binds banks' boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
