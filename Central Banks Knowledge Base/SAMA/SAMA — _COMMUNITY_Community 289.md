# Securitisation Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Qualitative narrative disclosure on securitisation activity, covering the bank's objectives, roles (originator, sponsor, investor, servicer), risk management approach and accounting treatment.

## How members connect

- Serves as the narrative counterpart that frames and gives interpretive context to the quantitative securitisation exposure templates.
- Uses the defined securitisation roles and exposure categories that drive the scope and row structure of the numeric templates.
