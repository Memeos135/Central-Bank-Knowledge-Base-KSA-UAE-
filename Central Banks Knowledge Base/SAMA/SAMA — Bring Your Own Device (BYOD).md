# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Framework sub-domain requiring a defined, approved and implemented cyber security standard where a Member Organization permits personal devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops) to be used for business purposes, supported by staff agreements and awareness training. It sets expectations on user responsibilities, consequences of control enforcement on personal devices, separation of business from personal data, application controls and mobile device management, with monitoring and periodic effectiveness evaluation. Binds Member Organizations and, through internal policy, their staff and contractors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
