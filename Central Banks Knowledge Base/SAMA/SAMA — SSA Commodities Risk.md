# SSA Commodities Risk

Covers commodities position risk under the simplified standardised approach, identifying the risks captured (directional/spot, basis, interest rate cost-of-carry, forward gap) and the two permitted measurement alternatives, including the maturity ladder approach. It cross-refers funding-related interest rate and FX exposures to the corresponding risk classes and counterparty credit risk on OTC derivatives to the CCR/CVA rules. Binds banks holding commodity positions and calculating market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
