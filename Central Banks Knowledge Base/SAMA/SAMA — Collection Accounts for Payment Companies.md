# Collection Accounts for Payment Companies

This node addresses collection accounts used by payment companies to deposit and safeguard client funds, opened under a prescribed account name identifying the payment company and its clients, on the basis of a letter from the chairperson or authorised representative stating the purpose. It sits within the juristic-person account rules of the Rules Governing Bank Accounts and binds banks opening such accounts, with practical consequences for licensed payment service providers relying on segregated client-money accounts.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, AML/CTF
