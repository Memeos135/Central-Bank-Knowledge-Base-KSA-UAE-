# Data Maintenance Requirements

A node on data quality and record-keeping obligations underpinning model-based and loss-based capital calculations: alignment, documentation, justification and notification to SAMA of adjustments to risk-theoretical P&L input data used in the market risk internal models approach and the profit-and-loss attribution test, together with the minimum standards for internal operational loss data under the standardised approach (observation period, linkage to current business, consequences of non-compliance including a floor at the business indicator component and possible supervisory multiplier, with Pillar 3 disclosure of exclusions). It binds banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy framework, particularly those using internal models for market risk and those above the business indicator threshold for loss-data use.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
