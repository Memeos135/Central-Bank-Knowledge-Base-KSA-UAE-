# Emergency Evacuation for Persons with Disabilities

A SAMA circular instructing supervised financial institutions to apply the emergency evacuation guide for persons with disabilities issued by the Authority for the Care of Persons with Disabilities, as a follow-on to SAMA's earlier instructions on serving persons with disabilities in financial institutions. It sets expectations for security and safety arrangements — evacuation planning, escape routes and assembly areas, alerting and communication systems, and accessible exits — so that persons with disabilities can be safely evacuated. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision, with the attached guide providing definitions of disability types and procedural steps.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
