# Offering of Financial Derivatives Products

Conduct and gatekeeping conditions specific to financial derivatives and complex/structured products: banks must demonstrate profit and loss scenarios, ensure customers understand the risks and sign off on terms, assess suitability against the customer's business or investment objectives, risk appetite and prior experience, and show the instrument has a bona fide economic purpose rather than mere speculation, leverage or regulatory arbitrage. Banks must also evidence internal organizational and operational capacity to manage the resulting risks; these products additionally require SAMA's prior non-objection. Binds licensed banks offering derivatives to customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, governance/risk
