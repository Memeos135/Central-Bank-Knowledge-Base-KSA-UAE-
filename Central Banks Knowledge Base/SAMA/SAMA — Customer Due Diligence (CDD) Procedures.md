# Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Procedures

The Customer Due Diligence section of SAMA's Money Exchange Business Procedures Guide, defining CDD for exchange houses as ongoing monitoring and understanding of customers' and beneficial owners' financial transactions, verification of the business they conduct, and satisfaction that the data underlying the membership relationship is reliable and clear. It operates with the KYC principle and with tiered identification/record-keeping duties by transaction size and customer type (foreign nationals, citizens and GCC nationals, high-income customers via membership only, and legal persons subject to enhanced due diligence). It binds licensed money exchangers and drives when a membership relationship, rather than a walk-in transaction, is required.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
