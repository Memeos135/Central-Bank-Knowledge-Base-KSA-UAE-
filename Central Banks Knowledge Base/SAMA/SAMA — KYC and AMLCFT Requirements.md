# KYC and AML/CFT Requirements

The AML/CFT-facing slice of the Rules for Bank Accounts, covering customer identification and verification at account opening and ongoing due diligence expectations embedded in the account rules. The supplied context focuses on correspondent banking due diligence — obtaining an AML/CFT commitment form from each correspondent, assessing the correspondent's internal controls and shell-bank exposure, adverse-media and investigation checks, senior management approval for new correspondent relationships, and restrictions on how correspondent accounts may be used — plus limits on accounts for non-resident investment companies, mutual funds and other financial institutions. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and should be read with the AML Law and its Implementing Regulation and SAMA's AML/CTF rules.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
