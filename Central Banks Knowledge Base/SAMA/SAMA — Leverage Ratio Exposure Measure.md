# Leverage Ratio Exposure Measure

Sets out the composition of the leverage ratio exposure measure — on-balance sheet assets, derivative replacement cost and PFE add-ons with the alpha factor, client-cleared and QCCP-related adjustments, and deductions mirroring Tier 1 regulatory adjustments — and its reconciliation to published accounting total assets. It covers permitted exclusions such as fiduciary assets, securitised exposures meeting risk transfer, and any temporary central bank reserve exemption. It binds all banks reporting the leverage ratio and completing the related mandatory disclosure templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
