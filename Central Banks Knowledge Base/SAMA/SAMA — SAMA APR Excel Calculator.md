# SAMA APR Excel Calculator

This node covers SAMA's mandated Excel-based Annual Percentage Rate (APR) calculator and the accompanying Rules that standardise APR computation for retail lending, including the net present value formula and the points at which APR must be disclosed (advertising, offer stage, contract, periodic statements and any other disclosure). It binds all finance providers engaging in retail lending, requiring them to use SAMA's calculator, update internal policies, maintain controls over the calculator's integrity, reconcile any automated tool against SAMA's version, and keep customer-facing website calculators aligned. It is read together with the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, the Rules Regulating Consumer Microfinance Companies, the interest-rate disclosure rules and the Regulations for Consumer Financing.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
