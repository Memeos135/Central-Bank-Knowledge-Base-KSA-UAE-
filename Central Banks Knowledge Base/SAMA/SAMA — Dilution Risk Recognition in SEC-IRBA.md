# Dilution Risk Recognition in SEC-IRBA

A provision within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk dealing with purchased receivables under the IRB approach, specifically how collateral, partial guarantees and other first-loss mitigants covering default risk, dilution risk or both are recognised, including the exposure-weighted LGD calculation where a bank uses the Securitisation Internal Ratings-Based Approach. It sets out substitution treatment for guarantees from the seller or a third party, distinguishing full cover of default and dilution risk, cover of only one component, and partial or tranched cover of the uncovered portion. It binds banks in the Kingdom that have been permitted to use IRB approaches for credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
