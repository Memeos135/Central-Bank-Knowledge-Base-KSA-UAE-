# Trading Book Securitisation Exposures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of securitisation positions held in the trading book, i.e. the quantitative template banks use to expose their securitisation activity and exposures on the trading side.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the SEC2 template stands alone as the trading-book leg of the securitisation disclosure suite.
- Legally it sits within the Pillar 3 disclosure framework, taking its scope from the defined terms 'securitisation exposure' and 'trading book' used across the capital rules.
- Read together with its banking-book counterparts (SEC1/SEC3/SEC4) to avoid double-counting or scope gaps in disclosed exposures.
