# Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities Reporting Guidelines

SAMA reporting guidelines governing how banks prepare and submit the quarterly return on interest/commission rates for assets and liabilities, superseding the earlier guidance note on quarterly prudential returns for loans and deposits commissions. It fixes scope (domestic-level data only), submission timing (within 30 calendar days of quarter end), CFO sign-off, format, and the methodology for weighted average rates, balances and maturity buckets, with templates by product, by sector (ISIC4) and by Sharia-compliant vs conventional. It binds all banks operating in Saudi Arabia, including branches of foreign banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
