# Use of Statistical Models in Rating

Node on the use of statistical models and mechanical rating inputs in credit risk assessment; the supplied context addresses the related rules on consistent and non-selective use of external credit assessments (ECAI ratings) for risk weighting and risk management, multiple-rating resolution, and the scope of short-term versus long-term ratings. Relevant to banks determining risk weights and to the broader IRB expectation that model outputs be used consistently and not chosen to minimise capital.
