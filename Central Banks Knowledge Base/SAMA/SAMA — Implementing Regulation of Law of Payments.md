# Implementing Regulation of Law of Payments

The Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services is the subordinate instrument containing the licensing articles and the substantive requirements for engaging in payment activities and providing payment services, including the Article 8 regulatory requirements referenced for in-principle approval. It defines the licence categories (Micro/Major EMI and Micro/Major PI) and the conditions for commencing activity after licensing. It binds applicants for, and holders of, SAMA payment services licences.
