# Terms of Reference for Finance Company Committees

Arabic SAMA circular from the General Department for Supervision of Finance Companies transmitting the Terms of Reference for Finance Company Committees, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51) and Article 98 of its Implementing Regulation, which empowers the Governor to form committees that propose measures to develop the finance sector. It sets the definitions (standing vs temporary committee), the committee structure (General Committee of General Managers, Executive Committee, subcommittees), and rules on formation, nomination and removal, meetings and quorum, agendas, minutes, reporting, responsibilities, and SAMA's role and representatives. It is addressed to finance companies and governs sector-level industry committee governance rather than firm-level prudential obligations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
