# SAFIU (Financial Investigation Unit)

A defined-term node for the Saudi Arabia Financial Investigation Unit (SAFIU) as used in the Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers — the authority receiving and analysing suspicious activity reports relating to money laundering and terrorist financing from financial and non-financial institutions. In this Guide, SAFIU is the reporting destination where a correspondent institution fails to supply complete originator information on a suspicious transfer and where there are reasonable grounds to suspect funds are criminal proceeds or ML/TF-related. Reporting duties fall on banks' remittance centers and licensed remittance companies.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments
