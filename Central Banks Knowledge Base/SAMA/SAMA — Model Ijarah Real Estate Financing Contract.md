# Model Ijarah Real Estate Financing Contract

This node covers SAMA's mandatory model contract for individual real estate financing structured as Ijarah (lease-to-own), issued under a circular updating the earlier standard forms. It fixes the contract architecture — financing summary page, parties, definitions, term, promise to transfer ownership, ownership and delivery of the leased asset, rental periods, fixed and variable rental instalments and how variable amounts are recalculated, late payment, early repayment/early ownership transfer, maintenance, total/partial loss, assignment, breach and termination, customer withdrawal, death or incapacity — plus annexes for rental schedules and acceptance/ownership-transfer certificates. It binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom, which must use the attached form (per their approved policies) from the stated compliance date and may not conclude or amend non-conforming contracts thereafter.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
