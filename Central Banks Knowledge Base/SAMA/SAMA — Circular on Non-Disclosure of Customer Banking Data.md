# Circular on Non-Disclosure of Customer Banking Data

A short SAMA Banking Supervision circular (in Arabic) reaffirming a series of earlier circulars on the mechanism for disclosing banking data and information. It prohibits supervised entities from providing any financial or banking information about customers or their transactions to any party — individuals, institutions, government bodies, or any other local or foreign entity — without first referring to SAMA and obtaining its no-objection. Addressed to banks and money exchange institutions operating in the Kingdom and to the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH), with a requirement to cascade it to all departments and branches and confirm compliance.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, banking prudential
