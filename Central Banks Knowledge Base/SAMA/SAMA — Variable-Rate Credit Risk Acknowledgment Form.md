# Variable-Rate Credit Risk Acknowledgment Form

The annexed acknowledgment form by which a customer confirms acceptance of the credit risks of variable-cost (variable profit/term-cost) real estate finance, in the standard wording attached to the circular. It must be included in the documents given to the customer as part of the financing offer for variable-rate products, with the lender documenting receipt, and it captures income, total credit obligations to income ratios before and after the finance, and other fees or costs. It binds banks and real estate finance companies offering variable-rate individual real estate finance.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
