# Equity Exposures

This node sets out how equity exposures are defined and risk-weighted under SAMA's credit risk capital framework, based on the economic substance of the instrument rather than its legal form, covering direct and indirect, voting and non-voting ownership interests and instruments structured to convey equity economics. Speculative unlisted equity (short-term resale, venture capital-type holdings) attracts a 400% risk weight; all other equity holdings 250%. It binds SAMA-licensed banks and, in specified cases, requires SAMA's agreement before an instrument is treated as debt or risks decomposed.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
