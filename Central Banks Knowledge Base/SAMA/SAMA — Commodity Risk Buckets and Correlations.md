# Commodity Risk Buckets and Correlations

Part of SAMA's market risk capital framework covering the commodity risk class under the sensitivities-based standardised approach: allocation of commodity risk factors into buckets by commodity type and grade/location, with prescribed risk weights and correlation parameters for aggregation. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks with commodity positions in the trading book, and to commodities risk in the banking book where it falls within the market risk capital scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
