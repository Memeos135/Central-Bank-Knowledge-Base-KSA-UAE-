# Equity Investment Risk

Section 11 (Principle 7.0) of SAMA's Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, requiring strategies, policies and reporting processes for equity investment risk, including Mudarabah and Musharakah profit-sharing investments. Banks must set investment objectives, risk tolerance, expected returns and holding periods, maintain structures for acquiring, holding and exiting such investments, monitor investee performance, and track how risks transform across investment lifecycle and contract stages. The framework took effect 3 March 2024 and binds domestic licensed banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
