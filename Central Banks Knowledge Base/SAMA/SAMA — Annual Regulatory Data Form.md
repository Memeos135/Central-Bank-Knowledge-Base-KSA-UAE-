# Annual Regulatory Data Form

A SAMA annual regulatory data template for money exchange centres, collecting ownership and partner shareholding details, licence issue/renewal/expiry dates, cash insurance policy validity, branch list and addresses, senior and control-function appointments (CEO/manager, internal audit, executive vs non-executive roles) and contracted external third-party firms. It is a supervisory data collection instrument supporting fit-and-proper, licensing and outsourcing oversight rather than a substantive rulebook. It binds licensed exchange centres reporting to SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
