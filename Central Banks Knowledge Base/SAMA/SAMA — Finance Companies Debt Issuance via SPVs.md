# Finance Companies Debt Issuance via SPVs

SAMA circular (General Department for Supervision of Finance Companies) on finance companies issuing debt instruments through special purpose entities (SPVs). It confirms that, consistent with Article 14 of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 66 of its Implementing Regulation, a finance company must apply to the Capital Market Authority to establish an SPV under the CMA's SPV rules, notify SAMA of that application, and obtain SAMA's prior no-objection before any debt instrument is issued through the SPV. It binds all finance companies operating in the Kingdom.
