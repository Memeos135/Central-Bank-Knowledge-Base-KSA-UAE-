# Financial Institution

Defined-term node for "Financial Institution" as used in the Saudi Central Bank Law: any natural or legal person subject to SAMA's supervision, oversight and regulation. The definition is the gateway to SAMA's powers — licensing, prudential policy, consumer protection directives, fintech platform rules, professional testing, and crime-prevention measures all attach to entities falling within it. Note the term is defined differently in other regimes (e.g. AML/CTF activity-based lists), so check which instrument's definition applies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, consumer protection
