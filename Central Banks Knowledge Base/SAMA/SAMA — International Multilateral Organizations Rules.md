# International Multilateral Organizations Rules

Sets the account-opening conditions for international and multilateral organizations and funds of a political, developmental or service nature (e.g. UN bodies, World Bank group, IMF, GCC, Arab League, OIC), requiring a request from the Chairman or deputy, a copy of the headquarters agreement, joint signatures and identification of signatories; it also addresses cross-border transfers by OIC, personal accounts of such organizations' staff (ordinary natural-person rules, with diplomats treated under the diplomatic rule), and accounts of relief committees and campaigns requiring Royal approval and SAMA consent. The node also carries the Rules' definitions chapter (SAMA, Bank Account, Freezing of Account, Bank Verification, Special Purposes Entities, Government Entities, Foreign Schools). It binds licensed banks in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
