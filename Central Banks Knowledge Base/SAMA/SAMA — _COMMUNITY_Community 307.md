# Exchange Center Customer Instructions

## Why this community

Conduct requirements governing how licensed exchange centers deal with customers — the baseline instructions on customer handling, disclosure and service delivery in the money-changing sector.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster functioning as the sector-specific conduct layer for exchange centers.
- Shares its addressee population with the airport traveller ID exemption (community 306); read together to separate conduct duties from AML identification duties.
- Interpretation should preserve the exchange-center licensing perimeter — obligations bind licensed centers, not their agents or counterparties, unless stated.
