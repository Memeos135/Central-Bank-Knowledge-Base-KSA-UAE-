# Account Information and Payment Initiation Services

This node covers Part Eight of SAMA's Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services, on the requirements for providing payment account information services and payment initiation services (open banking activities). It sets licensing scope, consent, access and conduct requirements for providers of these services and their interaction with account-servicing institutions. It binds entities licensed by SAMA to carry out these activities in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, consumer protection
