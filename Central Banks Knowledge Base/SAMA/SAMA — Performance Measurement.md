# Performance Measurement

Section of SAMA's remuneration rules for banks setting out the requirement to operate an objective, documented performance measurement system feeding into performance-based pay, and the related remuneration structure requirements (fixed/variable mix, bonus pools, control-function independence, risk adjustment). It requires appraisal processes free of undue influence and conflicts of interest, weighting of conduct and regulatory-compliance risks, and prohibits guaranteed minimum bonuses. Binds banks licensed by SAMA, their boards and remuneration committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
