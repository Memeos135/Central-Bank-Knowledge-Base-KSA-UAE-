# Transition Arrangements

## Why this community

Timing and phase-in of a regulatory regime: how existing positions, approvals or exposures are treated between the old and new rulebook.

## How members connect

- Standalone provision that qualifies the effective date of the substantive obligations it accompanies, so it must be read against the parent framework rather than alone.
- Legal effect is temporal scoping — grandfathering and cut-off dates — rather than creating new substantive duties.
