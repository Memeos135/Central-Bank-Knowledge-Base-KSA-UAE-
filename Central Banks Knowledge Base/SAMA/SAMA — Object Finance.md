# Object Finance

Object finance is one of three specialized lending subcategories within the corporate exposure class under SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (standardized approach). It covers funding for the acquisition of equipment (ships, aircraft, satellites, railcars, fleets) where repayment depends on cash flows from the financed asset pledged or assigned to the lender; where no issue-specific external rating is available, such exposures are risk-weighted at 100%. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA when calculating credit risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
