# Licensed Credit Bureau

This node concerns the creditor's obligations toward licensed credit bureaus: obtaining the consumer's consent to examine the credit record, registering all credit information on the granted finance, and keeping it updated throughout the relationship, with rejection of the application mandatory if consent is withheld. It also governs how obligations (including credit card minimum payments and variable term-cost finance) must be reflected in the consumer's credit report. It binds creditors subject to the responsible-lending rules, interacting with the credit information regime.
