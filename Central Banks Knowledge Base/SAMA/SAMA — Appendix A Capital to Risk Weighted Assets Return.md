# Appendix A: Capital to Risk Weighted Assets Return

Appendix A is the Capital to Risk Weighted Assets return for DTFCs, defining core and supplementary capital components (including term subordinated debt and the statutory loan loss reserve capped at 1.25% of risk weighted assets), on- and off-balance-sheet asset reporting, and the capital ratio calculations against minimum requirements. It operates with Chapter 4, which sets a 20% capital adequacy requirement, allows SAMA to impose higher minimum ratios on individual firms, and prescribes the risk-weight categories. Binds every deposit-taking finance company reporting to SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
