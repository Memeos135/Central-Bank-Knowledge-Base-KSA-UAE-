# Eligible Collateral and Valuation

Annexure to the provisioning Rules listing indicative eligible collateral for LGD and provision calculation (cash, gold, deposits, government and investment-grade securities, listed shares, receivables, financial and Kafalah guarantees, immovable and other physical collateral, leased assets) and setting collateral policy, monitoring, haircut and stress-testing expectations. Immovable collateral securing Stage 3/defaulted exposures must be revalued at least annually by licensed appraisers, with the valuation showing market and forced-sale values and the lower of two valuations used where judgement is material. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
