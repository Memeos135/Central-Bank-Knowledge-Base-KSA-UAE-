# Basel III Capital Buffers

## Why this community

Bank capital adequacy under the Basel III framework as applied by SAMA, specifically the macroprudential buffer layer sitting on top of minimum regulatory capital.

## How members connect

- The CCyB is not free-standing: it derives its calibration, capital quality (CET1) and scope from the Basel III capital standards adopted by SAMA, so the buffer instrument cites the parent framework.
- Hierarchy runs framework-to-instrument: the capital standards set the definition of capital and the buffer architecture; the CCyB instruction sets the rate and timing for activation/release.
- Shared consequence chain: breaching the buffer does not breach the minimum, but triggers distribution restrictions - a distinction that matters for dividend, bonus and AT1 coupon decisions.
