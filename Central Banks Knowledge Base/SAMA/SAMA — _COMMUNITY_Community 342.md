# Court Ruling Enforcement

## Why this community

Supervised entities' duty to execute judicial rulings and enforcement orders promptly, including compliance with court-directed actions on customer accounts and funds.

## How members connect

- Single directive; the obligation runs from SAMA to supervised institutions and reinforces statutory duties owed to the judiciary and enforcement authorities.
- Interacts with consumer-protection and account-operation rules: execution of rulings can override ordinary account-handling expectations, so the scope and timing of the duty must be read carefully.
- Consequence for decisions: delay or non-execution is itself a supervisory breach, separate from the underlying dispute's merits.
