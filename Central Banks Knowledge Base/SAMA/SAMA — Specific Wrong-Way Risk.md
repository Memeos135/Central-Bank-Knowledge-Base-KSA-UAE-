# Specific Wrong-Way Risk

Concept node on wrong-way risk in the counterparty credit risk framework, distinguishing general wrong-way risk (exposure positively correlated with deterioration in counterparty credit quality across a portfolio) from specific wrong-way risk (future exposure to a particular counterparty highly correlated with that counterparty's own credit quality). It requires identification, stress testing and scenario analysis, monitoring by product/region/industry, and regular reporting to senior management and the board or its delegated committee. Binds banks measuring and managing CCR under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
