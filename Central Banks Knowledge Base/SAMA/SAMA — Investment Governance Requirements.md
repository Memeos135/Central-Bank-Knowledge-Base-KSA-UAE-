# Investment Governance Requirements

Governance and general-requirement provisions of SAMA's Banks Investment Rules (April 2022), covering prudent investment management, documented investment rationale, and the definitions of Local and International Investment. It allocates ultimate oversight of investments to the bank's Board (or, for Foreign Bank Branches, the Head/Regional Office authority), requires a management-level investment committee, and sets the minimum content of the internal Investment Policy including eligibility criteria and prudential exposure limits. Binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA, including foreign bank branches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
