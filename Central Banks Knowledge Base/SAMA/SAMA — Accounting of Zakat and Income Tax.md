# Accounting of Zakat and Income Tax

A 2017 SAMA circular to all banks, insurance companies and finance companies clarifying the accounting treatment and financial statement presentation of Zakat and income tax. It discontinues the SAMA Accounting Standards for Commercial Banks from 1 January 2017 and requires financial statements to be prepared under IFRS as adopted by SAMA (i.e. IFRS except IAS 12 and IFRIC 21 insofar as they relate to Zakat), with standalone guidance annexed and applied consistently across the three sectors. Addressed to managing directors, CEOs and general managers of supervised entities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
