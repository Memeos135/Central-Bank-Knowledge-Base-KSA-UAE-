# Investment Account Holders (IAH)

Covers the defined term Investment Account Holder (bank clients holding Shari'ah compliant investment accounts) and the allocation of risk between IAHs and the bank under PSIA arrangements. It distinguishes unrestricted from restricted PSIAs, explains the bank's role as Mudarib or Wakeel, and states that IAHs bear investment and counterparty losses except where the bank's fraud, negligence, misconduct or breach of contract is proven. Relevant to banks offering PSIA products and to the protection of IAH interests.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
