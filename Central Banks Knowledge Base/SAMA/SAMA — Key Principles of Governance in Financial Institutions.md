# Key Principles of Governance in Financial Institutions

SAMA's Key Principles of Governance for financial institutions, setting minimum governance requirements across board qualifications, board formation and responsibilities, executive management, board committees, shareholder rights, and disclosure and transparency, with an appendix on money exchange sector controls. It requires separation of Chairman and CEO roles and a control framework comprising risk management, internal audit, compliance, internal controls and an external auditor. It binds entities supervised by SAMA other than insurance and reinsurance companies, and is read alongside other SAMA governance instructions without displacing them.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
