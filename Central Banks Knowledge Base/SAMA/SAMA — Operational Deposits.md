# Operational Deposits

LCR guidance node addressing deposits held for operational purposes (clearing, custody, cash management) and the surrounding classification rules for stable and less stable deposits and deposit insurance criteria. It confirms SAMA's position that, absent deposit insurance in the Kingdom, the reduced run-off treatment conditional on an effective insurance scheme does not apply to Saudi banks, and that less stable buckets carry a minimum 10% run-off. Relevant to banks calculating LCR outflows and to supervisory review of bucket allocation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
