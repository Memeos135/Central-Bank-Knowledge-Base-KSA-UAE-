# Template CMS1: Comparison Modelled and Standardised RWA

Node covering Template CMS1, the Pillar 3 disclosure comparing RWA produced by internal/modelled approaches that SAMA has approved against RWA that would result from applying the full standardised approach. It defines, risk category by risk category (credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation in the banking book, market risk, operational risk and residual RWA), which approach counts as "standardised" and how actual versus fully standardised RWA are to be allocated. It binds banks with SAMA model approvals that are subject to the Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
