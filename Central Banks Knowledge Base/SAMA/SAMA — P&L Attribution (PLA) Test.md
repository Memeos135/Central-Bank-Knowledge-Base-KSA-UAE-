# P&L Attribution (PLA) Test

Covers the profit and loss attribution (PLA) test used alongside backtesting to validate trading desks' eligibility for the internal models approach under SAMA's market risk standard. Banks must begin the PLA programme when the internal models capital requirement takes effect, provide a one-year report for model approval, and reassess desk eligibility quarterly; SAMA may impose capital add-ons or withdraw model permission. Binds banks using or seeking internal model approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
