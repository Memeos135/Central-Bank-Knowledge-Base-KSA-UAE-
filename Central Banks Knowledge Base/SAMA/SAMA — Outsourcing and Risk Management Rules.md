# Outsourcing and Risk Management Rules

The chapter of the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services addressing outsourcing, internal audit and risk management obligations of licensees. It frames when functions may be outsourced, the licensee's retained responsibility, and the governance/control expectations SAMA imposes. Binds SAMA-licensed payment service providers and payment system operators.
