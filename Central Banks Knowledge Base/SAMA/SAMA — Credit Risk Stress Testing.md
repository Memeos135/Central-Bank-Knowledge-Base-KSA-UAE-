# Credit Risk Stress Testing

Sets out how banks must stress test credit exposures under SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing, covering both on- and off-balance sheet exposures and estimating impacts on asset quality, profitability and capital. Banks may use the Standardised or IRB approach and risk parameters such as EAD, PD, LGD and maturity, and are expected to design their own scenarios (e.g. oil price declines, economic downturn, parameter shifts) rather than rely on SAMA-supplied standard scenarios. Accompanying FAQ material confirms that banks cannot limit testing to major portfolio segments and must reflect portfolio nature, size and mix, including retail and consumer books.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
