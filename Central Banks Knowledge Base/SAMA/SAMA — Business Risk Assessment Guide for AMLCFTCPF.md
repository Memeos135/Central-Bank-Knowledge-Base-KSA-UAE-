# Business Risk Assessment Guide for AML/CFT/CPF

SAMA guide (with covering circular) on assessing business risks related to money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, issued under the SAMA Law, the AML Law and the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing. It sets the minimum factors, criteria and controls a financial institution must consider when conducting its enterprise-wide business risk assessment, covers definitions, governance and institutional responsibility, and the stages of the assessment, while expressly not imposing a single mandatory methodology nor limiting other AML/CFT/CPF obligations. Binding on all financial institutions subject to SAMA regulation and supervision from the date of publication on SAMA's website.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
