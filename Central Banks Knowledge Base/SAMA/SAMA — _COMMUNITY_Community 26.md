# Crowdfunding & Outsourcing

## Why this community

Licensing and ongoing conduct obligations for debt-based crowdfunding companies, with outsourcing governance as the principal operational-risk control applied to them and to other supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the licensing guidelines into the substantive crowdfunding rules, which define the crowdfunding company and platform and impose risk management and creditworthiness assessment duties.
- Outsourcing compliance for crowdfunding entities is an express cross-reference into SAMA's general Rules on Outsourcing rather than a standalone regime.
- The outsourcing rules branch by scenario — material outsourcing, foreign bank branches, overseas service providers — each with distinct notification/approval and accountability consequences.
- Responsibility is non-delegable: outsourcing does not transfer the licensee's obligations toward beneficiaries or SAMA.
