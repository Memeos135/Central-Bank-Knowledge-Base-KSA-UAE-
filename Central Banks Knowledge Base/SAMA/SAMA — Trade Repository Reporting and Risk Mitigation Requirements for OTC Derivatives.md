# Trade Repository Reporting and Risk Mitigation Requirements for OTC Derivatives

The consolidated SAMA requirements (version 4.0, September 2025) governing over-the-counter derivative contracts, issued under SAMA's Law and the Banking Control Law. Section A imposes trade reporting to the SAMA-authorised Trade Repository Operator; Section B imposes risk mitigation obligations on banks that are contracting parties to non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives, regardless of notional size or hedging purpose, with centrally and indirectly cleared trades excluded. It binds banks licensed in KSA (including foreign bank branches and, for reporting scope, financial subsidiaries/SPVs), supersedes the 1442H circular, and takes effect 1 February 2026.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
