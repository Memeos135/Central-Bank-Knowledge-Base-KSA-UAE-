# Cyber Security Awareness

## Why this community

Organisation-wide cyber security awareness obligations, requiring supervised entities to maintain a programme that raises staff, and where relevant customer, understanding of cyber risk.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster representing an awareness control domain under the cyber security governance regime.
- Links upward to management responsibility for cyber risk culture and sideways to fraud and consumer protection duties where customer-facing awareness is required.
- Distinct from formal training: awareness is a continuous communication obligation, while competence-building is addressed by the training domain.
