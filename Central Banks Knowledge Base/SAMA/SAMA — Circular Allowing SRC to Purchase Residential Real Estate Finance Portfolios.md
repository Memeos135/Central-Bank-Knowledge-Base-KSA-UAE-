# Circular Allowing SRC to Purchase Residential Real Estate Finance Portfolios

A SAMA circular (Arabic text) permitting the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC) to purchase residential real estate finance portfolios from real estate financiers once at least three months have elapsed from the date the related financing was granted, or from receipt of the first instalment from the beneficiary. It is issued as an exception under the rules on disposal of finance assets and the rights arising from them, which allow SAMA to exempt certain disposals given their nature or size. It is addressed to banks and finance companies in the Kingdom and takes effect from its date.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential
