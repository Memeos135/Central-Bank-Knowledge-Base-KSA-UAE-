# Net Stable Funding Ratio

The Net Stable Funding Ratio itself: available stable funding divided by required stable funding, which must be at least 100% on an ongoing basis. It limits overreliance on short-term wholesale funding and requires banks to fund assets and off-balance-sheet activities with a stable funding profile; SAMA may impose more stringent standards on an individual bank reflecting its funding risk profile and compliance with the Sound Principles. Applies to banks in Saudi Arabia, based on the BCBS October 2014 standard.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
