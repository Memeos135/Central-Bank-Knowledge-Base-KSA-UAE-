# Airport Exchange ID Exemption

## Why this community

A narrow identification carve-out for exchange (money-changing) centers located at airports serving travellers — a scope limit on the standard CDD identification requirement for defined low-value or transit transactions.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster, but legally dependent on the general AML/CTF customer identification baseline it derogates from; the exemption only has meaning by reference to that baseline.
- Closely adjacent to the exchange-center customer conduct instructions (community 307): same licensed population, different obligation type (AML identification vs. customer dealing).
- Any reliance on the exemption must respect its stated scope (location, customer type, transaction character); outside that scope full CDD revives.
