# Use of Internal Ratings

Groups the internal-model use and control requirements with the internal risk transfer rules: the independent risk control unit and separate model validation unit (annual validation of all internal models used for the internal models approach), desk-level backtesting and profit-and-loss attribution, board and senior management engagement, alignment between capital models and day-to-day risk management models, and the conditions under which internal risk transfers between the banking book and trading book receive capital recognition. It binds banks using internal models for market risk capital requirements under SAMA's framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
