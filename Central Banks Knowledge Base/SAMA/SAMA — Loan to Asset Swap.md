# Loan to Asset Swap

Concept node describing the loan-to-asset (and loan-to-equity) swap as an additional, non-standalone restructuring measure in which all or part of a distressed loan is transferred into "other assets owned", with recovery dependent on subsequent sale of that asset. It is presented as something to be combined with other options (e.g. conditional forgiveness, partial repayment with maturity extension), requiring specialist asset/real-estate management expertise and a supporting business plan. It binds banks operating workout units, which must monitor swapped exposures by asset type and stay within any holding limits set by applicable national rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
