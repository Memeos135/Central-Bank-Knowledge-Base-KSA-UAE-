# Finance Lease Contract Registry

## Why this community

Registration of finance lease contracts in the statutory contract registry and the downstream right to obtain executive (enforceable) deed extracts, which determine when a lessor can enforce without a fresh court judgment.

## How members connect

- Both control sets derive their authority from the Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48) and its registry mandate — a law-to-implementing-controls hierarchy.
- Registry entry is the condition precedent: only a validly registered contract can generate an executive deed extract, so the two control sets form a sequential obligation chain.
- Shared defined terms (finance lease contract, registry, lessor/lessee, executive deed) must be read consistently across both instruments.
- Scope limit: these controls govern registration and enforceability mechanics, not the prudential licensing of the lessor.
