# Template CVA4: RWA Flow Statements SA-CVA

A Pillar 3 flow-statement template showing period-on-period movements in RWA for CVA risk exposures measured under the standardised approach (SA-CVA), completing the CVA disclosure suite with the CVA1–CVA3 templates and tables CVAA/CVAB. It is relevant only to banks approved for SA-CVA and meeting the CVA materiality threshold, and it links to the overall RWA overview reporting. Binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the CVA capital charge.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
