# License Application Guidelines for Finance Support Activities

Procedural guidance issued by SAMA for submitting an application to obtain a licence to practise finance support activities, supplementing the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities. It sets out who may apply (founders or an authorised representative), the duty to provide accurate and complete information, the channel for submission, and the checklist of documents required, including fit and proper forms for founders, 5%+ owners of corporate founders and board candidates, a feasibility study and three-year business plan, and an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of the minimum capital. It binds applicants seeking SAMA authorisation to incorporate and operate a finance support company.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
