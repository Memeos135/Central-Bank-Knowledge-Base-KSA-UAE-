# RWA for Default Risk

Concept node on calculating risk-weighted assets for default risk on purchased receivables under the IRB approach, including use of top-down pool estimates and the interaction with credit risk mitigants. It sets out how guarantees covering default risk (alone or together with dilution risk) allow substitution of the guarantor's risk weight, and how uncovered portions are treated under proportional or tranched coverage rules. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks using IRB for purchased receivable exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
