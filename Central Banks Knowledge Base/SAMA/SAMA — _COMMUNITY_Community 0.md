# Pillar 3 Capital Disclosures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 market-disclosure obligations for banks in KSA: the fixed-format templates and tables through which regulatory capital, leverage, RWA, TLAC and valuation positions must be published, plus the frequency and timing rules governing them.

## How members connect

- A hierarchy runs from the Basel III framework and the FSB TLAC term sheet, through SAMA's SACAP capital-reforms guidance and the SCRE credit-risk rules, down to the Pillar 3 framework that mandates each named template.
- The framework document is the hub: it cross-references every KM/CC/CCA/TLAC/LI/PV/LR/CCyB/CR/CCR/SEC template, so disclosure obligations are defined by reference rather than restated.
- Templates chain to each other through shared measurement inputs - key metrics draw on the capital-composition template, TLAC and capital-distribution templates draw on the same regulatory capital definitions, and the LI/PV reconciliations link accounting balances to regulatory exposure amounts.
- Counterparty concepts (CCP, qualifying CCP, default fund contributions) and the disclosure FAQ supply the defined terms and interpretive gloss applied across the credit and counterparty templates.
