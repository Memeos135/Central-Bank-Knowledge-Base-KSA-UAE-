# Publishing Penalties Instructions

SAMA circular (issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation) requiring finance and refinance companies to disclose SAMA enforcement actions taken against them in their annual board of directors' reports, under a dedicated heading for SAMA enforcement decisions. Disclosure must cover the current and prior financial year and state the subject of the violation, the number of enforcement decisions (monetary and non-monetary) and total fines in SAR, broken down into supervisory/regulatory breaches, consumer protection breaches, and AML/CTF customer due diligence breaches. SAMA will reconcile these disclosures against its own records, publish violations on its website, and act against non-compliant companies; effective from the FY2017 board report.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF
