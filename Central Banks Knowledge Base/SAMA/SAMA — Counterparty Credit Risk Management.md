# Counterparty Credit Risk Management

This node covers the counterparty credit risk management expectations applied to banks dealing with highly leveraged institutions (HLIs), including hedge funds: a board-approved credit risk strategy defining risk appetite, written policies governing HLI relationships, and an end-to-end credit process (documentation, financial information, due diligence, risk mitigants, exposure measurement, limits, ongoing monitoring). It stresses that commercial pressure must not override these policies and that collateral is not a substitute for day-to-day monitoring of a counterparty's strategies, leverage and concentrations. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia that have or contemplate HLI exposures, with responsibility allocated to the board and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
