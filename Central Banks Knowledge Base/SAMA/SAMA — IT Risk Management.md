# IT Risk Management

The IT Risk Management domain of SAMA's IT Governance Framework, requiring Member Organizations to define, approve, implement and communicate an IT risk management process aligned with enterprise risk management, covering risk identification, analysis and classification, treatment, reporting, monitoring and profiling. It prescribes a centralised IT risk register, analysis attributes (asset classification, threats, impact and likelihood, existing controls, risk and control owners, inherent and residual risk), key risk indicators, and treatment within the risk appetite approved by the ITSC. Scope extends to business processes and data, applications, infrastructure, people and third-party relationships.
