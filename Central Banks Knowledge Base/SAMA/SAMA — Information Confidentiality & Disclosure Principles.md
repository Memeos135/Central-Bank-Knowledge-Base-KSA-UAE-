# Information Confidentiality & Disclosure Principles

A section of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics for Financial Institutions dealing with safeguarding confidentiality of information and the permitted channels/mechanisms for disclosure, sitting alongside related sections on misuse or leakage of inside information, market manipulation and whistleblowing. The principles set a minimum conduct floor that each institution's board must translate into its own approved code of conduct policy. They bind all personnel of SAMA-supervised financial institutions — board and committee members, executives, permanent and contract staff, consultants and third-party-supplied staff — across banks, foreign bank branches, insurance/reinsurance and free-zone insurers, finance and finance-lease registration companies, and credit information companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, AML/CTF, consumer protection
