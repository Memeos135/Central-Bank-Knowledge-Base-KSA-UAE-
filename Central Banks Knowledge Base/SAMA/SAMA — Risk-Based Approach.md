# Risk-Based Approach

The core methodological concept of SAMA's AML/CTF guide: a six-step risk-based approach requiring institutions to assess, understand and document ML/TF risks across customers and beneficial owners, products and services, geography and delivery channels, then apply proportionate preventive measures. It requires customer risk classification and profile updates, pre-launch risk assessment of new products, services, practices and technologies, provision of risk assessment information to SAMA on request, and review/update of the approach at least every two years or on change of risk factors. It expressly discourages de-risking by wholesale exclusion of customer classes. Binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
