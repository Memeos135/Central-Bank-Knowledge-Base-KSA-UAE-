# Branch Network Policy

The defined-document requirement within the Branch Network Regulation obliging each bank to prepare a Branch Network Policy governing opening, closing and relocation of branches, service centers and remittance centers, and covering customer service strategy (including elderly and disabled customers), target market, delivery channels, digital modes, financial inclusion and CSR in priority areas. The policy must be approved by the Board (or a delegated board committee), or by the chief executive/authorized senior executive at head office for foreign bank branches, and submitted to SAMA within four months with specified historical data. Once approved, it replaces the Annual Branch Expansion Plan; it does not cover ATMs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
