# Change and Patch Management

## Why this community

Controlled modification of production systems: the discipline of authorising, testing and recording changes, with security patching as a governed sub-case.

## How members connect

- Patch management is treated as a change subject to the change and release management control, so patch deployment inherits change authorisation and rollback requirements.
- Both are operational controls within the wider cyber security and resilience obligations, and evidence of them is what supervisory review examines.
