# Inoperative Accounts (Dormant/Unclaimed/Abandoned)

The inoperative-account lifecycle under the Rules for Bank Accounts: classification of accounts as dormant after 24 calendar months without a customer-initiated debit or documented correspondence, then progression to unclaimed and abandoned status, with prescribed customer notification, reactivation controls, dual supervision, separate file safeguarding, balance-of-liability treatment, deduction of outstanding obligations, transfer to suspense, and closure of small balances (SAR 1,000 or less) with notice. It also sets internal audit coverage (at least once every two years, reported to the Audit Committee) and an annual statistical report to SAMA due end of March. Obligations bind banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk
