# Template TLAC2 - Creditor Ranking

Disclosure template requiring G-SIBs to set out, on a legal entity basis, the creditor hierarchy of each material subgroup entity that has issued internal TLAC to a resolution entity, showing total capital and liabilities, excluded liabilities, TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity bands. Reported semiannually in fixed format, grouped by resolution group so exposures to each resolution entity are identifiable, with narrative on jurisdiction-specific credit hierarchies. It binds G-SIBs and serves creditors assessing their ranking in resolution.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
