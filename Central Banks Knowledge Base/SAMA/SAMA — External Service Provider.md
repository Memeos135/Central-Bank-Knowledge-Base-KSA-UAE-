# External Service Provider

This node defines the External Service Provider — any provider to whom an activity is outsourced, whether a group member, related party or unrelated third party — and sets the contractual requirements governing the relationship. The outsourcing must be documented in a legally binding written contract covering, at minimum, scope, term, performance, audit and monitoring, business continuity, liability, confidentiality, reporting, adverse-development notification, SAMA/auditor access rights, data return or destruction on expiry, a ban on subcontracting Material Functions, Saudi judicial jurisdiction (departures needing SAMA's prior non-objection), and governing language. It binds licensed finance companies in their contracting with providers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, data/credit info
