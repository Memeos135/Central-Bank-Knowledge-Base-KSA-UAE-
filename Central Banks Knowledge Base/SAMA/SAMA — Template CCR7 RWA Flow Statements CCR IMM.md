# Template CCR7: RWA Flow Statements CCR IMM

Template CCR7 is the Pillar 3 RWA flow statement for counterparty credit risk exposures calculated under the internal models method (IMM), showing period-on-period movements in RWA and their drivers. The surrounding context sets out the OV1-style mapping of RWA and minimum capital requirements across credit risk, CCR, CVA, equity and other approaches, and the standardised/modelled RWA split definitions. It binds banks with SAMA approval to use IMM for CCR under the Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
