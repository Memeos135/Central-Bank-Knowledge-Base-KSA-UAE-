# Board of Directors Governance

The board-focused provisions of SAMA's governance principles, covering member independence, conflict-of-interest disclosure on nomination, multiple-directorship and tenure limits, the prohibition on sitting on the board of a similar financial institution in the Kingdom, and the Chairman's specific duties. It requires a prior written no-objection from SAMA before nominating board/committee members or appointing leadership positions, and notification to SAMA within five working days of resignation, termination, end of membership or loss of independence. It also requires disclosure of board committees to SAMA and adoption of social responsibility programs.
