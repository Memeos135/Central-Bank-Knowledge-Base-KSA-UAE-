# Net Independent Collateral Amount (NICA)

Concept node for the net independent collateral amount (NICA) used in measuring counterparty credit risk exposure on derivatives and in related capital deductions and credit risk mitigation disclosures under SAMA's Basel-aligned framework. It matters for how collateral other than variation margin is recognised in exposure and for how secured/collateralised exposures are presented in Pillar 3 templates. Applies to banks calculating regulatory capital and making mandated disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
