# IRB Approach: Overview and Asset Class Definitions

A concept node introducing the internal ratings-based (IRB) approach, its asset classes, and the distinction between foundation and advanced approaches and supervisory slotting. It sets SAMA's conditions for phased rollout across asset classes and business units, the requirement for a SAMA-agreed implementation plan, the all-exposures rule within an asset class and business unit, and the bar on capital relief from intra-group transactions during rollout. It binds banks seeking or operating IRB permission.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[SAMA — Supervisory Slotting Criteria Approach|Supervisory Slotting Criteria Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If the bank has adopted IRB for an asset class, the overview's roll-out expectation means specialised lending within that class must also be brought under an IRB treatment, and the supervisory slotting criteria are the route where the bank cannot meet the own-estimate requirements. The link is an obligation chain: the overview sets scope and adoption expectations across asset classes, while the slotting provisions supply the prescribed risk weights and expected-loss treatment for project, object, commodities finance, income-producing real estate and HVCRE. For a decision, partial IRB adoption within an asset class is not the default position, and any specialised lending left outside own estimates should be evidenced as slotted rather than simply excluded.
