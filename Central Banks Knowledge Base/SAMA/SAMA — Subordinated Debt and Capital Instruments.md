# Subordinated Debt and Capital Instruments

This node covers the treatment of subordinated debt, equity and other regulatory capital instruments issued by corporates or banks under SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. It applies only where such instruments are not deducted from regulatory capital, not already risk-weighted at 250% under the SAMA Basel III regulatory capital framework, not risk-weighted at 1250%, and excludes equity investments in funds. It binds SAMA-supervised banks and interacts with the regulatory capital rules for deductions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
