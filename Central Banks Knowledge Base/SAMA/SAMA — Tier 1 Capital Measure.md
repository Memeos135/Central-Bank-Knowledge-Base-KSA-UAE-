# Tier 1 Capital Measure

A concept node within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework covering the Tier 1 capital measure and its role in regulatory capital composition and disclosure templates (including capital distribution constraint triggers and the CC1-style line-by-line explanations of Tier 1/Tier 2 items and deductions). It anchors how CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 elements, regulatory adjustments, threshold deductions and TLAC-related holdings are reported. It binds banks licensed in KSA subject to SAMA's Basel III capital and disclosure rules, with cross-references to SACAP.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
