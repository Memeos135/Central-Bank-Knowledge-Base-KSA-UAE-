# Early Warning Signals

Defines Early Warning Signals (EWS) as quantitative or qualitative indicators based on liquidity, profitability, market, collateral and macroeconomic metrics, and sets the obligation for banks to build policies, procedures, tools and governance around EWS fully integrated into the risk management system. Banks must run EWS in three stages — identification (portfolio-specific indicators pointing to payment difficulty, factoring the economic environment), action/escalation with cause, severity and timelines, and ongoing monitoring calibrated to borrower risk. Applies to banks licensed under the Banking Control Law as part of problem loan prevention and identification.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
