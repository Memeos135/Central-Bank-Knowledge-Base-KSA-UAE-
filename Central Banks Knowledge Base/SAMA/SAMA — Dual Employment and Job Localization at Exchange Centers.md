# Dual Employment and Job Localization at Exchange Centers

A SAMA circular addressed to exchange centers (money changers) in the Kingdom prohibiting an employee from holding two jobs at the same time at more than one exchange center, and reaffirming job localization (Saudization) requirements. It relies on SAMA's Law, the Rules Governing Money Changing Business (including provisions of Articles 14(b) and 21(b)), the requirements for appointment to senior positions in SAMA-supervised institutions, and the principles of conduct and work ethics. Exchange centers must verify compliance and rectify dual-employment cases within six months and notify SAMA, and must restrict all activity positions to Saudi nationals without seeking non-Saudi work permits elsewhere.
