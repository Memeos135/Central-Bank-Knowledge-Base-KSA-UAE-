# Template CCR1: Analysis of CCR Exposures by Approach

A Pillar 3 template requiring banks to analyse counterparty credit risk exposures by the method used to determine exposure at default, showing replacement cost, potential future exposure, EAD post-CRM and resulting RWA across the CCR approaches. It is the lead quantitative template of the CCR disclosure section, which also covers standardised and IRB portfolio breakdowns, collateral, credit derivatives, IMM flow statements and CCP exposures. Applies to banks with exposures subject to a counterparty credit risk charge under the SAMA disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
