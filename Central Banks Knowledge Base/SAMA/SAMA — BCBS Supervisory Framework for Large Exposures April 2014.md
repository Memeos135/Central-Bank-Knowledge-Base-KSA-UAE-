# BCBS Supervisory Framework for Large Exposures April 2014

Reference node to the Basel Committee's April 2014 supervisory framework for measuring and controlling large exposures, which underpins SAMA's large exposure rules (including the CCP treatment in the appendix). It informs the exemptions and limits SAMA applies — intraday interbank exemption, intra-group exposures within KSA excluded from limits with non-bank financial subsidiaries capped at 25% of eligible capital, and quarterly reporting before and after credit risk mitigation. Relevant to banks calculating and reporting exposures on the basis of the latest published quarterly eligible capital base.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
