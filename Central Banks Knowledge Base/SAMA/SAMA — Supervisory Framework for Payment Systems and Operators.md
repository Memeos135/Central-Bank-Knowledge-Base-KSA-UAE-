# Supervisory Framework for Payment Systems and Operators

Extract from SAMA's supervisory/oversight framework for payment systems and their operators, stating that SAMA is the competent authority for regulating, supervising and overseeing payment systems and operators in the Kingdom under the relevant law and its implementing regulation. It frames oversight as monitoring and assessing existing and planned systems against safety and efficiency objectives and setting controls and standards aligned with the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures. Relevant to operators of payment systems and financial market infrastructure in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
