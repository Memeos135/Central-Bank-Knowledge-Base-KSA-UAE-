# SAMA Q17 Reporting Template

Node covering the disclosure/reporting template mechanics in the Pillar 3 part of the framework, including CCR and securitisation templates (e.g. CCR5, CCR7, SEC1, OV1) and interpretive Q&A on what to report where. It sets out the distinction between fixed-format templates (prescribed rows/columns, deletion allowed for irrelevant items but no renumbering) and flexible-format templates, plus signposting of disclosures made in other documents. Binds banks subject to SAMA disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
