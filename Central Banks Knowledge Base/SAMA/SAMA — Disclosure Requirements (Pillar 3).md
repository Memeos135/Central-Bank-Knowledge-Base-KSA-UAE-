# Disclosure Requirements (Pillar 3)

Conditions IRB eligibility on compliance with the Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework: failure to meet those disclosures makes a bank ineligible to use the relevant IRB approach. Related securitisation criteria impose documentation disclosure, legal review and investor transparency conditions for STC treatment. Binds SAMA-licensed banks using internal models for credit risk and originators/sponsors seeking preferential securitisation treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
