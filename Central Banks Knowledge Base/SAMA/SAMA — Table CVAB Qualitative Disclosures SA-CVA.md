# Table CVAB: Qualitative Disclosures SA-CVA

A qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table for banks that have obtained SAMA approval to use the standardised approach for CVA risk (SA-CVA), sitting alongside the quantitative SA-CVA templates CVA3 and CVA4. It supports the CVA risk disclosure block that applies once the materiality threshold in the revised counterparty credit risk capital framework is met, and it reflects the rule that BA-CVA is the default unless SAMA grants SA-CVA approval. Binds SAMA-supervised banks with covered derivative and securities financing transaction exposures using SA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
