# Liquidity Requirements

Chapter 5 of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020 imposing liquidity obligations on DTFCs: a Board-approved liquidity risk management plan (management structures and MIS, net funding requirements, contingency funding planning, internal controls); a statutory minimum liquid asset holding of 20% of deposit and short-term liabilities; statutory deposits with SAMA of at least 4% of deposit liabilities; and a cap of 15 times total capital on deposit liabilities with a one-month remediation route. It also sets monthly liquidity reporting to SAMA by the 15th business day, with Appendix B completion instructions for the liquidity statement.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, BNPL/finance
