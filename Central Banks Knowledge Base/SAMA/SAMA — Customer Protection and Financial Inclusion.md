# Customer Protection and Financial Inclusion

This node covers Part Five (Customer Protection and Financial Inclusion) of the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services, issued by SAMA to replace the earlier Rules Regulating Payment Service Providers. It sets conduct obligations owed by licensees to Customers and Consumers, including fair treatment, disclosure and financial inclusion expectations, alongside the Regulation's complaint definition (an expression of dissatisfaction submitted by a Customer or Consumer). It binds all SAMA-licensed payment service providers, and is relevant where payments conduct rules overlap with the general consumer protection regime.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
