# Finance Lease Contract Registration

## Why this community

Registration formalities for finance lease contracts, i.e. the perfection/recording step that conditions a lessor's ability to assert its rights in leased assets under the Finance Lease Law regime.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a SAMA circular operating as subordinate guidance under the Finance Lease Law and its implementing regulation.
- Linkage is to the finance-company licensing and contract-documentation chain rather than to internal cross-references.
- Relevance is decisional: whether a lease is registered affects enforceability and priority against third parties.
