# IRRBB Standardised Framework

Concept node for the updated standardised framework within the IRRBB standard adopted by SAMA, providing a prescribed methodology for measuring interest rate risk in the banking book. It sits alongside the enhanced disclosure requirements based on common interest rate shock scenarios and the outlier threshold set at 15% of Tier 1 capital. Applies in the context of domestic banks subject to the IRRBB circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
