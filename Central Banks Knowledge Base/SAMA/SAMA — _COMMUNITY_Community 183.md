# Trademark Product Requirements

## Why this community

Requirements applying where a licensed entity markets or issues products under more than one trademark or brand, addressing brand transparency and the risk that consumers cannot identify the responsible licensed institution.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the obligation stands alone as a product-labelling/branding condition rather than part of a referenced chain.
- Legally it sits downstream of consumer protection and licensing principles: the licensed entity remains accountable for products regardless of the trademark used.
- Read alongside consumer disclosure and advertising obligations in other regimes when assessing a multi-brand product launch.
