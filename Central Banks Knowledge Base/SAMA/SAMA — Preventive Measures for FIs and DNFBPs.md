# Preventive Measures for FIs and DNFBPs

This node covers the preventive-measures chapter of the KSA counter-terrorism and terrorism-financing law, setting TF risk assessment, customer due diligence (including enhanced due diligence for high risk and for new products/technologies), and record-keeping duties of at least ten years, extendable by the Public Prosecution for investigations. It also reaches information exchange by the Directorate (FIU) with domestic competent authorities and foreign counterparts subject to use-limitation undertakings, and the supervisory powers chapter. It binds financial institutions, DNFBPs and, for risk assessment and supervision purposes, NPOs, with detail left to the Implementing Regulation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
