# Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria

SAMA's Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria setting out the process and minimum standards for obtaining a licence to carry on banking business in KSA under the Banking Control Law, issued under SAMA's Charter and the BCL. It covers the restriction on use of the word "bank", minimum criteria on capital, ownership, corporate governance, risk management and controls, compliance and AML/CTF, IT, accounting systems and outsourcing, audit arrangements, and home-supervisor oversight for foreign bank branches, plus pre-application consultation, submission and processing steps. It binds all applicants for a banking licence, including foreign banks seeking to establish a branch.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF, other

### [[SAMA — Banking Control Law|Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For any bank licensing decision, the Licensing Guidelines and minimum criteria are not free-standing: they operate as SAMA's articulation of the licensing power conferred by the Banking Control Law and its implementing rules. The Law is the parent statute and hierarchy therefore runs from it to the implementing rules to the Guidelines, so the Guidelines cannot be read to narrow or displace a statutory licensing condition. Practically, an applicant must satisfy both the statutory conditions and the minimum criteria, and any ambiguity in the Guidelines should be resolved by reference back to the Banking Control Law.
