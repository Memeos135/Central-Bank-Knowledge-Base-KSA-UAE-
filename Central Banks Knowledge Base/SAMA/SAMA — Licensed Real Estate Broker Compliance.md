# Licensed Real Estate Broker Compliance

SAMA circular directing all institutions under its supervision to deal and contract only with real estate brokers licensed by the Real Estate General Authority (REGA), relying on the Real Estate Brokerage Law which prohibits practising brokerage or providing real estate services without REGA licensing. It points supervised entities to REGA's online register for verification, making licence status a counterparty due-diligence condition rather than creating new brokerage rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
