# Object Finance Slotting Grades

## Why this community

Specialised lending under the credit risk framework: supervisory slotting categories used to assign risk weights to object finance exposures where repayment depends on cash flows generated by the financed asset.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster providing the supervisory grade scale (strong through default) for one specialised lending sub-type.
- Depends on the upstream definition of specialised lending and object finance to determine which exposures are in scope.
- Functions as a mapping table between qualitative supervisory assessment and prescribed risk weights, rather than an independent obligation.
