# Exemption of Martyrs' Cheque Amounts from Judicial Seizure

A SAMA circular to banks operating in the Kingdom exempting cheque proceeds payable to families of martyrs from attachment under judicial enforcement orders, building on the Enforcement Law provision listing assets not subject to seizure and SAMA's earlier circular on the updated list of amounts exempt from judicial seizure. It sets the exempt proportion of such cheque amounts that must be released to the customer and conditions availability on a notary-issued declaration, using the attached form. Effective from its date; binds banks handling attachment orders on customer accounts.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
