# Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions

SAMA's Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions (v1.0, August 2019) sets the minimum controls financial institutions must adopt for receiving and handling violation reports. It covers general obligations, the unit handling reports, reportable cases, obligations of whistle blowers and of institutions, protection of whistle blowers from retaliation and against disclosure of identity (except to investigative or judicial authorities), report processing and approval arrangements, and reporting/performance indicators. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions and shapes duties of their employees and stakeholders.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
