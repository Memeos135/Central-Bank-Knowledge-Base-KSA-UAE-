# Shariah Committee

Defined-term node for the Shariah committee, the body responsible for supervising and issuing binding decisions on the Shariah compliance of a bank's Islamic banking transactions. It covers composition and independence criteria, appointment and performance assessment via the nomination and remuneration committee, disclosure of member CVs, meeting frequency (at least quarterly), quorum and voting, attendance thresholds and minuting requirements, as well as its supervisory duties over Shariah compliance reports and internal Shariah audit findings. It binds banks conducting Islamic banking and their committee members.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
