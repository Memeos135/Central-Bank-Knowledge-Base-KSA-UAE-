# IT Steering Committee

A concept node covering the role of the Member Organization's IT steering committee within SAMA's IT Governance Framework, principally as a mandatory internal approval gate before any request to amend the Framework or to obtain a waiver from a control requirement is escalated to SAMA through the CEO or managing director. It sits alongside the CIO sign-off, business-owner accountability and risk-committee endorsement of IT risk assessment results and residual risk acceptance. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations, not SAMA itself; the Framework continues to apply in full while any update or waiver request is pending.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
