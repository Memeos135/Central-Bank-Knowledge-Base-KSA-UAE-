# Cyber Risk Monitoring and Review

Covers the final stage of the framework's cyber risk cycle: monitoring risk treatment and reviewing the effectiveness of cyber security controls on an ongoing basis, with compliance subject to periodic review and audit. Links risk acceptance and treatment decisions back to reporting lines such as the cyber security committee. Applies to Member Organizations and is evaluated through the maturity model.
