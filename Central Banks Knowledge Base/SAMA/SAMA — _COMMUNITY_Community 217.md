# Counterparty Exposure Analysis

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of counterparty credit risk (CCR) exposure measurement, specifically the breakdown of CCR exposures by the calculation approach used (e.g. SA-CCR, IMM) and the resulting EAD and RWA. It frames the entry point for how a bank evidences its chosen CCR methodology to SAMA and the market.

## How members connect

- Single-member community: the template functions as the summary layer from which the other CCR templates disaggregate exposures by approach, counterparty rating, collateral and CCP type.
- Its scope depends on the CCR definitions and approach eligibility set in the underlying capital adequacy framework; the template imposes a disclosure obligation, not a measurement rule.
- Completion obligations are periodic and formatted, so scope limits (which products and netting sets fall within CCR) must be read from the framework rather than the template itself.
