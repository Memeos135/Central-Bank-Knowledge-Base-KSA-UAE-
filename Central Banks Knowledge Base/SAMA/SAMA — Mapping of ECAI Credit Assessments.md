# Mapping of ECAI Credit Assessments

Covers how SAMA assigns eligible ECAI rating grades to the risk weights available under the standardized risk weighting framework, together with the mapping table banks may apply and SAMA's right to review and revise that mapping. It sits alongside the ECAI eligibility and disclosure requirements. It binds banks calculating standardized-approach credit risk-weighted assets using external ratings.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
