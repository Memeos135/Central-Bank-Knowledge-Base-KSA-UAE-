# Liquidity Risk Management

## Why this community

Prudential liquidity risk management obligations for banks: the governance, control, stress-testing and contingency-planning requirements that an institution must have in place and evidence to SAMA.

## How members connect

- The Rules on Liquidity Risk Management are the parent instrument; governance, internal controls, stress testing and the contingency funding plan are mandated components cross-referenced from it.
- Stress testing and the contingency funding plan form an obligation chain: scenario results must feed the triggers and actions in the plan, so a deficient stress-testing programme undermines plan adequacy.
- Internal controls (general and liquidity-specific) provide the assurance layer, linking this cluster to board/senior-management accountability and internal audit duties.
