# Material Functions

This node defines Material Functions as any function whose default or disruption could affect the finance company's activities, reputation or financial position, or that involves sharing, transferring, processing or storing consumer data and information. Outsourcing or renewing such functions, or making material contract changes, requires SAMA's written non-objection requested at least 30 working days in advance, with a prescribed minimum content in the request; SAMA may restrict, condition, or require modification or termination. Illustrative non-exhaustive examples include external and internal audit, customer care and complaints, IT/security and cloud data storage, brokerage and agency activity, HR provision, debt collection and archiving.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, data/credit info
