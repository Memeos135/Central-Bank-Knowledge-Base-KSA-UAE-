# Obligations of Real Estate Valuation Clients (SAMA 5525)

A SAMA circular addressed to banks, finance (including real estate finance) companies and insurance companies in their capacity as clients of real estate valuation, requiring them to deal only with valuers holding Taqeem (Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers) fellow membership and to govern the valuation process. It mandates a written valuer-selection policy and separation between financing/underwriting staff and the valuation process, and prohibits influencing valuation outcomes by coercion, inducement, fee withholding, blacklisting threats, supplying target values, prescribing valuation methods, setting inadequate timeframes, demanding disproportionate bank guarantees, or linking fees to volume of work. It also requires supplying sufficient asset information and setting acceptance criteria for financeable properties.
