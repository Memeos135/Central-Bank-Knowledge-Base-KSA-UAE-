# IRB CCR Exposure Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of CCR exposures for banks using internal ratings-based approaches, presented by PD band with associated LGD, maturity, EAD and RWA density.

## How members connect

- Single-member community whose use is conditional on SAMA approval to apply IRB; it is the IRB counterpart to the standardised-approach CCR disclosure.
- Definitions of PD, LGD and EAD are inherited from the credit risk framework, so the template cannot be read independently of the IRB parameter rules.
- It disaggregates the approach-level totals reported in the summary CCR exposure template.
