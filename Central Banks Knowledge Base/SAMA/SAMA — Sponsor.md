# Sponsor

This node collects the treatment of the "sponsor" concept across the credit risk framework: as a rated strength factor in specialised lending/project and real estate exposure slotting criteria, and as the party required to provide full liquidity and credit protection support at conduit or transaction level under the ABCP STC criteria. It establishes that where multiple sponsors support an ABCP programme, the majority of support must come from a single "main sponsor", with limited exception on credit deterioration. Relevant to banks acting as sponsors or investors in ABCP programmes and to banks slotting specialised lending exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
