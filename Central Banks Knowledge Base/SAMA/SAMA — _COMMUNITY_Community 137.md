# Payment Technology Products

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for payment acceptance infrastructure in KSA - approved local technology products, POS terminal technical standards, and extension of electronic acceptance to non-commercial sectors such as charities.

## How members connect

- Common regulatory object: the POS terminal and its supporting technology stack, so the products guide and the 4G upgrade instruction operate on the same defined equipment population.
- Obligation chain on acquirers/PSPs: the guide constrains which products may be deployed, the 4G circular imposes a migration/compliance deadline for existing estates.
- The charities measure is a scope extension - it pushes the same acceptance obligations onto a new beneficiary category, referencing the terminal standards rather than creating new ones.
- Cross-regime flag: onboarding charities as merchants raises KYB/merchant-as-customer duties under the AML/CTF regime, not addressed by the technical circulars.
