# Treatment of Internal Risk Transfers

Sets the capital treatment of internal risk transfers — internal written records of risk moved within the banking book, between the banking and trading books, or between trading desks. Transfers from the trading book to the banking book get no capital recognition; transfers from the banking book to the trading book are recognised only where strict conditions are met, including an exactly matching external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider for credit and equity risk, and use of a SAMA-approved dedicated internal risk transfer desk for general interest rate risk. Binds banks determining market risk and banking book capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
