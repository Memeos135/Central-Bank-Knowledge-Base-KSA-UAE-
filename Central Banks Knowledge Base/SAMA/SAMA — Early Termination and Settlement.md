# Early Termination and Settlement

The Events of Default and close-out provisions of the SAMA securities sale and purchase master agreement. It lists default triggers (failure to transfer or margin, Act of Insolvency, untrue representations, anticipatory repudiation, suspension or expulsion by a securities exchange or Competent Authority on financial-resource or credit-rating grounds, and a 30-day cure period for other breaches), and sets the mechanics for designating an Early Termination Date on up to 20 days' notice, including Automatic Early Termination if elected in Annex I. It binds Buyer and Seller and determines how outstanding Transactions are accelerated and valued on close-out.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk
