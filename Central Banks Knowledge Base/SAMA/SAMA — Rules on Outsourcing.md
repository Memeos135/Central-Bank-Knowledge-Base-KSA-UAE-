# Rules on Outsourcing

SAMA's Rules on Outsourcing (Version 2.0) set the governance, policy, due diligence, contractual, business continuity, monitoring, audit and documentation requirements for banks entering outsourcing arrangements, including annual prudential reporting and notification of regulatory breaches. They apply to banks licensed under the Banking Control Law, including local and foreign bank branches and banking subsidiaries in Saudi Arabia, and cover domestic, foreign, third-party and related-party providers. Insourcing under the bank's direct supervision and certain arrangements (market data vendors, clearing/settlement, correspondent banking, utilities) fall outside scope; SAMA retains unrestricted access to outsourcing-related records and data.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[SAMA — Regulation of Agent Banking|Regulation of Agent Banking]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If a bank appoints or renews a banking Agent, assess the arrangement under the Rules on Outsourcing as well as the Regulation of Agent Banking, since agency is a form of delegating activity to a third party. The Outsourcing Rules require board (or delegated authority) approval of an outsourcing policy covering Material business activities and a framework for managing all outsourcing arrangements, which supplies the governance layer the agent banking regime presupposes. Consequently, the periodic reassessment before contract renewal should be evidenced at the governance level required by the Outsourcing Rules, and an agent arrangement that is Material attracts the fuller approval and oversight obligations.
