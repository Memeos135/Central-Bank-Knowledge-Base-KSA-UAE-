# Simple Approach (Collateral)

Sets out the simple approach to credit risk mitigation, under which the counterparty's risk weight is substituted by the risk weight of the collateral instrument for the collateralized portion of an exposure. It imposes recognition conditions, notably that collateral be pledged for at least the life of the exposure and be marked to market and revalued at least every six months, within the wider collateral management and custodian-segregation requirements. It binds banks applying the standardized approach to credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
