# Scenario Analysis

A methodology section of SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing describing scenario analysis as the measurement of portfolio value changes arising from simultaneous moves in multiple risk factors, alongside sensitivity analysis. It sets expectations for scenario design: scenarios must reflect the bank's own business environment and risk profile, may be historical or hypothetical (each with stated limitations), must capture all material risk factors and both on- and off-balance sheet exposures, and must include at least one viability-threatening (reverse) scenario. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA, which are expected to select methods proportionate to their nature, size, complexity and risk profile.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
