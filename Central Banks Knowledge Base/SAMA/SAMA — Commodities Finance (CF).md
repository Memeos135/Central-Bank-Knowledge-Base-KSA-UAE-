# Commodities Finance (CF)

Defines commodities finance (CF) as short-term lending against reserves, inventories or receivables of exchange-traded commodities (e.g. crude oil, metals, crops), repaid from sale proceeds where the borrower has no independent repayment capacity. Unrated CF exposures attract a 100% risk weight under the standardized approach; issue-specific ratings, not issuer ratings, apply where available. Binds banks calculating credit risk capital on specialized lending under SAMA's framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
