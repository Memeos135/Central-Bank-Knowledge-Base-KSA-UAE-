# Appendix B: Liquidity Statement

Appendix B is the monthly Liquidity Statement return for DTFCs, listing the liquid asset and liability lines (cash, balances and time deposits with banks, finance and mortgage finance companies, treasury bills and bonds) used to evidence compliance with Chapter 5. Chapter 5 requires a board-approved liquidity risk management plan, a minimum 20% holding of liquid assets against deposit and short-term liabilities, statutory deposits with SAMA of at least 4% of deposit liabilities, a deposit liabilities cap of 15 times total capital with a one-month cure, and submission by the 15th business day of the following month. Binds every deposit-taking finance company.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
