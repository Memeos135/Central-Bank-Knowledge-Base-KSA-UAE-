# Agent Banking Regulation

## Why this community

The agent banking regime: a bank may deliver defined services through third-party agents but remains the regulated and accountable entity, including for customer due diligence, KYC and AML/CFT performed at the agent's counter. The cluster sets eligibility, vetting, permitted/prohibited scope and registration conditions for that arrangement.

## How members connect

- Instrument hierarchy: the Regulation of Agent Banking is the parent instrument, and the article-level nodes (eligibility, fit & proper, agent due diligence, bank obligations, permissible/prohibited activities, registry) are its operative provisions.
- Two distinct diligence layers that must not be conflated: diligence on the agent itself (eligibility plus fit & proper assessment of the agent/its principals) versus customer due diligence performed through the agent on the bank's customers.
- Non-delegable accountability: board responsibility and the bank's obligations article mean outsourcing delivery does not transfer AML/CFT or KYC liability; the bank answers for agent conduct.
- Scope boundary: permissible and prohibited activities define the outer limit of what may be agent-delivered, and the registry provision conditions lawful operation on notification/registration with the supervisor.
