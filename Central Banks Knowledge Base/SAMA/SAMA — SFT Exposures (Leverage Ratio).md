# SFT Exposures (Leverage Ratio)

Addresses securities financing transaction (SFT) exposures as a component of the leverage ratio exposure measure and the associated prudential disclosure obligations under SAMA's Basel-aligned framework. It concerns how repos, reverse repos, securities lending/borrowing and margin lending are measured and disclosed in leverage and key-metrics templates. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks at consolidated group level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
