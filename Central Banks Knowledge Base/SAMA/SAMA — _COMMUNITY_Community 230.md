# Net Stable Funding Ratio Template

## Why this community

Liquidity risk reporting under the Basel-based prudential regime: the standardised return by which banks evidence that stable funding sources cover the funding requirements of their assets and off-balance-sheet exposures.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a reporting template that operationalises the NSFR standard rather than creating substantive obligations of its own.
- Legally dependent on the underlying NSFR rules (available vs required stable funding factors and category definitions), which supply the meaning of each template line.
- Sits in the hierarchy as supervisory reporting instrumentation beneath the liquidity framework; breaches are assessed against the ratio rules, not the form.
