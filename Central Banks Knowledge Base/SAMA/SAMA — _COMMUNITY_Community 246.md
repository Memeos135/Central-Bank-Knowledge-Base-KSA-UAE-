# Commodities Finance Slotting Grades

## Why this community

Capital adequacy treatment of specialised lending: assigning supervisory slotting grades to commodities finance exposures where a bank cannot rely on internal PD estimates.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster sitting within the credit risk / IRB framework, where slotting criteria drive risk-weight assignment.
- Depends on upstream definitions of specialised lending sub-classes and the supervisory grade scale (strong through default) used across the slotting family.
