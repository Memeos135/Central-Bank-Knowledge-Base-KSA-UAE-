# Group of Connected Counterparties

Defines when two or more natural or legal persons must be aggregated as a single risk for large exposure purposes under SAMA's Large Exposure (LEX) Rules for Banks, on the basis of a control relationship, economic interdependence, or any other connection the bank assesses as constituting a single risk. It sets illustrative economic-interdependence indicators (contagion of financial difficulty, linked insolvency, common funding source), allows a bank to rebut connectedness by demonstrating to SAMA that the counterparty can find alternative partners or funding, and sets a 5% of eligible capital base threshold above which economic-interdependence screening is expected. It binds banks in scope of the LEX Rules and drives both the large exposure limits and regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
