# Anti-Fraud Rules for Finance Companies (circular 381000103246)

This node captures the earlier Anti-Fraud Rules for Finance Companies issued by SAMA circular no. 381000103246, which governed fraud prevention and control obligations of licensed finance companies. Per the covering circular to the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements, these rules are replaced by the new framework with effect from 1 April 2026, so they remain the operative standard for finance companies only until that date. Researchers should treat this as a superseded instrument and check the transition date when assessing which obligations applied at a given time.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, AML/CTF
