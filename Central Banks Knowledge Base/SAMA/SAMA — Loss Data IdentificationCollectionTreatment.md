# Loss Data Identification/Collection/Treatment

Concept node covering SAMA's minimum standards for identifying, collecting and treating internal operational loss data used as a direct input to the operational risk capital calculation under the standardised approach. It sets the general criteria (e.g. observation period, documented identification procedures, linkage to current business activities) and the consequence of non-compliance, namely capital of at least 100% of the Business Indicator Component, a possible supervisory Internal Loss Multiplier above 1, and mandatory Pillar 3 disclosure of the exclusion. It binds banks above the specified Business Indicator threshold, with disclosure of loss data and thresholds in the prescribed reporting templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
