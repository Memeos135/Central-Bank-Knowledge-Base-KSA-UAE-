# Table MR3 - Market Risk Under Simplified Standardised Approach

Pillar 3 disclosure template MR3 requiring semiannual, fixed-format reporting of market risk capital requirements computed under the simplified standardised approach (SSA), broken down by interest rate, equity, commodity and FX risk for outright products plus option risk under the simplified, delta-plus and scenario methods, and securitisation. It binds banks that use the SSA to determine their market risk capital requirements under SAMA's adoption of the Basel market risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
