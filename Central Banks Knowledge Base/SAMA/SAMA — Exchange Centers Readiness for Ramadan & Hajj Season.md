# Exchange Centers Readiness for Ramadan & Hajj Season

Circular issued under Article 14(b) of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business requiring exchange centres in Makkah, Madinah, King Abdulaziz International Airport and Hajj City in Jeddah to be fully staffed and operationally ready for the Ramadan and Hajj 1447H seasons. An annex lists minimum operating conditions, including adequate cash and denominations, suitable opening hours, crowd management, staff ID badges, prominent and system-linked exchange-rate displays, receipts for every transaction, customer-rights signage, POS availability, working CCTV, counterfeit-detection equipment, and reporting of unlicensed currency exchange to security authorities and SAMA. Binds the named exchange centres.
