# Cyber Kill Chain Methodology

Explains the Cyber Kill Chain as the conceptual attack model underpinning the framework, with seven stages from reconnaissance through weaponization, delivery, exploitation, installation, command and control, to actions on objective, and expects defensive measures at each stage. It also distinguishes red teaming (resilience against realistic attacks, TTP-based, detection and response focus) from penetration testing (vulnerability oversight, predefined scope, preventive controls). Used by Member Organizations and Red Teaming Providers to structure attack scenarios and the evaluation report.
