# Stage 3: Live Testing

Covers Stage 3 of the Regulatory Sandbox: permitted innovators test their products live under the temporary permission for a minimum of six months and up to twelve months. Successful testing is expected to inform SAMA's issuance or amendment of regulations, enabling the innovator to apply for a full regulatory licence. Relevant to FinTechs operating under a Sandbox LoA and to teams assessing the status of live-tested propositions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
