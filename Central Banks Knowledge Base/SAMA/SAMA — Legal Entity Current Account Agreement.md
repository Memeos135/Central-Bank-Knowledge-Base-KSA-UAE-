# Legal Entity Current Account Agreement

This node covers the standard-form current account opening agreement for legal persons (juristic entities) under the same SAMA model-forms circular, updated to insert a clause in the rights and obligations section allowing confidential disclosure of account data (including commercial registration or unified number, contact details and account number) to Saudi Payments for subscription to additional Instant Payments System services. The extracted context reproduces mainly the parties, identification and general terms sections of the model agreement. It binds all banks operating in the Kingdom, which must adopt the website-published version and complete compliance steps within the stated period.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, AML/CTF, data/credit info
