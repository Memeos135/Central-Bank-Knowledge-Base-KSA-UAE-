# Required Documents for BNPL License

Lists the documentation that must accompany a BNPL licence application, including the application letter and SAMA form, draft or existing articles of association and commercial register, organisational structure with departmental mandates, Fit and Proper forms for each founder and each board/sub-committee candidate, a feasibility study and business plan, and an irrevocable, auto-renewing bank guarantee equal to 20% of the minimum required capital issued to SAMA by a local bank. Documents are filed in soft copy per the checklist with originals produced on request. It binds BNPL licence Applicants and their proposed governance appointees.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
