# Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding

SAMA rules governing debt-based crowdfunding, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law framework, defining the activity, the licensed joint-stock Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company, the Platform, Institutional Beneficiaries, Participants and Eligible Participants (with net-asset, experience and certification tests). They set operational obligations including segregation of Participants' funds in a dedicated Finance Amount Management Account, prohibition of cash repayment, controls on automatic participation options, and disclosure/risk-acknowledgment duties toward Participants and Institutional Beneficiaries. They bind companies licensed to conduct debt-based crowdfunding in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
