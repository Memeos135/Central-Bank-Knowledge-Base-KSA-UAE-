# On-balance Sheet Netting

A concept node on on-balance sheet netting as recognised for regulatory capital purposes, and how it differs from accounting netting and from the incremental netting applied under the Basel/SAMA counterparty credit risk rules. It also covers how netting sets and margin agreements interact when a single margin agreement spans several netting sets, and how such differences must be reflected in Pillar 3 reconciliation templates (LI1/LI2). It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating exposure values, RWA and regulatory disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
