# Agent Customer Due Diligence

Covers the bank's due diligence on prospective and existing Agents (distinct from CDD on end customers): a fit-and-proper assessment of the Agent, its proprietors and managers, verification of legal status and address, moral/business/professional suitability, adverse credit bureau information, criminal records relating to ML/TF, fraud or integrity, experience, and sources of funds. It also requires defined internal roles for agent management, periodic reviews with early-warning indicators and corrective action, and ongoing monitoring of agents for AML/CFT, embezzlement and financial fraud. The duty binds the contracting bank, which must communicate required actions to agents and monitor their compliance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
