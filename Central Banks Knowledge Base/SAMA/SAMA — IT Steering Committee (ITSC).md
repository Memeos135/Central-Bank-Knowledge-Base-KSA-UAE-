# IT Steering Committee (ITSC)

This node covers the IT Steering Committee (ITSC), the board-mandated body to which a Member Organization's board may delegate IT governance, responsible for defining IT governance and setting IT strategy. Requirements include a board-approved ITSC charter (objectives, roles, quorum, at least quarterly meetings, minutes retention), representation by senior managers (CRO, CISO, compliance officer, business heads) and the CIO with internal audit as observer, plus appointment of a full-time, qualified Saudi-national CIO subject to SAMA non-objection. It binds boards and senior management of SAMA Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
