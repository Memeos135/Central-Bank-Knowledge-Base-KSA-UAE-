# Backtesting

Sets out backtesting requirements at bank-wide and trading desk level under SAMA's market risk standard, comparing one-day VaR against actual and hypothetical P&L over the prior 12 months. It defines the green/amber/red zone response framework, including capital add-ons for amber zone outcomes, an automatic multiplier increase or model disallowance in the red zone, documentation of all exceptions, and limited grounds for disregarding exceptions driven by non-modellable risk factors. Binds banks using internal models for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
