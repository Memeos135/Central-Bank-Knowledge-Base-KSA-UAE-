# Physical Security

## Why this community

Protection of premises, data centres, equipment and media from unauthorised physical access, damage or interference — the physical counterpart to logical security controls.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster covering the physical and environmental security control domain.
- Cross-references asset management (classification and handling of physical media and equipment) and HR (badging, visitor and termination controls).
- Subject to the same assurance loop: physical controls fall within the scope of cyber security audits and board-level risk reporting.
