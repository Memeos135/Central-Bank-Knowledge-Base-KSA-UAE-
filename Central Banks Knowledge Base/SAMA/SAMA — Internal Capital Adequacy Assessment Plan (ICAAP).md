# Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan (ICAAP)

Material within SAMA's Stress Testing Rules linking stress testing outputs to the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan and to remedial action requirements. It identifies the impact parameters to be assessed (asset quality, profitability, capital adequacy/CAR, liquidity), requires results and their assumptions, methodologies and limitations to be communicated to the board, senior management and SAMA, and requires realistic, time-bound remedial plans including capital buffer adequacy assessment. ICAAP submission is annual and does not apply to branches of foreign banks; ICAAP reporting is separate from and additional to stress testing reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
