# Credit Derivatives Exposures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of counterparty credit risk arising from credit derivative positions, covering protection bought and sold and the resulting exposures that must be reported to the market.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster: the counterparty credit risk (CCR) disclosure series, sitting at the template layer beneath the Pillar 3 disclosure framework and the underlying CCR capital treatment rules.
- Relies on defined terms shared across the CCR templates (notional amounts, protection bought/sold, netting) rather than on cross-references to other clusters.
- Obligation chain runs from the disclosure requirement (who must publish, at what frequency and format) to the fixed template content, which banks may not vary.
