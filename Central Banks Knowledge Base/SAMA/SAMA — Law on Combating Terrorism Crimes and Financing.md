# Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing

A concept node for the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing (Royal Decree M/21, 12/02/1439H) and its Implementing Regulations, cited throughout SAMA's AML/CTF guide as one of the two primary statutes underpinning institutional obligations (notably ongoing monitoring under Article 69 and suspicious reporting under Articles 70–71). It also establishes SAMA as a supervisory authority for the sector. It binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA, whose staff, directors and board members are exposed to the penalties it prescribes.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
