# Whistle Blowing Policy

## Why this community

Governance requirement for financial institutions to maintain a whistle blowing framework enabling protected internal reporting of misconduct, fraud and regulatory breaches.

## How members connect

- Standalone policy instrument that attaches to the governance/internal control regime: board responsibility, independence of the reporting channel, and protection of the reporting person.
- Feeds obligation chains in AML/CTF and fraud regimes, where internally raised concerns may trigger suspicious transaction reporting and notification to SAMA.
- Interacts with confidentiality and banking secrecy duties, which must be read as not overriding the protected disclosure route.
