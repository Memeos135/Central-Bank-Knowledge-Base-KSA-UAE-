# Master Agreement for Securities Sale and Purchase

SAMA-issued standard Master Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Securities — a Shari'ah-compatible repo-style framework under which a Seller sells securities to a Buyer with unilateral undertakings to re-sell/re-purchase at a Second Purchase Price. This node carries the core definitional and close-out valuation machinery (Default Market Value, Net Value, Deliverable/Receivable Securities, Transaction Costs) used by the non-Defaulting Party after an Early Termination Date. It binds the two contracting counterparties (typically banks and other market participants transacting under SAMA-standardised documentation) rather than imposing supervisory obligations directly.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, banking prudential
