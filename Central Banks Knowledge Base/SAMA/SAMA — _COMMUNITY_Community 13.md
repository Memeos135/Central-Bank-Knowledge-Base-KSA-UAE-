# Counter-Fraud Framework

## Why this community

SAMA's counter-fraud regime: the obligation on supervised entities to establish governed, risk-assessed fraud prevention, detection and response capability, measured against a maturity model.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the Counter-Fraud Framework and its Fundamental Requirements down to the four domains (Govern, Prevent, Detect, Respond), with the Maturity Model as the assessment yardstick.
- Governance chain: board/Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, Head of Counter-Fraud and a dedicated Counter-Fraud Department own fraud risk appetite, the fraud risk assessment and KRIs.
- Sector-specific overlay: the Anti-Fraud Rules for Finance Companies sit alongside the Framework, so finance companies face both general and sectoral requirements.
- Cross-regime hooks: supervisory notification duties, the Sectorial Anti-Fraud Committee, the IT Governance Framework, authentication standards and the Whistle Blowing Policy — note the distinction between fraud reporting and AML/CTF suspicious transaction reporting.
