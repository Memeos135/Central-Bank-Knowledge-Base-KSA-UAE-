# Land ADC Exposures

Defines "land acquisition, development and construction" (ADC) exposures as one of the three components of the real estate exposure class under the standardized approach in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, alongside regulatory real estate and other real estate exposures. Real estate is defined as immovable property (land, including agricultural and forest land, and attached structures such as buildings). Binds banks applying the standardized approach to credit risk under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
