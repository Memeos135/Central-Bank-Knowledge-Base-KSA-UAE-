# Old Device Handling Controls

## Why this community

Supervisory controls on how local banks dispose of, replace or otherwise deal with old or damaged equipment, addressing operational integrity and the risk of data or asset leakage on decommissioning.

## How members connect

- Isolated instruction-type instrument addressed to banks; it operates as a conduct/operational-risk control rather than a licensing rule.
- Interacts with broader governance and operational-risk expectations, and with customer data protection duties where decommissioned devices may retain information.
- No internal cross-references in the cluster; read it as a discrete compliance obligation with its own scope.
