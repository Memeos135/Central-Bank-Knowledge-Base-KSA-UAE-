# Musharakah Contract

Defined-term node within the PSIA Rules describing Musharakah as a partnership where partners contribute capital to an enterprise, sharing profits as agreed and losses in proportion to capital contributed. It sits alongside the PSIA product governance requirements on disclosure, terms and conditions, and contract form that state parties' rights and loss-bearing circumstances. Relevant to banks and Islamic windows structuring Shari'ah-compliant products.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, other
