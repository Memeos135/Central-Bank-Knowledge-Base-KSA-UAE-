# Securitisation Exposures

This node sets out the default risk charge (DRC) treatment of securitisation exposures under SAMA's market risk standard, covering gross and net jump-to-default (JTD) calculation for non-correlation trading portfolio securitisations. It specifies that LGD is not separately applied (the market value of the tranche is the JTD), that offsetting is confined to tranches of the same underlying asset pool, and that maturity-based and decomposition-based netting is permitted only on strict conditions, with no hedging recognised across buckets. It binds SAMA-licensed banks holding securitisation positions in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
