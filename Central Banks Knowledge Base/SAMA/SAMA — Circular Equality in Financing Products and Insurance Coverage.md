# Circular: Equality in Financing Products and Insurance Coverage

A SAMA circular requiring equal treatment of all segments of society in access to financing products and in insurance coverage under finance lease contracts, invoking the fair-treatment and non-discrimination principles in the banking and finance customer protection principles. It cross-refers to SAMA's instruction to insurers prohibiting refusal of compulsory or comprehensive motor cover based on the age of the actual user of a leased vehicle, and correspondingly bars discrimination against finance customers on that ground. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
