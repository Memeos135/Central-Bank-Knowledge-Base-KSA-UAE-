# ECAI Usage and Multiple Ratings

Sets the eligibility criteria SAMA applies when recognising External Credit Assessment Institutions (resources, credibility, conflict-of-interest disclosure, cooperation with SAMA) and the rules banks must follow when using ECAI ratings for standardised-approach risk weighting. Key obligations: consistent use of chosen ECAIs and global rating scales, no cherry-picking, and prescribed treatment where two or more ratings map to different risk weights (higher of two, or higher of the two lowest). Binds banks using the standardised approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
