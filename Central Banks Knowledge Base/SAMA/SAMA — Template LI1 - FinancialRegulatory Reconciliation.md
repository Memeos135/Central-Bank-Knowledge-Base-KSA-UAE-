# Template LI1 - Financial/Regulatory Reconciliation

A Pillar 3 linkage template requiring banks to reconcile the carrying values in published financial statements with the balance sheet under the regulatory scope of consolidation, and to map those amounts to the credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation and market risk frameworks. It is the first step in the disclosure chain connecting accounting figures to regulatory capital (with CC2 and CC1) and to exposure-level differences shown in LI2. Mandatory for banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
