# Products and Services Policy Requirements

Sets the minimum content of a bank's internal policies and procedures for offering new products and services — integration into the governance, risk and internal control framework, defined stakeholder roles, approval and delegation parameters, pilot/testing phases — together with internal audit, product development function, ongoing monitoring, complaint handling, corrective action, documentation, retention and the annual product report to SAMA. It also frames the notification/non-objection gateway. It binds licensed banks and their control and business functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, banking prudential
