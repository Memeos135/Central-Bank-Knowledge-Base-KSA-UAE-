# Workout Unit

Requires banks to establish a dedicated Workout Department/Section/Unit, independent of business and loan-originating units to avoid conflicts of interest, and staffed with analytical, legal and financial-analysis skills to manage NPL resolution. Sets performance-management requirements: appraisal and compensation structures aligned with the NPL strategy and code of conduct, not based solely on NPL volume reduction, and including qualitative measures such as negotiation competency and quality of proposals. Related provisions cover mandatory time-bound transfer of loans from originating units to the Workout Unit, borrower appeal forms, and workout fee policy including impact analysis on borrower cash flows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
