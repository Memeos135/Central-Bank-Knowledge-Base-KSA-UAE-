# Hierarchy of Securitization Approaches

Concept node for the ordering rule that determines which securitisation capital approach a bank must apply to a given securitisation exposure, sitting within SAMA's credit risk/securitisation framework. It draws on the framework's definitional scaffolding (securitisation and resecuritisation exposures, underlying pools, originator/investor roles) and on the treatment of full, pro rata and tranched credit protection, where protection buyer and protection provider each calculate capital by reference to the hierarchy. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating risk-based capital requirements for banking book securitisation positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
