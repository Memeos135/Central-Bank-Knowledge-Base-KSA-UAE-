# Amendments to Finance Lease Implementing Regulations

A circular notifying a Governor's decision amending specific articles of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law (issued under the Law's Article 27 powers, agreed with the Minister of Justice). The amendments cover the lessor's duty to obtain a SAMA no-objection letter for finance lease products aimed at individuals or microfinance beneficiaries before launch to ensure fairness of terms, the requirement that contract signing take place at the lessor's premises with the lessor bearing the related costs, and a change to a paragraph of Article 25 referencing the Enforcement Law. Binds banks, finance companies and finance lease contract registration companies in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
