# Credit Record

Defined term in the Implementing Regulation of the Credit Information Law: the credit record (السجل الائتماني) is the report issued by a licensed credit information company containing a consumer's credit information. The Regulation devotes a dedicated chapter to the credit record, and links it to adverse decisions and negative information reported by Members against the consumer's interest. Relevant to credit bureaus, Members relying on records for credit decisions, and consumers exercising objection rights.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection
