# Simplified Standardised Approach

A definitional/reporting node explaining what counts as the "standardised approach" for each risk type (credit, counterparty credit, CVA, securitisation, market and operational risk) when banks populate SAMA's capital comparison template (modelled RWA vs RWA under full standardised approach, plus residual RWA). It fixes how banks split RWA between SAMA-approved internal models (IRB, IMA) and standardised calculations, and addresses the phase-in for equity exposures where IRB use is withdrawn. It binds SAMA-licensed banks subject to Pillar 1 capital and Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
