# Real Estate Appraisal Fees in Financing Contracts

SAMA circular on real estate appraisal fees in retail real estate finance contracts. Financiers may not deduct appraisal fees from the customer before the customer has received preliminary financing approval, and must inform the customer — and obtain an acknowledgement — before contracting that the fee is non-refundable if the financing is not completed for reasons attributable to the customer. It binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.
