# Mandatory Instructions for Individual Real Estate Finance

A SAMA circular imposing mandatory pre-contractual conduct requirements on banks and real estate finance companies before extending residential (individual) real estate finance. It sets out affordability/suitability assessment against approved credit-granting policies, a documented plain-language explanation of terms and risks by a competent officer, a written financing offer valid for a minimum period with a full document pack the customer may take away, a mandatory briefing by an independent credit advisor (not the officer who dealt with or made the offer), and a minimum reflection period before signing. Concluding an individual real estate finance contract is prohibited unless all steps are completed and evidenced in the financing file, with SAMA verification and enforcement action for breach.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
