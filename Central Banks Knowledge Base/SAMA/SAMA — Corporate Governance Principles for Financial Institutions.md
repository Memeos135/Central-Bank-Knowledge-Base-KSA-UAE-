# Corporate Governance Principles for Financial Institutions

Third edition (Dhu al-Qi'dah 1442H / June 2021) of SAMA's Key Principles of Governance for financial institutions under its supervision, replacing the earlier bank governance principles and mandatory from 01/01/2022. It sets definitions (Financial Institution, Board, Senior/Executive Management, executive, non-executive and independent member, relatives, major shareholders, related parties) and seven principles covering board qualifications, composition and appointment, board and executive management responsibilities, board committees, shareholder rights, and disclosure and transparency, with an annex on money exchange sector governance. It binds local banks, finance/real estate refinance/leasing-registration and finance-support companies, money exchangers, payment and fintech companies and credit bureaus — insurance and reinsurance companies are excluded.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
