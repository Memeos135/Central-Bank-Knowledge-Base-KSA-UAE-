# Unified Complaint Response Template - SAMA Cares

SAMA circular mandating use of a unified response template for complaints received through the "SAMA Cares" system, annexed in three forms: requesting missing documents, informing the customer of the outcome and corrective action taken, and responding to complaints escalated to SAMA with supporting documents and reference to the instruction, contract or terms relied on. It builds on the earlier service level agreement instructions for complaint handling and also requires firms to strengthen the customer care function, maintain and measure an internal SLA ensuring complaints are resolved within prescribed timeframes. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
