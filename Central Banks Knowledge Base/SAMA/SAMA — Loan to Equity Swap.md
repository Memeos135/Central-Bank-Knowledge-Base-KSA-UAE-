# Loan to Equity Swap

Covers debt/loan-to-equity conversion and related owner-contribution measures — conversion of owner loans into equity or another subordinated form, capital increases, personal guarantees, additional collateral, sale of excess assets and financial-indicator covenants — treated as additional measures that strengthen the bank's position rather than stand-alone restructuring solutions. It is presented with the borrower-type/workout-measure mapping for viable and marginal borrowers. Relevant to banks assessing equity participation and owner support as part of a workout package.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
