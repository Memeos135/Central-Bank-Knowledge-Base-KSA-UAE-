# Economic Cities Ownership Documents

## Why this community

Acceptance of ownership documentation issued by the Economic Cities Authority for property-related transactions, relevant to collateral, title verification and customer documentation.

## How members connect

- Single circular that broadens the set of legally acceptable title evidence, so it operates as an amendment to documentation requirements in lending and real estate finance practice.
- Cross-reads to KYC/documentation and collateral-perfection expectations, since acceptability of the document determines whether security can be relied on.
