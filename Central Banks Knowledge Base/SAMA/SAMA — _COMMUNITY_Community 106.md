# Default Risk Capital

## Why this community

The default-risk and residual-risk components of the standardised market risk framework: capturing jump-to-default loss in trading book positions and adding capital for risks not captured by delta, vega or curvature charges.

## How members connect

- The Standardised Approach is the hierarchical parent; the default risk capital requirement and the residual risk add-on are its mandated components alongside the sensitivities-based method.
- The DRC charge decomposes by portfolio type — non-securitisations, securitisations, and the correlation trading portfolio — each applying the same jump-to-default gross/net and hedge benefit ratio mechanics with different bucketing.
- Shared defined terms (jump-to-default exposure, hedge benefit ratio, securitisation exposure, correlation trading portfolio) create the cross-references; the CTP definition in particular determines which DRC sub-regime applies.
- Specific risk and credit derivative offsetting rules supply the legacy/offsetting logic that constrains how hedges may reduce the default charge.
