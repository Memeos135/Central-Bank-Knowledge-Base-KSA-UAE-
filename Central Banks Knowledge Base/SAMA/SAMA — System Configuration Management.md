# System Configuration Management

Control requirements obliging Member Organizations to define, approve, implement and communicate a configuration management process that keeps reliable and accurate records of configuration items (CIs) for their information assets. It covers roles and responsibilities, identification and criticality of CIs against supporting business processes, interrelationships between CIs, periodic verification, and maintenance of a configuration management database (CMDB). The process must also be monitored and periodically evaluated.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
