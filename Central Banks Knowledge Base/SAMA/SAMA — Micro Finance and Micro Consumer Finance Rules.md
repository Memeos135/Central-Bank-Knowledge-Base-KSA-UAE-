# Micro Finance and Micro Consumer Finance Rules

Node covering the repeal and absorption of the standalone micro finance and micro consumer finance regimes: the circular cancels the Rules for Practising Micro Finance Activity and the Rules Regulating Micro Consumer Finance Companies, folding both into the amended Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. Micro finance and micro consumer finance are now defined activities within the Regulation, subject to the controls in its Article 9. Finance companies were granted a transition period (stated in the circular) to align their positions.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
