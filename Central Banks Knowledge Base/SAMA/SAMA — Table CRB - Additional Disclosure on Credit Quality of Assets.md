# Table CRB - Additional Disclosure on Credit Quality of Assets

Table CRB is the additional qualitative and quantitative disclosure on the credit quality of assets, sitting in the general credit risk block of SAMA's Pillar 3 framework alongside Table CRA, Templates CR1 and CR2 and Table CRB-A. It supplements the quantitative default and impairment templates with the bank's definitions and breakdowns underpinning reported credit quality. It binds banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure framework; the section scope excludes securitisation positions and counterparty credit risk, which are disclosed elsewhere.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
