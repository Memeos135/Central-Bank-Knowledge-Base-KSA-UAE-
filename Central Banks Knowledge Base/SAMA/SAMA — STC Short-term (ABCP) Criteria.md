# STC Short-term (ABCP) Criteria

Covers the STC criteria for short-term securitizations, i.e. exposures to ABCP conduits and to transactions financed by such conduits, which must be met at conduit and/or transaction level for the alternative capital treatment to apply. Related context addresses use of short-term external ratings, their issue-specific nature, ECAI eligibility, and the interaction with preferential treatment for short-term bank exposures. Binds SAMA-supervised banks investing in, sponsoring or providing facilities to ABCP programmes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
