# Template CCyB1 - Geographical Distribution of Credit Exposures

A Pillar 3 template requiring disclosure of the geographical distribution of private sector credit exposures used to compute the bank-specific countercyclical capital buffer rate. It binds banks subject to a countercyclical buffer requirement, but only where they have exposures in jurisdictions with a buffer rate above zero; disclosure is semiannual with a flexible format adaptable to domestic implementation. Banks should allocate exposures on an ultimate risk basis where possible and explain the geographical allocation methodology used.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
