# Template CVA2: Full BA-CVA

A fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering risk-weighted assets for credit valuation adjustment risk computed under the full version of the basic approach (BA-CVA), broken into the reduced component, the hedged component and the total. It is mandatory, on a semiannual basis, for banks whose CVA RWA is measured in whole or part under the full BA-CVA, populated only with amounts from netting sets under that approach, and it cross-references the counterparty credit risk capital rules and the overall RWA template. Binds SAMA-supervised banks that meet the CVA materiality threshold set in the revised CCR capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
