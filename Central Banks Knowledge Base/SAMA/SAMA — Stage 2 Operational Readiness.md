# Stage 2: Operational Readiness

Sets out Stage 2 of the Regulatory Sandbox, in which an applicant holding a Sandbox initial approval letter must demonstrate compliance with the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria (AC) within roughly 120 days, covering matters such as fit and proper approvals for management, shareholder approvals and a three-year financial model. The initial approval letter allows company formation/update with the Ministry of Commerce as a FinTech but expressly does not permit commencement of operations or customer onboarding. Completion of the AC, monitored by the Sandbox Risk Management Unit, results in the temporary permission (Letter of Authorisation).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
