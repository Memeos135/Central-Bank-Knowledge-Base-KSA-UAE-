# Credit Information Law

## Why this community

The credit information regime governing licensed credit bureaus, the content and handling of credit records, and the dispute/violation mechanism available to data subjects and members.

## How members connect

- The Implementing Regulation is the parent instrument under the Credit Information Law and defines the licensed Credit Information Company and the Credit Record it maintains.
- Obligation chain: consent and accuracy duties of members and bureaus feed the content of the credit record, which in turn grounds the data subject's correction and objection rights.
- The Committee for Violations and Credit Information Disputes is the designated adjudicatory forum, making it the escalation endpoint for record-accuracy and unlawful-disclosure complaints.
- Interacts with consumer protection and data privacy expectations, and with lender affordability/credit assessment duties that rely on bureau data.
