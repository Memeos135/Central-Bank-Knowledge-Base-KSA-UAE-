# Circular: Determining Finance Company Holidays

SAMA circular fixing official holiday periods for finance companies under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation: four days for Eid al-Fitr beginning after 29 Ramadan (Umm al-Qura calendar), four days for Eid al-Adha beginning on the Day of Arafah, and National Day with defined weekend substitution rules. Any change to Eid holiday dates will be circulated separately by SAMA. It binds all finance companies operating in the Kingdom and their branches.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
