# Profit and Loss Attribution (PLA) Test

Node covering the Profit and Loss Attribution (PLA) test within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk, which compares a trading desk's risk-theoretical P&L against its hypothetical P&L to verify that the desk's risk management model adequately captures the risks driving actual valuation changes. It sets the gateway that determines whether a desk may use the internal models approach (IMA) or must fall back to the standardised approach, using green/amber/red zone outcomes from two statistical metrics. It binds SAMA-supervised banks operating trading desks seeking or maintaining IMA approval for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
