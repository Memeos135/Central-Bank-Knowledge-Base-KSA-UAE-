# SAMA Logo Prohibition

## Why this community

Restrictions on use of SAMA's name, logo and identity by supervised entities, addressing implied endorsement and consumer-misleading risk in marketing and communications.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster; the prohibition is self-contained and directly enforceable against licensed entities.
- Conceptually adjacent to consumer protection and advertising-conduct obligations, since the harm addressed is misleading representation of regulatory endorsement.
- Complements the supervisory-confidentiality theme: both limit how an entity may externally invoke its relationship with the supervisor.
