# Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (Duplicate)

Duplicate publication of SAMA's Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (First Edition), setting the minimum requirements for implementing UN Security Council sanctions on counter-terrorism/terrorist financing and on preventing WMD proliferation and its financing. The Rules cover definitions, governance and institutional responsibilities, screening controls, sanctions list management, alert handling and escalation, freezing/unfreezing, record keeping, and training. They apply mandatorily to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions and supersede the earlier SAMA circulars on UNSCR implementation guidance.
