# Government Support Calculation for Responsible Financing Circular

A SAMA circular reaffirming how government housing support is treated under the Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals when assessing affordability. It confirms that government subsidies are excluded from a customer's monthly income except periodic cash support paid by the Ministry of Municipal, Rural Affairs and Housing or the Real Estate Development Fund (non-cash or non-recurring support remains excluded), and that the applicable debt-burden ratio for such beneficiaries is computed on total monthly income where the support is paid monthly. It binds banks, finance companies and credit information companies.
