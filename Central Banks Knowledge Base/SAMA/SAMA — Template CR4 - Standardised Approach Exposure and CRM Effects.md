# Template CR4 - Standardised Approach Exposure and CRM Effects

Template CR4 is the standardised-approach disclosure showing credit risk exposures before and after credit conversion factors and credit risk mitigation, with resulting RWA and RWA density. The surrounding instructions define the standardised approach for these purposes (simple or comprehensive CRM approach with standard supervisory haircuts, inclusion of failed trades and non-DvP transactions) and the split between modelled and standardised portfolios. Applies to banks using the standardised approach under the Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
