# Correlation Trading Portfolio (CTP)

The Correlation Trading Portfolio is a defined subset of securitisation and related hedging positions treated separately within market risk capital, notably in credit spread risk (CSR securitisations (CTP)) bucketing, correlation parameters and curvature aggregation. Its distinct correlation treatment (name-based rather than basis/tenor) changes how capital is computed for those positions. Relevant to banks holding securitisation and correlation trading exposures in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
