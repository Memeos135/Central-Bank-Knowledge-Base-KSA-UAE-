# Financial Institutions Service Fee Guide (Duplicate)

Duplicate instance of the Financial Institutions Service Fee Guide circular: the guide itself (general provisions, tariff for basic services to individual customers, criteria for setting fees, final provisions) together with the amended administrative-fee texts in the Consumer Finance Regulations, the Financial Consumer Protection conduct rules, and the standard individual consumer finance contract. Key operative content is the requirement to follow SAMA's administrative fee instructions, the bar on deducting administrative fees before contract signature (with a real estate appraisal exception after preliminary approval), and the fee ceiling of the lower of SAR 5,000 or 1% of the finance amount. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions, including banks and finance companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
