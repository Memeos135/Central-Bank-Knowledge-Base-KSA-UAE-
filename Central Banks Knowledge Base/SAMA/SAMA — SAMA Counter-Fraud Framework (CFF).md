# SAMA Counter-Fraud Framework (CFF)

This is SAMA's Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements (Version 1.0, April 2025), issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law and circulated to finance companies and payment service providers operating in the Kingdom. It sets risk-based control requirements across counter-fraud governance and risk management, prevention, detection, response, counter-fraud technology, and additional controls for products and services of elevated fraud risk, with board-level accountability, a quarterly Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, and coordination between counter-fraud, cyber security and financial crime functions. The covering circular imposes a compliance pathway: gap assessment and board-approved roadmap submitted by end July 2025, quarterly status reporting from Q3 2025, full compliance by early 2026, and an internal audit compliance report by end Q2 2026, backed by SAMA inspection visits.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, payments, AML/CTF
