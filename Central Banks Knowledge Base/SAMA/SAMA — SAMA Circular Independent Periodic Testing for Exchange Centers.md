# SAMA Circular: Independent Periodic Testing for Exchange Centers

Arabic SAMA circular directing exchange centres to comply with the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business by conducting an independent test at least annually to assess the centre's operations, automated systems and adherence to internal policies and procedures, to be completed no later than end of July each calendar year. It also requires the external auditor's internal control systems report to include, as a minimum, any findings on the centre's compliance with those Rules. Binding on exchange centres operating in the Kingdom, with acknowledgement of receipt within one week and enforcement action for non-compliance.
