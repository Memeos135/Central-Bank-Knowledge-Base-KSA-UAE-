# SAMA Circular: Providing POS Service at Exchange Centers

Arabic SAMA circular requiring all exchange centres and companies in the Kingdom to provide and activate point-of-sale (POS) service for customers with immediate effect, following SAMA's development of a unified pricing model for POS transactions made with mada cards at exchange centres. Issued under the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business; non-compliance exposes the entity to regulatory penalties.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
