# SCCR - Minimum Capital Requirements for CCR and CVA

SCCR is the SAMA-adopted standard covering minimum capital requirements for counterparty credit risk (SCCR3–SCCR10) and the credit valuation adjustment risk charge (SCCR11), referenced in capital and output floor reporting to identify which RWA and capital charges belong in the CCR and CVA rows rather than the general credit risk rows. It defines the perimeter between counterparty exposure treatment and ordinary credit risk treatment. It binds banks computing regulatory capital for derivatives and securities financing exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
