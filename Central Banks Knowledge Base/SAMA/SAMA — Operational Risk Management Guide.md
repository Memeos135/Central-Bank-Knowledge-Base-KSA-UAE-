# Operational Risk Management Guide

SAMA's Operational Risk Management Guide for banks in the Kingdom, aimed at directors and senior management, explaining operational risk as pure risk (loss or no loss) distinct from financial risk and the techniques to control and finance it. It defines the governance architecture — Operational Risk Manager reporting to the Managing Director/General Manager, an Operational Risk Management Committee, a Board-approved and annually reviewed Operational Risk Management Plan, risk assessment and control matrices, risk financing and loss retention policies, and penalty/reward incentives — and is intended to be read with SAMA's internal control, disaster recovery and physical security guidelines.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
