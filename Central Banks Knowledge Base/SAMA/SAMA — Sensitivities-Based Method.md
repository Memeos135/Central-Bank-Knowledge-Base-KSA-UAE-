# Sensitivities-Based Method

Describes the sensitivities-based method component of the standardised approach for market risk, built on delta, vega and curvature risk measures applied to regulatory risk factors, with risk-weighted sensitivities aggregated using prescribed correlation parameters. Banks must compute the capital requirement under three correlation scenarios to reflect stressed conditions, and the method sits alongside the default risk charge and residual risk add-on. Binds SAMA-supervised banks using the standardised market risk approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
