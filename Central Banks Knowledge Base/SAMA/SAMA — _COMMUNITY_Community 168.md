# Exchange Company Regulations

## Why this community

Supervisory requirements imposed on exchange (money changing) companies, covering workforce nationalisation targets and periodic financial and internal control reporting to SAMA.

## How members connect

- Both circulars are addressed to the same licensed population — exchange companies — and form part of the ongoing licence-condition set rather than one-off approvals.
- Shared supervisory logic: SAMA uses reporting obligations and staffing/Saudization conditions as continuing compliance measures enforceable against the licence.
- The internal control report requirement links to the governance and AML/CTF control expectations applicable to exchange businesses, which are treated as higher-risk cash-intensive entities.
