# Effective Notional

Concept node for the effective notional of a derivative trade — the adjusted notional scaled by the maturity factor and supervisory delta — which feeds the add-on calculation under the standardised approach to counterparty credit risk. It governs trade-, entity-, maturity-bucket- and hedging-set-level aggregation for both margined and unmargined netting sets. Relevant to banks computing counterparty credit risk capital under SAMA's minimum capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
