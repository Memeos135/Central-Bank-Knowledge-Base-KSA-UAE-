# Real Estate Exposures

## Why this community

Credit risk capital treatment of real estate exposures under the standardised approach: how an exposure is classified as real estate, when it qualifies as regulatory real estate, and how risk weights follow from collateral value and borrower repayment source.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy of defined terms: the real estate exposure class is the parent category, with regulatory real estate, land/ADC exposures and defaulted exposures as mutually exclusive sub-classifications carrying different risk weights.
- Qualification as regulatory real estate is the gateway condition for the LTV-based treatment; failing the criteria pushes the exposure into harsher buckets.
- LTV is the shared measurement input feeding both the whole loan approach and the loan splitting approach, which are alternative methods for converting the same ratio into risk weights.
- Default status overrides the ordinary regulatory real estate treatment, creating a cross-reference chain from classification to defaulted exposure rules.
