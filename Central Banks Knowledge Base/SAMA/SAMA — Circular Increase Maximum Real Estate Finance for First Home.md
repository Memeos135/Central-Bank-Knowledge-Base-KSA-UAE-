# Circular: Increase Maximum Real Estate Finance for First Home

SAMA circular amending the maximum loan-to-value ratio under Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law, raising the cap for citizens acquiring a first home to 90% of the dwelling value. Banks remain at 70% and real estate finance companies at 85% for second and subsequent homes for all beneficiaries. It binds SAMA-licensed banks and real estate finance companies, and reiterates risk-policy review, credit concentration monitoring and affordability/repayment-capacity verification before granting finance.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, consumer protection
