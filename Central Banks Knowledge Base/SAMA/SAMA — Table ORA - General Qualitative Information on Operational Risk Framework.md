# Table ORA - General Qualitative Information on Operational Risk Framework

Table ORA is the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on a bank's operational risk management framework, requiring description of policies and governance structure, the operational risk measurement system, internal and board reporting, and risk mitigation/transfer (including insurance and outsourcing policy). It is mandatory for all banks, annual, and flexible in format. It binds banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework and links operational risk governance to disclosed capital outcomes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
