# Template TLAC2 - Material Subgroup Creditor Ranking

Pillar 3 template showing, on a legal entity basis, the creditor hierarchy of each material subgroup entity that has issued internal TLAC to a G-SIB resolution entity, with nominal amounts of excluded liabilities, TLAC-eligible liabilities and their residual maturity bands. Its purpose is to let creditors assess their ranking relative to internal TLAC. Mandatory for G-SIBs, semiannually, from the TLAC conformance date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
