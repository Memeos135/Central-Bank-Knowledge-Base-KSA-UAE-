# Countercyclical Capital Buffer

## Why this community

Macroprudential capital buffer setting — the countercyclical capital buffer rate applied to banks' capital adequacy requirements.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular instrument: sets or confirms the CCyB rate, operating as an add-on to the prudential capital adequacy regime rather than a self-contained rulebook.
- Its consequence is read together with capital adequacy and risk management obligations found in the broader prudential cluster.
