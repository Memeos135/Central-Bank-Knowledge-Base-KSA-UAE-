# RWA for Market Risk

This node addresses the market risk element of risk-weighted assets within the output floor and capital reporting framework, sitting alongside credit, counterparty credit, CVA, settlement, securitisation and operational risk components. Its function is to identify which market risk RWA and capital requirements are computed under modelled versus standardised approaches when deriving the floor comparison. It applies to banks subject to SAMA's minimum risk-based capital and output floor requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
