# Restructuring Agreement

Concept node on the restructuring agreement as the principal document governing a workout, setting out how the borrower will restructure debt and operations and how and when creditors will be repaid. It notes there is no standard form (a letter may suffice for simple rescheduling, while changes to maturity, principal or profit/interest may require a new or amending agreement) and lists the minimum content a typical agreement should cover. Directed at banks and their legal functions handling distressed borrowers, with simplified documentation expectations for MSMEs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
