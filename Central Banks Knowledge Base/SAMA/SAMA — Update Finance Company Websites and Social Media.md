# Update Finance Company Websites and Social Media

An August 2020 SAMA circular directing finance companies to keep their websites continuously updated, to verify (or otherwise delete) their social media accounts, and to add and maintain branch geolocations and business hours on mapping platforms such as Google Maps. Compliance was required within one month, with the steps taken to be reported to SAMA's communications officer within two weeks. Issued under SAMA's supervisory mandate over finance companies and framed as a customer-protection measure.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
