# Table CCRA - Qualitative Disclosure Related to CCR

Table CCRA is the qualitative disclosure requirement heading SAMA's counterparty credit risk (CCR) section of the Pillar 3 framework, which covers all banking book and trading book exposures attracting a CCR charge, including exposures to central counterparties. It introduces the full CCR template set (CCR1, CCR3–CCR8) and binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure regime. Useful as the entry point for locating which CCR disclosures apply and their scope boundaries against credit risk and securitisation sections.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
