# Template CR3: CRM Techniques Overview

Node for Template CR3, the Pillar 3 overview of credit risk mitigation techniques, disclosing carrying amounts of unsecured exposures versus exposures secured by collateral, financial guarantees or credit derivatives. It defines how multi-secured and over- or under-collateralised exposures are allocated across the columns and includes worked allocation scenarios; surrounding context also covers related credit risk templates (CR1, CCR4). It is mandatory for banks within the SAMA Pillar 3 disclosure scope.
