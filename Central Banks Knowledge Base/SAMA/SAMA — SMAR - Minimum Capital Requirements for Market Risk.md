# SMAR - Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk

Node for SAMA's market risk minimum capital requirements (SMAR) as referenced within the Basel-aligned Pillar 3 disclosure and capital framework documentation. It sits alongside the capital adequacy rules that determine risk-weighted assets and regulatory capital deductions, and is cross-referenced in disclosure templates covering RWA by risk type and capital distribution constraints. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating and disclosing Pillar 1 capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
