# Red Team

Defines the Red Team role within SAMA's Financial Sector ethical red teaming framework — the offensive testing party (staffed by the procured Red Teaming Provider) that simulates realistic adversary attacks against a Member Organization's critical information assets. It sits alongside the Green Team (SAMA IT Risk of Financial Sector Supervision, which approves the provider and appoints a Test Manager), the White Team (a maximum of five internal SPOCs, led by a White Team Leader, who control and can halt the exercise) and the Blue Team (the organization's monitoring/SOC function, kept unaware). Binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA that are in scope of the framework, and shapes their contracting, scoping and escalation duties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
