# Head of Contract Registry Powers Decision

A SAMA decision (transmitted by circular to the Saudi Company for Finance Lease Contracts Registration, "Sijil") defining the powers, appointment conditions and duties of the Head of the Contract Registry and the registry clerks, issued under the Finance Lease Law and its implementing regulation. It covers SAMA non-objection for appointment, reporting lines, SAMA's power to remove for breach of legal or professional duties, grievance routes where registration or attestation is refused, responsibility for accuracy of registered finance lease contracts and data, minimum ten-year record retention, verification of contracting parties' identity and capacity, Arabic-language documentation with certified translation, sealing of ownership documents (including by accepted electronic means), and safekeeping of registry entries.
