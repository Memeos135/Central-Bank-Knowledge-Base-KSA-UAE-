# D-SIBs Framework

SAMA's August 2014 circular and enclosed framework for identifying and designating Domestic Systemically Important Banks in Saudi Arabia, aligned with the BCBS D-SIB approach. It introduces an indicator-based measurement methodology and requires designated banks to meet additional Higher Loss Absorbency capital requirements, effective 1 January 2016. Addressed to all banks, at managing director / CEO / general manager level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
