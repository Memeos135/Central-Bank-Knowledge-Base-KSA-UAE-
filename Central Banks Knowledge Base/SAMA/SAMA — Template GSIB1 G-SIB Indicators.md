# Template GSIB1: G-SIB Indicators

A Pillar 3 template requiring disclosure of at least the twelve indicators used in the Basel methodology for assessing global systemic importance, with accompanying narrative on reference dates, estimates, structural changes and bucket allocation. Scope is limited: it applies to banks previously classified as G-SIBs, those with a leverage ratio exposure measure above EUR 200 billion, or those included in the assessment sample by supervisory judgment, and SAMA states it need not be completed unless SAMA identifies the bank as a G-SIB. Annual, flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
