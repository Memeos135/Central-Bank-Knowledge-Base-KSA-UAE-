# Pillar 3 Disclosure FAQ

Covers the guiding disclosure principles (meaningfulness, consistency over time, comparability across banks), Pillar 3 assurance expectations including a board-approved disclosure policy, and the conditions under which banks may signpost fixed-format disclosures to another document. Also introduces the frequency and timing rules requiring the Pillar 3 report to be published alongside the corresponding financial report. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
