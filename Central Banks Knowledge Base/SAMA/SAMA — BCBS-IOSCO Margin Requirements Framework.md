# BCBS-IOSCO Margin Requirements Framework

Framework node for SAMA's implementation of the BCBS-IOSCO margin requirements for non-centrally cleared derivatives, organised by Elements (scope, calculation methods, collateral, treatment of provided margin). Scope covers all non-centrally cleared derivatives except physically settled FX forwards and swaps, with a specific carve-out for the fixed physically settled FX legs of cross-currency swap principal exchanges; repos and securities lending are outside scope. Binds SAMA-supervised banks and covered entities dealing in bilateral derivatives.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
