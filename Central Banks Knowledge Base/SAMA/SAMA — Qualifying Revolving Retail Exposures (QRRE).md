# Qualifying Revolving Retail Exposures (QRRE)

Defines Qualifying Revolving Retail Exposures (QRRE) as one of the three IRB retail sub-classes (alongside residential mortgages and other retail), requiring the sub-portfolio to be revolving, unsecured and uncommitted, with criteria applied at sub-portfolio level consistent with the bank's retail segmentation. Relevant to IRB adoption scope, since QRRE is a standalone asset class for the roll-out expectation. Binds banks using the IRB approach under SAMA's credit risk rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
