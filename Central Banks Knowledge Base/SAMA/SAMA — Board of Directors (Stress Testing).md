# Board of Directors (Stress Testing)

Sets out the board of directors' duties under SAMA's Stress Testing Rules: overall responsibility and oversight of the stress testing framework, approval of the stress testing policy and revisions, ensuring adequate resources and infrastructure, reviewing major findings and ensuring remedial action, and requiring periodic reporting or independent evaluation by internal audit or a third party. Related implementation material confirms SAMA assesses board and senior management involvement during supervisory review, ICAAP meetings and on-site examinations. Binds boards of banks within scope of the Rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
