# Risk Committee

Concept node for the board-level Risk Committee as a standing committee required under SAMA's Key Principles of Governance for financial institutions (Principle on board committees), read together with the related governance instrument. It covers the committee's mandate to oversee the risk strategy, appetite and risk management framework, its composition and independence expectations, and its reporting line to the board. Binds boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions (banks, finance and refinance companies, exchange businesses, payment and fintech companies, credit bureaus), excluding insurers/reinsurers under the governance definition of financial institution.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
