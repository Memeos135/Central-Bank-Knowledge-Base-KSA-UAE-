# SAMA circular 42056371 (prior OTC requirements)

This node marks the superseded SAMA circular No. 42056371 (dated 10-08-1442H) within the updated Trade Repository Reporting and Risk Mitigation Requirements for OTC Derivative Contracts (v4.0, September 2025), which replaces it with effect from 1 February 2026. It is relevant for dating obligations and determining which text governs a transaction before and after the effective date. The superseding instrument binds banks licensed in KSA, including locally incorporated banks, their financial subsidiaries and SPVs, and SAMA-licensed foreign bank branches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
