# Repurchase & Securities Agreements

## Why this community

Documentation and prudential treatment of repo and reverse repo transactions in the Saudi market, covering who may transact, what collateral qualifies, how positions are valued and margined, and how default and termination are resolved.

## How members connect

- SAMA's repo guidelines sit at the top and pull in the operative concepts — eligible counterparties, eligible securities, custody, valuation sources, risk management and reporting — so each item is a condition of permissibility rather than a standalone rule.
- The master agreement layer (MRA/GMRA and the Master Agreement for Securities Sale and Purchase, with its annexes, confirmation and exercise notice forms) carries the contractual mechanics that the guidelines presuppose: representations, margin maintenance, events of default and early termination.
- Settlement and reporting obligations tie the contractual chain into market infrastructure (SARIE for cash legs, Tadawul/depository for securities and custody), while accounting and valuation practice cross-refers to SOCPA standards.
- Default provisions form an obligation chain: event of default triggers early termination, which in turn drives close-out valuation and margin/collateral resolution.
