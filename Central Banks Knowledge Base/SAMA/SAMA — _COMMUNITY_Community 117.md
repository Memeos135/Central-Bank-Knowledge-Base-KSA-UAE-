# Finance Companies Regulation

## Why this community

The finance-company prudential and conduct regime: licensing, ownership and control, internal governance (compliance and internal audit), outsourcing, and permitted portfolio and debt-transfer transactions.

## How members connect

- The Finance Companies Control Law is the parent statute; nearly every other member is issued under or expressly cites it, forming a law-to-rules-to-circular hierarchy.
- Governance chain: compliance principles and internal audit principles cross-reference each other, with the mandatory compliance-certificate circular imposing a personal qualification condition on compliance officers.
- Licensing chain: microfinance licensing guidance depends on the fit-and-proper form for founders, board and senior management.
- Activity-specific instructions (digital brokerage, consumer-debt purchase between finance companies, SRC mortgage-portfolio purchases) sit on top of the outsourcing rules and the Law's activity-authorisation scope.
