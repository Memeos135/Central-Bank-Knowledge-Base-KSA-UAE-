# Green Team

Defines the Green Team as the SAMA-side body (IT Risk of Financial Sector Supervision) that oversees red teaming exercises, appoints the Test Manager and Backup Test Manager, approves selection of the Red Teaming Provider, and supplies sector-specific threat intelligence. It sits outside the Member Organization and provides guidance and oversight rather than accountability for test outcomes. Relevant to understanding the supervisory role and approval gates in the F.E.E.R. process.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
