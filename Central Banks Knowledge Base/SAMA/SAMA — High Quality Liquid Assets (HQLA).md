# High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)

Node on the requirement for finance companies to hold a cushion of unencumbered high quality liquid assets as a readily available funding source to absorb unexpected net cash outflows and survive a liquidity stress event. It explains the qualifying characteristics (easy and immediate conversion to cash at little or no loss of value, ease and certainty of valuation, low price volatility, liquidity-generating capacity retained under market stress) and links sizing of the buffer to the company's funding strategy, diversification of funding sources and tenor, and liquid assets ratio monitoring. Pledged assets are excluded from the available liquid asset stock.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
