# Board Oversight of Liquidity Risk

Concept node on the Board of Directors' ultimate responsibility for liquidity risk in a finance company: setting risk tolerance, approving the liquidity strategy and policies (including contingency funding plan and stress testing framework) at least annually, monitoring the liquidity profile, and ensuring senior management operationalises them. Directed at boards and senior management of SAMA-licensed finance companies, with an ALCO recommended for oversight.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
