# Table MRB - Qualitative Disclosures for Banks Using IMA

A Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure table mandatory only for banks that have SAMA approval to use the internal models approach for market risk, requiring description of trading desk structure, model scope, expected shortfall methodology, default risk capital requirement and treatment of non-modellable risk factors. Disclosure must be at group-wide regulatory consolidation level and indicate the share of capital requirements covered by the described models. It binds IMA-approved banks annually.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
