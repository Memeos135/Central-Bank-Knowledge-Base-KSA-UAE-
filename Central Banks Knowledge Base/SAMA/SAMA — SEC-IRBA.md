# SEC-IRBA

Concept node for the Securitisation Internal Ratings-Based Approach, the model-based method at the top of the securitisation hierarchy, built on KIRB, attachment and detachment points and the supervisory parameter p, with an alternative treatment for qualifying STC securitisations. Related Pillar 3 disclosure rules require banking book securitisation exposures treated under the securitisation framework, including those risk-weighted under SEC-IRBA, to be reported in the dedicated securitisation templates. It binds banks with SAMA permission to use IRB approaches for the underlying pool.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
