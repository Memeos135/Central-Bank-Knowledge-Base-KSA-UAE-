# Financial Fraud via Preauthorization

SAMA circular addressing fraudulent misuse of the preauthorization and subsequent capture/purchase advice functions on POS terminals using cards on international payment networks. It obliges banks and payment service providers offering acquiring services to apply the relevant international scheme rules, restrict these transaction types to eligible merchant segments, monitor and investigate suspicious and high-risk preauthorization/capture activity by customer segment and amount, and enhance fraud monitoring and detection arrangements consistent with SAMA's Counter-Fraud Manual.
