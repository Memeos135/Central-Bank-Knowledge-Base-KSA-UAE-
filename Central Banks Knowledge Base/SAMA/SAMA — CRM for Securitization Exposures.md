# CRM for Securitization Exposures

A concept node on how credit risk mitigation is recognised for securitization exposures under SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk, sitting alongside the definitional provisions that identify what counts as a securitization exposure (including ABS, MBS, credit enhancements, liquidity facilities, swaps, credit derivatives, tranched cover and originator-recorded reserve/cash collateral accounts) and a resecuritization exposure. It also touches the related Pillar 3 disclosure context for problem assets and regulatory scope of consolidation. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA when calculating risk-weighted assets for, and disclosing, securitization positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
