# Table LIA - Differences Between Accounting and Regulatory Exposure Amounts

Covers the Pillar 3 linkage disclosures explaining gaps between accounting and regulatory figures: Template LI2 on the main sources of difference between financial statement carrying values (under the regulatory scope of consolidation) and regulatory exposure amounts across credit, counterparty credit, securitisation and market risk frameworks, with Table LIA providing the qualitative explanation. Also includes Table CRB-A requiring banks to state their definitions of non-performing and forborne exposures and whether they follow SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Assets. Mandatory for banks, annual, flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
