# Alternative Liquidity Approaches (ALA)

The alternative liquidity approaches (ALA) framework for jurisdictions whose domestic-currency supply of Level 1 (or Level 1 and Level 2) assets is insufficient to meet aggregate bank demand. It sets cumulative qualifying criteria — structural insufficiency, mitigation capacity, and commitment to supervisory monitoring, disclosure, self-assessment and peer review — assessed against a fully implemented 100% LCR. It is a jurisdiction-level eligibility regime; the context notes SAMA has not adopted any ALA option.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
