# Money Changer Licensing Requirements

Licensing-maintenance, supervisory reporting and license-termination provisions for Money Changers, listing grounds on which SAMA may terminate a license (failure to meet license or business requirements, false information, obstruction of inspection, insolvency, accumulated losses of 50% or more of capital, dissolution or death of a sole proprietor). It also requires appointment of a licensed external auditor (maximum five continuous years), permits SAMA to appoint an auditor at the Money Changer's expense, and mandates periodical data submissions and updates to SAMA. Binds licensed Money Changers and their employees.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
