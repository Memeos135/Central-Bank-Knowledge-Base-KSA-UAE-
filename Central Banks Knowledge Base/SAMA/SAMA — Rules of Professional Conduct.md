# Rules of Professional Conduct

This node sets out the professional conduct duties banks must observe when carrying on bancassurance activities under SAMA's bancassurance rules, covering honesty, transparency and fairness, competence and training of Authorised Employees, adequate resources, full and timely disclosure of policy coverage, conditions, exceptions and restrictions to Clients, accuracy of information, and confidentiality of Company and Client data. Surrounding provisions address the bancassurance unit's management (director appointment subject to SAMA non-objection), permitted electronic and non-electronic marketing and distribution channels, the Insurance Foundations Certificate Exam requirement for Authorised Employees, insurer approval plus SAMA notification before expanding channels, and the prohibition on conducting bancassurance with anyone other than the bank's own Clients. It binds banks (and their authorised staff) distributing insurance products in partnership with insurance companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other
