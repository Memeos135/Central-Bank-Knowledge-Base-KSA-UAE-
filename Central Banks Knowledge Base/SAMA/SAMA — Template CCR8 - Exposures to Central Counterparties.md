# Template CCR8 - Exposures to Central Counterparties

A mandatory, semiannual, fixed-format Pillar 3 template in which banks disclose all exposures to central counterparties — trade exposures, segregated and non-segregated initial margin, and pre-funded/unfunded default fund contributions — with EAD post-CRM and RWA, split between qualifying (QCCP) and non-qualifying CCPs. Banks must add narrative commentary explaining significant period changes. Applies to all SAMA-supervised banks under the Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
