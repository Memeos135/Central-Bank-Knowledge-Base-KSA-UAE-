# Template CC2 - Reconciliation of Capital to Balance Sheet

Pillar 3 template reconciling the balance sheet in a bank's published financial statements (accounting consolidation) with the balance sheet under the regulatory scope of consolidation, and expanding items that feed the regulatory capital components in Template CC1. It is the first step of the required link between accounting figures and disclosed capital numbers. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
