# Shariah Oversight (Compliance and Audit)

The Shariah oversight component of SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for finance companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulation. It addresses monitoring and verification that the company's finance business complies with Shariah rulings and principles, alongside board and senior management responsibilities, the Shariah committee's composition and operating controls, independence criteria and confidentiality. It binds finance companies licensed for real estate finance, productive assets finance, SME finance, finance lease, credit card finance and consumer finance, and is indicative (guidance) for other licensed finance activities unless other rules make provisions mandatory.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
