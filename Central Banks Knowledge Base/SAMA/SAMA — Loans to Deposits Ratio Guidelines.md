# Loans to Deposits Ratio Guidelines

SAMA's revised Loans to Deposits Ratio (LDR) guidelines, issued under the Central Bank Law and the Banking Control Law and superseding the prior LDR circulars, effective June 2023. They define the ratio as net loans over weighted deposits, specify the components of the numerator (loans net of provisions, unearned commission income and commission in suspense) and denominator (deposits, repos and long-term debt including sukuk/bonds, syndicated and subordinated debt), exclude interbank and SAMA transactions, set maturity-based weights, and state SAMA's expectation that total LDR stay below 90%. They bind all banks, which must report the ratio monthly on a consolidated basis covering local and foreign currency, resident and non-resident.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
