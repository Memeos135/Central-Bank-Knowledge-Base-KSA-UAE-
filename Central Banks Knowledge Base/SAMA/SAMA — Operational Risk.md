# Operational Risk

Concept node on operational risk in Shari'ah compliant banking, drawing on SAMA's Risk Management Framework, where operational risk is defined as loss from failed internal processes, people, systems or external events and expressly encompasses Shari'ah non-compliance and fiduciary failures. It sits within the framework's board and senior management oversight requirements for an independent risk management function, documented policies and accountability lines. Binds licensed domestic banks offering Shari'ah compliant products.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
