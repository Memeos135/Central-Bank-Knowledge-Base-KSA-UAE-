# IMA General Provisions

Node covering general internal-models provisions in SAMA's minimum capital requirements, spanning IRB risk quantification standards (own estimates of PD, LGD and EAD, long-run averages, data representativeness, conservatism) and the general framing of internal model use for capital purposes. It binds banks authorised to use internal ratings-based or internal model approaches; banks not meeting own-estimate conditions must fall back to supervisory parameters. Useful when checking estimation standards and the boundary between foundation and advanced approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
