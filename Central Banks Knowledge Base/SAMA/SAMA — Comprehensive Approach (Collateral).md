# Comprehensive Approach (Collateral)

Concerns the comprehensive approach to collateralised transactions, the alternative to the simple approach, under which exposure and collateral values are adjusted for volatility rather than substituting risk weights. The surrounding material addresses collateral reuse risks, custodian segregation, capital on both sides of repo and securities lending transactions, agent-arranged transactions with guarantees, and how F-IRB LGD interacts with recognised and non-recognised collateral. It binds banks recognising financial collateral for capital relief.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
