# Controls for Issuing Executive Deed Extracts for Registered Finance Lease Contracts

SAMA controls and procedures (Jan 2021, under the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation) for requesting and issuing executive deed extracts for registered finance lease contracts, which constitute enforceable instruments under the Enforcement Law. They cover three extract types — repossession of movable assets, delivery of a fixed asset (real estate), and a financial enforcement certificate — and require all parties to use electronic channels; lessors may repossess movable assets only through Ministry of Justice licensed specialised companies. Binds lessors (banks and finance companies) and SAMA-licensed finance lease contract registration companies, and replaces the earlier movable-asset repossession controls.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other
