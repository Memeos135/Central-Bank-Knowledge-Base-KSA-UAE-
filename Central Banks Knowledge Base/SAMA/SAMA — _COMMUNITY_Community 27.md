# Sensitivities-Based Market Risk

## Why this community

The sensitivities-based component of the standardised market risk capital charge, which aggregates delta, vega and curvature sensitivities across prescribed risk classes using regulator-set risk weights and correlations.

## How members connect

- The sensitivities-based method is the umbrella obligation; delta, vega and curvature are the three mandatory risk measures it requires to be computed and summed.
- Each risk class (general interest rate, credit spread, FX, equity) applies the same three-measure structure but with its own bucket, risk-weight and correlation tables — a parallel obligation pattern rather than a hierarchy.
- Correct classification of an instrument into a risk class and bucket is the shared definitional pivot: it determines weights, correlations and therefore the charge.
- Curvature and vega are linked because both attach only to instruments with optionality, narrowing their scope relative to delta.
