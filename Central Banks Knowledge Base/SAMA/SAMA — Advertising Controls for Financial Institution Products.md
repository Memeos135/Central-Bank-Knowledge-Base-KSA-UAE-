# Advertising Controls for Financial Institution Products

SAMA circular and attached rules (first edition, Sha'ban 1444H / March 2023) setting the minimum standards and procedures financial institutions must observe when advertising any product or service. It defines key terms (Financial Institution, Customer, Consumer, Products and Services, Advertisement, Medium) and requires due care over advertising content, alongside compliance with sectoral laws (banking control, insurance, finance companies, real estate finance, leasing, payments) and media-content rules. It binds all entities supervised by SAMA, effective from the circular date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance
