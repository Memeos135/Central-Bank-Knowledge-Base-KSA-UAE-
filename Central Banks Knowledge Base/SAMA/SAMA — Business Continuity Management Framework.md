# Business Continuity Management Framework

The SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework sets mandatory continuity and operational resilience requirements for Member Organizations, drawing on SAMA expectations and standards such as ISO 22301. It covers BCM strategy and policy, business impact analysis and risk assessment, resilient architecture and availability requirements, crisis management planning and IT disaster recovery. All Member Organizations must comply and formally integrate the requirements into their BCM programs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
