# Template CCR8 - CCP Exposures

Specification for Pillar 3 Template CCR8, a fixed-format semiannual disclosure of exposures at default (post-CRM) and RWA arising from dealings with central counterparties, split between qualifying and non-qualifying CCPs and by trade type, initial margin and default fund contributions. Mandatory for all banks, with narrative on period-on-period changes. The context also carries Table CRB-A on the prudential treatment of problem assets, requiring banks to disclose their non-performing and forborne exposure definitions.
