# Resident Companies Account Rules

This node covers account opening for resident companies under the Rules Governing Bank Accounts, differentiating by legal form (joint-stock, limited liability, general and limited partnerships and similar) and the documents and authorised signatories required, alongside the broader resident/non-resident juristic person categories. It also touches the operational rules for accounts of juristic persons, such as who may transact on a dormant account. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, governance/risk
