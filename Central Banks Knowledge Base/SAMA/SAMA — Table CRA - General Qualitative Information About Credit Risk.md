# Table CRA - General Qualitative Information About Credit Risk

Table CRA is the general qualitative credit risk disclosure in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, requiring banks to describe their credit risk profile and business model linkage, credit risk policy and limit-setting, the structure of the credit risk management and control function, its interaction with risk control, compliance and internal audit, and credit risk reporting to executive management and the board. Mandatory for all banks, annual, flexible format. It heads section 19 on credit risk, which excludes securitisation positions (section 21) and counterparty credit risk (section 20), and sets out the full suite of CR/CRB/CRC/CRD templates, with IRB disclosures required only where SAMA has approved IRB use.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
