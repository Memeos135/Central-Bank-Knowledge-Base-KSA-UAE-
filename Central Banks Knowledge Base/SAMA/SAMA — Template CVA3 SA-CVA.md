# Template CVA3: SA-CVA

The quantitative Pillar 3 template for disclosing RWA and components of the CVA risk capital requirement calculated under the standardised approach (SA-CVA), part of the CVA disclosure set that also includes the BA-CVA templates and the SA-CVA qualitative table. It applies only where the bank has SAMA approval to use SA-CVA and where the CVA materiality threshold in the revised CCR framework is satisfied. Binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating CVA capital for derivatives and fair-valued SFTs in both banking and trading books.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
