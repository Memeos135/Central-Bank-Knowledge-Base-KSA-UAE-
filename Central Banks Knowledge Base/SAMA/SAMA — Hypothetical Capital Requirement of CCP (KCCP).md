# Hypothetical Capital Requirement of CCP (KCCP)

Defines KCCP, the hypothetical capital requirement of a central counterparty computed from its counterparty credit risk exposures to all clearing members and their clients. It is a purely notional figure calculated on a consistent basis solely as the first step in determining a clearing member bank's capital charge for its default fund contribution, and does not represent the CCP's own regulatory capital requirement. Relevant to SAMA-supervised banks that are clearing members of QCCPs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
