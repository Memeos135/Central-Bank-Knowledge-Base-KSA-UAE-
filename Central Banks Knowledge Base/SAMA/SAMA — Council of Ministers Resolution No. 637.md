# Council of Ministers Resolution No. 637

Council of Ministers Resolution No. 637 (17/10/1441H) approves amending Article 12 of the Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism so that persons accused under that Law may be released provisionally only temporarily, by order of the Public Prosecution, where there is no prejudice to the investigation and no flight or disappearance risk. It also approves repealing Article 9 of the Law's Implementing Regulations. It is directed at the criminal-procedure aspects of the CTF regime and is implemented through an accompanying Royal Decree.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
