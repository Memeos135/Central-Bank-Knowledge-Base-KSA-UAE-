# Bank Licensing Guidelines

## Why this community

Entry-to-market authorisation for banks in KSA: the minimum criteria an applicant must satisfy to be licensed, plus the incremental conditions imposed on digital-only banking models.

## How members connect

- The core Licensing Guidelines act as the base instrument; capital adequacy, corporate governance and compliance/AML-CTF capability are each cited as mandatory assessment criteria rather than optional considerations.
- The guidelines are expressly aligned to the Basel Core Principles, which supplies the supervisory rationale behind the criteria.
- The Digital-Only Bank guidelines are additive — they do not displace the general criteria but layer further requirements keyed to the 'Digital-only Bank' definition.
- Technology/cybersecurity risk assessment and a credible Exit Plan are the distinguishing digital-only conditions, reflecting heightened operational-resilience and orderly-wind-down concerns.
