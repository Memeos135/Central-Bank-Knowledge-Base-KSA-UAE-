# Remittance Centers Rules

## Why this community

AML/CTF and operational controls governing bank-operated remittance centres and money transfer services, covering customer identification, transfer execution and suspicious activity reporting for cross-border and domestic flows.

## How members connect

- The Guide sits at the top of the hierarchy and pulls in the CDD/KYC obligation set as the entry condition for executing any outbound or inbound remittance.
- CDD measures are anchored to FATF standards, so the domestic rules inherit the international risk-based expectations and their interpretation.
- The remittance execution chain links settlement infrastructure (SARIE) on the operational side with SAFIU reporting on the AML side — the same transaction triggers both a processing and a reporting obligation.
- 'Money Transfer Services Providers' scopes who the rules bind; note the distinction between a bank's own remittance centre and a licensed standalone provider when applying the obligations.
