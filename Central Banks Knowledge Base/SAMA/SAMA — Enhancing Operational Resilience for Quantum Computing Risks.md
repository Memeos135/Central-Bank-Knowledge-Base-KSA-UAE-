# Enhancing Operational Resilience for Quantum Computing Risks

An Arabic SAMA circular on enhancing operational resilience against quantum computing risks, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law. It requires supervised institutions to conduct an enterprise-wide quantum risk assessment (covering operational, legal, regulatory, strategic and workforce risks) with remediation plans by end of Q1 2027, to complete inventory and classification of cryptographic assets by end of Q4 2026 including third-party dependencies, to plan for cryptographic agility or alternative solutions for priority assets, and to make quantum risk a standing monitoring item for the information security committee and the board risk committee. It binds banks, credit information companies, finance companies, payment service providers and SAMA-licensed finance support entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
