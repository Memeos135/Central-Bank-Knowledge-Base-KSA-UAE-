# External Credit Risk Assessment Approach (ECRA)

Concept node for the External Credit Risk Assessment Approach (ECRA), under which banks risk-weight rated exposures (notably bank exposures) by reference to external ratings from ECAIs recognised by SAMA, with mandatory due diligence that can only push a risk weight to a higher bucket, never lower. The surrounding context contrasts ECRA with the Standardised Credit Risk Assessment Approach (SCRA) for unrated bank exposures and sets out operational criteria for using external credit assessments, including in the securitisation framework. It binds banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk under SAMA rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
