# Template KM1: Key Metrics

Node covering Template KM1 (Key Metrics) within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements, part of the overview section on risk management, key prudential metrics and RWA. It sets the fixed content of a time-series disclosure of available capital and ratios (including buffers), RWA (pre- and post-capital-floor), the Basel III leverage ratio exposure measure, LCR and NSFR, plus fully loaded ECL variants where a bank applies the ECL transitional arrangement, and specifies cross-template linkages to CC1 and OV1. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime at the consolidated level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
