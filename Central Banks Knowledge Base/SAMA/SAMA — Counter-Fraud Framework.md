# Counter-Fraud Framework

SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, which sets control requirements and a six-level maturity model (0 non-existent to 5) used to assess Member Organisations' counter-fraud capability, with higher levels requiring satisfaction of all preceding criteria. It obliges Member Organisations to establish a dedicated Counter-Fraud Department responsible for the day-to-day Counter-Fraud Programme, including policy compliance monitoring, organisation-wide preventive and detective controls across people/process/technology, fraud risk assessment, intelligence analysis and sharing with SAMA and the sector, investigations and root-cause remediation, fraud risk appetite monitoring with crisis escalation, alignment with cyber security and financial crime functions, and periodic senior-management reporting. It also addresses the appropriate reporting line for the department.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection
