# Zakat and Tax Accounting

## Why this community

Treatment of Zakat and income tax in the financial statements of supervised entities, bridging accounting standards with SAMA reporting expectations.

## How members connect

- Standalone accounting-treatment instrument; it constrains how a supervised entity presents Zakat and tax charges rather than imposing a prudential requirement.
- Connects functionally to financial reporting, disclosure and capital-calculation rules, since Zakat/tax provisioning affects reported results.
- No intra-cluster cross-references; read it against the applicable accounting framework and any SAMA reporting circulars.
