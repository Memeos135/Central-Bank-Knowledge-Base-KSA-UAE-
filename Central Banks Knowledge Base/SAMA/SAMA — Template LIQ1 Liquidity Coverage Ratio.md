# Template LIQ1: Liquidity Coverage Ratio

Pillar 3 node for template LIQ1, the quantitative disclosure of the Liquidity Coverage Ratio, including high-quality liquid assets, net cash outflows and the resulting ratio, generally reported as simple averages of daily or monthly observations. It complements the LCR figures summarised in the key metrics template (KM1). Applies to banks under SAMA prudential supervision subject to the LCR standard and Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
