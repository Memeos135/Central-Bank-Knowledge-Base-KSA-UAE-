# Finance Company Holidays

## Why this community

Determination of official holidays for finance companies, which governs business-day computation for instalment due dates, notice periods and other time-bound obligations.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster addressed to finance companies as a sector-wide operational directive.
- Its legal significance is derivative: it fixes the calendar used when other finance and consumer-protection rules count days for payment, disclosure or cure periods.
