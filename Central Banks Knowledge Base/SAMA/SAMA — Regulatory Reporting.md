# Regulatory Reporting

Covers the scope, level of application and reporting hooks of the LEX Rules: they apply to locally incorporated banks, their foreign branches and subsidiaries, and foreign banks operating in KSA, on both consolidated and standalone bases at every tier of the banking group. Foreign bank branches under home-country consolidated supervision for credit concentration are largely carved out but must still document large exposure and concentration policies and report their 50 largest exposures under Section 7. Exposures to groups of connected counterparties are subject both to the limits and to the Section 7 regulatory reporting requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
