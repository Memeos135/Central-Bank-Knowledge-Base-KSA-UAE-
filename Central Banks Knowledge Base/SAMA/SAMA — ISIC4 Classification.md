# ISIC4 Classification

A concept node for the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC4) as used in SAMA's interest rates on assets and liabilities reporting. ISIC4 is the mandated basis for the sector-level template, requiring banks to categorise asset and liability balances and weighted average rates by economic activity rather than by product. It is relevant to all banks in Saudi Arabia, including foreign bank branches, completing the quarterly return.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
