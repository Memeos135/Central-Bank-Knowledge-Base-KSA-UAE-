# Remediation Plan (RP)

Defines the Remediation Plan the White Team must draft after the exercise, based on the Red Team Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report, setting out agreed improvements, risk-based priorities and a roadmap for prevention, detection, response and recovery capabilities. Progress must be monitored and reported periodically to the Member Organization's Cyber Security Committee and to the Green Team (SAMA). Also covers the replay sessions for IT staff and Senior Management that feed awareness and remediation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
