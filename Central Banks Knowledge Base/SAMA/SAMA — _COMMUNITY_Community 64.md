# Operational Loss Data

## Why this community

The loss-data component of the operational risk capital charge: what qualifies as an operational loss, how it must be identified, collected, classified and treated over the required observation history.

## How members connect

- The Loss Component is the quantitative output that depends on a compliant Operational Loss Data Set, creating a direct data-quality-to-capital linkage.
- Loss Data Identification/Collection/Treatment sets the eligibility, thresholds and treatment rules that govern what may enter the data set.
- Loss Event Type Classification and its detailed breakdown provide the shared taxonomy used for both data collection and supervisory disclosure.
- Feeds upward into the Internal Loss Multiplier and therefore into Operational Risk Capital (see the operational risk capital cluster).
