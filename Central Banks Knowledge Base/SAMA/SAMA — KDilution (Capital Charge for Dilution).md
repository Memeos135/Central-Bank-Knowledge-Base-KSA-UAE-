# KDilution (Capital Charge for Dilution)

This node addresses the capital charge for dilution risk on purchased receivables under the IRB framework, including how dilution PD, LGD and maturity inputs feed the risk weight calculation and how dilution risk is recognised when applying the securitisation internal ratings-based approach. It also relates to the worked examples distinguishing common and non-common loss waterfalls for default and dilution losses. It binds IRB banks holding purchased receivables or securitisation exposures to such pools.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
