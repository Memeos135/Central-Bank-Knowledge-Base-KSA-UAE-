# Table CVAA: Qualitative CVA Disclosure

The general qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table on credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk, sitting at the head of the CVA disclosure suite (CVA1–CVA4, CVAB) and triggered by the materiality threshold in SAMA's revised CCR capital framework. It frames CVA risk as loss from changes in CVA values driven by counterparty credit spreads and market factors, and covers scope of covered transactions and the BA-CVA/SA-CVA split. It binds banks with covered derivative and SFT exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
