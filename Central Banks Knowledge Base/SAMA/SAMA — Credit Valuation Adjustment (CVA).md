# Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA)

Credit Valuation Adjustment risk is the risk of loss from changes in the value of derivatives and securities financing transactions due to potential counterparty default; regulatory CVA is defined at counterparty level and may differ from accounting CVA. The node covers the CVA capital framework (RWA derived by multiplying the CVA capital requirement by 12.5) and its separate reporting in Pillar 3 RWA templates. It binds SAMA-licensed banks with derivative and SFT exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
