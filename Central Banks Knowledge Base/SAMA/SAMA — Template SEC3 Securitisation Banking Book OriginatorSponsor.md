# Template SEC3: Securitisation Banking Book Originator/Sponsor

Template SEC3 discloses banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as originator or sponsor, together with the associated capital requirements, broken down by risk weight bands and regulatory approach. It is limited to exposures actually treated under the securitisation capital framework, which for originators presupposes that the significant risk transfer recognition criteria are satisfied. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
