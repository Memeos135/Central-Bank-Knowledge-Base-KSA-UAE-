# Special Purpose Entity (SPE)

Concept node for the special purpose entity/vehicle used to hold securitized assets and issue securitization or commercial paper positions, and referenced in capital instrument disclosure as the issuer legal entity of regulatory capital instruments. Its relevance is to identifying who the originator, sponsor or issuer is and whether exposures to the vehicle are securitization exposures attracting capital. It binds banks applying the credit risk and market risk capital frameworks and the associated capital disclosure templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
