# Disclosure and Transparency

The shareholder rights and disclosure/transparency provisions of SAMA's governance principles, requiring a written board-approved disclosure policy that enables stakeholders to access financial and non-financial information, future orientations, strategic plans and credit ratings, and that disclosure be explicit, correct and non-misleading. It sets shareholder entitlements including timely information, General Assembly participation with 21 days' notice of venue, date and agenda, cumulative voting for board members, and queries on remuneration and practices. Related board duties include risk strategy oversight, auditor independence, and notifying SAMA of regulatory or judicial penalties within ten working days.
