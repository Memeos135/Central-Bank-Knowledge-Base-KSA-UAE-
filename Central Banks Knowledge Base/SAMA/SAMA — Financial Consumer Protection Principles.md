# Financial Consumer Protection Principles

Despite the label, this node is SAMA's covering circular and Financial Institutions Service Fee Guide (first issue, Rajab 1447H), setting the tariff framework financial institutions must observe when charging for services and products, plus amendments to three administrative-fee provisions: Article 9 of the Consumer Finance Regulations, Specific Conduct Rule 1 of the Principles and Rules for Financial Consumer Protection, and Article 11.1 of the standard individual consumer finance contract. It defines Financial Institution, Customer, documented channels, and standard documents (identification certificate, debt/no-liability letters, account statement), and caps combined administrative fees and costs at the lower of SAR 5,000 or 1% of the finance amount, excluding taxes. It binds all entities supervised and regulated by SAMA, with effect within a stated period from publication on SAMA's website, and overrides conflicting provisions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
