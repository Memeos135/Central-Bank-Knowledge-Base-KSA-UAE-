# Prompt Response to Judicial Authorities Circular

An Arabic-language SAMA circular (supplementing an earlier circular on executing judicial judgments without delay) requiring all supervised entities to respond promptly to judicial authorities, reply to their requests within the prescribed statutory periods, receive judgments and decisions, and implement them expeditiously. SAMA warns that delay or procrastination will attract regulatory action against both institutions and staff. Addressed to banks, finance companies, money exchangers and credit information companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
