# Validation of Internal Estimates

Covers the risk-quantification and validation standards for banks' own estimates of PD, LGD and EAD under the IRB approaches, including the requirement that PD be a long-run average of one-year default rates per grade or pool and that estimates be grounded in historical experience and empirical evidence rather than judgement alone. Estimates must incorporate all relevant, material and available data (internal, external or pooled) and be demonstrably representative of long-run experience. Binds SAMA-supervised banks on the IRB approach; related market risk material on internal risk transfer desks appears alongside it.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
