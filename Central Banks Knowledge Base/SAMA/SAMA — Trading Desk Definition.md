# Trading Desk Definition

Defines the regulatory concept of a trading desk for market risk capital purposes: an unambiguously identified group of traders or trading accounts with a designated head trader, a defined risk scope, a documented business strategy with annual budget and management information reporting, and a clear risk management and limits structure. Desk definitions require SAMA validation against these attributes, while purely internal operational sub-desks may be created without approval but carry no capital-framework effect. Binds banks calculating market risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
