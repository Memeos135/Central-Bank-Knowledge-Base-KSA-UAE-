# Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking

SAMA's Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law, setting supervisory expectations for managing risks specific to Shari'ah compliant activities (credit, market, operational, liquidity and equity investment risk). It allocates board/delegated committee and senior management responsibilities, requires an independent risk management function, and mandates processes for identifying, measuring, mitigating, monitoring and reporting such risks, integrated with the bank's overall risk framework. It applies to all domestic banks licensed by SAMA conducting Shari'ah compliant banking, including majority-owned banking subsidiaries and branches, and supersedes the 2021 version.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
