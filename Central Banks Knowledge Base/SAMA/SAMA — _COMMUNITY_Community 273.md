# IRRBB Quantitative Information

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of IRRBB outcomes — changes in economic value of equity and net interest income under the prescribed interest rate shock scenarios.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster that is the numerical output of the methodology and assumptions disclosed in the IRRBBA qualitative table; the two are designed to be read as a pair.
- Scenario definitions and measurement conventions are imported from the IRRBB standard, so the template cannot be applied without that upstream rule.
- Comparability is the regulatory purpose: deviations in assumptions must be explained in the qualitative table, not in the template.
