# Protection and Safeguarding of Protected Funds

A SAMA circular reinforcing the protected-funds safeguarding obligation in Article 93 of the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services, addressed to all payment service providers. It requires that any non-objection request be accompanied by policies and controls for managing and operating the protected funds safeguarding account, including defined authorities and authorised persons, with multiple-control principles based on risk assessment, involvement of internal control functions, and limitation of account authorities to designated senior positions. It also requires documented reconciliation between aggregate e-wallet balances or executed payment transaction values and the safeguarding account balance.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
