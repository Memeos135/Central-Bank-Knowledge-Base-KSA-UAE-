# Compliance with Prompt Execution of Court Rulings

A Governor-level SAMA circular requiring supervised entities to promptly receive, accept and execute judicial judgments and decisions issued against them, without delay or procrastination, and to raise any objection through the competent channels within the prescribed statutory periods. It is addressed to banks, insurance and reinsurance companies and related free professions, finance companies, money exchangers and credit information companies, which must inform their relevant internal departments to act on it.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
