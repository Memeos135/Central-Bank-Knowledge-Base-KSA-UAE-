# Template CR7 - Effect on RWA of Credit Derivatives

Template CR7 requires IRB banks to disclose the effect of credit derivatives used as a credit risk mitigation technique, comparing hypothetical pre-credit derivatives RWA with actual RWA across IRB portfolios (sovereign, banks, corporate, specialised lending, retail sub-classes, equity, purchased receivables). Mandatory for banks using A-IRB and/or F-IRB for some or all exposures; semiannual, fixed format, with narrative explaining the RWA impact.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
