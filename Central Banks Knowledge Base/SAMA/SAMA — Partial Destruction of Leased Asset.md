# Partial Destruction of Leased Asset

A clause-level node within SAMA's mandatory standard-form vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract for individuals, issued by circular to banks and finance companies in the Kingdom. It concerns the treatment of total and partial loss/damage of the leased asset, including how the parties' payment and insurance positions are settled when the vehicle is partially destroyed. It binds lessors (banks and licensed finance companies) writing consumer auto Ijarah, who may not conclude contracts departing from the approved form.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
