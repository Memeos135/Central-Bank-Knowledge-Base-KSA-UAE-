# Contract and Vendor Management

## Why this community

Control of third-party and vendor relationships through the contract as the primary compliance instrument — allocating responsibility, access rights and residual accountability of the regulated entity.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster that functions as the contractual hinge for outsourcing and third-party risk obligations.
- Chains to supervisory expectations that the regulated entity remains accountable notwithstanding delegation, including audit/inspection access for the entity and SAMA.
- Intersects AML/CTF third-party reliance and customer data confidentiality, where contractual terms determine permissible information sharing and disposal on exit.
