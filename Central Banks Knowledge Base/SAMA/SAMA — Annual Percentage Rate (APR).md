# Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

Defined-term node for the Annual Percentage Rate: the discount rate at which the present value of all instalments and other amounts payable by the beneficiary (the total amount repayable) equals the present value of the finance amounts made available, measured from the date the finance or its first drawdown is available, aligned with the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. It is the core comparability metric that banks and finance companies must disclose for financing products, and it sits alongside AER for savings and investment products.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
