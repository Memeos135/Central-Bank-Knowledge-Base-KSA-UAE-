# Supervisory Authority Powers

This node sets out the powers and duties of supervisory authorities under the AML Law — collecting data, on-site and off-site supervision, compelling production of documents, conducting sectoral ML risk assessments, issuing guidance and instructions, cooperating with foreign counterparts, verifying group-wide application to foreign branches and majority-owned subsidiaries, and fit-and-proper screening of managers and controllers. It also includes key statutory definitions (Supervisory Authority, Competent Authority, Beneficial Owner, Customer, Business Relationship, Shell Bank, Bearer Negotiable Instruments, Provisional Seizure, Confiscation). Relevant to SAMA as AML supervisor and to all supervised FIs, DNFBPs and NPOs.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
