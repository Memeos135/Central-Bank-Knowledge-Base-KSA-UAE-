# NSFR Disclosure Template

## Why this community

Standardised public disclosure of the Net Stable Funding Ratio, breaking funding into available stable funding and required stable funding components. It sits in the liquidity-standards limb of the prudential regime, where the obligation is to disclose in a prescribed, comparable format rather than to compute a new requirement.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the template is the disclosure instrument attached to the underlying NSFR liquidity requirement, so its legal force derives from the parent liquidity/Pillar 3 rules rather than standing alone.
- Depends on the defined ASF/RSF weighting categories used in the NSFR calculation rules — the template cannot be read without those definitions.
- Interacts with other prudential disclosure templates (capital, market risk) as part of one periodic disclosure obligation cycle to SAMA and the public.
