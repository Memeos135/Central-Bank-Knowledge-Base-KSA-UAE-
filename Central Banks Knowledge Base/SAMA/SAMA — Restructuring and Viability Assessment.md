# Restructuring and Viability Assessment

Sets the viability conditions banks must meet before granting long-term or short-term restructuring measures, including documented financial evidence that the borrower can afford the solution, treatment of outstanding arrears, and, for short-term measures, evidence of capacity to resume full principal and interest payments afterwards without leading to repeated consecutive restructurings. Where prior restructurings exist on the same loan, additional internal controls, early warning signals and approval by a designated Senior Management Committee are required. Viability assessment must be grounded in the borrower's financial characteristics and the workout plan must be approved under the bank's delegation of authority.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
