# Template LR2: Leverage Ratio Common Disclosure

Pillar 3 node for template LR2, the common disclosure of leverage ratio components — on-balance sheet exposures, derivative and securities financing transaction exposures, off-balance sheet items, Tier 1 capital and the resulting ratio. It forms part of SAMA's adopted Basel Pillar 3 template set, which also links the leverage ratio to capital distribution constraints and key metrics. Applies to banks under SAMA prudential supervision subject to Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
