# Development Process of Shariah-Compliant Products

This node sets the Shariah product development process within SAMA's Shariah governance framework: all Shariah issues in product design, structuring and process must be referred to the Shariah committee, which must approve every Islamic banking product and any subsequent amendment and review the related legal contracts and documents. It distinguishes pre-product approval (research, structuring, contract review before launch) from post-product approval review after offering. It binds banks conducting Islamic banking activity, their executive management and Shariah committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
