# Variation Margin

Concept node on margin calculation standards for bilateral derivatives, drawn from the same SAMA margin framework; the captured context focuses on the 99% confidence / 10-day horizon calibration including a stress period, the default expectation that banks use the standardised schedule, and the requirement for SAMA approval, validation and internal governance before any model may be used. Variation margin applies to all components of cross-currency swaps even where initial margin carve-outs apply. Binds SAMA-supervised banks and covered counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
