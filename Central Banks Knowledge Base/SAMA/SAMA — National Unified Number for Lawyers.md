# National Unified Number for Lawyers

Urgent SAMA circular adopting the requirement that lawyers hold the national unified entity number beginning with 7, obtained through the legal-practice entity register, following the Ministry of Justice/Saudi Bar Association correspondence and a Council of Ministers decision. It builds on earlier SAMA instructions substituting the unified national number for commercial registration and licence numbers for non-government entities. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA and credit information companies, and is relevant to identifying legal-person/professional customers (KYB) and to credit data records.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, AML/CTF, other
