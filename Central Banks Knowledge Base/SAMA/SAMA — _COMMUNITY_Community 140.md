# Charity Donation Integration

## Why this community

Channel-specific SAMA directives narrowing which external entities financial institutions may interface with for non-core services: charitable collections and digital document verification.

## How members connect

- Both are supervisory circulars restricting or designating a single authorized counterparty/channel for a service offered through bank platforms.
- Shared consequence: institutions must discontinue unapproved arrangements and route the activity through the named entity only.
- Charity-collection channelling carries CTF/terrorist-financing-abuse relevance; document linkage supports identity and documentary verification in CDD.
