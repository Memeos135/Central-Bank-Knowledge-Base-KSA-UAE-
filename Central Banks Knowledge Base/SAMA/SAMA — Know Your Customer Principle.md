# Know Your Customer Principle

Section 9 of the remittance centers guide sets customer acceptance policies and the Know Your Customer principle: verifying identity, address, business, income and source of funds, establishing the purpose of the remittance membership, and refusing fictitious, digital or anonymous names. It requires official identity documents (national ID, iqama or passport) verified against originals, membership numbers linked to name and ID, bilingual name and ID capture, branch-manager approval of membership opening, additional approvals and enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers, and termination of the relationship where identity or source of funds cannot be verified or the membership is misused. Binds banks' remittance centers and licensed remittance companies.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection
