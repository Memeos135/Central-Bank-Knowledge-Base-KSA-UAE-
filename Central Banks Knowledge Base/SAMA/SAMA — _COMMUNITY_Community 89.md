# OTC Derivatives Governance

## Why this community

Bilateral risk-mitigation and documentation governance for non-centrally-cleared OTC derivatives: valuation, portfolio reconciliation and compression, and dispute handling between counterparties.

## How members connect

- Governance of Trading Documentation is the umbrella obligation, requiring documented, agreed procedures that in turn invoke valuation, reconciliation, compression and dispute resolution requirements.
- These members are the substantive content of the risk-mitigation limb (Section B) of the OTC derivatives regime, so they should be read together with the trade repository reporting cluster.
- Valuation outputs feed reconciliation and dispute processes, creating a sequential obligation chain rather than independent duties.
