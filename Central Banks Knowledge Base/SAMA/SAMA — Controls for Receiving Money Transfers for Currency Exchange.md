# Controls for Receiving Money Transfers for Currency Exchange

Controls setting the minimum requirements for exchange centres and bank-affiliated remittance centres when receiving customer funds through electronic payment means for currency exchange or transfer purposes; they replace the earlier circular permitting such receipts. Key obligations include a dedicated bank account, restricting receipts to members with a unified reference number tied to the customer ID and to SAR amounts, a ban on receiving customers' transfers from banks or transfer companies outside the Kingdom for currency exchange, verification and record-keeping, restrictions and refund routing/timelines for cancelled transactions, pre-agreed and documented fees and exchange rates, customer awareness, CDD under the AML and counter-terrorism financing laws and their regulations, and continuous updating of internal procedures. Binds exchange centres and bank remittance centres supervised by SAMA (distributed also to banks).

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection
