# Circular: Prohibition of Using SAMA Logo

A SAMA circular prohibiting supervised entities from placing the SAMA logo on their own printed materials and publications except where expressly permitted, after SAMA observed unauthorised use without prior approval. It is aimed at preventing reputational harm to SAMA, commercial exploitation of its emblem and misleading of customers. It binds all SAMA-supervised entities — banks, insurance and reinsurance companies, finance companies, credit bureaus, insurance-related professional firms and exchange houses — and their branches, with enforcement action against breaches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
