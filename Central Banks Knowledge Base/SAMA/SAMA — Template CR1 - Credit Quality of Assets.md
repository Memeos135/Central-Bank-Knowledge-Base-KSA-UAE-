# Template CR1 - Credit Quality of Assets

Template CR1 is a mandatory semiannual, fixed-format disclosure of the credit quality of a bank's on- and off-balance sheet assets — gross carrying values of defaulted and non-defaulted loans, debt securities and off-balance sheet exposures, allowances/impairments and net values — with ECL-provision columns applicable only to banks using an ECL accounting model. Banks must disclose their definition of default in the accompanying narrative. It sits within the Pillar 3 credit risk section (Tables CRA/CRB/CRB-A/CRC/CRD and Templates CR2–CR5), which excludes securitization and counterparty credit risk positions; IRB templates apply only where SAMA has approved IRB use.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
