# Due Diligence Measures

This node covers customer due diligence under the SAMA AML/CTF Guide, in particular reliance on third parties (other financial institutions or DNFBPs, including same-group entities) to perform CDD, as permitted by Articles 7/10, 7/11 and 7/13 of the AML Law implementing regulations. Conditions include ensuring the third party is regulated and supervised and applies CDD and record-keeping, immediate access to CDD information, consideration of high-risk country information from AMLPC, PCCT and SAFIU, risk assessment of the reliance arrangement, at-least-annual verification of the third party's capability, and continuous compliance monitoring. Ultimate responsibility remains with the relying financial institution.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
