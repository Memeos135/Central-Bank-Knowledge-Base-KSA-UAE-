# Currency Bundling Card Templates Instructions

Fourth edition of SAMA's instructions on the banding/bundling card templates used for the sixth issue of banknotes, replacing the third edition and adding a template for the SAR 200 denomination following its circulation. It prescribes mandatory card designs, colour codes per denomination, and required content (bank name and logo, note count and denomination, issue reference, two staff names and signatures) for notes delivered to SAMA branches. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and cash centres, with a three-month implementation period.

**Regimes:** other
