# Template MR3: Market Risk Simplified Standardised Approach

A Pillar 3 template for banks applying the simplified standardised approach to market risk, presented within the same instruction set governing RWA and minimum capital requirement reporting across risk categories. It prescribes the fixed reporting rows and their definitional scope. It binds SAMA-supervised banks that qualify for and apply the simplified approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
