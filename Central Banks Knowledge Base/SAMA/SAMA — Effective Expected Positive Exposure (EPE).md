# Effective Expected Positive Exposure (EPE)

Concept node for Effective EPE, the exposure metric used under the Internal Models Method to derive exposure at default for counterparty credit risk (typically scaled by alpha). The cited source documents also carry the related Pillar 3 credit risk mitigation and problem-asset disclosure material, so the node spans both the capital measurement concept and its disclosure context. Relevant to banks with SAMA approval to use internal models for CCR, and to those preparing regulatory disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
