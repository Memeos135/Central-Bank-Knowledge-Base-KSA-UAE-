# Exchange Business Procedures

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct-of-business requirements for exchange (money changing) activity in KSA, covering how exchange houses must organise operations, controls and customer dealings.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA guide that consolidates procedural requirements for the exchange sector rather than cross-referencing other instruments in this graph.
- Sits below the primary banking/AML legislative layer as guidance interpreting licensing and operational duties for a distinct licensee class.
- Practical intersection with AML/CTF CDD and remittance obligations even though those instruments are clustered elsewhere.
