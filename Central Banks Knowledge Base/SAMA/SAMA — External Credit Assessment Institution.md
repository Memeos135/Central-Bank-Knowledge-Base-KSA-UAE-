# External Credit Assessment Institution

This node addresses eligible External Credit Assessment Institutions (ECAIs) and the use of external credit ratings to determine risk weights, including the stricter operational conditions applied in the securitisation context (public, non-selective, free-of-charge availability of ratings and methodologies; demonstrated securitisation expertise; treatment where multiple ECAIs disagree). It also covers how credit risk mitigation reflected in an external rating is treated to avoid double counting. It binds banks relying on external ratings for regulatory capital purposes under SAMA's capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
