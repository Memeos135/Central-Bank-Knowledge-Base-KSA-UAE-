# Tadawul (Saudi Exchange)

Defines Tadawul as the Saudi Exchange for purposes of the Repo Guidelines and underpins the delivery rules requiring non-SAMA issued eligible securities to be delivered through Tadawul (with cash through SARIE) where both counterparties are licensed agents and the transaction is in SAR. It also frames eligibility of bonds/sukuk listed on the Saudi Exchange as repo collateral. Applies to repo market participants transacting in the Saudi repo market.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, other
