# Cryptography

## Why this community

Cryptographic protection of data in transit and at rest, including key management expectations, as a mandated safeguard for confidentiality and integrity of customer and transaction data.

## How members connect

- Isolated node that functions as a cross-cutting control: it is invoked by authentication, application, infrastructure and data-protection requirements rather than standing alone.
- Interacts with data/credit information and consumer data confidentiality obligations, where encryption is the practical means of discharging the protection duty.
