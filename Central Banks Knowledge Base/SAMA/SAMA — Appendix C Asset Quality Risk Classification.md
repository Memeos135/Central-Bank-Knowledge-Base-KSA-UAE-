# Appendix C: Asset Quality Risk Classification

Appendix C of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020, the reference schedule for asset quality risk classification used with the Chapter 6 review, classification and provisioning duties. The surrounding return guidance explains reporting lines for loans and advances net of provisions, other investments, fixed and other assets, group exposures, total on-balance-sheet assets and credit risk equivalents for off-balance-sheet items feeding the capital ratio calculation. Binds DTFCs when classifying exposures and completing prudential returns to SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
