# Approval Requirements and Controls

Approval, control and portfolio-monitoring provisions of the Banks Investment Rules, including continuous portfolio performance monitoring, diversification, annual portfolio-level stress testing available to SAMA on request, and adequate staffing. It requires SAMA non-objection for investments needing regulatory approval under the Banking Control Law and Ministerial Decree No. 3/2149 (approved at portfolio level, with prior non-objection for changes to structure or limits) and sets conditions for investing in Money Market Funds through CMA-licensed entities. Binds SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
