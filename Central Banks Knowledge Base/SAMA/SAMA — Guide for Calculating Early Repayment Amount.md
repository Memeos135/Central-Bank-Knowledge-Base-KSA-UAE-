# Guide for Calculating Early Repayment Amount

An Arabic-language SAMA guidance manual (issued with a disclaimer of liability) explaining how the mandatory uniform method for calculating the early repayment amount works for monthly-instalment finance contracts such as personal finance, vehicle lease-to-own and real estate finance. It restates the beneficiary's right to early settlement without bearing term cost for the remaining period, the permitted lender compensation (reinvestment cost capped at three months' term cost on a declining-balance basis, plus non-recoverable third-party payments), and the amortization table disclosure, with worked examples and FAQs. It is explanatory of obligations resting on banks and finance companies under the Finance Companies Control Law and Finance Lease implementing regulations and the Consumer Finance Rules, rather than a free-standing rule.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
