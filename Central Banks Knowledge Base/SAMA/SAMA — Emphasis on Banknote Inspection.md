# Emphasis on Banknote Inspection

SAMA circular reinforcing earlier security instructions by requiring banks and exchange centres to strengthen banknote inspection procedures and exercise maximum care in verifying the authenticity of currency circulating in the Kingdom. Staff must immediately notify the security authorities on suspicion of any counterfeit note and inform SAMA. It binds banks and money exchange centres operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** other, AML/CTF
