# Quarterly Currency and Largest Customers Form

A SAMA reporting template (Version 2.0) issued by the Exchange Centres Supervision Division requiring money exchange centres to report quarterly data on foreign currency sales and purchases by counterparty type (local banks, exchange houses, individuals, cross-border), inbound and outbound transfers, largest customers, counterfeit currency detected, and staff data. It is a periodic supervisory return rather than a rule text, but the data points support AML/CTF and market-conduct supervision of exchange centres. It binds licensed exchange centres subject to SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
