# Executive Management Responsibilities

Provisions on the board's relationship with executive management under SAMA's governance principles, including forming and supervising executive management, defining clear responsibility and accountability limits with full separation of duties at executive level, approving internal policies and a written delegation-of-authority policy, and selecting senior executives with a succession policy. It carries the defining terms for Executive (Senior) Management, Executive, Non-Executive and Independent Member. Binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions other than insurance and reinsurance companies.
