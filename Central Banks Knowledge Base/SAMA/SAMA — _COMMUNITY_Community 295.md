# Unified Customer Service Number

## Why this community

Consumer-protection requirement that regulated entities provide customers a unified toll-free number for service and complaints, supporting accessibility of the complaint-handling channel.

## How members connect

- Standalone instruction that feeds the broader consumer protection and complaint-handling obligation chain (accessible channel → logged complaint → resolution timelines).
- Relevant to the remote customer service localization cluster, since the free number is a customer-facing channel subject to in-Kingdom requirements.
