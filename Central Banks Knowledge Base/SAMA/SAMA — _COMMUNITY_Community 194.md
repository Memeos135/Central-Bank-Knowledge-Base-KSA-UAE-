# National Address Requirements

## Why this community

The requirement to obtain and use the National Address for customers and correspondence, a KSA-specific identification and communication data point that intersects with CDD, consumer notification and records obligations.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the National Address / postal services requirement stands alone but functions as an input to identification and customer-communication duties elsewhere in the framework.
- Relevant where other regimes require verified address data (CDD/KYC records, consumer disclosures, statement delivery).
