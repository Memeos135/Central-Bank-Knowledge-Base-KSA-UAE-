# Finance Company Licensing

## Why this community

Licensing and ongoing conduct of finance companies and finance support activities under the Finance Companies Control Law, including debt collection, BNPL limits, credit card security instruments, governance and AML/CFT/CPF risk assessment.

## How members connect

- The Executive Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law is the hub: licensing rules, circulars and product-limit decisions all reference it as their legal basis.
- A clear hierarchy law → executive regulation → licensing rules → amending circulars; the debt collection circulars form a chronological chain where the later cancellation of the licence requirement supersedes earlier outsourcing conditions.
- Product-specific interventions (BNPL per-customer limit, prohibition on promissory notes for credit card financing) modify permitted conduct without altering the licence category, so both must be read alongside the Regulation.
- Governance principles and the AML/CFT/CPF business risk assessment guide attach as cross-regime obligations on licensed finance companies; note BNPL 'Consumer' does not map one-to-one to the AML 'Customer'.
- Duplicate nodes for the Regulation and the risk assessment guide are the same instruments, not independent authorities.
