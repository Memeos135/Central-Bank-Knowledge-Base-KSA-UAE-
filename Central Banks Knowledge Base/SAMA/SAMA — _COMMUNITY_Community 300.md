# Customer Identity Verification

## Why this community

Identity verification of customers by reference to a trusted/reliable independent source — the core CDD building block for onboarding and ongoing due diligence.

## How members connect

- Anchors the AML/CTF defined term 'Customer' and the requirement that identification be verified against independent, reliable documents or data.
- Feeds downstream obligation chains: ongoing monitoring, record-keeping, enhanced due diligence and reliance on third parties for CDD.
- Cross-regime relevance: payments/PSP onboarding, BNPL 'Consumer' onboarding and merchant/KYB checks all inherit this verification standard.
