# Liquidity Risk Management

## Why this community

Qualitative disclosure of how a bank governs and manages liquidity risk, providing the narrative context for the quantitative liquidity ratios.

## How members connect

- Table-format (qualitative) Pillar 3 item, paired by design with the quantitative liquidity templates it explains.
- Relies on the same defined liquidity concepts (funding strategy, stress testing, contingency funding) used in the LCR and NSFR requirements.
- Sits in the governance/risk regime: obligations run to the board and senior management as owners of the liquidity risk framework.
