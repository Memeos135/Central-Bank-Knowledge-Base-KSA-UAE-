# Table CR2 - Changes in Stock of Defaulted Loans and Debt Securities

Template CR2 is a fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework requiring banks to show the semiannual movement in their stock of defaulted loans and debt securities: opening balance, new defaults, cures back to non-defaulted status, write-offs and other changes. Defaulted exposures are reported net of write-offs and gross of allowances/impairments, with standardised-approach banks aligning default to the past-due-more-than-90-days concept. It is mandatory for all banks and must be accompanied by narrative explaining significant flows and whether off-balance sheet items are included.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
