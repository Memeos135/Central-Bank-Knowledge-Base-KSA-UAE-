# IRB CCR Exposures by PD

## Why this community

Pillar 3 CCR disclosure for banks permitted to use the internal ratings-based approach, requiring exposures to be presented by portfolio and probability-of-default band.

## How members connect

- Applies only to banks with supervisory approval to use IRB for counterparty exposures — a scope limit that distinguishes it from the standardised-approach template.
- Draws its exposure measure from the summary CCR approach template while adding IRB-specific risk parameters (PD, LGD, maturity, RWA).
- Sits in the same hierarchy: capital framework, then Pillar 3 disclosure requirement, then prescribed template.
