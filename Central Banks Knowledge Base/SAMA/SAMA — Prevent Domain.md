# Prevent Domain

This is the Prevent domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, covering proactive controls that stop fraud before harm occurs to the organisation or its customers. It requires a Fraud Risk Management Framework aligned to enterprise risk management (covering intelligence monitoring, Fraud Risk Assessment, Fraud Risk Appetite and KRIs) and defined, approved fraud prevention standards addressing internal and external fraud, with limits and thresholds set by reference to risk assessment outcomes, loss experience and risk appetite. It binds Member Organisations, with controls expected to be proportionate to assessed risk.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
