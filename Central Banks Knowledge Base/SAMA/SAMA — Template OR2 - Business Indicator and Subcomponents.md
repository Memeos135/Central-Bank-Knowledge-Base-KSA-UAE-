# Template OR2 - Business Indicator and Subcomponents

Template OR2 requires annual disclosure of the business indicator (BI) and its subcomponents — the interest, lease and dividend component, services component and financial component — together with the resulting business indicator component (BIC) used in the operational risk capital calculation. It is mandatory for all banks, fixed format, with narrative explanation of significant changes and of any SAMA-approved exclusion of divested activities. It binds banks disclosing under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
