# Mandatory Compliance Certificate for Finance Company Compliance Officers

A SAMA circular (Arabic) requiring compliance managers/officers at finance companies to obtain the "Compliance for the Finance Companies Sector" certificate, relying on SAMA's mandate under the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law to develop sector competence and training. The certificate must be obtained within one year of the circular's date, or at the point the finance company seeks SAMA's no-objection for an appointment to the role, and a copy must be provided to SAMA. It binds all finance companies operating in the Kingdom and is relevant to fit-and-proper/appointment approvals for the compliance function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
