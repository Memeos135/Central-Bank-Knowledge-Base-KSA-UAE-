# Financial Receivables as Collateral

Concerns the recognition of receivables as eligible collateral for credit risk mitigation purposes and how secured exposures are reported. The supplied material focuses on the disclosure side: how to allocate exposures across unsecured amounts, amounts secured by collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives (including over- and under-collateralised cases and which mitigant is expected to be extinguished first), and the breakdown of collateral types posted or received for counterparty credit risk in derivatives and SFTs. Binds SAMA-supervised banks applying CRM and completing the mandatory Pillar 3 templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
