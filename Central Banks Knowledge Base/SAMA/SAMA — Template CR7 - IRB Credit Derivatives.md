# Template CR7 - IRB Credit Derivatives

Specification for Pillar 3 Template CR7, a fixed-format semiannual disclosure comparing hypothetical pre-credit-derivative RWA with actual RWA to show the mitigation effect of credit derivatives under the IRB approach, broken down by regulatory portfolio. Mandatory for banks using A-IRB and/or F-IRB for any exposures, with narrative commentary on the RWA impact. The surrounding context also illustrates completion of Template CR3 on credit risk mitigation coverage.
