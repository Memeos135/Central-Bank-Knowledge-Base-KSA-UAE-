# Interest Rate Risk (IRRBB)

## Why this community

Measurement, management and capital treatment of interest rate risk arising from non-trading (banking book) positions, and how IRRBB outcomes feed internal capital adequacy assessment and supervisory reporting.

## How members connect

- The IRRBB instrument is the anchor; it incorporates the Basel 2004 interest-rate-risk principles as the governance and measurement baseline.
- The standardised framework is the fallback/comparator methodology that supervisors may apply where internal measurement is inadequate — relevant to whether a bank's own model is accepted.
- IRRBB results are an obligation chain input into ICAAP risk reporting, so a deficiency in IRRBB measurement propagates into the internal capital assessment.
- This is Pillar 2 territory: the risk is governed and reported rather than carrying a standalone Pillar 1 charge, which matters when advising on supervisory consequence.
