# Reverse Repurchase Agreement

Defined-term node for a reverse repurchase agreement (reverse repo) in SAMA's repo guidelines: the buyer purchases securities from a seller with a simultaneous arrangement to resell or repurchase the same or equivalent securities at an agreed price on a future date or on demand. The guidelines treat repo and reverse repo interchangeably unless otherwise specified, so the same policy requirements (MRA, eligible securities, eligible counterparties) apply. Relevant to repo market participants in the Saudi market.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
