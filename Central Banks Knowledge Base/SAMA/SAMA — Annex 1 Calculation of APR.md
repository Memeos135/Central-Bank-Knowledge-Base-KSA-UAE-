# Annex 1: Calculation of APR

Annex 1 to SAMA's Consumer Financing (Finance Consumer Protection) rules, setting the mathematical basis and assumptions for calculating the Annual Percentage Rate, together with the defining terms used (APR, Total Amount Payable by the Borrower, Amount of Financing, Consumer Financing, Borrower, Business/Calendar Day, Change in Circumstance, Authenticated Communication). It requires a 12-equal-month year, inclusion of all unavoidable costs and fees but exclusion of default/breach charges, assumes the contract runs to term with both parties performing, freezes unquantifiable variable charges at their initial level, and requires expression to at least two basis points with rounding. It binds Creditors (banks and finance companies) disclosing APR on consumer finance to natural-person Borrowers acting outside trade or profession.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
