# SAMA Circular: Compensation of Security-Inked Damaged Currency

A SAMA circular transmitting controls and procedures for compensating Saudi banknotes stained by security ink as a result of technical or human error, supplementing earlier instructions mandating use of secure cash containers in cash transport. It prohibits circulation of ink-stained notes, requires their direct presentation to a SAMA branch with a compensation request form and declaration, requires counting/sorting/deposit and self-service machines to detect and reject such notes, requires staff training, and makes banks liable for operational compensation costs where negligence is shown and for failures by their cash-transport contractor; stained notes found in bank deposits attract a penalty under Article 23 of the Banking Control Law. It binds banks, exchange institutions and companies, and licensed cash-transport companies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
