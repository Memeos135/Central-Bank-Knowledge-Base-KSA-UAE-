# Initial Viability Assessment

A concept node covering the stage-two initial viability assessment applied to NPLs not yet in legal proceedings, screening borrowers as viable, marginally viable or non-viable using financial (or cash-flow) ratios and loan-to-value. It lists indicative, non-prescriptive benchmarks including Debt/EBITDA leverage, loan service coverage against sector averages, EBITDA trend comparison with industry, and an LLCR of 1.4 for project finance and multi-year loans, with thresholds to be set from general market indicators. Directed at banks' Workout Units designing restructuring strategies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
