# Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements

SAMA's Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements: a baseline cyber security and resilience control set aimed at newly established entities in the early stages of operation, notably firms in or graduating from SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox. It is structured around domains covering cyber security leadership and governance, operations and technology, and resilience, applying a risk-based approach with uniquely numbered control requirements. SAMA owns and interprets the document and may refuse sandbox graduation or licensing where an entity cannot demonstrate compliance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
