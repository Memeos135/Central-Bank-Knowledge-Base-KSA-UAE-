# Written Credit Derivatives Treatment

Defines and scopes "written credit derivatives" for leverage ratio purposes — any instrument or option through which the bank effectively provides credit protection, not only CDS and total return swaps — and sets the conditions for offsetting the effective notional amount against purchased credit protection. Key tests are identical reference names (same legal entity), same or more conservative material terms (subordination, optionality, credit events, strike price), and economic equivalence where protection is bought on a pool rather than individual names. Binds SAMA-licensed banks computing derivative exposures in the leverage ratio.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
