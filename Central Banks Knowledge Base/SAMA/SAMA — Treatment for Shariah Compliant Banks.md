# Treatment for Shariah Compliant Banks

Addresses the LCR position of Shari'ah compliant banks, which cannot hold interest-bearing debt securities, by allowing national supervisors discretion to recognise Shari'ah compliant products such as Sukuk as alternative HQLA, subject to conditions and haircuts. The treatment must not result in a lower effective LCR standard than that applied to other banks. The context records that SAMA has not utilised any option under the alternative treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
