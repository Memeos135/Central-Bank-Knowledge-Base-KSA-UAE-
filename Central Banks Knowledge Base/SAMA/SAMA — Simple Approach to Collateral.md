# Simple Approach to Collateral

Describes the simple approach to recognising financial collateral in credit risk mitigation, under which the counterparty's risk weight is substituted by the risk weight of the collateral instrument. Recognition conditions include pledging for at least the life of the exposure and revaluation at least every six months; maturity mismatches are not permitted. Binds banks calculating credit risk RWA under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
