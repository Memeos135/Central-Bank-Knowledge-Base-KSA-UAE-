# Financial Institutions Service Fee Guide

This is SAMA's Financial Institutions Service Fee Guide (First Edition), which sets the tariff framework financial institutions must observe when charging for services and products, including fees for basic retail services (e.g. certificates of relationship, debt/liability confirmations, account statements, clearance letters) and criteria for setting product and service fees. It overrides conflicting provisions in earlier instructions, including the previously updated banking tariff. It binds all persons, natural or legal, subject to SAMA's supervision, in their dealings with customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, BNPL/finance
