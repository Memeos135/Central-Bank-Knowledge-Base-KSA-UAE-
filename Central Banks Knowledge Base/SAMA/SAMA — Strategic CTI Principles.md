# Strategic CTI Principles

The strategic tier of SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, addressing the specialized practice of identifying the objectives, motivations and intent of threat actors. It sits on top of the Core CTI Principles and feeds board- and risk-level decision-making on sector resilience. Applies to Member Organizations, including those outsourcing CTI, with discretion over phased implementation.
