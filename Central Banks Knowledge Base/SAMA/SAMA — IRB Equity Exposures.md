# IRB Equity Exposures

This node concerns equity exposures under the IRB framework and their interaction with the securitisation capital rules, including how IRB pools, mixed pools and standardised-approach pools are classified for risk-based capital. It also addresses implicit support to a securitisation, the resulting capital consequence, non-recognition of gain on sale, and the look-through risk-weight caps for senior securitisation exposures. It binds banks supervised by SAMA in determining credit risk capital, with several determinations reserved to SAMA approval or prohibition.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
