# Reliance on Third Party

Paragraphs 3.22–3.25 of SAMA's AML/CTF rules permitting a financial institution to rely on another financial institution or a DNFBP (including within the same financial group) to perform customer due diligence, subject to conditions. Conditions include that the third party is regulated and supervised, applies CDD and record-keeping consistent with the AML Law and CTF Law and their implementing regulations, provides CDD information immediately on request, and that high-risk country information from AMLPC, PCCT and SAFIU is considered. Ultimate responsibility remains with the relying institution, which must risk-assess reliance, monitor such customers, and verify the third party's capability at least annually.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
