# Basel III Post-Crisis Reforms

The framing and scope provisions of SAMA's operational risk capital framework implementing the Basel III post-crisis reforms (December 2017), adopting the revised standardized approach as the sole method for calculating operational risk capital. It defines operational risk, sets the ORC = BIC × ILM calculation with the Business Indicator thresholds, quarterly Q17 reporting within 30 days of quarter-end, and loss-data disclosure duties. It applies to domestic banks consolidated and standalone, excludes foreign bank branches, and supersedes conflicting prior Basel II guidance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
