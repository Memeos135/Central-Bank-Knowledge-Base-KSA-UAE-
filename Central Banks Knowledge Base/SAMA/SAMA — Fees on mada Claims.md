# Fees on mada Claims

SAMA circular imposing settlement fees on banks in connection with mada claims. Banks must refund customers for erroneous or incomplete transactions across ATM, POS and e-commerce services within two business days, and banks failing to do so incur per-claim settlement fees under a stated tariff, effective from the specified date. It binds banks, payment service providers and Saudi Payments, and rests on SAMA's statutory authority over payment and settlement systems.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
