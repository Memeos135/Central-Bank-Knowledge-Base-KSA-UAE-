# Liquidity Coverage Ratio

## Why this community

Basel III prudential quantification in the KSA context — liquidity (LCR/NSFR), capital scope and credit risk approaches, plus D-SIB add-ons — together with the Shariah-specific adaptations (HQLA alternatives, profit-sharing investment accounts) that SAMA layers on top.

## How members connect

- Definitional dependency: LCR is built from HQLA stock over total net cash outflows, which in turn is decomposed into retail, unsecured wholesale, operational-deposit and secured-funding run-off categories.
- Hierarchy law→standard→guidance: SAMA circulars and guidance documents transpose and amend the BCBS Basel III LCR and NSFR texts, so BCBS documents are cited rather than directly binding.
- Shariah bridge: the Treatment for Shariah Compliant Banks and Alternative Liquidity Approaches connect the liquidity regime to Mudarabah/Wakalah/Musharakah-based funding, PSIA rules, PER/IRR and Investment Account Holders, with Shari'ah Committee and board/senior-management governance duties attached.
- Capital-side branch: scope of application of the Basel framework, standardized vs IRB credit risk, ECRA/SCRA and D-SIB assessment with higher loss absorbency sit alongside liquidity as the other pillar of the same prudential package.
- Banks' investment rules (governance, risk monitoring, approval controls) feed the asset side that drives RSF/HQLA classification.
