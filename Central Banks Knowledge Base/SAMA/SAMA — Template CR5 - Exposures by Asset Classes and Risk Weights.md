# Template CR5 - Exposures by Asset Classes and Risk Weights

Template CR5 requires banks on the standardised approach to break down credit risk exposures (post-CCF and post-CRM) by asset class and risk weight band (0%, 20%, 50%, 100%, 150%, other). Mandatory for standardised-approach banks, semiannual and fixed format; banks phasing in IRB may use it for residual asset classes, and may omit it only where exposures are negligible, subject to explanation and SAMA's immateriality approval. Narrative must explain significant period-on-period changes and the sequence in which CCFs, provisioning and CRM are applied.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
