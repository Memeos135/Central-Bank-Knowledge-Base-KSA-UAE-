# Cyber Threat Intelligence

## Why this community

The operational tier of the cyber threat intelligence expectations — technical and tactical intelligence requirements applicable to supervised entities' security functions.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster that completes the CTI tiering alongside the core and strategic principle sets; read together with the CTI principles community.
- Its requirements are operational in nature but derive their enforceability from the parent cyber threat intelligence framework.
- Outputs at this tier feed detection and defensive activity that is separately tested under the ethical red teaming regime.
