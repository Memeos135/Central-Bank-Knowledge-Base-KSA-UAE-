# Restructuring Options

This node sets out how a bank identifies and selects workout options for defaulted or distressed borrowers, including the two pre-conditions for approving a restructuring plan (sufficient projected cash flow to clear past-due obligations within a reasonable period, and cooperative borrower behaviour) and the preference for out-of-court settlement where documented recovery value exceeds foreclosure or bankruptcy. It also carries the generic restructuring principles (good faith, transparency, prompt response, sustainable rather than repeated short-term fixes, confidentiality) and points dissenting minority lenders in syndicated deals to the Bankruptcy Law. It is directed at banks' Workout Units and credit/legal functions handling viable and marginally viable borrowers.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
