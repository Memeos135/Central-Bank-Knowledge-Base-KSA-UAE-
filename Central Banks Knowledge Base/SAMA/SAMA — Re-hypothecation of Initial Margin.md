# Re-hypothecation of Initial Margin

Concept node setting the general prohibition on re-hypothecation, re-pledge or re-use of cash and non-cash collateral collected as initial margin, and the narrow conditional exception. Re-use is permitted only to hedge the collector's derivatives position arising from customer transactions, requires case-by-case SAMA approval, express written customer consent after disclosure of the right to refuse and of insolvency risks, an option to individually segregate, liquidity-risk regulation of the collector, and customer-asset segregation by both collector and third party. Binds initial margin collectors supervised by SAMA and, by contract, downstream third parties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection
