# Template SEC4: Securitisation Banking Book Investor

Template SEC4 discloses banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as investor, with the related risk-weighted assets and capital requirements by risk weight band and regulatory approach. Like SEC3, it captures only exposures treated under the securitisation capital framework, so there is no double counting with the broader SEC1/SEC2 templates. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
