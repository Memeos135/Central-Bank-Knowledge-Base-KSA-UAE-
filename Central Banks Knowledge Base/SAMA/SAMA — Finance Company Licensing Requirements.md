# Finance Company Licensing Requirements

Licensing-focused view of the amended Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. It frames the single-activity licence categories — micro consumer finance company, Buy Now Pay Later (deferred payment) company, debt crowdfunding company and micro finance company — each licensed to conduct only that finance activity, and ties permitted activities to those listed in Article 10 of the Law or otherwise approved by SAMA. Relevant to any entity seeking or holding a SAMA finance licence, including BNPL providers whose activity is defined as financing a beneficiary's purchase of goods or services from merchants with no term cost charged to the beneficiary.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
