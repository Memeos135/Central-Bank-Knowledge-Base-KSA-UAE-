# Controls on Handling Old or Damaged Devices in Local Banks

A SAMA circular instructing local banks and licensed money exchangers to exercise caution when disposing of obsolete or damaged bank-owned equipment and media — ATMs, POS terminals, computers, imaging devices, related documents, tapes and recording/imaging discs. It requires firms to adopt policies and procedures for disposal that preserve the confidentiality of information stored on such assets and prohibits simply discarding them as waste, where they could be tampered with or misused. It binds local banks and exchange houses, reaffirming their legal responsibility for damaged devices and the data they contain.
