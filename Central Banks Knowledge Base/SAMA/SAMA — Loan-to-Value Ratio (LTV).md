# Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)

Specifies how the loan-to-value ratio is calculated and maintained for real estate exposures: loan amount (including undrawn committed amounts, gross of provisions, with limited netting for pledged deposits) over an independently and conservatively appraised property value, generally frozen at origination subject to downward revision, permanent-impairment adjustment, or value-enhancing modifications. It also requires mortgage underwriting policies assessing repayment ability and confirms that LTV bucket and risk weight are set before applying credit risk mitigation. Binds banks originating and risk-weighting mortgage lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
