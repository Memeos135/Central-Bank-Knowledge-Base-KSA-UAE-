# Founding Day Holiday

## Why this community

Observance of the national Founding Day holiday and its effect on financial-sector working days, service availability and the computation of regulatory and contractual time limits.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA holiday notification addressed to supervised entities.
- Links outward to operating-hours and business-continuity instructions, since a declared non-working day shifts settlement, reporting and customer-service obligations.
- Relevant to any deadline counted in business days under other SAMA rules (e.g. complaint handling, reporting).
