# Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP) Transactions

Delivery-versus-payment transactions are settlements providing simultaneous exchange of securities for cash, exposing the bank to loss on the difference between the agreed settlement price and current market price (positive current exposure). Banks must hold capital against such exposures where payment has not occurred five business days after the settlement date; failure of a counterparty to settle is not itself a default for credit risk purposes. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks trading securities, FX and commodities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
