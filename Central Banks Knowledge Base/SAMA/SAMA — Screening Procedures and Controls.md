# Screening Procedures and Controls

Chapter Three of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, covering screening procedures and controls that financial institutions must apply against sanctions lists. It sits within the mandatory TFS framework alongside governance, list management, alert escalation and freezing chapters, and determines when and against what populations screening obligations arise. Binds all financial institutions under SAMA regulation and supervision.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF
