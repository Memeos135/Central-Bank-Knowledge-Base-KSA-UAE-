# Stress Calibration of Effective EPE

Concept node on the stressed calibration requirement for Effective EPE under the internal models method: banks must compute credit RWA as the higher of current-parameter and stressed-parameter results, calibrating Effective EPE with at least three years of data including a period of stress to counterparties' credit spreads, or with market-implied data from a suitable stress period. It sets quarterly demonstration and data-update obligations, mapping rules where counterparty credit spread data is unavailable, and constraints on reverting from IMM to the standardised approach. Binds banks using IMM for counterparty credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
