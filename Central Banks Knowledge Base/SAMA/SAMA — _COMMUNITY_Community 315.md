# Asset Repossession Authorities

## Why this community

Repossession of financed assets by creditors, and the requirement that recovery be effected through the competent authorities rather than by self-help enforcement.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a SAMA circular directing finance providers on the lawful channel for taking possession of collateral or financed assets.
- Sits at the intersection of the finance-company/credit regime and consumer protection, since debtor treatment and due-process limits constrain collection practice.
- As a circular it operates as supervisory instruction layered on the underlying finance law and implementing regulations.
