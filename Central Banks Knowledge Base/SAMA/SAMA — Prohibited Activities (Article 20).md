# Prohibited Activities (Article 20)

Article 20 bars the Bank from permitting its Agent to undertake specified activities, including offline or manual operation during communication failure, transactions without a generated receipt, non-electronic transactions off-premises, soliciting customer account details or PINs, any form of manual cash services absent written SAMA non-objection, activities outside SAMA's non-objection for that Agent, payment services outside SAMA's payment system infrastructure, accessing credit bureau reports in loan processing, disclosing information obtained through the work, and breaching the Bank's code of ethics. SAMA may add further prohibitions. The prohibition is addressed to the Bank, which must prevent the Agent from performing these acts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk
