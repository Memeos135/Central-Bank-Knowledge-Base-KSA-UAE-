# IT Disaster Recovery Plan

Node covering the IT Disaster Recovery Plan requirements under the SAMA BCM Framework: an approved IT DRP aligned to the BIA, an alternative data centre (site chosen on risk assessment and subject to SAMA approval), mirrored configurations and security controls, backup/recovery processes, offsite backup storage, and continuity clauses in third-party contracts. Accountability sits with the IT manager for currency of the plans and with the BCM manager for integration into the BCM programme. Binds all member organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
