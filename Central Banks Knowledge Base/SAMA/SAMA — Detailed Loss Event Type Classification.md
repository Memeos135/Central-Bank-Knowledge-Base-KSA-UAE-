# Detailed Loss Event Type Classification

Concept node on the specific criteria for building the standardised approach operational loss data set, including definitions of gross loss, net loss and recoveries, the reference date and grouped losses. It specifies which items must enter the gross loss computation (direct P&L charges and write-downs, consequential external costs, provisions/reserves, and material pending losses) and restricts netting of recoveries to amounts actually received, with evidence available to SAMA on request. It binds banks calculating operational risk capital under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
