# Internal Audit Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies

The Internal Audit Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies, issued by SAMA under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation and effective after publication on SAMA's website. It defines the internal audit function, the Department, its head and internal auditors, plus independence, objectivity and conflict of interest, and sets principles on the audit charter, policy, annual plan, reporting, external quality assessment, record retention and the relationship with first- and second-line units. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies operating in the Kingdom.
