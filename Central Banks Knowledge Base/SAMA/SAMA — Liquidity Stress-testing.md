# Liquidity Stress-testing

The liquidity stress-testing component of the ILAAP guidelines, including Annexure (1) on stress-testing and the Contingency Funding Plan. It requires banks to run regular stress tests identifying sources of liquidity strain, to test institution-specific, market-wide and combined scenarios, to check exposures against the board-set liquidity risk tolerance, and to analyse governance of scenario design, assumptions and how results feed risk frameworks, strategic planning and liquidity recovery. It also covers restrictions on intra-group liquidity transferability and aggregation of results into an overall liquidity adequacy view.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
