# Definition of Bank Exposures

The IRB asset-class definition of bank exposures in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, delimiting which counterparties fall into the bank class versus corporate, sovereign or retail classes for capital purposes. It complements the standardised-approach chapter on exposures to banks, securities firms and other financial institutions. Binds banks calculating credit risk RWA under SAMA rules.
