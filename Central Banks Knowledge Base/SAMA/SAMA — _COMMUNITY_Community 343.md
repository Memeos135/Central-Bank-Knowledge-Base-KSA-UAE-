# Valuables Transport Law

## Why this community

Statutory regime governing the licensed transport of cash, precious metals and valuable documents in the Kingdom, including who may carry out such activity and the security and supervisory conditions attached to it.

## How members connect

- Single standalone primary-law instrument; it sits at the top of any hierarchy for cash-in-transit activity rather than deriving from another SAMA rulebook.
- Relevant as a scope boundary: transport operators are a distinct licensed category, separate from banks and payment institutions, though banks contracting them face outsourcing and operational-risk consequences.
- Its licensing and security obligations are the anchor for any downstream implementing rules or circulars on cash handling.
