# IRB Credit Derivatives CRM Effect

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of how credit derivatives used as credit risk mitigation affect risk-weighted assets for exposures under the internal ratings-based approach.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster: sits within the SAMA Pillar 3 disclosure framework for banks approved to use IRB approaches for credit risk.
- Depends on upstream definitions of eligible credit risk mitigation (guarantees and credit derivatives) and of the IRB exposure classes used to scope the disclosure.
- Complements the other IRB credit-risk templates by isolating the RWA effect attributable to CRM rather than the underlying exposure measurement.
