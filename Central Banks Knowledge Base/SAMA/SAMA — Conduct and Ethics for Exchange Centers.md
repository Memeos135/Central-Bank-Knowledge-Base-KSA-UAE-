# Conduct and Ethics for Exchange Centers

Short SAMA circular extending the scope of the Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions (issued under the earlier referenced circular) to exchange companies and establishments. It confirms that money changers fall within the "financial institution" definition for those principles, which address discipline, integrity, transparency, objectivity, competence and loyalty of staff. It binds exchange companies and establishments operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
