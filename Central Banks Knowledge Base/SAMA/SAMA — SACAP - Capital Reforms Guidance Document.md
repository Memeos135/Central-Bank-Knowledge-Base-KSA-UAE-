# SACAP - Capital Reforms Guidance Document

SACAP is SAMA's capital definition and reforms guidance, setting out the composition of CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 capital, regulatory adjustments and deductions (goodwill, intangibles, DTAs, cash flow hedge reserve, shortfall of provisions, own-share and reciprocal holdings) and the 10%/5% threshold treatment for investments in financial-sector entities and TLAC liabilities. It is the reference standard cited throughout the Pillar 3 capital composition templates. Binding on SAMA-licensed banks at solo and consolidated levels.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
