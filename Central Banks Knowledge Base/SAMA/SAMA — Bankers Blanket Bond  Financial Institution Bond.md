# Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond

A concept node identifying the Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond as a core insurance coverage for bank operational risk, typically bundling insuring agreements on employee dishonesty, loss of property on premises and in transit, and forgery. It is presented as the final layer of fraud control after training, prevention/detection programmes, operational management and internal audit. Guidance-level material for bank management structuring insurance coverage.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
