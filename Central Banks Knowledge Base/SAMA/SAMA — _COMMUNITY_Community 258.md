# CCR Collateral Composition

## Why this community

Disclosure of the collateral supporting counterparty credit risk exposures, split by collateral type and by whether it is used in derivative transactions or securities financing transactions, and whether it is segregated.

## How members connect

- Functions as the credit risk mitigation counterpart to the exposure templates: collateral disclosed here underpins the net exposure figures reported elsewhere in the CCR template set.
- Relies on the framework's eligible-collateral and segregation definitions, which determine recognition for capital purposes.
- Same disclosure obligation chain: capital adequacy rules, then the public disclosure requirement, then the fixed template format.
