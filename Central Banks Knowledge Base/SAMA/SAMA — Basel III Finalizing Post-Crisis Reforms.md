# Basel III: Finalizing Post-Crisis Reforms

Reference node for the Basel III post-crisis final reforms (BCBS, December 2017) as adopted by SAMA, identifying the family of SAMA frameworks derived from it — SCRE (credit risk), SCCR (CCR/CVA), SMAR (market risk), SOPE (operational risk), SLEV (leverage ratio), SLCR (LCR) and SNSF (NSFR) — plus the legacy SACAP guidance. Useful mainly as a glossary/orientation point for the abbreviations used across SAMA prudential and Pillar 3 texts; it binds banks through the individual adopting frameworks rather than directly.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
