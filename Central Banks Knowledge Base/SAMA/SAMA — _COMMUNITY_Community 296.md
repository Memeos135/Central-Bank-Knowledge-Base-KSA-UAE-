# Currency Bundling Card Templates

## Why this community

Prescribed form and content requirements for currency bundling/packaging card templates used in cash handling and distribution.

## How members connect

- Single-member operational instruction within SAMA's currency issuance and cash-handling authority, rather than a conduct or prudential regime.
- Compliance consequence is conformity of banknote bundle labelling with the mandated template; deviations are supervisory findings, not consumer-protection breaches.
