# Wakalah Contract

Defined-term node within the PSIA Rules identifying Wakalah as an agency-based contract under which the bank acts as Wakeel for Investment Account Holders, as an alternative to Mudarabah for raising PSIA funding. It is relevant to determining the bank's role, fee/profit entitlement and liability posture in Shari'ah-compliant investment accounts. Applies to banks and Islamic windows offering PSIAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
