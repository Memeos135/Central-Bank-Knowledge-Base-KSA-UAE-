# Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities

Cross-reference node for the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities, which supply the defined terms used in the Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity and the licensing gateway for the activity. Aggregation may not be carried out without a SAMA licence granted under those Rules, and the Instructions operate without prejudice to the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. Relevant to any entity seeking or holding a finance support activity licence.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
