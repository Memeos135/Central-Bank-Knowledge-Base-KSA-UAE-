# Scope of Application

Defines which portfolios, trading desks and risks fall within the market risk capital requirement, including the three-prong test for eligibility to use internal models (qualitative criteria, desk-by-desk nomination, and SAMA model approval). Desks not nominated or not approved must be capitalised under the standardised approach, and banks cannot exclude desks simply because the standardised outcome is lower. Binds banks seeking or holding IMA permission from SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
