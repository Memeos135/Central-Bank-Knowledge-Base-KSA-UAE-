# Operational Risk Stress Testing

Covers operational risk stress testing under SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing: banks must build scenarios from their own past operational loss event data plus professional judgement, using assumptions distinct from those applied to credit and market risk, and must feed any resulting additional capital need into capital planning. Plausible scenarios include major business disruption or system/telecommunication failures assessed for their effect on earnings and capital. The node also reflects the residual "other risks" expectation that banks identify and stress further material risks, and SAMA's supervisory monitoring of program effectiveness and compliance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
