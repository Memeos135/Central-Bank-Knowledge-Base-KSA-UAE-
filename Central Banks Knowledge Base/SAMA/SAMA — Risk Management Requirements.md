# Risk Management Requirements

This node sets the binding risk-management expectations for repo activity: documented policies and procedures covering governance, authorisation, risk management, internal controls and reporting; coverage of credit, counterparty, market, legal and operational risk; coordination across treasury, back office, risk, compliance and IT; SOCPA-endorsed accounting treatment; and exposure limits for counterparties and collateral issuers reviewed periodically. It also carries the guidelines' legal basis (SAMA Charter and Banking Control Law) and confirms all policy requirements are binding on Saudi repo market participants, to be read with SAMA, CMA and Bankruptcy Law requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
