# Bank Account Rules

Concept node for SAMA's Bank Account Rules, the standing framework governing who may open and operate bank accounts in the Kingdom, the documentation and identification requirements per customer category, and the operating controls and ongoing update duties applicable to each account type. The Rules bind banks operating in the Kingdom and are periodically amended by SAMA circular — in the cited source by adding rules for non-resident natural and legal persons covered by the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk
