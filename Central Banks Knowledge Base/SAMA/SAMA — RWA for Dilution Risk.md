# RWA for Dilution Risk

Concept node on the separate capital charge for dilution risk on purchased receivables — the risk that receivable amounts are reduced by credits or offsets to the obligor — and how its RWA is calculated and added to default risk RWA under the IRB approach. It covers recognition of collateral and guarantees that cover dilution losses, including first-loss protection interacting with the securitization framework and SEC-IRBA exposure-weighted LGD. Binds SAMA-supervised banks using IRB for purchased receivables.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
