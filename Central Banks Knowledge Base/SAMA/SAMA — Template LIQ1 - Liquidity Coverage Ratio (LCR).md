# Template LIQ1 - Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)

Template LIQ1 is the fixed Pillar 3 disclosure of the Liquidity Coverage Ratio, reported as averages of daily observations over the most recent three months, with HQLA shown after haircuts but before Level 2/2B caps, and inflows/outflows shown unweighted and weighted, then adjusted for applicable caps. It explains why reported components may not sum exactly to the disclosed LCR where the inflow cap binds. It applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime and is read with the KM1 key metrics template.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
