# Derivative Exposures (Leverage Ratio)

A component concept within SAMA's Basel-aligned capital/disclosure framework covering how derivative positions enter the leverage ratio exposure measure and the related prudential disclosure templates (e.g. key metrics and capital distribution constraint templates). It governs measurement of replacement cost and potential future exposure for derivatives and their reflection in publicly disclosed leverage metrics. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA on a consolidated group basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
