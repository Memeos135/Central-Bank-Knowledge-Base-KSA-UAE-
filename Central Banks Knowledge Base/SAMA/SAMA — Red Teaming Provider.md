# Red Teaming Provider

Sets out who may act as the external Red Teaming Provider and the selection, procurement and contracting expectations — proven experience and references, independent quality feedback, certified staff, and a documented red teaming methodology, governance, QA and risk management process. Provider selection requires Green Team (SAMA) approval before the Member Organization procures, with NDA, liability and Letter of Authorization terms agreed. Binds Member Organizations in their supplier selection and, contractually, the provider itself.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
