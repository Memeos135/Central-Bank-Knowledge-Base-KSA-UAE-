# Money Transfer Services Providers

A concept node within SAMA's Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers covering third-party money transfer services providers contracted by remittance centers. It sets pre-contract vetting (company profile, draft contract, AML/CFT compliance certificate), requires transfer to be the provider's core business, adequate AML/CFT and anti-fraud policies, and mandatory contract clauses on Saudi law and SAMA instructions, UN/FATF listings and warnings, banking confidentiality, KYC and ongoing CDD, and providing information to SAMA. It binds banks' remittance centers and licensed remittance companies, which retain supervisory responsibility over the providers operating through them, and includes escalation duties (restricting or terminating relationships, reporting suspicion) where providers repeatedly fail to supply originator information or cooperate.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk
