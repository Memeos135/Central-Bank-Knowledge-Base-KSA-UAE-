# Deductible Ratio

Sets the quantitative deduction caps on consumer indebtedness under the Responsible Lending Principles, tiered by total monthly income (bands starting at SAR 15,000 and SAR 15,000–25,000), limiting salary-linked monthly credit obligations (33.33% for employees, 25% for retirees), non-real-estate obligations (45%), and total obligations (55%/65%, with a higher cap for Ministry of Housing or Real Estate Development Fund mortgage beneficiaries). It also sits with the qualitative duties to use a documented creditworthiness methodology, obtain consumer consent, check and register credit-bureau records, and disclose other obligations. Binds creditors (banks and finance companies) granting credit to individuals.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
