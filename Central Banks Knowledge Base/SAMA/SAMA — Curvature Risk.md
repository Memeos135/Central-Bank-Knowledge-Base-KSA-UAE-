# Curvature Risk

Curvature risk captures non-linear (second-order) price sensitivity not covered by delta, computed by applying prescribed upward and downward shocks—for GIRR, CSR and commodities, a parallel shift based on the highest delta risk weight—and aggregated using squared delta correlations, with special treatment for CSR non-securitisations and securitisations (CTP). Banks may extend curvature to non-optional instruments only under stated consistency conditions. Binds banks applying SAMA's standardised approach to market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
