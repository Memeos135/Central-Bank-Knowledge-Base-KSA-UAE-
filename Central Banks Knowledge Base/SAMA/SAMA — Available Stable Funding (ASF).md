# Available Stable Funding (ASF)

Defines Available Stable Funding — the portion of a bank's capital and liabilities treated as reliable over the NSFR's one-year horizon — and the ASF factors applied to each liability and capital category. Calibration turns on funding tenor and on funding type/counterparty, with retail and small business deposits assumed more stable than comparable wholesale funding; factors range from 100% for regulatory capital and long-dated liabilities down to 0% for residual liabilities and equity. Relevant to banks calculating the NSFR numerator and to SAMA's supervisory assessment of funding risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
