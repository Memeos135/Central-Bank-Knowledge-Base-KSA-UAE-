# Licensing Disclosure Instructions (SAMA 5536)

SAMA's Licensing Disclosure Instructions (July 2019), circulated with a three-month implementation period, require financial institutions to disclose in the footers of their correspondence, printed material, email, websites, mobile apps, advertising and social media that they are licensed and subject to SAMA supervision, together with entity name as licensed, type, head office, commercial registration and licence number. Defined terms include "Financial Institution", "Correspondence", "Footers", "Advertising" and "Customers". It binds banks, finance companies, insurance/reinsurance and free-profession insurance firms, credit bureaus, money exchangers, payment and payment-systems companies and cash centres; foreign branches of Saudi institutions may add the disclosure subject to host-country law, and commercial-paper banking transactions are excluded.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, governance/risk, other
