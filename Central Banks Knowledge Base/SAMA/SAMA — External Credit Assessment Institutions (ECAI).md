# External Credit Assessment Institutions (ECAI)

Defines External Credit Assessment Institutions and the eligibility criteria SAMA applies before an ECAI's ratings may be used for regulatory capital, covering resources, credibility, independence, disclosure of conflicts of interest and compensation arrangements, and cooperation with SAMA. It also underpins bank internal assessment processes (e.g. SEC-IAA) that must map to ECAI standards and use the most conservative ECAI stress factors where ratings differ. It binds banks using external ratings and sets expectations on recognised ECAIs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** Whether a piece of collateral can be recognized for credit risk mitigation often turns on a rating, and that rating only counts if it comes from a credit rating agency recognized as an ECAI under the framework's recognition process. The collateral eligibility provisions therefore depend on the ECAI recognition rules as a precondition, linking the mitigation chapter to the ratings chapter of the same SAMA instrument. Consequence: if an instrument is rated only by a non-recognized agency, the bank cannot rely on that rating to treat the collateral as eligible, and the exposure must be capitalised unmitigated or under another recognized route.
