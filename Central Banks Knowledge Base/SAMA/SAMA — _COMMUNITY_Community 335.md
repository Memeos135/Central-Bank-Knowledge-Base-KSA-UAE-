# Regulatory Compliance

## Why this community

General regulatory compliance obligation — the duty of supervised entities to comply with applicable SAMA laws, regulations and instructions and to evidence that compliance.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in the graph: it functions as a catch-all obligation that other regimes (AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection) attach to rather than one with its own internal hierarchy.
- Read it as the anchor for compliance-function accountability and for supervisory findings that cite non-compliance generally.
