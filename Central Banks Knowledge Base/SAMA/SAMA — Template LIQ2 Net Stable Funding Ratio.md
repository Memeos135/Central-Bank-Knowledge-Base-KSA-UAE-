# Template LIQ2: Net Stable Funding Ratio

Pillar 3 node for template LIQ2, the quantitative disclosure of the Net Stable Funding Ratio, breaking out available stable funding and required stable funding by category and residual maturity. It is the structural-funding counterpart to LIQ1 and feeds the liquidity metrics summarised in KM1. Binding on banks subject to SAMA's NSFR standard and Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
