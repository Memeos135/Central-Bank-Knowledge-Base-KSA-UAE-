# Acceptance Certificate (Appendix 2)

Appendix 2 to SAMA's mandatory standard vehicle Ijarah contract for individuals: the certificate by which the lessee acknowledges receipt and acceptance of the leased vehicle. It evidences delivery/receipt of the asset and is the trigger point for the lessee's obligations under the delivery clauses; binding on lessors required to use the approved form unamended.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
