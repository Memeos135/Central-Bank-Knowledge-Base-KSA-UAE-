# Price Sources and Valuation

Section of SAMA's Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements dealing with how repo collateral is priced and valued, requiring counterparties to agree price sources in advance, to value securities on a dirty-price basis including accrued interest/profit, and to use a waterfall of fallback sources if a primary source fails. It sits alongside the eligible-collateral and custody provisions and connects to the default-valuation mechanics of the SAMA-approved Master Repurchase Agreement. It binds all repo market participants in Saudi Arabia, with additional custody obligations falling on licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
