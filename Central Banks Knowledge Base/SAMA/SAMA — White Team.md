# White Team

Defines the White Team within the Member Organization — a limited group (maximum five) of security and business experts led by a White Team Leader, being the only staff aware of the red teaming test and the single points of contact. The White Team contracts and manages the Red Teaming Provider, defines scope and critical information assets, monitors execution, and decides whether to stop, adjust or continue a test when detected by the Blue Team; it also organises debriefing and the replay exercise. Binds the Member Organization as the accountable internal owner of the exercise.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
