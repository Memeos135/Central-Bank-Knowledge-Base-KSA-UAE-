# Cyber Security Risk Identification

Sub-node of the framework's risk management cycle dealing with identification of cyber security risks as the first step before analysis, response and monitoring. It sits within the control set that requires documented risk treatment options and alignment with the organisation's risk appetite and SAMA regulations. Binds Member Organizations and is measured under the cyber security maturity model.
