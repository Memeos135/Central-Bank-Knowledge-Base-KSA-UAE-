# Unified POS Pricing Model

## Why this community

Pricing conduct in card acceptance: a unified tariff structure for point-of-sale and e-commerce merchant services, constraining what acquirers and payment service providers may charge.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster within the payments/PSP regime, giving effect to SAMA's authority over payment services and merchant acquiring pricing.
- Obligation chain runs to acquirers/PSPs as addressees, with merchants as the protected counterparty — a market-conduct overlay adjacent to consumer protection principles.
- Interacts with merchant onboarding duties: the same merchant relationship that is priced under this circular is a customer relationship for AML/KYB purposes.
