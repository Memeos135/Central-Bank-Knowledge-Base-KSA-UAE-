# Circular: Optional Compliance for SME/Large Enterprise Dealings

A SAMA circular making compliance with specified articles of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law optional for finance companies when dealing with small, medium and large enterprises, issued under SAMA's mandate to regulate and supervise the finance sector. It defines the relevant enterprise sizes by reference to an earlier SAMA circular adopting the SME Authority's size-classification decision. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and functions as a scope limitation on retail-style conduct obligations for business customers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
