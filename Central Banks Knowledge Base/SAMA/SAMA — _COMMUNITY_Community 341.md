# Problem Assets Prudential Treatment

## Why this community

Prudential classification and treatment of problem assets — how supervised lenders must identify, categorise and provision for non-performing and forborne exposures.

## How members connect

- Standalone prudential circular; its force comes from SAMA's supervisory powers over credit risk and capital adequacy rather than from cross-references within this cluster.
- Turns on defined asset-quality terms (non-performing exposure, forbearance) whose meaning governs downstream provisioning, capital and disclosure consequences.
- Cross-regime relevance: classification outcomes feed governance/risk reporting and credit-information reporting, and affect how restructuring is treated for consumer-protection purposes.
