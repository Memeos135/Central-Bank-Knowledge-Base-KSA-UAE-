# Instructions for Digital Brokerage Activity

SAMA circular approving the Instructions for Practising Digital Brokerage Activity, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and complementing the Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities. It sets minimum technical, organisational, disclosure, conflict-of-interest, record-keeping and contractual requirements for the licensed entity acting as a digital platform between customers and financing entities, including electronic identification and verification of the customer and validation of financing offers. It binds entities licensed by SAMA to carry out digital brokerage; related instructions (information security, business continuity, customer protection principles) continue to apply.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, other
