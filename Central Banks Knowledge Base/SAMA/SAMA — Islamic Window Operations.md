# Islamic Window Operations

This node covers the internal control and Islamic window provisions of SAMA's Shariah governance framework for banks, comprising the Shariah compliance function, Shariah non-compliance risk management and internal Shariah audit, plus the separate requirements applying where Islamic banking is conducted through a window within a conventional bank. It requires segregation of Shariah-compliant assets and funding sources, separate accounting records, separate (at least monthly) financial statements for Islamic operations, and at least annual internal audit. It binds SAMA-licensed banks, their boards and audit committees, Shariah committees, risk and internal audit functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
