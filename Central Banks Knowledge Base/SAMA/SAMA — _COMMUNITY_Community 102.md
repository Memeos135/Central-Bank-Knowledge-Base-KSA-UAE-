# Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles

## Why this community

Governing principles for cyber threat intelligence capability within supervised financial entities — how intelligence is produced, classified and shared, and at what level it must inform governance decisions.

## How members connect

- Core CTI Principles act as the baseline requirement; Strategic CTI Principles layer board- and risk-governance-level expectations on top of that baseline.
- The Intelligence Lifecycle supplies the process definition against which the Core Principles are assessed.
- The Traffic Light Protocol is the referenced handling/classification convention, constraining onward disclosure of shared intelligence and interacting with confidentiality duties.
