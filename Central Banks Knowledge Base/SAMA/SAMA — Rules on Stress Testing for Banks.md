# Rules on Stress Testing for Banks

SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing for banks, setting out mandatory requirements for stress testing as part of a bank's risk management and ICAAP, including types and frequency of tests, board and senior management responsibilities, framework design, methodologies, scenario selection and periodic reporting to SAMA. The document also includes SAMA's supervisory review approach and an FAQ clarifying that banks choose their own methodologies and risk factors, with SAMA able to impose standard scenarios. Binds banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
