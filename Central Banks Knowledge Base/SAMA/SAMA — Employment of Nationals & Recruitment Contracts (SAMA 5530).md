# Employment of Nationals & Recruitment Contracts (SAMA 5530)

SAMA circular (1434H) on Saudization/employment of nationals and on requirements for contracting with recruitment and outsourcing service providers, addressed to banks operating in the Kingdom and Class (A) money exchange companies. It requires localisation of all job grades including senior leadership, restricts branch, remittance-centre and cash-centre posts to Saudis (with a narrow exception for one non-Saudi customer-service assistant with no system access or account-opening authority), and reserves cash-handling, funds transfer, information security and AML/CTF roles for Saudis only. It also bans dealing with marketers acting in the Kingdom for foreign banks or financial institutions, mandates Saudization plans and timelines, and assigns HR ownership with Compliance oversight and quarterly progress reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
