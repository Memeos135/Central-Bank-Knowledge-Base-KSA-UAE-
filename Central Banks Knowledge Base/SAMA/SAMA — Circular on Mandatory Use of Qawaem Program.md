# Circular on Mandatory Use of Qawaem Program

A SAMA circular (Arabic text) making use of the Ministry of Commerce and Investment "Qawaem" programme mandatory as the source of financial statements for commercial entities required to file there, before any credit facility is extended. It is grounded in SAMA's supervisory powers over the finance sector under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, and is aimed at the reliability and transparency of accounting information used in credit decisions. It binds all finance companies licensed in the Kingdom, with credit information companies also in the distribution scope.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, data/credit info
