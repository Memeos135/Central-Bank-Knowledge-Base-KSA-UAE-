# SRC Purchase of Residential Mortgage Portfolios

SAMA circular amending the seasoning condition for the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC) to purchase residential real estate finance portfolios from real estate financiers, shortening the previously required three-month period to one month from grant of the finance or from receipt of the beneficiary's first instalment, whichever is later, while retaining recourse to the originator for six months from the grant date. It binds banks, finance companies operating in the Kingdom and SRC.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential
