# Rules for Advertising Products and Services (1715)

SAMA's Rules for Advertising Products and Services, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36), governing the content and conduct of advertising by SAMA-licensed entities. They require due diligence in advertisement content and approach, prohibit tempting, misleading, inaccurate or exaggerated claims, mandate Arabic (with a carve-out for non-Arabic-speaker advertisements), and set minimum disclosure items including institution identity, product name, key terms, target customer/consumer, fees and commissions including VAT, and, for finance products, the APR and finance term (with fixed/variable term-cost disclosure for real estate finance). They apply to all financial institutions supervised and regulated by SAMA whenever advertising any product or service.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, payments, other
