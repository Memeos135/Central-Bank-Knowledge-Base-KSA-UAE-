# One-Time-Password Mechanism (OTP)

The one-time-password mechanism required under SAMA's fintech cyber controls, used both as a registration verification step and as transaction authentication. OTP is required for wallet-to-wallet transfers below a defined value, marketplace transactions, first-time bill and government service payments, password resets, wallet reactivations and risk-flagged transactions; for higher-value, IBAN, international and high-risk transfers, the OTP must be delivered through a different channel. Session-timeout controls apply to all issued OTPs and the defined value threshold is communicated and revised separately by SAMA memo.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
