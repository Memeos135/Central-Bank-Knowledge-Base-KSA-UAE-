# Respond Domain

This is the Respond domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, governing action after fraud is suspected or detected in order to limit losses and maximise recovery. It requires a defined, approved and maintained Fraud Response Plan—aligned where appropriate with enterprise incident management—covering alerting and reporting channels for customers, employees and third parties, roles and responsibilities, escalation and decision-making authority, prompt investigation and fair resolution, plus root cause analysis and corrective action. It binds Member Organisations, with compliance and effectiveness to be monitored and periodically evaluated.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
