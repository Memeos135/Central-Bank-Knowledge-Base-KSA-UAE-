# Market Risk General Disclosure

## Why this community

The qualitative narrative disclosure obligation on market risk: how a bank must describe its market risk strategy, governance and management processes rather than report numbers.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster providing the qualitative frame that contextualises the quantitative market risk templates (MR1/MR2).
- Ties the disclosure duty to the bank's governance and risk-management arrangements, so content must be consistent with board-approved risk appetite statements.
- Functions as the interpretive gateway for the market risk measurement approach (standardised vs internal models) disclosed quantitatively elsewhere.
