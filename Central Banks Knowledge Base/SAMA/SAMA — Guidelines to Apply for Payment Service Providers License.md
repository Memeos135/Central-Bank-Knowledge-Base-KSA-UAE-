# Guidelines to Apply for Payment Service Providers License

SAMA's Guidelines to Apply for a Payment Service Providers License set out the regulatory procedures, documentation and conduct expected of applicants seeking authorisation to provide payment services. They describe the two-stage process (in-principle approval, then final licensing) and the four available licence categories: Micro and Major Electronic Money Institution (e-wallet) and Micro and Major Payment Institution. They bind prospective PSP applicants and are read together with the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services.

- **What this link tells you:** An applicant deciding whether its proposed activity requires a payment services licence should read the application guidelines as subordinate to, and interpreted by, the Law of Payments and Payment Services and its Implementing Regulation. The guidelines expressly rest on the Implementing Regulation's licensing articles, while the Law supplies the defined scope of Payment Services (execution and processing of payment transactions, payment instruments, transfers, e-money and payment wallets). Consequently, the statutory definition governs whether a licence is needed at all, and the guidelines only govern how to apply; where guidance appears narrower or broader, the Law and its Regulation control.
