# Regulatory Sandbox Process

## Why this community

SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox admission and testing framework: how an applicant enters controlled live testing of an innovative financial product and the conditions for progressing through and exiting the sandbox.

## How members connect

- The Guidance Notes on the application form is the parent instrument; each of the four stages is a sequenced sub-process it references.
- The stages form a linear obligation chain — admission criteria, operational readiness conditions, testing limits and safeguards during live operation, then graduation or exit.
- Sandbox participation is a time-limited, condition-bound permission, not a substitute for licensing; exit determines whether the firm must seek authorisation under the applicable regime (e.g. payments, finance).
