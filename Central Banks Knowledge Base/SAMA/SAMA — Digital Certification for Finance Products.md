# Digital Certification for Finance Products

A June 2020 SAMA circular permitting finance companies to offer all financing products and credit cards to individuals and SMEs through electronic channels using nationally approved digital certification (e-signature) services, subject to the Electronic Transactions Law (Royal Decree M/18) and its implementing regulations. It sets minimum conditions: level 3 maturity compliance with the Cyber/Information Security Framework verified by an independent party, prior SAMA no-objection for electronic services, use of a provider accredited by the National Digital Certification Center, no dilution of KYC and identity/capacity/authority verification, call-centre confirmation of the customer's consent, cooling periods of 24 hours for individuals and three business days for entities, security and data-privacy controls, record retention, contract updates, a financing cap aligned to risk policy, and periodic control review. It does not apply to products in SAMA's regulatory sandbox.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection
