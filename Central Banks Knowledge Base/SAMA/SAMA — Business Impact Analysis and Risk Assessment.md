# Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment

Node for the Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment requirements in the SAMA BCM Framework. It requires an approved BIA/RA methodology, periodic continuity risk assessment (threats, single points of failure, prioritisation, control selection and treatment plans) and prioritisation of activities with defined RTOs, RPOs and Maximum Acceptable Outage, interdependencies and recovery resources. Results and the prioritised list must be endorsed by the BCM committee; applies to all member organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
