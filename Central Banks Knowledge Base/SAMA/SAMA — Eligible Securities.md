# Eligible Securities

This node concerns what may serve as repo collateral and how it is valued, including listed bond/sukuk issuances and any other securities SAMA specifies, the rule that a security's legal maturity must be at least as long as the repo, and the prohibition on securities issued or guaranteed by the repo seller. The associated context also covers default-related valuation concepts from the master agreement (Net Value, Default Market Value, Deliverable/Receivable Securities, Transaction Costs) used by a non-defaulting party on early termination. Relevant to all repo market participants and their collateral and close-out practices.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
