# Regulatory Real Estate Exposures

Defines "regulatory real estate" and its four sub-categories, including what makes an exposure "regulatory residential" (secured by a lawful dwelling) versus "regulatory commercial" (the residual), and when an exposure is "materially dependent on cash flows generated by the property" (loan servicing and recovery depend on lease, rental or sale proceeds rather than borrower capacity). Notes the material-dependence test typically bites on corporate, MSME and SPV lending. Relevant to banks classifying mortgage and property-secured exposures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
