# Social Media Data Prohibition

## Why this community

Restriction on the channels used to exchange customer or institutional data, prohibiting social media and comparable public platforms for such exchanges.

## How members connect

- Standalone directive; reads as a data confidentiality and banking-secrecy control on communication channels rather than a data-processing regime in itself.
- Interacts with consumer protection and customer data confidentiality obligations elsewhere in the framework.
