# AML and Counter-Terrorist Financing Requirements

AML/CTF and enforcement provisions of the Rules Regulating Money Changing Business, requiring Money Changers to comply with the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations, the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Implementing Regulations, related SAMA circulars and controls, and the Guideline on implementing UN Security Council resolutions on proliferation financing of weapons of mass destruction. It also sets SAMA's supervisory sanctions ladder (notification, warning, corrective action, branch closure, service suspension, license suspension or revocation, fines under Article 12 of SAMA's Charter) with publication of penalties. Binds licensed Money Changers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments
