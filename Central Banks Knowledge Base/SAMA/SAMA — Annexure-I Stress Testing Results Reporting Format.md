# Annexure-I: Stress Testing Results Reporting Format

Refers to the reporting format annexed to SAMA's Stress Testing Rules, under which banks submit stress testing results and top-down data to SAMA on a half-yearly basis. The accompanying Q&A clarifies that the data template under Para 10(i) largely follows the existing semi-annual template, that SAMA may request additional data over time and will communicate revisions in advance, and that stress effects are applied to financial statements as at the reporting cut-off date. Methodology choice remains with the bank, though SAMA may prescribe standard scenarios.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
