# Trade Reporting Requirements (Section A)

Section A of the OTC derivatives requirements: the obligation on all banks in KSA with OTC derivative transactions to report reportable transactions to the SAMA-authorised Trade Repository, directly and electronically, at execution and on subsequent life-cycle events. It covers the manner of reporting, the reporting service agreement and consent clause, template completion, and life-cycle event handling such as new trades pending a counterparty-generated trade ID, modifications, novations and terminations (action types N, M, C), including the T+1 submission deadline.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
