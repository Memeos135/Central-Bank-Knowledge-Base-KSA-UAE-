# Resecuritization Exposure

Defines a resecuritization exposure as a securitization exposure whose underlying tranched pool contains at least one securitization exposure, including exposures to one or more resecuritization exposures, with a carve-out for retranchings whose cash flows can be replicated by exposure to a non-securitization pool. The classification drives the more punitive capital treatment and disclosure of such positions under the securitization framework. It binds banks in Saudi Arabia calculating minimum regulatory capital for credit risk and completing related Pillar 3 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
