# Complaints and Disputes

This node covers Part Eleven (Complaints and Disputes) of SAMA's Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services, read with the Regulation's definition of a Complaint as an expression of dissatisfaction or objection submitted to the licensee by a Customer or Consumer through available channels. It governs complaint handling, response timeframes and escalation/dispute resolution routes. It binds licensed payment service providers and is relevant to supervisory action under the Part on supervision and enforcement.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
