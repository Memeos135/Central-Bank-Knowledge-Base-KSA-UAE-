# Cash Flow Analysis

Node focused on cash flow analysis and the short-term cash budget as the core tools for judging a distressed borrower's capacity to service debt, sitting alongside balance sheet review of total obligations and priority claims. It stresses forecasted cash generation over accrual measures, monthly breakdown of sources and uses to reveal financing needs and timing of funds available for loan service, and ongoing monthly monitoring with immediate corrective action. Applies to bank workout teams assessing viability and restructuring terms.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
