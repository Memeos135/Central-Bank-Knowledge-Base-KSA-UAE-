# SAMA Circulars Portal

A concept node for SAMA's public circulars portal, announced in this circular as a transparency and financial-awareness initiative making SAMA's supervisory and regulatory instructions available to the public and practitioners. The circular stresses the portal's limited legal effect: it is an informational channel and does not substitute for, or discharge compliance with, instructions formally served on institutions by letter or official SAMA email.
