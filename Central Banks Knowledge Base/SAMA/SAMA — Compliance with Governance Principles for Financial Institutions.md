# Compliance with Governance Principles for Financial Institutions

SAMA circular (ref. 472039139, dated 09/07/1447H) extending mandatory application of the Key Principles of Governance for financial institutions supervised by SAMA (originally issued by earlier circular) to all finance companies and payment service providers. It prescribes a compliance pathway: a current-state governance gap assessment, a board/managers-approved roadmap, submission of both to the designated SAMA compliance mailbox within the stated deadline, and full compliance within one year of the circular or at the end of the sitting board's term, whichever is earlier, with SAMA able to extend on justified grounds.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, payments
