# E-Money Distributor Rules

Part of the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services dealing with e-money distributors alongside agents, and connected to the chapters on issuance and redemption of e-money and safeguarding of protected funds. It sets the conditions under which licensees may use distributors to distribute or redeem e-money and the licensee's accountability for them. Binds SAMA-licensed e-money issuers and other payment service providers.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk
