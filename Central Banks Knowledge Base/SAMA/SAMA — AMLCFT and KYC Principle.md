# AML/CFT and KYC Principle

Node anchoring the AML/CTF and KYC layer of the SAMA Bank Account Rules: the Rules must be read together with the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations and the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing and related guidance. It requires Compliance to have timely access to identification, due diligence and transaction records, verification of the relationship and documents for agents, custodians and authorized persons, and risk-based automated (not manual) ongoing transaction monitoring integrated with core systems; it also covers internal audit and annual SAMA reporting on unclaimed and abandoned accounts. Binds all banks.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
