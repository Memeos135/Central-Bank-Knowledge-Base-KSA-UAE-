# Regulatory Retail Exposures

Defines the "regulatory retail" sub-class within the retail exposure class under SAMA's standardised approach to credit risk capital, listing the product, low-value (SAR 4.46 million aggregate per counterparty) and 0.2% granularity criteria, and distinguishing it from "other retail". It drives the applicable risk weights (75% for non-transactor regulatory retail, 45% for transactors, 100% for other retail) and covers both individuals and qualifying MSMEs, with MSMEs failing the criteria pushed to corporate treatment. Binds banks in the Kingdom calculating minimum capital requirements for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
