# Timeframes for Clearance Letter and Account/Debt Transfer Instructions

SAMA instructions fixing the maximum processing times financiers must observe when a natural-person customer requests a clearance letter, an account transfer or a debt transfer: one business day for a clearance letter (seven business days where a credit or charge card is involved), one business day for account transfer and for consumer finance debt-transfer forms, and three/five business days for real estate finance debt transfer steps. They supersede the timeframes set in several earlier SAMA circulars while leaving the substance of those instructions intact, and exclude cases subject to judicial rulings or pending before competent authorities. Binds banks and finance companies supervised by SAMA, effective within thirty business days.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
