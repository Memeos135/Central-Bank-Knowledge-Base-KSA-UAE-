# Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law

The Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law, setting definitions (Lessor, Lessee, Leased Asset, Contracts Register, Contracts Registration Company, Securitization) and the conditions under which finance leasing is treated as a professional activity requiring licensing. It addresses lessor and lessee rights and obligations, licence requirements before concluding contracts, payment schedules separating lease value from the value of the ownership right, recovery on early termination, insurance indemnity, securitisation against lessor rights with continuing disclosure duties to SAMA, and SAMA's power to set contract standards. It binds lessors — joint stock companies licensed to conduct finance leasing, including commercial banks — and is read with the Finance Companies Control Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
