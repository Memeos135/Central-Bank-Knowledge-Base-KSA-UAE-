# Disability Financial Services

## Why this community

Accessibility obligations requiring financial institutions to make products, branches, channels and disclosures usable by persons with disabilities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the duty is best read as an extension of consumer protection principles (fair treatment, clear disclosure, non-discrimination) applied to a defined beneficiary group.
- Interacts with branch/channel operating rules and with account-opening and CDD requirements where assisted or alternative identification/consent arrangements are needed.
