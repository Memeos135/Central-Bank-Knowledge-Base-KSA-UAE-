# Compliance

Chapter VIII of the Real Estate Refinance Rules requires the company to establish a compliance department with a head of compliance reporting to the audit committee, a written compliance policy reviewed annually, and a compliance officer appointed by the board on audit committee recommendation after a SAMA no-objection letter, acting independently with no other administrative duties. It sets a semiannual compliance report to the audit committee and board, a risk-based compliance programme, and resourcing/reporting-line requirements. Notably it also requires the company to verify that real estate financiers have internal policies and procedures to combat financial crime such as money laundering — a counterparty-level AML assurance duty rather than direct customer CDD.
