# Close-Out Netting and Collateral Arrangements Regulation

Circular notifying issuance, by Governor's decision, of the Regulation organising close-out netting and related collateral arrangements, together with an annexed list of qualified financial contracts. It is issued under the SAMA Law and interacts with the Bankruptcy Law and its implementing regulation; the qualified-contract list was determined by SAMA and the CMA in agreement with the Ministry of Commerce. Applies to financial institutions supervised by SAMA, with effect from the date stated in the circular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
