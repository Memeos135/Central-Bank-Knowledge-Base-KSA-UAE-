# Stress Scenario Capital Requirement (SES)

A market-risk capital node within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk, covering the stress-scenario dimension of capital calculation: the mandatory stress testing programme for banks using the internal models approach (desk-level and bank-wide, quantitative and qualitative, with senior management and board reporting), and the three prescribed correlation scenarios (medium, high, low) under the sensitivities-based method, where the capital requirement is the largest of the three. It binds banks supervised by SAMA, with IMA obligations applying only to banks using internal models and the correlation-scenario mechanics applying to all banks calculating under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
