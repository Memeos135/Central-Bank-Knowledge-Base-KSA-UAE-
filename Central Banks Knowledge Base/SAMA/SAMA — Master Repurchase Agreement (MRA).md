# Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)

Node for the standard Master Repurchase Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Securities (2020 version) approved by SAMA and annexed to the repo guidelines. The guidelines require all repo transactions in Saudi Arabia to be governed by this MRA, with any variations recorded in annexures and mutually agreed, and made clear in trade confirmations or equivalent documentation; foreign-currency legs governed by the GMRA are not prohibited subject to applicable KSA rules. Binding on all repo market participants/eligible counterparties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
