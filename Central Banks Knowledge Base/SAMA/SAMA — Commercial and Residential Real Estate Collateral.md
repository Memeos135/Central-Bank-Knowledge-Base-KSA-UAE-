# Commercial and Residential Real Estate Collateral

Sets the definition and operational requirements for recognising commercial and residential real estate as eligible collateral for corporate claims under IRB, including legal enforceability and perfected lien, valuation at or below current fair value, and revaluation at least annually (more often in volatile markets); income-producing real estate falling within the specialised lending asset class is excluded from recognition as collateral. Related disclosure definitions distinguish general versus income-producing residential and commercial real estate and land acquisition, development and construction exposures. Binds SAMA-supervised banks applying credit risk mitigation and the related Pillar 3 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
