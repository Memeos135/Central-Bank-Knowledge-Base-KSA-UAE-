# Political Risk

A concept node within SAMA's operational risk management guidance for banks, treating political and regulatory instability in foreign markets as a component of operational (pure) risk. It expects Saudi banks with cross-border operations or significant international trade exposure to maintain monitoring systems, early-warning indicators and mitigation/contingency plans for political and regulatory risk. Directed at boards and senior management of banks operating in the Kingdom, as supervisory guidance rather than a prescriptive rule.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
