# Template TLAC3 - Creditor Ranking at Legal Entity Level

Disclosure template for the creditor hierarchy of each G-SIB resolution entity at legal entity level, mirroring TLAC2 but omitting the resolution-entity-as-creditor column, so only one column per hierarchy layer. Requires nominal values for total capital and liabilities, excluded liabilities, potentially TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity buckets including perpetual securities, semiannually in fixed format. Binds G-SIBs and informs external creditors of their standing in resolution.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
