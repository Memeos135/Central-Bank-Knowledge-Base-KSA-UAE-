# Cybersecurity Controls Compliance

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations on cybersecurity and data-cybersecurity control frameworks for SAMA-supervised entities, including adoption of and compliance reporting against national control sets.

## How members connect

- Both members are SAMA circulars in the governance/risk regime that impose adoption, self-assessment and compliance-status obligations on regulated entities rather than creating new prudential rules.
- The data-cybersecurity controls circular narrows the general cybersecurity compliance duty to data-specific controls — read as a lex specialis layer over the broader framework.
- Interaction to flag: data-protection and outsourcing/third-party arrangements, where cybersecurity control obligations follow data handled by service providers.
