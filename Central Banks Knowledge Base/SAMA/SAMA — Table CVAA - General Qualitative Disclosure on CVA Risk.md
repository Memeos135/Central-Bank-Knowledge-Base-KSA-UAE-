# Table CVAA - General Qualitative Disclosure on CVA Risk

Node covering SAMA's Pillar 3 section on credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk disclosures, including Table CVAA, the general qualitative disclosure on a bank's CVA risk exposures and how they are managed. The section applies only where the materiality threshold in SAMA's revised risk-based capital charge for counterparty credit risk is met, and it maps the full CVA disclosure set (CVA1, CVA2 under BA-CVA; CVAB, CVA3, CVA4 under SA-CVA).
