# Minimum Verification Controls

The baseline user-verification and authentication controls in SAMA's cyber controls document for fintech business models, requiring multi-factor authentication at each login, OTP verification for defined transaction and account-status events, verification of phone-number ownership through a licensed trusted party, and session-timeout and single-device restrictions. These are security/authentication obligations on member organizations and are distinct from, and do not substitute for, AML/CTF customer due diligence and identification requirements.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other

- **What this link tells you:** In deciding whether identity/verification controls for onboarding (including the additional controls applied to lending activity) are sufficient, read the Minimum Verification Controls together with the Cyber Security Framework rather than in isolation.
