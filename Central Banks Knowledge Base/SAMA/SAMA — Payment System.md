# Payment System

Covers the Law of Payments and Payment Services definitions and core scope: payment system, payment services, payment order, member, client, and SAMA's powers over licensing, capital and solvency, product approval, outsourcing, and designation of systemically important payment systems (SIPS). Establishes that no person may operate a payment system or provide payment services in the Kingdom without a SAMA licence, with aims of infrastructure integrity, user protection, and innovation/competition. Binds payment system operators, payment service providers, and applicants seeking to enter the market.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
