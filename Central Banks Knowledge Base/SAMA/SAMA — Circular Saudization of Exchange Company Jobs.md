# Circular: Saudization of Exchange Company Jobs

SAMA circular issued under Article 15(c) of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business requiring Saudization of all jobs in money exchange establishments and companies (category A/B). Licensees must restrict all activity positions to Saudi nationals, train and qualify Saudi staff, submit a full Saudization plan to SAMA within two weeks, and complete Saudization within one year, with enforcement action against non-compliant exchangers.
