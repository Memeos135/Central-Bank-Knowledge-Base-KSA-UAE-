# Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements Guide

SAMA circular adopting the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements Guide, issued under the SAMA Law and SAMA's supervisory mandate to raise financial-sector maturity against evolving fraud threats. It imposes a staged compliance pathway: a gap self-assessment against the Guide, a board-approved remediation roadmap, submission of both to SAMA by the stated deadline, quarterly compliance status reporting, and full compliance by the stated final date. Addressed to finance companies and payment service providers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance
