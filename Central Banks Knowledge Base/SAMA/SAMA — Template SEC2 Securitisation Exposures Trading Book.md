# Template SEC2: Securitisation Exposures Trading Book

Template SEC2 is the quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of securitisation exposures held in the trading book, again covering all such exposures by the bank's role (originator, sponsor, investor) irrespective of whether the securitisation framework applies for capital purposes. Capital charges for trading book securitisation positions are reported under the market risk disclosures rather than in this template. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
