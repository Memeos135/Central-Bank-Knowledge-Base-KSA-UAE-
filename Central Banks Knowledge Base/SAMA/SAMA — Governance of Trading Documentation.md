# Governance of Trading Documentation

Section B of SAMA's OTC derivatives framework setting risk-mitigation duties for non-centrally cleared OTC derivative contracts: trading relationship documentation must give legal certainty, capture all material rights and obligations (payment, netting, default/termination, transfer, governing law, confirmation/valuation/reconciliation/dispute processes, credit support and margin arrangements), be executed in writing or in non-rewritable, non-erasable electronic form, and be retained for at least ten years after termination. It further requires timely two-way trade confirmation of material terms (including novations and subsequent amendments) and agreement with counterparties on valuation processes. Binds SAMA-licensed banks dealing in non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives with financial and non-financial counterparties.
