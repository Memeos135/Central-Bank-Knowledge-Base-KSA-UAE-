# SME Definition and Dealing

## Why this community

Classification of counterparties as small and medium enterprises versus large enterprises, and the consequences of that classification for the applicability of certain compliance requirements.

## How members connect

- The optional-compliance circular expressly references the SME definition circular, so the definitional instrument is the predicate for the relief.
- Classic definition-then-obligation chain: the defined term fixes the perimeter, and the second circular sets differentiated treatment inside and outside it.
- Practical consequence: misclassification of a counterparty shifts whether a requirement is mandatory or elective, so the definition must be applied before any reliance on the relief.
- Cross-regime relevance where 'SME' as a prudential/conduct category does not align with 'Customer' or 'Consumer' definitions used in AML/CTF and consumer protection rules.
