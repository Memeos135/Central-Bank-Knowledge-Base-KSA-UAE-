# Basel Core Principles of Banking Supervision

This node addresses home-supervisor conditions for foreign bank branch licence applicants and the general application process. Applicants must hold written home-supervisor consent, be licensed and listed in their home country, and satisfy SAMA that home supervision meets the Basel Committee Core Principles for Effective Banking Supervision, including consolidated supervision and willingness to cooperate with SAMA. It also frames pre-application consultation and the general licensing overview, including that foreign branches are generally subject to the same requirements as locally incorporated banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
