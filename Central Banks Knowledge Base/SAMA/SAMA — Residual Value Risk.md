# Residual Value Risk

Addresses residual value risk — the exposure to the future value of leased or collateralised property at contract end — and the associated conservative valuation expectations, including independent appraisal excluding expected price increases and recognition of guarantees, financial collateral and mortgage insurance as mitigants. Also intersects with default and dilution risk treatment of purchased receivables. Binds banks calculating credit risk RWA and applying credit risk mitigation under SAMA rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
