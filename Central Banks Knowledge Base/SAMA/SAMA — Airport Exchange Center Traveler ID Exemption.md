# Airport Exchange Center Traveler ID Exemption

SAMA circular to exchange centres (categories A and B) supplementing the February 2010 manual on exchange business procedures regarding customer acceptance and identity verification. Based on approval from the Minister of Interior, customers of airport branches who are travelling outside the Kingdom only are exempted from presenting the national ID for executing transactions; a passport and boarding pass, matched against the customer's name, suffice. Effective from its date.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
