# MITRE ATT&CK

Glossary/concept node for MITRE ATT&CK as used in SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles: an open-source framework taxonomising the tactics, techniques and procedures used by threat actors. It is cited as an example of the structured attack taxonomy Member Organizations are expected to use when defining attack phases and identifying relevant TTPs under the operational CTI domain. Relevant to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations; the framework itself is a third-party standard, not a SAMA rule.
