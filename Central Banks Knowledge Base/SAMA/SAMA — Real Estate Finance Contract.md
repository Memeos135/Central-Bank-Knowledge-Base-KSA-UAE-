# Real Estate Finance Contract

Defines the Real Estate Finance Contract as a deferred payment contract extended to a borrower to own a dwelling, and anchors Chapter Two's contracting obligations: compliance with the Law, SAMA rules and any SAMA standard contracts, prior no-objection for products, and a written or electronic contract containing minimum disclosures (parties and ID details, term, finance amount, term cost and its reference rate, annual percentage rate, total amount payable). It binds real estate finance entities dealing with individual borrowers, read together with the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
