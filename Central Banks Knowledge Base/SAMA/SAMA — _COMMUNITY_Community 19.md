# Problem Loan Management

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for identifying, classifying, restructuring and resolving problem and non-performing loans, including governance of a dedicated workout function and fair treatment of distressed borrowers.

## How members connect

- The Rules on Management of Problem Loans sit at the head of the cluster and pull in the operational concepts (early warning signals, watch list, workout unit, NPL strategy, collateral management) as mandated components.
- An obligation chain runs from detection (early warning signals, watch list) to triage (portfolio segmentation, initial viability assessment) to remediation (workout plan, restructuring) to post-restructuring monitoring.
- Viability testing is anchored in defined quantitative metrics (LTV, loan life coverage, Debt/EBITDA) and qualitative analyses (business, cash flow, SWOT), which determine whether restructuring or exit is required.
- Cross-regime link to the Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning Rules ties workout outcomes to classification and provisioning consequences; the Code of Conduct adds a consumer-protection/fair-treatment overlay on collection and restructuring conduct.
