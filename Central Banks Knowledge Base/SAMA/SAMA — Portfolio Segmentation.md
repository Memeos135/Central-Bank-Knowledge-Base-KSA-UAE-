# Portfolio Segmentation

A concept node explaining NPL portfolio segmentation under SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans: dividing non-performing loans into homogeneous groups to drive differentiated resolution strategies (hold/restructure, dispose, legal enforcement). It sets timing (immediately on transfer to the Workout Unit, before assignment to an officer), the principles of proportionality and materiality for resource allocation, and stage-one criteria such as nature of business (MSME vs corporate), legal status, arrears buckets/days past due and loan balance. Applies to banks' Workout Units handling NPL stock.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
