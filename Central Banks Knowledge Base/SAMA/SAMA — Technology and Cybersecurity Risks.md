# Technology and Cybersecurity Risks

Covers the technology, cybersecurity and independent assessment expectations for digital-only bank applicants. Applicants must address information systems security, resilience and availability proportionate to proposed products, comply with SAMA's Cyber Security Framework and BCM Framework, and have regard to other national requirements such as National Cybersecurity Authority rules; SAMA may require a qualified third-party assessor, at the applicant's cost, to assess technology/cybersecurity and AML/CFT arrangements at design and implementation phases with remediation to SAMA's satisfaction. It binds digital-only bank licence applicants.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF

- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a proposed Digital-Only Bank, its technology and cybersecurity framework must be measured against the existing sector-wide framework rather than a bespoke standard. The Digital-Only Bank technology and cybersecurity expectations direct applicants to design and implement in line with the Cyber Security Framework (alongside national cybersecurity authority requirements), including performing a risk assessment at project inception so that cyber risks are either covered by existing CSF-based controls or newly built. The practical consequence is that a digital-only applicant carries the full CSF control expectations from project design onward, and evidence of early-stage risk assessment is a reviewable licensing input.
