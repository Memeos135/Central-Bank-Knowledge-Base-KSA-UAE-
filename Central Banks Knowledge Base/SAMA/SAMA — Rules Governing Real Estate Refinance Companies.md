# Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA's Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies, setting out the defined terms (Real Estate Refinance Company, Real Estate Financier, primary/secondary market, borrower, contractual rights) and the general framework for licensed joint stock companies conducting real estate refinance activity. It fixes the permitted business model — buying/holding real estate finance contractual rights, issuing securities, providing liquidity to financiers — and the prohibitions, notably no direct finance to borrowers and no dealings outside the sector without a SAMA no-objection letter. It binds companies licensed by SAMA for real estate refinance, alongside the Real Estate Finance Law and Finance Companies Control Law and their implementing regulations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
