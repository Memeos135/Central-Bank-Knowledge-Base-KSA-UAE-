# Ministerial Decision No. 112203 - Saudization of Remote Customer Service

A Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development decision restricting remote customer-service occupations and jobs to Saudi nationals, whether engaged through direct employment or indirect arrangements, including outsourcing contracts covering remote work forming the establishment's core activity. It applies to establishments serving customers remotely via telephone, email, chat, social media or other direct-interaction channels, read with the earlier ministerial decisions it references, and penalties follow the violations and penalties schedule. It binds employers in the Kingdom, including financial institutions and their outsourced customer-service providers.
