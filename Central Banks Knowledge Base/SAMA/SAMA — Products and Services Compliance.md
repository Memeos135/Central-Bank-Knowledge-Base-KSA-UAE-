# Products and Services Compliance

A sub-node of SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation (v2.0, Nov 2023) setting out the second- and third-line duties around new products: compliance function review of every new product/service from a compliance, regulatory and financial-crime angle, annual non-compliance risk assessment and Board reporting; internal audit of new products within about a year of launch with annual Audit Committee reporting; risk management duties on identifying, assessing and capital-impact-testing new product risks; product development (business unit) competence duties; and ongoing post-launch monitoring, target-market suitability and corrective action. It binds locally licensed banks and allocates accountability across compliance, internal audit, risk management and business units.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF
