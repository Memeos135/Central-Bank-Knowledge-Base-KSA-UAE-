# Cash Transport Contracting Rules

## Why this community

Restrictions on how supervised entities may outsource cash transport and physical security functions, in particular the prohibition on engaging non-Saudi security company agents.

## How members connect

- Both instruments constrain the same contracting decision: which cash transport/security providers a supervised entity may lawfully appoint.
- The general contracting circular sets the baseline outsourcing and due diligence expectations; the ban narrows the eligible counterparty pool by nationality/licensing status.
- Together they operate as an eligibility filter — compliance requires satisfying both the contracting conditions and the provider-identity prohibition.
