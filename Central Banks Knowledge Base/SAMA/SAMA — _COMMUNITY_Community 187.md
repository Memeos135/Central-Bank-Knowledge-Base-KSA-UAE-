# Close-Out Netting Regulation

## Why this community

Legal enforceability of close-out netting and collateral (financial security) arrangements in KSA, including their treatment on counterparty default or insolvency.

## How members connect

- Standalone regulation establishing the recognition and enforceability of netting and collateral arrangements as a distinct legal regime.
- Interacts with prudential/risk governance rules (exposure measurement on a net basis) and with insolvency law carve-outs.
- Scope turns on the defined terms for qualifying parties, eligible agreements and collateral — these should be read before applying the netting outcome.
