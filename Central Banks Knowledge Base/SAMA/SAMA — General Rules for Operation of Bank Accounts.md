# General Rules for Operation of Bank Accounts

Node within SAMA's Rules Governing the Opening of Bank Accounts and General Operational Guidelines, covering how banks must operate accounts after opening — in particular the account-status lifecycle (dormant after 24 months without a customer-initiated debit or documented contact, then unclaimed and abandoned), the dual-control and in-person/verified-identity conditions for reactivation and withdrawal, customer notification duties, and balance treatment (recorded as a liability, debts deducted before transfer to suspense, optional closure of balances of SAR 1,000 or less with prior notice). It binds all banks licensed in Saudi Arabia in respect of all natural- and juristic-person account holders.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF, banking prudential
