# Risk Rating System Operations

Node on the operation of rating systems for credit risk capital purposes; the supplied context concentrates on external rating usage under the standardised approach — consistent use of SAMA-recognised ECAIs for both risk weighting and risk management, prohibition on cherry-picking between ECAIs, use of global rating scales, and the multiple-rating and issue-specific versus issuer rating rules (including short-term rating treatment). Binds SAMA-licensed banks applying external ratings to determine risk weights.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
