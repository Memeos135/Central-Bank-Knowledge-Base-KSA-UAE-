# Shariah Governance Framework

Reference to SAMA's Shariah Governance Framework (circular no. 41042498 dated 18/06/1441H, as amended), used in the Related Parties Rules to identify who counts as a Shariah Committee Member. That status pulls the member, their relatives, and entities/companies they own, direct or hold 5%+ in, into the "Related Party" definition and the associated exposure limits and reporting duties. Binds banks applying the related-party regime, particularly those with Shariah governance structures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
