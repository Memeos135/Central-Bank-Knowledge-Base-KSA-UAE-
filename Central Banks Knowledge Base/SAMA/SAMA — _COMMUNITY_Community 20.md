# Stress Testing & ICAAP

## Why this community

SAMA's stress testing regime for banks: the mandatory programme covering scope, methodologies, risk-type coverage, and the link between stress results and capital adequacy assessment under ICAAP.

## How members connect

- The Rules on Stress Testing act as the parent instrument and expressly reference each technique (sensitivity, scenario, reverse) and each risk-type exercise (credit, market, liquidity, operational, top-down/macro) as required components of a bank's programme.
- Obligation chain runs outward to ICAAP: stress outcomes must feed the bank's internal capital adequacy assessment, so the same scenarios carry capital-planning consequences, not just risk-measurement ones.
- International standards are incorporated by citation to the BCBS sound stress testing principles, which informs how SAMA expectations should be interpreted where the Rules are principles-based.
- Annexure-I fixes the reporting format, converting the substantive testing obligations into a defined supervisory submission; Pillar 3 Table OVA sits alongside as the disclosure-side narrative on risk management approach.
