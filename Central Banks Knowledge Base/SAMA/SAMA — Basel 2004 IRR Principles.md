# Basel 2004 IRR Principles

Concept node for the Basel Committee's 2004 Principles for the management and supervision of interest rate risk, the predecessor framework that the SAMA-adopted IRRBB standard revises. It set supervisory expectations for banks' identification, measurement, monitoring and control of interest rate risk and guidance for supervisors. Relevant as baseline context when reading the enhanced IRRBB obligations applicable to domestic banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
