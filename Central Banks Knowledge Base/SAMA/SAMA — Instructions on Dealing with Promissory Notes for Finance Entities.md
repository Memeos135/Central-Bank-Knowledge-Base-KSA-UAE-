# Instructions on Dealing with Promissory Notes for Finance Entities

SAMA instructions (Ramadan 1443H / April 2022, effective 05/09/2022) on how finance entities may deal with and enforce promissory notes taken as security for financing. It sets minimum requirements including a board-approved promissory note policy, steps and authority levels before initiating enforcement, documentation required at execution (financing contract, due note, proof of default and of documented communication with the customer), and responsibilities where enforcement is outsourced to a third party. Binds banks and finance companies supervised by SAMA in their dealings with customers (natural or legal persons) holding a financing product; it does not displace related SAMA instructions on collection, outsourcing, blank promissory notes and standard financing contracts.
