# Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) Framework

Concept node for the Credit Valuation Adjustment capital framework: CVA is the counterparty-level adjustment to default risk-free prices of derivatives and securities financing transactions for potential counterparty default. It distinguishes regulatory CVA from accounting CVA, sets the conversion of CVA capital requirements into risk-weighted assets, and frames the SA-CVA/BA-CVA approaches and any alternative treatment. It binds banks with in-scope derivative and SFT exposures under SAMA's minimum capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
