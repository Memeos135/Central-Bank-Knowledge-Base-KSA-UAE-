# Template CMS2 - Modelled vs Standardised RWA Credit Risk Asset Class

Pillar 3 template CMS2 requiring the modelled-versus-standardised RWA comparison to be broken down by credit risk asset class, using the same definitions of standardised and IRB/supervisory slotting approaches as the risk-level comparison. It supports market assessment of the effect of internal model use on credit risk capital. Applies to banks with SAMA approval to use IRB approaches for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
