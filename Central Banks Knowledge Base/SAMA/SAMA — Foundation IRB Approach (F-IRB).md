# Foundation IRB Approach (F-IRB)

This node describes the foundation internal ratings-based approach, under which banks supply their own probability of default estimates and use supervisory values for the remaining risk components, always applying the prescribed risk-weight functions. It also sets out the three elements of the IRB framework (risk components, risk-weight functions, minimum requirements), the exclusions from A-IRB and from IRB generally (notably equities), and the conditions for phased rollout across asset classes and business units. It binds banks seeking or operating IRB permission, with the rollout plan requiring SAMA agreement.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
