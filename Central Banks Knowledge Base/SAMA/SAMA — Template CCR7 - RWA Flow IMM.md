# Template CCR7 - RWA Flow IMM

A Pillar 3 template presenting the flow statement of risk-weighted assets for counterparty credit risk exposures calculated under the internal model method (IMM), explaining movements over the reporting period. It forms part of the counterparty credit risk disclosure set, which is separated from general credit risk, credit valuation adjustment and securitisation RWA reporting. Applies to banks permitted by SAMA to use internal model approaches for counterparty credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
