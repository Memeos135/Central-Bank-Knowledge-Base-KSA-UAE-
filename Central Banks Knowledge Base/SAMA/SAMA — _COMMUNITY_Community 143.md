# Interest Rate Risk Standards

## Why this community

Prudential treatment of interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), specifically the prescribed shock scenarios banks must apply when measuring and reporting rate sensitivity.

## How members connect

- The standardised shock scenarios are drawn from and cite the Basel Committee IRRBB framework, so the local requirement operates as adoption of an international standard.
- Linkage is hierarchical in substance: international standard-setter guidance -> SAMA-applied prudential measurement requirement.
- Interpretation of scenario calibration and scope should follow the Basel source unless SAMA has expressly varied it.
