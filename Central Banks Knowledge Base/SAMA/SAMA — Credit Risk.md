# Credit Risk

Section 7 (Principle 2.0) of SAMA's Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, setting requirements for identifying, measuring, managing and reporting credit risk arising from Shari'ah compliant financing. It requires defined credit approval/renewal/refinancing criteria aligned to risk appetite and SAMA's responsible lending requirements, plus monitoring of counterparty repayment capacity, exposure classification, documentation completeness, collateral and contract-stage-specific risks. It binds domestic licensed banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking, whether full-fledged Islamic banks or Islamic Windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
