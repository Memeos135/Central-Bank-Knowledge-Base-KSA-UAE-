# Expected Loss (EL)

Concept node on expected loss (EL) under the IRB approach: how banks calculate EL amounts, compare them with eligible provisions, and treat any shortfall or excess in regulatory capital. It also covers the treatment of defaulted assets, where the bank must form a best estimate of expected loss and hold capital for the excess of LGD over that estimate, with supervisory scrutiny where the estimate falls below specific provisions and partial charge-offs. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks using IRB for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
