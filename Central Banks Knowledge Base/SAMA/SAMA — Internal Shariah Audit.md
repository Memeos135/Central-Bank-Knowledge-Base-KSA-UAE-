# Internal Shariah Audit

Concept node for the internal Shariah audit function: an independent, periodic assessment providing objective assurance on the degree of Shariah compliance of the bank's Islamic operations, scoped by risk and materiality and performed by internal auditors with adequate Shariah knowledge (who may use the bank's Shariah officer without compromising objectivity). Deliverables are set by the board audit committee after consulting the Shariah committee, and findings go to both. It sits within the framework's internal control chapter alongside Shariah compliance and Shariah non-compliance risk management, and extends to at least annual audit of Islamic window operations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
