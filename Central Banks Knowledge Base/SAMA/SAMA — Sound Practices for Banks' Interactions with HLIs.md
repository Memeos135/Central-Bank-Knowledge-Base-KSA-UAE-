# Sound Practices for Banks' Interactions with HLIs

The parent instrument: the Basel Committee's "Sound Practices for Banks' Interactions with Highly Leveraged Institutions", circulated by SAMA to Saudi banks as its supervisory expectation. It sets out prudent approaches to assessing, measuring and managing credit exposures to HLIs — opacity, leverage and rapidly shifting trading positions — covering credit strategy, information gathering and due diligence, exposure measurement, limit setting and collateral/documentation practices. It applies to banks with HLI or hedge fund counterparty relationships and complements general credit risk management principles.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
