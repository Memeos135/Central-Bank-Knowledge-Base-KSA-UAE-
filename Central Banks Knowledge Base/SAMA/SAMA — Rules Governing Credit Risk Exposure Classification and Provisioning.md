# Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning

SAMA's Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law (Art. 13) and its Implementing Regulation (Art. 62), setting minimum requirements for classifying credit exposures and setting provisions. It aligns regulatory classification with IFRS 9 expected credit loss concepts (PD, EAD, LGD, stages) and defines the supporting terminology. It binds licensed finance companies, which must maintain a credit risk management framework proportionate to their size and complexity as a precondition to compliance.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** For a decision on how a finance company must classify credit exposures and set provisions, read the Rules as the detailed elaboration of provisioning duties that originate in the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. The Law establishes the requirement to provision against losses in line with criteria set at regulation level, and the Rules supply those criteria within the broader credit risk management framework. Consequently, a provisioning judgment that satisfies the Rules is also discharging a statutory obligation, and any gap or ambiguity in the Rules should be resolved by reference to the parent Law rather than treated as unregulated.
