# Inferred Ratings

This node covers the concept of inferred ratings within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, specifically the securitization external-ratings-based approach (SEC-ERBA). It sets when a securitization exposure lacking its own external rating may nonetheless be risk-weighted off a rating inferred from another position, and it links to the operational requirements that must be satisfied before such a rating can be used. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk RWAs for securitization exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
