# CCTV Surveillance Objectives

This node is the technical core of SAMA's CCTV Specifications: the surveillance objectives framework (identification, recognition, detection) with required pixel density, face width and camera resolution, mapped to specific branch and facility areas such as entrances, cash counters, reception and waiting areas, locker/safe rooms, IT rooms and data centres, ATMs and CCDMs. It also fixes recording retention: a 90-day minimum for all bank facilities, one year for footage relating to a customer claim or complaint from receipt, and five years where a claim or complaint is rejected. These retention rules are the provisions most often relied on in customer dispute and complaint-handling matters.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
