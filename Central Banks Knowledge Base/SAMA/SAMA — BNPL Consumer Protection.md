# BNPL Consumer Protection

This node sets out the conduct and consumer-facing obligations of licensed BNPL companies, principally the Article 19 duties: compliance with SAMA's Responsible Lending Principles for Retail Consumers, consent-based verification and documentation of the consumer's credit record, registration and updating of credit information with licensed credit bureaus, documented creditworthiness and repayment-capacity methodology, clear pop-up disclosures and acknowledgements, identification and independent verification of the consumer's identity, and a prohibition on contracted stores passing additional fees to the consumer. It binds SAMA-licensed BNPL companies and reaches their merchant arrangements through monitoring duties. Note the cross-regime interaction: the identification/verification duty sits alongside AML/CTF customer due diligence, and the BNPL "Consumer" is not coextensive with the AML "Customer"; Saudization and non-Saudi appointment controls for control functions also appear here.
