# Anti-Money Laundering Law

Concept node for the Saudi Anti-Money Laundering Law, the statutory foundation cross-referenced by multiple SAMA instructions (TFS rules, AML/CTF guidance and related circulars). It establishes the ML offence, the obligations of financial institutions on customer due diligence, record keeping, suspicious transaction reporting and internal AML programmes, and the supervisory/penalty framework. It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs, with SAMA acting as supervisor for the entities under its remit.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS
