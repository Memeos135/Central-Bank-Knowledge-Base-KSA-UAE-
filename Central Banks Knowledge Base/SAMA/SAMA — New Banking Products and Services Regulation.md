# New Banking Products and Services Regulation

SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation, updating the 2017 guidelines, sets minimum requirements for how banks develop, approve, launch and monitor new or materially modified products and services, and when they must notify SAMA or obtain its non-objection before launch. It defines "product or service", "new product or service" and "existing product or service" by reference to banking business under the Banking Control Law. It binds all banks licensed in Saudi Arabia under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
