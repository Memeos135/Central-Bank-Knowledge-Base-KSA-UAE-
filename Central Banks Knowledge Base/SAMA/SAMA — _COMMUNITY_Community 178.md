# LCR and NSFR Regulations

## Why this community

Prudential liquidity regulation for banks: the Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Net Stable Funding Ratio, including the amended calibrations and the associated supervisory reporting returns.

## How members connect

- Direct cross-reference: the circular on amended prudential returns and NSFR operates off the revised LCR regulations, so the reporting obligation is derivative of the substantive ratio requirement.
- Amendment hierarchy matters — the later circular revises earlier LCR text, so effective dates and superseded provisions determine which calibration binds.
- Both sit in the same Basel-derived liquidity framework, sharing defined terms (high-quality liquid assets, net outflows, stable funding) that should be read consistently across the two.
