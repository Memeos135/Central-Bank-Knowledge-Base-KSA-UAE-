# Asset Management

A control domain within SAMA's Cyber Security Framework (section 3.3.3) requiring Member Organizations to define, approve, implement, communicate and monitor an asset management process supported by an accurate, unified information asset register. It covers asset ownership and custodianship, classification, labelling and handling, discovery of new assets, and links to dependent processes such as finance, procurement and IT. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA and sits alongside the framework's physical security and cyber security architecture controls.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
