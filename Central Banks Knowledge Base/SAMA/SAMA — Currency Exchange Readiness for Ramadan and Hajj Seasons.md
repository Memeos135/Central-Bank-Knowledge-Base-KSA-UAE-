# Currency Exchange Readiness for Ramadan and Hajj Seasons

SAMA circular issued under Article 14(b) of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, requiring exchange centres in Makkah, Madinah, King Abdulaziz International Airport and the Hajj city in Jeddah to raise their operational readiness for the Ramadan and Hajj seasons. Annex 1 sets minimum service standards, including sufficient cash and currency availability, extended working days and hours over the seasons and Eid holidays, crowd and queue management, staff identification and shift rosters, prominent and system-linked exchange-rate display, customer-rights signage, card/electronic payment acceptance, functioning CCTV, adequate security staffing, counterfeit-detection equipment and handling, mapped and bilingually named premises, and notification to SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other
