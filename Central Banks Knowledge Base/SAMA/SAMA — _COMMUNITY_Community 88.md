# Exchange Business Rules

## Why this community

Licensing and ongoing supervisory obligations of exchange centers (money changers) under SAMA's exchange business regime, covering permitted activities, supervisory reporting and independent control testing.

## How members connect

- All three circulars sit hierarchically beneath the Rules Governing Exchange Business and operate as amendments or clarifications to obligations already established there.
- Reporting obligations chain through the Returns Management System: the supervisory data templates circular fixes the form, frequency and channel by which exchange centers discharge their periodic reporting duty.
- The independent periodic testing circular imposes an assurance-layer obligation (third-party/independent review of controls), which in practice supports the reliability of the data submitted via RMS.
- The POS service circular is a scope-of-activity instrument: it determines whether an exchange center may offer a payment-adjacent service, engaging payments-regime boundaries alongside the exchange licence.
