# SEC-SA Standardized Approach

Concept node for the Securitisation Standardised Approach, the formula-based fallback in the securitisation hierarchy using the capital charge on the underlying pool, delinquency inputs and tranche attachment/detachment points. It applies where a bank cannot use SEC-IRBA or SEC-ERBA, and its outputs are reported as standardised-approach RWA in Pillar 3 templates for banking book securitisation exposures. It binds all SAMA-licensed banks with securitisation positions not eligible for the higher approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
