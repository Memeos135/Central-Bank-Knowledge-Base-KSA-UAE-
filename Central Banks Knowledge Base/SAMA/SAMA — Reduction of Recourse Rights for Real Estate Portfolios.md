# Reduction of Recourse Rights for Real Estate Portfolios

A SAMA circular that eases two conditions on residential real estate finance portfolios sold to the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company: it permits purchase after a minimum holding period measured from either the financing grant date or receipt of the borrower's first instalment (whichever is earlier) and shortens the recourse period against the originating financier, and it exempts such sales from the prior no-objection requirement under the rules on disposal of finance assets or rights arising from them. The exemption is conditional: financiers remain bound by all statutory periods applicable to the transferred contracts and must notify SAMA of the transaction details within five business days of signing the final sale contract. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom and SRC.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
