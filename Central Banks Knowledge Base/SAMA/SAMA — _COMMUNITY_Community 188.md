# Financial Statement Notification

## Why this community

The requirement that supervised entities notify SAMA in advance of publishing or issuing periodic financial statements, supporting supervisory oversight of disclosure timing.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a notification/pre-clearance obligation running from the entity to SAMA.
- Sits upstream of accounting, audit and public-disclosure requirements — the notice duty is procedural and additional to the substantive reporting rules.
- Relevant to listed entities where SAMA and capital-market disclosure timetables must be reconciled.
