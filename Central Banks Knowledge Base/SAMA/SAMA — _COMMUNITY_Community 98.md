# CCTV Specifications

## Why this community

Minimum physical security and video surveillance standards for financial sector premises, covering what must be monitored and the technical baseline for cameras and recording infrastructure.

## How members connect

- A parent specification document with three dependent components: stated surveillance objectives, camera requirements and recording-server requirements, all cross-referencing the parent standard.
- Hierarchy is standard-to-specification: the objectives define the control purpose, while the camera and NVR items set the compliance-testable parameters.
- Camera and NVR items are operationally coupled (capture, retention and retrieval of footage), which matters for evidentiary and retention obligations in fraud, AML and incident investigations.
