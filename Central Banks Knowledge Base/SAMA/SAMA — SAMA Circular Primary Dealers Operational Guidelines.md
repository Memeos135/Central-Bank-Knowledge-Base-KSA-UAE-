# SAMA Circular: Primary Dealers Operational Guidelines

Operational guidelines (March 2019) for Bank Primary Dealers (Bank-PDs) in the Saudi government securities market, setting eligibility conditions (capital adequacy under ICAAP, trading and risk-management capability, infrastructure, SAMA no-objection) and conduct obligations. It requires a Primary Dealers' Committee and code of conduct, a board-approved investment policy covering PD dealing, underwriting and market-making, application of held-for-trading classification and valuation rules, inclusion of PD holdings in SAMA liquidity ratios, separate books with audit trails, and reporting to SAMA of breaches of the Debt Management Office undertaking agreement.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
