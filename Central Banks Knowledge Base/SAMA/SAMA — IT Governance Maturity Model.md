# IT Governance Maturity Model

The IT Governance Maturity Model is the six-level scale (0 Non-existent through 5) that SAMA uses to grade Member Organizations' IT control maturity under the Information Technology Governance Framework. It underpins the periodic self-assessment questionnaire that Member Organizations complete and that SAMA reviews and audits to determine compliance and maturity level, with higher levels requiring all criteria of the preceding levels. It binds all SAMA-regulated Member Organizations and is the reference point for waiver requests, compensating controls and internal risk acceptance where a control cannot be met.
