# Template OR3 - Minimum Required Operational Risk Capital

Template OR3 is the Pillar 3 disclosure of the minimum required operational risk capital and associated RWA, drawing together the business indicator component and, where applicable, the internal loss multiplier. It sits within the operational risk disclosure set (ORA, OR1, OR2) of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework and feeds the operational risk line of the overall RWA overview. It binds banks subject to that disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
