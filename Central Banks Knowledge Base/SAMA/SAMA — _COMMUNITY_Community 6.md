# AML/CTF Due Diligence

## Why this community

The core AML/CTF customer due diligence regime: risk assessment, identification and verification of customers and beneficial owners, calibrated CDD intensity, and the record-keeping and governance obligations that support them.

## How members connect

- All members hang off the risk-based approach: ML/TF risk assessment determines whether standard, enhanced or simplified due diligence applies, so the three CDD tiers are variants of one obligation rather than separate regimes.
- Defined terms propagate — Beneficial Owner and PEP status are inputs that trigger EDD; correspondent relationships and wire transfers are named higher-risk contexts with their own additional measures.
- Accountability chain: the Board carries responsibility for the AML/CTF programme and the risk assessment, while reliance on a third party shifts execution but not ultimate responsibility for CDD.
- Hierarchy runs from the statutory base (terrorism crimes and financing law) and FATF standards down to the SAMA guide, which operationalises them; record keeping is the evidentiary backbone making all other duties supervisable.
