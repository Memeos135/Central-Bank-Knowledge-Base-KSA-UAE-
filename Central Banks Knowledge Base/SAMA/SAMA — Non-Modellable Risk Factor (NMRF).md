# Non-Modellable Risk Factor (NMRF)

Concept node for non-modellable risk factors: risk factors that fail the RFET or whose data SAMA deems unsuitable, which are excluded from the expected shortfall model and instead attract a stressed expected shortfall capital requirement. Includes the criteria for what counts as a real price, third-party vendor price conditions, and SAMA's narrow discretion during systemic market stress. Binds banks using the IMA for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
