# Template CVA2 - Full Basic Approach for CVA

Pillar 3 template CVA2 covering disclosure of RWA components under the full basic approach for CVA risk, i.e. the variant of BA-CVA that recognises eligible CVA hedges. It sits alongside CVA1 in SAMA's CVA disclosure set and binds banks measuring part or all of their CVA RWA under the full BA-CVA, subject to the counterparty credit risk materiality threshold.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
