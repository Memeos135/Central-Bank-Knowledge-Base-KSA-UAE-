# Individual Real Estate Finance

## Why this community

Mandatory contractual and disclosure standards for real estate finance and financial leasing extended to individual (consumer) borrowers, including variable-rate risk disclosure, early repayment, insurance, asset destruction and default/termination outcomes.

## How members connect

- The Mandatory Instructions set the binding minimum terms; the Variable-Rate Credit Risk Acknowledgment Form is the prescribed disclosure artefact evidencing the borrower's informed consent to rate risk.
- The Financial Lease Contract for Individuals is the operative template and incorporates by reference the clause sets on early partial repayment and early ownership, late payment/default and termination, insurance, and total or partial destruction of the leased asset.
- Early repayment entitlements in the instructions are quantified through the Guide for Calculating Early Repayment Amount, linking a consumer right to a prescribed computation method.
- Appendices (Acceptance Certificate, Ownership Transfer Certificate) and the contract registry powers decision govern the execution and registration steps that close out the lease lifecycle, including transfer following termination or full payment.
