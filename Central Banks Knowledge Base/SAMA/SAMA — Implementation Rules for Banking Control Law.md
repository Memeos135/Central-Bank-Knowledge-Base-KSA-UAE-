# Implementation Rules for Banking Control Law

SAMA circular transmitting the Implementation Rules for the Banking Control Law (Ministerial Resolution 3/2149), covering Article 12 (appointments to bank boards and senior positions, including SAMA prior approval and disclosure duties), Article 16 (conduct of banking business consistent with monetary and credit policy), Article 17 (periodic supervisory and statistical returns), Article 18 (SAMA inspections and staff conduct), and Article 22 (supervisory procedures and penalties). It binds all banks operating in the Kingdom and their directors, managers and employees.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
