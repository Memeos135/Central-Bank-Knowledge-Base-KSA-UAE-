# Capital Requirements for PSPs

The licensing-requirements chapter of the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services, covering capital and financial-soundness conditions for payment service providers and related licensing criteria (including controller and holding-company definitions relevant to ownership approval). Determines entry and ongoing prudential thresholds for licensees. Binds applicants for and holders of a SAMA payment licence.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential
