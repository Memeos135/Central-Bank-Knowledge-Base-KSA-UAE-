# Real Estate Finance Law

Node for the Real Estate Finance Law (Royal Decree M/50, 1433H) and its implementing regulation, the statutory basis for licensing and supervising real estate finance and refinance in the Kingdom. It underpins SAMA's rules on real estate refinance companies and is one of the instruments to be read together with the Responsible Lending Principles. It binds banks and finance companies engaged in real estate finance and the beneficiaries (natural persons) who obtain it.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
