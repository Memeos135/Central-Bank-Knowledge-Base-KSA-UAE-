# Credit Risk Governance

Concept node on the credit risk governance expectations embedded in the SAMA provisioning Rules: finance companies must operate an effective credit risk management framework of policies, procedures and controls to identify, measure, monitor, control and report credit risk, proportionate to the nature, size and complexity of exposures. Compliance with the classification and provisioning requirements is treated as unattainable without that framework, and the Rules require self-assessment of the existing framework. Obligations sit with the finance company and its board.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
