# Rules Regulating Money Changing Business

Core definitions, purpose and scope of SAMA's Rules Regulating Money Changing Business, establishing the licensing and supervisory framework for Money Changers and defining key terms (Money Changing Business, Money Changer, Customer, Invested Capital, Third Party). It prohibits unlicensed practice of, or holding out as conducting, money changing business, with a narrow carve-out allowing licensed hotels, hotel apartments and travel agencies to exchange currency for their own customers subject to AML Law compliance and onward sale to a licensed bank or Money Changer. Binds Money Changers licensed by SAMA and, through the prohibition, any person operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk
