# Template REM1 - Remuneration Awarded During Financial Year

Template REM1 is the quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of fixed and variable remuneration awarded during the financial year to senior management and other material risk-takers, under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. It sits alongside Table REMA (policy) and Templates REM2 (special payments) and REM3 (deferred remuneration), and is an annual requirement for banks. The surrounding context also covers transitional timing rules for first disclosure periods by fiscal-year calendar.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
