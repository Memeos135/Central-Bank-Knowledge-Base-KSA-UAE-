# Internal Audit Review of ECL Adequacy

Sets the governance framework finance companies must put in place over ECL estimation and reporting, placing ultimate responsibility on the board of directors for maintaining adequate provisions and appropriate credit risk practices. It requires documented policies on key definitions (default, SICR), roles, methodologies, data and assumptions, collateral effects, restructuring, write-offs, model validation and disclosures, plus ECL-related KPIs. Internal Audit must review, evaluate and report annually on ECL adequacy as third line of defence, with an independent third party permitted where Internal Audit cannot perform the review.
