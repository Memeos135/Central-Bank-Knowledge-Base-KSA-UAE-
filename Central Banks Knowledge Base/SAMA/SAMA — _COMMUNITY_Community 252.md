# IT Governance Glossary

## Why this community

Defined terminology for the IT governance regime, which fixes the scope and meaning of the obligations imposed on supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster acting as the definitional layer: terms defined here govern how the framework's control and committee requirements are read.
- Cross-regime relevance where the same terms recur in cyber-security, outsourcing and business continuity requirements; definitions should not be borrowed across regimes without checking scope.
