# Related Parties Rules for Banks

SAMA's Related Parties Rules for Banks (June 2022 update, aligned to IFSB standards) define related parties and impose governance, exposure-limit, security and reporting requirements on bank dealings with them, reinforcing Article 9 of the Banking Control Law. Key limits are 5% of eligible capital per non-bank related party (10% if Tadawul-listed, up to 25% for a non-banking financial-sector subsidiary) with a 50% cumulative cap, plus immediate breach notification to SAMA with a remediation plan. Binds all local banks; supersedes the earlier related parties rules circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
