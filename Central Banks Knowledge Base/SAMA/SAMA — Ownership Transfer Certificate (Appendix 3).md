# Ownership Transfer Certificate (Appendix 3)

Appendix 3 to SAMA's mandatory standard vehicle Ijarah contract for individuals: the ownership transfer certificate evidencing passage of title in the leased vehicle to the lessee on completion of the contract or early purchase. It supports the contract's ownership-transfer procedures and early-ownership clauses and binds lessors using the prescribed form.
