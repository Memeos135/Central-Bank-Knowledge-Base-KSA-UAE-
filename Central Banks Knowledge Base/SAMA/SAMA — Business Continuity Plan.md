# Business Continuity Plan

Node for the business continuity plan/policy layer of SAMA's BCM Framework, linking the required business continuity policy (objectives, scope, responsibilities, monitored compliance, documented and approved scope exclusions) to the governance structure above it. It establishes documented commitment to continuity and communication to relevant stakeholders. Applies to all SAMA member organizations; policy exclusions need BCM committee and senior management approval.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
