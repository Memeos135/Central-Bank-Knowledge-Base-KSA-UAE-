# Technical and Tactical CTI Principles

A domain within SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles covering the specialised CTI practice of identifying technical components and indicators of cyber attacks (as distinct from the Core, Strategic and Operational domains). It sits alongside expectations that intelligence be actionable, shared internally (e.g. with the SOC and IT) and classified under the Traffic Light Protocol. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA, including those that outsource their CTI capability, with phasing of implementation left to the organisation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
