# Banknote Inspection Emphasis

## Why this community

Operational obligation on banks and cash-handling entities to inspect banknotes, covering authenticity checks and handling of suspect or unfit currency at the teller and cash-centre level.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the obligation stands alone as a supervisory instruction to cash-handling institutions.
- Its practical significance is as a control feeding currency-integrity and counterfeit-reporting duties, and it interacts with AML suspicion-reporting where counterfeit or suspect cash is presented.
