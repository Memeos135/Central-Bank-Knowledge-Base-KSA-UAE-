# Institutional Beneficiary Creditworthiness Assessment

Sets the due diligence and credit-assessment duties owed in respect of the Institutional Beneficiary: AML/CTF-aligned policies verifying legal status, identity (including establishment) and address, conflict-of-interest checks, and assessment of solvency, credit history, existing financing, past performance and business plan. Results must be communicated to Participants as part of the risk assessment, and the platform must disclose credit-rating standards, fee details, eligibility criteria, default and material-change mechanisms, and contingency arrangements. Binds the licensed DBC company; note the overlap between the finance-side creditworthiness test and AML/CTF customer due diligence on a corporate borrower (KYB).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, consumer protection, data/credit info
