# Operational Risk Framework

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure of a bank's operational risk framework — how operational risk is defined, governed, measured and mitigated for capital purposes.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster acting as the narrative anchor for the operational risk disclosure set; the quantitative operational risk templates are the numeric counterpart.
- Relies on the operational risk definitions and Business Indicator concepts established in the operational risk capital requirements, so scope limits there flow through to what must be disclosed.
- Sits at the table (qualitative) level of the law -> capital rules -> disclosure requirement chain.
