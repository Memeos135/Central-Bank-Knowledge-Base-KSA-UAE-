# Template CCR5 - Composition of Collateral for CCR Exposures

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework (Dec 2022) covering the breakdown of collateral used in counterparty credit risk (CCR) exposures, including derivative and securities financing transaction collateral, segregated vs unsegregated and received vs posted. It sits in the CCR disclosure section alongside CCRA and CCR1–CCR8, which spans banking book and trading book exposures subject to a CCR charge, including exposures to CCPs. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the Pillar 3 regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
