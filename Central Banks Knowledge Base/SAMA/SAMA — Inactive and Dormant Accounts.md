# Inactive and Dormant Accounts

DTFC rules classifying a General Account as Inactive after one year without customer-initiated movement and Dormant after two years, with dual control (and higher-authority dual control) for reactivation, and a bar on withdrawals from Dormant accounts absent the duly authorised person. Periods during which the General Account is linked to a live Term Deposit Account do not count as inactivity. Requires board-approved policies for supervision, customer contact, file isolation and annual internal audit; applies to all customers including those holding other active accounts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, BNPL/finance
