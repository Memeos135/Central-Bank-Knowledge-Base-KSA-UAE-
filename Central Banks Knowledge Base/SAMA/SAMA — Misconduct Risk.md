# Misconduct Risk

Addresses how bank remuneration systems must capture misconduct risk alongside other hard-to-measure risks (liquidity, cost of capital, reputational, regulatory), using ex ante non-financial performance criteria such as compliance quality and fair treatment of customers, supported by training. Sets factors for determining individual accountability (proximity to misconduct, rank, motivation, negligence, history, supervisory failures, root cause) and severity indicators (fines, regulatory actions, losses, reputational and customer impact), including freezing awards during investigations. Binds banks' Boards, senior management, control functions and HR under the Banks Remuneration Rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
