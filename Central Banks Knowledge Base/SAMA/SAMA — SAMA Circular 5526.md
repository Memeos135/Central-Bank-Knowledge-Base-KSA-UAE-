# SAMA Circular 5526

A SAMA circular (dated 07/08/1438H, ref. 361000107381) addressed to supervised institutions concerning the periodic submission of financial/statistical returns to SAMA, including submission in the prescribed electronic (Excel) format within stated deadlines. It requires the returns to be accompanied by a Certification Statement signed at senior level (Chief Accountant, CFO and CEO or Managing Director). The scanned source is heavily degraded in Arabic OCR, so the precise scope and deadlines should be verified against the original before relying on it.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
