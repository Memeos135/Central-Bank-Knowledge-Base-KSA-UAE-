# Compliance Principles for Banks

SAMA's Compliance Principles for banks and commercial banks operating in the Kingdom, issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law to replace the earlier Compliance Manual (2008). It defines the compliance function, compliance policy, compliance unit, head of compliance, compliance officers/staff and compliance risk, and sets board and senior management responsibilities, reporting, sanctions, outsourcing of compliance tasks and cross-border/foreign-operations issues. It binds local banks and licensed branches of foreign banks (with the branch's senior officer standing in for the board), initially on a guidance basis and later as a mandatory requirement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, banking prudential
