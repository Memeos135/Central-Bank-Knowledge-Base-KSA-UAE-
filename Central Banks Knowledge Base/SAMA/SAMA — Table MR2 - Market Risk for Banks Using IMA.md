# Table MR2 - Market Risk for Banks Using IMA

Pillar 3 disclosure template MR2 under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, setting out the fixed-format quarterly breakdown of market risk capital requirements for banks using the internal models approach (IMA). It covers unconstrained and constrained expected shortfall by regulatory risk class, IMCC, the capital charge for non-modellable risk factors (SES), the default risk capital requirement, and the surcharge for 'amber zone' trading desks, plus backtesting exception data and narrative on outliers. Mandatory for banks with SAMA approval to use the IMA for part or all of their market risk, at the group-wide regulatory consolidation level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
