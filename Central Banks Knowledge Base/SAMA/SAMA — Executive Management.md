# Executive Management

Defined-term node for the bank's executive management under the Shariah Governance Framework, meaning senior executives who run the business and propose and implement strategic decisions. Their duties include referring Shariah issues to the Shariah committee with timely information and disclosure, monitoring and implementing the committee's decisions, providing ongoing Shariah and finance training to the board, committee and staff, and embedding a culture of Shariah compliance across Islamic banking activities. It binds management of local banks conducting Islamic banking.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
