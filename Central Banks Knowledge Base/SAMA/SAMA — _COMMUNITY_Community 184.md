# Surveillance Camera Compliance

## Why this community

Physical security obligations for supervised entities regarding installation, operation and retention standards for CCTV/surveillance systems at branches, ATMs and cash handling points.

## How members connect

- Isolated compliance requirement with no internal cross-references in this graph.
- Operationally linked to cash handling, branch network and premises-security regimes; breaches are supervisory findings rather than consumer-protection matters.
- Retention and access to footage interact with data protection and evidentiary/investigation duties.
