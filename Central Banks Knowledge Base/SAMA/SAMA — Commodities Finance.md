# Commodities Finance

Commodities finance is a specialized lending subcategory under the corporate exposure class in SAMA's credit risk capital framework, covering short-term lending against reserves, inventories or receivables of exchange-traded commodities where repayment comes from sale proceeds and the borrower has no independent repayment capacity. Absent an issue-specific external rating, these exposures carry a 100% risk weight; issuer ratings may not be substituted. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks computing credit risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
