# Circular on Registration of Finance Lease Contracts

A SAMA circular (Arabic text) directing all licensed banks and finance companies to begin registering finance lease contracts with the Saudi Finance Lease Contract Registration Company (Sijil) as of the stated commencement date. It rests on the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation, under which the lessor must register the contract and any amendments within ten business days of conclusion or amendment, and record all dispositions affecting the contractual rights of lessor and lessee. It binds banks and finance companies acting as lessors.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance
