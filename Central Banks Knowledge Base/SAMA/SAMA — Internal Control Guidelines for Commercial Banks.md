# Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks

This node reflects SAMA's 1999 circular transmitting the Basel Committee paper on banks' interactions with HLIs, in which SAMA states that any such exposures are to be managed in line with its Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks and the bank's own prudent internal credit policies. It also sets SAMA's expectations that credit and risk management staff be familiar with the practices described. It binds all banks in the Kingdom, addressed to general managers and managing directors.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
