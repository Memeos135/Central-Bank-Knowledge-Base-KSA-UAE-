# Income-Producing Real Estate Lending (IPRE)

This node defines income-producing real estate lending as funding where repayment and recovery depend primarily on cash flows generated by the property, distinguishing it from other corporate exposures merely collateralized by real estate, and contrasts it with high-volatility commercial real estate (including ADC financing). It also connects to the standardized approach real estate exposure class and the "materially dependent on cash flows" distinction. It binds banks classifying and risk-weighting real estate exposures under SAMA's credit risk capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
