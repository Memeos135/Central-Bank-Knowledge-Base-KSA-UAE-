# Banknote Sorting Instructions

## Why this community

Currency-handling standards for banks and cash handlers: fitness sorting, authentication and recirculation of a specific Saudi banknote issue.

## How members connect

- Isolated instruction-level item; its force comes from SAMA's currency-issuance mandate rather than cross-references to other regimes.
- Operationally binding on cash-handling institutions and their outsourced cash service providers, so obligations flow through outsourcing arrangements.
- Distinguish from counterfeit-reporting and AML cash-transaction duties, which are separate regimes.
