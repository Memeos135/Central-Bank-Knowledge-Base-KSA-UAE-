# External Auditor Accounting Standards

## Why this community

The accounting and auditing standards external auditors must apply when auditing SAMA-supervised entities, and the resulting basis of regulatory financial reporting.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA circular directing the standards framework applicable to external audits of supervised institutions.
- Feeds the audit/financial-reporting obligation chain - board and audit committee oversight, regulatory returns and disclosure.
- Circular-level instruction supplementing banking and finance-company governance requirements rather than creating standalone standards.
