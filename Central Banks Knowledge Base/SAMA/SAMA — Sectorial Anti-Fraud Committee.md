# Sectorial Anti-Fraud Committee

Node concerning sector-level counter-fraud coordination as reflected in the Counter-Fraud Framework's organisational requirements: Member Organisations must maintain a Counter-Fraud Department responsible for day-to-day operation of the Counter-Fraud Programme, including sharing counter-fraud intelligence with SAMA and other sector participants. Related material covers the department's mandate (risk assessment, control design, system tuning, investigations, risk-appetite monitoring, senior-management reporting) and the Counter-Fraud Maturity Model used to assess implementation. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organisations; open it for intelligence-sharing and counter-fraud organisational structure questions.
