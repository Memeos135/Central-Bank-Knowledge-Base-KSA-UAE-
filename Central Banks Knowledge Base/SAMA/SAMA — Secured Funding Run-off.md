# Secured Funding Run-off

LCR guidance node on funding treatment where obligations are collateralised by legal rights to designated assets, read against the definition of unsecured wholesale funding and the counterparty-based run-off factors in the same section. It sits within SAMA's adoption of the January 2013 Basel LCR standard and includes SAMA's discretion to apply a higher run-off to qualifying retail term deposits. Binds banks licensed in Saudi Arabia for LCR computation and prudential reporting.
