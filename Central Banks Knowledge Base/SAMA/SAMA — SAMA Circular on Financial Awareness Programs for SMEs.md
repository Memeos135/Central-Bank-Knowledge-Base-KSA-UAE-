# SAMA Circular on Financial Awareness Programs for SMEs

A SAMA circular urging all finance providers to develop periodic financial awareness programmes tailored to small and medium enterprises, aligned with the Financial Sector Development Programme and Vision 2030 objectives on financial literacy and private-sector growth. Programmes should be calibrated to the financier's customer segment and identified awareness gaps and delivered through channels that reach SMEs, with a quarterly report on the financier's contribution submitted to SAMA's financial awareness mailbox. Addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom; framed as encouragement rather than a prescriptive rule, though the quarterly reporting expectation is explicit.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
