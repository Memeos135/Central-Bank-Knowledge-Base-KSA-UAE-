# Core Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles

Domain 1 of the SAMA CTI Principles, establishing the baseline for planning, collection, processing, analysis and dissemination of threat intelligence around the intelligence lifecycle, plus continuous improvement of CTI capability and performance. Principle 1 requires Member Organizations to define internal CTI roles and responsibilities, stand up a dedicated production and dissemination team with skilled resources, tools and budget, and set communication channels with internal stakeholders and external organisations. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations.
