# Watch List

A concept node within SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans describing the watch list generated from the Early Warning Signals (EWS) process for borrowers showing potential payment difficulty. It sets out the minimum data content of the list (loan details, group/related-party status, materiality, date added, reviews and outcomes, mitigation measures, reasons for inclusion), the approval gate for removal, time-limited retention before transfer to the Workout Unit or return to the originating unit, and monthly escalation to a designated management committee and the Board for material cases. It binds banks' credit risk management, relationship management and governance functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
