# Primary Dealers Guidelines

## Why this community

The Primary Dealer framework for Saudi government debt: who may act as a primary dealer, the eligibility conditions for that status, and the operational duties owed in the primary and secondary markets.

## How members connect

- The operational guidelines circular is the operative instrument; eligibility conditions define the gateway criteria an institution must meet and continue to satisfy to retain PD status.
- The Debt Management Office is the counterparty/issuer-side body whose role and interface with dealers the guidelines presuppose, making this a cross-authority regime (SAMA plus the debt management function).
- Obligations here are status-conditional: failure to meet the eligibility conditions is the trigger for loss of the privileges and duties set out in the guidelines.
