# Clearing Member Capital Requirement (KCMi)

Explains KCMi, the clearing member bank's risk-sensitive capital requirement for its default fund contribution to a QCCP, derived in two steps from the CCP's hypothetical capital requirement and the member's contribution share. Related provisions address how clearing members capitalise exposures to clients as bilateral trades (with a reduced five-day margin period of risk) and how client-posted collateral passed to the CCP may be recognised on both legs. Applies to banks acting as clearing members, including in multi-level client structures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
