# Principles for Modellability of Risk Factors

A set of qualitative principles within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk that banks must apply, in addition to the risk factor eligibility test (RFET), to decide whether a risk factor can actually be modelled in the expected shortfall model. It stresses that passing the real-price observation count alone is not sufficient — data accuracy, sourcing, update frequency and stress-period representativeness also matter — and SAMA may reject data as unsuitable, forcing the factor into non-modellable (NMRF) capital treatment. Binds banks using the internal models approach for market risk; SAMA retains discretion, including an exceptional systemic-stress override that must not reduce capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
