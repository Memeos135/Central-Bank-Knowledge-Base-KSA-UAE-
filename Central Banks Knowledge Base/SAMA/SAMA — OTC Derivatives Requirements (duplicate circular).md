# OTC Derivatives Requirements (duplicate circular)

The consolidated SAMA Requirements for OTC derivative contracts, split into Section A (reporting of reportable OTC derivative transactions to the SAMA-authorised Trade Repository Operator) and Section B (risk mitigation for non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives). Scope covers interest rate, FX, equity, credit and commodity derivatives, with reporting allocated to the KSA-side counterparty; centrally cleared transactions (directly or via indirect/client clearing) fall outside the risk mitigation requirements. Binds all banks in KSA with OTC derivative transactions, regardless of notional size or hedging purpose.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
