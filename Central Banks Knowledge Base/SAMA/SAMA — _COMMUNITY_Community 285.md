# Deferred Remuneration Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of deferred and retained remuneration, including outstanding exposed and unexposed amounts and reductions applied through malus or clawback. The problem-space is ex-post risk adjustment of variable pay.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a prescribed template in the remuneration (REM) series of the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.
- Obligation chain runs from the compensation/governance rules (deferral, vesting, malus and clawback requirements) to the annual quantitative disclosure of how those mechanisms operated.
- Shares defined populations and pay categories with the other REM templates, so figures must reconcile across the series.
- Hierarchy: disclosure framework -> Pillar 3 requirements -> fixed-format template with specified reporting frequency.
