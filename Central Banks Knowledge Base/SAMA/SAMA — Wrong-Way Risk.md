# Wrong-Way Risk

Covers the identification and management of general and specific wrong-way risk — where counterparty exposure is positively correlated with deterioration in the counterparty's creditworthiness. Requires stress testing and scenario analysis designed to surface such correlations, monitoring by product, region and industry, and regular reporting to senior management and the board or its delegated committee. Binds banks running counterparty credit risk exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
