# Total Destruction of Leased Asset

The clause of the mandatory individual vehicle Ijara template addressing total and partial destruction (loss) of the leased asset, and the related insurance and procedural steps for handling such cases. It allocates risk of loss between lessor and lessee and determines settlement of outstanding amounts from insurance proceeds. Binding on lessors under the standard form, which may not be amended.
