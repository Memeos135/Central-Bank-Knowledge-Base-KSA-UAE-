# LGD Estimation

Node covering loss-given-default determination under the IRB approach in SAMA's credit risk capital rules, including the effective LGD for collateralised transactions (LGD* as an exposure-weighted blend of unsecured and secured LGD with haircuts for collateral type and currency mismatch), the unavailability of the standardised simple collateral approach to IRB banks, and supervisory LGD parameter floors by collateral type for corporate exposures (with pro-rata treatment for partially secured exposures). Binds banks using foundation or advanced IRB for credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
