# Finance Lease Law

Concept node for the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation, cited as the statutory basis for SAMA's instructions on finance-leased vehicles. The circular invokes the Law's article governing destruction/total loss of the leased asset as the provision to be applied when a finance entity determines total economic loss for an individual's finance-leased vehicle. It governs the rights and obligations of lessors (banks and finance companies) and lessees in finance lease arrangements supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
