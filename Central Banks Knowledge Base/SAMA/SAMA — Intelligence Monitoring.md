# Intelligence Monitoring

Sets the requirement for Member Organisations to define, approve and implement a fraud Intelligence Monitoring process drawing on internal and external sources to identify and monitor emerging fraud threats. Covers scanning, analysis and dissemination of threat information (modus operandi, actors, jurisdiction), acting on threats, periodic effectiveness evaluation, alignment with SAMA Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, and sharing of intelligence and confirmed-fraud log-in data (device ID, IP) via the Sectorial Anti-Fraud Committee. Binds Member Organisations supervised by SAMA under the Counter-Fraud Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
