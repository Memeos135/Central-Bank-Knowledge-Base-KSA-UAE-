# Table OVA - Bank Risk Management Approach

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table in which a bank describes its risk management approach — its risk strategy, risk appetite, governance, and how the board and senior management identify, assess and manage risks. It provides the narrative context for the quantitative RWA and capital metrics disclosed in OV1 and KM1. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements at consolidated level.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
