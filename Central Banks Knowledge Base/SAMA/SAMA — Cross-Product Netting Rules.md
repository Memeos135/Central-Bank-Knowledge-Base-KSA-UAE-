# Cross-Product Netting Rules

Sets out the conditions under which a bank using the internal models method (IMM) for counterparty credit risk may recognise netting across securities financing transactions, or across SFTs and OTC derivatives, for regulatory capital purposes. It imposes legal criteria: a written bilateral cross-product netting arrangement producing a single net obligation on default or insolvency, plus reasoned legal opinions confirming enforceability under the laws of all relevant jurisdictions (counterparty's charter/branch jurisdiction, transaction governing law, and law governing the netting contract). Binds SAMA-supervised banks seeking IMM/netting recognition, subject to SAMA approval and internal verification procedures before a trade enters a netting set.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
