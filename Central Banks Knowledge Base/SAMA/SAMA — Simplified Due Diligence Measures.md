# Simplified Due Diligence Measures

This section permits financial institutions to apply simplified due diligence where ML/TF risk is assessed as low and no suspicion exists, grounded in Article 5/5 of the AML Law implementing regulations and Article 17 of the implementing regulations of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing. Simplified measures must be documented in approved AML/CTF policies and are a streamlined, not waived, form of CDD: identity verification from independent authenticated sources, beneficial owner identification, understanding the purpose of the relationship, and understanding ownership and control of legal persons remain mandatory.
