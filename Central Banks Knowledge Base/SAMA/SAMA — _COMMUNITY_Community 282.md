# Operational Risk Qualitative Info

## Why this community

Pillar 3 narrative disclosure of a bank's operational risk framework — the qualitative account of how operational risk is identified, governed, measured and mitigated, which contextualises the quantitative operational risk capital figures.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the qualitative table is the descriptive counterpart to the operational risk quantitative templates, and is read with them rather than standalone.
- Sits in the disclosure hierarchy as a SAMA-adopted Pillar 3 requirement giving effect to the operational risk capital regime; obligation is periodic public disclosure in the prescribed format.
- Defined terms (e.g. business indicator, loss data) are inherited from the operational risk capital rules, not redefined here.
