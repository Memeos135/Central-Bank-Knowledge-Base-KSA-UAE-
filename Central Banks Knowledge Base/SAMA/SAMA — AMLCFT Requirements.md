# AML/CFT Requirements

This node collects the AML/CFT and KYC obligations embedded in SAMA's Agent Banking regulation, covering both the mandatory contract terms between bank and agent (compliance with AML/CFT and KYC, provision of documents and periodic AML/CFT reports to SAMA, ten-year record retention, bank ownership and confidentiality of customer data) and the bank's pre-application obligations (board approval, internal agent banking policy, and a description of customer due diligence and KYC processes). It binds banks contracting agents, with the bank retaining responsibility for ensuring the agent satisfies AML/CFT, anti-fraud/embezzlement and information security requirements. Useful where an agent performs customer-facing steps but the bank remains the accountable financial institution.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
