# Reduced Version of BA-CVA

Defines the reduced version of BA-CVA, in which CVA hedges are not recognised, calculated by aggregating stand-alone CVA capital across in-scope counterparties with a supervisory correlation parameter and a discount scalar. Intended for banks that do not hedge CVA risk, and also forms a component of the full BA-CVA calculation as a constraint on hedge recognition. Applies to banks using BA-CVA under SAMA's capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
