# STC Term Securitization Criteria

Covers the STC criteria applicable to non-ABCP (term) traditional securitizations, compliance with which makes exposures eligible for the alternative capital treatment available under the external-ratings-based and internal-ratings-based securitization approaches. It sits within the broader STC scope provisions, including the originator/sponsor disclosure duty and the investor's independent assessment obligation, and requires the criteria to be satisfied on a continuing basis. Relevant to SAMA-supervised banks holding or issuing term securitization positions, and to related Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
