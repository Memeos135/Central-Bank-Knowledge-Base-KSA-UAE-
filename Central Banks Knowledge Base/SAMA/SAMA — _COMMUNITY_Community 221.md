# CCR Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Qualitative narrative disclosure on counterparty credit risk - risk management objectives, policies, limits and collateral/CVA practices - as distinct from the quantitative CCR templates.

## How members connect

- Standalone table with no internal links captured; its legal force derives from the Pillar 3 disclosure framework that mandates it.
- Conceptually paired with the quantitative CCR templates and the CCP/QCCP definitions used in the capital-disclosure cluster.
