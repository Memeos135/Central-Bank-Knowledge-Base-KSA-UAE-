# Secure Disposal of Information Assets

Framework sub-domain requiring information assets to be securely disposed of once no longer required, under a defined standard and procedure. It extends the obligation to third party service providers used for disposal, transport and storage, whose compliance and control effectiveness must be periodically measured and evaluated. Binds Member Organizations, including for their outsourced arrangements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
