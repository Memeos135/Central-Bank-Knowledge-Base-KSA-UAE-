# KSA Capital Charge

A concept node for the KSA parameter — the capital charge of the underlying pool of exposures computed under the standardised approach to credit risk — used as the key input when applying the standardised securitization approach to determine risk weights for securitization positions. Supporting context links it to the wider regulatory capital and Pillar 3 disclosure material, including capital ratio and distribution-constraint templates. Relevant to SAMA-supervised banks calculating securitization RWAs; note the abbreviation is unrelated to "Kingdom of Saudi Arabia".

**Regimes:** banking prudential
