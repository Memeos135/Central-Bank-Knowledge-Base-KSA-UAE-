# Guidelines for Applying for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Licensing

SAMA guidance setting out how founders or their authorised representatives apply for a licence to conduct debt-based crowdfunding, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding. It specifies the unmodifiable application form, the document checklist (articles of association, organisational structure, fit and proper forms for founders and board/committee candidates, feasibility study and business plan, an irrevocable bank guarantee for 20% of minimum capital, third-party and related-party draft contracts), the submission channel and the licensing procedure. Binds applicants seeking SAMA authorisation; false or omitted material information is treated as a breach of the Finance Companies Control Law and may cause rejection or licence cancellation.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
