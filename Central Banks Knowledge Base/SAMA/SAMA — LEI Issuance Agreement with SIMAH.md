# LEI Issuance Agreement with SIMAH

The template agreement annexed to the LEI circular, concluded between SIMAH (as the GLEIF-accredited local operating unit) and a SAMA-supervised financial institution, enabling the institution to issue, update and renew LEIs for its legal-entity (business) customers. Its recitals frame SIMAH's authority under the ROC charter, the GLEIF–LOU master agreement and SAMA instructions, and the institution's role as holder of corporate customer data subject to SAMA supervision. It is operative only once signed by the institution; it does not itself create the LEI obligation, which arises from the circular.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk
