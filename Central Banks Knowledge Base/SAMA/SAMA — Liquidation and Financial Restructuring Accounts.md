# Liquidation and Financial Restructuring Accounts

Rule 300.1.7 of SAMA's bank account opening rules, covering accounts opened for liquidation, administrative liquidation and financial restructuring under the Bankruptcy Law, and liquidation under the Companies Law. It sets the court order, appointment evidence, identity and commercial registration documents, mandatory account naming convention, operating authority of the bankruptcy officeholder/liquidator/bankruptcy committee, and rules on account validity, renewal and closure. It binds SAMA-licensed banks dealing with insolvent or dissolving natural and juristic persons.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF
