# Non-Resident Account Rules

## Why this community

Bank account access for non-residents in the context of the new non-Saudi real estate ownership regime — i.e., when and on what basis a non-resident may hold an account tied to property acquisition.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: a royal-decree level ownership law drives a SAMA update, which in turn amends the Bank Account Rules.
- The update operates as an amendment instrument; its meaning is only complete when read into the parent Bank Account Rules provisions on non-resident eligibility.
- Shared defined scope around 'non-resident' status and the permitted account purpose, which limits the exemption to real-estate-related dealings.
- Downstream consequence for CDD: eligibility widening does not relax identification/verification duties under the AML regime.
