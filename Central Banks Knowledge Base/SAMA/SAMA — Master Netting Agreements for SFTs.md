# Master Netting Agreements for SFTs

Addresses the recognition of master netting agreements for securities financing transactions and derivatives, including calculation of the net exposure (E*) used as EAD, treatment of margin agreements spanning multiple netting sets, collateral taken outside netting sets, and the alternative VaR models approach (retaining 99% VaR) subject to SAMA recognition. It binds banks seeking netting benefits in counterparty credit risk capital calculations, conditional on the agreement meeting the legal enforceability criteria of the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
