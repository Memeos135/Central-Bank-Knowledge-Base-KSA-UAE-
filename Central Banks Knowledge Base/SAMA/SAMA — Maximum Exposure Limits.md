# Maximum Exposure Limits

This node covers the quantitative limits and exemptions under the Large Exposure Rules: a 15% eligible capital cap on exposures to a single non-bank counterparty (excluding individuals, sole proprietorships and commercial undertakings majority owned by the Saudi government), with exemptions for Saudi sovereign, SAMA, entities connected with the Saudi Government, GCC central governments and central banks, intra-day interbank exposures and consolidated intra-group entities. Non-banking financial-sector subsidiaries remain subject to a 25% limit, and exposures hedged by credit derivatives are recognised against the protection provider even where the original exposure is exempt. Any exposure not expressly exempted is fully subject to the limits; banks must still monitor exempted exposures internally.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
