# Risk Factor Modellability

Governs when a risk factor may be modelled inside a bank's expected shortfall model versus being capitalised as a non-modellable risk factor (NMRF), building on the risk factor eligibility test (RFET) and supporting data-quality principles. SAMA may deem data unsuitable and force NMRF treatment, and may in extraordinary systemic circumstances treat a failed risk factor as modellable without reducing capital; banks must evidence modellability (e.g. regression diagnostics, price recovery from risk factors) rather than relying on real price counts alone. Binds banks using the internal models approach for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
