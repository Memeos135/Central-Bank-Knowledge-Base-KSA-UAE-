# Technical and Tactical Cyber Threat Intelligence

A domain/concept node within SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, which organise CTI into core lifecycle principles plus strategic, operational and technical/tactical layers. The technical and tactical layer concerns machine- and analyst-level threat data (e.g. indicators of compromise, attacker tools and techniques) feeding detection and defensive action, as distinct from strategic intelligence aimed at executive management. It applies to Member Organizations, i.e. entities licensed and supervised by SAMA, as expected supervisory practice rather than a stand-alone law.
