# Cyber Security Event Management

Node covering the Framework's cyber security event management area together with its governance underpinnings: the six-level cyber security maturity model (0 to 5, cumulative) used to measure compliance, the required content of the cyber security policy, and the allocation of cyber security roles and responsibilities with ultimate accountability at the Board of Directors. It frames how controls must be defined, approved, implemented, demonstrable and reported, including reporting of breaches and suspected weaknesses. Binds Member Organizations, their boards and senior management.
