# Controls for Contract Registry Entry Procedures

A SAMA circular and Governor's decision (agreed with the Minister of Justice) issuing the controls on registration procedures in the finance lease contracts register, the data required for registration, and the rules and procedures for third-party access to the register, under the Finance Lease Law (M/48) and Article 30 of its Implementing Regulation. It covers definitions, registration duties (including the lessor's obligation to register a contract and its amendments within a set period via the register's electronic channel), registration documents, access to register data, and enforcement-related extracts. It binds banks, finance companies and licensed finance lease contract registration companies operating in the Kingdom, whose registers remain SAMA-supervised.
