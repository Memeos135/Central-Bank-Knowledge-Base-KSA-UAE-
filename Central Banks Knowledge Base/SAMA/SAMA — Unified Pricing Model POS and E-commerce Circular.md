# Unified Pricing Model POS and E-commerce Circular

A SAMA follow-up circular on the unified pricing model for POS and e-commerce services for mada card transactions, extending to transactions at money transfer and exchange centres and international transfers via e-wallets. It requests banks and payment service providers to report their level of compliance and application of the unified pricing model by 15 June 2022 to the designated SAMA mailbox. Binds banks and licensed payment service providers.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
