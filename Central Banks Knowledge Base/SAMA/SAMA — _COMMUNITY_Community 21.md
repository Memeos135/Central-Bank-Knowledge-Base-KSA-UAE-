# Shariah Risk Management

## Why this community

Prudential risk management for Shari'ah-compliant banking, taxonomising the risk categories a bank must identify, measure and control (credit, market, liquidity, equity investment, operational and Shari'ah non-compliance risk), together with the operational-risk sub-taxonomy and the insurance/internal-control mitigants available to management.

## How members connect

- The Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking sits at the top and enumerates each risk category as a distinct obligation area, with the Shari'ah Governance Framework and PSIAs Rules supplying the governance and profit-sharing-account context that makes those risks Shari'ah-specific.
- Shari'ah non-compliance risk is treated as a sub-set of operational risk, which creates an obligation chain: operational risk controls and reporting must also capture SNCR events.
- The Operational Risk Management Guide elaborates operational risk into criminal, professional and political sub-risks (fraud, forgery, electronic crime, professional/D&O liability) and links them to risk-transfer options — self insurance, regular insurance, bankers blanket bond, finite risk — plus the Risk Management Evaluation Questionnaire as the assessment instrument.
- Cross-regime anchors: SAMA Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks and the Companies Law provide the board/management accountability baseline on which the Shari'ah risk framework is layered.
