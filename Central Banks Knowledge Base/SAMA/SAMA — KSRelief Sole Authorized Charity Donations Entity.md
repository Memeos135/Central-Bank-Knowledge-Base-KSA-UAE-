# KSRelief Sole Authorized Charity Donations Entity

SAMA circular confirming, pursuant to a Royal Order and earlier SAMA instructions, that the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre (KSRelief) is the sole entity authorised to receive relief, humanitarian and charitable donations — whether from government or private sources — for delivery abroad. Supervised institutions must verify that such donations and outbound transfers are channelled through KSRelief, review donation transactions made via foreign platforms using bank and credit cards, and continue awareness, service-delivery controls and monitoring. Binds all financial institutions supervised by the Saudi Central Bank.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS
