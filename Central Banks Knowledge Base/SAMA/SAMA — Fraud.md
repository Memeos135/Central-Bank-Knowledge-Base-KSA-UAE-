# Fraud

A sub-element of the criminal-risk category in SAMA's operational risk guidance for banks, describing fraud as historically the largest single source of operational loss in the Saudi banking system. It characterises internal (employee) versus external fraud across cash handling, ATMs, credit card issuance (application, account set-up, embossing, statement distribution) and point-of-sale/merchant channels, and supports the expectation that banks identify, control and finance this exposure. It orients bank boards, senior management and the operational risk function rather than imposing product-specific rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments
