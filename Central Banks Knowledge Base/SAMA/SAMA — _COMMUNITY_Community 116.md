# Bankruptcy & Companies Law

## Why this community

Insolvency and corporate-existence law as it bears on account handling: how a licensed institution treats accounts of entities in liquidation or financial restructuring, and the statutory basis for those procedures.

## How members connect

- The accounts instruction is the operative SAMA-level obligation; the Bankruptcy Law and Companies Law supply the underlying procedures and defined statuses (liquidation, restructuring) it cross-references.
- Legal hierarchy runs law -> SAMA instruction: the two statutes define when an entity enters a procedure and who has authority to act; the accounts rules determine account status, signatory authority and operating restrictions.
- Shared reliance on court/trustee appointments and corporate dissolution concepts, which also condition KYC/mandate verification for the affected customer.
