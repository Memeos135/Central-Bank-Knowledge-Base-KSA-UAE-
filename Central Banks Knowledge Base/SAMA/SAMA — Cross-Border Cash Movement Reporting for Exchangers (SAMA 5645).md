# Cross-Border Cash Movement Reporting for Exchangers (SAMA 5645)

A SAMA circular to licensed exchange businesses (categories A and B) requiring monthly reporting, on the attached template, of inbound and outbound physical cash movements across the Kingdom's land, air and sea border crossings. It relies on the Rules Governing Money Changing Business (Article 15(a) and (c) on supplying SAMA with requested data and issuing supervisory instructions, and Article 17 on accounting records and additional data requests). It binds licensed money changers only, not banks.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
