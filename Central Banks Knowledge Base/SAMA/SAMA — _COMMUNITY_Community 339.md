# Cyber Security Training

## Why this community

Cyber security training obligations: ensuring staff with security-relevant roles hold and maintain the competence needed to discharge the entity's cyber risk controls.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster forming a human-resources control domain within the cyber security framework.
- Chains to the entity's overall cyber governance and staffing adequacy duties; deficient training is evidence of a control-environment failure rather than an isolated lapse.
- Read together with the awareness domain, with training carrying a role-specific, assessable competence requirement.
