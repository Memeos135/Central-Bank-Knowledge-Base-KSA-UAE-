# Board Responsibilities for Shariah Governance

The board-of-directors limb of SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies (Chapter Two, on responsibilities of the board and senior management). It allocates ultimate accountability for the finance company's Shariah governance arrangements, including approving policies and procedures regulating the Shariah Committee's work (a copy of which must be filed with SAMA) and maintaining a reasonable understanding of Shariah principles and their application in Islamic finance. It binds the board of a licensed finance company within the instructions' scope.
