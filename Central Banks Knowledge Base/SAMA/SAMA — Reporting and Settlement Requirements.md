# Reporting and Settlement Requirements

This node concerns reporting and settlement of repo transactions (including bank participants' reporting of repo trades to SAMA), with the attached context drawn from the master agreement's event-of-default, insolvency and margin/representation provisions, including express reference to proceedings under the Saudi Bankruptcy Law and to margin securities substitution mechanics. Useful for questions on when a repo counterparty is in default, close-out triggers, and the contractual representations each party gives. Applies to repo market participants, with reporting duties falling on bank participants.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
