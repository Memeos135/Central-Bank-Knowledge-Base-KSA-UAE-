# Template TLAC3 - Resolution Entity Creditor Ranking

Pillar 3 template disclosing the creditor hierarchy at legal entity level for each G-SIB resolution entity, mirroring Template TLAC2 but without reporting exposures to the resolution entity itself. It reports nominal capital and liabilities, excluded liabilities, potentially TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity buckets, including perpetual securities. Mandatory for G-SIBs on a semiannual basis from the TLAC conformance date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
