# Judicial Order Enforcement

## Why this community

Enforcement of judicial and execution-court orders against customer accounts and funds held by payment service providers, and the channel through which such orders and notifications are validly served on supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Both instruments address the same obligation chain: a competent judicial/execution authority issues an order, and the supervised payment entity must act on it within the terms SAMA sets.
- One addresses the substantive duty to execute (freeze, seize, release) while the other addresses the procedural gateway — electronic authorisation/receipt of judicial notifications — so service validity conditions the enforcement duty.
- Both interact with the payments/PSP licensing regime and with customer-protection duties (account access restrictions must rest on a valid legal basis, not commercial discretion).
- Practical cross-regime flag: account freezes here derive from judicial authority, and should not be conflated with AML/CTF suspicion-based or TFS sanctions freezes, which have separate legal triggers and reporting paths.
