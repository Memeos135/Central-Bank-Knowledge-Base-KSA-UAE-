# Stress Testing and Scenario Analysis

A section of SAMA's Rules on Liquidity Risk Management for finance companies requiring a comprehensive liquidity stress testing programme covering multiple scenarios, severities and time horizons, combining scenario and sensitivity analysis, and testing behavioural cash-flow assumptions for assets, liabilities and off-balance-sheet items. It sits alongside related requirements on unencumbered/pledged assets, collateral management and SAMA's power to impose specific liquidity exposure limits. The node also carries the Rules' defined terms (liquidity risk tolerance, net cash outflows, HQLA, unencumbered assets, contractual maturity mismatch, stress test, CFP) and binds finance companies licensed and supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
