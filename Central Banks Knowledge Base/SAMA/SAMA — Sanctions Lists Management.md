# Sanctions Lists Management

Chapter Four of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, addressing management of the sanctions lists issued under the UNSC terrorism-financing and proliferation-financing implementation mechanisms. It governs how financial institutions must obtain, update and maintain list data used for screening and freezing decisions. Binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, data/credit info
