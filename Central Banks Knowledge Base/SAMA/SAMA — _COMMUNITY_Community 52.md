# Consumer Protection & Certifications

## Why this community

Consumer protection governance inside supervised entities: the mandated customer care function, complaint handling, quality monitoring and financial awareness, plus the staff competence/certification and Saudization conditions attached to customer-facing roles.

## How members connect

- The Customer Care Department Rules act as the parent instrument and constitute the three sub-units (complaints, quality/performance analysis, awareness and education) as required organisational functions.
- The rules are anchored in the High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection, giving a principles-to-rules hierarchy for interpreting ambiguous complaint-handling duties.
- Professional certification requirements bind to the same customer-facing roles (credit advisory, retail banking), converting consumer-protection outcomes into individual staff eligibility conditions.
- Dual employment restrictions and localisation at exchange centres sit alongside certification as fit-and-proper style workforce controls.
