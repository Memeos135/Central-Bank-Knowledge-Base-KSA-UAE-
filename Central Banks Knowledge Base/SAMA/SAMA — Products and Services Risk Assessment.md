# Products and Services Risk Assessment

Requires banks to assign responsibility for product risk, perform a full life-cycle risk assessment as the basis for deciding whether to launch, apply risk management standards (due diligence, approvals, identification/measurement/monitoring/reporting/mitigation, change management), and assign an overall risk classification to each product. It also requires third-party risk controls where products are offered with Fintech companies, agents or similar entities, and gives the risk management function documentation, review and approval duties over product risk profiles. Binds banks and their risk management function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
