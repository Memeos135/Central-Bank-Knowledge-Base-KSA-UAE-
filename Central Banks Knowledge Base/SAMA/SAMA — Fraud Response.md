# Fraud Response

Node covering the fraud response limb of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, anchored on the defined Fraud Response Plan (actions, reporting protocols, team responsibilities and information logging once fraud is suspected or detected). It connects to the governance requirements placing ultimate responsibility on the Board and oversight with the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, and to expected coordination between counter-fraud, cyber security and financial crime functions. Applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments
