# Penetration Testing

A defined term in the glossary of SAMA's ethical red teaming framework, describing security testing that mimics real-world attacks against live systems and data to circumvent security features, typically chaining multiple vulnerabilities. It is distinguished from Red Teaming, which is a broader scenario-based simulation of adversarial compromise of missions and business processes. Relevant to Member Organizations scoping cyber resilience testing obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
