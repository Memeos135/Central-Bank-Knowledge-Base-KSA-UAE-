# OTC Derivatives Reporting

## Why this community

Mandatory trade repository reporting for OTC derivatives — who must report, to which SAMA-authorised trade repository, in what data fields and identifiers, and how lifecycle events and collateral updates must be maintained.

## How members connect

- The core circular splits obligations into Section A (trade reporting) and Section B (risk mitigation), and supersedes/builds on the earlier SAMA OTC circular, so scope and effective dates must be read against the prior instrument.
- Reporting duties run to the SAMA Authorised Trade Repository Operator, making TR authorisation status a precondition for discharging the reporting obligation.
- Data-level members (Common Data Reporting table, UTI, UPI, action type/lifecycle events, collateral evolutive fields) are the prescribed content of the reporting obligation; UTI and action type together govern how amendments, terminations and corrections are recorded.
- A duplicate circular entry is semantically equivalent to the core instrument — confirm which version is the operative text before citing.
