# Template CR8 - RWA Flow Statements IRB

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework requiring banks to present a flow statement explaining period-on-period movements in credit risk risk-weighted assets calculated under the internal ratings-based approaches. It sits alongside the wider credit risk suite, with the IRB scope excluding counterparty credit risk, CVA and banking book securitisation exposures, which are disclosed in separate templates. It binds SAMA-licensed banks that have supervisory approval to use F-IRB, A-IRB or supervisory slotting approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
