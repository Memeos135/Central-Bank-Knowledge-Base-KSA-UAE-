# Board Responsibilities toward Compliance

Node for the Board's duties and responsibilities toward compliance under SAMA's Compliance Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies (Principle One). It addresses the Board's accountability for approving and overseeing compliance policy, embedding a compliance culture across the whole company rather than only the compliance Unit, and allocating responsibilities among Board, Audit Committee, senior management and staff. It binds boards of SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.
