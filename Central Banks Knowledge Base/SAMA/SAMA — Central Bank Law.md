# Central Bank Law

Identifies the Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36, 11/04/1442AH) as one of the statutory bases, together with the Banking Control Law and its enforcing rules, on which SAMA issued the Related Parties Rules for Banks. Useful for tracing SAMA's rule-making authority and the instrument's hierarchy, effective date and supersession of the prior 1441AH circular. Relevant to anyone assessing the enforceability and legal footing of the Rules on licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
