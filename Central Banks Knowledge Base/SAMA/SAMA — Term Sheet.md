# Term Sheet

Concept node on the term sheet as the negotiation instrument used to record agreed restructuring terms before formal legal documents are drafted, revised at each stage of the workout and especially useful where multiple creditors or stakeholders are involved. It sets out indicative term-sheet content (parties, repayment schedule, pricing, revised events of default, security, governing law) and a sequencing checklist from draft term sheet through signed workout documents. Aimed at banks' workout units and legal counsel structuring restructurings of medium to large exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
