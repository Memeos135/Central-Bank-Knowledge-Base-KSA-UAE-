# Cybersecurity Compliance Circular

A short SAMA circular reaffirming, in light of rising cyber threats to the Saudi financial sector, that supervised entities must comply with SAMA's cybersecurity policies, frameworks and standards as well as the national cybersecurity authority's requirements. It specifically stresses implementation of the Essential Cybersecurity Controls and the Critical Systems Cybersecurity Controls and prioritising cybersecurity in approved budgets. Issued through the Payment Systems and Companies Control Department, so it is directed at banks and payment service providers under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
