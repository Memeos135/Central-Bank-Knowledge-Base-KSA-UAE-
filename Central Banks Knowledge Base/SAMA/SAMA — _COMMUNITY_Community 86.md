# Deposit Taking Finance Companies

## Why this community

Prudential regime for finance companies licensed to take deposits, covering capital adequacy, liquidity, asset quality classification and provisioning, and the internal loan review function. The problem-space is ensuring deposit-funded lenders hold adequate capital and provisions against credit deterioration.

## How members connect

- The 2020 Regulations are the parent instrument; the appendices are annexed reporting/classification templates that the Regulations expressly invoke, so appendix content carries the same obligation force as the body.
- Loan classification and provisioning obligations operationalise the asset quality risk classification grid in Appendix C — classification drives the provisioning outcome, not vice versa.
- The loan review function is the governance control that feeds the classification decision, linking the internal control layer to the supervisory reporting layer (Appendices A and B).
- Capital and liquidity returns are the periodic supervisory reporting chain arising from the same licensing regime; note this is finance-company prudential regulation, distinct from the bank prudential regime.
