# Shariah Compliance Function

The Shariah supervision/compliance element of SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies (Chapter Three), read with the broader compliance-function expectations. "Shariah supervision" is defined as monitoring and verifying the extent to which the finance company's business complies with Islamic Shariah rulings and principles, operating alongside the Shariah Committee's decisions. It binds licensed finance companies in scope and shapes how their compliance function interacts with Shariah rulings.
