# IRB Specialized Lending Sub-Classes

This node identifies the five specialized lending sub-classes within the IRB corporate asset class and their defining characteristics, together with the supervisory slotting approach used to assign risk weights (PF, OF, CF, IPRE and HVCRE) and to compute expected loss for SL exposures. It binds banks classifying banking-book exposures and calculating IRB capital, subject to demonstrating to SAMA that their classification methodology is appropriate and consistent.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[SAMA — IRB Corporate Exposures|IRB Corporate Exposures]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a bank on the IRB approach, the decision is whether a corporate exposure belongs to one of the specialized lending sub-classes, because that determines the permitted methodology. The IRB corporate asset class expressly subdivides into specialized lending sub-classes, so the sub-class definitions govern which risk-weight route is available within the same approval perimeter. The consequence is that a bank unable to meet the estimation standards for the relevant parameters must fall back to the supervisory slotting treatment for those exposures, and the slotting categories and associated weights (including any distinct treatment for high-volatility exposures) apply instead of the bank's own modelled outputs.
