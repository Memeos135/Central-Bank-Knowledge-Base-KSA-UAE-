# Liquidity Coverage Ratio

## Why this community

Standardised public disclosure of the short-term liquidity metric: stock of high-quality liquid assets against 30-day stressed net cash outflows.

## How members connect

- Quantitative Pillar 3 template giving effect to the underlying LCR minimum standard; the disclosure duty is distinct from, and dependent on, the prudential ratio rules.
- Shares defined terms (HQLA, inflow/outflow rates, averaging basis) with the liquidity rules and with the qualitative liquidity table.
- Complements the structural funding measure, so the two liquidity templates are read together as one disclosure set.
