# Off-Balance Sheet (OBS) Items

Covers the treatment and disclosure of off-balance sheet items — guarantees, irrevocable loan commitments and similar contingent exposures — as credit risk exposures under the Basel-aligned disclosure framework, including their gross carrying value measurement before credit conversion factors or credit risk mitigation. Relevant to credit quality templates (e.g. CR1) and to the leverage ratio exposure measure. Mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
