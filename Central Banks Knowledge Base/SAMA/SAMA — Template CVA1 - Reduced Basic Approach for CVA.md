# Template CVA1 - Reduced Basic Approach for CVA

Pillar 3 template CVA1 setting out the components of risk-weighted assets computed under the reduced basic approach for CVA risk: the aggregated systematic component (perfect correlation assumption), the aggregated idiosyncratic component (zero correlation assumption), and the total (K-reduced scaled by 12.5). Mandatory semiannually, in fixed format, for banks measuring part or all of their CVA RWA under the reduced BA-CVA, with narrative on hedges used even where not recognised; it reconciles to the overview template OV1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
