# Multi Factor Authentication (MFA)

The requirement in SAMA's fintech cyber controls that member organizations implement multi-factor authentication to authenticate each login to their applications. It sits alongside single-device assignment, disabling concurrent logins and OTP-based transaction verification, and binds any SAMA-supervised member organization offering e-wallet, lending, crowdfunding or other fintech services.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
