# Exposure Measurement

This node addresses how banks taking OTC derivatives positions against HLIs should measure credit exposure and feed those measures into management decision-making. It identifies three focus areas: more useful potential future exposure (PFE) measures, better assessment of unsecured risk embedded in collateralised derivatives positions (thresholds, margin gaps, liquidation delays), and stress testing of counterparty exposures under volatile markets, including collateral value and liquidity effects. It applies to banks with derivatives and trading exposures to HLI counterparties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
