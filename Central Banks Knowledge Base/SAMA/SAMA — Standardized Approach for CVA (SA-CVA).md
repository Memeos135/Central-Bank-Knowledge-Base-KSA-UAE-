# Standardized Approach for CVA (SA-CVA)

Covers the standardised approach for CVA risk capital (SA-CVA), the more risk-sensitive alternative to BA-CVA that a bank may use only with prior SAMA approval. Defines regulatory CVA (excluding own default), the scope of covered transactions including fair-valued SFTs where CVA loss exposure is material, and the standalone CVA portfolio basis of calculation. Binds banks seeking or holding SAMA permission for SA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
