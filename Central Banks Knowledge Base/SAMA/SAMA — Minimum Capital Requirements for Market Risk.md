# Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk

SAMA's framework setting the minimum regulatory capital requirements for market risk, implementing the Basel FRTB standards in KSA. It covers the trading book/banking book boundary, treatment of internal risk transfers (including CVA risk transfers and hedges with eligible third-party protection providers), the standardised approach, the internal models approach subject to SAMA approval, and the simplified alternative. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA, with distinctions drawn between D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[SAMA — Standardised Approach|Standardised Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For market risk capital, the standardised approach is not an optional method to be weighed against internal models: the parent market risk framework directs all banks, D-SIBs and non D-SIBs alike, to compute the charge on that basis. The standardised approach chapter is therefore the implementing detail of the framework's headline requirement, and the framework also supersedes earlier SAMA market risk circulars. The consequence is that legacy methodologies or prior circular-based positions cannot be defended; the standardised calculation is the baseline compliance obligation.
