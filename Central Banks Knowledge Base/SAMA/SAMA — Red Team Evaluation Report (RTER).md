# Red Team Evaluation Report (RTER)

Specifies the Red Team Evaluation Report produced by the Red Teaming Provider at the end of the exercise: scope and out-of-scope items, control framework references, execution methodology and kill chain/TTPs used, timeline, tools, risk rating method, recommendations, conclusions and evidence appendices, classified Confidential and shared only via secure channels to the White, Blue and Green Teams. It follows the reporting step after test execution and the White Team Leader's decisions on detection, continuation or stopping the test. It is the primary input to the Remediation Plan.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
