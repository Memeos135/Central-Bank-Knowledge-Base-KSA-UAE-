# PCI DSS Standard

Concept node for the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard as adopted within the SAMA Cyber Security Framework's compliance requirements, where Member Organizations are expected to comply with mandatory international industry standards including PCI-DSS and EMV. It links the cyber security regime to card payment and payment-systems controls (including mada and SARIE references elsewhere in the framework). Binds Member Organizations handling card data and payment systems.
