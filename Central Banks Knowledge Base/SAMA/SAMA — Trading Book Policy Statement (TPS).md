# Trading Book Policy Statement (TPS)

Node covering the Trading Book Policy Statement requirement within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk: the bank-level documented policy that sets out how instruments are assigned to the trading book, how trading desks are defined and approved, and how internal risk transfers between the banking and trading books are documented and treated for capital purposes. It binds SAMA-supervised banks applying the market risk capital framework, and its adequacy is subject to SAMA review/approval. Relevant when assessing whether a hedge or desk structure will be recognised for regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
