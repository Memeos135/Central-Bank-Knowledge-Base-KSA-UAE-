# Financial Statements and Prudential Data Reporting

A SAMA circular (1446H) imposing periodic reporting obligations on digital brokerage companies licensed by SAMA: quarterly financial statements with the auditor's report within 20 working days of March, June and September quarter-ends, and audited annual financial statements within 45 working days of calendar year-end. It also sets deadlines and format requirements for quarterly and annual prudential data returns on SAMA's approved templates, submitted in Excel with a signed data certification form (Chief Accountant, CFO and CEO/Managing Director), with annual prudential templates first reviewed by the external auditor. Effective from the second quarter of 2025.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
