# Principle 1: Board Member Qualifications

Principle 1 of SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, addressing the qualifications required of board members of supervised financial institutions. It operates alongside the definitional chapter's independence tests (shareholding of 5% or more, family ties, recent employment or dealings, remuneration thresholds, competing business, more than nine years' tenure, credit exposure above one million riyals). Relevant to fit-and-proper assessment of directors in banks, finance companies, money exchangers, payment/fintech firms and credit bureaus (insurance excluded).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
