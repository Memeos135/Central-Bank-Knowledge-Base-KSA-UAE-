# Conflict of Interest

This node covers the conflict-of-interest strand of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics for Financial Institutions, read together with the adjacent provisions on gifts and hospitality, compliance with laws and internal policies, reporting actual or potential violations, and remuneration and incentives. It states minimum conduct rules rather than detailed procedures, leaving each institution's board to adopt a conforming policy and managers to issue more detailed departmental instructions where needed. It binds all persons associated with SAMA-supervised financial institutions, with non-compliance carrying consequences addressed in the document's final section.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection
