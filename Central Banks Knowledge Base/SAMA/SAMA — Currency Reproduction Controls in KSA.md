# Currency Reproduction Controls in KSA

A SAMA circular (Treasury and Issue Department) transmitting updated controls on the reproduction, photographing, printing or design of Saudi and foreign currency in circulation in the Kingdom, issued under the Penal Law for Counterfeiting and Imitation of Currency (Royal Decree M/12) and the Anti-Forgery Law. It sets a licensing requirement for reproducing currency images in printed or electronic media, plus conditions such as size limits relative to the original note, mandatory 'specimen' marking, no distortion of the King's image, prohibition of use as plaques/objects, and destruction of all materials and digital files used. It binds any natural or legal person reproducing currency in KSA and supersedes earlier circulars on the subject.

**Regimes:** other
