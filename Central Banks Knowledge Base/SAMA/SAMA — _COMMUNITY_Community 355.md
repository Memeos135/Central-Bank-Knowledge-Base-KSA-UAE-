# Wage Protection Platform

## Why this community

Wage protection: obligations on banks and payment entities in relation to salary disbursement monitoring and the designated wage-protection platform used to verify employer compliance.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster implementing a labour-sector policy through the banking channel; SAMA instructions operationalise Ministry of Human Resources requirements for supervised institutions.
- Creates a data-provision and channel-integrity obligation for account-servicing institutions rather than a customer-facing conduct duty.
- Touches transaction-monitoring and data-sharing considerations, so should be read alongside AML/CTF and customer data confidentiality constraints.
