# Supervisory Risk Weights (RWc)

This node concerns supervisory risk weights used in the CVA capital calculations alongside the SA-CVA delta/vega risk-class structure and the CVA multiplier that SAMA may raise where model risk warrants. The surrounding material also covers IMM model validation, independent review, board and senior management involvement in counterparty credit risk control, and integration of the exposure model into day-to-day risk management. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating CVA and counterparty credit risk capital, particularly those using internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
