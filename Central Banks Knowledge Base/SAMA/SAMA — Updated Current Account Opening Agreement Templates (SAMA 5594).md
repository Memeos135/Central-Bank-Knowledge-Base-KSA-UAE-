# Updated Current Account Opening Agreement Templates (SAMA 5594)

A SAMA circular updating the standard-form current account opening agreement for individuals and legal persons issued under the earlier standard-form circular. It adds a clause under the parties' rights and obligations permitting, for activation of and subscription to additional services in the instant payments system, confidential sharing of specified account information — customer name, account number, mobile number, ID/commercial registration/unified number and email — with Saudi Payments as national system operator, and stresses that banks must always use the versions published on SAMA's website. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom, within the implementation timeframe set in the referenced circular.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, data/credit info
