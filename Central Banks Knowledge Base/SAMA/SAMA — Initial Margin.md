# Initial Margin

Concept node covering the initial margin obligation for non-centrally cleared derivatives under SAMA's adoption of the BCBS-IOSCO margin framework. It addresses how the required amount is determined (approved quantitative portfolio model versus the standardised margin schedule, including the net standardised formula using NGR), the prohibition on switching between methods to obtain more favourable terms, timing of collection at trade inception and on subsequent exposure changes, and the default bar on re-use of collected collateral. It binds SAMA-supervised banks and other covered entities transacting bilateral derivatives.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
