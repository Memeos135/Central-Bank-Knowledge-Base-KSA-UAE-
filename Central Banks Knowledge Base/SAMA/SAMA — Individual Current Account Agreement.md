# Individual Current Account Agreement

This is the standard-form current account opening agreement for natural persons issued under SAMA's model-forms circular, together with the update adding a clause to the parties' rights and obligations section permitting confidential sharing of specified account data (customer name, account number, mobile number, ID/CR/unified number, email) with Saudi Payments as national operator for activation of additional Instant Payments System services. It sets the mandatory contractual template, the customer data-capture fields (identity, national address, income, expected account activity, purpose of account, PEP and PEP-relative declarations, disability and marketing-consent options) and the general terms and definitions of the two parties. It binds banks and banking institutions operating in the Kingdom, which must use the version published on SAMA's website and implement the update within the circular's timeframe.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, AML/CTF, data/credit info
