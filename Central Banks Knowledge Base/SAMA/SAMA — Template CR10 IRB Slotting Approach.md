# Template CR10: IRB Slotting Approach

A Pillar 3 template covering exposures risk-weighted under the supervisory slotting criteria within the IRB framework (specialised lending and equity-type treatments), disclosed separately from modelled F-IRB/A-IRB and standardised credit risk exposures. It forms part of the credit risk disclosure suite in SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. Binds banks whose SAMA-approved credit risk approach includes supervisory slotting.
