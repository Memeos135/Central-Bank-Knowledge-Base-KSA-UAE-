# Transactors

Defines "transactors" for standardised credit risk purposes: obligors on credit card/charge card facilities repaid in full at every scheduled repayment date over the previous 12 months, and overdraft obligors with no drawdown over that period. The classification is consequential because regulatory retail exposures to transactors attract a 45% risk weight instead of 75%. Applies to banks assigning risk weights to retail exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
