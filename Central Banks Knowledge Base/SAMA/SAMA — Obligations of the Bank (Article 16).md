# Obligations of the Bank (Article 16)

Article 16 fixes the Bank as wholly responsible and liable for all acts and omissions of its Agents, including unauthorised acts connected to agent banking, and requires a clear, informed and documented decision to use Agents plus effective oversight. Related contractual requirements cover dispute resolution and indemnities (customer–agent, agent–bank, and agent non-compliance), termination grounds, risk mitigation measures, AML/CFT and KYC compliance with reporting to SAMA, and the Bank's ownership and confidentiality of all agent-collected customer data, surviving contract termination. Record retention of at least 10 years applies to transaction records held by the Agent and passed to the Bank.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF, consumer protection, data/credit info
