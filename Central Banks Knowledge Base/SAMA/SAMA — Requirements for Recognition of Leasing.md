# Requirements for Recognition of Leasing

Covers the conditions under which leasing exposures are recognised and treated within the credit risk capital framework, including the supervisory slotting criteria for income-producing real estate where leasing activity, pre-leasing and stabilisation are grading factors. Related material addresses collateral haircuts, master netting agreements and VaR-based treatment of securities financing transactions. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks holding lease exposures or specialised lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
