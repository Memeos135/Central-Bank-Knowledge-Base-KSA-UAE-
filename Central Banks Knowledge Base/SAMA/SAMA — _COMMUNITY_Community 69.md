# Account Agreements & Disclosure

## Why this community

Contractual and disclosure obligations at the bank–customer interface for current accounts: what must be disclosed at onboarding, what the standard account terms must contain, and the boundaries of customer confidentiality, including lawful disclosure to government bodies and AML/STR reporting.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: SAMA circulars (5536 licensing disclosure, 5594 updated templates, 5708 government-body access) sit above the standardised Individual and Legal Entity current account agreement templates that banks must adopt.
- Shared obligation chain: the account agreements incorporate the licensing/disclosure instructions, so template terms operate as the delivery mechanism for mandated pre-contractual disclosure.
- Confidentiality is the pivot: the banking secrecy/disclosure principles, the AML/STR principles and the government-information instructions define the permitted carve-outs from the confidentiality duty embedded in the account contract.
- The Code of Conduct sits as the conduct-level overlay, cross-referring to both the AML/STR and confidentiality principles as staff-level duties.
