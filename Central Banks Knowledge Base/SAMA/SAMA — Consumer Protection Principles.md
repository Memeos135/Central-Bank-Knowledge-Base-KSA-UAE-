# Consumer Protection Principles

The principles section of SAMA's consumer protection framework, articulating the general obligations financial institutions owe consumers: equitable and fair treatment (with special attention to low-income, less educated, elderly and special-needs consumers), disclosure and transparency on pricing, risks and termination, and consumer education and awareness. It is the interpretive backbone for the detailed conduct rules on documentation, fee changes and application notifications. Applies to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA in their consumer dealings.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
