# Template CC1 - Composition of Regulatory Capital

Pillar 3 disclosure template setting out the line-by-line composition of a bank's regulatory capital (CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2), including regulatory adjustments and deductions cross-referenced to the SAMA capital rules (SACAP). It is a fixed-format quantitative disclosure that must tie back, via the three-step approach and Template CC2, to the published balance sheet. Binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA that are subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
