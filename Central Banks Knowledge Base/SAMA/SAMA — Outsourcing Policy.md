# Outsourcing Policy

This node defines the written outsourcing policy that a finance company's board must approve and update annually, and the minimum content it must cover — board and senior management responsibilities, which functions may be outsourced, provider eligibility and due diligence, ongoing control and monitoring, conflict-of-interest criteria, information confidentiality, and verification of provider compliance including with the Finance Consumer Protection Principles. It also carries transitional duties to submit the approved policy to SAMA and to protect and retrieve or destroy consumer data on contract termination. It binds licensed finance companies and their boards.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, consumer protection
