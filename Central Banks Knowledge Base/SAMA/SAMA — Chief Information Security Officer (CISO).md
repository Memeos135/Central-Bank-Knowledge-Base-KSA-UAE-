# Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

This node covers the Chief Information Security Officer and the information security function: a function independent from information technology, headed by the CISO and reporting directly to the CEO/managing director or the general manager of a control function. Its mandate includes supporting security policies, defining roles and responsibilities, providing security and information risk management frameworks, identifying emerging issues and shifts in risk appetite, and assisting management in designing controls. It binds Member Organizations and is relevant to segregation-of-duties and reporting-line questions, with related definitions for cyber security awareness programmes, controls and examinations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
