# Exposures to Central Counterparties

A concept node within SAMA's large exposures rules covering how banks treat exposures to central counterparties. Clearing-related exposures to qualifying CCPs (QCCPs, defined consistently with the risk-based capital framework) are exempt from the large exposure limits but remain subject to quarterly regulatory reporting, while exposures to non-QCCPs must be aggregated (clearing plus other exposures) and respect the general 25% eligible capital limit; the connected-counterparty grouping concept does not apply to clearing-related CCP exposures. Binds banks licensed by SAMA and their boards, which retain oversight of large exposures and concentration risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
