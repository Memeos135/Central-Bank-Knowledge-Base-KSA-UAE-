# Logistics Zone Entities

## Why this community

Treatment of entities licensed or established within Saudi integrated logistics zones for purposes of financial-sector dealings, including how their special legal status affects onboarding and the application of standard requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a standalone SAMA circular setting supervised entities' expectations when dealing with special-zone entities.
- Connects outward rather than internally — relevant to customer/KYB identification, legal-form verification and beneficial-ownership evidencing under the AML/CTF and account-opening regimes.
- Scope limit matters: the special zone status affects the entity's incorporation and regulatory framework, not the supervised institution's own CDD and record-keeping obligations.
