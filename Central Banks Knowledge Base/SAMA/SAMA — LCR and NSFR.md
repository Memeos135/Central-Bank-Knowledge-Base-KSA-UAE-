# LCR and NSFR

A component of SAMA's ILAAP Guidelines (Version 2.0, October 2020) addressing how banks must set, disclose and evidence liquidity risk limits and funding strategy by reference to the regulatory liquidity metrics — Liquidity Coverage Ratio, Net Stable Funding Ratio, Loan to Deposit Ratio and the SAMA Liquidity Ratio. It requires banks to distinguish regulatory minimum requirements from internally set treasury limits, to show limit positions under normal and stressed conditions, to reconcile limits to SAMA returns, and to present a board-approved three-year funding strategy. It also sets out how liquidity risk quantification must be performed (pricing, collateral, intraday/overnight, cross-entity and cross-currency, funding diversification) and compared against LCR/NSFR outcomes. It binds banks supervised by SAMA that are required to prepare an ILAAP.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
