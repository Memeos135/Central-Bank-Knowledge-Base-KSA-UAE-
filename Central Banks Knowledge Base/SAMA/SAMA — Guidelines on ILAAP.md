# Guidelines on ILAAP

SAMA's updated Guidelines on the Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Plan (ILAAP), superseding the 1438AH circular and effective from the 2021 ILAAP submission. They define the ILAAP as the bank's processes for identifying, measuring, managing and monitoring liquidity under SAMA liquidity risk regulations, and set governance expectations placing ultimate responsibility on the Board, with approval of the governance framework, risk appetite alignment and annual Board assessment of liquidity adequacy. They bind banks, which remain responsible for ensuring their ILAAP is sound, effective and proportionate to their nature, scale and complexity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
