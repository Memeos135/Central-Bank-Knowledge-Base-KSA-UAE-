# IRRBB Risk Management Policies

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure of interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB): the bank's risk management objectives, governance, measurement assumptions and modelling choices for banking book rate exposures.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster providing the assumption set and methodology narrative needed to interpret the IRRBB quantitative template.
- Depends on IRRBB definitions (economic value of equity, net interest income, prescribed interest rate shock scenarios) set in the underlying IRRBB standard rather than in the disclosure table itself.
- Disclosure obligation is triggered by the bank's scope of application under SAMA's adopted Pillar 3 framework.
