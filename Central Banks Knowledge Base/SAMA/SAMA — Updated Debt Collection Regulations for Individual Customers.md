# Updated Debt Collection Regulations for Individual Customers

SAMA circular transmitting the updated Debt Collection Regulations (controls and procedures) for individual customers, replacing/supplementing the earlier collection rules. It defines key terms (customer, guarantor, third party, default, involuntary vs. voluntary change in customer circumstances, total disability, documented contact, complaint) and sets minimum standards for contacting customers and guarantors, rescheduling instalments for distressed borrowers, write-off/exemption on death or total disability, limits on phone contact, and deduction of due instalments from customer accounts (including joint/solidary borrowers per their agreed liability share). It binds banks and finance companies supervised by SAMA and any third party carrying out collection on their behalf.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
