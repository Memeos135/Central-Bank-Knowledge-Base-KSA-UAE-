# Recognition of Leasing

A node on how leasing activity features in SAMA's credit risk capital rules, principally in the supervisory slotting criteria for specialised lending (income-producing real estate), where pre-leasing, leasing performance against projections and stabilisation drive the strong/good/satisfactory/weak grading. It also sits alongside the collateral and netting provisions used to compute exposure at default. Relevant to banks using the IRB/supervisory slotting approaches for real estate and project-type exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
