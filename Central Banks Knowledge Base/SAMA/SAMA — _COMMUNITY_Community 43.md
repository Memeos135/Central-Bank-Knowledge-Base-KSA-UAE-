# Consumer Finance & Credit Cards

## Why this community

Retail credit conduct: how consumer finance (including credit cards) must be priced, disclosed, serviced and collected from individual customers, with fraud-control duties attached to card issuance.

## How members connect

- Shared subject population — the individual/consumer borrower or cardholder — so disclosure, APR/cost-of-borrowing and term-change rules in the finance rules clarification carry across to card products.
- Obligation chain: card issuance and operation rules expressly reference the debt collection regime, so post-default conduct (contact limits, treatment of arrears, permitted collection practices) is imported into the card lifecycle rather than sitting separately.
- Hierarchy is visible: base rules (credit cards, debt collection) sit under SAMA circulars that clarify specific articles and APR calculation, and the circular is the operative interpretation for compliance decisions.
- Cross-regime link: the counter-fraud guide attaches fraud prevention, detection and customer-liability expectations to card operations, bridging consumer protection and operational-risk obligations.
- Two members are duplicates of the debt collection and credit card rules — treat as the same instrument to avoid double-counting obligations.
