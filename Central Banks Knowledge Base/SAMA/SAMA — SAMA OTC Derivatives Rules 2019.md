# SAMA OTC Derivatives Rules 2019

Sets the additional conditions in the New Banking Products and Services Regulation for launching new financial derivatives and structured products: demonstrating suitability and prior investment experience of the intended customer, disclosing profit and loss scenarios, showing a bona fide economic purpose (not speculation, leverage or regulatory arbitrage), holding the organisational capacity to manage the risks, avoiding adverse effects on SAIBOR/SAR, observing the separate circular on SAR-foreign currency derivatives, and complying with SAMA's 2019 Rules on Trade Repository Reporting and Risk Mitigation for OTC Derivatives. It also states the documentation gate (complete Annexure 1 checklist or the application is not processed) and the 1 March 2024 effective date superseding the 2017 guidelines.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
