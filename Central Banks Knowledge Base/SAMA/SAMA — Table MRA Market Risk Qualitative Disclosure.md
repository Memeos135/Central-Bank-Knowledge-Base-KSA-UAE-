# Table MRA: Market Risk Qualitative Disclosure

Table MRA is the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on market risk, requiring banks to explain their market risk strategy, governance and risk management processes, including the structure and organisation of the market risk function, the scope and nature of risk reporting and measurement systems, and the definition and policies for allocating positions between the trading book and banking book. In this framework all banks, D-SIB and non-D-SIB, compute market risk capital under the standardised approach, and internal risk transfers between books are recognised only where the stated conditions are met. It binds banks subject to SAMA's market risk and Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
