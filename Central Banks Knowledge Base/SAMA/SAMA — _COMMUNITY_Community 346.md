# POS Sales KYC

## Why this community

Identity verification duties attaching to point-of-sale transactions - where merchant acceptance intersects with customer identification expectations.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the requirement stands alone but functionally bridges the payments/acceptance regime and the AML/CTF CDD regime.
- Key interpretive issue is whose 'Customer' is being verified - the cardholder at the terminal or the merchant onboarded by the acquirer - as the defined terms differ across regimes.
