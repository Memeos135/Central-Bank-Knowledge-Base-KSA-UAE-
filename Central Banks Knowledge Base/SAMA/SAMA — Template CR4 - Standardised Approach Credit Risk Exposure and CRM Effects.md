# Template CR4 - Standardised Approach Credit Risk Exposure and CRM Effects

A fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, showing credit risk exposures before and after credit conversion factors and credit risk mitigation, together with resulting RWA and RWA density, by asset class under the standardised approach. It applies the standardised credit risk rules (including simple or comprehensive CRM with supervisory haircuts, failed trades and non-DvP transactions) and excludes counterparty credit risk, CVA and banking book securitisation. It binds SAMA-supervised banks disclosing at the regulatory consolidation level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
