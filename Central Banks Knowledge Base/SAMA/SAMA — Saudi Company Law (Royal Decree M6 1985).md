# Saudi Company Law (Royal Decree M/6 1985)

This node captures the Saudi Company Law (Royal Decree M/6 of 1985), cited in SAMA's operational risk guidance for Articles 66–82 on the joint liability of board members to compensate the company, shareholders or third parties for damages. It appears in the context of lender liability and directors' and officers' exposure, both inside and outside the Kingdom. It binds board members of Saudi joint stock companies, including banks, as a source of civil liability rather than a SAMA-issued rule.
