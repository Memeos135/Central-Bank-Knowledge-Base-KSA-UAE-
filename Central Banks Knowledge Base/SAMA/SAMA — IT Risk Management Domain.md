# IT Risk Management Domain

Domain 3.2 of the IT Governance Framework sets requirements for a defined, approved and communicated IT risk management process aligned to the Member Organization's enterprise risk management, covering risk identification and analysis, treatment, reporting, monitoring and profiling. It requires a centralized IT risk register, inherent and residual risk analysis, key risk indicators, treatment within board/ITSC-approved risk appetite, and coverage of processes, applications, infrastructure, people and third-party relationships. It binds SAMA Member Organizations, with accountability at senior management and ITSC level.
