# IT Governance and Leadership

The structural and governance-leadership portion of SAMA's IT Governance Framework, explaining how the framework is organised into domains and subdomains, each with a principle and mandated Control Requirements, and covering IT governance and leadership topics such as IT strategy, enterprise architecture, IT policies and procedures, and roles and responsibilities. It also sets the compliance mechanics: periodic self-assessment by Member Organizations, SAMA review and audit, a six-level IT governance maturity model, and a formal waiver route where controls cannot be met. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
