# Independent Credit Risk Control Unit

A node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk framework dealing with the internal credit risk control function that banks must maintain as a condition of using internal ratings-based approaches, sitting alongside rules on recognition of credit risk mitigants, guarantees and off-balance-sheet conversion factors. It concerns independence of rating system oversight from business origination and the supervisory expectations attached to IRB eligibility. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating regulatory capital for credit risk, particularly those seeking or maintaining IRB permission.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
