# Confiscation Provisions

This node covers the AML Law's confiscation regime: mandatory court-ordered confiscation on conviction of laundered funds, proceeds (including intermingled proceeds up to value) and instrumentalities; confiscation regardless of whether funds are held by the defendant or a third party, subject to a bona fide purchaser defence; non-conviction-based confiscation where the perpetrator dies, absconds or cannot be identified; and value-based confiscation of substitute assets. Related articles address international cooperation — information exchange, mutual legal assistance in tracing, seizure and recovery, enforcement of foreign confiscation judgments, and extradition. It binds courts, prosecutors and competent authorities, and affects third parties holding potentially tainted funds.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other
