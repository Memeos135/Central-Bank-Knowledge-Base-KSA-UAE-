# Investment Risk Management and Monitoring

Risk management and monitoring provisions of the Banks Investment Rules, requiring banks to assess current and prospective investments against credit, market (including IRRBB), operational, liquidity and concentration risks and to report results to the responsible investment committee. It also requires the Investment Strategy to be proportionate to the bank's size and footprint, supported by operational guidelines integrated into the risk management framework, and mandates AML/CTF compliance before entering any investment. Binds SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF
