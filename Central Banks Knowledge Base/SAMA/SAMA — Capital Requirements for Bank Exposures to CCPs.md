# Capital Requirements for Bank Exposures to CCPs

Concept node covering the capital treatment and Pillar 3 disclosure of bank exposures to central counterparties, distinguishing qualifying CCPs (QCCPs) from non-qualifying CCPs. It links the capital requirement rules for trade exposures, initial margin and default fund contributions with the mandatory CCR8 disclosure template and the wider capital adequacy/disclosure architecture (including capital distribution constraint disclosures). Applies to SAMA-supervised banks acting as clearing members or clients of CCPs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
