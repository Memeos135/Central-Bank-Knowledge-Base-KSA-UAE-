# Circular: Signing All Contract Pages

A SAMA circular requiring finance companies to obtain the signature of natural-person customers on every page of contracts and agreements to which they are a party, grounded in the Finance Companies Control Law and the finance customer protection principles on disclosure, transparency and customer awareness. Application to legal persons is left to the finance company's discretion, but the company bears the risk arising from unsigned pages in corporate contracts. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
