# Internal Controls for Liquidity Risk

Node on senior management's internal control and audit duties for liquidity risk in finance companies. It requires management to manage liquidity in an integrated manner with processes and controls limiting and monitoring material liquidity risk sources, to understand how credit, market and operational risk affect the liquidity strategy and position, and to implement adequate internal controls and audit mechanisms safeguarding the integrity of the liquidity risk management process. It sits within the Board-led governance structure that must define roles and responsibilities across senior management and functional/business units.
