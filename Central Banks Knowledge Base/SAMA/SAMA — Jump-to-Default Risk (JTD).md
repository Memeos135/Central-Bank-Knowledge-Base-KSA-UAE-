# Jump-to-Default Risk (JTD)

Defines jump-to-default (JTD) risk as the core concept behind the default risk capital (DRC) requirement in SAMA's market risk framework, covering gross JTD computation, offsetting of long and short exposures to the same obligor, allocation of net JTD to buckets, and the limited hedge benefit recognition. It applies to SAMA-supervised banks holding instruments subject to default risk across non-securitisation, securitisation (non-CTP) and correlation trading portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
