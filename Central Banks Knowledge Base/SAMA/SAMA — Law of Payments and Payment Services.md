# Law of Payments and Payment Services

The Law of Payments and Payment Services (Royal Decree M/16) and its Implementing Regulation are the statutory basis making SAMA the competent authority for regulating, supervising and overseeing payment systems, payment service providers and their operators in the Kingdom. It underpins licensing of payment institutions and e-money institutions, classification of payment systems, and SAMA's power to issue supervisory frameworks and standards. It binds any person operating a payment system or providing payment services in Saudi Arabia.
