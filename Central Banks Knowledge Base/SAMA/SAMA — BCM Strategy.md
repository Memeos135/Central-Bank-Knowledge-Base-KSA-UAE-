# BCM Strategy

Node covering the BCM strategy requirement in SAMA's Business Continuity Management Framework, sitting alongside the BCM governance provisions (board accountability, BCM committee and charter, BCM function and manager). It requires each Member Organization to define, approve, implement and maintain a business continuity strategy setting long-term objectives, a dated roadmap and periodic validation of alignment with business objectives. Binds all SAMA member organizations, with ultimate responsibility on the board or a delegated executive.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
