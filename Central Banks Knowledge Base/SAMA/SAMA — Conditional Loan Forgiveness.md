# Conditional Loan Forgiveness

Describes conditional loan forgiveness as a restructuring tool used where the net present value of the borrower (including collateral and potential cash flow) is below the total loan, conditioned on owners making an additional financial contribution so their interests align with the bank's. It appears alongside related measures such as loan-to-asset swaps, interest rate reduction, maturity extension, partial repayment and balloon/bullet payments. Relevant to banks' Workout Units deciding write-off/concession terms for marginal borrowers.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
