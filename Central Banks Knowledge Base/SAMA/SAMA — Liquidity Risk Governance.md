# Liquidity Risk Governance

Sets the liquidity risk governance expectations for finance companies: the Board holds ultimate responsibility for liquidity risk, must set liquidity risk tolerance, approve the liquidity strategy, policies, contingency funding plan and stress-testing framework at least annually, and be kept informed of the liquidity position and any material change. Senior management must implement these into documented procedures and controls, understand interaction with credit, market and operational risk, and report cash-flow mismatches, maturity gaps, funding concentration and early warning indicators to the Board and SAMA, typically quarterly; establishing an ALCO is recommended.
