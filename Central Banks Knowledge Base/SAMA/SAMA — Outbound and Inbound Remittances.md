# Outbound and Inbound Remittances

This node covers the treatment of outbound and inbound remittances by remittance centers: originator information must be complete and traceable in wire transfers and batch files; where it is missing, the center must seek it from the correspondent institution or transfer service provider, reject and return the transfer if there is no response, report suspicion to SAFIU, and document and retain decisions for ten years. It also requires specified originating/correspondent institution and country details on inbound transfers, notification to the beneficiary of changes to originator information, enhanced due diligence for politically exposed persons, rejection of remittances to prohibited countries, and refusal of remittances to or from charitable or non-profit bodies unless authorised under the bank account opening rules. Related general provisions address SAMA circular compliance, disclosure of prices, commissions and fees, and complaint-handling mechanisms.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS, consumer protection
