# Supervisory Notifications

Node imposing an immediate notification duty on Member Organisations to inform SAMA's General Department of Cyber Risk Control of new fraud typologies (whether or not a loss occurred) and significant fraud incidents, including significant external frauds and significant internal fraud or serious integrity misconduct by employees. Its purpose is sector-level containment so other customers and institutions are protected. It sits alongside the framework's Management Information and senior-management reporting expectations, and is a supervisory reporting obligation separate from AML suspicious-transaction reporting to the FIU.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF
