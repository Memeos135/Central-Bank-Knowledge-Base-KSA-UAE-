# Royal Decree No. M/142

Royal Decree No. M/142 (19/10/1441H) enacts the amendment approved by Council of Ministers Resolution No. 637, replacing Article 12 of the Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism with a provision limiting provisional release of accused persons to temporary release ordered by the Public Prosecution where the investigation is not prejudiced and there is no flight or disappearance risk. It is addressed to ministers and heads of independent entities for implementation within their competences.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS
