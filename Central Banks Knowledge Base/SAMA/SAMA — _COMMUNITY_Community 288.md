# Banking Book Securitisation Exposures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of banking-book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as investor, including the associated capital requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster covering the investor role, distinct from originator/sponsor roles disclosed elsewhere in the SEC series.
- Obligation depends on correct role classification (investor vs originator/sponsor), a definitional determination made under the securitisation capital rules.
- Cross-reads to the trading-book securitisation template and to the overall RWA overview for consistency of reported amounts.
