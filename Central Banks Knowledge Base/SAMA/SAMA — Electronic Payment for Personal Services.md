# Electronic Payment for Personal Services

SAMA circular requiring banks and payment service providers to be ready to receive requests for electronic payment means from merchants in personal services activities, following the mandate obliging that sector to accept electronic payment from the stated date. Requirements include readiness to open bank accounts and e-wallets for such merchants, to provide SAMA-authorised payment means (POS devices or QR code) through branches, website and call centre, to apply the Merchant Category Codes designated for the sector, and to circulate the decision internally. It binds banks, PSPs and Saudi Payments.
