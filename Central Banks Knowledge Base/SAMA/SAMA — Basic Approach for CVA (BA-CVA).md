# Basic Approach for CVA (BA-CVA)

Covers the basic approach (BA-CVA) as the default method for calculating the CVA risk capital requirement, applicable to derivatives and fair-valued SFTs in both banking and trading book, excluding trades with qualified CCPs and other carved-out transactions. Explains that BA-CVA is mandatory unless SAMA grants approval to use SA-CVA, and that it has full and reduced variants. Binds banks with covered derivative/SFT exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
