# Professional Certifications Requirement

A SAMA circular reaffirming the mandatory professional certification requirement for staff of banks and their affiliated remittance centers, finance companies, and exchange centers, following earlier circulars on compulsory professional certifications and the updated target group for the Credit Advisor certification. It specifies the target populations: retail banking fundamentals certification for customer-facing bank staff (customer care, call centers, branch staff and managers), the Credit Advisor certification for bank and finance company staff directly involved in granting credit to individuals plus branch managers (excluding finance lease contract registration companies, the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company and support activity companies), and the exchange and remittance certification for front-line staff of bank remittance centers and exchange centers. Third-party contracted personnel are included; certification must be obtained within one year of joining, with a compliance rate of no less than the stated percentage.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, BNPL/finance
