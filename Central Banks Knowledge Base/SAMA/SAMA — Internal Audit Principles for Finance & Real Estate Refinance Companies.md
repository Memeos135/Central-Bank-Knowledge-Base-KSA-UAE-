# Internal Audit Principles for Finance & Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA's Internal Audit Principles setting out the definition, independence, mandate and reporting of the internal audit function, and the duties of the board, audit committee and executive management toward it. They apply to finance companies and real estate refinance companies licensed by SAMA, and serve as guidance for finance support companies and finance lease contract registration companies, which SAMA may require to apply all or part of the Principles. Best local and international internal audit standards may be followed only where not inconsistent with these Principles and SAMA instructions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

Practically, non-compliance with the internal audit Principles is actionable under the parent Law's supervisory and sanctioning framework, and their scope is limited to entities licensed under that Law.
