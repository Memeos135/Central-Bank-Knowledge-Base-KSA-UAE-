# Remote Customer Service Localization Requirements

SAMA circular reaffirming compliance with remote-work requirements for customer service roles, supplementing an earlier circular requiring remote workers to be recorded in the institution's official records and GOSI registers and their contracts documented on the designated portal. It attaches the MHRSD ministerial decision restricting remote customer service occupations to Saudi nationals (direct or outsourced), with its procedural guide and penalty regime. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA, effective from the circular's date.
