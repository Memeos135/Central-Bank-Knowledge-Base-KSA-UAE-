# SAMA Circular: Model Real Estate Financing Contracts (Murabaha & Ijarah)

A SAMA circular updating the mandatory model (standard-form) contracts for individual real estate financing under Murabaha and Ijarah, attached to the circular. It requires all real estate financiers to adopt the updated model forms within the stated deadline and prohibits concluding or amending contracts that depart from them; the annexed Ijarah template covers the contract summary, rental periods and payment schedule, variable-rate calculation, late payment, early repayment/early ownership, default and termination, withdrawal, and transfer of title. It binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom dealing with individual (retail) customers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
