# SAMA Circular on Statutory Reserve for Money Exchange Centers

A SAMA circular creating a statutory reserve requirement for money exchange centres, obliging them to appropriate a minimum percentage of annual net profit (after covering accumulated losses) until the reserve reaches a set proportion of paid-up capital, at which point appropriation may stop. Use of the statutory reserve requires SAMA's prior written no-objection. The circular expressly does not affect the separate cash reserve obligation under Article 7 of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, and applies to money exchange centres operating in the Kingdom with effect from its date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other
