# Compliance with (inter)national industry standards

Sub-domain 3.2.3 of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, obliging Member Organizations to comply with mandatory national and international industry standards, naming PCI-DSS, the EMV technical standard and the SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework (March 2017 version). It is the hinge between the Framework and card/payment-scheme and messaging-network rulebooks. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations, with obvious relevance to card issuers, acquirers and institutions on SWIFT.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
