# Electronic Banking Services

Framework sub-domain requiring a defined, approved, implemented and monitored cyber security standard for electronic banking services, to protect the confidentiality and integrity of customer information and transactions. Control expectations include multi-factor authentication at registration, sign-on, beneficiary and payee changes, high-risk transactions and password reset; channel separation for beneficiary and MFA activation; access revocation after failed attempts; restriction of mobile number changes to branch or ATM; service availability; notification of scheduled downtime to SAMA and customers; contractual allocation of roles and liabilities with customers; and SAMA approval in specified cases. Binds Member Organizations offering online and mobile banking to customers.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection
