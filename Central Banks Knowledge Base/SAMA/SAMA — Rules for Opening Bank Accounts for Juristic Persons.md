# Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons

This node is the structural heading for account opening by juristic persons under the Rules Governing Bank Accounts, spanning resident entities (licensed businesses, companies, money changers, embassies, multilateral organisations, liquidation and restructuring accounts), non-resident entities (GCC and non-GCC companies, banks, funds, insurers, payment card companies), foreign investors outside the Foreign Investment Law, and government entities. It sets the documentary and authorisation conditions, including AML-driven restrictions on foreign investors from jurisdictions not applying FATF Recommendations or subject to Security Council measures. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments
