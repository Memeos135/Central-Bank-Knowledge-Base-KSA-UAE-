# Commercial Register Law

## Why this community

Commercial registration and trade-name requirements as a gateway condition for lawful business identity in KSA, relevant to onboarding/KYB and licensing checks by supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the application of the Commercial Register and Trade Names Law, acting as the source of legal-entity identity evidence relied on elsewhere (e.g. customer/merchant due diligence).
- Links outward rather than inward — it supplies the registration status and trade-name concepts that licensing and KYB obligations presuppose.
