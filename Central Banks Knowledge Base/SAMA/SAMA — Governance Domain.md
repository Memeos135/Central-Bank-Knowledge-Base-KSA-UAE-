# Governance Domain

This is the Governance domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, the first of four domains (Governance, Prevent, Detect, Respond) each expressed as a Principle plus numbered Control Requirements. It places ultimate accountability for the Counter-Fraud Programme, Strategy, Policy and culture on the Board and Executive Leadership, and mandates a Counter-Fraud Governance Structure including a dedicated Counter-Fraud Governance Committee chaired by an Executive Committee member with defined minimum membership (Head of Counter-Fraud, CRO, COO, Head of Digital). It binds Member Organisations supervised by SAMA within the Framework's stated scope.
