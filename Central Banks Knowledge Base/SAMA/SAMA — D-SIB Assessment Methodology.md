# D-SIB Assessment Methodology

The assessment methodology component of the D-SIB framework: an indicator-based measurement approach using size, interconnectedness, substitutability and complexity, calibrated to the externalities a bank's failure would impose on the Saudi domestic economy and any cross-border effects. Outputs a systemic-importance score used by SAMA to designate D-SIBs and set their Higher Loss Absorbency requirement. Applies to banks assessed by SAMA's Banking Supervision Department.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
