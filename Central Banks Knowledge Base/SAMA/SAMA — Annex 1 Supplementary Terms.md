# Annex 1 Supplementary Terms

Annex I supplementary terms and the related Confirmation/undertaking mechanics of the SAMA securities sale and purchase master agreement. Annex I is where the parties elect transaction-specific and counterparty-specific variables (including whether Automatic Early Termination applies and the notice/contact details used for Exercise Notices), while the linked provisions describe Confirmation content (Second Purchase Date, Pricing Rate, settlement accounts) and the unilateral Seller and Buyer undertakings. It binds the two contracting counterparties and is the place to check elections before applying the general terms.
