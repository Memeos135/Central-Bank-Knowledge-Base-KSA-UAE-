# Investment Round Notification

## Why this community

Prudential/supervisory notification duty triggered when a supervised entity raises capital or undertakes an investment round, so SAMA can assess ownership, control and funding changes before they take effect.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the obligation stands alone as a notification/no-objection step rather than a substantive licensing rule.
- Legally it sits upstream of change-of-control and shareholding approval requirements — a notification failure is a supervisory breach independent of the merits of the round.
- Read with governance and licensing regimes for the entity type (fintech, PSP, finance company) to identify who signs and when the clock starts.
