# Grocery Electronic Payments

## Why this community

Acceptance and processing of electronic payments in grocery/retail settings, touching merchant acceptance obligations and the payments regime's treatment of merchants and payment service providers.

## How members connect

- Isolated node: legal significance comes from the merchant's position in the acceptance chain (PSP-merchant contract, merchant-as-customer/KYB duties) rather than intra-cluster edges.
- Cross-regime relevance: payments/PSP licensing and conduct rules on one side, AML customer identification of the merchant on the other.
- No internal references recorded; use as an entry point to broader payments and merchant-acquiring obligations.
