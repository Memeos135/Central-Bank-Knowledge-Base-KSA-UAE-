# Customer Onboarding Security

## Why this community

Identity verification and authentication controls for remote customer onboarding and ongoing access to digital financial channels, sitting at the intersection of AML/CTF customer identification, cybersecurity/fraud controls and personal data protection.

## How members connect

- Minimum Verification Controls is the hub obligation, referencing channel-specific overlays for e-wallets and lending applications and specifying authentication strength (MFA, OTP).
- Remote onboarding via biometric verification is tied to the national digital identity mechanism (Nafath), linking KYC adequacy to a specified trusted identity source.
- Processing of biometric and identity attributes triggers personal data protection duties, so verification design choices carry a parallel data-protection consequence.
- Registration/onboarding controls chain into OTP requirements, forming an onboarding-then-authentication obligation sequence.
