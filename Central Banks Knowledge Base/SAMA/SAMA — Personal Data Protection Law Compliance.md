# Personal Data Protection Law Compliance

A SAMA circular directing supervised financial institutions to align with the Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19) and with the data governance policies, controls and rules issued by SDAIA/NDMO, noting that the compliance scope of some of these instruments extends to SAMA-supervised entities. It requires institutions to review and amend internal policies and procedures within the statutory compliance window, perform a regulatory gap analysis against the Law and the SDAIA rules, and submit a remediation timeline to the board for approval, with correspondence routed to SAMA's designated compliance mailbox.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk
