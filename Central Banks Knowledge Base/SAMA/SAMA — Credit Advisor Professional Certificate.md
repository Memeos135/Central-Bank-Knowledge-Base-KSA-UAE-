# Credit Advisor Professional Certificate

A SAMA circular (1444) reaffirming the continuing obligation on supervised entities to meet mandatory professional certification requirements, here specifying the Credit Advisor professional certificate. It binds banks and finance companies in respect of staff directly involved in granting credit to individuals and branch managers, including persons engaged through third parties, while excluding finance lease contract registration companies, the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company and ancillary-services companies. The certificate must be obtained within one year of joining, with a compliance rate of not less than the stated threshold for covered staff.
