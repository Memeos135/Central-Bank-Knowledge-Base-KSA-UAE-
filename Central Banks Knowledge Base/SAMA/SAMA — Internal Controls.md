# Internal Controls

Concluding sections of SAMA's Rules on Liquidity Risk Management dealing with CFP testing and updating (at least annually, integrated with stress testing), internal controls over the liquidity risk management process, and the internal audit review role. It also records the implementation date of the Rules (in force 1 January 2023) and the obligation to align liquidity risk management and regulatory reporting systems. Binds finance companies supervised by SAMA.
