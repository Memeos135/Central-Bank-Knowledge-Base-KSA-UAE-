# Credit Information Data Sharing

## Why this community

Sharing of credit information between entities, covering permissible disclosure, consumer consent and the limits on use of credit data under the credit information regime.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a circular-level instrument that operationalises the credit information law and its implementing regulation.
- Interacts with consumer protection (consent, accuracy, correction rights) and with CDD/KYC use of credit bureau data, where reliance limits must be observed.
