# SAMA Circular: Updating Supervisory Data Templates for Exchange Centers (RMS)

SAMA guidance accompanying updated supervisory data templates and returns for exchange centres, submitted through the Central Bank's regulatory reporting (RMS) portal. The content is largely procedural: use of the template shared by the Central Bank, uploading the completed return file, validation of errors, submission for regulator approval, and resubmission requests after final submission. It binds exchange institutions and companies subject to periodic supervisory reporting (e.g. monthly returns covering sales and purchases and currency export/import).

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk
