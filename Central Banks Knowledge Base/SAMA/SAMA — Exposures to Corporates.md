# Exposures to Corporates

This node is the corporate exposure class definition in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (standardized approach), covering loans, bonds and receivables to incorporated entities, associations, partnerships, proprietorships, trusts and funds not falling in another exposure class. It absorbs securities firms and other financial institutions, and insurance/other financial corporates that are not bank exposures, while excluding exposures to individuals, and splits into general corporate and specialized lending subcategories. It binds banks classifying exposures for credit risk RWA purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
