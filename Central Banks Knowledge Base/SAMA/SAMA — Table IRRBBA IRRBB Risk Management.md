# Table IRRBBA: IRRBB Risk Management

A qualitative (with some quantitative) Pillar 3 table requiring banks to describe their interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB) definition, management and mitigation strategies, measurement frequency and metrics, shock and stress scenarios, and any divergence between internal measurement system assumptions and those prescribed for Template IRRBB1. Mandatory annually, flexible format, for banks within the Basel IRRBB scope of application. It binds banks and frames the narrative context for the quantitative IRRBB1 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
