# IT Governance and Leadership Domain

This is Domain 3.1 of SAMA's IT Governance Framework, covering IT governance structure and leadership: board accountability, the IT Steering Committee, the CIO role, IT strategy, enterprise architecture, IT policy and procedures, and roles and responsibilities. Each subdomain sets a Principle plus numbered Control Requirements that Member Organizations are expected to implement. It binds the boards and senior management of all SAMA-regulated Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
