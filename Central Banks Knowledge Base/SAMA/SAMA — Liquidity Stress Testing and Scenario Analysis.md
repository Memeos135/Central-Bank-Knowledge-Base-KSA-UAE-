# Liquidity Stress Testing and Scenario Analysis

Node on the liquidity stress testing and scenario analysis obligation for finance companies. It requires a comprehensive stress testing programme covering multiple scenarios of varying severity and time horizons, run regularly for company-specific and market-wide stresses individually and in combination, supplemented by sensitivity analysis, with documented assumptions on cash flow behaviour of assets, liabilities and off-balance sheet items; results are used to confirm exposures remain within the Board-set liquidity risk tolerance. Related material covers collateral management, exclusion of pledged assets from the liquid asset stock, and SAMA's power to impose specific liquidity limits.
