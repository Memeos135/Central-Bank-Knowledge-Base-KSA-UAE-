# Intelligence Lifecycle

Concept node for the intelligence lifecycle that structures Domain 1 (Core CTI Principles): planning, collection, processing, analysis and dissemination of threat intelligence, plus continuous improvement. It underpins principles on defining CTI roles and responsibilities, collecting from diverse intelligence sources (OSINT, TECHINT, SOCMINT, HUMINT, deep/dark web), maintaining standard operating procedures, and processing/classifying and securely storing intelligence. Applies to Member Organizations, including where the CTI function is outsourced.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
