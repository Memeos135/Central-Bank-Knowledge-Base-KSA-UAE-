# ISO 22301

A reference concept identifying the international standards on which SAMA's BCM Framework requirements are based, notably ISO 22301 and ISO 27001 alongside BCI and DRII good-practice guidelines. It signals that the SAMA requirements are aligned to, but not substituted by, these standards; SAMA remains owner and sole interpreter of the framework. Compliance obligations fall on Member Organizations, which must adopt and embed the requirements in their BCM programs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
