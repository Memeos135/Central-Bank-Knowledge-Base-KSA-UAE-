# Hedging Sets

Concept node covering the definition and construction of hedging sets used in the standardised approach for counterparty credit risk, where effective notional amounts are converted into asset-class add-ons using supervisory factors. It sets out one hedging set per currency for interest rate derivatives, per currency pair for FX, single sets for credit and equity, and four commodity categories, with separate sets (and a halved supervisory factor) for single-currency basis transactions and separate sets for volatility transactions. The surrounding material also touches Pillar 3 disclosure of encumbered and unencumbered assets; both bind banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
