# Procedural Requirements for Mortgage Documentation (SAMA 5709)

A SAMA circular (issued under the SAMA Law, the Banking Control Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law) imposing uniform procedural requirements for documenting and registering real estate mortgages before notaries, together with Ministry of Justice-aligned registration forms and an incident-recording form. It sets conditions such as attendance of the mortgagor and a duly authorised representative of the financier, the financier holding a valid real estate finance licence, ownership/eligibility of the mortgaged property, Shariah committee product approval evidence, and completion of transfer and mortgage in a single procedure. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, which must also report to SAMA cases where notaries refuse to act.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
