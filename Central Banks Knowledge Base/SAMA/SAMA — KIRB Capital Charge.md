# KIRB Capital Charge

This node concerns KIRB, the IRB capital requirement that would apply to the underlying pool had it not been securitized, and its use as a maximum capital charge (cap) for securitization exposures under SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA and SEC-SA, including mixed-pool aggregation and worked examples incorporating dilution and default components. It also notes interaction with the 15% risk weight floor. It binds originator, sponsor and investor banks calculating securitization capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
