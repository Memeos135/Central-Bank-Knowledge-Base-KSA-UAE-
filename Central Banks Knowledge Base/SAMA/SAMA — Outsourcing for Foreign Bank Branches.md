# Outsourcing for Foreign Bank Branches

Section of SAMA's outsourcing rules dealing specifically with foreign bank branches, for both Material and non-Material outsourcing. It requires KSA business to be booked in the Saudi branch absent SAMA agreement, sufficient local staffing for local control, and prohibits outsourcing of key management decision-making, compliance and AML/CFT functions; other functions may be outsourced proportionately. Outsourcing to head office or a related party does not reduce the branch's or its management's legal and regulatory obligations in Saudi Arabia, and such arrangements must be audited annually with findings — and any other regulator's control reports — shared with SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF
