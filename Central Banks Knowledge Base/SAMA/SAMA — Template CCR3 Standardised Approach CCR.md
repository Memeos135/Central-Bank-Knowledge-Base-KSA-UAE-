# Template CCR3: Standardised Approach CCR

This node sits within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework and covers the standardised-approach counterparty credit risk disclosure template (CCR3), which presents CCR exposures broken down by regulatory portfolio and risk weight. The accompanying definitions explain the standardised approach per risk type (credit, market, operational), the split between RWA from SAMA-approved modelled approaches and RWA from standardised approaches, and the residual/total rows used for comparison purposes. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime, as a periodic public disclosure obligation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
