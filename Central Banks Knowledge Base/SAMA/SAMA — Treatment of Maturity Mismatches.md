# Treatment of Maturity Mismatches

A capital-adequacy node dealing with how maturity differences between an exposure and its hedge or credit protection are treated, both in the default risk charge (DRC) under the standardised market risk approach and in credit risk mitigation. It sets recognition conditions (original maturity of at least one year, residual maturity of at least three months for credit protection), the pro-rata adjustment formula for partially recognised protection, the conservative definition of effective maturity for exposure and hedge, and the three-month floor on maturity weighting for short-dated positions. It binds SAMA-licensed banks computing regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
