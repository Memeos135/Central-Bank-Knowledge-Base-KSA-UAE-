# Mortgage and Land Registration

## Why this community

Registration of real-estate mortgages by financiers, covering alignment of the registered mortgage with the actual contractual terms and the treatment of properties subject to the white land fee regime.

## How members connect

- Both are SAMA circulars addressed to lenders taking real-estate security, so they sit at the same instrument level and operate as supervisory overlays on the mortgage registration process.
- Shared subject matter: the accuracy and permissibility of what is entered in the mortgage register for a given property and financing contract.
- Cross-regime link to real-estate finance/consumer protection obligations, since registration defects affect enforceability of the security against the borrower's property.
- Only a conceptual (non-citation) linkage in the graph: neither circular amends the other, so each must be read on its own terms.
