# Whole Loan Approach

Node for the "whole loan approach" — the method of assigning a single LTV-based risk weight to the entire real estate exposure amount (as used in the commercial real estate tables) rather than splitting the loan. The supplied context for this node is largely document contents listing and IRB roll-out provisions rather than the whole loan approach text itself, so verify against the real estate chapter before relying on it. Concerns banks calculating credit risk RWAs on property-secured exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
