# Table CCA - Main Features of Capital Instruments

Pillar 3 table requiring disclosure of the main features of each regulatory capital instrument (and, where relevant, TLAC-eligible instrument) issued by the bank, such as issuer, governing law, regulatory treatment, maturity, call and loss-absorption terms. It supports market assessment of the quality and terms of instruments counted toward CET1, AT1 and Tier 2. Mandatory for banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
