# Internal Audit Policy

Refers to the seventh principle of SAMA's Internal Audit Principles: the internal audit policy, a formal document prepared by the head of internal audit and approved by the board, whose required content is set by that principle. It is the constitutive charter-type document establishing the department's mandate, authority and scope. Applies to SAMA-licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
