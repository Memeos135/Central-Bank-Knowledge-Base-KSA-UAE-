# SSA Equity Risk

Covers the equity risk component of the simplified standardised approach for market risk, sitting alongside the RWA/minimum capital reporting explanations (Pillar 1 capital as RWA × 8%, and where equity positions, simple risk weight approach and internal models method amounts are reported). It also includes the internal risk transfer rules that determine regulatory capital recognition between trading desks. Binds SAMA-licensed banks computing and disclosing market and credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
