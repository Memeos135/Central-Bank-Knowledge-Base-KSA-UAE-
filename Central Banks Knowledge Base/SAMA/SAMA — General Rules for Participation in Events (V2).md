# General Rules for Participation in Events (V2)

Second edition (2025) of SAMA's event rules, renamed General Rules for Participation in Events, updating and replacing the first edition. It broadens the defined term "Participation in an Event" to cover full or partial hosting, sponsorship, and presence via a "temporary booth/stand" for institutional promotion and marketing, and exempts such temporary-stand presence from the SAMA no-objection requirement; it applies to participation in any event for any purpose, inside or outside the Kingdom, directly or through a third party. Retains board-approved policy, file documentation and annual board reporting, conduct duties of representatives, and risk assessment/due diligence obligations. Binds all SAMA-licensed and supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
