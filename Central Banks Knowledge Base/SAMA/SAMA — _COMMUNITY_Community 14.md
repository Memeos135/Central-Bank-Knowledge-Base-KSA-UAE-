# Aggregation & Business Continuity

## Why this community

Two intersecting regimes: licensing and contracting requirements for aggregation (finance support) entities, and the operational resilience obligations - business continuity, disaster recovery, crisis management and cyber resilience - that licensed entities must satisfy, including sandbox-stage participants.

## How members connect

- The aggregation instructions define the Aggregation Entity and Platform and mandate the two contractual relationships (with the consumer and with the financier), while citing the licensing rules for finance support activities as the authorising base.
- The same instructions pull the Business Continuity Management Framework into the aggregation perimeter, so a resilience failure is a licensing-condition failure, not merely an operational lapse.
- Within the BCM framework, governance, strategy, business impact analysis, the continuity plan, IT disaster recovery, crisis management and testing form a single obligation chain, benchmarked against ISO 22301.
- Cyber resilience requirements and the Cyber Security Framework cross-reference BCM, and the resilience domain links back to the continuity plan; the sandbox framework and its FAQ determine how these duties apply to pre-licence testing.
