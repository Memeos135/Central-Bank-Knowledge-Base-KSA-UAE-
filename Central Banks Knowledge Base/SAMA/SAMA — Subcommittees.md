# Subcommittees

Sub-node covering the subcommittees (standing or temporary) formed under the Terms of Reference for Finance Company Committees, issued under Article 98 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. It concerns their formation, member nomination and removal, meetings and quorum, agendas, minutes and reporting, and responsibilities, including the limited lifespan of temporary committees formed for a specific task. Applies to finance companies participating in the sector committee structure overseen by SAMA.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
