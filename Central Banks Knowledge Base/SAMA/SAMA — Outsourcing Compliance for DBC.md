# Outsourcing Compliance for DBC

Covers the supervisory and compliance perimeter for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Companies: duties to provide SAMA with information, documents and access to premises and digital data, the prohibition on concealment, SAMA's inspection powers and its ability to appoint a third party at the company's expense. It also requires internal controls and records evidencing compliance, confidentiality limits on third-party disclosure, and sits alongside licensing provisions (five-year term, renewal, revocation grounds, fees) and the requirement for written policies including outsourcing. Binds licensed DBC companies and their staff.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
