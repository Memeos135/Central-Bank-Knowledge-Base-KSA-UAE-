# Exposure Restructuring (Forbearance/Renegotiation)

Concept node distinguishing the two permitted forms of restructuring of a credit exposure by a finance company: forbearance (a concession granted because the borrower is in financial difficulty) and renegotiation/refinancing/rescheduling (a borrower-convenience change with no financial deterioration and no reduction in present value). Forborne exposures are identified at individual exposure level and may only be reported in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B, with specified events (partial write-off, collateral repossession pending disposal, further forbearance) that cannot restore performing status. It binds finance companies in classification, ECL model inputs and regulatory reporting of restructured exposures.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
