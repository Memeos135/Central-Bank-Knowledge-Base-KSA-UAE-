# SOCPA

Defines SOCPA (Saudi Authority of Auditors and Accountants) in the Repo Guidelines and links it to the requirement that repo market participants apply the accounting standards for repo transactions endorsed by SOCPA. It appears within the risk management requirements, which also mandate documented policies covering governance, authorisation, credit, counterparty, market, legal and operational risks. It binds banks and other repo market participants.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other
