# Backtesting Traffic Light Zones

Sets out SAMA's traffic-light interpretation of backtesting exceptions for bank-wide and trading desk-level internal models: green generally attracts no add-on, amber triggers a higher capital requirement scaled to the number of exceptions, and red leads automatically to an increased multiplication factor or possible withdrawal of model permission. It also covers documenting and explaining exceptions, quarterly rescoping of the backtested portfolio, and the narrow conditions for disregarding an exception driven by a non-modellable risk factor. Binds banks using internal models for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
