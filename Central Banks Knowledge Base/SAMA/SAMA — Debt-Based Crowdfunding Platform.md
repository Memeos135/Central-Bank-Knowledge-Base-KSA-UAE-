# Debt-Based Crowdfunding Platform

Defines the Debt-Based Crowdfunding Platform as the web-based or digital channel (websites, mobile apps) operated by a licensed DBC company to conduct the crowdfunding activity, and anchors the related defined terms (Participant, Eligible Participant, Institutional Beneficiary). It is the medium through which disclosure, risk acknowledgements (pop-up consent) and pledges to Participants and Institutional Beneficiaries must be delivered before use. Relevant to determining when a digital interface falls within the licensed activity and triggers platform-level disclosure duties.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
