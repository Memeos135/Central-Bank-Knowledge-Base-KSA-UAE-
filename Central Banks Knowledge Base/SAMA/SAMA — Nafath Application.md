# Nafath Application

Nafath is the national digital identity authentication application referenced in this SAMA circular as the channel whose user verification mechanism has been upgraded to biometric authentication. SAMA requires supervised institutions to use this biometric feature, at a high or higher verification level, when establishing a customer relationship remotely, and to complete connectivity with the approved service provider by the stated deadline. Relevant to banks, finance companies and payment companies performing remote individual customer identity verification.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
