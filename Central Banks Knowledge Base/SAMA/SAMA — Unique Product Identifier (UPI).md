# Unique Product Identifier (UPI)

A reporting data-field concept within the OTC derivatives requirements covering product and trade identification: product identifier type (ISIN or Unique Product Identifier under ISO 4914), CFI classification, the internally generated unique trade identifier, and the Unique Transaction Identifier. It defines who generates each identifier (generally the reporting counterparty, but potentially a CCP, clearing member or electronic confirmation platform in cross-border or cleared trades) and how identifiers must be structured and communicated in time to meet reporting deadlines. Relevant to reporting banks completing the SAMA Trade Repository templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other
