# Advanced IRB Approach (A-IRB)

This node covers the advanced internal ratings-based approach, where banks estimate PD, LGD and EAD and calculate maturity themselves subject to minimum standards, while still using the prescribed risk-weight functions. It identifies exposures for which A-IRB is not available — including large general corporates above a consolidated revenue threshold and bank/financial institution exposures — and equities for which IRB is not permitted, and it frames the phased migration from foundation to advanced. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using or applying for IRB permission.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

The advanced approach is one of the two recognition routes the general IRB provisions expressly contemplate, the other being the foundation route using supervisory loss given default values. The consequence is that permission to use own estimates does not displace the general IRB conditions, and any mitigation benefit claimed must still satisfy the framework-level requirements.
