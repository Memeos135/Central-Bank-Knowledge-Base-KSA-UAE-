# Sensitivity Analysis

A definitional node within SAMA's stress testing Rules describing sensitivity analysis as a stress testing technique: measuring the change in portfolio value from shocks of varying degrees to a single risk factor or a small set of closely related factors, without evaluating the relationships between risk factors, with capital typically the dependent variable. It sits alongside scenario analysis, financial models and reverse stress testing among the methodologies banks may combine, chosen according to the nature, size, complexity and risk profile of the bank.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
