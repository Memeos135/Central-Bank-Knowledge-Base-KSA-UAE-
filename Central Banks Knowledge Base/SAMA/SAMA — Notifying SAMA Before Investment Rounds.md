# Notifying SAMA Before Investment Rounds

A SAMA circular requiring non-bank financial institutions to notify SAMA in advance of any investment round, within the minimum business-day notice period stated, and to submit all related information — including timetable, purpose, value, impact on ownership structure and financial position, and the type and structure of the investment instrument (equity, convertibles, debt and similar) — plus any supporting or further documents SAMA requests. The notification duty is additional to and does not displace existing obligations, notably obtaining SAMA's prior no-objection where required. It binds finance companies, payment service providers, exchange centres and SAMA-licensed finance support activity firms, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance
