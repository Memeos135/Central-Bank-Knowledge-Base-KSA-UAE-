# Hajj and Umrah Entity Account Rules

Part of SAMA's Rules for Bank Accounts dealing with accounts of Hajj and Umrah organizers and related entities (including Muslim World League-type bodies), covering opening prerequisites, SAMA pre-approval via the bank's head-office compliance department, dual control, IBAN notification to the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, service limitations, and deposit/withdrawal controls on main and sub-accounts. It also imposes timelines (same-day escalation of documents, maximum two working days to open) and a single-bank rule limiting the organizer to accounts at one bank absent Ministry and SAMA approval. It binds licensed banks and constrains Hajj/Umrah organizers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection
