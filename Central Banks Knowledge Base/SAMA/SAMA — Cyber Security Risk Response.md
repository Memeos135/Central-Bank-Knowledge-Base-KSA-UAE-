# Cyber Security Risk Response

Sets out how Member Organizations must respond to identified and analysed cyber risks: documenting treatment options of accepting, avoiding, transferring/sharing or mitigating through controls. Risk acceptance requires predefined limits, business owner sign-off, reporting to the cyber security committee, and must not conflict with SAMA regulations; mitigation requires evaluation, costing, feasibility and compliance review before control selection. Binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
