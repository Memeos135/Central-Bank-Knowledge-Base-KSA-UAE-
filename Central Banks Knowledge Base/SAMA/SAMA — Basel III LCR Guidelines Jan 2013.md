# Basel III LCR Guidelines Jan 2013

The overarching SAMA circular and guidance notes implementing the Basel Committee's January 2013 Liquidity Coverage Ratio standard in Saudi Arabia, including general and specific guidance, the prudential return, FAQs and national discretions. It sets the HQLA definition and Level 1/Level 2/Level 2B composition caps, the phased minimum LCR path to 100%, and cross-references each requirement to the corresponding Basel paragraph; SAMA also continues NSFR monitoring under a separate 2012 circular. Binds Saudi banks completing the amended LCR prudential return.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
