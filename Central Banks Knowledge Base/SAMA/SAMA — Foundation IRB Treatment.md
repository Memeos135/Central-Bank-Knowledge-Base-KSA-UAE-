# Foundation IRB Treatment

This node covers the foundation variant of the internal ratings-based approach within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk, where a bank uses its own probability of default estimates but supervisory values for other risk components such as loss given default and exposure at default. It sits alongside the rules on asset class definitions, adoption of IRB per asset class, phased roll-out and the implementation plan that must be agreed with SAMA. It binds banks that have obtained SAMA approval to use IRB for regulatory capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
