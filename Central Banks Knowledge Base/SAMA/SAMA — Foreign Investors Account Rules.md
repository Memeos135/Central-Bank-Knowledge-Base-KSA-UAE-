# Foreign Investors Account Rules

Section 400 of SAMA's bank account opening rules, governing accounts for resident and non-resident foreign investors not covered by the Foreign Investment Law, including accounts for linking investment deposits. It conditions acceptance on source and return of funds through the investor's account in a jurisdiction applying FATF standards, and bars applicants from jurisdictions that do not adequately apply the FATF Recommendations or that are subject to UN Security Council measures. It binds SAMA-licensed banks and directly engages AML/CTF and sanctions screening obligations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, banking prudential
