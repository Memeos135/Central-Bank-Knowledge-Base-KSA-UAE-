# Expected Credit Loss Provisioning

Definitional and governance core of SAMA's Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning for finance companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. It fixes IFRS 9-aligned terms (PD, EAD, LGD, financial asset, 12-month and lifetime expected credit loss, stage definitions) and imposes board-level duties to adopt, document, approve and at least annually review ECL methodologies, report quarterly on adequacy of provisions, and subject credit risk measurement to annual internal audit. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies; effective 1 July 2021, superseding the earlier Provisioning Guidelines circular.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
