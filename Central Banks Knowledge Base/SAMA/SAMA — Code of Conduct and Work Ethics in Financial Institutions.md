# Code of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions

SAMA principles on conduct and work ethics in financial institutions (August 2019), setting the minimum code-of-conduct standards that each institution's board must adopt in its own policy and cascade to all personnel. Topics include professional conduct, dealings with stakeholders, combating financial and administrative corruption (money laundering, terrorist financing and suspicious transactions; bribery), gifts and hospitality, conflicts of interest, confidentiality and disclosure, misuse of inside information and market manipulation, whistleblowing on actual or potential violations, remuneration and incentives, and consequences of non-compliance. Binds board and committee members, executives, employees (permanent and contracted), consultants and third-party staff of banks, finance and leasing-registration companies, insurers/reinsurers and credit bureaus supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection
