# Limit Setting

This node sets expectations for counterparty credit limits: banks should establish overall limits at individual counterparty level that aggregate different exposure types on a comparable basis, anchored in meaningful PFE measures and covering on- and off-balance-sheet activity. Actual and mark-to-market exposures must be monitored against initial limits, with clear procedures to reduce exposure as limits are approached; limits should be binding and not driven by customer demand. It applies to banks with HLI and other derivatives counterparty relationships.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
