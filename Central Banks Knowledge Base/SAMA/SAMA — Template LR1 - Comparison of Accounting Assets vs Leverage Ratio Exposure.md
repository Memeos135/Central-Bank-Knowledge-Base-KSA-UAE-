# Template LR1 - Comparison of Accounting Assets vs Leverage Ratio Exposure

A fixed-format Pillar 3 template reconciling total assets in published financial statements with the leverage ratio exposure measure, listing the prescribed adjustments (scope of regulatory consolidation, securitisation risk transfer, temporary central bank reserve exemption, fiduciary assets, regular-way purchases and others). It is mandatory for all banks on a quarterly basis, normally quarter-end, with more frequent averaging only subject to SAMA approval or requirement. Banks must state the calculation basis used and explain material differences.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
