# Specialized Lending Slotting

## Why this community

Classification and capital treatment of specialised lending — exposures whose repayment depends primarily on the income generated by a specific asset or project rather than the general capacity of a broader obligor. It sets the boundary between sub-classes and the supervisory slotting fallback where banks cannot estimate own risk components.

## How members connect

- A single defined asset class (specialised lending) is subdivided into named sub-classes — project, object, commodities, income-producing real estate and the higher-risk HVCRE category — each definition gating a different risk-weight outcome.
- The slotting approach operates as the conditional alternative within the IRB hierarchy: where PD/LGD estimation requirements are not met, supervisory categories map directly to risk weights and to expected loss amounts.
- HVCRE is carved out of, and treated more conservatively than, ordinary income-producing real estate, so the definitional test determines the capital consequence.
- Expected loss links this cluster to the wider IRB provisioning treatment rather than being a standalone concept.
