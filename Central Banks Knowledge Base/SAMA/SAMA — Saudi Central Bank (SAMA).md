# Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)

Concept node for the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), the statutory regulator established under the Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36), which licenses, supervises and issues binding rules, circulars and frameworks for banks, finance companies, payment service providers, electronic money institutions and other supervised financial institutions. Its powers also derive from sectoral laws such as the Finance Companies Control Law and the Law of Payments and Payment Services and their implementing regulations. SAMA additionally conducts inspections, sets supervisory reporting requirements, and takes enforcement action against non-compliant institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance, consumer protection, banking prudential, AML/CTF
