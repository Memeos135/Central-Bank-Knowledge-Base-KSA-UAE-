# Licensing Guidelines for Finance and Refinance Activities (SAMA 5765)

SAMA guidelines explaining how to apply for a licence to practise finance or refinance activities under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. They set out submission channel and format, the unmodifiable application form, required attachments (draft Articles of Association, organisational structure, fit and proper forms for founders, 5%+ owners of corporate founders and candidate board/committee members, feasibility study and business plan, draft third-party and related-party agreements), and an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of the minimum required capital. They bind applicants (founders/shareholders seeking SAMA approval), who are warned that false or omitted material information is a violation of the Law and may cause rejection or cancellation of the licence.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
