# AML/CTF Training

## Why this community

The obligation to provide AML/CTF training to staff, calibrated to role and risk exposure, as a component of the required AML programme rather than an optional control.

## How members connect

- Standalone node that operates as a programme element: inadequate training is typically assessed as a deficiency in the overall AML/CTF programme owned by the compliance function.
- Relevant to any function performing CDD or monitoring, including outsourced or agent-performed activity where the institution retains responsibility.
