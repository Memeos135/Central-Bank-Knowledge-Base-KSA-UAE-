# Fit and Proper Form

This node isolates the fit and proper documentation element of the consumer microfinance licence application: a SAMA Fit and Proper Form must be completed and signed by each founding member and by each candidate for board membership and board sub-committees, and approved by the person authorised to submit the application. Where a founding member is a legal entity, a form is also required for every natural or legal person holding 5% or more of that entity. It binds applicants, founders, significant indirect owners and proposed board/committee members of prospective finance companies.
