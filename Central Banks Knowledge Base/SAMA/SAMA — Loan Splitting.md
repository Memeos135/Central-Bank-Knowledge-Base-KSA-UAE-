# Loan Splitting

Explains loan splitting as a workout technique addressing collateral and cash-flow shortfalls: the sustainable portion is amortised in equal principal-and-interest instalments to a set maturity, while the residual 'excess loan' may be subordinated, tranched, made non-interest bearing, or repaid from asset sales or at maturity. It sits within the additional-measures and marginal-borrower workout guidance, together with restrictions on fresh money and roll-overs (permitted only in limited situations). Addressed to banks structuring restructurings for marginally viable borrowers.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
