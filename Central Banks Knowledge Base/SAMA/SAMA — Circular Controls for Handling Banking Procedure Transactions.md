# Circular: Controls for Handling Banking Procedure Transactions

A SAMA circular (Banking Policies Department) setting binding controls for how banks and exchange companies handle "banking procedure" transactions received from SAMA on behalf of authorities legally empowered to act on customer accounts — e.g. judicial enforcement, seizure/garnishment, lifting of holds, disclosure of account information and documents. It fixes maximum turnaround times by category (judicial, civil/general, security), requires an immediate nil reply where no relationship exists, and mandates dedicated, independent and adequately resourced units with full authority to execute, plus business-continuity planning and strict confidentiality on the data involved, with a limited carve-out for the customer or their special agent enquiring about execution on their own accounts. Banks and exchange companies bear responsibility toward the requesting authorities, SAMA and the customer for breach, and SAMA may shorten the deadlines and take enforcement action.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, data/credit info, other
