# Qualifying Central Counterparty (QCCP)

Concept node relating to counterparty credit risk scope within SAMA's CCR framework, covering the bilateral nature of CCR and the transaction categories that attract a CCR charge (OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, long settlement transactions and securities financing transactions). The qualifying central counterparty notion sits within this scope as the basis for preferential treatment of cleared exposures. Applies to banks supervised by SAMA when calculating credit RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
