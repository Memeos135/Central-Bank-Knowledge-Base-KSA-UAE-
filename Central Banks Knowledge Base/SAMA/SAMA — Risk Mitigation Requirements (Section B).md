# Risk Mitigation Requirements (Section B)

Section B of the OTC derivatives requirements, imposing specified risk mitigation obligations on banks that are contracting parties to non-centrally cleared OTC derivative transactions, applying regardless of outstanding notional amount or whether the trade is for hedging. Centrally cleared trades, whether directly or through indirect client-clearing arrangements, fall outside these obligations. Related reportable data points include confirmation timestamps, master agreement type and version, settlement and early termination dates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
