# Annex 2 Confirmation Form

Annex II sets out the standard form of Confirmation documenting an individual Transaction under the SAMA master agreement, restating the reciprocal Seller and Buyer unilateral undertakings and recording economic terms such as Second Purchase Date, Second Purchase Price, Pricing Rate and settlement accounts. A Confirmation supplements and forms part of the master agreement, is prima facie evidence of agreed terms absent prompt objection, and prevails over the master agreement in case of conflict for that Transaction only. It binds the Buyer and Seller to the specific trade.
