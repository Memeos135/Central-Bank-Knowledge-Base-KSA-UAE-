# Compliance & AML/CTF

This node sets the risk management and AML/CTF preconditions within SAMA's banking licensing criteria. Applicants must demonstrate adequate policies, people and systems for credit, market, liquidity, compliance, AML/CTF and operational risk, reporting arrangements consistent with SAMA prudential standards and the Banking Control Law, and compliance capability against the Rules Governing Anti-Money Laundering & Combating Terrorist Financing and SAMA's compliance manual for banks. Adequacy is assessed proportionately to the size, nature and complexity of the proposed operations; it binds banking licence applicants, including foreign bank branch applicants reporting to both SAMA and head office.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential
