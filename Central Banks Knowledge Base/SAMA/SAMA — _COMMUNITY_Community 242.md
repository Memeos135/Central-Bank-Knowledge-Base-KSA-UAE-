# Capital Adequacy Stress Tests

## Why this community

Forward-looking assessment of capital sufficiency: the requirement that a bank test its capital ratios against adverse but plausible scenarios rather than relying on point-in-time adequacy.

## How members connect

- Standalone node linking the capital adequacy regime to the supervisory review / ICAAP obligation chain.
- Obligation is continuous and internal-governance anchored: board and senior management must own scenario design, severity and the management actions assumed.
- Outputs feed supervisory dialogue and can drive capital planning, buffer usage and distribution decisions.
