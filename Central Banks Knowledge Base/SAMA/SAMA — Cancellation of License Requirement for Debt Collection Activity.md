# Cancellation of License Requirement for Debt Collection Activity

SAMA circular (ref. 472039028, dated 08/07/1447H) announcing the Central Bank's decision to abolish the licensing requirement for carrying out debt collection activity on behalf of finance entities, issued under the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law following approved amendments to the Implementing Regulation and to the licensing rules for finance support activities. It directs entities that already hold a SAMA licence or initial approval for this activity to comply with the applicable rules on updating their activity in the commercial register. Addressed to banks, finance companies operating in the Kingdom, and SAMA-licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
