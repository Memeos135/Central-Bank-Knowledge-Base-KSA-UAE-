# Multi-factor Authentication

Sets the SAMA Cyber Security Framework expectations for multi-factor authentication in electronic banking, requiring MFA at customer registration and across all electronic banking services, password protection of hard and soft tokens, revocation after three successive incorrect passwords or PINs, mobile number changes only via branch or ATM, and use of different delivery channels for requesting/activating MFA and for adding/activating beneficiaries. MFA is mandated for sign-on, adding or modifying beneficiaries, adding utility and government payment services, high-risk transactions above predefined limits, and password reset. Related identity and access management controls apply need-to-have/need-to-know access governance internally; the provisions bind Member Organizations offering online and mobile banking to customers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, consumer protection
