# Securitisation Risk Weights

## Why this community

Capital treatment of securitisation exposures under the external ratings-based approach, including the preferential risk weights available where a transaction meets the simple, transparent and comparable criteria.

## How members connect

- Standalone node dependent on defined terms: securitisation exposure, tranche seniority, external rating and STC eligibility.
- Eligibility is conditional - the concessional weights apply only where the qualifying criteria and due diligence obligations are satisfied, otherwise the general hierarchy of approaches applies.
- Cross-references the broader credit risk and external credit assessment institution recognition rules.
