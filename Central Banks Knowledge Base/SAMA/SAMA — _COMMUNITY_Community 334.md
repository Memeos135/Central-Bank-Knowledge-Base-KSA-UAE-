# Cyber Security Project Management

## Why this community

Embedding cyber security requirements into the project and change lifecycle, so that new systems, services and changes introduced by a supervised entity are risk-assessed and controlled before go-live.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a control domain within SAMA's cyber security framework rather than a standalone instrument.
- Its obligations attach to the board- and senior-management-level cyber governance duties, and interact with outsourcing, third-party and business continuity requirements where projects involve external providers.
- Compliance consequence is preventive: security requirements must be a gating condition in project approval, not a post-implementation remediation.
