# Template TLAC1 - TLAC Composition

Pillar 3 disclosure template specifications for total loss-absorbing capacity at resolution group level, covering the KM2 key-metrics template (quarterly, fixed format, five reporting periods) and the TLAC1 composition template that breaks down regulatory capital and eligible TLAC instruments, deductions, adjusted RWA, leverage exposure, resulting TLAC ratios and buffer requirements. It applies only to G-SIBs and is reproduced per resolution group under an MPE approach; SPE groups may align certain rows with Template CC1. Binding on banks designated as G-SIBs subject to SAMA's adoption of the Basel Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
