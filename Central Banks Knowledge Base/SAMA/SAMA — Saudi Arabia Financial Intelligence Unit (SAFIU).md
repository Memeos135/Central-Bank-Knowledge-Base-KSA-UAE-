# Saudi Arabia Financial Intelligence Unit (SAFIU)

Addresses the financial institution's suspicious transaction reporting duties to the Saudi Arabia Financial Intelligence Unit (SAFIU) under SAMA's AML/CTF rules, including the prohibition on reporting based on speculation, the grounds constituting reasonable suspicion, and the option to forgo CDD (with reasons reported) where applying it risks tipping off the customer. It requires use of SAFIU's approved reporting mechanism and form plus a detailed supplementary report and a technical report containing account statements, CDD documents and analysis. Context also includes wire transfer provisions on intermediary institutions, confidentiality of exchanged information and currency limits.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
