# Fall-Back Approach (FBA)

The Fall-Back Approach is the most conservative tier in the hierarchy for risk-weighting equity investments in funds, applied where neither the look-through nor the mandate-based approach can be used. It results in a punitive risk weight for the fund exposure and sits alongside rules on partial use of approaches, leverage adjustment and funds investing in other funds. It applies to banks computing credit RWA under SAMA's credit risk framework and feeds the related Pillar 3 RWA reporting rows.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
