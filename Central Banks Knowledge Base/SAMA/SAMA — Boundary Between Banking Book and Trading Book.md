# Boundary Between Banking Book and Trading Book

Node on the regulatory boundary between the banking book and the trading book: scope of the trading book, standards and supervisory expectations for assigning instruments to each book, documentation of instrument designation, restrictions on reclassifying instruments between books, and the capital consequences of internal risk transfers across the boundary. It determines which capital regime (credit/banking book versus market risk) applies to a position and binds SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
