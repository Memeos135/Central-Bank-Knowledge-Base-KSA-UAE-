# Solutions and Controls for Elevated Risk

Node for the additional, mandatory control requirements applying to products and services of elevated fraud risk: licensed EMIs, AISPs, and Member Organizations offering digital payment services allowing funds to be sent and received, credit cards, microfinance, BNPL services, or safeguarding customer funds. It requires real-time fraud detection and prevention operating continuously with resourced handling of alerts, rules customised to the entity's products and risks, real-time intervention such as account freezing and transaction blocking on suspected compromise, and intelligence-driven screening or blocking of high-risk accounts, locations, IPs, devices and VPN use. Entities outside the mandatory list must still take a documented risk-based approach to these requirements.

**Regimes:** payments, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
