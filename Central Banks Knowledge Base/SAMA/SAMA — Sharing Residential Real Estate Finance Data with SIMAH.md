# Sharing Residential Real Estate Finance Data with SIMAH

A SAMA circular directing all financing entities to share individuals' residential real estate finance data with the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH), in line with guidance and templates to be issued by SAMA. It is grounded in the SAMA Law, the Credit Information Law, the Banking Control Law and the Finance Companies Control Law, and is aimed at credit-sector data integration, transparency and credit-risk assessment. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom, real estate finance companies and credit information companies, and must be applied consistently with SAMA and other regulators' instructions and the Personal Data Protection Law; effective from its date.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, BNPL/finance, consumer protection
