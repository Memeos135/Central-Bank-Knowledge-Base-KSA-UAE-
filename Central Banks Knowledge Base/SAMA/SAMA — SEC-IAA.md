# SEC-IAA

Node on the Internal Assessment Approach (SEC-IAA), one of the hierarchy of methods for risk-weighting securitisation exposures, used for unrated exposures (e.g. liquidity facilities, credit enhancements) to pools within an ABCP programme. Use requires SAMA approval of the bank's IRB approach for non-securitisation exposures and consultation with SAMA on its application. Binds IRB-approved banks in Saudi Arabia calculating capital on ABCP-related securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
