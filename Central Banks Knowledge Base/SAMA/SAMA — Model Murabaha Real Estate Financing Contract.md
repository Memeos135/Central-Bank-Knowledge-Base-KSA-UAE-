# Model Murabaha Real Estate Financing Contract

This node covers SAMA's mandatory model contract for individual real estate financing on a Murabaha basis, issued in the same circular that updated both the Murabaha and Ijarah standard forms. It standardises disclosure and contractual terms for retail mortgage financing — the financing summary (amount, term cost, APR, instalments, administrative fees), definitions, payment terms, late payment consequences, early repayment, withdrawal rights, security arrangements, breach and termination, and notices. It binds banks and real estate finance companies in the Kingdom, which must adopt the attached form by the deadline set in the circular and may not issue or amend contracts departing from it.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
