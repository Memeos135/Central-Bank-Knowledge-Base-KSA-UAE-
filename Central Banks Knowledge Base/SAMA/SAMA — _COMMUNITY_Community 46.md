# New Products Compliance

## Why this community

SAMA's pre-launch control regime for new banking products and services: internal governance and policy approval, risk assessment, consumer protection screening, and the notification or non-objection required before a product may be offered.

## How members connect

- The New Banking Products and Services Regulation is the parent instrument; it cross-refers to board and senior management accountability, the product policy requirements, consumer protection screening, and the notification/non-objection gate.
- The policy requirements sit above the risk assessment and compliance obligations, and pull in the Cybersecurity Framework as an applicable control standard for new offerings.
- The notification/non-objection node is the enforcement trigger: it determines whether a product may proceed and carries forward into the derivatives-specific route, which in turn cites the OTC Derivatives Rules 2019.
- Cross-regime interaction: governance/risk approval obligations run in parallel with consumer protection checks and cyber-resilience expectations for the same product.
