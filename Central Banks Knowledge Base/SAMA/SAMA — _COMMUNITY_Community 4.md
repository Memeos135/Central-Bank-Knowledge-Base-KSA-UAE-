# SAMA Financial Regulations

## Why this community

The core SAMA legal architecture — central bank mandate, real estate finance and leasing, refinance companies and the secondary market — intersecting with AML/CTF, consumer protection, responsible lending and advertising rules applicable to financial institutions.

## How members connect

- Law-to-regulation chains: the Saudi Central Bank Law grants supervisory authority; the Real Estate Finance Law and Finance Lease Law each have implementing regulations, with further rules governing real estate refinance companies and Capital Market Law interaction for secondary-market securitisation.
- Shared defined terms — notably Financial Institution, Customer Due Diligence, Licensed Credit Bureau and Real Estate Finance Contract — bind the prudential, AML/CTF and consumer-facing instruments together.
- Conduct layer: Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules, complaints handling, data and privacy protection, outsourcing and the advertising rules (including compliance-department pre-review) apply across product lines.
- Credit-granting chain: Responsible Lending Principles require creditworthiness assessment and observance of the deductible ratio using credit bureau data; amendments (deletion of Article 4, amendment of Article 16) must be checked for the version in force.
- Prudential obligations for refinance companies — capital adequacy and liquidity, corporate governance, risk management and compliance — cross-reference the counter-fraud and AML regimes.
