# Profit Equalization Reserve and Investment Risk Reserve

Defines and situates two Shari'ah-banking reserve mechanisms under SAMA's Profit Sharing Investment Accounts Rules: the Profit Equalization Reserve (appropriated from mudarabah profit to smooth returns to the mudarib and unrestricted IAHs) and the Investment Risk Reserve (appropriated from IAH profit after the mudarib's share, to absorb future investment losses). It ties these reserves to board oversight of reserve levels and fairness between existing and new IAHs, and to senior management policies on reserve management and reversion on write-off or recovery. Binds local SAMA-licensed banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking that operate PSIAs.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection
