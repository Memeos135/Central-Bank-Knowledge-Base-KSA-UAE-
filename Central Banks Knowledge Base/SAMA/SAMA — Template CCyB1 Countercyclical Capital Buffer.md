# Template CCyB1: Countercyclical Capital Buffer

A Pillar 3 template setting out the geographical distribution of a bank's private sector credit exposures (and related RWA) used to compute the bank-specific countercyclical capital buffer, including the buffer rate applicable in each jurisdiction and the resulting buffer amount. It specifies that pre-announced but not-yet-applicable buffer rates are excluded and that the bank-specific rate is a weighted average calculated under the SAMA capital adequacy rules. Binds banks subject to the Pillar 3 macroprudential disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
