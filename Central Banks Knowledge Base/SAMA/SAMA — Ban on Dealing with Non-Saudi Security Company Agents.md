# Ban on Dealing with Non-Saudi Security Company Agents

An Arabic-language SAMA circular (issued by the Deputy Governor for Supervision) addressed to all banks operating in the Kingdom, prohibiting them from dealing or negotiating with non-Saudi representatives of private civil security-guarding and cash-transport companies. Banks must instead report the employing company to the competent regional police authority for legal action, and must fully comply with SAMA's prior instructions on the Private Civil Security Guards Law and the Law on Transport of Cash, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents and their implementing regulations. It is a conduct/counterparty restriction on banks in procuring guarding and cash-in-transit services, not a customer-facing rule.
