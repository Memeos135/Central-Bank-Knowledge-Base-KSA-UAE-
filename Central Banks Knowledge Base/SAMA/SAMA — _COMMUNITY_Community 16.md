# Large Exposures Framework

## Why this community

Prudential concentration-risk control for banks: hard limits on exposures to single counterparties and connected groups, plus the measurement inputs (exposure value, eligible capital, CRM, margin for non-cleared derivatives) that make those limits operative.

## How members connect

- A single hierarchy: SAMA's LEX Rules transpose the BCBS April 2014 large exposures framework and sit alongside the Basel risk-based capital framework, which supplies the eligible capital base denominator.
- Obligation chain: identify the group of connected counterparties, compute exposure value (including trading book positions and SA-CCR for derivatives), apply recognised credit risk mitigation, then test against maximum exposure limits and report.
- Carve-outs and special treatments (central counterparties, covered bonds) and tighter treatment for G-SIB/D-SIB counterparties qualify the general limit.
- The non-cleared derivatives margin rules (initial/variation margin, eligible collateral, re-hypothecation limits) reduce and reshape counterparty exposure and share the collateral-eligibility logic used in CRM; CR3 is the disclosure counterpart.
