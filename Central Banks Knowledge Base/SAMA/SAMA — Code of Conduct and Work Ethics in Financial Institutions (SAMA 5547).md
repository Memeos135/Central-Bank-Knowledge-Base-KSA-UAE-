# Code of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions (SAMA 5547)

SAMA circular (04/12/1440H) issuing the Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions (August 2019), with a three-month implementation period. The Principles set a minimum standard of behaviour covering professional conduct, dealing with stakeholders, combating financial and administrative corruption (including AML/CTF and bribery), gifts and hospitality, compliance with laws and internal policies, conflicts of interest, confidentiality and disclosure, misuse of inside information, whistleblowing on actual or potential violations, remuneration and incentives, and consequences of non-compliance. They bind all personnel of a Financial Institution — board and committee members, executives, permanent and contract staff, consultants and third-party staff — and the board must adopt a conforming code, with managers responsible for staff acknowledgement and awareness.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, other
