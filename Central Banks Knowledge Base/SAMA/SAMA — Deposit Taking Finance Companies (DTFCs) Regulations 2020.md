# Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020

SAMA's Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFC) Regulations 2020, setting the regulatory approach, corporate governance and prudential requirements for finance companies authorised to take savings and time deposits from non-individual customers and to lend from those deposits. It covers permitted deposit activities, depositor record-keeping and retention duties, liquidity and other prudential ratios, and detailed account-opening rules for general and term deposit accounts (linkage, naming, rollover, one deal per account). It binds all DTFCs in KSA, in addition to the Finance Companies Control Law and general finance company regulations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
