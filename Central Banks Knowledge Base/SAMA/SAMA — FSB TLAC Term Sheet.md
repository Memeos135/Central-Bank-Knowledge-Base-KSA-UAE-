# FSB TLAC Term Sheet

Disclosure content keyed to the FSB Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity (TLAC) Term Sheet, setting out how a resolution entity reports TLAC-eligible regulatory capital and non-capital instruments, deductions, adjusted RWA, leverage exposure, TLAC ratios and buffer requirements. It reflects Term Sheet conditions on subordination, funding-vehicle instruments, ex ante recapitalisation commitments and the 2.5%/3.5% caps, with separate reporting for single-point-of-entry and multiple-point-of-entry G-SIB resolution groups. Relevant to G-SIB resolution groups and to banks disclosing TLAC-related holdings under the SAMA disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
