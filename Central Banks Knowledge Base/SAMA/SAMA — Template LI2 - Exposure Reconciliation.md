# Template LI2 - Exposure Reconciliation

A mandatory Pillar 3 template setting out the main sources of difference between financial statement carrying values (on the regulatory consolidation basis) and the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes, covering off-balance sheet amounts, valuation and netting differences, provisions and prudential filters. It complements LI1 (which handles scope-of-consolidation differences) and is explained narratively in Table LIA. Applies annually to all banks within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
