# Freezing and Unfreezing Procedures

This node covers the freezing and unfreezing chapter of SAMA's Targeted Financial Sanctions (TFS) Rules, which set the minimum obligations financial institutions must meet when a match to a designated person or entity is identified. It governs the without-delay freezing of funds and other assets, the prohibition on making funds or assets available directly or indirectly to listed persons, and the procedural route for lifting a freeze (including de-listing and false-positive resolution) under the UN Security Council mechanisms adopted in the Kingdom. It binds all financial institutions regulated and supervised by SAMA, with board and senior management accountability expressly required.
