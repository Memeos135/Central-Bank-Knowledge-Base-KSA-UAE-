# Frequency and Timing of Disclosures

Addresses how often and when Pillar 3 disclosures must be made: quarterly, semiannual or annual depending on the template, as summarised in Annexure 2, with the Pillar 3 report published concurrently with the corresponding financial report. Read together with the disclosure principles, assurance requirements and the signposting conditions for fixed-format templates. Binds banks preparing Pillar 3 reports under SAMA's framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
