# Quality and Performance Analysis Unit

One of the three minimum units banks must set up inside the Customer Care Department, focused on quality assurance and performance analysis of complaint handling, including root cause analysis of recurring complaints and measuring effectiveness of remediation policies. The surrounding rules also cover the department head's SAMA written no-objection before appointment, settlement authority for complaints below a stated riyal threshold, service-level agreements and escalation mechanisms with other departments (reviewed at least every two years), capacity analysis of staffing, and minimum professional certification and annual training for staff. Binds banks licensed by SAMA in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
