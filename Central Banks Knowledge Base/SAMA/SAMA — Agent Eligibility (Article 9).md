# Agent Eligibility (Article 9)

Article 9 of SAMA's Agent Banking Rules sets the minimum eligibility gate a prospective Agent must clear before a Bank may contract with it: a commercial register permitting agent banking activity, any prior approval from its own supervisory/regulatory authority, and satisfaction of the Article 13 fit and proper criteria. The Bank must submit a CEO (or designated senior officer) declaration attesting to the assessment and the Agent's competence, and SAMA notifies the Bank of the outcome of the non-objection application. It binds licensed Banks, which retain overall responsibility for the agency relationship even where Agent employees perform the work.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF
