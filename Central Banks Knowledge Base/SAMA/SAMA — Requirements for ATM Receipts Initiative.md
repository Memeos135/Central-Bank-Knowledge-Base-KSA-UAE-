# Requirements for ATM Receipts Initiative

A SAMA/Saudi Payments requirements document (Nov 2021) for the ATM Receipts initiative, aimed at reducing reliance on paper receipts for the four most common ATM transactions by standardising ATM home-page and screen workflows across on-us and off-us ATMs. It states that paper receipts must still be available on cardholder request, and separates requirements into a cash-withdrawal stream and a non-cash stream (balance enquiry, mini statement, cash deposit). It binds mada Members (issuers and acquirers); it does not cover certification procedures or scheme terms and conditions.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
