# DRC for Securitisations (CTP)

Covers the default risk capital requirement for securitisation exposures in the correlation trading portfolio (CTP), and its relationship to the non-CTP securitisation treatment: gross JTD based on market value of the tranche, restricted offsetting rules, tranche-based risk weights derived from the banking book securitisation framework with the maturity component set to zero, and inclusion of non-securitisation hedges in the CTP calculation. Binds SAMA-supervised banks running correlation trading and securitisation positions in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
