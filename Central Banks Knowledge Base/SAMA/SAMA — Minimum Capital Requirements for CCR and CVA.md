# Minimum Capital Requirements for CCR and CVA

This node covers the CVA risk capital component of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Counterparty Credit Risk and Credit Valuation Adjustment framework, setting out the scope of covered transactions (derivatives other than those with a QCCP, plus fair-valued SFTs where CVA loss exposure is material), the definition of CVA risk, and the standalone CVA portfolio. It fixes the choice of calculation method — BA-CVA by default, SA-CVA only with SAMA approval — and the treatment of eligible versus ineligible internal and external CVA hedges relative to the market risk framework. It binds banks supervised by SAMA that hold covered transactions in either the banking or trading book, and includes a supervisory notification/justification duty where a bank deems SFT CVA exposures immaterial.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
