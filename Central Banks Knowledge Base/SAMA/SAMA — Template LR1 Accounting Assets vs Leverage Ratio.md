# Template LR1: Accounting Assets vs Leverage Ratio

Pillar 3 disclosure template node covering LR1, the reconciliation between a bank's published accounting total assets and the leverage ratio exposure measure. It sits within SAMA's adopted Basel disclosure framework, alongside key-metrics (KM1) and capital distribution constraint (CDC) templates, and specifies purpose, scope, content, frequency and format of the disclosure. Binding on banks supervised by SAMA that are subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
