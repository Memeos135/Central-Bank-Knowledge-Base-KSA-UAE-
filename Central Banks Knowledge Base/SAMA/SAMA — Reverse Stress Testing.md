# Reverse Stress Testing

This node covers the reverse stress testing requirement in SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing, together with the adjacent guidance on use of financial/statistical models. Banks must stress test their business plan to the point of failure — where loss of market confidence makes the business unviable — identify the adverse circumstances that would produce that outcome, assess their likelihood, and take remedial measures where the resulting failure risk is inconsistent with the bank's risk appetite or tolerance. It also reflects SAMA's supervisory review of stress testing programs through ICAAP discussions and on-site examinations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
