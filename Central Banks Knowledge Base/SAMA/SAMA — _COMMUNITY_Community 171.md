# IFRS Accounting Standards Adoption

## Why this community

Adoption of international accounting standards by supervised entities, including the sequencing of IFRS transition and the specific implementation of IFRS 9 for financial instruments and expected credit losses.

## How members connect

- Both members are SAMA circulars setting transition expectations, so they share the same addressees and a common supervisory-reporting purpose.
- The general timing circular frames the adoption schedule; the IFRS 9 circular is the standard-specific implementation step within that schedule.
- Together they drive downstream prudential consequences: provisioning, capital and regulatory reporting consistency.
