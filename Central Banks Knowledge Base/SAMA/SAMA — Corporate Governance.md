# Corporate Governance

Chapter III of the Real Estate Refinance Rules governs board composition and fitness, required committees (audit, risk and credit management, executive, nomination and remuneration), an assets and liabilities committee at senior management level, and the requirement to adopt internal governance regulations and SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for supervised financial institutions. Policies on refinance business take effect only after a SAMA no-objection letter. It places personal liability on the board, CEO/general manager, managing director and senior executives for the company's breaches of these Rules and the underlying laws.
