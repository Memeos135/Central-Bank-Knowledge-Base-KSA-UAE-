# SA-CCR Standardized Approach

A concept node on the standardised approaches used for credit and counterparty credit risk capital calculation, including the standardised approach for credit risk (simple or comprehensive CRM with supervisory haircuts) and the SA-CCR exposure measure feeding CCR RWA. It clarifies the boundary between modelled (IRB/IMM) and standardised RWA for Pillar 3 reporting rows. It binds banks computing and disclosing RWA under the SAMA capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
