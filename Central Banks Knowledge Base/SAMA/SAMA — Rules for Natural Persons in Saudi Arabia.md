# Rules for Natural Persons in Saudi Arabia

This node sets out the account-opening rules for natural persons resident in Saudi Arabia (Saudi citizens, minors, GCC nationals, expatriates and special categories) and for natural persons outside the Kingdom, including acceptable identity documents and operation by guardians or custodians. It also carries the general timing obligation to open accounts within one or two business days depending on whether internal approvals are needed, and to notify applicants in writing of missing requirements. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, consumer protection
