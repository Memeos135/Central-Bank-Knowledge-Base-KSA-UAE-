# Counterparty Credit & SFTs

## Why this community

Capital treatment of counterparty credit risk on derivatives and securities financing transactions, including exposure measurement, netting and collateral haircut requirements.

## How members connect

- CCR sits as a sub-regime of the credit risk capital rules, so the CCR/CVA requirements are read as an extension of and cross-reference to the credit risk framework.
- Exposure measurement chain: Effective EPE modelling, its stress calibration, and cross-product netting determine the exposure amount on derivatives and SFTs.
- SFTs are the shared defined term linking netting rules, minimum haircut floors and trading-book CCR treatment.
- Template CCR5 is the disclosure consequence of the SFT collateral rules, tying calculation obligations to reporting obligations.
