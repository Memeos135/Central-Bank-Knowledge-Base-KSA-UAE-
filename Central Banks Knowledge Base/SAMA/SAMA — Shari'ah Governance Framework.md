# Shari'ah Governance Framework

The Shari'ah Governance Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking (SAMA, February 2020), referenced as the companion instrument to be read alongside the Risk Management Framework. It underpins the role of the bank's Shari'ah Committee, the Shari'ah compliance and Shari'ah audit functions, and the requirement that products, contracts and activities comply at all times with approved Shari'ah rules and principles. Relevant here for the allocation of assurance roles over Shari'ah non-compliance risk and the treatment of reputational risk from Shari'ah non-compliance.
