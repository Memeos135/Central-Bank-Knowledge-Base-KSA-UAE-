# Personal Data Protection Law Amendment M/148

Royal Decree M/148 together with the accompanying Council of Ministers resolution enacting amendments to the Personal Data Protection Law (originally issued by Royal Decree M/19), revising defined terms such as destruction, disclosure, sensitive data and data subject, and amending provisions including data subject rights and cross-border transfer/adequacy assessment and fee arrangements. It also repeals a clause of the earlier decree and sets the effective date of the amendments. It binds controllers and processors handling personal data in the Kingdom, with SDAIA as the competent authority, and is relevant to financial institutions' data handling obligations.

**Regimes:** data/credit info
