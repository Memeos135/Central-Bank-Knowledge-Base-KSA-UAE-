# Simplified Alternative to Standardised Approach

An optional, less granular calculation regime that SAMA may permit for banks with smaller or simpler trading books, subject to SAMA approval and ongoing oversight. Indicative eligibility criteria exclude G-SIBs/D-SIBs, IMA users and holders of correlation trading positions, and SAMA may still mandate the full standardised approach for banks with complex or sizeable risks. It includes maturity ladder and simplified commodity approaches and delta-plus/scenario options treatments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
