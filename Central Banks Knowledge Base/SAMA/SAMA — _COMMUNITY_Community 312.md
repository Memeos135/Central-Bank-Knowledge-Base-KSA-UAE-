# Legal Staff Statutory Representative

## Why this community

Whether and how legal department employees of a supervised entity may act as its statutory/legal representative in proceedings and dealings, and the governance limits SAMA places on that role.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone SAMA circular addressed to supervised institutions setting a representation/authorisation condition.
- Links outward to governance and internal-control expectations (fit-and-proper style conduct of designated officers) rather than to a prudential or AML chain.
- Sits in the law -> circular layer: the circular operationalises entity-representation practice without creating a separate regulation.
