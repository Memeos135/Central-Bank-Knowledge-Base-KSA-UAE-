# IRB Retail Exposures

## Why this community

Definition and segmentation of retail credit exposures for capital purposes, including the boundary between retail and corporate/MSME treatment and the identification of lower-risk revolving borrowers.

## How members connect

- All members hang off a single eligibility definition: whether an exposure qualifies as retail determines which risk-weight regime (standardised regulatory retail or IRB retail) applies.
- Sub-categorisation chains downward — retail class into regulatory retail, qualifying revolving retail, and MSME exposures treated as corporate where thresholds are exceeded.
- The transactor concept operates as a carve-out inside the revolving sub-class, reducing the applicable weighting for borrowers who settle in full.
- The currency mismatch multiplier is an add-on applied to retail exposures, so it depends on the class definition being settled first.
