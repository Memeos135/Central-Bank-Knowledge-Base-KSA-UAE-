# Event Hosting Rules

## Why this community

SAMA's conduct expectations for supervised entities' involvement in conferences, exhibitions and similar events — both as host/sponsor and as participant — covering prior notification/approval, branding and sponsorship limits.

## How members connect

- Two sibling rule sets in the same instrument family, split by role: hosting/sponsoring versus participating.
- The participation rules cross-refer to the hosting and sponsoring rules, so scope carve-outs and approval pathways must be read together to avoid double-counting obligations.
- Versioning matters: V1 and V2 are distinct issuances, so the effective requirement depends on which activity and which version governs.
