# Table CRD - Use of External Credit Ratings Under Standardised Approach

Table CRD is an annual, flexible-format qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on the use of external credit ratings under the standardised (or simplified standardised) approach for credit risk. Banks must name the ECAIs used, the asset classes each is used for, the issuer-to-issue rating transfer process for banking book assets, and the mapping of each agency's rating scale to risk buckets per SAMA circular No. B.C.S 242 (11 April 2007). It binds banks that both use the standardised approach and rely on external ratings; disclosure may be omitted only where amounts are negligible and the bank explains why, describing the portfolios and aggregate RWA concerned.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
