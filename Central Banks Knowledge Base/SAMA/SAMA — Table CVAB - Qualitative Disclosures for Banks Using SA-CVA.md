# Table CVAB - Qualitative Disclosures for Banks Using SA-CVA

Table CVAB requires annual, flexible-format qualitative disclosure of a bank's CVA risk management framework, including senior management involvement and governance arrangements such as documentation, an independent control unit, independent review and independence of data acquisition from business lines. Mandatory for all banks using the standardised approach for CVA (SA-CVA) to calculate CVA risk RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
