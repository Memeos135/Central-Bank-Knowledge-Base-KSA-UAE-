# Equity Risk Buckets, Risk Weights and Correlations

A component of SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk dealing with the equity risk class under the sensitivities-based standardised approach: how equity risk factors are grouped into buckets (by issuer characteristics such as market capitalisation, economy and sector) and the associated risk weights and intra/inter-bucket correlations used to aggregate delta, vega and curvature sensitivities. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA that hold trading book or otherwise in-scope equity positions and must compute market risk RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
