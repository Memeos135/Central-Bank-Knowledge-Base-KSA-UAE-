# Template OR3: Minimum Required Operational Risk Capital

A Pillar 3 template in the operational risk disclosure series presenting the bank's minimum required operational risk capital (and related loss/RWA inputs feeding the Pillar 1 requirement). It sits alongside OR1/OR2 in the same SAMA Pillar 3 framework and binds banks subject to the disclosure requirements. Useful when tracing how operational risk RWA and capital requirements reconcile to the overview template OV1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
