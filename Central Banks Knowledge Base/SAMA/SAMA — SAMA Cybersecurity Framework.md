# SAMA Cybersecurity Framework

A sub-node of the New Banking Products and Services Regulation dealing with the technology, fraud and cyber information a bank must supply in the product application form, including whether the product is onboarded to fraud monitoring with scenario configurability, third-party adherence to SAMA's cloud computing cybersecurity controls, integration method, the internal function monitoring the product, and customer cancellation terms and fees. It supports the product notification/no-objection package rather than setting standalone cyber rules, and binds banks submitting new products to SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other
