# Capital Requirements

Chapter 4 of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020 on minimum capital: DTFCs must maintain records enabling computation of a 20% capital adequacy ratio and the prescribed minimum capital, with SAMA determining compliance and empowered to impose higher ratios (e.g. capital deficiency, significant risk exposure, poor asset quality, rapid growth, holding-company contagion). It also sets risk-weighting of on-balance-sheet exposures into categories and includes completion instructions defining core capital, supplementary capital components (term subordinated debt, statutory loan loss reserve capped at 1.25% of RWA) and total capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk
