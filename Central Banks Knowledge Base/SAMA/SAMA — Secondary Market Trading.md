# Secondary Market Trading

This is the secondary-market provision (plus the concluding provisions and definitions) of SAMA's Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies, addressed to the licensed Real Estate Refinance Company. It conditions any trading of real estate finance contractual rights on a prior SAMA no-objection letter and on submission of a comprehensive plan covering collection and sukuk structuring, credit enhancement methodology, and a feasibility study assessing balance-sheet, housing-sector and capital-market impact. It preserves SAMA's power to impose further requirements or to restrict or prohibit such trading, confirms the Rules are read with the Finance Companies Control Law and the Real Estate Finance Law and their implementing regulations, allows case-by-case exemptions, and sets a 180-day entry into force.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
