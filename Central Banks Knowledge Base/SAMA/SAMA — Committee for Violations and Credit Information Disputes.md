# Committee for Violations and Credit Information Disputes

The Committee for Considering Violations and Adjudicating Credit Information Disputes established under the Implementing Regulation of the Credit Information Law, with its own chapter in the Regulation. It handles violations of the Law/Regulation and resolves disputes over credit information, sitting alongside the objections and complaints mechanism available to consumers. Relevant to licensed credit information companies, Members and consumers contesting record accuracy or alleging breaches.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection, governance/risk
