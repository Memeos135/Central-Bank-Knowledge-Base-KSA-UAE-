# Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements

The Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements framework itself, structured as numbered control requirements addressed to Member Organizations, covering counter-fraud governance and risk management, fraud prevention, and supporting definitions (including Fraud, External Fraud, Fraud Risk Appetite, Fraud Risk Assessment, Fraud Response Plan, Electronic Money and EMI, and a broad definition of Employee). It sets expectations for a risk-based counter-fraud programme, board accountability, a Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, KRIs/KPIs, intelligence monitoring, enterprise-wide fraud risk assessment, new-product fraud review, oversight of outsourced counter-fraud resources, and independent audit. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations within scope of the adopting circular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance, other
