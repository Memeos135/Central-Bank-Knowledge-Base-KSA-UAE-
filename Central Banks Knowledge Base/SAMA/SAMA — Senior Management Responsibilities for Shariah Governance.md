# Senior Management Responsibilities for Shariah Governance

The senior-management limb of Chapter Two of SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies. It sets executive-level duties for implementing board-approved Shariah governance arrangements, supporting the Shariah Committee, and ensuring finance business is conducted consistently with Shariah rulings and principles, with an expectation of reasonable Shariah literacy at management level. It binds the senior management of finance companies within the instructions' scope.
