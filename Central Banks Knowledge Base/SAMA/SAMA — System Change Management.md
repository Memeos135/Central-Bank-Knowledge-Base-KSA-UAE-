# System Change Management

Section of the SAMA IT Governance Framework establishing system change management as a governed process for defining, designing, testing and deploying changes to information assets (applications, software, devices, data). It requires an approved change management policy and procedure, classification, risk and impact assessment, asset-owner and Change Advisory Board approval, cyber security function review, and controlled handling and post-implementation review of emergency changes. Applies to Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
