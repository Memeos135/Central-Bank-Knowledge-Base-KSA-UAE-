# Recognition of Credit Risk Mitigants

This node sets out how credit risk mitigants are recognised for purchased receivables under the IRB approach, in particular the substitution treatment for guarantees from the seller or a third party depending on whether cover extends to default risk, dilution risk or both, and the treatment of partial or tranched cover. It also notes when collateral or partial guarantees providing first-loss protection fall to be treated under the securitisation chapters. It binds IRB banks calculating capital on receivables pools.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
