# Template LI2 - Sources of Differences Regulatory Exposure

Template LI2 is a mandatory annual Pillar 3 disclosure explaining the main drivers of difference between financial-statement carrying values (on the regulatory consolidation basis) and the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes — off-balance sheet amounts, valuation differences, netting rules, provisions and prudential filters. Its format is flexible so rows can be adapted to a bank's most meaningful drivers, with narrative support via Table LIA. The context also references Table CRB-A on prudential treatment of problem assets, including a bank's definitions of non-performing and forborne exposures relative to SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans. Binds all banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
