# Template CRE - Qualitative Disclosure on IRB Models

A mandatory annual qualitative Pillar 3 table for banks using foundation or advanced IRB approaches, describing the main characteristics of internal credit risk models used at group level. It covers model development, approval and change governance, independence of model review from model development, internal reporting on credit risk models, the scope of SAMA's model approval by entity/portfolio/exposure class, and the share of EAD covered by standardised, F-IRB and A-IRB approaches. It binds SAMA-supervised banks with IRB permission.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
