# Project Finance

This node defines project finance (PF) as a specialized lending sub-class where the lender looks primarily to revenues of a single project for repayment and security, typically with an SPV borrower confined to developing, owning and operating the installation. It sets out the standardized-approach risk weights where no issue-specific rating is available (higher in the pre-operational phase, lower in the operational phase, with a reduced weight for high-quality operational exposures) and the conditions for the high-quality classification, including creditor protections, reserve funding and availability-based or rate-of-return revenues. It binds banks risk-weighting project finance exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
