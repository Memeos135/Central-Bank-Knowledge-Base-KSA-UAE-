# Basel III Framework

Glossary and template material from SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework identifying the SAMA-issued Basel III component standards (SACAP, SCRE, SCCR, SMAR, SOPE, SLEV, SLCR, SNSF) that underpin disclosure obligations. It includes Template KM1 on key prudential metrics, mandatory for all banks, requiring quarterly fixed-format disclosure of risk-based capital, leverage and liquidity metrics with narrative explanation of significant changes. Binds banks subject to SAMA's capital and disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
