# SAMA Circular: Approval of Ownership Documents from Economic Cities Authority

Arabic SAMA circular instructing all real estate finance companies to accept the ownership document issued by the Economic Cities Authority for property or land inside economic cities as an official, approved title document equivalent to a title deed (sakk) used outside economic cities. Grounded in the Finance Companies Control Law and the Finance Lease Law implementing regulation, together with the Economic Cities Authority statute. Binds real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other
