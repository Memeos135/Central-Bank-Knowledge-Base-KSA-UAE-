# Liquidity & Credit Risk Governance

## Why this community

Combined prudential regime for liquidity adequacy and credit risk classification/provisioning: board-level liquidity governance, ILAAP and stress-testing on one side, and IFRS 9-based staging, forbearance, default, write-off and collateral rules on the other.

## How members connect

- Two obligation chains meet at governance: board and ALCO oversight sets liquidity risk tolerance, which drives stress testing, LCR/NSFR monitoring, ILAAP and the contingency funding plan.
- The Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning are the parent instrument for the credit-side members, importing IFRS 9 as the accounting basis while imposing SAMA-specific staging, default, forbearance and write-off requirements — where they diverge, the prudential rule governs.
- Restructuring taxonomy (short/long-term measures, forgiveness, loan splitting, debt-to-equity or asset swaps, NPV analysis) operates as defined forbearance treatments that trigger staging and provisioning consequences rather than as commercial options.
- Internal audit review of ECL adequacy and collateral valuation rules are the assurance and measurement anchors linking classification outcomes back to capital and provisioning adequacy.
