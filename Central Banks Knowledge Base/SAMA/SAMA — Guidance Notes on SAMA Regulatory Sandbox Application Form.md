# Guidance Notes on SAMA Regulatory Sandbox Application Form

Guidance notes explaining how to complete the SAMA Regulatory Sandbox application form and describing the four-stage sandbox operating model: application assessment against eligibility criteria (60 business days), operational readiness against the Assessment Criteria (120 days, leading to a temporary permission or Letter of Authorisation), live testing (six to twelve months), and graduation/exit toward a full regulatory licence. It clarifies that the initial approval letter permits company formation as a FinTech with the Ministry of Commerce but does not authorise commencing operations or onboarding customers. It is directed at FinTech innovators applying to test products under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other
