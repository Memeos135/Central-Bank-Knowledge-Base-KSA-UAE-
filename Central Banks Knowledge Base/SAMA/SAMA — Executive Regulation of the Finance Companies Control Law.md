# Executive Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law

Another instance of the amended Executive Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law with its issuing circular, covering definitions and general provisions (Part One) and SAMA's regulatory and supervisory role over the finance sector. It carries the key economic and governance definitions used across finance regulation — instalment, term cost, total cost of financing, total amount payable by the beneficiary, APR, senior management, exposure thresholds and influential ownership — and repeals the separate micro finance and micro consumer finance rule sets. Addressed to finance companies, licensed digital brokerage companies and licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
