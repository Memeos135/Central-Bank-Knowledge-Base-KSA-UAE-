# Article 22: Rules for Dealing with Borrowers

Article 22 (Section Six) of SAMA's consumer financing rules requires Creditors to give each Borrower at least a quarterly Consumer Financing account statement (written or, if pre-agreed, electronic) disclosing statement period dates, opening/closing balances, each draw-down, Term Cost debited and when, and all fees and charges. It also requires at least 60 calendar days' advance notice by Guaranteed Communication of changes to account charges or payment method, and places on the Borrower a duty to keep contact details current, failing which the Creditor is released from the related notification liability. Context includes Article 14 (33.33% salary deduction cap, 25% for pensioners, mandatory credit bureau check and registration with Borrower consent) and the 5-year maximum term for deferral/skip-payment offers.
