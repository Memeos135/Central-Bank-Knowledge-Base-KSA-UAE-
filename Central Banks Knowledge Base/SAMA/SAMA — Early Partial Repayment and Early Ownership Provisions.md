# Early Partial Repayment and Early Ownership Provisions

The clauses of SAMA's mandatory individual vehicle Ijara template dealing with early partial repayment and early ownership (early acquisition of the leased asset by the lessee), including the associated procedures set out in the contract summary. They determine the lessee's exit and prepayment rights and the lessor's permitted charges and steps for transferring title. Binding on banks and finance companies acting as lessors, which cannot vary these terms.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
