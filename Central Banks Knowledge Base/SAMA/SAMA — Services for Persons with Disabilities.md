# Services for Persons with Disabilities

SAMA circular transmitting the Instructions on Services Provided to Persons with Disabilities in Financial Institutions, aimed at fairness, financial inclusion and removal of physical and behavioural barriers to accessing financial services. It defines "person with disability", "disability", "financial institution" and "accessibility", and imposes requirements such as establishing a dedicated administrative unit, accessible built environment, self-service/electronic channels, transparency and fair treatment, and financial education. It binds all SAMA-supervised entities — banks and foreign bank branches, insurance/reinsurance firms, finance and finance-lease registration companies, credit information companies, exchange houses, and payment companies — with compliance due within three months and a compliance plan within one month.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance, data/credit info
