# Electronic Distribution of Investment Fund Units

A SAMA circular to payment service providers permitting electronic money institutions to contract with CMA-licensed capital market institutions to distribute units of publicly offered investment funds through their e-wallet applications. It sets conditions: public offerings only, prior notification to SAMA with confirmation that internal policies have been updated, compliance with the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services (including licensing conditions and limits), due diligence on the products, contractual allocation of liability and risk to the CMA-licensed institution, an EMI role limited to distribution and document completion without opening investment accounts, subscription-only functionality with no redemption or cancellation, and completion of subscription only after the capital market institution confirms suitability to the customer. It also stresses segregation between payment activities and investment activities and the EMI's continuing responsibility for legal and technical soundness of its services.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk
