# Updating Exchange Business Procedures (SAMA 5712)

A SAMA circular issued under the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business updating operational requirements for exchange houses and exchange companies. It requires minimum one-year retention of CCTV footage, provision of account statements to membership holders on request, safekeeping of all customer transaction documents (originals or copies) with electronic archiving for at least ten years, recording of residential or national address at relationship establishment, a prohibition on deleting any transaction or membership record, and business continuity/disaster recovery plans tested periodically. It binds all exchange institutions and companies, with a four-month compliance window and the prospect of supervisory action for non-compliance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk
