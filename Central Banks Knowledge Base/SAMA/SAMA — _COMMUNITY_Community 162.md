# Securitisation Exposure Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of banking book securitisation exposures, splitting total exposure amounts from the capital requirements applicable where the bank acts as originator or sponsor.

## How members connect

- The two templates share underlying exposure data: amounts reported in the general banking book template feed the originator/sponsor capital requirement breakdown.
- Both depend on the same defined roles (originator, sponsor, investor) and banking book scope boundary, so a classification decision in one drives the other.
- Both sit beneath the securitisation framework's capital treatment rules, with the qualitative table supplying the explanatory context.
