# Retail Banking Fundamentals Professional Certificate

Part of the same SAMA circular confirming continued compliance with mandatory professional certification requirements, covering the Retail Banking Fundamentals certificate. It applies to bank front-line staff dealing directly with customers — customer care, call centre, branch staff and branch managers — including individuals contracted through third parties, with the certificate to be obtained within one year of joining and a minimum compliance rate for covered employees.
