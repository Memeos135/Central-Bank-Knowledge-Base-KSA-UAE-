# Standstill Agreement

Concept node describing the standstill agreement as typically the first step in a multi-creditor workout: creditors agree not to enforce rights against a defaulting borrower for a limited period so the borrower can gather information and prepare a survival strategy while creditors form a joint approach. It may also carry further obligations such as new working-capital funding or deferral of principal and profit/interest payments, may be simplified for MSMEs, and may be dispensed with where a single cooperative creditor relationship exists. Guidance for banks as creditors in distressed-borrower workouts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
