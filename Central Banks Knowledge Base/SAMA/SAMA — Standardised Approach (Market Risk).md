# Standardised Approach (Market Risk)

Node on the standardised approach in the market risk capital framework and its interaction with standardised risk-weighted asset reporting across risk types. It covers the general provisions and structure of the standardised approach — the sensitivities-based method, default risk charge and residual risk add-on — and how standardised RWA and minimum capital requirements are identified relative to modelled/internal ratings-based approaches. Binds SAMA-supervised banks computing Pillar 1 capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
