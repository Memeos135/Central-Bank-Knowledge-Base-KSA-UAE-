# Entity-Financier Contract

Sets the minimum content of the paper or electronic contract between the aggregation entity and each Financier (banks, finance companies and other licensed finance providers). Required clauses include the financier's licence number and date, contract term aligned with both parties' licence validity, data-exchange and offer-update mechanisms, service levels and escalation, notification when the finance contract is issued, confidentiality and security, an express prohibition on subcontracting, and confirmation that the financier remains responsible for its own regulatory obligations. Binds the licensed aggregation entity and shapes its outsourcing-style arrangements with financiers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
