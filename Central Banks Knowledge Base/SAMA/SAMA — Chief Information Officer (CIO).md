# Chief Information Officer (CIO)

A glossary/role node from SAMA's IT Governance Framework materials identifying the Chief Information Officer and related IT roles (asset owner, database administrator, enterprise/application architect, COO) used across the framework's control requirements. It clarifies defined terminology and accountability for IT strategy, operations, compliance monitoring and incident oversight within Member Organizations. Relevant to any SAMA-regulated Member Organization allocating IT governance responsibilities.
