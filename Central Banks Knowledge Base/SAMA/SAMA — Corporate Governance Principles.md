# Corporate Governance Principles

This node covers the corporate governance, ownership/fit-and-proper, IT and accounting systems, outsourcing and audit elements of SAMA's banking licensing criteria. It requires applicants to evidence board composition and functioning under SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia, fit-and-proper founders, directors and significant shareholders under the Banking Control Law, adequate prudential reporting systems, BCM/DRP arrangements, and compliant outsourcing and internal/external audit arrangements. It binds applicants for a banking licence in KSA, including foreign banks applying for branch status and their significant shareholders.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
