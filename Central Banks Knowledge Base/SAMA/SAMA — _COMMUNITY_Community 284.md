# Special Payments Remuneration

## Why this community

Pillar 3 remuneration disclosure covering guaranteed bonuses, sign-on awards and severance payments made during the reporting year. It addresses transparency over discretionary pay outside the standard variable-remuneration cycle, where governance and risk-alignment concerns are highest.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a prescribed SAMA disclosure template forming part of the remuneration (REM) series within the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.
- Legally dependent on the underlying rules on compensation practices and the governance framework that defines material risk takers and senior management as the population in scope.
- Sits in a hierarchy: capital/disclosure framework -> Pillar 3 disclosure requirements -> prescribed template with fixed rows, columns and reporting frequency.
- Read alongside the other REM templates, since scope of covered staff and definitions of fixed/variable pay are shared across them.
