# Blue Team Report (BTR)

A defined deliverable within SAMA's ethical red teaming (F.E.E.R.) framework: the Blue Team's own report on a completed red teaming test, covering alerts and events detected, actions taken and their outcomes, plus improvement recommendations. It is produced alongside the Red Teaming Provider's report and feeds the joint replay exercise, 360-degree feedback and lessons-learned phase. It binds the Member Organization's monitoring function (e.g. SOC) as part of the test lifecycle coordinated by the White Team.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
