# Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies

SAMA circular approving the Rules for Regulating Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law, covering licensing procedures and minimum conduct and operational standards for the activity of financing a customer's purchase of goods or services from merchants with no term cost to the customer. The covering circular also exempts BNPL companies and other finance companies performing BNPL from the debt burden ratios for aggregate finance below the stated threshold, without displacing the remaining Responsible Lending obligations. The rules bind joint-stock companies licensed by SAMA for BNPL activity; other SAMA instruments (leadership appointments, information security, business continuity, customer protection, individual collection rules) continue to apply.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
