# Information Confidentiality and Classification

This node covers the confidentiality and information-disclosure strand of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics for Financial Institutions, including the duty to preserve confidentiality, the permitted channels for disclosing information, and the related prohibition on using or leaking inside information to manipulate the market. It operates as a minimum conduct standard, with the institution's board required to adopt a consistent internal policy and managers responsible for staff acknowledgement. It applies to all persons associated with SAMA-supervised banks, insurers, finance companies, finance-lease registration companies and credit information companies.
