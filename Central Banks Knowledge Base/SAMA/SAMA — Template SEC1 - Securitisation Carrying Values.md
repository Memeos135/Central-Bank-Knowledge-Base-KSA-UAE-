# Template SEC1 - Securitisation Carrying Values

Sets out the scope of the Pillar 3 securitisation disclosure chapter and Template SEC1 on carrying values of securitisation exposures. All securitisation exposures, including those failing significant risk transfer recognition, are reported in SEC1/SEC2 and Table SECA, while SEC3/SEC4 are confined to exposures capitalised under the securitisation framework; trading book securitisation capital charges are excluded. Binds banks preparing Pillar 3 reports, with related guidance on reconciliation to Template LI1/LI2.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
