# Portfolio Compression

Requires banks to assess, by reference to risk exposure profile, size, volatility and number of outstanding transactions, whether to undertake portfolio compression with counterparties, and to maintain policies and procedures for regular assessment and engagement in compression of non-centrally cleared OTC derivative portfolios, proportionate to activity. Related reporting fields on collateralisation (including collateral portfolio and initial margin posted) appear in the reporting data tables. Binds banks holding non-centrally cleared OTC derivative portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
