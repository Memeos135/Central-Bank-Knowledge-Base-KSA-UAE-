# SAMA Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks

This node points to SAMA's Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks Operating in the Kingdom (1989), referenced within a SAMA Banking Inspection guide on operational risk management and insurance. It is cited as one of the foundational internal-control instruments to be read together with the Disaster Recovery Planning Guideline (1993) and the Guidelines on Physical Security for Saudi Banks (1995) to build an integrated operational risk control programme. It is directed at directors and senior management of SAMA-licensed commercial banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
