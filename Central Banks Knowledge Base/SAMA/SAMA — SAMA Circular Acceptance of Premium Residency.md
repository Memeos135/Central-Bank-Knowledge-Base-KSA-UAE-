# SAMA Circular: Acceptance of Premium Residency

A SAMA circular concerning acceptance of the Saudi Premium Residency (both limited-term and permanent categories) as issued by the Premium Residency Center. The supplied content is limited to the Premium Residency Center material rather than operative circular text, so the specific obligations (typically acceptance of the premium residency card as valid identification in customer onboarding and identity verification) must be confirmed from the underlying document. Relevant to financial institutions supervised by SAMA that verify customer identity documents.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, consumer protection, other
