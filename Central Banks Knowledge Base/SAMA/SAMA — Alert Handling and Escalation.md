# Alert Handling and Escalation

Chapter Five of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, governing how screening alerts and potential matches are handled, reviewed and escalated internally, and flagged to SAMA. It links to the freezing/unfreezing chapter, since confirmed matches trigger immediate asset-freezing and notification duties. Binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.
