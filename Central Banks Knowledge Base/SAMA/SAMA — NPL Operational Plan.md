# NPL Operational Plan

Node on the design, implementation and embedding of a bank's NPL strategy and its supporting operational plan, including monitoring of results, at least annual review, documented roles and reporting lines, and staff goals/incentives aligned to NPL reduction targets. It also covers strategic inputs such as NPL investor demand, the NPL servicing market, and the capital implications of the strategy, which must be modelled against the risk appetite framework and ICAAP and reflected in capital planning. Binds bank boards and senior management responsible for NPL strategy, budgeting and capital adequacy.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
