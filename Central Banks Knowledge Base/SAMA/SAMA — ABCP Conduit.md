# ABCP Conduit

Addresses asset-backed commercial paper (ABCP) conduits within the securitisation/STC framework: it defines conduit-level roles (sponsor, transaction, beneficial interest) and distinguishes conduit-level from transaction-level compliance with the short-term STC criteria. Exposures at conduit level qualify only if criteria are met at both conduit and transaction levels; transaction-level exposures need only transaction-level compliance. Binds banks sponsoring, supporting or investing in ABCP programmes for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
