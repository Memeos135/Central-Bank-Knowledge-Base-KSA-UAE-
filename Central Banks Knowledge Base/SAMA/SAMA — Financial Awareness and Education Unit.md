# Financial Awareness and Education Unit

The third mandatory unit of the bank's Customer Care Department, responsible for financial awareness and education of customers, aligned with the products and services the bank offers. The rules require a dedicated awareness/education policy that, at minimum, defines the staff member's duty to give the customer a full explanation of the product or service and all resulting obligations. Binds banks licensed by SAMA in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
