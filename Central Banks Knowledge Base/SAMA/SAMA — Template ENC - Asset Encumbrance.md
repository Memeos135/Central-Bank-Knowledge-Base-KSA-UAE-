# Template ENC - Asset Encumbrance

Template ENC is a mandatory semiannual, fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure of encumbered versus unencumbered on-balance sheet assets at carrying amount, on the regulatory scope of consolidation including securitization exposures. It uses a template-specific definition of encumbered assets (restricted from liquidation, sale, transfer or assignment by legal, regulatory, contractual or other limitations) that deliberately differs from the LCR definition by excluding the monetization aspect, and that definition does not carry over to other parts of the Basel framework. Binds all banks, with narrative explanation of material changes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
