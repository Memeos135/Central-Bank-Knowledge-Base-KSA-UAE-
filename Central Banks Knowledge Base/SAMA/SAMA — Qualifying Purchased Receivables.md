# Qualifying Purchased Receivables

Defines eligible purchased receivables for the IRB approach, split into retail and corporate receivables, and sets when the "top-down" treatment may be used instead of assessing individual obligor default risk. Corporate purchased receivables may use top-down only where the programme meets the eligibility criteria and minimum operational requirements and where applying full corporate IRB minimums would be an undue burden (primarily receivables bought for asset-backed securitisation structures; on-balance sheet use requires SAMA approval); retail purchased receivables are A-IRB only, and corporate purchased receivables are a separate asset class for the IRB roll-out requirement. Binds banks using internal ratings-based approaches under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
