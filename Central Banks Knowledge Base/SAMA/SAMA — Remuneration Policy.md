# Remuneration Policy

The core governance obligation under the Banks Remuneration Rules: a written Remuneration Policy for Senior Management approved by the General Assembly and a separate policy for all other employees approved by the Board, designed to ensure prudent management of remuneration-related risk. Prescribes minimum policy content including objectives, scope (regular, contractual and outsourced staff), pay structure and mix, perquisites, an approval authority matrix, and the role of control and HR functions in misconduct identification and reporting. Binds banks' Boards, General Assemblies and Nomination and Remuneration Committees, with independent annual review and reporting to SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
