# Disability Emergency Evacuation

## Why this community

Accessibility obligation on supervised entities to ensure persons with disabilities can be safely evacuated from premises in an emergency, sitting at the intersection of consumer protection/fair treatment and operational safety duties.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone accessibility requirement not yet cross-linked to the broader consumer protection or business continuity nodes in the graph.
- Reads as a service-accessibility duty owed to customers and visitors on the entity's premises, rather than a prudential or conduct-of-sale rule.
- Practical linkage points for a researcher are the consumer protection principles (fair treatment, accessibility of banking services) and operational/BCM requirements.
