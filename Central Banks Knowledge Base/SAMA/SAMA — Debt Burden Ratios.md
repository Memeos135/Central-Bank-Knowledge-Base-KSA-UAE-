# Debt Burden Ratios

Concept node for the debt burden (deduction) ratios within the Responsible Lending Principles, built on the defined inputs of monthly credit obligations, gross salary and total monthly income drawn from licensed credit bureau reports and the customer's own disclosure. It governs the maximum share of a natural-person customer's income that may be committed to finance repayments. It binds banks and finance companies granting individual finance; note the separate SAMA exemption for BNPL exposures below the stated threshold.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, data/credit info
