# Sector Electronic Payments

## Why this community

Sector-specific mandates to enable and accept electronic payment at points of sale across merchant categories (groceries, general retail, personal services), forming part of SAMA's cash-reduction and merchant-acceptance policy track.

## How members connect

- Each member applies the same core obligation — merchant enablement/acceptance of electronic payment instruments — to a different merchant sector, so the operative rule is common and only scope differs.
- Linked by shared defined terms (merchant, point-of-sale device, electronic payment means) and by the acquirer/PSP-side duties that make merchant acceptance effective.
- Cross-regime relevance: merchants onboarded for acceptance are the acquirer's customers for KYB/CDD purposes, so sector rollout obligations feed into AML/CTF onboarding duties.
- Sequenced as a phased extension of the same policy instrument rather than a law→regulation hierarchy; read them as parallel scope extensions.
