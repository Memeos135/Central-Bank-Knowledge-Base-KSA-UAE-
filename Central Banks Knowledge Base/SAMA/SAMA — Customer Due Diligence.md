# Customer Due Diligence

Sets out the CDD obligations under the AML Law's Implementing Regulation (Article 7 provisions), covering identification and verification of customers and beneficial owners, timing of verification, risk-based deferral where ML risk is low, and enhanced measures. It also addresses life/investment insurance beneficiaries, the duty to refuse or terminate a relationship where CDD cannot be completed (with consideration of an STR), the tipping-off exception, and reliance on a third-party financial institution or DNFBP subject to conditions. Binds financial institutions and designated non-financial businesses and professions in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF
