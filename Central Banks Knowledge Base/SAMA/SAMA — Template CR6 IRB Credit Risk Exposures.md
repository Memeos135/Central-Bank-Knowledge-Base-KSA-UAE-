# Template CR6: IRB Credit Risk Exposures

Node for Template CR6, the Pillar 3 disclosure of credit risk exposures and IRB parameter estimates broken down by portfolio and PD scale. It sits in the credit risk disclosure set alongside CR1 (credit quality of assets) and CR3 (CRM techniques), and the surrounding material defines the IRB/standardised boundary, EAD post-CRM, PD, LGD and maturity inputs used for RWA. It binds banks permitted to use foundation or advanced IRB approaches for credit risk under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
