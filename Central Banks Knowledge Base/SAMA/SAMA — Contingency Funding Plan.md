# Contingency Funding Plan

This node covers the Contingency Funding Plan requirement in SAMA's Rules on Liquidity Risk Management for finance companies, together with the surrounding funding-strategy and liquid-asset-cushion expectations. It requires a CFP setting out crisis strategy, available funding sources and volumes, activation authority, roles, escalation, early warning indicators and periodic testing. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies, including those using originate-to-distribute/securitisation funding models.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
