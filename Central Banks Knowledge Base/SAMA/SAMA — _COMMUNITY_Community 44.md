# Specialized Lending Slotting

## Why this community

Specialized lending as a distinct sub-class of corporate exposure, where repayment depends primarily on the cash flows of the financed asset or project rather than the borrower's broader capacity, and where supervisory slotting may substitute for internal estimates.

## How members connect

- Specialized lending is the parent definition; project, object, commodities finance and income-producing real estate are its enumerated sub-categories, each with its own qualifying characteristics.
- The supervisory slotting criteria approach applies across all four sub-categories, mapping each to supervisory categories and prescribed risk weights where a bank cannot meet the estimation requirements of the general corporate IRB treatment.
- The cluster is hierarchically subordinate to the IRB asset-class definitions: classification as specialized lending is a carve-out from the general corporate exposure class.
