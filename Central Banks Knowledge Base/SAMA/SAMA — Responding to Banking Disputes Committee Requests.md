# Responding to Banking Disputes Committee Requests

SAMA circular directing banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom to respond promptly to requests from the General Secretariat of the Committees for Banking and Finance Disputes and Violations when it prepares cases for hearing. It binds addressees to the response deadlines set out in an annexed schedule (generally three, seven, ten or fifteen business days by document type, e.g. contracts, statements, transfer and SWIFT records, POS and card-dispute evidence, CCTV, call recordings, guarantees, pledges), and requires immediate notification with a justification and a substitute date not exceeding thirty days where a deadline cannot be met. Non-compliance exposes the institution to supervisory action.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
