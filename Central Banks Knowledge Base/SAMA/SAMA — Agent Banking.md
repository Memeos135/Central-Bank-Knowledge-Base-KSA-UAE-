# Agent Banking

Agent Banking is the delivery of permissible banking products and services to Customers by a contracted Agent acting on behalf of a bank, under a direct written agency contract and only after SAMA's non-objection. The Agent must hold a commercial register permitting the activity and obtain approval from its own supervisory authority before the bank seeks non-objection; agents may operate on an exclusive or multiple (non-exclusive) basis, and bank employees may perform or supervise the activity on agent premises. The concept binds banks, which retain full responsibility for the agency relationship, agent selection, and the agent's acts and omissions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection, banking prudential
