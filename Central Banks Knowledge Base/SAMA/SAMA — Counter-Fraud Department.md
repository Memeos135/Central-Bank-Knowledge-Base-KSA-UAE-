# Counter-Fraud Department

This node covers the requirement for Member Organisations to establish and maintain a Counter-Fraud Department responsible for day-to-day operation of the Counter-Fraud Programme. Its mandated functions include monitoring compliance with counter-fraud policies, standards and procedures, designing organisation-wide controls, conducting the Fraud Risk Assessment, intelligence analysis and sharing with SAMA and the sector, tuning detection systems, investigating fraud and documenting root causes, monitoring Fraud Risk Appetite breaches, aligning with Cyber Security and Financial Crime functions, and periodic senior management reporting. The reporting line must be assessed against organisational structure and independence considerations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other
