# Cyber Security Incident Management

Concept node for cyber security incident management under the SAMA cyber security regime, covering identification, reporting and handling of cyber security breaches and suspected weaknesses, including the expectation that reporting channels are embedded in the cyber security policy and linked to business continuity. Maturity of this process is measured under the framework's maturity model and is subject to periodic review. Binds Member Organizations; relevant where incidents also trigger notification duties to SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
