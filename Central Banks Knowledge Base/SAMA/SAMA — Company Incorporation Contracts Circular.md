# Company Incorporation Contracts Circular

An informational circular conveying the Ministry of Commerce's approach to approving company incorporation contracts (articles of association) and their amendments, under which partners and the submitting party bear responsibility for the contents in line with the Companies Law. SAMA notes this arrangement does not relieve financial institutions of their obligations under SAMA-issued regulations (relevant to onboarding/KYB verification of corporate customers). Circulated to payment sector entities for information only.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other
