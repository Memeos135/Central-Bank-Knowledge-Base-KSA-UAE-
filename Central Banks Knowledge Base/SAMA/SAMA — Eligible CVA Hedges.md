# Eligible CVA Hedges

Covers the eligibility conditions for CVA hedges and their capital treatment: eligible external hedges are removed from market risk capital calculations, ineligible external hedges are capitalised as trading book instruments, and internal hedges only qualify where the trading desk offsets its position externally in defined cases. It also notes materiality-based alternative treatments under which CVA hedges are not recognised at all. It binds banks calculating CVA capital under BA-CVA or SA-CVA, including where SAMA approval limits SA-CVA scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
