# Disclosure and Enforcement on Accounts

Provisions of the Rules for Bank Accounts governing disclosure of, and enforcement on, bank balances, accounts and relationships. Enforcement is defined to include blocking, compulsory deduction, check issuance and transfers from customer accounts; requests are channelled through SAMA (save where SAMA's instructions provide otherwise), must be executed in SAMA's prescribed form, manner and timeframe, and must be treated as strictly confidential. Banks must search all relationships with the customer — active, closed, suspended and inoperative accounts, deposits, safe deposit boxes, credit cards and remittance memberships — including sole proprietorships and company participations, and verify the accuracy of what is reported. The context also touches account operation and disbursement controls for trial and enforcement court accounts.
