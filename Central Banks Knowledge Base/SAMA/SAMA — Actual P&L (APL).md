# Actual P&L (APL)

Defines actual P&L (APL), the P&L produced by the bank's daily P&L process including intraday trading, time effects and new or modified deals, but excluding fees, commissions and valuation adjustments subject to separate capital treatment. APL is used alongside HPL in backtesting internal market risk models against VaR measures at desk and bank-wide level. Relevant to SAMA-supervised banks with IMA-approved or candidate trading desks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
