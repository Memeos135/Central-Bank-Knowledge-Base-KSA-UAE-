# Default Definition

Defines "default" and the related IFRS 9 credit-risk measurement terms (PD, EAD, LGD, financial asset, 12-month and lifetime ECL, Stage 1/2 exposures) used in SAMA's rules on credit risk exposure classification and provisioning for finance companies. Default is established by either a qualitative "unlikeliness to pay" trigger (e.g. Stage 3B classification, charge-off or specific provision, sale at material credit loss, concessionary modification, bankruptcy filing, referral to the Enforcement Court) or the quantitative 90-days-past-due test on any material credit obligation. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies, which must adopt the regulatory default definition consistently for regulatory and financial reporting or document justified departures.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
