# SAMA Guidance Document on Basel III (Circular 341000015689)

This node is SAMA's Basel III guidance issued under Circular 341000015689, covering minimum capital requirements for credit risk, including IRB rating system governance and securitization rules. The extracted material sets documentation obligations for internal rating systems (design, rating criteria, exception approval, review frequency, model methodology, default/loss definitions, change history) and securitization STC criteria on investor voting/enforcement rights and disclosure/legal review of transaction documentation. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating regulatory capital, particularly those using internal ratings-based approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
