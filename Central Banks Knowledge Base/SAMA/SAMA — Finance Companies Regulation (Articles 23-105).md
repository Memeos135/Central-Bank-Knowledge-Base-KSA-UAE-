# Finance Companies Regulation (Articles 23-105)

This is the amended Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, issued by SAMA under the Law and circulated in Rajab 1447 (Dec 2025), together with the covering circular that repeals the Microfinance Activity Rules and the Micro Consumer Finance Companies Rules and gives finance companies a transition period to regularise their position. It sets the definitional architecture (finance laws, BNPL company, micro consumer finance company, debt-based crowdfunding company, microfinance company, finance contract, instalment, term cost, total cost of finance, APR, board and senior management, exposure, large exposure, influential holding) and the prudential, licensing, governance and conduct provisions applying to finance activity. It binds finance companies licensed in the Kingdom, and the distribution list also reaches SAMA-licensed digital brokerage companies and licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
