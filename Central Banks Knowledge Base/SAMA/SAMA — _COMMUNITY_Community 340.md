# Nebras Drug Prevention Project

## Why this community

Financial-sector participation in the national drug prevention initiative (Nebras) — an awareness and social-responsibility directive addressed to supervised entities rather than a prudential or AML control requirement.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular with no internal cross-references in this cluster; obligation flows directly from SAMA to addressed institutions.
- Sits in the corporate/social-responsibility and staff-awareness space; note it does not amend AML/CTF customer due diligence or suspicious-transaction reporting duties, though drug-related predicate offences remain an AML/CTF matter.
- Practical consequence: treat as a compliance-communications obligation (participation, awareness dissemination) and check the circular's addressee list for scope.
