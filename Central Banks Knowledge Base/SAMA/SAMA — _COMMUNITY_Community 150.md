# Digital Identity Acceptance

## Why this community

Recognition of government digital credentials (Absher and Tawakkalna) as acceptable identification evidence, bearing directly on customer identification and verification under the AML/CTF and KYC regimes.

## How members connect

- The two instruments are directly cross-referenced: the family record acceptance builds on and extends the earlier acceptance of digital identity from the same platforms.
- Both operate as permissions/clarifications layered onto the underlying CDD obligation to identify and verify customers and, where relevant, dependents or related persons using reliable independent source documents.
- Shared defined terms: what constitutes a valid identification document, and which government-issued digital artefact is deemed equivalent to a physical original.
- Consequence is cross-regime: the same acceptance standard feeds account opening, onboarding for PSPs and finance companies, and consumer-facing identity checks.
