# Financial Action Task Force (FATF)

A concept node within SAMA's AML/CTF guide identifying the Financial Action Task Force as an international standard-setter whose instructions and typologies financial institutions must take into account, alongside SAMA, the PCCML and the PCCT, when developing and updating ML/TF indicators for transaction monitoring. The context also records Saudi Arabia's FATF membership (2019) and founding role in MENAFATF as background to the national AML/CTF framework. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions indirectly, through the guide's monitoring and indicator-updating obligations.
