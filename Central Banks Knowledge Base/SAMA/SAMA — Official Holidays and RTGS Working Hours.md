# Official Holidays and RTGS Working Hours

A SAMA circular (Arabic) notifying all SAMA-supervised financial institutions that the Central Bank has approved the official holiday schedule and working hours for financial institutions and for the Saudi Real Time Gross Settlement system for the coming four Hijri years (1447–1450H), published on SAMA's website. It binds supervised institutions to observe the published holiday dates and working-hour instructions, to notify SAMA in advance of branches operating during official holidays, and to announce those branches appropriately. Issued under SAMA's Law (Royal Decree M/36).

**Regimes:** payments, other
