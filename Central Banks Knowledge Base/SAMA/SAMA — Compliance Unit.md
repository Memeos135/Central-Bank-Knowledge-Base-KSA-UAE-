# Compliance Unit

Node covering the "Unit" (compliance function/department) as defined and regulated under SAMA's Compliance Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. It defines the compliance function as reporting directly to the Audit Committee, together with the Compliance Officer and Unit staff, and sets the minimum requirements, key attributes, duties and responsibilities enabling the Unit to operate competently and effectively. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies operating in the Kingdom, without prejudice to other applicable laws (AML, CTF, BNPL, real estate finance, consumer protection, governance).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, consumer protection
