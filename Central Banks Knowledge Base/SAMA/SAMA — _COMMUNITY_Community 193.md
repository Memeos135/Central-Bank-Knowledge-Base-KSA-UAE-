# Holidays and RTGS Hours

## Why this community

SAMA notifications fixing official holidays and the corresponding RTGS (real-time gross settlement) operating hours, which determine settlement value dates and payment cut-offs.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an operational notice rather than a rule-making instrument, but it conditions payment-system obligations and timing defences across the payments regime.
