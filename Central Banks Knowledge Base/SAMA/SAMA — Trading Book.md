# Trading Book

The trading book concept as used for market risk capital purposes, and in particular the rules on internal risk transfers between the banking book and trading book. Internal transfers from trading book to banking book get no capital recognition; banking-book credit, equity and general interest rate risk hedged via the trading book are recognised only where an exactly matching external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider (or a SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk arrangement for GIRR) is in place. Applies to banks calculating market risk capital under this framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
