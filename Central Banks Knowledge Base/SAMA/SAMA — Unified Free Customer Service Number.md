# Unified Free Customer Service Number

Short SAMA circular on customer communication channels requiring financial institutions to replace the 920 unified customer service number with the toll-free unified 800 number, reaffirming an earlier circular on making contact channels available. It obliges institutions to make the free unified number accessible to all customer segments. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** consumer protection
