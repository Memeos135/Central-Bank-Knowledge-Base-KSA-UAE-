# Threat Intelligence

Covers the scenario phase (up to five weeks) in which the Green Team supplies sector-wide and national threat intelligence, the White Team supplies internal/incident intelligence, and the Red Teaming Provider combines these with its own external sources to build high-level then detailed attack scenarios mapped to critical information assets. Scenarios, strategy and TTPs require Green Team agreement, and the final red teaming plan must also set escalation procedures and communication protocols. Binds Member Organizations and their providers during test preparation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
