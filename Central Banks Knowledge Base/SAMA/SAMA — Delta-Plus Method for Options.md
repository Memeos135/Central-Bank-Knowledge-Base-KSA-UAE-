# Delta-Plus Method for Options

A market risk capital methodology node within SAMA's Basel-aligned capital framework, setting out how banks that write options may include delta-weighted option positions in the simplified standardised approach, with the delta-equivalent amount carrying the applicable general market risk charge. Because delta alone does not capture option risk, banks must additionally measure and hold capital against gamma and vega sensitivities, computed via an approved exchange model or the bank's own options pricing model under SAMA oversight. Binds SAMA-licensed banks with options exposures using the simplified standardised market risk treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
