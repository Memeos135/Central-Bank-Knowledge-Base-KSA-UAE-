# Technical Linkage with Watheq Beneficial Owner Verification

A SAMA circular (Financial Conduct Deputy Governorship) directing supervised financial institutions to establish technical linkage with the Ministry of Commerce's "Watheq" service to verify the identity of beneficial owners of companies using updated official data. It also requires notifying the Ministry of Commerce through the platform where a company has not disclosed a beneficial owner or where discrepancies appear in beneficial ownership information, based on the outcome of the institution's own customer due diligence, without prejudice to suspicious transaction reporting duties or personal data protection requirements; implementation difficulties are to be reported to SAMA's Financial Integrity General Department. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision and takes effect from publication.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
