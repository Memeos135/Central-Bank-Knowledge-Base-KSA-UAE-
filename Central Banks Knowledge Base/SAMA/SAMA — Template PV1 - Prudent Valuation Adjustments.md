# Template PV1 - Prudent Valuation Adjustments

Template PV1 requires an annual, fixed-format breakdown of prudent valuation adjustments (close-out uncertainty, model risk, operational risk, investing and funding costs and other adjustments) across equity, interest rate, FX, credit and commodity exposures, split between trading and banking book. It applies to all banks that record PVAs, and is anchored to the Basel prudent valuation guidance together with SAMA's circular on assessing banks' financial instrument fair value practices. Narrative explanation of significant changes and of unlisted "other adjustments" is expected.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
