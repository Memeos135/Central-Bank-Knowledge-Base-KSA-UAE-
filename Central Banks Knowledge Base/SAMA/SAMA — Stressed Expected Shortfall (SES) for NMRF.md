# Stressed Expected Shortfall (SES) for NMRF

Concept node for the Stressed Expected Shortfall (SES) capital add-on for non-modellable risk factors (NMRFs) — risk factors in model-eligible trading desks that fail modellability criteria and therefore attract a separate aggregate capital measure rather than inclusion in the ES model. It is one of the three IMA regulatory model components (ES, DRC and SES for NMRFs) that banks must describe qualitatively and quantify in Pillar 3 disclosures. Binds banks using the internal models approach for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
