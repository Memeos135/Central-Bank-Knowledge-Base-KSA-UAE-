# Risk Factor Modellability

## Why this community

The test for whether a risk factor may be treated as modellable, which determines if it can sit inside an internal model or must be capitalised separately as non-modellable.

## How members connect

- Gatekeeping provision: it conditions the availability of internal-model treatment, so it is logically prior to model-based capital rules even though it appears unlinked here.
- Currently isolated in the graph — researchers should read it against the internal models and standardised fallback provisions to see the consequence of failing the test.
