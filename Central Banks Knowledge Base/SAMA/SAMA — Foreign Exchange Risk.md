# Foreign Exchange Risk

Concept node for foreign exchange (FX) risk as one of the market risk classes under SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk, including its treatment in the simplified standardised approach and in internal risk transfers (FX and commodities risk in the banking book fall within the market risk capital scope). Capital requirements for FX risk are summed with interest rate, equity and commodity risk, plus non-delta option risk add-ons. Applies to banks computing market risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
