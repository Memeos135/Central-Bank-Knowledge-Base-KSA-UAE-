# Expired National ID and Iqama Handling Circular

A SAMA circular from the Finance Companies Supervision Department (1442H) transmitting earlier e-mailed instructions on how financial institutions must treat expired National ID and Iqama documents. Relying on the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, the CTF Implementing Regulations and the CDD section of SAMA's AML/CTF Guide, it confirms that IDs covered by the Ministry of Interior and Passports Directorate validity-extension announcements (COVID-era) may be treated as valid for customer due diligence purposes. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, BNPL/finance
