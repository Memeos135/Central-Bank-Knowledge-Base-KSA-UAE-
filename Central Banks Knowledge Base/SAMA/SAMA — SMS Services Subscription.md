# SMS Services Subscription

SAMA circular, following a communication from the Minister of Communications and Information Technology on class (B) licence terms, requiring all financial institutions operating in the Kingdom to contract only with SMS service providers licensed by the Communications and Information Technology Commission (CITC), with licence status verifiable on the CITC website. It binds banks, finance companies, insurance-sector companies, payment companies and exchange centres (categories A and B), effective from its date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other
