# Definition of Loss (Economic Loss)

Defines loss for operational risk capital purposes: gross loss, net loss and recoveries, what must be included in gross loss (direct P&L charges, consequential external costs, provisions, pending losses), and the loss event thresholds and data used in the internal loss multiplier and related disclosure. It requires documented policies for building and maintaining the loss data set, with evidence available to SAMA on request. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to operational risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
