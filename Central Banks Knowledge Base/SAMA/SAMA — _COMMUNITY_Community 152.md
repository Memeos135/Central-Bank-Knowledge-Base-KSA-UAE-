# Delivery-versus-Payment Settlement

## Why this community

Capital treatment of transactions that have not settled on the agreed date, distinguishing delivery-versus-payment (and payment-versus-payment) settlement from free-delivery exposures. The problem-space is how long a trade remains unsettled and what capital charge attaches from the contractual settlement date onward.

## How members connect

- All members turn on the same defined settlement mechanism (simultaneous exchange of delivery and payment), which is the gateway condition for the lighter unsettled-transaction treatment.
- The capital-treatment provision is the parent obligation; the DvP entries supply the definitional scope it cross-references.
- Duplicate DvP labels indicate the same defined term surfacing in more than one part of the framework, so scope should be read consistently across both.
- Linkage is definition-to-charge: failure to meet the DvP condition shifts the exposure into a different, more punitive capital category.
