# AML/CTF & Suspicious Transactions Principles

The AML/CTF component of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions, placed within the section on combating financial and administrative corruption alongside anti-bribery. It obliges personnel of a Financial Institution to observe conduct standards on anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing and suspicious transactions as part of the institution's board-approved code of conduct, and interacts with the separate, substantive AML/CTF Law and Rules and with the whistleblowing and confidentiality principles in the same document. It binds board members, executives, employees, contractors, consultants and third-party staff of banks, insurers, finance and finance-lease registration companies and credit bureaus supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
