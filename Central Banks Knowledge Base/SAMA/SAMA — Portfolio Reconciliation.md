# Portfolio Reconciliation

Sets the requirement for banks to agree, document and implement portfolio reconciliation processes with financial and non-financial counterparties for non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives, ensuring material terms are exchanged and valuations (including variation margin) reconciled at regular intervals. Scope and frequency must be calibrated to the counterparty's risk exposure profile, size, volatility and number of outstanding transactions. Binds banks entering non-centrally cleared OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
