# Template OV1 - RWA Overview

Pillar 3 template giving an overview of total risk-weighted assets — the denominator of the risk-based capital ratios — broken down by risk type and calculation approach, with corresponding minimum capital requirements (normally RWA × 8%). Rows cover credit risk (standardised, F-IRB/A-IRB, supervisory slotting), counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity positions, equity investments in funds, settlement risk, securitisation, market risk, operational risk, trading/banking book switches and amounts below deduction thresholds. Binds banks making SAMA Pillar 3 disclosures; linked to KM1 row 4.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
