# SAMA Circular on Debt Collection Controls via External Service Providers

A SAMA circular reaffirming that finance providers remain responsible for compliance with SAMA's debt collection controls and procedures for individual customers when collection tasks are outsourced to external service providers. Financiers must verify that outsourced collectors comply and that those providers have procedures in place to ensure compliance, with SAMA reserving supervisory action against violators. Addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom; note it does not displace the underlying collection rules but allocates accountability for third-party conduct.
