# Acceptance of Digital Identity from Absher and Tawakkalna

A SAMA circular requiring financial institutions to accept the electronic/digital national identity available through Absher (Afrad) and Tawakkalna — including the QR-coded digital ID, which carries the same legal effect as the physical ID card under the Civil Affairs Law and its implementing regulations — for existing customers in transactions that do not require taking a copy of the ID. Institutions must continue to assess and review risks arising from reliance on electronic identity and must establish a suitable means of obtaining an electronic copy where a copy is required. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA, effective from its date, and interacts directly with identification/verification duties under the AML/CTF regime.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection, data/credit info
