# Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies

SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations and effective from the date stated in the covering circular. It sets a minimum standard for Shariah governance practice, covering preliminary provisions and definitions (Committee, Committee members, independent member, Shariah supervision), board and senior management duties, Shariah supervision, rules governing the Shariah Committee's work, independence and confidentiality. It binds licensed finance companies carrying on real estate finance, production asset finance, SME finance, finance lease, credit card finance and consumer finance; for other licensed finance activities it is guidance only unless other rules make provisions mandatory, and SAMA may extend it at any time.
