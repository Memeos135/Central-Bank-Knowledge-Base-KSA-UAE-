# BCM Governance

Sets the governance layer of the SAMA BCM Framework: the board or a delegated executive holds ultimate responsibility for the BCM program, must allocate sufficient budget, and must mandate a BCM committee with a charter and at least quarterly meetings. It also requires a staffed BCM function, an appointed and suitably qualified BCM manager/head with adequate authority, and cross-functional strategic, tactical and operational teams. Binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
