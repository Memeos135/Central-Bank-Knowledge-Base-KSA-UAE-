# Currency Law

The Currency Law, issued by Royal Decree No. 6 of 1379H, is the foundational monetary statute establishing the Saudi Riyal as the national monetary unit, its subdivisions and parity value, and SAMA's exclusive privilege to mint, print and issue Saudi currency. It sets the rules on full gold and foreign-currency cover for issued notes and coins, legal tender status and discharge power, replacement and withdrawal of unfit or damaged currency, seizure of counterfeit money, record-keeping and periodic publication of the issuance position. It binds SAMA (with roles reserved to the Council of Ministers and the Minister of Finance) and, as to legal tender and acceptance limits, the public generally.
