# Payment Systems Standards

## Why this community

Oversight of systemically important payment systems — the standards a designated system operator must meet for safety, efficiency and risk management.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; the standards function as the apex of the payments oversight regime and should be read alongside the payments/PSP licensing and operational rules held elsewhere in the knowledge base.
- Key scope question is designation: the obligations bite only on systems assessed as systemically important, so applicability analysis precedes compliance analysis.
