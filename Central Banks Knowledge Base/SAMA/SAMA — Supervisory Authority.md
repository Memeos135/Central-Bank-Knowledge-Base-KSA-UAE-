# Supervisory Authority

This node defines the Supervisory Authority under the counter-terrorism financing law as the body responsible for verifying compliance by FIs, DNFBPs and NPOs with the Law, its Implementing Regulation and related decisions, and sets out the ladder of administrative measures available on non-compliance. Measures range from written warnings and compliance orders to fines up to SAR 5,000,000 per violation, employment bans, restriction or removal of directors and senior management, business suspension and licence withdrawal. It binds supervised entities and their directors, board members and executive or supervisory management personally.
