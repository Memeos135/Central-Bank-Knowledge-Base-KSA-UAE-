# Template LI1 - Accounting vs Regulatory Consolidation

This node covers the Pillar 3 linkage disclosures reconciling accounting and regulatory consolidation, principally Template LI1 (mapping financial-statement carrying values to regulatory risk categories) alongside the related CC1/CC2 capital composition and reconciliation templates and the G-SIB TLAC1–TLAC3 templates. It sets out the three-step approach banks must follow to connect published balance sheet figures to disclosed regulatory capital. Mandatory for banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, with TLAC templates applying only to designated G-SIBs from the TLAC conformance date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
