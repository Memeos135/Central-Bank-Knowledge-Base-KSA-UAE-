# Loan Splitting Approach

A node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (Version 2.1, December 2022) addressing the loan-splitting method for determining risk weights, used in the real estate exposure class where an exposure is divided into a portion secured by property value and a residual portion weighted on the counterparty. Binds banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy framework when computing risk-weighted assets. Note that the context extracted here concerns the phased rollout of the IRB approach across asset classes and business units (implementation plan agreed with SAMA, no capital relief for intra-group transactions), so confirm the exact paragraph before relying on it.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
