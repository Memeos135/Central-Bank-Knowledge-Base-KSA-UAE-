# Rules on Outsourcing for Finance Companies

The Rules on Outsourcing for Finance Companies set the regulatory framework for licensed finance companies that have entered or intend to enter outsourcing contracts with external service providers, read alongside the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. They impose a board-approved annual outsourcing policy, risk review at inception and renewal, due diligence on provider licensing and capability, mandatory contract content, SAMA non-objection for material functions, monitoring, business continuity and record retention. Non-compliance is a violation of the Finance Companies Control Law, and the finance company remains responsible for the provider's conduct.
