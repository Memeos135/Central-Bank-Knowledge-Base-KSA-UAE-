# Collateral and Early Termination Provisions

A section of SAMA-adopted Basel Committee sound practices on banks' interactions with highly leveraged institutions (HLIs), addressing collateral, early termination and other contractual provisions as credit risk mitigants. It expects banks to align margining terms, haircuts, initial margin, minimum transfer amounts, loss thresholds, two-way margining and rehypothecation rights with counterparty credit quality, and to recognise residual liquidity, legal, custody and operational risks and wrong-way correlation between counterparty default and collateral impairment. It binds banks dealing with HLI counterparties in secured lending, repo and OTC derivatives.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
