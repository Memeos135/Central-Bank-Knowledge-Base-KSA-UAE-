# SEC-IAA Internal Assessment Approach

Concept node for the Internal Assessment Approach, which permits a bank to use its own internal assessments of exposures to ABCP-type programmes as a proxy for external ratings when determining securitisation capital charges, subject to supervisory qualifying conditions. It sits within the securitisation hierarchy alongside SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA and SEC-SA and is referenced in RWA reporting as a modelled approach requiring SAMA approval. It binds banks seeking to apply internal assessments to unrated securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
