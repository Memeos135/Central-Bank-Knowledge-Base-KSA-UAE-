# Template CR3 - Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques

Template CR3 is the credit risk mitigation overview disclosure sitting in the credit risk section of SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, alongside Table CRC (qualitative CRM disclosure). It shows the extent to which exposures are secured by collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives. It binds banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure regime, with the surrounding section defining the overall credit risk disclosure suite (CRA, CR1–CR5) and its exclusions for securitisation and counterparty credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
