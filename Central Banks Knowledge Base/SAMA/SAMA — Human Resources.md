# Human Resources

Sub-domain 3.3.1 of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework (Operations and Technology domain), requiring cyber security requirements to be embedded in HR processes across the employment lifecycle. Covers security responsibilities and non-disclosure clauses in staff agreements, awareness training at onboarding and during employment, disciplinary triggers, screening and background checks, and post-employment steps such as revoking access and returning assets. Binds Member Organizations in respect of their staff.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
