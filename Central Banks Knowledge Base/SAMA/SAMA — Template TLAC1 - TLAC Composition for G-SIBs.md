# Template TLAC1 - TLAC Composition for G-SIBs

Pillar 3 template disclosing the composition of total loss-absorbing capacity (TLAC) at resolution group level, including eligible instruments, deductions, adjusted RWA, leverage exposure measure, TLAC ratios and buffer requirements. Completed once for single point of entry groups and per resolution group under the multiple point of entry approach. Mandatory only for banks designated as G-SIBs, effective from the TLAC conformance date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
