# Template LR2 - Leverage Ratio Common Disclosure Template

The common disclosure template for the leverage ratio within SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, presenting the components of Tier 1 capital and the leverage ratio exposure measure and the resulting ratio. It sits alongside Template LR1 (reconciliation) and feeds the leverage metrics reported in Template KM1, binding banks on a quarterly, fixed-format basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
