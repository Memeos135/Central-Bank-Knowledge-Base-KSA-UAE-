# Basel III Counterparty Exposure

## Why this community

Basel III post-crisis measurement of counterparty credit exposure and leverage: SA-CCR and the Internal Models Method for deriving EAD on derivatives and SFTs, capital for exposures to central counterparties, and the leverage ratio exposure measure.

## How members connect

- SA-CCR is the computational spine: EAD is built from replacement cost plus the PFE add-on, which in turn depends on netting sets, hedging sets, effective notional, supervisory delta, supervisory factors, maturity factor and the PFE multiplier.
- IMM is the alternative permission-based route, conditioned on model validation, stress testing, margin period of risk, alpha multiplier and wrong-way risk (including specific WWR) controls.
- CCP exposure capital reuses the same EAD outputs: trade exposures and default fund exposures to a qualifying CCP are calculated from KCCP and clearing-member contributions, so SA-CCR must be settled first.
- The leverage ratio framework consumes these measures as inputs — derivative exposures, SFT exposures, off-balance-sheet items with credit conversion factors, written credit derivatives and cash variation margin — against the Tier 1 capital measure; the Basel III finalisation document is the parent instrument for all of it.
