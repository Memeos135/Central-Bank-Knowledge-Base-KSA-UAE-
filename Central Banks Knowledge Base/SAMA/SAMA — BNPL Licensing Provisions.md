# BNPL Licensing Provisions

Extract from the Rules Governing BNPL Companies setting the definitions chapter and the licensing regime: BNPL activity is defined as financing enabling a Consumer to purchase goods or services without bearing a term cost, carried on by a joint stock company licensed by SAMA. It prohibits conducting BNPL activity without a SAMA licence, restricts licensees to the licensed activity absent SAMA's prior written non-objection, and covers verification steps, a five-year licence term with renewal three months before expiry, revocation on request subject to creditor/consumer rights and financial stability, and licence fees. It binds companies licensed (or applying to be licensed) by SAMA for BNPL activity, alongside the Finance Companies Control Law and senior-appointment requirements.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk
