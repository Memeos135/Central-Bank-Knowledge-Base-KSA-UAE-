# Remote Opening of Bank Accounts

This node covers the Rules Governing Bank Accounts provisions on opening bank accounts remotely (non-face-to-face), including for Saudi resident corporations and for foreign companies licensed under the Foreign Investment Law. It requires identity verification of the entity and its ownership/control structure from reliable, independent sources, risk standards set before approval, and a secure mechanism for activating the ATM card; for foreign companies the service is limited to the general director named in the constitutive documents. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk
