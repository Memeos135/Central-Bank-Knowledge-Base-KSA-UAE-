# Rules on Liquidity Risk Management

SAMA Rules on Liquidity Risk Management for finance companies, covering liquidity risk governance, board and senior management responsibilities, risk tolerance, strategies and policies, contingency funding plans and stress testing. Binds finance companies licensed under the Finance Companies Control Law, with reporting expectations toward the Board and SAMA.
