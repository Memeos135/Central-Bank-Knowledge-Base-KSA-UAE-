# Physical Security

Sub-domain 3.3.2 of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework (Operations and Technology domain), addressing physical protection of information assets and facilities as a component of the overall cyber security control set. Like other sub-domains, its controls must be defined, approved, implemented, monitored and periodically evaluated, and are scored against the six-level maturity model. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
