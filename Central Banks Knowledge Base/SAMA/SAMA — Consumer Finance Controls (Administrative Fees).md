# Consumer Finance Controls (Administrative Fees)

This node covers the administrative-fee amendments issued alongside the Service Fee Guide, which revise Article 9 of the Consumer Finance Controls, the relevant conduct rule in the Principles and Rules of Financial Consumer Protection, and the administrative-fee clause of the standard-form individual consumer finance contract. The effect is that banks and finance companies must charge administrative fees only in line with SAMA's fee instructions, may not deduct them before contract signature (with a carve-out for real estate valuation costs after preliminary approval), and remain subject to a ceiling expressed as the lower of a fixed riyal amount or a percentage of the finance amount, excluding taxes. It binds financiers (banks and finance companies) dealing with individual consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance
