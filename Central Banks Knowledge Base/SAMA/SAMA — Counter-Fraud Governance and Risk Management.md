# Counter-Fraud Governance and Risk Management

Section 3.1 of the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements, setting the governance and risk-management control expectations for Member Organizations. It requires a proportionate risk-based counter-fraud programme monitored via KPIs, ultimate Board responsibility for the governance structure and tone from the top, a Counter-Fraud Governance Committee meeting at least quarterly to approve and monitor the programme, policy and MI, operational collaboration with cyber security and financial crime functions, defined KRIs against fraud risk appetite, intelligence monitoring, periodic enterprise-wide fraud risk assessment, counter-fraud involvement before new product launch, vetting of third-party counter-fraud providers, and periodic independent audit.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance
