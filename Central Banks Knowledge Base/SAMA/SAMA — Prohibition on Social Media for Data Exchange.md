# Prohibition on Social Media for Data Exchange

Short SAMA circular (issued through the Payment Systems and Companies Control Department) prohibiting the use of social media and instant messaging applications to exchange any data or official documents, directly or indirectly. Such exchanges must instead occur through official, secure and approved channels, given the risks to institutions' activities and to individuals' interests. It binds all financial institutions and takes effect from its date.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, payments
