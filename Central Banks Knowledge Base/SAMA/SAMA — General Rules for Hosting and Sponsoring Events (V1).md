# General Rules for Hosting and Sponsoring Events (V1)

First edition of SAMA's General Rules for Hosting and Sponsoring Events, setting minimum requirements a supervised Financial Institution must meet when hosting or sponsoring conferences, forums, seminars and similar gatherings inside or outside the Kingdom. It covers board-approved event policy, documentation and annual board reporting, conduct obligations of representatives (confidentiality, avoidance of political/sensitive topics, no binding commitments without express authority), and due diligence on event content, participants, sponsors and speakers. Binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision; it excludes temporary offices opened for marketing/product display, which fall under separate SAMA instructions, and has been superseded by the second edition.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
