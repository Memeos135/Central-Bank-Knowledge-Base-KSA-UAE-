# Board Remuneration Report

## Why this community

Governance disclosure of board and board-committee remuneration — what must be reported, to whom and in what form.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; no internal cross-references, so its obligations must be read against the wider corporate governance and disclosure regime rather than against peers here.
- Relevance is mainly as a reporting duty feeding supervisory oversight of governance and remuneration practices.
