# License Application Guidelines for Consumer Microfinance

SAMA guidelines explaining how founders/shareholders apply for a licence to engage in consumer microfinance activity under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. It sets submission instructions (unmodified forms, authorised submitters, soft-copy filing, accuracy of information) and lists the documents required, including articles of association, shareholder list, organisational structure, feasibility study and business plan, fit and proper forms, and a bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital. Providing false or incomplete information is treated as a breach of the Finance Companies Control Law and can lead to refusal or cancellation of the licence.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk
