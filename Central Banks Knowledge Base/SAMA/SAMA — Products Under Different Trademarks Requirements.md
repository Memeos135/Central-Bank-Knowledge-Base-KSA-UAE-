# Products Under Different Trademarks Requirements

SAMA circular setting minimum standards where a financial institution offers or promotes products under a trademark different from its original corporate identity and trade name, issued under the SAMA Law and aimed at customer protection, disclosure and transparency. Requirements include addressing brand/identity impersonation, cyber and financial fraud risks in the risk management strategy and policy, engaging brand protection service providers to detect impersonation (including on social media and websites), updating related policies such as usage and data privacy policies, registering the new/updated trademark with the competent authorities before use and notifying SAMA, running a promotional campaign clarifying the trade name, using trade name and trademark together in phone contact and in first SMS contact, and stating the trade name, trademark and licence number on websites and whenever referring to SAMA supervision. It binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA from the circular's date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk
