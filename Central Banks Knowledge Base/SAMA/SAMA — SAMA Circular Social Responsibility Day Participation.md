# SAMA Circular: Social Responsibility Day Participation

Arabic SAMA circular inviting supervised financial institutions to support the Kingdom's Social Responsibility Day (23 March annually, per Council of Ministers resolution) by launching development initiatives or awareness campaigns and reporting their publication contribution to SAMA. It is a participation/awareness request rather than a prudential requirement, addressed to all institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** other
