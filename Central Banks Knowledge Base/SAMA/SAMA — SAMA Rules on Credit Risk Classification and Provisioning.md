# SAMA Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning

Refers to SAMA's Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning, which the Problem Loans Rules must be read alongside; the extracted material here covers eligibility and management of credit risk mitigants (leased assets, third-party cash deposits and assimilated instruments, and guarantees) and general collateral requirements. It sets conditions for legal enforceability, documentation, notification of pledges/assignments, valuation and concentration risk for collateral used to mitigate credit exposures. Binds banks licensed under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
