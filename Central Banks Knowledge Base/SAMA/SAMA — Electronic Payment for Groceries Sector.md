# Electronic Payment for Groceries Sector

SAMA circular transmitting instructions (originally sent by email, effective from that notification) on readiness to provide electronic payment acceptance to the groceries and foodstuff retail sector, following the joint decision with the anti-commercial-concealment programme and other ministries mandating such outlets to accept electronic payment. It requires banks and payment service providers to be ready to open merchant bank accounts and e-wallets, to receive and process requests for SAMA-authorised acceptance means (POS terminals, QR codes) across all channels under the applicable regulatory and operational rules, to use merchant category code 5411, and to brief staff internally. It binds banks and payment service providers.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
