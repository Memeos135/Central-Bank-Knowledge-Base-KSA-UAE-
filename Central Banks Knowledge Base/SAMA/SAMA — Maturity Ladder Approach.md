# Maturity Ladder Approach

Concept node for the maturity ladder method used to measure commodities position risk under the simplified standardised approach (time-band based capture of forward gap and interest rate risk), read together with maturity-related rules in the standardised default risk charge, including the three-month maturity floor and the use of derivative rather than underlying maturity for offsetting. It also links to the RWA reporting definitions distinguishing standardised from modelled approaches. Binds banks computing market risk capital under SAMA's framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
