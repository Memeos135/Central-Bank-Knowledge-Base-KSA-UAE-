# PSIAs Rules

The PSIAs Rules (SAMA, September 2022) governing profit sharing investment accounts, cross-referenced by the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking. Banks offering PSIAs must monitor that account holders' funds are used in line with agreed terms, comply with the PSIAs Rules' risk management requirements, and adopt a formal policy addressing conflicting roles, fiduciary and agency duties, fair treatment of fund providers, and allocation of assets and profits between the bank and PSIA holders across investment periods. Liquidity policies and reporting must cover current accounts and PSIAs individually and in aggregate.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
