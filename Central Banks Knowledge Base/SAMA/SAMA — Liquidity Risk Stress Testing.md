# Liquidity Risk Stress Testing

This node sits within SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing and is framed by the implementation and monitoring provisions, under which SAMA assesses the adequacy of banks' stress scenarios, methodologies, assumptions, frequency and board/senior management involvement through supervisory review, ICAAP meetings and on-site examinations. The associated FAQ material confirms banks must identify their own risk factors and apply stress effects as at the reporting date, while SAMA may mandate ad-hoc tests using standard scenarios. Binding on SAMA-supervised banks; open the source for the specific liquidity risk scenarios and parameters.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
