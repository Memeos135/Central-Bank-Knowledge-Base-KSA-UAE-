# Independent Member

Defines and regulates the status of the Independent Member on the board of a SAMA-supervised financial institution, including annual board assessment of continuing independence, the member's duty to disclose circumstances that may impair it, and notification to SAMA within five working days if independence is lost. It sits within the wider board-formation rules (board size of five to eleven, at least two independent members or one third whichever is greater, maximum two executive members, three-year renewable terms, preferred twelve-year tenure cap) and the prior written no-objection requirement for nominating board/committee members or appointing leadership positions. It binds the board, individual members and nominees of the financial institution, and imposes conflict-of-interest disclosure duties on candidates and limits on concurrent directorships.
