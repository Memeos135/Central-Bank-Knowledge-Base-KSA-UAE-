# Personal Data Protection Compliance

## Why this community

Compliance by supervised financial entities with the KSA Personal Data Protection Law, covering lawful processing, data subject rights and accountability for customer personal data held under SAMA-regulated activities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the PDPL obligation set sits outside SAMA's core prudential regimes but binds the same regulated entities as a parallel legal duty.
- Interacts with SAMA regimes where customer data is collected or shared — CDD/KYC records, credit information, outsourcing and complaint handling — so scope overlaps must be reconciled rather than treated as alternatives.
- Hierarchy point: PDPL is primary legislation with its own supervisory authority; SAMA instructions do not displace it and should be read as cumulative.
