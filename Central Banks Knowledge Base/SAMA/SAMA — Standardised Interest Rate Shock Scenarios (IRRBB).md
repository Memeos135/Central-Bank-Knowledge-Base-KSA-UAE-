# Standardised Interest Rate Shock Scenarios (IRRBB)

SAMA's recalibrated standardised interest rate shock scenarios for interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), prescribing six shocks for economic value of equity (EVE) — parallel up/down, steepener, flattener, short-rate up/down — and two for net interest income (NII). It specifies currency-specific absolute shock sizes in basis points (including SAR 275/375/250 parallel/short/long), the parameterisation formulas and decay scalar (x = 4) for short, long and rotational shocks, a zero floor on post-shock rates, and worked examples. It binds banks with material IRRBB positions in each relevant currency, applied per currency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
