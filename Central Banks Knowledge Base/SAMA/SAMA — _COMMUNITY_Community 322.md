# Damaged Currency Compensation

## Why this community

Currency handling: the compensation treatment for banknotes damaged by security ink (e.g. from anti-theft dye packs), and the limits on redeeming such notes.

## How members connect

- Single circular within SAMA's currency-issuance and cash-handling authority, separate from prudential or conduct regimes.
- Practically linked to cash-in-transit incident and suspicious-cash handling, but the instrument itself governs only compensation eligibility.
