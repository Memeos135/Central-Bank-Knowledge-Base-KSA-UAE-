# Foundation IRB (F-IRB) Approach

Concept node for the Foundation Internal Ratings-Based (F-IRB) approach to credit risk as it appears in SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure instructions, where own-estimated PD is combined with supervisory values for other risk parameters. It matters here mainly for scoping and populating disclosure templates: F-IRB exposures are reported as modelled (non-standardised) RWA and, for counterparty credit risk, require a separate portfolio breakdown from A-IRB exposures in template CCR4. Binds SAMA-licensed banks that have supervisory approval to use IRB models for credit and counterparty credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
