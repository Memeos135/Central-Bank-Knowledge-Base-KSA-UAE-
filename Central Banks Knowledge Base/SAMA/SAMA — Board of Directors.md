# Board of Directors

Defined-term node for the bank's board of directors under the Shariah Governance Framework: the board holds primary responsibility for the overall framework and for the Shariah compliance of Islamic banking activities, approving the framework and all Shariah policies, exercising continuous oversight proportionate to the bank's size and complexity, supervising implementation of Shariah committee decisions, and providing risk-management mechanisms protecting profit-and-loss-sharing investment account holders. It also connects to the audit committee's role over internal Shariah audit deliverables.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
