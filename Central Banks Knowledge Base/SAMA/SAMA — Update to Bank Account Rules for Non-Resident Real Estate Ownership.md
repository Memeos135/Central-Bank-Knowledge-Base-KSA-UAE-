# Update to Bank Account Rules for Non-Resident Real Estate Ownership

A SAMA circular to banks operating in the Kingdom that amends the Bank Account Rules by inserting new account-opening categories for non-resident non-Saudi customers covered by the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law — one for natural persons and one for legal persons — so they can access banking services for the purpose of acquiring property or other real rights in the Kingdom. It sets the required documents (Kingdom-issued digital identity, passport, contact details and a Saudi mobile number linked to that identity, broker/developer agreement, foreign bank statement; for legal persons, MISA/NCNP registration with a unified number, authorised representative's ID, constitutive documents and board details) and general controls (verification through a reliable independent Kingdom source including biometric/attribute verification, no joint accounts or non-resident authorised signatories, limits on remote accounts, no payment or credit cards, property-related transactions only through approved Saudi electronic channels such as the Real Estate Registry, Ejar and SADAD, and ongoing validity checks and periodic data updates). It binds banks with immediate effect.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk, other
