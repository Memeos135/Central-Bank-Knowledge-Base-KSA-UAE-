# Prohibition on Requesting Promissory Notes for Credit Card Financing

SAMA circular prohibiting finance entities from requesting promissory notes or any commercial paper from individual (retail) customers when offering credit card finance products, issued in response to observed market practice and read alongside the existing instructions on finance entities' dealings with promissory notes and the Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals. It requires internal policies, procedures and controls to be updated, applies from 1 February 2026, and obliges firms to submit a remediation plan to SAMA's Consumer Protection Supervision Department within 30 days covering return or cancellation of previously obtained instruments, executed within six months. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
