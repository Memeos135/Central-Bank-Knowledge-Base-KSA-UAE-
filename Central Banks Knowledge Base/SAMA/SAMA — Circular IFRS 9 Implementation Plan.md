# Circular: IFRS 9 Implementation Plan

A SAMA circular, grounded in the requirement under the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law that finance companies follow international accounting standards, directing all licensed finance companies to begin immediate preparation for adopting IFRS 9 (Financial Instruments) covering classification and measurement, impairment (expected/forward-looking loss recognition) and hedge accounting. Firms must submit quarterly progress reports to SAMA starting from the end of the first quarter of the stated year and acknowledge receipt within one week. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
