# Beneficial Owner Inquiry Service for Non-Profit Associations

A SAMA circular informing supervised financial institutions of a National Center for Non-Profit Sector service allowing them to query beneficial ownership information for associations and non-profit foundations via the Center's portal. SAMA stresses that the service must be used to complete customer due diligence on non-profit customers; where an undisclosed beneficial owner or discrepancies in beneficial ownership data are found, the institution must apply its CDD measures and the suspicious transaction reporting requirements in the AML/CTF framework, consistent with the Personal Data Protection Law and SAMA instructions, and notify the Center.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk
