# Template CCR7 - RWA Flow Statements of CCR Exposures Under IMM

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring a flow statement explaining period-on-period movements in risk-weighted assets for counterparty credit risk exposures calculated under the internal models method (IMM). It is applicable only to banks with SAMA approval to use IMM and forms part of the CCR disclosure section of the Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. Binds SAMA-supervised banks using modelled CCR approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
