# Bring Your Own Device

## Why this community

Governance of personally-owned devices used to access member-organisation information assets, treated as a controlled-access and information-confidentiality obligation rather than a purely technical matter.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the BYOD control stands alone as a discrete requirement within the cyber security / information asset protection domain.
- Legally dependent on upstream duties for asset classification, access authorisation and accountability of the member organisation for data accessed off-premises.
- Interacts with consumer protection and data confidentiality regimes where customer information is reachable from non-corporate devices.
