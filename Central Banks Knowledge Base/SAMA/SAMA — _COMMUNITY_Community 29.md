# Trading Book Boundary

## Why this community

The regulatory boundary between banking book and trading book, and the classification and capital consequences that follow for instruments, fund exposures and internal risk transfers.

## How members connect

- Shared defined terms (trading book, banking book, trading desk) drive assignment, with the trading book policy statement and desk definition as the governance evidence for classification.
- Classification determines which capital regime applies: market risk minimum capital requirements versus SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk, with CVA attaching to derivative counterparty exposures.
- Fund and securitisation exposures are routed through a tiered measurement hierarchy — look-through, then fall-back — depending on data availability.
- Internal risk transfer rules are the anti-arbitrage hinge preventing free movement of positions across the boundary.
