# Bank Obligations

The bank-facing duties under the Bancassurance Rules: the bank is fully responsible for Authorised Employees' conduct of Bancassurance Activities and must ensure they are carried out consistently with SAMA instructions and transparency principles. A linked professional-conduct article requires honesty, fairness and competence, ongoing staff training and market awareness, adequate managerial, financial, operational and human resources, timely and clear disclosure of coverage, conditions, exclusions and restrictions so Clients can make an informed decision, and confidentiality of Company and Client data. It binds banks licensed under the Banking Control Law that market insurance products to their Clients.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential
