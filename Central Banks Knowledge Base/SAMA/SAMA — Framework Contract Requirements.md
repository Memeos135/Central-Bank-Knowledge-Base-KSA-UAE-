# Framework Contract Requirements

This node covers the contract and information-provision requirements chapter (Part Six, Chapter One) of SAMA's Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services. It governs what a licensee must agree with and disclose to the Customer before and during a payment services relationship — terms, fees, information duties and amendment/termination mechanics — and sits alongside chapters on e-money issuance and redemption and the provision of related payment services. It binds payment service providers licensed by SAMA, with a six-month corrective-action window from the Regulation's effective date.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection
