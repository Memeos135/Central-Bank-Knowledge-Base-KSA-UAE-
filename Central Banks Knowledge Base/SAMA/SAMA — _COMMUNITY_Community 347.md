# E-Wallet Fee Amendment

## Why this community

Pricing and fee treatment for e-wallet services, i.e. what a payment service provider may charge wallet holders and how changes to that schedule are authorised.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster amending an existing fee arrangement; its legal force is as a modification to the prevailing fee/tariff position rather than a standalone rule.
- Consumer protection interaction: fee changes to retail wallet users engage disclosure and prior-notification expectations, which the amendment presupposes.
