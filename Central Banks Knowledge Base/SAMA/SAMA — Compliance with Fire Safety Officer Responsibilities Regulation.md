# Compliance with Fire Safety Officer Responsibilities Regulation

A SAMA circular reminding all supervised financial institutions to comply with the Ministry of Interior / Civil Defense regulation on the responsibilities and duties of the fire safety, prevention and protection officer. It highlights appointing or designating a qualified safety officer meeting the prescribed conditions, maintaining and testing fire safety systems, periodic inspection and maintenance, preparing and updating evacuation and emergency plans with drills, and documenting and retaining safety records. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision as an operational safety compliance obligation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other
