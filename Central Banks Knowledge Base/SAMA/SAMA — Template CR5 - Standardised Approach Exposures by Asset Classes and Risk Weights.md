# Template CR5 - Standardised Approach Exposures by Asset Classes and Risk Weights

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to break down standardised approach credit risk exposures (post-CCF and post-CRM) across regulatory asset classes and the applicable risk weight buckets. It supports comparison of risk weight distribution and sits alongside CR4 in the standardised credit risk disclosure set, excluding counterparty credit risk, CVA and banking book securitisation exposures. It binds banks supervised by SAMA that use the standardised approach for some or all exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
