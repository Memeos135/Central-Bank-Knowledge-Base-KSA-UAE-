# SAMA Circular: Revised Amended LCR Regulations

SAMA circular (November 2014) to all banks issuing a revised package of Amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio regulations and guidance, following a comprehensive review of SAMA's Basel III rules against BCBS standards to close identified gaps. The package comprises general guidance, specific guidance, a prudential return, FAQs and national discretions, and states that banks are expected to benchmark liquidity practices against the BCBS 2008 principles for sound liquidity risk management. It supersedes/updates the earlier 2013 Amended LCR circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
