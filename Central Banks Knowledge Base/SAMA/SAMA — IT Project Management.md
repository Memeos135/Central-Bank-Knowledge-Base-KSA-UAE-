# IT Project Management

Sets out the formal IT project management requirements for Member Organizations: an approved framework, policy and procedure covering the project lifecycle from initiation to closure, detailed project plans (scope, milestones, deliverables, roles, risks), and mandatory documentation such as the project charter, requirements and information-flow analysis, feasibility and cost-benefit analysis. It also requires an IT project steering committee with business and technical representation, and risk assessment, mitigation and monitoring throughout the project lifecycle, with process effectiveness monitored and periodically evaluated.
