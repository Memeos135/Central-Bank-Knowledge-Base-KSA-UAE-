# Market Risk IMA Disclosures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure obligations for banks permitted to use the internal models approach (IMA) for market risk, covering how the model is scoped, governed and validated.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a prescribed disclosure table whose content obligation is triggered only by supervisory approval to use IMA for market risk.
- Sits in the disclosure layer of the market risk framework, reporting on the capital treatment applied under the substantive market risk rules rather than creating it.
- Definitional dependence on trading book / market risk scoping terms used across the market risk regime; disclosure scope follows model permission scope.
- Read alongside the quantitative market risk templates and the simplified/standardised disclosures, which are the alternative path where no IMA permission exists.
