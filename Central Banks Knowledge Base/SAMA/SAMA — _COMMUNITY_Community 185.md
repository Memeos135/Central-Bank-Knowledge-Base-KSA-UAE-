# eSAMA Portal Access

## Why this community

Administrative obligation on supervised entities to designate and maintain a main user for SAMA's eServices portal, governing authorised access to regulatory submissions and communications.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular; no internal references in this cluster.
- Functions as the procedural gateway for filings and notifications required under substantive regimes, so failures here can cascade into reporting breaches.
- Designation and delegation of the main user role carries accountability implications for governance and authorised-signatory arrangements.
