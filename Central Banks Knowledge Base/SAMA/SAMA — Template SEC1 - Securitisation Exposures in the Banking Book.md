# Template SEC1 - Securitisation Exposures in the Banking Book

A Pillar 3 template requiring banks to disclose securitisation exposures in the banking book, broken down by the bank's role (originator, sponsor, investor) and by traditional/synthetic structures. Together with SEC2 it captures all securitisation exposures, including those failing the risk transfer recognition criteria, whereas SEC3 and SEC4 are limited to exposures treated under the securitisation capital framework. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks subject to Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
