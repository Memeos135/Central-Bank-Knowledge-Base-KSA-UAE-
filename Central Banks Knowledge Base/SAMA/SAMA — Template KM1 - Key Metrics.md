# Template KM1 - Key Metrics

Fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to publish a five-quarter time series of key prudential metrics at consolidated level: available capital (CET1, Tier 1, total), RWA (including pre-floor RWA), risk-based ratios, buffer requirements, Basel III leverage ratio, LCR and NSFR. It also requires banks applying the ECL transitional arrangement to show 'fully loaded' ECL comparatives so users can see the transitional impact on capital and leverage ratios. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework, with prescribed row instructions and cross-template linkages to CC1 and OV1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
