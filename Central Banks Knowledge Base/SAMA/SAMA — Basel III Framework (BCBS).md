# Basel III Framework (BCBS)

Concept node for the Basel III standards as adopted by SAMA, covering the leverage ratio framework, LCR and NSFR liquidity standards and the associated available/required stable funding factors, netting and collateral treatment for derivative exposures. It operates through SAMA circulars that transpose BCBS documents with Saudi-specific adjustments (e.g. residential mortgage risk weights). Binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
