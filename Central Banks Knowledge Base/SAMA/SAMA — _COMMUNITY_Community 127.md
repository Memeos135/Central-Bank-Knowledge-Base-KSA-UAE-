# Operational Risk Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of operational risk capital under the standardised approach: historical loss experience, the business indicator and its subcomponents, and the resulting minimum required operational risk capital.

## How members connect

- OR1 (loss data) and OR2 (business indicator components) are input templates whose figures feed directly into the OR3 capital calculation — an explicit computational and cross-referencing chain.
- All three share the operational risk capital definitions (business indicator, business indicator component, internal loss multiplier) from the underlying capital framework.
- Together they operationalise the qualitative framework described in the ORA table; inconsistency between narrative and templates is a disclosure compliance risk.
