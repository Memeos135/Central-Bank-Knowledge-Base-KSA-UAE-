# Ongoing Monitoring of HLI Positions

This section addresses ongoing monitoring of exposures to highly leveraged institutions, expecting at least quarterly credit quality reviews of material HLI exposures (more often on exposure or volatility triggers), use of quantitative risk information such as VaR and internal stress results rather than net asset value alone, and understanding of risk concentrations. It also sets expectations for collateral management systems to capture all positions, mark to market at least daily, and revise haircuts regularly. It binds banks that deal with HLI counterparties, alongside board-approved credit risk strategy and policy requirements.
