# Resilience Domain

This node is the Resilience domain of SAMA's Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements, one of the framework's structural domains alongside Cyber Security Leadership and Governance and Cyber Security Operations and Technology. It frames the risk-based control architecture and numbering scheme, with the stated aim of safeguarding sensitive data, transactions and continuity of financial services in the Saudi financial sector. It is directed at entities recently established or at an early operational stage, including firms testing solutions under SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox.

**Regimes:** governance/risk
