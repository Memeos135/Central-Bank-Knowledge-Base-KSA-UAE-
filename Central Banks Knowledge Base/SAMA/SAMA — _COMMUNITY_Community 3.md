# IRB Risk Components

## Why this community

The core mechanics of the internal ratings-based approach to credit risk capital: the risk components banks estimate, how those feed the supervisory risk-weight functions, and the split between foundation and advanced permissions.

## How members connect

- A common input set (PD, LGD, EAD, maturity) is shared by every member; the risk-weight functions consume those inputs, so definitional changes to a component propagate through the whole capital calculation.
- Foundation versus advanced IRB is a permissions hierarchy over the same components — which parameters a bank may estimate itself versus take from supervisory values.
- Expected loss and provisions treatment closes the loop, linking the same parameters to the capital deduction/add-back mechanism.
- Adjustments such as the MSME firm-size factor sit as modifiers on the function rather than as separate regimes; the market risk framework is referenced for exposure measurement overlap.
