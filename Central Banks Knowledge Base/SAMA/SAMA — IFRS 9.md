# IFRS 9

Concept node for IFRS 9 as adopted by SAMA as the accounting reference for credit risk measurement by finance companies, supplying the definitions of financial asset, ECL (12-month and lifetime), PD, EAD, LGD, past due and default. The Rules map IFRS 9 stages onto SAMA regulatory categories (Regular, Special Monitoring, Substandard, Doubtful, Loss), with sub-tiers (Stage 2A/2B, 3A/3B) used for regulatory reporting only, not accounting. It matters to finance companies determining staging, default at 90 days past due, and the superseded definitions in the earlier Provisions Guidelines circular.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk
