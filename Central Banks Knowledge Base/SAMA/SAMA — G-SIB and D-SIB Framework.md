# G-SIB and D-SIB Framework

Appendix material explaining systemically important bank classification — FSB's SIFI concept, the BCBS G-SIB methodology (cross-jurisdictional activity, interconnectedness, size, substitutability, complexity) and the D-SIB assessment framework adopted through named SAMA circulars, including annual list review and disclosure. Its operative consequence in the LEX Rules is the tighter 15% (rather than 25%) interbank exposure limit where the lending or counterparty bank is a D-SIB or G-SIB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
