# Endowments and Bequests Account Rules

A node on accounts for endowments (awqaf) and bequests under SAMA's Rules for Bank Accounts; the retrieved context sets out the general dormant account regime (24-month threshold, dual supervision for reactivation, permitted credits, customer contact before five years) and the high-risk classification and conditions for expatriate accounts opened on temporary 90-day work visas, including employer notification, freezing and closure. It binds licensed banks in Saudi Arabia. Open the source for the endowment-specific opening conditions, which are not fully reproduced in this extract.
