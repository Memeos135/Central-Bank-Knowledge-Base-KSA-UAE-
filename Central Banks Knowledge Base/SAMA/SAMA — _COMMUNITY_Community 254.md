# System Configuration Management

## Why this community

Control over changes to the configuration of IT assets, addressing the governance/risk regime's requirement that system settings are authorised, documented and traceable rather than altered ad hoc.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the configuration management control stands alone but sits downstream of the IT governance framework's control objectives.
- Legally relevant as an operational-risk control whose failure is evidenced in audit and incident reporting to SAMA.
