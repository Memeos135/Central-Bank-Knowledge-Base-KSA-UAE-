# Short-term STC Capital Criteria for ABCP Conduits

This node addresses the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) framework as applied to asset-backed commercial paper conduits and the transactions they finance, alongside rules on use of short-term external ratings. It sets who determines STC compliance (originator/sponsor must disclose; investor must self-assess; originators retaining positions with significant risk transfer determine it), requires STC criteria to be met at all times, and restricts banks to solicited ratings from eligible ECAIs. It binds banks holding or originating securitization and ABCP exposures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
