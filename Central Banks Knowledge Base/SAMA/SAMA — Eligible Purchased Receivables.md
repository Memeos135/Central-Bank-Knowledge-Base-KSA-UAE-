# Eligible Purchased Receivables

Defines eligible purchased receivables and splits them into retail and corporate receivables, setting when the "top-down" IRB approach may be used instead of obligor-by-obligor default risk assessment. Corporate purchased receivables may use top-down only where eligibility and minimum operational requirements are met (chiefly for securitisation-bound receivables, and for on-balance sheet exposures only with SAMA approval); retail purchased receivables use the A-IRB approach. Binds banks applying the IRB approach, including for asset-class roll-out scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
