# EAD Calculation under SA-CCR

A node on exposure at default (EAD) estimation, covering both the advanced IRB own-estimate requirements (long-run default-weighted averages, margin of conservatism, treatment of off-balance sheet items and additional drawings) and the standardised counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR) route for derivative exposures. It sets expectations on reference data quality, non-capping of EAD data, and avoidance of instability in undrawn limit factor CCF approaches. It binds banks using internal ratings-based or SA-CCR approaches under SAMA's credit risk capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
