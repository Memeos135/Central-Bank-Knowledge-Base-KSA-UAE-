# Alternative Treatment for HQLA Options

Sets out the three alternative treatment options for HQLA shortfalls (central bank committed liquidity facilities, foreign-currency HQLA, and expanded Level 2 use) and the supervisory cap on their usage, expressed as a percentage of required domestic-currency HQLA including any regulatory buffer above 100%. It also imposes supervisor-level obligations on monitoring, disclosure and periodic self-assessment where options are used. Relevant to Saudi banks only as background — SAMA has not utilised any alternative treatment option.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
