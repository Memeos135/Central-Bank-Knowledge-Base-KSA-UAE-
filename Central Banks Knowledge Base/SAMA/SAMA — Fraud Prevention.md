# Fraud Prevention

The Fraud Prevention component of the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements, following the governance controls and read together with the framework's definitions section (Fraud, Fraud case, External Fraud, Fraud Landscape, Fraud Response Plan, Fraud Risk Appetite, Fraud Risk Assessment, Employee, Electronic Money, Electronic Money Institution). It frames preventive control expectations for Member Organizations, including e-money issuers and payment service providers, across internal and external fraud exposure. Useful for scoping which actors and fraud typologies fall inside the framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance, other
