# Supervisory Data Sharing Consent

## Why this community

Controls on onward disclosure of supervisory information: a supervised entity must obtain SAMA's prior no-objection before sharing data or correspondence received from or generated for the supervisor with third parties.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: the circular stands alone as the operative consent requirement, with no internal cross-references in the graph.
- Legally it operates as a supervisory confidentiality gate layered on top of general licensing/governance duties — the consequence is a prior-approval step, not a post-hoc notification.
- Interacts with other regimes where disclosure is otherwise compelled (e.g. outsourcing, audit, group reporting), so scope limits should be checked before relying on any exception.
