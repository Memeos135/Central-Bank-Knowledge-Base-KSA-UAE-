# Template CCR4 - IRB CCR Exposures by Portfolio and PD Scale

Template CCR4 requires disclosure of the IRB parameters used to calculate counterparty credit risk capital — EAD post-CRM, average PD, number of obligors, average LGD, average maturity and RWA — broken down by regulatory portfolio and PD scale. It is mandatory for banks using A-IRB or F-IRB for CCR exposures regardless of the EAD method, excluding CVA charges and CCP-cleared exposures, with separate templates where both IRB approaches are used. Disclosure is semiannual in fixed format (SAMA sets the portfolio breakdown) with narrative on model scope and RWA coverage.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
