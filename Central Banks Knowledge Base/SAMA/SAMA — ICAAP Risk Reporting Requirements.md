# ICAAP Risk Reporting Requirements

A SAMA Banking Supervision instruction specifying additional risk information banks must include in their ICAAP submissions, covering connected-party lending concentrations, liquidity metrics (LCR, NSFR, top-ten depositor and wholesale funding concentrations), off-balance-sheet derivative activity by currency, and leverage ratios including the legal leverage ratio under the Banking Control Law. Data is generally required on a year-end basis with three-year history. It binds all banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
