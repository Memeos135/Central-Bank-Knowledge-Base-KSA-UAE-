# Supervisory Slotting Criteria Approach

Concept node for the supervisory slotting criteria approach, used for specialised lending exposures where a bank maps exposures to supervisory categories with prescribed risk weights instead of full own estimates. In SAMA's Pillar 3 instructions it is reported as a distinct 'of which' line within credit risk RWA, separate from the standardised, F-IRB and A-IRB lines, and is grouped with the IRB approaches for template scoping. Relevant to banks using IRB-family treatments for specialised lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
