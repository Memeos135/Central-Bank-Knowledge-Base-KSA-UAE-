# SFT Exposures Treatment

Part of SAMA's leverage ratio framework, setting how securities financing transaction (SFT) exposures enter the leverage ratio exposure measure. It covers the bank-as-principal general treatment (gross SFT assets with no accounting netting, exclusion of securities recognised as assets, and the cumulative conditions for netting cash payables/receivables with the same counterparty), novated trades cleared through QCCPs, and the agency model — including the requirement that a bank indemnifying or guaranteeing both the securities lender and borrower compute the measure separately for each party. Binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating the leverage ratio.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
