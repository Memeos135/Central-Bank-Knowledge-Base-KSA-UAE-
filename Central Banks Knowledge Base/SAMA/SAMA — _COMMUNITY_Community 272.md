# G-SIB Indicators Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 public disclosure of the indicators used to assess global systemic importance, feeding the G-SIB identification and higher loss-absorbency regime.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster: a fixed-format disclosure obligation sitting in the Pillar 3 framework hierarchy (Basel standard adopted via SAMA rules -> disclosure templates).
- Scope is limited to banks meeting the size/indicator thresholds for the G-SIB assessment exercise; it is an indicator-reporting duty, not a capital requirement in itself.
- Links outward to capital adequacy and buffer obligations, since the indicators drive any systemic-importance surcharge.
