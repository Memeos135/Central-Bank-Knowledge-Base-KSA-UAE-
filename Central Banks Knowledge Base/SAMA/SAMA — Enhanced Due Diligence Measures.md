# Enhanced Due Diligence Measures

Section 4 of the SAMA AML/CTF Guide addresses enhanced due diligence for customers and business relationships classified as high risk, requiring institutions to define the risk factors driving high-risk classification and to collect additional information to understand risk and monitor transactions more closely. The surrounding context links EDD to third-party reliance arrangements, where enhanced measures and related information must also be obtained. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions applying the risk-based approach.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk
