# Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework

SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework sets the guiding principles for market disclosure (clarity, comprehensiveness, meaningfulness, consistency over time, comparability across banks), requires a formal board-approved disclosure policy and internal assurance over Pillar 3 data, and governs use of signposting to other documents, reporting location, frequency (quarterly/semi-annual/annual) and publication timing alongside the financial report. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Basel III disclosure regime, with responsibilities at board and senior management level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
