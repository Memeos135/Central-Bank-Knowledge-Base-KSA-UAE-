# Rating Overrides

## Why this community

Governance of departures from model- or rating-driven outputs in credit risk assessment, i.e. when and how a firm may override an assigned internal or external rating and what documentation and control expectations attach to that discretion.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the override concept sits inside the internal ratings-based credit risk regime and depends on the defined terms used for rating assignment and validation.
- Legally it operates as a conditional permission plus an evidentiary burden - the exercise of judgement must be justified, recorded and subject to independent review.
- Interacts upward with governance/risk expectations (board and risk function oversight of rating systems) and downward with capital calculation outcomes.
