# Audit Committee

Concept node on the audit committee as a board committee of SAMA-supervised financial institutions, drawing on the Key Principles of Governance (Principle 5 on board committees) and a related SAMA instrument on audit committees. Relevant to committee formation, membership and independence, and its mandate over internal audit, external auditor relations and financial reporting oversight. Applies to boards of institutions supervised by SAMA, excluding insurance and reinsurance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
