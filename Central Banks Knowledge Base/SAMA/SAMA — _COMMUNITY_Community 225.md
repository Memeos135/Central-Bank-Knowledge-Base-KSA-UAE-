# Foreign Exchange Risk Buckets

## Why this community

Bucketing and correlation parameters for foreign exchange exposures feeding the FX component of the market risk capital charge.

## How members connect

- Standalone calibration node whose legal effect arises only when applied through the FX risk charge provisions.
- Parallel in structure and function to the commodity bucket parameters, so the two should be read as companion annex-level tables.
