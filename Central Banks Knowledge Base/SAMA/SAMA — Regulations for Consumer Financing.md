# Regulations for Consumer Financing

Node covering SAMA's consumer finance rulebook material, with the supplied text being the Rules Governing the Calculation of the Annual Percentage Rate (APR): definitions (finance provider, borrower, amount of finance, total amount payable, total cost of finance), scope, and the requirement to compute and disclose APR consistently in advertising, offers, contracts, periodic statements and any other APR disclosure. The Rules bind all finance providers engaged in retail lending — banks and licensed finance companies — and are to be read together with the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, the Regulations for Consumer Financing, the microfinance rules, and the related SAMA clarification circular on APR calculation.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection
