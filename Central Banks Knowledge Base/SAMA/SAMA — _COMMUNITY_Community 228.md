# IRRBB Risk Management

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure on interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB): how a bank identifies, measures, monitors and controls IRRBB, including modelling and behavioural assumptions.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster in the banking-book risk branch of the disclosure hierarchy; it narrates the assumptions underlying the quantitative IRRBB measures.
- Depends on the IRRBB standards' defined terms (economic value of equity, net interest income, interest rate shock scenarios) and on the governance and risk-appetite obligations imposed on the board and senior management.
- Cross-regime link: the disclosure duty is a transparency consequence of the underlying Pillar 2 / supervisory review expectations on interest rate risk management.
