# Counter-Fraud Technology

Node on the technology and assurance dimension of the Counter-Fraud Programme, which must address people, process and technology proportionate to the size and nature of the business. It ties to requirements for intelligence monitoring, key risk indicators, pre-launch counter-fraud assessment of new products or services, vetting and monitoring of third-party or managed-service counter-fraud resources, and periodic independent audit of control design and operation. Binds SAMA Member Organizations; note the outsourcing/third-party accountability point.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments
