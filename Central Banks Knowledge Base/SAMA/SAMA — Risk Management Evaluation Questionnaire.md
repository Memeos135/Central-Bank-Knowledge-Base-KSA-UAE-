# Risk Management Evaluation Questionnaire

A concept node referring to the operational risk management evaluation questionnaire associated with SAMA's operational risk guidance, used as a self-assessment tool against the expected elements of a bank's Operational Risk Management Plan and Programme. It links to the governance architecture described in the guidance, including the Operational Risk Manager reporting to the Managing Director/General Manager and the Operational Risk Management Committee. Intended for bank directors, senior management and supervisory review.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential
