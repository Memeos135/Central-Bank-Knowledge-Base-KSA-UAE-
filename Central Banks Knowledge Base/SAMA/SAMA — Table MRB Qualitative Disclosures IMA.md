# Table MRB: Qualitative Disclosures IMA

A qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table for banks permitted to use the internal models approach (IMA) for market risk, requiring narrative explanation of risk quantification and model governance; the surrounding framework text sets out estimation standards for PD, LGD and EAD and related model requirements. It supports interpretation of modelled capital numbers rather than setting the calculation itself. It binds SAMA-supervised banks with model approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
