# Market Risk Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure of a bank's market risk profile — the narrative explanation of market risk strategy, governance and measurement that must accompany the quantitative market risk templates.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a qualitative disclosure table within the market risk section of the Pillar 3 disclosure regime.
- Sits in the hierarchy Basel framework as adopted by SAMA → disclosure rules → prescribed table format, so the obligation is to populate the table in the prescribed form and frequency.
- Paired in obligation terms with the quantitative market risk templates (standardised and internal models), which it contextualises for users of the disclosures.
- Defined terms (market risk, trading book, risk measurement approach) are inherited from the capital adequacy rules, not redefined in the table.
