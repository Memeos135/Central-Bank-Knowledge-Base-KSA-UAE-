# LEI Issuance and Renewal Agreement via Financial Institution

A template bilateral agreement between the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH), acting as the GLEIF-accredited Local Operating Unit, and a SAMA-supervised financial institution, allowing that institution to arrange issuance, updating and renewal of Legal Entity Identifiers (LEI) for legal entities it deals with. It covers the LEI governance framework (ROC charter, GLEIF master agreement, SAMA instructions), fees per issuance and renewal request with monthly invoicing and VAT exclusion, data protection obligations under the Credit Information Law and its Implementing Regulations plus SAMA and national cybersecurity requirements, amendment and severability terms, notices, and amicable settlement followed by Saudi courts for disputes. It binds the contracting financial institution and SIMAH.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, other
