# Write-off

Sets SAMA's expectations on when finance companies must write off credit exposures: only after collection and recovery options are reasonably exhausted and subject to Internal Auditor review, and within fixed maximum periods from Stage 3 classification (360 days unsecured, 720 days secured, 1,080 days for mortgages and corporate exposures) unless a more conservative internal policy applies. It confirms that write-off is accounting de-recognition only and does not extinguish recovery rights against the borrower or collateral, and that multiple exposures to one counterparty are treated at counterparty level. It also lists situations (including partial write-off of a forborne exposure) that do not allow re-categorisation of a forborne exposure as performing.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential
