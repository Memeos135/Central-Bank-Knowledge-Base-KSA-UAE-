# Annex 3 Exercise Notice Form

Annex III provides the form of Exercise Notice and the surrounding exercise mechanics under the SAMA securities sale and purchase master agreement. It operates with the Seller Exercise Condition and Buyer Exercise Condition (tested by comparing Market Value, or Default Market Value after an Early Termination Date, against the Second Purchase Price) to determine which party is the Exercising Party; delivery of the notice is an offer that the Undertaking Party accepts orally or in writing, with deemed acceptance after five hours. It binds the two counterparties and governs settlement of the Second Purchased Securities and Second Purchase Price.
