# SAMA Circular on Quarterly Risk Reporting for Debt Crowdfunding

A SAMA circular imposing periodic risk and financial reporting duties on licensed debt crowdfunding entities: a quarterly risk report covering the risks identified in the updated debt crowdfunding rules, mitigation measures, breaches of permitted limits, assumptions underlying risk assessment, provisions and adequacy of disclosure standards, to be reviewed by the risk and credit committee (if any) and approved by the board. It sets filing deadlines with SAMA for the risk report, quarterly and audited annual financial statements with auditor and board reports, and quarterly/annual prudential data templates certified by the chief accountant, CFO and CEO or managing director, with annual templates reviewed by the external auditor. It also requires publication of the auditor report, board report and quarterly and audited annual financial statements on the entity's website.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, banking prudential
