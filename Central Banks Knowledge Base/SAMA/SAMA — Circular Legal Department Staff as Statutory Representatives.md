# Circular: Legal Department Staff as Statutory Representatives

A SAMA circular notifying finance companies that, following a Ministry of Justice decision adding a provision to the Implementing Regulation of the Code of Law Practice, employees of in-house legal departments of private legal persons are treated as statutory representatives once licensed by the Ministry's General Department of Advocacy. Accordingly, finance company legal department staff may litigate for the company before the competent judicial bodies in no more than three cases at a time, subject to listed licensing conditions and documents (including a valid power of attorney and employment contract). It is addressed to finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance
