# Secondary Market

Defines the Secondary Market as trading of real estate finance entities' rights arising from primary-market contracts, and frames the Real Estate Refinance Company's mandate to build liquidity and stability in that market, acquire and manage finance obligations/rights, issue securities under the Capital Market Law with SAMA no-objection, and price its services under SAMA oversight. Relevant to real estate finance entities selling or assigning contractual rights — notably assignment to the refinance company without borrower or guarantor consent — and to the PIF-anchored ownership condition.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, other
