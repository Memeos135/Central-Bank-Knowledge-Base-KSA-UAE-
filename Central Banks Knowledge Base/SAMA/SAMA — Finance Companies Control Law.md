# Finance Companies Control Law

The primary statute governing the licensing, permitted activities and conduct of finance companies in the Kingdom, including core defined terms (Finance Company, Finance, Borrower, Consumer, License) and the requirement that finance activity be Sharia-compliant. It lists the licensable finance activities (real estate, production asset, SME, finance lease, credit card, consumer finance, microfinance and any other activity approved by SAMA) and empowers SAMA to license entities carrying on activities supporting finance activity. It also imposes hard prohibitions on finance companies — non-finance business, trading in currencies/metals/securities/real estate, deposit-taking without SAMA licence, unsecured finance save as the Implementing Regulations allow, and related-party/connected finance restrictions — and binds licensed finance companies, their board members and external auditors.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection
