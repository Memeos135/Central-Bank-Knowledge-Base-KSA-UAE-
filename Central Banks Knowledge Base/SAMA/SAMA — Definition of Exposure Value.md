# Definition of Exposure Value

This node sets out how banks must measure the exposure value of positions for large exposure purposes under SAMA's Large Exposure (LEX) Rules, covering derivatives-based positions (including sold credit protection, credit-linked notes and options) and exposures arising from clearing activity with a central counterparty. It specifies that option exposure values are default-driven rather than the risk-based capital measure, and tabulates CCP-related items (trade exposures, segregated vs non-segregated initial margin, funded/unfunded default fund contributions, equity stakes) with their applicable exposure values. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA when computing exposures against the large exposure limit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk
