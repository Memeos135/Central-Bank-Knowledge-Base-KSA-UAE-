# Penalties Publication Instructions

## Why this community

SAMA's framework for publicly disclosing supervisory penalties imposed on supervised entities — the transparency and reputational consequence layer of enforcement.

## How members connect

- Standalone instrument in this graph: it sits downstream of substantive obligations across all regimes, since any enforceable breach can feed into publication.
- Legal significance is consequential rather than conduct-defining — it governs what SAMA publishes about a sanction, not what the entity must do operationally.
- Relevant to decisions on remediation, self-reporting posture and reputational risk assessment across AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection and governance breaches.
