# Synthetic Securitization

Defines synthetic securitization as a tranched structure where credit risk of an underlying pool is transferred through credit derivatives or guarantees rather than by transferring the assets themselves, and places it alongside traditional securitization within the securitization capital framework. Related provisions define securitization exposures (including credit enhancements, liquidity facilities, swaps, tranched cover and reserve accounts), resecuritization and when a bank is an originator. Binds banks calculating regulatory capital for securitization exposures, with SAMA consultation encouraged where classification is uncertain.

**Regimes:** banking prudential
