relevant instructions, guidelines and notices

Article (4):

Without prejudice to any of the sanctions or
measures contained in any other legislation, the
Central Bank may, upon the violation by any
Financial Institution of any of the provisions
referred to in Article (2) of this Decision, impose
any of the administrative sanctions specified in
the Decree Federal Law.

Article (5):

The violator may appeal against the decision on
the violation, in accordance with the procedures
prescribed by the Central Bank in this regard.

Article (6):

The Central Bank may publish its administrative
sanctions in the various means of publication.
