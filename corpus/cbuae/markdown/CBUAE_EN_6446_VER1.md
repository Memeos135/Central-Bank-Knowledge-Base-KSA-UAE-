5. powers of the Central Bank with regard to the
supervision of Representation Offices.

In exercising its powers and functions under this

Representation Offices of Foreign Insurance

Companies, as well as public confidence in
the insurance sector.

This Regulation is applicable to all Representation
Offices of Foreign Insurance Companies and
Foreign Reinsurance Companies operating in the
State.

Article (16) Financial Reporting

16.1 A Representation Office must prepare
and submit to the Central Bank an annual
report with a summary of activities
undertaken by the Representation Office.

16.2 A Representation Office must submit a

copy of the Foreign Insurance Company
or Foreign Reinsurance Company annual
audited financial statements, which may
include an electronic copy, within one
hundred and twenty (120) days of the end
of the financial year after the laying of
such statements at its annual general
meeting in the country where it is
incorporated or established.

Article (17) Supervision

The Central Bank will examine the activities of
the Representation Office and/or conduct on-site
inspections of the Representation Office
whenever deemed necessary to ensure the
soundness of the application of the provisions of
this Regulation and other relevant laws and
regulations.

Regulation may be subject to supervisory
action and sanctions as deemed
appropriate by the Central Bank.

and sanctions by the Central Bank may
include withdrawing, replacing or
restricting the powers of the Director or
other members of the Staff of the
Representation Office, providing for the
interim management of the
Representation Office, or barring
individuals from the State insurance
