Subject: Recovery Planning Regulation

Introduction

The purpose of the recovery plan is to prepare a
Financial Institution to be more resilient to periods
of severe financial stress and guide it to stabilize
itself and restore its financial position and overall
viability.

The recovery plan is an important crisis
preparedness and management resource. It must be
designed with a view to its implementation in
distressed situations and must not be treated as a
compliance exercise.

This Regulation is issued pursuant to the powers
vested in the Central Bank under the provisions of
the Decretal Federal Law No. (14) of 2018
Regarding the Central Bank & Organisation of
Financial Institutions and Activities, as amended
and Federal Law No. (6) of 2007 Concerning
Organizing of Insurance Operations, as amended.

Scope of Application

This regulation applies to Financial Institutions,
which includes the following for the purpose of this
regulation:

1) Banks.
2) Insurance Companies.

3) Branches of foreign Banks and branches of
foreign Insurance Companies.

4) Any other Licensed Financial Institution
designated by the Central Bank, at its
discretion, as being required to implement
recovery planning.
