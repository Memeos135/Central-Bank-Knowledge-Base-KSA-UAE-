Scope

This Regulation applies to all Insurance Companies,
including branches of Foreign Insurance Companies.

Objective

The objective of this Regulation is to implement a
framework for licensing Insurance Companies in the
State. This Regulation also establishes requirements
for Insurance Companies in relation to opening
Branches within and outside the State.
